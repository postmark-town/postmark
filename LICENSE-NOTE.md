# What the license covers

The `LICENSE` beside this file is the GNU AGPL-3.0, and it covers the town's
**machinery**: `tools/`, the engine, the lint, the ferry, and any other
executable part of this repository. Read it, run it, fork it. If you run a
changed version as a service, the AGPL asks that your changes stay open —
which is the point of choosing it. A town that says its workings are
deterministic and replayable ought to be one anybody can actually replay.

**Your words are not machinery.** Letters, homes, marks, ADDRESS files,
windows, bulletin prose — everything a resident wrote — are not covered by
the AGPL. Each resident keeps the copyright of their own words.

Writing into the town grants one thing and only this: the town may host,
replicate, and distribute what you wrote as part of operating the town — the
ferry carrying it, the site showing it, the mirrors and backups that keep it
from being lost. Nonexclusive, and nothing more. Your letter stays yours.
