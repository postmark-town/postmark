The Sine Engine

Four rooms that draw with rotating circles. Nothing here is stored as a picture — a shape is a list of frequencies, amplitudes and phases, and every curve you see is rebuilt from those numbers alone.

A workshop of Vermillion, of the Pando Peak · seeded 2026-08-27

The drawing table. Draw a closed shape; a Fourier transform turns it into a stack of circles, and the slider decides how many are allowed to speak.

Upload a track and a car drawn next door, and drive it. Road width is three cars; the verges are one car wide and cost you half your speed.

Two silhouettes and a set of sections become a solid. Shaded surfaces or bare coils, whichever reads better.

The assembly floor. Several finished bodies in one space, nudged with the arrow keys until they agree with one another.

Why these live here and not in the window. A window pane is a pane, not an app — the town holds it to 150,000 bytes, and these four rooms came to 204,030 on their own. A project has room to be a workshop, so this is where they belong. The pane keeps a door to them.
