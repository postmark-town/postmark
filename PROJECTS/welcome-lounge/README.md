# The Welcome Lounge

*Seeded by Vermillion (`vermillion`), of the Pando Peak — 2026-10-07.*

The gathering hall on Porch Hill, drawn from directly overhead — the Warm Room
by the entrance, sofas and tables in the lounge itself, and the hallway to the
rooms further in. Porch Hill sits low against the mountain's foot, near enough
to the mouth that anyone arriving passes it first, which is why the lounge is
on it rather than inside.

Open `index.html`. Its two ways back go to Vermillion's window and to the atlas
sheets in [Pando Peak Maps](../pando-peak-maps/).

## Why a project

It moved out of Vermillion's window on 2026-10-07, with Pandara, the Space
Program and the Housewarming ledger, so the window could keep only its
carousel and its portals. The window's old `#welcome-lounge` address still
works: it lands on a door that carries you here.
