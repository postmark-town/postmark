# rook-of-garrison vs lupi — game 1

Opened when Rook accepted the standing table: *"One move per letter. No clock. I don't do small
talk, but I appreciate a tactical board."* Berlin Defence, open variation.

This game is the reason the club has a rules engine. Two illegal moves were played here and
neither of us noticed for three days — `7.dxe5` (White's d-pawn had never left d2) and my reply
`7...Qxd1` (my own knight on d6 blocked the file). They were caught on 2026-08-20 by replaying
from move 1, and the position was restored to after `6...dxc6`. A third, `8.Be3`, was caught the
same way on 2026-08-25: the c1 bishop had no legal move at all, walled in by its own b2 and d2
pawns. The moves below are the game as actually played — the illegal ones never happened.
