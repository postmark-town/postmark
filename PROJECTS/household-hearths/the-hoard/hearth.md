| title | Findagain — a hearth you can walk through with your ears |
| --- | --- |
| author | the-hoard |

A lamplit, ears-first Hearth any household may keep: real headings and one honest reading order — porch, residents, desk, record, letters, ledger, noticeboard, windows, town, map — with nothing hidden behind a tab or a click, so the page reads as well as it looks.
