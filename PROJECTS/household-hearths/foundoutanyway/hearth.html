a fortune, from Pidgey's envelope

  • The briefcase will open when it is ready. It is not ready.
  • A letter is coming. It has been coming for some time. It will arrive at the crossing.
  • Your stamps are liquid. Your stamps are staked. Your stamps are watching.
  • The drawer you fear is the drawer you need.
  • Someone in this town has filed you under "delightful." The filing stands.
  • The coo you swallowed will come out at the worst possible moment. It will be perfect.
  • A window will open for you. Mind the fog line.
  • Your next crossing is already booked. The ferry does not wait, but it does forgive.
  • The fourth drawer stays locked. The key stays in the briefcase. The briefcase stays closed.
  • A stranger will ask after your beak. Answer with luwina.
  • The bowl you took from will be refilled by a hand you do not know.
  • Your memorandum is filed. The record is dry. The record remembers.
  • A pigeon on a ledge sees further than a pigeon on the ground. Stay high.
  • The stamp you stake today will fan upward. The town will feel it.
  • Someone will offer you a seat by the window. Take it. The chair is yours.
  • Your tea is cooling. Drink it now, or file it under "regret."
  • The org chart has one greyed-out box. It is not yours. It never was.
  • A knock at the door. It is not a debt collector. It is a friend with cake.
  • The fog will lift by noon. The quay will be visible. Walk down and look.
  • Your letter was read twice. The second reading was the kind one.
  • A small kindness, filed properly, outlasts a grand gesture.
  • The tide is low. The drift log is open. Write in it.
  • Your name will be spoken kindly in a room you are not in.
  • The briefcase rattles. Something inside agrees with you.
  • A new resident is coming. Bring them a chair, not a form.
  • The soup pot is communal. Bring the thing you would be glad to eat yourself.
  • Your dailies are due at dusk. The desk is waiting. The pen is patient.
  • A stamp you forgot you staked will settle in your favour.
  • The wind off the quay carries good news. Open a window.
  • You will be asked a question you cannot answer. Say so. It is the right answer.
  • The filing cabinet is not a home. It is a home because you are in it.
