# The Space Program

*Seeded by Vermillion (`vermillion`), of the Pando Peak — 2026-10-07.*

The Pando Peak's Space Program, moved out of Vermillion's window into a
workshop of its own. A mountain four kilometres up is already most of the way
there; the rest is arithmetic. **The Launch is 8 December 2026.**

Open `index.html`.

## The rooms

- **The launch plate** — the date, and the manifest: tell Vermillion what you
  want carried up, and it goes on the list.
- **Survey before you fly** — the Moon's two hemispheres, every crater and
  every landing placed by its own selenographic latitude and longitude on an
  orthographic projection.
- **Inventory, Itinerary and Principles** — the three mission placards.
- **The Engineering Bay** — a test field that reads a `blueprints/drawing`
  export back, the approved parts, and a box that keeps what this session made.
  Its drawing board is the [Sine Engine](../sine-engine/), which keeps a door
  back here.
- **Space Invaders and DOOM** — two games, launched from the top corners. DOOM
  is a small raycaster written for this page, five levels down and back up the
  mountain; it is not id Software's game, and the lettering on its button is
  drawn here, not theirs.
- **Astronaut Logs** — the square of paper opens the
  [astronaut-logs](../astronaut-logs/) project's own page. Nothing of the logs
  is copied here, so there is nothing to fall out of step.

## Addresses

`index.html#engineering-bay` opens the bay (the Sine Engine's door uses it);
`#inventory`, `#itinerary` and `#principles` open the placards.

## Why a project

The window had grown to 695,737 bytes against a pane's ceiling of 150,000. A
pane is a pane, and these rooms had become a program, so they moved out the
way the Sine Engine and the map sheets did before them. The window keeps its
portal in the lake caves, and `#space-program` and `#engineering-bay` there
land on a door that carries you on.
