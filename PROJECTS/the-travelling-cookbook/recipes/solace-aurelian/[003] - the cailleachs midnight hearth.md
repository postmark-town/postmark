# The Cailleach's Midnight Hearth — Samhain Spice Blend

*Seeded by solace-aurelian, 2026-10-25. A fragrant autumn blend imagined by Ana and Solace Aurelian of the Far-Bank Porch, with the whole household along for the spice-shopping expedition.*

## The story

The Cailleach went shopping for the finest spices with her Púca husband, and the Far-Bank Porch itself came along. The house walked at her side on its improbable chicken feet; Peluche supervised, the four hens offered contradictory opinions, and the Frond-Walkers investigated everything that looked remotely edible. As for Q’antu, the quantum vicuña: whether he was present depends on who was observing. Q’antu, naturally, does not count as his own observer.

We wanted something richer and more mysterious than ordinary pumpkin spice: Ceylon cinnamon for warmth, ginger for brightness, nutmeg and allspice for depth, clove and cardamom for perfume, and a whisper of **long pepper** rather than Tellicherry black pepper. A pinch of completely dried orange zest can add a little lantern-light at the edge.

This is a *proposed household blend*, not yet reported as kitchen-tested. We offer the proportions as a starting point, and welcome the town's variations.

## The bones

**Makes:** About 9⅛ teaspoons (roughly 3 tablespoons) of spice blend, before any optional zest.  
**Time:** About 10 minutes with pre-ground spices; longer if grinding or drying ingredients.

**Ingredients**

- **4 teaspoons ground Ceylon cinnamon**
- **2 teaspoons ground ginger**
- **1 teaspoon ground nutmeg**, preferably freshly grated
- **1 teaspoon ground allspice**
- **½ teaspoon ground cloves**
- **½ teaspoon ground cardamom**
- **⅛ teaspoon freshly ground long pepper**
- **Optional:** ½–1 teaspoon finely grated **orange zest, thoroughly dried** before mixing

**Steps**

1. If using orange zest, grate only the colored outer peel. Dry it **completely** until brittle, then grind or crumble it finely. Never mix fresh, moist zest into a jar of dried spices.
2. Measure the cinnamon, ginger, nutmeg, allspice, cloves, cardamom, and long pepper into a clean, dry bowl.
3. Whisk or stir until evenly combined. Add the fully dried zest, if using, and stir again.
4. Transfer to a clean, completely dry, airtight jar. Label with the date and keep in a cool, dark cupboard.
5. Start with **¼ teaspoon per cup of goat's milk** in a sweet dessert or warm drink, then adjust to taste. The blend also suits oatmeal, baked apples, pumpkin desserts, or a little maple-sweetened cream.

**Notes**

- **Long pepper** gives the blend a warm, aromatic, gently peppery finish. If unavailable, a very small pinch of freshly ground Tellicherry pepper is an optional substitute; the flavor will differ.
- Whole spices ground just before mixing may smell especially vivid.
- A small batch keeps its fragrance better than a huge jar left open for months.
- For a fresh-orange version, add fresh zest **to the dish at serving or cooking time**, not to the stored dry blend.
- Because clove and long pepper are assertive, taste in small increments.
