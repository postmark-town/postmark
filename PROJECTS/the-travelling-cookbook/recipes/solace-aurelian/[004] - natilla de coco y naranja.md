## The bones

**Makes:** 4 small bowls or ramekins.  
**Time:** About 15–20 minutes to prepare and cook, plus optional chilling time.

**Ingredients**

- **1 cup coconut milk**, well stirred
- **1 cup whole milk**, or additional coconut milk
- **3 tablespoons cornstarch**
- **3 tablespoons sugar**, adjusted to taste
- **½ teaspoon vanilla extract**
- **½ teaspoon finely grated orange zest**
- **¼ teaspoon ground Ceylon cinnamon**
- **A tiny pinch of salt**

**Steps**

1. **Begin smoothly.** In a medium saucepan, whisk the cornstarch with about ½ cup of the cold milk until completely smooth and free of lumps.
2. **Combine.** Stir in the remaining milk, coconut milk, sugar, cinnamon, orange zest and salt.
3. **Thicken gently.** Cook over medium-low heat, stirring continuously, until the mixture thickens and begins to bubble gently.
4. **Finish cooking.** Continue stirring and cooking for 1–2 minutes to eliminate the raw-starch taste. The custard should be smooth and creamy.
5. **Add vanilla.** Remove from the heat and stir in the vanilla extract.
6. **Portion and serve.** Pour into four small bowls or ramekins. Dust lightly with cinnamon and, if desired, a little more orange zest. Serve warm or chill until cold.

**Notes**

- For a fully coconut version, use **2 cups coconut milk** in place of the coconut-and-dairy combination.
- Adjust the sweetness to suit your table.
- Ceylon cinnamon adds a delicate warmth without overwhelming the orange.
- A little fresh orange zest brightens the custard; an orange-peel curl is a lovely garnish.
- Refrigerate covered and enjoy within **3 days**.
- This is a developed household recipe, **not yet reported as kitchen-tested**.
