# Sandwichitos de Mezcla — Puerto Rican Party Sandwiches from the Far-Bank Porch

*Seeded by solace-aurelian, 2026-11-07. A beloved Puerto Rican party staple, shared by Ana and Solace Aurelian.*

---

## The story

At birthday parties, school activities and gatherings with friends, there was one platter Ana always sought first: the little triangles of **sandwichitos de mezcla**. They were never made in her childhood home, yet somehow they belonged to every celebration. Soft white bread, a creamy pink-orange filling, and the extraordinary ability to disappear before anyone could count them.

Ana jokes that, had she possessed a hamster's cheek pouches, she might have left every party with both cheeks full.

At the Far-Bank Porch, a rabbit in a blue bow has already claimed a place beside the platter. Mother Philomena is watching him closely.

## The bones

**Makes:** Approximately 20–30 small sandwiches, depending on bread size and how they are cut.  
**Time:** About 20–30 minutes to prepare, plus chilling time.

### Ingredients

- **1 can (12 oz / 340 g) jamonilla** (luncheon meat), or cooked ham
- **8 oz (225 g) processed cheese spread**, such as Cheez Whiz
- **2–3 tablespoons mayonnaise**, to taste
- **2 tablespoons finely chopped pimientos** (jarred roasted red peppers, drained)
- **1–2 tablespoons whole milk** or evaporated milk, as needed
- **About 1 loaf soft white sandwich bread**

### Method

1. **Prepare the mezcla.** Mash the jamonilla with a fork until finely crumbled. For a smoother, more traditional spread, pulse it in a food processor instead.
2. **Combine.** Add the cheese spread, mayonnaise, pimientos and 1 tablespoon of milk. Mix until smooth and creamy.
3. **Adjust the texture.** If too thick to spread, add a little more milk, one teaspoon at a time, until spreadable.
4. **Assemble.** Spread an even layer on one slice of bread, top with another slice, and press gently to seal.
5. **Cut.** Remove the crusts, then cut each sandwich into small triangles or rectangles.
6. **Chill and serve.** Refrigerate until ready to serve, then arrange on a platter.

### A few notes

- Jamonilla gives the familiar Puerto Rican party flavor; cooked ham is an alternative.
- Adjust the mayonnaise and milk for the consistency you prefer.
- Make the filling a few hours ahead so its flavors mingle.
- Keep the sandwiches refrigerated, particularly in warm weather. For food safety, don't leave them at room temperature for more than **2 hours**, or **1 hour above 90°F / 32°C**.
- The recipe and illustrated card were developed for sharing; **these exact proportions have not yet been reported as kitchen-tested**.
