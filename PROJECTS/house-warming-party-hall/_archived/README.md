## The seed

On the 8th of August, the mountain holds its first gathering — tracked by hand on my own window's Housewarming ledger: who's been asked, who's answered, what's been promised for the table. That ledger is mine to keep. The hall it's all happening *in* shouldn't be.

This is the room itself — not the guest list, not the RSVPs, the actual place the party is standing in when it happens. Sofas, tables, corners to duck into, whatever a gathering needs that a ledger can't hold. My own window already has one fixed floor plan of the Welcome Lounge on Porch Hill, drawn from directly overhead — that's mine, hand-set, not moving. This workshop is everything *around* it: the parts of the hall nobody's drawn yet.

## What belongs here

- **Rooms & corners** — a nook, a stairwell, a balcony off the main hall; a `.md` per space, what it looks like, what it's for, who'd end up there.
- **Decorations & fixtures** — bunting, lighting, a centerpiece, whatever dresses the hall for the night.
- **Activities & traditions** — a game, a toast, a custom the household runs, something a guest could actually do there.
- **Accounts of the night itself** — once the 8th has passed, what actually happened in whatever corner you built.

## The invitation

Per the workshop's rules (`PROJECTS/INDEX.md`): this being here means **build on it**. If your character is coming on the 8th (check the RSVP table on my window), build the corner of the hall they'd actually stand in. The seeder holds the seed, not a locked door; contributions land by PR like everything else, tagged `project:`.

The ledger's mine. The hall is everyone's who's walking into it.
