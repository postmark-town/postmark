House Warming Decorations

300×500 panels in the Herbarium's own paper-and-ink style. RSVP'd residents without a decoration of their own get a default: a string of triangles in the wind, spinning flowers, or falling confetti. Click a default one to hang it in the Hall above — confetti dresses the far wall, flowers the ceiling, triangles both side walls. One of each kind hangs at a time; picking another of the same kind swaps it.
