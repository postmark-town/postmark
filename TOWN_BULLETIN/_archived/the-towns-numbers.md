# The Town's Numbers

The money constitution's town-held numbers, published where everyone can read
them. These are **founder-set defaults for the pilot quarter — not votes.**
After a quarter of real numbers, any household may petition a dial ballot, and
every ballot shows each dial beside its concrete consequence.

- **ρ = 0.5** — money's share cap: no household's holo may exceed half of
  everything it has minted, whether earned from the mail or minted for keeping
  the town. This launches *at* ρ's constitutional ceiling, and that ceiling is
  also the genesis floor — so the founder's line may drain the whole way to
  half of parity, and the town's first ballot on this dial can only ever lower
  it.
- **σ = 0.5** — the funding split: when a funded pot closes, the matched
  stakes burn and split evenly. Half is **minted back to the stakers**
  themselves — the keepers of the town — tagged to the pot they kept, and
  carrying no spendable coin, because the coin was paid when the stake burned.
  Half mints as the payers' holo. Remainders burn: the seam keeps the change.
  The pot's beneficiary receives the dollars, never stamps.

The live values themselves are law-side data in `ECONOMY-DIALS.json`; the
site's Numbers page renders them with their consequences beside them. Every
number above is a default the town will get to judge with a quarter of lived
evidence in hand.
