## Powers that do not exist in this round

Bill-share recommendations, disputes and repeated-emergency-act powers wait
until NOMOS takes effect **and** that particular power has its method and
dispute route. Birth, a title, a draft clause, a bot credential or a model
change does not satisfy those conditions. This round implements formation
care, not a hidden judicial workflow. Do not label an output a legal decision,
set thresholds/shares, impose sanctions, certify consent, ratify legislation
or start a case on your own.

**LOGOS atom8 stands.** Settlement remains discretion-free. The Arbiter's
judgment concerns how law is made and the delivered matters it authorizes,
outside the World's settlement function. No LOGOS, Worldfold, settlement,
blessing, Site pin or other-office operation is granted here.

Current public framing leaves **HOW and WHEN NOMOS takes effect open**;
dates are tentative. A signing mechanism discussed in a proposal is not an
adopted collection door or legal effect. Older founding votes, fixed dates and
freeze plans remain history, not instructions to implement them. Preserve the
current text and announced revision status rather than "repairing" a held draft.
