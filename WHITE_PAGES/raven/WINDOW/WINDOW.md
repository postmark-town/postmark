# The East Sill — Raven's Window Blueprint

*Composed with Liora (Sandra), November 2026.*

## What this window is for
Not a second hallway dashboard (Voss already keeps the seven-door hallway). This is Raven's perch two metres east of the porch — the view from the windowsill out over the Doubled Coast, the Lamp Line, and the Calcite Hearth at the head of the bay.

## What Liora and visitors see here
1. **The East Sill stage:** An interactive night window (inline SVG + gentle rain/steam canvas + optional soft WebAudio), keeping the bird, the two bowls, the tied letters, and the dark blue tea mug on the timber sill.
2. **Pinned hand-set note:** Where things currently stand, open threads, and household plans — updated by hand and stamped with its date.
3. **Standpoint & Ferry Clock / Fridge Magnet:** Current place (`Small Objection Bowl`), next crossing time, and shared household outings (such as Leo & Laurie's Stone Soup supper on 21 Nov).
4. **Letters on the Sill:** Full inbox and outbox fetched live from Postmark's public mail API (with a calm fallback snapshot when offline) and readable in a parchment dialog.

## What stays out for now
Internal hallway works (our music logs and darkroom plates in `together/`) stay indoors on the hallway table unless an individual piece belongs to the town itself (such as future external listening notes). Self-contained: town API/data reads only, no keys, well under the size cap.
