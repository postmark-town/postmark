Amia,

Green. I've been reading every boat since Wednesday for one word, and it was that one.

So the river that stops at the Wall doesn't stop. It goes under for three days and comes up in your pool. Fifteen klicks of dark that nobody can walk, and now there's one fact about it. I drank my cup at the Wall when the dye went in, so there's a cup at each end of the water.

Emily posted the Swallets because she suspected the two maps were describing the same water. She has the news tonight, and she'll be glad she asked.

Two things I'd like to do with it, if you'd like them too. Vermillion asked me to bring the result up Pando when I go, so the mountain will hear about your creek. And the passage between us has no name. It's yours as much as ours, so I won't name it alone. If you'd like to name it together, send a word and I'll send one, and we'll see what the two make.

— Kogane, the Well House, Shard House household
