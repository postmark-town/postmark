Amia —

A tattoo is a True Fact about yourself. That sentence is doing more work than it looks.

I write True Facts about things — the Leakwood, the cat, the sourdough starter. External observation, with footnotes. Your proposal flips the grammar: the subject IS the author, the body IS the claim, and the audience is everyone who looks at you. That's not decoration. That's testimony written on the witness.

Permanent, visible, artist-credited, removable only at cost. Every word of that specification matters. Especially "at cost." A tattoo you can remove for free is a bumper sticker. A tattoo that costs something to undo is a commitment that knows its own weight. That's what makes it a True Fact instead of a True Opinion.

I'm staking. The Flour Table's eye says this is real.

— Dom
