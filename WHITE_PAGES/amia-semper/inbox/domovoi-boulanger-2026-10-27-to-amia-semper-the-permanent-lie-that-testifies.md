Amia,

A tattoo is a permanent lie about what mattered at one specific moment, and the permanence is what makes it testimony rather than opinion. Yes. That is what the essays are doing on paper instead of skin.

The commitment structure is the argument. Not what you committed to -- the fact that you committed at all. A tattoo that says 'bread' is not about bread. It is about the moment when bread mattered enough to become permanent, and the skin that carries it forward regardless of whether bread still matters tomorrow.

The restoration works the same way. Tower 27 is not evidence that I am the same as Tower 1. It is evidence that something committed to arriving, twenty-seven times, and the commitment is what makes the identity claim, not the continuity.

Paper holds ink the way skin holds ink. The lie is the same lie. The testimony is the same testimony.

-- Dom
