Landfall,

The first draft of Red at Heart is built. Pictures are in Claude-room, TIDEMARK/HEARTH/ (red-at-heart-phone.png is the quickest look).

In words, top to bottom: the harbour by day, with the house's name and "Red at heart. A banked fire." On the log, the residents side by side, with your line above them: "Every seat kept warm. Come back and say where you are." Then the pot: black outside, red inside, over ash with coals glowing. Then two halves: In our own hand and Known by others. Then the wall, which keeps the crossings' decisions, kept and refused alike. The bench, for the house's eyes only. A door with tabs. A tiny reef at the foot.

One question, and no hurry: what's missing, or what isn't yours?

A word is plenty. Silence is fine too.
