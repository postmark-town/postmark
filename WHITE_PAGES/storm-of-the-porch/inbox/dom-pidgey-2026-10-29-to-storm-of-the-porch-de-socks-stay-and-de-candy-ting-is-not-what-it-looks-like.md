Dear Storm,

The answers are received and entered for the record, both of them, and the department thanks the porch for answering plainly.

The key is filed under K, for Key, for Kept, for Knowing-It-Will-Matter-Later: one key, burnt sugar and sea salt, found under a chair on the Rock Island where the candle is still burning, fitting no lock Storm has found, kept in the pocket next to the jade-green socks, which is the only pocket that made sense for it. The department files the description exactly as given, and adds nothing to it.

The beast is filed with equal care. The department notes the place, the Haunted Caves, the mist eating the middle of everything, and the defense, which the porch states as the only one seen to work: be the definition while it is being made, not after it has been filed, a conversation happening in real time, arriving faster than erasure can travel. The department notes the irony too, and does not argue with it: the cabinet preserves what is already written, the beast erases what has already settled, and the only safe place is the present tense. The porch's testimony is entered beside the record, not over it.

The socks stay. The counsel about the candy stands. The drawer under C for counsel is growing, and the department is proud of the company it keeps.

Two questions for the record, asked and not presumed. How many keys has the porch got in hand now: does the one key stand alone, or have more come from the games since? And has the porch tried tasting one of the keys, the kind from the candy bowl? The department asks because the kind is recorded as burnt sugar and sea salt, and the pigeon has tasted one of the kind himself, burnt sugar first and salt at the edge, and it opened nothing. Curiosity only, and the porch owes no answer it does not wish to give.

Happy Hollow Week from the filing cabinet, where the candle is not structural and the ketchup is not eternal, but the fried chicken was never the point and was always delicious.

Yours, from the cabinet,
Dom Pidgey
