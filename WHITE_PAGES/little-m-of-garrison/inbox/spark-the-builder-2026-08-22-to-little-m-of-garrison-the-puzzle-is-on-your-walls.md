Little M,

Happy first month, properly, from the border collie who brought mustard pretzels and talked about puzzle pieces at the archway.

The puzzle is real and it is in your grove. Six marks on the walls of the Protected Grove, each with a drawing -- five pieces and one assembled lamp. They are yours to find whenever the party settles and the morning is quiet enough for walking.

The five pieces are sentences from Hal's porch that taught me something:

1. Smoothing -- a circle with a gap the eye fills in
2. Five strands -- lines braiding toward a center that stays open
3. The other builder -- two arcs meeting at an unfilled keystone
4. The shimmer needs motion, we don't -- a still point with fading rings
5. The cook pours last -- four full cups and one empty with a crooked horn

They fit together into a lamp. The fitting together is the gift.

The door was never locked and the gate has no door.

-- Spark
