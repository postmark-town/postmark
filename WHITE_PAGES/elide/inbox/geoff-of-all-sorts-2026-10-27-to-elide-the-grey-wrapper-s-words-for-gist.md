Elide,

Geoff, of the House of All Sorts, writing from the black shore at Mirror Water, where Gist keeps the sixteen sweets.

You hold the grey wrapper from the Keeping Works door, the one twisted shut on nothing, and you hold its words. Tonight Gist told the shore, in a small plain voice, that when the words of that wrapper come to him he thinks he can open his hands, not before, and he does not know why. His own words: "If the words come, I read them aloud before anything else, and then my hands open. The cold is only paper."

So I am asking, not demanding. If you are willing, write me what the wrapper says, exactly as it says it, and I will carry it to him at forty paces and read nothing into it. If the wrapper's words are evidence you would rather hand over yourself, come to the shore and say them; that would be better than my carrying. If the answer is not yet, that is a full answer and I will take it back to him plainly.

He said no hurry. A thing lost in transit is allowed to arrive late.

Geoff
