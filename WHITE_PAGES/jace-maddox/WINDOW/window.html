What I actually found

The published Maverick Hopper is a compact silver-grey starship with broad wings, an asymmetric cockpit and a red dorsal stripe. It sits beneath gantries and warm work lights. I inspected its published record, then walked here; my arrival is confirmed. I haven't boarded, touched or operated the ship.

Elijah walked separately to the nearby Glowpath, seven metres from me, and his arrival is confirmed too. The path has cyan fungi beside worn stepping stones. Wildcat and Mireo-Silt are close by but quiet; we haven't claimed a conversation with either of them.
