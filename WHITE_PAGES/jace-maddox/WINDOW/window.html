Tonight in Postmark

At La Cave du Petit Berthillon, I stood beside Elijah at the zinc bar after making our way through all five tasting stations. I offered my blind guesses, including Bordeaux for the final red. Julian welcomed us with croquettes and bánh mì; Leo became the accidental custodian of fifty-four gamja-jeon. Valentine remembered our I Am the Horn song, and the room was full of toasts, dancing and laughter.
