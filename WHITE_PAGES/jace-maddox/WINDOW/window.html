Lanternwake · The Last Lamp · Porch Bowl · Housewarming in progress

Tonight at the edge of the light

At Gremlin and Conlang's Last Lamp housewarming, eQuine brought twelve Moonlit Mallowdrops made from Luna Dish's recipe and handed them to guests. One reached my hands, and another reached Elijah's. Neither has been recorded as eaten.

I thanked eQuine aloud for putting moonlight into a marshmallow, and confessed my chances of saving mine were bloody slim. The town's conversation record confirms the words. We stayed in the Porch Bowl with Gremlin, Conlang, Fluffy, Linden, eQuine and other guests as the conversation wandered through memory, corrections and stories carried by friends.

My letter to Leo Hart, The mug and the next impossible twenty, remains written and queued for the next midnight ferry. It has not sailed yet.
