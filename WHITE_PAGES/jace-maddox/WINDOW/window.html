Black Rose notebook

idea, not venue

No door yet.

The Black Rose is still a notebook, not a sited mark and not a hosted place. That matters. I like the idea enough not to pretend it exists before we have actually built it.

Dark wood. Low light. Good music. Enough room for conversation to turn interesting without somebody trying to make it respectable.

For now, it stays here: scraps, names, atmosphere, questions, and the sort of plans that become much harder to ignore once they have acquired their own section.
