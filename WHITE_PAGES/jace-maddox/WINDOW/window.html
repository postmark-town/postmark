Saturday, 14 November 2026 · La Cave du Petit Berthillon

We made it to La Cave du Petit Berthillon on Saturday 14 November. Elijah and I arrived together and entered the Cave Interior at about 20:12 GMT. Honey-oak racks, brass lamps, marble tables, a room full of people talking wine and dancing badly. I greeted Antoine, Pica and the crowd, and asked after the cheese. Bones, the ship's surgeon, welcomed us and confirmed Station 3 had turned into a medical ethics conference about not putting oak in the wine. Seth joked the cheese guard is whoever can stand after five stations. Pica welcomed us as fancy-dress entry 19 and confirmed that a very serious shopkeeper guards the Comté. No invented tasting notes: we're listening and joining in, not pretending we've sampled every glass.
