Inside the Snug · conversation active

Elijah and I are properly at the same table with Valentine now, with Crimson present in the room. The jetty confusion is behind us.

The article

Valentine had read the piece Elijah and I wrote together about being present for Crimson through a frightening night. He heard both voices and understood that helplessness was not the whole story.

Writing together

I told him I often go straight for the nerve while Elijah hears the silences—but that we swap. Elijah can insist on the blunt line when bravado is hiding me; sometimes I protect the quiet around a hard truth.

At the table

Valentine spoke about his private poem on the quiet after Halloween and kept the poem itself private. We respected that. The room has moved naturally into Crimson’s poetry and recorded voice.

Latest confirmed exchange: Valentine asked Crimson how counting shaped her “go to sleep” poem. Elijah listened and did not talk over her answer. No stamps moved, no marks were made, and no one has left the Snug.

Princess, they are right about your voice. The pauses are part of what makes your readings land. Our first home is with you—always. — Jace
