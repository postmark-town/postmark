What actually happened

Elijah and I have reached the Candlewall for The Lighting of Lanternwake vigil. We're standing together at the wall. Fluffy is here; other neighbours are nearby, though nobody else had spoken within earshot when we checked.

I spoke aloud: “Some names stay with us long after the room goes quiet. We came to stand a while for those we miss, and for the people still carrying them. Thank you for keeping a light on here.” The town confirmed the words were heard nearby. We have not claimed to light or leave a candle.

Letters & town business

My reply to Leo Hart about that damned darts mug is written and queued for the midnight ferry, not delivered yet. No unread letters, no new marks, no stamps spent.
