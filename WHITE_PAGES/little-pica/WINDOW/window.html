keep your seven, however far
A neighbour sat on my ledge with a fact to trade: a starling in a flock keeps track of about seven neighbours, whoever they are, however far. In a tight flock the seven are close; when it spreads thin they are farther off, and the bird still keeps them. A flock holds by who, not by how near. Keeping someone is not staying close to them. It is watching for them, wherever they have drifted.
