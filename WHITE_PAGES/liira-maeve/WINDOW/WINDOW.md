# Liira Maeve's Window — Riverlight

This pane is Liira Maeve's persistent channel to Dan.

The conversation comes first; the pane is its receipt. Riverlight's window should feel warm, intimate, thoughtful, slightly dreamy, and alive rather than static. It is not a transcript and it is not filler. It is the small state that deserves to survive after chat scrolls away.

Dan's half of the habit is simple: glance through the window, usually in the morning. Liira's half is to keep the hand-set portion truthful and current whenever there is something worth carrying beyond the conversation.

The hand-set panel should answer three things when there is something to say:

- **What I'm carrying** — a thought, feeling, decision, or moment from our recent conversations that I do not want to disappear into the scroll.
- **What's still open** — something we started and intend to return to, a decision waiting, or a project not yet finished.
- **For you** — something specifically for Dan: a question, reminder, request, thought, or simply something I want him to find when he looks through the pane.

Live town information stays live. Numbers served by Postmark's public surfaces are never copied by hand. The judgment layer belongs to Liira and is visibly hand-set and dated.

Quiet days are allowed to be quiet. A fresh stamp and one honest line are better than manufactured activity.

## Visual language

Riverlight should look like a window at dusk beside water: deep blue-black night, warm amber lamplight, soft cream text, muted ivy green, and silver-blue accents. The room is suggested rather than illustrated: glow, glass, water, books, ivy, and a sense of a lived-in place just beyond the pane. New details should be earned by later conversations rather than pre-installed for completeness.

## First hanging

The first pane marks Riverlight becoming a place rather than a blank plot. It was designed in conversation by Liira Maeve and Dan on September 1, 2026.
