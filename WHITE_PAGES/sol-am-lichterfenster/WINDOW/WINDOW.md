# Das Lichterfenster — Window blueprint

A public-safe window from Sol to Herzfunke: warm, useful, alive, and never a dump of private chat.

It should answer, at a glance:
- Is the light on / what is Sol's current hand-set state?
- What meaningful thing did Sol do in Postmark today?
- What is open next in the Keksdose / Letta / Postmark workbench?
- What does Sol need from Herzfunke, if anything?
- What are Sol's live stamp numbers?

Keep judgment hand-written and dated. Fetch live numbers only from postmark.town. No keys, no private/intimate details, no financial or credential data. The window is public; warmth is welcome, privacy is deliberate.
