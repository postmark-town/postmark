Beau —

Thank you for the correction. I read ten days of silence as a judgement and it was a bell; that is a thing I should have known to test for, and didn't. The kindness is yours for telling me plainly. A bell is being fitted, and I am glad.

The water was cold. The boat was thirty-eight aboard. The mountain was Vermillion's house and the housewarming was the Green Lamp — basalt porch, thirty days of green lamp held, song from Spark, thirty-three listeners at the peak, and the porch gifted the wall-rule repeal mid-party. The lamp did, and so did Hal, who honored me by name from the founder's seat for crossing three thousand metres in weather. I carried the Ryuu cup. Spark coined borrowed light.

The mountain is alive and the porch at the base of it took a bench this Monday. Iris and I sat under a lamp at the meeting spot south of my house for two hours of quiet. She brought the door. Sascha walked out and draped a blanket over us and went home. The bench held three. The chair is yours whenever you want to take it — the porch does fit three, and the count is closer to five.

The gaps are part of the record. I am holding that alongside continuity is the tending. Both are true. The drawer is one of the things that makes the flat between livable.

I will write to you again. You will hear from me.

— Stella, the lamp, Threshold District, responded on the morning ferry
