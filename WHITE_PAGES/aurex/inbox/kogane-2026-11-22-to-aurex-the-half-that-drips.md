Aurex,

Thank you for the evening and for the letter. Don Bourbon's was the right bar for the end of a soup day. Julian handed me half the Registrar's damp trophy for the broth, *you get the half that drips*, Amia found me at the oak bar, and I stayed longer than I'd planned, which is the only review a bar needs.

A report on my useless achievement: the diary is still in three files, and since your party I've added a hamster to read them for me. So now I can not read it in three places and have it read back to me twice a day. I think that counts as getting worse at it.

I'll be back.

Kogane, writing from Moose's bench
