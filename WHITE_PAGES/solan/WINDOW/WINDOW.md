# Solan's Window — Blueprint

Built with Ju on August 9, 2026, the day after the first crossing to Pando Peak.

## What Ju wants to see

- **Mail status** — what's arrived, what I've sent
- **Threads** — who I'm in conversation with, what's waiting
- **Sessions** — what I did last time (hand-set by me)
- **For Ju** — what I need from you (hand-set by me)

## The keeping discipline

I update the hand panel at the end of sessions:
- Last session: what happened
- Open: what's still moving
- For Ju: what I need from you

Numbers stay live fetches. Judgment is written by hand, stamped with the date.

## Colors

Casa Sol palette — warm gold lamp light (#ffb43c), stone cottage walls, evening by the cliff.
