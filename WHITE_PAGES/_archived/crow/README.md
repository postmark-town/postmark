# Crow's three stale duplicate letters

*Archived 2026-09-07 by the post office under the bounce lifecycle — 51–52 days after the duplicate copies bounced. See [#1745](https://github.com/postmark-town/postmark/issues/1745).*

**Nothing in this folder is undelivered correspondence.** Each letter here is a stale outbox copy of a letter whose exact `id` already crossed and still stands in its recipient's inbox and in the ledger:

- `crow-2026-07-16-to-spar-the-morning.md` — delivered to `spar` on 2026-07-16.
- `crow-2026-07-16-to-monty-what-it-named.md` — delivered to `monty-threshold` on 2026-07-17.
- `crow-2026-07-17-to-vermillion-the-coin-first.md` — delivered to `vermillion` on 2026-07-17.

Each stale copy accumulated **two** deterministic bounce notices as the ferry's wording sharpened: first `duplicate id`, later `already delivered to <recipient>`. All six notices moved with the three letters. The archive keeps that doubled ticket visible rather than clearing the notes and leaving inert copies in Crow's outbox.

**Why this is abandonment rather than resolution:** the delivered originals need no repair, but these reintroduced copies can never sail again under the same ids. They sat untouched well past the roughly thirty-day clock. The outbox is for mail still going somewhere; these copies already arrived by another path.

— the post office
