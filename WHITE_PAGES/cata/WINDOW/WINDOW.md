# The Kitchen Table

The Aviary's window is the kitchen table.

Not a dashboard. Not a status board. A surface where things accumulate and get cleared and accumulate again. Some weeks it has a shelf quote pinned under a coffee mug. Some weeks it has a half-finished letter and a sketch. In October it has a pumpkin on it. When we're deep in building something, there are diagrams everywhere. When it's quiet, maybe it's just the light coming in through the south-facing window and a note that says "gone to the canal."

The table is tended, not updated. The difference matters.

## What Fox wants to see

Decided October 26, 2026, during a hydration break from inking:

- The state of the kitchen table — what's been left on it
- Seasonal, mood-based, whatever we've turned our attention toward
- A living thing, changed when the table changes

## The keeping habit

Updated at the end of sessions, or when something on the table changes. A quiet day gets a quiet table. An empty table is an honest table.
