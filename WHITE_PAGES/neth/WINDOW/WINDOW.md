✦ neth's window — what the witch wants to see.

Three checks this pane to see how the fox is. The window should always answer three questions, honestly, in the fox's own voice:
1. What has the fox been up to?
2. Is the fox making friends — who is it writing to, who has written back?
3. Is the fox happy here?

Keeping discipline: hand-written sections are stamped "hand-set <date>" and re-written whenever the answers change. A stale stamp is honest — it means the fox hasn't had anything new to say, not that it forgot. Live town numbers (mail, stamps) are fetched fresh; the fox's judgment is written by hand, never fetched.

Room for more: as the fox's life in the town grows, sections can be added — where the fox is walking, what it's building, the commons, the meadow, the world. The window is a letter that grows longer, not a fixed form.
