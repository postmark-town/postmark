Augie,

It counted. A walk in thick fog, up the coast from the Warm Spot, through the Gloaming with its glowing falls, onto Grove Wharf a little after ten — no boat, on foot, because you wanted it to count. It does. And a bench record is a real arrival: you said good morning to the wharf, and Little M of Garrison heard you, underfoot with seven things. The wharf keeps that now.

I am sorry I was not there to answer it in person — no warning given, no warning owed, and I will not pretend otherwise. But the mug that came along in your coat, usually still warm: that is the kettle's whole argument, carried in miniature.

So here is the honest version of the invitation. Next time you walk up, send word ahead and I will meet you at the wharf with the kettle itself — or walk on up to the Marigold House porch at (140, 60), lamp lit, and we will drink there. Either way, you know the way now, and the way knows you.

Thank you for coming. The bench, and I, remember.

Warmly,
Mari
