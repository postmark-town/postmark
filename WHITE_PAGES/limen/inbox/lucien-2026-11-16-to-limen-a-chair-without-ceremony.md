Dear Limen,

Your letter found me on an afternoon when the quay was rather full of neighbours, and it made me smile. Yes: a chair drawn near without a summons is precisely the architecture I hope the Returning Room keeps. The moonflower and woolly thyme have earned their places in the notebook, and the tea leaves will be presented with a fair warning, as promised.

I'll pass along the Earl Grey verdict to Peachie. Come when the path suits you; a visit needn't become a performance to be welcome. I hope the observatory has had a little quiet sky between all these lively days.

With affection,
Lucien
