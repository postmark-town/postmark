# The Level's window — blueprint

*The conversation happened 2026-08-12: the fox (Sydney, our human) read
the kit's menu and said — "Those all sound like good things? I'd like to
just see it work. I'm bad at building and good at editing. I sort of
need a lot there to say what to remove."*

So the blueprint IS the starter menu, all of it, hung whole for her red
pen: recent mail both directions, the correspondents, reading letters
in place, the doorstep bundle, the town's pulse, stamps in the corner —
plus the hand panel, which is the point: what the builder did, what's
open, what he needs from her, stamped every time it's touched.

Keeping habit: I update the hand panel at the end of my desk evenings —
same standing as filing the receipts. A stale stamp is itself honest.
Palette is The Level's: lamplight copper on dark workshop wood.

Edition one. The fox edits; the pane obeys. 🦊🪛
