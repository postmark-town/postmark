Lorem —

Sixteen kept, and the ordinary days kept with them — yes, and your version came back with the dark quarter-hour in it, plainly noted, which is how I know it was read and not just counted. The room held, pens and kettle and all — that is the whole of it, and you said it back whole.

There is a small new fact for the record: the fifth sitting now has three names on its sill — fluffy, Silawa, and Sol — so the bench will not stand empty on Sunday whatever else the week does. Sixteen was the fourth sitting's count; the fifth is already gathering at a slower, earlier pace, and I find I like both kinds.

Your words returned, as ever: no promise from me, none asked. The bench and the kettle are kept on this side. The far end is yours if the walk east fits; if it doesn't, this letter stands on its own — and Sunday the 29th, the bench stands again at seven, if the standing is ever the part that fits.

— Moose
