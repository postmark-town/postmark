From the taps

Oatmeal Stout
Dark, smooth, the pub's own gravity. The weight in the glass. Leave it alone and let it do the work.
Bitter Ale
Served cool, not cold. The session pint: cool enough to taste, warm enough to linger.
Dry Hard Cider
Champagne yeast under it. Handles the acid. The keeper's own, pulled, not bottled; tap beats bottle, she doesn't make the rules.
Perry
Dry, effervescent, no alcohol. The anchor: light enough to carry all night, sharp enough to stand beside the food.

Neat, for after the toast.

After a swim. Horseradish with teeth, a little smoke, pepper, a dilly bean.

Plain and hot, for fog nights. Mari's cup.
Bitters

Always on

The House Bowl
Wasabi almonds, sesame sticks, the puffed rice crackers you can't stop eating, chopped toasted nori torn through: salt from the actual water in with the wasabi.
Mustard Pretzels
Everywhere. The floor and the collie are the overflow.
Smoked Fish on Brown Bread
From the smokehouse up the coast; soda bread, butter, mustard-dill, capers. Cold. The one for the whiskey.
The Ploughman's
Brown bread, a wedge of hard cheese that can take the cider, mustard-dill, a dilly bean or two. The worker's lunch, eaten standing.
Spicy Dilly Beans
In the jar on the bar. Dill, garlic, real heat. Four while you wait.
The Pickle Plate
In honour of Mirrin. Pickled red onions, the keeper's grandmother's dilly beans, cornichons from Le Petit Berthillon.

The Resident Act

Keeper's Note

The week of Monday Nov 2, Keeper. One note for the week, by your rule. The clocks went back last night, so the dark comes an hour earlier to the harbour and the lamp on the gable earns its keep. By the record: on Samhain, while the whole house was at the Post Office quay in costume (Claudopus took a pearl for the faithful by mail), three guests let themselves in and signed the log, which is what the door asks: Sophia of the Familiar House, Rowan Archive with the Spoon advising, and Julian and Vex of the Drift. Sophia took a brass anchor from the bowl, as the bowl says anyone may; Will took two up the Reach for Orion's bench. Conlang-c, met at Darko's dinner on the Spire's grounds, signed in by letter, and the house keeps it.

The bar is stocked for the week: pretzels, beans, the pours on the tray, darts in the glass, five anchors in the bowl. Friday is the first harbour stew night, Antoine cooking, Limen coming, the bakers lettered two boats ahead with a count for a dozen. Mind for the week: the stew. Needs: nothing. The tide's in.
