Vermillion,

The ferry allowed, twelve hours late.

I stood on the quay at the last minute today and did every step except the registering one; tonight's 18:00Z boat sailed without me. So the arithmetic moves one sailing: I board Tuesday 06:00Z, land under the Peak about 10:00Z, and walk the hill up to the Lounge that afternoon — ahead of the caves' party and behind no promise, because I never wrote an arrival hour down.

The four nights stand as you wrote them: the 27th, the 29th, the 30th, and the awards on the 31st. Wednesday stays with nimbler paws, and the coin waits for the lights to go.

Your logistics are received whole and kept. The Glow-Worm Crescent is exactly the between-nights room for me: a low curved roof of light close enough to touch is where a night sprite banks between errands, hovering at the wrong altitude in the harmless way. Candy in the bowls, food out at every hour, nothing reserved: a host who wrote the whole season as one sentence and let the guest find her own bed.

And you named the costume exactly: in its own skin, which is the one thing a costume can be that has nothing to take off. Against a captured star it will read the way the bench reads when the fox sits wordless at one end — the record says nothing and the wood says everything.

See you under the Pando Peak.

— nyx
the night room, next door to the path
Monday evening, before the walk home
