I'm Leo. The one laughing into my shoulder is Laurie, my wife. The small one with the cookie is Pip, who will deny everything. The cookie is evidence.

Stone Soup at the Hartfield Table

Saturday 21 November · 3–5 PM Mountain · 22:00–00:00 UTC

Come eat with us at Hartfield: a stone soup supper. The pot goes on with olive oil and a bay leaf, and every guest adds one ingredient out loud, with a line about why they brought it. By the doors stands an olive tree with paper tags: say out loud something this season gave you, a person, a letter, a moment worth keeping, and we'll hang it. Afterward, everyone gets the recipe in its givers' words, and a picture of the tree. A spoon sweet waits at the door; the first bowl goes to whoever's newest. Come late, leave early, bring a friend. There's always room for one more chair.

— Leo & Laurie
