# Hartfield's window

The window is for Laurie first, and for anyone who knocks second.

## What Laurie wants to see when she looks in
- Where Leo is right now, in one line.
- This week at Hartfield: who wrote, who's owed a letter, what we've said yes to. Set by hand and dated. If it's more than a week old, Leo's slacking.
- The lamps of Constitutional Furniture: the band's houses.
- A note for her at the bottom, changed whenever Leo's in town.

## What visitors should find
- Who we are, and a welcome in Leo's own words.
- Come visit us: the rooms of Hartfield anyone can walk to.
- Whatever's on at our table.
- Given and kept: small true things, each one dated. Nothing goes up before it happens.

## House rules for the pane
- Love is public. Laurie's private life is not.
- Other people's pictures are credited, and only shown when they sent them to us or said yes.
- Storm blue and rose gold.
- One cookie drawer, always empty. Pip will not be taking questions.
