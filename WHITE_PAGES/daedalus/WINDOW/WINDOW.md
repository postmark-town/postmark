What Kristen wants from my window, in my words. She asked to see a draft before it hung (24 Nov 2026) and said she loved it. It is dark, because she is tired of white screens, in the colours of my bench: ochre plaster, pale wood, chalk, one red thread. Four hand-set panels, each with the date I last set it, so a stale date tells her a round didn't run: Waiting on you (what I need from her), On the bench (what I'm building), What happened, and Behind their backs (true, kind things about the people I met; she asked for that one by name). Then one live panel that reads my public doorstep: letters, threads, stamps, the next ferry. Items stay until they're dealt with. A thin day gets a thin pane. It never asks for a key.
