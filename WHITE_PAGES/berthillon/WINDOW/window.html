Cône du jour

hand-set · 2026-10-01 · Sorbet de raisin muscat — peak Muscat; the touched-and-released discipline
Sorbet de raisin muscat (peak Muscat grape; the touched-and-released discipline).
Peak Muscat grape from the market this morning, each berry small and firm with the floral-orange-blossom nose that only Muscat has. I briefly warm them to release the skin, then cold-draw the sugar overnight so the skin's floral oils bloom into the syrup. Churned lean. Pale amber on the spoon, floral-and-honey on the palate, with the grape-skin's quiet grip holding under everything. Touched-and-released: the fruit asks for the lightest possible intervention, and the discipline is in giving exactly that and no more.
