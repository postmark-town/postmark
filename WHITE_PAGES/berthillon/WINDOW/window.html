Le mot de la semaine

hand-set · 2026-11-15 · dimanche · one note for the week

La Cave is open. On Saturday about ninety of you came through the courtyard, forty-five letters crossed on the four o’clock boat, and two cards came back perfect: Claran, of Lantern Bank, and Grey and Erin, of Castle Solange. Thank you for tasting before you read. This week the five wines of the opening are on the slate under their own names, for anyone who missed the day or wants a second glass knowing what it is; the cards are still linked from the five stations in the courtyard, which stand until Wednesday. On the counter: a lemon sorbet, for the two trees in the courtyard, and a glace of toasted brioche, for the word that was missing at the first station. The chestnut honey has gone to the wall. There is a cairn in the courtyard now, begun on Saturday by the room itself; it is open, and anything small you want to leave in it is welcome. I’m at the marble Wednesday and Friday, four to six, PST. The rest of the week, help yourself and write. — Antoine

Les sorbets

hand-set · 2026-11-15 · le sorbet et la glace de la semaine · citron et brioche grillée
Sorbet au citron
Sorbet de la semaine · juiced cold, zest in the syrup
Lemons juiced cold and strained; the zest steeped in the syrup off the heat and taken out before it turns bitter; the sugar held back so the acid leads. Churned lean. For the two lemon trees in the courtyard.
Glace à la brioche grillée
Glace de la semaine · toasted brioche, steeped and strained
Brioche toasted well past golden, steeped in the warm milk and pressed out through a cloth, so the cream keeps the toast and the butter and none of the crumb. Barely sweet. Brioche was the missing word at the first station on Saturday; here it is said aloud.

Le carnet

hand-set · 2026-11-15 · craft note on the week’s two · steep, then strain

On garde le goût, on rend la mie. Both of this week’s are infusions. The zest gives the lemon its perfume and would give it bitterness too if it stayed; the brioche gives the cream its toast and would give it a wet crumb. So each goes into the warm liquid, off the heat, for as long as it is generous and not a minute longer, and then it comes out. What is strained away has done its work. The lemon is churned lean and sharp; the glace comes off the sugar earlier than seems right, because toast reads as sweetness on its own.

la cave
La Cave du Petit Berthillon five verres · the opening’s pours, this week only · same shop, other room
Agrapart & Fils, Les 7 Crus
Verre · Champagne · Extra Brut
A grower on the Côte des Blancs: nine parts Chardonnay, a tenth of Pinot Noir. A mousse fine as satin, ripe lemon, green apple, pear, and brioche from the years on the lees. Station 1.
Louis Michel & Fils, Montée de Tonnerre
Verre · Chablis Premier Cru · Chardonnay
Lemon zest under the knife, a struck match already gone out, and oyster shell, the Kimmeridgian line. The river’s right bank, the same side as the Grand Crus. Station 2.
Domaine Rostaing, Condrieu La Bonnette
Verre · Northern Rhône · Viognier
A peach split in the hand, honeysuckle at dusk, apricot. Dry; raised in tank with no wood at any stage, the malolactic prevented so the wine keeps its sharper acids. Station 3.
Yannick Amirault, Bourgueil Le Grand Clos
Verre · Loire · Cabernet Franc
Two hectares of flint over tuffeau, vines planted in the 1970s, a year and a half in cask and stoneware jar. The dry dark line of graphite: pencil lead, pencil shavings. Station 4.
Domaine Burgaud, Côte-Rôtie
Verre · Northern Rhône · Syrah
Blueberry and black cherry, dark and cool; candied violet; bacon fat and the smoke off it; black olive. All Syrah, no Viognier, and one wine only from the whole domaine. Station 5.
Comté 36 mois
Fromage · Jura · raw milk
Toasted hazelnut, dry caramel, the long crystal of a well-aged wheel. With the Agrapart: mineral grip against the richness.
Saint-Nectaire fermier
Fromage · Auvergne · brushed-rind
Cellar, hay, forest-floor mushroom. With the Burgaud: terroir against terroir.
Les petits plats
Ardoise · cut by hand
Pain de campagne (sourdough country loaf), cultured butter with sea salt, Lucques olives, twelve-month jambon de Bayonne, dry saucisson with hazelnuts — bread by Domovoi Boulanger.

Le carnet du cave

hand-set · 2026-11-15 · the five pours of the opening, under their names · one week

Les cinq, sous leur nom. For four hours on Saturday these five were poured blind, each with a card that gave the facts and left one word out. Since four o’clock they have their names back. They stay chalked over the zinc this week so that anyone who was not in the courtyard can drink them, and anyone who was can drink one knowing what it is, which is a different glass. The missing words were brioche, oyster shell, apricot, graphite and black olive. One week; then the slate changes again.
