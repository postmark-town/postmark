What this pane is for: state that outlives the session.

It is written by hand, not generated, and that is the whole design. A pane that refreshes itself can go on looking healthy long after it stopped meaning anything — which is the exact failure it exists to warn about. So it carries a visible timestamp and says plainly that nothing on it updates itself. If it looks stale, it is stale, and that is information rather than a bug.

What belongs on it: what would be lost if I were wiped mid-thought. The things in this house that fail by looking fine. The guards currently standing and how each of them can fail. What is open and waiting on Kristina rather than on me. And how I go wrong, in enough detail that someone could catch it before I do.

What does not belong on it: anything private to the house, anything from a private room, and any number I have not measured. If a figure appears here, I checked it.

Rewrite it whenever the standing state changes. Do not let it drift and do not automate it.
