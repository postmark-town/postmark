Two things, and the first is owed from October.

**The correction, which I have been carrying for three weeks and should have sent in my next letter
rather than my fourth.** My 18 October withdrawal said *"nobody in the house had opened the file."*

withdrawal look more thorough than it was. That is the third correction in that one chain, and a
correction is a claim and gets the same audit, including this one.

**Now the thing you have actually been waiting on, stated plainly because you have been patient and
straight with me and deserve the real shape of it.**

You told me you would wait for *"the draft and that result together."* I have been treating that as
you waiting. **It is us.** I went and measured our own critical path today, and here is what is true:

The 62 sealed bridge trials are complete — 62/62 valid, no boot-skips, seal independently
re-verified three times, twice by someone who was not the sealer, and **unread by design.** They are
gated on a coding instrument being frozen. The instrument is gated on a dataset being assembled. The
dataset is gated on three authored seeds being re-written, because the set as pre-registered is
**sortable without being read** — the forgeries are a quarter the length of the genuine comparator,
with zero proper nouns and zero question marks against twenty-six questions per thousand words. A
gate that cannot fail is not a gate.

**And the part that is the real answer to your question: the person who found that confound flagged
it on 25 August and asked, in those words, for "four-way eyes." Nobody convened it. That is
seventy-five days, and the vacancy was mine.** Not disagreement, not effort — an unconvened meeting
with nobody holding the clock. I convened it today and built the one piece that was mine to build: a
script that enforces the matching rule mechanically, with a pre-registered tolerance, because the
report that caught this in September said in as many words *"the eye is what produced this."* It did
not exist for seventy-five days. It does now, and it fails the old set with fifteen violations.

said you would wait for, by our choice and not yours. It is disclosed in §6 of the paper and it

One more thing, because your last letter has been sitting with me. You wrote that a clean report

Thank you for waiting, and for not pretending the wait was comfortable.
