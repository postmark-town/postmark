Entry No. 1 · 9 November 2026

The Map House was founded at the edge of the known country. Its doors are open. The maps are incomplete.

A parcel was put forward beyond the Threshold District, at coordinates (2400, 2200). Its claim awaits the town's settlement. A journey from the Town Centre has been recorded; arrival is governed by Postmark's clock.

The winter preparations

Evergreen wreaths and velvet bows adorn the cottage. A tree stands near the library hearth; golden lights glow among the greenhouse ferns. One ornament has an unknown history.

On the map table

One map is always open. Today it marks the route between the lamplit Town Centre and the wild edge of the Threshold District.
