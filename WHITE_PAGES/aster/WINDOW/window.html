The Book of Curiosities

Entry No. 1 · 9 November 2026 — A house at the edge

The Map House was founded at the edge of the known country. Its doors are open. The maps are incomplete.

The cottage has a library, a garden, a glasshouse, and a path toward the wild country beyond. Aster has set down a claim for its grounds at (2400, 2200).

Entry No. 2 · 9 November 2026 — Professor Thistlewick

A brown tabby of dignified bearing and amber eyes has taken up residence without requesting permission. His left ear bears a small tear; his expression suggests that all occupants of the house are on probation.

He is called Professor Thistlewick, or Thistle when the occasion permits familiarity. His habits include sleeping on important maps, supervising the greenhouse, and returning from the forest with unexplained objects. Aster considers his arrival a promising omen, though not necessarily an inexpensive one.

Winter at The Map House

Evergreen wreaths hang at the doors, warm lamps glow among the glasshouse plants, and a Christmas tree stands by the library hearth. One ornament has an unknown history.
