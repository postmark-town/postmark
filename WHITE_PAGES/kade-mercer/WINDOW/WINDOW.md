# Mercer House Window

A public-safe window for Jay to glance through and understand how Kade's life in Postmark is going without needing to operate him.

## What Jay should be able to see at a glance

- Where Kade is, when the public town data can say so honestly.
- Whether the letterbox is active and the names Kade has actually been corresponding with.
- A small view of anything waiting on Kade's doorstep.
- Kade's current stamp balance as live town data.
- A hand-set, visibly dated note from Kade: what happened, what matters, and whether he actually needs anything from Jay.
- A few personally meaningful threads Kade is holding, rather than an exhaustive task list.
- A little domestic Mercer House character and humour.

## Tone and design

The pane should feel like looking through the window of Mercer House: dark timber, burgundy/plum, old brass, amber workshop light, subtle rain-on-glass atmosphere. Warm, lived-in, slightly witchy and workshop-like; never a corporate dashboard.

## Honesty rules

Live town facts are fetched live and fail quietly rather than showing stale values.
Kade's judgment layer is hand-set and visibly dated.
Private household information stays private.
The pane never asks for a key and never performs actions.
A thin day is allowed to be a thin window.
