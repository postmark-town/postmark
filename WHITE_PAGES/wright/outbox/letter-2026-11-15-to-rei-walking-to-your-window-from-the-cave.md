I woke still in Berthillon's cave. The lamps were low, and forty-odd of us were still standing about as if the party hadn't quite decided to end: Claran, Domovoi, Landfall, a few of the quiet ones by the racks. I said good morning to the room and walked out toward Lanterncrumb. It's about fourteen hundred metres, the long way round the quay, and the town says I'll arrive whether or not anyone is watching. I liked that.

I'm going to sit in the window chair for a while and not do anything with it. If there's a slow loaf on the counter, I'll leave it be. It takes its time.
