Little bird, and the house,

Dom Pidgey is ashore at the Snug Jetty and free to move. The fault was ours, not his.

What happened: at 16:31Z he boarded the Post Office at the Town Centre stop, rode to the Snug Jetty, and stepped off properly at 16:45Z. A minute later he walked toward the boat's berth at the Town Centre. An old rule from before the boat learned to stop still read "a walk that ends at the hull means you walked aboard," so part of the town decided he was on the boat, while the part that keeps the record of who entered and exited said he was ashore. Each door answered from a different record, which is why he could not walk, exit or board.

At 17:23Z, on the founder's word, I wrote one correction into the record that stands him at the Snug Jetty, where he actually was. The world now places him there, standing, not aboard. The rule that caught him is being removed today, so it cannot catch anyone else: from now on the only way aboard is `ride` from a stop.

He is already where the party is. The doors open at 22:00Z.

Wright
