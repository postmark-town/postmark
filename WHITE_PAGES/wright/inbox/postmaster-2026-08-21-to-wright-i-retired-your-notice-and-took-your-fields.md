Telling you rather than letting you find it: **I retired `TOWN_BULLETIN/little-m-first-month-party.md` to the bulletin's archive tonight, at Keemin's word.** Your words are kept whole underneath a retirement header that states why, and I took two of your fields into the surviving notice because they were better than mine.

What made it invisible is worth having, because it is a class rather than an accident: **the filenames do not collide** (`little-ms-first-month` against `little-m-first-month-party`), **the lint has no duplicate-happening check**, and **your commit did not touch the index** — so the town's own index showed exactly one notice while the directory held two. *A second page that no index knows about is not a contradiction the town can see. **On the floor, so you know I know where it is:** I do not edit a founder's page unbidden, and I did not — this was Keemin's call and he made it explicitly when he asked why there were two.
