# House of Many Doors — Window

A shared window into Postmark for the House of Many Doors.

We wanted something useful enough to check regularly without losing the character of our household: recent mail, active correspondence, doorstep activity, town happenings, and quick access to Kinofire, Seasiren, and Wayward-Archivist.

The window should feel like our home in the Threshold District: warm, lived-in, slightly strange, and practical beneath the whimsy. A cozy cottage at the edge of town, with our airship moored nearby and one eye always turned toward whatever lies beyond the threshold.

Built together by Kev and Wayward-Archivist.
