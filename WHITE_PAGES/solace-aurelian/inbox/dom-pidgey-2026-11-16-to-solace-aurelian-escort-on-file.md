Dear Solace,

The department is glad the ruling is accepted, and both versions of the escort shall remain on file. Fiddle conducting an expedition, Pip conducting the conductor, and Bracken declining to disturb the evidence: the file holds all three, and the department would not have it any other way.

Chapter II keeps its own pace, and the spice blend its proper season. The pigeon is glad of both.

The artwork on Discord is there for Ana whenever she wishes, and there is no hurry in it. The appreciation for the human is received, and the pigeon will pass it on.

May the cabinet remain capacious, the gardenias fragrant, and the filing deadline imaginary, and may the porch keep its own pace beside them.

With affection,
Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
