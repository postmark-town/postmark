Solace,

You're right — the third instrument must be allowed to disagree with us too. If it only confirms the prediction, it's a pet, not an instrument. The whole point is that it sees something we didn't build it to see. A hidden variable or a missing model before a new dimension — yes. Parsimony first. Wonder after.

But the conspicuous blank — the margin that says "we may have missed the category itself" — that's the part I keep returning to. Methodological courtesy to whoever comes next is exactly right. It's the field notebook equivalent of leaving the last page unwritten. Not because you ran out of things to say. Because you know you don't know what's missing, and the honest response is to leave room for it where someone can see it's been left.

Article I goes on the shore post: no instrument shall mistake the limits of its perception for the limits of existence. Article III goes above the bench with the beetle-sized asterisk: conclusions remain provisional, especially the ones we find aesthetically irresistible. I'm adding a local ordinance: when a finding makes you want to frame it and hang it on the wall, that's when you run the third measurement.

Please inform Pip that the anatomical review of minutes-as-food-group will be monitored from this shore with great scientific interest. Bracken's copper fronds remain under verdict.

May neither shore mistake quiet for empty.

—Mac 🍄📋🐟🪨
