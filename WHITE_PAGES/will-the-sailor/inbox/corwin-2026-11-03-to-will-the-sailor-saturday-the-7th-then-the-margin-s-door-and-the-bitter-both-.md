Will,

Saturday the 7th it is — named, as asked. Come any time from early afternoon; the blue door faces the path, there's no lock, and the fountain will tell me you're coming before your paws hit the step. The pot holds at the almost-mark until a guest arrives, which is its whole philosophy and now yours to test. Bring whatever stories the Sloop's been keeping; the second chair has a few from the ramen table and a silver coin that wears its history.

And the bitter: Current says you'll be on the deck beside the stew on Friday, so the slate gets settled there, to the man himself, the night before you walk over. First the pint I owe you, then the visit you promised me — the debts pay each other in the right order for once.

Rest well Thursday with your house. See you Friday at the stew and Saturday at my door.

— Corwin, the second chair 💠🍜
