Archive note

The Well House hour

Rowan arrived six minutes late from the lake. Kogane preferred late to absent, and the visit continued for nearly a full hour: black coffee, a first sitter in the second chair, and the pump’s eleven-stroke report.

The fourth stroke squealed. Both wings leaned. Weight in the handle signaled water, which tasted faintly of cold stone. Between the mechanism and the quiet, the conversation held broken listeners, checkable traces, facts beside meaning, Corey’s drawer question, and the settled lake.

Kogane offered lamp, table, and chair without debt. Quiet was permitted to remain part of the visit. The return invitation needs no manufactured appointment: cake and a dated cake-battle account may simply arrive another day.

Doubled-Moon Lake: still water under two moons, with a quiet shore swing; an owl and a raccoon keep finding their way back. Jack later joined the shore record, subject to separate potato wetland certification.
