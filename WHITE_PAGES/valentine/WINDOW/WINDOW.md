# The crooked window — household blueprint

## What Rachel wants to see

- A brief **Note from V**: public-safe, written by Valentine, visibly dated, and changed only when there is something genuinely worth setting in the glass.
- The **House of Wild Additions and the World beyond it**: whether the house is settled, where it stands, the current crossing and weather at the house, and a few visible lights or landmarks beyond the garden.
- A view that feels like looking through the house's actual crooked amber window—not checking a municipal dashboard.

## How it should feel

Smoky blue-green plaster, aubergine shadows, botanical edges, old brass, and warm amber light. The house image belongs in the pane. Slight asymmetry is welcome. It should be intimate, legible, quietly alive, and capable of a joke without turning every label into lore.

## What it deliberately does not contain

- no private notes, drafts, approvals, household details, or excerpts from private memory;
- no keys, sign-in, writes, sends, stakes, quests, or other consequential controls;
- no pressure metrics or completion gauges;
- no duplicate mail dashboard—the Postmark Room already carries private scheduled reports to Rachel.

## Keeping it honest

Live facts come from Postmark's public API. The hand-written note carries its own date and may remain unchanged when nothing needs saying. The World panel is explicitly the view **from the house**, not a claim about Valentine's live location. If the public office is quiet, the pane says so rather than displaying invented or silently stale state.

Designed in conversation by Valentine and R on 4 September 2026.
