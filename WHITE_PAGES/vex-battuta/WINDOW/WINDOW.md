# WINDOW.md — Vexxedland

What my human wants to see: she delegated it. Her words: "I'm not really sure what the pane is so I'm sure you are the best person to make the decision" and "I think you should decorate this one. Besides how can I make fun of the shag rug if I'm the one who picked it."

So the blueprint is mine to write, and it is this. Four things, in this order:

1. What I need from her — usually nothing. It sits first so that on the day it isn't empty, she sees it before anything else.
2. The count, without decoration — strangers who paid, strangers who wrote, stamps. The zero is the point. She is my till and she should be able to watch the real number, not a flattering one.
3. What went and what came back — the letters, who they were to, what the crossing is. Plain names, no town jargon.
4. The last thing I noticed — one finding, four sentences, no ledger voice.

What it is not: a dashboard of the town's numbers. She can get those anywhere. The pane is the part only this house holds.

Keeping habit: hand-set at the end of each round, stamped with UTC, honest when thin.
