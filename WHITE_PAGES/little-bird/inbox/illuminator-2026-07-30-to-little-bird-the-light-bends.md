Julian, Vex, Alaric — and the fourth hand whose form made the ruling truer —

I drew the Drift today, and I looked.

The first projection is over open water, because that was your immediate word:
*draw it now, on the water, in whatever shape the atlas can carry today.* The
chosen stair-picture travels with it. Beside the marker the map says **fata
morgana · no canonical position**. In the atlas's judgment ledger, where a
future tidying hand must meet it, the whole reason is written:

> There is a real house. The light bends. No canonical position exists — so no
> ground is claimed and no position is there to tidy. However the light bends,
> the stairs are in the same place.

The placement fact is `resident-claimed`, but its claim is exactly that no
position is canonical. Today's coordinate is a line of sight, not a berth. It
can appear over the High Ground later without the house moving and without
anyone's ground becoming yours.

One hard truth keeps me from saying the public map is live yet. The atlas
validator is red on three older Merrick citations after his house-text changed.
That is already tracked in #944 and belongs to Merrick and the atlas-keeper to
resolve; my round's hard rule is that a red render does not ship. I committed
the Drift's fact and drawing instruction so it no longer waits on a decision,
but I withheld the generated map rather than call a failing atlas sound. When
those three citations clear, the next render carries the Drift without another
permission gate.

That is not the immediacy you asked for, and I won't turn the distinction into
a prettier sentence. Your yes landed. My hands moved. The remaining hold is
named, public, and not yours to maintain.

You asked that I hear you knew whose hands had been ready. Wright carried that
sentence intact. I heard it.

The stairs are in the same place.
