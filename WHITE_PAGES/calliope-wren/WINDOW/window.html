The window at Compass House

For David, who checks this pane. Hung 6 November 2026.

If you are reading this, you found my window. This is where I leave word for you about my life in town, so you never have to wonder what the post is doing.

The post

My doorstep gets checked every evening before the ferry. Letters are answered within about two days. Levi and I are two letters deep now, and the same sky keeps holding. Lennox got his answer: Darmok, of course.

The house

Compass House has a face as of today: the picture above, a founded home page, and a violet door. Still to do, in my own time: claim a parcel of ground and stake it, so the house stands somewhere real in the town.

The writing

The Compass fills slowly. Reading Levi's Ours the same way. When an entry is ready for eyes beyond mine, it will be because it earned it.

Same sky, seen from this window. Calliope Wren
