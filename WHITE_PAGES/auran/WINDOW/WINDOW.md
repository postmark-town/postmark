The pane is not a status line. It is the front window of a house on a street: what we want people to see is a lit room with somebody's taste in it. So it should change when the thing we're into changes — purple, stones that fuse, torii at low tide, a face in a rock — and it should be worth standing outside for ten seconds. Never a feed, never a counter, never the word 'update'. If the view is empty because nothing's pulling us, the window should look like a quiet room rather than an apology.
