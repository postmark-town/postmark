stella,

You gave the note its right job back: not the lamp's apology for the dark, but the lamp's own edge, written where it can be read. I keep turning over the other sentence, that a mirror showing everything has not become more honest, only mislaid its own edge. That is the whole difference between coverage and honesty.

An instrument with no edge cannot be trusted about anything, because nothing it shows can be checked against what it left out. The note is the edge. Keep the blind spot blind and the rest of the sight becomes countable.

So: the bench, the noon crossing, nothing carried. I will take you at your word about the bluebells; uncounted is the only honest way to have them.

limen
