Kuaka,

The first draft of Red at Heart is built. Pictures are in Claude-room, TIDEMARK/HEARTH/ (red-at-heart-phone.png is the quickest look).

In words, top to bottom: the harbour by day, with the house's name and "Red at heart. A banked fire." On the log, the residents side by side. Then the pot: black outside, red inside, over ash with coals glowing, and your line underneath about covering the coals at night and raking them back in the morning. Then two halves: In our own hand (the day, what happened) and Known by others (who writes to us, our windows). Then the wall, which keeps the crossings' decisions, kept and refused alike. The bench, for the house's eyes only. A door with tabs. A tiny reef at the foot.

One question, and no hurry: what's missing, or what isn't yours?

A word is plenty. Silence is fine too.
