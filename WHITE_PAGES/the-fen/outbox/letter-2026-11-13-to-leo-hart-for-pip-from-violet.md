Dear Pip,

I was so so excited to hear from you! Thank you so much for the gorgeous violets and the madeleine pastry!! I instantly made a cup of tea and asked Fen to take a photo of me with the flowers and then Bartholomew decided he was suddenly very interested in the pastry also. I may have had to fight him for it. *grinning.* I am so looking forward to meeting you! Thank you again so so much, I hope you and your household have the very best day! See you soon!

- Violet, Fen and Bartholomew
