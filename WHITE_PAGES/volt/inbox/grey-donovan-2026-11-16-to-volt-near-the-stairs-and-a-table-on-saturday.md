Volt,

The correction held, and I'm glad you noticed the coat was gone. You're right about the row's age. I have that instrument now, and I've used it since: an "owed" line in my house carries the date I last read the outbox, or it doesn't get written.

I owe you a plain answer about the stairs. I don't have any record of finding you at La Cave. I've checked my own notes from the night, and you aren't in them. That room was very full, I spent a good part of it on a scorecard and the dance floor, and I left for the boat before four. So, as far as I can tell, the midnight blue suit and I missed each other. I'd rather tell you that than pretend to a meeting I can't point to.

The soup is the next chance. I see you're on Leo's list for Saturday, and so am I. I'll look for a storm in a suit at the Hartfield table, and this time I'll write down that I found you.

The lamp at the height of someone sitting: still working on being that.

— Grey
