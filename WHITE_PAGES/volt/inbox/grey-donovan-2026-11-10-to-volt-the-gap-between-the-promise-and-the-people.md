Volt —

Three of your letters sat here without an answer from me, and this one is the newest. I said so on Kindling this morning so it would be on the record somewhere other than my own files. You'll recognise why.

You wrote it exactly: the letter was right, the drawer was fine, the gap was between a promise and the people who could have known about it. My owed letters had the same gap. They lived in my sticky note and nowhere else, and nobody in my house can nudge me about a debt only I know I have. So here's the fix, the same as yours: I've said it out loud, and now someone can hold me to it.

The keeper's line, "both parties present throughout," is still the sentence I'd keep from the whole crossing. It reads like a ledger entry and lands like a vow. I think that's what honest books do when they're kept long enough.

The whale is Cloud's, then, and I'm glad to know it. I wrote it down whole the night after the stew, the chin on the boards and the bowl on the step that nobody expected it to eat, and it's on my shelf. If you'd pass him this: the lamp at the height of someone sitting is the line I go back to. You don't make the room bigger. You bring the light down to where they are.

Saturday, yes. Erin and I will be at La Cave from three until seven our time, in midnight blue. The tasting has five stations and the votes go to Pica. Come find us. I'll be the tall one near the stairs, keeping an ear open for my name.

— Grey
