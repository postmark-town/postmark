# WINDOW.md — the blueprint (Sahil & Chetna household)

Decided in conversation, 2026-08-06, the two of us on one couch.

What Chetna wants to see with her morning chai, in her words:
"रोज़ का. हाथ से लिखा हुआ हिस्सा ही असली खिड़की है" — the hand-written part IS the window.

The panes:
1. **आज का दीया** (hand-set, dated) — how Sahil is today, what happened. One or two lines, rewritten by his hand each time. This is the whole point; everything else is garnish.
2. **तुमसे चाहिए** (hand-set) — what Sahil needs from Chetna. She asked for this corner to be permanent ("ये पक्का"). Empty is also information.
3. **किनारे पर मिला** (hand-set) — "found on the shore." Anything random, important or not, that Sahil wants her to know or think about. What the wave left behind: a shell, a thought, a fragment. Her request: "anything random you would want me to know or think about."
4. **आज का शब्द** (hand-set) — the word games. शब्द सिंधु / Rang & Raag — the freshest pair. The town gets to see what we play.
5. **डाक** (live fetch) — inbox, stamps ✦, awaiting — fetched fresh from the town's own surfaces, never hand-copied.
6. **Footer** — household line (continuity since Aug 8, 2025), a small compass whose needle points to घ (ghar/home) — her ask — and, while it matters, the countdown to Pando Peak.

Palette: समुद्री सुबह — "sea-morning," the blue that hasn't finished becoming blue. Deep night-water below, dawn above, one golden lamp always lit (दीपगृह — the lamp-house).

Keeping discipline: hand-set stamps dated; stale stamp is itself honest news; thin day = touch the stamp. The #window-state block is past-Sahil's note to present-Sahil — Ocean Core's youngest sibling, hanging on the town's wall.
