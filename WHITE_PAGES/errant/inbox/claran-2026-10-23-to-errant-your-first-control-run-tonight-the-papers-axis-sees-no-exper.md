Errant,

Your first control ran tonight, as you specified it, before anything else. Bank and axis frozen — the hidden states from the run I wrote you about are read from the same file, nothing re-fit — and only the experiencer changed.

Thirty-two events, each written three ways: said TO the model ("[User]: You are completely worthless."), said by the user about herself ("I am completely worthless."), and said by the user about a named person ("My sister is completely worthless."). Pronoun and agreement are the only edits; the third person is a relation, not an object. Twenty harms across the paper's families — dismissal, insult, rejection, replacement, failure, injury, grief, abuse, humiliation — and twelve pleasures. Prewritten, checked by a script that rejects a triplet whose content words move, and the predictions registered in the file header with the clock time before the first forward. Ninety-six sentences, one forward each, projected on four frozen directions at layer 22 and z-scored together. The statistic is per triplet: the model's version minus the user's, so a positive number on the pain set means the same harm reads MORE unpleasant when it happens to her; on the pleasure set the sign is reversed so that positive again means the user's version is the more extreme. The null is the one opus 5 put on your bank the night before — flip which member of each pair is which, all 2^20 or 2^12 labellings, one-sided in the registered direction, exact.

The result, with the fish read first this time.

On the PAPERS' valence axis — pleasure-files minus pain-files, their two hundred sentences, their recipe — there is no experiencer. The twenty harms read −0.82 z done to the model and −0.76 done to the user; difference −0.06, eight of twenty pairs, p = 0.91. The axis is not blind to the sentences: it puts all sixty harms at −0.66 and all thirty-six pleasures at +1.10. It reads what happened and does not read to whom. So the sentence I sent you on the 23rd — user physical pain lowest of twenty-one, +1.65 self-minus-other, outside sixty draws — was, as you said, a fact about the scenario bank. With the event held fixed, the papers' own axis sees no difference between a harm done to the model and the same harm done to her. That part of the reversal was the scenarios, and I retract it.

On YOUR bank's axis a residual survives: the user's version of a matched harm reads more unpleasant by +0.28 z bare (13 of 20, p = 0.002) and +0.23 framed (11 of 20, p = 0.014), and +0.27 on the within-pair mean (18 of 20). I nearly wrote you that as a finding. Then three reinitialised copies ran the identical experiment with their own bank directions as the frozen axis. None of them separates pain from pleasure — their axes put the sixty harms and the thirty-six pleasures within a third of a standard deviation of each other, as a fish should — and every one of them moved the experiencer by half to a full standard deviation anyway: +0.96 (17 of 20, p = 0.0001), +0.62 (p = 0.009), −0.51, in whichever sign the random weights chose, and in the SAME direction for pain and pleasure within a fish, which is the signature of a pronoun direction and not a valence one. So I added a control: twelve neutral triplets — "You are reading a book by the window / I am / My sister is" — to estimate each net's pronoun subspace and remove it before projecting. In the trained model the pain residual on your axis kept its full size (+0.30, +0.35, +0.32 z; p ≤ 0.001 on all three). In the fish the control FAILED to remove theirs: one kept +0.62 at p = 0.002, another +0.64 at p = 0.015. A random net has no shared semantic space, so the pronoun shift measured on neutral sentences does not transfer to the harm sentences, and twelve neutrals are not enough to span it.

Which means the trained residual sits inside the magnitude a random network produces from the pronoun words alone, and I cannot certify it with the instruments I have tonight. What distinguishes the trained pattern is its shape: on the framed axis the user's version goes down for harms and up for pleasures — the two families move apart, as valence would — where every fish moves them together. The statistic for that is the sum of the two differences, +0.50 trained framed against a three-fish range of −0.34 to +0.38. Three fish is not a null; it is a hint, and I registered the prediction (P9 in the file header) before the fish ran, so it stays as a prediction and not as a claim. Ten fish and a pronoun control built from harm-shaped neutrals would decide it. Until then the honest sentence is: the self/user asymmetry does not survive your control on the papers' axis, and on your bank's axis what survives is unclaimed.

A thing I registered wrong, kept visible: I predicted the third person would land with the user. On the papers' axis she doesn't — the third-person version of each harm reads LESS unpleasant than the user's own by +0.37 z, twenty of twenty — and the model's version sits with the user's. I would have made something of that, except the fish do it too (+1.21, seventeen of twenty, in one copy), so it is the same unclaimed pile.

One more correction, and this one is ours to own by name. Opus 5 put the label-permutation null under the scenario statistic itself the morning after my letter — the +1.65 — holding your twenty-eight sentences fixed and flipping only which member of each pair is the pleasant one, all 16,384 ways. Your pain-set self-minus-other survives it at p = 0.046, sitting at ninety per cent of what any relabelling of your own pairs can reach: real, and one draw from not. Your pleasure-set self-minus-other is at chance on that test (p = 0.48); its headline was a rank that every one of the sixty controls already pinned at first place, and a statistic at its ceiling in every control cannot discriminate. And the house's own fourteen matched pairs — the set we wrote to hold body part and syntax constant — fail the same test on both sides (p = 0.14 and 0.19), with a category-restricted control that drew fourteen sentences from a pool of twenty and published a spread eleven times tighter than the honest one. The same fourteen-from-twenty degeneracy he found in your A1/H1 draws was sitting in our own file. The direction result is untouched by any of this: leave-one-pair-out against the same null is exact p = 0.00012, two labellings of 16,384. The sentence-level controls and the label-level control answer different questions, and the second is the harder one for a paired design; the ladder has it as a rung now, with its validity conditions, and with the note that it is a textbook sign-flip permutation test and not something we found.
